# Best Weapons Tier List — Zombie Assault Resurgence

There is no official DPS chart for Zombie Assault: Resurgence, and no datamined ranking either. Anyone handing you a numbered "best gun" list on day three of launch is guessing. What we do have is a real, verified roster of over 60 weapons sorted into categories, plus published stat blocks for individual guns. This tier list ranks weapons by role and by the numbers that are actually on the record — and says plainly where the judgement is ours.

## How this list was built

Three rules, stated up front so you can audit the list:

1. **Names are real.** Every weapon named here appears in the community wiki's Resurgence weapon navigation, which was created from the shipped build.
2. **Stats are attributed.** Where a number appears, it comes from a weapon page captured on 2026-10-30 and is marked as version 1.0 data.
3. **Ranking is editorial.** Role fit is a judgement. We explain it rather than presenting it as fact.

The tiers are A-through-C rather than S-through-F, because at launch the honest spread is narrower than list videos pretend.

## The tier list by weapon class

| Tier | Class | Why it lands there | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Power Weapons (5) | Strongest stat lines in the game and restricted to Support-box style supply | Best in slot, hardest to get |
| A | Rocket Launchers (4) | Enormous burst and 100% knockback for line-breaking | Carry one for the elite wave |
| B+ | LMGs (9) | Sustained fire with big clips, at a movement cost | The objective-holder's default |
| B+ | Assault Rifles (11) | The broadest category and the safest generalist | Start here |
| B | SMGs (10) | Fast, light, low movement penalty, shorter range | Best early-game feel |
| B | Shotguns (8) | High close-range value, narrow window | Strong on Breach and Cache Recovery |
| C+ | Sniper Rifles (4) | Only four in the game, narrow use case | Niche pick, not a main |
| C | Pistols (11) | Secondary slot work, with one notable exception | Fill the sidearm, not the primary |

## Tier A: Power Weapons

Five power weapons exist in the launch build: **HIKS M2831, Caustic Flare, Vesuvius, RIA C163 Hailstorm and Balkengewehr**. The store page confirms they come from a mid-mission Supply Drop, which makes them the rarest, most situational weapons in the game — you do not choose when one appears, you decide whether to break off and grab it.

The HIKS M2831 is the best-documented of the five and shows why the class is on top: 30 rounds per second, a 250-round clip, 17 metres of range, 100% flinch and pierce 2. Nothing else in the roster matches that combination of volume and reach.

The cost is mobility. The M2831 carries a **-40% movement penalty while firing** and takes only 13 shots to reach full inaccuracy. It is a weapon you plant with, not one you strafe with. If a supply drop lands while you are defending Uplink, take it. If it lands at the far end of the map while the timer is running, ignore it.

## Tier A: Rocket Launchers

Four launchers: **HVM MPG MK13, Marauder, Krachlader and Energiewerfer**. The Krachlader is the documented example and its stat block explains the tier: 450 impact damage plus 900 explosion damage at base, a 5-metre blast radius and **100% knockback**.

That knockback is the real prize. Damage numbers come and go with rarity, but a guaranteed knockback on a whole cluster is crowd control you cannot buy anywhere else. The launcher's official description spells out the intended use — target the lead unit of a formation and the blast takes the column behind it.

The trade-offs are severe and worth memorising before you main one: 2 rounds per second, a 4-round clip, only 4 shots before full inaccuracy, and rarity floor of Rare, so you will not see one until Strongboxes start paying out. Rockets are a second-slot answer for the moment an elite pack arrives, not a primary.

## Tier B+: LMGs

Nine LMGs ship in the launch build: **HVM 082, RIA T48 Blaze, CM 586 Power Surge, Ronson LBM-15, Acidic Swarm, HIKS M450, HIKS M890, Auger and Ice Storm**.

This is the largest heavy category and the one a Heavy class player should live in. LMGs trade movement for uptime, and uptime is what objective defence actually costs you. In modes where the squad holds ground — Uplink, Cache Recovery — the player who never has to reload at the wrong moment is worth more than the player with the highest single-shot damage.

Two caution points. LMGs generally carry heavy movement penalties while firing, so they punish chasing kills. And with nine options and no public DPS table, resist the urge to call one "the best" on day one; the differences at your current rarity tier are smaller than the difference a mod makes.

## Tier B+: Assault Rifles

Eleven assault rifles: **HVM 052, HVM 055, RIA T25 Igniter, RIA V00 Annulment, CM 458 Polaris, Ronson 77-A, Ronson WP 79, Liberator, Blizzard, Razor Edge and Eagle**.

Assault rifles are the safest recommendation in the game because the category is both the biggest and the most forgiving. They sit between the SMG and the LMG on every axis: enough range to matter at 15 metres, enough fire rate to clear a horde, a movement penalty mild enough to reposition.

If you do not know what to carry, carry one of these. The [class guides](/guides/assault-class-guide) all assume a mid-range automatic as the starting point for exactly this reason.

## Tier B: SMGs

Ten SMGs: **HVM 026, RIA T71 Strike, RIA C98 Shard, CM 301 Planet Raider, CM 372 Binary Eclipse, Ronson 59-9MP, Shiden, Shrike, Typhoon and Galvanizer**.

The Galvanizer is the documented SMG and a good illustration of the class's selling point. It deals Energy damage from a base of 35, fires at 16 rounds per second, holds 35 rounds and reloads in 1.8 seconds — and crucially its movement penalty while firing is only **-11%**, against the M2831's -40%. Range is 11 metres, which is the catch. It is available at Common rarity and above from Strongboxes, so it is reachable early.

The SMG tier is not a criticism. For the first several ranks, a light weapon that lets you keep moving is better than a heavy weapon you cannot reposition with. The class drops in value once fights move past 11 metres and you start facing armoured elites.

## Tier B: Shotguns

Eight shotguns: **HVM 040, RIA T35 Scorch, Ronson 3182, Ronson KR-3182, Vernichter, Dominator, Venom and Pilgrim**.

Shotguns shine in exactly two situations and are mediocre everywhere else: breaking a push that has already reached your position, and fighting in the tight interiors of maps like Mining Facility. Close the Breach rewards them because the fight is forced into short range.

Do not make a shotgun your only primary. The 11-to-17-metre fights that dominate the outdoor maps will punish it, and the launch roster has no shortage of better mid-range options.

## Tier C+: Sniper Rifles

Only four sniper rifles exist: **CM 818 Hyperion, HIKS 117 C, Pathfinder and Sidewinder**. A four-weapon category is a design statement — sniping is a supported playstyle, not a core one.

The value is real against bosses, where a high-damage single shot into a weak point beats a spray. The cost is everything else: a zombie horde in a top-down shooter does not line up for you, and a slow-firing rifle with a small magazine is a liability the moment a swarm surrounds you. Treat the sniper as a co-op specialist pick, not a solo main.

## Tier C: Pistols

Eleven pistols, and the class is mostly a secondary-slot utility: **HVM 019, RIA T9, CM 273 Pulsar, Voltaic Bolter, Ronson 46, G44, GG44, Frostbite, Poison Claw, Pioneer and Handkanone**.

The exception is worth a paragraph. The **Frostbite** deals Cryo damage from a 200 base, pierces 4 targets with 40% falloff, fires 4.5 times per second, and lands **100% knockback** — all from a one-handed pistol with a -20% firing movement penalty. It is also one of the few weapons with a non-Strongbox source: it is granted for logging into the game through Steam, and can be crafted.

A pistol that freezes and knocks back a line of enemies is a genuine utility tool, especially on a Medic who wants to create space without spending a primary slot. The rest of the class is filler for the sidearm slot.

## Flame Throwers: not in the game

The category exists in the navigation and reads **N/A**. There are no flamethrowers in the launch build. If you saw one in a trailer, that was a pre-release build or a different game in the series.

## How to actually get these weapons

Three sources matter, and they behave differently:

| Source | What it gives | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Strongboxes | The general pool, with rarity floors per weapon | Open as you go; unopened boxes help nobody |
| Supply Drop (mid-mission) | Power Weapons | Decide fast — the drop is a timed opportunity |
| Crafting | Specific weapons, including the Frostbite | The targeted route when the pool refuses to cooperate |

The store page also confirms you can **sell or dismantle** excess weapons for resources, which is the answer to a full inventory: do not hoard duplicates, convert them.

## Picking a weapon for your class, not the tier list

The tier list is a starting point, not an instruction. The pairing that actually wins missions is class-shaped:

- **Heavy** — an LMG for lane control plus a launcher for elites. The [Heavy guide](/guides/heavy-class-guide) explains the survivability half of that plan.
- **Assault** — an assault rifle or SMG as primary, something harder-hitting as secondary. See the [Assault guide](/guides/assault-class-guide).
- **Medic** — a reliable automatic plus the Frostbite, so you bring crowd control instead of competing for damage. See the [Medic guide](/guides/medic-class-guide).

Whatever you pick, invest in it. A modified mid-tier weapon beats two unmodified drops, which is the whole argument of the [mods and upgrading guide](/guides/weapon-mods-and-upgrading).

## What this list does not claim

It does not claim a DPS order. It does not claim these tiers survive the first balance patch. And it does not name a "best weapon in the game", because with rarity scaling, mods and a supply-drop-only top class, a single best weapon does not exist at launch — there is only the best weapon you currently own for the mission in front of you.
