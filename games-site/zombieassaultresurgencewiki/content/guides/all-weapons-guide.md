# All Weapons List — Zombie Assault Resurgence Arsenal

Zombie Assault: Resurgence advertises over 60 primary and secondary weapons, and the launch build's weapon navigation confirms the exact shape of the arsenal: nine gun categories plus a scrapped list, split across seven manufacturers. This page is the complete roster, organised the way the game organises it — by gun type, then by maker — with a note on what each category is actually for.

## The arsenal at a glance

| Category | Count | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Pistols | 11 | Secondary and utility |
| SMGs | 10 | Fast close-range primary |
| Assault Rifles | 11 | Generalist primary |
| Shotguns | 8 | Close-range burst |
| Sniper Rifles | 4 | Long-range specialist |
| Rocket Launchers | 4 | Crowd control and elites |
| Flame Throwers | N/A | Not in the launch build |
| LMGs | 9 | Sustained fire |
| Power Weapons | 5 | Supply Drop only |
| Scrapped | 7 | Not obtainable |

That is 62 obtainable entries across eight live categories, which lines up with the "over 60" claim on the store page. The Flame Throwers row is not a placeholder on our side — the game's own weapon navigation lists it as **N/A**, meaning the category exists in the data structure with nothing in it.

## Pistols (11)

**HVM 019 · RIA T9 · CM 273 Pulsar · Voltaic Bolter · Ronson 46 · G44 · GG44 · Frostbite · Poison Claw · Pioneer · Handkanone**

Pistols fill the sidearm slot and, with one exception, are not worth building a character around. They are one-handed, light, and give you something to fire during a reload without costing your primary slot.

The exception is the **Frostbite**, Morlok's cryo pistol. It deals Cryo damage from a 200 base with 4 pierce at 40% falloff, fires 4.5 rounds per second and lands 100% knockback, all at a modest -20% movement penalty while firing. It is also the most reliably obtainable "special" weapon in the game: awarded for logging into the game through Steam and craftable afterwards, rather than being locked behind a Strongbox roll.

Two naming quirks are worth knowing so you are not confused by wiki links. **G44** and **GG44** are listed separately — one is the manufacturer tag, the other the weapon entry, and searches for either land in the same family. **Handkanone** is German for "hand cannon" and sits in the pistol class despite the name.

## SMGs (10)

**HVM 026 · RIA T71 Strike · RIA C98 Shard · CM 301 Planet Raider · CM 372 Binary Eclipse · Ronson 59-9MP · Shiden · Shrike · Typhoon · Galvanizer**

SMGs are the early-game comfort class: short, light, fast to reload, cheap in movement penalty. The documented example is the **Galvanizer**, a Morlok Energy SMG at 35 base damage, 16 rounds per second, a 35-round clip and a 1.8-second reload, with only an 11% movement penalty while firing.

The category's limit is range — the Galvanizer reaches 11 metres, which is comfortable indoors and cramped on the outdoor maps. Pair an SMG with something that covers distance, or accept that you will be closing gaps you would rather not close.

## Assault Rifles (11)

**HVM 052 · HVM 055 · RIA T25 Igniter · RIA V00 Annulment · CM 458 Polaris · Ronson 77-A · Ronson WP 79 · Liberator · Blizzard · Razor Edge · Eagle**

The biggest category, and the one to start in. Assault rifles do not win any single comparison — an SMG is faster, an LMG holds more, a sniper reaches further — but they are competitive on every axis at once, which is what a generalist needs.

The RIA entries cluster around T-series and V-series designations (T25 Igniter, V00 Annulment) while Critical Mass uses a CM-plus-number scheme (CM 458 Polaris). The unbranded names — Liberator, Blizzard, Razor Edge, Eagle — are what the game calls its named or unique-tier rifles.

## Shotguns (8)

**HVM 040 · RIA T35 Scorch · Ronson 3182 · Ronson KR-3182 · Vernichter · Dominator · Venom · Pilgrim**

Shotguns trade range for close-range authority. In a top-down shooter where enemies converge on a point, that is a real trade rather than a joke pick — the problem is only that the surrounding maps are often more open than the weapon wants.

Note the Ronson pair: **3182** and **KR-3182** are separate entries, with the KR variant sitting alongside the base model the way real firearms families do. Vernichter ("destroyer") and Dominator sit at the named end of the class.

## Sniper Rifles (4)

**CM 818 Hyperion · HIKS 117 C · Pathfinder · Sidewinder**

Four weapons is a deliberate design statement. Sniping exists in Resurgence, but it is a specialist role rather than a main. The payoff is single-target damage into boss weak points; the cost is a slow cycle rate in a game whose default threat is a swarm.

HIKS uses a mixed naming scheme here — **HIKS 117 C** is a numbered entry, while Pathfinder and Sidewinder are named models, both of which appear in other games' arsenals and should be read as this game's own versions.

## Rocket Launchers (4)

**HVM MPG MK13 · Marauder · Krachlader · Energiewerfer**

Launchers are the crowd-control slot. The **Krachlader** is the documented example, and its pairing of 450 impact plus 900 explosion damage with 100% knockback explains the class's purpose: break a formation, not chip it.

Two naming notes. First, the Krachlader was previously listed as **Auslegerlader** during the pre-release builds; the rename landed around the 0.9 patch cycle, so older guides and videos use the old name for the same weapon. Second, these are the only weapons in the arsenal with a German naming streak — Krachlader, Energiewerfer ("energy thrower") and the shotgun Vernichter all follow it.

## LMGs (9)

**HVM 082 · RIA T48 Blaze · CM 586 Power Surge · Ronson LBM-15 · Acidic Swarm · HIKS M450 · HIKS M890 · Auger · Ice Storm**

The largest heavy category, and the one that gives the [Heavy class](/guides/heavy-class-guide) its identity. LMGs are built for uptime: big clips, sustained fire, and a movement penalty that punishes you for wandering.

The category mixes numbered manufacturer entries (HVM 082, HIKS M450 and M890, Ronson LBM-15) with named models (Auger, Ice Storm, Acidic Swarm). **Acidic Swarm** and **Ice Storm** signal elemental identity in the name — a useful hint that damage type varies within the class, which matters when you meet an enemy that resists one flavour.

## Power Weapons (5)

**HIKS M2831 · Caustic Flare · Vesuvius · RIA C163 Hailstorm · Balkengewehr**

The top of the arsenal, and the only class the store page singles out: these come from a **mid-mission Supply Drop** rather than from Strongboxes. The HIKS M2831 shows the payoff — 30 rounds per second, a 250-round clip, 17 metres of range, 100% flinch — and the cost, a 40% movement penalty while firing.

Because the supply drop is a timed mid-mission event, power weapons are a decision as much as a reward. Breaking off to grab one can be the right call or the run-ending mistake, depending on how far the objective timer has advanced.

## The manufacturers

Seven makers appear across the roster, and the prefixes are how you tell guns apart at a glance in the Strongbox screen.

| Maker | Prefix | Typical entries | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| HVM | HVM | HVM 019, HVM 040, HVM 082, HVM MPG MK13 | Numbered, broad spread across classes |
| RIA | RIA | RIA T9, RIA C98 Shard, RIA T48 Blaze | T-series and C-series designations |
| Critical Mass | CM | CM 273 Pulsar, CM 586 Power Surge, CM 818 Hyperion | CM plus a number and a name |
| Ronson | Ronson | Ronson 46, Ronson 59-9MP, Ronson LBM-15 | Model numbers, some with suffixes |
| HIKS | HIKS | HIKS 117 C, HIKS M450, HIKS M2831 | Heaviest stat lines, power weapons |
| Morlok | — | Frostbite, Galvanizer | Named only, no number prefix |
| G44 / GG | G44, GG | G44, GG44, GG17 | Pistol and scrapped entries |

Morlok is the odd one out: it does not use a numeric prefix at all, and its two documented weapons — the Frostbite pistol and the Galvanizer SMG — are both named rather than designated. When you see a weapon with a name and no number, Morlok is the likely maker.

## The scrapped list, and why it matters

Seven entries are marked **Scrapped**: **HVM 004, HIKS A10, HIKS S300, HIKS R40, HIKS 888 CAW, GG17 and Festungsbrecher**.

These are weapons that exist in the data but are not obtainable. It matters for two practical reasons. First, if you see one in a pre-release video or a data-mining thread, do not go looking for it in Strongboxes — it is not there. Second, when a guide lists "all weapons" and includes HVM 004, that guide is reading the data files rather than playing the launch build.

## Where weapons come from

The store page defines four acquisition routes, and they are not interchangeable:

| Route | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Strongboxes | The general pool, with a rarity floor per weapon (Galvanizer at Common and higher, Krachlader at Rare and higher) |
| Supply Drop | Power Weapons only, mid-mission and timed |
| Crafting | Targeted acquisition, including the Frostbite |
| Vendors and reputation | Confirmed on the store page as part of the wider economy |

Selling and dismantling excess weapons feeds the last two routes, so a duplicate drop is not a wasted drop.
