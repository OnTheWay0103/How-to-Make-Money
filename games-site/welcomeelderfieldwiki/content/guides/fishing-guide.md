# Welcome to Elderfield Fishing Guide

Fishing is one of Elderfield's most reliable **food and income** sources — but it is not available from the start. In the demo, you have to **unlock the fishing rod**, and the route to it is a small quest of its own. This guide covers the community-verified route and what fishing is for.

## Do You Need the Rod Early?

Yes. Fishing is a confirmed life-sim activity — the official description calls the game a "full RPG life sim with relationships, customization, **fishing** and more." In practice, it is one of the best ways to turn idle time into food and money, and it gives you a reason to explore the lake area.

## Where Is the Fishing Rod?

The **fishing rod is located at the lake**, but you cannot simply walk there from town. The community-verified route:

1. **Find the playground manhole** — the sewer entrance is at the playground, past the garbage man and another character [Community].
2. **Enter the sewer** and make your way through.
3. **Reach the lake** — the rod is there.

Expect to fight **two monsters** on the way (three if you take the wrong path) [Community].

## The Faster Shortcut

There is a quicker route with fewer fights:

- The **manhole at the shop** connects directly to the lake [Community].
- This path involves **only one fight**.
- **Avoid the house path** — it triggers a **third monster encounter**; the other path to the lake has a ladder leading up [Community].

So: use the **shop manhole → lake** shortcut to minimize fights, especially early when your equipment is basic.

## What to Bring on the Trip

- **Combat supplies** — you will fight (1–3 encounters depending on path). Bring healing/items.
- **Nothing else specific** — the rod is a pick-up, not a boss reward.

## How to Fish

The exact fishing mini-game mechanics (cast timing, bite timing, reel) are **not officially documented** [Unconfirmed]. Based on the confirmed system and life-sim genre norms:

- Equip the rod at the lake.
- Cast into the water and wait for a bite.
- React to the bite prompt (timing/button press).

We will document the exact fishing mechanic after launch, once it is verified.

## Why Fishing Is Worth It

- **Reliable food** — fish feed you and support the rest loop.
- **Income** — surplus fish sell for money, funding upgrades and seeds.
- **Exploration synergy** — the lake trip also covers **2 of the 7 demo mysteries** (sewer + a lake-adjacent area) and connects to the map network.

## Fishing Tips (Pre-Release)

- **Unlock the rod early** in the demo — the lake route is short and high-value.
- **Take the shop-manhole shortcut** to minimize fights.
- **Pair the trip with mystery hunting** — the sewer/lake network covers several investigation points.
- **Fish as a daily routine** once unlocked — it turns low-energy days into productive ones.

## What Fish Are For

Beyond selling, fish feed the core loops [Editorial]:

- **Food for rest** — fish are a renewable ingredient for the cooking system, which supports the rest tradeoffs (baths, sleep, curses).
- **Quest ingredients** — life-sim games routinely use fish in cooking and request quests; expect the same here [Unconfirmed].
- **Gift items** — townsfolk relationships often accept fish as gifts, though Elderfield's gift system is [Unconfirmed].

Treat fishing as a **multi-purpose resource**, not just a money printer: the value compounds through cooking and requests.

## When to Fish (Daily Rhythm)

Fishing has no confirmed time/season restrictions in the full game [Unconfirmed], so in the demo the best rhythm is:

- **Fish in the morning** after farm chores, before exploration — fish are a safe, low-risk use of leftover energy.
- **Fish on rest-heavy days** — if you plan to bath/sleep, fishing gives you productive income without combat risk.
- **Pair with mystery runs** — the lake route covers the sewer and lake mysteries, so combine trips.

## Common Fishing Mistakes

- **Postponing the rod quest** — the route is short (1–3 fights), and the payoff is your best early income.
- **Taking the long path** — use the shop-manhole shortcut; the house path adds a third fight.
- **Selling all fish raw** — cook some for food and rest support; it pays off over a playthrough.
