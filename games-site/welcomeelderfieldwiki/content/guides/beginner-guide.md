# Welcome to Elderfield Beginner Guide

## What Is Welcome to Elderfield?

**Welcome to Elderfield** is a **cosy horror RPG** developed by solo developer **Chris Cote** and published by **Kwalee**. You arrive in the isolated town of Elderfield — your birthplace, with a mysterious past — where the townsfolk are strange, the local news stops making sense, and leaving rarely seems like an option.

The game pairs **Stardew Valley-style farming and life-sim** mechanics with **Lovecraftian turn-based combat**, hand-drawn horror-manga art, a dark lofi soundtrack by **Dated**, and daily mystery investigation. It is often described as "cozy horror": relaxing on the surface, unsettling underneath.

It launches **September 10, 2026** on Steam (PC, Windows only, single-player). A free **demo** covering roughly **1/6** of the planned game is available now.

## Before You Start

### 1. Try the Demo First

The demo is a vertical slice with one area of the town, the farm and ranch, the first dungeon (a local mall), and a sewer/lake shortcut. It is the best way to decide if the "cozy horror" blend is for you, and it currently holds a ~96% "Overwhelmingly Positive" rating. Your demo progress does not carry over to the full game [Unconfirmed] — treat it as a learning experience, not a head start.

### 2. Pick a Mode That Fits You

The Steam store description confirms two difficulty modes:

- **Cozy Mode** — a laid-back experience for players who want a peaceful life of harvest and discovery.
- **Challenge Mode** — for players who crave a challenge; the Old Gods are waiting. This tests your skills and resource building through in-depth turn-based combat.

If you are new to farming games **or** new to turn-based combat, start with **Cozy Mode** on your first run. You can always restart — the game is single-player and save-based, so there is no permanent penalty for switching.

### 3. Know the Daily Loop

Elderfield is a day-based life-sim. A typical day looks like:

1. **Wake up** — check your energy/health and any lingering effects from the night.
2. **Tend the farm** — water crops, harvest what is ready, manage livestock, and deal with any tentacles that have grown overnight.
3. **Gather resources** — mine, forage, and fish to stockpile materials.
4. **Cook and craft** — turn what you gathered into food (for rest and energy) and crafted goods.
5. **Investigate** — look for mysteries in the town and the mall.
6. **Rest** — choose how to spend the night (see the rest guide for the tradeoffs).

## The Core Loop Explained

Farming is the engine that feeds everything else:

- **Crops** include ordinary corn and pumpkins alongside unsettling ones like **teeth and eyeballs**. Crops give you food, income, and crafting materials.
- **Livestock** live on the ranch. Manage them daily for their produce.
- **Tentacles** grow on your land and must be stopped — the store description names this as a core farm chore.
- **Mining, foraging, and fishing** give you resources the farm cannot produce. The demo community has verified that the fishing rod is found at the lake, reached through the sewer via the playground manhole.

Turn-based **combat** is triggered by exploring: cutting grass or breaking rocks can start a fight [Community], and dungeon areas like the mall contain real encounters. Fights use action points (AP), gathered resources, buffs, and equipment — and **fleeing is a legitimate option**.

## Mistakes Beginners Should Avoid

- **Ignoring rest.** Rest is a real tradeoff: baths restore health but reset monster positions, and sleeping can lift curses or introduce them [Community]. Plan your night instead of going to bed by default.
- **Skipping the fishing rod.** It is gated behind a small quest (sewer → lake), but fishing is one of the most reliable food and income sources in the demo.
- **Under-preparing for the mall.** The mall is the demo's first dungeon — stock food and supplies before you go in.
- **Treating combat as a chore to avoid.** In Challenge Mode, combat and resource management are the point. Learn the AP system in the demo.
- **Expecting romance at launch.** The developer has said romance/marriage is planned but **not yet implemented** [Unconfirmed]. Enjoy the friendship system now; treat romance as a future feature.
