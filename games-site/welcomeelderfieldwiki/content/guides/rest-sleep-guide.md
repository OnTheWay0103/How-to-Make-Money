# Welcome to Elderfield How Rest Works

In most farming games, rest is a formality — you sleep, you wake up healed. **In Elderfield, rest is a tradeoff.** The demo's rest mechanics turn the end of the day into a decision: how you recover shapes where the monsters are and whether you wake up cursed.

Because the full game launches September 10, 2026, the demo's confirmed behavior is your best guide, and anything deeper is [Unconfirmed].

## The Two Rest Options

### Bathing

Community-verified demo behavior [Community]:

- **Baths restore health** — a strong, direct heal.
- **But baths reset monster positions** — the creatures of Elderfield reposition, which can change your next day's routes.

This makes the bath a **strategic heal**: take it when you need the health back and can handle whatever the reset brings.

### Sleeping

Community-verified demo behavior [Community]:

- **Sleeping can lift curses** — a way to clear negative effects.
- **Sleeping can also introduce curses** — going to bed carries a chance of waking up worse off.

Sleep is therefore **uncertain**. You trade the risk of a curse for a new day. This is a core part of Elderfield's horror tension — even your own bed is not entirely safe.

## How This Changes Your Daily Loop

Because rest has consequences, the end of the day is a planning step, not an afterthought:

- **Check your health and curse status** before choosing.
- **If you are healthy and curse-free**, a plain sleep is lower-risk.
- **If you are badly hurt**, the bath's heal is valuable — but budget for the monster reset (avoid planning a low-combat next day right after).
- **If you are cursed**, sleeping is your known way to try to lift it — accept the risk.

## Rest in Cozy Mode vs Challenge Mode

- **Cozy Mode** — rest tradeoffs are manageable; the uncertainty is more atmosphere than threat.
- **Challenge Mode** — the rest decision matters more: a monster reset or a surprise curse can cascade into a bad day. Stocking food and planning nights is part of "resource building" that the store description ties to Challenge Mode.

## Tips for Managing Rest

- **Stock food** — good meals support recovery and give you options at night.
- **Don't auto-sleep** — always check your status first.
- **Plan post-bath days carefully** — right after a bath, monsters are somewhere new; avoid heading straight into the mall or sewer.
- **Use curses as quest fuel** — some interactions may interact with curse state [Unconfirmed], so a curse is not always pure downside.
- **Accept the unknown** — Elderfield is a cozy *horror* game; a little uncertainty at night is the point.

## Signs You Need a Bath vs a Plain Sleep

A quick pre-release decision guide [Editorial]:

| Situation | Best Choice |
|-----------|-------------|
| Low health, monster positions don't matter today | **Bath** — take the heal, accept the reset |
| Low health, but you plan to avoid combat tomorrow | **Bath** — the reset is less risky when you stay home |
| Healthy, curse-free | **Sleep** — low risk, gets you to the next day |
| Cursed | **Sleep** — the known way to try to lift it, at the risk of another curse |
| In Challenge Mode, mid-quest | **Sleep and re-prepare** — a fresh day is safer than a bath that relocates monsters onto your route |

The theme is: **bath when health matters more than position; sleep when status matters more than certainty.**

## Curse Management (Pre-Release)

Curses are a real mechanic in the demo, but their full catalogue is unverified [Community]. General management principles:

- **Don't panic-clear every curse.** Some interactions may use curse state as a trigger [Unconfirmed] — a curse is not always pure downside.
- **Clear curses before big content.** If you plan to tackle the mall or a dungeon, don't go in cursed if you can help it — sleep first and accept the risk.
- **Track what caused it.** Knowing which actions introduced a curse helps you avoid it next time.

We will document the full curse list and their exact effects after launch.

## What Is Still [Unconfirmed]

- **Curse types and effects** — the demo shows curses exist; the full catalogue of curse effects is unverified.
- **Rest mechanics in the full game** — whether bath/sleep remain the only options, and whether beds/inns differ, is unknown.
- **Other healing** — food-based healing and any other recovery methods are expected but not fully documented.
