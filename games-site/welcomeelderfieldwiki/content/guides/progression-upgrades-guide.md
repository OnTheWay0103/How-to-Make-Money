# Welcome to Elderfield Progression & Upgrades Guide

Elderfield is a life-sim, so "progression" is more than leveling — it's the growth of your farm, your home, your relationships, and your understanding of the town. The full game launches September 10, 2026, so this guide covers the **confirmed** progression systems and flags the deeper structure as [Unconfirmed].

## Confirmed Progression Systems

### Farm & Economy Growth

Your farm is the base progression engine [Official]:

- Expand **crops and livestock** to raise daily income.
- Unlock **fishing, mining, and foraging** to add income streams.
- **Money funds everything else** — seeds, upgrades, customization, and combat items. See the [Money Guide](/guides/money-making-guide).

### Home & Character Customization

The store description confirms **customization** as a core life-sim system [Official]:

- **Home customization** — decorate and improve your living space.
- **Character customization** — personalize your farmer/character.

The depth (furniture catalogues, outfit options, upgrade tiers) is [Unconfirmed], but the system is real.

### Relationships & Friendship

Relationships are a confirmed progression track [Official]:

- **Befriend the townsfolk** — each has a relationship tree.
- The developer plans for players to **reach the end of a relationship with any character** [Developer Statement].
- **Romance/marriage** — planned but **not yet implemented**; friendship is the playable track at launch [Unconfirmed].

Relationship progress likely unlocks gifts, perks, and story beats — full details are [Unconfirmed].

### Mystery & Investigation Progress

Mysteries are the town's quest/progression loop [Official/Community]:

- Investigating mysteries completes quests (the demo's 7-mystery quest).
- Mystery progress likely drives the main story and unlocks areas.

### Combat & Equipment Growth

Combat progression feeds off resources [Official]:

- Turn-based combat uses **diverse equipment** and crafted items.
- The mall dungeon (demo) is the progression gate — clearing it advances your combat capability.
- In **Challenge Mode**, combat and resource-building are the core progression pillars.

## Expected Long-Term Systems [Unconfirmed]

Coverage suggests deeper systems that are not yet confirmed in detail:

- **Old God alignment** — a progression/alignment layer tied to the Old Gods difficulty framing [Unconfirmed].
- **Calendar-based events** — seasonal/festival events expected in the full game [Unconfirmed].
- **Full town scope** — the full map is expected to be ~3x the demo town, with at least two more dungeons [Unconfirmed].

Treat all of these as expectations, not facts, until launch.

## A Practical Progression Order

Based on the confirmed systems, a sensible pre-launch plan:

1. **Stabilize income** — crops + fishing first (see the [Money Guide](/guides/money-making-guide)).
2. **Unlock the fishing rod** — the lake route adds your best early income.
3. **Clear the mall** — the demo's progression gate; better gear + loot.
4. **Complete the mysteries** — quest progress and story.
5. **Befriend the townsfolk** — relationship trees are a real progression track.
6. **Customize your home** — cosmetic, but a confirmed system that makes the town yours.

## Milestones to Aim For

A demo-testable milestone order that works as a pre-launch dry run [Editorial]:

1. **Stable income** — crops + fishing running on a daily rhythm.
2. **Fishing rod unlocked** — the lake route becomes your income upgrade.
3. **Mall cleared** — your combat and loot milestone.
4. **All 7 mysteries solved** — your investigation milestone.
5. **Every townsperson befriended** — your relationship milestone.
6. **Home customized** — your personalization milestone.

Each milestone feeds the next, and all are reachable in the demo — which makes it a solid rehearsal for the full game's progression rhythm. If you only have time for two, do the fishing rod and the mall clear: they unlock the biggest income and combat gains.

## How to Track Your Progress

Because Elderfield is a life-sim, progress runs on several tracks at once. A simple pre-release tracking habit [Editorial]:

- **Daily:** farm income and rest quality (bath vs sleep tradeoffs).
- **Weekly:** mysteries solved, mall clears, relationship heart gains.
- **Always:** money in the bank and combat supplies stocked.

Keeping a mental scoreboard of *income + combat-readiness + relationships + mysteries* stops you from fixating on one track and neglecting the others.

## Common Progression Mistakes

- **Chasing money without a goal** — income is a means to upgrades, not an end.
- **Ignoring relationships** — friendship is a confirmed progression track; don't skip the townsfolk.
- **Under-using the mall** — it is the demo's main content gate; clear it.
- **Expecting romance at launch** — planned but not implemented [Unconfirmed]; invest in friendship instead.
