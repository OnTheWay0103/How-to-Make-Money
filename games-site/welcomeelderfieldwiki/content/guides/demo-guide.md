# Welcome to Elderfield Demo Guide

## Is There a Demo?

Yes. **Welcome to Elderfield has a free demo on Steam** (App ID **3195680**), first released on **October 7, 2024**. It is a **vertical slice** of the full game — the developer has said it covers roughly **1/6** of the planned content, so it is a real chunk of Elderfield rather than a timed teaser.

The demo currently holds a **~96% positive** rating across **~550 reviews** on Steam — "Overwhelmingly Positive" — which is unusually strong for a pre-release slice.

## How to Download the Demo

1. Open the [Steam demo page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3195680/Welcome_to_Elderfield_Demo/).
2. Click **"Download Demo"** (free).
3. Install and launch from your Steam library.

You do **not** need to own the full game or wishlist it to play the demo, though wishlisting the full game (App 3195440) is the easiest way to get a launch notification.

## What Is in the Demo?

According to the official demo listing and community reports, the demo includes:

- **One area of the town** — a slice of Elderfield proper, including NPCs and the local news vibe that defines the atmosphere.
- **The farm and ranch** — the core farming loop: crops (including unsettling ones like teeth and eyeballs), livestock, and the tentacle problem.
- **The first dungeon: the local mall** — a "dead mall" to explore and fight through.
- **The sewer and lake** — a connecting area; the fishing rod is found at the lake, reached via the sewer entrance at the playground manhole.
- **Several smaller locations** — investigation spots, including the demo's 7 mysteries (5 in the mall, 1 in the sewer, 1 in Dave's house).

### What Is NOT in the Demo

The full game is expected to be **roughly 3x the demo's town size** with **at least two more dungeon areas** [Unconfirmed]. Systems that are confirmed as planned but not yet implemented include **romance and marriage** (developer statement) and, per coverage, **Old God alignment and calendar-based events** [Unconfirmed]. None of these are in the demo.

## How Long Is the Demo?

There is no official time estimate. Community reports suggest a thorough playthrough — farming, fishing, the mall, and all 7 mysteries — takes roughly **2–4 hours**, depending on how much you optimize. A quick look at the systems can be done in under an hour.

## Does Demo Progress Carry Over?

**Not confirmed.** The demo is a separate Steam app (3195680) from the full game (3195440), and the developer has not stated that demo saves transfer. Expect your demo progress to be a learning experience rather than a head start, and treat that as [Unconfirmed] until the developer addresses it.

## What to Prioritize in the Demo

- **Grab the fishing rod early.** It is at the lake, reached through the sewer at the playground manhole (community-verified route; expect a couple of fights on the way).
- **Clear the mall at least once.** It is the demo's only dungeon and the best preview of the full game's combat.
- **Find the 7 mysteries.** They are community-verified and give you a feel for how investigation works in the full game. See the [Mysteries Guide](/guides/mysteries-guide) for locations.
- **Test both difficulty modes.** If you are deciding between Cozy and Challenge for launch, the demo is your only hands-on chance before September 10.

## Frequently Asked

**Is the demo free forever?** Yes — it is a free, separate Steam app (3195680) that stays downloadable [Official].

**Can I play the demo without owning the full game?** Yes — no purchase or wishlist is required [Official].

**Does the demo include all of the farm systems?** It includes the core farm loop (crops, ranch, tentacles) but not the full upgrade/expansion tree [Unconfirmed].

**Will the demo be updated before launch?** The developer has not announced further demo updates; the full game launches September 10, 2026 [Unconfirmed].

## Should You Play the Demo?

Play it if you:
- Like farming/life-sim games (Stardew Valley, Moonlight Peaks) and are curious about a horror twist.
- Want to evaluate the combat before committing to Challenge Mode.
- Want to see whether the "cozy horror" tone lands for you — the demo's art, music, and daily-loop tension are representative of the full game.

Maybe skip or wait if you:
- Prefer to experience games completely fresh at launch without spoiling the town.
- Only buy games after verified full-release reviews (the demo is strong, but the full game is unreleased).
