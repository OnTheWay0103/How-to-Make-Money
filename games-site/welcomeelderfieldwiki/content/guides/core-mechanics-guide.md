# Welcome to Elderfield Core Mechanics

Welcome to Elderfield is a **day-based cosy horror life-sim**: you manage a farm, gather resources, befriend odd townsfolk, investigate mysteries, and survive nights that are never quite safe. This guide explains how the core systems connect, using the official Steam description and community-verified demo reports. Because the full game launches September 10, 2026, some depth is still [Unconfirmed].

## The Daily Loop

A full day in Elderfield follows a loop that feeds into itself:

1. **Morning** — wake, check energy and any lingering effects, plan the day.
2. **Farm work** — water, harvest, manage livestock, stop tentacles.
3. **Gathering** — mine, forage, and fish to bring in resources the farm can't produce.
4. **Crafting & cooking** — turn resources into food, tools, and items.
5. **Exploration & investigation** — explore the town and the mall, look for mysteries.
6. **Combat (as needed)** — dungeon areas and random encounters.
7. **Rest** — choose a night activity, accepting its tradeoffs.

## Farming & Resource Systems

Farming is the economic engine. The official description confirms: **"Build your farm, manage animals, mine and forage to build resources."**

- **Crops** — ordinary corn and pumpkins, plus unsettling ones like **teeth and eyeballs** [Community]. Crops provide food, income, and crafting materials.
- **Tentacles** — unruly growth on your land that you must **stop**; the store description names this as a farm chore.
- **Livestock** — otherworldly animals on the ranch that need daily care and give produce.
- **Mining & foraging** — resource gathering in the wilds that complements crop income.
- **Fishing** — available once you reach the lake (via the sewer); a reliable food and money source.

## Turn-Based Combat

Combat is **turn-based with action-point (AP) costs**, per the store description. Key mechanics reported from the demo [Community]:

- Fights use **gathered resources**, **strategic buffs**, and **diverse equipment**.
- **Fleeing is a legitimate option** — you do not have to fight everything.
- Mundane actions can trigger fights: **cutting grass or breaking rocks** can start an encounter.
- Dungeon areas (the local mall in the demo) contain real, unavoidable-style encounters.

In **Challenge Mode**, combat and resource management are the core challenge; in **Cozy Mode**, they are much lighter.

## Rest as a Tradeoff

Rest is not a free heal. Community-verified demo mechanics [Community]:

- **Baths restore health but reset monster positions** — a strong heal with a positioning cost.
- **Sleeping can lift curses or introduce them** — an uncertain night can change your next day.

This makes rest a **decision**, not a routine. See the [Rest & Sleep Guide](/guides/rest-sleep-guide) for the full picture.

## Probabilistic Interactions

Elderfield adds dice-roll tension to mundane life-sim actions [Community]:

- **Opening trash cans** involves a dice roll.
- Resource actions (cutting grass, breaking rocks) can trigger fights.

The effect is that even "safe" daily tasks carry a small chance of consequences — a core part of the cozy-horror tone.

## Mystery Investigation

The town is full of oddities. Investigating **mysteries** — interaction points in the world — is a confirmed activity. The demo contains **7 community-verified mysteries** (5 in the mall, 1 in the sewer, 1 in Dave's house). See the [Mysteries Guide](/guides/mysteries-guide).

## Relationships & Customization

The store description confirms a **"full RPG life sim with relationships, customization, fishing and more."** You can befriend the townsfolk; **romance and marriage are planned but not yet implemented** per the developer [Unconfirmed]. Home and character customization are confirmed but their depth is undocumented.

## How the Systems Feed Each Other

The loop is self-reinforcing:

```
Farm → food + income + materials
  ↓
Cook → food for rest/energy → longer, safer days
  ↓
Mine/forage/fish → resources for crafting + combat items
  ↓
Combat → loot + progress → harder areas open
  ↓
Mysteries → unlocks, story, and progression
  ↓
Rest → tradeoffs that shape the next day
```

Every system eventually routes back into the farm or the combat kit, so there is no "right" daily template — prioritize based on your mode and goals.
