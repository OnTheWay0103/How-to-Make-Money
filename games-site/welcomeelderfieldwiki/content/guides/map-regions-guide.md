# Welcome to Elderfield Map & Areas Guide

Welcome to Elderfield takes place in the isolated town of Elderfield — your birthplace, with a mysterious past. The **demo** (roughly 1/6 of the planned game) shows a meaningful slice of the map. This guide covers every confirmed area and what the full release is expected to add.

## Confirmed Areas (Demo + Official)

### The Town

The demo includes **one area of the town**. Elderfield is described as a town whose residents are increasingly inexplicable and whose local news stops making sense. The demo's town slice includes several buildings and NPCs, plus the **playground** with its **manhole** that leads to the sewer.

### Your Farm

The farm is your home base and the core loop. In the demo you can:

- **Cultivate land** and plant crops (including unsettling ones like teeth and eyeballs).
- **Harvest** and manage your produce.
- **Deal with tentacles** that grow on your land.

The full farm's size and expansion options are [Unconfirmed].

### The Ranch

Next to the farm, the **ranch** holds your livestock. The store description confirms you **manage animals**; demo reports describe **otherworldly livestock**. The ranch is a daily-care area that feeds the economy.

### The Local Mall (First Dungeon)

The mall is the demo's **first dungeon** — a "dead mall" with:

- **Real combat encounters** (a preview of the full game's turn-based fights).
- **Mystery investigation spots** — 5 of the demo's 7 mysteries are in the mall.
- **Closed stores and an arcade** — the arcade machine mystery is in a closed store at the mall [Community].

Treat the mall as the demo's main content gate and the best preview of full-game combat.

### The Sewer

The sewer is reached via the **playground manhole** [Community]. It connects to:

- **The lake** (where the fishing rod is found).
- **Dave's house** (one of the 7 mysteries is in Dave's house).

There is a shortcut: the manhole at the shop connects to the lake and involves only one fight, versus the longer house path which triggers a third encounter [Community].

### The Lake

The lake is where you get the **fishing rod** — the unlock that enables fishing. It is the demo's fishing area and one of the best food/income sources. (See the [Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide).)

### Smaller Locations

The demo also includes **several smaller locations** — investigation spots and side areas. The 7 mysteries are spread across the mall (5), the sewer (1), and Dave's house (1) [Community].

## What the Full Release Is Expected to Add

Because the demo is a vertical slice, the full map is larger. Based on official/coverage statements, expect [Unconfirmed]:

- **~3x the town size** — the full game's town is expected to be about three times the demo's slice.
- **At least two more dungeon areas** beyond the mall.
- **Full NPC schedules** — more town residents with daily routines.
- **Old God / mystery content** — deeper investigation and story areas.

None of the above is confirmed in detail; treat it as scope expectation, not fact.

## Navigation Tips

- **Unlock the lake shortcut early.** The shop manhole → lake route is faster and has fewer fights than the house path [Community].
- **The mall is the demo's difficulty spike.** Go stocked with food and combat supplies.
- **Playground manhole is your sewer gateway.** Remember it — it connects the town to the sewer/lake network.
- **Investigate everything.** Mystery spots are interaction points; some are event-based, while at least 6 are always accessible [Community].

## Frequently Asked

**How big is the demo map?** One slice of the town, your farm, the ranch, the mall dungeon, the sewer, and the lake — roughly 1/6 of the planned game [Official].

**How do I get to the lake?** Through the sewer, via the playground manhole; the shop-manhole shortcut reaches it with only one fight versus the longer house path [Community].

**Is the mall a real dungeon?** Yes — it is the demo's first dungeon with turn-based combat encounters and 5 of the 7 mystery spots [Official/Community].

**Can I leave Elderfield?** Narratively no — the game's premise is that those who arrive rarely find a reason or option to leave [Official]. There is no world map beyond the town's areas.

**Are there more dungeons in the full game?** Expected — at least two more dungeon areas beyond the mall, per coverage [Unconfirmed].

**Is the whole farm playable in the demo?** The farm is functional (plant, harvest, deal with tentacles), but its full expansion options are [Unconfirmed].
