# Welcome to Elderfield Release Date, Price & Platforms

**Welcome to Elderfield releases on September 10, 2026** on Steam. The date was confirmed in an official **release-date reveal trailer on August 5, 2026**, after the game had been demo-able since October 7, 2024.

**The official price has not been announced** as of August 30, 2026 — the Steam page lists the game as "not yet available" with no price [Unconfirmed].

- The **demo is free** (App 3195680) and is a substantial vertical slice (~1/6 of the planned game).
