# Welcome to Elderfield Price, Release Date & Platforms

## Is Welcome to Elderfield Out Now?

**Yes.** Welcome to Elderfield was released on **September 10, 2026** on Steam for PC. It is a full release, not an Early Access title. The Steam appdetails API returns `coming_soon: false` and `release_date: "Sep 10, 2026"` as of October 24, 2026.

## How Much Does Welcome to Elderfield Cost?

The base game costs **$17.99 USD** on Steam (checked October 24, 2026, with no active discount).

A few things worth knowing:

- The **demo is free** (App 3195680) and remains a substantial vertical slice (~1/6 of the game).
- The only extra listed on Steam is a **$4.99 soundtrack DLC** (App 5145770). There is **no Deluxe or Ultimate edition** on the store page as of October 24, 2026.
- Steam uses **regional pricing**, so the price in your currency may differ from the US figure. The $17.99 USD amount is the verified US listing.

## Regional Pricing & Currency

Steam adjusts local prices by region, so the storefront in your country may show a different amount and currency from the US $17.99 listing. We have only independently verified the US price.

- **Buy from the Steam storefront** rather than third-party key resellers.
- **Check the current price at checkout** — Steam prices and discounts can change.
- **Soundtrack DLC is optional** and does not affect access to the game.
