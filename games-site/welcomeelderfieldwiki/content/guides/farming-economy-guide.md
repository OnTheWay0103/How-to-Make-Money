# Welcome to Elderfield Farming & Resources Guide

The farm is the heart of Welcome to Elderfield. The official store description confirms: **"Build your farm, manage animals, mine and forage to build resources."** Everything else — cooking, combat prep, income, rest — feeds off what the farm and the wilds provide. Because the full game launches September 10, 2026, crop lists and exact prices are [Unconfirmed] where not in the demo.

## The Farm Loop

The basic farm day is:

1. **Cultivate land** — till, water, and plant seeds.
2. **Harvest** — collect ripe crops.
3. **Manage livestock** — tend the ranch animals for their produce.
4. **Stop tentacles** — deal with the unruly growth that appears on your land.
5. **Sell or process** — turn harvest into money, food, or crafting materials.

## Crops: The Familiar and the Unsettling

The demo shows two kinds of crops [Community]:

- **Ordinary crops** — corn, pumpkins, and standard farm staples.
- **Horror crops** — strange ones like **teeth and eyeballs**, which fit the game's horror-manga art style.

Because crop lists and season/price data are not officially published, treat any full crop catalogue as [Unconfirmed] until launch. What matters mechanically is that crops are the farm's **income and food engine** — prioritize consistent harvests over exotic crops early on.

## Livestock on the Ranch

The store description confirms you **manage animals** on the ranch. Demo reports indicate **otherworldly livestock** — animals that fit the town's tone rather than standard farm animals. Details (which animals, what they produce, daily care requirements) are [Unconfirmed] until verified after launch.

## Tentacles: Your Recurring Chore

The official description explicitly says you must **"stop the growth of unruly tentacles."** In the demo, tentacles appear on the farm and must be dealt with regularly [Community]. Treat them as a recurring maintenance task — like weeds in Stardew Valley, but with more personality. Leaving them unchecked may cost you crop space or safety [Unconfirmed].

## Mining & Foraging

Mining and foraging are confirmed on the store page as resource sources. In the demo, resource gathering in the wilds **can trigger fights** when you cut grass or break rocks [Community] — so bring a little combat readiness even on gather runs.

- **Foraging** — wild plants and materials in the town's outskirts.
- **Mining** — ore and stone for crafting and building upgrades.

Exact node locations and material lists are [Unconfirmed] beyond what the demo shows.

## Fishing

Fishing is a confirmed life-sim activity and one of the most reliable early income and food sources. The **fishing rod** is found at the **lake**, reached through the **sewer** via the **playground manhole** (community-verified route; expect a couple of fights on the way). See the [Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide) for the full route.

## Cooking & Crafting

Cooking turns farm produce and gathered ingredients into **food**, which supports rest and recovery. Crafting turns materials into **tools and equipment** — including combat items. The demo includes a cooking/crafting loop, but the full recipe list is [Unconfirmed].

## Resource Priorities by Mode

**Cozy Mode:**
- Farm and fish first — they are low-pressure and profitable.
- Gather resources for cooking so your nights are comfortable.
- Let combat come to you rather than seeking it out.

**Challenge Mode:**
- Treat **every** resource as combat prep — stock buffs, equipment, and healing.
- Mine and forage aggressively; combat triggers mean resources and fights arrive together.
- Keep a reserve of food for rest tradeoffs (baths reset monsters, sleep can curse you).

## Common Mistakes

- **Ignoring tentacles** — they are a named chore for a reason; stay on top of them.
- **Skipping fishing** — it is gated but high-value; the rod is worth the short quest.
- **Hoarding everything** — crops and fish are meant to be sold/processed; money funds the real progression.
- **Gathering without combat prep** — grass/rock actions can start fights; carry at least a basic loadout.

## A Sample First-Week Farm Plan

A low-risk plan you can run in the demo to learn the loop [Editorial]:

- **Day 1:** Till and plant a consistent crop (corn/pumpkin), clear any tentacles, and water everything before night.
- **Day 2:** Unlock the fishing rod via the sewer/lake route, then fish a little to stock food.
- **Day 3–4:** Harvest, replant, and process surplus into food; do one short forage run for materials.
- **Day 5:** Before the mall, stock healing and combat supplies — the mall is the demo's difficulty spike.
- **Day 6–7:** Clear the mall once for the loot bump, then reinvest into seeds or livestock.

This keeps farming, gathering, and combat in balance instead of over-focusing on any single system.
