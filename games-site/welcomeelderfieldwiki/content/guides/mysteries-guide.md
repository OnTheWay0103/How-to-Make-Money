# Welcome to Elderfield Mysteries & Investigation Guide

Elderfield is full of things that make you want to **"investigate further?"** These interaction points are called **mysteries**, and investigating them is a confirmed part of the game's daily loop. The demo contains **7 mysteries** with community-verified locations — this guide shows you where to find them.

## How Investigation Works

- Mysteries are **interaction points in the world** — standing near one prompts "investigate further?" [Community].
- **Some are event-based** (they appear or unlock under conditions), while **at least 6 are accessible at any time** [Community].
- **TV/news events do not count** as mysteries [Community] — they are atmospheric, not collectible.
- Solving any **television mystery** after finding the other mysteries **completes the 7-mystery quest** [Community].

## All 7 Mystery Locations in the Demo

Community-verified locations [Community]:

| # | Location | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | **Goo pile at the food court** | In the mall, food court area |
| 2 | **Counter at the sports store** | Mall, sports store |
| 3 | **ATM** | Mall |
| 4 | **Arcade** | Mall — check the **arcade machine in the closed store** |
| 5 | **Sewer** | One mystery is in the sewer area |
| 6 | **Dave's house** | One mystery is in Dave's house |
| 7 | **The Tree (under the sports store)** | Stand on the plank near the hole and interact to the left |

> **Tip:** The 7th mystery (The Tree) is easy to miss. It is **under the sports store** — stand on the plank near the hole and interact to the left [Community].

## The Mall Route (Mysteries 1–5)

The mall holds **5 of the 7 mysteries**, so a single mall run can clear most of the investigation:

1. **Food court** — check the goo pile.
2. **Sports store** — check the counter, then the **Tree** underneath (stand on the plank near the hole, interact left).
3. **ATM** — interact with it.
4. **Arcade** — check the arcade machine, including in the **closed store** at the mall.

## The Sewer & Dave's House (Mysteries 6–7)

- The **sewer** (reached via the **playground manhole**) contains one mystery.
- **Dave's house** contains one mystery.

These two connect to the sewer/lake network — if you are going for the fishing rod anyway, you can grab both on the same trip.

## Why Mysteries Matter

Beyond the collectible/quest value, mysteries are the **investigation loop** of the game:

- They teach you how investigation works before the full game's larger mystery web.
- They feed the game's **cosy-horror tone** — the town's oddities are the story.
- Completing the 7-mystery quest gives a sense of the full game's quest structure.

## What the Full Game Will Add

The demo is ~1/6 of the planned game, so the full release is expected to have **far more mysteries** across a town ~3x the demo size, with more dungeons [Unconfirmed]. Event-based mysteries and the TV/news thread will likely expand into the full story.

## Tips

- **Investigate everything you see** — some mysteries are always accessible, so there is no downside to poking around.
- **Do the mall in one trip** — 5 of 7 mysteries are there; combine it with a dungeon run.
- **Pair sewer mysteries with the fishing-rod trip** — the sewer/lake network covers 2 mysteries plus the rod.
- **Watch the TV news** — solving a TV mystery after finding the others completes the quest [Community].

## Frequently Asked

**Do I have to solve all 7 mysteries to finish the demo?** No — the 7-mystery quest is a completionist goal. The mall (mysteries 1–5) is the demo's dungeon content; the quest is optional [Community].

**Are mysteries different from secrets?** The community uses "mysteries" for the count-able interaction points that appear as "investigate further?" prompts; other hidden details may exist but are not part of the 7-mystery count [Community].

**Can a mystery disappear if I don't act?** Most are always accessible; at least 6 are, and event-based ones are a minority [Community]. There is no penalty for returning later.

**Will the demo mysteries carry into the full game?** Unknown — the demo is ~1/6 of the game, and the developer has not stated how demo progress transfers [Unconfirmed].

**What if I miss The Tree (mystery 7)?** It is under the sports store; if you missed it, return to the plank near the hole and interact to the left — it does not go away [Community].

## If You Only Do One Investigation Run

The most efficient demo investigation order [Editorial]:

1. **Mall run** — clear mysteries 1–5 in one trip, combining it with a dungeon clear.
2. **Playground manhole → sewer** — grab mystery 5 (sewer) and the fishing rod on the same journey.
3. **Dave's house** — pick up mystery 6 on a town visit.
4. **Finish with a TV mystery** — solving it after the others completes the 7-mystery quest [Community].

This route turns all 7 mysteries into two focused excursions instead of a scattered hunt.
