## What is Welcome to Elderfield?

Welcome to Elderfield is a **cosy horror RPG** developed by solo developer **Chris Cote** and published by **Kwalee**. You arrive in the isolated town of Elderfield — your birthplace, with a mysterious past — where the townsfolk are strange and increasingly inexplicable, and the local news stops making sense.

The game blends **Stardew Valley-style farming and life-sim** gameplay with **Lovecraftian turn-based combat**, hand-drawn horror-manga inspired art (in the tradition of Junji Ito's contaminating-the-familiar), a dark lofi soundtrack by composer **Dated**, and daily mystery investigation. It is often described as "cozy horror" — relaxing on the surface, unsettling underneath.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Farming with stakes** — cultivate land and harvest crops, including strange ones like teeth and eyeballs; stop unruly tentacles; keep creatures from the woods off your farm
- **Ranch & livestock** — manage otherworldly animals alongside the farm
- **Life-sim systems** — fish, mine, forage, cook, craft, customize your home and character
- **Turn-based combat** — AP-cost battles against Lovecraftian horrors, using gathered resources, strategic buffs, and diverse equipment; fleeing is always an option
- **Two difficulty modes** — **Cozy Mode** for relaxed farming and discovery; **Challenge Mode** for those who want the Old Gods to test them
- **Mystery investigation** — investigate the town's oddities and daily mysteries (the demo contains 7 community-verified investigation points)
- **Relationships** — befriend the odd townsfolk; romance and marriage are planned but not yet implemented, per the developer [Unconfirmed]
- **Rest as a tradeoff** — baths restore health but reset monster positions; sleeping can lift curses or introduce them
