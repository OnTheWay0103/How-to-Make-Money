# DREADMOOR Wiki — Unofficial Guide Hub

**Welcome to the unofficial DREADMOOR Wiki — a guide hub for the first-person post-apocalyptic fishing survival horror game from Dream Dock, published by Digital Vortex Entertainment.**

DREADMOOR enters **Steam Early Access on November 9, 2026** for **PC at $19.99**. It is a **single-player** game set in the flooded **Submerged Lands**, where you repair a trawler, fish for mutated species, salvage scrap, upgrade your boat, and try to reach civilization. A free demo has been available since **October 6, 2026**.

---

## What Is DREADMOOR?

DREADMOOR is a first-person fishing adventure with survival-horror pressure. You start marooned with a damaged boat and a debt to the traders at Betsy and Sons. The core loop is:

1. **Fish** — choose bait, cast, fight the fish, and land it before the line snaps
2. **Process** — turn catches into Fish Meat, Chromatic Scales, bait materials, or fuel
3. **Upgrade** — repair the hull, install modules at the Dry Dock, and raise the boat's speed
4. **Explore** — sail between the Trading Post, Angler's Island, and The Throat, salvaging materials along the way
5. **Survive** — manage fuel and hull condition, and treat night water as dangerous

The Early Access build is planned to include the complete core loop, one major region, the full story, multiple fish species and mutations, and free exploration after the story ends.
