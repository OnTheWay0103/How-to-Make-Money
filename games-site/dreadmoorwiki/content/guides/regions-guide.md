# DREADMOOR Regions Guide — Every Named Location in the Demo

DREADMOOR takes place in the **Submerged Lands**, a flooded post-apocalyptic region navigated by trawler through inlets and lakes. The demo covers a small but dense slice of that world: one hub, one workshop, one island, and a gate complex that ends the build.

This guide describes what each named location is for, what services it offers, and how the route fits together. It is a map-purpose guide, not a step-by-step walkthrough. If you want the quest order, read the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide).

## The Demo Map at a Glance

The demo’s geography is a loop around the Trading Post:

- **Abandoned House** — the starting point
- **Trading Post / Betsy and Sons** — the hub
- **Bunkhouse** — your bed and the Simple Rod
- **Dry Dock** — boat repair and upgrades
- **Angler’s Island** — Coral Fiber and exploration
- **The Throat** — the gate complex and demo finale

A hands-on preview also mentions **The Slough**, a marshy lagoon used for the playtest. The Slough is not confirmed as an Early Access region, so treat it as preview-only until the launch build says otherwise.

## Location-by-Location

### Abandoned House

**What it is:** the starting shelter.

**What it offers:**

- The first radio interaction
- The Axe
- Early Metal Plates and Rope
- A safe place to learn the controls before sailing

**Why it matters:** the Axe is required for Coral Fiber later, and the Metal Plates feed bait and repair recipes. Search the house before you leave. Missing the Axe means a second trip back to the starting area, which is a slow way to begin.

**Risk:** low. This is the tutorial space.

### Trading Post

**What it is:** the main hub of the demo, and the place where the bait shop **Betsy and Sons** is located.

**What it offers:**

- Contracts from Gill and Bozur
- The Essence deal for the Blight
- The Bunkhouse key
- Processing stations (Grinder and Thresher)
- Access to John the Fifth’s workshop and the Dry Dock
- The merchants around the pier

**Why it matters:** almost every progression thread runs through the Trading Post. If you are lost, go back here and check the objectives.

**Risk:** low. The surrounding water is the demo’s safest fishing area.

### Betsy and Sons

**What it is:** the shop inside the Trading Post, named after Betsy. The official material describes the hub as a bait shop built on giant crab legs, which matches the demo’s trading-post structure.

**What it offers:**

- The opening Snapper contract
- The Softgrazer and Harpooner contracts
- Bait blueprint access
- Processing services
- The Carburetor purchase after the Harpooner delivery

**Why it matters:** this is where the demo’s economy is anchored. Gill and Bozur are the characters who turn fish into progression.

**Risk:** low.

### Bunkhouse

**What it is:** your lodging at the Trading Post.

**What it offers:**

- A bed for time-skipping to morning
- The **Simple Rod**
- Storage for the Chest you carry from the pier

**Why it matters:** sleeping triggers the first fishing assignment, and the Simple Rod is easy to leave behind. Check the Bunkhouse before you sail out for the first contract.

**Risk:** low.

### Dry Dock

**What it is:** the boat workshop behind the Trading Post.

**What it offers:**

- Hull cleaning (Pressure Pump and Water Cannon)
- Hull repair (Repair Gun and Bug Slime)
- The Boat Lift
- Module crafting at John’s Lathe
- Module installation and storage
- The Upgraded Engine and Hull Protection chain

**Why it matters:** the Dry Dock is the gate for every meaningful boat upgrade. You cannot reach The Throat comfortably without the engine upgrade, and you cannot install the engine without the Dry Dock.

**Risk:** low once powered; the surrounding water is still part of the hub area.

### Angler’s Island

**What it is:** the demo’s main excursion island.

**What it offers:**

- **Coral Fiber**, harvested with the Axe
- Salvage and creature materials
- A dock for mooring and on-foot exploration

**Why it matters:** Coral Fiber is required to repair John’s Moonshine Still, which unlocks the Dry Dock. This is the first location that forces you to leave the hub with a specific material goal.

**Risk:** moderate. The island is explorable on foot, and the surrounding water is farther from the hub than the starting fishing spot. Bring fuel and leave hold space.

### The Throat

**What it is:** a huge waterway gate complex and the demo’s final area.

**What it offers:**

- The gate puzzle
- The Generator and Fuel Can
- Waterway machinery repairs
- The “Set Out” objective and the demo’s ending

**Why it matters:** The Throat is the skill and logistics check for everything the demo taught. It combines fuel management, hull condition, on-foot exploration, and a multi-step machine repair.

**Risk:** high. This is the one location where arriving unprepared is likely to cost a full trip.

## The Throat Gate — What the Puzzle Asks

This is a summary of the objective structure, not a full walkthrough:

1. Dock at the gate complex.
2. Find a **Fuel Can** nearby.
3. Refuel and start the **Generator**.
4. Try the **Gate Control**. It fails.
5. Repair the waterway machinery: a **Thermal Coil**, a **Gear or Axle** in the Gear Slot, **two Buckets** for the water wheels, and the **Water Pump** in place.
6. Adjust the **four water-flow controls** until both water wheels turn.
7. Return to the Gate Control and open the gate.
8. Sail through during the “Set Out” objective.

The parts are scattered around the workshop, storage areas, and lower platforms. Search before you craft. In particular, the Water Pump should be repaired in place rather than replaced, which saves materials.

The gate opens only when **both** water wheels are turning. If one wheel is still, recheck the flow controls before looking for another broken part.

## Route Logic: How to Plan a Trip

The demo rewards a simple hub-and-spoke routine:

1. **Start at the Trading Post.** Check contracts, process fish, and repair the hull.
2. **Choose one objective.** A fishing contract, a material run, or a story step. Do not combine a long salvage run with a contract deadline unless you have the hold space.
3. **Sail out with a full tank and a repaired hull.**
4. **Return before night** unless the trip specifically requires night fishing.
5. **Process and deliver before upgrading.** Materials in the bank are worth less than materials in the engine.

The most efficient early route is Trading Post → Dry Dock → Angler’s Island → Dry Dock. That loop unlocks the Still, the Dry Dock, and the hull repair chain without wasting a trip.

## Demo Map vs Early Access Map

The official Early Access description says the launch build includes **one major region** and the complete story. The demo is a slice of that region, not the whole thing.

That means:

- The demo’s locations will likely return in Early Access.
- The demo’s geography may be expanded, rearranged, or rebalanced.
- New locations are confirmed as a category but not named.
- **The Slough** appeared in a hands-on preview and may or may not be part of the launch region.

Do not assume the demo map is the final map. Treat it as a verified sample.

## What Is Not Confirmed

As of October 24, 2026, the following are not verified:

- A full Early Access map or region count beyond “one major region”
- Named locations outside the demo and The Slough
- Coordinates, fast-travel points, or map markers
- Whether regions are gated by story progress, boat upgrades, or both
- Whether The Throat remains the final area in Early Access

If you see a “full DREADMOOR map” with named regions that are not on this page, check whether it cites the demo or an official announcement. We have not found one.
