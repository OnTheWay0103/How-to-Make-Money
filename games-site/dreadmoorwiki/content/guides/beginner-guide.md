# DREADMOOR Beginner Guide — Your First Hours in the Submerged Lands

DREADMOOR does not open with a tutorial pop-up that explains everything. It opens with a damaged boat, a radio, and a world that would rather you stayed put. The first two hours are about turning a stranded hull into a working trawler and learning the three systems everything else depends on: **the fishing fight, processing, and fuel**.

This guide is a spoiler-light route through the demo build that has been available since October 6, 2026. It avoids story details and focuses on what to do, in what order, and what not to waste.

## The Short Version

If you only remember five things:

1. **Do not process fish that a contract asks for whole.** Deliver contract catches unprocessed.
2. **Learn Line Tension before you chase better fish.** Most failed catches are line breaks, not weak rods.
3. **Unlock the Dry Dock early.** It gates the engine upgrade, hull repair, and every meaningful boat improvement.
4. **Keep Chromatic Scales.** They are used both for bait and for hull reinforcement.
5. **Check fuel and hull before every long crossing.** The game will not do it for you.

## First 90 Minutes — A Practical Route

The exact quest text may change before Early Access, but the demo’s structure is stable enough to plan around.

| Step | What to do | Why it matters |
|------|------------|----------------|
| 1 | Search the abandoned house. Take the Axe, Metal Plates, and Rope. Tune the radio. | The Axe is needed for Coral Fiber later; Metal Plates feed bait and repair recipes. |
| 2 | Sail west to the Trading Post and enter Betsy and Sons. | This is the only hub with contracts, processing, and the Dry Dock chain. |
| 3 | Talk to Gill and Bozur. Accept the Essence deal for the Blight. | It is recorded as a debt. You cannot avoid it in the demo, so plan around it rather than trying to refuse. |
| 4 | Take the Bunkhouse key, carry the Chest from the pier, repair the wall opening, and sleep. | Sleeping advances to the next morning and triggers the fishing assignment. |
| 5 | Pick up the Simple Rod before you leave the Bunkhouse. | It is easy to walk past, and you cannot start the first contract without it. |
| 6 | Fish from the platform behind the Dry Dock. Catch 5 Snappers on Earthworms. | This is the tutorial fight: slow fish, forgiving tension, no bait crafting required. |
| 7 | Deliver the Snappers whole to Gill and Bozur. | Processing them first can invalidate the contract turn-in. |
| 8 | Meet John the Fifth and start repairing his Moonshine Still. | This unlocks the Dry Dock Key and the entire upgrade chain. |

### Step 6 in Detail: The First Five Snappers

The Snapper is the demo’s teaching fish. Use the Simple Rod and Earthworms, cast, and keep the rod still while you wait. When a fish bites, pull in the opposite direction to its movement. If it runs left, pull right. If it reverses, reverse with it.

Two mistakes end this contract:

- **Reeling while the fish is still fighting.** Line Tension climbs, and at maximum the line snaps.
- **Assuming an empty Stamina bar means the fish is caught.** Zero Stamina only means you can start retrieving. You still have to bring it close enough for the Land Fish prompt.

If you break a line, recast. Snappers are plentiful and the bait is free. Treat the first five as practice, not as a test.

## The Three Systems to Learn First

### 1. The Fishing Fight

Fishing is a two-meter negotiation. **Stamina** is the fish’s ability to resist; drain it by pulling opposite its movement. **Line Tension** is your failure state; it rises when you reel against a resisting fish or let the fish get too far away.

The rule that matters: when tension is high, stop reeling. Let the fish run, let the meter fall, then resume. A longer fight costs time. A snapped line costs the fish, the bait, and the trip.

### 2. Processing

Fish can be used whole, processed into **Fish Meat** at the Grinder, or processed into other materials such as **Chromatic Scales** at the Thresher. Those outputs are not interchangeable with the whole fish. A contract that asks for whole Snappers will not accept Fish Meat, and a bait recipe that asks for Fish Meat will not accept a whole fish sitting in your inventory.

Before you process anything, ask: **is this fish for a contract, for bait, for a module, or for sale?** If you do not know, leave it whole until you do.

### 3. Fuel

The boat does not run on a conventional fuel item you buy at a pump. In the demo, fuel comes from fish: process catches into Fish Meat, feed the Fuel Larva, then use the Fuel Gun to transfer fuel into the motorboat. Feeding the Larva and filling the tank are separate actions, so check the tank before you leave.

This loop is the reason “sell every fish” is a bad early habit. You need a reserve for fuel, bait, and repairs.

## Unlocking the Dry Dock Without Wasting a Trip

The Dry Dock is the most important unlock in the opening hours. The chain is:

1. Repair John the Fifth’s Moonshine Still.
2. Remove the lodged prosthetic leg.
3. Gather **Coral Fiber** on Angler’s Island with the Axe.
4. Collect **Bug Slime** from the small blue creatures using the Grappling Hook Gun.
5. Salvage Metal Plates from barrels and floating containers.
6. Return, repair the Still, and receive the Dry Dock Key.

Do not sail to Angler’s Island empty-handed. Bring the Axe, leave hold space for Coral Fiber, and grab Metal Plates on the way. A second trip for one missing material is the most common early time sink.

Once inside the Dry Dock, the demo walks you through power restoration, hull cleaning, and hull repair. Repair the Fuse Box with the Cable you find nearby, activate the Power Switch, place the boat on the Boat Lift, then use the Pressure Pump and Water Cannon to clear Polyps. Load Bug Slime into the Repair Gun and repair each damaged hull section.

## Materials You Should Not Sell or Waste

| Material | Where it comes from | Why to keep it |
|----------|---------------------|----------------|
| Metal Plates | Salvage around the Trading Post and floating containers | Used in bait recipes and repairs |
| Fish Meat | Grinder | Fuel, bait, and survival supplies |
| Chromatic Scales | Thresher, from suitable peaceful fish | Bait ingredient and hull reinforcement |
| Coral Fiber | Angler’s Island, harvested with the Axe | Moonshine Still repair and upgrade chain |
| Bug Slime | Small blue creatures, collected with the Grappling Hook Gun | Hull repair gun ammunition |
| Rubber | Tires and salvage | Useful later in the upgrade chain |

If a recipe competes for Chromatic Scales, check whether the other use is a one-time progression gate or a repeatable convenience. Progression wins.

## Beginner Mistakes That Cost Real Time

1. **Processing contract fish.** Deliver whole fish when the contract asks for whole fish.
2. **Spending every Chromatic Scale on bait.** Keep enough for hull reinforcement.
3. **Buying the Carburetor before completing the Harpooner contract.** The contract unlocks the purchase; it does not hand you the part.
4. **Forgetting to install a crafted module.** Crafted boat parts can end up in the Storage Locker instead of your inventory. Check the locker, then install the module in the correct slot.
5. **Sailing at night “to save time.”** Night water is explicitly more dangerous, and the Early Access feature list says tentacles can attack the boat. In the demo, the risk is lower than the warnings suggest, but do not build a habit around that.
6. **Ignoring hull condition.** A damaged hull turns a short trip into a repair detour. Check it at the dock, not mid-crossing.
7. **Treating the demo as the full game.** The demo ends at The Throat. Combat, more fish, and more regions are Early Access features, not demo features.

## What to Do When the Demo Ends

The demo concludes when you restore the waterway machinery at The Throat, open the gate, and sail through. Before you stop:

- Finish any optional research you can afford.
- Install the Upgraded Engine if you have not already. The 2 kn to 4 kn speed change is the largest single quality-of-life improvement in the build.
- Note which materials you were short on. That is your shopping list for Early Access.

Early Access begins November 9, 2026 at $19.99 and is planned to include the complete core loop, one major region, the full story, and free exploration after the story. Demo progress is not expected to carry over, and no save transfer has been announced.
