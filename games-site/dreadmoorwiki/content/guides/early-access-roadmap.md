# DREADMOOR Early Access Roadmap — What Ships on November 9

DREADMOOR launches in **Steam Early Access on Monday, November 9, 2026** for **PC at $19.99**. It is a single-player game from developer **Dream Dock** and publisher **Digital Vortex Entertainment**, and the Early Access period is planned to last **6–12 months**.

This page separates what is confirmed for day one from what the developers have only described as a plan, and what is still unannounced. If you are deciding whether to buy at launch or wait for 1.0, the distinction matters.

## The Fact Box

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Early Access date | November 9, 2026 |
| Price | $19.99 (planned increase at 1.0) |
| Platform | PC via Steam |
| Developer | Dream Dock |
| Publisher | Digital Vortex Entertainment |
| Early Access length | 6–12 months (planned) |
| Genre | First-person post-apocalyptic fishing survival horror |
| Setting | The Submerged Lands |
| Players | Single-player only |
| Demo | Free, launched October 6, 2026 |
| Languages | 12 text languages; English and Russian voice audio |
| Wishlists | Roughly 400,000 by September 2026; Steam top 100 most-wishlisted unreleased |

The wishlist figure is a developer/publisher claim, not an independently audited number. The top-100 placement was part of the official announcement.

## What Early Access Includes

According to the official feature list, the November 9 build includes:

### The complete core gameplay loop

Fishing, processing, fuel, salvage, boat repair, upgrades, and sailing are all in the launch build. This is not a combat-only demo or a fishing-only vertical slice. The core loop is the product.

### One major region

The Early Access build is described as containing **one major region**. The demo covers a slice of the Submerged Lands around the Trading Post, the Dry Dock, Angler’s Island, and The Throat. The launch region is expected to be larger than the demo slice, but the exact boundaries have not been published.

### The complete story

The official material says the Early Access build includes the **complete storyline**. That is unusual for Early Access and worth reading carefully: it means the narrative is expected to be finishable on day one, with additional regions, fish, and mechanics added during the Early Access period rather than the ending being held back for 1.0.

### Multiple fish species and mutations

The launch build is confirmed to include multiple fish species and mutations, with more dangerous catches selling for more to traders. The demo names only three species, so expect the launch build to expand the list. No complete fish list has been published.

### Free exploration after the story

After the story ends, the game is described as supporting free exploration. That suggests the region remains open for fishing, salvage, and upgrade completion rather than ending at the credits.

### Real-time combat and night danger

The launch build is confirmed to include real-time combat against mutated creatures on sea and land, harpoons and craftable reinforced harpoons, night water that is more dangerous, and tentacle attacks that can destroy the boat. A destroyed boat drags you to the nearest dock and costs cargo.

### Boat progression

The confirmed upgrade categories are engine, propeller, hull, searchlight, and afterburner, plus rod and lure upgrades for fishing. The demo only lets you complete a subset of these.

## Demo vs Early Access

The free demo is a vertical slice, not a save-compatible preview. Here is the practical comparison:

| Feature | Demo (October 6, 2026) | Early Access (November 9, 2026) |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Price | Free | $19.99 |
| Story | Opening slice; ends at The Throat | Complete story, per official material |
| Region | Trading Post, Dry Dock, Angler’s Island, The Throat | One major region, expected larger than the demo |
| Fish | Three named species | Multiple species and mutations |
| Combat | Scripted monster moments; little conventional combat | Real-time combat on sea and land |
| Night danger | Present but lightly enforced in the preview build | Officially described as dangerous; tentacle attacks confirmed |
| Boat upgrades | Hull Protection, Upgraded Engine, optional research | Full confirmed categories: engine, propeller, hull, searchlight, afterburner |
| Save transfer | — | No save transfer has been announced |
| Post-story play | Demo ends at a “Thank You for Playing” screen | Free exploration after the story |

The save-transfer row is deliberately cautious. The developers have not announced that demo progress carries over, and Early Access builds often change save formats. Assume you will start fresh.

## The 6–12 Month Plan

The official plan is a 6–12 month Early Access period. The stated goals for the full release include:

- New regions
- More fish species
- New fishing mechanics
- Further boat upgrades

No content calendar has been published. There is no confirmed date for the first major patch, no confirmed number of regions at 1.0, and no confirmed date for the price increase beyond “at 1.0.”

That is normal for an indie Early Access launch, but it means you should not buy the game expecting a live-service cadence. Buy it for the launch build, not for a roadmap you cannot verify.

## What Is Still Unannounced

As of October 24, 2026, the following are not confirmed:

- The exact 1.0 release date
- The exact 1.0 price
- The number of regions at 1.0
- A complete fish or mutation list
- A full weapon or combat system breakdown
- Console versions (no PlayStation, Xbox, or Switch announcement)
- Steam Deck compatibility rating
- Mod support
- Save transfer from the demo
- Whether Early Access saves will be compatible with 1.0
- Whether there will be post-launch paid DLC

If a roadmap graphic circulates with dated content drops, check whether it comes from Dream Dock or Digital Vortex. We have not seen an official dated roadmap.

## Should You Buy at Launch?

This is editorial judgment, not a recommendation from the developers.

**Buy on November 9 if:**

- You played the demo and want the complete story and the full region.
- You are comfortable with balance changes and possible save-format changes during Early Access.
- You want the lower $19.99 price before the planned 1.0 increase.
- You want to support a small studio’s Early Access launch.

**Wait if:**

- You want a polished, content-complete game with a stable save.
- You need console or Steam Deck confirmation.
- You are mainly interested in combat and want to see post-launch impressions first.
- A 6–12 month Early Access period with an unannounced patch cadence is a dealbreaker.

The demo is free, and it is the best available test of whether the core loop clicks for you. If the demo’s fishing and repair loop feels repetitive after two hours, the launch build’s additional content may not fix that; if it feels like a foundation, Early Access is the cheaper way in.
