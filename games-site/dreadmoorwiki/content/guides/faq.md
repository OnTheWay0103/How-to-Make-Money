## When does DREADMOOR release?

**November 9, 2026.** That is the Steam Early Access date, not the 1.0 date. The Early Access period is planned to last 6–12 months, which puts a full release somewhere in the second half of 2027 if the plan holds. No exact 1.0 date has been announced.

A free demo launched on **October 6, 2026** and was part of the Steam Next Fest cycle.

## How much does DREADMOOR cost?

**$19.99** during Early Access. The developers have said the price will increase for the 1.0 release. They have not announced the 1.0 price.

There is no confirmed deluxe edition, season pass, or pre-order bonus. If a store page offers one, check that it is the official Steam listing before buying.

## What platforms is DREADMOOR on?

**PC via Steam.** That is the only confirmed platform.

There is no Epic Games Store, GOG, or Microsoft Store listing as of October 24, 2026. There is no PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo Switch version. The official FAQ does not commit to consoles, and the phrase “console release planned” that circulates in some coverage is not backed by a dated announcement.

## Is DREADMOOR multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** DREADMOOR is a single-player game. The official Steam FAQ states that it does not support multiplayer, co-op, or crossplay. There is no PvP mode.

This is one of the most common pre-launch questions because the game looks like a co-op fishing trip. It is not. You will sail alone.

## Is DREADMOOR a sequel to Dredge?

**No.** DREADMOOR is a separate game from Dream Dock and Digital Vortex Entertainment. It is not a Dredge sequel, spin-off, or licensed title.

The comparison is understandable: a small boat, strange catches, an ominous world, and a fishing loop with horror pressure. But the stories, developers, and publishers are unrelated. If you are looking for a Dredge follow-up, this is a different game in a similar genre.

## What is the difference between the demo and Early Access?

The demo is a vertical slice. It covers the opening at the Trading Post, the first fishing contracts, the Dry Dock upgrade chain, Angler’s Island, and The Throat, then ends at a “Thank You for Playing” screen.

Early Access is planned to include:

- The complete core gameplay loop
- One major region, expected to be larger than the demo slice
- The complete story
- Multiple fish species and mutations
- More boat upgrades
- Free exploration after the story ends

Demo progress is not expected to carry over. No save transfer has been announced.

## Does DREADMOOR have combat?

**Yes**, in the Early Access build. The official feature list describes real-time combat against mutated creatures on both sea and land, with harpoons and other tools. Craftable **reinforced harpoons** are confirmed.

The demo shows very little conventional combat. It focuses on fishing, salvage, and boat repair, with scripted monster moments. If you are buying for the combat, read post-launch impressions rather than judging the demo’s combat content.

## How does fishing work?

The short version:

1. Choose a rod and bait at a marked fishing spot.
2. Cast and wait for a bite, or retrieve a moving lure for a predator.
3. Pull the rod opposite the fish’s movement to drain **Stamina**.
4. Stop reeling when **Line Tension** gets too high, or the line snaps.
5. Bring the fish close and use the **Land Fish** prompt.

The demo confirms three species and three bait pairings: Snapper with Earthworm, Softgrazer with Wind-Up Tin, and Harpooner with Rainbow Fin. Our [Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide) covers the full fight loop.

## What are the PC system requirements?

The Steam demo lists:

**Minimum**

- 64-bit Windows 10
- Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600
- 8 GB RAM
- NVIDIA GTX 1060 (6 GB) or AMD RX 580 (8 GB)
- DirectX 12
- 20 GB storage

**Recommended**

- 64-bit Windows 10
- Intel Core i5-12400F or AMD Ryzen 5 5600
- 16 GB RAM
- NVIDIA RTX 4060 or AMD RX 7600
- DirectX 12
- 20 GB storage

These are **demo** requirements. The Early Access build may revise them, so check the store page before launch. Older pre-alpha spec sheets circulating online list different hardware; those were explicitly described by the developers as temporary.

## Is DREADMOOR on Steam Deck?

**Not confirmed.** Valve has not issued a Steam Deck compatibility rating as of October 24, 2026, and the developers have not announced official Deck support.

The demo’s minimum GPU (GTX 1060 / RX 580) is above what the original Steam Deck comfortably matches in demanding first-person games. That is an inference, not a benchmark. Wait for a verified compatibility rating or hands-on tests.

## How long is DREADMOOR?

No official playtime estimate has been published. The Early Access build includes the complete story and one major region, with free exploration afterward. Story length, completionist length, and the size of the region are all unannounced.

Any specific hour count you see before launch is an estimate, not a confirmed figure.

## Will my demo progress carry over to Early Access?

No save transfer has been announced. Early Access builds frequently change save formats, and the demo ends at a scripted completion screen. Plan to start fresh on November 9.

If the developers announce save compatibility, we will update this answer.

## Is DREADMOOR on Xbox Game Pass or PS Plus?

No. There is no Game Pass, PS Plus, or subscription-service announcement. The only confirmed storefront is Steam on PC.

## Is DREADMOOR scary or gory?

The game is marketed as **survival horror** and carries a horror atmosphere: a flooded world, mutated creatures, night attacks, and a bleak story premise. The Steam demo page lists tags including “Psychological Horror,” “Gore,” and “Survival Horror.”

The demo itself is more atmospheric than graphically violent. The Early Access build’s combat and creature content may be more intense. If you are sensitive to horror, try the free demo first.

## Is there a DREADMOOR wiki?

Yes — you are on it. DREADMOOR Wiki is an unofficial fan resource covering fishing, bait, fish species, boat upgrades, survival, regions, combat, and Early Access details. Every guide lists its sources and labels unconfirmed information.

## What should I do before November 9?

Three things:

1. **Play the free demo.** It is the best test of whether the fishing and repair loop clicks for you.
2. **Check your PC against the demo requirements.** The gap between minimum and recommended is large.
3. **Decide whether you are buying the Early Access build or waiting for 1.0.** Our [Early Access Roadmap](/guides/early-access-roadmap) breaks down the tradeoff.

If you are buying at launch, start with the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) so your first two hours are not spent relearning the fuel chain.
