# DREADMOOR Fish Species Guide — Confirmed Catches & Processing

The DREADMOOR demo names exactly **three fish species**. The Early Access feature list promises multiple species and mutations, but it does not publish a complete list. That distinction matters: this page only presents species that can be verified in the demo, and it labels everything else as unconfirmed instead of filling the gap with invented names.

If you are looking for a full launch-day fish database, this page is the honest version of what is known on October 24, 2026.

## Confirmed Species at a Glance

| Species | Bait | Where it appears | Contract use | Fight profile |
|---------|------|------------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Snapper** | Earthworm | First fishing spot beneath the Trading Post pier, near the merchants | 5 whole Snappers for the opening assignment | Slow, forgiving; the tutorial fish |
| **Softgrazer** | Wind-Up Tin | Marked “peaceful fish” location | 3 for the Chromatic Scales step | More Stamina than a Snapper; recovers during retrieval |
| **Harpooner** | Rainbow Fin | Marked “predatory fish” splash zone | 3 for the Carburetor chain | Requires a moving lure; bites after a follow |

That is the complete named list from the demo. There is no confirmed fourth species name, no rarity tier, and no legendary fish name in the sources we checked.

### Snapper

The Snapper is the fish the game uses to teach the fight. It bites static Earthworm bait, does not require crafted materials, and has enough Stamina to demonstrate the pull-opposite rule without punishing a beginner.

**What to do with Snappers:** the opening contract wants **five whole Snappers**. Deliver them unprocessed. After that contract, Snappers are a reasonable default catch for Fish Meat, because the bait is free and the fight is short. They are not a high-value sale item.

**What not to do:** do not grind your first five Snappers into Fish Meat before turning in the contract. A whole fish and Fish Meat are different items for recipe and contract purposes.

### Softgrazer

The Softgrazer is the first fish that asks you to craft bait. The **Wind-Up Tin** recipe is 1 Metal Plate + 1 Fish Meat, and the bait is designed to attract scavenging fish with a clicking metal tin.

The fight is longer than a Snapper’s. A Softgrazer has more Stamina and can recover while you are retrieving it, so the correct response is to stop pulling, re-drain Stamina, and then resume. Do not interpret a long fight as a wrong bait.

**What to do with Softgrazers:** the demo requires three for the Chromatic Scales step. Process them at the **Thresher** at Betsy and Sons to obtain **Chromatic Scales**. Those scales are used for both Rainbow Fin bait and John’s hull reinforcement, so they are one of the most contested materials in the opening hours.

**What not to do:** do not release a Softgrazer by mistake after a long fight. Landing and keeping are separate prompts, and a released fish cannot be processed for the contract.

### Harpooner

The Harpooner is a predatory fish, not a static-bait target. You need **Rainbow Fin** (1 Fish Meat + 1 Metal Plate + 1 Chromatic Scales) and a cast into the disturbed-water splash zone. Then you retrieve the lure at a controlled pace so the predator follows and bites.

This is the most expensive fish to practice on. Every failed cast costs crafted materials, so check the bait, the splash zone, and your retrieval speed before blaming the spot.

**What to do with Harpooners:** the demo requires three for the contract that unlocks the Carburetor purchase. Deliver them as required. After that, Harpooners are a candidate for sale or processing depending on your material needs, but no reliable sell value has been published.

## Processing: Whole Fish Is Not the Same as Fish Meat

The demo has two processing stations, and they produce different things:

| Station | Input | Output | Used for |
|---------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Grinder** | Whole fish | Fish Meat | Fuel Larva feed, Wind-Up Tin, Rainbow Fin, survival supplies |
| **Thresher** | Suitable peaceful fish, including Softgrazers | Chromatic Scales | Rainbow Fin, hull reinforcement |

The key rule: **a whole fish in your inventory does not count as the Fish Meat a recipe asks for.** You must process it first. Conversely, a contract that asks for whole Snappers will not accept Fish Meat.

This is the single most common source of wasted trips in the demo. Before processing a catch, decide which of four destinations it has:

1. **Contract turn-in** — keep it whole.
2. **Bait crafting** — process to Fish Meat or scales first.
3. **Fuel** — process to Fish Meat, then feed the Fuel Larva.
4. **Sale** — check whether the trader wants whole fish or processed goods.

If you do not know, leave it whole. Whole fish can always be processed later; processed fish cannot be reassembled.

## Hold Management and Carnivorous Fish

The official feature list confirms that fish are stored in **hold compartments**, and that some fish coexist while carnivorous fish can eat others and reduce your profits. That is a real economic mechanic, not flavor text.

The practical rules:

- **Do not mix predators with small catches** if you can avoid it. The official material explicitly warns that carnivorous fish eat others.
- **Check the hold before a long crossing.** A hold full of eaten fish is worse than an empty slot.
- **Process or sell high-risk catches before adding more.** If a fish is both valuable and predatory, it is a candidate for immediate processing.
- **Leave contingency space.** Contracts and salvage both need room, and a full hold forces you to choose between profit and progression.

No numeric hold capacity or exact predation rate has been published for Early Access. Treat any specific number you see elsewhere as unverified unless it cites the build it came from.

## Mutated Fish: Confirmed Category, Unconfirmed Names

The official Early Access description says the game includes **multiple fish species and mutations**, and that more dangerous catches sell for more to mysterious traders. Hands-on coverage of the playtest also notes that night adds stranger species.

What is **not** confirmed:

- The name of any mutated fish species
- Whether mutations are separate species, variants, or a rarity tag
- Which mutations are night-only
- Exact sell values or contract requirements for mutated fish
- Whether mutated fish can be safely stored with normal fish

We are not going to invent a “mutated fish list” to fill this section. After November 9, we will update it from the Early Access build and cite what we see.

## Keep, Sell, or Process — A Decision Table

| If the fish is… | Do this | Reason |
|-----------------|---------|--------|
| Required whole by an active contract | Keep whole and deliver | Processing can invalidate the turn-in |
| Needed for a bait recipe | Process to Fish Meat or scales | Recipes check processed items |
| Your only fuel reserve | Process to Fish Meat and feed the Larva | Fuel gates every trip |
| A predator mixed with small fish | Separate, process, or sell it | Official material warns carnivorous fish eat others |
| A duplicate with no contract use | Sell or process based on your material gap | Do not hoard without a purpose |
| An unidentified mutated catch | Check the hold and trader options before selling | Values and uses are unconfirmed |
