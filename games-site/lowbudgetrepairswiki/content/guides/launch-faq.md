# Low-Budget Repairs Launch FAQ — Everything About the August 13 Release

Low-Budget Repairs launches in **six days — Thursday, August 13, 2026**. With over 1 million Steam wishlists, it's one of the most anticipated simulator releases of the year. This FAQ answers the launch-specific questions: when exactly the game unlocks, what you get on day one, what's still unconfirmed, and how the demo fits into launch week. For general questions about the game itself (multiplayer, mods, length), see the main [FAQ](/guides/faq). Everything here is current as of August 7, 2026.

## When Exactly Does the Game Unlock?

**August 13, 2026, at 14:00 UTC** — a simultaneous global release on Steam, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. That's 10:00 EDT in New York, 16:00 in Warsaw, 23:00 in Tokyo, and August 14, 00:00 in Sydney. The full timezone table is in our [release hub](/guides/release-hub). Steam games typically unlock within a few minutes of the listed time; console stores may roll out regionally.

## Is There a Preload?

**Unconfirmed.** No preload has been announced for any platform as of August 7. Steam preloads are usually announced in the final days before launch — if one appears, we'll flag it in the [release hub](/guides/release-hub). The PC install is **30GB**, so on slower connections, plan for the download to run after unlock. Console preloads, if they happen, would appear for preordered games in the last few days.

## What's in the Full Game vs. the Demo?

The demo (released July 24, 2026) is a vertical slice: the flooded bathroom job, the full core loop, and a handful of starter contracts. The full game adds:

- The **complete campaign** — the full job list and many more flats across the block
- **Harder contract versions** — bigger flats, worse damage, tighter budgets
- The **full progression loop** — reputation, unlocks, and job availability that the demo only hints at

Same mechanics, much more scope. The [demo guide](/guides/demo-guide) compares them in detail.

## Will Demo Progress Carry Over?

**Unconfirmed — assume no.** The developers have not announced demo save transfer, Steam Cloud notes, or any carry-over system. For a demo this short, a fresh start is the norm. Treat the demo as practice: every shortcut you learned is a head start, even if your zloty balance isn't.

## What About the Demo at Launch?

Demos often get delisted around launch or between promotional events. If the demo disappears from the Steam page, it may return for a launch-week event or Steam Next Fest. The full game contains everything the demo had and more, so a delisted demo costs you nothing — but if you haven't played it yet, **play it before August 13** while it's available.

## What Price and Editions Are Expected?

- **Price:** ~$19.99 USD expected (unconfirmed). Regional pricing will vary.
- **Editions:** standard edition only — no deluxe, collector's, or season pass announced.
- **Launch discount:** not announced. PlayWay-published sims frequently launch with a 10–20% first-week discount, and a game with 1M+ wishlists is a prime candidate for a launch-week deal — expect an effective price around $16–18 if that pattern holds. It's speculation, not a promise; details in the [price & editions guide](/guides/price-editions-guide).

## Will There Be a Day-One Patch?

**Unconfirmed — no announcement.** Most games ship with post-launch fixes, but nothing has been said for Low-Budget Repairs. The demo build was stable enough for broad playtesting, so there's no reason to expect major problems — but as with any launch, server-store issues at unlock time (Steam's standard launch-week congestion) are possible.

## What Are the Post-Launch Plans?

**No roadmap announced.** PlayWay-published simulators have a well-established pattern of post-launch DLC — the House Flipper family runs on expansions — but nothing is announced for Low-Budget Repairs. There's also no official mod support or Workshop integration announced; with the game's popularity, unofficial mods are likely to appear eventually, but don't plan on them at launch.
