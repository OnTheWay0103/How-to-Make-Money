# Low-Budget Repairs System Requirements — Can Your PC Run It?

> Specs below are the official system requirements from the Steam store page (as of August 2026). They may be adjusted slightly at release.

Low-Budget Repairs is a first-person renovation simulator, so it's not a graphically demanding game — but it does simulate a lot of objects in the environment, which matters for CPU and RAM. Here are the minimum and recommended specs as listed on the official Steam store page, and what they actually mean for your machine.

---

## Official System Requirements

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| OS | Windows 10 64-bit | Windows 10 64-bit |
| CPU | AMD Ryzen 5 1st gen / Intel Core i5 7th gen | AMD Ryzen 5 3rd gen / Intel Core i5 10th gen |
| RAM | 8 GB | 16 GB |
| GPU | AMD RX 580 4GB / NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB | AMD RX 5700 XT / NVIDIA RTX 2070 |
| Storage | 30 GB | 30 GB |

---

## What Each Spec Means for Performance

### CPU — Ryzen 5 1st gen / i5 7th gen (min) vs Ryzen 5 3rd gen / i5 10th gen (rec)

The CPU matters more than you'd expect in Low-Budget Repairs. Renovation sims track lots of interactive objects — every can of paint, tile, and fitting is simulated, and physics-heavy scenes (flooded bathrooms, falling debris) hit the CPU. The minimum tier runs the game with occasional hitches in the busiest flats; the recommended tier stays smooth throughout.

### RAM — 8 GB (min) vs 16 GB (rec)

8GB is the floor and it works, but 16GB is the safer choice — Windows 10 alone uses several gigabytes, and the game plus a browser on top will squeeze an 8GB system. If your PC has 8GB, close everything else while playing; full flat makeovers are the most memory-hungry contracts.

### GPU — RX 580 4GB / GTX 1060 6GB (min) vs RX 5700 XT / RTX 2070 (rec)

The minimum GPUs are very old at this point (both launched in 2016), which is great news: almost any gaming PC from the last several years beats them. At minimum spec you can expect a playable experience at 1080p with settings dialed down — the game's art style isn't a graphics showcase, so it holds up fine. The recommended tier comfortably handles 1080p at higher settings, with headroom for high refresh rates if you want them. Exact frame rates are unconfirmed before release.

### Storage — 30 GB

You'll need about 30GB of free space for the install. The spec sheet doesn't require an SSD, but an SSD will noticeably cut loading times when you travel between flats and the shop. An HDD works, especially at minimum settings.

---

## Can You Run It on a Laptop?

Probably, but check your specs first:

- **Gaming laptops** with a dedicated GPU (even a GTX 1650 or RX 5500M-class chip) sit comfortably above the minimum and should run Low-Budget Repairs well at 1080p.
- **Laptops with integrated graphics only** are risky. The CPU part of the requirement is usually fine, but Intel Iris/AMD Radeon integrated GPUs are well below the minimum GPU tier. If that's your setup, the demo (when available) is your best way to test.
- **8GB laptops** should work but will stutter if you multitask — keep the laptop plugged in and close background apps.

---

## Quick Checklist Before Buying

1. **Windows 10 64-bit** — required for both tiers. Windows 11 works fine too.
2. **CPU:** i5 7th gen / Ryzen 5 1st gen or newer.
3. **RAM:** 8GB minimum, 16GB recommended.
4. **GPU:** GTX 1060 6GB or RX 580 4GB minimum — nearly any dedicated gaming GPU from 2018 or later passes.
5. **Storage:** 30GB free (SSD recommended, HDD acceptable).

If you're near the minimum tier, try the July 2026 demo (it may return for Steam Next Fest) before committing — a flooded bathroom is a great benchmark for how the full game will run.
