# Low-Budget Repairs Price & Editions — How Much, What You Get

> Pricing was verified on the Steam store page on 2026-10-24: **$19.99 USD**. The game was released on **August 13, 2026**; see our [release hub](/guides/release-hub) for the full timeline. Regional and console prices that we could not verify are marked **[Unconfirmed]**.

## Price: $19.99 USD

Low-Budget Repairs costs **$19.99 USD on Steam** (verified 2026-10-24 via the Steam appdetails API; no discount at the time of writing). That matches the mid-tier simulator price point.

At that price, the game lands in the "impulse buy" simulator tier rather than the premium $40–60 range — the same territory as the original House Flipper.

## Regional Pricing

Steam applies regional pricing, so players in many countries may pay less than the USD list price. Console pricing is **[Unconfirmed]** (see the [PS5 guide](/guides/ps5-guide)):

- **Poland** (the game's home turf) is very likely to see a favorable PLN price — PlayWay titles consistently price well for the Polish market.
- **Steam regional pricing** will convert the USD price for Latin America, Southeast Asia, and other regions, as it does for most titles.
- **Console stores** (PS5, Xbox) usually mirror regional pricing too, though console prices can differ from Steam in some countries.

We did not independently verify regional prices in this update; treat the regional figures as expectations, not confirmed numbers. For a game set in 1990s Poland, the Polish price is the one to watch.

## What the Standard Edition Includes

Only one edition is confirmed, and it includes the whole game:

- **The full single-player campaign** — the release build includes the full job list and flats (the demo covers only the first few contracts). We have not independently verified the complete release-build job list — see the [renovation jobs guide](/guides/renovation-jobs-guide).
- **All core systems**: painting, tiling, plumbing, furniture assembly, the hardware store and bargain bin, the inspection system, and the full progression loop.
- **No pay-to-win and no DLC gating at release** — no microtransactions or content passes are listed on the Steam page as of 2026-10-24. (Post-launch DLC is common for PlayWay sims, but nothing is announced.)

## Deluxe, Collector's, or Preorder Editions

**None listed.** There is no deluxe edition, collector's edition, soundtrack bundle, season pass, or preorder bonus on the Steam store as of 2026-10-24. If the publisher adds an edition, this page will be updated — but at the time of writing, it is standard edition or nothing.
