# Low-Budget Repairs Price & Editions — How Much, What You Get

> Pricing information is current as of August 5, 2026. The final price is set by the publisher — everything here is expectation, not confirmation. The launch date (August 13, 2026) is confirmed; see our [release hub](/guides/release-hub) for the full timeline.

## Expected Price: ~$19.99 USD

The expected price for Low-Budget Repairs is **around $19.99 USD**, matching the mid-tier simulator price point. The exact number has not been officially announced as of early August 2026, so treat $19.99 as a strong estimate until the Steam page is updated.

At that price, the game lands in the "impulse buy" simulator tier rather than the premium $40–60 range — the same territory as the original House Flipper.

## Regional Pricing

Steam and console stores apply regional pricing, so players in many countries will pay less than the USD list price:

- **Poland** (the game's home turf) is very likely to see a favorable PLN price — PlayWay titles consistently price well for the Polish market.
- **Steam regional pricing** will convert the USD price for Latin America, Southeast Asia, and other regions, as it does for most titles.
- **Console stores** (PS5, Xbox) usually mirror regional pricing too, though console prices can differ from Steam in some countries.

Nothing is confirmed until launch, but for a game set in 1990s Poland, the Polish pricing is the one to watch.

## What the Standard Edition Includes

Only one edition is confirmed, and it includes the whole game:

- **The full single-player campaign** — every job type and flat in the release build (the demo covers only the first few contracts; the full job list is still being finalized — see the [renovation jobs guide](/guides/renovation-jobs-guide)).
- **All core systems**: painting, tiling, plumbing, furniture assembly, the hardware store and bargain bin, the inspection system, and the full progression loop.
- **No pay-to-win and no DLC gating at launch** — there are no confirmed microtransactions or content passes. (Post-launch DLC is common for PlayWay sims, but nothing is announced.)
