## What Kind of Game Is Low-Budget Repairs?

Low-Budget Repairs is a **satirical first-person renovation simulator** developed by Gray2RGB and published by PlayWay S.A. You take on renovation jobs in 1990s Polish apartment blocks, working to budgets where every shortcut counts. It's a game about doing the cheapest possible job that passes inspection — painting over mold, tiling without a level, and pocketing the difference.

It sits in the same comedy-simulator family as other PlayWay titles, with a focus on humor, money management, and chaotic problem-solving rather than realistic renovation.
