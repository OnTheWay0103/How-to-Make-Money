# Low-Budget Repairs Demo Guide — Content, Length & What Carries Over

> Demo details are based on the July 2026 build. The full game was released on **August 13, 2026**; release-build details we could not verify are marked **[Unconfirmed]**.

## Is There a Demo? (Yes — And It's Free)

Low-Budget Repairs has a **free demo on Steam**, released **July 24, 2026**. It's a genuine slice of the game, not a teaser: you take contracts, shop at the hardware store, paint, tile, fix plumbing, and get inspected exactly like in the full release.

Demos on Steam often get delisted between promotional events, so if the demo is gone when you check the store page, don't panic — the full game is available at $19.99 and the demo may return for a future event. This wiki's guides are based on the July 2026 demo build plus verified store facts, so you'll know exactly what you're getting.

## How to Download the Demo

The demo installs like any Steam demo, but here's the full walkthrough:

1. Open the Low-Budget Repairs page on the Steam Store.
2. Look for the **"Download Demo"** button on the store page. If it isn't there, the demo is temporarily unavailable — see above.
3. Click it and let Steam download. The demo is a fraction of the full game's **30GB** install (see [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements)), so it downloads fast.
4. Launch it from your Steam library.

The demo doubles as a free specs test: if the flooded bathroom job runs smoothly on your PC, the full game will too.

## What's in the Demo

The demo is a condensed version of the core game, including:

- **3–5 job types**, depending on how you count them: the flooded bathroom repair (the demo's signature contract), plumbing emergencies, and light painting-and-refresh work — the same job families covered in detail in our [renovation jobs guide](/guides/renovation-jobs-guide), scaled down to starter difficulty.
- **The full core loop**: accept a job → buy supplies → do the work → get inspected → get paid (or not). Every mechanic in the loop is the real deal.
- **Painting, tiling, and plumbing** with full mechanics — watering down paint, mismatched tiles, and duct tape pipes. Whether every release-build value matches the demo is **[Unconfirmed]**; we have not independently audited each one.
- **The hardware store with the bargain bin** — the complete shopping experience, including the bin where half of all great strategies start. See our [hardware store guide](/guides/hardware-store-guide).
- **The inspection system** — clients walk the flat and judge your work with their own eyes. Our [inspection guide](/guides/inspection-guide) explains exactly what they notice and what they don't.

What it doesn't include: the full campaign, the complete job list, and later-game flats. It's a vertical slice — the same game, cut short, not simplified.

## How Long Is the Demo?

Short — that's by design. A single contract like the flooded bathroom takes roughly **30–60 minutes** for a first-time player. Completing every available job, replaying contracts to try faster and cheaper strategies, and testing all the tricks in our [cost-cutting tips guide](/guides/cost-cutting-tips) stretches it to about **2–4 hours** total.

That's plenty of time to internalize the game's central lesson: profit comes from what you *don't* spend, not from what you do.

## Does Demo Progress Carry Over to the Full Game?

**官方未公布 [Unconfirmed].** The developers have not published an official statement on whether demo saves transfer to the full release, and we did not find one as of 2026-10-24.

Here's the honest read: for a demo this short, a fresh start is the norm, and the demo's contracts cover roughly the first hour of the full game anyway, so a save transfer would save you almost no time. **Assume no carry-over** and treat the demo as practice — if it does transfer, it's a bonus, not a plan.

## Why Play the Demo

- **Free practice.** Learn painting, tiling, and the inspection system before the full game's budgets get tight. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide) walks you through your first contracts step by step.
- **A specs test.** Confirm your PC can run it before spending money — the [system requirements guide](/guides/system-requirements) tells you what to check.
- **Decide if the satire lands.** The 1990s Polish block humor is the whole point of the game. If the demo makes you laugh, the full release is for you.
