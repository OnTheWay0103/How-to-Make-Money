# Low-Budget Repairs Languages — Full List & What's Supported

Language support matters more in Low-Budget Repairs than in most simulation games — half the comedy lives in the dialogue, the 1990s Polish setting, and the suspicious neighbors who watch you work. The good news: **the Steam page confirms a generous 18-language lineup, with interface, full audio, and subtitles in every single one.** Here's the complete list and what it means for you.

## What "Full Audio" Means

Every language on the list gets **voice acting, not just translated text** — the Steam page lists full audio for all 18. That's an unusually thorough commitment for a $20-tier simulator; many sims ship interface-only support for most languages. Here, the client complaints, the neighbor gossip, and the handyman's muttering are all voiced in your language.

The exception worth knowing about: the game's humor is deeply Polish — 1990s block-of-flats culture, price complaints about zlotys, and Polish-flavored sarcasm. The English translation carries the jokes well (the demo's reception suggests it does), but if you want the *full* cultural context, Polish players get the most authentic version of the comedy. Keep subtitles on either way — see our [controls guide](/guides/controls-guide) for the language settings menu.

## The AI-Assisted Translation Disclosure (Official)

For transparency: the developer's Steam page disclosure states that **AI was used for some translations, music, and visual assets** in the production of the game. The disclosure also confirms AI does **not** generate content during gameplay and was **not** used for the game's code. The AI-assisted translation is worth flagging for language-sensitive players — the Polish original and English are the flagship translations, so if you notice odd phrasing in a less-common language, that's why.

## How to Change the Language

Standard Steam behavior applies (exact menus will be verified at launch):

- **Before install:** Steam's game properties → Language dropdown sets the install language.
- **In game:** the settings menu should include a language option (the demo's settings structure is documented in our [controls guide](/guides/controls-guide)).
- **Audio vs subtitles:** with full audio across 18 languages, you can play fully voiced in your language rather than relying on subs — unless you specifically want the Polish voice track with your language's subtitles, which is a fun combo for bilingual players.

## Console Versions

The 18-language list is from the Steam listing. The **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** versions launch the same day (August 13, 2026 — see the [PS5 guide](/guides/ps5-guide)) and will almost certainly mirror the PC language set, but their store listings haven't confirmed it yet. If you're buying on console specifically for a less-common language, check the store page before purchase or wait for post-launch confirmation.
