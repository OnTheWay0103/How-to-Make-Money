## Playing Renovation With a Controller

Low-Budget Repairs is a first-person game built around physical actions — rolling paint, hammering tiles, carrying furniture. On a controller, expect:

- **Painting** — the roller follows your aim; sticks handle the slow, sweeping strokes of the painting mechanics well.
- **Precision tasks** — tiling and assembly involve placement; controller aiming is typically less precise than mouse, which slightly favors PC for speedrunners but changes nothing for passing inspections.
- **Carrying and throwing furniture** — movement-based, controller-friendly.

No official controller-layout details are announced. If keyboard/mouse is also supported on PS5 (some games do), the [controls guide](/guides/controls-guide) covers the PC layout as a reference.
