## What is Low-Budget Repairs?

Low-Budget Repairs puts you in the scuffed work boots of a shady handyman running a small renovation business in a **1990s Polish block of flats**. The goal is simple: make as much money as possible while spending as little as possible. That means diluting paint with water, laying tiles without a level, patching plumbing with duct tape, and collecting payment before the client notices the defects.

It's a comedy of cheap repairs — every job is a balancing act between speed, cost, and the risk of the client spotting your shortcuts. Furniture that doesn't fit through the door, barely-functional bargain-bin tools, and a cast of suspicious neighbours keep the humor front and center.
