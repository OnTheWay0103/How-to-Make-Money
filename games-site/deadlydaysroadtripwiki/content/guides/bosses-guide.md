# Deadly Days: Roadtrip Boss Guide — Laz & Final Fights

Every Deadly Days: Roadtrip run ends at a boss, and 1.0 added a fifth final boss: Laz. This guide covers the bosses the developer has named, explains how the Headquarters Run fits, and gives a prep checklist for every final fight. Boss health, damage, and phase timings are not published, so this page does not invent them.

## How Bosses Work in 1.0

A run is a road trip with a destination. If you survive the days and reach the end of the route, a final boss waits there. The 1.0 announcement refers to Laz as the **fifth final boss**, which tells us the structure: there are five final-boss slots, and each one sits at the end of a progression step.

The 0.21 update reworked the whole boss presentation:

- **New intro screens** with artwork and flavour text.
- **Rebranding** to fit the MKing Corp corporate theme, including new names and visuals.
- **A boss-defeat summary screen** with banners for what the run achieved or unlocked.

Because of that rebranding, older community guides may use names that no longer match the game. If a guide calls a boss something that sounds like a generic zombie title, it is probably pre-0.21.

## Confirmed Bosses

### Laz — the 1.0 Final Boss

Laz is the headline boss of 1.0. The official description: a former IT freelancer who stole a fast-food chain's cleaning robots and built a robot army, now "a serious security risk" and the **fifth final boss**.

What that tells you mechanically:

- **Robot army** implies adds. Expect the arena to fill with mechanical enemies rather than only zombies.
- **Fifth final boss** implies a step up in difficulty from the earlier finals. Do not arrive with a half-finished backpack.
- **Security-risk theme** suggests area denial or turret-like threats, but the exact attacks are `[Unconfirmed]` — the announcement does not list them.

The practical read: Laz is a crowd-plus-boss fight. A pure single-target build will spend too long clearing adds, and a pure AoE build will struggle to finish the boss. Bring one of each, or bring a weapon that pierces.

### Mr. Kyde — the Store Manager

Mr. Kyde arrived in the 0.21 update. Officially described as a store manager with "somewhat dualistic tendencies," he:

- **Throws bombs from his briefcase.** Expect ranged area denial.
- **Sheds his suit and pirouettes across the arena** once damaged enough. That is a phase change, not a cosmetic detail: expect his movement and attack pattern to change at the threshold.

Mr. Kyde is a positioning fight. Bombs punish standing still, and the phase change punishes a build that only works at one range. Keep a dash available for the transition and do not commit to a long melee animation when his health bar is near the threshold.

### The Research Boss

Patch 0.21.5 includes a fix for "the research boss" sometimes getting stuck in an invincible state. That confirms a research-themed boss exists, but the patch notes do not name it or describe its attacks. Treat the name as `[Unconfirmed]` and do not rely on a guide that claims a specific mechanic without a source.

### The Other Final Bosses

The 1.0 announcement says Laz is the fifth final boss, and the 0.21 update rebranded all bosses. The announcement does not list the current names of the other four. We are not going to invent them. If you need a route for a specific earlier final, use the in-game intro screen as the authority and treat community names as `[Unconfirmed]` until they match.

## The Headquarters Run — the Real Endgame

The Headquarters Run, added in 0.21, is the story climax and the endgame layer above normal runs:

1. **Survive enough runs** to gather what you need to strike back at MKing Corp.
2. **Build a bomb.**
3. **Infiltrate the MKing headquarters** across a multi-stage assault.
4. **Defeat the CEO**, the final boss of the headquarters.
5. **Blow up the building.**

Two systems make this harder than a normal run:

- **MKing Response Level.** While you prepare for the headquarters run, this level rises and gradually increases the game's difficulty.
- **Memo snippets.** Beating bosses repeatedly drops memo snippets you can read at the base, which fill in the backstory. They are collectibles, not power, but they are the reason to replay bosses after you have already beaten them.

The Headquarters Run assumes you can handle the earlier final bosses. Do not treat it as a separate mode you can jump into with a fresh save.

## Boss Prep Checklist

Before any final fight in 1.0:

- **One crowd weapon and one boss weapon.** The Grinder or Flamethrower for adds; the Mega Shotgun or Desert Eagle for the boss. See the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list).
- **A defensive item.** Armor, extra health, or a character item with a safety effect.
- **A movement answer.** Dash timing is the main defensive skill; a heavy weapon that slows you is a real cost.
- **A rearranged backpack.** Do not enter a boss with a half-finished inventory. Craft and rotate before the door.
- **Fuel and route slack.** You want to reach the boss with time to spare, not after a forced fight.
- **A coffee.** The 1.0 base coffee stand gives your next run increased movement and loot speed. It is a small buff that matters most in the first minute of a boss attempt.

## Boss-Fight Mistakes That End Runs

1. **Standing still to DPS.** Bombs, projectiles, and adds all punish it. Fire, move, fire.
2. **Ignoring adds.** A robot army or zombie wave will chip you down while you focus the boss.
3. **Hoarding cooldowns for the "real" phase.** Use them; the phase change is usually a reset, not a free window.
4. **Fighting the phase change.** When a boss changes form, reposition first and re-engage second.
5. **Bringing a build that only works at one range.** Mr. Kyde's phase change and Laz's adds both punish it.

## Co-op Boss Notes

Couch co-op makes boss fights easier to learn and harder to execute. Two players can split roles: one clears adds, one focuses the boss. The risk is the shared screen — if both players kite in the same direction, you lose the arena. Agree on a split before the fight: one player holds the left side, the other rotates.

Steam Remote Play works for online co-op, and the friend does not need to own the game. One player needs a controller. Latency is usually fine for a bullet heaven, but dash timing is the first thing to suffer.

Laz is the 1.0 benchmark: a robot-army final boss that punishes single-target tunnel vision. Mr. Kyde is the positioning check that arrived in 0.21. The Headquarters Run is the endgame that ties them together. Build one crowd weapon, one boss weapon, and a backpack you can rearrange under pressure — that is the whole boss curriculum.
