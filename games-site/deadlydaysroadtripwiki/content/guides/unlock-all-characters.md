# Deadly Days: Roadtrip — How to Unlock Every Character

Deadly Days: Roadtrip hides most of its roster behind in-run achievements and base-camp secrets. Some unlocks are concrete, like crafting three legendaries; others depend on random map events or surviving a long timer. This page lists every survivor we can source, gives the exact unlock method, and marks clearly which methods are official and which are community-reported.

## Unlock Table

| Survivor | Unlock method | Evidence | Difficulty |
|----------|---------------|----------|------------|
| Survivor | Available from the start | Community | None |
| Banana Person | Run over the banana costume in the base camp | Community | Very easy |
| Sheriff | Win one run | Community | Easy |
| Chicken | Craft 3 legendary items (workbench required) | Community | Medium |
| Astronaut | Find and defend the spaceship event in Canyon, Mountains, or Snowy Peaks | Community | Random |
| Death (Grim Reaper) | Stay in one location for 12 minutes, then touch or kill Death when it appears | Community | Hard |
| Bushy | Complete Sharp-Shooter: kill 1,000 zombies with the rifle | Official | Medium grind |
| Penny the Clown | Complete Juggler: have 3 Rubber Balls in your backpack and active | Official | Easy |
| Vampire | Meet the shady merchant (official hint; exact steps not published) | Official hint | Medium |
| Surfing survivor | Have 6 dashes worth of stamina | Official | Medium |
| President | Enter the secret password on the start menu | Community | Very easy |
| Santa | Unlock method not published | `[Unconfirmed]` | Unknown |

Two names that appear in search data are **not** confirmed survivors. "Knabe" is associated with a Knabe Kola collaboration in press material, not a playable character. "Pumpkin" appears in patch notes as a pumpkin turret, which is an item or enemy, not necessarily a survivor. We are not going to invent unlock steps for either.

## Starters and Easy Unlocks

### Survivor

The default character. No unlock, no gimmick, and the right choice for your first run because it keeps the tutorial simple. Once you have another survivor, the Survivor becomes a baseline rather than a main.

### Banana Person

**Community-reported:** run over the banana costume lying on the ground in the base camp. The costume is a physical object in the camp, not a menu item. Walk or drive over it and the unlock fires.

This is the fastest unlock in the game and the least useful character. Treat it as a co-op joke or a challenge run.

### President

**Community-reported:** on the start screen, click the key icon in the bottom-left corner and enter:

```
erstknacktsdannknallts
```

No spaces. The password is a built-in secret, not a promotional code. Since Deadly Days: Roadtrip is a buy-to-play game, there is no official code system, no newsletter codes, and no redeemable item codes. Do not trust a site that claims otherwise.

## Combat Unlocks

### Sheriff

**Community-reported:** win one run. This is the natural second unlock for most players, because finishing a run is the first milestone anyway. If you are consistently dying at the first boss, the Sheriff is not the problem — your backpack or route is. Read the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide) before you blame the character.

### Bushy

**Official:** complete the Sharp-Shooter achievement by killing **1,000 zombies with the rifle**. This is a grind, not a puzzle. The rifle is a common weapon, so the unlock happens naturally if you commit to rifle runs. Bushy's Trap Seeds are strong enough to justify the time; see the [character tier list](/guides/character-tier-list).

### Penny the Clown

**Official:** complete the Juggler achievement by having **3 Rubber Balls in your backpack and active**. The important word is "active": the balls must be equipped and functioning, not sitting in the stash. Three Rubber Balls is a concrete target, which makes Penny the best early unlock for most players.

### Vampire

**Official hint:** the 1.0 announcement says "a meeting with the shady merchant might be mandatory." That confirms the merchant is involved, but the developer did not publish the full sequence. Community guides may have a step-by-step route; treat any unverified step as `[Unconfirmed]`.

The Vampire is the strongest 1.0 survivor once it snowballs, so the unlock is worth chasing after Penny and Bushy. It starts without a weapon, so do the unlock run on a difficulty you can survive.

## Crafting and Base Unlocks

### Chicken

**Community-reported:** craft **3 legendary items**. This requires the workbench, and the workbench requires tape. If you have not opened the workbench yet, the Chicken is not available no matter how many items you loot.

The Chicken's identity is a mechanical egg that spawns chicks. It is a build-hungry character: getting three legendaries means committing to crafting, which means giving up inventory space you could spend on immediate power. Unlock it when you are already comfortable with the workbench loop.

## Random and Hidden Unlocks

### Astronaut

**Community-reported:** find and defend the **spaceship event** in The Canyon, The Mountains, or The Snowy Peaks. The event is random, so the practical method is to replay those biomes until it appears. Once you find the shuttle, defend it and take the spacesuit.

Official patch notes reference an Astronaut spacesuit armor upgrade, which confirms the spacesuit is a real character item. The randomness is the cost; there is no known way to force the event.

### Death (Grim Reaper)

**Community-reported:** stay in one location for **12 minutes**. Death then appears on the radar as a skull icon. Touching it — or killing it — unlocks the character.

This is the hardest unlock in the game because it forces you to fight continuously instead of kiting between stops. Build for survival, not speed: armor, life steal, and crowd clear. The 0.20 patch notes mention a fix for the Reaper failing to spawn when the player is too close to the bus, so if the timer completes and nothing appears, move away from the bus and wait.

### Surfing Survivor

**Official:** the 0.22 update added a survivor whose character item is a surfboard and whose ability is "surf instead of dash." The unlock condition is to **have 6 dashes worth of stamina**. The announcement does not give the character's name, so this page does not either.

## Santa and Other Unconfirmed Names

Santa appears in patch notes as a "Santa character item," which suggests a Santa-themed survivor or item set. The unlock method is **not published**, and we could not find a reliable community method. `[Unconfirmed]`.

The same applies to any character name you see only in search suggestions. Search suggestions are not evidence. If a guide cannot point to an official announcement or a reproducible in-game step, treat it as a rumour.

## Do Unlocks Carry Between Runs and Saves?

Character unlocks are permanent account progress, not per-run unlocks. The 1.0 announcement confirms that Early Access save games carry over to 1.0, with a possible flood of achievement unlocks on first load. If you played during Early Access, check your roster before re-grinding an unlock.

Co-op does not change unlock rules. Both players use the host's roster, and unlocks are tied to the save profile rather than the session.

## Recommended Unlock Order

1. **Sheriff** — finish one run; confirms you understand the loop.
2. **Penny** — three Rubber Balls is the easiest concrete target.
3. **Chicken** — if you enjoy crafting; otherwise skip until later.
4. **Bushy** — rifle grind, predictable.
5. **Vampire** — best 1.0 payoff, but needs a stable run.
6. **Astronaut** — random event; do it when you are replaying biomes anyway.
7. **Death** — the long-term project.
8. **Banana Person / President** — novelty.

## Bottom Line

Penny is the best first unlock, Bushy is the best grind, and Vampire is the best payoff. The Astronaut and Death depend on randomness and endurance, so leave them for later. There are no promotional codes in this game; the only password is the community-reported President secret, and it is a built-in unlock, not a redeemable code.
