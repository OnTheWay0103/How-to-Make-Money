# Crimson Moon FAQ — Release, Co-op, Builds & Systems

## Release & Purchase

### When does Crimson Moon come out?
**September 1, 2026.** The date was confirmed on August 4, 2026. ([Price & Editions Guide](/guides/price-platforms))

### How much does it cost?
$19.99 for the **Standard Edition**, $29.99 for the **Deluxe Edition** (adds future content expansions + 2 cosmetic/weapon DLC packs).

### What platforms is it on?
**PC (Steam + Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.** Steam App ID: **4317690**. PS5 Pro gets PSSR support.

### Is it on Game Pass or PS Plus?
No day-one Game Pass or PS Plus inclusion has been announced as of August 28, 2026.

### Who made Crimson Moon?
**ProbablyMonsters**, the studio founded by Harold Ryan (former Bungie CEO). It was revealed at State of Play on February 12, 2026.

## Co-op & Multiplayer

### Is Crimson Moon co-op?
**Yes — 2-player online co-op** with a shared life pool and revive. ([Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide))

### Can I play solo?
**Yes — solo is fully supported** and difficulty scales with player count. ([Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide))

### Is there crossplay?
**Not confirmed as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** The game is on all three platform families; crossplay details were not published.

### Is there couch co-op or split screen?
**Not confirmed [Unconfirmed].** Co-op is described as online. If couch co-op is a requirement, wait for official confirmation.

## Builds & Systems

### Does Crimson Moon have classes?
**No.** Builds combine **Equipment + Boons + Weapon Arts** freely. ([Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide))

### What are Incursions?
**Repeatable 15–45 minute missions** with dynamic maps and enemy configurations — the core loop. ([Incursions Guide](/guides/incursions-guide))

### How does purification work?
Gear found in runs can be **purified to become permanent** in your arsenal; everything else is lost when the run ends. ([Purification Guide](/guides/purification-guide))

### What are Boons?
**Run-scoped temporary powers/affixes** you pick up inside an Incursion. ([Boons Guide](/guides/boons-guide))

### What are Weapon Arts?
**Special abilities attached to weapons** — the combat centerpiece, with emphasis on co-op coordination. ([Weapon Arts Guide](/guides/weapon-arts-guide))

## Story & Bosses

### What is Crimson Moon about?
A soulslike action RPG set in the **fallen gothic city of Gildenarch**. You play a **Nephilim** pushing back the **Hellgrowth** corruption and hunting the vampire lord **Solomon Moore**.

### What are the Dead Gods?
The **final boss encounter**, unlocked after defeating the world boss in each of the three Wards. ([Dead Gods Guide](/guides/dead-gods-boss-guide))

### How many Wards are there?
**Three Wards**, each ending in a world boss. ([Three Wards Guide](/guides/wards-gildenarch-guide))

## Practical Questions

### What are the PC system requirements?
**Not published as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** PS5 Pro PSSR is confirmed; PC specs will be added to the Steam page. ([System Requirements Guide](/guides/system-requirements))

### How long is Crimson Moon?
**No official estimate [Unconfirmed].** Three Wards + the Dead Gods campaign, plus repeatable Incursions. ([How Long to Beat](/guides/game-length-guide))

### Is there a demo?
The game was playable at Summer Game Fest 2026 and has run playtests. A public demo has **not been confirmed [Unconfirmed].**

### Where is the hub?
**Sanctus Clypeus** cathedral — upgrades, forging, manuscript translation, War Table quests, and NPCs including a vampire blacksmith. ([Hub Guide](/guides/sanctus-clypeus-hub-guide))

### What is the soundtrack like?
**Metal** — every boss has its own track, featuring HEALTH, Jared Dines, Misha Mansoor (Periphery), and Ricky Armellino (Ice Nine Kills).
