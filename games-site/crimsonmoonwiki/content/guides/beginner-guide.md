# Crimson Moon Beginner Guide — First Steps in Gildenarch

## What Is Crimson Moon?

**Crimson Moon** is a soulslike action RPG by **ProbablyMonsters** — the studio founded by Harold Ryan, former CEO of Bungie. You play a **Nephilim** (half-human, half-angel) in the fallen gothic city of **Gildenarch**, fighting a corrupting force called **Hellgrowth** and hunting the vampire lord **Solomon Moore**.

The loop blends Dark Souls-style melee combat with Diablo-like loot and a roguelike run structure. You enter repeatable missions called **Incursions**, fight through dynamic maps, and bring gear back to the hub — **Sanctus Clypeus** cathedral — where you can keep the pieces worth keeping.

## Before Your First Run

### 1. Pick a Mode That Fits You

Crimson Moon is fully playable **solo** or in **2-player online co-op**. If you have a friend, co-op is the fastest way to learn — you share a life pool, can revive each other, and difficulty scales with player count. Solo is completely viable, but expect to be more careful with positioning.

### 2. Know the Combat Fundamentals

Previewers (IGN, GamesBeat) describe the combat as deliberate and melee-focused, with strong movement and blocking. The core skills to practice early:

- **Blocking / parrying** — the foundation of soulslike defense. Master the timing before you rely on it.
- **Dodging / repositioning** — movement was called out as a highlight by early hands-on previews.
- **Executions** — finishing stunned or staggered enemies is a low-risk way to take them out of a fight.
- **Weapon Arts** — your weapon's special ability. The official Builds overview trailer emphasized coordinating these in co-op.

### 3. The Nephilim / Angel Transformation

IGN's preview highlighted a powerful **angel transformation** for the Nephilim. Treat it as a strong, limited resource — save it for the hardest fights rather than using it on trash. Exact mechanics (duration, cooldown, unlock) will be confirmed at launch [Unconfirmed].

## Your First Incursion

Incursions are the core loop — repeatable missions that last roughly **15–45 minutes**. Here is what to expect:

1. **Enter a run** from the hub, Sanctus Clypeus. Check the **War Table** quest board for objectives.
2. **Fight through a dynamically laid-out map** — enemy configurations vary between runs.
3. **Loot gear** along the way. Weapons, armor, and materials drop from enemies and chests.
4. **Purify what you want to keep.** This is the key decision: purified gear becomes a permanent part of your arsenal in storage. Everything else is lost when the run ends.
5. **Finish the objective or extract.** Clear the run to bank your rewards.

## What to Keep in Your First Few Runs

A simple rule for beginners: **purify gear that fits how you want to fight, and be picky.** Since the purification system makes gear permanent, you do not want to fill storage with items you will replace in two runs.

- **First run:** keep a weapon type that feels good — try each weapon's **Weapon Art**.
- **First few runs:** keep one solid weapon for your preferred range and one defensive/utility piece.
- **Check set bonuses** — the equipment set guide covers why matching pieces can matter more than raw stats.

## Where to Upgrade

Return to **Sanctus Clypeus** between runs. The cathedral hub is the confirmed home for:

- **Upgrades and forging** — improving the gear you have purified
- **Manuscript translation** — a progression system teased in official material
- **The War Table** — the quest board that gives you objectives
- **NPCs including a vampire blacksmith** — crafting and gear services

## Mistakes Beginners Should Avoid

- **Purifying everything.** Storage is a long-term asset — be selective.
- **Ignoring Weapon Arts.** They are a core part of the classless build system; experiment early.
- **Saving the angel transformation forever.** In a 15–45 minute run, use it before a fight turns bad, not when you are already dead.
- **Skipping the hub.** Upgrades and War Table quests are account-wide progression — do not run straight into Incursions forever.
- **Rushing a dynamic map.** Incursion layouts change every run; scout before you commit.
