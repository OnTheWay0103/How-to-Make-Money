# Crimson Moon Boons Guide — Run-Scoped Power & Affixes

## What Are Boons?

**Boons** are the run-scoped powers and affixes in Crimson Moon. You pick them up **inside an Incursion**, they empower you for the rest of that run, and they are gone when the run ends. They are the roguelike layer of the game: the part that makes every run different even when you use the same weapon and gear.

Boons are one of the three build layers (Equipment + Boons + Weapon Arts) and the most temporary one.

## How Boons Differ From Equipment

| | Boons | Equipment |
|---|-------|-----------|
| **Duration** | One run only | Permanent (once purified) |
| **When you get them** | Inside an Incursion | Found in runs, kept via purification |
| **What they do** | Temporary buffs / affixes | Base stats, set bonuses, permanent power |
| **Build role** | Make a run feel different | Define your long-term identity |

The trade-off is the core of Crimson Moon's progression: Boons give you power **now**, while purified equipment builds power **forever**. A good player balances both — take the Boon that wins this run, but make sure you are also pulling gear out for the runs after it.

## What Kinds of Boons Are There?

Specific Boon names and their exact effects are **[Unconfirmed]** until launch, but the official material and previews point to the familiar roguelike roles:

| Boon Type | Effect Direction | Example Intention |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Damage affixes** | Increase damage output | + damage to a weapon type, crit-style bonuses |
| **Survivability** | Keep you alive | HP, healing, damage reduction |
| **Mobility / utility** | Change how you move or interact | Faster dodges, better recovery |
| **Weapon Arts synergy** | Amplify your Art | Cooldown reduction, empowered Arts |
| **Resource / economy** | Improve loot or rewards | Better drops, more purification value |

Previewers described Boons as **build-defining within a run** — the right Boon can transform a loadout. Noisy Pixel was more cautious, suggesting some affixes leaned on **numeric stacking** (simple "+X%" bumps) rather than transformative effects. Both takes will be resolved once players get hands-on with the final build.

## How Do You Get Boons?

The exact acquisition flow inside an Incursion is part of the run structure and was not fully detailed in official material [Unconfirmed]. The [Incursions Guide](/guides/incursions-guide) documents what is confirmed about the run loop. Expect Boons to come from the same loop that produces gear — encounters, rewards, and mission objectives.

## How to Choose Boons

Because a Boon only lasts one run, the question is always: **"What makes this run better?"** Not "what builds my account?"

1. **Support your Weapon Art.** If your Art is crowd control, damage affixes that capitalize on locked-down enemies are high value. If your Art is self-sustain, you can take more aggressive Boons.
2. **Match the mission type.** In a long Incursion, survivability and sustain Boons compound. In a short one, front-loaded damage wins.
3. **Co-op: fill the gap.** With a partner, take the Boons your partner is not covering — one sustain, one damage.
4. **Watch the shared pool.** In co-op, survivability Boons help both of you because you share one life pool.

## Boons vs. Purification: The Resource Decision

The cleanest way to think about it:

- **Boons = win this run.** Take the ones that make the current Incursion easier or more profitable.
- **Purification = win every run.** Selectively keep gear that supports the build identity you are committing to.

They do not really compete — Boons are spent, gear is kept. The skill is in **not over-committing to a Boon-heavy run** at the expense of extracting the gear worth keeping. See the [Purification Guide](/guides/purification-guide) for the keep-versus-spend decision.
