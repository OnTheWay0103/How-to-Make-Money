# Crimson Moon Equipment Sets & Weapons — What We Know

## How Equipment Works in Crimson Moon

Equipment is the **permanent layer** of the classless build system. It is the gear you purify from Incursions and keep in your arsenal. While Boons are temporary and Weapon Arts define how you fight, equipment defines **what you are built from** — your stats, your resistances, and your set bonuses.

Because there are no classes, your equipment does the work that classes would normally do: it decides whether you are a tank, a damage dealer, or something in between.

## Weapon Types

Crimson Moon is a melee-first soulslike, so weapon variety is central. The **exact weapon list and their names are [Unconfirmed]** until launch, but the weapon system is confirmed to include:

- **Melee weapons** — the core of soulslike combat (swords, axes, hammers, and similar archetypes)
- **Weapon-specific Weapon Arts** — each weapon carries its own Art (see the [Weapon Arts Guide](/guides/weapon-arts-guide))
- **Weapon materials ("weapon mats")** — materials are implied by the hub's forging system; you will upgrade weapons at the blacksmith

Previewers consistently described the combat identity as melee-driven with deliberate timing, so most builds will center on a chosen melee weapon and its Art.

## Armor and Set Bonuses

The official material confirms **equipment sets with set bonuses**. This is the Diablo-style layer of the loot system:

- Matching pieces of the same set grant **set bonuses** — additional effects you only get by wearing a complete (or partial) set.
- Set bonuses reward **committing to an identity** rather than mixing random high-stat pieces.
- Because gear is purified permanently, set-building is a long-term project: you are collecting the pieces of the sets you want, run after run.

### Set-Bonus Decision Framework

| Situation | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| Two pieces complete a set bonus | Strongly consider purifying them even if raw stats are lower |
| A single piece is a big stat upgrade but breaks a set | Weigh the set bonus value vs the stat gain |
| You keep finding the same set's pieces | Commit — a completed set beats mixed pieces |
| Pieces conflict with your Weapon Art identity | Skip; the Art is your combat identity (see [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide)) |

## The Permanent Arsenal

Purified equipment lives in your **permanent arsenal** — the storage at the hub. This is what makes builds durable:

- **Every run adds to it.** Good drops you purify become available forever.
- **It is the backbone of builds.** Your long-term identity is the set of equipment you have accumulated.
- **It changes the risk calculation.** Even a failed run can advance your arsenal if you purified something worth keeping before the end.

## Equipment, Boons, and Weapon Arts Together

The three layers combine into a build:

1. **Equipment / Sets** — permanent stats and set bonuses (this guide)
2. **Boons** — temporary run power ([Boons Guide](/guides/boons-guide))
3. **Weapon Arts** — combat identity ([Weapon Arts Guide](/guides/weapon-arts-guide))

When planning a build, choose the **Weapon Art** you enjoy, then match **equipment sets** that support it, then take **Boons** that amplify it during a run.

## Forging and Upgrading

The hub — **Sanctus Clypeus** — includes forging services and a **vampire blacksmith** NPC. This is where weapon materials feed into upgrading your permanent gear. The exact upgrade system (tiers, costs, materials) was not fully detailed before launch [Unconfirmed]. See the [Sanctus Clypeus Hub Guide](/guides/sanctus-clypeus-hub-guide) for the hub overview.
