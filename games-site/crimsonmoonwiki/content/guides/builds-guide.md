# Crimson Moon Builds Guide — Classless Builds with Boons + Weapon Arts

## Does Crimson Moon Have Classes?

**No.** Crimson Moon has **no classes, no jobs, and no locked roles.** This is the single most important thing to understand about building a character in the game — and it is why "Crimson Moon builds" became a top search before the game even launched.

Your build is whatever you make it from **three freely combined layers**:

1. **Equipment** — weapons, armor, and gear with set bonuses
2. **Boons** — run-scoped temporary powers and affixes
3. **Weapon Arts** — the special ability attached to your weapon

## The Three Build Layers

### Equipment (Permanent)
The gear you **purify** from Incursions becomes a permanent part of your arsenal. Equipment defines your stats, resistances, and **set bonuses**. Because it is permanent, equipment is your long-term power source — the pieces you decide to keep are the backbone of every future run.

### Boons (Run-Scoped)
Boons give you power **inside a single Incursion**. They are temporary — picked up or earned during a run and lost when it ends (unless tied to gear). Previewers described Boons as build-defining within a run, though some felt certain affixes leaned toward numeric stacking. The [Boons Guide](/guides/boons-guide) covers them in depth.

### Weapon Arts (Combat Identity)
Your weapon's special ability — the flashiest layer and the one the official **Builds overview trailer** spent the most time on. Weapon Arts define how a fight actually plays out: how you close distance, create space, apply crowd control, or burst damage. See the [Weapon Arts Guide](/guides/weapon-arts-guide).

## How to Plan a Build

Because there are no classes, a good Crimson Moon build is a **three-layer answer to a question**:

> "How do I want to fight, and what do I need to support that?"

### Step 1 — Pick a Combat Identity
Start with the **Weapon Art** you enjoy. Do you want to:
- Get in close and stagger enemies? Pick a melee weapon whose Art applies pressure.
- Fight at range and kite? Pick a ranged weapon and boons that keep enemies away.
- Be the co-op partner who locks enemies down? Pick an Art with crowd control.

### Step 2 — Support It with Boons
Within a run, take Boons that reinforce the identity. If your Weapon Art is a burst damage tool, stack damage affixes. If you are the defensive co-op partner, take survivability and sustain Boons. Boons are where a build "feels" different run to run.

### Step 3 — Make It Permanent with Equipment
Purify gear that matches the identity — same weapon type, armor that supports your positioning, and **set bonuses** that line up. Because equipment is permanent, this is the layer that takes planning: you are committing to an identity by what you keep.

## Solo vs Co-op Builds

| | Solo | Co-op |
|---|------|-------|
| **Priority** | Survivability + self-sustain + reliable damage | Coordination + role split |
| **Weapon Arts** | Cover your own gaps (self-heal, crowd control, escape) | Chain with your partner (lockdown → burst) |
| **Boons** | Defensive affixes matter more — no one can revive you | Complementary (one sustain, one damage) |
| **Equipment** | Versatile set bonuses | Sets that match your half of the duo |

The [Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide) covers the shared life pool, which changes how much defense you need — in co-op your partner's mistakes drain the same pool, so some defensive investment helps both of you.

## Build Ideas (Pre-Release Frameworks)

These are frameworks from official material and previews — **specific weapon and Boon names will be added after launch [Unconfirmed]:**

- **"Angel Burst"** — use the Nephilim angel transformation as the centerpiece, stacking damage Boons to make the transformation window count. Save Boons that extend or enhance it.
- **"Co-op Control"** — one partner builds crowd-control Weapon Arts + lockdown Boons; the other builds burst. Chain the Art into the other's damage window.
- **"Purified Tank"** — prioritize defensive set bonuses and survivability Boons, using Weapon Arts with self-sustain. Strong solo and co-op.
- **"Run Engineer"** — focus on Boon synergy over raw gear, cycling Incursions to stack the right temporary affixes for the mission type.

## What Previewers Said About the Build System

- **IGN** compared it to "Diablo loot with Dark Souls melee" — the loot/build layer is substantial.
- **Noisy Pixel** (most cautious) felt some roguelike affixes leaned on numeric stacking rather than transformative choices.
- **Official Builds trailer** centered **Weapon Arts coordination** in co-op — that is the clearest signal of what the developers value in a build.

Take the preview takes with context: they are impressions of a pre-release build, not a review of the final game.
