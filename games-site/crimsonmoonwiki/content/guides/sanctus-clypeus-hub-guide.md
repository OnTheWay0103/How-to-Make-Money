# Sanctus Clypeus Hub Guide — Upgrades, Forging & NPCs

## What Is Sanctus Clypeus?

**Sanctus Clypeus** is the cathedral hub of Crimson Moon — your home base in the fallen city of Gildenarch. It is where you go **between Incursions** to turn the loot you extracted into permanent power.

If the Incursions are the game's "action," Sanctus Clypeus is the "management" — the place where the classless build system, the purification economy, and mission progression all come together.

## What the Hub Offers

### 1. Upgrades

The hub is where your **permanent gear gets stronger**. Combined with purification, this is how a run's loot becomes a long-term asset: you purify what you keep, then upgrade it at the hub.

### 2. Forging (Vampire Blacksmith)

The hub includes a **vampire blacksmith NPC**. This is the confirmed crafting / gear-services NPC. Forging is where **weapon materials ("weapon mats")** from your runs get spent — the weapon upgrade economy. The exact forge recipes and material types are [Unconfirmed] pre-release.

### 3. Manuscript Translation

One of the more unusual confirmed systems: you **translate manuscripts** at the hub. This is a progression track that appears to tie into the game's lore and unlocks. Its exact rewards are [Unconfirmed].

### 4. War Table (Quest Board)

The **War Table** is the mission board. It gives you quests and objectives — the primary way you choose what to run next, both for the Wards' main progression and for Incursions. See the [Three Wards Guide](/guides/wards-gildenarch-guide) for how War Table quests feed into Ward progression.

### 5. NPCs

Beyond the vampire blacksmith, the hub hosts other NPCs. The full roster and their services are [Unconfirmed], but the hub is confirmed as the social/service center of the game.

## How the Hub Fits the Loop

The full Crimson Moon loop:

1. **Run Incursions** → loot gear and materials
2. **Purify what to keep** → gear becomes permanent
3. **Return to Sanctus Clypeus** → forge, upgrade, translate, take new quests
4. **Progress the Wards** → world bosses → Dead Gods

The hub is the glue: without it, the loot from Incursions would not turn into the builds you need for the boss ladder.

## Efficient Hub Habits

- **Purify before you upgrade.** Only upgrade gear you are keeping permanently — spending materials on throwaway gear wastes the hub's services.
- **Check the War Table every visit.** Quests guide what to run next and often line up with the materials or set pieces you need.
- **Translate manuscripts as you find them.** It is a confirmed progression system — do not leave it ignored.
- **Bank materials for the blacksmith.** Weapon mats are the forge currency; hoard the ones matching your weapon type.
