# Crimson Moon Co-op Guide — Shared Life Pool, Revive & Crossplay

## Is Crimson Moon Co-op?

**Yes.** Crimson Moon supports **2-player online co-op**. It is one of the most asked questions about the game, and the official material is explicit: you can fight through Gildenarch with one partner, sharing a life pool and reviving each other.

The game is also **fully playable solo** — co-op is an option, not a requirement.

## How Many Players Can Play Co-op?

Crimson Moon is designed for **two players** (you + one partner). There is no confirmed 3–4 player mode. If you want to play with a larger group, that is not part of Crimson Moon's design as announced.

## How Does the Shared Life Pool Work?

Crimson Moon uses a **shared life pool** for the co-op pair. Instead of two entirely separate health bars, the two of you draw from a common pool of life.

- When a partner takes damage, it reduces the shared pool.
- The pool is the team's combined health — you are literally surviving together.
- This is the key difference from standard co-op: careless deaths on one side cost both of you.

**Practical implication:** in a shared-life-pool game, defense and positioning are team responsibilities. One player facetanking while the other stacks pure damage can drain the pool fast.

## How Does Revive Work?

Partners can **revive each other**. If your partner goes down, you can bring them back — this is the intended counter to the shared life pool's risk.

- Revive is a core reason to play co-op: a partner can pull you back from a mistake.
- Reviving under fire is risky — the official material frames the revive as a tactical decision (when to stop fighting to revive).
- Solo players do not get a partner revive, which is one of the main reasons solo is more demanding.

## Does Difficulty Scale for Co-op?

Yes — difficulty scales dynamically with player count. A 2-player run is tuned for two players rather than simply doubling solo health values. This is what keeps both solo and co-op viable.

## Can You Play Crimson Moon Solo?

**Yes, 100%.** The game is designed to be played solo or with a partner. Solo play is not a locked-out mode — you simply do not have a partner to share the life pool or revive you. The [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) covers solo-specific strategy.

## Co-op Build Coordination

The official **Builds overview trailer** emphasized coordinating **Weapon Arts** between the two partners. Because there are no classes, the "tank and damage" or "control and burst" split is something you build into your loadout rather than pick at character creation.

Good co-op thinking:

- **One player leans defensive/sustain, the other leans damage** — a natural division in a classless game.
- **Chain Weapon Arts** — one partner locks an enemy down (crowd control) while the other unloads.
- **Watch the shared pool together** — call out when the pool is getting low; that is the moment to retreat and regroup, not push.

## Crossplay Status

**Crossplay support across PC, PS5, and Xbox had not been officially detailed as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** The game is confirmed on all three platform families, and 2-player online co-op is confirmed, but whether PC players can join PS5 players (and so on) was not stated in official material we could verify. This guide will be updated the moment crossplay is confirmed.

## Local / Couch Co-op and Split Screen

**Local/couch co-op and split screen were not confirmed as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** Co-op is described as online (invite/join a partner). If you are specifically shopping for a couch co-op game, wait for official confirmation before assuming Crimson Moon fills that slot.

## Common Co-op Questions

### Does co-op have matchmaking or need friends?
The official material describes playing "with a friend" / "with one partner." Whether there is open matchmaking with strangers was not detailed [Unconfirmed].

### Is co-op the same content as solo?
Yes — the same Wards, world bosses, and Dead Gods. The difference is the shared life pool, revive, and dynamic difficulty scaling.

### Can I switch between solo and co-op?
You can play solo or with a partner at different times; the game scales accordingly. Save/session specifics will be confirmed at launch.
