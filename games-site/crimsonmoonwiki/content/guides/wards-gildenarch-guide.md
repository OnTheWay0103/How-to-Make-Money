# Crimson Moon Three Wards Guide — Gildenarch Progression

## What Is Gildenarch?

**Gildenarch** is the setting of Crimson Moon — a **fallen gothic city** described in official material as styled after the **High Renaissance**. It is the place you are fighting to reclaim from a corrupting force called **Hellgrowth**.

Important structural fact: Gildenarch is **not an open world**. Crimson Moon is **mission-based** — you progress through the city via structured missions rather than free exploration. This is a confirmed design choice that shapes how you should think about the game.

## The Three Wards

Gildenarch is split into **three Wards** (districts). Each Ward is a major chunk of the main progression and ends in a **world boss**. This is the confirmed macro-structure:

| Ward | Content | Boss |
|------|---------|------|
| **Ward 1** | First district, intro missions | World boss #1 |
| **Ward 2** | Second district, higher difficulty | World boss #2 |
| **Ward 3** | Third district, endgame Wards | World boss #3 |

After all three Ward bosses fall, the **final encounter — the Dead Gods** — unlocks. See the [Dead Gods & World Bosses Guide](/guides/dead-gods-boss-guide) for the boss ladder.

**Ward names and zone-by-zone details were not published as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** This guide documents the confirmed framework; the specifics will be filled in after launch.

## How Progression Works

1. **Start from the hub** — Sanctus Clypeus cathedral is your base of operations.
2. **Take missions** — the War Table quest board gives you objectives to progress through the Wards.
3. **Clear a Ward** — work through its missions until you reach and defeat its world boss.
4. **Repeat for the next Ward** — difficulty ramps.
5. **Unlock the Dead Gods** — once all three world bosses are down.

Along the way, **Incursions** (the repeatable 15–45 minute runs) feed you the gear you need. The [Incursions Guide](/guides/incursions-guide) covers the run loop; this guide covers the main progression track.

## The Hellgrowth Corruption

**Hellgrowth** is the corrupting entity spreading through Gildenarch. It is the environmental threat of the setting — the reason the city is "fallen." Its exact gameplay role (enemy types, map hazards, boss influence) is [Unconfirmed] pre-release, but it is confirmed as the corruption you are pushing back.

## The Sanctus Clypeus Hub

Your home base in Gildenarch is **Sanctus Clypeus** cathedral. It is where you:

- **Upgrade and forge** gear
- **Translate manuscripts** (a confirmed progression system)
- **Pick up War Table quests**
- **Talk to NPCs**, including a **vampire blacksmith**

The [Sanctus Clypeus Hub Guide](/guides/sanctus-clypeus-hub-guide) covers the hub in depth.

## What to Do First in Gildenarch

1. **Learn the mission loop** — take a few War Table quests to understand how mission-based progression feels.
2. **Don't rush the Wards** — farm Incursions for purified gear before pushing a Ward's world boss. The boss ladder is the difficulty gate; gear is your answer to it.
3. **Build for the boss you face** — before each Ward's world boss, make sure your Weapon Art and equipment set match the fight ([Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide)).
4. **Use co-op for the world bosses** — the shared life pool and revive make the tougher Ward bosses far more manageable with a partner.
