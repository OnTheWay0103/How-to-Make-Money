# Crimson Moon Incursions — How 15–45 Minute Runs Work

## What Is an Incursion?

An **Incursion** is the repeatable mission type at the heart of Crimson Moon's core loop. It is a **15–45 minute run** with a **dynamically generated map and enemy configuration** — no two Incursions play out the same way.

Officially, ProbablyMonsters published a **"How Runs Work"** gameplay explainer that walks through this structure. It is the clearest signal of what the game actually is: not a pure open-world soulslike, but a **run-based action RPG** where you repeatedly dive into missions, loot, and return to the hub stronger.

## The Incursion Loop

1. **Prepare at the hub** — Sanctus Clypeus cathedral. Check the **War Table** quest board, equip your permanent gear, choose your weapon.
2. **Launch an Incursion** — pick a mission and enter the dynamic map.
3. **Fight and loot** — clear enemies, find gear and materials, and pursue the mission objective.
4. **Purify what you keep** — gear you purify becomes permanent in your arsenal ([Purification Guide](/guides/purification-guide)).
5. **Finish or extract** — complete the objective to bank the run's rewards.

## Why Dynamic Maps Matter

The dynamic map and enemy configuration mean Crimson Moon's Incursions are designed for **replayability**:

- **No memorized routes** — you scout each run fresh.
- **Difficulty variety** — enemy compositions change, so the same mission can play very differently.
- **Roguelike identity** — combined with Boons, each run is a new puzzle of "what am I working with this time?"

This is why the keyword report flagged "how runs work" as a gap worth filling — the official explainer was the only English-language coverage of the run structure before launch.

## What to Do Inside a Run

### Set a goal before you drop in
Every Incursion should have an intent:

- **Farm a specific set piece** — target missions that drop what your build needs.
- **Test a Weapon Art** — use a run to experiment with a new weapon's Art.
- **Gather materials** — weapon mats for the hub's forging services.
- **Progress a War Table quest** — the quest board gives missions a purpose.

### Be selective with purification mid-run
Remember the golden rule from the [Purification Guide](/guides/purification-guide): purify for permanence, not panic. A run's haul is temporary until you purify the pieces that fit your build.

### Manage the shared life pool (co-op)
In co-op, an Incursion drains a **shared life pool**. If you push too deep with a low pool, you risk failing the run for both of you — learn when to extract. See the [Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide).

## Incursions vs. the Main Wards

Crimson Moon has two content tracks:

| | Incursions | Wards (Main Progression) |
|---|------------|--------------------------|
| **Purpose** | Repeatable farming / core loop | Story and boss progression |
| **Structure** | 15–45 min dynamic runs | Fixed zones with world bosses |
| **Reward** | Gear, materials, purification targets | Progress toward the Dead Gods |

The [Three Wards Guide](/guides/wards-gildenarch-guide) covers the main progression track. Incursions are the repeatable engine that feeds the gear you need for it.
