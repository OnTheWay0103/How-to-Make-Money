# Crimson Moon Dead Gods & World Bosses — All Bosses Overview

## The Boss Structure

Crimson Moon's main progression is built around a clear boss ladder:

1. **Three Wards** — the city of Gildenarch is split into three districts (Wards).
2. **One world boss per Ward** — each Ward culminates in a world boss you must defeat to progress.
3. **The Dead Gods** — defeat all three world bosses to unlock the **final boss encounter: the Dead Gods**.

This is the confirmed macro-structure of the game. Think of it as the skeleton the Incursions and builds hang off of.

## Who Are the Bosses?

### The Three World Bosses (one per Ward)

The three Wards each hold a world boss. **Their names and full details had not been published as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** What is confirmed is the structure: each Ward ends with its own boss, and clearing all three opens the final fight.

The [Three Wards Guide](/guides/wards-gildenarch-guide) covers the zone progression. As each Ward's boss becomes documented after launch, this page will grow into a full boss reference with tactics.

### The Dead Gods (Final Boss)

The **Dead Gods** is the final encounter, unlocked after all three world bosses fall. It is the capstone of the main progression. Its mechanics are naturally [Unconfirmed] until players reach it at launch — **no guide site will have verified Dead Gods strategies before September 1.**

### Solomon Moore (The Antagonist)

The main villain of Crimson Moon is **Solomon Moore, a vampire lord**. He is the face of the threat you are fighting. Whether Moore is himself one of the bosses (or the Dead Gods encounter's focal point) is [Unconfirmed] — his role as the narrative antagonist is confirmed, his encounter structure is not.

## The Metal Soundtrack

One of Crimson Moon's standout confirmed details: **every boss has its own metal track**, with an original soundtrack featuring musicians from the metal scene. Confirmed contributors include:

- **HEALTH**
- **Jared Dines**
- **Misha Mansoor** (Periphery)
- **Ricky Armellino** (Ice Nine Kills)

The boss + metal pairing is a deliberate part of the game's identity — each boss fight is scored to feel like a live metal showpiece.

## What This Means for Boss Preparation

Because boss names, attacks, and weaknesses are [Unconfirmed] pre-release, a boss guide published now would be fabrication. Here is how to prepare with confirmed information:

1. **Build a versatile loadout** — the classless system ([Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide)) means you should have a reliable Weapon Art and a purified gear set ready before you push into a Ward's boss.
2. **Purify upgrades as you clear Wards** — the gear you bank ([Purification Guide](/guides/purification-guide)) is what will carry you through the boss fights.
3. **Practice the combat fundamentals** — blocking, dodging, and Weapon Arts are the tools every boss fight will test.
4. **Co-op the tough ones** — the shared life pool and revive give you a safety net for world bosses in co-op ([Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide)).
