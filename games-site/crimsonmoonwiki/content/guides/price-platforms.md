# Crimson Moon Release Date, Price & Editions

## When Does Crimson Moon Come Out?

**Crimson Moon releases on September 1, 2026.** The date was confirmed on August 4, 2026 alongside the release-date trailer, after the game was first revealed at PlayStation's State of Play on February 12, 2026 and made playable at Summer Game Fest Play Days in June.

## How Much Does Crimson Moon Cost?

There are two editions at launch:

| Edition | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **Standard Edition** | $19.99 | Base game |
| **Deluxe Edition** | $29.99 | Base game + upcoming content expansions + 2 cosmetic/weapon DLC packs |

The $19.99 base price is unusually low for a soulslike action RPG — ProbablyMonsters has positioned Crimson Moon as "this fall's best value for a hellish action RPG, solo or with a friend." The Deluxe Edition adds planned post-launch content expansions and two cosmetic/weapon DLC packs, which makes it the better value if you expect to keep playing after the main Wards.

## Is It Worth Buying?

- Repeatable run structure (15–45 minute Incursions)

- Only play games with fully verified reviews at launch (Crimson Moon has no reviews yet — it is unreleased)
