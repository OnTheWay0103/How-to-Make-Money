# Crimson Moon Release Date, Price & Editions

## When Does Crimson Moon Come Out?

**Crimson Moon released on September 1, 2026** and is available now on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. The date was confirmed on August 4, 2026 alongside the release-date trailer, after the game was first revealed at PlayStation's State of Play on February 12, 2026 and made playable at Summer Game Fest Play Days in June.

## How Much Does Crimson Moon Cost?

There are two editions at launch:

| Edition | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **Standard Edition** | $19.99 | Base game |
| **Deluxe Edition** | $29.99 | Base game + upcoming content expansions + 2 cosmetic/weapon DLC packs |

The $19.99 base price is unusually low for a soulslike action RPG — ProbablyMonsters has positioned Crimson Moon as "this fall's best value for a hellish action RPG, solo or with a friend." The Deluxe Edition adds planned post-launch content expansions and two cosmetic/weapon DLC packs, which makes it the better value if you expect to keep playing after the main Wards.

## Is It Worth Buying?

**Buy it if you like:**
- Soulslike melee combat (Dark Souls, Lies of P)
- Diablo-style loot and progression loops
- Co-op play with a shared life pool
- Repeatable run structure (15–45 minute Incursions)
- A $19.99 entry price with planned post-launch content

**Maybe wait, if you:**
- Prefer to buy only after you have read verified reviews (review embargoes lifted September 2, 2026 — scores are now appearing)
- Need **crossplay** confirmed before you commit (cross-platform play and local co-op are still [Unconfirmed]; online 2-player co-op is confirmed)
- Prefer a longer single-player campaign without repeated runs
