# Crimson Moon How Long to Beat — Playtime Estimate

## The Short Answer

**Official playtime estimates for Crimson Moon had not been published as of August 28, 2026 [Unconfirmed].** The game launches September 1, 2026, and "how long to beat" data will only be reliable once players finish it — typically a week or two after launch.

This page explains the **structure that determines playtime** so you can set expectations, and will be updated with real hour counts as soon as the community reports them.

## What Determines Crimson Moon's Length

Crimson Moon's length is set by two very different content tracks:

### 1. Main Progression: The Wards + Dead Gods

The campaign is the **three Wards** of Gildenarch, each ending in a **world boss**, with the final **Dead Gods** encounter after all three fall (see the [Three Wards Guide](/guides/wards-gildenarch-guide)).

This is the "main story" track. Its hour count depends on:

- **How many missions each Ward contains** — not yet documented [Unconfirmed]
- **How often you die** — it is a soulslike; deaths add real hours
- **How much gear you farm before bosses** — purification and upgrades gate progress

### 2. Repeatable Content: Incursions

**Incursions** are the 15–45 minute repeatable runs that form the core loop. They are not "extra" content you skip — they are how you earn the gear to progress. Because each run is dynamic, they add **effectively unlimited replay time**:

- **15–45 minutes per run** (confirmed)
- **Farming set pieces and materials** is the long tail
- **Build experimentation** (Boons + Weapon Arts) invites many more runs than the campaign needs

## Reasonable Expectations (Editorial)

Without official numbers, here is how to think about it before launch:

- **A focused main progression run** is likely in the range you'd expect from a $19.99 AA soulslike — the Wards are a finite campaign, not an endless open world.
- **Total time with Incursions** is much higher — the game is explicitly built around repeatable runs, so "how long to beat" and "how long you'll play" are very different numbers.
- **Co-op can change pace** — the shared life pool makes deaths less punishing (a partner revives you), which can shorten boss attempts, while co-op farming can extend total time.

Treat these as editorial context, not confirmed numbers [Unconfirmed].

## How to Decide Before You Buy

If game length is your deciding factor:

- **Price it fairly** — at $19.99, Crimson Moon is priced as a compact AA title, not a $70 epic. The Incursions loop is designed to give it staying power.
- **Expect the campaign + loop, not endless free-roam** — the game is mission-based, not open-world.
- **The Deluxe Edition** adds future content expansions — if you value long-term play, that's the edition that grows ([Price & Editions Guide](/guides/price-platforms)).
