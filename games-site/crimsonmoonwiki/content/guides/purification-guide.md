# Crimson Moon Purification System — Keeping Gear Permanently

## What Is Purification?

**Purification** is Crimson Moon's answer to the classic roguelike question: *what do I get to keep?*

Gear you find inside an **Incursion** is not automatically yours. To make it permanent, you **purify** it — at which point it becomes a permanent part of your arsenal in the hub's storage. Anything you do not purify is lost when the run ends.

This is the core of Crimson Moon's long-term progression: every run produces temporary loot, and you decide which pieces graduate to your permanent collection.

## How the Loop Works

1. **Run an Incursion** — fight through a dynamic map, looting weapons, armor, and materials along the way.
2. **Decide what to purify** — select the pieces worth keeping from the run's haul.
3. **Purify to keep** — purified gear is stored permanently.
4. **Use it forever** — purified gear is available for future runs and upgrades, forming the backbone of your builds.

The exact interface and purification cost (if any) are part of the run's reward flow and were not fully detailed before launch [Unconfirmed]. What is confirmed is the direction: **purified gear = permanent, everything else = temporary.**

## Why Purification Matters

Purification is what separates Crimson Moon from a pure roguelike. In most roguelikes, every run starts from scratch. In Crimson Moon:

- **Your account gets stronger over time** — the permanent arsenal grows with each good run.
- **Builds become possible** — a build identity (see the [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide)) needs a stable set of equipment, not just temporary Boons.
- **Risk has a payoff** — a successful run lets you bank the best pieces, so failure never fully resets your progress.

## What Should You Purify?

The golden rule: **purify for permanence, not for panic.**

### Purify if:
- It fits the build identity you are committing to (weapon type, armor role, set bonus).
- It is a direct upgrade to something you already use — even small upgrades compound.
- It completes or improves a **set bonus** — sets are covered in the [Equipment Sets Guide](/guides/equipment-sets-guide).

### Skip (leave behind) if:
- It is only slightly better than what you have, but the wrong type for your build.
- It duplicates something you already own with no meaningful difference.
- It does not fit any build you plan to play.

Storage is a long-term asset. Filling it with "well, it's a tiny upgrade" pieces makes it harder to find the pieces that actually matter when a great drop comes along.

## Purification and the Incursion Economy

Because Boons are temporary and gear is permanent, purification is how a run "pays" your account long-term. The [Boons Guide](/guides/boons-guide) covers the other half of the economy — the temporary power you spend during a run. The two decisions together define how much you get out of any single Incursion:

- **Boon decision:** make this run easier / more profitable now.
- **Purification decision:** pull the best gear out for every run after this one.

## Purification and Builds

Purified gear is the permanent foundation of a build. The [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide) describes the three layers:

1. **Equipment** (permanent, via purification) — the backbone
2. **Boons** (run-scoped) — the temporary power
3. **Weapon Arts** (on your weapon) — the combat identity

A strong build starts with **intentional purification** — keeping the equipment that supports the identity, and skipping the shiny-but-wrong pieces.
