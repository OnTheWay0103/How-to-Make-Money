## What is Crimson Moon?

Crimson Moon is a soulslike action RPG developed and published by **ProbablyMonsters** — the studio founded by Harold Ryan, former CEO of Bungie. Set in the fallen gothic city of **Gildenarch**, you play as a **Nephilim** (half-human, half-angel) pushing back a corrupting entity called **Hellgrowth** while hunting the vampire lord **Solomon Moore**.

The game fuses Dark Souls-style melee combat with a Diablo-like loot and progression layer and roguelike run structure. It was first revealed at PlayStation's State of Play in February 2026, playable at Summer Game Fest in June, and confirmed for a September 1 release on August 4, 2026.

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **No Classes** — your build = equipment + Boons + Weapon Arts, freely combined
- **3 Wards + Dead Gods** — each Ward has a world boss; defeat all three to unlock the final encounter
- **Incursions** — repeatable 15–45 minute runs with dynamic maps and enemy configurations
- **Purification** — gear found in runs can become a permanent part of your arsenal
- **2-Player Co-op** — shared life pool, revive, dynamic difficulty; solo fully supported
- **Sanctus Clypeus Hub** — upgrades, forging, manuscript translation, War Table quests, NPCs including a vampire blacksmith
- **Metal Soundtrack** — original music from bands and artists in the metal scene
