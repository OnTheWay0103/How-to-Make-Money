You publish a ranking. A week later, the community's ranking is different, and someone in a Discord says yours is wrong.

There are two bad responses: quietly change it to match, or double down and call the community wrong. Both destroy the reason anyone reads a ranking page in the first place.

## A ranking is an argument, not a verdict

If the page says "A is S tier", the reader cannot evaluate that. If it says "A is S tier because it has no bad matchup, and it stays that way even with the weakest support", the reader can — and can disagree for a reason.

That is the difference between a ranking and a list of opinions with letters attached. Only the first survives being wrong, because when the conclusion changes, the reasoning tells you what changed.

## Where disagreement usually comes from

Almost always one of three things, none of which are about skill:

1. **Different content.** Your ranking is for the base game; theirs includes a mode you have not covered.
2. **Different criteria.** You are ranking consistency; they are ranking peak performance.
3. **Different patch.** Yours is a week old; theirs is current.

Naming the criteria explicitly resolves most disagreements before they start. "Ranked for solo play, current patch, assuming average gear" is a sentence that saves an argument.

## Updating without losing credibility

- **Change the ranking when the reasoning changes**, and say what changed. Silent edits to a tier list are the fastest way to lose repeat readers.
- **Keep a short changelog** on the page. Two lines per update is enough.
- **Do not chase consensus.** If you move an entry because people complained, the criteria were never real.

## The unwritten rule

Rankings get traffic because people want a shortcut. The honest version of that service is not "here is the answer" — it is "here is the answer, here is what it assumes, and here is how to tell whether your situation is the assumed one".

The [best builds in Phantom Tower](https://phantomtowerwiki.vercel.app/guides/best-builds) page on [Phantom Tower Wiki](https://phantomtowerwiki.vercel.app) is written with the criteria stated at the top for exactly this reason. It does not make the ranking correct forever. It makes it falsifiable — which is the best a ranking page can be.
