## Your First Week Roadmap

Mistfall Hunter does not hold your hand. This roadmap tells you exactly what to do from your first login through your first week of endgame content.

### Overview

| Day | Focus | Key Milestone |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| Day 1 | Learn the basics | First successful extraction |
| Day 2 | Build your camp | Camp Level 3 |
| Day 3 | Master extraction | 10 successful extracts |
| Day 4 | Try PvP | First player kill |
| Day 5 | Join a guild | Guild Level 2+ |
| Day 6 | Weekly challenges | 50% completion |
| Day 7 | Endgame prep | Ready for competitive play |

---

## Day 1: First Extraction

### Before You Drop

1. **Complete the tutorial** — it rewards your first gear set
2. **Pick Mercenary** as your starting class — most forgiving for beginners
3. **Equip the default gear** — do not buy anything yet
4. **Learn the controls** — practice blocking, dodging, and using items

### First Raid Strategy

| Step | Action | Goal |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Drop into Ruined City (easiest map) | Familiarize with the zone |
| 2 | Stay in the Outer Ring | Avoid players |
| 3 | Loot 2-3 chests | Get some gear |
| 4 | Find an extraction point | Learn extraction locations |
| 5 | Extract | First successful extraction! |

**Goal for Day 1**: Complete 3 extractions (even if 1-2 fail). Learn the basic loop.

### Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Better Approach |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Rushing to the Woodling | You will die to experienced players | Farm edges, build confidence |
| Fighting every enemy | Wastes resources and attracts attention | Avoid unnecessary fights |
| Ignoring the mist timer | Death by mist | Always watch the timer |
| Bringing good gear | Lose it on first death | Use starter gear for first 5 raids |

## Day 2: Camp Upgrades

Your camp is your home base. Upgrading it unlocks permanent advantages.

### Upgrade Priority

| Upgrade | Cost | Benefit | Recommended Level |
|---------|------|---------|-------------------|
| Warehouse (Level 1) | 500 GB | +30 stash slots | Start here |
| Merchant Stand (Level 1) | 300 GB | Buy/sell basic items | Priority 1 |
| Healing Station (Level 1) | 400 GB | Craft healing items | Priority 2 |
| Workbench (Level 1) | 600 GB | Repair and upgrade gear | Priority 3 |
| Camp Defenses (Level 1) | 200 GB | Prevents small-scale raids | Nice to have |

### Day 2 Checklist

- [ ] Upgrade Warehouse to hold more loot
- [ ] Build Merchant Stand to sell junk items
- [ ] Craft 5 healing items at Healing Station
- [ ] Complete 5 extractions
- [ ] Try a different class (unlock at Camp Level 2)

## Day 3: Extraction Mastery

### Focus Areas

1. **Learn one map** — Pick Ruined City and learn every extraction point, chest location, and patrol route
2. **Practice the Woodling** — Kill it at least once
3. **Inventory management** — Learn what to keep and what to sell

### Loot Priority Table

| Item | Keep? | Sell? | Use? |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| Gyldenblood | Always | — | — |
| Green weapons/armor | No | Yes | Use only if you have nothing better |
| Blue weapons/armor | Keep spares | No | Your primary gear target |
| Healing items | Keep (stack of 5) | Yes (excess) | Always carry 2-3 |
| Crafting materials | Keep | No | Needed for upgrades |
| Quest items | Keep | No | Complete quests first |

### Extraction Goals

- [ ] 10 successful extractions total
- [ ] 3 consecutive extractions without dying
- [ ] Kill the Returner Woodling at least once
- [ ] Extract with a full inventory of blue+ items

## Day 4: PvP Introduction

### When to Start PvP

Do not start PvP until you:
- Have 10+ successful extractions
- Know the Ruined City map layout well
- Have a full set of blue gear
- Can consistently kill the Woodling

### Your First PvP Engagement

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| You see a player at range | Assess. Are they alone? Are they fighting? |
| They are fighting enemies | Third party opportunity — wait, then engage |
| They are looting a chest | Ambush while they are distracted |
| They see you first | Either fight or retreat — hesitation is death |
| You get the drop on them | Open with your highest burst damage |

### Recommended PvP Class

Start with **Mercenary** (Sword & Shield):
- Block 70% of incoming damage
- Forgiving when you make mistakes
- Strong in both PvE and PvP

### Day 4 Checklist

- [ ] Kill 1 player in PvP
- [ ] Extract after killing a player
- [ ] Try the Bounty Board once
- [ ] Die at least 3 times in PvP (learning experience)

## Day 5: Joining a Guild

### Why Join Early

Guilds accelerate your progression significantly:

| Benefit | Solo Player | Guild Member |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Stash space | 50 slots | 50 + guild shared |
| Bounty access | Public only | Guild-exclusive contracts |
| Group play | LFG channel | Guildmates |
| XP bonus | None | +10% from guild perks |
| Gear sharing | None | Guild stash donations |

### Finding a Guild

Look for:
- **Active members** (check last-online times)
- **Level 2+** (perks unlocked)
- **Beginner-friendly** (some guilds require minimum level)
- **Your timezone** (active when you play)

## Day 7: Endgame Preparation

### Level 25+ Checklist

By the end of your first week, aim for:

- [ ] Player Level 25+
- [ ] Camp Level 5+
- [ ] Full Rare (blue) gear set
- [ ] At least 1 Epic (purple) weapon
- [ ] Guild membership in an active guild
- [ ] 50+ total extractions
- [ ] 10+ player kills
- [ ] Weekly challenges at 50%+ completion

### What Comes Next

| Activity | Unlock Condition | What It Offers |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Guild Wars | Guild Level 5 | Competitive PvP with territory control |
| Legendary Weapons | Level 30 + rare materials | Best-in-slot gear |
| Hard Mode Maps | 80%+ extraction rate | Epic+ loot, unique bosses |
| Season Pass | Account Level 10 | Exclusive cosmetic progression |

## Common New Player Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Selling all materials | Need them for camp upgrades | Keep everything until camp is Level 3 |
| Hoarding gear | Stash fills up, risk losing in raids | Sell or use green gear freely |
| Playing one class only | Miss out on versatility | Try each class once |
| Ignoring the camp | Slower progression | Upgrade camp between raids |
| Rushing endgame content | Die repeatedly, lose gear | Build fundamentals first |
