## Endgame Overview

Hitting level 50 in Mistfall Hunter is just the beginning. The real game starts with gear optimization, high-tier extractions, and competitive rankings.

## Endgame Progression Path

```
Hit Level 50
  ↓
Gear Score 400+ (basic legendary set)
  ↓
Tier 4+ Extractions (farm rare materials)
  ↓
Gear Score 500+ (optimized legendaries)
  ↓
Tier 5-6 Raids (endgame bosses)
  ↓
Competitive PvP / Leaderboard Rankings
```

## Gear Score Optimization

After level 50, your power comes from gear score:

| Gear Score | Content Unlocked |
|:--:|------|
| 350-400 | T3 extractions, basic raids |
| 400-450 | T4 extractions, heroic dungeons |
| 450-500 | T5 extractions, mythic raids |
| 500+ | T6 endgame, competitive PvP |

### How to Increase Gear Score
1. **Enhance** gear at the Anvil (+1 to +10)
2. **Enchant** with boss-drop enchant stones
3. **Awaken** legendary items (max enhancement)
4. **Set bonuses** — wearing 4+ pieces of the same set

## Endgame Content

### Tier 5-6 Extractions
- Highest risk, highest reward
- Unique legendary materials
- Solo or coordinated squads only

### Mythic Raids
- 8-player content
- Weekly lockout
- Best-in-slot gear drops

### Competitive PvP Arena
- Seasonal rankings
- Top 100 get exclusive titles and mounts
- MMR-based matchmaking

### Leaderboard Challenges
- Fastest extraction times
- Highest-value single extraction
- Most boss kills (weekly)

## Daily/Weekly Checklist

| Frequency | Tasks |
|-----------|-------|
| Daily | 3× extraction runs, daily quests, camp maintenance |
| Weekly | Mythic raid clear, PvP arena placement matches |
| Seasonal | Rank push, event participation |
