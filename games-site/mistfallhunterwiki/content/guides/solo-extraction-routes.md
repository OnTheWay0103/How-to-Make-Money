## Route Planning Is the Solo Skill

In Mistfall Hunter, squads can improvise — a trio that misroutes can still muscle through a fight. A solo player cannot. Your **route** is your entire plan: where you loot, when you engage, and which extraction point you commit to. This guide gives you route archetypes that work on **Weavereach** (the first map, and currently the only map solo players can queue into since the July 30, 2026 release) and the decision rules for when to swap between them mid-run.

## The Four Route Archetypes

| Route | Goal | Time | Risk | Typical Haul |
|-------|------|------|------|--------------|
| Speed Loot | Extract fast with low risk | 5-7 min | Very Low | 300-600 Gyldenblood |
| Standard Farm | Clear one zone, boss optional | 10-15 min | Low | 800-1,200 Gyldenblood |
| Boss Run | Kill the Returner Woodling, extract | 15-20 min | Medium | 1,500-2,500 Gyldenblood |
| Stealth Path | Ghost the map, cherry-pick vaults | 10-12 min | Low-Medium | Variable — can spike high |

Pick your archetype **before you drop** — mixing goals mid-run is how solo players die with full bags.

## Route 1: Speed Loot (The Bankroll Builder)

The route that funds everything else. You are not playing Mistfall Hunter this run; you are playing a 7-minute money printer.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Spawn and read your position relative to the nearest 3 chest zones |
| 2 | Hit the 2-3 nearest low-risk chests (avoid boss-adjacent areas) |
| 3 | Loot only Common-Uncommon gear and Gyldenblood piles |
| 4 | Extract at the nearest standard exit (30s channel) |

**Key rule:** never fight for loot on a speed run. If a Corroded elite or another player contests a chest, let it go — a 300 Gyldenblood run repeated three times beats a 1,000 run that dies at extraction.

## Route 2: Standard Farm (The Mid-Risk Balance)

The default solo route once you know the map's zone values:

1. Choose **one** high-value zone (e.g., the Blacksmith-equivalent loot zone, or the north crypt pattern) and clear it methodically.
2. Kill Corroded packs on the way, but **skip the elite** unless it is an easy isolated spawn.
3. Check the boss room **only if the zone is quiet** — a glance, not a commitment.
4. Extract through the **least-traveled exit**, even if it is a longer channel.

**Exit selection rule:** on maps where exits A and B sit at map edges, the one farther from the boss room has the highest solo survival rate. Third parties camp the exits near high-value zones, not the quiet ones.

## Route 3: Boss Run (The High-Reward Gamble)

The Returner Woodling is your extraction objective because it drops the **Soul of Return** — and it is also the biggest third-party magnet on the map.

**Solo boss-run rules:**

- **Scout first.** Approach the Woodling's zone perimeter and listen for 10-15 seconds. If you hear fighting, you are the third party — reposition and take the boss when the winner is weak, or leave.
- **Kill fast or leave.** The Woodling's AoE root and summon phases give it time to attract attention. Commit to a burst-kill window; if you do not secure it in that window, the odds shift against you.
- **Extract immediately after.** The Soul of Return marks you. Do not linger to loot the room — take the Soul and the cache and move.

## Route 4: Stealth Path (The Shadow Route)

For Shadowstrix and Blackarrow players who want value without a fight:

| Priority | Target | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Locked chests (if you have a key) | Rare+ gear with low contest risk |
| 2 | Hidden caches | High-tier items, rarely camped |
| 3 | Supply drops (if uncontested) | Mixed material value |
| 4 | Boss room check, last | Only if completely silent |

The stealth route fails when you **force** it — if the quiet path is occupied, reroute to the standard farm rather than sneaking through a contested corridor.

## The Bail Decision (When to Change Routes)

Your route should have a pre-planned trigger. The moment any of these fire, switch to "extract now":

| Trigger | Response |
|---------|----------|
| You take 50%+ HP damage from a player | Do not trade — move to extraction |
| A third-party fight breaks out near your exit | Take the longer exit instead |
| You see 2+ players moving toward your zone | Abandon the zone immediately |
| Mist is closing and you are far from an exit | The safest path, even if longer |
| You land a rare/God-tier item | Job done — extract now |

**Solo rule #1:** an extracted green item is worth more than a purple item in your death bag. No haul justifies an exit you cannot reach.

## Solo Route Checklist

- [ ] Archetype chosen before the drop
- [ ] Two exit options identified (primary + fallback)
- [ ] Bail triggers agreed with yourself in advance
- [ ] Loadout matches the route (stealth for ghost runs, burst for boss runs)
