## Weekly Challenges Overview

Weekly Challenges in Mistfall Hunter are a set of **repeatable objectives** that reset every Monday. Each challenge rewards Gyldenblood, gear, and exclusive items. Completing all challenges in a week grants a **bonus reward**.

### Why Do Weekly Challenges?

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Guaranteed Gyldenblood | 12,000+ GB per week from challenges alone |
| Exclusive Gear | Some rewards are not available anywhere else |
| Guild XP | Completing challenges contributes guild XP if you are in a guild |
| Season Pass Progress | Challenges count toward Season Pass milestones |
| Practice | Forces you to try different playstyles and classes |

---

## Challenge Rotation Schedule

### Weekly Themes

Each week has a theme that affects available challenges:

| Week Theme | Bonus Focus | Active Dates |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| Extraction Week | Bonus rewards for extraction challenges | Week 1 of month |
| PvP Week | Bonus rewards for bounty/PvP challenges | Week 2 of month |
| Exploration Week | Bonus rewards for map exploration | Week 3 of month |
| Boss Hunt Week | Bonus rewards for boss kills | Week 4 of month |
| All Out Week | All challenge types active at once | Last week of season |

### Daily Challenge Reset

Within each week, challenges rotate daily:

| Day | Challenge Focus |
|-----|----------------|
| Monday (reset) | All challenges refreshed |
| Tuesday | Extraction + survival focus |
| Wednesday | PvP + bounty focus |
| Thursday | Exploration + gathering focus |
| Friday | Boss hunting focus |
| Saturday | Guild-focused challenges |
| Sunday | Catch-up — all challenge types active |

## Challenge Categories

### Extraction Challenges

| Challenge | Objective | Reward |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Clean Extract | Extract without killing any players | 500 GB |
| Full Inventory | Extract with a completely full inventory | 750 GB |
| Mist Walker | Extract within 60 seconds of mist closure | 1,000 GB |
| No Damage Extract | Extract without taking damage | 1,500 GB |
| Triple Extract | Successfully extract 3 times in a row | 2,000 GB + Rare item |

**Strategy**: Play cautiously. Prioritize survival over combat. Run with a Mercenary class for survivability.

### PvP Challenges

| Challenge | Objective | Reward |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| First Blood | Kill a player within 2 minutes of dropping in | 500 GB |
| Bounty Collector | Claim 3 bounty rewards in a week | 1,000 GB |
| Payback | Kill the player who killed you last raid | 750 GB |
| Dominator | Kill 5 players in a single raid | 2,500 GB + Epic gear |
| War Contribution | Earn 500 war points for your guild | 3,000 GB + Guild XP |

**Strategy**: Play aggressive classes (Shadowstrix, Slayer). Drop into high-traffic zones. Hunt near the Woodling.

### Exploration Challenges

| Challenge | Objective | Reward |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Cartographer | Visit 3 different map regions in one raid | 500 GB |
| Treasure Hunter | Open 10 chests in a single raid | 750 GB |
| Deep Dive | Reach the Core zone of any map | 500 GB |
| Full Clear | Loot every zone on a single map in one raid | 1,500 GB |
| Secret Seeker | Find and interact with 2 hidden map locations | 2,000 GB + Cosmetic |

**Strategy**: Learn the maps thoroughly. Use a high-mobility class. Avoid combat — just move quickly and loot.

### Boss Challenges

| Challenge | Objective | Reward |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Woodling Slayer | Kill the Returner Woodling | 500 GB |
| Speed Kill | Kill the Woodling within 30 seconds of engaging | 1,500 GB |
| Solo Boss | Kill the Woodling without a squad | 1,000 GB |
| Boss Collector | Kill 5 different boss-type enemies in a week | 2,000 GB + Rare gear |
| No-Hit Boss | Kill the Woodling without taking damage | 3,000 GB + Epic item |

**Strategy**: Learn Woodling attack patterns. Use high-burst damage builds. Bring a friend for Speed Kill.

## Optimal Strategy Guide

### Time-Efficient Order

Complete challenges in this order for maximum efficiency:

| Priority | Challenge Type | Why First |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| 1 | Extraction — Quick ones (Clean Extract, No Damage) | Fast, low risk, builds momentum |
| 2 | Exploration — Cartographer, Treasure Hunter | Passive — complete while doing others |
| 3 | Boss — Woodling Slayer | You will fight the Woodling anyway |
| 4 | PvP — Bounty Collector | Requires setup, save for when you are warmed up |
| 5 | Guild — War Contribution | Only if in a guild and war is active |
| 6 | Hard challenges — Dominator, No-Hit Boss | Save for last, requires best gear |

### Class Recommendations per Challenge

| Challenge | Best Class | Why |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Clean Extract | Mercenary | Block + survive, avoid fights |
| No Damage Extract | Shadowstrix | Invisibility to avoid all combat |
| First Blood | Slayer | High burst damage, rush down |
| Dominator | Sorcerer | AoE damage for multi-kills |
| Speed Kill (Woodling) | Slayer or Shadowstrix | Highest DPS builds |
| Cartographer | Hunter | Movement speed bonuses |
| Treasure Hunter | Hunter | Passive loot detection |

### Loadout Strategy

| If You Are... | Use This Loadout |
|--------------|------------------|
| Chasing extraction challenges | Light armor, healing items, smoke bombs |
| Chasing PvP challenges | Medium armor, burst weapon, offensive utilities |
| Chasing exploration challenges | Light armor, movement items, lockpicks for chests |
| Chasing boss challenges | Heavy armor, boss-killer weapon, healing items |
