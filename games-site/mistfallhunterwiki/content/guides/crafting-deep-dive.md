## Advanced Crafting Overview

Once you've mastered basic crafting, it's time to optimize for legendaries and efficiency. This guide covers everything beyond the beginner level.

## Legendary Crafting Recipes

Legendary items require rare materials from boss drops and specific conditions:

| Item | Materials | Boss Source | Success Rate |
|------|-----------|-------------|:--:|
| **Voidsteel Blade** | Void Essence ×3, Mythril Ingot ×10 | Shadowstrix | 60% |
| **Phoenix Plate** | Phoenix Feather ×2, Obsidian ×15 | Final boss | 50% |
| **Stormcaller Staff** | Storm Core ×5, Crystal ×20 | Tempest boss | 55% |
| **Dragonbone Bow** | Dragon Fang ×1, Ancient Wood ×10 | World boss | 45% |

### Improving Craft Success Rate
- Upgrade your **Workbench to Tier 5** (+15% success)
- Equip **Crafter's Charm** accessory (+10%)
- Craft during **full moon** in-game event (+5%)
- Use **Blessed Oil** consumable (+10% for next craft)

## Crafting Efficiency Tips

1. **Batch craft during events** — Crafting XP events give +25% mastery XP
2. **Use alts for storage** — Store overflow materials on alt characters
3. **Track boss spawn timers** — Join community Discord for spawn alerts
4. **Sell T3-T4 crafts** — Mid-tier crafted items sell well on the market
5. **Never craft below 50% success** — Always use boosters for legendaries
