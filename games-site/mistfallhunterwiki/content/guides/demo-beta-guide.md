## Playtest History

Mistfall Hunter went through several test phases before its full release on **July 30, 2026** (Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, day-one Game Pass). This page records what those phases were like for players who are curious about the game's history — none of the beta builds are available anymore.

## How Access Worked (Pre-Launch)

### Steam (PC) — Playtest

Before launch, the playtest was available through Steam via the store page's **Request Access** flow. Approval times varied from same-day to 1-2 weeks depending on the wave. Once approved, the game appeared in the Steam Library as "Mistfall Hunter Playtest."

### Steam Next Fest Demos (Limited Time)

During Steam Next Fest events, a **timed demo** was offered that anyone could download without requesting access. These demos:
- Were available for approximately 7 days during the event
- Did not require approval — install and play immediately
- Contained a subset of the playtest content
- Were separate from the playtest — progress did not carry between them

### Console Beta Status

Console versions were announced for the full release; there was no public demo or beta for PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S before launch.

## What the Playtest Contained

The playtest was a partial build. Details below are from community reports of the era and are no longer verifiable in-game:

| Feature | Playtest Status | Details |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| **Classes** | Partial roster | Early classes included Mercenary, Sorcerer, Blackarrow, Shadowstrix, and Withered Knight; the final roster is six classes including Seer |
| **Maps** | 2 early maps | Early map names and layouts differed from the final five-map set |
| **PvE Enemies** | Corroded variants | Plus the Returner Woodling mini-boss |
| **PvP** | Yes | Player-vs-player combat in extraction zones |
| **Extraction** | Yes | Full extraction loop with Soul of Return mechanics |
| **Camp Upgrades** | Partial | Only early tiers of camp upgrades |
| **Crafting** | Basic | Weapon repairs and consumable crafting only |
| **Cross-Play** | PC only | Console crossplay arrived with the full release |
| **Progression** | Capped | Progress caps were lower than the final game |

### What Was NOT in the Playtest

- The full five-map set
- The final boss encounter
- High-tier legendary gear
- The full camp upgrade tree
- Endgame systems (leaderboards, ranked PvP, weekly challenges)

## Progress Carry-Over and Wipes

### Did Playtest Progress Carry Over to Full Release?

**No.** All playtest and beta progress was wiped before the full release. This included:
- Character levels and skill points
- Camp upgrades
- Stash items and gear
- Gyldenblood currency
- Unlocked cosmetics earned during playtest

### Why Wipes Happen

1. **Balance changes** — skills, gear stats, and upgrade costs change between builds
2. **Economy reset** — the extraction economy needs a fresh start for launch
3. **Data cleanup** — test accounts, bugged items, and exploit-generated wealth are cleared

### What May Have Carried Over

Participation rewards (such as playtest-exclusive cosmetics) were mentioned by the developers and may be granted to eligible accounts. Gear, currency, and camp progress did not carry over.

The safest approach during any test phase: treat it as **practice and learning**. Experiment with classes and builds you would not normally try, because nothing is permanent.

## Key Takeaway

The demo and playtest phases are over — Mistfall Hunter is fully released as of July 30, 2026. If you played a beta build, your progress was wiped; if you are new, you are not missing anything except early familiarity. For everything that changed between the playtest and launch, see the [Patch Notes](/guides/patch-notes) page.
