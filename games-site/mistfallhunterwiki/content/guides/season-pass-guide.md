## Season Pass System Overview

Mistfall Hunter's Season Pass is a tier-based reward system that runs alongside each seasonal content cycle. Players earn Pass XP by completing in-game activities, which progresses them through 50 free tiers and 50 premium tiers.

### Pass Types

| Pass Type | Price | Tiers | Value |
|-----------|-------|-------|-------|
| **Free Pass** | Free | 1-50 (Free track only) | Good — essential cosmetics and currency |
| **Premium Pass** | 1,200 Gyldenblood (or $9.99 USD) | 1-50 (Free + Premium track) | Excellent — 2x rewards, exclusive items |
| **Premium+ Pass** | 2,400 Gyldenblood (or $19.99 USD) | 1-50 (Free + Premium) + 10 tier skips + exclusive emote | Good — only if you want the skips |

**Note**: The Premium Pass pays for itself. Completing all 50 premium tiers returns 1,500 Gyldenblood, a net profit of 300 Gyldenblood over the purchase price.

## Free Pass Rewards (Tiers 1-50)

The Free Pass contains a mix of cosmetics, currency, and consumables. These are available to all players regardless of pass ownership.

| Tiers | Reward Type | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1-10 | Consumables | Healing Potions (x5), Stamina Potions (x3), Repair Kit (x1) |
| 11-20 | Currency + Cosmetics | 200 Gyldenblood, Free Player Title, Weapon Skin (Common) |
| 21-30 | Cosmetics + Currency | 300 Gyldenblood, Armor Skin (Uncommon), Emote: "Victory Salute" |
| 31-40 | Currency + Consumables | 400 Gyldenblood, Legendary Key Fragment (x3), XP Boost (x2) |
| 41-50 | Premium Cosmetics | 500 Gyldenblood, "Free Runner" Title, Legendary Cape Skin |

**Total Free Pass Value**: ~1,400 Gyldenblood equivalent + 3 cosmetic items + consumables worth ~300 Gyldenblood

## Premium Pass Rewards (Tiers 1-50)

The Premium Pass doubles your rewards and adds exclusive items that will not be available again.

| Tiers | Reward Type | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1-10 | Exclusive Cosmetics | "Season Veteran" Title, Weapon Skin (Rare), Armor Skin (Rare) |
| 11-20 | Currency + Cosmetics | 300 Gyldenblood, Emote: "Season's Greetings", Mount Skin (Uncommon) |
| 21-30 | Premium Items | 400 Gyldenblood, Legendary Key (x1), Camp Decoration: "Season Banner" |
| 31-40 | Exclusive Cosmetics | 500 Gyldenblood, Weapon Skin (Epic), Armor Skin (Epic) |
| 41-50 | Premium Rewards | 300 Gyldenblood, "Season Pass Veteran" Title, **Exclusive Legendary Weapon Skin**, Premium Avatar Frame |

**Notable Premium-Only Rewards:**
- **Tier 50**: Exclusive Legendary Weapon Skin (class-specific, account-bound, never returns)
- **Tier 45**: Premium Avatar Frame (animated, shows your season completion)
- **Tier 35**: "Season Completer" Title
- **Tier 25**: Mount Skin (unique to premium pass)

**Total Premium Pass Value**: ~2,800 Gyldenblood + 8 exclusive cosmetics + 1 Legendary weapon skin

## Season Pass Efficiency Guide

### Fastest Way to Level the Pass

| Method | XP/Hour | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Daily Contracts** | ~2,000 XP/15 min | Do these every day — 30 min = 4,000 XP |
| **Weekly Contracts** | ~5,000 XP/60 min | Stack weeklies and complete in one session |
| **Rush extractions** | ~1,500-2,000 XP/hour | Fast 10-min runs with guaranteed extraction |
| **Boss farming** | ~1,200-1,500 XP/hour | Boss kills + chests + extraction |
| **PvP-only runs** | ~800-1,200 XP/hour | High risk, variable returns |

### Optimal Weekly Routine

| Day | Activity | XP Earned |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| Monday | Daily Contract (1st) + 2-3 extractions | ~2,500 |
| Tuesday | Daily Contract + 2-3 extractions | ~2,500 |
| Wednesday | Daily Contract + focus on discovery challenges | ~2,500 |
| Thursday | Daily Contract + boss rush | ~2,500 |
| Friday | Daily Contract + Weekly Contract | ~5,000 |
| Saturday | Catch up — any missed dailies + Weekly Contract #2 | ~5,000 |
| Sunday | Stack remaining Weekly Contracts + boss farming | ~5,000 |

**Weekly total**: ~25,000 XP = ~5-6 tiers per week

### Season Timeline to Complete

| Play Style | Tiers Per Week | Weeks to Complete | Hours Per Week |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Casual (Daily only) | 3-4 tiers | 12-16 weeks | 3-5 hours |
| Regular (Daily + Weekly) | 5-6 tiers | 8-10 weeks | 8-12 hours |
| Hardcore (All activities) | 8-10 tiers | 5-7 weeks | 15-20 hours |

Since seasons last ~12 weeks, casual players can still complete the pass by doing dailies consistently.

## Premium Pass Value Analysis

### Should You Buy the Premium Pass?

| Factor | Verdict |
|--------|---------|
| **Cost recovery** | Yes — premium pass returns 1,500 Gyldenblood against 1,200 cost |
| **Cosmetic exclusivity** | Tier 50 Legendary weapon skin is unavailable elsewhere |
| **Time investment** | You must reach tier 50 to get full value |
| **Free player value** | Free pass is generous — you miss cosmetics but not gameplay content |

### When NOT to Buy

- You play less than 2 hours per week — you may not reach tier 50 before the season ends
- You do not care about cosmetics — gameplay content is not locked behind the premium pass
- The season is ending within 3 weeks — you may not have time to complete it

### Purchase Recommendation

> **Buy the Premium Pass if you play 5+ hours per week and want exclusive cosmetics. The 1,500 Gyldenblood return makes it free in net cost.**

## Season Pass FAQ

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does Pass XP carry over to next season? | No — all progress resets each season |
| Can I buy tiers with Gyldenblood? | Yes — 150 Gyldenblood per tier skip |
| Is the pass account-wide or per character? | Account-wide — all characters share pass progress |
| Can I complete old pass rewards? | No — previous season rewards are exclusive to that season |
| Do premium rewards ever return? | Some premium cosmetics return in loot boxes after 2 seasons, but Tier 50 weapon skins do not |
