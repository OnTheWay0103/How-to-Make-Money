## Overview

Mistfall Hunter's PvP is unique — it's a **PvPvE extraction** experience where you're never safe. You must balance fighting other players, managing the Mist, clearing monsters, and extracting with your loot. This advanced guide covers the combat fundamentals, map strategies, and loadout optimization that separate top-tier players from the rest.

---

## Combat Fundamentals

### Engagement Decision Matrix

| Scenario | Your Gear | Enemy Visual | Recommendation |
|----------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| You have high-value loot | Any | Unknown | Avoid — extract immediately |
| Near full HP, good gear | Purple+ | Solo | Engage with burst opener |
| Low HP, decent loot | Green/Blue | Group of 2+ | Disengage, use smoke |
| Boss spawn nearby | Any | Any | Third-party after they engage boss |
| Extraction zone camped | Any | 3+ players | Find alternate extract or wait |

### The Engagement Triangle

```
        BURST
       /      \
      /        \
 CONTROL ------ SUSTAIN
```

- **Burst:** High front-loaded damage. Wins 1v1s quickly but vulnerable to kiting.
- **Control:** Debuffs, stuns, slows. Wins through attrition and enemy errors.
- **Sustain:** Healing, shields, DOTs. Wins long fights but weak to coordinated focus fire.

Understanding which triangle corner your loadout occupies — and which the enemy's does — determines your win condition.

## Map: Factory District

A close-quarters industrial map with three floors and tight corridors.

**Hot Zones:**

| Zone | Danger Level | Contest Reason |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Main Assembly | Extreme | Central loot spawn + boss arena |
| Catwalk | High | High ground control point |
| Warehouse Bunker | Medium | Guaranteed blue+ weapon spawn |
| Extraction Point A | Low–High | Camped by ~20% of squads |

**Factory District Strategy:**
1. Land at Warehouse Bunker for guaranteed blue weapon
2. Third-party the Assembly fight — never engage first
3. Take Catwalk control for rotation to any extraction point
4. Extract at Point C (least camped, but longer rotation)

## Map: Mistfall Ridge

Open terrain with elevation changes and long sightlines.

**Elevation Control** is everything. The team holding the highest ground wins 75% of engagements, per Mistfall Hunter's statistical analysis.

| Position | Advantage | Counter |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Peak (highest point) | 360-degree view, cover | Cannot be easily dislodged without snipers |
| Mid-slope | Good sightlines, multiple escapes | Vulnerable from both above and below |
| Valley floor | Cover-rich, concealment | No high-ground advantage |

**Mistfall Ridge Strategy:**
1. Rush Peak at round start — this is the only contested position
2. One player watches the flank while two watch the main approach
3. Use the Mist timings to rotate: when Mist shrinks, move with the wall
4. Sniper + SMG is the meta loadout for this map

## Map: Undercity Tunnels

Underground labyrinth with vertical shafts and zero visibility zones.

**Light Management:**
- Flashlights reveal enemy position to you — but also broadcast yours
- Night vision goggles consume battery but keep you hidden
- Firing an unsilenced weapon reveals you on the minimap for 3 seconds

**Undercity Tunnels Strategy:**
1. Shotgun + Pistol is optimal — most fights happen within 10 meters
2. Memorize the three main shaft locations (they connect all zones)
3. Never fight in the dark zone without night vision
4. Extraction points activate on a 90-second cycle — plan your route

## Advanced Combat Techniques

### Peeker's Advantage

In Mistfall Hunter, the peeker sees the target ~150ms before the target sees them thanks to netcode. Always initiate line-of-sight instead of holding an angle.

### Reload Canceling

Reload cancel by sprinting for 0.2 seconds mid-animation. This shaves 0.5 seconds off each reload. Essential for aggressive play.

```text
Shoot → Reload start → Sprint 0.2s → Cancel sprint → Shoot
```

### Third-Party Timing

The ideal moment to engage a fight is **8–12 seconds** after the first gunshot. At this point:
- Shields are depleted
- Healing cooldowns are spent
- Both teams have committed to the engagement

### Mist Walking

When the Mist closes in, take the damage and use a Medkit rather than running the long way. The direct path through the Mist is often 30 seconds faster than the safe route.

## Extraction Priority

When you secure high-value loot, stop fighting. The goal is extraction, not kill count.

| Loot Value | Extract Priority | Strategy |
|------------|-----------------|----------|
| Legendary item | Absolute | Use Mist Ward Charm, avoid all fights |
| Epic + Purples | High | Camp extraction point 30 sec early |
| Blues + Greens | Medium | Fight only if necessary |
| Trash loot | Low | Play aggressive, seek better loot |
