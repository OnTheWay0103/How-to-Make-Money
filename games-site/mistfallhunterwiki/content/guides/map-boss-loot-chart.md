## Map Boss & Loot Quick-Reference Chart — Mistfall Hunter

> Version 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026 · Game launched July 29–30, 2026 (PC, PS5, Xbox, day-one Game Pass)

Every Mistfall Hunter map has one boss encounter, and bosses are the gate to the best loot in the game. This chart condenses the full [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) into one scan — where each boss lives, its phases, what it drops, and the key items you need to know. For Mist Lord / Einherjar Mode encounters, see the [Boss & Mist Lord Chart](/guides/boss-mist-lord-chart).

## Map Boss Quick-Reference Chart

| Map | Boss | Difficulty | Phases | Key Mechanics | Loot Value |
|-----|------|------------|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Ruined Catacombs** | **Returner Woodling** (mini-boss) | Easy | 2 (100–50%, below 50%) | Sweeping Slam (blockable, ~1.5s telegraph), Bone Charge (dashes at furthest player — sidestep), Mist Aura (DoT within 5m, Phase 2), skeleton adds every 20% HP | Medium — **drops Soul of Return** |
| **Forsaken Hollow** | **Hollow Warden** | Medium | 3 (100–60%, 60–30%, below 30%) | Chain Pull (dodge with a timed roll), Poison Pools (linger 12s / 24s in Phase 2), Phase 3 Frenzy (+50% attack speed, random chain pulls) | High |
| **Mistveil Forest** | **Verdant Ancient** | Medium | 3 (100–70%, 70–40%, below 40%) | Root Slam (ground-target AoE), Vine Sweep (75% of the arena — jump or block), Seed Pod (destroy within 3s), Root Eruption (targets each player in Phase 2) | High |
| **The Bloodfen** | **Blood Matriarch** | Hard | 2 (100–50%, below 50%) | Lunge Bite (roll perpendicular), Tail Whip (360° — jump), Blood Leeches (roll to shake off), Boiling Water DoT in Phase 2 — fight from the raised platforms | Very High |
| **Obsidian Depths** | **Shattered King** | Very Hard | 3 (100–75%, 75–40%, below 40%) | Crystal Barrage (hide behind a pillar), Shatter (destroys pillars — hug the boss, center is safe), Floor Collapse in Phase 3 (stay on the center platform); **portal unlocks after killing the Obsidian Guard mini-boss** | Legendary |

## Key Items Quick-Reference

| Item | Where It Comes From | What It Does |
|------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Soul of Return** | Dropped by the **Returner Woodling** mini-boss (spawns in high-value, contested areas 4–6 minutes into a run) | The extraction key item — on most maps you need it to extract with your loot; one Soul of Return is enough for a whole squad |
| **Obsidian Guard kill** | Mini-boss in the Obsidian Depths | Unlocks the portal to the Shattered King fight |
| **Dried Flower Knot** | Farmed item | Activates **Einherjar Mode** — the high-difficulty Mist Lord encounters (see the [Boss & Mist Lord Chart](/guides/boss-mist-lord-chart)) |
| **Holy weapons** | **Map bosses only** | Each boss holds a unique Holy weapon with exclusive affixes — every build routes to the boss whose Holy weapon completes it |
| **Gyldenblood** | Boss kills, loot runs, bounties | The primary loot currency — funds camp upgrades, gear and the Auction House (see the [Economy Guide](/guides/economy-guide)) |

## Boss-Fight Cheat Sheet

1. **Clear the area first** — kill nearby mobs before engaging; adds + boss is a wipe
2. **Watch for third-parties** — a boss fight is audible across the map; spend 30 seconds observing the arena before engaging
3. **Bring 2+ healing potions** — running out mid-fight is the #1 cause of boss deaths
4. **Damage through phase transitions** — transitions have a 1–2 second animation window; unload your highest damage abilities
5. **Don't chase fleeing bosses** — if a boss resets and runs back to spawn, let it; chasing often walks into an ambush
6. **Pick your target by Holy weapon** — every build has one boss whose drop completes it
