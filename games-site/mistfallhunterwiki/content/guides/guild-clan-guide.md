## Guild System Overview

Guilds in Mistfall Hunter are player-run organizations that provide **shared bonuses, exclusive content, and competitive play**. Whether you want a casual social group or a hardcore PvP guild, the system supports both.

> **Note:** Guild costs, XP values, perk effects, and war rewards below are community-reported approximations and may differ from the live game.

### What Guilds Offer

| Feature | Unlock Condition | Benefit |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Guild Stash | Guild Level 1 | Shared storage for members |
| Guild Perks | Guild Level 2 | Passive stat bonuses for members |
| Guild Bounties | Guild Level 3 | Exclusive PvP contracts |
| Guild Hall | Guild Level 4 | Customizable social space |
| Guild Wars | Guild Level 5 | Competitive territory control |
| Guild Shop | Guild Level 6 | Exclusive gear and cosmetics |

## Creating a Guild

### Requirements

| Requirement | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| Player Level | 15+ |
| Gyldenblood Cost | 5,000 |
| Members Needed (to create) | 2 (founder + 1) |
| Minimum Members to Stay Active | 3 |
| Guild Name | 4-16 characters, no profanity |

### Creation Process

1. Visit the **Guild Registrar** in your camp (unlocked after Camp Level 3)
2. Pay 5,000 Gyldenblood
3. Choose a name and tag (max 4 characters, e.g., [WOLF])
4. Set privacy settings (Open, Request, or Invite Only)
5. Recruit your first members

## Guild Ranks and Permissions

| Rank | Permissions | Max per Guild |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Founder | All permissions, can delete guild | 1 |
| Officer | Invite/kick members, manage stash, declare wars | 3 |
| Veteran | Invite members, access stash, start guild bounties | 5 |
| Member | Access stash, participate in wars | Unlimited |
| Recruit | Basic chat, limited stash access | Unlimited |

### Custom Rank Names

Founders can rename ranks. Common alternatives:

| Default | PvP Guild | Casual Guild |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Founder | Warlord | Mayor |
| Officer | Captain | Councilor |
| Veteran | Raider | Regular |
| Member | Warrior | Citizen |
| Recruit | Initiate | Visitor |

## Guild Perks

### How Perks Work

- Each perk slot provides a passive bonus to all online guild members
- Perks are purchased with Guild Funds (Gyldenblood donated by members)
- Higher-tier perks cost more but provide stronger effects

## Guild Wars

### How Guild Wars Work

Guild Wars are week-long competitive events between two guilds.

| Phase | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Declaration | Monday 00:00 UTC | Officers declare war on target guild |
| Preparation | Monday-Tuesday | Members prepare loadouts |
| Active War | Wednesday-Saturday | Earn war points through PvP and objectives |
| Resolution | Sunday | Winner determined, rewards distributed |

### War Points

| Action | Points | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Kill enemy guild member | 10 | In any raid |
| Extract after killing enemy guild member | 25 | Must survive |
| Capture war flag | 50 | Special war objective, spawns in raids |
| Defend war flag for 2 minutes | 75 | Hold the capture zone |
| Kill enemy guild officer | 100 | Bonus for high-value targets |

### Rewards

| Winner Rewards | Loser Rewards |
|---------------|---------------|
| 10,000 Gyldenblood (guild funds) | 3,000 Gyldenblood (guild funds) |
| Guild War Trophy (decorative) | Participation reward (minor) |
| +25% Guild XP for 1 week | — |
| Exclusive war winner banner | — |

## Finding the Right Guild

### Guild Types

| Type | Focus | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|
| Casual | Social, relaxed play | New players, limited playtime |
| PvE-focused | Boss hunting, extraction | PvE enthusiasts |
| PvP-focused | Bounty hunting, wars | Competitive players |
| Mixed | Balanced activities | Most players |
| Trading | Economy-focused | Merchant playstyle |

### What to Look For

- Active member count (not total, but how many play weekly)
- Guild level and unlocked perks
- Stash quality (well-stocked = active management)
- Timezone overlap with your play schedule
