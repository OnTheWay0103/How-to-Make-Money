# Mistfall Hunter Update Roadmap — Where the Game Is Going

**Mistfall Hunter / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Mistfall Hunter is a live-service game by Duskfall Interactive, and its roadmap has been unusually public. Now that 1.0 is out (July 30, 2026, with a day-one hotfix), this guide maps what has shipped, what is confirmed next, and how to prepare for each milestone so the updates feel like bonuses instead of surprises.

---

## 1. What 1.0 Already Shipped (July 30, 2026)

| Area | What's In |
|------|-----------|
| Release | Steam / PS5 / Xbox Series X\|S, day-one Xbox Game Pass; $24.99 standard / $39.99 Deluxe |
| Matchmaking | Solo and Trio queues only (no Duo); the second map is trio-only |
| Servers | US / DE / BR / SG / CN launch regions (no dedicated EU/OCE yet) |
| Season 1 | Live at launch — Withered Knight gained a new Polearm + Shield weapon stance |
| Balance | Full rebalance of every class vs. the playtest — all beta tier lists are invalid |

The day-one hotfix addressed the worst launch issues within hours; the [Patch Notes](/guides/patch-notes) page tracks everything since.

## 2. The Confirmed Upcoming Milestones

| Milestone | Window | What's New |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Playtest Wave 3 content | Late Q3 2026 | Third map — **Sunken Cathedral** — and new enemy types |
| Season of the Withering finale | September 2026 | The current season's event-ending mechanics (see the [Season Guide](/guides/season-guide)) |

| Season 2 | Post-launch cycle | First full new season: new map, new class, new battle pass |

**What is NOT confirmed:** exact dates for ranked mode or Season 2. Duskfall's stated philosophy is to never commit to dates until features are ready — any "leaked" date online is speculation.

---

## 3. What the Sunken Cathedral Brings (Confirmed Details)

The third map has been confirmed since the playtest wave 3 announcement. Known facts:

- It is **trio-only**, matching the second map's design.
- It introduces **new enemy types** alongside the standard Mistfall corruption.
- Expect it to ship with the Season 1/Withering mechanics active — afflicted zones will likely appear there from day one.

**How to prepare:** the new map means new loot tiers and affixes. Bank Gyldenblood and hold off on overpaying for current-tier gear — score thresholds will shift, and the [Gear Score Guide](/guides/gear-score-guide) explains why affix-rich current gear stays relevant across patches.

## 4. How to Prepare for Each Milestone

| Milestone | Prep Action | Payoff |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Sunken Cathedral (Q3) | Hold 10-20% of your Gyldenblood liquid | Buy the new-map gear before prices normalize |
| Season finale (September) | Complete your Withering-affix collection now | Afflicted-zone loot quality bumps disappear with the season |
| Ranked mode | Reach endgame score brackets now | Ranked matchmaking is score-aware; entry is smoother prepared |
| Season 2 | Save weekly challenge tokens | New-season passes often accept carried currency |

**The universal prep rule:** camp upgrades and warehouse storage persist across seasons — but season-specific items (Withering affixes, seasonal tokens) do not. Spend seasonal currency before the finale; invest in the camp after.

## 6. Common Roadmap Mistakes

1. **Over-investing in seasonal gear near the finale.** Withering affixes lose their one-tier bump in September — sell before the season ends.
2. **Ignoring the trio-only map.** If you play Solo queue, plan around the fact that the Sunken Cathedral is a trio map — find a squad now.
3. **Hoarding seasonal currency past the season.** Seasonal tokens and passes don't carry over; spend them before the reset.
4. **Trusting leaked dates.** Duskfall has never missed shipping confirmed content, but it also never promises dates — plan around windows, not dates.

**Final note:** the roadmap is a guide, not a promise — the one certainty is that Mistfall Hunter's meta changes every season. The smart play is to stay flexible: keep your camp strong, your currency liquid, and your class roster versatile (see the [Beginner Class Guide](/guides/beginner-class-guide) on free class switching).
