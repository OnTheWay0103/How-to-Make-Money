# Loadout Optimizer — Build the Right Kit for Every Run

Every Mistfall Hunter death costs you gear. That makes the loadout screen the most important menu in the game. A well-built loadout multiplies your extraction rate; a careless one turns a good run into an insurance payout for whoever kills you. This guide breaks down loadout construction into three layers: **slots**, **affixes**, and **budget** — then gives you presets you can copy for each class.

## 1. Gear Slot Priorities

You have limited slots, and they are not worth equal amounts of Gyldenblood. Upgrade in this order:

| Priority | Slot | Why It Wins |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Weapon | Biggest raw power spike per upgrade; weapon affixes define your build |
| 2 | Chest armor | Highest armor rating of all armor slots; block DR scales off it |
| 3 | Boots | Movement speed affixes are the best survival stat in extraction |
| 4 | Helmet | Small armor gain, but roll focus/energy affixes here cheaply |
| 5 | Gloves / Rings | Fill with whatever affixes you are missing; cheapest to replace |

**Rule**: never carry a purple item in a slot whose affix is useless to your class. A blue boot with Move Speed + Escape cooldown beats a purple boot with poison damage on any class.

## 2. Affix Rules of Thumb

Affixes are what make gear valuable. The rarity system matters, but affixes matter more:

| Rarity | Handle |
|--------|--------|
| Green | Vendor unless the affix is perfect and you are pre-Camp Level 3 |
| Blue | Keep to +2 if it has 1+ relevant affix |
| Purple | Keep if 2+ relevant affixes; Auction House if it rolls for another class |
| Legendary | Keep, always — even bad legendaries sell for 500+ Gyldenblood |

- **2 good affixes > 4 bad affixes.** A purple with two dead affixes is worse than a blue with two perfect ones.
- **Weapon affixes outweigh everything.** Spell Power on a Sorcerer weapon is worth more than a full purple armor set.
- **Armor Penetration** beats flat damage on most builds past Camp Level 5, because elite mobs and players stack armor.
- Check the Auction House before vendoring purple+. Some "useless" affix combos are exactly what another class is searching for.

## 3. Loadout Budgets

The best loadout system is three-tiered. Maintain all three and your bank balance will thank you:

| Loadout | Gear | Use It For | Loss on Death |
|---------|------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | Green/blue, +0–+2 | Learning maps, warm-up, farming low-risk zones | 500–1,000 Gyldenblood |
| Serious | Blue/purple, +3–+4, 2+ good affixes | Ranked PvP, boss zones, high-value extraction | 3,000–8,000 Gyldenblood |
| Luxury | Purple/legendary, +5+, perfect affixes | Season-ending pushes, tournament-style runs | 10,000+ Gyldenblood |

**Loadout discipline checklist:**
- Extract 3 times in a row with a budget loadout → equip the serious kit
- Die twice in a row with the serious kit → step down a tier and fix the problem first
- Never take luxury gear into a zone you have not extracted from at least once
- Keep consumables identical across all three tiers — deaths usually come from missing pots, not missing stats

## 4. Class-Specific Loadout Presets

| Class | Weapon | Key Affixes | Consumables |
|-------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Sorcerer | Elemental Focus | Spell Power > Mana Regen > Cast Speed | Mana pots ×3, heal pots ×2 |
| Shadowstrix | Dual Daggers | Attack Speed > Crit Damage > Move Speed | Stealth tonic, heal pots ×3 |
| Mercenary | Sword & Shield | Armor Rating > Block DR > Max HP | Heal pots ×4, stamina tonic |
| Withered Knight | Greatsword | Burst Damage > Armor Penetration > HP | Heal pots ×3, sigil charges |
| Blackarrow | Longbow | Crit Rate > Armor Penetration > Range | Arrow bundle, heal pots ×2 |
| Seer | Lantern & Catalyst | Cooldown Reduction > Healing Power > Mana Regen | Mana pots ×3, revive totem |

Two extra presets worth keeping in your stash:

- **Anti-boss loadout**: weapon with Armor Penetration, chest with Block DR, boots with dodge — built for the zone's boss, not for players.
- **Loot-runner loadout**: boots with Move Speed + two inventory slots freed of utility tools, so you can carry more payload per extract.

## 5. Common Loadout Mistakes

- **Carrying one "do-everything" loadout** — a PvP kit and a PvE kit are different builds. Swap between them per run type.
- **Ignoring weight/value** — a 10-slot inventory full of green junk blocks the one purple drop that makes the run.
- **Over-upgrading early** — spend Gyldenblood on Camp upgrades first; gear is a consumable, camp is permanent.
- **Equipping gear for the class you are leveling next** — equip for the class you are playing *now*. Your next class can wear your stash.
