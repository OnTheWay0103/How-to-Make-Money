## What Is Mistfall Hunter?

Mistfall Hunter is a dark fantasy PvPvE extraction ARPG. You drop into mist-shrouded ruins, fight Corroded (AI enemies) and rival players, scavenge Gyldenblood (the game's primary loot currency) and gear, and extract before the closing mist kills you. If you die, you lose everything you are carrying.

## The Extraction Loop in 30 Seconds

1. **Gear up** at your camp — choose a class and equip weapons/armor from your stash
2. **Drop in** solo or with up to 2 squadmates
3. **Loot and fight** — kill Corroded for drops, avoid or engage rival players
4. **Find the Returner Woodling** — a mini-boss that drops the Soul of Return
5. **Extract** — channel at an extraction point (~30 seconds, you are vulnerable)
6. **Survive and bank your loot** — everything in your warehouse is permanently safe

## Beginner's Priority Checklist

### 1. Pick the Right Class

For your first few extractions, play **Mercenary** (Sword & Shield). It is:
- The most forgiving class (strong damage reduction on block)
- Easy to learn extraction mechanics without dying constantly
- Effective in both PvE and PvP

Avoid Sorcerer and Shadowstrix until you understand the maps and extraction flow — they die fast if caught.

### 2. Bank Early, Bank Often

Any gear in your **warehouse** is safe forever. After every successful extraction:
- Deposit high-value items immediately
- Equip common/green gear for practice runs
- Save blue/purple gear for when you are confident

### 3. Learn the Map Before Pushing Deep

Spend your first 3-5 extractions just learning:
- Where extraction points are located
- Where the Returner Woodling spawns
- Common player pathing routes
- Safe zones with cover

Map knowledge matters more than gear.

### 4. Know When to Leave

The #1 beginner mistake: **getting greedy**. A successful extraction with modest loot is infinitely better than dying with a bag full of treasure.

- Extracted with 3 items? Win.
- Pushed deeper for one more chest and died? Loss.
- If you hear combat near extraction, wait or find another exit.

### 5. Upgrade Your Camp First

Between extractions, upgrade in this order:
1. **Goddess Statue** — unlocks core progression
2. **Gyldenblod Pool** — healing bonuses
3. **Shop & Warehouse** — more storage, better starting gear
4. **Tavern** — passive buffs

## The 10 Most Common Beginner Mistakes

### Mistake #1: Hoarding Gear You Never Use

**The fix — the 3-rule system:**
1. If you haven't used it in 5 extractions → vendor it or list it on the Auction House
2. If it's for a class you don't play → sell it
3. If it's green and not perfect affixes → vendor it instantly

### Mistake #2: Bringing Your Best Gear Every Run

**The fix — the 30% rule:** never bring gear that represents more than roughly 30% of your total net worth. Have one "serious" loadout for focused play and two budget loadouts (green/blue) for practice and learning maps. One death can set you back several extractions of progress — and gear fear makes you play worse.

### Mistake #3: Not Banking Loot Between Extractions

**The fix — the post-extraction habit:** extract → open warehouse → deposit ALL valuable items → equip next loadout → then queue. Never queue with loot from a previous run still in your inventory.

### Mistake #4: Fighting Every PvP Encounter

**The fix — ask three questions before engaging:**
1. "What am I carrying?" — if the answer is "a lot," disengage.
2. "Do I know where their teammates are?" — if no, assume they have backup.
3. "Can I disengage if this goes wrong?" — if you are in a dead-end room, don't start a fight.

**Rule of thumb:** if you are carrying a significant amount of valuable loot, your primary goal is extraction, not PvP. Most deaths while carrying high-value loot happen in fights you could have avoided.

### Mistake #5: Ignoring the Mist Timer

**The fix — the 5-minute rule:** when the timer hits 5 minutes, stop looting and start moving toward an extraction point. Know where all extraction points are before you need them, and do not engage fights after the 5-minute mark.

### Mistake #6: Over-Upgrading Trash Gear

**The fix:** never upgrade Common gear. Only upgrade Green if it has perfect affixes and you are early game. For Blue and above, upgrade only if the upgrade cost stays well below the item's market value — if the upgrade costs more than the item is worth, sell it and buy something better.

### Mistake #7: Playing the Wrong Class for Your Experience

**The fix — a progression path:**

| Extractions | Class | Why |
|------------|-------|-----|
| 1-15 | Mercenary | Learn maps, extraction loops, enemy patterns |
| 15-30 | Blackarrow or Withered Knight | Learn positioning and timing |
| 30+ | Sorcerer or Shadowstrix | Now you know enough to play fragile classes |

### Mistake #8: Not Using Your Consumables

**The fix:** use potions at 60% HP, not 20% HP — if you wait until 20%, you can be bursted before the heal completes. Bind healing to an easy key and use one healing item per PvE engagement as a habit.

### Mistake #9: Ignoring Camp Upgrades for Cosmetic Purchases

**The fix — camp upgrades first, cosmetics never (well, maybe much later).** Camp upgrades multiply your efficiency: the Goddess Statue unlocks core progression, the Warehouse multiplies your storage, and the Gyldenblod Pool saves you potions. Cosmetics give zero gameplay benefit.

### Mistake #10: Going Solo Into High-Stakes Content

**The fix — know the risk per map:**

| Content | Minimum Party |
|---------|--------------|
| Ruined Catacombs | Solo fine |
| Forsaken Hollow | Solo fine (careful in center) |
| Mistveil Forest | Duo recommended |
| The Bloodfen | Duo recommended |
| Obsidian Depths | Trio required |

If you must solo high-stakes content, run budget gear and prioritize stealth and avoidance over combat.

## What to Do in Your First 5 Extractions

1. **Extraction 1**: Pick Mercenary. Find one extraction point. Die. Learn.
2. **Extraction 2**: Find the Returner Woodling. Watch someone else kill it. Learn the fight.
3. **Extraction 3**: Kill the Returner Woodling. Extract with common loot. Success!
4. **Extraction 4**: Bring a green weapon. Extract with green/blue loot.
5. **Extraction 5**: You know the basics. Now read specific class/build guides.

## Your First Week Roadmap

| Day | Focus | Key Milestone |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| Day 1 | Learn the basics | First successful extraction |
| Day 2 | Build your camp | Camp Level 3 |
| Day 3 | Master extraction | 10 successful extracts |
| Day 4 | Try PvP | First player kill |
| Day 5 | Join a guild | Active guild membership |
| Day 6 | Weekly challenges | Partial completion |
| Day 7 | Endgame prep | Ready for competitive play |

### Day 1: First Extraction

1. **Complete the tutorial** — it rewards your first gear set
2. **Pick Mercenary** as your starting class — most forgiving for beginners
3. **Equip the default gear** — do not buy anything yet
4. **Learn the controls** — practice blocking, dodging, and using items

**Raid strategy:** drop into Ruined Catacombs (the first map), stay on the edges away from players, loot 2-3 chests, find an extraction point, and extract. Goal for Day 1: complete 3 extractions (even if 1-2 fail).

### Day 2: Camp Upgrades

Your camp is your home base. Upgrading it unlocks permanent advantages. Start with the **Warehouse** (more storage), then the **Shop** (buy/sell basic items), the **Gyldenblod Pool** (healing), and the **Goddess Statue** (core progression). Costs scale per level — see the [Camp Upgrades Guide](/guides/camp-upgrades-guide) for exact priorities.

### Day 3: Extraction Mastery

1. **Learn one map** — pick Ruined Catacombs and learn every extraction point, chest location, and patrol route
2. **Practice the Woodling** — kill the Returner Woodling at least once
3. **Inventory management** — learn what to keep and what to sell

**Loot priority quick table:**

| Item | Keep? | Sell? | Use? |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| Gyldenblood | Always | — | — |
| Green weapons/armor | No | Yes | Use only if you have nothing better |
| Blue weapons/armor | Keep spares | No | Your primary gear target |
| Healing items | Keep (stack of 5) | Yes (excess) | Always carry 2-3 |
| Crafting materials | Keep | No | Needed for camp upgrades |
| Quest items | Keep | No | Complete quests first |

### Day 4: PvP Introduction

Do not start PvP until you have 10+ successful extractions, know the Ruined Catacombs layout well, and have a full set of blue gear.

**Your first engagement:** assess before acting — is the player alone? Are they fighting enemies (third-party opportunity)? Are they looting (ambush window)? If they see you first, either fight or retreat — hesitation is death. Open with your highest burst damage.

### Day 5: Joining a Guild

Guilds accelerate progression: shared stash space, guild-exclusive activities, and easier group play. Look for an **active** guild (check last-online times) that is **beginner-friendly** and in **your timezone**.

### Day 6: Weekly Challenges

Beginner-friendly challenges are usually the easiest ones — clean extracts, map discovery, chest looting, and boss kills. Prioritize rewards in this order: Gyldenblood → gear crates → material packs → weapon shards. See the [Weekly Challenges Guide](/guides/weekly-challenges) for the current rotation.

### Day 7: Endgame Prep

By the end of your first week, aim for:

- [ ] Player Level 25+
- [ ] Camp Level 5+
- [ ] Full Rare (blue) gear set
- [ ] At least 1 Epic (purple) weapon
- [ ] Active guild membership
- [ ] 50+ total extractions
- [ ] Weekly challenges partially complete

**What comes next:** higher-tier extraction content opens up as your gear improves, and competitive systems (seasonal rankings, guild-level content) become available to established players. The [Leveling Guide](/guides/leveling-guide) covers the fastest path to max level.

## Common New Player Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Selling all materials | Need them for camp upgrades | Keep materials until camp is Level 3 |
| Hoarding gear | Stash fills up, risk losing in raids | Sell or use green gear freely |
| Playing one class only | Miss out on versatility | Try each class once |
| Ignoring the camp | Slower progression | Upgrade camp between raids |
| Rushing endgame content | Die repeatedly, lose gear | Build fundamentals first |

## Key Takeaway

Mistfall Hunter rewards patience and risk management over raw mechanical skill. Play slow, learn the maps, extract often, and the gear will accumulate naturally. An extracted green item is worth more than a purple item in your death bag.
