## Beyond "Extract More"

The base economy guide teaches you to extract, sell, and survive. This guide covers the layer above it: **why prices move, how to profit from the movement, and how to structure your wealth for the long game.** In Mistfall Hunter's player-driven Auction House (AH), the players who get rich are rarely the best extractors — they are the ones who understand the price cycle and their fees.

## What Drives Prices

| Driver | Effect | Window |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Population cycles | More players online = more supply and demand | Friday-Sunday |
| Raid/boss resets | Fresh consumable and repair demand | Tuesday/Wednesday |
| Meta shifts | Old meta gear crashes, new meta gear spikes | Within days of balance patches |
| Season progress | Season Token value rises as the pass fills | Whole season |
| Bulk listing discounts | Stacks of 10+ list 15-20% cheaper per unit | Constant |

The three rules that fall out of this table:

1. **Buy when the player count is low, sell when it is high.**
2. **Never hold a full inventory of a meta item through a patch.**
3. **Bulk lots are both a discount and an entry fee** — stack trading is how volume players dominate.

## The Weekly Price Cycle

Mistfall Hunter's market has a confirmed, predictable rhythm:

| Day | Market State | Solo Flipper Action |
|-----|--------------|---------------------|
| Monday | Post-reset demand spike | Sell what you held; buy nothing |
| Tuesday | The dip — cheapest buys of the week | **Buy week's inventory** |
| Wednesday | Low supply, quiet | Buy leftovers, list crafts |
| Thursday | Ramp begins | List mid-value items |
| Friday | Price peak begins | **Sell everything held** |
| Saturday-Sunday | Peak trading, weekend spike | Sell, sell, sell |

**The flip:** buy on Tuesday/Wednesday, hold 2-5 days, list Friday evening. This single loop outperforms every farming strategy per hour of effort.

## Flipping with Fee Math

Flipping only works when your margin survives the fee structure. Always compute profit as:

```
Profit = Sale Price − Buy Price − Listing Fee − Success Fee
```

| Item Type | Typical Margin | Fee Drag | Net Viable? |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| Common materials (stacks) | 20-40% | Low (bulk listing amortizes) | Yes — volume play |
| Blue gear with 1 good affix | 30-60% | Medium | Yes — fastest sellers |
| Purple gear with wrong affixes | 15-40% | Medium | Maybe — check AH price history |
| Legendary / God-tier rolls | 50-100%+ | Low relative to price | Yes — but capital-heavy |
| Vendor-trash commons | Negative | — | Never flip; vendor these |

**Rule of thumb:** if the expected net is under 25% after fees, the item is not a flip — it is a distraction.

## Vendor vs Auction House: The Decision Table

| Item Situation | Vendor | Auction House |
|----------------|--------|---------------|
| Common gear, broken items, gray mats | **Sell** | Never |
| Green gear with bad affixes | Sell | Only in bulk stacks |
| Blue+ gear with 1+ good affix | Never | **Sell** |
| Materials (Mist Essence, Ancient Residue) | Emergency only | **Sell** — crafting demand is constant |
| Gear for your own class | Keep | Keep |

The vendor exists to convert trash into capital; the AH exists to convert luck into wealth. Using the vendor for blue gear is leaving money on the table — and using the AH for trash costs you listing fees on items nobody wants.

## Materials: The Steady Market

| Material | Source | Why It Holds Value |
|----------|--------|--------------------|
| Mist Essence | Mini-bosses, elite mobs | Camp upgrade tier 4+ requirement |
| Ancient Residue | Bosses | Legendary crafting |
| Corroded Core | Corroded elites | Consumable crafting, steady demand |
| Rune Fragment | Hidden caches | PvP consumable crafting |

Materials are the **least volatile** trade in the game because their demand is structural (upgrades and crafting never stop). A flipper who cannot predict gear prices can still profit from materials year-round.

## Season Tokens: The Second Currency

Season Tokens (from the Season Pass and challenges) buy season-exclusive items — and their value compounds if you convert correctly:

- **Spend on exclusives first** — season-exclusive cosmetics and gear rise in resale value after the season closes.
- **Don't hoard past the season** — unspent tokens typically lose value when the next pass starts.
- **Check the [Season Pass Guide](/guides/season-pass-guide)** for the current token economy before buying anything.

## The Wealth Roadmap

| Stage | Capital | Strategy |
|-------|---------|----------|
| 1. Extraction grinder | 0-5,000 Gyldenblood | Speed-loot runs, vendor everything, bank upgrades |
| 2. First flips | 5,000-30,000 | Flip materials and blue gear on the weekly cycle |
| 3. Volume trader | 30,000-150,000 | Bulk stacks, hold through the dip, list at the peak |
| 4. Market player | 150,000+ | God-tier gear, season speculation, corner slow markets |

**The one rule that never changes:** wealth is measured in what you extract, but it is *built* on what you sell — the only item that loses value is the one sitting in your stash.
