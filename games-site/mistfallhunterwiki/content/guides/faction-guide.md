# Faction Guide — Reputation, Ranks, and Exclusive Rewards

Factions in Mistfall Hunter are the endgame reputation system. Once your Camp reaches Level 4, the Faction Liaison appears and you can align with one of three factions. Reputation unlocks exclusive gear, contracts, cosmetics, and a weekly reward chest — and it is the only way to obtain some of the strongest consumables in the game.

> **Note:** Reputation thresholds, daily and weekly gains, decay rates, and shop prices below are community-reported approximations and may differ from the live game.

## The Three Factions

| Faction | Focus | Best For | Reputation Source |
|---------|-------|----------|-------------------|
| **The Ember Court** | PvE, boss hunting, dungeon clearing | Sorcerers, Withered Knights, Seers | Boss kills, elite kills, dungeon clears |
| **The Pale Exchange** | Looting, economy, extraction value | Blackarrows, Mercenaries, loot-runners | Item extracts, Gyldenblood Shards, high-value sales |
| **The Hollow Syndicate** | PvP, bounties, player kills | Shadowstrix, Mercenaries, PvP mains | Player eliminations, bounty contracts, arena wins |

You can switch factions — but only once per season, and switching resets your reputation progress to Neutral. Choose deliberately.

## Reputation Ranks

Every faction tracks the same six ranks. Higher ranks gate the best rewards:

| Rank | Reputation Needed | Unlocks |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| Neutral | 0 | Faction dailies, basic shop |
| Friendly | 1,000 | Faction weekly contract, rank gear (green/blue) |
| Honored | 3,000 | Faction consumables, rank gear (blue/purple) |
| Revered | 6,000 | Faction-only weapon skin, purple gear with unique affixes |
| Exalted | 10,000 | Faction legendary, exclusive title, weekly chest upgrade |
| Paragon | 15,000 (season cap) | Season-exclusive cosmetic + permanent +5% faction rep gain |

**Reputation tips:**
- Faction dailies give 150–250 reputation each and take under 10 minutes — do all of them every day
- Weekly faction contracts give 800–1,200 reputation and are the single biggest jump per session
- Reputation decays by 10% of your current progress if you miss two consecutive weekly contracts — stay active
- Gyldenblood Shards always count as reputation currency for The Pale Exchange; vendor them nowhere else

## Weekly Faction Quests

Every faction offers one weekly chain. Structure is identical across all three:

1. Complete 5 faction dailies
2. Win or extract from 3 faction-tagged runs
3. Complete the weekly faction contract (boss hunt / high-value extract / bounty)

**Reward**: 800–1,200 reputation, one faction gear chest, and progress toward the weekly faction bonus chest (extra 2,500 Gyldenblood + crafting materials).

## Which Faction Should You Pick?

| Playstyle | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Solo extractor who sells everything | The Pale Exchange |
| Squad that hunts bosses every session | The Ember Court |
| PvP-first player queueing for fights | The Hollow Syndicate |
| Balanced player | The Ember Court — boss rewards sell best on the Auction House |
