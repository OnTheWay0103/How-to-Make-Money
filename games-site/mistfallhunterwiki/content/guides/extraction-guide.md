## The Extraction Loop Explained

Extraction is the core mechanic of Mistfall Hunter. Everything you do in a raid leads to one goal: **escape with your loot**. Here is exactly how it works.

## Step-by-Step Extraction

### Step 1: Drop In

Select your class and loadout from your camp stash. Drop into the mist-shrouded ruins solo or with up to 2 squadmates. You spawn at a random entry point on the map.

### Step 2: Loot and Fight

Kill Corroded (AI enemies) for gear and Gyldenblood. Open chests. Avoid or engage rival players. The map is PvPvE — every sound you make can attract danger.

### Step 3: Find the Returner Woodling

The Returner Woodling is a **mini-boss** that spawns in high-value, contested areas of each map. It is your primary extraction objective because it drops the **Soul of Return**.

The Woodling is always in the same general zone per map, but its exact position varies. Look for:
- Marked ritual circles or corrupted tree formations
- Areas with higher Gyldenblood density
- Locations where you hear its distinctive chime/groan audio cue

### Step 4: Kill the Returner Woodling

The Woodling is not a pushover. It has:
- **AoE root attack** — dodge the vine eruption
- **Summon adds** — Corroded spawn during the fight at roughly 50% HP
- **Charge attack** — telegraphed, dodge sideways

**Warning**: Attacking the Woodling signals other players. Expect PvPvE chaos. Either kill it fast or be ready to fight off third parties.

### Step 5: Secure the Soul of Return

The Soul of Return drops as a lootable item. It:
- Occupies one inventory slot
- Is **single-use** — one extraction attempt per Soul
- Is **lost on death** — if you die carrying it, it is gone

If you are in a squad, only **one person needs the Soul of Return** for the whole squad to extract together.

### Step 6: Find an Extraction Point

Extraction points are marked on your map. They are fixed locations per map. Travel to one with your Soul of Return.

### Step 7: Channel and Extract

Channel at the extraction point for approximately **30 seconds**. During this time:
- You are **vulnerable and visible**
- A loud audio cue plays, alerting nearby players
- If you take damage, the channel **does not reset**, but you can be killed
- If you move or attack, the channel **cancels**

Once the channel completes: **you and your squad extract successfully**. All carried loot is banked.

## Alternative Extraction Methods

Some maps have **fixed exits** that do not require a Soul of Return:
- May have longer channel times
- May have limited uses (first-come-first-served)
- May require specific activation items
- Often in more dangerous or exposed locations

Learn these alternative exits — they can save a run if someone steals the Woodling kill.

## The Closing Mist

As the raid timer progresses, the **mist closes in** from the edges of the map:
- Deals increasing damage over time
- Forces players toward the center
- Creates natural choke points and final showdowns
- Full map coverage = death for anyone still inside

Always track the mist timer. Give yourself a comfortable buffer to reach extraction — the mist damage scales fast and you cannot outrun it.

## Advanced Tactics

### Core Mindset Shifts

| Beginner Mindset | Advanced Mindset |
|-----------------|------------------|
| "Kill everything I see" | "Engage only when necessary" |
| "Extract as fast as possible" | "Extract when conditions are optimal" |
| "Run toward combat for loot" | "Run toward extraction with good loot" |
| "Soul of Return = safe extract" | "Soul of Return = signal to third parties" |

### Pre-Raid Preparation

Always classify your loadout before dropping:

| Tier | Gear Value | Risk Strategy |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Scavenger | Green/Common | Fight only if cornered. Prioritize loot. |
| Standard | Blue/Rare | Pick fights carefully. Extract with 1-2 good items. |
| High Value | Purple/Epic+ | Avoid combat. Extract at first opportunity. |
| Revenge | Whatever you want | You already lost the expensive set. Play aggressive. |

**Inventory management:** leave 2-3 empty slots for Gyldenblood, carry 1-2 healing items max (more space = more loot), bring 1 mobility item (smoke bomb or a speed option), and never bring your best gear on the first raid of the session.

### Map Awareness and Route Planning

**The three-zone model** — every map divides into:

| Zone | Description | Strategy |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Outer Ring | Low-tier loot, few player encounters | Farm here first. Build confidence. |
| Middle Ring | Medium loot, Returner Woodling area | Approach carefully. Listen for combat. |
| Core | High loot, PvP hotspot | Only enter if you are hunting players. |

**Route planning principles:**
1. **Enter from the edge**, work inward
2. **Never run in a straight line** toward the Woodling — you will walk into an ambush
3. **Use high ground** to scout before committing to a path
4. **Mark extraction points** as soon as you drop — plan your exit route before you need it
5. **Have two extraction routes** — one primary, one fallback

**Audio cues to track:**

| Sound | Meaning | Response |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Woodling chime/groan | Returner Woodling is nearby | Approach slowly, scout first |
| Combat sounds + no Woodling | Players fighting each other | Third party opportunity OR avoid |
| Combat sounds + Woodling | Players fighting the Woodling | Wait for them to kill it, then ambush |
| Extraction channel sound | Someone is extracting | Push to contest, or note their location and avoid |
| Mist alarm | Closure is near | Begin extraction immediately |

### Fighting the Returner Woodling

**Solo strategy:**

| Phase | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Approach | Scout from range first. Look for other players. |
| Engagement | Start the fight near cover. Keep the Woodling between you and the most likely approach path. |
| Adds phase (~50% HP) | Kite the adds away before finishing. Do not stand still. |
| Loot | Grab Soul of Return immediately. Do not linger. |
| Exit | Move to extraction immediately — do not farm more. |

**Squad strategy:** assign roles — one player on the Woodling, one watching the two most likely approach routes, one ready to swap if combat starts. A scout watching the perimeter is worth more than a third DPS.

### Third Party Management

Third parties (players who attack while you are fighting) are the #1 cause of death in Mistfall Hunter.

**How to avoid them:**
- **Kill the Woodling fast** — the longer the fight, the more players arrive
- **Use quieter weapons** if possible — bows and daggers make less noise than heavy weapons
- **Finish early** — the first minutes of a raid have the fewest players at the Woodling
- **If you hear fighting at the Woodling**, wait. Let them kill it. Kill them.

**How to handle being third-partied:**
1. **Disengage immediately** — break line of sight
2. **Smoke bomb** if you have one — creates confusion
3. **Reset** — heal, reposition, re-evaluate
4. **Let the third party fight the original enemy** — they might kill each other
5. **Only re-engage if you have clear advantage**

### Extraction Point Tactics

**Pre-channel scouting checklist:**
- Is the extraction point clear? (No players visible)
- Is there cover nearby? (Can you break line of sight?)
- Are there multiple approach routes? (Can you see all of them?)
- Is the mist closing? (How much time do you have?)

**Channeling positioning:**

| Situation | Positioning |
|-----------|-------------|
| Solo, open area | Behind cover, facing the most likely approach angle |
| Solo, near cover | Inside cover, with escape route behind you |
| Squad | One channels, others fan out in a triangle covering approaches |
| Under attack | Cancel channel, reposition, re-channel after clearing |

**Extraction denial:** if you want to prevent someone else from extracting, stand at range and shoot the channeling player (damage does not cancel the channel, but killing them does), or simply wait — they must channel for roughly 30 seconds, which is plenty of time to push.

## Solo Extraction Routes

Route planning is the single most important solo skill. Squads can improvise; a solo player cannot. Your **route** is your entire plan: where you loot, when you engage, and which extraction point you commit to.

### The Four Route Archetypes

| Route | Goal | Time | Risk | Typical Haul |
|-------|------|------|------|--------------|
| Speed Loot | Extract fast with low risk | ~5-7 min | Very Low | A few hundred Gyldenblood |
| Standard Farm | Clear one zone, boss optional | ~10-15 min | Low | Roughly 800-1,200 Gyldenblood |
| Boss Run | Kill the Returner Woodling, extract | ~15-20 min | Medium | Roughly 1,500-2,500 Gyldenblood |
| Stealth Path | Ghost the map, cherry-pick caches | ~10-12 min | Low-Medium | Variable — can spike high |

*Times and hauls are community-reported approximations. Pick your archetype **before you drop** — mixing goals mid-run is how solo players die with full bags.*

### Route 1: Speed Loot (The Bankroll Builder)

The route that funds everything else — a fast, low-risk money printer.

1. Spawn and read your position relative to the nearest chest zones
2. Hit the 2-3 nearest low-risk chests (avoid boss-adjacent areas)
3. Loot only common/uncommon gear and Gyldenblood piles
4. Extract at the nearest standard exit (roughly 30s channel)

**Key rule:** never fight for loot on a speed run. If an elite or another player contests a chest, let it go — a modest run repeated three times beats a big run that dies at extraction.

### Route 2: Standard Farm (The Mid-Risk Balance)

The default solo route once you know the map's zone values:

1. Choose **one** high-value zone (see the [Map Guide](/guides/map-guide) for zone values) and clear it methodically
2. Kill Corroded packs on the way, but **skip the elite** unless it is an easy isolated spawn
3. Check the boss room **only if the zone is quiet** — a glance, not a commitment
4. Extract through the **least-traveled exit**, even if it is a longer channel

**Exit selection rule:** the exit farthest from the boss room has the highest solo survival rate. Third parties camp the exits near high-value zones, not the quiet ones.

### Route 3: Boss Run (The High-Reward Gamble)

The Returner Woodling is your objective because it drops the **Soul of Return** — and it is also the biggest third-party magnet on the map.

**Solo boss-run rules:**
- **Scout first.** Approach the Woodling's zone perimeter and listen for a few seconds. If you hear fighting, you are the third party — reposition and take the boss when the winner is weak, or leave.
- **Kill fast or leave.** The Woodling's AoE root and summon phases give it time to attract attention. Commit to a burst-kill window; if you do not secure it in that window, the odds shift against you.
- **Extract immediately after.** The Soul of Return marks you. Do not linger to loot the room — take the Soul and the cache and move.

### Route 4: Stealth Path (The Shadow Route)

For Shadowstrix and Blackarrow players who want value without a fight:

| Priority | Target | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Locked chests (if you have a key) | Rare+ gear with low contest risk |
| 2 | Hidden caches | High-tier items, rarely camped |
| 3 | Supply drops (if uncontested) | Mixed material value |
| 4 | Boss room check, last | Only if completely silent |

The stealth route fails when you **force** it — if the quiet path is occupied, reroute to the standard farm rather than sneaking through a contested corridor.

### The Bail Decision (When to Change Routes)

Your route should have a pre-planned trigger. The moment any of these fire, switch to "extract now":

| Trigger | Response |
|---------|----------|
| You take heavy HP damage from a player | Do not trade — move to extraction |
| A third-party fight breaks out near your exit | Take the longer exit instead |
| You see 2+ players moving toward your zone | Abandon the zone immediately |
| Mist is closing and you are far from an exit | Take the safest path, even if longer |
| You land a rare/legendary item | Job done — extract now |

**Solo rule #1:** an extracted green item is worth more than a purple item in your death bag. No haul justifies an exit you cannot reach.

## Post-Raid Analysis

After every extraction (or death), ask yourself:

| Question | If Yes | If No |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Did I scout before engaging? | Good habit | #1 cause of death |
| Did I have an exit plan? | Good habit | Always plan your exit |
| Did I overstay? | Extract earlier next time | Keep good habits |
| Did I hear audio cues I ignored? | Pay attention next time | Good awareness |
| Did I bring the right loadout? | Good judgment | Adjust next run |

## Common Extraction Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Waiting until mist closes to start extracting | Plan your extraction route well before mist closes |
| Not clearing extraction area first | Scout the extraction point before channeling |
| Channeling immediately after getting the Soul | Other players know you just killed the Woodling — reposition first |
| Forgetting the Soul takes an inventory slot | Keep one slot free before engaging the Woodling |
| Assuming the Woodling is unguarded | It is a PvP magnet — approach with caution |
