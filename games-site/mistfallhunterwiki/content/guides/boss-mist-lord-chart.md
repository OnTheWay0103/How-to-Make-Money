## Boss & Mist Lord Chart — Mistfall Hunter

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026 · Game launched July 29, 2026 (PC, PS5, Xbox, day-one Game Pass)

Quick-reference chart of every confirmed boss in Mistfall Hunter — where they live, how to find them, and what they drop. Bosses are the gate to the best loot in the game: **map bosses are the only source of Holy weapons**, and **Mist Lords are the most reliable source of top-tier gear** on the hardest settings.

---

## Confirmed Bosses

| Boss | Type | Location / How to Find | Phases & Behavior | Drops |
|------|------|------------------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Sálmar, the Cursed Moonwane** | Mist Lord | **Hallowgrove** (first map) — two-phase; in the launch build he roams as a roaming elite and can intercept you mid-run | 2 phases; born of a failed forbidden experiment to summon a deity — "fused flesh and departed souls" | Top-tier gear; Mist Lord table |
| **Otto, the Flesheater** | Boss (Glutton/Devourer-type) | **Einherjar Mode** — find the **Dried Flower Knot** to challenge him | 2 phases (second phase completed for Beta 2) | Unique Holy weapon + boss table |
| **General Harald (Harald, the Fallen General)** | Mist Lord / Einherjar boss | **Einherjar Mode, Chaos Trio setting** — activated with a **Dried Flower Knot**; guards the ruins | Former hero of Brandrgarde; "relentless executioner"; **enrages if not killed quickly** | Unique Holy weapon + top-tier gear; hardest boss on the map |
| **Prayer of the Moon** | Boss | Seen in the June 2026 Open Beta; summons phantoms during the fight | Phantom summons | Status unconfirmed — possibly a phase of Sálmar |

## Mist Lord Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **What they are** | The apex bosses of the world — the marquee encounters |
| **Where** | Challenged in the high-difficulty **Einherjar Mode** |
| **How to access** | Einherjar Mode is activated with a **Dried Flower Knot** |
| **Rewards** | The most reliable source of top-tier gear, especially on **Cataclysm difficulty** (the hardest setting) |

## Map Boss Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Spawn** | Bosses randomly spawn in **two Boss areas** on each map |
| **Health** | Bosses have **three layers of health** |
| **Engage** | A **temporary barrier** seals the arena when the fight starts |
| **Holy weapons** | **Map bosses are the only source of Holy weapons** — each boss holds a unique Holy weapon with exclusive affixes |
| **Build routing** | A specific boss is the route to a specific build — choose your target by the Holy weapon you want |

## Boss-Fight Cheat Sheet

1. **Find the boss area first** — bosses spawn in 2 fixed zones; the barrier traps you, so clear the area beforehand
2. **Kill fast** — General Harald enrages if the fight drags; bring burst
3. **Budget for 3 health layers** — it's a marathon, not a burst check; manage resources across all three layers
4. **Einherjar runs need a Dried Flower Knot** — farm the knot, then decide which boss you're targeting
5. **Target bosses by Holy weapon** — every build has one boss whose drop completes it
