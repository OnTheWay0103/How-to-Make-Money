## Beyond the Basics

The extraction guide covers the core loop. This guide covers **advanced tactics** for players who already know the basics and want to improve their survival rate.

### Core Mindset Shifts

| Beginner Mindset | Advanced Mindset |
|-----------------|------------------|
| "Kill everything I see" | "Engage only when necessary" |
| "Extract as fast as possible" | "Extract when conditions are optimal" |
| "Run toward combat for loot" | "Run toward extraction with good loot" |
| "Soul of Return = safe extract" | "Soul of Return = signal to third parties" |

## Pre-Raid Preparation

### Loadout Tiers

Always classify your loadout before dropping:

| Tier | Gear Value | Risk Strategy |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Scavenger | Green/Common | Fight only if cornered. Prioritize loot. |
| Standard | Blue/Rare | Pick fights carefully. Extract with 1-2 good items. |
| High Value | Purple/Epic+ | Avoid combat. Extract at first opportunity. |
| Revenge | Whatever you want | You already lost the expensive set. Play aggressive. |

### Inventory Management Before Drop

- Leave 2-3 empty slots for Gyldenblood
- Carry 1-2 healing items max (more space = more loot)
- Bring 1 mobility item (smoke bomb, dash crystal, or speed potion)
- **Never** bring your best gear on the first raid of the session

## Map Awareness and Route Planning

### The Three-Zone Model

Every map can be divided into three zones:

| Zone | Description | Strategy |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Outer Ring | Low-tier loot, few player encounters | Farm here first. Build confidence. |
| Middle Ring | Medium loot, Returner Woodling area | Approach carefully. Listen for combat. |
| Core | High loot, PvP hotspot | Only enter if you are hunting players. |

### Route Planning Principles

1. **Enter from the edge**, work inward
2. **Never run in a straight line** toward the Woodling — you will walk into an ambush
3. **Use high ground** to scout before committing to a path
4. **Mark extraction points** as soon as you drop — plan your exit route before you need it
5. **Have two extraction routes** — one primary, one fallback

### Audio Cues to Track

| Sound | Meaning | Response |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Woodling chime/groan | Returner Woodling is nearby | Approach slowly, scout first |
| Combat sounds + no Woodling | Players fighting each other | Third party opportunity OR avoid |
| Combat sounds + Woodling | Players fighting the Woodling | Wait for them to kill it, then ambush |
| Extraction channel sound | Someone is extracting | Push to contest, or note their location and avoid |
| Mist alarm | 3 minutes until closure | Begin extraction immediately |

## Fighting the Returner Woodling

### Solo Strategy

| Phase | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Approach | Scout from range for 10 seconds. Look for other players. |
| Engagement | Start the fight near cover. Keep the Woodling between you and the most likely approach path. |
| Adds phase (50% HP) | Kite the adds away before finishing. Do not stand still. |
| Loot | Grab Soul of Return immediately. Do not linger. |
| Exit | Move to extraction immediately — do not farm more. |

### Squad Strategy

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shot Caller | Decides engage/disengage, calls extraction timing |
| DPS | Focuses Woodling, clears adds |
| Scout | Watches perimeter, calls out approaching players |
| Support | Heals, provides utility (smoke, CC) |

**Squad formation**: One person on Woodling, one watching the two most likely approach routes. Swap if combat starts.

## Third Party Management

Third parties (players who attack while you are fighting) are the #1 cause of death in Mistfall Hunter.

### How to Avoid Third Parties

- **Kill the Woodling fast** — the longer the fight, the more players arrive
- **Use silent weapons** if possible — bows and daggers make less noise than greatswords and rifles
- **Finish early** — the first 5 minutes of a raid have the fewest players at the Woodling
- **If you hear fighting at the Woodling**, wait. Let them kill it. Kill them.

### How to Handle Being Third-Partied

1. **Disengage immediately** — break line of sight
2. **Smoke bomb** if you have one — creates confusion
3. **Reset** — heal, reposition, re-evaluate
4. **Let the third party fight the original enemy** — they might kill each other
5. **Only re-engage if you have clear advantage**

## Extraction Point Tactics

### Pre-Channel Scouting

Before starting extraction, check:

- [ ] Is the extraction point clear? (No players visible)
- [ ] Is there cover nearby? (Can you break line of sight?)
- [ ] Are there multiple approach routes? (Can you see all of them?)
- [ ] Is the mist closing? (How much time do you have?)

### Channeling Positioning

| Situation | Positioning |
|-----------|-------------|
| Solo, open area | Behind cover, facing the most likely approach angle |
| Solo, near cover | Inside cover, with escape route behind you |
| Squad | One channels, others fan out in a triangle covering approaches |
| Under attack | Cancel channel, reposition, re-channel after clearing |

### Extraction Denial

If you want to prevent someone else from extracting:
- Stand at range and shoot the channeling player (damage does not cancel, but killing them does)
- Place traps on approach routes to their extraction point
- Simply wait — they must channel for 30 seconds, you have plenty of time to push

## Post-Raid Analysis

After every extraction (or death), ask yourself:

| Question | If Yes | If No |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Did I scout before engaging? | Good habit | #1 cause of death |
| Did I have an exit plan? | Good habit | Always plan your exit |
| Did I overstay? | Extract earlier next time | Keep good habits |
| Did I hear audio cues I ignored? | Pay attention next time | Good awareness |
| Did I bring the right loadout? | Good judgment | Adjust next run |
