# Patch Notes — Update History and Balance Changes

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

This page tracks confirmed updates for **Mistfall Hunter**. We only include information that can be verified against official developer announcements or consistent community reports.

**Note:** The game launched on July 30, 2026 on Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S (day-one on Xbox Game Pass) and is actively being updated. Some mechanics and values below may change. Check the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com) and the official Steam Community hub for the latest official information.

---

## Confirmed Updates

### Version 1.0 — Launch (July 30, 2026)

The launch version introduced the core game systems:

- **Extraction Loop:** Deploy, loot, fight, extract before the mist closes
- **PvPvE Zones:** Competing against other players and the Corroded in every raid
- **Six Classes:** Mercenary, Sorcerer, Blackarrow, Shadowstrix, Seer, Withered Knight
- **Matchmaking:** Solo and Trio queues only (no Duo queue); the second map is trio-only
- **Season 1 (Season of the Withering):** Live at launch, with seasonal affixes and a Season Pass
- **Camp System:** Persistent camp upgrades (Goddess Statue, Gyldenblod Pool, Warehouse, Shop, Tavern)
- **Crossplay:** Full crossplay between Steam, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S
- **Launch regions:** US / DE / BR / SG / CN servers (see the [Server Regions & Ping Guide](/guides/server-regions-ping-guide))

### Day-One Hotfix (late July 2026)

Within hours of launch, the developers addressed several community-reported launch issues. Based on Steam Community discussions and developer announcements, the hotfix covered:

- Balance adjustments for the Withered Knight (community reports describe buffs, with specific values unconfirmed)
- A fix for the Soul of Return being consumed incorrectly when entering/exiting the spirit tree
- Console reconnect improvements for players disconnecting mid-raid
- Item splitting and various crash fixes

> **Note:** Specific numerical balance changes (damage values, timers, etc.) have not been independently verified. If you have confirmed patch note details from official sources, please [contact us](/contact).

### Known Issues (community-reported, as of early August 2026)

- **No dedicated EU or OCE server** — EU players report higher ping; acknowledged by the developers, no ETA confirmed
- **Rubber banding / desync** in peak hours on all regions — server-side, acknowledged
- **PC reconnect after disconnect** still reported as inconsistent (console was addressed in the hotfix)
- **Second map locked for solo players** (trio-only queue) — widely discussed design decision, no change confirmed
