# Season Meta — Best Builds, Affixes, and Priorities This Season

Every Mistfall Hunter season reshuffles the meta. Season of the Withering brought two temporary mechanics — **Withering affixes** on gear and the **Miststorm events** — that have already changed what S-tier means. This report covers the current meta, what is rising, what is falling, and how to spend your remaining season time.

## The Withering Affix System

Withering gear drops only during the season and carries a purple-crimson glow. Its unique affixes rotate out when the season ends, so anything you collect now is time-limited:

| Withering Affix | Effect | Value |
|-----------------|--------|-------|
| Withering Strike | +12% damage to enemies below 30% HP | High — finish-off power |
| Rotting Aura | Enemies near you lose 5% of their max HP per second | Medium — zone control |
| Grasping Vines | On hit, 15% chance to root the target for 1.5s | High — PvP lockdown |
| Dying Light | +8% all damage while under 50% HP | Situational — glass cannon builds |
| Withering Heart | +10% max HP, but -5% healing received | Low — self-harm trade |

**Note**: Withering affixes do NOT persist after the season. Do not build your permanent stash around them — the next season's "Void" affixes replace them.

## Current Tier List (Season of the Withering)

| Tier | Class | Season Verdict |
|------|-------|----------------|
| S | Sorcerer | Elemental cycling + Withering Strike deletes low-HP targets before they extract |
| S | Shadowstrix | Grasping Vines on daggers makes ambush combos nearly unescapable |
| A | Mercenary | Unchanged and consistent; block DR still wins extraction wars |
| B | Withered Knight | Sigil detonation is strong in squads, fragile without support |
| C | Blackarrow | PvE king, but Withering metas favor close-range burst |
| D | Seer | Still team-dependent; revived slightly by Rotting Aura procs on shields |

**Biggest meta shift**: Grasping Vines turned dual-wield classes (Shadowstrix) from hit-and-run into hard lockdown. If you fight one in a narrow corridor, the root is a death sentence — carry a dash tonic and never engage them in chokepoints.

## Priority Checklist — Final Weeks of the Season

- [ ] **Farm Withering gear now** — affix drops are boosted in the last month; sell the duplicates before prices crash
- [ ] **Complete the Miststorm events** — each one drops Withering keys; see the Events Calendar for the rotation schedule
- [ ] **Spend Season Pass tokens** — unused tokens convert at a poor 1:50 rate after the season
- [ ] **Do not chase "Extract 50,000 Gyldenblood"** — the season reward is a title; the time is worth more
- [ ] **Bank resources for next season** — camp upgrades persist; sink gold into Camp Level 9+ before the reset
- [ ] **Prepare for Void season** — data miners report the Sunken Citadel map and a Runemaster class for next patch; start saving gear score boosts

| PvP bounty rotations | Syndicate marks + player loot | 30 min | Variable, high ceiling |

## End-of-Season Warning

When the season ends at the ~3-month mark:

- Withering affixes become inactive — your seasonal gear becomes normal purple
- Season Pass tiers lock permanently; Premium pass owners keep exclusive cosmetics
- Leaderboards reset — final rank rewards are granted on the last day
- Faction Paragon status resets to Revered, but the seasonal cosmetic stays

Plan your last month around the checklist above and you will enter the Void season with a stocked warehouse, upgraded camp, and a head start on the new meta.
