## Solo Build Philosophy

Playing Mistfall Hunter solo means you are always outnumbered. Solo builds prioritize **survivability, disengage potential, and burst damage** over sustained DPS or team utility. Your goal is to kill targets quickly or escape efficiently — there is no one to peel for you.

### Core Principles for Solo Builds

| Principle | Why It Matters |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Self-sustain** | No healer means you must heal yourself |
| **Disengage tools** | When outnumbered, you need an escape button |
| **Burst damage** | Kill one target fast to even the odds or scare off remaining enemies |
| **Stealth options** | Avoid fights you cannot win; pick your engagements |
| **Mobility** | Faster rotation = harder to pin down |

## Best Solo Builds by Class

### 1. Shadowstrix — S-Tier Solo

Shadowstrix is the strongest solo class thanks to stealth that lets you control engagements entirely.

#### Recommended Solo Build

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Dual Daggers (Rare+) | Highest burst damage in the game |
| **Weapon Affix 1** | Crit Damage +20% | Maximum burst potential |
| **Weapon Affix 2** | Lifesteal on Crit | Sustain during extended fights |
| **Armor Affix 1** | Move Speed +10% | Faster rotations, harder to hit |
| **Armor Affix 2** | Stealth Detection Range +25% | See enemies before they see you |
| **Ability Slot 1** | Shadow Strike (gap closer) | Initiate or escape over walls |
| **Ability Slot 2** | Smoke Bomb (AoE blind) | Disengage in open areas |
| **Ability Slot 3** | Vanish (stealth reset) | Emergency escape |

#### Playstyle

1. **Scout first** — use stealth to locate enemies before they see you
2. **Pick isolated targets** — wait for a squad to split up, then assassinate the straggler
3. **One combo, one kill** — open with Shadow Strike → backstab → Dagger Flurry → Vanish out
4. **Do not commit to extended fights** — if the first burst does not kill, disengage

#### Solo Build Priority for Shadowstrix

| Priority | Stat | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | Crit Rate | Reliable burst is your win condition |
| 2 | Crit Damage | Multiplies your burst potential |
| 3 | Move Speed | Positions you for the perfect ambush |
| 4 | Lifesteal | Keeps you healthy between fights |

### 2. Mercenary — A-Tier Solo

Mercenary is the most forgiving solo class due to its shield block and high survivability.

#### Recommended Solo Build

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Sword & Shield (Rare+) | Block is your safety net |
| **Weapon Affix 1** | Block Stamina Reduction | Block more attacks before exhaustion |
| **Weapon Affix 2** | Counter Damage +30% | Punish enemies who hit your shield |
| **Armor Affix 1** | HP +50 | More survivability buffer |
| **Armor Affix 2** | Healing Received +15% | Better potion efficiency |
| **Ability Slot 1** | Shield Charge (stun + gap close) | Initiation or escape |
| **Ability Slot 2** | War Cry (AoE taunt + shield) | Crowd control when outnumbered |
| **Ability Slot 3** | Retreat (backstep + invuln frames) | Emergency dodge |

#### Playstyle

1. **Block and counter** — hold block, wait for attacks, counter during recovery
2. **Use terrain to limit angles** — fight with your back to a wall so you cannot be flanked
3. **Bait overcommitment** — play weak, let them push, then Shield Charge + punish
4. **Know when to hold** — Mercenary wins attrition fights; if you have potions, you have the advantage

### 3. Sorcerer — A-Tier Solo

Sorcerer excels at kiting and can handle 1v2 scenarios better than most classes, but struggles in close quarters.

#### Recommended Solo Build

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Elemental Focus (Fire or Ice) | Burst damage + utility |
| **Weapon Affix 1** | Spell Cast Speed +15% | Faster burst combo |
| **Weapon Affix 2** | Mana Regen +20% | Sustain for multiple fights |
| **Armor Affix 1** | Damage Reduction +10% | Squishy class needs defense |
| **Armor Affix 2** | Movement Speed While Casting | Kite more effectively |
| **Ability Slot 1** | Glacial Blink (teleport) | Best escape in the game |
| **Ability Slot 2** | Firestorm (AoE damage) | Zone control, area denial |
| **Ability Slot 3** | Ice Barrier (shield + slow attackers) | Emergency defense |

#### Playstyle

1. **Keep distance** — Sorcerer's best defense is range
2. **Use fire + ice combos** — Firestorm to damage, Ice Blink to escape
3. **Third-party** — let two squads fight, then clean up with AoE
4. **Never fight indoors** — Sorcerer needs space to kite

### 4. Withered Knight — B-Tier Solo

Withered Knight is strong in 1v1 but vulnerable when third-partied due to low mobility.

#### Recommended Solo Build

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Greatsword (Rare+) | High single-target damage |
| **Weapon Affix 1** | Life Steal on Hit | Sustain in extended fights |
| **Weapon Affix 2** | Attack Speed +10% | Faster combos |
| **Armor Affix 1** | Armor +20% | Tankiness for trading |
| **Armor Affix 2** | Stun Resistance | Do not get locked down |
| **Ability Slot 1** | Soul Cleave (life steal + damage) | Sustain + damage |
| **Ability Slot 2** | Dark Charge (gap closer) | Initiation |
| **Ability Slot 3** | Necrotic Shield (damage return) | Punish attackers |

#### Playstyle
- Play aggressively in 1v1 situations — you win most fair fights
- Avoid open areas where you can get kited or third-partied
- Use terrain to limit enemy approach angles

---

### 5. Blackarrow — C-Tier Solo

Blackarrow struggles in solo play due to poor close-quarters combat. Only viable on open maps with long sight lines.

| Weapon | Longbow |
|--------|---------|
| Key Affix | Range +15%, Headshot Damage +25% |
| Core Ability | Arrow Rain (AoE), Eagle Sight (zoom), Trap (proximity alarm) |
| Strategy | Stay at maximum range, use traps to cover your flanks, extract early |

### 6. Seer — D-Tier Solo

Do not play Seer solo. Seer has no escape, no burst damage, and relies entirely on teammates for protection. If you must play Seer solo, build for maximum survivability and accept that you are playing on hard mode.

## Universal Solo Build Tips

### Gear Priority

| Priority | Affix | Works On |
|----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Move Speed | All classes |
| 2 | Self-healing (Lifesteal / Life on Kill) | All classes |
| 3 | Stamina/Mana Regen | All classes |
| 4 | Stealth Detection | Soft counters Shadowstrix ambushes |
| 5 | Damage Reduction | Squishy classes (Sorcerer, Blackarrow) |

### Camp Upgrade Priority for Solo Players

| Upgrade | Cost | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Workshop (self-repair)** | 500 Gyldenblood | Repair gear without returning to base |
| **Medic Station (self-heal)** | 750 Gyldenblood | Heal between extractions without potions |
| **Scout Tower (map reveal)** | 1,000 Gyldenblood | See player movements on minimap |
| **Stash Expansion** | 1,500 Gyldenblood | More storage means fewer forced extractions |

### Solo Loadout Templates

| Budget | Weapon | Armor | Consumables | Strategy |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|----------|
| < 200g | Green weapon, no affixes | Green armor | 2 healing potions | Loot edges, extract early |
| 200-500g | Blue weapon, 1 affix | Green armor | 3 healing, 1 stamina potion | Mid-map, pick fights carefully |
| 500+ g | Blue+ weapon, 2 affixes | Blue armor | Full consumables (heal, stamina, antidote) | Full map, contest POIs |
