# Mistfall Hunter Season 1 Reset — What Carries Over to Season 2 and What Doesn't

> **Last verified: October 23, 2026.** Based on the official DevNote #10, published October 21, 2026.

Mistfall Hunter is not wiping your account. It is also not letting you keep your stash. The Season 1 reset is a **partial reset with progressive returns**: characters and cosmetics survive, seasonal assets are converted into a currency, and six camp buildings go back to level one.

That middle category is where most players get confused, because "converted" can sound like "kept." It is not the same thing. This guide separates the three outcomes — retained, converted, reset — using the developer's own wording.

---

## 1. The Short Answer

| Outcome | What happens |
|---------|--------------|
| **Retained** | Characters (appearance, level, skills, talent points), outfits and cosmetics, Fate Coins, Soul Coins, Moonlight Nectar, Star Silver, Starsilver Spindles, unlocked core features, friends, Shop, Auction House, Collection Pedestal, Watchtower level, story and contract-reward progress |
| **Converted** | Gyldenblod, Echoes of Fate, the three Soul Cocoon types, inventory and warehouse items, Combat Bags, Soulcatch Nets → Contribution → Gyldenblod Leaves |
| **Reset** | Six camp buildings, Pip's Pouch and Keychain activation timers, unused Pip's Rare Pouch Vouchers, Path of Glory tiers compressed |

If you only remember one line: **your character is safe, your stuff is not.**

---

## 2. What Is Actually Retained

The DevNote lists the retained category first, and it is broader than the community doom-posting suggests.

Your **created characters** stay, including appearance, level, skills, and talent points. You are not re-levelling from scratch. **Outfits and cosmetic items** stay. So do **Fate Coins, Soul Coins, Moonlight Nectar, Star Silver, and Starsilver Spindles** — the hard currencies and upgrade materials named in the post are explicitly on the keep list.

Account-level systems also survive:

- Unlocked core features and your friends list
- The Shop and Auction House
- The **Collection Pedestal**, including placed collectibles and its upgrade progress
- **Watchtower level**
- Story task progress, contract reward unlock progress, and Pip's Gift progress

Two item-specific rules are easy to miss. **Contract Sigils convert one-to-one into Faded Contract Sigils**, and the **Slumbering Contract is permanently retained as long as it still has unredeemed rewards**. You can spend either type of sigil on those rewards at any time. If you were worried about losing an unfinished contract track, the official answer is that you do not.

---

## 3. What "Converted" Means — and Why It Matters

This is the part that decides whether you should keep farming this week.

The following are pooled and converted into **Contribution** at "designated ratios":

- Gyldenblod
- Echoes of Fate
- All three types of Soul Cocoons
- Items in your Inventory and Warehouse
- Combat Bags
- Soulcatch Nets

Contribution is then converted into **Gyldenblod Leaves**, the currency for the **Fateweaver Shop**. The exchange uses a **tiered system where the rate progressively diminishes across higher brackets**. In plain English: the more you have, the more Leaves you get, but the marginal rate drops at the top. The developer's stated goal is to compress the gap between a 300-hour hoarder and a fresh player without erasing the hoarder's effort.

What this means practically:

- A warehouse full of spare gear is **not** a retirement fund. It becomes Contribution, then Leaves, at a ratio the developer controls.
- **Unused Pip's Rare Pouch Vouchers stored in the Warehouse are reset** under the same rules, even though the Warehouse building upgrade itself is retained.
- **Pip's Pouch and Keychain activation timers reset.** If a timer is mid-run, do not expect it to carry over.

The conversion ratios themselves are not published in the DevNote. Anyone quoting exact "X Gyldenblod = Y Leaves" numbers should be treated as estimating. **[Unconfirmed]**

---

## 4. The Camp Reset Is Real

Six buildings have their **levels reset**:

1. Goddess Statue
2. Gylden Pool
3. Tavern
4. Workshop
5. Scavenger Squad
6. Soulcatch Tree

The rationale in the DevNote is economic: these buildings drive seasonal resource production and material acquisition, and keeping their levels would skip the early progression curve and distort the market at season start. You receive Contribution based on their current levels, so the investment is not simply deleted.

There is a meaningful mercy clause. If you already consumed rare materials such as **Stardust Blooms, Faded Divine Tree Hearts, Messenger Bug Jars, or Celestigold** to upgrade these buildings, you **will not need to consume them again** when re-upgrading the corresponding building in the new season. The grind is reduced even though the level is gone.

The **Soulcatch Tree is the exception**. In Season 2 it becomes "a completely new form" and needs brand-new materials to upgrade. Soulcatch Nets and their core function remain unchanged.

Also note the split: **Warehouse building upgrades are retained, but the items inside the Warehouse are converted.** The building stays; the contents do not.

---

## 5. Path of Glory and Ranked Standing

Your Season 2 starting tier is **inherited from your final Season 1 standing**, with "moderate tier compression" at the top. Lower tiers generally keep their progress; higher tiers are pulled back toward a shared baseline.

This is neither a full rank wipe nor a full rank carry. If you finished high, expect to start lower than you finished but higher than a new player. If you finished low, your progress is largely preserved.

---

## 6. What the Fateweaver Shop Sells

Gyldenblod Leaves are spendable once Season 2 is live. The DevNote names these redemption categories:

- **Pip's Rare Pouches**
- **Unbound Holy Collectibles (Selection Chest)**
- **Holy Soulcatch Nets**
- **Combat Bags**
- Two Season 1 commemorative cosmetics: the Gyldjar **"Mistchaser's Messenger Bug Jar"** and the medal **"Proof of the Soul Hunt"**

The first four are progression-relevant. The two cosmetics are the "you were there" items, and they are the only entries on the list that are purely commemorative.

The in-game reset page was scheduled to appear on the season page at **October 22, 2026, 10:00 AM**. The DevNote does not state a Season 2 start date. One German outlet reported November 11, 2026, but no official post in the Steam news feed confirms that date, so treat it as **[Unconfirmed]**.

## 7. What to Do Before the Reset

These are editorial recommendations based on the rules above, not official instructions.

1. **Redeem Slumbering Contract rewards you actually want.** The contract is retained while rewards remain, but there is no reason to leave a claimable reward sitting.
2. **Stop hoarding gear for "next season."** Inventory and Warehouse items become Contribution. If an item has use now, use it; if it does not, its conversion value is the only value it has.
3. **Do not buy stash space as an investment.** Warehouse upgrades are retained, but the contents are settled. Space is convenience, not an asset.
4. **Check your Pip's Pouch and Keychain timers.** They reset; start long timers only if they will finish before the season rolls.
5. **Spend down currencies that convert.** Gyldenblod and Echoes of Fate are on the conversion list. Holding them does not preserve them in their current form.
6. **Screenshot your final Path of Glory rank.** The compression formula is not published, and the new season is when you will want the record.

One correction to our older coverage: an earlier version of the [Season Guide](/guides/season-guide) stated that camp upgrades and warehouse stock persist through a season. That was wrong for the Season 1 reset. The developer's DevNote explicitly resets six building levels and converts warehouse contents. The Season Guide has been corrected.

## 8. FAQ

**Do I lose my character level?** No. Characters, level, skills, and talent points are retained.

**Do I lose cosmetics?** No. Outfits and cosmetic items are retained.

**Is my stash wiped?** Not wiped — converted. Items in Inventory and Warehouse become Contribution, then Gyldenblod Leaves.

**Do I keep my camp?** The Warehouse upgrade stays. Six production buildings reset to level one, with Contribution compensation and a rare-material rebate on re-upgrade.

**Is there a full wipe?** No. The developer calls it a "partial reset with progressive returns."

**When does Season 2 start?** No official date appears in the October 21 DevNote. November 11 has been reported by one outlet but is **[Unconfirmed]**.
