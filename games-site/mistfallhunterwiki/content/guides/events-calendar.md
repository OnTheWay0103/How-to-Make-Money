## Event System Overview

Mistfall Hunter runs regular events — some seasonal, some weekly, some tied to community milestones. Missing an event means missing exclusive rewards.

> **Note:** The game launched on July 30, 2026, so any event can only have started at or after launch. Dates, rewards, and upcoming-event details below are community-reported or speculative — nothing in the tables is an official developer announcement unless noted.

## Recurring Weekly Events

| Event | Schedule | Rewards |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Double Extraction XP** | Every Saturday | 2× extraction rank XP |
| **Boss Rush** | Every Sunday | Extra boss drops, leaderboard rewards |
| **Material Surge** | Random weekday | Double material drops in specific biomes |
| **PvP Arena Weekends** | Fri-Sun | Arena tokens, exclusive PvP cosmetics |

These weekly patterns are community-observed; specific reward values may vary.

## Seasonal Events

### Summer 2026 — "Scorched Earth" (Live)
- **Duration**: Started around launch (late July); expected to run through mid-August (exact dates community-reported)
- **Content**: Fire-themed bosses, heat wave survival mode
- **Exclusive Rewards**: Inferno weapon skins, Phoenix pet, Scorched title (community-reported)

### Fall 2026 — "Harvest of Shadows" (Expected, unconfirmed)
- **Speculated**: September — October
- **Expected rewards**: Halloween-themed cosmetics, special difficulty modes (community speculation only — no official announcement)

### Winter 2026 — "Frozen Extraction" (Expected, unconfirmed)
- **Speculated**: December — January
- **Expected rewards**: Ice weapon skins, holiday cosmetics, double loot week (community speculation only — no official announcement)

## Special Limited Events

- **100K Players**: Community reports describe a celebration reward for all players; the exact reward and amount have not been officially confirmed

## How to Prepare

1. **Save premium currency** — Seasonal cosmetics are limited and may not return
2. **Stockpile materials** — Event crafting recipes often use extra materials
3. **Join the Discord** — Event announcements drop there first
4. **Clear your schedule** — Major events run 2-4 weeks, don't miss the end
