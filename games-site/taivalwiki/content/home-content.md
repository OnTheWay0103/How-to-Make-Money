## What is Taival?

Taival ("journey" in Finnish) is a 1-4 player cooperative adventure RPG set in a hand-crafted voxel-style open world. Developed by the Finnish indie studio Bonobo Software and published by Level Up Gaming, it launched in Steam Early Access on July 15, 2026 for $19.99.

The game's defining feature is your **Henki** — a shapeshifting companion with no fixed form that can absorb creature essences to transform into combat beasts, swift mounts, stealth predators, gliders, and more. You and your Henki grow in parallel through dual progression systems, unlocking new abilities and forms as you explore the world.

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Henki Companion** — Shapeshifting ally with combat, mount, and utility forms. Rare Golden and Purple Clone variants
- **4 Biomes** — Highlands, forests, mountains, and ancient ruins in a seamless open world
- **4-Player Co-op** — Online, local split-screen, or mixed mode with group story voting
- **Crafting & Professions** — Gather materials, unlock recipes, and craft gear at stations
- **Skill Trees** — Dual progression: character skills + Henki form mastery
- **Henki Dungeons** — Solo companion challenges with unique puzzles and exclusive rewards
- **Player Housing** — Place and upgrade homes across the world with storage and crafting stations
