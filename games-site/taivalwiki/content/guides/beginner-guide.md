# Getting Started in Taival: Complete Beginner's Guide

> Early Access Version / Updated: July 27, 2026

Welcome to Taival, a handcrafted co-op adventure RPG developed by Bonobo Software and published by Level Up Gaming. Taival means "journey" in Finnish, and that is exactly what awaits — a sprawling open world filled with mysterious creatures, ancient ruins, and a shapeshifting companion called a Henki at the center of everything.

This beginner's guide covers the core loop, your first steps, the Henki companion system, early game priorities, and practical tips to help you get started on your adventure. Whether you are playing solo or with friends, this guide will have you exploring the four biomes with confidence.

---

## First Steps: What to Do When You Start

When you launch Taival for the first time, you will create your character and be dropped into the world with only your Henki companion. Here is what to prioritize in your first hour:

### 1. Complete the Tutorial Area
The opening area introduces the basic mechanics — movement, interaction, and your first Henki form. Follow the initial quest marker to meet the NPC who explains the Heartstone and the Henki's lost heritage. Pay close attention to the taming tutorial, as it is the most important system in the game.

### 2. Tame Your First Creature
Your Henki starts with only its base form (a support-oriented shape with healing abilities). Your first task is to find a wild creature and begin the taming process. Look for small, non-aggressive animals near the starting area — they are easier to study and tame. Check the creature's entry in your journal to learn what food it likes, then offer that food to begin the taming sequence.

### 3. Gather Basic Resources
The early biomes are rich with wood, stone, fibers, and berries. Collect everything you see. These materials fuel your first crafting attempts and are used for campfires, basic tools, and healing items. Wood and stone are especially critical for your first crafting station.

### 4. Unlock Your First Mount Form
One of the biggest quality-of-life improvements early on is unlocking a mount form. Look for larger, non-hostile creatures in the highlands that have the mountable trait. Taming a mount form drastically cuts down travel time across the seamless open world.

## Understanding the Henki Companion

The Henki is the heart of Taival. It is an ancient shapeshifting creature that has lost its original form. As you explore the world, your Henki absorbs the essence of creatures you encounter and shifts into their forms. Each form grants unique abilities for combat, traversal, and utility.

**Key things to know about your Henki:**

- **Taming is the unlocking mechanic.** To unlock a new form, you must study a creature in the wild, learn its preferred food, and offer that food while your Henki observes.
- **Forms are swappable at any time.** You can switch between unlocked forms from the companion menu, allowing you to adapt to different situations on the fly.
- **The base form is a healer.** Your starting Henki form can heal you and even revive you if you fall in combat. Do not underestimate it.
- **Traits make every form unique.** Each form has a unique Henki Trait — such as Leech (heals you when the companion deals damage), Jump Pad (launches you to higher ground), or Pack Mule (carries extra loot).

## Basic Controls

Taival supports keyboard and mouse, controller, and split-screen input. Here are the essential controls:

| Action | Keyboard & Mouse | Controller |
|--------|-----------------|------------|
| Move | WASD | Left Stick |
| Interact | E | A (Xbox) / X (PlayStation) |
| Jump | Space | A / Cross |
| Attack | Left Click | Right Trigger |
| Block / Dodge | Right Click | Left Trigger |
| Swap Henki Form | Tab | D-Pad Up |
| Open Inventory | I | Menu Button |
| Open Map | M | View Button |
| Henki Ability | Q | Right Bumper |
| Sprint | Shift | Left Stick Click |

Many controls are remappable in the settings menu. If you are playing on controller, the game supports full rebinding.

## First Quests and Where to Go

The main questline follows the restoration of the Heartstone — the source of the Henki's power. Your starting quest giver will point you toward the first Heartstone fragment. The game is open world from the start, so you are free to explore, but following the main quest early on will naturally guide you through the four biomes:

1. **The Highlands** — Your starting area. Rolling green hills, gentle creatures, low-level threats. Ideal for learning the basics.
2. **The Forests** — Dense woodland with more aggressive creatures and hidden ruins. Expect tougher enemies and better resources.
3. **The Mountains** — Rocky, vertical terrain. Mount forms and gliding become essential here. Rich in rare minerals.
4. **Ruins & Settlements** — Ancient structures and NPC villages. Quest hubs, traders, and crafting stations are concentrated here.

Side quests are plentiful. Early on, accept quests from NPCs in settlements — many reward useful recipes, Henki form locations, or skill points. Even cozy tasks like helping a farmer with a rodent thief can yield valuable early-game resources.

## Early Game Priorities

Your first five hours should focus on building a solid foundation:

| Priority | Goal | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | Tame 3-4 creature forms | Variety is key. Having a combat form, mount, and utility form ready gives you options for any situation. |
| 2 | Build a basic crafting station | Unlocks healing items, tools, and equipment upgrades. Place it at your camp or settlement. |
| 3 | Explore the Highlands thoroughly | Learn the terrain, find early quests, and discover creature spawns before moving to harder biomes. |
| 4 | Level your first combat skill | Pick one weapon type and invest early skill points to make fights manageable. |
| 5 | Save materials for player housing | Once you unlock housing, you will need a stockpile of wood, stone, and fibers to build a functional base. |

## Tips for New Players

### Stick to One Biome at First
The world is seamelessly connected, but enemy difficulty scales with distance from the starting area. Do not rush into the mountains before you have a solid combat form and a few skill upgrades.

### Feed Your Henki
Your Henki companion has its own needs. Keep an eye on its energy or mood (indicated in the companion UI). A well-fed Henki performs better in combat and tames creatures faster.

### Use the Group Dialogue System
If you are playing co-op, the Group Quest Dialogue system lets every player suggest story decisions using their cursor. Discuss major choices as a team — some decisions affect which quests are available later.

### Invest in Storage Early
Player housing is not just cosmetic. Build storage chests as soon as you can. Your inventory space is limited, and materials pile up quickly. A hut with a few chests will save you many trips.

### Talk to Every NPC
Settlement NPCs often share rumors about rare creature locations, hidden treasures, or Heartstone fragment clues. The game rewards curiosity.

### Do Not Fear Experimenting with Forms
You lose nothing by trying a new form in combat. If a form does not fit your playstyle, simply swap back. The taming system encourages exploration and experimentation.

Before diving in, make sure your system can run Taival:

- Graphics: GTX 1660 Ti or equivalent
- DirectX: Version 11

- RAM: 16 GB

## Parting Advice

Taival is a game about discovery. The Henki system rewards players who explore every corner of the world and experiment with different forms. Take your time in the Highlands, build a solid foundation of forms and skills, and let the world guide you. The Heartstone fragments are not going anywhere.
