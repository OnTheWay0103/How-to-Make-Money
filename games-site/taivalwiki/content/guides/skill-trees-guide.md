# Taival — Skill Trees & Progression Guide

> Early Access Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Taival features a dual progression system — your character levels independently from your Henki companion. Every skill point, essence absorption, and profession rank contributes to unlocking new abilities, traversal options, and combat efficiency. Understanding how both tracks work together is the difference between struggling through biomes and exploring them with confidence.

This guide breaks down every progression system and tells you what to prioritize in the early game.

**Accuracy note (August 2026):** the dual progression system — a character track (abilities, combat skills, professions like fishing) plus a Henki track (forms, mount abilities) — is confirmed by official materials, and in co-op each player has their own skill tree. The specific skill names, effects, percentages, mastery levels, and respec costs below are community speculation from Early Access and are unconfirmed (TBD) — treat them as a framework for verification, not official documentation.

---

## Progression Overview

| Track | What It Unlocks | How It Levels |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Character Skills** | Combat abilities, passive boosts, profession efficiency | Spending skill points earned from quests, exploration, and leveling |
| **Henki Forms** | New creature forms, mount abilities, combat traits | Absorbing creature essences and completing Henki-specific challenges |
| **Professions** | Crafting recipes, resource bonuses, gathering upgrades | Performing profession actions (fishing, mining, woodcutting, etc.) |

## Character Skill Trees

Your character has three primary skill trees. You earn skill points by completing quests, discovering new locations, and gaining character levels.

### How to Earn Skill Points

| Method | Skill Points Earned | Frequency |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| Completing Main Quests | 1–3 per quest | One-time per quest |
| Completing Side Quests | 1 per quest | One-time per quest |
| Discovering New Locations | 1 per 3 discoveries | Repeatable (each biome) |
| Character Level Up | 2 per level | Every level |
| Henki Dungeon Completion | 1 per dungeon | One-time per dungeon |
| Lore Page Collections | 1 per collection milestone | Per set completed |

### Skill Tree 1: Combat

The Combat tree focuses on weapon damage, survivability, and combat efficiency.

| Skill | Tier | Effect | Priority |
|-------|------|--------|----------|
| **Weapon Mastery** | 1 | +10% damage with all weapons | High — immediate damage boost |
| **Sturdy Stance** | 1 | -15% damage taken while stationary | Medium — situational but useful |
| **Critical Eye** | 2 | +5% critical hit chance | High — scales well into late game |
| **Second Wind** | 2 | Automatically heal 20% HP when below 15% HP once per combat | High — life-saving passive |
| **Adrenaline Rush** | 3 | +25% attack speed for 10 seconds after killing an enemy | Medium — excellent for crowd fights |
| **Iron Hide** | 3 | +10% damage resistance | Low — flat reduction has diminishing returns |
| **Executioner** | 4 | +50% damage against enemies below 30% HP | High — boss fights and elite enemies |
| **Combo Strike** | 4 | Consecutive hits deal +3% damage each (stacks to 30%) | Medium — requires sustained combat |

**Early Priority:** Weapon Mastery → Critical Eye → Second Wind → Executioner

## Henki Progression Path

Your Henki companion grows by absorbing creature essences. Each creature type you defeat yields essence that unlocks or upgrades forms.

### How Henki Forms Work

| Stage | What Happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **Essence Absorption** | Defeat a creature with a unique essence type to absorb it |
| **Form Unlock** | After absorbing enough essence, the corresponding Henki form becomes available |
| **Form Mastery** | Using a form repeatedly unlocks traits and ability upgrades for that form |
| **Evolution** | Some forms evolve after meeting specific conditions (essence threshold + quest completion) |

### Form Categories

| Category | Examples | Primary Use |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Combat Forms** | Guardian, Shadow Stalker, Boulder Beast | Direct combat, tanking, stealth attacks |
| **Traversal Forms** | Traveler, Cliff Strider, Swiftwing, Water Strider | Movement, climbing, gliding, swimming |
| **Utility Forms** | Burrower, Glimmer Moth, Howler | Resource detection, secret finding, creature scaring |

### Henki Form Mastery Levels

| Mastery Level | Requirement | Bonus |
|---------------|-------------|-------|
| 1 — Unlocked | Absorb initial essence | Basic form abilities available |
| 2 — Practiced | Use form for 10 minutes total | +10% ability effectiveness |
| 3 — Adept | Use form for 30 minutes total + complete 1 form challenge | +20% ability effectiveness + unlock secondary ability |
| 4 — Mastered | Use form for 60 minutes total + complete 3 form challenges | +30% ability effectiveness + unlock passive trait |

## Profession Skills

Professions are standalone progression tracks that improve as you perform related activities.

### Available Professions (Early Access)

| Profession | How to Level | Key Rewards |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Fishing** | Catch fish at designated fishing spots | Cooking recipes, rare fish for quests, decorative items |
| **Mining** | Mine ore and crystal nodes | Metal ingots, gems, mining-exclusive crafting materials |
| **Woodcutting** | Chop trees and gather rare wood | Construction materials, bow components, housing upgrades |
| **Cooking** | Prepare meals from gathered ingredients | Buff foods, healing items, trade goods |
| **Crafting** | Craft weapons, tools, and items at workbenches | All gear upgrades, Henki form enhancements |

### Profession Leveling Tips

- Focus on **one gathering profession** (Fishing, Mining, or Woodcutting) and **one crafting profession** to complement it.
- Mining + Crafting is the strongest early combination — ore nodes are common and crafted weapons are a major power spike.
- Fishing is the most relaxing profession but offers the least combat benefit. Prioritize it for completion, not power.
- Cooking becomes valuable in the mid-game when you can craft stat-boosting meals before boss fights.
- Profession levels unlock unique cosmetic items for player housing. Each profession has housing decorations tied to its milestones.

## Respeccing

If you want to change your skill point allocation:

| Method | Cost | Availability |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Respec Potion** | 500 gold (purchased from the starting village alchemist) | Unlimited — buy as many as needed |
| **Free Respec** | Free | One-time — available at the beginning of Early Access |
| **Skill Reset Shrine** | 1 Ancient Essence (crafted from relics) | Located in the ruins biome |

**Tip:** Save your free respec until you reach the ruins biome. The Survival tree's higher-tier skills are more valuable there, and you can reallocate points from early combat investment into Master Crafter and Treasure Hunter.
