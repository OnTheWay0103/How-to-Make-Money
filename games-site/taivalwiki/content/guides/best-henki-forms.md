# Best Henki Forms Tier List: S to C Rank

> Early Access Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Not all Henki forms are created equal. Some dominate combat encounters, others excel at traversal, and a few occupy very specific niches. This tier list ranks every form archetype based on combat effectiveness, traversal utility, and overall versatility in both solo and co-op play. Use this guide to prioritize which forms to tame first and which ones to keep in your active rotation.

**Tier rankings reflect the Early Access build and will evolve as Bonobo Software adds new forms and balance patches.**

**Naming note (August 2026):** official materials confirm the form archetypes — "a hulking beast that absorbs damage, a swift mount that scales cliffsides, a cunning predator that strikes from the shadows" — plus community-reported snail (wall-climbing) and bird glider forms. The names used in this guide (Tank Beast, Stealth Predator, etc.) are descriptive community labels; official in-game form names are not yet documented. Rankings are community judgment based on the Early Access build, not official stats — treat them as guidance, not data.

---

## S-Tier: Best-in-Class

S-Tier forms excel in multiple areas and fit into almost any party composition. Prioritize taming these as soon as you encounter them.

### Swift Mount
**Role:** Traversal | **Combat:** B | **Traversal:** S | **Utility:** A

The Swift Mount is the single most impactful form you can unlock early. It transforms your Henki into a rideable creature that covers ground at roughly three times your running speed. Many mount forms can scale cliffs and leap across gaps, opening up shortcuts and hidden areas that are otherwise inaccessible.

**Why it ranks S:** The open world is large. Without a mount, getting between biomes and quest objectives is slow. A Swift Mount dramatically reduces travel time and makes exploration feel fluid. Some mount forms also have usable combat abilities, letting you fight while mounted.

**Best use case:** Overland travel, escaping dangerous encounters, reaching high-altitude areas.

### Base Healer
**Role:** Support | **Combat:** C | **Traversal:** D | **Utility:** S

Your starting Henki form never leaves your arsenal for long. The Base Healer form provides on-demand healing, status cleanse, and revival capabilities. In solo play, it serves as your emergency reset button. In co-op, a dedicated healer form keeps the entire party alive through extended fights.

**Why it ranks S:** The ability to heal and revive without consuming items is invaluable. Potions and food are finite resources — the Base Healer is infinite. No other form provides this level of sustain.

**Best use case:** Extended expeditions, boss fights, supporting squishier party members.

## A-Tier: Excellent Specialists

A-Tier forms excel in their specific roles and are essential for certain content types.

### Tank Beast
**Role:** Tank / Defender | **Combat:** S | **Traversal:** D | **Utility:** C

Tank Beasts have massive health pools, damage reduction abilities, and taunts that force enemies to target your Henki instead of you. In co-op, a Tank Beast player can hold aggro while the rest of the party deals damage freely.

**Why it ranks A:** In solo play, the Tank Beast is less essential since you control aggro naturally. In co-op, it is S-Tier. The ranking reflects solo play where it falls just short of S due to lower damage output.

**Best use case:** Boss fights, protecting squishy teammates, holding chokepoints.

### Glider (Bird Form)
**Role:** Aerial Traversal | **Combat:** D | **Traversal:** S | **Utility:** B

Bird forms let you grab your Henki's legs and glide across the world. Gliders are unmatched for descending from mountain peaks, crossing deep chasms, and surveying biomes from above. Some gliders can gain altitude using thermal updrafts.

**Why it ranks A:** Gliders are situational but irreplaceable when you need them. Certain areas in the Mountains biome are designed specifically for glider traversal. Without one, you will need to take long detours.

**Best use case:** Mountain exploration, reaching floating islands, scouting dangerous areas before entering.

### Stealth Predator
**Role:** Assassin / Scout | **Combat:** A | **Traversal:** B | **Utility:** B

Stealth Predators excel at silent takedowns and ambushes. This form reduces your detection radius, boosts backstab damage, and can mark enemies through walls. It is the best form for hunting rare creatures without scaring them off.

**Why it ranks A:** The Stealth Predator is fantastic for its niche but lacks the broad utility of S-Tier forms. In a party with an aggro-heavy Tank Beast, the Stealth Predator can dish out enormous damage from the shadows.

**Best use case:** Hunting rare variants, clearing camps silently, initiating combat with a damage spike.

## B-Tier: Situationally Strong

B-Tier forms are useful in specific scenarios but have limited applications compared to higher tiers.

### Pack Mule
**Role:** Utility / Inventory | **Combat:** D | **Traversal:** D | **Utility:** S (limited)

The Pack Mule form dramatically increases your carrying capacity. In a resource-gathering expedition, this form is invaluable. However, it offers almost nothing in combat or traversal.

**Why it ranks B:** The Pack Mule is the best form for farming runs, but you will rarely need it during story quests or boss fights. Its usefulness depends entirely on what you are doing. In co-op, having one player on Pack Mule duty while others handle combat is a solid strategy for long expeditions.

**Best use case:** Mass resource gathering, material farming, deep exploration runs.

### Purple Clone Variant
**Role:** Damage Dealer | **Combat:** A | **Traversal:** D | **Utility:** C

The Purple Clone Variant is a rare spawn that grants your Henki the ability to summon clones in combat. These clones duplicate your damage output for a short duration, making this form excellent for burst phases.

**Why it ranks B:** The Purple Clone Variant is powerful but relies on finding and taming a rare spawn. Its effectiveness is also limited by its cooldown — you cannot keep clones up permanently. Save this form for bosses and tough encounters.

**Best use case:** Boss burst phases, large group fights, time-limited combat challenges.

### Golden Variant
**Role:** Loot / Economy | **Combat:** C | **Traversal:** D | **Utility:** A (farming)

Golden Variants are rare spawns that, when tamed, let your Henki find gold and valuables passively. Over time, this form generates significant wealth, making it excellent for funding gear upgrades and housing construction.

**Why it ranks B:** The Golden Variant does not help in combat or traversal. Its value is long-term economic gain. If you are grinding for materials or currency, switch to this form while gathering and swap back for combat.

**Best use case:** Gold farming, funding housing projects, building a material stockpile.

## C-Tier: Niche Utility

C-Tier forms are highly specialized. They serve specific functions well but lack the versatility to rank higher.

### Aquatic Form
**Role:** Underwater Traversal | **Combat:** C | **Traversal:** A (water only) | **Utility:** C

Aquatic forms let your Henki swim and dive underwater. This unlocks underwater caves, sunken ruins, and aquatic creature taming. Outside of water, this form is nearly useless.

**Why it ranks C:** The Aquatic Form is essential for accessing water content but offers nothing on land. Its usefulness depends entirely on how much underwater exploration you plan to do. In the current Early Access build, underwater content is present but limited.

**Best use case:** Exploring underwater caves, taming aquatic creatures, reaching sunken loot.

### Jump Pad Form
**Role:** Vertical Mobility | **Combat:** D | **Traversal:** B (vertical) | **Utility:** C

Jump Pad forms have the Jump Pad trait, letting your Henki launch you to higher ground. This is useful for reaching elevated platforms and shortcuts but is outclassed by gliders and mounts for general traversal.

**Why it ranks C:** The Jump Pad form is a worse mount and a worse glider. Use it only until you unlock better traversal options. It fills a niche in puzzle-solving where precise vertical movement is required.

**Best use case:** Early-game traversal puzzles, reaching platforms before unlocking a mount or glider.

## Final Thoughts

Your Henki form loadout should match your current objective. Use Swift Mount for travel, swap to Stealth Predator for hunting, and keep Base Healer ready for emergencies. As you unlock more forms and level up your Henki bond, you will develop a personal rotation that fits your playstyle.

Remember that tier lists are guidelines, not rules. If you find a C-Tier form that clicks with your playstyle, use it. The best form is the one you enjoy using.
