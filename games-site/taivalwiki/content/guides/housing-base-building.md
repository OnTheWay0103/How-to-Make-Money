# Taival — Player Housing & Base Building Guide

> Early Access Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

After your first few hours in Taival, you will unlock the ability to place a player home. Housing is more than a cosmetic feature — it provides crafting stations, storage, rest bonuses, and a shared space for your co-op party to gather between adventures.

This guide covers everything you need to build, upgrade, and optimize your home base.

**Accuracy note (August 2026):** upgradeable player homes placed in the world are confirmed (retailer copy confirms "upgradable homes"). The plot costs, upgrade tiers, storage slots, rest bonuses, and decoration effects below are community speculation from Early Access and are unconfirmed (TBD). Reviewers also describe Taival as deliberately downplaying traditional building systems in favor of co-op adventure and Henki progression — keep expectations modest.

---

## How to Unlock Housing

Housing becomes available after completing **Quest 1.3 — The Missing Patrol** in Act 1. The village elder gives you a Land Deed and directs you to available housing plots.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Quest Unlock** | Complete "The Missing Patrol" (Act 1) |
| **Item Required** | Land Deed (quest reward) |
| **Initial Cost** | 200 gold (furnishing materials) |
| **Location Access** | Highlands plot is free with deed; other biome plots cost additional gold |

## Housing Locations

Each biome offers at least one housing plot. Your choice affects which resources are most accessible and what kind of view you wake up to.

### Highlands Plot — "Meadowview"

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Cost** | Free (with deed) |
| **Size** | Small (2 upgrades available) |
| **Nearby Resources** | Wood, stone, berries |
| **Proximity to Village** | 30-second walk |
| **Best For** | First-time players, solo players |

**Pros:** Closest to the starting village, easiest to defend, no environmental hazards.
**Cons:** Smallest maximum size, basic resource variety.

### Forest Plot — "Canopy Rest"

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Cost** | 500 gold |
| **Size** | Medium (3 upgrades available) |
| **Nearby Resources** | Rare wood, herbs, honey |
| **Proximity to Village** | 5-minute walk |
| **Best For** | Crafters, herbalists |

**Pros:** Access to rare forest resources, larger build area, treehouse aesthetic.
**Cons:** Occasional insect swarm attacks, dim natural lighting.

### Mountain Plot — "Summit Haven"

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Cost** | 800 gold |
| **Size** | Medium (3 upgrades available) |
| **Nearby Resources** | Iron ore, crystals, feathers |
| **Proximity to Village** | 10-minute walk |
| **Best For** | Endgame players, miners |

**Pros:** Best view in the game, nearby ore nodes, defensive position.
**Cons:** Cold weather (slow damage if outside too long), furthest from traders.

### Ruins Plot — "Ancient Rest"

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Cost** | 1,000 gold |
| **Size** | Large (4 upgrades available) |
| **Nearby Resources** | Relics, gems, essence deposits |
| **Proximity to Village** | 8-minute walk |
| **Best For** | Endgame players, co-op groups |

**Pros:** Largest plot, closest to late-game content, exotic resource access.
**Cons:** Most expensive, close to barbarian territory (occasional nearby patrols that do not attack your home but can be annoying).

## Housing Upgrades

Each home can be upgraded multiple times. Upgrades require gold and specific materials gathered from the surrounding biome.

### Upgrade Tiers

| Tier | Cost | New Features | Requirement |
|------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **1 — Shelter** | Free (initial) | Basic bed, small storage crate, campfire | Complete "The Missing Patrol" |
| **2 — Cottage** | 300 gold + 50 wood + 30 stone | Medium storage, simple crafting bench, cooking station | Tier 1 |
| **3 — Homestead** | 600 gold + 100 wood + 60 stone + 20 iron | Large storage, advanced crafting station, alchemy table, fishing rack | Tier 2 + Profession Level 3 in one craft |
| **4 — Estate** | 1,200 gold + 200 rare wood + 100 iron + 20 gems | Extra large storage, all crafting stations, Henki rest area, guest rooms | Tier 3 + Clear all 6 Henki Dungeons |

**Tip:** The jump from Tier 2 to Tier 3 is the biggest power spike. The advanced crafting station unlocks the best gear recipes and the alchemy table lets you craft permanent stat boost potions.

## Housing Benefits

### Storage

| Upgrade | Item Slots | Special Feature |
|---------|------------|-----------------|
| Small Crate (Tier 1) | 20 | None |
| Medium Storage (Tier 2) | 50 | Organization tabs |
| Large Storage (Tier 3) | 100 | Shared party access |
| Extra Large Storage (Tier 4) | 200 | Shared party access + search function |

### Crafting Stations

| Station | Unlock Tier | What It Lets You Craft |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| Campfire | 1 | Basic meals, torches |
| Simple Crafting Bench | 2 | Basic tools, simple weapons, wooden furniture |
| Cooking Station | 2 | Advanced meals, buff foods |
| Advanced Crafting Station | 3 | All weapons, armor, advanced tools |
| Alchemy Table | 3 | Potions, stat boosts, essence enhancements |
| Henki Rest Area | 4 | Faster Henki bond recovery |

### Rest Bonus

Sleeping in your home grants stacking bonuses that make a significant difference in your next expedition.

| Rest Duration | Bonuses Gained |
|---------------|---------------|
| Short Rest (5 minutes slept) | +10% XP gain for 10 minutes |
| Full Rest (15 minutes slept) | +20% XP gain for 20 minutes + full HP/Sanity recovery |
| Extended Rest (30 minutes slept) | +25% XP gain for 30 minutes + full HP/Sanity/Mana recovery + Henki bond fully restored |

**The Rested Bonus skill** (Survival tree, Tier 3) extends the duration of all rest bonuses by 50%. If you plan to use housing heavily, invest in this skill early.

## Co-op Shared Housing

When playing in a group, housing works as a shared space.

### How Shared Housing Works

| Feature | Solo Play | Co-op Play |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Home Ownership** | One player owns the home | One player owns, others are "residents" |
| **Storage Access** | Personal only | Owner controls shared access |
| **Crafting Station Use** | Full access | All residents can use stations |
| **Rest Bonus** | Applies to owner only | Applies to all residents who sleep |
| **Upgrades** | Owner pays | Owner pays, but residents can contribute materials |
| **Decorating** | Owner places items | Residents can place items in designated areas |

### Co-op Housing Tips

- Designate one player as the "home owner" who invests in the highest-tier plot (Ruins — Ancient Rest is the best for groups).
- All group members should contribute materials to upgrades. The Tier 3 upgrade is expensive but benefits everyone.
- Use shared storage for group resources (ore, rare wood, crafting materials) and keep personal storage for quest items and gear.
- Only one home can be active per session. If multiple players own homes, the party votes on which one to use for that session.

## Housing Location Recommendations

| Playstyle | Best Location | Why |
|-----------|--------------|-----|
| **Solo Player** | Highlands — Meadowview | Low cost, safe, close to all early content |
| **Crafter** | Forest — Canopy Rest | Easy access to rare wood and herbs |
| **Endgame Solo** | Mountains — Summit Haven | Ore for endgame gear, beautiful scenery |
| **Co-op Group (2 players)** | Forest — Canopy Rest | Medium size, good resource variety |
| **Co-op Group (3-4 players)** | Ruins — Ancient Rest | Largest plot, closest to late-game content |
| **Completionist** | Buy all four | Each home has unique decoration rewards |

**Budget Option for Groups:** Start with the Highlands plot (free) and save up for the Ruins plot together. The Highlands plot is sufficient for Tier 2 upgrades, which cover everything you need before the endgame.
