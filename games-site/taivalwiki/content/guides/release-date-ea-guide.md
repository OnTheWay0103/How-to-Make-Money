## Taival Release Date & Early Access Guide

**Taival entered Steam Early Access in July 2026** — announced available July 14–15, 2026 (press coverage dated the announcement July 14; the Early Access launch was reported as July 15). If you are deciding when to jump into this 1–4 player co-op adventure RPG, this page rounds up everything confirmed: the launch, price, platforms, demo, and what the Early Access state actually includes.

**Honesty note:** "Taival" means **"journey"** in Finnish — fitting for a game about a shapeshifting companion and a quest across four realms. Where launch details below come from press coverage rather than the Steam page itself, they are marked as reported.

## What Is Taival?

A **co-op adventure RPG for up to 4 players** — online, local split-screen, or mixed — set in a vibrant, handcrafted voxel open world. You begin by finding the **last Henki**, a shapeshifter who once kept the world in balance; someone destroyed its **Heartstone**, and restoring it is your journey.

Confirmed systems at launch:

- **The Henki** — absorbs creature essences and shifts into forms with unique battle and exploration abilities (see the [Henki Companion Guide](/guides/henki-companion-guide))
- **Four seamlessly connected realms** — rolling highlands, dense forests, mountains, ancient ruins
- **Group quest dialogue with voting** — all players follow pivotal story decisions and vote on the outcome
- **Dual progression** — your character and your Henki level separately
- **Taming, professions, and multiple combat styles**
- **Main quest + side quests** — investigations, NPC disputes, and combat encounters
- Inspirations per the developer: *Cube World*, *Breath of the Wild*, and *Skyrim*

## How Long Is Early Access?

The developer has stated Early Access will last **6–12 months**, and that the world and story are **not yet finished** at launch — the main questline will be extended with more biomes and quests toward 1.0. Treat the current story as incomplete by design.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When did Taival release?**
July 15, 2026 into Steam Early Access (announced July 14).

**Is Taival free?**
No — it is paid (¥77 RMB / ~$11 USD base, with a 20% launch discount). A free demo was available from June 2026.

**Is Taival on console?**
Not announced — PC via Steam is the only confirmed platform.

**How many players can play together?**
Up to 4 — online, local split-screen, or any mix.

**How long will Early Access last?**
6–12 months, per the developer.

**Is the story complete?**
No — the developer has confirmed the world and story are not finished at Early Access launch; more biomes and quests are planned.

## Should You Buy Now?

- **Co-op fans:** this is the game's core — 4-player mixed online/split-screen co-op is confirmed and central to the voting quest system.
- **Wait-and-see:** the story is explicitly incomplete, and console versions are unannounced. The demo is the risk-free way to test (see the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) for what to expect).
- **Cheap entry:** the launch discount is reported at 20% — if you were going to buy anyway, launch window pricing is the low point.
