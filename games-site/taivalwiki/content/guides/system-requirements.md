## Taival System Requirements — PC Specs & Performance Guide

Taival is a handcrafted voxel-style open world for 1–4 players — the art style keeps requirements modest, and the published numbers are surprisingly low. This guide covers the reported specs, what is officially confirmed, and what to expect on your machine.

**Honesty note:** as of August 8, 2026, the Steam page's own spec listing has not been confirmed by our team — the figures below come from third-party spec aggregators (PCGameBenchmark) and are labeled accordingly. If they disagree with the store page, trust the store page.

## Reported System Requirements (Third-Party Aggregated)

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **OS** | Windows 10/11 | Windows 10/11 |
| **Processor** | Intel Core 2 Duo E8400 | Intel Core 2 Duo E8400 |
| **Memory** | 4 GB RAM | 4 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 |
| **Storage** | 5 GB available | 5 GB available |

**What the numbers mean:**

- The CPU and RAM requirements are extremely low — any gaming PC from the last 10+ years clears them easily.
- The graphics card is the real gate: **GTX 970 minimum**, **GTX 1660 recommended** for the highest settings.
- 5 GB of disk space is the reported footprint — small for a 2026 open world.
- Note: the Core 2 Duo figure (a 2008-era chip) looks anomalously low next to a GTX 970; treat the exact CPU figure as approximate until official specs are published.

## Performance Expectations (Based on the Art Style)

- **Voxel/blocky rendering:** Taival's vibrant voxel world renders with modest GPU load compared to AAA open worlds — a mid-range GPU should hold high settings.
- **Co-op sessions:** 4-player mixed online/split-screen sessions add network overhead but not dramatically more GPU load; the confirmed split-screen support (see the [Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide)) is the bigger compatibility question.
- **Storage:** 5 GB means an SSD is not a requirement, though one always helps load times in open worlds.
