## Taival System Requirements — PC Specs & Performance Guide

Taival is a handcrafted voxel-style open world for 1–4 players — the art style keeps requirements modest. This guide covers the official system requirements as listed on the Steam store page, plus what to expect on your machine.

**Honesty note:** an earlier version of this page listed third-party spec-aggregator figures (GTX 970 minimum, Core 2 Duo CPU, 4 GB RAM) that do **not** match the Steam store page. The table below reflects the store page's published requirements; if you see conflicting numbers elsewhere, trust the official store page.

## Official System Requirements (Steam Store Page)

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **OS** | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) |
| **Processor** | 64-bit processor required | AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| **Memory** | 8 GB RAM | 16 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Ti / 1660 Super (or equivalent) | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Ti (or equivalent; some sources list RTX 2070 Super) |
| **DirectX** | Version 11 | Version 11 |
| **Storage** | ~5 GB available space (one source lists 10 GB) | ~5 GB available space, SSD recommended |

**What the numbers mean:**

- Any gaming PC from the last ~8 years meets the minimum — the requirements are modest for a 2026 open world.
- The graphics card is the real gate: **GTX 1660 Super minimum**, **RTX 3060 Ti class recommended**.
- The 64-bit processor requirement is the only hard CPU gate at minimum settings.

## Performance Expectations (Based on the Art Style)

- **Voxel/blocky rendering:** Taival's vibrant voxel world renders with modest GPU load compared to AAA open worlds — a mid-range GPU should hold high settings.
- **Co-op sessions:** 4-player mixed online/split-screen sessions add network overhead but not dramatically more GPU load; the confirmed split-screen support (see the [Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide)) is the bigger compatibility question.
- **Storage:** ~5 GB means an SSD is not a requirement, though one always helps load times in open worlds (recommended by the store page).

## Is My PC Good Enough?

| Your GPU | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| GTX 1660 Super / RTX 2060 or better | Meets the official minimum — playable |
| RTX 3060 Ti / RTX 2070 Super or better | Meets the recommended specs — high settings |
| Older cards (GTX 970 and below) | Below the official minimum — may still run at reduced settings, but the demo is the safe test |

**Best advice:** a free demo was available on Steam from June 2026 — if it is still live, run the demo on your machine before buying (see the [Release Date & EA Guide](/guides/release-date-ea-guide) for demo status).
