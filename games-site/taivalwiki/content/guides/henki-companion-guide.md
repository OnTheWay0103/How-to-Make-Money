# Henki Companion Guide: All Forms, Abilities & How to Unlock

> Early Access Version / Updated: July 27, 2026

The Henki is the defining feature of Taival — a shapeshifting companion that absorbs the essence of creatures to unlock new forms. Without your Henki, traversing the four biomes, surviving combat encounters, and solving environmental puzzles would be nearly impossible. This guide explains everything you need to know about your Henki companion, from the taming process to every form archetype, Henki traits, rare variants, and advanced swapping strategies.

---

## What Is a Henki?

In the world of Taival, the Henki are an ancient race of small, shapeshifting beings who once protected the world by maintaining balance among its creatures. By the time your story begins, the Henki are nearly extinct. You discover the last surviving Henki — a shapeshifter without a fixed form of its own — and together you embark on a journey to recover the shattered fragments of the Heartstone, the source of the Henki's power.

Your Henki companion is always with you. It cannot be lost, dismissed, or permanently killed. If it takes too much damage in combat, it retreats to a dormant state and regenerates over time. The bond between you and your Henki grows as you unlock more forms and progress through the story.

## How to Tame and Absorb Creatures

Taming is the process of unlocking a new Henki form. Each creature species in the world has a unique form that your Henki can learn. Here is the step-by-step process:

### Step 1: Study the Creature
Approach a wild creature carefully. Aggressive creatures may attack on sight, so keep your distance. Use your journal to study the creature — this reveals its preferred food and behavioral patterns. Some creatures only appear at certain times of day or in specific biomes.

### Step 2: Learn Its Preferences
Each creature has one or two preferred food items. These can range from berries and fruits to rare materials. Your journal entry for the creature will hint at what it likes. If you are stuck, try offering different food types from your inventory.

### Step 3: Offer Food
Select the preferred food from your inventory and offer it to the creature. While it feeds, your Henki studies the creature's movements, behaviors, and physical structure. The creature may become temporarily friendly during this process — use the opportunity to observe it closely.

### Step 4: Form Unlocked
After the feeding completes, the creature's form is permanently unlocked in your companion menu. You can now swap to this form at any time. The creature itself is not harmed or consumed — it simply departs after sharing its essence.

**Pro tip:** If a creature is hostile, you can sometimes pacify it by throwing food from a distance. Alternatively, defeat it in combat first, then study its remains for taming data.

## All Form Archetypes

Henki forms fall into several broad archetypes. Each archetype serves a different purpose in combat, traversal, or utility.

### Tank Beast
A large, heavily armored form designed to absorb damage. Tank beasts have high health pools and taunt abilities that draw enemy aggression away from you. Use this form when facing tough enemies or when you need to protect squishier party members in co-op. Tank beasts often have the Leech trait, healing you while they sustain damage.

### Swift Mount
Forms with the mountable trait allow you to ride your Henki for faster travel across the open world. Swift mounts excel at covering ground, scaling cliffsides, and traversing rough terrain. Some mounts have special movement abilities like double jumps or short dashes. Unlocking a mount form is a top early-game priority.

### Stealth Predator
A cunning form built for silent takedowns and ambushes. Stealth predators have abilities that let you move without alerting enemies, deal bonus damage from behind, and see creature heat signatures through foliage. This form is invaluable for hunting rare creatures and bypassing dangerous encounters.

### Glider (Bird Forms)
Bird forms let you grab your Henki's legs and soar through the air. Gliding is the best way to descend from mountains, cross large gaps, and scout biomes from above. Gliders are essential for reaching hidden areas and are one of the most satisfying traversal forms in the game.

### Pack Mule
A utility form that expands your carrying capacity. Pack mules can carry additional items, resources, and loot beyond your normal inventory limit. In co-op, one player dedicating their Henki to pack mule duty keeps the group well-supplied on extended expeditions. Pack mule forms often have the Pack Mule trait for maximum storage.

### Base Healer
Your starting Henki form is a support-oriented shape with healing and revival abilities. The base healer form can restore your health over time, cleanse status effects, and revive you if you fall in combat. This form is always available and does not require taming. Keep it in your rotation for emergencies.

## Henki Traits Explained

Every form has a unique Henki Trait that sets it apart from other forms. No two forms are identical. Here are the key traits to look for:

| Trait | Effect | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Leech** | Henki heals you for a percentage of damage it deals | Tank beasts, aggressive forms |
| **Jump Pad** | Henki can launch you to higher ground or across gaps | Exploration, puzzle-solving |
| **Pack Mule** | Increases total carrying capacity significantly | Resource gathering, long expeditions |
| **Thorns** | Reflects a portion of incoming damage back at attackers | Defensive playstyles |
| **Regeneration** | Henki slowly regenerates health while out of combat | Solo play, sustainable exploration |
| **Vampiric** | Henki heals itself for a percentage of damage dealt | Extended fights without healing |

Henki traits are fixed per form — you cannot change or upgrade them. Choosing the right form for the situation means choosing the right trait.

## Rare Variants: Golden and Purple

Some creatures have a rare chance to spawn as special variants. These are visually distinct from their standard counterparts and offer enhanced forms when tamed.

### Golden Variants
Golden creatures shimmer with a golden aura. When tamed, the golden effect carries over to the unlocked form. Golden forms have a passive ability to find gold and valuable items for you — they will occasionally dig up treasure or highlight valuable resource nodes on your map. They are extremely rare and highly sought after.

### Purple Variants
Purple creatures have a dark, shimmering aura. When tamed, the unlocked form gains the ability to summon clones of itself in combat. These clones fight alongside you for a limited duration, multiplying your damage output. Purple variant forms are excellent for boss fights and large group encounters.

**Where to find them:** Rare variants can spawn anywhere, but they are more likely to appear in harder biomes (Mountains, Ruins) and during specific weather conditions. Listen for unique sound cues — variants have a distinct audio signature that sets them apart from normal creatures.

## Form Swapping Tips

You can swap Henki forms at any time from the companion menu, even in the middle of combat. Here are some advanced tips for getting the most out of your form arsenal:

- **Pre-combat setup.** Before engaging a tough enemy, switch to a tank beast or offensive form. Keep your base healer or a defensive form as a secondary option.
- **Traversal chain.** Use a swift mount to cover ground, then swap to a glider to descend into a valley, then swap to a stealth predator for a surprise attack. Mastering form chains makes you incredibly mobile.
- **Co-op coordination.** In a group, coordinate Henki forms so that at least one player has a healer form, one has a tank, and one has a pack mule. The fourth player can flex into any role needed.
- **Quick-swap hotkeys.** Bind your most-used forms to hotkeys for instant access. Muscle memory with form swapping separates average players from great ones.

## Henki Progression Track

Your Henki has its own progression track separate from your character. As you use forms in combat and traversal, your bond with the Henki grows. Higher bond levels unlock:

- Mount ability upgrades (faster sprint, higher jumps)
- Passive stat bonuses for each form archetype
- New form slots for your quick-swap rotation
- Special form abilities unique to each creature

Keep your Henki active and engaged. The more you use a particular form, the faster it levels up and unlocks its full potential.
