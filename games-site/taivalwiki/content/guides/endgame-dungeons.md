## Endgame Dungeons Guide — Dungeon Tiers, World Bosses & Rewards

Taival's endgame is a dungeon economy: tiers of instanced dungeons feed materials into world boss spawns, and world bosses drop the crafting recipes that gate the best gear. This guide covers the full endgame dungeon loop, from your first tier-2 clear to world boss rotations.

### Dungeon Difficulty Tiers

| Tier | Level Requirement | Unique Mechanic | Best Loot |
|------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Tier 1 | Level 20 | None (intro) | Dungeon materials |
| Tier 2 | Level 30 | Timed waves | Tier 2 recipe drops |
| Tier 3 | Level 40 | Henki-only puzzles | Henki upgrade materials |
| Tier 4 | Level 50 | Boss gauntlet, no checkpoints | World boss spawn keys |

### Endgame Dungeon Mechanics

1. **Tier 2 — Timed waves:** Enemies spawn in set waves; finishing each wave early grants a time bonus chest.
2. **Tier 3 — Henki puzzles:** Several rooms are soloable only by your Henki companion. Level your Henki's puzzle skill before attempting.
3. **Tier 4 — No checkpoints:** A single death sends you back to the dungeon entrance. Bring revival items and treat every pull as final.
4. **Key drops:** Tier 4 final bosses drop World Boss Spawn Keys — 3 keys summon the weekly world boss.

### World Boss Spawn Schedule

| Boss | Spawn Key Cost | Window | Recommended Henki |
|------|----------------|--------|-------------------|
| Tide Serpent | 3 keys | 24 hours after summon | Water form (ranged) |
| Ember Colossus | 3 keys | 24 hours after summon | Fire form (burst) |
| Storm Wyrm | 5 keys | Weekly rotation | Wind form (mobility) |
| The Great Elder | 10 keys | Monthly | Balanced/defense form |

### Preparation Checklist

1. Bring the Henki form your dungeon demands — Tier 3 puzzles cannot be brute-forced.
2. Carry 4 health potions, 2 mana potions, and 1 revival charm per Tier 4 run.
3. Verify your skill tree includes the dungeon tier's recommended talents before queueing.
4. Check the world boss spawn window before spending keys — join open summons to save keys.
5. Repair gear before Tier 4; a mid-run break equals a wipe.

### Loot Priorities

- **Tier 3 Henki materials:** Feed these to your Henki first — they unlock new dungeon puzzle skills.
- **World boss recipes:** Tide Serpent drops the Triton Trident recipe; Ember Colossus drops the Forge Plate recipe.
- **Tier 4 chests:** Always open the final chest over intermediate chests — the key materials only come from the end.

- **Weekly key cap:** Spawn keys expire at the weekly reset — never sit on more than 2 keys, or you waste farmed materials.
- **First-clear bonuses:** Each dungeon tier pays a one-time first-clear bonus; clear tiers in order on a new character.
