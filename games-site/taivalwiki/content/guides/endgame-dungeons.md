## Endgame & Companion Dungeons Guide

Taival's "endgame" is a work in progress — the game launched in Early Access on July 14–15, 2026, and the developer states the current build offers "a complete gameplay experience, but with the world and story not yet finished." This guide explains what endgame-adjacent content exists now, what the 1.0 roadmap promises, and how to prepare.

**Honesty note:** an earlier version of this page described a four-tier instanced dungeon system (levels 20–50) with spawn keys, weekly world boss rotations, and named bosses (Tide Serpent, Ember Colossus...). **None of that was real.** No dungeon tiers, level caps, or spawn-key systems have been published. This page has been corrected.

## Companion Dungeons — The Real Endgame

The standout endgame-style feature confirmed for Early Access is the **companion dungeon**: an instanced challenge where you take direct control of your Henki instead of your character.

### What We Know

- You control the Henki directly inside these challenges (confirmed).
- They test your Henki's forms — combat, traversal, and abilities (community reports).
- They fit Taival's core design: your Henki is a full progression track, so challenging it directly is the natural endgame loop.

### What We Don't Know (TBD)

- How many companion dungeons exist and where the entrances are
- Difficulty tiers or level requirements
- Whether they're solo-only or party-playable
- Rewards (community reports mention exclusive form upgrades — unconfirmed)

Companion dungeon specifics are being documented by the community as of August 2026; verified details will be added here as they surface.

## Preparing for the Endgame (Confirmed-System Prep)

Since the 1.0 content isn't here yet, the best "endgame prep" is mastering what exists:

1. **Collect Henki forms.** Creature collection is the game's core hook (Steam tag: Creature Collector). Every new realm and creature means new forms — build a deep roster.
2. **Level both tracks.** Character abilities and combat skills, plus Henki forms and mount abilities — the dual progression is confirmed and both matter.
3. **Master the professions.** Crafting and fishing are confirmed professions; leveled professions will pay off as harder content arrives.
4. **Take on the bosses that exist.** The barbarian camp beast, insect pest control, and sudden exploration bosses are the current "hard content."
5. **Track the roadmap.** The developer plans to extend the main questline across more biomes and zones, adding side quests, "new enemy and boss encounters," items, creatures, and lore, plus optimization, through the 6–12 month Early Access period. Expect the hardest content in the last months of EA and at 1.0.

## What to Expect at 1.0

Confirmed roadmap items from the developer's Early Access statement:

- Extended main questline across multiple biomes and zones
- More side quests
- **New enemy and boss encounters**
- New items, creatures, and lore
- Optimization and polish
- Price increases as content ships (the game currently costs ¥77, ~US$16)

Consoles (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch 2) are also planned later.
