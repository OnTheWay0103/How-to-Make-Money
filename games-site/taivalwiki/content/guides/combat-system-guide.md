# Combat System & Weapons Guide: Battle Tactics

> Early Access Version / Updated: July 27, 2026

Combat in Taival blends real-time action with Henki companion synergy. While the game leans into cozy exploration, the combat system has genuine depth — multiple weapon types, a dodge and block system, combo chains, and Henki abilities that can turn the tide of battle. This guide covers everything you need to know about fighting in Taival, from basic mechanics to advanced co-op tactics.

---

## Combat Basics

Before diving into weapons and strategies, here is how the core combat system works:

- **Health and Stamina:** Both are displayed in your HUD. Attacks consume stamina; getting hit depletes health. Stamina regenerates slowly out of combat. Food and Henki abilities can speed this up.
- **Lock-On:** You can lock onto an enemy with the middle mouse button (or right stick click). Locked-on attacks track the target more accurately.
- **Stagger:** Hitting an enemy repeatedly builds up stagger. A staggered enemy is briefly stunned and takes bonus damage from the next attack.
- **Enemy Awareness:** Most enemies have detection ranges and awareness states. Unaware enemies take bonus damage from the first hit. Use the Stealth Predator form to maximize this.

## Weapon Types

Taival features multiple weapon types, each with distinct attack patterns, reach, speed, and special abilities. You can carry up to two weapons at a time and switch between them during combat.

### Swords
Balanced weapons with moderate speed and damage. Swords have versatile movesets — light attacks are fast and chain into combos, while heavy attacks break enemy guards.

**Best for:** New players, general-purpose combat, combo-heavy playstyles

**Special ability:** Parry — with precise timing, a sword can deflect enemy attacks, leaving them open for a counter-attack.

### Spears
Long-reach weapons that excel at keeping enemies at a distance. Spears have slower attack speed but higher damage per hit and excellent reach. The charged heavy attack can pierce through multiple enemies.

**Best for:** Keeping distance, fighting large enemies, crowd control

**Special ability:** Charge — hold the attack button to thrust forward, covering ground and dealing bonus damage.

### Axes
Slow but devastating. Axes have the highest single-hit damage in the game. They are weak against fast enemies but demolish armored foes and structures.

**Best for:** Boss fights, armored enemies, breaking objects

**Special ability:** Armor Break — heavy axe attacks ignore a percentage of enemy armor.

### Bows
Ranged weapons for engaging enemies from a distance. Bows require arrows (crafted or purchased). Headshots deal bonus damage and can stagger most enemies.

**Best for:** Initiating fights, picking off ranged enemies, hunting

**Special ability:** Aimed Shot — holding the draw increases damage and range. Perfect release at full draw grants bonus crit chance.

### Staves
Magical weapons that consume a resource called Essence (regenerates slowly or from gathering). Staves have medium reach but deal elemental damage with unique effects.

**Best for:** Status effects, group fights, elemental weaknesses

**Special ability:** Elemental Infusion — staff light attacks apply a damage-over-time effect. Heavy attacks trigger a burst that spreads the element to nearby enemies.

### Shields
Off-hand equipment that enables blocking. Shields cannot be used as primary weapons, but they dramatically improve survivability. Paired with a one-handed weapon (sword or axe).

**Best for:** Tank builds, blocking large attacks, protecting teammates

**Special ability:** Shield Bash — interrupts enemy attacks and briefly stuns them.

## How Henki Assists in Combat

Your Henki is not just a traversal tool — it is an active combat participant. Depending on the current form, your Henki will:

- **Auto-attack** nearby enemies (damage scales with form type and bond level)
- **Use its Henki Trait** passively (Leech heals you, Thorns reflects damage, etc.)
- **Activate a special ability** when you press the Henki Ability button (Q / Right Bumper)

### Henki Combat Ability Examples

| Henki Form | Combat Ability | Cooldown | Effect |
|-----------|---------------|----------|--------|
| Tank Beast | Taunt | 15s | Forces nearby enemies to attack the Henki |
| Stealth Predator | Shadow Strike | 20s | Teleports behind target, deals massive damage |
| Base Healer | Heal Pulse | 25s | Heals you and nearby allies for 30% health |
| Purple Clone | Summon Clone | 45s | Creates a copy that fights for 15 seconds |

Mastering the timing of your Henki's ability is essential for harder fights. A well-timed Heal Pulse can save a party member, and a perfectly placed Taunt can prevent a wipe.

## Enemy Types

Taival's four biomes are home to distinct enemy types with unique behaviors.

### Highlands Enemies (Beginner)
- **Wild Canines** — Fast, aggressive, low health. Easy to stagger. Best handled with sword combos or bow headshots.
- **Grazing Herbivores** — Passive unless provoked. Not dangerous but can summon predators if you attack them.
- **Bandits** — Humanoid enemies with basic weapons. They dodge occasionally and can parry. Use heavy attacks to break their guard.

### Forests Enemies (Intermediate)
- **Forest Spiders** — Cling to ceilings and drop on unsuspecting players. Weak to fire (staff fire infusion). Their attacks apply a slow debuff.
- **Corrupted Boars** — Charge attacks with high stagger. Dodge sideways (not backward) to avoid the charge. Vulnerable after they miss.
- **Thorn Vines** — Stationary enemies that grab you if you get too close. Free the grab by mashing the interact button or having a teammate attack the vine.

### Mountains Enemies (Hard)
- **Stone Golems** — Massive health pools and high armor. Almost immune to light attacks. Use axes or spears with the Armor Break special. Attack the glowing crystal on their back for critical damage.
- **Mountain Harpies** — Flying enemies that swoop down for hit-and-run attacks. Ranged weapons (bows or staves) are most effective. They retreat to high ledges when low on health.
- **Ice Wraiths** — Spectral enemies that phase in and out of visibility. They are vulnerable only when attacking. Watch for the shimmer before they strike, then dodge and counter.

### Ruins Enemies (Endgame)
- **Ancient Guardians** — Constructs with complex attack patterns. They have a shield phase where they block all frontal damage. Use flanking tactics or shield-breaking abilities.
- **Cursed Spirits** — Apply a stacking curse debuff that reduces your maximum health. The Base Healer form's status cleanse is essential here.
- **Boss Variants** — Each biome has a major boss with unique mechanics. Boss fights require coordinated form swapping and communication in co-op.

## Dodge and Block Mechanics

Survival in Taival depends on mastering defensive options.

### Dodge Roll
Press the dodge button (Right Click / Left Trigger) while moving to perform a roll. The roll has invincibility frames (i-frames) that let you pass through enemy attacks unscathed. Timing matters more than distance — dodge toward the attack, not away from it, for the best i-frame window.

**Dodge tips:**
- Dodging consumes stamina. Running out of stamina mid-dodge leaves you vulnerable.
- Chain dodges have diminishing i-frames. The second dodge has fewer invincibility frames than the first.
- You can cancel attack recovery with a dodge. This is essential for aggressive playstyles.

### Blocking
With a shield or certain two-handed weapons, you can hold the block button to reduce incoming damage. Blocking does not negate damage entirely — it reduces it by 60-80% depending on the shield/weapon.

**Block tips:**
- Blocking consumes stamina. A broken guard (zero stamina while blocking) stuns you for 3 seconds.
- Some enemy attacks are unblockable (indicated by a red flash). These must be dodged.
- Perfect Block (block right as an attack lands) negates all damage and stamina cost, and staggers the attacker.

### Healing
You can heal using food items, potions, or the Base Healer Henki form. Healing takes 2 seconds and can be interrupted by taking damage. Position yourself safely before healing — behind cover or while a teammate holds aggro.

## Combo System

Taival has a light attack chain system. Successive light attacks form combos with escalating damage. The third hit in a chain deals bonus damage, and the fourth hit triggers a special finisher unique to each weapon type.

| Weapon | 4-Hit Finisher Effect |
|--------|----------------------|
| Sword | Wide sweep that hits all nearby enemies |
| Spear | Thrust that pierces through multiple targets |
| Axe | Overhead slam that staggers all enemies in a cone |
| Bow | Quick shot with 100% crit chance |

Heavy attacks do not chain into combos but deal more damage per hit and have armor-breaking properties.

**Advanced combo:** Light attack x3 → Heavy attack → Henki Ability. This is a universal damage rotation that works with most weapon types. The heavy attack at the end of the light chain benefits from the combo damage multiplier.

## Difficulty Scaling in Co-op

Enemy difficulty scales with the number of players in the party. Here is what changes when you add more players:

- **Enemy health increases** by approximately 50% per additional player
- **Enemy damage increases** by approximately 25% per additional player
- **Elite enemies and minibosses** spawn more frequently in 3-4 player parties
- **Boss attack patterns** change — some bosses gain new attacks in larger parties

Because enemies get tougher but not necessarily more numerous, party role specialization becomes more important as group size increases. A group of four generalists will struggle more than a balanced tank-DPS-support-flex setup.

## Best Weapon Combos

### Solo Play
- **Sword + Base Healer** — Sword's parry keeps you safe, Base Healer provides healing. The most forgiving solo setup.
- **Spear + Stealth Predator** — Spear's range keeps enemies at a distance while the Predator handles burst damage. Great for kiting.
- **Bow + Swift Mount** — Hit-and-run tactics. Shoot, move, shoot again. Effective against slow enemies like Golems.

### Co-op Play
- **Tank (Sword + Shield) + DPS (Spear or Axe)** — Classic tank-and-spank. The tank holds aggro while the DPS unloads.
- **Support (Staff + Base Healer) + Ranged (Bow + Glider)** — Aerial bombardment. The Support applies status effects while the Ranged player rains arrows from above.
- **Full Aggro (Axe + Purple Clone)** — Maximum burst damage for boss burn phases. Both players use high-damage weapons and clone abilities simultaneously.

## Combat Tips Summary

- **Always have a Henki combat ability ready.** Do not waste it on weak enemies. Save your cooldowns for elites and bosses.
- **Watch enemy attack tells.** Every enemy telegraphs its attacks with visual or audio cues. Learn the timing for each enemy type.
- **Use terrain.** High ground gives you an advantage against ground-based enemies. Chokepoints let you fight enemies one at a time.
- **Swap weapons per biome.** Swords and shields for the Forests (fast enemies), Spears for the Mountains (reach against large enemies), Axes for the Ruins (armored constructs).
- **Coordinate cooldowns in co-op.** Have the Tank burn through their cooldowns first, then the DPS, then the Support. Rotating cooldowns maintains consistent pressure.
