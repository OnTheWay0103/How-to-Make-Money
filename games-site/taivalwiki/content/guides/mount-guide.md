# Taival Mount Guide — How to Unlock Riding & Traversal Forms

The fastest way across the world of Taival is not a horse or a cart — it is your Henki. The shapeshifting companion can take the form of creatures you tame, and several of those forms are mounts and traversal tools: a swift mount that scales cliffsides, rideable large forms, and (per community reports) a snail that climbs walls and a bird you can glide with. This guide covers everything confirmed about mounts in Taival, how to unlock them, and which form fits each of the four realms.

## What Mounts Are in Taival

Mounts are **Henki forms**, not separate creatures you keep. When your Henki absorbs a creature's essence and unlocks its form, you can shift into that form — and some forms are mountable:

| Mount Type | Confirmed? | What It Does |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Swift mount** | Yes (official copy) | "Scales cliffsides" — the official example of a traversal mount; covers ground fast and climbs vertical terrain |
| **Large riding forms** | Reported | Community reports describe large creature forms you can ride for fast travel |
| **Giant snail form** | Reported (community) | Climbs vertical walls — reported from the live build |
| **Bird / glider form** | Reported (community) | You grab the form's legs and glide through the air |

**Key confirmed facts:**

- Mount abilities are part of the **Henki progression track** — the Steam page says players unlock "mount abilities for faster exploration" as the companion grows.
- The developer's official form examples are "a hulking beast that absorbs damage, a swift mount that scales cliffsides, a cunning predator that strikes from the shadows" — the swift mount is one of only three forms official materials name.
- Mount forms are tied to **taming**: you unlock creature forms by absorbing their essence, so your mount options grow with your exploration.

## How to Unlock a Mount

Mounts unlock through the standard taming process — the confirmed loop for every Henki form:

### Step 1: Find the Right Creature

Not every creature is mountable. Look for **larger, rideable-looking creatures** — community reports point to big grazing and beast-type animals. The official swift mount description ("scales cliffsides") suggests a climbing-capable creature is the key early mount.

### Step 2: Study It

Approach carefully — aggressive creatures attack on sight. Use your journal to study the creature: this reveals its behavior and its preferred food. Some creatures only appear in certain realms or conditions.

### Step 3: Feed It

Offer the creature's preferred food. While it feeds, your Henki studies its essence. Throwing food from a distance can pacify hostile creatures first.

### Step 4: Unlock the Form

Once the feeding completes, the creature's form is permanently unlocked — you can switch to it at any time. The creature itself is unharmed and departs.

**Early-game priority:** a mount form is the single most useful early unlock. The world is large and seamless; walking between realms is slow, and the swift mount is the difference between an evening of travel and an hour.

## The Confirmed Traversal Forms

### Swift Mount — The Essential Form

Official copy: "a swift mount that scales cliffsides." This is the mount you want first:

- **Ground speed** — community reports describe roughly triple running speed.
- **Climbing** — scales cliffsides, opening vertical routes the highlands and mountains demand.
- **Traversal ability** — the form's traversal powers improve as you progress (mount abilities are a confirmed progression track).

**Why it matters:** the game's vertical realms (mountains, forests) have content that is only reachable by climbing. A player without a climbing mount will take long detours; a player with one simply walks up the cliff.

### Giant Snail — Wall Climber (Community Report)

Community reports from the Early Access build describe a snail form that can **climb vertical walls** — reportedly tested on sheer surfaces. If confirmed, this is the most vertical form in the game, useful for:

- Scaling surfaces too smooth for the swift mount's cliff-scaling
- Reaching cave entrances and ruin ledges
- Escaping ground-level boss attacks by climbing out of reach

**Status: unconfirmed** — the developer has not documented this form. Treat details as TBD.

### Bird Glider — Air Traversal (Community Report)

Community reports describe grabbing a bird form's legs and gliding through the air. Gliding gives you:

- Safe descents from mountain peaks and high ledges
- Cross-gap traversal in the mountains and ruins
- Aerial scouting of unfamiliar realms

**Status: unconfirmed** — no official documentation. The glider-style traversal is consistent with the game's BotW inspiration (developer-cited), but specifics are TBD.

## Mount Abilities and Progression

The Steam page confirms you unlock "mount abilities for faster exploration" — mount progression is real and confirmed:

| Stage | What Unlocks |
|-------|--------------|
| Form unlocked | Basic riding and the form's starting traversal |
| Henki progression | Faster sprint, better jumps, improved climbing (community-reported structure; specifics TBD) |
| Companion customization | The Henki is customizable per official copy — expect mount appearance options |

Because mount abilities sit on the Henki track, the way to get better mounts is the same as the way to get any form: **use the Henki, tame creatures, and progress the companion.** Bond and usage-based progression are community-reported; exact unlock conditions are unconfirmed.

## Best Mount Form by Realm

| Realm | Best Form | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Rolling highlands** | Swift mount (climbing) | Open terrain where ground speed wins; cliffsides hide secrets |
| **Dense forests** | Swift mount + reported snail form | Canopy-level content needs vertical climbing |
| **Mountains** | Swift mount + reported bird glider | Vertical cliffs and chasms — climb up, glide down |
| **Ancient ruins** | Any + reported glider | Structure-heavy terrain rewards careful traversal; gliding lets you scout |

**General rule:** unlock the swift mount first, then add the reported snail and bird forms as you find their creatures in the forests and mountains.

## Co-op Mount Tips

- **Everyone needs a mount.** A party where only one player has a mount splits constantly. Get each player their own swift mount early.
- **Scout with the glider.** One player glides ahead and calls out routes while the party rides.
- **Two riders, four roles.** In a 4-player party, mounts let you cover ground in pairs — one pair scouts, one pair gathers.
- **Mounts don't replace forms.** The hulking beast (tank) and cunning predator (burst) still matter for combat — keep a mount as a secondary form, not your only one.

## Mount Etiquette and Practical Tips

Small things that make mount life smoother:

1. **Keep a non-mount form ready.** Switch back before combat — your hulking beast and cunning predator forms are combat tools, and the swift mount is officially a traversal form.
2. **Unlock mounts early, upgrade them steadily.** Mount abilities are a confirmed progression track on the Henki side. Use your mount often; the more you use the Henki, the more the companion track advances (community-reported structure; exact unlock conditions TBD).
3. **Feed your taming targets, not your mount.** Mounts are forms, not pets — they don't need feeding. Save your food for taming new creatures and cooking.
4. **Mark routes.** Community reports suggest resource nodes and secrets are easier to loop when you have a fast route between them; a mount makes route-farming dramatically faster.
5. **Glide before you fall.** If the reported bird glider form is available, use it for descents — it beats walking down a mountain by hours.

## Common Mount Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Skipping the first mount | Walking the seamless world is slow; the highlands-to-forests trip wastes hours | Tame a large creature in the starting region as your second or third form |
| One mount for the whole party | The party splits — riders outpace walkers constantly | Every player gets their own mount form |
| Mounting into combat | The swift mount is traversal, not a battle form; mounted combat is undocumented | Dismount and shift to a combat form before fights |
| Ignoring cliff content | The swift mount's cliff-scaling is its whole point — flat-ground riding misses it | Use the mount's climb, not just its speed |
| Waiting for an official "horse" | There are no separate mounts in Taival — the Henki *is* the mount system | Embrace the shapeshifter; every new creature is a potential vehicle |

## FAQ

**How do I get a mount in Taival?**
Tame a large creature through the standard taming loop — study it, learn its food, feed it — and your Henki unlocks its form. The swift mount (confirmed) is the target early form.

**Can my Henki be a mount?**
Yes — that's exactly what mount forms are. Riding the Henki in a large or swift form is how travel works.

**Is there a fast travel system?**
Not confirmed. The seamless open world has no loading screens, and mounts are the confirmed travel tool; official fast-travel options are not documented (TBD).

**Do mounts help in combat?**
The swift mount is officially a traversal form. Combat mounts are not documented — don't plan your boss strategy around mounted combat (TBD).
