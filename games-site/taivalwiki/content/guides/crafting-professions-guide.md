# Crafting & Professions Guide: All Materials, Recipes & Tips

> Early Access Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Crafting and professions form the backbone of character progression in Taival. While your Henki handles shapeshifting and combat forms, your character levels up through hands-on activities — gathering resources, crafting equipment, and mastering professions. The crafting system in Taival is designed to complement exploration: the more biomes you discover, the more materials and recipes become available.

This guide covers every profession type, how crafting works, where to find materials, and what to craft at each stage of the game.

**Accuracy note (August 2026):** professions & crafting are confirmed Early Access systems, and fishing is explicitly named by the developer as a levelable skill. However, the specific stations, profession list, materials, and recipes below are community reports from launch and largely unconfirmed (TBD). Community reports also vary on which stations exist (some mention an Enchanting Altar and a Mystic Loom alongside the list below) — no official station list has been published. Reviewers also note Taival deliberately downplays the traditional building/gear-crafting loop in favor of Henki progression — treat the details below as a roadmap for verification, not documentation.

## How Crafting Works

Crafting in Taival revolves around two core concepts: crafting stations and recipe unlocks. You cannot craft items from your inventory alone — you need the appropriate station.

### Crafting Stations

Crafting stations are built at your player housing or found in settlement workshops. Each station unlocks a category of recipes:

| Station | Unlocks | Where to Build |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Workbench | Tools, weapons, armor upgrades | Housing, settlement workshops |
| Campfire | Food, healing items, basic materials | Anywhere (portable) |
| Alchemy Table | Potions, buffs, status cures | Housing |
| Forge | Metal weapons, armor, upgrades | Housing (requires advanced materials) |
| Loom | Fabric, bags, cosmetic items | Housing |
| Cooking Station | Advanced meals, party buffs | Housing |

You start with access to the Campfire (a portable crafting station). The Workbench is the first permanent station you should build at your housing plot. Each additional station requires specific materials to construct.

### Recipe Unlocks

Recipes are unlocked through several methods:

- **Leveling professions** — using a profession unlocks new recipes automatically
- **Quest rewards** — many side quests reward unique recipes
- **NPC vendors** — some recipes are sold by settlement traders
- **Exploration** — finding recipe scrolls in chests and ruins
- **Henki forms** — certain forms reveal hidden recipe locations when used in specific biomes

## All Profession Types

Professions in Taival are skill-based activities that you level up through use. Each profession has its own skill tree with unlockable perks and recipes.

### Foraging
The most basic profession. Foraging involves gathering plants, berries, mushrooms, and fibers from the wild. Every biome has unique forageable items.

**Key materials:** Highland herbs, forest mushrooms, mountain roots, ruin moss

**Leveling tips:** Forage everything you see. Even common berries are useful for creature taming and early cooking. Higher foraging levels reveal rare materials on your minimap.

### Mining
Extracts ores, gems, and stone from resource nodes. Mining nodes are concentrated in the Mountains and Ruins biomes, though some appear in caves throughout the world.

**Key materials:** Iron ore, copper ore, silver ore, gemstones, stone blocks

**Leveling tips:** Carry a pickaxe at all times. Mine every node you pass — ore is the bottleneck for most mid-game crafting. The Mountains biome has the highest concentration of nodes.

### Woodcutting
Harvests wood from trees and fallen logs. Wood is the most versatile building material and is used in nearly every crafting station and housing component.

**Key materials:** Softwood, hardwood, ancient wood, resin

**Leveling tips:** The Forests biome offers the best woodcutting yields. Hardwood appears in the deeper forest areas, while Ancient Wood is found only in the Mountains.

### Fishing
Fishing lets you catch aquatic creatures and resources. Fish are used in cooking recipes, creature taming (many creatures prefer fish), and some alchemy recipes.

**Key materials:** Freshwater fish, saltwater fish, rare aquatic creatures, seaweed

**Leveling tips:** Each biome has unique fish. The Highlands have easy catches, while Mountain lakes contain rare fish needed for high-tier recipes. Fishing also levels up as an activity in its own progression track.

### Cooking
Transforms foraged ingredients into meals with buff effects. Cooking is essential for long expeditions — meals provide health regeneration, stamina boosts, and resistance to biome hazards.

**Key recipes:** Highland Stew (health regen), Forest Salad (stamina boost), Mountain Roast (damage buff), Ruin Tea (sanity/safety buff)

**Leveling tips:** Cook in batches. Do not hoard raw ingredients — cooked food lasts longer and provides better benefits. The Cooking Station at your housing unlocks advanced recipes that the portable Campfire cannot make.

### Crafting (General)
The general crafting profession covers tool and equipment creation. This is the profession you use at every crafting station to produce usable items.

**Key recipes:** Tools (pickaxe, axe, fishing rod), weapons, armor, bags, housing components

**Leveling tips:** Craft tools and equipment one tier above your current level. The XP gain from crafting items appropriate to your level is significantly higher than crafting low-tier items.

## Material Gathering Locations

Each of the four biomes offers different materials. Here is what to prioritize in each area:

| Biome | Primary Resources | Rare Resources | Best Profession |
|-------|------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Highlands | Berries, herbs, softwood, copper ore | Silver ore, rare herbs | Foraging, Woodcutting |
| Forests | Hardwood, mushrooms, fibers, iron ore | Ancient Wood, truffles | Woodcutting, Foraging |
| Mountains | Iron ore, stone blocks, gems, ancient wood | Gemstones, rare minerals | Mining, Woodcutting |
| Ruins/Settlements | Stone blocks, rare metals, recipe scrolls | Relic materials, unique recipes | Mining, General Crafting |

**Pro tip:** Use a Swift Mount to cover more ground while gathering. Mark rich resource nodes on your map for return trips. If you have a Pack Mule form, equip it before major gathering runs — the extra inventory space doubles your haul.

## Best Items to Craft by Game Stage

### Early Game (First 5 Hours)

| Item | Crafting Station | Why You Need It |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Stone Pickaxe | Workbench | Enables mining — unlocks ore gathering |
| Basic Axe | Workbench | Faster woodcutting — essential for building |
| Campfire Upgrade | Workbench | Better cooking, longer-lasting food |
| Leather Bag | Workbench (or Loom) | +5 inventory slots — huge quality of life |
| Healing Salve | Campfire | Basic healing without Henki reliance |
| Highland Stew | Campfire | Health regeneration for early exploration |

**Priority:** Build the Workbench first. Then craft a Pickaxe and Axe. Then upgrade your Campfire. The Leather Bag should be your fourth craft.

### Mid Game (Hours 5-15)

| Item | Crafting Station | Why You Need It |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Iron Sword or Spear | Forge | Significant damage upgrade over starter weapons |
| Iron Armor Set | Forge | Makes Mountain biome exploration viable |
| Alchemy Table | Housing | Unlocks potions and buffs |
| Advanced Fishing Rod | Workbench | Unlocks rare fish in Mountain lakes |
| Storage Chests (x3) | Workbench | Critical for managing material inventory |
| Forest Salad | Cooking Station | Stamina boost for extended exploration |

**Priority:** Build the Forge and upgrade your weapon and armor. Then build Storage Chests before your inventory overflows. The Alchemy Table comes next for potion support.

### Late Game / Endgame

| Item | Crafting Station | Why You Need It |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Gem-Infused Weapon | Forge | Best weapon damage available in Early Access |
| Reinforced Armor Set | Forge | Maximum defense for boss fights |
| Party Buff Meals | Cooking Station | Group-wide stat boosts for co-op |
| Legendary Fishing Rod | Workbench | Required for the rarest aquatic catches |
| Housing Decorations | Loom / Workbench | Unlocks housing perks and bonuses |
| Relic Tools | Special (Ruins) | Opens locked doors in Ruins biome |

**Priority:** Focus on Gem-Infused Weapons first for the damage spike. Then Reinforced Armor for survivability. Party buff meals become essential for the hardest encounters.

## Profession Synergies with Henki Forms

Your professions and Henki forms can support each other in several ways:

| Profession | Synergistic Henki Form | Benefit |
|-----------|----------------------|---------|
| Foraging | Swift Mount | Cover more ground, find resources faster |
| Mining | Tank Beast | Survive cave encounters while mining |
| Fishing | Aquatic Form | Reach underwater fishing spots |
| Woodcutting | Pack Mule | Carry more wood per trip |
| Cooking | Base Healer | Combine passive healing with food buffs |
| Gathering Resources | Golden Variant | Find gold while gathering |

**Best profession setup for co-op:** Have one player focus on Foraging + Mining (resource gathering), one on Cooking + Alchemy (consumable production), and everyone level Woodcutting for housing materials.

## Profession Leveling Tips

- **Do not skip low-tier materials.** Even common resources are needed for profession XP. High-tier gathering only gives good XP at high profession levels.
- **Use profession boosts.** Some food items and Henki forms provide temporary profession XP bonuses. Stack them before a dedicated farming session.
- **Craft in batches.** Crafting a single item gives minimal XP. Crafting 10 items in a row gives escalating XP bonuses for repeated crafts.
- **Keep a notebook.** Remember which biomes have which resources. Efficient routing saves enormous time when farming specific materials.
