# Taival — Henki Dungeons Guide

> Early Access Version / Updated: July 27, 2027

Henki Dungeons are one of Taival's most unique features — self-contained challenge areas where you temporarily take direct control of your Henki companion instead of your character. These dungeons test your mastery of each Henki form through combat encounters, traversal puzzles, and environmental challenges.

Completing Henki Dungeons unlocks exclusive form upgrades, new traits, and permanent bonuses that carry over to the main game.

---

## What Are Henki Dungeons?

Henki Dungeons are special instanced areas scattered across the world. When you enter one, your character remains at the entrance while your Henki ventures inside alone. You control the Henki directly using its unique moveset for that form.

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Player Control** | You control the Henki, not your character |
| **Co-op** | Each player's Henki enters separately — you cannot help each other |
| **Form Locked** | Each dungeon is designed for a specific Henki form |
| **Death Consequences** | Henki takes bond damage (recoverable by using the form afterward) |
| **One-Time Rewards** | Each dungeon grants unique rewards on first completion |
| **Repeatable** | Can be replayed for practice, but rewards are one-time |

## How to Access Henki Dungeons

### Finding Dungeon Entrances

Henki Dungeon entrances appear as glowing, rune-marked archways. They are typically located near the biome where you first unlock the corresponding Henki form.

| Dungeon | Location | Requirement |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Burrower's Warren** | Highlands — near the starting village well | Burrower form unlocked |
| **Shadow's Trial** | Forests — central grove, behind a mossy wall | Shadow Stalker form unlocked |
| **Cliff Strider's Peak** | Mountains — base of the first climbable cliff | Cliff Strider form unlocked |
| **Guardian's Relic** | Ruins — temple courtyard | Guardian form unlocked |
| **Glimmer Moth's Labyrinth** | Ruins — hidden behind an illusion wall (Forests lore page reveals location) | Glimmer Moth form unlocked |
| **Boulder Beast's Crag** | Mountains — summit of the eastern peak | Boulder Beast form + Swiftwing or Cliff Strider to reach entrance |

### Unlocking Dungeon Access

Dungeon entrances are visible from the moment you enter a biome, but they remain sealed until:

1. You have unlocked the required Henki form
2. You have used that form for at least 10 minutes (Practiced mastery level)
3. You interact with the seal at the entrance to activate the challenge

## Controlling Your Henki Solo

When you enter a Henki Dungeon, the controls change to reflect your Henki's unique abilities instead of your character's.

### Basic Henki Controls

| Action | Input | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Move** | WASD / Left stick | Henki movement speed varies by form |
| **Primary Attack** | Left click / R2 | Unique to each form |
| **Secondary Ability** | Right click / L2 | Unique to each form |
| **Form Ability** | Q / L1 + R1 | The form's signature ability |
| **Dodge** | Space / Circle | Henki dodge is faster than character dodge |
| **Heal** | E / Triangle | Limited heals per dungeon (2–3 charges) |

### Henki Health & Bond

Your Henki has its own health pool inside dungeons. If it reaches zero, the dungeon run fails and the Henki takes bond damage:

| Bond Level | Effect of Dungeon Failure |
|------------|--------------------------|
| 100–75% | No penalty — Henki returns at the entrance immediately |
| 74–50% | 5-minute cooldown before you can re-enter a dungeon |
| 49–25% | 15-minute cooldown + Henki form abilities reduced by 20% until recovered |
| Below 25% | Cannot enter dungeons until bond recovers |

**Recovering Bond:** Use the Henki form in the open world for 5–10 minutes to restore bond to full.

## Dungeon Types

### Combat Dungeons

These dungeons focus on defeating waves of enemies using your Henki form's combat abilities.

**Example: Guardian's Relic**

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Form Required** | Guardian |
| **Enemies** | 3 waves of ruin constructs + 1 mini-boss |
| **Time Limit** | None |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |

**Strategy:**
1. Guardian form excels at absorbing damage. Use your primary ability (shield bash) to stagger groups.
2. The mini-boss has a charge attack with a visible wind-up. Use your secondary ability (stone barrier) to block it.
3. Between waves, use the healing charges sparingly — save at least 1 for the mini-boss.

**Rewards:** Guardian trait: "Unyielding" — +20% damage resistance while Guardian form is active.

### Puzzle Dungeons

These dungeons test your understanding of the Henki form's utility abilities rather than combat prowess.

**Example: Glimmer Moth's Labyrinth**

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Form Required** | Glimmer Moth |
| **Puzzle Count** | 5 |
| **Time Limit** | 10 minutes |
| **Difficulty** | Medium-Hard |

**Strategy:**
1. Glimmer Moth's light-reveal ability shows hidden platforms and paths — use it constantly.
2. Some platforms appear only when you stand on specific pressure plates. Use the moth's hover ability to stay airborne while the plate is pressed.
3. The final room has a rotating maze with walls that shift every 30 seconds. Memorize the pattern before moving.
4. Time is tight. Do not pause between puzzles.

**Rewards:** Glimmer Moth trait: "Luminous Trail" — reveals hidden objects for 5 seconds after switching out of Glimmer Moth form.

### Traversal Dungeons

These dungeons are obstacle courses designed to test your movement mastery with a specific Henki form.

**Example: Cliff Strider's Peak**

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Form Required** | Cliff Strider |
| **Checkpoints** | 3 (at 25%, 50%, 75% completion) |
| **Time Limit** | 5 minutes |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |

**Strategy:**
1. Wall jumps are the core mechanic. You can chain up to 3 wall jumps before needing to grab a ledge.
2. Some walls crumble after one use — do not linger on them.
3. Moving platforms appear in the second section. Time your jumps to match their cycle.
4. The final section has wind gusts that push you away from walls. Angle your jumps into the wind to compensate.

**Rewards:** Cliff Strider trait: "Sure-Footed" — wall jumps no longer consume stamina in the open world.

### Hybrid Dungeons

The most complex dungeon type, combining combat, puzzles, and traversal in sequence.

**Example: Shadow's Trial**

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Form Required** | Shadow Stalker |
| **Phases** | Traversal → Stealth → Combat → Boss |
| **Time Limit** | 15 minutes |
| **Difficulty** | Hard |

**Strategy:**
1. **Traversal Phase:** Navigate through a dark forest canopy using jump pads and narrow branches. Use Shadow Stalker's pounce ability to close gaps.
2. **Stealth Phase:** Avoid detection by roaming light-beasts. Shadow Stalker's invisibility ability has a short duration — time it between the beasts' patrol cycles.
3. **Combat Phase:** Fight 2 elite shadow creatures simultaneously. Hit-and-run tactics work best — pounce, strike, retreat to shadow.
4. **Boss Phase:** A large shadow elemental. It is immune to direct attacks. Lure it into the light crystals around the arena to stun it, then strike.

**Rewards:** Shadow Stalker trait: "Deep Shadow" — invisibility duration increased by 50%.

## General Dungeon Tips

- **Enter with full bond.** If your Henki bond is below 50%, recover it before attempting a dungeon. Failed runs are frustrating and costly.
- **Learn the form first.** Before attempting any Henki Dungeon, spend 15–20 minutes using that form in the open world. Understanding its movement and attack timings is essential.
- **Healing charges are limited.** Do not waste them on minor damage. Wait until you are below 50% HP to heal.
- **Time-limited dungeons are punishing.** If you fail a timed dungeon, watch your recording or mentally note where you lost time. Most failures come from hesitation, not lack of skill.
- **Co-op note:** Each player's Henki dungeon run is independent. You cannot help your friends inside, but you can offer advice from your own experience. Share strategies before attempting hard dungeons as a group.
- **Dungeon order recommendation:** Burrower's Warren (easiest) → Guardian's Relic → Cliff Strider's Peak → Shadow's Trial → Glimmer Moth's Labyrinth → Boulder Beast's Crag (hardest).
