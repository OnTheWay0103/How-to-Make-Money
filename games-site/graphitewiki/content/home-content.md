## What is Graphite?

Graphite is a pen-and-ink tactical roguelike RPG developed by RipRed (Colombia) and published by indie.io. Released on July 27, 2026, it combines the strategic depth of turn-based tactics with roguelike replayability, wrapped in a distinctive hand-drawn ink art style.

The game takes place inside a child's school notebook: pencil-drawn heroes, unaware they are figments of a child's imagination, are tasked by a mysterious figure called **The Scribe** with growing stronger and gathering allies before **Death** arrives. Each run is shaped by the Tarot adventure deck — a dynamic event system of narrated, branching choices influenced by your party, your items, and your prior decisions. Combat unfolds on a visual timeline where every action has a duration, and the Break system rewards precise timing with interrupts.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **7 Heroes** — the roster count is confirmed; only The Knight (shield + Break) and The Mage (spells + defensive support) are confirmed by name
- **Timeline Combat** — a shared timeline where every action has a visible duration; multiple units act simultaneously
- **Break System** — depleting an enemy's Poise to zero cancels their scheduled action and pushes their turn down the timeline; the rules are fully symmetric
- **Unlimited Artifact Stacking** — no cap on artifacts; duplicates amplify, and enemies acquire artifacts too (an arms race)
- **Tarot Adventure Deck** — a card-based event system with branching, narrated choices shaped by party, items, and prior decisions
- **Friendship System** — heroes build relationships during a run (up to level 1 in the demo); how this connects to unlocks is unconfirmed
- **4-Act Campaign** — four acts of 15 days each, per the developer's launch materials; details of acts 2–4 are unconfirmed
- **Meta-Progression** — a roguelite: artifacts, events, and unlockables persist across runs in some form; the exact scope is unconfirmed
