## What is Graphite?

Graphite is a pen-and-ink tactical roguelike RPG developed by RipRed (Colombia) and published by indie.io. Released on July 27, 2026, it combines the strategic depth of turn-based tactics with roguelike replayability, wrapped in a distinctive hand-drawn ink art style.

Assemble a party of up to 3 heroes (from a roster of 7) and embark on a 4-act campaign. Each run is shaped by the Tarot adventure deck — a dynamic event system that determines encounters, shops, rest sites, and story beats. Combat unfolds on a visual timeline where every action's speed determines turn order, and the Break system rewards precise timing with bonus damage and crowd control.

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **7 Heroes** — Warrior, Rogue, Hunter, Monk, Mage, Bard, Tamer — each with 4 unique abilities and passive skills
- **Timeline Combat** — Turn order is visible and dynamic; fast actions move you earlier, heavy actions push you back
- **Break System** — Stagger enemies during their turn window for bonus damage and action denial
- **Unlimited Artifact Stacking** — Equip unlimited artifacts; duplicates stack for multiplicative power
- **Tarot Adventure Deck** — A card-based event system that shapes each run's path, encounters, and rewards
- **Bond System** — Heroes build relationships during a run, unlocking shared stats and combo attacks
- **4-Act Campaign** — Progressive difficulty with unique biomes, enemy rosters, and boss encounters per act
- **Meta-Progression** — Earn currency across runs to unlock permanent upgrades, artifacts, and hero enhancements
