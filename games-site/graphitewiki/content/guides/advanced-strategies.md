## Advanced Strategies Guide — Artifact Stacking & Deck Control

Graphite's signature mechanic is unlimited artifact stacking — but power comes with consequences, because enemies scale alongside you. Advanced play is about managing that scaling: when to stack, when to hold, and how to steer the tarot deck toward events that multiply your artifacts instead of your enemies.

### Artifact Stacking Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Individual artifacts | Stack per hero, apply at each level-up |
| Group artifacts | Apply to the whole party simultaneously |
| Scaling trigger | Enemy power scales off your total artifact count every region |
| Cap | No hard cap, but diminishing value past 12 stacks |

1. **Stack to 12, then stop:** 12 artifact stacks beat any enemy scaling curve. Past that, each artifact's value is outweighed by the enemy HP increase it triggers.
2. **Group artifacts first:** A group artifact (e.g., +10% party damage) stacks once for the whole team — always prioritize these when offered.
3. **Timing matters:** Take combat artifacts before a boss region, utility artifacts after — scaling checks happen at region entry.

### Tarot Deck Control

- **Deck knowledge:** The adventure deck has defined event pools per region. Memorize which region holds the artifact-bonus events.
- **Branch choices:** Events that offer "artifact + enemy buff" are almost always worth it before a boss; avoid them early.
- **Party composition affects draws:** Bringing a Mage increases spell-event frequency; a Bard increases social events. Build your deck by building your party.
- **Key events:** "The Armory" (2 random artifacts), "Echo of Power" (double an existing artifact), and "Bargain" (trade HP for artifact levels) are run-defining.

### Team Composition Strategies

| Composition | Core | Strength | Weakness |
|-------------|------|----------|----------|
| Stack Party | Warrior + Monk + Hunter | Massive single-target burst | Fragile early |
| Control Party | Mage + Bard + Rogue | Event control, safe scaling | Low burst |
| Sustain Party | Monk + Bard + Tamer | Long fights, high safety | Slow clears |

### Boss Fight Tactics

1. **Scouting:** Check the boss's scaling count before engaging — a boss with 15+ scaled stats is a trap; farm more artifacts instead.
2. **Burst windows:** Stack all group artifacts before the boss's mid-fight phase change, then trigger your heaviest cooldowns.
3. **Fallback plan:** Keep one utility artifact (shield or heal) unstacked as a panic button — enemies that scale HP don't outscale a full heal.

### Advanced Checklist

1. Track your artifact count against enemy scaling each region.
2. Save deck-altering events for the region before the boss.
3. Never enter a boss region below 8 stacks in late game.
4. Reroll risky events when your party lacks the counters.
5. End every run by calculating what you could have stacked more efficiently.
