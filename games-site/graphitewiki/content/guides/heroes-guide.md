## Hero Roster Overview

Graphite has **7 heroes**, each with a unique personality, backstory, and playstyle. You start each run by selecting one hero, and you gather allies as you progress. Two important confirmed facts shape how you should read any hero content:

1. **Only two heroes are officially confirmed by name**: **The Knight** and **The Mage**. The other five have not been revealed in public coverage.
2. **Your starting hero changes the story** — the game is designed to be played with all 7 heroes to see the full narrative.

This guide covers the confirmed heroes in detail and is honest about what is still unconfirmed. Any content naming other heroes (Warrior, Rogue, Hunter, Monk, Bard, Tamer, etc.) and detailing their abilities is unsourced — treat it as speculation.

## Hero Roster Status

| Hero | Status | Known Details |
|------|--------|---------------|
| **The Knight** | Confirmed (demo) | Shield-based abilities, exploits the Break system, significant damage |
| **The Mage** | Confirmed (demo) | Offensive spells, boosts allies' defenses |
| **5 other heroes** | Unconfirmed | Unique personality, backstory, playstyle each — names and abilities TBD |

The free **Graphite: Act 1** demo includes 4 of the 7 heroes. Of the four, only Knight and Mage are named in coverage so far.

## 1. The Knight — Shield and Break

**Play if**: You want a durable frontline hero built around the game's core mechanic, or you're new to the game.

The Knight fights with a shield and is built around the **Break system** — depleting an enemy's Poise to cancel their scheduled action and push their turn down the timeline. Preview coverage describes shield-based abilities that take advantage of Break and deal **significant damage**.

**Strengths**: durable, Break-focused kit that teaches the game's signature system, forgiving for new players, strong damage despite the defensive identity
**Weaknesses**: the defensive posture means the Knight is not a pure glass-cannon damage dealer — burst output is not his specialty (exact trade-offs unconfirmed)

**Best teammate**: The Mage — the Mage's defensive support stacks with the Knight's shield for strong frontline coverage.

**Bond tip**: As a starter, the Knight gives the most forgiving first act in the demo — learn Break timing with him before trying squishier heroes.

## 2. The Mage — Offense and Support

**Play if**: You prefer ranged offense and don't mind managing a less durable hero.

The Mage casts offensive spells and doubles as a support unit by **boosting allies' defenses**. Coverage highlights both sides of the kit: real spell damage output and party protection.

**Strengths**: flexible — primary damage dealer that can pivot to protecting the party, strong against groups, natural pairing with frontline heroes
**Weaknesses**: spellcasters are typically less durable in close combat (specific weaknesses unconfirmed)

**Best teammate**: The Knight — he holds the frontline and Breaks while the Mage deals damage and shields the party.

## The Other Five Heroes — Honest Status

Five heroes are unconfirmed. Everything known about them comes from developer statements:

- Each has a **unique personality, backstory, and playstyle**
- Each has their own **narrative arc**
- Choosing any of them as a starter changes story events and progression

Names, abilities, and unlock methods are **TBD**. This section will be updated as official reveals and the July 27, 2026 release provide real information. Until then:

- Do not trust claimed names or ability lists for these heroes
- Do not trust tier rankings of them
- Do not trust "unlock requirements" for them
