## Hero Overview

Graphite launches with 7 heroes, each with a unique role, abilities, and Break properties. You start each run with one primary hero and unlock additional party members through the Bond system — spend camp time and complete combat objectives together to raise Bond levels.

| Hero | Role | Difficulty | Break Style | Best For |
|------|------|------------|-------------|----------|
| **Warrior** | Durable Melee | Easy | Physical interruption | Beginners, frontline control |
| **Rogue** | Stealth/Crit | Hard | Precision strike | Burst damage, Break chaining |
| **Hunter** | Ranged Damage | Medium | Distance pressure | Kiting, sustained damage |
| **Monk** | Martial Arts | Medium | Combo building | High skill ceiling, fast pace |
| **Mage** | Spell Damage | Easy | Arcane disruption | AoE damage, crowd control |
| **Bard** | Support/Buffs | Medium | Timeline manipulation | Team synergy, control |
| **Tamer** | Pet/Companion | Hard | Dual-body pressure | Multi-target, battlefield control |

## 1. Warrior — The Unbreakable Vanguard

**Play if**: You want a durable frontline hero who controls enemy positioning and soaks damage.

The Warrior excels at holding the line. His taunt forces enemies to target him, his shield blocks grant Break resistance, and his heavy strikes push enemies back on the timeline. He is the only hero who can reliably tank multiple enemies without losing turn order.

**Strengths**: Highest base HP, built-in Break resistance, taunt controls enemy targeting, simple and effective toolkit
**Weaknesses**: Slow action speed, predictable attack patterns, low damage output compared to dedicated damage dealers

**Best Artifacts**: Iron Will (increases Break resistance while above 50% HP), Guardian's Crest (reduces damage to adjacent allies), Heavy Timber (adds knockback to all physical attacks)

**Bond Tip**: Pair the Warrior with the Mage early — her arcane shields stack with his natural defenses for near-impenetrable frontline coverage.

## 2. Rogue — The Shadow Striker

**Play if**: You love critical hit chains, stealth positioning, and deleting high-priority targets.

The Rogue operates in the shadows. Her stealth ability removes her from the timeline entirely — enemies cannot target her and her next attack deals massive bonus damage. The challenge is timing: stealth has a long action duration, so you must plan your invisibility windows carefully.

**Strengths**: Highest single-target damage in the game, stealth bypasses enemy Break mechanics, critical hits push enemies further back on the timeline
**Weaknesses**: Extremely fragile when exposed, long cooldowns on stealth, requires precise timeline reading

**Best Artifacts**: Shadow Clasp (stealth actions take fewer ticks), Serrated Edge (crits apply a bleed that ticks on the enemy's turn), Duskwalker's Boots (reduce threat generation by 40%)

## 3. Hunter — The Patient Marksman

**Play if**: You prefer ranged combat, consistent damage, and controlling enemy approach.

The Hunter maintains distance with a bow and tracking abilities. Her marking system applies debuffs that increase damage from all sources, and her traps can delay enemy actions on the timeline. She is the safest hero to play but requires map awareness to maintain optimal range.

**Strengths**: Safe ranged positioning, consistent damage output, traps manipulate enemy timeline position, tracking reveals hidden nodes on the map
**Weaknesses**: Struggles in close range, low Break damage, traps have setup time

**Best Artifacts**: Hawk-Eye Lens (increases range and accuracy), Barbed Arrows (marks also slow enemy action speed), Trapper's Satchel (traps deploy one tick faster)

## 4. Monk — The Flowing Fist

**Play if**: You enjoy fast-paced combo systems and high-skill-ceiling gameplay.

The Monk builds combo stacks with each successive hit on the same target. At maximum stacks, his finisher attack deals massive damage and applies a heavy Break. The Monk's action durations decrease as his combo counter rises, letting him act more frequently in extended fights.

**Strengths**: Fastest attack speed at full combo, excellent Break application, mobility skills reposition quickly, scales well into late game
**Weaknesses**: Vulnerable during combo startup, loses stacks if forced to change targets, requires consistent timeline access

**Best Artifacts**: Flowing River Style (combo stacks decay slower), Iron Fist (each stack adds Break damage), Wind Step (dodge actions are faster and grant a free combo stack)

## 5. Mage — The Arcane Storm

**Play if**: You want powerful area damage, crowd control, and battlefield shaping.

The Mage channels spells that hit multiple enemies and slow their timeline progress. Her arcane shields provide party-wide Break protection, and her ultimate ability can reset the entire battlefield timeline. She is the strongest AoE hero in the game and a staple of most winning compositions.

**Strengths**: Best area damage, arcane shield protects the whole party from Break, timeline reset ultimate, strong against grouped enemies
**Weaknesses**: Low physical defense, long cast times leave her vulnerable, mana management adds resource pressure

**Best Artifacts**: Arcane Focus (spells deal 20% more damage but take one extra tick), Crystal Shield (arcane shield also reflects Break damage), Timekeeper's Sand (timeline reset ultimate charges faster)

## 6. Bard — The Master of Time

**Play if**: You enjoy supporting your team, controlling the flow of battle, and manipulating the timeline.

The Bard does not deal the highest damage, but he controls when damage happens. His songs speed up allies, slow enemies, and create Break windows that your damage dealers exploit. A good Bard makes every other hero better. He is the strongest force multiplier in the game.

**Strengths**: Only hero who can speed up allies' actions, debuffs slow enemy timeline, creates Break windows on demand, camp bonuses improve Bond gains
**Weaknesses**: Low personal damage, fragile in direct combat, songs require setup ticks before they activate

**Best Artifacts**: Lute of Acceleration (action speed buff applies to entire party), Discordant Note (debuffed enemies take bonus Break damage), Traveler's Song (reduces travel time on the map)

## 7. Tamer — The Beast Whisperer

**Play if**: You enjoy managing two units on the battlefield and controlling space with a companion.

The Tamer fights alongside a summoned beast that acts independently on the timeline. You control both the Tamer's actions and the beast's behavior, effectively giving you two turns per timeline cycle. This dual-body pressure makes the Tamer incredibly powerful but also the most complex hero to pilot.

**Strengths**: Two units on the timeline doubles your action economy, beast can tank separately from the Tamer, versatile pet types for different situations, excellent at controlling multiple enemies
**Weaknesses**: Highest cognitive load of any hero, beast death leaves the Tamer severely weakened, pet AI can be unpredictable, requires strong multitasking

**Best Artifacts**: Beastmaster's Call (pet respawns faster after defeat), Pack Leader (both Tamer and beast gain buffs when fighting the same target), Bonded Collar (beast inherits 30% of the Tamer's Break resistance)

## Hero Synergies

| Core Pair | Why It Works |
|-----------|-------------|
| Warrior + Mage | Tank holds aggro while Mage AoE destroys grouped enemies |
| Rogue + Bard | Bard speeds up Rogue's stealth, creating more crit windows |
| Hunter + Tamer | Both control space — traps plus beast body block enemies |
| Monk + Bard | Bard's speed buff accelerates Monk's combo building |
