## What Resources Does Graphite Actually Have?

Let's be honest about one thing first: Graphite has **no confirmed gold, currency, or shop economy**. No official source describes buying, selling, or trading items for money, and the launch materials never mention a material-crafting economy. Guides that quote gold costs or merchant prices for Graphite are unsourced — treat them as fabricated.

What the game actually has is a **resource economy built around five things**: artifacts, skill points, bonds, time, and run-to-run meta-progression. Each one is earned differently, spent differently, and misused differently. This guide covers what is confirmed about each, per the official press release, the Steam listings, and verified launch coverage.

## Artifacts — Your Main "Currency"

Artifacts are the closest thing Graphite has to wealth. Per the official launch press release, **artifacts are pulled from a deck of cards** — each draw is a choice that shapes the run ahead rather than a purchase. The confirmed rules:

- **Unlimited stacking** — there is no cap on artifacts, and duplicates of the same artifact amplify its effect
- **Rare and legendary tiers** — the launch materials confirm artifacts come in at least these two tiers; legendaries "build and detonate across consecutive hits"
- **Enemies acquire artifacts too** — the other side gets the same tools, so every fight is an arms race

The free Act 1 demo contains **50 stackable artifacts** (per the Steam listing), which makes the demo an ideal place to learn how draws and stacks feel before the full game. Because the demo is a full act (roughly 30 minutes per run), you get enough draws across a few runs to feel the difference between a committed stack and a grab-bag. Management advice: **stack with intent**. Because duplicates compound, a deliberate single-direction stack outperforms a grab-bag of singles — and unequipped artifacts contribute nothing. The full build logic lives in the [Best Builds & Artifacts Guide](/guides/builds-artifacts-guide).

## Skill Points — Spend Them Early

After each battle your party levels up, and you **spend points to upgrade chosen skills**, steering your build as the run develops. This is confirmed by launch coverage and the demo. The common mistake is hoarding: committed builds outperform unspent points, because enemy encounters scale alongside your progress. Spend toward a direction early — you can always lean further into it when duplicate artifacts arrive.

## Bonds — The Relationship Resource

Graphite's launch materials confirm a **bond system**: players "deepen relationships with party members across the journey to unlock new story and progression." The demo's Steam listing confirms friendship levels between characters (up to **level 1** in the demo build). The Scribe's mission — grow stronger and **gather allies** — frames hero relationships as a campaign resource, not a side feature.

What is **not** confirmed: how bonds are gained (fights together, events, camp activities — all unconfirmed), any friendship thresholds, and how bonds connect to unlocking heroes. No official numbers exist; any guide quoting specific friendship thresholds is unsourced. See the [How to Unlock Heroes guide](/guides/how-to-unlock-heroes) for the full honest picture.

## Time — The One Resource You Can't Recover

Graphite runs on an in-game clock. The launch materials confirm the campaign spans **four acts, 15 days each**, with "possible loops and a climactic fourth act" — and the clock is always visible: *"The clock is always visible. The pressure is always real."*

Time matters because it converts directly into enemy power: encounters are procedurally generated and **scale alongside your progress**, and enemies pick up artifacts mid-fight. Dragging fights out and wasting days gives the other side more time to stack its own economy. Practically: finish fights efficiently, don't skip events (their rewards feed your artifact stack and bonds), and treat every day as spendable.

## Meta-Progression — What Carries Between Runs

Graphite is a roguelite: **artifacts, events, and unlockables persist across runs in some form** — this is confirmed in general. The exact scope (which unlocks carry, how hero progression works between runs, what resets) is **unconfirmed**, and the story also responds to prior decisions, so earlier runs influence later events. Because the details are unpublished, treat any specific "unlock carries over" tables as unsourced.

## Resource Management Checklist

1. **Artifacts**: commit to a direction — one focused stack beats a grab-bag; draws are the only acquisition method confirmed so far
2. **Skill points**: spend after every battle; committed builds outperform hoarded ones
3. **Bonds**: use heroes together across fights and events — the confirmed mechanic is "deepening relationships," the exact triggers are unconfirmed
4. **Time**: respect the 15-day act clock and the visible countdown; long fights let enemies scale
5. **Meta-progression**: play for learning and unlocks, not just one winning run — the deck changes between runs and decisions echo forward
