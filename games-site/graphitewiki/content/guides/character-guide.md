## Graphite Character Guide

Graphite features four playable characters, each with a distinct artistic style that manifests as unique gameplay abilities. The story weaves their personal journeys into the central mystery, and your choice of which character to control in any given scene affects both puzzle solutions and narrative outcomes. This guide covers each character's abilities, skill progression, and optimal use cases.

## Character Overview

| Character | Artistic Style | Primary Ability | Role in Story |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Clara** | Sketch artist | Reality Sketching | The seeker of truth |
| **Marcus** | Sculptor | Material Manipulation | The protector |
| **Elena** | Musician | Sound Weaving | The empath |
| **Dante** | Painter | Chromatic Shift | The visionary |

## Clara — The Sketch Artist

Clara uses her sketchbook to draw objects into existence. Her Reality Sketching ability allows her to create temporary platforms, bridges, and tools. She is the most versatile character for environmental puzzles.

### Abilities

- **Sketch Platform**: Draws a flat surface that persists for 15 seconds. Can support one character's weight. Upgraded versions last longer and support multiple characters.
- **Sketch Bridge**: Creates a bridge spanning gaps up to 5 meters. Unlocked at skill level 3.
- **Sketch Tool**: Creates a simple object (key, lever, crank) that interacts with the environment. Unlocked at skill level 5.
- **Master Sketch**: Creates a complex object that persists for 60 seconds. Unlocked at skill level 8.

### Skill Progression

| Skill Level | Unlock | Upgrade Effect |
|-------------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Sketch Platform (basic) | 10 second duration |
| 2 | Sketch Platform (upgrade 1) | 15 second duration, supports 2 characters |
| 3 | Sketch Bridge | Basic bridge, 5 meter span |
| 4 | Sketch Platform (upgrade 2) | 20 second duration, animated surface |
| 5 | Sketch Tool | Lever, crank, and key variations |
| 6 | Sketch Bridge (upgrade) | 8 meter span, can be angled |
| 7 | Sketch Platform (max) | 30 second duration, supports 3 characters |
| 8 | Master Sketch | Complex objects, 60 second duration |

### Recommended Use Cases

Clara is essential for navigating vertical environments and reaching inaccessible areas. Use her to:
- Create platforms to reach high ledges
- Bridge gaps between buildings in the Cityscape chapter
- Sketch keys for locked doors when the proper key cannot be found
- Provide traversal support for other characters in co-op puzzle scenarios

## Marcus — The Sculptor

Marcus shapes the environment directly, moving and reshaping physical objects. His Material Manipulation is limited to existing inorganic matter but offers permanent changes rather than temporary sketches.

### Abilities

- **Shape Stone**: Molds stone objects into new forms. Can create staircases, pillars, and barriers.
- **Move Object**: Lifts and moves heavy objects up to 3 meters. Unlocked at skill level 2.
- **Crack Surface**: Creates fractures in weak walls and floors. Unlocked at skill level 4.
- **Bind Material**: Fuses loose objects into permanent structures. Unlocked at skill level 7.

### Recommended Use Cases

Marcus is the brute-force problem solver. Use him to:
- Clear blocked pathways by moving debris
- Create permanent staircases in areas that require repeated visits
- Crack open sealed doors and hidden passages
- Stabilize collapsing structures during chase sequences

## Elena — The Musician

Elena uses sound to interact with the world. Her Sound Weaving ability is the most abstract but also the most powerful for solving auditory puzzles and influencing living beings.

### Abilities

- **Resonance Tone**: Emits a tone that resonates with specific materials, causing them to vibrate or shatter.
- **Harmonic Bridge**: Creates a bridge of solidified sound waves. Unlocked at skill level 3.
- **Empathic Echo**: Reveals the emotional state of non-player characters and influences their behavior. Unlocked at skill level 5.
- **Symphony of Form**: Massively alters the environment by matching its resonant frequency. Unlocked at skill level 9.

### Recommended Use Cases

Elena excels in social and auditory challenges. Use her to:
- Shatter glass barriers with resonance
- Calm aggressive characters using Empathic Echo
- Navigate the Sound Maze in the Opera House chapter
- Reveal hidden passages by finding their resonant frequency

## Dante — The Painter

Dante alters reality by changing colors. Chromatic Shift allows him to change the properties of objects by painting them with different colors taken from his palette.

### Abilities

- **Paint Shift**: Changes an object's color, altering its physical properties. Red is heavy, Blue is light, Yellow is conductive, Green is organic.
- **Chromatic Bridge**: Paints a path across gaps. The path's durability depends on the color used. Unlocked at skill level 3.
- **Color Bomb**: Releases a burst of color that affects all objects in a radius. Unlocked at skill level 6.
- **Masterpiece**: Paints the entire scene, changing the fundamental rules of the area. Unlocked at skill level 10.

### Color Properties

| Color | Object Property | Best Used For |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Red | Heavy, solid, sinks in water | Weighing down platforms, triggering pressure switches |
| Blue | Light, floats, drifts upward | Crossing water, reaching floating platforms |
| Yellow | Conductive, attracts electricity | Powering circuits, redirecting lightning |
| Green | Organic, grows, interacts with nature | Activating plant growth, natural barriers |

### Recommended Use Cases

Dante is the most creative problem solver. Use him to:
- Change platform properties to solve weight-based puzzles
- Conduct electricity through yellow-painted pathways
- Make heavy objects light enough to carry across gaps
- Paint environments to match the required color state for progression

## Character Synergies

Certain puzzles require multiple characters working together:

- **Clara + Marcus**: Clara sketches a platform, Marcus shapes it into a permanent structure
- **Elena + Dante**: Elena finds the resonant frequency, Dante paints the object to match
- **Marcus + Dante**: Marcus moves a heavy object, Dante makes it blue to float across a gap
- **All Four**: The endgame puzzles require all four characters to act in sequence
