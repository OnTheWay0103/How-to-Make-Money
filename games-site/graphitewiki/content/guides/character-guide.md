## Graphite Character Guide

Graphite features **seven playable heroes**, each with a distinct personality, backstory, and playstyle. The game is set inside a child's school notebook: the heroes are pencil-drawn characters who do not know they are figments of a child's imagination, and they are tasked by a mysterious figure called **The Scribe** with growing stronger and gathering allies before **Death** arrives.

This guide covers the characters confirmed so far, how hero choice shapes the story, and what we know about progression. Anything not yet confirmed by official sources is marked unconfirmed.

## Character Roster Status

| Character | Status | Known Details |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **The Knight** | Confirmed (demo) | Shield-based abilities, exploits the Break system, deals significant damage |
| **The Mage** | Confirmed (demo) | Offensive spells, supports allies by boosting defenses |
| 5 other heroes | Unconfirmed | Present in the full game; names and abilities not yet revealed in public coverage |

The free **Graphite: Act 1** demo on Steam includes 4 of the 7 heroes. Of those, launch coverage has confirmed only The Knight and The Mage by name. The remaining demo heroes — and the three heroes exclusive to the full game — are not officially named anywhere in public coverage at the time of writing. Any guide that assigns them specific names or abilities is working from unconfirmed community speculation.

## The Knight — Shield and Break

The Knight is the most clearly documented hero in pre-release coverage. He fights with a shield and is built around Graphite's signature **Break system**: depleting an enemy's Poise to cancel their scheduled action and push their turn down the timeline.

- **Playstyle**: defensive frontline; shield abilities create Break opportunities and absorb pressure
- **Damage**: coverage notes the Knight "deals significant damage" despite the defensive kit
- **Beginner fit**: the shield gives you room to learn timeline reading and Break timing without being punished for every mistake

Because the Break system is the game's core combat mechanic — and because it works symmetrically against you — the Knight is widely expected to be the most forgiving starting hero for a first run. This is consistent with the demo's design, though a full difficulty assessment is unconfirmed.

## The Mage — Offense and Support

The Mage is the other hero confirmed in demo coverage. She casts offensive spells and also acts as a support unit by boosting other characters' defenses.

- **Playstyle**: ranged offense with a support side; the defensive buffs let you protect fragile party members
- **Damage**: strong spell output, especially against groups
- **Team role**: flexible — a primary damage dealer that can pivot to protection

The Mage's defensive support pairs naturally with the Knight's frontline: the Knight holds attention and Poise pressure while the Mage deals damage and shields the party. That pairing appears in multiple previews, though dedicated synergy analysis will have to wait for the full game.

## Starting Hero Choice Changes the Story

One of Graphite's most distinctive design choices is that **your starting hero changes the story**. Choosing a character as your starter alters story events and progression to fit that character's themes. The developers have stated the game is intended to be played with all characters to get the full picture of the narrative — each hero has their own narrative arc, and their stories "cross and accumulate" across runs.

What this means in practice:

- Your first run is a single character's perspective on Act 1
- A second run with a different hero will not be a copy-paste of the first — different events, different framing
- The tarot adventure deck's branching choices respond to your party composition, so who you bring changes which choices are even available

## Skill Progression

After each battle you level up and spend points to upgrade chosen skills, directing your build. The game supports both balanced builds and hyper-specialized ones — you can stack upgrades and items freely, even duplicating the same item many times to amplify one skill.

Between runs, Graphite uses roguelite-style meta-progression: artifacts, events, and unlockables persist across runs. The exact scope of what carries over is unconfirmed; the demo itself includes friendship levels (up to level 1 between characters in the demo build).

## Characters Not Yet Confirmed

Five of the seven heroes are not yet publicly documented. What we can say about them is limited to the developer's general statements:

- Each hero has a unique personality, backstory, and playstyle
- Each hero has their own narrative arc
- Hero names, abilities, and unlock methods are **TBD** until official reveals or the full release

This section will be updated as more information becomes available. Until then, treat any claimed names or abilities for these five heroes as unverified.

## Recommended First Pick

For your first run (demo or full game): **The Knight** is the safest confirmed choice — the shield-based kit directly teaches the Break system, and the durability forgives early mistakes. If you prefer aggressive offense, **The Mage** is the confirmed alternative. Both are solid entry points; the rest of the roster is unconfirmed.
