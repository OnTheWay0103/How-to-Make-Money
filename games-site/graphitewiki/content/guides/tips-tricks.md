## Tip 1: Break Meter Overflow

The Break meter does NOT cap at 100%. If you continue attacking an enemy after the Break bar fills, the **overflow damage carries over** into the next Break window.

**How it works**: Each enemy has a Break meter that fills as you land attacks. When the meter reaches 100%, the enemy enters a Break state (stunned, takes bonus damage). If you keep attacking a **different** enemy while the first is in Break state, the extra Break progress you generate on the second enemy is partially retained.

**Practical application**: In multi-enemy fights, do this:
1. Attack Enemy A until Break triggers (uses all accumulated Break progress)
2. Attack Enemy B while A is in Break (generate Break progress on B)
3. By the time A recovers from Break, B is close to Break too
4. Break B, repeat — you maintain near-permanent Break uptime on the enemy group

This technique turns difficult multi-enemy encounters into trivial ones. It is especially effective with the Monk, who generates Break meter faster than any other hero.

## Tip 2: Artifact Stacking — The "Same Name" Rule

Graphite allows **unlimited artifact stacking**, but there is a critical hidden rule: **artifacts with the exact same name do NOT stack their effects**. Artifacts with different names but similar effects *do* stack.

**Example**:
- Two "Break Booster" artifacts: Effect applies only once (the higher-rarity one).
- One "Break Booster" and one "Strike of Opening": Both effects apply separately, even though both grant "+Damage on Break."

**Optimization strategy**:
1. Never carry duplicate-named artifacts — sell or purge extras.
2. Prioritize artifacts with **different names but overlapping bonus types**.
3. The "Resonance" artifact series (Resonance of Power, Resonance of Speed, Resonance of Guard) are deliberately designed to stack with each other. Collecting all three is a priority.

This is the single most impactful optimization most players miss. A correctly stacked artifact loadout can double your effective combat power.

## Tip 3: The Camp Cycle — Maximize Bond Gains Per Rest

Camp sites are your primary source of healing, bond-building, and hero unlocks. Most players underutilize them.

**The optimal camp cycle**:
1. **Enter camp** — this does not advance the day counter.
2. **Use Camp Ability 1** on your primary target (the hero you want to unlock). This requires the resource "Camp Uses," which you start with 2-3 per camp.
3. **Use Camp Ability 2** on a secondary target.
4. **Rest** — this advances the day. You heal fully AND spend any remaining Camp Uses automatically.
5. **Repeat** if you are at a multi-day camp location.

**Key points**:
- Each Camp Use grants approximately +3-5 Friendship. Three Camp Uses on the same hero in one camp cycle gives +9-15 Friendship.
- You can reach Friendship 15 (unlock threshold for most Act 1 heroes) in 1-2 camp cycles if you focus.
- The Monk's "Meditation" camp ability is unique — it grants +7 Friendship to a single hero instead of the usual +3-5. Use the Monk for your unlock grinding.

## Tip 4: Break Window Sequencing

The Break window is the most important 2 seconds in every fight. How you sequence your attacks during Break determines your damage output.

**Correct sequence**:
1. **Buff first**: Apply any party-wide buffs (Bard's Inspire, artifact activations)
2. **Break attack**: Use the hero with the highest "Bonus on Break" artifact
3. **Follow-up**: Use heroes with "Bonus on damaged enemy" effects
4. **Finisher**: Use the Rogue's ultimate or highest single-target skill

**Wrong sequence**:
1. Finisher first (wastes the damage bonus window)
2. Buff after attacking (buffs miss the Break)
3. Break attack after other attacks (you wasted the bonus)

Mastering this sequence adds 30-40% more effective damage to every Break window.

## Tip 5: Tarot Event Reroll Secrets

Most tarot events present a binary or ternary choice, but some have **hidden outcomes based on your party composition**.

**Known hidden triggers**:
- **"The Locked Chest"**: Bring a Rogue or Tamer for an extra loot roll. Without them, the chest is a trap (-health).
- **"The Mysterious Altar"**: Bring a Monk or Mage to "read the inscriptions." This adds a third option — "Purify" — which grants a permanent stat boost instead of a one-time artifact.
- **"The Hunt"**: Bring a Hunter to double the reward from this event.
- **"The Negotiation"**: Bring a Bard for a peaceful resolution option, avoiding a difficult fight.
- **"The Beast's Den"**: Bring a Tamer to potentially recruit a temporary pet for the next 5 days.

Always check your party before clicking through tarot event choices. The optimal choice changes based on who you have with you.

## Tip 6: Day Skipping — When to Rest Without Exploring

Camping is not the only way to spend a day without fighting. **Deliberate day skipping** is a valid strategy in specific situations:

**When to skip days**:
- **Waiting for a specific tarot event**: The adventure deck cycles. If you know a good event is coming (e.g., the Mystic Forge), rest 1-2 days to cycle the deck faster.
- **Healing without a camp**: If you are at full health but low on resources, resting in a safe zone advances the deck without risking combat.
- **Act boss preparation**: It is better to skip 2-3 days and ensure full health than to enter a boss fight at 70% HP.

**When NOT to skip**:
- During the first 10 days of any act — you need every artifact you can find.
- If you have unspent Camp Uses — always use them before resting.

## Tip 7: The "Resonance Trinity"

The three Resonance artifacts (Resonance of Power, Speed, and Guard) are individually mediocre. Together, they are **game-breaking**.

| Artifact | Solo Effect | Combined Bonus |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Resonance of Power | +5% damage | +15% damage, +5% crit |
| Resonance of Speed | +5% dodge | +15% dodge, +1 action point |
| Resonance of Guard | +5% defense | +15% defense, -1 damage taken |

The combined bonus only activates when you have **all three** equipped on the same hero. The effect is +15% to all stats plus the secondary bonuses listed above.

**Where to find them**: Each Resonance artifact has a specific tarot event ("The Resonance Chamber") that appears once per playthrough. Do not pass this event — always take it, even if you have to walk through a dangerous zone to reach it.

## Tip 8: Hero Ultimate Timing

Every hero has an ultimate ability that charges over 4-6 turns. Using ultimates at the right time matters more than using them at all.

**Optimization rules**:
- **Do not use ultimates outside Break windows**. The damage difference is massive (up to 3x more damage during Break).
- **Chain ultimates**: Using two ultimates in the same Break window creates a "Double Break" effect — the Break window extends by 1 turn.
- **Save the Bard's ultimate for the second Break window**, not the first. Bard's ultimate applies a buff that lasts 3 turns. Using it in Break window 1 means it expires before Break window 2.

## Tip 9: Pathing Optimization on the Map

The map in Graphite is not fully open. You navigate along preset paths between zones. Optimizing your path saves 3-5 days per act.

**Pathing rules**:
- The shortest path to the act boss is NOT always the best path.
- **Dead-end zones** with tarot event markers often contain high-value events.
- **Loop paths** are traps — they look efficient but cost extra days.
- Use the map's zone preview feature (hover over a zone before entering) to check what is there. If it is a low-value combat encounter with no artifact reward, skip it.

**Optimal pathing priority**:
1. Tarot events (especially those that match your party composition)
2. Artifact nodes (sparkling map icons)
3. Camp sites (needed for healing and bonds)
4. Combat encounters with "Bonus rewards" tag
5. Empty combat encounters (skip if possible)

## Tip 10: Speed Unlocking Heroes via Camp Abilities

This technique deserves emphasis. The fastest way to unlock any hero is **spamming camp abilities on the hero you want**.

**Step-by-step unlock formula**:
1. Identify which hero you want to unlock and their Friendship threshold (see the [full unlock guide here](/guides/how-to-unlock-heroes)).
2. Rush to a camp site.
3. Use ALL camp abilities on the target hero.
4. Rest. Repeat at the next camp site.
5. Most heroes unlock in 2-3 camp cycles (6-9 total Camp Uses).

**The Monk shortcut**: If you have already unlocked the Monk, use their Meditation camp ability on the target hero. Meditation grants +7 Friendship per use (vs +3-5 standard). This cuts unlock time in half.
