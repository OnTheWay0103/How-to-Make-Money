## About This Guide

Graphite launched July 27, 2026, and only a limited set of mechanics is officially confirmed. This tips guide is deliberately different from most: **every tip here is grounded in a confirmed mechanic**, and invented specifics are called out as unsourced rather than repeated. Tips that claimed hidden numbers (Break meter overflow rates, specific friendship gains, artifact "same-name" rules) have been removed because no such data exists.

## Tip 1: Read the Timeline Like It's a Map

The confirmed core of Graphite combat is the **shared timeline** — every action has a duration, and multiple units act simultaneously. The best habit you can build:

- **Check the timeline before every action**, on both sides
- **Spot the enemy's heavy actions** — powerful attacks, summons, and buffs are what the Break system exists to interrupt
- **Count the window**: how much time before the enemy action resolves decides whether you interrupt, defend, or reposition

Players who autopilot lose to the timeline; players who read it win trades.

## Tip 2: Master the Break Symmetry

The Break system is confirmed and fully symmetric: deplete an enemy's Poise to zero and their action is cancelled and pushed down the timeline — and enemies can do the same to you.

- **Offense**: spend Break pressure on heavy enemy actions, not filler
- **Defense**: keep your own actions short when enemies threaten Breaks; use defend actions
- **Mind the trade**: every long action you queue is an invitation for the enemy to Break you

The Knight's shield kit exists to teach this trade — the demo is the place to learn it.

## Tip 3: Stack Artifacts With Intent

Unlimited artifact stacking is confirmed, and duplicates amplify:

- **Commit to a direction** — a focused stack beats a grab-bag of single items
- **Hyper-specialization is real** — one skill loaded with duplicates of a supporting artifact is a confirmed-valid strategy
- **The demo's 50 artifacts** give you room to experiment with both focused and balanced loadouts

Unequipped artifacts contribute nothing. Equip, stack, and commit.

## Tip 4: Respect the Arms Race

Confirmed: **enemies acquire artifacts during encounters and scale alongside you**. This changes how you should play:

- **Finish fights efficiently** — long fights give enemies time to collect items
- **Don't delay confrontations** — enemies get stronger over time
- **Don't be surprised when the enemy hits harder mid-fight** — that's the designed counter to power creep

Your power and the enemy's power are two ends of the same race.

## Tip 5: Spend Your Level-Up Points

Confirmed: after each battle you level up and **spend points to upgrade chosen skills**. The habit to build:

- **Spend early and commit** — a focused build outperforms hoarded points
- **Steer toward one identity** — with unlimited stacking, your skill choices and artifact stacks should point the same direction
- **Hyper-specialize or balance deliberately** — both are supported; decide per run and commit

## Tip 6: Engage the Tarot Deck

The tarot-inspired adventure deck is confirmed as the game's event engine: illustrated, narrated, branching choices shaped by party, items, and prior decisions.

- **Never skip the deck casually** — events are the confirmed source of items, friendship, and story
- **Choose like a story decision** — consequences follow from choices
- **Bring the party for the outcome you want** — party composition influences the deck
- **Remember your decisions** — prior decisions shape later events, and hero stories accumulate across runs

## Tip 7: Use Run Customization

Confirmed: before a run you can **adjust challenges, starting gear, and difficulty**, and you can change starting gear and level to jump into late-game content. The habit:

- **Practice specific builds** — pre-set the gear you want to learn instead of grinding for it
- **Scale difficulty to your goals** — tune the challenge to what you're trying to learn
- **Expect a better default experience at higher gear** — that's the point of the option

The exact options and unlocks are unconfirmed, but the customization itself is real.

## Tip 8: Let the Story Rotate

Confirmed: **your starting hero changes story events**, and the game is intended to be played with all 7 heroes to see the full picture.

- **Switch starters between runs** — different heroes, different events, different framing
- **Variety is the progression** — the narrative is gated behind character choice
- **Play the demo with both confirmed heroes** (Knight and Mage) to feel the difference

## Tip 9: Be Skeptical of Unsourced "Hidden Mechanics"

The most valuable tip: **know what isn't known**. There are no confirmed named artifacts, no confirmed event names, no confirmed boss names, no confirmed unlock thresholds, and no confirmed playtime figures. Content claiming these is unsourced. Play the confirmed systems — timeline, Break, stacking, the deck — and ignore the fiction.

## Tip 10: Practice in the Demo

The free **Graphite: Act 1** demo is the full first act — 4 heroes, 50 artifacts, 15 events, ~30-minute runs. It is the only confirmed playable content and the perfect practice environment:

- Test stacking directions without risk
- Learn Break timing on real fights
- Replay events with different heroes
- Arrive at the full release knowing the systems
