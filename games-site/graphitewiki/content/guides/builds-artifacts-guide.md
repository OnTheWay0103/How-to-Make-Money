## How the Artifact System Works

Graphite's artifact system has **no cap**. You can stack unlimited artifacts on a hero, and each stack of the same artifact increases its effect. This is the confirmed core of the system:

- **Unlimited stacking** — no upper limit on artifacts
- **Duplicates amplify** — stacking multiples of the same artifact compounds its effect
- **Both build styles are supported** — balanced builds and hyper-specialized builds (one skill loaded with many copies of a supporting item) are both valid
- **The demo contains 50 stackable artifacts** to experiment with

The strategic question is not "should I collect artifacts?" but "which artifacts should I stack, and on which heroes?"

## The Arms Race — Enemies Collect Artifacts Too

Graphite's signature twist is **symmetric scaling**: enemy parties also acquire artifacts during encounters. Opponents pick up items and grow stronger over time, creating an arms race:

- Dragging out a fight gives enemies more time to accumulate artifacts
- Enemy power climbing mid-encounter is by design — coverage explicitly describes enemies that "get stronger over time and can eventually use items too"
- A runaway loadout on your side may not dominate the way it would in other games — the other side gets the same tools

Practical consequence: **fights are a race**. Build your stack with intent, but finish fights efficiently.

## Build Archetypes

Archetypes below are derived from confirmed mechanics, not from confirmed game data. They describe how the stacking system is designed to work.

### 1. The Stacked Specialist

Focus all artifact investment on one primary hero — stack duplicates of a single effect that amplifies that hero's chosen skill.

**Why it works**: duplication is confirmed to amplify; a single skill with many copies of a supporting artifact is the confirmed hyper-specialization design
**Risk**: if that hero is taken out or countered, the whole build collapses — and enemy artifact scaling makes any single-sided bet fragile
**Best for**: players who understand the game's Break economy and want maximum effect concentration

### 2. The Balanced Approach

Distribute artifacts across party members, mixing effects rather than duplicating one.

**Why it works**: balanced builds are explicitly supported; less concentrated power means less vulnerability to a single Break or a focused enemy
**Risk**: may lack the concentrated punch needed to break through tough late-act fights

### 3. The Break Engine

Stack effects that support the Break system — the game's core interrupt mechanic. The goal is controlling the timeline: cancel enemy actions, protect your own Poise.

**Why it works**: Break is the confirmed core combat mechanic; a build built around it plays the game's strongest lever
**Risk**: Break-focused builds trade raw damage for control, and fights take longer — giving enemy artifact scaling more time to ramp

## Build Strategy by Stage

Without confirmed act-level detail, stage guidance comes from the run loop itself:

### Early Run (Act 1 / demo)

- Focus on **learning and committing**: pick one direction and stack toward it
- The demo's 50-artifact pool is generous — test both a focused stack and a balanced loadout across runs
- Level up early and spend points — committed builds outperform hoarded ones

### Mid and Late Run

- **Watch enemy scaling**: if opponents are collecting artifacts aggressively, either finish fights faster or strengthen your stack to keep pace
- **Specialize toward the act's confrontation**: the act finale is the true test — arrive with a stack you understand
- The full game's pacing and act structure are unconfirmed, so treat any day-based or act-based build advice as unsourced

## Artifact Hunting Tips

The confirmed sources of artifacts are limited to the game's structures:

- **Combat rewards**: fights generate levels and items as part of the loop
- **The tarot adventure deck**: event cards are the confirmed source of choices, items, and consequences — engage events
- **Enemy drops**: the arms race implies defeated enemies yield their items, though the details are unconfirmed
- **Run customization**: coverage confirms you can adjust starting gear before a run — pre-set loadouts are a real option

## Common Build Mistakes

- **Hoarding instead of stacking** — unequipped artifacts contribute nothing; duplicate and commit
- **Ignoring the arms race** — a slow, dragging fight lets enemies pick up artifacts and out-scale you
- **All-in on one hero without protection** — hyper-specialization is real, but so is the counterplay (Break, targeted focus)
- **Trusting named artifacts** — no official artifact names exist yet; plan around effects, not claimed items
- **Skipping event cards** — the tarot deck is a confirmed source of items and build-defining choices
