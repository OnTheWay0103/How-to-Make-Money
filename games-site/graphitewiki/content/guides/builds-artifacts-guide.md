## How the Artifact System Works

Graphite's artifact system has no hard cap. You can stack unlimited artifacts on a single hero, and each stack of the same artifact type increases the effect. However, this comes with a hidden cost: the game's scaling system tracks your total artifact power and increases enemy strength proportionally.

The core strategic question is not "should I collect artifacts?" but "which artifacts should I stack and on which heroes?"

### Individual vs. Group Artifacts

**Individual Artifacts** — Equip to a specific hero. Bonuses only apply to that hero's actions, Break power, and timeline position.

- Examples: Serrated Edge (Rogue crits bleed), Iron Fist (Monk Break stacks)
- Best used on your primary damage dealer
- Stacking 3+ of the same individual artifact on one hero creates exponential returns

**Group Artifacts** — Apply their effect to the entire party when equipped.

- Examples: Guardian's Crest (reduces damage to adjacent allies), Traveler's Song (reduces map travel time)
- Only one group artifact per category can be active — duplicates do not stack
- Pool your group artifacts across the party for maximum coverage

### The Power Curve Trade-off

Every artifact you equip raises your party's hidden power rating. The game's enemy scaling system uses this rating to determine enemy HP, damage, artifact drops, and Break resistance. This creates a natural tug-of-war:

- **Under-invest**: Enemies are weak but drop low-tier artifacts — you may lack the power to break Act 3-4 bosses
- **Over-invest**: Enemies become stat-check nightmares with high Break resistance and deadly damage
- **Balanced**: Stack artifacts smartly on your core heroes while avoiding wasteful equips on bench characters

## Build Archetypes

Builds in Graphite fall into three broad archetypes. Your choice depends on your hero composition and act progression.

### 1. The Stacked Specialist

Focus all artifact investment on one primary damage dealer. Equip 4-5 copies of the same damage-boosting individual artifact on your carry hero while everyone else equips group artifacts and defensive items.

**Best for**: Rogue, Mage, Monk
**Why it works**: Exponential stacking creates Break windows that single-target bosses cannot survive
**Risk**: If your carry gets Broken or killed, the entire run collapses
**Example stack**: 4x Serrated Edge on Rogue + 2x Shadow Clasp + Duskwalker's Boots

### 2. The Balanced Approach

Distribute artifacts evenly across all party members. Each hero has 2-3 useful artifacts rather than one hero with 6. Group artifacts take priority over individual stacks.

**Best for**: Warrior, Bard, Hunter
**Why it works**: More forgiving of mistakes, flexible against different enemy types, lower risk against ambushes
**Risk**: May lack the concentrated power needed to Break Act 4 bosses
**Example loadout**: Guardian's Crest (Warrior) + Arcane Focus (Mage) + Lute of Acceleration (Bard) + one individual artifact each

### 3. The Break Engine

Stack every artifact that increases Break damage and Break resistance. The goal is not to kill enemies through HP damage but to keep them permanently interrupted while your party slowly chips away.

**Best for**: Monk, Bard, Tamer
**Why it works**: Enemies cannot fight back if they never get a turn — the ultimate control strategy
**Risk**: Low raw damage means fights take longer, giving the enemy's own artifact scaling more time to ramp
**Example loadout**: 3x Iron Fist (Monk) + Discordant Note (Bard) + Beastmaster's Call (Tamer)

## Best Artifact Combinations by Act

### Act 1 — Establish Your Foundation

- 1 group artifact (any category)
- 2-3 individual artifacts on your main hero
- Focus on survivability — Act 1 bosses have low Break resistance but hit hard when they connect

### Act 2 — Specialize Your Strategy

- 2-3 individual artifacts stacked on carry hero
- 1-2 group artifacts distributed
- Start leaning toward one build archetype
- Introduce a secondary damage dealer with 1-2 artifacts

### Act 3 — Commit to Your Build

- Maximum stacks on primary carry (4-5 of the same individual artifact)
- Fill remaining slots with group artifacts
- Ensure every party member has at least one artifact equipped
- By now, enemy Break resistance has scaled significantly — your Break power must match

### Act 4 — Optimize for the Final Boss

- Pure specialization. Your build should be fully committed.
- Replace weaker artifacts with upgraded versions found in Act 3-4
- Stack at least one Break-resistance artifact on each hero — the final boss applies heavy Break pressure

## How Enemies Collect Artifacts Too

Graphite's signature twist is symmetrical scaling. Enemy parties also collect artifacts as days pass. The longer you spend in an act, the stronger the enemies become. This creates a natural time pressure:

- **Day 1-3 enemies**: Weak, no artifact bonuses, predictable patterns
- **Day 4-6 enemies**: Begin showing artifact effects — faster actions, more Break resistance
- **Day 7+ enemies**: Fully artifact-loaded enemies that can rival boss difficulty
- **Boss scaling**: Each unused rest day adds one random artifact to the act boss

The optimal strategy is to reach each act's boss by day 5-6. Any slower and the boss gains artifact stacks that make the fight exponentially harder.

## Artifact Hunting Tips

- **Merchant nodes** let you buy specific artifact types — save gold for these
- **Elite enemy nodes** drop individual artifacts 80% of the time
- **Event nodes** (tarot deck) can grant powerful rare artifacts you cannot find elsewhere
- **Scout reveals** artifact caches on the map — always scout before moving into unknown territory
- **Sell duplicates** at merchants for gold — no benefit from a third group artifact of the same type
