## An Honest Preface About Tier Lists

Tier lists for Graphite need a disclaimer that most ranking pages skip: **there is no confirmed tier data for this game.** Graphite launches July 27, 2026, and only two of the seven heroes have been officially named — **The Knight** and **The Mage**. Any ranking that scores heroes named "Warrior," "Rogue," "Monk," "Bard," "Hunter," or "Tamer" with numbers like "9/10" is fabricating its roster and its data. Neither the hero names nor the scores are sourced.

This page gives you the honest version: what is confirmed about each known hero, and a reasonable early-pick recommendation based on confirmed mechanics rather than invented stats.

## What We Know About the Confirmed Heroes

### The Knight — Recommended First Pick

- **Confirmed details**: fights with a shield, uses abilities that take advantage of the **Break system**, and deals significant damage
- **Where the strength comes from**: the Break system is Graphite's core combat mechanic — depleting an enemy's Poise cancels their scheduled action and pushes their turn down the timeline. A hero built around Break is built around the game's strongest lever.
- **Who it suits**: new players (the shield forgives timing mistakes), and players who like controlling the tempo of fights

The Knight is the community's expected default starter: durable, teaches the game's signature system, and effective. This is an assessment based on confirmed kit details, not a ranked score.

### The Mage — The Offensive Alternative

- **Confirmed details**: casts offensive spells and acts as support by boosting allies' defenses
- **Where the strength comes from**: offense plus party protection covers two roles in one slot — a flexible damage dealer that can also keep fragile teammates alive
- **Who it suits**: players who prefer ranged offense and don't mind managing a squishier hero

The Mage pairs naturally with the Knight: the Knight holds the frontline and Breaks while the Mage deals damage and shields. Expect this pairing to be a strong early composition — though "strong" here means based on confirmed kit synergy, not tested performance data.

## The Unconfirmed Roster — TBD

Five of the seven heroes have no officially confirmed names, abilities, or roles. Coverage confirms only that each hero has a unique personality, backstory, and playstyle, and that **starting hero choice changes story events**. Until official reveals or post-release testing, no ranking of these heroes is possible — and any ranking you find is unsourced.
