## Tier Criteria

All 7 heroes are ranked based on four weighted criteria:

- **Combat Power** (30%): Raw damage output and Break efficiency in typical combat scenarios.
- **Artifact Synergy** (25%): How well the hero benefits from the artifact pool. Heroes with broad artifact compatibility score higher.
- **Ease of Use** (20%): How forgiving the hero is for new players. Heroes with straightforward gameplans rank higher.
- **Boss Performance** (25%): Effectiveness against act bosses, especially the final boss. Heroes with consistent Break generation shine here.

## S-Tier Heroes

### Monk — The Break Master

The Monk is the strongest hero in Graphite for one reason: **Break-charge ability**. While other heroes need to build Break meter over several turns, the Monk can generate Break progress with every attack, and their unique ability speeds up the team's Break cycle dramatically.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Fastest Break generation, excellent Artifact synergy (almost every "+Break" artifact works), top boss performance |
| **Weaknesses** | Requires understanding of Break timing, slightly fragile, below-average raw damage outside Break windows |
| **Best Artifacts** | Break Acceleration, Flow State, Spirit Stones (Bonus on Break) |
| **Best Team Role** | Break engine — build the Break bar fast, let teammates profit from the windows |
| **Unlock** | Act 1, requires Friendship 15 with a starting hero |

**Why S-Tier**: The Monk is the only hero who fundamentally changes how fast your team generates Breaks. In a game built around the Break system, the Monk is essential for high-difficulty content.

### Bard — The Artifact Engine

The Bard is the ultimate force multiplier. While the Bard's direct combat contributions are average, their **artifact synergy is unmatched** — the Bard can activate bonus effects from artifacts that no other hero can use.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Best artifact compatibility in the game, strong team buffs, easy to play |
| **Weaknesses** | Low personal damage, requires decent artifacts to function well |
| **Best Artifacts** | Resonance Chain, Echoing Vessel, Conducting Rod (all Bard-exclusive synergies) |
| **Best Team Role** | Support/buffer — stack party-wide artifact bonusses and keep buffs active |
| **Unlock** | Act 2, requires Friendship 25 with two different heroes |

**Why S-Tier**: A well-equipped Bard turns a good team into an unstoppable one. Their ability to stack multiple artifact bonusses simultaneously creates compound growth that no other hero matches.

## A-Tier Heroes

### Warrior — The Solid Foundation

The Warrior is the game's default starter hero. Balanced stats, straightforward gameplay, and reliable performance across all situations.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Highest base survivability, easy to play, works well with any team composition |
| **Weaknesses** | No standout strength, average Break generation, outscaled by specialists late-game |
| **Best Artifacts** | Iron Heart, Veterans Plate, Sturdy Shield |
| **Best Team Role** | Frontline tank — absorb damage while the Monk builds Break |
| **Unlock** | Starting hero (always available) |

**Why A-Tier**: The Warrior is never the best hero on your team, but also never the worst. Every team needs a frontline, and the Warrior fills that role perfectly without demanding specific artifacts.

### Rogue — The Glass Cannon

The Rogue is the highest raw damage dealer in Graphite. If you want to see the biggest numbers, the Rogue is your hero.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Highest single-target DPS, excellent critical hit artifact synergies, high Break damage |
| **Weaknesses** | Very fragile, requires careful positioning, punishing for new players |
| **Best Artifacts** | Shadow Blade, Critical Edge, Poisoned Dagger |
| **Best Team Role** | Finisher — let the Monk build Break, then unload with the Rogue |
| **Unlock** | Act 1, requires Friendship 20 with Warrior |

**Why A-Tier**: The Rogue's damage ceiling is enormous, but the skill floor is real. Players who master positioning and Break timing will be rewarded with the fastest boss kills in the game.

### Tamer — The Flexible Specialist

The Tamer fights alongside a summoned creature, effectively giving you an extra body on the battlefield.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Extra damage from pet, pet can absorb hits, good utility skills |
| **Weaknesses** | Pet requires investment, pet dies = reduced effectiveness, situational artifact pool |
| **Best Artifacts** | Beast Bond, Taming Whistle, Pack Leader |
| **Best Team Role** | Off-tank/DPS hybrid — pet frontline while main team attacks |
| **Unlock** | Act 2, requires Friendship 25 with Hunter |

**Why A-Tier**: The Tamer's value depends on keeping the pet alive. With proper artifact support (especially "Beast Bond" which shares damage reduction), the Tamer is S-tier. Without pet-supporting artifacts, the Tamer drops significantly in effectiveness.

## B-Tier Heroes

### Hunter — The Ranged Specialist

The Hunter deals consistent damage from range but lacks the game-changing capabilities of higher-tier heroes.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Safe ranged attacks, decent consistent damage, good for exploring |
| **Weaknesses** | Poor Break generation, weak boss performance, limited artifact synergy |
| **Best Artifacts** | Hawkeye Lens, Pierce Shot, Ranger's Boots |
| **Best Team Role** | Consistent damage dealer — steady output while others set up Breaks |
| **Unlock** | Act 1, requires Friendship 15 with Warrior or Rogue |

**Why B-Tier**: The Hunter is functional but unremarkable. Their ranged attacks are useful for map exploration (saving health from chip damage), but in boss fights the Rogue or Monk outclasses them significantly.

### Mage — The Situational Nuker

The Mage has the highest AoE damage in the game and unique Mana Break mechanics. In theory, this should be powerful. In practice, the Mage struggles with consistency.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Strengths** | Highest AoE damage, Mana Break is unique and powerful, good in Act 3 boss fight |
| **Weaknesses** | Long cooldowns, fragile, requires specific artifacts to function, difficult to play effectively |
| **Best Artifacts** | Mana Orb, Spell Weaver, Arcane Amplifier |
| **Best Team Role** | AoE specialist — bring for Act 3 boss and artifact-rich exploration zones |
| **Unlock** | Act 2, requires Friendship 30 with a hero that has completed a Bond event |

**Why B-Tier**: The Mage has the widest gap between potential and practical performance. In the right situation (AoE-heavy encounters, specific artifacts), the Mage is S-tier. In most situations, the Mage struggles with cooldown management and survivability.

## Recommended Team Compositions

| Composition | Heroes | Best For | Strategy |
|-------------|--------|----------|----------|
| **The Break Engine** | Monk, Warrior, Bard, Rogue | General play | Monk builds Break, Bard buffs, Rogue executes, Warrior tanks |
| **The Pet Squad** | Tamer, Monk, Bard, Hunter | Exploration | Tamer's pet frontlines, Monk builds Break, Bard supports |
| **The Glass Cannon** | Mage, Rogue, Bard, Monk | Speed runs | Extremely high damage, very fragile — kill before being killed |
| **The Safe Six** | Monk, Warrior, Bard, Rogue, Tamer, Hunter | Act 4 final push | Balanced team for the hardest content |
| **Entry Level** | Warrior, Rogue, Hunter | New players (Act 1) | Three straightforward heroes that teach the core mechanics |
| **AoE Farm** | Mage, Warrior, Monk, Rogue | Artifact farming | Mage AoE clears mobs fast, Monk keeps Break up |

## Which Hero Should You Pick for Your First Run?

| Your Situation | Recommended First Focus |
|----------------|------------------------|
| New to tactical roguelikes | **Warrior** — learn the game safely |
| Experienced, want highest power | **Monk** — core of every strong team |
| Love big damage numbers | **Rogue** — eventual goal, learn with Monk first |
| Prefer support roles | **Bard** — team carries you while you learn |
| Want the easiest path to victory | **Monk first, then unlock Bard** — the S-tier duo |
