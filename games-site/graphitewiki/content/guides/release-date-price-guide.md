## Graphite Release Date, Price & Platforms Guide

Graphite — the pen-and-ink roguelike RPG from Colombian studio **RipRed**, published by **indie.io** — launched on **July 27, 2026**. This page collects everything confirmed about the launch: dates, price, platforms, and what you get before you buy.

The full game was $7.19 during the launch window; if you are reading this after August 10, expect the standard $8.99.

## The Free Demo: Graphite: Act 1

Released **December 19, 2024**, the free **Act 1** demo covers the full opening act and is still available on Steam. Confirmed demo contents:

- **4 playable heroes** with unique playstyles (the full game has 7)
- **50 stackable artifacts** to experiment with
- **15 hand-drawn adventure events** with party-based outcomes
- **Up to Level 1 friendship** between playable characters
- Run customization: challenges, starting gear, and difficulty

**Run length:** Act 1 runs about **30 minutes per run**. A full-game run length has not been published — the demo's 30-minute act is the only official figure.

## Developer & Studio Background

- **Developer:** RipRed — a Colombian indie studio; Graphite is a debut-scale project.
- **Publisher:** indie.io.
- **Origin story:** the game is set inside a kid's school notebook — a child processing grief has drawn a pen-and-paper fantasy campaign, and its heroes (unaware they are figments of imagination) are tasked by a figure called **The Scribe** to grow stronger before Death arrives.

## Early Reception (Community-Reported)

- Steam user reviews for the Act 1 demo were **"Mostly Positive"** in early community reporting (~70–72% positive from a small early review count — a sample too small to treat as settled; check the Steam page for current review data).
- Previewers (Turn Based Lovers and others) praised the **timeline-based combat** (actions have durations on a dynamic timeline; reading enemy intentions matters) and the symmetric design where **enemies can pick up artifacts too**.
- The community's most common response word: **originality**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Graphite free?**
No — the full game is $8.99 (launch discount ended August 10, 2026). The Act 1 demo is free.

**When did Graphite come out?**
July 27, 2026.

**Is Graphite on console?**
Not announced as of August 8, 2026.

**How long is Graphite?**
A full run length is unpublished. The Act 1 demo is ~30 minutes per run; a complete playthrough of all 7 heroes' content will be substantially longer (exact figure awaiting community data).

**Is Graphite a puzzle game?**
No — it is a tactical roguelike RPG. The "puzzle" some coverage refers to is the **timeline/Break combat system**, where reading and disrupting enemy actions is the core skill (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)).
