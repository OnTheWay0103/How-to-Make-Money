# Witchspire Patch Notes & Roadmap Tracker — Update History Through 0.1.4

> Applies to EA v0.1.4 / Updated: August 1, 2026

Witchspire moves fast. In its first month of Early Access (June 10 – July 31, 2026) it shipped **nine patches**, and the developer's pace is one of the game's biggest selling points — community feedback is often fixed within days. This page tracks every update and the official roadmap, so you always know what changed and what is coming. **Note: all of our guides are written for v0.1.4 unless stated otherwise.**

---

## Current Version: 0.1.4 (July 31, 2026)

The "final July build" before the developer's announced summer break.

| Type | Change |
|------|--------|
| **New content** | 6 legendary items added, including **Ben's Big Wand** |
| **New content** | **Asterine Gems** added to the shop (new currency/items) |
| **QoL** | **Incense durations increased** — Simple/Potent 20s/15s → 30s each; Rich/Shimmering 10s → 20s (big bonding buff; see the [Familiar Taming Guide](/guides/familiar-taming-guide)) |
| **Cosmetics** | New skins for Vex and Carsce |
| **Fixes** | "Dream Between Worlds" issue fixed |
| **QoL previewed** | The developer confirmed the following are "coming soon": **inventory sort buttons, Quick Deposit, controller menu improvements, and desync fixes** |

## Official Roadmap (through 2027)

The developer shared the full Early Access plan — **no 1.0 date yet, Early Access expected to last at least a year, and the 1.0 price will be higher than the current $22.99–23.**

| Phase | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **"A Touch of Magic"** (next major update) | **Weapon Crafting 2.0**, Salvaging, the **Familiar Wheel** (familiar QoL overhaul), a gothic building set, and new familiars |
| **Frozen Tundra + Act III** | New biome and the third act of the story |
| **"Throne of Corruption"** | The roadmap's endgame arc |

**What this means for you:** the current content is about half the story (two acts so far). If the roadmap holds, the game roughly doubles between now and 1.0 — a strong argument for buying now at the EA price before the increase.

## FAQ

**Q: When does Witchspire leave Early Access?**
No date. The roadmap runs through 2027 and the developer has said EA lasts "at least a year" from the June 10, 2026 launch.

**Q: Will the price go up?**
Yes — the developer has stated the 1.0 price will be higher than the current $22.99–23 EA price.

**Q: How often do patches ship?**
About nine patches in the first month; the developer announced a summer break after 0.1.4, so expect a slower August.

**Q: Are our guides up to date?**
Guides written before August 1, 2026 may describe v0.1.1d mechanics — notably familiar bonding, which was widely misreported. See the [Familiar Taming Guide](/guides/familiar-taming-guide) for the correct 0.1.4 mechanics.

**Q: Where are the official patch notes?**
On the Steam announcements page (https://steamcommunity.com/app/2679100/allnews/) — we compile them here within 48 hours of each patch.
