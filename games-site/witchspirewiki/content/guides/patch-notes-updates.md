# Witchspire Update Log & Roadmap — Version History and Changes

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

This page tracks confirmed updates for **Witchspire** (co-op survival RPG, Early Access on Steam). We only include information that can be verified against official developer announcements or consistent community reports.

**Note:** Witchspire entered Early Access on June 10, 2026, and is actively being updated. Some mechanics and values below may change. Check the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com) and [Steam Community hub](https://steamcommunity.com) for the latest official information.

- **Reported current build:** 0.1.4 (the version shown in-game and in community discussions as of early August 2026)
- **Early Access launch:** June 10, 2026 (as listed on the official Steam store page)

## Confirmed Update Themes (Community-Verified)

Based on Steam Community discussions and developer announcements, the following update themes are consistently reported since launch:

- **Co-op improvements:** frequent fixes to multiplayer connection issues, version-matching requirements, and desync problems. See the [Co-op Troubleshooting Guide](/guides/coop-troubleshooting-guide) for the current state.
- **Familiar bonding adjustments:** community reports describe changes to bonding rates and incense durations since launch. Exact numerical values have **not been independently verified** — see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/all-familiars-guide) for the bonding mechanics as they currently work.
- **Balance and QoL fixes:** regular bug fixes, resource-spawn fixes, and quality-of-life improvements (including community-requested inventory management features reported as "in progress").

> **Note:** specific numerical balance changes (damage values, timers, durations, etc.) have not been independently verified. If you have confirmed patch note details from official sources, please [contact us](/contact).

## Developer Roadmap

Based on official developer communications, the team has shared the following roadmap phases (community-reported, dates not confirmed):

| Phase | Reported Content |
|-------|------------------|
| **"A Touch of Magic"** (next major update) | Weapon Crafting 2.0, Salvaging, the Familiar Wheel (familiar QoL overhaul), a gothic building set, and new familiars |
