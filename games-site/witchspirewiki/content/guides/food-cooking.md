# Food and Cooking Guide — Recipes and Buffs

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 29, 2026**

Food in Witchspire is not just for survival — it provides powerful buffs that can make the difference between life and death. This guide covers every recipe, ingredient, and optimal meal plan.

---

## Cooking Stations

| Station | Hearth Tier | Fuel | What It Cooks |
|---------|-------------|------|---------------|
| Campfire | Tier 1 (free) | Wood | Basic roasted food, boiled items |
| Oven | Tier 2 | Wood/Coal | Bread, pastries, complex dishes |
| Stove | Tier 3 | Coal | Soups, stews, sauces |
| Brewing Stand | Tier 4 | Coal + Magic | Drinks, elixirs, special beverages |
| Smokehouse | Tier 3 | Wood | Preserved meats (no spoilage) |
| Cauldron | Tier 4 | Wood + Magic | Group meals (feeds 4+ players) |

## Cooking Skill Progression

| Skill Level | Unlocks | How to Level |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Campfire recipes | Cook 5 basic meals |
| 2 | Oven recipes | Cook 10 meals |

| 4 | Brewing Stand | Cook 25 meals |
| 5 | Cauldron (group meals) | Cook 40 meals, complete Cooking Mastery quest |

### Cooking Mastery Perks

| Level | Perk |
|-------|------|
| 2 | 20% chance for double yield when cooking |
| 3 | Buff durations +25% |
| 4 | Ingredient requirements reduced by 1 for some recipes |
| 5 | Can cook special recipes with improved effects |

## Ingredient Substitution Table

| Missing Ingredient | Substitute | Effect Change |
|-------------------|-----------|---------------|
| Boar Meat | Wolf Meat | Same hunger, slightly different buff |
| Nectar | Honey | Sweeter, healing boost instead of hunger |
| Wheat | Mushroom flour (milled mushrooms) | Less hunger, random effect |
| Herbs | Moonleaf | Magic-focused instead of balanced |
| Berries | Thornberries | Spicier, may add damage buff |
