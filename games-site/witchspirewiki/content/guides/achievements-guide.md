# Witchspire Achievements Guide — What We Know and How to Earn Them

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

**Honest note first:** Witchspire lists achievements on Steam, but the full achievement list — names, unlock conditions, and counts — has **not been independently verified** by this site. Older guides that list 86 achievements with exact requirements, floors, and difficulty ratings described a different game and should be ignored. This page covers what we can say with confidence: the kinds of play that reliably earn achievements in the current Early Access build.

## What Players Report Earning Achievements For

Based on Steam Community discussions and player reports, achievements are commonly tied to these activities:

| Area | Examples of What Triggers Achievements |
|------|----------------------------------------|
| **Story progression** | Completing main quest acts, defeating the three confirmed bosses |
| **Boss encounters** | First kills of the Al'uunian Golem, the Shudder Matriarch, and Lumi |
| **Familiar collection** | Bonding your first familiar, bonding familiars of each element |
| **Crafting milestones** | Crafting your first items, upgrading your Hearth through tiers |
| **Exploration** | Reaching each region for the first time, activating Flight Pillars |
| **Co-op play** | Playing with friends, reviving teammates, completing content together |

> **Note:** the exact achievement names, counts, and requirements have not been independently verified against official sources. These categories are based on community reports of when achievements pop.

## How to Check Your Achievement Progress

1. Open the **Steam Overlay** (Shift+Tab) while playing
2. Select **Achievements** from the overlay menu
3. Review what has unlocked and what remains

Witchspire's achievements will appear in your Steam Library under the game's Community hub as they unlock.

If your goal is 100% completion, these habits will serve you no matter what the official list looks like:
