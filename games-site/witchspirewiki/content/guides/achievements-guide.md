| Dungeon Master | Discover all 30 secret rooms | "Dungeon Master" crown | See secret room map page |

### Secret Rooms

Each floor has 2–3 secret rooms. They are always indicated by one of three signs:

1. A wall with a slight color variation (check with light spell)
2. An enemy that drops no loot (it's guarding a hidden lever)
3. An incomplete map corridor (the secret is at the dead end)

> **Key tip:** The "Radiant Sight" spell reveals all secret room entrances within 30 meters. Unlock it from the Arcane Vendor on Floor 8.

## Challenge Achievements (Hardest)

These are the achievements that separate casual players from completionists.

### The Immortal

**Condition:** Complete a Hardcore Nightmare run without dying once.
**Estimated attempts:** 15–40
**Reward:** "Immortal" title + Legendary armor set (The Eternal Set)

**Strategy:**
1. Play as Cleric (best survivability)
2. Over-level to 15+ above the floor requirement
3. Equip the Regeneration Charm (passive healing every 3 seconds)
4. Never explore secret rooms — the risk is not worth it
5. Extract early if your HP dips below 50%

### Speed Demon (Nightmare)

**Condition:** Floors 1–15 in under 60 minutes on Nightmare difficulty.
**Estimated attempts:** 10–25
**Reward:** "Speed Demon" title + Nightmare Mount

**Strategy:**
1. Run with a coordinated 4-player group
2. Assign: 1 Scout (pulls enemies), 2 DPS, 1 Support
3. Skip all optional rooms
4. Only loot chests that are directly on the path
5. Bosses: burn all cooldowns immediately

### Completionist

**Condition:** All 85 other achievements.
**Estimated attempts:** 200+ hours
**Reward:** "Completionist" title + Exclusive Gold Aura

This is the final achievement. Every other achievement must be obtained first.
