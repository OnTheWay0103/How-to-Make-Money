# Lumi Boss Guide — Witchspire Tower Final Encounter

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Lumi, the Tower Guardian, is the final boss of Witchspire's current Early Access content. This sentient crystalline entity reportedly guards the top of Witchspire Tower. Community reports describe a fight with three distinct phases, each demanding different positioning and tactics. This guide covers everything currently known about securing your first clear.

> **Note:** this fight's specifics (phase thresholds, damage values, drop rates) come from community reports and have not been independently verified. Treat numbers as approximate.

---

## Boss Info

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Witchspire Tower — Summit Chamber |
| Type | Ethereal (Crystal / Light) |
| Weakness | Nox damage (community-reported weakness — corrupts the crystal form) |
| Resist | Community reports describe physical and Lux damage as less effective |
| Recommended Level | 30+ (community report) |
| Recommended Gear | Matriarch Silk armor or equivalent, Steel+ weapon with Nox enchant |
| Recommended Hearth | Tier 6 — community reports describe this as the current max |
| Recommended Luminary | 40+ points allocated (community report) |

### Accessing the Tower

1. Defeat the **Shudder Matriarch** in the Sunken Arena
2. Collect the **Matriarch Core**
3. Community reports describe **Witchspire Tower** becoming accessible at the map center after the fight — not independently verified; follow the main quest
4. Enter the tower via the ground-floor entrance
5. Climb several floors of enemies (community reports describe strong enemies — bring potions)
6. The Summit Chamber door is at the top — enter to trigger the Lumi fight

## Phase 1 — Crystal Sentry (reported: 100% — 65% HP)

Lumi begins as a floating crystal entity in the center of a circular chamber. The room reportedly has 4 pillars for cover.

### Attack Patterns (Phase 1, community-reported)

| Attack | Telegraph | Damage | Counter |
|--------|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Crystal Lance** (Ranged beam) | Lumi's core glows, fires a thin beam | High (piercing) | Hide behind a pillar or block with shield |
| **Shard Volley** (Aimed projectiles) | Lumi spins, fires crystal shards | Medium per shard | Dodge roll through the gaps between shards |
| **Crystal Push** (AoE knockback) | Lumi pulses white, ring expands | Low + knockback | Jump over the ring or brace against a pillar |
| **Summon Fragments** (Adds) | Lumi splits off crystal fragments | Low (fragments explode on death) | Kill fragments before they reach Lumi (they reportedly heal the boss on contact) |
| **Glare** (Blind) | Lumi flashes bright | Blind | Turn your camera away from Lumi when you see the flash charge |

### Phase 1 Strategy

**All players:**
- Use the 4 pillars as cover — always have one between you and Lumi
- Kill Crystal Fragments immediately — they reportedly heal Lumi if they reach the boss
- When Lumi casts Glare, turn your camera 180 degrees (look at the wall) to avoid the blind
- Attack windows: after Shard Volley (dodge then attack) and after Crystal Push (attack during cooldown)

**Solo:**
- Keep one familiar on aggressive (attack Lumi) and one on defensive (kill adds)
- Circle strafe around the room, using pillars as cover between attack windows
- Do not chase Lumi when it teleports — wait for it to re-engage

**Co-op:**
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Primary DPS | Focus on Lumi, time attacks to Shard Volley cooldown |
| Add Clear | Dedicated player kills Crystal Fragments before they reach Lumi |
| Pillar Keeper | Ensure someone is always behind a pillar when Crystal Lance charges |

## Phase 2 — The Gauntlet (reported: 65% — 35% HP)

Community reports describe this phase as a vertical descent: Lumi shatters the floor, revealing a shaft, and the fight continues while falling / flying downward through the tower's central core. Broom flight is reported as mandatory.

### Phase 2 Mechanics (community-reported)

| Mechanic | Description | Counter |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Descending Arena** | You and Lumi fall through a vertical shaft | Stay on broom, control descent speed |
| **Crystal Walls** | Walls of the shaft have crystal outcroppings | Dodge or destroy to clear path |
| **Beam Barrage** | Lumi fires beams while falling | Weave between the beams |
| **Floor Platforms** | Small platforms for brief rests | Land to regenerate stamina briefly |
| **Wind Gusts** | Random gusts push you sideways | Counter-steer (lean into the gust) |

### Phase 2 Strategy

**Solo:**
- **Control your descent** — do not hold the descend key continuously. Tap it to maintain height
- Focus on dodging beams rather than dealing damage — survival comes first
- Use platforms to briefly land and refresh potion buffs
- The Aerial Combat Luminary node is reported as critical here
- Crystal Walls that block your path: destroy them with a ranged attack

**Co-op:**
- Call out "beam incoming" for vertical beams (dodge left/right)
- Call out "gust" for wind gusts (counter-steer in the opposite direction)
- DPS players focus Lumi; the support player clears crystal walls ahead of the group
- If a player gets knocked off their broom, community reports describe another player flying close and pressing `F` to "catch" them (grants brief hover)

## Phase 3 — Lumi's True Form (reported: 35% — 0% HP)

At the shaft's bottom, Lumi reportedly reforms into its true shape: a massive crystal humanoid with a visible cracked core. The arena is a flat stone circle. All previous abilities return, plus new ones.

### New Attacks (Phase 3, community-reported)

| Attack | Telegraph | Damage | Counter |
|--------|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Crystal Prison** (Trap) | Ground crystals rise around a target | Locks target in place, ticking damage | Other players must destroy the crystals |
| **Shatter Wave** (Room-wide AoE) | Lumi's core flashes rapidly, cracks spread | Very High (lethal without evasion) | Jump + dodge roll at the moment the wave reaches you |
| **Core Beam** (Channeled beam sweep) | Lumi's core opens, charges, then sweeps beam across arena | Instant kill (any hit) | Mount broom and fly behind Lumi — the beam reportedly cannot turn 180 degrees |
| **Desperation Cascade** (low HP) | Lumi's core explodes outward in rings | Lethal | Time consecutive jumps to clear each ring |

### Phase 3 Strategy

**Solo:**
1. Stay close to Lumi (mid-range) — the Shatter Wave is reportedly hardest to dodge at long range
2. When Crystal Prison traps you, switch to your second familiar's active ability (many can break crystals) or rapidly spam dodge
3. Core Beam: the instant you see the core open, mount broom and fly to Lumi's back. The beam sweeps forward; you are safe behind Lumi
4. At low HP: pop all remaining consumables and damage buffs
5. Desperation Cascade: time three consecutive jumps. Watch the ring visuals — jump when the flash reaches the ring edge

**Co-op:**
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Break Team | 2 players dedicated to breaking Crystal Prison crystals on trapped teammates |
| Bait | 1 player flies behind Lumi during Core Beam to bait the beam direction |
| Burst DPS | All players unload cooldowns at low HP |
| Caller | One player calls Shatter Wave timing for the team |

**Critical co-op note**: When a player is trapped in Crystal Prison, the other players have limited time to break them out or that player takes massive damage. Assign two players to handle this — one may not be enough.
