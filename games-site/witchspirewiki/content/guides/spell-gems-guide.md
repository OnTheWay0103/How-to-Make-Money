# Witchspire Spell Gems Guide — How to Get, Socket, and Upgrade Them

> **Last verified: October 23, 2026 — Witchspire 0.3.2.**

If you last played Witchspire before October 21, your weapons do not work the way you remember. The **Dormant Powers** update (v0.3.0) replaced the old fixed-weapon system with weapon bases plus **Spell Gems**, and patch 0.3.1 then made gems recoverable when you salvage gear. This guide covers the whole loop: where gems come from, how to identify them, how to socket and swap them, what Sigils do, and how upgrading actually works.

The short version: a weapon is now a shell. The spell is the gem inside it.

---

## 1. What Changed in 0.3.0

Before Dormant Powers, a weapon's abilities were fixed. A Stellar Burst wand always came with the same two spells. The official changelog is explicit that this is no longer true: as you find or craft weapons, you can **switch their Primary Spell and Invoked Spell freely between other weapons**, and you can rename the weapon.

Three consequences follow:

- A weapon with no Spell Gem inserted **cannot be used**. The official notes say it is "just a simple base" until a gem goes in.
- The same gem can move between weapon bases, so a favourite spell does not become obsolete when you find a better base.
- Crafted Superior and Master weapons roll a **randomized rarity** with varied stat rolls, so two weapons of the same name are not identical.

The **Weapon Enhancement Table** is the station where this happens. It is a new optional unlock in the redesigned Luminary tree, alongside the Salvage Station. If you had already unlocked Luminaries that were removed in the rework, the changelog says those points are refunded when you load into a world after the update.

---

## 2. Where Spell Gems Come From

There are **52 Spell Gems** in the game as of 0.3.0, according to the official changelog. They drop from creatures and can also be found while exploring. The announcement gives partial examples rather than a full drop table:

- **Chirili or Traxio** may carry a **Lux Gem**.
- **Specra or Xilvant** may carry a **Nox Gem**.
- **Froblins** may carry gems because they hoard shiny objects.
- **Mysterious Spell Gems** are unidentified gems. You take them to a **Salvage Station** to find out what is inside.

That last point matters more than it looks. Do not vendor or discard an unidentified gem because the tooltip is vague. The official notes describe Mysterious Spell Gems as a container to be identified, not as junk.

Patch 0.3.2 fixed an issue where **certain Spell Gems were not obtainable via Mysterious Spell Gems**. If you ran a stack of unidentified gems before October 23 and got nothing useful, that was a real bug, not bad luck.

> **Not a complete drop table:** the changelog names only four creature types and does not map every gem to a source. Treat any community table claiming a full 52-gem drop list as unverified until it is checked in-game. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 3. How to Socket and Swap Gems

The loop is:

1. **Get a weapon base.** Found weapons already come with gems inserted; crafted bases may not.
2. **Take it to the Weapon Enhancement Table.** This is where gems are inserted, removed, and swapped.
3. **Insert a Primary Spell and an Invoked Spell.** The changelog describes both slots; a weapon without a gem cannot be used at all.
4. **Swap freely between bases.** The gem is the persistent part, not the weapon.

This is the single biggest quality-of-life change in the update. Your first good gem is worth keeping and upgrading rather than replacing. The official advice is to keep favourite gems with you and move them into stronger weapons as you find them.

Patch 0.3.1 added the safety net: **salvaging a weapon returns any inserted Spell Gems**, and **salvaging a Spell Gem returns any inserted Sigils**. Before that patch, it was possible to lose a gem or sigil by scrapping the item holding it.

---

## 4. Sigils — the Second Layer

**Sigils** modify how a spell behaves. The changelog lists four examples:

| Sigil | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Ricochet | Projectiles bounce off walls |
| Summoner | Summon spells create more allies for longer |
| Bleed | Adds damage over time |
| Multishot | Fires bonus projectiles with every attack |

There are **25 Sigils** at launch. Rarer gems have **more Sigil Slots**, which is the main reason a high-rarity gem is worth more than a common one even when the base spell looks similar.

Two sourcing details from the official post are worth remembering:

- **Some Sigils are purchased from a particular shop.** The post does not name the vendor.
- **Many drop from two particular types of foe.** The post does not name the foes.

Because the official notes deliberately withhold those names, any guide that gives you an exact Sigil vendor location should be treated as community-reported unless it shows a screenshot. **[Unconfirmed]**

Sigils stack in at least one case: the changelog explicitly confirms you can add **more than one Multishot Sigil to a single spell**. Whether every Sigil stacks is not stated. Test before committing rare Sigils. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 5. Upgrading Weapons and Gems

Two separate upgrade tracks exist, and confusing them wastes materials.

**Weapon level** is upgraded at the Weapon Enhancement Table. Upgrading the weapon raises its damage, and that damage **benefits any spells inserted into it**. This means a high-level base makes every gem you put in it hit harder.

**Spell Gem level** is upgraded separately. The changelog says gems can be upgraded to increase "their power level and specific effects." Because gems move between weapons, an upgraded gem keeps its investment when you swap it into a new base.

The changelog does not publish material costs, upgrade caps, or success rates. Plan for costs to scale with rarity, but treat exact numbers as **[Unconfirmed]** until the game shows them.

### Upgrade priority for a new character

1. Pick one damage gem you actually like and upgrade it first.
2. Put it in the best base you have, then upgrade the base.
3. Add a second gem for utility: shields, healing, or a summon.
4. Only then start spreading upgrades across alternate elements.

The reason is simple: a moved gem keeps its level, a replaced weapon does not. Investing in the gem is the portable choice.

---

## 6. The Salvage Station and Scrappy

The **Salvage Station** recycles unwanted items into materials. It is not optional flavour: the changelog says certain recipes, including **Scrappy** and weapon/gem upgrades, require materials that only come from salvaging.

Things the official notes establish:

- Salvageable items have a **special tag on their tooltip**.
- **Rarity affects the returns**, and exact rewards are "unpredictable."
- **Scrappy** is the first craftable Familiar. You unlock it through Luminaries, then craft it at a **Spinner**. It cannot be found in the wild. There are **six variants**, one per element, each with a different signature ability and randomized rarity when crafted.
- Patch 0.3.1 extended salvaging to **Amulets, Rings, and Trinkets**, and allowed **weapons** to be salvaged with gems returned.
- Some **Legacy Items** obtained before the update may remain unsalvageable. This is an official warning, not a bug report.

Do not salvage every drop blindly. Check the tooltip tag first; an item without the tag will not recycle.

---

## 7. Common Problems After the Update

**"My old weapon does not appear at the Enhancement Table."** The update changed how weapons work, and the changelog warns that some legacy items remain unsalvageable. It does not promise that every pre-0.3.0 weapon converts into the new system. If a legacy weapon is unusable, replace it with a fresh drop rather than waiting for a fix. **[Unconfirmed]** on a per-item basis.

**"My Mysterious Spell Gems gave nothing."** Fixed in 0.3.2 for the affected gems.

**"My Luminary points are missing."** Removed Luminaries are refunded on loading a world after the update. Check your unspent points before re-speccing.

**"My summon or heal spell disappeared when I swapped weapons."** The gem, not the weapon, holds the spell. Check whether the gem stayed in the old base; patch 0.3.1 returns gems when you salvage that base.

**"Can I use the same gem on two weapons?"** No. A gem is a single item. You move it; you do not duplicate it.

---

## 8. A Practical Checklist

- Bring every **Mysterious Spell Gem** to a Salvage Station.
- Before salvaging anything, check for the salvage tag and remove gems/sigils if the patch level predates 0.3.1.
- Keep one upgraded damage gem across weapon upgrades instead of re-investing in each new drop.
- Use the second gem slot for a party role: shields and healing are new spell types in 0.3.0, and summons add bodies.
- Do not chase a full 52-gem collection early. Rarity and Sigil slots matter more than owning every element.
- Re-check the Weapon Enhancement Table after every major drop; a better base is worthless until a gem is inserted.

The Dormant Powers rework makes Witchspire's weapon system a build system rather than a loot ladder. Once you internalise that the gem is the character and the weapon is the socket, the update stops feeling like a reset and starts feeling like the point.
