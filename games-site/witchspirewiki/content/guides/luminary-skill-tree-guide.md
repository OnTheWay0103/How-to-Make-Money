# Luminary Skill Tree Guide — Best Builds

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

The Luminary skill tree governs your passive abilities, familiar slots, and stat boosts. Community reports describe **no way to reset your points** in the current build — every point is permanent. This guide tells you where community reports recommend investing for every playstyle.

> **Note:** branch names, node costs, and effects below come from community reports and have not been independently verified. Treat point costs and percentages as approximate.

---

## Critical Warning: No Respec (Community-Reported)

There is no respec option reported in the current build. It may be added in a future patch, but treat every point you spend as locked.

**What this means:**
- Do not spend points immediately — read the full tree first
- Do not spread points evenly — specialize
- Save points for key mid-game nodes you will unlock later

---

## Skill Tree Structure (Community-Reported)

The Luminary tree reportedly has 4 branches:

| Branch | Focus | Best For |
|--------|-------|----------|
| **Covenant** | Core passive, familiar capacity | Every build (reported priority) |
| **Vitality** | HP, defense, regeneration | Melee / solo builds |
| **Essence** | Mana, cooldown reduction, spell power | Caster builds |
| **Familiar** | Familiar stats, extra slots | Familiar-heavy builds |

Each branch reportedly has minor nodes (small stat gains) and major nodes (unique abilities).

## Recommended Builds (Community-Reported)

### Universal Starter Build (Any Coven)

Community reports recommend this order for the first points:

1. **Extra Familiar Slot** (Covenant)
2. **Quick Recovery** (Vitality)
3. **Familiar Bond** (Familiar)
4. **Arcane Flow** (Essence)
5. **Efficient Crafting** (Covenant)

This reportedly gives you 2 familiars, better healing, more familiar damage, lower cooldowns, and lower crafting costs. It works on every Coven.

### Spellblade Build (Cloudpiercers / Starsmiths / Foretellers)

After the starter build, add:
- **Tough Hide** (Vitality) — you are in melee range
- **Mana Font** (Essence) — Spellblade combos cost mana (reported)
- **Quick Recovery** upgrades (Vitality) — even more healing
- Save remaining points for future tiers

### Wand Build (Wildroots / Tomekeepers / Nightscribes)

After the starter build, add:
- **Arcane Flow** upgrades (Essence) — maximize casting
- **Mana Font** (Essence) — wand builds are mana-hungry (reported)
- **Wanderer's Step** (Covenant) — kiting is easier with speed
- Save remaining points

### Familiar-Master Build (Any Coven — Advanced)

After the starter build, add:
- **Collector's Instinct** (Familiar) — more materials
- **Familiar Bond** upgrades (Familiar) — more familiar damage
- **Tough Hide** (Vitality) — let your familiar tank
- Remaining points: save for reported future familiar evolution nodes

## Mistakes to Avoid (Community Advice)

1. **Spreading points across all 4 branches** — you get no powerful major nodes
2. **Skipping Extra Familiar Slot** — two familiars is twice the power (reported)
3. **Investing in C-tier nodes** — reported as traps
4. **Not saving points for Hearth milestones** — check what each Hearth tier unlocks before spending everything
