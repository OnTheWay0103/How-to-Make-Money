# Enemy Factions Guide — Foes of the Wilds

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 29, 2026**

Witchspire's world is divided into enemy factions — organized groups of hostile creatures that control territories, raid your base, and drop unique loot. This guide covers every faction, their behavior, and how to fight them.

---

## Faction Overview

| Faction | Territory | Difficulty | Aggression | Primary Damage |
|---------|-----------|------------|------------|----------------|
| Corrupted Beasts | Vyrs (night), Timberline | Easy-Medium | Reactive | Physical |
| Fae Remnants | Timberline Basin | Medium | Territorial | Magic |
| Sunken Ones | Mira Isles, waterways | Medium-Hard | Aggressive | Physical + Poison |
| Ash Legion | Ash Fields | Hard | Always hostile | Fire |
| Frostbound Cult | Frostbound Peaks | Hard | Always hostile | Ice |
| Void Touched | Shuddersands (night) | Very Hard | Always hostile | Dark Magic |

## 1. Corrupted Beasts

### Territory

| Zone | Spawn Rate | Time of Day |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Vyrs (outskirts) | Low | Mostly night |
| Timberline Basin (forest edge) | Medium | Day and night |
| Crystal Caverns | High | Always (underground) |

### Enemy Types

| Enemy | HP | Damage | Speed | Special |
|-------|-----|--------|-------|---------|
| Corrupted Wolf | 150 | 15 | Fast | Packs of 3-5, bleeds |
| Corrupted Boar | 300 | 25 | Medium | Charge attack (knockback) |
| Corrupted Bear | 600 | 45 | Slow | AoE swipe, enrage at 30% HP |
| Corrupted Sprite | 80 | 10 magic | Very fast | Flies, dodges melee, ranged attack |

### Weaknesses

| Element | Effectiveness |
|---------|--------------|
| Physical (slash) | Normal |
| Physical (blunt) | +20% damage |
| Fire | +30% damage |
| Ice | Normal |
| Magic | -10% damage (resistant) |

### Loot Table

| Item | Drop Rate | Quantity |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Beast Hide | 60% | 1-2 |
| Raw Meat | 50% | 1 |
| Corrupted Fang | 30% | 1 |
| Tough Sinew | 20% | 1 |
| Corrupted Core (rare) | 5% | 1 |

### Combat Tips

- Use blunt weapons for bonus damage
- Bears can be kited — they are slow
- Sprites are weak to AoE attacks
- Wolves call reinforcements if you do not kill them fast

| Fae Hunter | 200 | 30 physical | Very fast | Invisibility, backstab crit |
| Woodland Warden | 500 | 35 magic | Slow | AoE root spell, healing aura |
| Ancient Fae | 1,000 | 50 magic | Medium | Summons adds, shield spell |

### Weaknesses

| Element | Effectiveness |
|---------|--------------|
| Physical (slash) | +10% damage |
| Physical (blunt) | Normal |
| Fire | +40% damage |
| Ice | -20% damage (resistant) |
| Magic | -30% damage (highly resistant) |

### Loot Table

| Item | Drop Rate | Quantity |

| Luminescent Fiber | 40% | 1-2 |
| Trickster Coin | 25% | 1 |

### Combat Tips

- Fire damage is king here — bring fire weapons or spells
- Fae Tricksters will teleport behind you — keep moving
- Kill Wardens first (they heal others)
- Fae Hunters have a 2-second delay before backstabbing — dodge sideways

### Combat Tips

- Tide Callers can heal — focus them first

### Weaknesses

| Element | Effectiveness |
|---------|--------------|

| Physical (blunt) | Normal |
| Ice | +50% damage |
| Water | +30% damage |

### Loot Table

| Item | Drop Rate | Quantity |
|------|-----------|----------|

### Combat Tips

- Do not use fire weapons or spells — they are immune

## 6. Void Touched

### Territory

| Zone | Spawn Rate | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Shuddersands (night only) | Very High | Despawns at dawn |
| Random events | Low | Void rifts can open anywhere |

### Enemy Types

| Enemy | HP | Damage | Speed | Special |
|-------|-----|--------|-------|---------|
| Void Imp | 100 | 20 dark magic | Very fast | Teleports, debuff stacks |
| Void Strider | 400 | 45 dark magic | Fast | Phase through walls, silence |
| Void Weaver | 600 | 50 dark magic | Medium | Summons void imps, life drain |
| Void Walker (boss) | 3,000 | 100 dark magic | Fast | All abilities, reality warp |

### Weaknesses

| Element | Effectiveness |
|---------|--------------|
| Physical | Normal |
| Fire | Normal |
| Ice | Normal |
| Lightning | Normal |
| Holy/Light | +80% damage (if you have light source) |

### Loot Table

| Item | Drop Rate | Quantity |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Void Shard | 50% | 1 |
| Dark Essence | 30% | 1 |
| Void Silk | 20% | 1 |
| Reality Fragment | 10% | 1 |
| Astral Shard (legendary) | 2% | 1 |

### Combat Tips

- Fight Void enemies only if you have light sources — they take 80% more damage in light
- Void Striders can phase through walls — do not rely on walls for safety
- Silence from Void Striders stops all magic — physical builds are safer
- Void Touched enemies only spawn at night — plan expeditions accordingly

## Faction Reputation

| Faction | Default Standing | Effects of Low Rep | Effects of High Rep |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Corrupted Beasts | Hostile | Always attack | Cannot improve (beasts) |
| Fae Remnants | Neutral | Attack on sight | Fae merchants (unlock rare trades) |
| Sunken Ones | Hostile | Always attack | Cannot improve (hostile) |
| Ash Legion | Hostile | Always attack | Cannot improve (hostile) |
| Frostbound Cult | Hostile | Always attack | Cannot improve (hostile) |
| Void Touched | Hostile | Always attack | Cannot improve (hostile) |

### Fae Remnants Reputation

The only faction whose reputation can be improved:

| Rep Level | Requirement | Unlocks |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Neutral (default) | — | Basic Fae merchant (Timberline) |
| Friendly | Complete 3 Fae quests | Better prices, Fae Dust bulk purchase |
| Honored | Complete 6 Fae quests | Exclusive Fae weapon recipes |
| Exalted | Complete 10 Fae quests + boss item | Legendary Fae armor set |
