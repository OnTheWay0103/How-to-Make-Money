# Rare Materials Guide — Locations and Crafting Uses

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 29, 2026**

Rare materials unlock the best gear, potions, and base upgrades in Witchspire. This guide tells you exactly where to find each rare material and what it is used for.

---

## Material Rarity Tiers

| Tier | Color | Examples | Typical Sources |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| Common | White | Wood, Stone, Fiber | Surface gathering anywhere |
| Uncommon | Green | Iron Ore, Leather, Nectar | Vyrs region, basic enemies |
| Rare | Blue | Crystal Shards, Coal, Sturdy Fiber | Timberline Basin, cave systems |
| Epic | Purple | Ethereal Dust, Titan Hide | Elite enemies, boss drops |
| Legendary | Orange | Astral Essence, Phoenix Feather | Night events, legendary bosses |

## Early-Game Rare Material Farming

### Route 1: Timberline Basin Loop (30 minutes)

| Step | Location | Collect |
|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Enter Timberline Basin from Vyrs north exit | — |
| 2 | Crystal Caverns (follow left wall) | 8-16 Crystal Shards |
| 3 | Western cave system | 4-8 Coal |
| 4 | Misty Grove (east, set to night) | Ethereal Dust |
| 5 | Al'unean Golem arena | Titan Hide |
| 6 | Return to base via teleport | — |

**Total haul per loop**: 8-16 Crystal Shards, 4-8 Coal, 2-4 Ethereal Dust, 1 Titan Hide

### Route 2: Shuddersands Night Run (45 minutes)

Requires: Hearth Tier 3+, heat-resistant gear

| Step | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 1 | Arrive in Shuddersands at dusk |
| 2 | Scout for meteor impact sites (purple glow in sky) |
| 3 | Collect Astral Essence from impact crater |
| 4 | Fight Ethereal enemies (spawn during night) |
| 5 | Return to base before dawn |

**Note**: Shuddersands is very dangerous. Bring food, water, and heat-resistant armor.

## Storage Strategy

| Material Type | How to Store | Quantity to Keep |
|---------------|-------------|------------------|
| Common (Wood, Stone, Fiber) | Chests near Workbench | 100+ each |
| Rare ores (Iron, Coal) | Chests near Furnace | 50+ each |
| Crystal Shards | Chest near Witchcraft Circle | 30+ |
| Epic materials | Locked chest (secure storage) | 10-15 |
| Legendary materials | Locked chest + backup | 3-5 (do not waste) |
