# Al'uunian Golem Boss Guide

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

The Al'uunian Golem is the first major boss in Witchspire and your gateway to the second region, Timberline Basin. It is a stone construct awakened by the player's actions in the Eastern Ruins. This guide covers its reported attack patterns, the best positioning, and both solo and co-op strategies.

> **Note:** the attack patterns and numbers below come from community reports and have not been independently verified. Treat specifics (durations, ranges, exact effects) as approximate.

## Attack Patterns (Community-Reported)

The Al'uunian Golem is reported to have several attacks. Learn the telegraphs:

### 1. Ground Slam (AoE)
- **Telegraph**: Golem raises both arms, ground glows
- **Counter**: Dodge roll away. Do not block — it reportedly breaks guard
- **Safe spot**: Behind the golem (it slams forward)

### 2. Rock Throw (Ranged)
- **Telegraph**: Golem reaches into its chest, pulls out a boulder
- **Counter**: Sidestep when the boulder is thrown. Do not run backward
- **Safe spot**: Stay close — the boulder cannot hit you at point-blank

### 3. Rock Armor (Buff)
- **Telegraph**: Golem crouches, stone skin glows
- **Effect**: Damage reduction for a duration (reported)
- **Counter**: Stop attacking and focus on dodging. Wait it out

### 4. Stomp Combo (Melee)
- **Telegraph**: Golem stomps several times in a row, each stomp creating a shockwave
- **Counter**: Dodge the first shockwave, then run in a circle around the golem

### 5. Slam + Rock Shower (Enrage — low HP)
- **Telegraph**: Golem roars (audible cue), slams both arms, sends rock fragments across the arena
- **Counter**: Broom flight — get airborne immediately when you hear the roar

## Solo Strategy

### Spellblade (Recommended: Cloudpiercer)
1. Let your familiar engage first to draw aggro
2. Circle-strafe behind the golem
3. Use light + heavy combos to hit its weak points
4. Dodge backward when you see the arm raise (Ground Slam telegraph)
5. Save mana for emergency attacks during Rock Armor phase
6. At low HP, mount your broom and stay airborne until the enrage ends

### Wand (Recommended: Wildroot or Tomekeeper)
1. Keep maximum range at all times
2. Use your familiar to tank
3. Cast and move — never stand still
4. Nature builds: keep your heal-over-time on yourself
5. When the golem turns to you, sprint to the opposite side of the arena
6. At low HP, mount your broom and kite from the air

## Co-op Strategy (2-4 Players)

| Role | Player Count | Responsibilities |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Tank | 1 (Wildroot / Cloudpiercer) | Face golem, hold aggro, dodge slams |
| DPS | 1-2 (Any) | Stay behind golem, maximize damage |
| Healer | 0-1 (Wildroot) | Maintain heals on tank |

### Positioning
- Tank holds golem at arena center, facing away from the group
- DPS and healer stay at the golem's back/sides
- When Rock Armor activates, all players stop attacking and spread out

### Co-op Tips
- Designate one player to call out telegraphs — it helps the team react
- If you have 2+ Wands, stagger your spells so mana regen is constant
- Set a return point at your Hearth before starting
- If someone dies, the tank must hold aggro while they run back

## Post-Boss Checklist

After defeating the Al'uunian Golem:
1. Collect the Golem Core from the arena floor
2. Return to your Hearth
3. Head to the Vyrs South Passage — the route to Timberline Basin

> **Note:** community reports differ on whether the Golem Core alone opens the passage or whether a quest chain is involved. Follow the main quest — see the [Main Quest Walkthrough](/guides/main-quest-walkthrough).
