# Witchspire Familiars Guide — Full Database & How to Catch Them

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Familiars are magical creatures you bond with by **killing them and holding F on their lingering spirit**. This page is the master resource: the real bonding mechanic, the incense table, starter picks, the best early-game targets, and the full database of every known familiar — its element, where it spawns, and the crafting stations it powers. There are **30+ familiars** across 4 rarity tiers (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary), plus Celestial/"Shifty" recolor variants.

> **Note on naming:** this is Early Access and creature names vary between community sources and patches. Where a familiar is known by multiple names, we list them. If you spot a new one, it belongs here.

---

## How Bonding Actually Works (Step by Step)

"I killed a creature and nothing happened. How do I actually catch these things?" — the most common question in Witchspire's early game, and the answer surprises most players: **you do not weaken and throw orbs. You kill the creature and pray its spirit stays.**

1. **Kill the target creature** — not just weaken it. Kill it.
2. **Watch what its spirit does.** On death, a spirit ball appears. Two outcomes:
   - **Spirit flies up into the sky** with a purple streak → failed bonding attempt. The creature is gone for good this spawn.
   - **Spirit lingers near the ground** as a floating ball → success is on the table.
3. **Walk up to the lingering spirit and HOLD F** to bond. It enters your inventory as a Familiar.
4. **Repeat** until the spirit lingers — whether it stays is RNG.

That is the entire mechanic. There are **no capture orbs, no HP thresholds, no throwables**. If a guide tells you to weaken to 30% and throw an orb, it is describing a different game.

### Why Spirits Fly Away — The RNG Modifiers

The "lingers or flies away" roll is your bonding chance. You can push it:

| Modifier | Effect | How to get it |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **Incense** | +10% to +25% depending on tier (see table below) | Crafted at the Workbench / looted from chests and bosses |
| **Spirit Charmer (Luminary node)** | "Increases how often spirits linger" — the single biggest permanent boost | First node: Hearth Level 3 + Bonding Skill Level 5; more nodes at higher Hearth tiers |
| **Luck stat** | Community-confirmed to help the roll | Gear, consumables with Luck |
| **Foretellers Coven** | +40% bonding rate — the only real gameplay difference between the six covens | Chose the Foretellers at character creation (see [Best Coven Guide](/guides/best-coven-guide)) |

**Practical rule:** save your incense for **Epic and Legendary** creatures. Common and Rare familiars linger often enough that incense is wasted on them.

### FAQ on the Mechanic

- **Q: I killed a creature and no spirit appeared at all.** Normal. Only a portion of creatures are familiar candidates at all; the rest drop regular loot. Kill again — you can't tell which are candidates until they die.
- **Q: The spirit flew into the sky. Can I chase it?** No. That is a failed roll. The creature is gone until it respawns.
- **Q: Do I need the creature at low HP?** No — that is a false mechanic from older guides. Kill it outright; the linger roll happens on death.
- **Q: Does incense work in co-op?** Yes, but it only benefits the player who used it.

## Incense Guide — Recipes, Effects & Timing

| Incense | Recipe | Duration | Bonding Bonus |
|---------|--------|:--:|:--:|
| Simple Spirit Incense | 3 Spirit Dust + 1 Simple Ritual Candle | ~20s | +10% |
| Potent Spirit Incense | 5 Greater Spirit Dust + 1 Simple Ritual Candle | ~15s | +15% |
| Rich Spirit Incense | 3 Brilliant Spirit Dust + 1 Ritual Candle | ~10s | +20% |
| Shimmering Incense | 3 Greater Spirit Dust + 1 Shimmerdust | ~10s | +25% |

> **Note:** durations and bonuses are approximate, based on community reports. Community reports also suggest that a recent update lengthened incense durations; the exact values have not been independently verified. See the [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes-updates) page.

**Timing matters:** the buff starts the moment you *use* the incense, not when the creature dies. Light it late in a fight — when the target is low and about to die — so the window covers the kill. Also: **a failed bonding attempt consumes the incense.** That is normal, not a bug.

## Familiar Slots, Equipping & Abilities

- **Three familiar slots** in the inventory (the bottom circles). Stand near a **Hearth**, open the menu with **Tab**, and drag familiars into slots.
- **One familiar is active in combat** — press **C** to cycle between your three equipped.
- Remove: minus button on the slot. Replace: rotation button at the top of the circle.
- Familiars also work **passively as crafting helpers**: certain stations need (or are sped up by) specific familiars — Charlings speed up Cookers, Xilvants and Shudders power Witchcraft Circles, Lety runs the Sawmill, Treetop the Forester's Hut, Bompus and Detrox the Miner's Cabin, and so on. See the crafting cheat sheet below.
- **Community role framework:** players loosely group familiars into support roles (tank, damage, utility) based on their passive effects. These categories are community shorthand, not an official system — check a familiar's passive description in-game before relying on it.
- **Celestial / "Shifties"** are alternate color patterns of normal familiars — same tier, same bonding rules, just rarer to roll.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many familiars can I equip?**
Three slots; one active in combat (press C to cycle). Equip/swap at a Hearth via the Tab menu.

**Q: Do familiars level up?**
Familiars support your build with passives and crafting roles; the deep leveling/progression system is still EA-shaped. The "A Touch of Magic" update (Familiar Wheel) is the developer's planned overhaul — see the [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes-updates).

**Q: Can I find every familiar in the wild, including the starters?**
Yes. All four starters spawn in the world (Fien at night in Timberline Basin; the others on their home islands).

**Q: Is there a guaranteed way to catch Legendaries?**
Not in the current build. Stack incense + Spirit Charmer + Luck + Foretellers, and expect re-rolls. The roadmap's "A Touch of Magic" update (Familiar Wheel) is aimed at making bonding friendlier — see the [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes-updates).

**Q: Do bonded familiars persist when I visit a friend's world?**
Your bonded familiars stay in your inventory — they travel with you (see the [Co-op Troubleshooting Guide](/guides/coop-troubleshooting-guide) for exactly what carries over).

**Q: What is the best familiar in the game?**
There is no single answer in the current build — meta coverage is thin and patch-flavored. The current community picks: Rockling (early value), Quol (starter versatility), and Xilvant (crafting utility). Check the [Tier List](/tier-list) for rankings as they develop.
