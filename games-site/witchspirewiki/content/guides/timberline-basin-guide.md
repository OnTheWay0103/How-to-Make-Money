# Timberline Basin Region Guide — Full Exploration

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Timberline Basin is the second region you reach after defeating the Al'uunian Golem in Vyrs and passing through the South Passage. Dense forests, crystal caves, and elevated ruins define this mid-level zone. This guide maps every point of interest, resource hotspot, and familiar spawn.

> **Note:** exact enemy levels, chest contents, and NPC services below come from community reports and have not been independently verified.

---

## Region Overview

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Level Range | Mid-game (noticeably tougher than Vyrs) |
| Area Size | Large |
| Flight Pillars | 4 |
| Bosses | None confirmed (quest-based encounters reported) |
| NPCs | Several (community reports vary) |
| Hidden Chests | 8 (reported) |
| Resource Caves | 3 |
| Recommended Stay | Until the mid-game / before Mira Isles |

### How to Enter

1. Defeat the Al'uunian Golem in Vyrs Eastern Ruins
2. Collect the **Golem Core** from the arena floor
3. Head to the Vyrs South Passage gate and unlock the way through

> **Note:** community reports differ on whether the Golem Core alone opens the gate or whether a quest chain is involved. Follow the main quest — it routes you through at the right time.

**Don't forget**: buy the Timberline Basin map in Vyrs before leaving if a vendor sells one — it reportedly reveals the Flight Pillar locations.

## Key Locations

### Al'uunian Tower

The central landmark of Timberline Basin — a massive stone spire surrounded by floating crystalline shards.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Location | Center-north of the map |
| Enemies | Spectral-type witches, crystal golems (reported) |
| Purpose | Main quest location, lore exposition, reported mid-boss encounter |
| Flight Pillar | Reported on the tower's mid-level platform |
| Hidden Chests | 2 (reported) |

Inside the tower, community reports describe:
- **Scholar Vex's Journal** (lore item, starts a side quest)
- **Crystal Console** — reportedly activates the basin's Flight Pillar network
- **Reported mid-boss:** a corrupted crystal golem whose drops are not independently verified

### Shard Caverns

An underground cave system beneath the region. Glowing crystal formations light the path.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Entrance | East side of the map, behind a waterfall (reported) |
| Enemies | Crystal-type crawlers and wraiths (reported) |
| Resources | Crystal Shards (abundant), Coal deposits, Iron Ore |
| Hidden Chests | 3 (reported) |

**Key spots inside Shard Caverns:**

- **Crystal Vein Junction** — reported as the best farming spot in the region for Crystal Shards
- **Echo Pool** — an underground lake; community reports mention mana-restoring water and rare fishing
- **Deepest chamber** — a reported optional boss ("Crystal Crawler Queen") whose drops are not verified

### Flight Pillars (4)

| # | Location | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | Basin Entrance | North gate from Vyrs |
| 2 | Central Grove | Mid-zone waterfall |
| 3 | Hollow Log | Eastern section |
| 4 | Basin Summit | Highest point in the zone |

## Resource Farming Route

Optimal loop for Timberline Basin farming:

```
Southern Gate → Basin Crossroads (vendor + pillar) → Al'uunian Tower 
(chests + pillar) → Shard Caverns (crystals + coal + chests) 
→ Western Ridge (fiber + chest) → Northern Lookout (chest) 
→ Back to Crossroads
```

Total time: ~30 minutes. Total haul: ~25 Crystal Shards, ~10 Coal, ~15 Iron Ore, ~8 Moonleaf, ~15 Sturdy Fiber (community-reported averages).

## Leaving Timberline Basin

The route onward to the **Mira Isles** is by sea — community reports describe a dock/harbor route (the boat route to the Isles). Requirements reported by players:

- **Decent gear** (the Isles are tougher than the Basin)
- **Hearth Tier 4** recommended
- **Boat fare** (community-reported gold cost)

**Completion checklist before leaving:**
- [ ] Activate all 4 Flight Pillars
- [ ] Find the hidden chests
- [ ] Bond a Shardling in Shard Caverns
- [ ] Stock up on Coal from Merchant Reyes
- [ ] Upgrade to Steel tools
