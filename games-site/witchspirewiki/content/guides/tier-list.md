## Witchspire Tier List — Covens, Familiars, Weapons & Gear

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

> **Honest caveat first:** Witchspire's six covens are far more similar than most MMOs. Per reviews and the developer's own forum answers, the real differences in the current Early Access build are mostly **starter weapon (Wand vs Spellblade), magic school, and one stand-out perk** — not six fully distinct classes. Treat this list as "what to expect," not "the only way to play."

### Coven Tier List

| Tier | Coven | Why |
|:--:|------|-----|
| **S** | **Foretellers** | The only mechanically meaningful coven choice: **+40% familiar bonding rate**. If you plan to collect familiars (and you should — they're half the game), this is the pick. Higher rarity fams = bigger early advantage |
| **S** | **Wildroots** | Best solo survivability — Nature healing and thorn armor let you face-tank early content. The community's "safest first game" pick |
| **A** | **Cloudpiercers** | Spellblade + Lux burst. Best newcomer damage, simple rotation, no self-heal |
| **A** | **Nightscribes** | Highest burst ceiling (Astral crits) but self-damage makes every fight risky — for players who know boss patterns |
| **B** | **Tomekeepers** | Reliable DoT sustain (Nox), slow ramp — fine in co-op, sluggish solo |
| **B** | **Starsmiths** | Crystal CC/utility shines in a coordinated team, underwhelming solo |

**The real decision:** 1) do you want the +40% bonding rate (Foretellers)? 2) do you prefer Wand or Spellblade? 3) solo or co-op? Everything else is flavor. See the [Best Coven Guide](/guides/best-coven-guide) for full class breakdowns.

### Familiar Tier List (Early Game)

| Tier | Familiar | Why |
|:--:|----------|-----|
| **S** | **Rockling** | Easy to bond in the starting zone, tanky, the community's universal "grab one" recommendation |
| **S** | **Quol** | Best starter pick — Ethereal, melee-friendly, useful passive value |
| **A** | **Taileaf** | Early Nature coverage, easy hunt left of spawn |
| **A** | **Xilvant** | Common Timberline spawn, runs Witchcraft Circles — crafting workhorse |
| **A** | **Fien** | Nox coverage; wand-friendly starter or night hunt in Timberline |
| **B** | **Shardling** | Crystal coverage while mining Shard Caverns — efficient detour |
| **B** | **Lety / Bompus / Detrox** | Station-required familiars (Sawmill / Miner's Cabin) — grab when you build those stations |
| **C** | **Epic/Legendary fams** | Best passives, but bonding RNG makes them unreliable early — bond Commons first (see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/all-familiars-guide)) |

### Weapon Tier List (Wand vs Spellblade)

| Tier | Weapon | Playstyle |
|:--:|------|------|
| **A** | **Spellblade** | Melee + magic hybrid; the current community favorite for active play. Best with Cloudpiercers/Starsmiths/Foretellers |
| **A** | **Wand** | Ranged, safer, pairs with Wildroots/Tomekeepers/Nightscribes. Perfectly viable — pick by coven, not by "meta" |

There is no dominant weapon in the current build — they're tied to your coven pick. See [Spellblade vs Wand Guide](/guides/spellblade-vs-wand-guide) for the full comparison.

## Gear Progression by Game Stage

> **Note:** Witchspire gear stats are not published by the developer, and the exact stat values in the community's comparisons have not been independently verified. The progression *framework* below — what tier of gear you should be aiming for at each stage — is consistent across player reports. Treat specific item names and numbers as approximate.

### Early Game (Hearth Tier 1-2, Vyrs Region)

| Slot | Item | How to Obtain |
|------|------|---------------|
| Weapon | Starter wand or spellblade → Iron-tier upgrade | Craft at Workbench |
| Broom | Basic Broom | Craft after Hearth Tier 2 (8 Refined Wood, 4 Iron Ingot, 6 Sturdy Fiber, 3 Nectar) |
| Armor | Fiber Set (full) | Craft at Workbench |

**Focus:** upgrade your weapon and unlock the Basic Broom before anything else — travel speed is the best early investment.

### Mid Game (Hearth Tier 3-4, Timberline Basin)

| Slot | Item | How to Obtain |
|------|------|---------------|
| Weapon | Next weapon tier using Timberline materials (Crystal Shards, Steel) | Craft at Workbench / Advanced Workbench |
| Broom | Higher-tier broom (community names: Swift, Ember, Frost) | Craft at Advanced Workbench |
| Armor | Iron-tier armor set | Craft at Workbench |

**Focus:** farm Crystal Shards and Steel for your next weapon and broom upgrades.

### Late Game (Hearth Tier 5+, Shuddersands, Mira Isles & Witchspire Tower)

| Slot | Item | How to Obtain |
|------|------|---------------|
| Weapon | Top-tier wand or spellblade (community names: Moonlit, Shadow) | Advanced materials from end-game zones |
| Broom | Top-tier broom (community names: Storm, Phantom) | Craft at Enchantment Table |
| Armor | Enchanted Steel set | Craft with Ethereal Dust |

**Focus:** Ethereal Dust and Astral Essence are the late-game bottleneck materials — night events in Shuddersands are the community-reported best source. See the [Events Guide](/guides/events-guide).

### Recommended Gear by Coven

| Coven | Weapon Priority | Broom Priority | Playstyle |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|-----------|
| Cloudpiercer (Spellblade + Lux) | Spellblade upgrades first | Speed-focused brooms | Aggressive melee |
| Wildroot (Wand + Nature) | Wand with sustain | Stamina-efficient brooms | Kiting and sustain |
| Nightscribe (Wand + Astral/Nox) | Highest burst damage wand | Speed-focused brooms | Glass cannon burst |
| Foreteller (Wand + Nox) | Wand with control | Stamina-efficient brooms | Crowd control and kiting |

> **Note:** higher-tier broom and weapon names (Swift, Ember, Frost, Storm, Phantom, Moonlit, Shadow) come from community reports. Exact stats, materials, and station tiers for these items have not been independently verified — check in-game crafting menus before committing materials.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there a "best class"?**
Not the way most games mean it. The six covens share more than they differ; pick Foretellers for bonding or Wildroots for survival, then play what feels good.

**Q: Does the tier list change every patch?**
Yes — this is Early Access and balance changes frequently. We relabel guides by version and update this page after significant balance changes (tracked on [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes-updates)).

**Q: What about PvP?**
Witchspire has no PvP in the current build — see the [PvP Status page](/guides/pvp-guide) for details.
