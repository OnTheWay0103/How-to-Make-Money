# Witchspire Mounts Guide — All Mount Types, How to Unlock, and Best Mounts

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 27, 2026**

Getting around in Witchspire quickly is essential for efficient farming, exploration, and retreating from dangerous encounters. Mounts are the primary form of fast travel within a region, and different mount types excel in different situations. This guide covers every mount available, how to unlock them, and which ones to prioritize.

---

## Mount System Overview

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Mount Types** | 3 (Broom, Land Mount, Familiar Mount) |
| **Unlock Method** | Crafting or quest completion |
| **Speed Tiers** | 3 (Basic, Improved, Masterwork) |
| **Upgrade Method** | Materials + Gold at Hearth Workbench |
| **Combat While Mounted** | Only Familiar Mounts allow combat |
| **Fall Damage** | Reduced by 50% while mounted |

---

## Mount Type Comparison

| Mount Type | Speed | Terrain | Unlock Difficulty | Combat Allowed | Best For |
|------------|-------|---------|-------------------|----------------|----------|
| **Broom** | Fast | Air (all terrain) | Medium | No | General travel, exploration |
| **Land Mount** | Medium | Ground only | Easy | No | Early game, resource hauling |
| **Familiar Mount** | Variable | Air or Ground | Hard | Yes | Combat relocation, escaping |

## 1. Broom — The All-Rounder

The broom is the default mount in Witchspire and the most versatile. It is unlocked early in the main quest and can be upgraded to become the fastest mount in the game.

### How to Unlock

| Step | Quest / Requirement | Details |
|------|--------------------|---------|
| 1 | Complete "A Witch's First Broom" quest | Given by Magister Elara in Vyrs (level 5+) |
| 2 | Gather materials | 10x Twisted Wood, 5x Spider Silk, 3x Moonstone Shard |
| 3 | Craft at Hearth Workbench | Requires Workbench Tier 2 |

### Broom Speed Tiers

| Tier | Materials Required | Speed Bonus | Special Effect |
|------|-------------------|-------------|----------------|
| Basic (Tier 1) | Default | 100% (baseline) | None |
| Improved (Tier 2) | 15x Refined Wood, 8x Silkweave, 5x Glowing Dust | +25% | 30% less stamina drain while flying |
| Masterwork (Tier 3) | 20x Petrified Wood, 10x Phantom Silk, 3x Luminary Crystal | +50% | Can carry 1 passenger (co-op only) |

### Broom Tips

- **Stamina management**: Flying drains stamina. Hold sprint sparingly and glide downhill to conserve energy.
- **Flight pillars**: Use unlocked Flight Pillars to teleport between regions. These are separate from the broom but complement it well.
- **Weather impact**: Flying in rain or storms drains 50% more stamina. See our [Weather Guide](/witchspire/content/guides/weather-guide) for details.

## 2. Land Mount — The Workhorse

Land mounts are ground-based creatures that you can tame and ride. They are slower than brooms but can carry significantly more weight, making them ideal for resource gathering runs.

### Available Land Mounts

| Mount | Location | Taming Requirement | Carry Capacity | Speed |
|-------|----------|-------------------|----------------|-------|
| **Meadow Stag** | Vyrs Meadows | 20x Sweet Berries, rope trap | +50% carry weight | 80% of broom speed |
| **Stoneback Tortoise** | Shuddersands | Defeat Shell Guardian, offer 10x Cactus Fruit | +100% carry weight | 50% of broom speed |
| **Mira Runner** | Mira Isles | Complete "Isle Sprint" race quest | +30% carry weight | 110% of broom speed (on flat ground) |

### How Taming Works

1. Locate the creature's habitat (see table above)
2. Approach slowly (sneaking) — do not run or attack
3. Deploy the **Rope Trap** from your inventory near its patrol path
4. Lure the creature into the trap using its preferred bait
5. Once trapped, approach and press **F** to begin the taming minigame (rhythm-based button presses)
6. Success = mount acquired. Failure = creature flees, must wait 24 in-game hours to retry

### Best Land Mount by Use Case

| Use Case | Best Mount | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| Farming runs | Stoneback Tortoise | Double carry capacity means fewer trips |
| Exploring new regions | Mira Runner | Fastest ground speed, good for covering ground |
| Early game (first 10 hours) | Meadow Stag | Easiest to tame, decent all-around stats |

## 3. Familiar Mount — The Combat Mount

Some Familiars can serve as mounts. These are the most expensive to unlock but offer unique combat advantages, including the ability to fight while mounted.

### Familiar Mounts

| Familiar | Type | How to Unlock | Mount Speed | Combat Bonus |
|----------|------|---------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Storm Hawk** | Flying Familiar | Luminary Skill Tree (Nature branch, Tier 3) | Very Fast | Can attack while mounted (ranged peck) |
| **Ember Wolf** | Ground Familiar | Defeat Alpha Ember Wolf in Shuddersands and capture with Familiar Orb | Fast | Mounted sprint sets ground on fire (damages enemies) |
| **Crystal Moth** | Flying Familiar | Hidden quest in Timberline Basin caves | Slow | Glowing trail reveals hidden objects and chests |

### Why Use a Familiar Mount

- **Combat mobility**: You can cast spells and use items while mounted on a Familiar
- **No stamina drain**: Familiar mounts use the Familiar's stamina pool, not yours
- **Unique traversal**: Crystal Moth can reveal hidden pathways invisible to other mounts

### Familiar Mount Drawbacks

- Very expensive to unlock (requires max Familiar bond level + rare materials)
- If the Familiar dies in combat, the mount is unavailable for 30 minutes (real time)
- Not available until mid-game (level 20+)

## Common Mount Mistakes

- **Ignoring stamina management while mounted** — a broom with no stamina is slower than walking
- **Taming a mount before unlocking the Hearth stable** — stables cost 2,000 gold and require Workbench Tier 3
- **Using a land mount in mountainous regions** — most land mounts cannot climb steep terrain; stick to brooms in Timberline Basin and Shuddersands
- **Not feeding your mount** — mounts have a hidden "energy" stat that depletes over time. Feed them Hay or Sweet Berries to restore it
