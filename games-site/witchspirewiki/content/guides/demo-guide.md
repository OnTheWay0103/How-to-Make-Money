# Witchspire Demo & Purchase Guide — Price, Platforms, and System Requirements

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Witchspire entered Steam Early Access on June 10, 2026, as listed on the official Steam store page. Whether you are considering the free demo or deciding which edition to buy, this guide covers everything from price tiers to console availability to minimum PC specs.

> **Note:** demo content details, price history, and level caps below come from community reports and have not all been independently verified.

---

## Is There a Witchspire Demo?

**Yes.** Community reports describe a free Witchspire demo released around Steam Next Fest in early 2026, and it reportedly remains available on the Steam store page.

To download it:

1. Visit the [Witchspire Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2679100/Witchspire)
2. Click **"Download Demo"** below the purchase options
3. Install and play — no purchase required

The demo reportedly has no time limit — you can play as long as you like within its content boundaries.

---

## Demo Content Scope

The demo offers a slice of the Early Access experience (community-reported):

| Feature | Demo | Full EA Game |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Playable Region | Vyrs (starting area) | Vyrs + Timberline Basin + Mira Isles + Shuddersands |
| Level Cap | Reported cap in the low single digits | No confirmed cap — community reports describe the current max level around 30 (unverified) |
| Playable Covens | Community reports describe a limited selection (e.g., Cloudpiercers) | All 6 Covens |
| Familiars | Starter familiars (Quol, Locto, Bril, Fien) | 25+ familiars (reported) |
| Building | Basic cabin + furnishings | Full base building |
| Farming | Not available | Full farming system |
| Hearth Upgrades | Tier 1 only (reported) | Up to Tier 6 |
| Co-op | Up to 4 players | Up to 4 players |
| Crafting | Basic tools + workbench | All stations and recipes |

**What you can do in the demo (reported):**
- Explore the Vyrs region freely
- Fight the Al'uunian Golem (first boss)
- Bond your first familiar
- Build a starter cabin
- Play solo or co-op with up to 4 players
- Encounter a mysterious NPC near the starting area (community-reported; name not confirmed)

**What is NOT in the demo (reported):**
- Farming and automation systems (added in Early Access)
- The full Luminary skill tree
- Most familiars and crafting recipes
- Higher-tier Hearth upgrades

## Does Demo Progress Carry Over?

**Community reports say demo saves do NOT transfer to the Early Access game** — when you purchase the full game, you start a new character from scratch. This is standard practice for demo builds (they use a separate app ID on Steam, and save data is typically not shared).

**What you keep:** Knowledge of the early game. Use the demo to decide if the gameplay loop clicks for you, then start fresh with a clear plan.

## Price and Versions

Prices below are **as listed on the official Steam store page** (US pricing; regional prices vary).

### Standard Edition — $22.99

The game itself. Includes the full Early Access build and all future EA updates. The full 1.0 release is included at no extra cost (per the store page).

- Base price: **$22.99**
- A launch discount was reported (community reports); current price is the store page price
- The store page notes the price may increase when the game leaves Early Access

### Founder's Edition DLC — $14.99

An add-on content pack (does NOT include the base game). The store page lists:

- **Digital Art Book** — concept art and behind-the-scenes visuals
- **Digital Soundtrack** — full official OST
- **Royal Decoration Pack** — throne, banners, carpets, royal-themed bed
- **Plushie Crafting Pack** — craftable plushie decorations
- **Mystic Style Pack** — unique hat and broom cosmetic skins
- **Familiar Accessory Pack** — bow ties, headbands, mini hats for your pets
- **Early Bird Cosmetic Pack** — exclusive EA-only decorations

Per the store page, Founder's Edition content unlocks when the game reaches 1.0.

### Founder's Pack Bundle — $37.98

Standard Edition + Founder's Edition DLC in one bundle.

- Bundle price: **$37.98**
- Saves roughly $5 compared to buying separately

## System Requirements

The specs below are **as listed on the official Steam store page**. Note that Early Access system requirements can change as the game is updated.

### Minimum (Low settings, 1080p)

| Component | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| OS | Windows 11 (64-bit) |
| CPU | Intel Core i5-10400 / AMD Ryzen 5 5600G |
| RAM | 16 GB |
| GPU | NVIDIA GTX 1070 or AMD Radeon RX 6600S |
| Storage | 12 GB (SSD strongly recommended) |
| DirectX | Version 12 |
| Network | Broadband internet required |

### Recommended (High settings, 1080p 60+ FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| OS | Windows 11 (64-bit) |
| CPU | Intel Core i7-10700K / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| RAM | 16 GB |
| GPU | NVIDIA RTX 3070 / RTX 3080 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT |
| Storage | 12 GB (SSD strongly recommended) |
| DirectX | Version 12 |
| Network | Broadband internet required |

### Notes
- The store page lists Windows 11 (64-bit) only
- An SSD is strongly recommended even for minimum spec
- Hosting multiplayer sessions with multiple players may impact performance
- DLSS and FSR are listed as supported for lower-end hardware
