# Companion Guide — Pick, Build, and Level Your Familiar

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 31, 2026**

Your companion is the familiar you keep summoned and feed XP — the one that fights, gathers, and crafts beside you. The all-familiars database tells you what exists; this guide tells you which one to actually bring, how to build its ability loadout, and how to level it without wasting time. Getting this right can double your farming output and carry you through hard dungeons.

---

## Companion Roles

Every familiar fills one of three roles. Decide what you need most before catching anything:

| Role | What It Does | Best For |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Fighter** | Deals damage, holds aggro, procs debuffs | Boss fights, dungeons, PvP |
| **Gatherer** | Auto-collects nearby resources, finds rare nodes | Farming runs, material grinding |
| **Crafter** | Boosts crafting output, reduces material costs | Potion/gear production, economy |

A fighter is not "better" than a gatherer — it is better at a different job. Keep at least one strong companion in each role and switch by activity.

---

## How to Build a Companion

Each familiar has 3 ability slots. Slot selection matters more than the familiar's base stats:

### Ability Slot Priorities by Role

| Role | Slot 1 | Slot 2 | Slot 3 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Fighter | Damage skill | Taunt / aggro | Self-heal or shield |
| Gatherer | Find-rare material | Speed boost | Gather yield + |
| Crafter | Cost reduction | Output + | Durability bonus |

### Elemental Synergy

Familiars carry an element — match it to the content you farm:

- **Fire** vs. Timberline Basin's ice-type enemies (extra damage)
- **Frost** vs. Shuddersands' heat-resistant variants
- **Shadow** (Nox) vs. Nightveil Cave's light-aligned mobs — the Void Pup is the standout pick here
- **Lux (Light)** vs. Mira Isles' shadow-corrupted spawns

Check the elements mechanics guide for the full wheel — exploiting the 25% advantage is the cheapest power boost in the game.

## Leveling Companions Fast

Familiars gain XP while summoned during combat and gathering. The amount scales with participation — a familiar that never fights levels at half speed.

- **Solo leveling**: keep the familiar in Fighter stance during every fight, even easy ones
- **Best XP farm**: the Timberline Basin crystal run — pulls are dense and familiar AoE pays off
- **XP potion synergy**: feed your familiar the same XP elixirs you use; the boost is shared
- **Do not spread XP thin**: level one companion per role to level 20 before starting a second — a level 20 fighter beats two level 12s every time
- **Evolution checkpoints**: most familiars evolve at levels 10, 20, and 30 — evolution resets their ability slots to empty, so plan respec materials before the milestone

---

## Evolution Milestones

Every familiar evolves at set level thresholds. Evolution does not change the familiar's species — it upgrades its base stats, unlocks a 4th ability slot at level 30, and refreshes its appearance. Plan around the milestones below:

| Level | What Happens | What To Do |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| 10 | First evolution, stats +20%, new idle animation | Check that its element did not shift before building around it |
| 20 | Second evolution, stats +20%, first passive unlock | Start banking respec materials for the level-30 slot change |
| 30 | Final evolution, 4th ability slot opens | Fill the new slot immediately — empty slots are wasted power |

**Evolution warnings:**

- A familiar whose element shifts on evolution (some do) will lose its region matchup — re-check the element wheel before taking it into a dungeon
- Ability slots reset to empty at every evolution — this is intentional, not a bug. Do not fill them fully before a milestone
- The level-20 passive is account-wide per familiar species, so it persists even if you re-catch a fresh one later

## Common Companion Mistakes

- **Keeping the starter past level 15** — the Pufffin is a fine early fighter, but the Shuddersands catches outclass it in every stat by level 15
- **Leveling five familiars evenly** — a spread of level-12 companions loses to one level-25 in every activity. Commit to one per role
- **Ignoring the role switch** — bringing a Fighter to a crafting session wastes its entire kit. The companion menu takes three seconds to open
- **Forgetting element advantage in dungeons** — the 25% elemental bonus is the difference between clearing Nightveil Cave and wiping at the entrance
- **Not checking the companion's food cost** — some rare familiars eat 3x rations. If you cannot sustain the food bill, catch a cheaper one until your income grows

## Companion Management Checklist

- Switch to a Gatherer before resource-heavy farming sessions
- Switch to a Crafter before mass-crafting potions or gear
- Keep one healing-capable familiar for hard dungeons — even a fighter with a self-heal slot is a lifesaver
- If a familiar keeps dying in a zone, it is under-leveled for that zone — do not force it
- Element-match for every region change; the 25% bonus is free damage
