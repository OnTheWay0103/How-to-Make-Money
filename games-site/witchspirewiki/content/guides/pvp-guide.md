## PvP Overview

Witchspire features both structured and open-world PvP. Players can duel, participate in faction wars, or engage in arena battles for rewards and ranking.

## PvP Modes

### Arena (1v1 / 3v3)
- Access via the Arena NPC in any major city
- Ranked and unranked matches
- Rewards: Arena Points, exclusive gear, titles

### Open World PvP
- Toggle PvP flag in PvP-enabled zones
- Attack any flagged player
- Dying drops a portion of your carried resources

### Faction Wars (GvG)
- Weekly large-scale battles between Covens
- Capture objectives, hold territory
- Rewards distributed to all participating Coven members

## Best PvP Builds

### Burst Mage (S-Tier)
- **Weapon**: Wand (fast cast speed)
- **Luminaries**: Fire-based for maximum burst
- **Strategy**: Open with CC, unload full combo, retreat
- **Weakness**: Squishy — die if caught

### Tank Brawler (A-Tier)
- **Weapon**: Spellblade
- **Armor**: Heavy
- **Strategy**: Soak damage, close distance, melee pressure
- **Weakness**: Kited by ranged builds

### Support Controller (A-Tier)
- **Weapon**: Wand
- **Luminaries**: Ice/CC-focused
- **Strategy**: Lock down priority targets, enable teammates
- **Weakness**: Low solo kill potential

## PvP Tips

1. **Know your burst window** — Most kills happen in 3-5 second windows
2. **Manage cooldowns** — Never engage without escape options
3. **Position is everything** — High ground and cover matter
4. **Learn matchups** — Know which builds counter yours
5. **Communicate in group PvP** — Focus fire wins fights
