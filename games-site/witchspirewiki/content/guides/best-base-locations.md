# Witchspire Best Base Locations Guide — Where to Build Your Hearth

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 27, 2026**

Your Hearth is the center of your Witchspire operations. Choosing where to place it affects your farming efficiency, travel times, and safety from hostile creatures. This guide compares the best base locations across all regions and helps you choose based on your playstyle and progression stage.

---

## Base Location Criteria

Each location is evaluated on these factors:

| Factor | Weight | Why It Matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Resource Proximity** | High | Nearby wood, stone, and ore nodes reduce gathering time |
| **Travel Hub Access** | High | Close to Flight Pillars, docks, and fast travel points |
| **Enemy Density** | Medium | Fewer enemies = safer base, but fewer farming opportunities |
| **Building Space** | Medium | Flat terrain allows larger and more complex builds |
| **Water Access** | Low-Medium | Needed for Garden Plots and fishing |
| **Aesthetics** | Low | Personal preference — view and atmosphere |

---

## Region-by-Region Comparison

| Region | Overall Rating | Best For | Worst For |
|--------|---------------|----------|-----------|
| Vyrs Meadows | S | Beginners, balanced gameplay | Endgame resource diversity |
| Timberline Basin | A | Resource gathering, solo players | Group content access |
| Mira Isles | A | Mid-game progression, explorers | Early game (too dangerous) |
| Shuddersands | B | Endgame farming, challenging content | Beginners (too dangerous) |

## 1. Vyrs Meadows — Best for Beginners

Vyrs Meadows is the starting region and the safest location for your first Hearth. It offers balanced access to all early-game resources and is centrally located for travel to other regions.

### Best Building Spots in Vyrs

| Spot Name | Coordinates | Resource Proximity | Travel Access | Rating |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| **Hillside Overlook** | (X: 245, Y: 180) | Very High (wood, stone, berries, ore) | 2 min to Vyrs Town | S |
| **Riverside Meadow** | (X: 200, Y: 120) | High (water, fiber, clay) | 3 min to Vyrs Town, 1 min to river | A |
| **Eastern Ridge** | (X: 320, Y: 200) | Medium (stone, ore) | 5 min to Flight Pillar | B |

### Recommended: Hillside Overlook

- **Why**: Close to 3 wood spawns, 2 stone nodes, 1 copper ore vein, and a berry bush cluster
- **Nearby Flight Pillar**: Vyrs Central (2 min south)
- **Water access**: Stream at the bottom of the hill (30s walk)
- **Enemy threat**: Low — only passive Meadow Stags and occasional low-level Spriggans
- **Building space**: Large flat area at the top, room for a 10x10 foundation

### Vyrs Base Strategy

- Build your Hearth here for the early and mid game (levels 1-20)
- Upgrade to Hearth Tier 3 before considering a move
- Use the central location to fast-travel to other regions for resource runs

## 2. Timberline Basin — Best for Resource Gatherers

Timberline Basin is a forested mountain region rich in wood, ore, and rare herbs. It is the best region for players who prioritize crafting and gathering over combat.

### Best Building Spots in Timberline Basin

| Spot Name | Coordinates | Resource Proximity | Travel Access | Rating |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| **Pine Crest** | (X: 150, Y: 400) | Very High (wood, herbs, iron ore) | 3 min to Timberline Flight Pillar | S |
| **Lakeview Terrace** | (X: 180, Y: 350) | High (water, fish, clay) | 4 min to Eastern Docks | A |
| **Deepwood Clearing** | (X: 100, Y: 450) | Extreme (rare herbs, rich wood) | 8 min to nearest travel point | B |

### Recommended: Pine Crest

- **Why**: Surrounded by 5+ iron ore nodes and dense wood spawns. Short walk to the Timberline Flight Pillar.
- **Nearby Flight Pillar**: Timberline Central (3 min west)
- **Water access**: Small pond nearby (adequate for Garden Plots)
- **Enemy threat**: Medium — Timber Wolves and the occasional Bear spawn at night
- **Building space**: Moderate — terraced hillside, requires foundation pillars for large builds

### Timberline Basin Base Strategy

- Move your Hearth here at level 15+ when you need iron and rare herbs
- The region has no direct dock to Mira Isles — you must travel through Vyrs first
- Nighttime brings tougher enemies; build walls and place lanterns to reduce enemy spawns near your base

## 3. Mira Isles — Best for Mid-Game Players

Mira Isles offers tropical beauty, unique resources (coconuts, exotic wood, volcanic stone), and access to some of the best late-game farming spots.

### Best Building Spots in Mira Isles

| Spot Name | Coordinates | Resource Proximity | Travel Access | Rating |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| **Welcome Beach North** | (X: 400, Y: 300) | Medium (coconuts, sand, shells) | 1 min to Welcome Beach dock | S |
| **Volcano Ridge** | (X: 500, Y: 350) | Very High (volcanic stone, obsidian) | 5 min to Volcano Flight Pillar | A |
| **Ruins Courtyard** | (X: 450, Y: 250) | High (ancient relics, rare ore) | 4 min to Ruins Isle Flight Pillar | A |

### Recommended: Welcome Beach North

- **Why**: Immediate access to the Mira Isles dock for fast travel back to Vyrs. Plenty of flat sand for building.
- **Nearby Flight Pillar**: Mira Isles Shore (2 min east)
- **Water access**: Oceanfront — unlimited water for Garden Plots
- **Enemy threat**: Medium — Shore Sentinels patrol the beach but can be avoided
- **Building space**: Very large — entire northern beach is flat and open

### Mira Isles Base Strategy

- Move here at level 16+ when you unlock the region
- A second "satellite base" is recommended — keep your main Hearth in Vyrs and build a smaller outpost here
- The region has unique weather patterns (tropical storms) that can damage exposed structures — build roof coverage

## 4. Shuddersands — Best for Endgame Players

Shuddersands is the highest-level region and the most dangerous. Only experienced players with strong gear should attempt to build here.

| Spot Name | Coordinates | Resource Proximity | Travel Access | Rating |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| **Valas Oasis** | (X: 600, Y: 500) | High (water, cactus fiber, ancient relics) | 1 min to Valas NPC hub | S |
| **Dune Watch** | (X: 650, Y: 450) | Medium (desert glass, rare ore) | 3 min to Shuddersands Flight Pillar | B |

**Note**: Building in Shuddersands is not recommended until you have Hearth Tier 4+ and gear level 30+. The region has extreme heat that damages structures not built with heat-resistant materials.

## How to Move Your Hearth

If you want to relocate your Hearth to a new region:

1. Build a **Hearth Relocation Kit** at Workbench Tier 3 (cost: 20x Refined Wood, 10x Iron Ingot, 5x Moonstone, 2,000 gold)
2. Deconstruct your existing Hearth using the Kit (all attached structures are stored as materials)
3. Travel to the new location and place your Hearth
4. Structures are placed automatically from the Kit's stored materials — you do not lose anything

**Important**: You cannot move a Hearth while it is upgraded past Tier 2. Downgrade it first at a cost of 50% of upgrade materials.

## Common Base Location Mistakes

- **Building too close to enemy spawns** — check for nearby enemy camps before placing foundations
- **Ignoring Flight Pillar distance** — a 5+ minute walk to the nearest pillar adds up over hundreds of trips
- **Building in a flood zone** — low-lying areas in Vyrs and Mira Isles can flood during storms; build on elevated terrain
- **Starting in Shuddersands** — the heat damage and aggressive enemies make it unsuitable for early-game bases
