# Farming Guide — Coins, XP, and Materials

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Whether you need gold for vendor recipes, XP for Luminary points, or rare materials for endgame gear, this guide covers the most efficient farming methods in Witchspire. Each route is optimized for time-to-reward ratio, with familiar recommendations to maximize yield.

> **Note:** rates, prices, and yields below come from community reports and have not been independently verified. Treat numbers as approximate.

---

## Quick Coin Farming

### Best Overall (Reported): Shuddersands Relic Run

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Lost Oasis (Shuddersands) |
| Coins per Hour | ~3,000-4,000 (reported) |
| Requirements | High-level gear, Steel tools, Heat Resist potions |
| Time per Run | 15-20 min |

**Route:**
1. Start at Valas Oasis (stock up on Heat Resist potions, repair gear)
2. Travel east to Lost Oasis
3. Loot all buildings for **Ancient Relics** (community reports describe 5-7 per run)
4. Kill Dust Golems for Ancient Relic rare drops and Stone Blocks
5. Check the buried chest (requires a digging tool, reported) for extra gold
6. Return to Valas Oasis and sell to the oasis vendor

**Total per run** (community-reported averages): ~7 Ancient Relics + enemy drops + chest gold ≈ 2,000 gold per 15 min.

### Best Early Game: Vyrs Nectar Loop

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Vyrs Fields (starting region) |
| Coins per Hour | ~800-1,000 (reported) |
| Requirements | None |
| Time per Run | 5-7 min |

**Route:**
1. Start at Hearth
2. Run through Vyrs Fields collecting **Nectar** from yellow flowers (20-25 per run)
3. Sell Nectar to Mira the Vendor at Vyrs Crossroads
4. Also collect raw Wood and Stone — sell excess to the traveling merchant

**Why it works**: Nectar reportedly respawns quickly and vendors buy it at a fair price. Perfect for brand new characters.

### Best Mid-Game: Timberline Crystal Shard Run

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Shard Caverns (Timberline Basin) |
| Coins per Hour | ~2,000-2,500 (reported) |
| Requirements | Iron+ tools |
| Time per Run | 10-12 min |

**Route:**
1. Enter Shard Caverns (east Timberline Basin, behind a waterfall — reported)
2. Head directly to **Crystal Vein Junction** (follow the crystal glow)
3. Mine all Crystal Shard nodes (20-30 per run)
4. Kill Crystal Crawlers for Chitin (reported to sell well)
5. Check the hidden chests inside the caverns
6. Exit and sell at Merchant Reyes (Basin Crossroads)

**Crystal Shard vendor price**: community reports describe it as one of the better material prices — exact value unverified.

## XP / Luminary Point Farming

### Best XP Farm (Reported): Shuddersands Nightveil Cave

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Nightveil Cave (Shuddersands, west edge) |
| XP per Hour | ~8,000-10,000 (reported) |
| Requirements | High-level gear, Steel gear, access at night only (reported) |
| Time per Clear | 20-25 min |

**Strategy:**
1. Arrive at the cave entrance at night (community reports describe a night-only entrance window)
2. Clear all enemies (reported: shadow-type crawlers and witches give good XP)
3. Leave and rest at Valas Oasis inn — repeat after the night cycle

**Pro tip**: Community reports describe XP-boosting food; exact recipes and values are not independently verified — see the [Food & Cooking Guide](/guides/food-cooking).

### Best Early XP: Vyrs Boar Hunting

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Vyrs Meadow (north of Hearth) |
| XP per Hour | ~2,000-3,000 (reported) |
| Requirements | None |
| Time per Run | Continuous |

**Method:**
1. Kill Thornback Boars in Vyrs Meadow (3-5 per spawn cycle, reported)
2. Gather Raw Meat and Leather — craft into food or sell
3. Boars reportedly respawn quickly — stay in the area and loop

### Best Mid XP: Timberline Witch Circuit

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Location | Al'uunian Tower (Timberline Basin) |
| XP per Hour | ~5,000-6,000 (reported) |
| Requirements | Iron+ gear |
| Time per Run | 15-20 min |

**Route:**
1. Enter Al'uunian Tower from the ground floor
2. Clear Spectral Witches on the ground floor (reported: good XP each)
3. Climb to upper floors — more witches + Crystal Golems (reported)
4. Exit and repeat

## Material Farming Routes

### By Region

| Region | Best Materials | Best Location | Route Duration |
|--------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Vyrs** | Wood, Stone, Iron Ore, Fiber, Nectar | Vyrs Meadow → South Cave → Fields loop | 15 min |
| **Timberline** | Crystal Shards, Coal, Moonleaf, Ethereal Dust | Shard Caverns + Al'uunian Tower loop | 20 min |
| **Mira Isles** | Tropical Fruit, Crystal Shards, Moonleaf, Ancient Wood | Soley Cave + Volcano Isle loop | 25 min |
| **Shuddersands** | Shimmer Sand (reported), Astral Essence, Ancient Relics, Ethereal Dust | Glimmering Dunes + Lost Oasis loop | 30 min |

### Best Crystal Shard Farm
- **Location**: Shard Caverns (Timberline Basin) — Crystal Vein Junction
- **Yield**: 20-30 per run (reported)
- **Best Familiar**: **Shardling** (Crystal) — found in Shard Caverns; community reports describe a resource boost while it is bonded

### Best Ethereal Dust Farm
- **Location**: Nightveil Cave (Shuddersands) or Al'uunian Tower (Timberline)
- **Yield**: 4-6 per cave clear (reported)
- **Best Familiar**: a Nox-element familiar (Xilvant or Fien) — community reports describe improved drop rates, unverified

### Best Coal Farm
- **Location**: Shard Caverns (Timberline Basin) — dark stone deposits
- **Yield**: 8-12 per run (reported)
- **Alternative**: Buy from Merchant Reyes (Basin Crossroads) — community-reported price

## Farming Tips

- **Use food buffs**: community reports describe damage and XP food buffs — recipes are in the [Food & Cooking Guide](/guides/food-cooking)
- **Upgrade gathering tools first**: An Iron Pickaxe mines faster than a Stone Pickaxe. Upgrade priority: Pickaxe > Axe > Weapon
- **Empty your inventory before a farming run**: Keep only potions
- **Set a Hearth flag at your farming location**: Quick travel to a vendor when full — see the [Flight & Fast Travel Guide](/guides/flight-unlock-guide)
- **Farm at night in Shuddersands**: community reports describe slower heat build-up at night = less downtime
- **Salvage gear from enemies**: Don't leave it on the ground — salvage at the Workbench for partial materials (reported)
- **Co-op split farming**: In co-op, have each player farm a different resource type, then share
