# Witchspire Beginner Guide — First 5 Hours & Survival Tips

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Starting fresh in Witchspire can feel overwhelming. Six Coven options, a sprawling open world, and no hand-holding. This guide covers exactly what to do in your first session so you waste no time — and the survival basics that keep you alive while you do it.

---

## Your First 5 Hours: Priority Checklist

| Time Mark | Objective | Why It Matters |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| 0-30 min | Complete tutorial, pick Coven, explore Hearth | Establishes your core loop |
| 30-90 min | Gather basic resources (wood, stone, fiber) | Unlocks first crafting stations |
| 90 min-2 hr | Build Workbench + Garden Plot | Passive food + gear crafting |
| 2-3 hr | Catch your first Familiar in Vyrs | Combat + gathering boost |
| 3-4 hr | Upgrade Hearth to Tier 2 | Unlocks Furnace + Broom crafting |
| 4-5 hr | Farm Al'uunian Golem materials | First boss kill + region unlock |

---

## Starting Resource Priority

Gather in this order the moment you spawn in Vyrs:

| Resource | Use | Where to Find |
|----------|-----|---------------|
| Wood | Workbench, walls, tools | Trees near Hearth (any forest patch) |
| Stone | Furnace, walls, upgrade parts | Rocky outcrops in Vyrs fields |
| Fiber | Rope, basic armor, garden plots | Tall grass clusters |
| Iron Ore | Tools, weapons (unlocks after Furnace) | Vyrs caves (south-east area) |
| Nectar | Food, healing potions | Yellow flowers, fields |

**Pro tip**: Hold E to auto-collect. Do not manually click each node.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Picking the Wrong Coven for Your Playstyle
Cloudpiercers and Wildroots are the most forgiving for new players. Avoid Nightscribes and Foretellers until you understand combat mechanics — their glass-cannon builds require precise positioning.

See [Best Starting Coven Guide](/guides/best-coven-guide) for detailed breakdowns.

### 2. Spending Luminary Points Recklessly
**You cannot reset your Luminary skill tree.** Do not spend points until you have read the nodes ahead. New players should rush the "+1 Familiar Slot" node — it doubles your early power.

See [Luminary Skill Tree Guide](/guides/luminary-skill-tree-guide) for recommended builds.

### 3. Ignoring the Hearth Upgrade
The Hearth is your main progression gate. Each upgrade tier unlocks critical stations (Workbench at T2, Furnace at T3, Witchcraft Circle at T4). If you are stuck, you probably need to upgrade your Hearth.

See [Hearth Upgrade Guide](/guides/hearth-upgrade-guide) for material requirements.

### 4. Not Using Your Familiar in Combat
Your familiar is not just a pet. It deals damage, gathers materials, and can tank boss aggro. Bind your familiar's active ability to a hotkey and use it every fight.

### 5. Wandering Into High-Level Zones
Enemies get tougher the further you get from Vyrs — the Timberline Basin and Shuddersands are significantly more dangerous. Stay in Vyrs until you have at least Tier 2 Hearth and iron tools.

## Fastest Progression Path

### Step 1: Choose Cloudpiercers or Wildroots
- **Cloudpiercers** (Spellblade + Lux): Best weapon range, good damage, easiest to learn.
- **Wildroots** (Wand + Nature): Healing ability keeps you alive, pets tank for you.

### Step 2: Rush Hearth Tier 2 → Workbench
1. Gather wood and stone
2. Upgrade your Hearth and build a Workbench
3. Craft Stone Axe + Stone Pickaxe
4. Keep gathering — the next Hearth tier unlocks the Furnace

### Step 3: Catch a Familiar
Head out in the Vyrs starting area and catch a **Rockling** (Nature, Common) or **Taileaf** (Nature, Common) — both are excellent early-game familiars. Remember the real mechanic: kill the creature, then HOLD F on the spirit if it lingers. No capture orbs exist. See the [Familiars Guide](/guides/all-familiars-guide) for the full taming breakdown.

### Step 4: Build a Broom
After Hearth Tier 2, craft a Broom at the Workbench. Flight massively speeds up exploration.

See [Flight & Fast Travel Guide](/guides/flight-unlock-guide) for broom recipes.

### Step 5: Fight Al'uunian Golem
The first major boss is in the eastern Vyrs ruins. Bring healing potions, a bonded familiar, and watch for the ground slam AoE. See the [Al'uunian Golem Boss Guide](/guides/alunean-golem-boss-guide) for the full strategy.

## Survival Basics

> **Note:** Witchspire's exact survival values (decay rates, thresholds, heal amounts) have not been published by the developer. The framework below comes from community reports; treat specific numbers as approximate.

### Core Survival Stats

| Stat | What It Does | Recovery Method |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Health | Player HP, reaching 0 = death | Healing potions, food, bed rest |
| Hunger | Affects stamina regeneration | Cooked food |
| Thirst | Affects magic regeneration | Water, nectar-based drinks |
| Body Temperature | Affects damage taken in hot/cold zones | Clothing, fire, shade |

**Darkness and night** are also factors in Witchspire — night is more dangerous than day in most regions, and night-only creatures and materials exist (see the [Events Guide](/guides/events-guide)). Some community guides describe a separate sanity system; this has not been independently verified, so we do not list it as confirmed.

### Hunger Management

- **Starvation signs (community-reported):** stamina regen slows when hunger is low; below roughly 20%, health regeneration stops; very low hunger drains health. Exact thresholds are unverified.
- **Best early food sources:** berries, eggs, cooked meat, bread, and Nectar (which covers both hunger and thirst). See the [Food & Cooking Guide](/guides/food-cooking) for recipes and buffs.
- **Always carry 2-3 emergency food items.**

### Thirst Management

- **Water sources:** rivers, rain, and Nectar. Water from unknown sources is safer when boiled at a campfire first.
- **Warning signs (community-reported):** magic regeneration slows when thirst is low; very low thirst stops health regen and eventually drains health.
- **Tips:** build a **Rain Catcher** at your base, carry a **Waterskin**, and treat Nectar as your best early thirst item — it counts for both hunger and thirst.

### Temperature Management

| Zone | Temperature | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| Vyrs (starting area) | Mild | Low |
| Timberline Basin | Cold | Frost risk at night |
| Mira Isles | Warm | Low |
| Shuddersands | Very Hot | Heat risk during day |

- **Cold survival:** campfires warm nearby areas; warmer clothing and hot meals help. The [Weather Guide](/guides/weather-guide) covers the cold weather effects in detail.
- **Heat survival:** light clothing and shade help in Shuddersands; drink more, and carry water.
- **Frostbound Peaks / Ash Fields:** community reports occasionally mention extreme cold and heat zones beyond the four main regions. These have not been independently verified.

### Health and Healing

| Item | Reported Heal | Best Use Case |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Bandage | ~50 HP | Quick combat heal |
| Healing Salve | ~100 HP | General use |
| Minor Healing Potion | ~100 HP | Emergency |
| Major Healing Potion | ~300 HP | Boss fights |
| Familiar Heal (active) | Portion of max HP | Passive sustain |

**Rule of thumb:** keep Minor potions for exploration and save Major potions for boss fights. Full recipes and crafting sources are in the [Potions Guide](/guides/potions-guide).

### Death and Recovery

| Item | Lost on Death? | Recoverable? |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Equipped gear | Yes | Yes (death marker) |
| Inventory items | Yes | Yes (death marker) |
| Hearth upgrades | No | — |
| Learned recipes | No | — |
| Familiars (unless dead) | No | — |

1. **Respawn at your Hearth** — base upgrades and recipes are kept
2. **Return to your death marker** — your items are in a gravestone with a timer (community reports suggest roughly 30 minutes; not verified)
3. **If the timer expires** — items are permanently lost
4. **Ask a friend for help** — in co-op, teammates can guard your run back

### Daily Routine Checklist

| Time of Day | Activity |
|-------------|----------|
| Morning (dawn) | Water plants, harvest gardens, cook food |
| Midday | Explore new areas, gather resources |
| Afternoon | Craft, upgrade base, fight bosses |
| Evening | Return to base, cook dinner, stock supplies |
| Night | Stay near base, organize storage, sleep |

## Quick Tips

- **Food buffs stack**. Eat before boss fights for stat increases.
- **Night = danger**. Enemies get tougher after dark. Build a bed to skip to morning.
- **Set a return point** at your Hearth before exploring new areas — see the [Flight & Fast Travel Guide](/guides/flight-unlock-guide) for all travel options.
- **Salvage old gear** at the Workbench instead of discarding it — you get partial materials back.
- **Watch the weather** before long trips — storms disrupt flying (see the [Weather Guide](/guides/weather-guide)).
