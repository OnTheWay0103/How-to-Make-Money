# Witchspire Flight & Fast Travel Guide — Brooms, Mounts & Flight Pillars

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Flight opens up Witchspire's world. Without a broom, you are stuck on the ground dodging enemies between every resource node. This guide covers the full travel toolbox: how to unlock the Broom, every Flight Pillar location, Hearth flags, docks, the Teleport Network — and what to make of the community's mount reports.

---

## Travel Method Overview

| Method | Type | Range | Unlock | Speed |
|--------|------|-------|--------|-------|
| Walking/running | On foot | Anywhere | Default | Slow |
| Broom | Flight | Within a region | Hearth Tier 2 + Broom recipe | Fastest overland |
| Flight Pillars | Teleport | Between regions | One-time activation per pillar | Instant |
| Hearth Flag | Return teleport | Back to your Hearth/base | Hearth upgrade | Instant (one-way) |
| Docks/boats | Sea travel | Between coastal regions | Mira Isles dock, etc. | Medium |

**Rule of thumb:** between regions, use the pillars; within a region, use the broom; when a storm is active, ground it — a crashed flight is slower than a walk.

---

## Step 1: Upgrade Hearth to Tier 2

Flight requires a Broom. Brooms are crafted at the **Workbench**, which unlocks at Hearth Tier 2.

**Hearth Tier 2 requirements (community-consistent):**
- 30 Wood
- 25 Stone
- 10 Fiber

Build the Hearth upgrades from the Hearth menu (interact with the central crystal). Once Tier 2 is active, you unlock the Furnace and Workbench recipes, including Brooms.

See [Hearth Upgrade Guide](/guides/hearth-upgrade-guide) for full upgrade trees.

## Step 2: Gather Broom Materials

### Basic Broom Recipe

| Material | Amount | Source |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Refined Wood | 8 | Furnace: 2 Wood → 1 Refined Wood |
| Iron Ingot | 4 | Furnace: 3 Iron Ore + 1 Coal → 1 Ingot |
| Sturdy Fiber | 6 | Furnace: 3 Fiber → 1 Sturdy Fiber |
| Nectar | 3 | Yellow flowers in Vyrs fields |

### Where to Find Iron Ore

Iron ore appears in several places in Vyrs:

| Location | Yield | Danger Level |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| Vyrs South Cave | 3-5 per node | Low |
| Vyrs East Ridge | 2-3 per node | Medium |
| Timberline Basin | 4-6 per node | High |

Stick to Vyrs South Cave for your first batch.

---

## Step 3: Craft Your Broom

1. Open your Workbench
2. Navigate to the "Tools" tab
3. Find "Basic Broom" in the list
4. Craft (takes ~30 seconds)

**Congratulations — you can now fly.**

## Flight Controls

| Action | PC Control | Controller |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Mount / Dismount | F | Y |
| Ascend | Space | A |
| Descend | Left Shift | Left Bumper |
| Forward | W / Forward on stick | Left stick forward |
| Boost (sprint) | Left Ctrl | Click left stick |
| Brake | S | Back on left stick |

> **Note:** control bindings come from community reports and may differ across builds — check the in-game settings screen for the definitive layout.

Your broom has a stamina bar. When it runs out, you slowly descend. Land on any solid ground to regenerate stamina. Storms disrupt flying — during severe weather, dismount and walk or use pillars instead.

## Flight Pillars — The Backbone of Fast Travel

**Flight Pillars are the primary way to teleport between regions.** Unlock each pillar once by interacting with it; after that, any unlocked pillar teleports you to any other unlocked pillar. Activating a pillar also refills your broom's stamina.

- **How it works:** activate a pillar, open the network, select a destination region.
- **What it costs:** nothing — one-time activation is the only requirement.
- **Priority order:** unlock the pillar in every region you visit as soon as you arrive — a 10-second interaction saves a 10-minute trek every later visit.

## Other Travel Methods

### Hearth Flags — The One-Way Return

A Hearth flag is the "get home free" button:

- **How it works:** set a flag at a location, and later return to your **Hearth Hub** instantly from anywhere (one-way — you cannot teleport *to* the flag).
- **Best use:** farming loops. Set the flag at your farming location, fill your bag, teleport home to vendor, teleport back out via Flight Pillar. The [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide) recommends exactly this pattern.

### Docks and Boats — Coastal Routes

- **Mira Isles dock:** confirmed sea travel between the Isles and the mainland — and a fast return to Vyrs.
- Bases near the dock combine fast travel access with the pillars.

### The Teleport Network (Hearth Tier 5)

At **Hearth Tier 5** (80 Steel Ingots, 10 Crystal Shards, 5 Ethereal Dust), the Hearth unlocks the **Teleport Network** — a hub that links Hearth-linked points directly. This is the endgame travel upgrade: instant travel between your own bases and facilities.

- **Cost to build:** significant — tier 5 is a late-game investment.
- **When it pays off:** once you maintain multiple bases or remote facilities, the network pays for itself in travel time within days.

## Mounts — What Is Real?

The **Broom is the only mount type we can confirm** in the current build. Older community guides describe:

- **Land mounts** (community names: Meadow Stag in Vyrs, Stoneback Tortoise in Shuddersands, Mira Runner in Mira Isles), reportedly tamed by baiting and trapping, with the ability to carry more weight than the broom
- **Familiar mounts** (community names: Storm Hawk, Ember Wolf, Crystal Moth), reportedly unlocked through the Luminary tree or rare spawns

These mechanics — taming traps, taming minigames, "Familiar Orbs" — have **not been independently verified** and appear to describe planned or removed features. Treat all mount reports as unconfirmed until the developer announces them. If you see a guide describing orb-based familiar catching, that guide is wrong about the current build (familiars are bonded by killing and holding F — see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/all-familiars-guide)).

## Region-by-Region Flying Tips

### Vyrs
Open fields and low obstacles. Use boost generously. Flight Pillars are close together.

### Timberline Basin
Dense trees and elevation changes. Fly above the canopy line to avoid obstacles. The Basin Summit pillar is essential for navigating this zone.

### Mira Isles
Water landings are safe — your broom hovers above water. Use flight to reach underwater cave entrances from above.

### Shuddersands
Sandstorms reduce visibility while flying. Stay low and follow the Flight Pillar paths. Boost through storm patches to minimize stamina drain.
