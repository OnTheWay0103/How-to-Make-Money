# Base Defense Guide — Protect Your Hearth

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 29, 2026**

Your Hearth is your lifeline in Witchspire. If it is destroyed, you lose your upgrades and respawn point. This guide covers everything you need to build a base that can survive any raid.

---

## Raid Mechanics

### How Raids Work

| Mechanic | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Raid trigger | Building too close to enemy camps, killing faction leaders, high player level |
| Warning time | 2 minutes before raid arrives |
| Raid duration | 5-10 minutes depending on size |
| Enemy types | Based on your location (see Enemy Factions guide) |
| Raid frequency | 1 raid per 3 in-game days minimum, more if provoked |

### Raid Waves

| Wave | Enemies | Focus | Difficulty |
|------|---------|-------|------------|
| Scout (always first) | 2-3 weak enemies | Test defenses, alert faction | Easy |
| Assault (main wave) | 6-12 enemies | Rush the Hearth | Medium |
| Siege (occasional) | 3-5 heavy enemies + battering ram | Break walls | Hard |
| Boss Raid (rare) | 1 boss + 8-10 followers | Destroy everything | Very Hard |

## Wall Types and Defenses

### Wall Tiers

| Wall Type | HP | Materials | Hearth Tier Required | Best For |
|-----------|-----|-----------|---------------------|----------|
| Wooden Fence | 200 | 10 Wood | Tier 1 | Immediate perimeter |
| Stone Wall | 800 | 20 Stone | Tier 2 | Main perimeter |
| Reinforced Stone | 2,000 | 30 Stone, 10 Iron | Tier 3 | Outer walls |
| Iron-Reinforced | 5,000 | 20 Iron, 10 Coal | Tier 4 | Heavy defense |
| Enchanted Barrier | 10,000 | 5 Ethereal Dust, 30 Iron | Tier 5 | Endgame |

### Wall Placement Strategy

| Ring | Position | Wall Type | Notes |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Outer | Perimeter of claimed area | Reinforced Stone | First line of defense |
| Middle | Around main buildings | Stone Wall | Slows enemies if outer falls |
| Inner | Directly around Hearth | Iron-Reinforced | Last stand |

### Wall Design Principles

- **Do not build a single wall** — layers are essential
- **Leave gaps with traps** — funnel enemies through kill zones
- **Raise walls on foundations** — prevents dig attacks from burrowing enemies
- **Corner towers** — place towers at wall corners for overlapping fire

| Trap | Effect | Cost | Best Use |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| Slow Field | Enemies move 60% slower | 10 Stone, 3 Nectar | In front of damage traps |

### Trap Kill Zone Design

```
[Wall]  [Wall]  [Wall]  [Wall]
   |       |       |       |
[Gate]--[Slow]--[Fire]--[Spikes]--[Gate]
   |       |       |       |
[Wall]  [Wall]  [Wall]  [Wall]
```

Enemies enter through the first gate, get slowed, take fire damage, walk into spikes, and exit through the second gate (manned by you).

## Defense Towers

### Tower Types

| Tower | Range | Damage | Cost | Unlock |
|-------|-------|--------|------|--------|
| Arrow Tower | Medium | 20 per shot | 20 Wood, 10 Stone | Hearth Tier 2 |
| Ballista | Long | 100 per shot | 30 Wood, 15 Iron | Hearth Tier 3 |
| Flame Tower | Short | 50 + burn | 20 Stone, 10 Nectar | Hearth Tier 3 |
| Arcane Tower | Medium | 80 magic | 15 Iron, 10 Crystal | Hearth Tier 4 |
| Sentry Golem | Short | 60 + block | 30 Iron, 5 Ethereal Dust | Hearth Tier 5 |

### Tower Placement

| Position | Tower Type | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Wall corners | Arrow Tower | Cover approaches |
| Near gate | Ballista | Destroy battering rams |
| Behind walls | Flame Tower | Area denial |
| Hearth adjacent | Arcane Tower | Last line |
| Inside base | Sentry Golem | Anti-flanker |

### Tower AI Notes

- Towers prioritize nearest enemy
- Ballistas target the highest-HP enemy (battering rams first)
- Flame Towers do friendly fire — place away from your position
- Arcane Towers have an ammo mechanic (recharge with Crystal Shards)

## Active Defense Tips

### During a Raid

1. **Stay calm** — check raid composition (enemy types)
2. **Repair mid-raid** — if you have materials, repair walls between waves
3. **Kill battering rams first** — they destroy walls in 3-4 hits
4. **Use magic** — AoE spells are excellent against clustered enemies
5. **Kite boss enemies** — away from walls and buildings
6. **Prioritize healers** — enemy support units keep others alive

### Post-Raid Recovery

| Task | Priority | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| Repair walls | High | Next raid could come soon |
| Restock traps | High | Empty traps = wasted space |
| Restore Hearth HP | Medium | Lose if Hearth is destroyed |
| Craft healing items | Medium | You likely used some |
| Loot enemy remains | Low | Collect rare materials from raid enemies |

## Common Defense Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Building one wall only | Single breach = entire base exposed | Layer your walls |
| No towers | You must defend everything manually | Build at least 2 towers |
| Gates as only entrance | Enemies path directly to gate | Funnel through traps |
| Building too big | Harder to defend | Build compact and expand later |
| Ignoring air threats | Flying enemies bypass walls | Arcane Towers target air |
