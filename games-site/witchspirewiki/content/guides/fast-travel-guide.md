# Witchspire Fast Travel Guide — Flight Pillars, Hearth Flags & Getting Around

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: August 6, 2026**

Witchspire's regions are large, and its hazards are tuned to punish slow travel — rain cuts visibility, storms disrupt flying, and heat waves drain stamina. Knowing every fast travel option — and which one fits the situation — is a core efficiency skill. This guide covers all confirmed travel methods, how to unlock them, and the fastest routes between regions.

---

## Travel Method Overview

| Method | Type | Range | Unlock | Speed |
|--------|------|-------|--------|-------|
| Walking/running | On foot | Anywhere | Default | Slow |
| Mounts | Ground mount | Within a region | Early (tame/mount unlock) | Fast on ground |
| Broom | Flight | Within a region | Hearth Tier 2 + Broom recipe | Fastest overland |
| Flight Pillars | Teleport | Between regions | One-time activation per pillar | Instant |
| Hearth Flag | Return teleport | Back to your Hearth/base | Hearth upgrade | Instant (one-way) |
| Docks/boats | Sea travel | Between coastal regions | Mira Isles dock, etc. | Medium |
| Teleport Network | Teleport hub | Between Hearth-linked points | Hearth Tier 5 | Instant |

---

## 1. Flight Pillars — The Backbone of Fast Travel

**Flight Pillars are the primary way to teleport between regions.** Unlock each pillar once by interacting with it; after that, any unlocked pillar teleports you to any other unlocked pillar.

- **How it works:** activate a pillar, open the network, select a destination region.
- **What it costs:** nothing — one-time activation is the only requirement.
- **Complements the broom:** the pillars teleport *between* regions; the broom handles travel *within* a region. Use both in sequence for the fastest possible routing.

**Priority order for unlocking:** unlock the pillar in every region you visit as soon as you arrive — a 10-second interaction saves a 10-minute trek every later visit.

## 2. The Broom — Within-Region Speed

The Broom is crafted at the Workbench (unlocked at Hearth Tier 1, materials require Tier 2):

| Material | Amount | Source |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Refined Wood | 8 | Furnace: 2 Wood → 1 Refined Wood |
| Iron Ingot | 4 | Furnace: 3 Iron Ore + 1 Coal |
| Sturdy Fiber | 6 | Furnace: 3 Fiber → 1 Sturdy Fiber |
| Nectar | 3 | Yellow flowers in Vyrs fields |

- **Iron ore:** Vyrs South Cave (3-5 per node, low danger) is the best first source.
- **Caveat:** storms disrupt flying — during severe weather, dismount and walk or use pillars instead.
- **Full guide:** see the [Flight Unlock Guide](/guides/flight-unlock-guide) for controls and all pillar locations.

## 3. Hearth Flags — The One-Way Return

A Hearth flag is the "get home free" button:

- **How it works:** set a flag at a location, and later return to your **Hearth Hub** instantly from anywhere (one-way — you cannot teleport *to* the flag).
- **Best use:** farming loops. Set the flag at your farming location, fill your bag, teleport home to vendor, teleport back out via Flight Pillar. The [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide) recommends exactly this pattern: *"Set a Hearth flag at your farming location: quick teleport to vendor when full."*

## 4. Docks and Boats — Coastal Routes

- **Mira Isles dock:** confirmed sea travel between the Isles and the mainland — and a fast return to Vyrs.
- **Best base locations note:** bases near the dock combine fast travel access with the pillars — see the [Best Base Locations Guide](/guides/best-base-locations) for dock-adjacent build spots.

## 5. The Teleport Network (Hearth Tier 5)

At **Hearth Tier 5** (80 Steel Ingots, 10 Crystal Shards, 5 Ethereal Dust), the Hearth unlocks the **Teleport Network** — a hub that links Hearth-linked points directly. This is the endgame travel upgrade: instant travel between your own bases and facilities.

- **Cost to build:** significant — tier 5 is a late-game investment.
- **When it pays off:** once you maintain multiple bases or remote facilities, the network pays for itself in travel time within days.

## Fastest Routes Between Regions

| Route | Fastest Path |
|-------|--------------|
| Vyrs → Timberline Basin | Activate the bridge (Ancient Seal quest) **or** Flight Pillar if unlocked |
| Timberline → Mira Isles | Dock at Timberline harbor → boat |
| Any region → Home | Hearth flag (one-way) or Flight Pillars |
| Within a region | Broom; mounts on the ground during storms |

**Route rule:** between regions, always use pillars; within a region, use the broom; when a storm is active, ground it — a crashed flight is slower than a walk.
