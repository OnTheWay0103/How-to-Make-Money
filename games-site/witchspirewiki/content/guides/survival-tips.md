# Survival Tips — Stay Alive in the Wilds

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 29, 2026**

Survival in Witchspire is about more than just fighting. You need to manage hunger, thirst, temperature, and health while exploring a hostile world. These tips will keep you alive longer and save you from common deaths.

---

## Core Survival Stats

| Stat | What It Does | Danger Threshold | Recovery Method |
|------|-------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Health | Player HP, reaching 0 = death | Any | Healing potions, food, bed rest |
| Hunger | Affects stamina regen | Below 30% = slow regen | Cooked food |
| Thirst | Affects magic regen | Below 30% = no regen | Water, nectar-based drinks |
| Body Temperature | Affects damage taken | Extreme hot/cold = damage | Clothing, fire, shade |
| Sanity | Affects enemy spawn rate | Below 50% = more spawns | Light sources, rest |

## Hunger Management

### Hunger Decay Rates

| Activity | Hunger Decay per Minute |
|----------|------------------------|
| Idle/sitting | 1% |
| Walking | 2% |
| Running | 4% |
| Combat | 6% |
| Swimming | 5% |
| Mining/Gathering | 3% |

### Best Early Food Sources

| Food | Hunger Restored | How to Get | Spoils? |
|------|----------------|------------|---------|
| Berries (raw) | 8% | Forage in Vyrs | Yes (2 days) |
| Boiled Eggs | 15% | Bird nests + campfire | Yes (3 days) |
| Roasted Boar Meat | 25% | Boar hunt + campfire | Yes (4 days) |
| Bread | 20% | Wheat fields + oven | Yes (5 days) |
| Preserved Meat | 30% | Salt + roasted meat | No (preserved) |
| Nectar | 10% + thirst | Yellow flowers | No |

### Starvation Warning Signs

- **Below 40%**: Stamina regen slowed by 50%
- **Below 20%**: Health regen stops. No stamina regen.
- **Below 10%**: Health starts draining (1 HP per 5 seconds)

**Always carry 2-3 emergency food items.**

## Thirst Management

### Water Sources

| Source | Safety | Purification Needed? |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| Rainwater | Safe | No (collect with buckets) |
| River water (Vyrs) | Moderate | Boil or use purification tablets |
| River water (Timberline) | Safe | No |
| Swamp water | Unsafe | Must boil (causes poison otherwise) |
| Nectar | Safe | No (gives thirst + hunger) |
| Potions | Safe | No (but wasteful) |

### Thirst Warning Signs

- **Below 40%**: Magic regen stops
- **Below 20%**: Health regen stops, screen blurs
- **Below 10%**: Health drains (2 HP per 3 seconds)

### Water Tips

- Build a **Rain Catcher** at your base (unlocked at Hearth Tier 2)
- Carry a **Waterskin** (craft at Workbench: 4 Leather, 2 Fiber)
- **Boil water** at a campfire before drinking from unknown sources
- Nectar counts for both hunger and thirst — best early-game item

## Sanity System

Sanity decreases when you:
- Spend time in dark areas
- Kill certain creatures
- Witness traumatic events (boss kills, player deaths)
- Stay alone for too long

### Effects of Low Sanity

| Sanity Level | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| 100-70% | Normal |
| 70-50% | Occasional auditory hallucinations, slightly more enemy spawns |
| 50-30% | Visual distortions, 50% more enemy spawns |
| 30-10% | Aggressive enemy spawns, phantom enemies appear |
| Below 10% | Enemies gain damage buff against you, permanent night visual |

### Restoring Sanity

| Method | Sanity Restored | Cooldown |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| Sleep in bed | +30% | Per night |
| Sit near fire | +5% per minute | None |
| Craft light source | +10% | Per craft |
| Pet familiar | +5% | 10 min |
| Cook complex meal | +15% | Per meal |

## Death and Recovery

### What You Lose on Death

| Item | Lost? | Recoverable? |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Equipped gear | Yes | Yes (death marker) |
| Inventory items | Yes | Yes (death marker) |
| Hearth upgrades | No | — |
| Learned recipes | No | — |
| Completed quests | No | — |
| Familiars (unless dead) | No | — |

### Recovering from Death

1. **Respawn at Hearth** — keep your base upgrades
2. **Return to death marker** — your items are in a gravestone (30-minute timer)
3. **If timer expires** — items are permanently lost
4. **Use Death Ward** (Hearth Tier 4) — extends gravestone timer to 1 hour
