# Vyrs Region Guide — Full Exploration

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Vyrs is the starting region of Witchspire. Green fields, ruined structures, and low-level enemies make it the safest place to learn the game. But even experienced players miss hidden chests and NPCs in Vyrs. This guide covers everything.

> **Note:** chest contents, NPC services, and exact counts below come from community reports and have not been independently verified.

---

## Region Overview

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Level Range | Low (early game — the safest region) |
| Area Size | Medium |
| Flight Pillars | 5 |
| Bosses | Al'uunian Golem |
| NPCs | Several (community reports vary) |
| Hidden Chests | 6 (reported) |
| Resource Caves | 2 |
| Recommended Stay | Until the first boss kill / main quest Act I |

| NPC | Location | Services |
|-----|----------|----------|
| Mira the Vendor | Vyrs Crossroads (center) | Sells seeds and basic recipes |
| Old Finn | North of Hearth (farmhouse) | Gives a fishing side quest, sells bait |
| Scholar Vex | Vyrs Eastern Ruins (library) | Lore items, maps of other regions |
| Traveler Kael | Vyrs South Passage gate | Explains region unlock mechanics |

**Talk to Scholar Vex before leaving Vyrs.** She reportedly sells a Timberline Basin map (community-reported price: 500 gold) that reveals Flight Pillar locations in the next zone.

## Points of Interest

### The Hearth Meadow
Your starting area. Flat, safe, ideal for base building. The Hearth crystal sits at the center of a stone circle.

### Vyrs Crossroads
Central hub with a vendor, a traveling merchant who rotates stock daily, and access to all Vyrs sub-zones.

### Vyrs South Cave
Iron ore hotspot with low-level enemies. Bring a torch (crafted at the Workbench). There is a hidden chamber behind a destructible wall on the right side at the midpoint.

### Eastern Ruins Arena
Large circular stone platform surrounded by ruined pillars. This is the **Al'uunian Golem** boss arena. Arrive prepared — see the [Al'uunian Golem Boss Guide](/guides/alunean-golem-boss-guide).

### Vyrs Swamp (Southeast)
Small hazard area — dark clouds deal damage over time; come with healing potions or resist gear. (Community reports of a Nox-type familiar living here have not been confirmed.)

## Vyrs Farming Route

Optimal loop for materials before tackling Al'uunian Golem:

```
Hearth → Vyrs Meadow (wood + fiber) → Crossroads (vendor) 
→ Vyrs South Cave (iron) → Fields (nectar) 
→ Eastern Ruins (stone + chests) → Back to Hearth
```

Total time: ~20 minutes. Total haul: ~50 Wood, ~40 Fiber, ~15 Iron Ore, ~20 Stone, ~20 Nectar (community-reported averages).

## Leaving Vyrs

When you are ready to leave, head to the **Vyrs South Passage** at the south edge of the map — the gate to Timberline Basin. Community reports differ on the unlock method (a Region Unlock Token versus an "Ancient Seal" quest chain); the main quest routes you through at the right time. See the [Main Quest Walkthrough](/guides/main-quest-walkthrough) and [Elements & Mechanics Guide](/guides/elements-mechanics-guide).

**Don't rush out. Complete everything in Vyrs first:**
- [ ] Bond at least 2 familiars
- [ ] Upgrade Hearth to Tier 3
- [ ] Craft your broom
- [ ] Find the hidden chests
- [ ] Defeat the Al'uunian Golem
- [ ] Buy the Timberline Basin map from Scholar Vex (if the vendor stock cooperates)
