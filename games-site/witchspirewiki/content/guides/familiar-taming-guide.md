# Witchspire Familiar Taming Guide — How to Catch & Bond Every Familiar

> Applies to EA v0.1.4 / Updated: August 1, 2026

"I killed a creature and nothing happened. How do I actually catch these things?" — the most common question in Witchspire's early game, and the answer surprises most players: **you do not weaken and throw orbs. You kill the creature and pray its spirit stays.** Familiars are the game's biggest collectible loop and its most misunderstood one, so let's get the real mechanics straight.

---

## How Bonding Actually Works (Step by Step)

1. **Kill the target creature** — not just weaken it. Kill it.
2. **Watch what its spirit does.** On death, a spirit ball appears. Two outcomes:
   - **Spirit flies up into the sky** with a purple streak → failed bonding attempt. The creature is gone for good this spawn.
   - **Spirit lingers near the ground** as a floating ball → success is on the table.
3. **Walk up to the lingering spirit and HOLD F** to bond. It enters your inventory as a Familiar.
4. **Repeat** until the spirit lingers — whether it stays is RNG.

That is the entire mechanic. There are **no capture orbs, no HP thresholds, no throwables**. If a guide tells you to weaken to 30% and throw an orb, it is describing a different game.

### Why Spirits Fly Away — The RNG Modifiers

The "lingers or flies away" roll is your bonding chance. You can push it:

| Modifier | Effect | How to get it |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **Incense** | +10% to +25% depending on tier (see table below) | Crafted at the Workbench / looted from chests and bosses |
| **Spirit Charmer (Luminary node)** | "Increases how often spirits linger" — the single biggest permanent boost | First node: Hearth Level 3 + Bonding Skill Level 5; more nodes at higher Hearth tiers |
| **Luck stat** | Community-confirmed to help the roll | Gear, consumables with Luck |
| **Foretellers Coven** | +40% bonding rate — the only real gameplay difference between the six covens | Chose the Foretellers at character creation (see [Best Coven Guide](/guides/best-coven-guide)) |

**Practical rule:** save your incense for **Epic and Legendary** creatures. Common and Rare familiars linger often enough that incense is wasted on them.

## Incense Guide — Recipes, Effects & Timing

| Incense | Recipe | Duration | Bonding Bonus |
|---------|--------|:--:|:--:|
| Simple Spirit Incense | 3 Spirit Dust + 1 Simple Ritual Candle | 20s (+30s after 0.1.4) | +10% |
| Potent Spirit Incense | 5 Greater Spirit Dust + 1 Simple Ritual Candle | 15s (+30s after 0.1.4) | +15% |
| Rich Spirit Incense | 3 Brilliant Spirit Dust + 1 Ritual Candle | 10s (+20s after 0.1.4) | +20% |
| Shimmering Incense | 3 Greater Spirit Dust + 1 Shimmerdust | 10s (+20s after 0.1.4) | +25% |

**Patch 0.1.4 (July 31, 2026) raised all incense durations** — the buff lasts longer now, which fixes the old "buff expires before the creature dies" frustration. See the [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes-updates) page.

**Timing matters:** the buff starts the moment you *use* the incense, not when the creature dies. Light it late in a fight — when the target is low and about to die — so the window covers the kill. Also: **a failed bonding attempt consumes the incense.** That is normal, not a bug.

## Which Starter Should You Pick?

The four starters (all findable in the wild later) match your playstyle:

| Starter | Element | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Quol** | Ethereal | Melee players — the community default pick |
| **Locto** | Lux (Fire) | Melee players who want damage output |
| **Bril** | Crystal (Ice) | Wand users |
| **Fien** | Nox (Dark) | Wand users — also spawns in Timberline Basin at night |

There is no wrong pick: every starter is findable in the world again, and your first familiar is more about momentum than meta. Pick the element that matches your weapon.

## Best First Familiars to Hunt (Early Game)

While you are still in the Vyrs starting zone, these are the highest-value targets:

| Priority | Familiar | Element | Where | Why |
|:--:|----------|---------|-------|-----|
| 1 | **Rockling** | Nature | Vyrs starting area + Rockling Breeding Village | Easy to bond, tanky, the community's "just grab one" recommendation |
| 2 | **Taileaf** | Nature | Left of the starting area (also Mira Isles) | Early Nature coverage, useful gatherer |
| 3 | **Specra** | — | Beach routes (farm Carapace materials on the way) | Two birds, one trip — loot + bond |
| 4 | **Shardling** | Crystal | Shard Caverns beneath Timberline Basin | Grab while mining — the Shard Caverns are a mining detour anyway |
| 5 | **Fien / Locto / Bril / Quol** | Any | Starter companions, or Fien in Timberline at night | Fill your missing element slot |

**The "Common beats Legendary" rule:** at launch, several rare familiars were borderline unobtainable (spirits almost never lingered). The community consensus is: a bonded Common that gives you a useful passive is worth more than an unbonded Legendary you re-roll for two hours. Bond what you can, upgrade later.

## Familiar Slots, Equipping & Abilities

- **Three familiar slots** in the inventory (the bottom circles). Stand near a **Hearth**, open the menu with **Tab**, and drag familiars into slots.
- **One familiar is active in combat** — press **C** to cycle between your three equipped.
- Remove: minus button on the slot. Replace: rotation button at the top of the circle.
- Familiars also work **passively as crafting helpers**: certain stations need (or are sped up by) specific familiars — Charlings speed up Cookers, Xilvants and Shudders power Witchcraft Circles, Lety runs the Sawmill, Treetop the Forester's Hut, Bompus and Detrox the Miner's Cabin, and so on. See the [All Familiars Guide](/guides/all-familiars-guide) for the full crafting table.
- **Celestial / "Shifties"** are alternate color patterns of normal familiars — same tier, same bonding rules, just rarer to roll.

## Element Coverage Strategy

Familiars come in six elements: **Nature, Lux (light), Nox (dark), Crystal (ice), Ethereal, and Astral.**

For early game, aim for **three different elements across your three slots** so you always have something effective against whatever zone you enter:

| Zone (roughly) | Element that shines |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Vyrs | Nature / Lux |
| Mira Isles | Lux / Nature |
| Timberline Basin | Crystal / Nox (plus Astral on the beaches) |
| Shuddersands | Nox / Astral |

Check the zone's creatures before you head out and swap at your Hearth accordingly — swapping is free and instant.

## FAQ

**Q: I killed a creature and no spirit appeared at all.**
Normal. Only a portion of creatures are familiar candidates at all; the rest drop regular loot. Kill again — you can't tell which are candidates until they die.

**Q: The spirit flew into the sky. Can I chase it?**
No. That is a failed roll. The creature is gone until it respawns.

**Q: Do I need the creature at low HP?**
No — that is a false mechanic from older guides. Kill it outright; the linger roll happens on death.

**Q: Does incense work in co-op?**
Yes, but it only benefits the player who used it.

**Q: Is there a guaranteed way to catch Legendaries?**
Not at launch. Stack incense + Spirit Charmer + Luck + Foretellers, and expect re-rolls. The 0.1.4 patch and the "A Touch of Magic" roadmap update (Familiar Wheel) are both aimed at making bonding friendlier — see the [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes-updates).

**Q: Do bonded familiars persist when I visit a friend's world?**
Your bonded familiars stay in your inventory — they travel with you (see the [Co-op Troubleshooting Guide](/guides/coop-troubleshooting-guide) for exactly what carries over).
