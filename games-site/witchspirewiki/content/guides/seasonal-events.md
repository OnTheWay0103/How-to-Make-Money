# Seasonal Events Guide — Festivals, Tokens, and Rewards

> **Applies to EA v0.1.1d / Updated: July 31, 2026**

On top of the in-session events (Night Events, supply pods, world boss rotations), Witchspire runs seasonal festivals on a real-world schedule. These are the best windows of the year for exclusive cosmetics, unique familiars, and seasonal materials that do not drop anywhere else. This guide covers the festival calendar, the seasonal token economy, and how to prepare.

---

## Festival Calendar

| Festival | Window | Theme | Signature Reward |
|----------|--------|-------|------------------|
| Spring Bloom | April 1 – 21 | Nature, growth | Bloomling familiar (Lux-type, rare) |
| Emberfall | June 1 – 21 | Fire, festival of light | Emberwing familiar (Fire-type, rare) |
| Harvest Moon | October 1 – 21 | Harvest, spirits | Hauntling familiar (Nox-type, rare) |
| Winter's Wake | December 1 – 21 | Frost, renewal | Frostling familiar (Frost-type, rare) |

Each festival runs three weeks, resets its shop weekly, and repeats annually. If you miss a festival familiar, it returns next year — but the seasonal title and skins are one-time.

---

## How Seasonal Tokens Work

Every festival introduces its own token currency (Bloom Petals, Ember Coals, Moon Seeds, Wake Crystals). Tokens come from:

| Source | Tokens | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Festival quests (daily) | 50–100 | 3 dailies per day during festival |
| Festival world events | 20–50 each | Spawn hourly in every region |
| Seasonal boss kills | 100–150 | Each festival adds a limited-time boss variant |
| Gathering festival plants | 5–10 each | Special nodes spawn only during the festival |
| Turn-in of leftover prior-year tokens | 1:5 | Bad rate — spend before the festival ends |

**Rules to remember:**

- Tokens do NOT carry over between festivals of the same year (they convert at 1:5 at the end)
- Tokens from last year DO carry into the same festival this year — hoarding for a year is a valid strategy
- The festival shop rotates weekly; the best items (familiar egg, weapon skin) are in week 1, not week 3

## Festival Shops

Each festival shop follows the same structure:

| Shop Category | Token Cost | Contents |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| Familiar Egg | 1,500 | The festival-exclusive familiar (guaranteed rare) |
| Weapon Skin | 1,200 | Exclusive festival-themed cosmetic |
| Gear Box | 300–500 | Blue–purple gear scaled to your level |
| Materials | 50–150 | Seasonal materials for festival recipes |
| Title | 800 | Seasonal title, one-time unlock |
| Food/Consumables | 25–100 | Festival buff foods (best buffs of the year) |

**Priority order for casual players**: Familiar Egg → Title → Consumables → Gear Box → Skin. The skin is pure vanity; the egg and title are account-persistent.

---

## Seasonal Boss Variants

Every festival reskins one world boss with new mechanics. The seasonal variant always has:

- 25% more HP and a new AoE pattern (memorize the telegraph — it is always wider than the normal version)
- A guaranteed festival token drop (100–150)
- A small chance at the seasonal familiar egg as a direct drop (approx. 5%)

**Boss strategy**: bring the festival's counter-element. Bloomling events favor Frost; Emberfall favors Nox; Harvest Moon favors Lux; Winter's Wake favors Fire. A full party with element-matched builds clears the variant in under 10 minutes.

## Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Stock 20+ potions before festival week 1 — festival dailies are combat-heavy
- [ ] Clear your inventory of junk the night before the festival starts
- [ ] Save one week of cooldown-free playtime for festival week 1 (best shop rotation)
- [ ] Do all 3 dailies every day: 50–100 tokens × 3 = 300+/day, plus event spawns
- [ ] Park a Gatherer familiar with "find special node" abilities for festival plants
- [ ] Check the region guides for festival spawn points in your main farming area
- [ ] Spend tokens before the final week — leftover conversion is 1:5

## Maximizing Token Income

The gap between a casual and a focused player during a festival is roughly 800 tokens. The focused schedule:

| Day | Activity | Expected Tokens |
|-----|----------|-----------------|
| Daily | 3 festival dailies (30–45 min) | 150–300 |
| Daily | 2–3 festival world events (catch the hourly spawn) | 40–150 |
| Daily | 10–15 festival plants while farming | 50–150 |
| Weekly | Seasonal boss kill (plan one per week) | 100–150 |
| Total | One focused week | ~1,600–2,100 |

With ~1,500 tokens needed for the familiar egg, one committed week buys the headline reward; three casual weeks also reach it. Everything beyond the egg is bonus.

**Token efficiency rules:**

- Festival world events spawn at the top of every hour — set a mental clock and route your farming between event spawns
- Festival plants spawn only in the region the festival theme favors; check the region guides for exact node patterns
- The seasonal boss is on a weekly reset — kill it once a week, not once per festival
- Do not buy Gear Boxes with tokens early — they are level-scaled and the same gear drops from normal dungeons

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get a festival familiar outside the festival?** No — the egg is festival-only. Your only second chance is the 5% direct drop from the seasonal boss.

**Are festival foods worth hoarding?** Yes, but they expire. Festival consumables last 30 real-world days from the festival end.

**Do festival events appear in co-op?** Yes, and event spawns scale with party size — a duo gets more tokens per hour than two solo players.
