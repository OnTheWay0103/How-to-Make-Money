# Mira Isles Region Guide — Island Exploration

> Last verified: August 10, 2026

Mira Isles is the island region of Witchspire — a tropical archipelago reachable by boat from the mainland. With its mix of sandy beaches, volcanic terrain, and ancient ruins, Mira Isles presents unique environmental hazards and some of the game's most rewarding hidden content.

> **Note:** the region sits at the upper end of the current content. Exact enemy levels, chest contents, and NPC details below come from community reports and have not been independently verified.

---

## Region Overview

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Level Range | Mid-to-high (tougher than Timberline Basin) |
| Area Size | Medium-large (4 main islands + smaller islets) |
| Flight Pillars | 3 |
| Bosses | Reported: R'hyrrgar (Volcanic Guardian) — not independently verified |
| NPCs | Several (community reports vary) |
| Hidden Chests | 7 (reported) |
| Luminary Shrines | 2 (reported) |
| Recommended Stay | Mid-game, before Shuddersands |

### How to Enter

1. Reach the **Eastern Docks** in Timberline Basin (east edge of the map)
2. Take the boat passage (community-reported gold fare) to **Welcome Beach** on the main isle

> **Note:** community reports describe a guardian at the docks and a boat fare of a few hundred gold — details are not independently verified.

---

## Island Layout

Mira Isles consists of 4 main islands connected by broom flight:

| Island | Key Features |
|--------|--------------|
| **Main Isle** | Welcome Beach, Soley Cave, Flight Pillar |
| **Volcano Isle** | R'hyrrgar's Peak (reported), lava tubes, Flight Pillar |
| **Ruins Isle** | Ancient Temple, reported Lux shrine, Flight Pillar |
| **Shrine Isle** | Reported Nature shrine, Flight Pillar |

---

## Key Locations

### R'hyrrgar's Peak

The volcano at the center of Volcano Isle. Community reports describe a volcanic guardian boss, **R'hyrrgar**, at the summit — this encounter has **not been independently verified** and is not part of our confirmed boss coverage.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Location | Volcano Isle — summit crater (reported) |
| Enemies | Lava sprites, ember golems (reported) |
| Purpose | Reported boss arena and region gate |
| Flight Pillar | Reported on the outer crater rim |

**Climbing the volcano (community reports):** a switchback trail from the southern beach with rest points along the way; at the summit, a **Crater Seal** reportedly summons R'hyrrgar.

**Heat mechanic:** reports describe faster hunger drain on the volcano — bring Nectar and water.

### Soley Cave

A submerged sea cave accessible from the Main Isle's eastern coast (underwater entrance).

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Entrance | Underwater cave mouth (Main Isle east coast) |
| Enemies | Coral-type guardians, jellyfish (reported) |
| Resources | Pearls, Moonleaf (reported) |
| Hidden Chests | 2 (reported) |

**Inside Soley Cave (community reports):**
- **Pearl Bed** — reported pearl deposits used for high-tier enchanting
- **Coral Sanctuary** — reported safe room with a campfire
- **Hidden Chamber** — behind a cracked wall, reportedly holds a legendary crafting component

### Luminary Shrines

Two shrines are reported in Mira Isles, each said to grant bonus Luminary Points when solved:

| Shrine | Location | Reported Reward | Requirement |
|--------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Lux Shrine | Ruins Isle — Ancient Temple rooftop | Bonus Luminary Points | Solve a light-reflection puzzle (rotate mirrors) |
| Nature Shrine | Shrine Isle — central grove | Bonus Luminary Points | Offer Moonleaf at the altar |

> **Note:** the existence and rewards of these shrines have not been independently verified. If you find them, the [Luminary Skill Tree Guide](/guides/luminary-skill-tree-guide) covers how to spend the points.

## Farming Route

Optimal loop for Mira Isles:

```
Welcome Beach (fruit + chest) → Soley Cave (pearls + chests) 
→ Volcano Isle (lava tubes + chests) 
→ Ruins Isle (temple + shrine + chest) 
→ Shrine Isle (moonleaf + shrine + chest) → Back to Welcome Beach
```

Total time: ~40 minutes. Requires broom flight for island travel.

---

## Leaving Mira Isles

The onward route to the **Shuddersands** is reported to pass through the islands' northern edge. Community reports describe a submerged passage gated behind the volcanic guardian's defeat — this has **not been independently verified**. Follow the main quest for the current route.

**Completion checklist:**
- [ ] Activate all 3 Flight Pillars
- [ ] Find the hidden chests
- [ ] Bond Chirili and Charling in Chirili Crater
- [ ] Solve the reported shrine puzzles if you find them
- [ ] Restock before the desert — see the [Shuddersands Guide](/guides/shuddersands-guide)
