## The Climb Is a Route, Not a Checklist

Aincrad has roughly 10 cleared floors, and the game lets you skip ahead via teleport gates unlocked by other players. The optimal order is still **numerical** — but *how* you climb each floor matters more than which floor you do. This guide covers the best floor order, what to grab on each floor, and the three rules that keep the climb smooth.

> **Note on sources:** Floor details for Floors 1–3 are well-documented by the community. Details for Floors 4–10 (enemy rosters, quest names, boss names) are community-reported and not officially published — treat specifics as approximations.

## Floor-by-Floor Walkthrough

### Floor 1 — Town of Beginnings (Levels 1-10)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Theme** | Green fields, forests, starter town |
| **Enemies** | Dire Wolves, Goblins, Giant Bats, Frenzy Boars |
| **Field Boss** | Goblin Chieftain |
| **Floor Boss** | Illfang the Kobold Lord |

**Key Quests:**
- "Welcome to Aincrad" — tutorial quest, rewards starter weapon
- "Goblin Problem" — kill goblins, rewards healing potions recipe
- "The Foreboding Fortress" — unlocks the Floor 1 dungeon

**Notable Rewards:**
- First weapon from the tutorial quest (choose wisely — it is your main weapon for 5+ levels)
- Smithy unlock after reserving an Inn room
- Basic crafting recipes from shop vendors

**Tips:**
- Complete every side quest before attempting the floor boss
- Farm the goblin camp for Iron Ore (your first upgrade materials)
- The Floor 1 dungeon has a hidden chest with an Uncommon weapon

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Theme** | Twin-peaked valley, green fields, the town of Marunne |

| **Field Boss** | Reported near the Split Gate |
| **Floor Boss** | The Twin Wardens |

**Key Quests:**

- Mana Shards and Tough Hide (crafting materials)
- Uncommon armor pieces from quest rewards and the field boss

**Tips:**
- The North Valley is the best early source of Iron Ore after Floor 1
- Wisp Mages in the South Valley drop Mana Shards for magic builds
- The Split Gate arena sits at the valley's center — activate the teleport gate there first

## The Three Rules of the Climb

### Rule 1: Always Unlock the Teleport Gate First

The first thing you do on any new floor is **find and activate its teleport gate**. Everything else — quests, farming, bosses — becomes easier once you have a return route. Skipping the gate turns a bad boss attempt into a long hike back to town.

### Rule 2: Complete Every Side Quest Before the Boss

Side quests award **skill points, EX-Mod slots, and permanent stat bonuses** — the game's actual progression currency. Fighting a floor boss without the floor's side quests is fighting at reduced power by choice.

### Rule 3: Shop the Floor Vendors Before You Leave

Each floor's vendor sells unique items you cannot get elsewhere:

| Floor | Vendor Item | Why It Matters |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | Starter crafting recipes, torch | The torch is required for darker areas |
| 7 | Fire resistance gear | Important for Floor 7 itself and strong on Floor 8 |
| 8 | Frost-resistant consumables | Softens the Tundra's status effects |

## Handling the Difficulty Spikes

The difficulty jump between floors is **not linear** — Floors 4 and 7 are the two spikes that end climbs.

### Floor 4 — Plains of War (First Spike)

- **Why it spikes:** Harpies punish melee-only builds; War Golems punish unupgraded weapons.
- **How to survive:** your gear must be at least **Uncommon tier** before the floor boss. Farm the War Golem Commander field boss once for the Rare drop head start.
- **Counter-play:** bring ranged options for harpies, or wait for their dive before attacking.

### Floor 7 — Lava Fields (Second Spike)

- **Why it spikes:** Fire elementals punish anything without fire resistance; the lava environment punishes positioning.
- **How to survive:** buy the floor's fire resistance gear from the vendor **before** farming. Ice damage is reported to out-damage Fire here.
- **Counter-play:** never fight on lava-adjacent ground — pull enemies to the safe paths.

## When to Detour (Going Back Down)

Returning to earlier floors is not wasted time — some quests and secrets require high-level stats to access:

| Detour | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Floor 1-2 revisit at Level 20+ | Hidden chests and secret quests gated by stats |
| Floor 3 Crystal Caverns revisit | Soul Crystal and Crystal Shard farming |
| Floor 6 elite patrol route at Level 35+ | Rare materials and EX-Mod drops |

**Schedule:** do one "cleanup day" per 5 levels — sweep every previous floor for missed secrets, hidden chests, and quest chains you outleveled.

## After Floor 10: The Endgame Bridge

Floor 10 is the current endgame — but the climb is not the only content. The **Golden Gate** repeatable boss system (1 Gold Crystal per summon) is where top-end legendary weapons and maximum-value EX-Mods are reported to drop. Treat it as the next step after the climb: finish the floors, then keep climbing the gate. See the [Golden Gate Bosses Guide](/guides/golden-gate-bosses-guide).

**Final advice:** the fastest climbers are not the ones who skip floors — they are the ones who complete each floor fully on the first pass, so they never have to return for gear, quests, or vendor items they missed.
