## Boss Mechanics Overview

Bosses in Echoes of Aincrad are **gate-locked behind barriers**. Once the fight starts, it ends only when one side dies — no retreat, no escape.

### Pre-Boss Checklist
Before every boss fight:
- [ ] Weapon tuned with EX-Mods (Reduced Stamina Cost + damage boost minimum)
- [ ] Partner with healing or defensive Support Skills equipped
- [ ] Full stock of healing items
- [ ] Cardinal Rank, weapon upgrades, and stats aligned
- [ ] Recent save (use checkpoint before boss room)

### Universal Boss Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Arena lock** | Boss rooms are gate-locked — the fight ends only when one side dies |
| **Parry windows** | A **blue flash** signals a parry window — use it for counter damage |
| **Partner usage** | Use **Switch Mode** to have your partner draw aggro and tank hard phases |
| **Combination Skills** | Fire them once damage is built up — don't save them for the end |
| **Learning first** | Open with light attacks and parries to learn patterns; save heavy attacks for confirmed openings |

> **Note on sources:** Floors 1–3 boss details below are well-documented by the community. Golden Gate bosses are covered in the [Golden Gate Bosses Guide](/guides/golden-gate-bosses-guide). The game reportedly launched July 9–10, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

## Floor 1: The Violet Fencer

**Location**: Floor 1 — End of the Forest Dungeon
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Recommended**: Vitality 3+, Endurance 3+, Damage stat 3+, Uncommon weapon with 2 EX-Mods

### Attack Patterns
- **Thrust Combo** (3 hits): Dodge sideways on the third thrust
- **AoE Slash** (360°): Backstep immediately — large radius
- **Lunge** (gap closer): Parry the blue flash for a counter window
- **Enrage** (below 30% HP): Attacks become faster (reported ~30%)

### Strategy
1. **Phase 1 (100-50%)**: Play defensively. Block/dodge, learn the rhythm. Attack only after her Thrust Combo finishes.
2. **Phase 2 (50-30%)**: She adds the AoE Slash. Stay close — the AoE is easier to dodge at close range (smaller effective radius).
3. **Phase 3 (30-0%)**: Enrage. Her combos are faster but the patterns are the same. Do not panic-dodge. Save the Combination Skill for the finish.

### Best Weapons
- **Sword & Shield**: Block everything, punish after combos
- **Rapier**: Parry the Lunge for massive counter damage

### Partner Setup
Bring a partner with a healing Support Skill. Use Switch Mode to let them tank the Enrage phase while you heal.

## Floor 1: Ark Guardian (Optional)

**Location**: Floor 1 — Sealed Chamber (requires key from side quest)
**Difficulty**: Hard
**Recommended**: Vitality 5+, weapon with 3 EX-Mods

### Attack Patterns
- **Ground Slam** (AoE shockwave): Jump or dodge
- **Charge** (linear): Sidestep — do NOT block (guard break)
- **Summon Adds**: At 50% HP, summons 3 goblins — kill them first
- **Beam Attack** (linear, telegraphed): Easy dodge but punishing if it hits

### Strategy
Kill adds immediately when they spawn. The Guardian's beam attack has a long wind-up — use the time to heal and rebuff. Mace is surprisingly effective — stagger buildup interrupts the beam charge.

## Floor 2: Twin Wardens

**Location**: Floor 2 — The Split Gate
**Difficulty**: Very Hard
**Recommended**: Vitality 8+, Damage stat 8+, Rare weapon with 4 EX-Mods

### Attack Patterns
Two bosses fight simultaneously:
- **Warden of Blades** (fast, melee): Flurry attacks, bleed debuff
- **Warden of Flames** (ranged, magic): Fire projectiles, AoE flame pools

### Strategy
1. **Kill Blades first** — his bleed will drain your healing items if left unchecked
2. Use Switch Mode to have your partner occupy Flames while you fight Blades
3. **Parry Blades' Flurry** — the third hit has a blue flash window
4. After Blades dies, Flames enrages (faster projectiles, larger AoE). Close the gap and stay aggressive.
5. **Dodge, don't block** — Flames' attacks cannot be blocked

### Best Weapons
- **Dagger**: Speed to match Blades, throw to poke Flames at range
- **Greatsword**: Charge attacks during Flames' long cast animations

### Rewards
Clearing the Twin Wardens opens the Split Gate to **Floor 3 — Fae Wilds**.

## Floor 3: Corrupted Fae Lord

**Location**: Floor 3 — Crimson Canyon (main quest)
**Difficulty**: Very High danger zone
**Recommended**: Cardinal Rank 7–9, Vitality 8–12, Damage stat 8–12; Rare+ weapon with 3–4 EX-Mods; light resistance potions

### Attack Patterns (community-reported)
- **Phase 1 (100-50%)**: Magic barrages, teleport, curse debuff
- **Phase 2 (50-0%)**: AoE curse explosions + dark fae adds

### Strategy
1. **Phase 1**: Chase the teleports and punish the recovery after barrages; cleanse the curse debuff promptly
2. **Phase 2**: Kill the dark fae adds immediately when they spawn — they chain-cast and interrupt your damage window
3. Bring light resistance consumables; the curse explosions cover a wide radius in Phase 2

### Rewards
Clearing the Corrupted Fae Lord opens access to **Floor 4**.

## Floor 3: Crystal Guardian (Zone Boss)

**Location**: Floor 3 — Crystal Caverns
**Difficulty**: Zone boss
**Recommended**: Whatever you bring to the Fae Wilds — it is not a story gate

### Attack Patterns
- High HP, mixes physical and magic attacks
- Reported to resist multiple damage types — bring an elemental spread

### Strategy
A tough optional fight worth doing for the drop. **Drops the Soul Crystal** — the required material for the legendary weapon crafting chain (see the [Legendary Weapons Guide](/guides/legendary-weapons)).
