## Golden Gate Bosses Guide — Echoes of Aincrad

> Version 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026

The **Golden Gate** is Echoes of Aincrad's repeatable endgame boss system. Opening a gate costs **1 Gold Crystal** and summons a powerful boss; defeating it rewards **Rank S (Legendary) weapons** and **EX-MODs**. If you want maximum-value EX-MODs, Golden Gate is the only place to get them.

---

## Golden Gate Quick Reference

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Cost to open** | 1 Gold Crystal per Golden Gate |
| **Level requirement** | Encounters start at **Level 60** and scale with your level |
| **Rewards** | Rank S (Legendary) weapons + EX-MODs |
| **Unique property** | Golden Gate Legendary weapons are **distinct from crafted Legendaries** — their own item pool, and the **only source of maximum-value EX-MODs** |
| **How to spot max EX-MODs** | When an EX-MOD reaches maximum value, its text turns **blue/violet** |

---

## EX-MOD Farming Loop

1. **Farm Gold Crystals** — the gate currency; budget them for gate runs
2. **Open Golden Gates at a level where the boss is beatable** — they scale with you, so time your runs
3. **Identify max-value EX-MODs by their blue/violet text** — these are the keepers
4. **Move EX-MODs onto your favorite base weapon** — up to **4 EX-MODs** can be combined onto a single weapon **from the same weapon type**
5. **Keep Golden Gate Legendaries separate** — they're a different pool from smithy-crafted Legendaries and cannot be replaced by crafting
