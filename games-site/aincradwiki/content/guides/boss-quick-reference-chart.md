## All Bosses Quick-Reference Chart — Echoes of Aincrad

> Version 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026 · Game launched July 9–10, 2026 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC)

Quick-reference chart of every boss documented in the wiki — the Floor 1–3 story and optional encounters covered by the [Boss Guide](/guides/bosses-guide) and [Floor 3 Guide](/guides/floor-3-guide), plus the repeatable endgame Golden Gate fights (detailed in the [Golden Gate Bosses Guide](/guides/golden-gate-bosses-guide)).

Bosses in Aincrad are **gate-locked behind barriers**: once the fight starts it ends only when one side dies — no retreat, no escape. Stock healing items, tune your weapon with EX-Mods, and save at the checkpoint before entering.

## All Bosses Quick-Reference Chart

| Boss | Floor / Location | Difficulty | Phases & Key Mechanics | Recommended Setup | Notable Drops / Notes |
|------|------------------|------------|------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| **Violet Fencer** | Floor 1 — End of the Forest Dungeon | Medium | 3 phases; Thrust Combo (3 hits — dodge sideways on the third), 360° AoE Slash (backstep), Lunge gap-closer (parry the blue flash); **enrage below 30% HP** (attacks 30% faster) | Vitality 3+, Endurance 3+, Damage stat 3+; Uncommon weapon with 2 EX-Mods | First story boss |

## Universal Boss Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Arena lock** | Boss rooms are gate-locked — the fight ends only when one side dies |
| **Parry windows** | A **blue flash** signals a parry window — use it for counter damage |
| **Partner usage** | Use **Switch Mode** to have your partner draw aggro and tank hard phases |
| **Pre-boss checklist** | Weapon tuned with EX-Mods · partner with healing Support Skills · full stock of healing items · stats/rank aligned · checkpoint save before the room |
| **Combination Skills** | Fire them once damage is built up — don't save them for the end |
