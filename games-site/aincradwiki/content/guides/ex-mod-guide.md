## What Are EX-Mods?

EX-Mods are weapon modifiers that significantly enhance your combat capabilities. They are crafted, combined, and transferred at the Smithy in the Town of Beginnings.

> **Note on numbers:** Echoes of Aincrad does not publish official modifier statistics. Effect ranges and costs below are community-reported and may vary with version. Treat exact percentages as approximate.

### EX-Mod Slots

Each weapon can hold up to **4 EX-Mods**. A **5th slot unlocks at 100% weapon familiarity** — plan your best EX-Mod around it. Legendary weapons are reported to hold 5 slots natively.

## Unlocking the Smithy

The Smithy unlocks after you reserve a room at the Inn. This happens early in the main quest — usually within the first 1–2 hours.

## Core Systems

### 1. Improve Base Parameters

Use materials and spare weapons to boost a weapon's base stats (attack power, durability). This is the simplest upgrade path — good for early game when you lack EX-Mods.

### 2. Combine Weapons (EX-Mod Pooling)

Combine two weapons of the same type to pool their EX-Mods onto one weapon. The resulting weapon keeps up to 4 EX-Mods (the highest-rank ones are prioritized).

**Example**: Weapon A has EX-Mods [Attack +5%, HP +10]. Weapon B has EX-Mods [Crit +3%, Stamina -8%]. Combining A + B at the Smithy creates a weapon with [Attack +5%, HP +10, Crit +3%, Stamina -8%].

### 3. EX-Mod Transfer

Move an EX-Mod from one weapon to another. This costs materials and **Col** (in-game currency). Use this to:
- Move a rare EX-Mod from a weak weapon to your main weapon
- Preserve EX-Mods when upgrading to a higher-rarity weapon

**Transfer cost increases with EX-Mod rarity.** Epic/Legendary EX-Mods are expensive to move — plan transfers carefully.

## EX-Mod Tier Rankings

### S-Tier — God Tier, Farm These First

Universally useful EX-Mods that provide massive power spikes for any build.

| EX-Mod | Effect (Max, reported) | Best For | Why S-Tier |
|--------|-------------|----------|------------|
| **Reduced Stamina Cost** | -20% SP cost | All builds | Every build needs SP. No build skips this |
| **Improved Critical Rate** | +12% crit chance | DEX builds, DPS | One of the largest DPS gains per slot |
| **Counter Damage Up** | +20% counter damage | Parry builds (Rapier, S&S) | Rare separate multiplier bucket — massive gains per slot |
| **Improved Normal Attack** | +16% basic attack | All builds | Basic attacks make up a large share of total damage |

**God Roll**: Reduced Stamina Cost + Improved Critical Rate + Counter Damage Up + Improved Normal Attack.

### A-Tier — Strong, Build-Specific Powerhouses

These EX-Mods are as strong as S-Tier options within their specific builds, but weaker or useless outside them.

| EX-Mod | Effect (Max, reported) | Best For | Why A-Tier |
|--------|-------------|----------|------------|
| **Improved Charge Attack** | +20% charge damage | Greatsword, Two-Handed Axe | Core damage for heavy weapons |
| **Attack Speed Up** | +12% attack speed | DEX builds, Dagger | More crit rolls per second, better uptime |
| **Stamina Recovery Up** | +20% SP regen | Heavy weapons | Combines with Stamina Cost for near-unlimited SP |
| **Guard Break Up** | +20% stagger buildup | Mace, Greatsword, Two-Handed Axe | Essential for stagger builds |
| **HP Recovery on Kill** | 8% HP per kill | Farming builds | Strong farming sustain, weaker vs bosses |
| **Improved Block** | -20% block damage | Sword & Shield | Near-unbreakable tank setup |

### B-Tier — Situational or Stat-Stacking Fillers

These EX-Mods are useful in specific situations or as stat-stacking filler when you cannot get S/A-Tier options.

| EX-Mod | Effect (Max, reported) | Best For | Viability |
|--------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **HP Up** | +16% max HP | All builds, beginners | Safety net, falls off as you improve |
| **Improved Skill Damage** | +16% skill damage | Burst builds | Skills are only part of total damage |
| **Ranged Damage Up** | +16% ranged damage | Mage, hybrids | Situational |
| **Elemental Damage Up** | +16% elemental | Elemental builds | Strong on weakness, weak otherwise |
| **Drop Rate Up** | +10% drop rate | Farming | Zero combat value |
| **Vitality Up** | +8% Vitality | Tank builds | Worse than HP Up (diminishing returns) |
| **Movement Speed Up** | +10% move speed | Kiting builds | Minor combat benefit |
| **Parry Window Extension** | +15% parry window | Beginners learning | Training wheels, swap out later |

## Best EX-Mod Combinations by Build

### Rapier (Dex Crit) — God Roll
**Slot order**: Reduced Stamina Cost > Improved Critical Rate > Counter Damage Up > Improved Normal Attack. Alternate: swap Counter Damage Up for Attack Speed Up.

### Sword & Shield (Balanced)
**Slot order**: Reduced Stamina Cost > Improved Block > Improved Normal Attack > HP Up. Alternate: HP Up for Counter Damage Up if confident in parry timing.

### Greatsword / Two-Handed Axe (Heavy Strength)
**Slot order**: Improved Charge Attack > Reduced Stamina Cost > Stamina Recovery Up > HP Recovery on Kill. Farming setup: replace Reduced Stamina Cost with Guard Break Up.

### Dual-Wield (Burst)
**Slot order**: Reduced Stamina Cost > Attack Speed Up > Improved Critical Rate > Improved Normal Attack.

### Mace (Stagger)
**Slot order**: Guard Break Up > Reduced Stamina Cost > Improved Normal Attack > HP Up.

### Dagger (Speed Crit)
**Slot order**: Improved Critical Rate > Attack Speed Up > Reduced Stamina Cost > Improved Normal Attack.

### Mage (Elemental)
**Slot order**: Reduced Stamina Cost > Elemental Damage Up > Improved Skill Damage > Improved Normal Attack.

## How to Farm the Best EX-Mods

**1. Weapon Combining (Cheapest)**: Farm weapon drops (Floor 1 goblin camps), combine same-type weapons to pool EX-Mods, keep S/A-Tier donors. Combining is cheaper than individual transfers.

**2. Boss Farming (Fastest)**: Bosses drop higher-rarity weapons — reported progression: Violet Fencer (Uncommon), Ark Guardian (Rare), Twin Wardens (Rare), Floor 3+ (Epic). Kill → check → transfer → repeat.

**3. Vendor Scouting**: Some players cycle vendor inventory to find discounted weapons with good EX-Mods. Results vary by version — if the vendor does not refresh after a few attempts, wait for the natural restock timer.

**4. Material Crafting (Endgame)**: Crafting recipes (community-reported):
- Reduced Stamina Cost = Iron Ore x5 + Beast Hide x3
- Improved Critical Rate = Crystal Shard x2 + Refined Steel x1
- Counter Damage Up = Boss Essence x1 + Crystal Shard x3
- Improved Normal Attack = Iron Ore x3 + Wood Scraps x5

**Pro tip**: Do not craft common-rarity EX-Mods. The material cost is close to Uncommon+, but stat values are reported 40–60% lower.

## Material Farming

### Common Materials
- **Iron Ore** — dropped by goblins and mined from Floor 1 caves
- **Beast Hide** — dropped by Dire Wolves (Forest area, Floor 1)
- **Wood Scraps** — from wooden crates and barrel objects

### Rare Materials
- **Crystal Shards** — mined in the Floor 3 Crystal Caverns; also from select boss drops
- **Refined Steel** — Smithy crafting using Iron Ore x5
- **Boss Essence** — reported guaranteed drop from floor bosses

### Best Farming Route (Early Game)
1. Start at Floor 1 Forest checkpoint
2. Clear Dire Wolf packs (Beast Hide + XP)
3. Clear goblin camps (Iron Ore + weapon drops)
4. Fast travel back to checkpoint
5. Repeat

This loop reportedly yields enough materials for 2–3 EX-Mod upgrades per hour (community-reported).

## Common EX-Mod Mistakes

- **Stacking duplicates**: Same-type bonuses reportedly do not stack. The second slot is wasted.
- **Over-investing in early weapons**: Transfer costs for low-rarity EX-Mods are often not worth it.
- **Skipping Reduced Stamina Cost**: Widely considered the best EX-Mod in the game. Do not ignore it.
- **Crafting common rarity**: The stat difference between Common and Uncommon is reported at 40–60%. Save materials.
- **Ignoring the 5th slot**: Unlocked at 100% weapon familiarity. Plan your best EX-Mod around it.

## Quick Tips

- **Combine before transferring** — pooling EX-Mods via combination is cheaper than individual transfers
- **Keep a "donor" weapon** — store a weapon with good EX-Mods to transfer onto future upgrades
- **Don't over-invest in early weapons** — you will replace them. Save rare EX-Mods for weapons you will use for 5+ hours
- **Check the Smithy after every boss** — bosses drop materials needed for high-tier EX-Mod crafting
