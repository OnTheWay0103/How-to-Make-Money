## Partner System Overview

You never fight alone in Echoes of Aincrad. AI partners join every quest with their own:
- Fighting style (aggressive, defensive, balanced)
- Weapon preference
- Support Skills (healing, buffs, debuffs)
- Combination Skills (duo attacks)

A partner played passively is a damage loss, not a damage gain. Optimization means matching the partner to your weapon, managing their Support Skills, building the Combination gauge deliberately, and leveling them on the right schedule.

> **Note on numbers:** Echoes of Aincrad does not publish official partner statistics. Percentages and timers below (heal amounts, buff durations, cooldowns) are community-reported and may vary with version and Cardinal Rank.

## How Partners Work

### Combat Behavior
Partners fight autonomously but respond to your commands:
- **Free Mode**: Partner attacks your target or the nearest enemy
- **Switch Mode**: Partner draws aggro while you reposition, heal, or set up combos

### Support Skills
Assigned to your action wheel. Partners use them on cooldown or when conditions are met (e.g., you drop below a health threshold).

### Combination Skills
Powerful duo attacks triggered after both you and your partner build enough damage on the same target. A gauge fills during combat — when full, unleash the Combination Skill for massive damage.

## Partner Types

### Asuna (Balanced Fighter)
- **Weapon**: Rapier
- **Style**: Aggressive, high DPS
- **Support Skill**: Healing (restores roughly 30% HP)
- **Best for**: General use, speedrunning content

### Klein (Tank)
- **Weapon**: Sword & Shield
- **Style**: Defensive, draws aggro
- **Support Skill**: Defense Up (reported ~20% damage reduction for ~30s)
- **Best for**: Difficult bosses, survival-focused runs

### Agil (Heavy Hitter)
- **Weapon**: Two-Handed Axe
- **Style**: Aggressive, AoE focus
- **Support Skill**: Attack Up (reported ~15% damage increase for ~30s)
- **Best for**: Farming, clearing mobs quickly

### Lisbeth (Support Specialist)
- **Weapon**: Mace
- **Style**: Supportive, stagger-focused
- **Support Skill**: Party Heal (restores ~20% HP to you and your partner)
- **Best for**: Long boss fights, dungeon runs

## Partner Selection by Weapon

| Your Weapon | Best Partner | Why |
|-------------|--------------|-----|
| Rapier / Daggers (burst DPS) | Asuna | Healing covers your aggression; her rapier combos chain with your staggers |
| Greatsword / Two-Handed Axe (slow heavy) | Klein | His aggro windows let you land slow finishers safely |
| Sword & Shield (tanky) | Asuna | You hold the line; she provides the missing damage |
| Mage (ranged) | Klein | He holds the line while you channel spells safely |
| Any solo content | Klein | Survivability beats DPS when you have no backup |
| Boss content | Klein | Bosses target the highest threat — make it the partner |

**Rule**: if your weapon is slow, pick Klein. If it is fast, pick Asuna. Speed mismatch wastes the partner's windows.

## Support Skill Priority

Partners learn Support Skills as they level. Not all are equal:

| Priority | Skill | Why |
|----------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Healing | Restores ~30% HP when you drop low — the best skill in the game |
| 2 | Buff (attack up) | Flat damage increase on every rotation |
| 3 | Debuff (defense down) | Feeds into the Combination gauge faster |
| 4 | Cleanse | Situational — only for status-heavy floors |
| 5 | Shield | Better than nothing, worse than heal |

**Minimum viable setup**: always keep at least one healing-capable partner equipped. If you switch partners for a fight, make sure the replacement has Healing.

## Best Partner by Situation

| Situation | Best Partner | Why |
|-----------|-------------|-----|

| Boss fights | Lisbeth | Party heal sustains long fights |
| Speed farming | Agil | Attack Up buff + AoE clears packs fast |
| Solo DPS play | Asuna | Highest partner DPS, healing for emergencies |

## Combination Skill Optimization

The Combination gauge fills when both you and your partner damage the same target. It persists between fights — build it on trash, spend it on the boss.

| Build Phase | Action | Reported Gauge Yield |
|-------------|--------|----------------------|
| Trash packs | You and partner focus the same mob | Fast — 15–25% per pack |
| Split focus | Different targets | Slow — 5–10% per pack |
| Boss fight | Full gauge unloaded | Large damage window |

**Optimization rules:**

- Never split targets in normal floors — full focus roughly doubles gauge build speed
- Save the full gauge for boss Phase 2 (most bosses enrage or summon adds then)
- The Combination Skill scales with your weapon's damage — upgrade your weapon before relying on it
- Dual-Wield signature skills + full gauge = one of the biggest burst windows in the game

The gauge also fills faster when you land consecutive hits without taking damage and when you trigger counter windows (parries). Once full, the gauge stays full until used — do not waste Combination Skills on normal enemies.

## Switch Mode Tactics

Switch Mode is activated from the command wheel. Your partner becomes the focus of enemy attention for roughly 15 seconds, with a cooldown of roughly 30 seconds.

### When to Switch
- **You need to heal** — Switch, pop healing items, switch back
- **You are low on SP** — Switch, let SP recover, switch back
- **Setting up a combo** — Switch, position behind the enemy, unleash your full combo
- **Partner is low HP** — Do NOT switch. Switch Mode on a dying partner gets them killed.

### Optimization Rules
- **Do it BEFORE your SP is empty** — the classic panic-switch wastes the window
- **Use it to set up finishers**: Switch → enemy faces partner → land your slowest, hardest skill in safety
- **Use it to heal**: switch, then drink potions / regen SP while the partner tanks
- Do not waste it on trash mobs — save it for elites and bosses

## Partner Progression

### How Partners Improve
Partners scale with your Cardinal Rank. At higher ranks:
- They learn new Support Skills
- Their stats increase
- Combination Skill damage increases
- New dialogue and story interactions unlock

### Partner-Specific Quests
Each partner has optional side quests that improve their abilities. Complete these the moment they appear — the upgrades are permanent and significant.

### Progression Checkpoints (community-reported)

| Cardinal Rank | Partner Milestone |
|---------------|-------------------|
| 1–10 | Unlock first Support Skill (pick Healing first) |
| 11–20 | Second Support Skill + new combos |
| 21–30 | Combination Skill rank up (gauge fills faster) |
| 31+ | Final Support Skill + signature combo |

**Leveling tips:**

- A partner that falls in combat leaves you fighting alone until the next checkpoint — keep them healed, not just you
- Partners gain experience for fighting, so a partner you never swap outpaces the curve — pick one per build and commit
- Keep your partner's level at or above your Cardinal Rank

## Quick Optimization Checklist

- [ ] One partner per build, committed (not rotated every floor)
- [ ] Healing equipped on every partner you bring into boss content
- [ ] Gauge built on trash, spent on boss Phase 2
- [ ] Switch Mode used proactively, before SP is empty
- [ ] Partner side quests done on sight
- [ ] Partner level ≥ your Cardinal Rank at all times

## Quick Tips

- **Healing Support Skill is the best** — always have at least one heal-equipped partner
- **Partners cannot die permanently** — but if they fall in combat, you fight alone until the next checkpoint
- **Switch Mode is not a panic button** — use it proactively, not reactively
- **Combination Skill gauge persists between fights** — build it on trash, spend it on the boss
- **Experiment with all partners** — their playstyles complement different weapon choices
