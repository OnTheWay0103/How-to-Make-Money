## Farming Efficiency Overview

Time is the most valuable resource in Echoes of Aincrad. This guide covers the fastest methods for everything you need to farm.

## Best XP Farming

| Method | XP/Hour | Requirements |
|--------|:--:|------|
| **Boss Rush Mode** | 50,000-80,000 | Level 30+, good gear |
| **Floor 3 Elite Route** | 30,000-50,000 | Level 20+ |
| **Dungeon Speed Runs** | 25,000-40,000 | Level 15+ |

### Optimal XP Route (Floor 3)
1. Start at Floor 3 entrance
2. Clear left wing elites (5 spawns)
3. Skip to boss room
4. Kill boss, reset floor
5. Repeat — ~8 minutes per loop

|-----------|--------|:--:|
| Legendary Weapons | Floor Bosses (Hard) | 5% |
| EX-Mods | Boss Rush (S-Rank) | 10% |
| Accessories | Dungeon Chests | 15% |

## Speed Farming Tips

1. **Use Movement Speed gear** — Every second matters in farming
2. **Skip cutscenes** — Enable auto-skip in settings
3. **One-shot build** — Max damage, ignore defense for farming
4. **Farm during events** — Double drop events multiply efficiency
5. **Know reset timers** — Bosses and nodes respawn on fixed schedules
