## Status Effects Overview

Status effects in Echoes of Aincrad can make or break a fight. Understanding how they work — and how to apply or counter them — is essential for mid-game and endgame content.

> **Note:** durations, tick rates, and percentages below are community-reported and may change with patches.

### Effect Categories

| Category | Examples | Duration | Stacking |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Damage Over Time (DoT) | Burn, Bleed, Poison | 10-30 seconds | Same type overwrites, different types stack |
| Crowd Control (CC) | Stun, Freeze, Root | 2-5 seconds | Most do not stack (immunity period after) |
| Stat Debuffs | Curse, Weaken, Slow | 15-60 seconds | Stack up to 3 times per stat |
| Buffs | Attack Up, Defense Up, Haste | 30-120 seconds | Same buff overwrites, different buffs stack |
| Unique | Mark, Soul Link, Taunt | Varies | Exclusive per effect type |

## Damage Over Time Effects

### Burn

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Damage per tick | 3% of max HP |
| Tick interval | 2 seconds |
| Total duration | 12 seconds (6 ticks) |
| Source | Fire weapons, fire EX-Mods, fire-based enemy attacks |
| Can crit? | No |

**Counters:**
- Antidote Salve (cures instantly)
- Water-element weapon swap (halves remaining duration)
- Rolling in water (removes instantly if water body is nearby)

**Best applications:**
- Bosses with high physical defense (bypasses armor)
- Enemies that regenerate HP (burn reduces regen)
- Elites and zone bosses — sustained chip damage between their attack windows

### Bleed

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Damage per tick | 5% of max HP |
| Tick interval | 3 seconds |
| Total duration | 15 seconds (5 ticks) |
| Source | Dagger weapon skills, critical hits, bleed EX-Mod |
| Can crit? | Yes (extends duration by 3 seconds) |

**Counters:**
- Bandage item (cures instantly, common drop)
- Defense buff (reduces tick damage by 50%)
- Resting at a safe zone (cures instantly)

**Best applications:**
- High-HP bosses (percentage-based damage)
- High-mobility enemies — apply bleed, then focus on dodging
- Dual-wield builds (stack multiple bleed applications fast)

### Poison

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Damage per tick | 2% of max HP + 50 flat |
| Tick interval | 2 seconds |
| Total duration | 20 seconds (10 ticks) |
| Source | Poison-infused weapons, certain enemy types, poison traps |
| Can crit? | No |

**Counters:**
- Antidote (cures instantly)
- Holy-element buff (prevents poison application for 60 seconds)
- High Vitality stat (reduces flat damage component)

**Best applications:**
- Long boss fights (20 seconds of chip damage adds up)
- Kiting strategies (poison and run)
- Farming packs (poison spreads chip damage across a crowd)

## Crowd Control Effects

### Stun

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Duration | 2-4 seconds (bosses: 1-2 seconds) |
| Source | Greatsword heavy attacks, shield bash, stun EX-Mod |
| Immunity period | 10 seconds after stun ends |
| Boss effectiveness | Reduced by 50% |

**Usage**: Interrupt boss charge attacks, set up Combination Skills, create breathing room.

### Freeze

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Duration | 3-5 seconds |
| Source | Ice weapons, ice magic, freeze EX-Mod |
| Breaks on | Any damage (first hit deals 50% bonus damage) |
| Boss effectiveness | 30% duration, no damage bonus |

**Usage**: Excellent for locking down one enemy while dealing with another. The bonus damage on break makes it valuable for burst builds.

### Root

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Duration | 5 seconds |
| Source | Nature weapons, certain enemy attacks, trap items |
| Can attack while rooted? | Yes (ranged only) |
| Boss effectiveness | Immune |

**Usage**: Lock down fast enemies while you reposition or line up a heavy attack. Useless against bosses.

## Stat Debuffs

| Debuff | Effect | Max Stacks | Duration | Common Source |
|--------|--------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Curse | -20% damage dealt per stack | 3 | 30s | Dark Fae enemies, curse EX-Mod |
| Weaken | -15% defense per stack | 3 | 30s | Heavy attacks, shield skills |
| Slow | -30% movement speed per stack | 2 | 15s | Ice damage, ranged attacks to legs |
| Silence | Cannot use skills | 1 | 5s | Certain enemy attacks (rare) |
| Blind | Reduced accuracy, reduced lock-on range | 1 | 10s | Smoke bombs, certain enemy abilities |

## Buffs

### Self-Buffs

| Buff | Effect | Duration | Source |
|------|--------|----------|--------|
| Attack Up | +20% damage | 30s | Battle Cry skill, certain accessories |
| Defense Up | +30% damage reduction | 30s | Guard Stance skill, certain items |
| Haste | +20% attack speed, +15% movement speed | 20s | Light-element buffs, certain EX-Mods |
| Regen | 2% HP per 3 seconds | 60s | Healing Support Skill, certain items |

### Partner Buffs

The game is single-player only — the only "party" is you and your AI partner, whose Support Skills apply to both of you.

| Buff | Effect | Duration | Source |
|------|--------|----------|--------|
| Attack Up (partner) | +15% damage for you and your partner | 20s | Partner Support Skill |
| Defense Up (partner) | +20% damage reduction | 20s | Partner Support Skill |
