## Why Play Greatsword in Echoes of Aincrad?

A Greatsword build in Echoes of Aincrad is the hardest-hitting Heavy Strength weapon setup. This guide covers the best Sword Skills, charge attack combos, stat allocation, and EX-Mods for dominating with massive damage and AoE farming strategies.

## Greatsword Core Mechanics & Charge Attack System

### Charge Attack

Hold the attack button to charge. Release at max charge for:
- +100% damage
- Guard break (cannot be blocked)
- Wide AoE (hits multiple enemies)

Charge attacks are slow. You are vulnerable while charging. Timing is everything.

### Cyclone

Spin-to-win AoE Sword Skill. Covers 360° around you. Your best farming tool.

### Avalanche

Heavy overhead slam. Highest single-hit damage in the game. Use against staggered or slow enemies.

### Dodge Roll

Unlike shield weapons, Greatsword users dodge-roll instead of blocking. This is less safe but more mobile — use it to reposition for charge attacks.

## Greatsword Stat Priority for Heavy Builds

| Stat | Investment | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Strength | 50% | Greatsword scaling — your damage |
| Vitality | 25% | You WILL take hits during charge animations |
| Endurance | 25% | Charge attacks cost heavy SP |

**Do not invest in Dexterity.** Greatsword scales with Strength and has low base crit rate.

## Greatsword Combat Flow for PvE Content

### Vs. Mobs (Farming)
Charge attack → walk into pack → release → Cyclone to clean up survivors. HP Recovery on Kill keeps you topped off.

### Vs. Bosses
1. Dodge their attacks, stay at mid-range
2. Charge attack during their recovery animations
3. Helm Breaker to close gaps after dodging
4. Avalanche when the boss is staggered
5. Repeat

### Vs. Fast Enemies (Goblins, Wolves)
Do NOT charge. Use light attacks instead. Charge only when they are staggered or recovering from an attack.

## Greatsword Farming Strategy for XP and Materials

Greatsword is one of the best farming weapons thanks to AoE — see the [Leveling Guide](/guides/leveling-guide) for optimal XP routes:

1. Find a mob-dense area (Floor 1 Forest goblin camp is ideal)
2. Run in circles to group enemies together
3. Charge attack into the pack
4. Cyclone to finish
5. Collect drops, move to next pack

**With HP Recovery on Kill EX-Mod**, you can farm indefinitely without healing items.

## Quick Tips

- **Patience > aggression** — one charged hit is worth five light attacks
- **Learn enemy recovery windows** — that is when you charge
- **Dodge roll has i-frames** — use them to roll through attacks, not away from them
- **Do not charge against fast enemies** — you will get hit every time
- **Weapon weight matters** — Greatsword is heavy. You cannot cancel animations. Commit to each swing.

## Common Greatsword Build Mistakes to Avoid

### Over-Committing to Charges

The #1 killer of Greatsword players is charging at the wrong time. A full charge leaves you locked in animation for nearly two seconds. If you start a charge while the enemy is already winding up an attack, you will eat the hit and likely get staggered out of your charge.

**Fix**: Only begin charging when the enemy has already committed to a recovery animation or is clearly out of range.

### Ignoring Vitality

New Greatsword players often go 100% Strength for the biggest numbers. This works on paper and fails in practice. You will trade hits — the weapon demands it. Without Vitality, one or two stray hits send you to the respawn point.

**Fix**: Stick to the 50/25/25 split (Str/Vit/End). Growth Points are widely reported as permanent — treat every point as final — and dying at the boss door is far more expensive.

### Spinning in Tight Spaces

Cyclone is amazing in the open, but its long animation locks you in place. Using it in narrow corridors or near ledges gets you hit by attacks you could have dodged, or worse — knocks you off an edge.

**Fix**: Use Earth Splitter in hallways. Save Cyclone for open areas with 3+ enemies surrounding you.

### Forgetting to Re-apply EX-Mods After Gear Swap

EX-Mods are slotted to gear, not your character. When you upgrade to a new Greatsword, your old EX-Mods stay on the old weapon. Players frequently arrive at a boss fight wondering why their HP recovery and charge damage bonuses aren't working.

**Fix**: Every time you equip a new weapon, open the EX-Mod menu and slot your mods before heading out.

## Greatsword Build Variants for Every Playstyle

### Full DPS Greatsword (Glass Cannon)

**Stats**: 70% Strength, 15% Vitality, 15% Endurance
**EX-Mods**: Quick Charge, Power Surge, Critical Edge, Blood Price
**Playstyle**: You kill everything in one or two hits — or you die trying. Dodge perfectly. Charge only during guaranteed openings. Pair with Light Armor for faster stamina regen. Best for players who know every boss moveset by heart.
**Risk**: High. One mistake and you are down.

### Tanky Greatsword (Heavy Bruiser)

**Stats**: 35% Strength, 40% Vitality, 25% Endurance
**EX-Mods**: Quick Charge, Power Surge, Life Steal, Fortress
**Playstyle**: You trade blows and win the war of attrition. Charge attacks still hit hard, but you can afford to eat a hit or two. Pair with Heavy Armor and a Vitality-boosting accessory. Slower but far more forgiving.
**Risk**: Low. You lose some one-shot potential but gain the ability to face-tank most non-boss enemies.

### Hybrid Support (Partner Buffer)

**Stats**: 40% Strength, 25% Vitality, 35% Endurance
**EX-Mods**: Power Surge, Vengeance, Fortress, Quick Charge
**Playstyle**: Built for partner-assisted play — you spam heavy attacks to stagger the boss while your AI partner deals damage. Your damage is secondary; your role is opening the window. Works fine on your own too, just slower.
**Risk**: Medium. Relies on your partner's positioning. Weaker in true solo play.

## FAQ

### Does Greatsword work for PvP?

There is no PvP in Echoes of Aincrad — the game is single-player only (see the [PvP Status page](/guides/pvp-duel-guide)). In normal combat, though, the Greatsword is high-risk, high-reward: fast enemies move unpredictably, making charged attacks harder to land. Use Helm Breaker to close distance and Earth Splitter to zone groups. Light attack pressure is more important than heavy charges against fast enemies.

### Should I use a shield with Greatsword?

Greatsword is a two-handed weapon class. You cannot equip a shield while wielding a Greatsword — the weapon takes both hands. Some new players mistake the "Heavy Weapon" tag for "Sword and Shield." If you want a blocking playstyle, check the [Shield Build](/guides/shield-build) guide instead.

### Why does my charge attack sometimes not stagger enemies?

Stagger buildup per hit depends on the enemy's stagger threshold. Tougher enemies (elites and bosses) have stagger resistance that reduces buildup. If your charge attack is not staggering, you need one or more of: a higher-rank stagger EX-Mod, the Vengeance EX-Mod, or a weapon with an innate stagger bonus (Titan's Edge and above have this). Against bosses, expect to land 3-4 charged hits before they stagger regardless of your gear.

### Is Greatsword good for solo leveling?

Greatsword is one of the best solo leveling weapons in the game. The AoE on charge attacks and Cyclone lets you clear mob packs faster than any single-target weapon. With HP Recovery on Kill, you effectively have infinite sustain. The only downside is boss fights take a bit longer since some bosses have few punish windows. Stick to the [Tanky Greatsword variant](#tanky-greatsword-heavy-bruiser) while leveling and you will rarely die.

### What are the best EX-Mods for a Greatsword build in Echoes of Aincrad?

The best EX-Mods for a Greatsword build are, in order: Quick Charge (faster skill charge), Power Surge (+skill damage), Life Steal (heal on hit), and Vengeance (+damage after blocking). The god roll set combines all four for maximum damage and sustain. Vengeance is valuable for progressing past mid-game floors, as shielded enemies will bounce your attacks without good timing. Life Steal enables extended farming sessions without healing items. Check the [EX-Mod Guide](/guides/ex-mod-guide) for detailed crafting information.
