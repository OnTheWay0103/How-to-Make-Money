## Farming Overview

Echoes of Aincrad requires significant grinding for Col (currency), experience, materials, and gear. This guide covers the **most efficient spots per resource type** so you spend less time farming and more time progressing.

> **Note on numbers:** Echoes of Aincrad does not publish official drop tables or spawn schedules. All rates, drop percentages, and timers below are community-reported estimates and vary with version, difficulty, and Cardinal Rank.

### Progression-Based Recommendations

| Floor Range | Focus | Best General Farm |
|-------------|-------|-------------------|
| Floor 1-2 | Col + Basic Materials | Forest dungeon loop / Boar Hunt |
| Floor 3-4 | Crafting Materials | Glimmerwood loop |
| Floor 5-6 | EXP + Rare Materials | Floor 6 elite patrol route |
| Floor 7+ | Endgame Gear | Boss Rush mode |

## Col Farming Routes

### Floor 1: Town of Beginnings Courier Loop

Best for: **Early game (Rank 1-5)**

| Step | Action | Col Earned (reported) | Time |
|------|--------|------------|------|
| 1 | Accept courier quests from the Merchant Guild | 0 | 1 min |
| 2 | Deliver to NPCs in town | ~1,500 | 5 min |
| 3 | Repeat | — | — |

**Efficiency**: ~18,000 Col/hour (reported)
**Requirement**: Cardinal Rank 3+

### Floor 2: Marunne Fields Boar Hunt

Best for: **Mid-early game (Rank 5-10)**

Kill Giant Boars in the North Valley central field. Each drops (reported):
- 200-400 Col
- Tough Hide (sells for ~50 Col each)
- Boar Meat (sells for ~30 Col each)

**Route**: Spawn at Marunne, run north to central field, kill 5-6 boars, return to town, repeat.

**Efficiency**: ~25,000 Col/hour plus materials
**Pro tip**: Equip a Col-boosting accessory if you have one.

### Floor 5: Col Mining Route

Best for: **Mid-game (Rank 15+)**

The Floor 5 mines have Orichalcum Ore deposits that reportedly respawn on a fixed schedule (roughly every 8 minutes). Each ore stack sells for 500-800 Col to the merchant. Combined with enemy drops:

| Run | Col from Ore | Col from Enemies | Total (reported) |
|-----|-------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Per run (15 min) | 3,000-4,800 | 2,000-3,000 | 5,000-7,800 |
| Per hour | 12,000-19,200 | 8,000-12,000 | 20,000-31,200 |

## EXP Farming Spots

### Floor 1: Forest Dungeon (Rank 1-5)

| Enemy | EXP per kill (reported) | Spawns per loop |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Frenzy Boar | ~120 | 8 |
| Forest Wolf | ~150 | 6 |
| Violet Fencer (boss) | ~2,000 | 1 |

**Best loop**: Clear the Forest Dungeon from entrance to Violet Fencer, reset, repeat.
**EXP/hour**: ~15,000 (reported)
**Bonus**: Col per run from drops.

### Floor 3: Crystal Caverns (Rank 8-14)

The Crystal Caverns offer dense enemy spawns in a compact area.

| Enemy | EXP per kill (reported) | Spawns per loop |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Dark Fae Grunt | ~350 | 12 |
| Crystal Scorpion | ~450 | 8 |
| Crystal Guardian (mini-boss) | ~5,000 | 1 |

**Best loop**: Enter the Caverns from the Glimmerwood sinkhole, clear the first two chambers (12 grunts, 4 scorpions), exit and reset. Skip the Guardian unless you need Soul Crystals.
**EXP/hour**: ~45,000 (reported, ignoring Guardian), ~55,000 (with Guardian)

### Floor 3: Elite Route (Rank 10+)

1. Start at the Floor 3 entrance
2. Clear the left-wing elite spawns
3. Skip to the boss room
4. Kill the boss, reset the floor
5. Repeat — roughly 8 minutes per loop

**EXP/hour**: ~30,000-50,000 (reported)

### Floor 6: Elite Patrol Route (Rank 15-20)

Best EXP farm in the mid-game. A patrol of elite knights walks a circuit around the Floating City outer ring.

| Elite Type | EXP per kill (reported) | Respawn |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| City Knight | ~1,200 | ~5 minutes |
| Knight Captain | ~3,000 | ~15 minutes |
| Patrol Guardian | ~8,000 | ~30 minutes |

**Route**: Follow the outer ring clockwise. You will encounter all 8 knights and 2 captains per full circuit.

**EXP/hour**: ~80,000-100,000 depending on speed (reported)

## Speed Farming Tips

1. **Use Movement Speed gear** — every second matters in farming
2. **Skip cutscenes** — enable auto-skip in settings if available
3. **One-shot build** — maximize damage and ignore defense for farming sessions (see the [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide))
4. **Farm during events** — if the game runs special drop-rate events, farm then
5. **Know reset timers** — bosses and nodes respawn on fixed schedules; learn the route order that lines them up
