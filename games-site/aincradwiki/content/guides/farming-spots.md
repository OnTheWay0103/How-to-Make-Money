## Farming Overview

Echoes of Aincrad requires significant grinding for Col (currency), experience, materials, and gear. This guide covers the **most efficient spots per resource type** so you spend less time farming and more time progressing.

### Progression-Based Recommendations

| Floor Range | Focus | Best General Farm |
|-------------|-------|-------------------|
| Floor 1-2 | Col + Basic Materials | Violet Fencer repeat |
| Floor 3-4 | Crafting Materials | Glimmerwood loop |
| Floor 5-6 | EXP + Rare Materials | Floor 6 elite patrol route |
| Floor 7+ | Endgame Gear | Boss rush mode |

## Col Farming Routes

### Floor 1: Town of Beginnings Merchant Loop

Best for: **Early game (Rank 1-5)**

| Step | Action | Col Earned | Time |
|------|--------|------------|------|
| 1 | Accept all 3 courier quests from Merchant Guild | 0 | 1 min |
| 2 | Deliver to 3 NPCs in town | 1,500 | 5 min |
| 3 | Repeat | — | — |

**Efficiency**: ~18,000 Col/hour
**Requirement**: Cardinal Rank 3+

### Floor 2: Marunne Fields Boar Hunt

Best for: **Mid-early game (Rank 5-10)**

Kill Giant Boars in the North Valley central field. Each drops:
- 200-400 Col
- Tough Hide (sells for 50 Col each)
- Boar Meat (sells for 30 Col each)

**Route**: Spawn at Marunne, run north to central field, kill 5-6 boars, return to town, repeat.

**Efficiency**: ~25,000 Col/hour plus materials
**Pro tip**: Equip a Col-boosting accessory if you have one.

### Floor 5: Col Mining Route

Best for: **Mid-game (Rank 15+)**

The Floor 5 mines have Orichalcum Ore deposits that respawn every 8 minutes. Each ore stack sells for 500-800 Col to the merchant. Combined with enemy drops:

| Run | Col from Ore | Col from Enemies | Total |
|-----|-------------|------------------|-------|
| Per run (15 min) | 3,000-4,800 | 2,000-3,000 | 5,000-7,800 |
| Per hour | 12,000-19,200 | 8,000-12,000 | 20,000-31,200 |

## EXP Farming Spots

### Floor 1: Forest Dungeon (Rank 1-5)

| Enemy | EXP per kill | Spawns per loop |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Frenzy Boar | 120 | 8 |
| Forest Wolf | 150 | 6 |
| Violet Fencer (boss) | 2,000 | 1 |

**Best loop**: Clear the Forest Dungeon from entrance to Violet Fencer, reset, repeat.
**EXP/hour**: ~15,000
**Bonus**: 2,000+ Col per run from drops.

### Floor 3: Crystal Caverns (Rank 8-14)

The Crystal Caverns offer dense enemy spawns in a compact area.

| Enemy | EXP per kill | Spawns per loop |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Dark Fae Grunt | 350 | 12 |
| Crystal Scorpion | 450 | 8 |
| Crystal Guardian (mini-boss) | 5,000 | 1 |

**Best loop**: Enter Caverns from Glimmerwood sinkhole, clear first two chambers (12 grunts, 4 scorpions), exit and reset. Skip the Guardian unless you need Soul Crystals.
**EXP/hour**: ~45,000 (ignoring Guardian), ~55,000 (with Guardian)

### Floor 6: Elite Patrol Route (Rank 15-20)

Best EXP farm in the mid-game. A patrol of 8 elite knights walks a circuit around the Floating City outer ring.

| Elite Type | EXP per kill | Respawn |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| City Knight | 1,200 | 5 minutes |
| Knight Captain | 3,000 | 15 minutes |
| Patrol Guardian | 8,000 | 30 minutes |

**Route**: Follow the outer ring clockwise. You will encounter all 8 knights and 2 captains per full circuit.

**EXP/hour**: ~80,000-100,000 depending on speed

## Material Farming

### Common Materials

| Material | Best Spot | Per Hour | Competing Farmers? |
|----------|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| Wood | Floor 1 Forest | 200-300 | Yes (low traffic) |
| Stone | Floor 1 Quarry (south) | 150-200 | Yes |
| Iron Ore | Floor 2 North Valley cliffs | 80-120 | Moderate |
| Leather | Floor 2 Boar field | 40-60 | High (popular) |

### Rare Materials

| Material | Best Spot | Per Hour | Drop Rate |
|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Fae Dust | Floor 3 Glimmerwood (night) | 15-25 | 40% from Fae Tricksters |
| Soul Crystal | Floor 3 Crystal Guardian | 1-2 | 100% drop, 8 min respawn |
| Orichalcum Ore | Floor 5 Mines (deep section) | 8-12 | Fixed spawn points, 8 min respawn |
| Legendary Essence | Floor 6 Elite Knights | 3-5 | 25% drop per knight |
| Moonlight Crystal | Floor 4 Night-only node | 1-2 | Rare spawn, 30 min respawn |

### Gathering Route: Full Loops

**Floor 1-3 Material Loop (1 hour):**
1. Floor 1 Forest: 200 Wood, 100 Stone (10 min)
2. Floor 2 North Valley: 80 Iron Ore, Tough Hides (20 min)
3. Floor 3 Glimmerwood (set to night): 20 Fae Dust, Luminescent Wood (30 min)

**Floor 4-6 Material Loop (1.5 hours):**
1. Floor 4 Moonlight Crystal node (5 min)
2. Floor 5 Deep Mines: 10 Orichalcum Ore (40 min)
3. Floor 6 Elite Patrol: 5 Legendary Essence (45 min)
