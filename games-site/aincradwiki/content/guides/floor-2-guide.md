## Floor 2 Overview

Floor 2 of Aincrad, known as the Twin Valleys, is connected to Floor 1 by a spiral staircase in the Town of Beginnings northwest tower. The theme is **rolling green hills, scattered ruins, and two parallel valleys** split by a central ridge.

### Entry Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| Cardinal Rank | 4 |
| Recommended Vitality | 6+ |
| Recommended Damage Stat | 6+ |
| Weapon Tier | Uncommon with 2+ EX-Mods |
| Healing Items | 8+ minimum |

### Map Layout

Floor 2 is divided into three distinct zones:

| Zone | Danger Level | Key Features |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Marunne Fields (West) | Low | Starting town, merchants, quest givers |
| Marunne Fields (East) | Low-Medium | Open fields, scattered ruins, resource nodes |
| The Split Gate | Very High | Final dungeon, Twin Wardens boss room |

---

## Key Locations

### Marunne (Safe Zone)

The only safe town on Floor 2. It sits at the western edge of the floor and offers:

- **Weapon Smith** — upgrades Uncommon weapons to Rare tier
- **Item Merchant** — sells antidotes and burn salves (critical for this floor)
- **Inn** — rest and save point
- **Quest Board** — 6 side quests available

### Twin Valleys

Two valleys run eastward from Marunne. Each leads to the Split Gate from a different direction:

**North Valley:**
- More physical enemies (hobgoblins, giant boars)
- Shorter path but tighter spaces
- Contains the Blacksmith's Cache side dungeon

**South Valley:**
- More magical enemies (wisp mages, shadow foxes)
- Longer path with more resource nodes
- Contains the Moonlit Shrine side dungeon

### The Split Gate

The final dungeon of Floor 2. A massive gate structure built into the central ridge. Two entrances (north and south) converge at the boss room.

## Weapon and Gear Progression

By the time you complete Floor 2, your gear should look like this:

| Slot | Recommended | Alternative |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Weapon | Rare (3 EX-Mods) | Uncommon+ (2 EX-Mods with damage focus) |
| Head Armor | Uncommon+ | Common with Vitality boost |
| Body Armor | Uncommon+ | Common with Endurance boost |
| Accessory 1 | Moonlit Amulet (quest reward) | Any Vit/Damage ring |
| Accessory 2 | Guardian Core (boss drop) | Any resist accessory |

**Pro tip**: Upgrade your weapon to Rare at the Marunne Smith before attempting the Twin Wardens. The damage difference is substantial.

## Boss: Twin Wardens Strategy

See the [full boss guide](/guides/bosses-guide) for in-depth strategy, but here is the floor-specific summary:

1. **Kill the Warden of Blades first** — bleed stacks will drain your healing
2. Use **Switch Mode** to split aggro between you and your partner
3. Save **Combination Skills** for the enrage phase (both wardens below 30%)
4. **Dodge, do not block** the Warden of Flames' projectiles

### Recommended Builds

| Playstyle | Weapon | Partner Role |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Tank | Sword & Shield | Healer with Support Skills |
| Aggressive | Rapier | Decoy with Switch Mode |
| Balanced | Two-Handed Axe | Damage dealer with attack buffs |
