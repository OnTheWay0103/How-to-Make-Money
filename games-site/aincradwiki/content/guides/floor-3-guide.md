## Floor 3 Overview

Floor 3 of Aincrad, the Fae Wilds, is accessible from the Split Gate on Floor 2. The theme shifts from rolling hills to an **enchanted forest with giant luminescent trees, glowing flora, and a deep crimson canyon** cutting through the center.

### Entry Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum | Recommended |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Cardinal Rank | 7 | 9 |
| Vitality | 8 | 12 |
| Damage Stat | 8 | 12 |
| Weapon | Rare (3 EX-Mods) | Rare+ (4 EX-Mods) |
| Healing Items | 12 | 20 |

### Floor Theme and Atmosphere

The Fae Wilds are beautiful but deadly. Day/night cycles affect enemy behavior:

- **Day** — Fae enemies are passive-aggressive (attack only if provoked)
- **Night** — All enemies become aggressive. Night-only enemies spawn.
- **Twilight** — Transition period. Highest resource spawn rates.

## Zone Layout

Floor 3 has four major zones:

| Zone | Danger Level | Day/Night | Key Feature |
|------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Luminara Fields | Low | Safe at day | Starting area, safe zone |
| Glimmerwood | Medium | Dangerous at night | Dense forest, crafting materials |
| Crystal Caverns | High | Always dark | Subterranean area, rare ores |
| Crimson Canyon | Very High | Always hostile | Final dungeon, boss encounter |

### Luminara Fields (Safe Zone)

The only safe settlement on Floor 3. Features:

- **Fae Enclave** — new faction with unique quests
- **Luminara Forge** — upgrade Rare weapons to Rare+ tier
- **Alchemist** — craft status-effect remedies and buff potions
- **Teleport Crystal** — fast travel back to Floor 1 and 2

### Glimmerwood

A dense enchanted forest covering the eastern half of the floor.

**Resources:**
- Luminescent Wood — used for Rare+ weapon crafting
- Fae Dust — enchanting material for EX-Mods
- Glimmerberries — crafting material for light-based consumables

**Enemies:**

| Enemy | Behavior | Weakness | Drops |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| Fae Trickster | Ambush from trees | Fire | Fae Dust, Trickster Coin |
| Luminescent Wolf | Packs at night | Dark | Glowing Fang, Wolf Pelt |
| Woodland Sprite | Flees from combat | None (catch) | Sprite Essence (quest item) |
| Thorn Beast | Patrols clearings | Slash damage | Tough Thorn, Beast Hide |

### Crystal Caverns

An underground network accessible from a sinkhole in northern Glimmerwood.

**Features:**
- Crystal deposits — yield Crystal Shards and rare Soul Crystals
- Dark Fae spawns — stronger enemies with magic attacks
- Hidden passages — shortcuts to Crimson Canyon
- **Mini-boss**: Crystal Guardian (drops Soul Crystal, required for Legendary weapon crafting)

### Crimson Canyon

The final zone. A deep gorge filled with hostile fae and corrupted crystals. Leads to the Floor 3 boss room.

**Warning**: Once you enter the canyon, there are no save points until the boss is defeated or you die. Come prepared.

## Preparation for the Boss

The Crimson Canyon boss is a **Corrupted Fae Lord** with two phases:

| Phase | HP Range | Abilities | Strategy |
|-------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Phase 1 | 100%-50% | Magic barrages, teleport, curse debuff | Stay mobile, use light resistance potions |
| Phase 2 | 50%-0% | AoE curse explosions, summon dark fae adds | Kill adds immediately, save Combination Skill for stun window |

**Recommended consumables:**
- 5x Light Resistance Potion (Glimmerberries + Nectar)
- 10x Standard Healing Items
- 3x Curse Remedy (available from Alchemist in Luminara)
