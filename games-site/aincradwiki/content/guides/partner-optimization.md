# Partner Optimization — Max Synergy, Skills, and Combat Performance

The partner system hands you a second fighter — but a partner played passively is a damage loss, not a damage gain. Optimization means matching the partner to your weapon, managing their Support Skills, building the Combination gauge deliberately, and leveling them on the right schedule. This guide goes beyond the basics of the partner guide and gets into the numbers.

## Partner Selection by Weapon

| Your Weapon | Best Partner | Why |
|-------------|--------------|-----|
| Rapier / Daggers (burst DPS) | Asuna | Healing covers your aggression; her rapier combos chain with your staggers |
| Greatsword / Battle Axe (slow heavy) | Klein | His aggro windows let you land slow finishers safely |
| Sword & Shield (tanky) | Asuna | You hold the line; she provides the missing damage |
| Any solo content | Klein | Survivability beats DPS when you have no backup |
| Boss content | Klein | Bosses target the highest threat — make it the partner |

**Rule**: if your weapon is slow, pick Klein. If it is fast, pick Asuna. Speed mismatch wastes the partner's windows.

## Support Skill Priority

Partners learn Support Skills as they level. Not all are equal:

| Priority | Skill | Why |
|----------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Healing | Restores ~30% HP when you drop below 50% — the best skill in the game |
| 2 | Buff (attack up) | Flat damage increase on every rotation |
| 3 | Debuff (defense down) | Feeds into Combination gauge faster |
| 4 | Cleanse | Situational — only for status-heavy floors |
| 5 | Shield | Better than nothing, worse than heal |

**Minimum viable setup**: always keep at least one healing-capable partner equipped. If you switch partners for a fight, make sure the replacement has Healing.

## Combination Skill Optimization

The Combination gauge fills when both you and your partner damage the same target. It persists between fights — build it on trash, spend it on the boss.

| Build Phase | Action | Gauge Yield |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| Trash packs | You and partner focus the same mob | Fast — 15–25% per pack |
| Split focus | Different targets | Slow — 5–10% per pack |
| Boss fight | Full gauge unloaded | 2,000+ damage window |

**Optimization rules:**

- Never split targets in normal floors — full focus doubles gauge build speed
- Save the full gauge for boss Phase 2 (most bosses enrage or summon adds then)
- The Combination Skill is affected by your weapon's damage — upgrade your weapon before relying on it
- Dual Wield signature skills + full gauge = the single biggest burst window in the game

## Switch Mode Tactics

Switch Mode redirects enemy attention to your partner for 15 seconds. Reused correctly it is the best defensive tool you have:

- **Do it BEFORE your SP is empty** — the classic panic-switch wastes the window
- **Do NOT switch when the partner is low HP** — a dying partner under focus dies; the 15 seconds become 3
- **Use it to set up finishers**: Switch → enemy faces partner → land your slowest, hardest skill in safety
- **Use it to heal**: switch, then drink pots / regen SP while the partner tanks

## Partner Progression Schedule

Partners level from quest XP and combat participation. Keep them at or above your Cardinal Rank.

| Cardinal Rank | Partner Milestone | Checkpoint |
|---------------|-------------------|------------|
| 1–10 | Unlock first Support Skill | Level 5: pick Healing first |
| 11–20 | Second Support Skill + new combos | Level 12: add Buff |
| 21–30 | Combination Skill rank up | Level 20: gauge fills 10% faster |
| 31+ | Final Support Skill + signature combo | Level 30: pick by your build |

**Leveling tips:**

- Complete every partner side quest the moment it appears — the upgrades are permanent and significant
- A partner that falls in combat fights alone with you until the next checkpoint — keep them healed, not just you
- Partners gain XP for fighting, so a partner you never swap is a partner that outpaces the curve — pick one per build and commit

## Quick Optimization Checklist

- [ ] One partner per build, committed (not rotated every floor)
- [ ] Healing equipped on every partner you bring into boss content
- [ ] Gauge built on trash, spent on boss Phase 2
- [ ] Switch Mode used proactively, before SP is empty
- [ ] Partner side quests done on sight
- [ ] Partner level ≥ your Cardinal Rank at all times
