The Trial of the Forest Spirit, usually called Leshy's trial, is the questline that unlocks **free wood cutting**. Start in the town of **Barzlin**, talk to the locals to reveal map markers, complete **any three favours** for the forest spirit, then finish at the **Big Larch** by collecting pinecones and throwing them at stumps. After that, trees are fair game.

## What the trial actually gates

Before this quest, wood is not a resource you can farm freely. Rutab's walkthrough is direct about the payoff: after the Big Larch stage, "wood can be gathered freely". That makes the trial the single most important early progression gate for anyone who wants to build.

It also sits at the entrance to the second map. Critical Hits' appearance guide places the magic mirror after "the Trial of the Forest Spirit" in the second area, and 2UpSkill notes that crossing into a new region requires prepared food for Baba Jaga, Reka and the cottage. In other words, this is not an optional side quest you can postpone forever; it is the story's first real gate.

## Before you start

Three preparation points save a wasted trip:

- **Talk to locals first.** Upcomer says the deer icon and other quest locations are revealed by questioning people in Barzlin. Walking out without doing that leaves you with no markers.
- **Bring berries.** The first favour is feeding deer, and berries come from bushes across the map.
- **Expect procedural placement.** The number of hunter stands, scarecrows and cages varies by seed. One guide found three stands; another found four scarecrows. Treat every count below as typical, not fixed.

## The three required favours

You only need **three** favours, and Upcomer says they can be done in any order. The sources describe a slightly different third task, so both documented versions are listed.

| Favour | What to do | How it completes |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed the deer** | Find the deer icon, approach a deer, press **E** to give it a berry | Feeding several deer triggers Leshy's first appearance |
| **Help the Luthi** | Talk to the large Luthi elder in their marked area; it asks you to destroy the hunters' stands or lookout post | Throw your crows at the stands until they break; return to the elder |
| **Break a trap** (Rutab version) | Go to the trap marker learned from a villager, aim at the trap and interact | The trap breaks |
| **Free trapped animals** (Upcomer version) | Find cages while exploring, break them with crows | The animal is released; you may pick it up and bring it home |

Both third-favour versions count toward the same total, so if one marker is missing on your seed, look for the other. The requirement is three completed favours, not three specific scripted tasks.

### Feed the deer, step by step

1. Ask locals in Barzlin about the area.
2. Open the map and find the deer icon.
3. Gather berries from bushes on the way.
4. Approach a deer and press **E**.
5. Repeat until Leshy appears and the favour completes.

The deer do not flee, which makes this the safest of the three. It is also the moment the forest spirit enters the story, so do not skip the dialogue.

### Help the Luthi, step by step

1. Go to the Luthi area, marked from the start.
2. Talk to the main Luthi elder.
3. Find the hunters' stands or the lookout post it complains about.
4. Throw crows at it until it collapses.
5. Return to the elder for the reward.

The stands are placed procedurally. If you cannot find the last one, circle the area rather than assuming the quest is bugged.

### Break the trap or free the animal

Rutab's version sends you to a trap marker and asks you to aim and interact until it breaks. Upcomer's version has you find cages in the open world and break them with crows, after which you can carry the animal home.

Either way, the tool is the same: **crows**. This is also the mechanic you will use throughout the game to interact with objects at range.

## Optional favours worth doing

Extra favours are not required for the trial, but they pay in useful unlocks.

| Optional favour | Requirement | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Feed the graveyard keeper's birds | **1 fruity porridge + 3 normal porridges**, placed in bowls outside the home | Immediate reward from the keeper |
| Destroy scarecrows | Find and destroy the scarecrows near Barzlin (one guide found four) | Unlocks **garlic**, then a reward from the keeper |
| Lutki beehive follow-up | Let the Luthi keep the stolen beehive, then leave honey porridge by the tree that split the rock | A new map location and a reward |

The porridge order is a pure grain sink: two grain and one fruit for the fruity porridge, three grain for each normal one. If you have been foraging grain, this is a good use for it. Our [cooking recipes page](/guides/reka-cooking-recipes) has the exact combinations.

## The Big Larch finale

Once three favours are done, Leshy invites you to the sacred place and adds a map marker. This is the **Big Larch**, the fog-covered tree. Visiting it earlier just leaves you lost in the fog.

The sequence, cross-checked between Upcomer and Rutab:

1. **Talk to Leshy** beside the tree to begin the trial.
2. **Send crows into the tree** and harvest the cones.
3. **Place the cones in the tree's centre**, in its blood-like sap.
4. **Throw cones at the surrounding stumps.** Rutab gives the count: up to **20 pinecones** collected, and after about **10 throws** new trees grow.
5. **Leshy calls you back.** The forest is satisfied and the area is complete.
6. **Return to Baba Yaga** to close out the story beat.

Do not leave the area mid-finale if you can avoid it. The quest state depends on objects around the tree, and the sources do not describe a recovery path beyond reloading.

## After the trial: free wood and a moral choice

Completing the larch stage grants free wood gathering. It also triggers Leshy's final choice: **rescue the three loggers** lost in the fog, or leave them trapped.

| Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Rescue all three | Find them in the area; later meet them by the merchant's cart in town |
| Leave them | No rescue reward; the trial still completes |

Rutab lists the rescue rewards as a **headwear item** and a **large tapestry** for the house, handed over by the loggers at the merchant's cart. The tapestry needs considerable wall space, so clear a wall before you hang it.

## Common blockers

**Leshy is not appearing after three favours.**
Upcomer's fix is blunt: save the game, exit and reload. He should then be beside the tree.

**I cannot find the last hunter stand.**
The stands are procedurally placed. Sweep the area in a wider circle instead of retrying the same spot.

**The pinecones will not place.**
Check that you are interacting with the tree's sap area, not the ground. Upcomer describes placing the cones in the tree, then scattering them at stumps as a separate step.

**The fog will not let me leave.**
Finish the larch sequence and speak to Leshy. Rutab and Upcomer both describe the area as a scripted finale rather than an open exit.

**Can I do the favours in any order?**
Yes. Upcomer states that only three are required and the order is free.

## What this quest unlocks

| Unlock | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Free wood gathering | The main building material becomes farmable |
| Access to the next region | The larch finale closes the forest area |
| Garlic (via optional scarecrows) | Ingredient for garlic bread and garlic soup |
| Headwear and tapestry (via rescue) | Cosmetic and decoration rewards |
| The magic mirror becomes findable | Critical Hits places it after this trial |

That last row matters for appearance changes. If you have been waiting to fix your witch's look, the mirror hunt starts after this quest. Our [appearance guide](/guides/reka-character-appearance) covers where to find it.

## FAQ

**Is the Leshy trial required?**
It gates free wood and the next region, so yes in practice. The sources describe it as the second area's main questline.

**How many favours do I need?**
Three. Extra favours are optional.

**What is the reward for the trial?**
Free wood gathering, story progression, and the choice to rescue three loggers for a headwear item and a tapestry.

**Do I lose anything by leaving the loggers?**
No item reward is documented for leaving them. The quest still completes.

**Can I come back later?**
The forest area is scripted around the finale; the sources do not describe returning after crossing the fog. Finish optional favours first.
