You change appearance in REKA with a **mirror**, and there are two. A large mirror in the misty transition area right after character creation is your last free change; a **magic mirror** from an abandoned building after the Trial of the Forest Spirit can be taken home and placed with the build menu. Settings has no appearance option.

## The mirror you should not walk past

After you finish the character creator, the game puts you in a foggy transition area. 2UpSkill's beginner guide is explicit: the **large mirror** there is the last chance to change your witch's look before it locks in.

This is not a cosmetic tutorial you can revisit. Walk too far into the mist and the appearance is committed until you find the craftable mirror later. The detour to find that mirror is a full questline away, so spend a minute at the starting mirror even if you are eager to start.

What the starting creator exposes is not documented on the store page. The safe statement is that it covers the witch's appearance and outfit; any specific claim about changing name or gender later is `[Unconfirmed]` on this site.

## Where the magic mirror is

The second mirror is the permanent solution. Critical Hits describes its location and unlock conditions:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Story progress | After you settle into your house and complete the **Trial of the Forest Spirit** in the second area |
| Location type | An **abandoned building** |
| Map marker | The map marks it, but maps are procedurally generated, so the exact spot differs per save |
| Access | The mirrors inside are unlit until you light the candles |

Because the world is procedurally generated, do not expect a fixed coordinate. The reliable instruction is "check abandoned buildings in the second area after the trial". The unreliable version, "it is southwest of the N", describes one player's seed.

## Light the candles, then take the mirror

The mirror is not simply sitting there waiting to be picked up. The room contains several mirrors, and they are unlit.

1. Enter the abandoned building.
2. Find the **candles** around the room.
3. **Interact with every candle** to light it.
4. Once the room is lit, interact with the **magic mirror**.
5. Choose to take it.
6. Check the **back room or the area behind the building** for a chest with clothes.

Critical Hits mentions the clothes chest as part of the same visit. Do not leave without opening it; outfits are one of the slower resources to accumulate.

If a candle will not light, check that you are interacting with the candle itself rather than the mirror. The order is candles first, mirror second.

## Place the mirror at home

The mirror is furniture, not an inventory toggle. To use it permanently:

1. Open the **build menu** in your cottage.
2. Choose a spot with **substantial free space**; the mirror comes with a carpet and occupies a large footprint.
3. Place it.
4. **Interact with the mirror** whenever you want to change your appearance.

The space requirement is the part players underestimate. Critical Hits specifically warns that the mirror takes up a lot because of its attached carpet. Clear a wall or a corner before placing it, or you will be rearranging furniture to make room.

Once placed, the mirror is reusable. There is no documented cost or cooldown for changing appearance.

## Where outfits come from

Clothes are not purchased from a single vendor in the sources we checked. They come from exploration and quests:

| Source | Example |
|---|---|
| The mirror building | A chest behind or near the building |
| Abandoned structures | Chests and containers in ruins |
| NPCs and quests | Rewards for helping villagers |
| General exploration | Loot found while travelling |

2UpSkill's beginner guide supports the exploration loop: ruined houses, broken wagons and graveyards are the places to check for furniture, clothes and coins. The same habit that furnishes your house also dresses your witch.

## What you can and cannot change

The sources support a clear core and leave the edges uncertain.

**Supported by sources:**

- Appearance can be changed at the starting mirror before it locks.
- The magic mirror allows changes after it is placed.
- Outfits are separate items obtained through play.
- The mirror is placed and used inside your home.

**Not supported, therefore `[Unconfirmed]`:**

- Changing the witch's **name** after creation.
- Changing **gender** after creation.
- A full list of sliders or preset categories.
- Any "appearance change cost" in coins or resources.

This site would rather leave those blank than invent a menu. If a future 1.0-era source documents them, the page will be updated with the source and date.

## If you already walked past the starting mirror

You are not locked out forever; you are locked out until the second area. The recovery path is:

1. Progress the story until you settle the house.
2. Complete the Trial of the Forest Spirit. Our [Leshy walkthrough](/guides/reka-leshy-trial) covers the three favours and the Big Larch finale.
3. Search abandoned buildings in the second area.
4. Light the candles, take the mirror, place it at home.

That is a long detour for a cosmetic change, which is why the starting mirror is worth the extra minute.

## Appearance and the cottage

The mirror is also a decoration object, and it is placed through the same build menu as other furniture. That makes it worth placing deliberately rather than dropping it in the first free corner. Whether placed furniture travels with the cottage on every relocation is not stated in the sources we checked, so treat that specific detail as `[Unconfirmed]`; the house itself does move.

If you are about to cross a fog gate into a new region, finish your appearance changes first. The crossing is one-way, and the sources do not describe returning to the previous map. Our [cottage guide](/guides/reka-cottage-moving) explains the travel limits.

## FAQ

**How do I change clothes in REKA?**
Use a mirror. The starting mirror works once; after that, find the magic mirror in the second area, place it at home and interact with it.

**Where is the magic mirror?**
In an abandoned building in the second area, available after the Trial of the Forest Spirit. The exact location is procedural.

**Why can't I take the mirror?**
The candles are not all lit. Interact with every candle in the room first.

**Can I change my name or gender later?**
Not documented in the sources we checked. Treat it as `[Unconfirmed]` and decide before leaving the starting mirror.

**Does changing appearance cost anything?**
No cost is documented.

**Where do I get more outfits?**
Chests in ruins, the chest near the mirror building, NPC quests and general exploration.

**Does the mirror move with the cottage?**
The sources do not state this directly, so it is `[Unconfirmed]`. The cottage itself relocates inside a region; placed furniture is normally part of the house, but that detail is untested here.
