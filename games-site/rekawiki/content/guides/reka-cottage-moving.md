Your cottage in REKA does two different things that players often merge into one: it can **walk to a new spot inside the current region**, and you can **recall yourself back to it** with a talisman. Neither is a free teleport network. Both are limited to the map you are on, and crossing into a new region is a one-way trip by design.

## The two movement systems

| Action | What it does | How you trigger it | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Relocate / summon the cottage** | Moves the house itself to a new position | Build Mode → cottage move/summon option → place the preview → confirm | Current region only |
| **Recall** | Returns the witch to the cottage (or Baba Jaga's hut early on) | `Ctrl` utility wheel → `Mortar and Pestle` → close wheel → hold interact | Current region only |
| **Region travel** | Crosses the fog into the next map | Travel with the cottage and meet the journey requirements | One way; the old map unloads |

The distinction matters because relocation is a building tool and recall is a convenience tool. If you want to bring the house to a new clearing, you relocate it. If you are stranded across the map with a full inventory, you recall.

## Unlock the recall talisman first

Fast travel is not available from the start. 2UpSkill's guide lists the opening sequence in **Kopnik**: advance the early story and complete the opening tasks, including the recipe books, the merchant's wagon wheel and the **Trial of the Chicken**. Then recover the talisman stolen by the **Ludki**.

That item, the **Witch's Talisman**, is what enables return-to-home travel. Some guides spell the creature name Luthi or Lutki; the sources disagree on transliteration, but they describe the same early theft quest. If you have not done that quest, the recall option will not work and no amount of menu searching will fix it.

### Recall controls, step by step

1. Hold `Ctrl` to open the utility wheel.
2. Select **Mortar and Pestle**.
3. **Fully close the wheel** and return to normal third-person play.
4. **Hold the main interaction input** for several seconds.
5. Wait for the animation; you arrive back at your base.

The awkward part is step 3. If the wheel is still open, the hold does nothing. 2UpSkill is explicit that you must exit the wheel before holding interact.

Early in the game the recall destination is **Baba Jaga's hut**. Later it becomes your own cottage. No cost is documented for either use.

## Relocate the cottage, step by step

1. Enter **Build Mode** using its control.
2. Choose the **cottage move/summon** option.
3. A **ghostly outline** of the house appears at the target position.
4. **Rotate and drag** the outline until it sits where you want.
5. Watch the indicator: **green means valid**.
6. Confirm the placement.
7. The cottage walks or materialises into the new spot.

The preview is the whole mechanic. You are not guessing at clearance; the game tells you whether the position works before you commit. The skill is in reading terrain quickly and moving on when a spot refuses to turn green.

## Placement rules that actually matter

| Rule | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Needs level, open ground | Slopes and awkward angles block the footprint |
| Avoid narrow gaps | The house is large; tight passages do not fit |
| Avoid dense obstacles | Trees and props occupy the same space |
| Avoid water edges and deep water | The legs need solid ground |
| Look for a wider clearing | If the preview stays red, move rather than retry |
| Green indicator | The only confirmation that the position is valid |

The most common mistake is stubbornness. Players find a scenic ledge, the outline stays red, and they rotate it for two minutes hoping it will accept. It will not. Move to the next clearing.

## What to check after the house moves

2UpSkill recommends checking two areas after a relocation:

- **Around the cottage's legs**, where loose building materials and debris can appear.
- **The old site**, which may leave items behind.

The **Faithful Crows** tool can collect available items, but the guide warns that drops are not guaranteed. Treat the sweep as a habit, not a reliable resource farm.

This is also a good moment to reorganise. The build menu's **Items & Storage** tab is where 2UpSkill places food and small decorations; stacking books and skulls on chairs is the guide's example of saving space. Storage behaviour itself is not documented in detail, so any claim about shared containers is `[Unconfirmed]`.

## Crossing regions is one-way

The fog at the map edge is not a wall you can walk through casually. 2UpSkill describes region travel as a journey with requirements, including **preparing food for Baba Jaga, Reka and the cottage** — examples given are pancakes, pierogies and berry cakes.

Two consequences follow:

1. **Finish regional business first.** Quests, furniture chests, resource runs and storage sorting should be done before you cross. Rutab's Leshy walkthrough, for example, has you return to town to collect rewards from rescued loggers.
2. **Do not expect to come back.** Leaving unloads the previous map, so the fog crossing is effectively a point of no return.

That second point is the one that catches players. If a guide tells you to "come back later" for an item on the previous map, that advice does not apply after the crossing.

### Pre-crossing checklist

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Active quests turned in | The old map unloads |
| Optional favours completed | Graveyard keeper, scarecrows, Luthi follow-ups |
| Food prepared for the journey | Required for Baba Jaga, Reka and the cottage |
| Furniture chests opened | Loot does not travel with the map |
| Inventory sorted | Storage space is limited |
| Recipes learned | Recipe books are early-story collectibles |

The journey food is not optional flavour. Pancakes need flour, a raspberry and a honeycomb; pierogies need flour, salt and a vegetable; berry cakes need an egg, flour and a raspberry. If you cross without stock, you are crossing without the requirement. Our [recipes page](/guides/reka-cooking-recipes) lists the exact counts.

## If the map breaks

2UpSkill notes a troubleshooting option in the pause menu: **Regenerate Map**. It is intended for a seriously broken procedural layout, not routine navigation.

Use it carefully. Read the confirmation prompt, and understand that regenerating a map can move the things you were planning to interact with. If a quest marker is unreachable, reloading a save is usually the safer first attempt.

## Building and decorating, without overclaiming

The official store page sells the fantasy clearly: "Build Without Limits", with selectable materials, furniture and decorations for a **chicken-legged moving house**. That is the confirmed scope.

What the sources do **not** document is a full material tier list, a structural integrity system, or a room-count limit. If you see a guide ranking building materials by durability or claiming a maximum house size, treat it as `[Unconfirmed]` unless it cites a 1.0 source. This site does not publish that ranking.

What you can rely on:

- The house is the build target, and it moves with you inside a region.
- Furniture and decorations are placeable through the build menu.
- Food can be placed as decoration and may show steam when fresh.
- Villager requests can reward **blueprints**, such as a window design from an early pumpkin soup request.

## FAQ

**Can I move the cottage at any time?**
Yes, through Build Mode, as long as the placement preview turns green. The guide does not list a cost.

**Does moving the cottage cost resources?**
No cost is documented by the sources we checked.

**Can I fast travel between towns?**
No. Recall returns you to your base within the current region; it is not a map-wide teleport network.

**Can I return to a previous region?**
The sources describe the fog crossing as one-way and say the old map unloads. Plan accordingly.

**What if the cottage will not turn green?**
Move to flatter, wider, drier ground. Retrying the same invalid spot does not help.

**Is there a storage system?**
The build menu has an **Items & Storage** tab, but detailed storage rules are not documented in the sources we checked. `[Unconfirmed]`.
