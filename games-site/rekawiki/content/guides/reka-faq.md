## When did REKA release?

REKA launched version 1.0 on **26 October 2026**. Before that it was in Early Access, which began on **12 September 2024** — just over two years.

That timeline matters when you read guides. A large amount of REKA content was written during Early Access, when the UI and quest flow were different. The release date, price and platform details on this page come from the 1.0 store page fetched on 26 October 2026; older community guides are used only for mechanics that still appear in 1.0 sources.

Famitsu's September 2026 article independently lists the same date, describing a **26 October** release on PC/Steam. The two sources agree, which is the standard this site uses before treating a date as confirmed.

## Which platforms can I play on?

REKA is available on **PC via Steam**, on Windows. The store page lists Windows system requirements only.

Searches for `REKA PS5`, `REKA Switch` and `REKA Xbox` exist, which is why console questions keep coming up, but we found **no official console announcement**. The honest answer is: PC now, consoles `[Unconfirmed]`. This site will not publish a "coming soon" date that no store page supports.

If you are on a handheld, the store page's Steam Deck compatibility badge is the only authority. Our captured sources did not include it, so this page does not claim Verified, Playable or Unsupported either way.

## Is REKA multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** The Steam page lists **Single-player** only, alongside Steam Cloud and Family Sharing. It does not mention co-op, online play or multiplayer modes.

Community searches for `REKA multiplayer` and `REKA multiplayer mod` are common, but the sources we checked document no official multiplayer and no verified co-op mod. Treat any multiplayer claim as `[Unconfirmed]` until an official channel announces it.

What the game does support is **Steam Cloud** for saves and **Family Sharing** through Steam's library system. Those are not multiplayer features, but they are the only sharing-related items on the store page.

## Is REKA combat-free?

Yes, by the game's own marketing. The store page lists **"Cozy, Combat-Free Gameplay"** as a pillar and describes the focus as decorating, crafting, foraging and "gentle questing". The guides we checked describe no death loop or combat encounter.

That does not mean there is no friction. The game has resource scarcity, quest gates and a one-way region structure. You can waste a session, but the sources do not describe a way to lose the game.

## Is 1.0 a sequel or DLC?

No. 1.0 is the base game leaving Early Access, not a separate product.

The store page still sells a **Deluxe Edition** and bundles, which are packaging options rather than a different game. The Deluxe price at capture was ¥91.80 before discount.
