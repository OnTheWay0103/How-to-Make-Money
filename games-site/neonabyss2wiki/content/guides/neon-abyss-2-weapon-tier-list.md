Veewo does not publish damage numbers, so no honest tier list can rank individual weapons by DPS. What it does publish, in full, is how six sets now work — and that is enough to rank builds by how reliably they convert your stats into damage.


## 1. What This List Measures

Read the tiers as **build priority for a fresh 1.0 run**, scored on three questions that the official notes can actually answer:

1. **Does it work without perfect input?** A set that fires on its own keeps working while you dodge.
2. **Does it scale with stats you already build?** Attack speed and keys are common pickups; sets that convert them twice are stronger in practice.
3. **How much did 1.0 change it?** A reworked set has current, documented behaviour; an untouched set is still running on pre-1.0 assumptions.

It does **not** measure raw damage per second. If a page gives you exact DPS rankings for Neon Abyss 2 without a damage table, it is guessing.


## 2. The Tier List

| Tier | Set | Why it sits here (official mechanics quoted) |
|:--:|---|---|
| **S** | **Arcane Echo** | "Halves the Crystal cost of active Curios, with a minimum cost of 1 Crystal." Using a Curio stores "Crystal Daggers, up to a maximum of 6, which fire periodically in battle at a rate affected by attack speed." Cost halving plus automatic fire that scales with attack speed is the cleanest stat conversion in the patch. |
| **S** | **Carnival Frenzy** | Jump Marks "are generated and triggered continuously, with every third mark replaced by an auto-targeting turret cloud that fires homing projectiles." Continuous generation plus homing means the set keeps dealing damage during movement — the strongest "play normally and it works" option. |
| **S** | **Tao Unfolds** | "Now generates 10 Purple Wisps in each battle. Wisp respawn time is reduced to 10% of its previous value, charge speed is doubled." Ten persistent sources with a 90% respawn cut is a large, unambiguous buff. |
| **A** | **Sky Heist** | "Replaces paid manual hacking with a permanent rainbow key and automatic hacking of nearby targets. Carrying more regular keys increases the number of targets you can connect to, while attack speed affects the hacking rate." Strong, but it asks you to hold keys, and keys compete with doors. |
| **A** | **Alloy Resolve** | Explosion immunity and free grenades, now with "grenade blast range … 1.5× its previous value." Defensive value is high; damage ceiling depends on how often grenades drop. |
| **B** | **Lovely Legion** | "Improved the visuals of Hatchmon eggs turning into shields, along with the set's effects." The 1.0 entry is a clarity pass, not a numbers buff, and the payoff needs Hatchmon eggs — a condition you cannot always meet mid-run. |

Anyone who tells you the "best weapon" is a specific gun is skipping a step. In this game the set decides the shape of the build and the weapon fills in the damage.


## 3. Why These Six and Not Others

The 1.0 notes name exactly six reworked sets. Those six are the only sets with current, quotable behaviour, so they are the only ones this page ranks. Other sets exist; Veewo has not described them in any 2026 announcement, and ranking them would mean repeating community guesswork as fact.

If a set you like is missing, that is the reason — not an omission. `[Unconfirmed]` is the correct label for every set effect not listed above.


## 4. Weapon Affixes Matter More Than Weapon Names

Since the March Weapon Reforge update, the weapon you pick up is only half the pickup:

| Affix element | Rule from the patch notes | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Static affix | Comes attached to the weapon | Compare it before you swap |
| Dynamic affix | Rolls per drop; "a little more randomness to the weapons you pick up" | Re-check the same weapon later — it will not be the same |
| Rarity | Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary, on both weapons and affixes | Prefer a Legendary affix over a small base-damage gain |
| Scaling | "the potency and variety of weapon Affixes only scales with weapon levels" | Level the weapon you plan to keep |
| Tempering | A third upgrade option that "add[s] on a specific Affix instead" | Use it when the base weapon is good but the affix is dead weight |

This is also why affix bugs are worth knowing about: 1.0 fixed projectile-count bonuses on **JIMMY, NOISE, SALUTE, and MIRA**, plus GLITZ's projectile-count affixes and their co-op sync. If your build revolved around projectile count before 1.0, it plays differently now.


## 5. Combat Styles: Which One Fits Which Build

The store page describes three styles — "smash enemies up close with a bat or saber, unleash attacks with deadly plants, or command fierce dragon-like creatures." They are not ranked by the developer, so here is how they interact with the sets above instead of a fake power order:

| Style | Natural fit | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Bat / saber melee | Carnival Frenzy, Alloy Resolve | Melee uptime rewards continuous marks and free grenades; you are already in blast range |
| Plants | Tao Unfolds, Arcane Echo | Ranged setup + persistent wisps and auto daggers cover the reload gap |
| Dragon-like creatures | Sky Heist, Lovely Legion | Companion-heavy builds benefit from auto-hacking and egg-to-shield conversion |

Treat this as a compatibility table, not a power ranking. The 1.0 notes do not describe style-specific damage values, and we will not invent them.


## 6. Agent Tiers: Deliberately Missing

"Neon Abyss 2 character tier list" is a high-volume search, and the honest status is that **no Agent tier list is defensible yet**.

What official sources support:

- Agents are individually tracked: 1.0 added per-Agent profiles with "boss kill record and highest Fate record."
- At least ten Agents exist: the October patch reduced a clear achievement from **10 different agents to 3**, which only makes sense if a roster of ten or more exists.
- Named Agents: R-7, Cassie, Mira, Tino, Zapbit, plus a Supporter Pack skin for Saya.
- One documented ability: Tino "duplicate[s] Wonder Jars (at the cost of a little life)."

That is one documented ability across a ten-plus roster. A tier list built on that would be nine guesses. The full roster page is queued for when a verifiable database is reachable again — the community database that covers Neon Abyss 2 agents, CapyDB, returned a Cloudflare origin error on 2026-10-26.


## 7. Build Recipes for the First Three Floors

These are editorial, assembled from the mechanic rules above.

**Arcane Echo opener.** Take every Curio you are offered, keep Crystals for the halved cost, and treat the stored daggers as your reload. Level one weapon only — the affix potency scales with weapon level, so spreading levels wastes the set's payoff.

**Carnival Frenzy mover.** Keep jumping. The marks generate continuously and every third one becomes a turret, so standing still is the only way to lose value. Pair with a melee weapon you can swing between jumps.

**Tao Unfolds floor-holder.** Let the ten wisps do the room clearing and spend your input on positioning. The respawn cut means losing a wisp is no longer a real loss.

**Sky Heist key-keeper.** Decide before the floor whether you are spending keys on doors or holding them for hack targets. Splitting the difference is how this set underperforms.


## 8. FAQ

**Is there a best weapon in Neon Abyss 2?**
`[Unconfirmed]`. No damage table is published. Weapon power comes from base stats plus affixes, and affixes reroll per drop, so a "best weapon" name would be meaningless without the affix attached.

**Are the tiers based on clear rates?**
No. They are based on documented mechanics and 1.0 change scope. Clear-rate data is not published per build.

**Does the list change with patches?**
Yes. It was written against the 1.0 build and the October 10–16 hotfixes. The six set descriptions come from the 1.0 announcement, so a later rebalance invalidates the order, not the mechanics.

**Where are the other sets?**
Unranked on purpose — no 2026 official post describes them.
