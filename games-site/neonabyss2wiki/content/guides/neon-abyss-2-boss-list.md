Veewo has named nine bosses in official patch notes, across four labels: Titans, Manager Bosses, Regular Bosses, and Old Gods. This page lists each one with its stated unlock or challenge condition, explains why no official boss order exists, and marks what is still unconfirmed.


## 1. How Bosses Are Labelled

The game does not use a single tier system. Veewo's announcements use four overlapping words, and they are not interchangeable:

| Label | Used for | Example from the notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Titan** | Top-tier, tied to Titan Group, the story's corporate antagonist | Theia, "Our new Titan has landed in the Abyss" |
| **Manager Boss** | A stronger variant of a regular boss | Dark Aphrodite, "A New Manager Boss" |
| **Regular Boss** | A standard floor-ending fight | Michel, "A New Regular Boss" |
| **Old God** | Deities from the game's pantheon, often introduced in major updates | Aphrodite, "another Old God boss" |

Treat "Old God" and "Titan" as lore categories, not difficulty tiers. A manager boss is explicitly defined by its unlock route — defeat the regular version first — which makes it a progression marker rather than a separate boss family.

## 3. Why There Is No Official Boss Order

"Neon Abyss 2 bosses in order" is one of the most-searched phrasings for this game, and the honest answer is that **Veewo has never published a boss order**. What the patch notes do define is the *gating logic*, which is more useful than an invented list:

- **Boss portals gate each floor.** The safety valve added in October only triggers "when all boss portals are locked" — the safehouse gives you an exit so a locked portal cannot soft-lock a run.
- **Manager bosses come after their regular version.** Dark Aphrodite is unlockable only by defeating Aphrodite, so the order between those two is fixed by the game's own rule.
- **One challenge line requires a specific boss kill.** Dark Aphrodite's challenge sends you through Hermes, so Hermes must be reachable before that challenge completes.
- **Dark Zeus is time-gated, not order-gated.** Its entry check is 30 minutes of active playtime, explicitly excluding pauses and loading, and an already-open door does not close when the limit expires.
- **The hidden boss is chest-triggered.** The fishing room encounter starts from chests, and 1.0 fixed "some chests failing to trigger the encounter."

Anything beyond that — a numbered floor-by-floor list — would be a guess dressed up as data. If a page gives you a fixed 1-to-30 boss sequence, check whether it cites an official post. Ours does not, because there is none.


## 4. Fight Notes, Boss by Boss

### Aphrodite — Goddess of Seduction

Veewo's own description is the most reliable fight brief: Aphrodite "weaves fantasies of love and devotion (this is lore translation for 'loads of exploding hearts, make sure they don't hit you')." Treat the hearts as the real damage source and keep lateral movement available rather than committing to long melee strings. She was added as "another heavy-hitting, and dynamic boss," so expect a longer phase structure than the early-floor regulars.

### Dark Aphrodite — Goddess of Seduction

Same silhouette, different rules. The Wonder Flow post describes her as turning "love on its head, weaving fantasies and desires to bewitch players." Two practical consequences follow from the stated unlock and challenge:

- You cannot fight her until **regular Aphrodite** is down, so a first clear should route through Aphrodite.
- Her **challenge** line asks you to reach the next floor via **Hermes while holding at least 10 Hatchmons**. That turns Hatchmon collection into a boss-route constraint, not just a build preference. Plan the roster before the floor, because you cannot spend your way to 10 mid-fight.

### Michel — God of Graffiti

A regular boss added alongside Dark Aphrodite, with no published condition. Veewo's post is jokey ("spray and pray") and carries no mechanical detail, so treat him as the "normal" encounter of that update and lean on whatever build the floor gave you rather than a boss-specific setup.

### Theia — Goddess of Vanity

The 1.0 Titan. As Titan Group's Chief Brand Officer, Theia "turns aesthetics into shackles and blinds Agents with dazzling light and the dangerous lure of vanity." The blind-light wording is the useful part: assume line-of-sight disruption and prefer damage that does not require precise aim while the screen is brightened. No official phase guide exists yet.

### Zeus and Dark Zeus

Zeus was directly patched: 1.0 fixed "Zeus not acting" and "some boss phases failing to advance," and the October roundup hid the countdown and red warning while Dark Zeus is locked. Dark Zeus's **30-minute active-playtime** gate is the single most misunderstood number in the game: it excludes pauses and loading, it keeps elapsed time when you continue a run, and a door that is already open stays open.

### God of Screens

The October roundup fixed target handling for "the second phase of the God of Screens fight," which tells you the fight has at least two phases and that add-targeting was interfering with it. If your damage was hitting the wrong object before 2026-10-19, that was the bug, not your aim.

### Apollo

The only official detail is a 1.0 fix: lingering attacks from Apollo's final phase were cleared when the fight ends. Before that patch, dying to "empty" space after the kill was a known outcome. If you play on a build older than 1.0, do not trust the arena once the health bar is empty.

### The fishing room hidden boss

1.0 added a day-night cycle to the fishing room and, with it, a hidden boss. Veewo deliberately did not publish the trigger. The patch notes do confirm the shape of it: chests start the encounter, some chests previously failed to trigger it, and linked chests kept charging a fee after the boss in question was already defeated. Start from the chests, and expect the room to change with the day-night cycle.


## 5. The Boss Bugs Worth Knowing About

Several "this boss is impossible" reports trace to patched bugs rather than design. Confirmed fixes from the 1.0 and October notes:

| Symptom | Status |
|---|---|
| Boss phases never advance | Fixed — "some boss phases failing to advance" |
| Boss room entrance unreachable | Fixed — "some boss room entrances being inaccessible" |
| Combat does not resume after mechanical guards hide | Fixed |
| Zeus stands still | Fixed — "Zeus not acting" |
| Attacks linger after Apollo's final phase | Fixed |
| Dark Zeus door checked wall-clock time, not play time | Fixed — now uses active playtime |
| Fishing-room boss fails to start from chests | Fixed |
| Boss objectives missing from older saves | Fixed in the October roundup |

All of these are in the default branch as of 2026-10-19. If a fight behaves like the bug is still present, check that your client is not sitting on an older build and that nobody in the party is on the Beta branch — the same branch mismatch that blocks co-op also blocks bug fixes.


## 6. FAQ

**How many bosses does Neon Abyss 2 have?**
Veewo has named nine in official posts. That is a floor, not a total: some bosses are still unnamed publicly, and the 1.0 fishing-room boss was added without a name.

**Who is the final boss?**
`[Unconfirmed]`. Veewo has not labelled any boss as the final one. Theia is the 1.0 headline Titan, and Dark Zeus is the most heavily gated fight, but calling either "final" would be inference.

**What is the fastest route to a boss?**
Storm Breach, the mode that left beta in 1.0. Veewo describes it as "a faster, more streamlined game mode" with no dungeon exploration — you go battle to battle instead of exploring floors.

**Do bosses drop anything guaranteed?**
The notes describe boss portals, boss objectives and kill records, but no guaranteed loot table. Loot is described as random drops, and 1.0 fixed cases where "unique Wonder items dropping more than once per run."

**Is Eris a Neon Abyss 2 boss?**
`[Unconfirmed]`. Eris does not appear in any official announcement from March to October 2026. We do not list it as a boss until a source does.
