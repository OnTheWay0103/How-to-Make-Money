## Gauntlet Mode Overview

Gauntlet mode is Sephiria's wave-based survival challenge, unlocked after completing the main story. Unlike the standard tower climb, gauntlet mode strips away progression between rooms — you face an endless series of increasingly difficult waves with a fixed loadout. Your goal is simple: survive as long as possible and score as many points as you can.

This mode is the ultimate test of your Sephiria skills and the primary source of endgame rewards.

## How Gauntlet Mode Works

### Core Mechanics

- **Fixed Loadout** — Before starting, you choose your weapon, blade arts, relics, and inscriptions. You cannot change them mid-run.
- **No Rest Points** — There are no healing shrines between waves. Health and Focus carry over from wave to wave.
- **Wave Scaling** — Every 5 waves, the difficulty increases. Enemy health, damage, and spawn density grow each tier.
- **Break Waves** — Every 10 waves, a mini-boss or elite wave appears. These give bonus score and rare loot.
- **Endless Format** — The gauntlet continues until you die. There is no final wave.

### Unlocking Gauntlet Mode

To unlock gauntlet mode, you must:

1. Complete all 6 chapters of the main story at least once
2. Reach floor 20 in New Game+ or higher
3. Speak to the **Gauntlet Master** NPC in the Tower Hub
4. Pay 500 Memoriam Shards as the entry fee

## Scoring System

Your score determines your rank and rewards. The scoring formula considers several factors:

### Score Breakdown

- **Kill Score** — Base points per enemy killed. Standard enemies = 100 pts, Elites = 500 pts, Bosses = 2000 pts.
- **Speed Bonus** — Clear each set of 5 waves within 3 minutes to earn a 1.5x multiplier on kills during those waves.
- **Combo Multiplier** — Kill enemies within 3 seconds of each other to build a combo chain. Each successive kill adds 0.1x to your multiplier (max 5.0x).
- **No-Hit Bonus** — Complete a wave without taking damage to earn 2x points for that wave. Resets when you take a hit.
- **Parry Bonus** — Each successful parry adds 50 bonus points + extends the combo timer by 1 second.

## Rewards

Gauntlet mode offers exclusive rewards you cannot get anywhere else:

### Gauntlet-Exclusive Items

- **Gauntlet Crest** — Consumable that grants 50% more score for the next 10 waves (use at wave 20+ for maximum effect)
- **Gauntlet Crown** — Permanent unlock: start each run with +1 Focus charge
- **Eternal Emblem** — Cosmetic title displayed next to your name in multiplayer
- **Ancient Relics** — Unique relics only found in gauntlet mode, including the Infinity Stone (all blade arts cost 1 less Focus) and the Unyielding Heart (revive once per gauntlet run)

### Weekly Leaderboard Rewards

At the end of each weekly cycle, top-ranked players receive:

- **Top 10**: Ghost weapon skin, 5000 Memoriam Shards, unique title
- **Top 100**: Epic weapon skin, 2000 Memoriam Shards
- **Top 1000**: Rare weapon skin, 1000 Memoriam Shards
- **Top 5000**: 500 Memoriam Shards

## Survival Strategies

### Loadout Recommendations

For getting past wave 30 consistently:

- **Weapon**: Moonlit Greatsword (wide arcs, good for crowd control)
- **Light Art**: Gale Thrust (piercing, which prevents overcommitment)
- **Heavy Art**: Frost Fang (slow enemies, buy time)
- **Special Art**: Sacred Radiance (the only reliable healing source in gauntlet)
- **Relics**: Guardian Charm (damage reduction), Focus Crystal (+2 Focus), Everflowing Essence (slow Focus regen during combat)

### Wave-by-Wave Strategy

- **Waves 1-10**: Conserve Focus. Use basic attacks primarily. Save blade arts for elite waves.
- **Waves 11-20**: Start using Light arts more freely. Build your combo multiplier during standard waves.
- **Waves 21-30**: Positioning becomes critical. Never stand still. Use Frost Fang to control enemy approach angles.
- **Waves 31-40**: Parry everything you can. The Focus regeneration from parrying is your only reliable resource income.
- **Waves 41-50**: Kite enemies constantly. Use Gale Thrust to maintain distance and pick off stragglers.
- **Waves 50+**: Focus on survival, not kills. Let enemies group, then use Void Cascade or Tempest Fury for quick clears.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Over-investing in offensive relics at the expense of survivability — gauntlet is a marathon, not a sprint
- Using all Focus charges too early — always save at least 1 charge for emergency dodges or parries
- Ignoring parry opportunities — parrying is the single most valuable skill in gauntlet mode
- Not moving during fights — standing still against swarms is a death sentence past wave 30
