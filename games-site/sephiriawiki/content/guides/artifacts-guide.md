# Artifacts Guide — The ~300 Per-Run Items & How to Use Them

**Artifacts** are Sephiria's per-run passive items — there are **~300 at 1.0**. They modify your stats and abilities, and they reset every run. They are the core of the build system: a coherent artifact setup (matching an element or playstyle) matters more than almost anything else in a run.

## How Artifacts Work

### The Grid Inventory

Artifacts are arranged in an **inventory grid** — the game's signature build system. Where you place artifacts matters, because **tablets empower the artifacts adjacent to them**:

- **Artifacts** give you stats and passives
- **Tablets** are grid-enhancement items that boost every adjacent artifact (stat boosts, element conversion, synergy multipliers)
- **Placement is a spatial puzzle** — a tablet touching 3-4 artifacts does 3-4x more work than one touching a single artifact

For the full grid mechanics, placement strategy, and best tablet combinations, see the [Tablets Guide](/guides/tablets-guide).

### Rarity Tiers

| Rarity | Typical Power | When You'll See Them |
|--------|:--:|------|
| Common | Small stat bonuses | Chapter 1 onwards |
| Rare | Meaningful bonuses | Mid-run |
| Epic | Build-defining | Late-run, secret rooms, boss drops |
| Legendary | Run-defining | Endgame, high-rarity sources |

**Soft caps:** crit rate and dodge i-frames cap around 60% — stacking beyond that gives diminishing returns. Diversify instead.

### Permanent Artifact Slots

Some permanent progression adds artifact capacity: Destiny Inscriptions include an **Artifact Slot +1** unlock (a second permanent artifact slot), and a **Side Bag** (added in 1.0) expands your inventory space.

## Notable Artifacts

A few artifacts are well-documented by the community (unlocked permanently through specific conditions):

| Artifact | Effect | Unlock Condition |
|------|------|------|
| **Golden Maple Leaf** | +15% XP — faster leveling during runs | Clear Chapter 2 |
| **Astral Compass** | Reveals secret rooms on the map | Find 10 secret rooms |
| **Vault Key** | +1 artifact slot during runs | Clear Gauntlet Mode |
| **Mini Ballista** | Summons a ballista that fights alongside you | Achievement-based unlock |

**The general rule:** artifacts that match your build's element or damage type outperform generic stat sticks. Pick the weapon whose element matches the artifacts and tablets you find, not the other way around.

## How to Get Artifacts

| Source | Notes |
|------|------|
| **Run pickups** | Chests, enemy drops, breakable objects, and shops throughout every chapter |
| **Secret rooms** | Higher-rarity artifacts; the best source for rares — see the [Secret Rooms Guide](/guides/secret-rooms-guide) |
| **Boss kills** | Chapter bosses drop high-rarity artifacts |
| **Permanent unlocks** | Some artifacts unlock permanently via achievements and conditions (like the table above) — once unlocked, they enter your run pools |
| **Mystic Pot conversion** | Re-roll artifacts: 1-for-1 at the same rarity, or 2-for-1 up to the next rarity — see the [Mystic Pot Guide](/guides/mystic-pot-guide) |

## Artifact Strategy by Chapter

| Chapter | Priority |
|------|------|
| 1-2 | Grab anything; start committing to one element by the end of Chapter 2 |
| 3 | Filter by element — sell off-theme artifacts, buy rerolls |
| 4 | Upgrade to Rare/Epic; the hardest boss in the climb demands a coherent setup |
| 5-6 | Epic+ only; position your grid for the boss gauntlet (no recovery rooms in Chapter 6) |

## FAQ

**Q: How many artifacts are in Sephiria?**
~300 at 1.0, plus ~70 tablets that empower them.

**Q: Are artifacts permanent?**
Artifacts themselves reset each run — they are per-run passive items. Permanent progression lives in Talents, Destiny Inscriptions, weapon mastery, and the artifact *unlocks* that add items to your run pools.

**Q: Do artifacts stack?**
Yes. Multiple artifacts with the same effect stack additively, and tablets multiply the effects of everything they touch.

**Q: What's the difference between artifacts and tablets?**
Artifacts are the passive items themselves; tablets are grid-enhancement items that boost adjacent artifacts. See the [Tablets Guide](/guides/tablets-guide).
