## What Are Artifacts?

**Artifacts** are a new item class introduced in the 1.0 launch, found exclusively in and beyond the **Astral Vault** (Chapter 7). They are different from relics in three important ways:

1. **Permanent** — Relics are per-run pickups; artifacts stay unlocked forever once obtained, like inscriptions.
2. **Slotted** — You equip up to **2 artifacts** from the account-wide artifact menu before a run.
3. **Build-defining** — Every artifact is a major passive that changes how a build plays, not a stat bump.

You unlock your first artifact slot by defeating the **Rabbit Goddess**; the second opens after clearing **Hard Mode**.

## Where to Get Artifacts

| Source | Artifact Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Astral Guardian (Chapter 7 mid-boss) | Combat artifacts | Guaranteed drop on first kill |
| The Rabbit Goddess (Chapter 7 final boss) | Boss artifacts | 2 drops per kill on Normal |
| Hard Mode boss kills | Cursed artifacts | Hard Mode exclusive pool |
| Infinite Mode floor milestones (25, 50, 100) | Endless artifacts | Floor 100 guarantees a legendary artifact |
| Star-alignment secret rooms (Chapter 7) | Utility artifacts | Requires solving the constellation puzzles |

## The Artifact Catalog

### Combat Artifacts

| Artifact | Effect | Best With |
|---|---|---|
| **Charged Core** | Charge attacks gain +50% damage and 30% faster charging | Greatsword, Spear |
| **Spellweaver's Star** | Spell-weave combos can double-cast at 15% chance | Tome, Staff |
| **Blood Moon Sigil** | Life-steal applies to all damage sources, +5% lifesteal | Fist, Scythe |
| **Twin Fang** | Your first attack on a new enemy deals 100% bonus damage | Bow, Fist |

### Boss Artifacts

| Artifact | Effect | Best With |
|---|---|---|
| **Goddess's Halo** | Every 30 seconds, your next hit heals for 25% of damage dealt | Any weapon, defensively |
| **Vault Key** | +1 relic slot during runs (stacks to a max of 9 relics) | Any build |
| **Astral Compass** | Reveals all secret rooms and challenge chambers on the minimap | Exploration |

### Cursed Artifacts (Hard Mode)

| Artifact | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Void Crown** | +60% all damage | Max HP reduced by 30% |
| **Obsidian Heart** | Dodge i-frames extended by 50% | Potions heal 50% less |
| **Starve's Token** | Kills grant +1% stacking damage | Gold pickups halved |

### Endless Artifacts (Infinite Mode)

| Artifact | Effect | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| **Ascension Rune** | +10% damage per 10 floors climbed, resets each run | Floor 25 |
| **Floorwalker's Boots** | Enemies drop healing motes every 10 kills | Floor 50 |
| **Eternity Shard** | Revive once per run with 50% HP | Floor 100 |

## Best Artifacts by Build

- **Melee DPS (Greatsword, Fist):** Charged Core + Twin Fang — front-loads the damage you already scale
- **Mage (Tome, Staff):** Spellweaver's Star + Goddess's Halo — sustain for fragile casters
- **Hard Mode survivability:** Obsidian Heart + Vault Key — longer i-frames and an extra relic for defense
- **Co-op support:** Astral Compass + Floorwalker's Boots — your team never misses loot or healing

## Farming Tips

- The **Astral Guardian** is the fastest artifact farm: reachable in ~20 minutes on a practiced run, guaranteed drop each time.
- Hard Mode **cursed artifacts** share a drop pool — equip the Explorer's Map relic to maximize chests per run.
- Artifacts are account-wide, so there is no reason to hoard duplicates — they simply don't appear twice.
