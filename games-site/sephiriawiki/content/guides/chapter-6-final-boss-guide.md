# Sephiria Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide — The Ending Content (1.0)

**Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Chapter 6 is the **final chapter** of Sephiria — the story's endgame arc added in 1.0, with **2 bosses, 1 mini-boss, and a full ending with credits**. The game has 6 chapters, and the story ends here. This guide covers how to reach it and what the fights demand.

---

## How to Unlock Chapter 6

- Complete **Chapter 5's boss** — Chapter 6 unlocks immediately after in the tower climb.
- Chapter 6 is the point where a completed weapon upgrade line and a coherent artifact/tablet build stop being optional — the final fights punish unoptimized setups.

## Chapter 6 Structure

| Segment | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Mid-chapter | **1 mini-boss** — a shorter, gimmick-focused fight that teaches the chapter's mechanics |
| Chapter boss | **1 full boss** — the **Pillar Summoner**, an endgame summoner fight; destroy the summoned pillars to keep it manageable |
| Final boss | **The ending fight** — the tower's climax; beating it grants the ending + credits |

## The Final Boss Fight — What the Community Knows

- **It's a bullet-hell-style fight** in the game's tradition — full-screen attacks are telegraphed and can be **blocked or dodged in all directions**. This is the fight where blocking matters most in the entire game.
- **Block, don't just dodge** — blocking is the correct answer to the all-direction barrages. Keep your block key ready during the "wind-up" phases.
- **Stagger windows** exist between attack patterns — Greatsword's stagger chains are the community's favorite answer here.
- **No recovery rooms** — once the boss gauntlet starts, there is no healing between fights. Stock up before you commit.

## Recommended Prep

| Prep | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| Weapon | **Greatsword** (stagger) or **Crossbow** (safe range) — the community's top two for this fight |
| Upgrade line | Complete your weapon's upgrade line — the 1.0 upgrade expansions were designed for the endgame |
| Artifacts/tablets | Bring your strongest element synergy (ice/storm still works; fire is viable) — see the [Artifacts Guide](/guides/artifacts-guide) |
| Health economy | Stock healing before the climb — Chapter 6 has no recovery rooms once you're in the boss gauntlet |
| Hard Mode note | If you're on Hard, expect the Tension modifier (no potions in boss fights) to make the final boss dramatically harder — see the [Hard Mode Guide](/guides/hard-mode-guide) |

## Ending & Post-Credits

- Beating the final boss plays the **ending and credits** — the story arc the Early Access build was missing is complete.
- After the credits, the standard loop continues: **Hard Mode to level 60**, artifact re-rolling at the Mystic Pot, and Gauntlet Mode score chasing (see the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide)).

## FAQ

**Q: Does Chapter 6 have a secret ending or a Chapter 7?**
No — Sephiria has 6 chapters, and the story ends at Chapter 6 with one ending and credits.

**Q: How many bosses does Chapter 6 have?**
Two bosses (the Pillar Summoner and the final boss) and one mini-boss.

**Q: I beat the final boss — what now?**
Hard Mode to level 60 (6 modifiers), artifact/tablet min-maxing, and Gauntlet Mode. The developers have confirmed continued post-launch support.

**Q: Does difficulty affect the ending?**
The ending unlocks on any difficulty; Hard Mode is for the challenge and rewards, not story content.
