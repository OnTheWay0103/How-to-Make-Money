# Best Relic Combos — Top Builds for Every Playstyle

Relics are the core of Sephiria's build system. Each character can equip up to **five relics** — three major slots and two minor slots. The difference between a good build and a great one is synergy: relics that amplify each other's effects.

This guide covers the **top five builds** for every major playstyle, ranked by damage output, survivability, and ease of gearing.

---

## Build Comparison

| Build Name | Core Relics | Strengths | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flame Berserker** | Heart of Still Flame, Berserker Shard, Ember Core | Highest melee burst | NG+1–3 speed runs |
| **Frost Weaver** | Frostbrand Sigil, Permafrost Crown, Iceblood Catalyst | Infinite CC chain | Boss fights, crowd control |
| **Void Reaper** | Void Core, Shadow Step, Grim Harvest | Extreme risk-reward | Speedrunners, challenge runs |
| **Paladin Shell** | Architect's Crown, Aegis Fragment, Holy Ward | Unkillable sustain | First-time NG+ cycles |
| **Elementalist** | Prism Orb, Mana Font, Conduit Ring | Mixed damage, no immunities | General exploration, farming |

---

## Build 1: Flame Berserker (Melee)

This is the highest sustained-DPS setup in the game. It relies on stacking flat fire damage and attack-speed modifiers.

**Relic Loadout:**

| Slot | Relic | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major 1 | Heart of Still Flame | +25% melee damage, +15% fire res | Must-have |
| Major 2 | Berserker Shard | +5% damage per 10% missing HP | High |
| Major 3 | Ember Core | Attacks apply burning; +15% fire damage | High |
| Minor 1 | Ember Ring | Burning enemies take +10% damage | Medium |
| Minor 2 | Blood Amulet | +3% lifesteal | Optional |

**Stat Priority:** STR (soft cap 60) > Fire Damage % > Attack Speed > VIT (min 25)

**Playstyle:** Stay below 40% HP for max Berserker Shard stacks. Use the *Wild Swing* weapon skill to apply Ember Core procs. Heal only when below 15%.

---

## Build 2: Frost Weaver (Mage / CC)

A control build that locks enemies in place while stacking ice-damage multipliers.

| Slot | Relic | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major 1 | Frostbrand Sigil | 20% phys→ice conversion, +30% ice damage | Must-have |
| Major 2 | Permafrost Crown | Enemies hit by ice are slowed 40%; stacks to 80% | High |
| Major 3 | Iceblood Catalyst | Ice damage restores mana equal to 5% of damage | High |
| Minor 1 | Crystal Core | +15% freeze duration | Medium |
| Minor 2 | Glacial Band | Killing a frozen enemy creates an ice explosion | Low |

**Stat Priority:** INT (soft cap 60) > Ice Damage % > Mana Regen > Cooldown Reduction

**Playstyle:** Keep enemies Permafrost-stacked at all times. Use the *Blizzard Storm* spell for multi-target freeze. Never let anything get close.

---

## Build 3: Void Reaper (Glass Cannon)

Maximum damage at maximum risk. Every relic reduces survivability in exchange for raw power.

| Slot | Relic | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major 1 | Void Core | +20% all damage, -30% max HP | Must-have |
| Major 2 | Shadow Step | Dodge: no i-frame penalty, +10% distance | High |
| Major 3 | Grim Harvest | Killing an enemy restores 20% HP and 10% stamina | High |
| Minor 1 | Glass Cannon | +15% damage, -15% defense | Medium |
| Minor 2 | Echo Locket | +8% skill crit chance | Optional |

**Stat Priority:** DEX (hard cap 80) > Crit Damage > Crit Chance > Movement Speed

**Playstyle:** Hit-and-run. Never trade blows. Use dodge to maintain distance; Grim Harvest sustains you through kill chains. One mistake = death.

---

## Build 4: Paladin Shell (Survival / Tank)

Designed for players pushing into higher NG+ cycles who want a forgiving experience.

| Slot | Relic | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major 1 | Architect's Crown | All elemental damage +15%, incoming -10% | Must-have |
| Major 2 | Aegis Fragment | Blocking an attack heals 3% HP | High |
| Major 3 | Holy Ward | Once per 60s, survive a fatal hit at 1 HP | High |
| Minor 1 | Fortress Seal | +20% armor effectiveness | Medium |
| Minor 2 | Restoration Band | Healing flasks heal +40% | Low |

**Stat Priority:** VIT (soft cap 50) > Armor > All Elemental Resist > HP Regen

**Playstyle:** Slow and steady. Block, heal, and wait for openings. Holy Ward gives you one mistake per minute. Only use this build if you're struggling to survive.

---

## Build 5: Elementalist (Hybrid / Farming)

A general-purpose build for exploration, farming, and early NG+ cycles. Deals decent damage to everything and doesn't care about enemy resistances.

| Slot | Relic | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major 1 | Prism Orb | +8% damage per unique element type (max 4) | Must-have |
| Major 2 | Mana Font | Mana regen +100% while out of combat | High |
| Major 3 | Conduit Ring | Elemental skills cost 20% less mana | High |
| Minor 1 | Rainbow Shard | +5% to all elemental damage | Medium |
| Minor 2 | Gem Pouch | +15% gold from kills | Optional |

**Stat Priority:** INT > DEX > All Elemental Damage > Gold Find

**Playstyle:** Equip one skill of each element (fire, ice, lightning, holy). Cycle through them to maintain Prism Orb stacks. Use Mana Font between packs for infinite sustain.

---

## Quick Synergy Cheat Sheet

- **Heart of Still Flame** + **Berserker Shard** = 50%+ damage boost below 20% HP
- **Frostbrand Sigil** + **Permafrost Crown** = perma-slow on every hit
- **Void Core** + **Shadow Step** = high mobility with massive burst potential
- **Architect's Crown** + **Aegis Fragment** = best defensive core in the game
