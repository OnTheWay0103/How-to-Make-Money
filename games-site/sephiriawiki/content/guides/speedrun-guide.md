## Speedrunning Sephiria

Sephiria's procedural tower layout and deep combat make it an excellent speedrun game. A well-optimized run clears all 6 chapters **far faster than a casual run** — the difference between a first-time player's climb and a speedrun is mostly routing and decision-making, not raw skill.

This guide covers the fundamentals: route philosophy, the fastest weapons, what to skip, and how to practice.

## Recommended Speedrun Loadout

### Best Weapons for Speed

| Weapon | Speedrun Tier | Why |
|--------|---------------|-----|
| **Crossbow** | S-Tier | Safe range + fast room clear. The community's default speedrun weapon — you almost never need to stop moving |
| **Greatsword** | A-Tier | Stagger chains delete elites and bosses quickly. Slightly slower room clear |
| **Staff** | A-Tier | Elemental AoE wipes rooms. Requires tablet investment to shine |
| **Sword & Shield** | B-Tier | Reliable and safe, but block-first play slows runs down |
| **Dagger** | B-Tier | Fast, but short range forces risky positioning |
| **Katana** | B-Tier | High burst, high skill — great once you're experienced |

**Recommendation:** start with the **Crossbow**. Its safety and room-clearing speed make it the most forgiving option while still achieving top-tier times.

### Artifacts & Inscriptions for Speed

Speedruns benefit from permanent progression (see the [Inscriptions Guide](/guides/inscriptions-guide)):

- **Sapphires are the speedrun currency** — Destiny Inscriptions that boost damage, movement, and sustain make every subsequent run faster.
- **Damage-multiplier artifacts** are the in-run priority — anything that speeds up boss kills beats defense when you're racing.
- **Element tablets** (see the [Tablets Guide](/guides/tablets-guide)) — commit to one element and let it carry your damage.

## Route Philosophy

### Skip Everything Optional

In a speedrun, **don't full clear**:

1. **Only visit rooms on the critical path** — bosses gate progression, everything else is optional.
2. **Skip chests** unless they're directly on your path — a guaranteed small upgrade is rarely worth the time it takes.
3. **Skip challenge rooms** — they're the biggest time sink for the smallest payoff.
4. **Skip secret rooms** — unless you need a specific artifact to survive the boss ahead.

### Know the Bosses

Boss fights are where speedruns live or die. The fights are **telegraphed and learnable** — the fastest players never take avoidable damage because they know every pattern (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)).

## Speedrun Tips That Actually Matter

1. **Rush the first chapters.** Chapters 1-3 are easy enough that you can rush them with base stats — the run is won or lost in Chapters 5-6.
2. **Spend gold before the boss.** Shops and upgrade points between floors are the cheapest power in the game — buy damage, not defense.
3. **Don't reroll.** Starting weapon choice matters less than route discipline. Take the first reasonable pick.
4. **Learn the layout rules.** Secret rooms and alternate paths follow procedural rules — experienced runners learn to identify the critical path faster.
5. **Your perm-progression carries runs.** Veteran runners with maxed Destiny Inscriptions have a massive advantage over new saves — that's intended.

## Practice Routines

1. **Run Chapter 1 alone** — drill it until you can clear it without thinking.
2. **Practice each boss** — run normal climbs up to each boss and replay the fight until it's clean.
3. **Full runs** — once individual chapters are clean, link them together.
4. **Review recordings** — watch your runs and identify time loss (hesitation, wrong pathing, unnecessary fighting).
