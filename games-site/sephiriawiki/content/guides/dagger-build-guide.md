# Dagger Build Guide — Lightning Dagger Deer & the 1.0 Balance Meta

**Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: September 1, 2026**

The Dagger is Sephiria's highest-risk melee weapon — fastest attack speed, short range, and built around burst and crits. For most of Early Access that risk came with a catch: without the right artifact and tablet support, the Dagger's floor was inconsistent. After 1.0, that changed. The launch patch gave it a real endgame upgrade line, and **patch 1.0.26 (Aug 13, 2026)** buffed the weapon directly — the Dagger's dash attack can now chain into the **Parry** and **Fury** special attacks [Official].

This guide covers the build the community settled on as the strongest Dagger setup in the 1.0 meta: the **Lightning Dagger** build — 电刀鹿 ("Electric Dagger Deer") in the Chinese community. It's the build most cited for clearing the highest **Difficulty 60** runs, with a fully reproducible recipe below [Community].

---

## Why the Dagger in 1.0?

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **1.0.26 direct buff** | Dash now chains into Parry and Fury specials — a defensive-and-burst tool the weapon didn't have before [Official] |
| **New +4 upgrade line (1.0)** | English tier lists note the Dagger's endgame line turns it into a "melts bosses" weapon once built; rated B-tier overall — high ceiling, conditional floor [Editorial] |
| **Electrocution synergy** | Fast attack speed = many hits = many Electrocution stacks, which the Lightning line multiplies [Community] |

The honest 1.0 take: the Dagger is still a commitment pick, not a default — but built for status stacking it's one of the highest-ceiling melee weapons in the game [Editorial].

## The Lightning Dagger Build (Electrocution Stacking)

**Core idea:** stack **Electrocution** on enemies with every hit, then let the periodic Electrocution proc do the damage. The Dagger's attack speed is the entire build — more hits mean more stacks, and more stacks mean bigger procs [Community].

### How Electrocution Works

Electrocution is the status the build revolves around. Per the community guide that popularized it, Electrocution works on a **timer**: every **2 seconds** it settles and deals damage roughly equal to **90% × current stacks × remaining duration**, then clears the stacks [Community]. The stat that matters is **stacks per proc** — which is why attack speed is king.

The **Shaman Necklace** is the build's most important artifact: it **extends Electrocution duration**, and because the proc formula includes duration, one settlement can hit **100+ stacks** [Community].

### Character: Deer or Lizard Scholar

The build recommends the **Deer** character — or the **Lizard Scholar** — because both start with **90 base crit**. That baseline means you never spend talent points on crit, freeing your permanent talents for drop rate, speed, and damage [Community].

### Weapon Line

Normal Dagger → **Lightning Dagger** → **Lightning Wrath** (Stage 2). At Stage 2 the Dagger gains **very high attack speed** and applies an Electrocution stack on essentially every hit — exactly what the build needs [Community].

### Talents (Permanent)

| Talent | Points | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| **Luck** | Max | Raises red-rarity and lightning-artifact drop rates [Community] |
| **Defense** | 5 | Early-run survival [Community] |
| **GG20** | Core | Grants bonus damage per completed set you own; the build runs small 2/4-piece sets instead of forcing a full set, so GG20 converts whatever you find into damage [Community] |
| **Swiftness** | 5 | Attack speed is stack frequency [Community] |
| **Crit / mana regen / crit damage** | **Skip** | The character's 90 base crit already covers the crit axis [Community] |

For **Difficulty 50–60**, shift leftover points into survival-related talents instead [Community].

### Secondary Branch & Miracle

- **Secondary branch:** **Curse** [Community].
- **Miracle:** **Electro Mage** (电击术士) — boosts Electrocution settlement damage. It's the build's biggest single damage multiplier [Community].

### Core Artifacts (Priority Order)

1. **Shaman Necklace** (duration extension) + **Electric Orb** (lightning damage) — the core pair [Community]
2. **Electric Charm**, **Electric Chakram**, **Thunder Earrings** — secondary lightning pieces [Community]
3. Red-rarity Shaman Necklace — reported to **double shock damage** [Community]

Curate the artifact pool at the [Mystic Pot](/guides/mystic-pot-guide) so these pieces actually show up — the 2-for-1 rarity upgrade is the fastest way to push the Shaman Necklace and Electric Orb to red.

### Playstyle: Never Stop Attacking

The build executes itself once the pieces are in place, but the rule is strict:

1. **Right-click** (lightning special) to blanket the room in Electrocution.
2. **Left-click** basic attacks to **keep the stacks alive** — the community rule is "never stop attacking" [Community].
3. **Dash only to cancel dagger recovery or dodge red zones** — never for travel. If you're dashing around the map, you're not stacking [Community].
4. **Kill order:** ranged archers and mages first — they punish the Dagger's short range hardest [Community].

### Milestones

- **Starting pieces** (no Shaman/Orb yet): ~**2,000+ DPS**, comfortable through mid difficulty [Community].
- **Shaman Necklace + 4-piece Curse set:** Electrocution procs start hitting **10,000+ damage** — the build's first power spike [Community].
- **Full build** (Shaman Necklace + Electric Orb + Electric Charm + Electro Mage miracle): can stand and kill **Difficulty 60** bosses [Community].

## The 1.0.26 Fury/Parry Variant

Patch 1.0.26 changed the Dagger's defensive options: the dash now chains into **Parry** and **Fury** special attacks [Official]. That gives the aggressive "Fury parry" style a real mechanical foundation — dash into a **Parry** to turn an incoming hit into a punish, or dash into **Fury** for a burst window after closing distance. Pre-1.0.26, the Dagger simply didn't have this option [Editorial].

## The Fire-Dagger Alternative (Lizard Scholar)

If your artifact pool rolls fire instead of lightning, the Lizard Scholar has a second viable 1.0 direction: a **fire-dagger** build around the **Blazing Blade**, which benefits from the **Ember** bonus. Same skeleton — fast hits, status stacking, one big periodic proc — with fire instead of lightning, riding the Ember bonus. Crit investment differs from the lightning build, so follow a community Lizard Scholar guide for the exact split [Community].

## Hard Mode & the Final Boss

The Lightning Dagger is the community's go-to answer for the hardest 1.0 content — it can **stand-tank most bosses at Difficulty 60**, the top of the Hard Mode climb [Community]. It's not a "safe" build: it trades the Crossbow's distance and Sword & Shield's block for raw speed, so know the modifiers before committing. See the [Hard Mode Guide](/guides/hard-mode-guide) and [Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide](/guides/chapter-6-final-boss-guide).

## Co-op

In co-op the Dagger player is the **melee DPS role** — the fastest-attack-speed weapon applies statuses quickly for the whole party. It pairs naturally with a Sword & Shield frontliner who holds the boss while you stack Electrocution; avoid stacking two melee DPS in the same party [Editorial].

## Quick Build Checklist

1. Pick **Deer** or **Lizard Scholar** — 90 base crit means zero crit talents
2. Take the weapon line to **Lightning Wrath** (Stage 2)
3. Max **Luck**, take **Defense 5**, make **GG20** your core, take **Swiftness 5**; skip crit / mana regen / crit damage
4. Get **Shaman Necklace + Electric Orb**, upgrade both to red at the [Mystic Pot](/guides/mystic-pot-guide)
5. Pick the **Electro Mage** miracle and run **Curse** as your secondary branch
6. Right-click to apply Electrocution, left-click to keep stacks — **never stop attacking**
7. Dash only to cancel recovery or dodge red zones

**Q: What's the best Dagger build?**
The community consensus build for the 1.0 meta is the **Lightning Dagger** (Electric Dagger Deer) — Electrocution stacking with the Shaman Necklace, the Electro Mage miracle, and the Deer/Lizard Scholar's 90 base crit. It's the build most cited for clearing Difficulty 60 [Community].

**Q: How does Electrocution damage work?**
Electrocution settles every **2 seconds**, dealing roughly **90% × current stacks × remaining duration**, then clears the stacks. Attack speed is the entire stat axis, and the Shaman Necklace extends the status so one settlement can exceed 100 stacks [Community].

**Q: Did the Dagger get buffed in 1.0?**
Yes — patch **1.0.26 (Aug 13, 2026)** let the Dagger's dash chain into the Parry and Fury special attacks, adding a defensive/burst option it didn't have before [Official].

**Q: What difficulty can the Lightning Dagger clear?**
Community reports put the full build at **Difficulty 60** viability — it can stand and kill most bosses [Community].
