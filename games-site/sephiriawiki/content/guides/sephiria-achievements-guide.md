# How to Get All 27 Sephiria Achievements — Hidden Triggers and the Hard Mode Grind

> **Build-number note (October 24, 2026):** this guide cites **1.0.33** as the current build. The 1.0.33 announcement link could not be reopened during a QA pass on October 23, 2026, so treat the exact build number as **[Unconfirmed]**. The achievement list itself is unchanged and is cross-checked against two independent community sources.

Sephiria has **27 Steam achievements** as of the 1.0.33 build, and they are not one list — they are two. Fifteen unlock by playing the campaign, and twelve require optional bosses, hidden rooms, a two-step conversation, or a Hard Mode climb. The official Steam global stats also show a clean difficulty curve: **77.2%** of players finish Chapter 1, **32.4%** watch the ending, and only **26.2%** clear Hard Mode tier 30. [Official]

That gap is the useful part. Most players are not stuck on the story achievements — they are missing the optional layer on top. This guide separates the two lists, explains the triggers that the game does not explain, and gives an order that avoids re-running content you have already cleared.

## What the official data says

The numbers below come from Steam's global achievement feed for app 2436940, retrieved **October 23, 2026**. They are global averages, not a difficulty ranking, and they move as the player base grows. [Official]

| Milestone | Global unlock rate |
|---|---:|
| Defeat the Mole Chief (Stronghold Exploration) | 90.8% |
| Complete Chapter 1 | 77.2% |
| Complete Chapter 5 | 37.5% |

| Clear Hard Mode tier 1 (Root's Retreat) | 54.0% |

Two readings matter. First, **Hard Mode tier 1 is more common than finishing the story** (54.0% versus 32.4%), which tells you players are experimenting with the endgame before the credits. Second, the drop from tier 15 to tier 30 is only about thirteen points — the last stretch is hard, but it is not a wall that most Hard Mode players bounce off. [Official]

## The 15 campaign achievements

These are the story spine. You do not need to farm them; you need to finish the game and talk to the right NPCs at the right moments.

| Achievement | Trigger |
|---|---|

| Sky of the Roofless Tower | Defeat the Pillager Leader on Floor 2 |
| This Is Not Stalking | Defeat the Mad Scientist on Floor 3 |

| At the End of the Cave | Defeat the first Guardian Demon of Qliphoth |

The chapter achievements are **conversation-gated**, not just kill-gated. If you beat a boss and the achievement does not appear, check whether the game expects you back at the World Tree Seed before the next run. [Community]

### The four triggers the game does not explain

**Traveler** is the easiest to miss because nothing marks the wall. Community reports describe fine cracks along the upper edge of a dungeon wall; attack the wall to open a hidden room. You do not need a key or an item. [Community]

**Where the Blue Being Lives** is tied to the blue portal event room, the same Pocket Dimension where Sapphires are spent on artifacts. It is a random event node, so the achievement is a matter of entering it when it appears rather than forcing a specific floor. [Community]

**Soul Plan** is the one that produces the most "I talked to Perr and nothing happened" reports. The community-documented sequence is: talk to Perr at the top-left of the carrot fields in Bunnyville, view the tree cutscene, then **talk to Perr a second time**. The cutscene alone is not the trigger. [Community]

**The alternate bosses** are floor replacements rather than new floors. The Demolition Expert replaces the Floor 1 boss, the Shadow Blade Panther replaces the Floor 2 boss, and Mutated Askard is the stronger Floor 4 version. The Panther is explicitly RNG-dependent, so plan multiple runs rather than expecting a single clean sweep. [Community]

### One honest disagreement

Two community references describe **Chapter V** differently. One treats it as the Chapter 5 story completion; the other ties it to rescuing three hidden animals and carrying them to the Temple of Qliphoth. The official achievement data cannot resolve the dispute because it only exposes the internal name `CHAPTER_5_COMP` alongside `CHAPTER_5_COMPLETE`. Treat the three-animal rescue as an optional objective worth doing either way, and do not assume the achievement is bugged if it arrives at a different moment than a guide predicted. [Community]

## The Hard Mode ladder

The three Root's Retreat achievements are the real endgame commitment. Tier 1 is reachable for most players, tier 15 is the mid-point, and tier 30 is the rarest achievement in the game at **26.2%**. [Official]

Community reporting says tier 30 can be entered directly rather than climbing through every intermediate tier, and that clearing tier 30 also awards the tier 15 achievement. That routing is player-reported, not developer-documented, so verify it in your own build before planning a skip. [Community] The modifier loadout and build advice for the climb are covered in the [Hard Mode Guide](/guides/hard-mode-guide).

## The efficient completion order

1. **Finish the story once on Normal.** This clears the 15 campaign achievements and unlocks the ending context. Do not stop to farm optional bosses on the first run.
2. **Clean up the hub and hidden rooms in the same runs.** Repair the house, break cracked walls, and enter blue portals whenever they appear. These cost minutes, not runs.
3. **Hunt the alternate bosses after the story flags are set.** The Demolition Expert, Shadow Blade Panther and Mutated Askard appear after Chapter 1 or Chapter 2, so earlier attempts are wasted.
4. **Do the Perr conversation before you stop visiting Bunnyville.** It is a two-step interaction and the most commonly missed non-combat unlock.
5. **Climb Hard Mode last.** The ladder is the longest single block; doing it after the cleanup means every remaining achievement is a Hard Mode one.

## What not to worry about

No achievement is **confirmed permanently missable**. The sources disagree about whether Soul Plan can be lost after Chapter 4, but the claim rests on a pre-1.0 report and has not been retested on 1.0.33, so it should be treated as unconfirmed rather than as a reason to restart. [Community]

**Journey's End** has a known unlock failure. Re-entering town on 1.0.23 or later can restore it, so check that before assuming a corrupted save. [Community]

Finally, ignore third-party rarity tables that quote exact percentages without a date. Global averages move. The official Steam feed is the only number worth comparing against your own progress. [Editorial]

## FAQ

**How many achievements does Sephiria have?**
27 as of the 1.0.33 build. [Official]

**Are any achievements permanently missable?**
None are confirmed permanently missable. Soul Plan is the only serious candidate, and that claim is unconfirmed on 1.0.33. [Community]

**Do I need Hard Mode for 100%?**
Yes. Three achievements — Root's Retreat, Root's Retreat 15 and Root's Retreat 30 — require the Hard Mode ladder. [Official]

**Why did Journey's End not unlock?**
It has a known unlock failure. Re-enter town on 1.0.23 or later to give the game another chance to grant it. [Community]
