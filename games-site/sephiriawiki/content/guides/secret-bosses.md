# Secret Bosses Guide — Locations, Triggers & Rewards

Sephiria hides **five secret bosses** across its world. None of them appear on the map, and most require specific conditions — a certain NG+ cycle, a particular relic equipped, or a puzzle solved. Defeating them rewards **unique relics** that cannot be obtained anywhere else.

---

## Secret Boss Overview

| Boss | Zone | Unlock Condition | Recommended Cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Hollow Martyr** | Ashen Catacombs | Beat the game on any difficulty, then enter the catacombs with the **Broken Crown** relic equipped | NG+1 |
| **Echo of the First Flame** | The Crucible | Solve the three-flame puzzle in the Crucible while wearing zero armor pieces | NG+2 |
| **Twilight Revenant** | Silent Woods | Collect all 12 Memory Shards, then stand at the central obelisk during in-game night | NG+2 |
| **Starving Void** | Depths Below | Use the **Abyssal Key** (found in NG+3 Echo Shrine) on the sealed gate in Depths Below | NG+3 |
| **The Architect** | Celestial Spire | Complete all 5 Echo Shrines in a single NG+3+ cycle without resting at a bonfire | NG+4 |

## Boss-by-Boss Breakdown

### 1. The Hollow Martyr

A towering knight wielding a greatsword that leaves trail-flames. His attacks are slow but cover wide arcs.

- **Weakness:** Holy damage (+30%), Stagger (after 3 consecutive parries)
- **Resistance:** Fire damage (-50%)
- **Notable Moves:**
  - *Martyr's Slam* — Delayed overhead slam that creates a shockwave. **Jump, don't roll.**
  - *Ring of Fire* — Summons a ring of flame pillars that slowly close in. Sprint to the gap.
- **Drop:** **Heart of Still Flame** (relic) — +25% melee damage, +15% fire resistance. BiS for strength builds.

### 2. Echo of the First Flame

A spectral mage that teleports frequently and summons flaming minions.

- **Weakness:** Ice damage (+40%)
- **Resistance:** All elemental damage (-20%) when its shield is active
- **Mechanic:** Break the shield by dealing 500 raw physical damage, then burst with ice attacks.
- **Drop:** **Frostbrand Sigil** (relic) — Converts 20% of your physical damage to ice; ice damage +30%.

### 3. Twilight Revenant

A fast, dual-dagger assassin that applies stacking bleed. The hardest secret boss in terms of raw speed.

- **Weakness:** Bleed (immune), but vulnerable to **poison** (+35%)
- **Resistance:** Critical damage (-60%)
- **Strategy:** Stack poison relics and use a shield with high stability. Let it bleed out its stamina bar, then counter.
- **Drop:** **Shadow Step** (relic) — Dodge rolls have no i-frame penalty; +10% dodge distance.

### 4. Starving Void

A grotesque mass of tendrils and eyes. Fights in a small arena with environmental void damage.

- **Weakness:** Light-based relics (+50%)
- **Mechanic:** Void orbs spawn every 30 seconds. Absorb them (take damage) or destroy them before the boss heals 15% HP per orb.
- **Drop:** **Void Core** (relic) — +20% all damage, but -30% max HP. High-risk, high-reward.

### 5. The Architect

The ultimate secret boss. A construct that cycles through four element forms.

- **Weakness:** Each form has a single elemental weakness (Fire → Ice → Lightning → Holy → Fire)
- **Enrage Timer:** 3 minutes. After that, Insta-kill unless you have the **Chrono Shell** relic.
- **Drop:** **Architect's Crown** (relic) — All elemental damage +15%; reduces all incoming elemental damage by 10%.

## Preparation Checklist

Before attempting any secret boss, confirm you have:

- [ ] Full healing flask upgrades — at least +8 charges
- [ ] A weapon upgraded to the current NG+ tier cap
- [ ] Elemental resistance charms matching the boss you're targeting
- [ ] At least 30 VIT to survive a single non-telegraphed hit
- [ ] The specific relic or key item required for the unlock
