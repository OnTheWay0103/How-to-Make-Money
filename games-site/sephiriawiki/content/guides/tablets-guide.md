# Tablets Guide — Inventory Grid Power & Best Tablet Combos

Tablets are what make Sephiria's build system genuinely different from other roguelites: **tablets multiply the power of the artifacts around them** — and where you place them in your inventory grid is a spatial puzzle that can make or break a run. There are **~70 tablets at 1.0**.

This guide covers how tablets work, the element directions that define builds, and the placement strategy that turns a decent build into a Chapter 6 contender.

---

## How Tablets Work

Tablets are **grid-enhancement items** that occupy slots in your inventory. Each tablet affects the **adjacent artifacts** (touching it in the grid) in a specific way:

| Tablet Effect | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Stat boost** | Boosts a stat for every artifact it touches |
| **Element enhancement** | Adds or amplifies element damage on adjacent artifacts |
| **Synergy multiplier** | Multiplies effects when touching artifacts of the same element |

### The Grid Puzzle

Your inventory is a **grid of slots**. Artifacts and tablets each take one slot. The goal is to:

1. **Group same-element artifacts together** so tablets buff multiple targets at once
2. **Position tablets at intersections** — a tablet touching 3-4 artifacts does 3-4× more work than one touching just 1
3. **Match element directions** — commit to one element and let your tablets amplify it

### Rarity Tiers

Tablets come in rarity tiers, from **Common** up to **Legendary**. Higher-rarity tablets grant stronger bonuses per adjacent artifact — a Legendary tablet touching several matching artifacts can define a whole run.

## The Element Directions

The community-consistent tablet meta organizes builds by element. Each direction plays differently:

### Fire — Sustained Pressure
Fire builds apply burn and damage over time, wearing enemies down while you stay safe. Strong room clear; pairs well with weapons that hit often or wide.

### Ice — Control
Ice builds slow and freeze enemies, locking down threats before they reach you. Excellent control for boss fights where you need breathing room.

### Storm — Area Burst
Storm builds focus on chain and area lightning — damage that jumps between enemies. Fast room clear; weaker single-target unless the tablets align.

### Corrosion — Wearing Down
Corrosion builds stack weakening damage over time on tough enemies. Great against elites and bosses; slower in trash rooms.

**The core rule:** tablets decide your element direction — commit to one element and build your artifact grid around it. The [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide) has full builds for each direction.

## Placement Strategy

### The Cross Pattern

Place your best tablet in the center of a 3×3 area, with 4 artifacts touching it (top, bottom, left, right). This gives 4× the tablet's bonus from one item.

### The Element Cluster

For element-synergy tablets, place same-element artifacts adjacent to the tablet so every artifact gets the bonus and the tablet touches multiple targets.

### When to Replace Tablets

- **Common tablets** are early-game filler — replace them with higher rarities when they drop
- **Off-theme tablets** that don't match your element — swap for ones that do
- **Tablets touching only 1 artifact** — either reposition or replace

## Tablet FAQ

**Q: How many tablets are there?**
~70 tablets at 1.0, in rarity tiers from Common to Legendary.

**Q: Do tablets stack?**
Yes — multiple tablets can empower the same artifacts, and the synergies between tablets are where the real power is.

**Q: Should I always fill every slot?**
No. A tablet touching 3 good artifacts is worth more than 2 tablets each touching 1 mediocre one. Quality of adjacency beats raw count.

**Q: Which element should I commit to?**
Whichever fits your weapon and playstyle — fire for pressure, ice for control, storm for speed, corrosion for bosses. The [Staff Build Guide](/guides/staff-build-guide) and [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide) break down each direction.
