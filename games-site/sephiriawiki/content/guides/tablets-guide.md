# Tablets Guide — Inventory Grid Puzzle & Best Tablet Combos (1.0)

Tablets are what make Sephiria's build system genuinely different from every other roguelite. Where artifacts give you stats and relics give you passives, **tablets multiply the power of everything around them** — and where you place them in your inventory grid is a spatial puzzle that can make or break a run.

This guide covers how tablets work, the best tablet combinations players have discovered since 1.0, and the placement strategy that turns a decent build into a Chapter 6 killer.

**Last updated: August 9, 2026 (1.0).**

---

## How Tablets Work

Tablets are **grid-enhancement items** that occupy slots in your inventory. Each tablet affects the **adjacent artifacts** (touching it in the grid) in a specific way:

| Tablet Effect | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Stat boost** | +X% to a stat for every artifact it touches |
| **Element conversion** | Adds fire/ice/lightning/corruption damage to adjacent artifacts |
| **Synergy multiplier** | Multiplies effects when touching artifacts of the same element |
| **Level scaling** | Boosts adjacent artifact levels, unlocking stronger passives |
| **Slot expansion** | Creates new grid connections, letting you link more artifacts |

### The Grid Puzzle

Your inventory is a **grid of slots**. Artifacts and tablets each take one slot. The goal is to:

1. **Group same-element artifacts together** so tablets buff multiple targets at once
2. **Position tablets at intersections** — a tablet touching 3-4 artifacts does 3-4× more work than one touching just 1
3. **Rotate tablets** (Q/E keys) to change which adjacent slots they affect
4. **Leave strategic empty spaces** for certain tablet types (Astral Focus builds)

### Rarity Tiers

| Rarity | Bonus per Adjacent Artifact | Best Use |
|--------|:--:|------|
| Common | +5% | Early filler, sell later |
| Rare | +12% | Mid-run stabilizer |
| Epic | +22% | Build core — worth building around |
| Legendary | +35% | Run-defining — pivot your whole grid to support it |

**Soft caps**: crit rate and dodge i-frames cap around 60%. Stacking beyond that gives diminishing returns — diversify instead.

## Best Tablet Combinations (1.0 Meta)

### 1. Undead Inferno (Fire + Summon)

**Core tablets**: Ignition, Soul-Weaver, Catalyst

- **Ignition** — adjacent fire artifacts apply burn on every hit
- **Soul-Weaver** — defeated burning enemies spawn spectral allies
- **Catalyst** — increases status effect frequency on adjacent artifacts

**How it plays**: Stack fire artifacts around Ignition and Catalyst so every attack applies burn. When enemies die (and they will, fast), Soul-Weaver floods the screen with allied specters that tank and deal damage. Excellent crowd control for Chapters 5-6. Weaker single-target burst — bring a hard-hitting weapon for bosses.

**Best weapon**: Greatsword (wide swings apply burn to groups) or Sword & Shield (survivability while burn ticks).

### 2. Planet Symphony (Magic + Space)

**Core tablets**: Astral Focus, Chrono, Amplification

- **Astral Focus** — empty grid slots increase adjacent artifact power
- **Chrono** — reduces cooldowns on adjacent artifacts
- **Amplification** — increases projectile size and splash radius

**How it plays**: The opposite of cramming your grid. Leave 2-4 empty slots touching Astral Focus, then use Chrono and Amplification to make magic projectiles huge, fast, and spammable. Screen-clearing potential in Chapter 6.

**Best weapon**: Magic Staff. Planet artifacts (orbiters that deal passive damage) pair naturally.

### 3. Storm Cloud Lightning (Lightning)

**Core tablets**: Lightning Stone, Stormcaller, Chain

- **Lightning Stone** — adjacent artifacts deal bonus lightning damage
- **Stormcaller** — lightning procs have a chance to summon a storm cloud
- **Chain** — lightning damage jumps to nearby enemies

**How it plays**: Build a tight cluster of lightning artifacts in the grid center. Use cross-shaped tablet placement to touch 4 artifacts with one tablet. The result: constant lightning procs chaining through rooms, with storm clouds adding passive AoE.

**Best weapon**: Sword & Shield or Spear (consistent attack speed to trigger procs).

### 4. Frostbite Freeze (Ice)

**Core tablets**: Frost Relic, Permafrost, Shatter

- **Frost Relic** — adjacent ice artifacts apply slow
- **Permafrost** — slowed enemies take increased crit damage
- **Shatter** — killing frozen enemies deals AoE damage

**How it plays**: Stack frost artifacts vertically with cooldown reduction items nearby. Enemies are permanently slowed, crit hard, then explode on death. Very strong against bosses who can be consistently slowed.

**Best weapon**: Crossbow with Harpoon evolution (safe range + freeze application).

### 5. Unstoppable Whirlwind (Physical / Crit)

**Core tablets**: Sharpened Edge, Vanguard, Echoing Strike

- **Sharpened Edge** — adjacent artifacts gain crit damage
- **Vanguard** — increases melee range of adjacent weapon artifacts
- **Echoing Strike** — melee attacks repeat on a short delay

**How it plays**: The pure physical build. Stack these around melee artifacts for massive crit damage, extended reach, and double-hit attacks. Extra reach is especially valuable in Hard Mode where positioning is tighter.

**Best weapon**: Greatsword, Daggers, or any fast melee weapon.

## Placement Strategy

### The Cross Pattern

Place your best tablet in the center of a 3×3 area, with 4 artifacts touching it (top, bottom, left, right). This gives 4× the tablet's bonus from one item.

### The Diagonal Stack

For element-synergy tablets, place same-element artifacts diagonally touching two tablets. Each artifact gets bonus from two sources, and each tablet touches multiple targets.

### The Astral Spread (for Planet Symphony)

Leave every other slot empty around Astral Focus. The tablet reads empty slots as "space" and multiplies power. Fill only the slots that actually touch it.

### When to Sell Tablets

- Common tablets past Chapter 2 — replace with Rare+
- Off-theme tablets that don't match your element — sell and buy rerolls
- Tablets touching only 1 artifact — either reposition or sell

## Tablet FAQ

**Q: Can I move tablets after placing them?**
A: Yes — you can rearrange your entire grid between rooms. Use Q/E to rotate tablet orientation.

**Q: Do tablets stack?**
A: Yes. Two tablets can both affect the same artifact for additive bonuses, but synergy multipliers (like Permafrost + Frost Relic) are where the real power is.

**Q: Should I always fill every slot?**
A: No. Some builds (Planet Symphony) want empty slots. In general, a tablet touching 3 good artifacts is worth more than 2 tablets each touching 1 mediocre one.

**Q: How many tablets should I aim for by endgame?**
A: 4-6 tablets is the sweet spot. More than 6 and you run out of artifact slots to actually benefit from them.
