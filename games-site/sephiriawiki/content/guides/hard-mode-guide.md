# Hard Mode Guide — the 60-Level Endgame & All 6 Modifiers (1.0)

**Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Hard Mode is Sephiria's endgame challenge — **expanded to level 60 at the 1.0 launch on July 31, 2026**, with **6 new modifiers** that change how every run plays. It's the post-ending arc: beat the game, climb again against a harder tower, and push your builds to their ceiling.

**A note on older coverage:** pre-1.0 and early-1.0 guides described a "Corruption System" in Hard Mode and an "Infinite Mode" unlock after clearing it. **Neither exists in the released game.** The real Hard Mode runs to level 60 with the six modifiers below — this page has been rewritten to match the 1.0 patch records.

---

## How to Unlock Hard Mode

Hard Mode unlocks after you **complete the base game** — finish a full climb through all 6 chapters and the Chapter 6 ending (the final boss + credits). Once unlocked, you can select Hard Mode when starting a run.

(Note: some launch-day guides tied the unlock to defeating a specific "First Rabbit" boss. That name is not confirmed in the released game — the confirmed unlock is simply completing the full climb, which ends at Chapter 6.)

- **Why do it?** Hard Mode is the current endgame: the 6 modifiers, the level 60 arc, and the highest-tier challenge the game offers. The story itself is already complete at Chapter 6 — Hard Mode is for the climb, not new story.
- **Difficulty affects nothing story-wise:** the ending unlocks on any difficulty. Hard Mode is purely the challenge-and-rewards ladder.

## What Changes in Hard Mode (1.0)

### The Level 60 Arc

- The climb now runs to **level 60** (vs. the base-game cap), with **higher defense-tree upgrade caps** to match.
- Hard Mode adds **6 new modifiers** on top of the base difficulty — they're the real content, so this guide is built around them.

### The 6 New Modifiers

| Modifier | Type | Effect |
|----------|------|--------|
| **Blood Festival** | [Demon] | Enemies lifesteal — healing scales with party size |
| **Pseudo-Vanguard** | [Demon] | Elite enemies gain vanguard-style buffs |
| **Deterioration** | [Plan] | Progressive pressure over the climb |
| **Tension** | [Plan] | **No potions in boss fights** |
| **Heavy Heart** | [Plan] | You carry junk artifacts |
| **Proliferation** | [World] | Enemies multiply/scale world-wide |

**Modifier naming:** the lifesteal modifier is called **Blood Festival** in the 1.0.21 patch notes (some guides write "Blood Feast"). Same modifier.

### The 1.0.21 Fix That Matters

At launch, Blood Festival's lifesteal/healing was **unfair in 3–4 player co-op** — it didn't scale with party size. The **1.0.21 hotfix** (July 31) fixed it:

| Party Size | Blood Festival Healing |
|:--:|:--:|
| 2 players | 66% |
| 3 players | 50% |
| 4 players | 33% |

So co-op Hard Mode is now balanced by design — but the modifier still demands the same answer: **don't get hit.** 1.0.21 also fixed Hard Mode wrongly marking itself complete, so the 60-level progression records correctly now.

## The Modifier Counter-Guide

### Blood Festival [Demon] — Lifesteal

Enemies heal on hit. This punishes trading and rewards range.

- **Best answer: the Crossbow.** The [Weapons Tier List (1.0)](/guides/weapons-tier-list) calls it "the safest pick for Hard Mode" — distance beats lifesteal outright.
- **If you're melee:** stagger before you trade (Greatsword) or block the hits (Sword & Shield) so enemies never actually land the lifesteal attack.
- **In co-op:** the 1.0.21 scaling fix made this fair — 2P 66%, 3P 50%, 4P 33%.

### Pseudo-Vanguard [Demon] — Buffed Elites

Elites arrive with extra buffs. They're the priority target in every room.

- Kill them first, at range when possible — a vanguard elite left alone buffs the whole room.
- Greatsword stagger chains shut elites down before their buffs activate.

### Deterioration [Plan] — Progressive Pressure

Runs grind you down over time. Sustain matters.

- Lean on tablet/artifact passives over consumables; see the [Mystic Pot Guide](/guides/mystic-pot-guide) for curating that pool.
- Efficient routes > thorough routes — Deterioration punishes over-farming.

### Tension [Plan] — No Potions in Boss Fights

The single most punishing modifier. Your health bar is final in every boss room.

- **Prep before the boss room:** full health, best artifacts equipped, nothing on cooldown.
- **The final boss on Hard is dramatically harder under Tension** — the Chapter 6 ending fight with no potions is the hardest content in the game. Learn the patterns on Normal first ([Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide](/guides/chapter-6-final-boss-guide)).
- Weapons with natural defense — Sword & Shield's block, Crossbow's range — are the answers.

### Heavy Heart [Plan] — Junk Artifacts

You're forced to carry junk artifacts. This taxes your inventory and your build.

- Skip artifact pickups that don't fit your element — every carried junk artifact is dead weight.
- Feed the junk to the [Mystic Pot](/guides/mystic-pot-guide) when you can: same-rarity re-rolls turn dead artifacts into rolls you might use.

### Proliferation [World] — Scaling World

Enemies scale world-wide. The climb snowballs — the late levels hit hardest.

- Don't lag on upgrade opportunities; the +4 weapon lines (Dagger, Crossbow, Staff at 1.0) exist precisely for this.
- Co-op helps: more players = more coverage, and Blood Festival scaling is now fair.

## Best Weapons & Builds for Hard Mode

| For | Pick | Why |
|-----|------|-----|
| **Safest overall** | **Crossbow** | Range beats lifesteal; the tier list's Hard Mode pick — full build: [Crossbow Build Guide](/guides/crossbow-build-guide) |
| **Consistent all-rounder** | **Sword & Shield** | Block replaces the potions Tension takes away; no bad matchups |
| **Boss fights** | **Greatsword** | Stagger chains interrupt the no-potion boss fights — [Greatsword Build Guide](/guides/greatsword-build-guide) |
| **Element ceiling** | **Staff** | Highest output with the right tablets; fragile under Tension — [Staff Build Guide](/guides/staff-build-guide) |

**The build rules that apply to every weapon:**

1. **Tablets and artifacts decide more than the weapon** — match your element direction and curate it at the [Mystic Pot](/guides/mystic-pot-guide) (2-for-1 rarity upgrades are the fastest power growth).
2. **Respect Tension:** no potions in boss fights means defense passives, not consumables, are your safety net.
3. **Learn patterns on Normal first** — the bosses' 1.0.21-telegraphed attacks are learnable; go in knowing them.
4. **Don't get hit.** It's trite, but Blood Festival + Tension make it the actual strategy.

## Rewards & What Comes After

- **The reward is the climb itself** — the 60-level arc, the higher defense-tree caps, and the bragging rights of a Hard Mode clear.
- **There is no Infinite Mode** — some guides promised an endless tower after Hard Mode; it does not exist in the released game. The endgame is: Hard Mode to 60, artifact re-rolling at the Mystic Pot, and the gauntlet-style challenges (see the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide)).
- **Post-launch support is ongoing** — hotfixes shipped fast (1.0.21 and 1.0.22 both landed within 48 hours of launch; 1.0.22 fixed the final boss's Phase 1 bullet spread and two Sword & Shield skills). Watch Steam News for the next patch.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I unlock Hard Mode in Sephiria?**
Complete the base game — a full climb through all 6 chapters and the Chapter 6 ending. Then select Hard Mode when starting a run.

**Q: Does Hard Mode have a Corruption System?**
No. Early guides described a "Corruption System" with a corruption meter — it does not exist in the released game. Hard Mode's real systems are the level 60 climb and the 6 modifiers above.

**Q: Does Hard Mode unlock Infinite Mode?**
No. Infinite Mode does not exist in Sephiria. The endgame is Hard Mode to 60, the Mystic Pot rework, and the gauntlet-style challenges.

**Q: What are the 6 Hard Mode modifiers?**
Blood Festival (lifesteal, party-scaled), Pseudo-Vanguard (buffed elites), Deterioration (progressive pressure), Tension (no potions in boss fights), Heavy Heart (junk artifacts), and Proliferation (scaling world).

**Q: Is Hard Mode better in co-op?**
Yes, post-1.0.21 — Blood Festival healing now scales properly with party size (2P 66%, 3P 50%, 4P 33%), making 3-4 player runs fair. The revive economy still makes co-op more forgiving overall.

**Q: What's the best weapon for Hard Mode?**
The Crossbow is the community's safest pick — range counters the lifesteal modifiers. Sword & Shield is the best all-rounder, and Greatsword wins the boss fights.
