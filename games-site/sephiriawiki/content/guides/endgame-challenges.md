## Endgame Challenges Guide — Hard Mode, Gauntlet & Secret Bosses

Once you finish the chapter campaign, Sephiria's endgame revolves around three challenge pillars: Hard Mode runs, the endless Gauntlet, and the hidden secret bosses. Each rewards different currencies and materials, and each demands a different build philosophy. This guide explains how to approach all three.

### Endgame Challenge Overview

| Challenge | Unlock | Best Reward | Difficulty |
|-----------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Hard Mode | Clear all chapters | Hard-exclusive relics | High |
| Gauntlet Mode | Reach Chapter 4 | Gauntlet tokens | Medium |
| Secret Bosses | Specific conditions per boss | Unique blade arts | Extreme |
| New Game+ | Clear Hard Mode | NG+ relics, +stats | Very High |

### Hard Mode Modifiers

1. **Enemy scaling:** All enemies gain +40% HP and +25% damage, with new attack patterns on elite units.
2. **Relic restriction:** Two random relic slots are locked per run — plan for build flexibility.
3. **Hard-exclusive relics:** Drop only from Hard Mode chapter bosses; they enable the strongest endgame builds.
4. **Parry timing:** Perfect parry windows shrink by 2 frames in Hard Mode. Re-train your timings before committing.

### Gauntlet Strategies

- **Wave pacing:** Waves 1-10 are warm-up — build your combo, don't spend resources. Waves 11+ require active relic use.
- **Relic priority in gauntlet:** Sustain relics (heal-on-kill, damage reduction) outperform raw damage after wave 15.
- **Elemental rotation:** The gauntlet rotates elemental resistances every 5 waves. Keep two damage types equipped.
- **Token spending:** Spend gauntlet tokens on the Gauntlet Vendor's rotating relic — it cycles the strongest relics each week.

### Secret Boss Unlock Conditions

| Boss | Unlock Condition | Unique Drop |
|------|------------------|-------------|
| Veilwalker | Clear Chapter 3 without taking fire damage | Veil Blade Art |
| The Twin Echo | Win 10 gauntlet runs | Echo Relic |
| Hollow Monarch | Reach floor 50 in gauntlet, then touch the empty throne | Monarch Blade Art |

### Endgame Progression Order

1. Clear the campaign, then immediately attempt Hard Mode to unlock NG+.
2. Run the gauntlet to floor 30 weekly for tokens and sustain relics.
3. Hunt secret bosses once your build has two damage types covered.
4. Enter New Game+ with Hard-exclusive relics equipped for maximum stat growth.

### Resource Priorities

| Resource | Best Use | Wasteful Use |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| Gauntlet tokens | Weekly vendor relic | Buying cosmetic flairs |
| Hard-mode relics | Endgame build cores | Equipping on story gear |
| Blade art drops | Matching your element | Selling for gold |
| NG+ stat points | Cover build weaknesses | Spreading across stats |

- **Token discipline:** The Gauntlet Vendor rotates relics weekly — save tokens for the week your build's core relic appears.
- **Hard-mode exclusives:** Never use hard-mode relic drops to enhance story relics; they are the endgame's permanent power floor.
- **Stat point rule:** In NG+, dump points into your two core stats first; hybrid builds underperform against single-purpose endgame builds.
