# Greatsword Build Guide — Stagger Chains, Boss Shutdown & the 1.0 Weapons (A-Tier)

**Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: August 4, 2026**

The Greatsword is the **raw damage and stagger weapon** of Sephiria — A-tier in the 1.0 tier list, and the community's favorite answer to boss fights. Where the Staff wins on element stacking and the Crossbow wins on safety, the Greatsword wins by **not letting bosses play the game**: its stagger chains lock enemies down and turn fights into mechanics drills.

The 1.0 launch was a great patch for this weapon — it shipped two standout greatsword items (**Thor Greatsword S3G** and **Red Snake Crusher**) that the tier list calls out by name, and the balance patch's fire/corrosion attention gave heavy-element builds real options.

## Why the Greatsword Is A-Tier in 1.0

From the [Weapons Tier List (1.0)](/guides/weapons-tier-list):

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Standout 1.0 weapons** | **Thor Greatsword S3G** and **Red Snake Crusher** are called out as the standouts of the 1.0 weapon batch |
| **Stagger chains** | Heavy stagger makes boss fights mechanical — bosses spend more time stunned than attacking |
| **Raw damage** | The simplest scaling axis in the game: big hits, big numbers |
| **Best For** | Boss rushing — the weapon whose output is front-loaded into fights |

The honest trade: the Greatsword is **Medium difficulty** — heavy attacks commit you to animations, and if you miss the stagger window you eat the counterattack. It's a weapon that punishes sloppy timing and rewards pattern knowledge.

## The Standout 1.0 Weapons

Two named weapons anchor the Greatsword's 1.0 build:

- **Thor Greatsword S3G** — the poster child of the 1.0 greatsword lineup. Watch for it in the weapon pool and build around it when it drops.
- **Red Snake Crusher** — the other standout. Different identity (the name says it: crushing, pressure-based) but the same tier-list endorsement.

Both weapons are part of the 1.0 branch expansion — all six weapon branches received new upgrade lines at launch, totaling 200+ upgrades. If you see either of these in a run, they're your sign to commit to the Greatsword that run.

## The Core Mechanic: Stagger Chains

The Greatsword's identity is **stagger**. Bosses in Sephiria have stagger windows between attack patterns — and the Greatsword is the weapon built to exploit them:

- **Lock down the boss.** Chain stagger into stagger so the boss spends more time interrupted than attacking. This is why the community calls the Greatsword "mechanical" in boss fights.
- **Watch for the windows.** Every boss cycles attack patterns → stagger window → attack patterns. The Greatsword's job is converting each window into as much damage as possible.
- **Don't overcommit.** The weapon's weakness is animation commitment — a whiffed heavy attack during a boss's wind-up is how Greatsword players die. Learn each boss's tells (the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) and [Boss Weakness Chart](/guides/boss-weakness-chart) cover the cast).

**Build direction:** raw damage, with element support from tablets/artifacts. The 1.0 balance patch made fire and corrosion viable — a fire or corrosion-flavored Greatsword with the right tablet set is a legitimate endgame build.

## The Final Boss (Chapter 6) — The Greatsword's Best Fight

The Chapter 6 ending fight is a bullet-hell, and the **Greatsword is one of the two community-top picks for it** (with the Crossbow):

- **"Stagger windows exist between attack patterns — Greatsword's stagger chains are the community's favorite answer here."** That's the exact verdict from the [Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide](/guides/chapter-6-final-boss-guide). The final boss has patterns you can't out-damage — you *can* interrupt them.
- **Phase 1 (bullet-hell) and Phase 2 (pillar summons):** 1.0.21 made full-screen attacks telegraphed/blockable and cut pillar summon HP by 33%. The Greatsword's stagger is the answer to Phase 1's barrage gaps, and its raw damage deletes the pillars in Phase 2.
- **Block during wind-ups.** Even on the Greatsword, keep your block key ready during the wind-up phases — the universal 1.0.21-era tip.

If your goal is "beat the final boss," this is the community's weapon of choice.

## Hard Mode

The Greatsword in Hard Mode is a high-risk, high-reward proposition — the 60-level climb's modifiers test its two weaknesses:

| Modifier | How to Play It |
|----------|----------------|
| **[Demon] Blood Festival** (lifesteal, party-scaled) | Stagger before you trade — locked-down enemies can't lifesteal. Commit to a window, get out |
| **[Plan] Tension** (no potions in boss fights) | The Greatsword's weakness: no potions + animation commitment = death. Learn the boss patterns on Normal first (see the Hard Mode guide's prep advice) |
| **[Demon] Pseudo-Vanguard elites** | Stagger chains are even more valuable against elites — shut them down before they activate their buffs |
| **[Plan] Heavy Heart** (carry junk artifacts) | Skip junk artifact pickups; keep the pool clean so the Mystic Pot feed stays efficient |

The 1.0.21 party-scaling fix (Blood Festival: 2P 66%, 3P 50%, 4P 33%) makes co-op Hard Mode fairer — and in co-op, the Greatsword is the designated boss-killer while the Sword & Shield player holds aggro.

## Co-op

The Greatsword is the **boss-killer role** in 1-4 player co-op. In a four-man team:

| Role | Weapon |
|------|--------|
| Front line / aggro | Sword & Shield |
| Boss damage / stagger | **Greatsword** |
| Room control / AoE | Staff |
| Safe DPS / backup | Crossbow |

Your job: stagger the boss while the S&S player eats its counterattacks and the casters layer damage. The division of labor is why the community calls Greatsword + Sword & Shield the classic bossing pair.

## Quick Build Checklist

1. Commit to the Greatsword when Thor Greatsword S3G or Red Snake Crusher drops
2. Learn stagger windows — the weapon's entire identity is converting them into damage
3. Match tablets/artifacts to one element (fire/corrosion is viable post-1.0; the Mystic Pot curates the pool)
4. For the final boss: stagger the phases, block the wind-ups, delete the pillars
5. In Hard Mode, respect Tension — practice patterns on Normal before the no-potion fights

## FAQ

**Q: Is the Greatsword good in 1.0?**
A-tier, and the 1.0 weapon batch is a big part of why — Thor Greatsword S3G and Red Snake Crusher are standout launch weapons.

**Q: Is the Greatsword the best weapon for the final boss?**
It's the community's top pick along with the Crossbow — its stagger chains interrupt the ending fight's attack patterns, and 1.0.21/1.0.22 made the fight fairer to play.

**Q: Is the Greatsword good for beginners?**
Medium difficulty. The stagger mechanic is forgiving once you learn boss patterns, but the animation commitment punishes sloppy timing. New players are usually pointed at Sword & Shield first.

**Q: What element should I build on the Greatsword?**
The 1.0 balance patch made fire and corrosion viable. Build the element your tablets and artifacts support — the weapon itself is element-flexible, its identity is stagger.
