# Sephiria Price Increase Went Live — It's $17.99 Now (Was It Worth Buying Early?)

> **Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: September 16, 2026**

TEAM HORAY posted a **Price Update Notice** on the Sephiria Steam hub on September 12, 2026, and the increase it described **took effect on schedule**. Sephiria moved from **$14.99 to $17.99**, with matching rises in every other region, at **2:00 PM KST on September 14, 2026**. We re-read the store page on **September 16, 2026**: it shows **$17.99 with no discount**. [Official]

## The short answer

- **The increase is live.** It landed **September 14, 2026 at 2:00 PM KST** — **05:00 UTC on September 14**, which was **10:00 PM PDT on September 13** and **1:00 AM EDT on September 14** in North America. [Editorial, converted from the official time]
- **US price: $14.99 → $17.99** — a **$3.00 rise, roughly +20%**, confirmed on the store page on September 16, 2026. [Official]
- **This was signposted.** TEAM HORAY announced on July 29, 2026 that once the launch discount ended and Steam's one-month price-change cooldown passed, the price would go up. [Official]
- **Bundles are the one remaining discount route.** Sephiria is in three bundles, each at **-10%**, which prices the game at **$16.19** rather than $17.99 if you buy through one. [Official, store page bundle data]
- **There is a free demo**, so you can test the opening stretch before committing. [Official]
- **No season pass, expansion or DLC is listed** as of September 16, 2026 — features are single-player, online co-op, Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud, so $17.99 is the whole cost. [Official]

The notice describes these as "some examples" of the new regional pricing, so other currencies have moved too — your region's store page is the authoritative number at the moment you buy. [Official]

## Why the price went up at all

The July 29, 2026 "Launch Time Details" announcement — published a day before the 1.0 release — said plainly that after the launch discount ended and Steam's one-month price-change cooldown had passed, **the price would be increased**. [Official]

So September 14, 2026 was the last step of a plan about six weeks old: launch discount → cooldown → permanent rise. The practical point is that $14.99 was always a launch-window price, not the game's steady-state price. [Editorial]

The official store description for app 2436940 lists the contents:

- **60+ distinct enemies and 10+ bosses** across **6 chapters**
- **A grid inventory system** built on Artifacts and Tablets — the arranging puzzle is the game's signature, not a side menu
- **Online co-op for up to 4 players**, with item trading and revives
- **Single-player, Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud**; **Windows and macOS** builds; a **2 GB** install
- **Released July 31, 2026**, by **TEAM HORAY**, the studio behind Dungreed

All from the store page, read September 16, 2026. [Official]

The **$16.19** figure comes from the store page's own package data for app 2436940 (`m_nFinalPriceWithBundleDiscount` 1619 on package 876926) — it is Steam's bundle arithmetic, not ours, and it is what you pay for Sephiria's share if you buy a bundle rather than the game alone. That matters for the "is it worth it" question: if you were going to buy Dungreed or Heroes of Hammerwatch II anyway, the increase is largely cancelled out. If you were not, the bundle is not a saving — it is $25+ instead of $17.99. The Dungreed bundle is the natural one to consider, since Dungreed is TEAM HORAY's own earlier roguelite and the closest thing to a like-for-like sample of the studio's design. [Editorial]

Cost per hour is the fair way to read a price rise, and Sephiria is awkward to attack on that measure. Owner playtimes in public reviews run from under 20 hours to 230 hours, and the permanent progression layers ([Talents](/guides/talents-guide), [Inscriptions](/guides/inscriptions-guide), weapon mastery, [Hard Mode](/guides/hard-mode-guide) to level 60) are built for replay rather than a single clear. ## What to weigh before you pay more

None of these are deal-breakers for everyone, but all are documented:

- **Long-distance co-op is the weakest part of the package.** Attacks, blocks and enemy hitboxes are server-authoritative, and a heavily-upvoted negative review (August 11, 2026, 99 helpful votes) describes a 2–3 second lag spike ending an hour-long run; reviews from August 13 and September 6, 2026 say the same. Cross-region play is where it hurts most. Our [co-op freeze & disconnect recovery](/guides/coop-freeze-recovery-guide) and [multiplayer connection fixes](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide) cover the workarounds. [Community]
- **There is a history of losing progress.** A September 11, 2026 review reads "deleted all my data due to an error with my save file", and an August 11, 2026 review reports progression loss on crash. Back up before a long run — see the [save file guide](/guides/save-file-guide). [Community / Official]
- **A bug is still open as of 1.0.33.** The developers' September 11, 2026 update opens with a Known Issues note: enemies moving outside of walls, suspected to come from 1.0.31's collision changes, not yet reproduced in-house, with the team asking players for video. Unresolved as of September 16, 2026 — no patch has shipped since 1.0.33, so the price increase did not arrive with a fix. [Official]
- **Late runs get visually loud.** An August 23, 2026 review at 40.3 hours describes chakram, pet and AoE builds turning the screen into a cluster that is hard to read. [Community]
- **The difficulty curve can invert.** Reviewers argue the game gets easier once a strong build comes together (September 9 and September 1, 2026), and that a [costume](/guides/costume-stats-and-character-unlocks-guide) pick commits you to a stat package for a whole run (August 30, 2026). [Community]

## Was it worth buying before the deadline? (retrospective)

The deadline has passed, so this section is now a retrospective rather than advice — here is how the call looks with the increase live and the store page at $17.99.

**Buying early was the right call** for anyone who already knew they wanted it, and it is the one decision that is now settled: $3.00 saved in the US, comparable elsewhere, and no discount on the standalone game since. Waiting cost you nothing else — nothing in the game changed on September 14. The build shipping today is the same one that shipped before the deadline; the most recent patch (1.0.33, September 11, 2026) predates the increase by three days. [Editorial / Official]

**The reasons to hesitate are unchanged.** Cross-region co-op is still the loudest complaint in the review pool, and a price rise does not touch it; one-playthrough buyers still face $17.99 for roughly a 30-hour experience. Play the free demo before deciding. [Editorial / Community]

**If you are still deciding:** the bundles above are the only discount on the table, at $16.19 for Sephiria's share. Whether the game returns to $14.99 or lower in a future Steam sale is still **[Unconfirmed]** — no sale schedule has been published, and the only discount we can verify is the launch discount announced July 29, 2026. Treating the current price as the floor remains the conservative read. [Editorial]

## FAQ

**When exactly did the price increase happen?**
September 14, 2026 at 2:00 PM KST — 05:00 UTC on September 14, or the evening of September 13 in the Americas. It took effect on schedule: the store page reads $17.99 with no discount as of September 16, 2026. [Official]

**How much is Sephiria going up?**
$14.99 to $17.99 in the US, about +20%. The euro rises €14.79 to €18.25, yuan ¥58 to ¥68, yen ¥1,700 to ¥1,980, and won ₩16,500 to ₩19,000. [Official]

**Was this announced in advance?**
Yes. The July 29, 2026 Launch Time Details announcement said the price would rise once the launch discount ended and Steam's one-month price-change cooldown passed. [Official]

**Is Sephiria worth $17.99?**
Owners who have played it longest say yes without qualification; owners who finished one 30-hour run tend to price it nearer $10–$15. If you expect to replay it the new price is easy to justify; if you expect one clear, it is closer to the line. [Community / Editorial]

**Is there DLC or a season pass on top?**
None listed as of September 16, 2026 — but the game is in three -10% bundles (Heroes of Hammerwatch II, Dungreed, Ascend to ZERO), which is the only way to currently pay under $17.99. And yes — a free demo is available from the store page, so you can try it before you pay anything. [Official]

**Did anything else change when the price went up?**
No. The most recent update is 1.0.33 on September 11, 2026, three days before the increase, and no patch has shipped since. The September 14 move was commercial only. [Official]
