# Sephiria Price Increase — What Changes on September 14, 2026 (and Is It Still Worth $17.99?)

> **Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: September 13, 2026**

TEAM HORAY posted a **Price Update Notice** on the Sephiria Steam hub on September 12, 2026. Sephiria goes from **$14.99 to $17.99**, with matching rises in every other region, at **2:00 PM KST on September 14, 2026**. The store page still showed **$14.99 with no discount** when we read it on September 13. [Official]

## The short answer

- **The increase lands September 14, 2026 at 2:00 PM KST** — **05:00 UTC on September 14**, which is **10:00 PM PDT on September 13** and **1:00 AM EDT on September 14** in North America. [Editorial, converted from the official time]
- **US price: $14.99 → $17.99** — a **$3.00 rise, roughly +20%**. [Editorial arithmetic]
- **This was signposted.** TEAM HORAY announced on July 29, 2026 that once the launch discount ended and Steam's one-month price-change cooldown passed, the price would go up. [Official]
- **There is a free demo**, so you can test the opening stretch before committing. [Official]
- **No season pass, expansion or DLC is listed** as of September 13, 2026 — features are single-player, online co-op, Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud, so $17.99 is the whole cost. [Official]

## Why the price is going up at all

The July 29, 2026 "Launch Time Details" announcement — published a day before the 1.0 release — said plainly that after the launch discount ended and Steam's one-month price-change cooldown had passed, **the price would be increased**. [Official]

So September 14 is the last step of a plan about six weeks old: launch discount → cooldown → permanent rise. The practical point is that $14.99 was always a launch-window price, not the game's steady-state price. [Editorial]

## What you are actually buying at $17.99

The official store description for app 2436940 lists the contents:

- **6 weapons, each with over 50 unique upgrades** — permanent meta-progression, not per-run picks
- **60+ distinct enemies and 10+ bosses** across **6 chapters**
- **A grid inventory system** built on Artifacts and Tablets — the arranging puzzle is the game's signature, not a side menu
- **Online co-op for up to 4 players**, with item trading and revives
- **Single-player, Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud**; **Windows and macOS** builds; a **2 GB** install
- **Released July 31, 2026**, by **TEAM HORAY**, the studio behind Dungreed

All from the store page, read September 13, 2026. [Official]

Cost per hour is the fair way to read a price rise, and Sephiria is awkward to attack on that measure. Owner playtimes in public reviews run from under 20 hours to 230 hours, and the permanent progression layers ([Talents](/guides/talents-guide), [Inscriptions](/guides/inscriptions-guide), weapon mastery, [Hard Mode](/guides/hard-mode-guide) to level 60) are built for replay rather than a single clear. See the [Game Length guide](/guides/game-length-guide) for pacing. [Community / Editorial]

## What to weigh before you pay more

None of these are deal-breakers for everyone, but all are documented:

- **Long-distance co-op is the weakest part of the package.** Attacks, blocks and enemy hitboxes are server-authoritative, and a heavily-upvoted negative review (August 11, 2026, 99 helpful votes) describes a 2–3 second lag spike ending an hour-long run; reviews from August 13 and September 6, 2026 say the same. Cross-region play is where it hurts most. Our [co-op freeze & disconnect recovery](/guides/coop-freeze-recovery-guide) and [multiplayer connection fixes](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide) cover the workarounds. [Community]
- **There is a history of losing progress.** A September 11, 2026 review reads "deleted all my data due to an error with my save file", and an August 11, 2026 review reports progression loss on crash. Back up before a long run — see the [save file guide](/guides/save-file-guide). [Community / Official]
- **A bug is still open as of 1.0.33.** The developers' September 11, 2026 update opens with a Known Issues note: enemies moving outside of walls, suspected to come from 1.0.31's collision changes, not yet reproduced in-house, with the team asking players for video. Unresolved as of September 13, 2026. [Official]
- **Late runs get visually loud.** An August 23, 2026 review at 40.3 hours describes chakram, pet and AoE builds turning the screen into a cluster that is hard to read. [Community]
- **The difficulty curve can invert.** Reviewers argue the game gets easier once a strong build comes together (September 9 and September 1, 2026), and that a [costume](/guides/costume-stats-and-character-unlocks-guide) pick commits you to a stat package for a whole run (August 30, 2026). [Community]

## Buy now or wait?

**Buy before the deadline if** you already know you want it (the savings are $3.00 in the US and comparable elsewhere), you play roguelites for the long tail, or you want co-op with people in your own region — that is where the netcode behaves. [Editorial]

**Wait if** you would be buying mainly for cross-region co-op (the loudest complaint in the review pool, and a price rise does not change it), or you only want one playthrough, in which case $17.99 is a different proposition than $14.99. Try the free demo first in either case. [Editorial]

**On waiting for a discount:** whether Sephiria returns to $14.99 or lower in a future Steam sale is **[Unconfirmed]** — no schedule has been published, and the only discount we can verify is the launch discount announced July 29, 2026. Treating the current price as the floor is the conservative read. [Editorial]
