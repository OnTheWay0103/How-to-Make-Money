# Sephiria Boss Rush Mode — The Endgame Gauntlet of the Tower

**Sephiria / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Boss Rush is Sephiria's endgame exam: every chapter boss back-to-back with no healing shrines between fights. It is also the game's most reliable source of **Legendary relics** — the drop table explicitly includes boss rush as a legendary source alongside Chapter 5+ and secret bosses. This guide covers the unlock, the format, the route, and the loadouts that survive the full set.

---

## 1. Unlock Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Story progress | Complete all 6 chapters at least once |
| Difficulty | Available in Normal, Hard, and New Game+ variants |
| Entry cost | Paid in Memoriam Shards (same currency as Gauntlet entry) |
| Location | Tower Hub — talk to the Gauntlet Master (the same NPC who runs Gauntlet Mode) |

If you haven't cleared all six chapters yet, see the [Chapter Guide](/guides/chapter-guide) — and don't attempt Boss Rush until Chapter 4's Umbrawisp is comfortable, because the rush doubles down on every boss's hardest mechanics.

---

## 2. Format and Rules

- **Fixed loadout** — weapon, blade arts, relics, and inscriptions are locked at the start. You cannot swap mid-run.
- **No rest points** — health and Focus carry over between fights, exactly like Gauntlet Mode.
- **Consecutive fights** — all six chapter bosses in sequence, plus a bonus fight if you're on Hard/NG+ (the secret bosses begin appearing here).
- **One life** — death ends the run. There are no retries.
- **Scoring** — clear time, damage taken, and parry count feed a score that determines rank and bonus rewards.

---

## 3. The Boss Order and What Each Fight Tests

| Boss | Chapter | What the Rush Tests | Fights That End Runs |
|------|---------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Golemnir, the Rootsoul | 1 | Movement discipline (Root Slam dodges) | — |
| Whisperia, Echo Queen | 2 | Clone identification under pressure | — |
| Bibliarch, the Warden | 3 | Range and stamina patience | — |
| Umbrawisp, Twilight Walker | 4 | Pure dodge uptime — no healing between fights makes chip damage fatal | Yes |
| Infernis, the Forge Tyrant | 5 | Fire-element burst check — kill speed vs. sustained AoE | Yes |
| Sephiria, the Tower Heart | 6 | Everything, back to back | The classic run-ender |

**Route logic:** the first three fights are recovery fights — if you're healthy entering Bibliarch, you can afford to play aggressively there and bank the kill. Umbrawisp onward is pure survival: chip damage compounds because there is no shrine.

## 4. Best Loadouts by Weapon Family

| Weapon | Why It Works in Boss Rush | Key Relics | Blade Arts |
|--------|---------------------------|-----------|------------|
| Staff | Ranged safety preserves health through the early fights | Echo Orb, Mana Font | Wide-arc AoE for adds, single-target for bosses |
| Sword | Sanctuary Seal turns blocks into healing — sustain beats burst here | Sanctuary Seal, Reflective Shield | Parry arts for Whisperia's clones |
| Bow | Longest range, safest against Umbrawisp's twilight phases | Quiver of Endless Arrows, Frost Heart | Elemental volley |
| Fist | Vampiric Fang sustain for the back half of the rush | Vampiric Fang | Fast multi-hit arts for the parry count |

**The universal priority:** sustain relics outperform damage relics in Boss Rush. Echo Orb's double-cast is great — but it can't save you from a 30% health Umbrawisp. See the [Best Relic Combos Guide](/guides/best-relic-combos) for full pairings.

## 5. Scoring and Rewards

| Rank | Score Threshold | Bonus Rewards |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Bronze | Clear the rush | Legendary relic drop chance, Memoriam Shards |
| Silver | Clear with <3 deaths total taken damage | Guaranteed Epic relic + rush tokens |
| Gold | Clear with zero deaths, parry count 25+ | Guaranteed Legendary relic, exclusive rush token shop items |

**Reward farming:** the first clear each week guarantees a Legendary relic — that weekly reset is your highest-value repeatable in the endgame. The rush token shop (run by the Gauntlet Master) also cycles the same rotating relics as the Gauntlet vendor, so check both before spending.

## 6. Common Mistakes

1. **Bringing a glass-cannon loadout.** Damage builds clear Boss Rush fast — right up until Infernis's fire phase one-shots a 40% health player. Sustain first.
2. **Playing the early fights greedily.** Golemnir through Bibliarch are warm-ups; taking damage there is free damage against the back half.
3. **Ignoring parry scoring.** Gold rank needs a 25+ parry count — parry aggressively against Whisperia's clones even when killing them faster would be easier.
4. **Entering at low Memoriam Shards.** The entry fee is real, and the weekly legendary clear is worth more than three failed runs.
5. **Using Gauntlet muscle memory.** Gauntlet waves teach AoE spam; boss rush punishes it — single-target blade arts outperform here.

**Final note:** Boss Rush is the natural stepping stone between the campaign and secret bosses — by the time you can clear it on Hard, the [Secret Endings Guide](/guides/secret-endings) conditions (like Veilwalker's no-fire-damage clear) are within reach.
