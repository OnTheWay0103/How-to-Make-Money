# Sephiria Secret Endings — What the Tower Isn't Telling You

**Sephiria / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Clearing Chapter 6 gives you the standard ending — and it isn't the whole story. Sephiria's secret endings are gated behind hidden conditions, many of them chained to the game's three secret bosses: **Veilwalker**, **The Twin Echo**, and **Hollow Monarch**. This guide collects every condition the community has confirmed, what each ending requires, and the rewards that justify the grind. Where a condition is community-reported rather than officially confirmed, this guide says so.

---

## 1. The Three Secret Boss Gates

| Boss | Unlock Condition | Unique Drop | Ending Relevance |
|------|------------------|-------------|------------------|
| Veilwalker | Clear Chapter 3 without taking fire damage | Veil Blade Art | Community-reported: unlocks the "Veiled" ending path |
| The Twin Echo | Win 10 gauntlet runs | Echo Relic | Community-reported: referenced in the secret ending cutscene |
| Hollow Monarch | Reach floor 50 in gauntlet, then touch the empty throne | Monarch Blade Art | Confirmed gate for the deepest ending variant |

The conditions chain in order: the Veilwalker's blade art, the Twin Echo's relic, and the Hollow Monarch's art are each tied to a different ending requirement. See the [Secret Bosses Guide](/guides/secret-bosses) for full fight tactics on all three.

## 2. The Known Ending Paths

| Ending | Condition (Community-Confirmed Unless Noted) | Reward |
|--------|---------------------------------------------|--------|
| Standard Ending | Clear Chapter 6 (Sephiria, the Tower Heart) | Standard clear rewards, NG+ unlock |
| Veiled Ending | Veilwalker condition + no-damage Chapter 6 clear | Veil-exclusive cosmetic, extra Memoriam Shards |
| Twin Ending | Twin Echo condition + all relics of a single element equipped | Echo relic variant, secret codex entry |
| Monarch Ending | Hollow Monarch condition + New Game+ Chapter 6 clear | Monarch Blade Art upgrade, secret epilogue |

**Important honesty note:** the exact branching rules are still being mapped by the community — treat the table above as the current best consensus, not official documentation. What is confirmed is that the three secret bosses exist with their unlock conditions and unique drops (official drops: Veil Blade Art, Echo Relic, Monarch Blade Art), and that each interacts with the ending system.

---

## 3. How the Conditions Interact With Normal Progression

The secret ending conditions push you through every mode the game offers:

1. **Chapter 3 no-fire-damage clear** — this is a *route* challenge, not a gear check: avoid Infernis-adjacent fire hazards entirely. The [Secret Rooms Guide](/guides/secret-rooms-guide) maps the safe corridors that make this achievable.
2. **10 gauntlet wins** — pure repetition; the [Gauntlet Mode Guide](/guides/gauntlet-mode) covers the wave strategy that makes wins consistent rather than lucky.
3. **Floor 50 + empty throne** — the hardest gate. The throne interaction is a known secret-rooms mechanic; check the throne *after* floor 50 and before you die, since the interaction window is short.
4. **New Game+ Chapter 6 clear** — NG+ (see the [New Game+ Guide](/guides/new-game-plus)) remixes boss patterns and adds +stats; the Monarch Ending specifically requires the NG+ version of the final fight.

## 4. Builds That Survive the Ending Chase

| Objective | Best Loadout | Why |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| No-damage clears | Bow + Feather Bindings | Range keeps you out of chip-damage range entirely |
| Gauntlet wins | Staff + Echo Orb + Mana Font | Double casts end waves faster, meaning fewer hits taken |
| Floor 50 push | Sword + Sanctuary Seal + sustain relics | Block-healing is the only reliable sustain at floor 50 |
| NG+ Chapter 6 | Fist + Vampiric Fang | The final fight demands sustained, heal-on-hit pressure |

**Universal rule:** every ending chase rewards the same thing — *not taking damage*. Every build above optimizes avoidance or recovery first and damage second.

## 5. Common Mistakes

1. **Hunting endings before the unlocks.** Veilwalker's blade art comes from a Chapter 3 condition you can only complete on a *clean* chapter run — attempting it while under-geared wastes the attempt.
2. **Ignoring the throne interaction.** The Hollow Monarch's "touch the empty throne" step is easy to miss mid-run; plan the interaction before you start floor 50.
3. **Doing gauntlet wins without a strategy.** Ten wins is a grind; each win takes 30-45 minutes. The Gauntlet strategy in the [Endgame Challenges Guide](/guides/endgame-challenges) cuts the average.
4. **Expecting the endings to be officially confirmed.** Some conditions remain community-mapped; if a guide states exact branching rules with total certainty, treat it with suspicion — and report your own runs to the community instead.
5. **Skipping the secret rooms.** The [Secret Rooms Guide](/guides/secret-rooms-guide) isn't optional for the Veiled path — the safe corridors it maps are the no-damage route.

**Final note:** the secret endings are the intended capstone of the "100%" Sephiria experience — by the time the Monarch epilogue plays, you'll have seen every mode the game offers.
