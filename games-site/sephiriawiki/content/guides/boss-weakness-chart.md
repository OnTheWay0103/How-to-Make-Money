## Sephiria Boss Weakness Chart (1.0) — Verified Bosses & Fight Prep

> Version 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026

**An honesty note first:** Sephiria 1.0 has **10+ unique bosses across 6 chapters**, but official sources have only confirmed a handful of names. This chart uses **verified names only** and marks everything else as unconfirmed — older pages that named every boss (Golemnir, Whisperia, etc.) were not accurate. Sephiria is also a **bullet-hell-style action roguelite**: most boss fights are pattern gauntlets where **blocking matters as much as dodging** (1.0.21 made full-screen volleys telegraphed and blockable in all directions).

## Verified Boss Reworks (1.0 & 1.0.21)

The 1.0 launch (July 31, Patch 1.0.19) and the same-day 1.0.21 hotfix reworked several boss fights. Know what changed before you fight:

| Boss | 1.0 / 1.0.21 Change | What It Means for You |
|------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| **Askard** | Projectiles are now **blockable in all directions**; warning/telegraph indicators fixed | Keep your block key ready during the wind-up phases — blocking, not just dodging, is now the correct answer |
| **Qliphoth** | Hitbox leniency improved; **fewer projectiles** | The fight is fairer in 1.0 — your old EA dodge timings still mostly work, with more room for error |
| **Spinning Staff Demon** | Base form **simplified** and moved into **Hard Mode** | If you're on Normal and remembered this as a hard fight — it no longer appears on Normal |
| **Pillar Summoner** | **HP -33%** (1.0.21) | The endgame summoner fight is now much faster to kill — focus the summoned pillars quickly and it melts |
| **Final boss** | Full-screen attacks are now **properly telegraphed and blockable/dodgeable in all directions** | The #1 pre-1.0.21 complaint (un-telegraphable full-screen volleys) is fixed; the fight went from unfair to hard-but-learnable |

## Boss-Fight Prep — the Four Verified Build Systems

Sephiria builds layer four systems, and every boss fight rewards having all four coherent:

| Layer | What It Does for Boss Fights | Where to Learn More |
|-------|------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Weapon + upgrade path** | 6 weapon branches, **200+ upgrades**. Greatsword = stagger chains (community favorite vs bosses); Crossbow = safe ranged DPS | [Weapons Tier List](/guides/weapons-tier-list) |
| **Artifacts + Tablets** | Grid inventory — artifacts are empowered by the tablets slotted around them; pick the element synergy your tablets support | [Artifacts Guide](/guides/artifacts-guide) |
| **Talents** | Permanent trees (Combat / Resilience / Utility) — boss-relevant picks are damage, sustain and co-op revives | [Talents Guide](/guides/talents-guide) |
| **Miracle abilities** | Run-unlocked special abilities that change how a build executes — cooldown talents keep your burst up for phase windows | [Build Archetypes Chart](/guides/build-archetypes-chart) |

**General boss rules that hold across all 10+ fights:**

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Block, don't only dodge** | Post-1.0.21, full-screen and all-direction barrages are blockable — blocking is the meta answer |
| **Expect a fury phase around 50% HP** | Most bosses change behavior at roughly half health — save your burst for the phase shift |
| **Learn the tell, then punish** | Boss patterns are readable by design; the 1.0.21 telegraph fixes made this even more true |
| **Overgear before Hard Mode** | Hard Mode returns all chapter bosses with tuned-up stats |

## Hard Mode Boss Modifiers (1.0)

Hard Mode extends the climb to **level 60** and adds **6 new modifiers** — the two that change boss fights the most:

| Modifier | Effect on Boss Fights | Counter |
|----------|------------------------|---------|
| **[Plan] Tension** | **No potions during boss fights** | Overheal before the door; lean on Miracle cooldowns and defensive talents |
| **[Demon] Blood Feast** | Enemies (incl. bosses) heal via lifesteal | Kill adds first so the boss has nothing to heal from; 1.0.21 fixed its party-scaling in co-op |
| **[Demon] Pseudo-Vanguard** | Elite enemies buffed | Focused single-target damage (Greatsword stagger) over clear-speed |
| **[Plan] Deterioration / Heavy Heart** | Boss/handicap pressure (incl. carrying a junk artifact) | Audit your inventory before the fight — drop the dead weight |
| **[World] Proliferation** | More enemies per fight | AoE (Staff) clears the room so you can 1v1 the boss |

## FAQ

**Q: How many bosses are in Sephiria?**
10+ unique bosses across 6 chapters (one per chapter, 6 mandatory) plus optional encounters. Chapter 6 added 2 new bosses, 1 mini-boss and the final boss.

**Q: Which bosses have confirmed names?**
Askard, Qliphoth, Spinning Staff Demon and Pillar Summoner. The rest are unconfirmed in public sources — we mark them honestly instead of inventing names.

**Q: Is the final boss in Chapter 6?**
Yes — beating it unlocks the ending and credits. See the [Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide](/guides/chapter-6-final-boss-guide) for the full fight breakdown.

**Q: What changed in the 1.0.21 hotfix for bosses?**
Final boss telegraphing + all-direction blocking, Pillar Summoner HP -33%, full-screen attacks dodgeable/blockable, and Blood Feast party scaling.
