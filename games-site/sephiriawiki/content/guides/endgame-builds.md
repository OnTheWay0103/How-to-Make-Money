## Endgame Build Overview

Once you complete Sephiria's main story and enter New Game+, the difficulty curve steepens dramatically. Enemies hit harder, have more health, and new modifiers appear on elite enemies. To survive, you need a well-optimized build that synergizes your weapon, blade arts, relics, and inscriptions.

This guide covers the most powerful endgame builds, what makes them work, and the stat priorities you should follow for each content type.

## Understanding Endgame Scaling

In the endgame, raw damage is not enough. You need to understand how different stats scale and how they interact with your chosen equipment.

### Stat Priority

| Stat | Priority | Why It Matters |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Focus Capacity | Critical | More Focus = more blade arts = more damage and utility |
| Elemental Damage % | High | Multiplies all damage of that element, including weapon base damage |
| Critical Chance | Medium | Critical hits deal 1.5x damage, some relics proc on crit |
| Critical Damage | Medium | Only valuable if you have at least 40% crit chance |
| Attack Speed | Situational | Great for on-hit builds, wasted on burst builds |
| Defense | Low | Not worth stat investment; learn to dodge instead |

### The Soft Cap System

Most stats have soft caps where diminishing returns kick in:

- **Elemental Damage**: Soft cap at 150%. Hard cap at 300%.
- **Critical Chance**: Soft cap at 60%. Hard cap at 100%.
- **Critical Damage**: Soft cap at 200%. Hard cap at 500%.
- **Attack Speed**: Soft cap at +50%. Hard cap at +100%.

## Top Endgame Builds

### 1. Inferno Berserker (Fire Burst Build)

This build focuses on stacking Fire damage for massive burst windows. Best for players who enjoy aggressive, in-your-face combat.

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Weapon | Emberfang (legendary greatsword) | Converts 50% of physical damage to Fire, applies Burn on crit |
| Light Art | Ember Strike | Synergizes with Fire damage stacking |
| Heavy Art | Void Cascade | Deals Fire damage if infused, covers huge area |
| Special Art | Eclipse Blade | Applies all debuffs including Burn |
| Relic 1 | Fury Shard | +40% Heavy art damage |
| Relic 2 | Everflame Essence | Infusion lasts entire chapter |
| Relic 3 | Phoenix Feather | Revive once per run, +20% Fire damage |
| Inscription | Inferno Mastery | All attacks apply Burn, Burn deals 50% more damage |

**Stat Priority**: Fire Damage % > Focus Capacity > Critical Chance > Attack Speed
**Playstyle**: Infuse with Fire at the start of each room. Use Ember Strike to apply Burn, then spam basic attacks until the gauge is full. Use Void Cascade for room clears and Eclipse Blade for bosses.

### 2. Frost Walker (Ice Control Build)

A control-oriented build that keeps enemies slowed while you pick them off. Excellent for gauntlet mode and hard mode.

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Weapon | Glacial Spear (legendary spear) | Hits multiple enemies, applies Slow on every 3rd hit |
| Light Art | Gale Thrust | Wind pierces, keeps distance |
| Heavy Art | Frost Fang | AoE slow, core crowd control |
| Special Art | Shadow Waltz | Invisibility to reposition |
| Relic 1 | Permafrost Core | Frozen enemies take 2x damage from next hit |
| Relic 2 | Focus Crystal | +2 Focus capacity |
| Relic 3 | Windwalker Boots | Movement speed +20%, synergizes with kiting |
| Inscription | Arctic Dominion | Ice effects last 50% longer, enemies slowed below 50% HP are frozen |

**Stat Priority**: Ice Damage % > Focus Capacity > Attack Speed > Defense
**Playstyle**: Keep enemies at maximum range. Apply Frost Fang to slow approaching groups. Use Gale Thrust to push back enemies that get close. Shadow Waltz to escape when surrounded.

### 3. Stormcaller (Lightning Speed Build)

Maximum attack speed and chain lightning effects. Ideal for clearing rooms quickly.

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Weapon | Storm Edge (legendary dual blades) | Fast attack speed, chain lightning on 5-hit combo |
| Light Art | Thunder Rend | Chain lightning, cooldown resets on kill |
| Heavy Art | Tempest Fury | Multihit, each hit can proc chain lightning |
| Special Art | Moonlit Slash | Wide arc, low cost, spamable |
| Relic 1 | Conductor's Ring | Lightning chains to 2 additional targets |
| Relic 2 | Berserker Charm | +5% attack speed per kill, stacks 10 times |
| Relic 3 | Energy Battery | 10% chance to refund Focus on Lightning crit |
| Inscription | Storm's Embrace | Lightning attacks have 25% chance to stun |

**Stat Priority**: Attack Speed > Lightning Damage % > Critical Chance > Critical Damage
**Playstyle**: Build attack speed stacks early in each room. Once stacked, spam light attacks and use Thunder Rend every time it's off cooldown. The chain lightning clears entire groups.

### 4. Void Knight (Balanced All-Rounder)

The most versatile endgame build, adapting to any situation. Recommended for first-time New Game+ players.

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Weapon | Null Blade (legendary longsword) | Balanced stats, bonus damage to elites |
| Light Art | Moonlit Slash | Reliable AoE for any situation |
| Heavy Art | Stone Breaker | Staggers, opens enemies for follow-up |
| Special Art | Sacred Radiance | Healing, keeps you alive in long fights |
| Relic 1 | Guardian Charm | 20% damage reduction |
| Relic 2 | Infinity Crystal | Slowly generates Focus during combat |
| Relic 3 | Adaptive Shard | Changes element to match enemy weakness |
| Inscription | Versatility | No elemental penalty, blade arts deal 5% more damage per element used |

**Stat Priority**: Focus Capacity > Elemental Damage % > Critical Chance > Defense
**Playstyle**: Adapt to each encounter. Use Stone Breaker to stagger dangerous enemies, Moonlit Slash for groups, and Sacred Radiance to stay healthy. The Adaptive Shard ensures you always hit weaknesses.

## Choosing the Right Build

Consider these factors when picking your build:

- **Gauntlet Mode**: Frost Walker or Void Knight — survivability matters more than burst
- **Boss Hunting**: Inferno Berserker — highest single-target DPS
- **Speed Farming**: Stormcaller — fastest room clear speed
- **Hard Mode**: Void Knight — adaptability is key against unknown modifiers
- **Co-op Play**: Frost Walker — crowd control helps your teammates more than raw damage
