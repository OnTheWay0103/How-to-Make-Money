## What is Sephiria?

Sephiria is a **pixel-art action roguelite** developed by **TEAM HORAY** (the studio behind Dungreed). You play as a rabbit warrior climbing the Tower of Sephiria, fighting through 6 unique chapters against 10+ bosses. The game supports **1-4 player co-op** and has earned a **94% positive rating** on Steam.

**1.0 is live now (launched July 31, 2026)** — this guide covers everything you need to get started in the full release.

## Core Gameplay Loop

Every run in Sephiria follows the same cycle:

1. **Select a weapon** — Pick from 6 weapon branches (200+ upgrades total). The Starting Weapon Choice inscription lets you pick freely; otherwise your starting weapon may be limited
2. **Climb the tower** — Fight through procedurally-arranged rooms across 6 chapters
3. **Collect artifacts** — Artifacts modify your stats and abilities and define each run; tablets in your inventory grid empower the artifacts around them
4. **Equip inscriptions** — Socket permanent bonuses into your gear for passive progression
5. **Defeat the chapter boss** — Each chapter ends with a unique boss encounter
6. **Die or reach the top** — Roguelite permadeath means you restart, but keep permanent upgrades

## Choosing Your First Weapon

As a beginner, weapon choice matters less than you think — all six are viable. However, these are the most forgiving:

| Weapon | Difficulty | Why It's Good for Beginners |
|---|---|---|
| **Sword & Shield** | Easy | Balanced stats, straightforward combos, and a reliable block. The community's default recommendation |
| **Crossbow** | Easy-Medium | Ranged attacks let you observe enemy patterns from safety |
| **Staff** | Medium | Powerful AoE and safe range, but it needs element support to shine |

Avoid the **Dagger** on your first few runs — it requires precise positioning and aggressive play that rewards game knowledge.

## First Few Chapters: What to Expect

### Chapter 1 — The Rooted Depths
The easiest chapter. Enemies are slow and attack patterns are telegraphed. Take your time exploring every room and collecting **2-3 artifacts** before the boss.

### Chapter 2 — The Whispering Halls
Introduces ranged enemies and traps. The difficulty step is moderate. This is where you should start thinking about your **build direction** — what kind of damage type or playstyle you're leaning into.

### Chapter 3 — The Mirrored Library
Boss difficulty spikes here — it's the community's "newbie wall." You should have a coherent artifact setup and an upgraded weapon before attempting the chapter boss. Enemy density increases substantially.

> **Tip for new players:** Don't be afraid to die and restart. Permanent upgrades (unlocked via Inscriptions, Talents, and artifact unlocks) make subsequent runs significantly easier. Dying is learning.

## Essential Survival Tips

| Tip | Details |
|---|---|
| **Block AND dodge** | Blocking is a core answer to attacks, not a last resort — hold block during telegraphed attacks and dodge through gaps in patterns |
| **Explore every room** | Artifacts and weapon upgrades are hidden in side rooms and secret rooms |
| **Read artifact descriptions** | Some artifacts have synergies. An artifact that seems weak alone might be great with the right tablet arrangement |
| **Save healing items** | Don't chug potions after every hit. Save them for boss rooms and emergency situations |
| **Teamwork in co-op** | In co-op, stick together. The game scales enemy health and damage per player. Splitting up makes dealing with packs much harder |

## Permanent Progression

Even when you die, you keep:
- **Unlocked inscriptions** — Permanent bonuses socketed into your gear
- **Talents** — Permanent talent points across the Combat / Resilience / Utility trees
- **Artifact unlocks** — Some artifacts unlock permanently through achievements and join future run pools
- **Weapon mastery** — Using a weapon raises its mastery level, unlocking unique perks

This means every run, even failed ones, makes you stronger.
