# New Game Plus Guide — Rewards, Scaling & Optimal Path

New Game Plus (NG+) unlocks immediately after you defeat the **Lich of the First Flame** on any difficulty. Each NG+ cycle increases enemy stats and loot quality, introduces tier-rare relics, and unlocks **exclusive boss phases** that don't appear in a fresh save.

---

## What Carries Over

The table below shows exactly which progress elements transfer to NG+ and which reset.

| Carries Over | Resets |
|---|---|
| Character level and stat points | Main-story quest progress |
| All unlocked relics (including equipped ones) | Map exploration / fog of war |
| Gold and upgrade materials | Side-quest completion state |
| Weapon mastery levels | NPC affinity scores |
| Recipe / crafting knowledge | Dungeon keys and quest items |
| Cosmetic unlocks and achievements | World-boss kill credit |

> **Note:** Consumables and ammo-type items do **not** carry over. Sell them before starting NG+.

---

## Difficulty Scaling per Cycle

Each NG+ cycle applies a flat multiplier to enemy health, damage, and stagger resistance. The formula is:

**Enemy Power = Base × (1 + 0.35 × NG+ Cycle)**

| Cycle | Enemy HP Multiplier | Enemy Damage Multiplier | New Relic Tiers Unlocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| NG+1 | 1.35× | 1.35× | Tier-7 relics |
| NG+2 | 1.70× | 1.70× | Tier-8 relics |
| NG+3 | 2.05× | 2.05× | Tier-9 relics (cap) |
| NG+4+ | +0.30× each cycle | +0.30× each cycle | No new tiers; drop-rate bonus +5% per cycle |

Gold and relic-drop rates also scale linearly up to **NG+5**, after which only cosmetic rewards improve.

---

## Exclusive NG+ Content

Each NG+ cycle unlocks the following:

- **NG+1** — Alternate final boss (The Hollow Martyr) with a unique relic drop.
- **NG+2** — "Echo Shrines" appear on the map; each contains a mini-boss that drops a **Tier-8 relic**.
- **NG+3** — "Nightmare Blessing" altars let you spend health for permanent stat boosts (+1 STR / DEX per 100 HP spent).
- **NG+4+** — Every boss gains a third phase; defeat them for **Prismatic Shards** used to re-roll relic substats.

---

## Recommended Route

1. **Skip all side content** — Only main-story gates matter for unlocking the next cycle. Revisit side zones in NG+2+ when Echo Shrines spawn.
2. **Equip all HP-increasing relics** — The first few cycles punish glass-cannon builds harder than survivable ones.
3. **Farm the Hollow Martyr (NG+1)** — His relic drop, **Heart of Still Flame**, is BiS for nearly every melee build.
4. **Reset at NG+3** — Tier-9 relics and Nightmare Blessings make NG+3 the sweet spot for power gain vs. time investment. Going beyond is for achievement hunters only.

---

## Tips for Smooth Progression

- **Stockpile upgrade materials** before each cycle — merchant prices increase with each NG+.
- **Avoid elemental relics** in early cycles; raw physical damage outperforms until you can stack two matching elemental boosts.
- **Use the fountain in Eastmere** at the start of each cycle — it grants a 30-minute XP buff that stacks with gear bonuses.
