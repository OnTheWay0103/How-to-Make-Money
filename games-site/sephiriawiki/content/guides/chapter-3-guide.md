# Chapter 3 Guide — How to Beat The Mirrored Library Boss & Survive the Difficulty Spike (1.0)

Chapter 3 is where Sephiria stops being gentle. Players consistently report hitting a wall here — the enemy density jumps, the boss has three phases, and the bullet-hell patterns that were optional in earlier chapters become the main event. If you've died to the Mirrored Library boss five times in a row, you're not alone. This guide breaks down exactly how to push through.

**Last updated: August 9, 2026 (1.0).**

---

## Why Chapter 3 Is the Wall

Three things change at Chapter 3 that catch players off guard:

1. **Enemy bullet density doubles.** Living Books and Mirror Constructs fill the screen with projectiles. In Chapters 1-2 you could dodge individual attacks; Chapter 3 demands you read the whole screen at once.
2. **The boss has three distinct phases.** Most Chapter 1-2 bosses have one phase with a soft enrage. The Chapter 3 boss changes behavior at ~66% and ~33% HP — each phase has different attack patterns you need to learn separately.
3. **Your build hasn't come online yet.** By Chapter 3 you have 2-3 relics and maybe one rare tablet. You don't have the synergies that make Chapters 5-6 manageable. You're fighting the hardest relative challenge in the game.

## Chapter 3 Layout: The Mirrored Library

The Mirrored Library is a **four-floor chapter** with the boss on the fifth floor. Each floor ends with an elite mini-boss.

| Floor | Theme | Key Threat |
|:--:|------|------|
| 1 | Library entrance | Living Books (ranged magic), Animated Shelves (melee) |
| 2 | Reading halls | Mirror Constructs (reflect projectiles), Mage Apparitions (AoE) |
| 3 | Restricted section | Elite: Grand Librarian — first real bullet-hell test |
| 4 | Mirror gallery | Elite: Reflected Self — copies your weapon type; harder for ranged builds |

### Floor Strategy

- **Floors 1-2**: Conservative. Save potions, build gold. Break everything — you want 400+ gold for the pre-boss shop on Floor 5.
- **Floor 3**: First serious test. The Grand Librarian elite fires three-wave projectile patterns. **Block, don't dodge.** The 1.0.21 patch made these patterns properly telegraphed — the red laser shows you where projectiles will land.
- **Floor 4**: The Reflected Self elite copies your weapon. **Melee builds** have an easier time here — the mirror's melee pattern is predictable. **Ranged builds** should switch to hit-and-run: fire once, dash to new position, repeat. Do not trade — the mirror has more HP than you.

## Chapter 3 Boss: Phase-by-Phase Breakdown

The boss is a **three-phase fight** with bullet-hell adds spawning throughout.

### Phase 1 (100%–66% HP): Summoner Phase

**Attack pattern**: summons Living Books and Mirror Constructs while firing slow, aimed projectiles.

**Strategy**:
- Kill adds immediately — the boss itself isn't dangerous yet, but the combined projectile volume from boss + adds will chip you down
- Use your weapon's wide attacks (Greatsword sweep, Sword & Shield arc) to hit the boss while clearing adds
- Save your dash for the aimed projectile — it tracks your position and fires after a 1.5s delay
- **Do not use potions in Phase 1.** If you're taking damage, you're not clearing adds fast enough

**Build check**: If Phase 1 takes more than 60 seconds, your damage is too low. Go back and farm sapphires for damage-focused Destiny Inscriptions.

### Phase 2 (66%–33% HP): Bullet Hell Phase

**Attack pattern**: the boss stops summoning and fills the room with patterned projectile waves — concentric rings, spirals, and cross-patterns.

**Strategy**:
- **Blocking is critical here.** The 1.0.21 patch made these patterns blockable from all directions. Hold block and walk through gaps — don't try to dash through dense patterns
- Find the **safe zones** in each pattern. Ring patterns have gaps; spiral patterns let you walk clockwise with the rotation
- Use a **Potion of Fortitude** at the start of this phase. The +30% defense turns chip damage into negligible damage
- If you have summons (Undead Inferno build, Mini Ballista artifact), let them deal damage while you focus purely on survival

**Pattern memorization**: You'll die to this phase a few times. That's normal — learning the patterns is part of the game. Each death teaches you one more pattern. Focus on lasting 10 seconds longer each attempt.

### Phase 3 (33%–0% HP): Desperation Phase

**Attack pattern**: combines Phase 1 (adds) and Phase 2 (bullet patterns) simultaneously, with faster projectile speed.

**Strategy**:
- **Use your Greater Healing Potion now.** You should have saved at least one. Pop it at ~40% HP
- **Burn the boss.** Use your Potion of Strength, any offensive consumables (TNT, Fire Bomb), and unload everything
- **Ignore adds unless they're directly in your way.** The boss is low — finishing it is more important than perfect play
- **Dash through the boss**, not away from it — many projectile patterns originate from the boss's position, so being behind it is safer than being at range

## Best Builds for Chapter 3

### Recommended (High Survival)

| Build | Weapon | Why It Works |
|------|------|------|
| **Sword & Shield** with Frost tablets | Sword & Shield | Built-in block/parry handles bullet hell. Frost slow makes patterns easier to dodge. Highest forgiveness. |
| **Undead Inferno** (Ignition + Soul-Weaver) | Greatsword | Summons tank adds while you focus on pattern dodging. Burn damage ticks during your defensive windows. |
| **Guardian** (Fortitude + Vanguard tablets) | Sword & Shield | Maximum survivability. "Boring" but the best way to learn boss patterns while staying alive. |

### Avoid for First Clear

| Build | Why Not |
|------|------|
| **Pure Dagger / Blitz** | Requires aggressive positioning — you'll eat projectiles while learning patterns |
| **Glass cannon Magic Staff** | No survivability; one mistake during bullet hell = dead |
| **Unfocused hybrid** | Scattered artifact/tablet choices won't have the damage to clear Phase 1 adds fast enough |

## Pre-Boss Checklist

Before entering the Chapter 3 boss door:

- [ ] **2+ Healing Potions** (at least 1 Greater)
- [ ] **1 Fortitude Potion** for Phase 2
- [ ] **Weapon at +2 or higher** (upgrade at the Anvil on Floor 4 or 5 if available)
- [ ] **3+ relics** that match your build (sell off-theme relics at the Floor 5 shop)
- [ ] **At least 1 Epic tablet** touching 2+ artifacts (if you're still on Commons, shop reroll before the boss)
- [ ] **HP above 80%** — if you're lower, use a potion before entering (the pre-boss shop always sells potions)

## After You Beat Chapter 3

The difficulty curve actually softens after Chapter 3. Chapter 4 is harder in absolute terms, but your build has come online — you have 4+ relics, an Epic tablet setup, and you've learned fundamental pattern-reading skills that carry through the rest of the game.

Your first Chapter 3 clear is the real rite of passage. Once you can clear it consistently, you're ready for the endgame.

**Next steps**: farm Chapter 1-3 for sapphires to unlock Artifact Slot +1 (see the [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide)), then push into Chapter 4 with a focused build.
