## Mastering Blade Arts in Sephiria

Blade arts are the heart of Sephiria's combat system. Unlike basic attacks, blade arts are powerful special techniques that consume Focus charges and can be chained together for devastating combos. Each blade art has unique properties, damage types, and utility — mastering them is the key to climbing the Tower of Sephiria.

This guide covers every blade art, how to unlock them, how elemental infusions interact with them, and which combinations work best for your preferred playstyle.

## Blade Arts Overview

Blade arts are unlocked as you progress through the tower and spend **Memoriam Shards** at the Blade Shrine. Each blade art occupies one of three slots: Light, Heavy, or Special. You can equip one art per slot, giving you three active blade arts per run.

### How Blade Arts Work

- **Focus Gauge** — Each blade art consumes Focus charges. You start with 3 Focus charges per rest point. Charges regenerate slowly over time or instantly when you parry an enemy attack.
- **Animation Priority** — Blade arts cannot be interrupted once started, but you cannot dodge during the animation either. Timing is critical.
- **Elemental Matching** — Using a blade art that matches your currently infused element increases its damage by 30% and may add status effects.

### Blade Art Types

Blade arts fall into three categories:

- **Light Arts** — Quick execution, low Focus cost (1 charge), short cooldown. Best for weaving between basic attacks.
- **Heavy Arts** — Slower execution, high Focus cost (2 charges), long cooldown. High damage and crowd control.
- **Special Arts** — Unique effects, variable cost (1-3 charges). Utility, healing, or massive damage.

## Combo Chains

The real power of blade arts comes from chaining them together. Here are the most effective combo chains:

### Beginner Combos

- **Basic Burst** — `Light Attack x3 → Moonlit Slash → Light Attack x2` — Simple, safe, and effective for Chapter 1-2 encounters.
- **Elemental Opener** — `Dodge → Ember Strike → Light Attack x4` — Use the dodge to close distance, then apply Burn before your basic attack chain.

### Advanced Combos

- **The Blender** — `Gale Thrust → Frost Fang → Thunder Rend` — Wind groups enemies, Ice slows them, Lightning chains between them. Excellent for crowd clearing.
- **Shadow Assassin** — `Dodge behind enemy → Shadow Waltz (invis) → Light Attack x5 (backstab bonus) → Eclipse Blade` — The invisible approach guarantees critical hits. Best against bosses.
- **Elemental Overload** — `Infuse with Fire → Ember Strike → Light Attack x3 → Void Cascade` — The initial Fire infusion empowers Ember Strike, then Void Cascade clears the battlefield.

### Boss Combos

- **Parry Punish** — `Parry → Sacred Radiance → Frost Fang → Light Attack x3 → Thunder Rend` — After a successful parry, heal immediately, then slow the boss before unloading your Lightning damage.
- **Full Focus Dump** — `Stone Breaker (stagger) → Tempest Fury → Eclipse Blade (finisher)` — Use this only when the boss is staggered or recovering from a big attack. Dumps all Focus in seconds.

## Elemental Infusions

Elemental infusions are temporary buffs that apply an element to all your attacks, including blade arts. You can find infusion pickups throughout the tower or unlock the **Infusion Mastery** inscription to start each run with a random infusion.

### Elemental Interactions

| Element | Status Effect | Best Blade Arts | Best Against |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Fire | Burn (damage over time) | Ember Strike, Void Cascade | Ice enemies, Regenerating enemies |
| Ice | Slow (movement/attack speed -50%) | Frost Fang, Gale Thrust | Fast enemies, Bosses (phase 2+) |
| Lightning | Chain (hit bounces to nearby targets) | Thunder Rend, Tempest Fury | Groups of enemies, Shield enemies |
| Wind | Knockback (pushes enemies away) | Gale Thrust, Shadow Waltz | Crowded rooms, Cliff edges |
| Earth | Stagger (interrupts enemy attacks) | Stone Breaker, Eclipse Blade | Heavy enemies, Bosses during charge attacks |
| Dark | Weaken (enemy deals 20% less damage) | Shadow Waltz, Void Cascade | Bosses, Elite enemies |
| Light | Bless (you deal 15% more damage) | Sacred Radiance, Moonlit Slash | Undead enemies, Boss final phases |

### Infusion Strategy Tips

- Always match your infusion to the chapter's enemy weaknesses. Chapter 1 enemies are weak to Fire; Chapter 3 enemies are weak to Lightning.
- Dual-infusion runs (via the **Twin Essence** relic) let you carry two elements at once, but each wears off twice as fast.
- The **Everflame** inscription makes your infusion last the entire chapter but prevents you from switching elements.

## Playstyle Recommendations

### Aggressive Playstyle

Focus on Heavy and Special blade arts that deal maximum burst damage. Recommended setup:

- **Light Art**: Ember Strike (applies Burn for extra damage)
- **Heavy Art**: Void Cascade (screen clear)
- **Special Art**: Eclipse Blade (boss finisher)
- **Key Relic**: Fury Shard (increases Heavy art damage by 40%)

### Defensive Playstyle

Prioritize blade arts that provide healing, crowd control, and stagger immunity.

- **Light Art**: Gale Thrust (keeps enemies at range)
- **Heavy Art**: Frost Fang (slows enemies)
- **Special Art**: Sacred Radiance (healing)
- **Key Relic**: Guardian Charm (reduces damage during blade art animations)

### Balanced Playstyle

Mix consistent damage with utility. Great for learning new chapters.

- **Light Art**: Moonlit Slash (reliable AoE)
- **Heavy Art**: Thunder Rend (situational burst)
- **Special Art**: Shadow Waltz (escape option)
- **Key Relic**: Focus Crystal (increases Focus gauge by 2)

### Speedrunner Playstyle

Optimize for maximum speed and single-target elimination.

- **Light Art**: Gale Thrust (pierces through lines of enemies)
- **Heavy Art**: Ember Strike (quick activation)
- **Special Art**: Tempest Fury (fast multihit)
- **Key Relic**: Windwalker Boots (movement speed +20%)

## Unlocking All Blade Arts

To efficiently unlock every blade art, follow this progression:

1. **Early Game (Chapters 1-2)**: Prioritize Moonlit Slash and Ember Strike. They are cheap and cover both Physical and Fire damage types.
2. **Mid Game (Chapters 3-4)**: Save Memoriam Shards for Frost Fang and Thunder Rend. These two cover elemental weaknesses for most of the mid-game encounters.
3. **Late Game (Chapters 5-6)**: Invest in Shadow Waltz and Void Cascade. Bosses in these chapters require precise positioning and high burst damage.
4. **Post-Game (New Game+)**: Unlock Eclipse Blade and Sacred Radiance. These are essential for the hardest optional bosses and the Gauntlet mode.
