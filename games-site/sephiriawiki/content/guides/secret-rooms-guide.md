## How Secret Rooms Work

Every chapter in the Tower of Sephiria hides rooms that do not appear on the minimap. They contain high-rarity relics, weapon upgrades, gold caches, and sometimes boss-fight shortcuts. Secret rooms are **procedurally placed** — the exact layout changes each run, but the triggers follow fixed rules. Learn the triggers and you will find a secret room in almost every run.

## How to Detect a Secret Room

| Method | How It Works | Available From |
|---|---|---|
| **Cracked walls** | Walls with visible cracks break when attacked; room behind them | Chapter 1 |
| **Explorer's Map relic** | Reveals all hidden rooms on the minimap | Chapter 1+ (relic drop) |
| **Astral Compass artifact** | Same effect, permanent once unlocked | Chapter 7 / 1.0 |
| **Audio cues** | Some hidden chambers emit a faint hum or whisper near the entrance | Chapter 2+ |
| **Torch relics** | Light sources reveal illusionary walls in dark chapters | Chapter 4 |
| **Floor patterns** | In the Astral Vault, star constellations on floors mark hidden entrances | Chapter 7 |

> **Pro tip:** Equip Explorer's Map at the start of a run, clear the chapter's secret rooms, then swap it out. You only lose a relic slot for the first rooms.

## Chapter-by-Chapter Breakdown

### Chapter 1 — The Rooted Depths
- **5 cracked walls** per run, each hiding a relic or upgrade
- **Vendor shortcut** — a hidden door near the halfway vendor leads directly to the boss floor (skip 3 rooms)
- Break every cracked wall: some require ranged attacks from a distance or a charged attack

### Chapter 2 — The Whispering Halls
- **3 hidden music notes** — collect all three to spawn a secret chest near the boss door (and to enable the Echo Revenant secret boss)
- **Mimic chests** are NOT secret rooms, but always hit chests once before opening
- Whispers get louder as you approach a hidden chamber's entrance

### Chapter 3 — The Mirrored Library
- **Book-wall rooms** — a bookshelf that looks identical to its neighbors can be pulled to reveal a study full of relic pedestals
- Challenge chambers are more common here: time trials and survival rooms with a guaranteed relic

### Chapter 4 — The Twilight Forest
- **Illusionary walls** — only visible with a torch relic or light source equipped
- Hidden platform routes above the tree line lead to treasure; listen for the wisp hum

### Chapter 5 — The Forge Depths
- **The Architect's door** — a hidden door in the third room's forge wall leads to the hardest secret boss in the base game
- Lava-safe alcoves sometimes hide chests visible only from a specific angle

### Chapter 6 — The Apex Sanctuary
- **Elite challenge rooms** — secret rooms here are gauntlets: kill 3 elite enemies for a guaranteed Epic+ relic
- No healing inside — bring potions

### Chapter 7 — The Astral Vault (1.0)
- **Star-alignment puzzles** — standing on constellation floor patterns in the right order (indicated by starlight) opens vault rooms containing **Artifacts** and legendary relics
- Gravity wells inside secret rooms reverse projectile arcs — a Tome or Staff makes these rooms trivial

## Secret Room Rewards

| Room Type | Reward | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Cracked wall room | Relic or weapon upgrade | All (1-6) |
| Music note chest | Epic+ relic | 2 |
| Book-wall study | 2-3 relic pedestals | 3 |
| Challenge chamber | 1 guaranteed relic | 3+ |
| Elite gauntlet | Epic+ relic | 6 |
| Constellation vault | Artifact or legendary relic | 7 |
