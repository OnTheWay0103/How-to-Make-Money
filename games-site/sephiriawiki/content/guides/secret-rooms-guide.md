## How Secret Rooms Work

Every chapter in the Tower of Sephiria hides rooms that don't appear on the minimap. They contain **artifacts, gold, potions, and upgrade materials** — and they're a major source of Inscription Shards. Secret rooms are **procedurally placed** — the exact layout changes each run, but the trigger rules are consistent. Learn the rules and you'll find a secret room in most runs.

## How to Detect a Secret Room

| Method | How It Works | Available From |
|---|---|---|
| **Cracked walls** | Walls with visible cracks break when attacked; a room hides behind them | Chapter 1 onward |
| **Edge-of-room paths** | Rooms sometimes connect to hidden areas at their edges — check dead ends | Chapter 1 onward |
| **Astral Compass artifact** | Permanently reveals secret rooms on your map once unlocked | Late-game unlock (see below) |
| **Room awareness** | Secret rooms are most common in dead-end rooms — always check them | Every chapter |

> **Pro tip:** Make checking dead-end rooms a habit. The cracked-wall trigger is easy to miss when you're rushing, and secret rooms are the best source of Inscription Shards (see the [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide)).

## The Astral Compass Artifact

Finding enough secret rooms **permanently unlocks the Astral Compass artifact** — which reveals every hidden room on your map for all future runs. It's the single most valuable exploration unlock in the game:

1. **Early:** break every cracked wall you see and check dead ends
2. **Unlock:** find 10 secret rooms total to unlock the Astral Compass
3. **After:** equip it and every hidden room appears on your minimap

## Chapter-by-Chapter Breakdown

### Chapter 1 — The Rooted Depths
- Cracked walls are common — learn to spot them here
- Dead-end rooms frequently hide entrances

### Chapter 2 — The Whispering Halls
- The same triggers, but tucked into more surprising spots
- Check rooms that look smaller than they should be

### Chapter 3 — The Mirrored Library
- **Challenge rooms become more common** — time and survival challenges with guaranteed rewards
- Bookshelves sometimes hide passages; try pushing them

### Chapter 4
- Hidden areas hide behind more subtle wall variations
- Listen and look closely — some entrances are easy to walk past

### Chapter 5
- The longest chapter — secret rooms here are worth the detour
- Elite-packed secret rooms start appearing; bring potions

### Chapter 6 — Finale
- Secret rooms in the final chapter are high-reward but high-risk
- **No recovery rooms once the boss gauntlet starts** — do your exploring before committing

## Secret Room Rewards

| Room Type | Reward |
|---|---|
| Cracked wall room | Artifacts, gold, potions, or upgrade materials |
| Challenge room | Guaranteed reward (artifacts, shards, or gold) |
| Elite room | Higher-tier artifacts, more shards |

**Why explore:** secret rooms are the best source of Inscription Shards (which expand your socket capacity permanently) and often hold artifacts that define a run. The [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide) has the full shard-farming route.
