## Co-op in Sephiria

Sephiria supports **1-4 player online co-op**. In co-op, enemy health and damage scale with player count — and so do rewards. Coordination separates a successful team from a wipe.

### How Co-op Scaling Works

- Enemy health and damage **scale with player count** — a 4-player lobby faces a significantly tougher tower than solo
- **Solo is fully balanced and viable** — co-op is an option, not a requirement
- Rewards scale with the party, so there's no penalty for playing together

## Optimal Team Composition

A well-balanced 4-player team covers all situations. The roles below are the community-consistent setup:

### Recommended 4-Player Setup

| Role | Weapon | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontline** | Sword & Shield (or Greatsword) | Hold the line, block attacks aimed at the team, revive safely |
| **Ranged DPS** | Crossbow | Sustained single-target damage, pick off elites from range |
| **AoE / Control** | Staff | Room clear, element coverage, layered damage |
| **Flex / Support** | Any — matches team needs | Add control, utility artifacts, revive guard |

### 2-Player Recommended Pairs

| Pair | Synergy |
|---|---|
| Sword & Shield + Staff | Frontline holds aggro while the Staff AoEs freely |
| Crossbow + Sword & Shield | Safe ranged damage plus a wall that never dies |

### 3-Player Note

A frontline + ranged DPS + AoE composition covers every boss encounter — the three core roles are more important than having all four filled.

## The Revive System

When a teammate's HP hits zero, they enter a **downed state**:

- A **revive prompt** appears above their body
- **Reviving is a short interaction** — you must hold it uninterrupted
- **Enemies can hit you during revive** — the team must clear space
- If fully killed (HP depletes during the downed state), they are eliminated for the current room

### Revive Priority

1. **Frontline revives DPS** — the tank has the HP to absorb hits during the revive window
2. **Support covers the reviver** — keep the area clear while the revive happens
3. **Assign a revive guard** in boss fights — one player's job is covering the downed teammate

## Communication is Key

### Pre-Run Planning

Before entering the tower, decide:

1. **Who covers which role** — frontline, ranged DPS, AoE, revive guard
2. **Boss fight roles** — who holds the boss, who clears adds, who guards revives
3. **Loot distribution** — who gets priority on which artifacts

### In-Run Callouts

Essential callouts in co-op:
- **"Down!"** — You're downed, need a revive
- **"Secret room!"** — Hidden room found
- **"Elite!"** — Strong enemy spotted
- **"Shop!"** — Vendor found, check gold
- **"Boss room!"** — Final room, prepare

## Co-op Boss Strategies

The four co-op boss roles that apply to every fight:

| Role | Job |
|------|-----|
| **Frontline** | Tank the boss's melee patterns — Greatsword stagger chains |
| **Ranged DPS** | Crossbow/Staff — keep damage up through barrages |
| **Revive guard** | One player covers the revive window when a teammate goes down |
| **Add control** | Clear summoned minions (Pillar Summoner fights) so the boss stays focusable |

**The classic bossing pair:** Greatsword + Sword & Shield. One player staggers the boss, the other eats its counterattacks.

## Co-op Tips That Actually Matter

- **Stick together.** Splitting up makes packs much harder to deal with — the scaling doesn't care about your positioning.
- **The host's game state determines shop inventory** — coordinate purchases so the host doesn't buy out the best items before teammates can see them.
- **Potions are per-player** — you can't trade them, but you can ping their locations.
- **Healing effects that cover the area** help everyone — position near your lowest-HP teammate.
