## Sephiria at a Glance

Sephiria's 1.0 release contains **6 chapters**, a full ending with credits, and a deep endgame — Hard Mode (a level-60 climb with 6 modifiers), Gauntlet Mode (wave-based scoring with a leaderboard), and a huge collection layer (~300 artifacts, ~70 tablets, 30+ potions, 20+ costumes, 27 achievements).

Here's how the playtime breaks down.

| Goal | Rough Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **One full run** | Roughly an hour or more | All 6 chapters, assuming a decent build |
| **First clear** | Several full attempts | You will die — the roguelite loop expects it |
| **Hard Mode completion** | Many hours on top | The 6-modifier, level-60 climb |
| **All 27 achievements** | The long-term grind | Achievement hunting covers all systems |
| **100% completion** | Dozens of hours | All artifacts, tablets, and costumes collected |

Roguelite variance (run quality, artifact luck) swings these numbers a lot — that's the genre.

## Chapter-by-Chapter Pacing

A full run climbs all 6 chapters. The pacing is roughly:

| Chapter | Pacing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — The Rooted Depths | Quick | Tutorial-pace, exploration-friendly |
| 2 — The Whispering Halls | Quick | Mechanics deepen; traps and ambushes |
| 3 — The Mirrored Library | Moderate | The first real difficulty spike |
| 4 | Moderate | Enemy density rises |
| 5 | Long | The longest chapter — resource management matters |
| 6 — Finale | Hardest | Boss gauntlet with no recovery rooms — bring full stock |

A clean, focused run through all 6 chapters takes roughly **an hour or more**. A realistic first playthrough takes longer — you will die — but the permanent progression (Inscriptions, Talents, unlocks) makes every death count toward the total.

## What Changes Your Playtime

- **Exploration style:** exploring every room adds time per chapter but means more artifacts and upgrades — and a stronger run. Explore more, die less.
- **Co-op (2-4 players):** enemy health and damage scale with player count, but coordinated teams clear rooms faster and the revive system forgives mistakes. Net time is usually similar — more fun, same hours.
- **Hard Mode:** expect longer per-run time — the 6 modifiers (like Tension, which bans potions in boss fights) force careful play.
- **Completionism:** the collection layer (~300 artifacts, ~70 tablets, 20+ costumes) is where dozens of hours go after the story is done.

## Is It Worth It After the Story?

Yes — the endgame ladder (Hard Mode → Gauntlet Mode → collections) is where the game's depth lives. If you only have a few hours, a single full run plus some achievement hunting gives a complete experience. If you love build-crafting, budget for the long haul.
