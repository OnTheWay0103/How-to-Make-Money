## Sephiria 1.0 at a Glance

With the 1.0 launch, Sephiria's playtime grew substantially: the new Chapter 7 (The Astral Vault), Hard Mode rework, and Infinite Mode add dozens of hours on top of the Early Access campaign. Here are the numbers for the launch version.

| Goal | Average Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **First clear** (reach the Astral Vault) | 12-16 hours | Includes learning the basics and some failed runs |
| **Beat the Rabbit Goddess** | 18-24 hours | First clear + post-boss grind |
| **Complete Hard Mode** | +15-25 hours | The real campaign for veterans |
| **Reach Infinite Mode floor 100** | +20-40 hours | Endless scaling, best done gradually |
| **All achievements (42)** | 40-60 hours | A few are time-gated or RNG-heavy |
| **100% completion** | 70-90 hours | All masteries, artifacts, cosmetics |

Speedrunners clear the full game in around 40 minutes; casual players who explore everything can take 30+ hours on the main path alone. Roguelite variance (relic RNG, run quality) swings these numbers a lot — that's the genre.

## Chapter-by-Chapter Pacing

| Chapter | Average Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — The Rooted Depths | 30-45 min | Tutorial pace, exploration-friendly |
| 2 — The Whispering Halls | 45-60 min | Traps and mimics slow you down |
| 3 — The Mirrored Library | 1-1.5 hrs | First real difficulty spike |
| 4 — The Twilight Forest | 1-1.5 hrs | Darkness mechanics slow navigation |
| 5 — The Forge Depths | 1.5-2 hrs | Heat timers and lava force caution |
| 6 — The Apex Sanctuary | 1.5-2 hrs | Elite gauntlets, no checkpoints |
| 7 — The Astral Vault (1.0) | 2-3 hrs | Two bosses, gravity puzzles, secret rooms |

A clean, focused run through all 7 chapters takes **8-10 hours** if everything goes right. A realistic first playthrough is 12-16 because you will die — permanent progression (inscriptions, talents, unlocks) makes every death count toward the total.

## What Changes Your Playtime

- **Co-op (2-4 players):** ~20-30% faster clears, but you will want to re-run chapters to see secret rooms your team missed. Net playtime is usually similar — more fun, same hours.
- **Exploration style:** Exploring every room adds ~30% to chapter time but meaningfully shortens the *total* time-to-clear, because relics and upgrades carry the run. Explore more, die less.
- **Hard Mode:** Nearly double the time per chapter — enemies have 150% HP and you will play defensively. Plan 15-25 hours.
- **Infinite Mode:** Endless by design; most players stop between floor 50 and 100.

## Is It Worth It After the Story?

Yes — the 1.0 endgame ladder (Hard Mode → Infinite Mode → artifacts and masteries) is where the game's depth lives. If you only have 15 hours, a single clear plus some practice mode boss fights gives a complete experience. If you love build-crafting, budget 60+.
