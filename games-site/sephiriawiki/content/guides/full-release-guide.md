## Sephiria 1.0 Is Live

Sephiria **1.0 launched on July 31, 2026** on Steam — Early Access is over and the full tower climb is available to everyone. After **7 months of Early Access** (which began December 15, 2025) and **7 major updates**, the complete version includes a new chapter, two new weapons, a reworked Hard Mode, Infinite Mode, and hundreds of new items. The game's **94% positive** Steam rating carried straight into launch.

New players start fresh with the full experience; Early Access owners keep all their progress. Everything you earned in EA — inscriptions, character levels, weapon mastery, discovered relics — carries over into 1.0 automatically.

## What's New in 1.0

### New Chapter: 7 — The Astral Vault

The climb no longer ends at the Apex Sanctuary. Beyond it lies **The Astral Vault**, a cosmic star-field arena where the Rabbit Goddess awaits. This chapter introduces:

- **4 new enemy types** — Astral Sentinels, Void Wraiths, Stellar Beacons, and Cosmic Hounds
- **New mechanic** — Gravity wells that alter movement and projectile trajectories
- **2 new bosses** — The Astral Guardian (mid-chapter) and the Rabbit Goddess (final boss)
- **Secret rooms** — New hidden chambers tied to star-alignment puzzles

### 2 New Weapons (8 Total)

| Weapon | Style | Unique Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **Greatsword** | Ultra-heavy melee | Charge attacks build power for a massive AoE slam; 20% chance to stagger enemies on hit |
| **Tome** | Magic ranged | "Spell weave" — combo sequences cast different spells (fire, ice, lightning) |

Both weapons have their own 50+ upgrade trees and mastery lines, so veterans get two entirely new playstyles to master, not just re-skins.

### Hard Mode — Full Rework

Hard Mode was completely redesigned for 1.0: +150% enemy HP, +100% enemy damage, armored enemy variants, new phase-3 boss attacks, and the **Corruption System** — a meter that punishes chip damage. Completing it unlocks Infinite Mode and the First Rabbit's Golden Crown cosmetic.

### Infinite Mode

An endless tower climb unlocked after Hard Mode. Difficulty scales continuously — enemy HP, damage, speed, and spawn rates rise with every floor. Compete for the highest floor on the global leaderboard.

### 200+ Relics

The relic pool grows from 150+ to **200+** with three new types:

- **Class-specific relics** — Only appear when using a specific weapon type
- **Cursed relics** — Powerful bonuses with a drawback (e.g., double damage, reduced healing)
- **Set relics** — Equipping 2+ from the same set grants additional bonuses

### New Talents & Artifacts

Character progression was reworked: the flat stat growth of Early Access is replaced by the **Talent system** (three trees: Combat, Resilience, Utility). The Astral Vault also introduces **Artifacts** — permanent equippable items with build-defining effects. See the dedicated guides for both.

### Quality of Life

- **Build save slots** — Save up to 5 relic/inscription loadouts
- **Practice mode expansion** — Fight any boss you've encountered, including 1.0 bosses
- **Enhanced co-op lobbies** — Filter by chapter progression, Hard Mode status, and region
- **Replay system** — Save and share your best runs
- **Achievements** — 15 new, 42 total

## Known Issues at Launch

The team is targeting fixes in the 1.0.1 hotfix:

- Occasional desync in 3–4 player co-op during boss fights
- Tome projectile collision with certain terrain types
- "Hardened" achievement can fail to trigger in rare cases

## Quick Start Checklist for Launch Day

1. **Pick a weapon** — All 8 are viable. New players: Sword or Spear. Veterans: try the Tome.
2. **Climb to Chapter 7** — A completed run on an old save immediately unlocks the Astral Vault.
3. **Farm the new relic types** — Cursed and set relics drop most often in Chapters 5+.
4. **Clear Hard Mode** — Unlocks Infinite Mode and the leaderboard grind.
5. **Set up loadout slots** — Save your Hard Mode build before starting an Infinite run.
