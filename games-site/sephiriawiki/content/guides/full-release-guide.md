## Sephiria 1.0 Is Live — What Actually Changed

Sephiria **1.0 launched July 31, 2026** on Steam. Developed by **TEAM HORAY** (the Dungreed studio), it ends **484 days of Early Access (April 2025 → July 2026) and 58 EA updates**, and shipped a same-day hotfix (1.0.21). The rating at launch: **Very Positive** (~5,400+ reviews, building on the EA-era 97%).

**A correction for anyone who read pre-launch coverage:** 1.0 adds the **final Chapter 6** — there is no "Chapter 7 Astral Vault." The weapon pool stays at **6 weapons** (no new weapon types; the "Tome" and "Greatsword" weapons from some guides do not exist). Hard Mode is extended to **level 60**, not reworked into a "Corruption System." We've fixed this page to match the official patch records.

## What's New in 1.0

### 1. Final Chapter: Chapter 6 + Ending

- The story **ends in Chapter 6** with **2 new bosses, 1 mini-boss**, and a proper **ending with credits** — the narrative arc is complete (players describe the pacing as the closest thing to Hades' story flow).
- The final-boss fight got tweaks in 1.0.21 (telegraphing and blocking improvements). Full breakdown: **[Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide](/guides/chapter-6-final-boss-guide)**.

### 2. Weapon Branch Expansions (6 Branches, 200+ Upgrades)

No new weapon types — instead, all **6 weapon branches** (Sword & Shield, Greatsword, Dagger, Katana, Crossbow, Staff) received **new upgrade lines**, totaling **200+ weapon upgrades** in 1.0. Highlights per the 1.0 balance patch:

- **New +4 upgrade lines** for Dagger, Crossbow, and Staff
- New/updated weapons: **Glacial Blade**, **Eternal Winter**, **Frozen Egg** (ice family), **Firelighter**, **Solis Missio**, **Thor Greatsword S3G**, **Red Snake Crusher**
- The balance patch touched ice and storm items specifically — the ice/storm-dominant meta was reworked, and fire/corrosion got attention. See the **[Weapons Tier List (1.0)](/guides/weapons-tier-list)** for what the patch actually did.

### 3. Side Bag

A **second inventory bag** — effectively bonus backpack space. Unlocked through the **Destiny Inscription** (blue-gem) tree. See the [New Systems Guide](/guides/new-systems-guide) for unlock order and what to store in it.

### 4. Mystic Pot Rework

The artifact-rolling station was reworked: **trade 1 artifact for 1 of the same rarity, or 2 for 1 of the next rarity up**. Re-rolling up a rarity is the efficient endgame path. Details and priority order: [New Systems Guide](/guides/new-systems-guide).

### 5. Hard Mode Extended to Level 60

Hard Mode now runs to **level 60** with **6 new modifiers** ([Demon] Blood Feast lifesteal, [Demon] Pseudo-Vanguard elites, [Plan] Deterioration, [Plan] Tension — no potions in boss fights, [Plan] Heavy Heart — carry junk artifacts, [World] Proliferation) and higher defense-tree upgrade caps. Full modifier counter-guide: **[Hard Mode Guide (1.0)](/guides/hard-mode-guide)**.

### 6. Quality of Life & Localization

- **5 new languages** (Thai, German, French, Polish, Swedish) — 12 total. Translations are still being polished.
- **Build save slots raised 5 → 7** (a community-requested change)
- **Photosensitivity warning** added
- **Preset/replay improvements** and the usual bug pass

### 7. Same-Day Hotfix 1.0.21

| Fix | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| Co-op balance | Blood Feast (lifesteal) modifiers now scale properly with party size |
| Pillar Summoner | HP reduced by 33% |
| Full-screen attacks | Now dodgeable/blockable — telegraphed properly |
| Ultra-wide | Ultra-wide monitor support fixed |
| Other | Crash and desync fixes from the 1.0 batch |

## Early Access Save Carryover — The Official Answer

- **Official announcement (July 9):** EA saves **continue working in 1.0** — the game updates and your progress carries on automatically.
- **Why people are confused:** a summary line in the 1.0.19 patch record read "EA save cannot be inherited; progress ends and returns to town." This **contradicts the official 7/9 announcement** and appears to be an internal change note that leaked into the summary. The official position is: **your EA save works in 1.0.**
- If your save still reads as a fresh start after updating, verify the game version is 1.0.21 and check the FAQ. (New players: your new save is fully compatible with everything above.)

## Quick Start Checklist for Launch Day

1. **Pick a weapon** — 6 branches, all viable. New players: Sword & Shield or Staff are the most forgiving.
2. **Climb to Chapter 6** — the final chapter unlocks after Chapter 5's boss.
3. **Reroll artifacts at the Mystic Pot** — 2-for-1 rarity upgrades are the fastest power growth.
4. **Unlock the Side Bag** early via Destiny Inscription — extra space compounds every run.
5. **Clear Hard Mode to 60** — it now has a proper endgame arc with the 6 new modifiers.
