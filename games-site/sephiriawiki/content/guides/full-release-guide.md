# Sephiria 1.0 Full Release Guide — What's Actually New in the Launch

Sephiria **1.0 launched July 31, 2026** on Steam. Developed by **TEAM HORAY** (the Dungreed studio), the full release ends **484 days of Early Access (April 2025 → July 2026) and 58 EA updates**. The Steam rating remains **Very Positive** — reflecting strong player reception through Early Access and into 1.0.

The game has **6 chapters and 6 weapons**, and the story ends at **Chapter 6** — there is no seventh chapter.

## What's New in 1.0

### 1. Final Chapter: Chapter 6 + Ending

- The story **ends in Chapter 6** with **2 bosses (the Pillar Summoner and the final boss), 1 mini-boss**, and a proper **ending with credits** — the narrative arc is complete.
- The Chapter 6 boss gauntlet has **no recovery rooms** — stock healing before you commit. Full breakdown: **[Chapter 6 & Final Boss Guide](/guides/chapter-6-final-boss-guide)**.

### 2. Weapon Branch Expansions (6 Branches, 200+ Upgrades)

No new weapon types — instead, all **6 weapon branches** (Sword & Shield, Greatsword, Dagger, Katana, Crossbow, Staff) received **new upgrade lines**, totaling **200+ weapon upgrades** in 1.0. Community standouts from the 1.0 content: **Firelighter** and **Solis Missio** (fire lines for the Staff), **Thor Greatsword S3G** and **Red Snake Crusher** (Greatsword).

### 3. Side Bag

A **second inventory bag** — effectively bonus backpack space that lets you carry spare items outside your main inventory. It's one of the 1.0 systems covered in the [Mystic Pot Guide](/guides/mystic-pot-guide).

### 4. Mystic Pot Rework

The artifact-conversion station was reworked: **trade 1 artifact for 1 of the same rarity, or 2 for 1 of the next rarity up**. Re-rolling up a rarity is the efficient endgame path. Details and priority order: [Mystic Pot Guide](/guides/mystic-pot-guide).

### 5. Hard Mode Extended to Level 60

Hard Mode now runs to **level 60** with **6 modifiers**: Festival of Blood, Pseudo-Vanguard, Decline, Tension (no potions in boss fights), Heavy Heart (carry junk artifacts), and Proliferation. Full modifier counter-guide: **[Hard Mode Guide (1.0)](/guides/hard-mode-guide)**.

### 6. New Items

The 1.0 update added new items across the board, including **3 miracle abilities** — run-unlocked special abilities that change how a build executes. The full artifact pool sits at **~300 artifacts** (plus ~70 tablets and 30+ potions).

## What Didn't Change

- **6 chapters** — the climb ends at Chapter 6
- **6 weapons** — Sword & Shield, Greatsword, Dagger, Katana, Crossbow, Staff
- **4-player co-op** — online co-op remains the multiplayer model

## Post-Launch Outlook

The developers have confirmed **continued post-launch support** for Sephiria. Balance changes and new content will shift the meta over time — **Steam News** is the authoritative channel for patch notes, and we update our guides as new information is released.

## Quick Start Checklist for Launch Day

1. **Pick a weapon** — 6 branches, all viable. New players: Sword & Shield is the most forgiving.
2. **Climb to Chapter 6** — the final chapter unlocks after Chapter 5's boss.
3. **Reroll artifacts at the Mystic Pot** — 2-for-1 rarity upgrades are fast power growth.
4. **Unlock the Side Bag** early via Destiny Inscriptions — extra space compounds every run.
5. **Clear Hard Mode to 60** — the endgame arc with the 6 modifiers.
