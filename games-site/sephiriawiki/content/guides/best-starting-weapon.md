# Best Starting Weapon — What to Pick for Your First Runs

**Sephiria 1.0 / Updated: August 6, 2026**

Your first weapon in Sephiria decides your first 10 hours — not permanently (you can switch between runs, and mastery is per-weapon), but efficiently. Picking a forgiving weapon first means learning the tower's enemies and rooms before you learn a hard weapon's timing. This guide ranks all 6 weapons for beginners, explains why the easy picks are easy, and gives you the early upgrade path for each.

---

## Beginner Ranking Summary

| Weapon | Difficulty | Beginner Rating | Why |
|--------|------------|-----------------|-----|
| **Sword** | Easy | ★★★★★ | Balanced speed/damage, straightforward combos, reliable block |
| **Spear** | Easy | ★★★★★ | Long reach keeps you safe while you learn enemy spacing |
| **Bow** | Medium | ★★★★ | Ranged observation is safe; resource management is the challenge |
| **Staff** | Medium | ★★★ | Powerful AoE but mana management adds a resource layer |
| **Fist (Gauntlets)** | Hard | ★★ | Aggressive combos punish positioning mistakes |
| **Scythe** | Hard | ★★ | Slow heavy sweeps require game knowledge to time |

**Bottom line:** take the **Sword** if you want the all-rounder, the **Spear** if you want maximum safety, the **Bow** if you prefer ranged play. Avoid the Scythe and Fists on your first few runs.

## The Three Beginner Weapons in Depth

### Sword — The Balanced All-Rounder

| Stat | Score |
|------|-------|
| Damage | 6/10 |
| Speed | 7/10 |
| Range | 4/10 |
| Difficulty | Easy |

**Why it works for beginners:** the sword has no weaknesses that punish mistakes — medium range is workable, medium speed forgives mistimed inputs, and the block covers the classic first-run panic. Its upgrade tree branches into:

- **Slash path** — wider arcs, AoE coverage
- **Counter path** — parry bonuses and counter damage
- **Mastery 15:** unlock "Sword Dance" — a rapid 6-hit slash

**Early upgrade advice:** take the slash path first. Wider arcs turn the sword's weakness (short range) into a strength in the crowded early rooms.

### Spear — The Patient Poke

| Stat | Score |
|------|-------|
| Damage | 5/10 |
| Speed | 6/10 |
| Range | 9/10 |
| Difficulty | Easy |

**Why it works for beginners:** the spear's range is its entire defense. You can hit enemies who cannot yet hit you — the best possible teacher for spacing, the skill every other weapon needs. Upgrade paths:

- **Phalanx path** — defensive stances and counter-thrusts
- **Dragoon path** — jump attacks and aerial combos
- **Mastery 15:** unlock "Whirlwind Thrust" — multi-direction stab

**Early upgrade advice:** Phalanx first. The defensive stance covers the spear's one real weakness (getting rushed) while you learn.

### Bow — The Ranged Specialist

| Stat | Score |
|------|-------|
| Damage | 7/10 |
| Speed | 5/10 |
| Range | 10/10 |
| Difficulty | Medium |

**Why it works for beginners:** the bow lets you watch enemy patterns from safety — the fastest way to learn bosses. The cost is resource management: charged shots consume resources, and reloads are your vulnerability window.

- **Volley path** — multishot and spread (best for rooms)
- **Sniper path** — single-target crits (best for bosses)
- **Mastery 15:** unlock "Rain of Arrows" — AoE bombardment

**Early upgrade advice:** Volley. Rooms are where you die early; spread damage fixes the bow's worst situation (being rushed).

## What the Other Three Weapons Do

| Weapon | Stats | Role | When to Try |
|--------|-------|------|-------------|
| Staff | 8 dmg / 4 spd / 8 range | AoE elemental mage; the only mana weapon | After run 5+ — you must learn to manage mana |
| Fist (Gauntlets) | 9 dmg / 9 spd / 2 range | Rushdown combo monster | After you know boss patterns — combos need timing |
| Scythe | 8 dmg / 3 spd / 6 range | Heavy sweeps + life-steal | When you can position deliberately |

The 1.0 release added **200+ new weapon upgrades** across all six branches, so every weapon has a deeper tree than the Early Access days — the "easy" weapons only get easier with the new nodes.

## Weapon Choice by Chapter

| Chapter | Best Beginner Pick | Why |
|---------|--------------------|-----|
| Chapter 1 — Rooted Depths | Sword or Spear | Slow enemies, telegraphed attacks — learn basics |
| Chapter 2 — Whispering Halls | Bow | Ranged enemies appear; observe from range |
| Chapter 3+ | Stick with your pick | Switching mid-campaign resets your momentum; mastery grows on one weapon |

**The mastery rule:** weapon mastery unlocks perks at levels 5/15/30 — and it only grows while you use that weapon. Switch weapons between runs freely, but commit to one per campaign.

## Co-op: Cover the Team

In co-op (1-4 players), weapon choice becomes composition:

- One **Spear** for safe frontline poke
- One **Bow** for ranged damage and boss observation
- One **Sword or Fist** for aggressive finishers
- One **Staff** for AoE crowd clear
