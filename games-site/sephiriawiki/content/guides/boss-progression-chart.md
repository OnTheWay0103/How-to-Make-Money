## Boss Progression Chart — Sephiria 1.0

> Version 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026

Sephiria 1.0 contains **10+ unique bosses** spread across **six chapters**, with more than 60 enemy types on the way up. The 1.0 update added the **sixth chapter, its final boss, and a new hard mode**. Use this chart to plan your tower climb.

---

## Chapter Progression Chart

| Stage | Content | Boss Encounter | Notes |
|-------|---------|----------------|-------|
| Chapters 1–5 | 5 chapters of tower floors | One mandatory boss per chapter | Each chapter boss has unique attack patterns and phases |
| Chapter 6 (NEW in 1.0) | Final chapter | **Final boss** (added in 1.0) | The true ending content — see the dedicated guide |
| Any chapter | Optional encounters | **Secret bosses** | Hidden triggers and optional rewards |

**Known named boss:** **Askard** — a verified chapter boss famous for its theme music; one of the 10+ unique encounters.

## Challenge Modes Chart

| Mode | What It Is | Boss Relevance |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Gauntlet Mode** | Wave-based scoring mode | Wave bosses and score thresholds — practice boss patterns without run commitment |
| **Hard Mode** | New in 1.0 — raised difficulty for the full climb | All chapter bosses return with tuned-up stats; bosses are where most runs end |
| **New Game Plus** | Repeat climb with carried-over power | Bosses scale; rewards repeat |
| **Co-op (up to 4 players)** | Online co-op with item trading and revive | Bosses are the co-op coordination test — one player revives, others cover |

## Boss Prep Checklist

1. **Check the chapter's boss type** before the climb — melee vs ranged matchups matter
2. **Bring a defensive option** — dodge timing is learnable, but one defensive Artifact saves runs
3. **Learn the fury/phase shift** — most bosses change behavior at ~50% HP
4. **Co-op: assign revive + cover roles** before the door
5. **Hard Mode: overgear first** — farm gauntlet for upgrades before attempting
