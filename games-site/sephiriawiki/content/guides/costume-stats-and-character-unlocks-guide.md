# Sephiria Costume Stats & Character Unlock Guide — How to Unlock Every Costume and Which One to Pick

**Costumes in Sephiria are not skins.** Each one is effectively a **class**: pick it in the tower-top village before a run and it changes your stats — and often your whole build — for the entire climb. That is not community folklore: the developers' own 1.0.33 patch notes (September 11, 2026) contain a Costumes section reading *"Fixed an issue where the costume effect could be removed by a certain weapon."* The phrase "the costume effect" is theirs. [Official]

This guide covers what each costume actually does, how the hidden ones unlock, which to pick, and — importantly — where the published lists disagree so you can verify in-game instead of trusting a number.

---

## The short answer

- **Eight costumes are available from your first run.** They are not sidegrades with flavour text; each pairs a bonus with a penalty.
- **At least ten more unlock through gameplay** — stat thresholds, one-run challenges, boss repetitions and random events.
- **There is no "best" costume**, because every one gives and takes. The right question is *which penalty can my build absorb.*
- **You choose before the run**, so the costume is the first build decision you make, not the last.

## How to change your costume

Equip it in the **village at the top of the tower** (the hub) before you descend. The effect is fixed for that run — you cannot swap mid-climb. Because the mods are permanent for the run, treat costume choice as step zero of build planning, before you know which artifacts you will be offered. [Editorial]

## The 8 starter costumes and their stats

These eight are unlocked on a new save. The stat lines below are the ones the English guides agree on exactly.

| Costume | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Pink Bunny** | Experience gained **+3%** | Nothing specific — a pure progression pick |
| **Armored Bunny** | **+10 Defense**, **−15 Max HP** | Tanky, block-heavy builds that never dodge |
| **Brown Rabbit** | **+6% Evasion**, **−6% Crit Chance** | Learning the game — the evasion is worth more than the crit you lose |
| **Braided Hair Rabbit** | **+7% Attack Speed**, **+12% Move Speed**, **−20% Dash Recovery** | Fast, aggressive play; the dash penalty is the price |
| **Orange Rabbit** | **+10 Max HP**, **−15 Max MP** | Melee builds that rarely cast |
| **Red-Hooded Rabbit** | **+8 MP Regen**, **−20 Max MP** | Spell-heavy runs that want throughput over a big pool |
| **White Rabbit** | **+10 Barter**, **−5% Evasion** | Economy and shop value over survivability |
| **Wing-Eared Rabbit** | Buffs allies within **5 tiles**, ally revive time **−30%**, spell MP cost **+50%**, Weapon Attack **−10%**; **starts with Blessing Grimoire** | Explicitly a co-op support pick |

Two of these are the community's standard beginner recommendations: **Brown Rabbit** for forgiveness while you learn combat flow, and **Braided Hair Rabbit** if you already play fast. [Community / Editorial]
The Wing-Eared Rabbit is the only starter built for team play — it is the one costume whose main effect does nothing at all in a solo run. [Editorial]

## Unlockable costumes and how to get them

These are the hidden tier. Unlock conditions are consistent across sources; the effects are not, so each row notes the agreement level.

| Costume | Unlock | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Wizard Bunny** | Defeat 50 enemies with magic | Cold-damage lean; starts with an ice projectile [Community] |
| **Adventurer Bunny** | Own **Dungreed** on the same Steam account | +1 Dash, dash recovery **+10%**; grimoire cooldown reduction [Community] |
| **Frog** | Raise **Lightning Damage to 50** | Lightning lean, physical-damage penalty [Community] |
| **Red-Clothed Cat** | Raise **Fire Damage to 50** | Fire lean, cold-damage penalty [Community] |
| **Red Fox** | Collect **10 or more Tablets** in a single run | **+6 inventory slots**, healing received **−50%** |
| **Mole** | Defeat the first boss | Companion damage **+10%**, **−20 Defense**, **−10% Move Speed**; starts with Golden Handbell |
| **White Wolf** | Defeat the **Chapter 1 pillager leader** more than 5 times — spelled **Rataka** in the English guides and **Lataka** on the Korean wiki **[Unconfirmed]** | **Infinite dashes**, but dashes **no longer grant invincibility frames** |
| **Skeleton** | Take **333 cumulative damage** in one run | Survivability gimmick — sources disagree on the exact effect **[Unconfirmed]** |
| **Wingless Bat** | Complete the **Blood Donation event 5 times** | Lifesteal bonus, **dash count fixed at 1** |
| **Ghost** | Hold **5 Grimoires** in one run | Grimoires **cost no MP**, Weapon Damage **−40%** |
| **Otter** | Reach **175% Crit Damage** | **+9 Crit Chance**, movement-speed penalty |

### Watch out for three rows

- **White Wolf** is the cleanest data point in the table — the English and Chinese lists agree word for word: infinite dashes, no i-frames. For any build that uses dash as a defensive tool this is a downgrade, not an upgrade. [Editorial]
- **Wingless Bat** is the one players complain about most, because its unlock is gated behind a **random** event. One Steam reviewer reported roughly 80 hours without completing five Blood Donation events; a community description of the same event places it at an **Iron Maiden** in a special room on an early floor, traded to a wandering NPC in exchange for health. If you are hunting it, that is the thing to look for. [Community]
- **The White Rabbit does not match across languages.** The English guides list Barter and Evasion; the Chinese listing gives special-attack damage, Max MP and an Evasion penalty instead. One of the two lists is describing a different build. Check the in-game wardrobe rather than trusting either. **[Unconfirmed]**

## How many costumes are there, really?

The Chinese full listing counts **27**, and names entries the English guides do not cover at all — **Crocodile, Deer, Eagle, Lucky Fairy, Farmer Squirrel, Forest Cat, Scholar Lizard** and **Turtle**. **Scholar Lizard** is corroborated: it is the costume named in the official 1.0.33 notes. [Official / Editorial] Treat any specific total as approximate — the wardrobe in game is the authority, and it hides costumes you have not unlocked yet, so an apparent "empty slot" is normal. [Community]

## Which costume should you pick?

- **First playthrough:** **Brown Rabbit.** Evasion shortens your death spiral while you learn boss patterns; the crit penalty is the least painful stat to lose early. [Community]
- **Aggressive melee:** **Braided Hair Rabbit** if you can live with the dash recovery, otherwise **Armored Bunny** to trade Max HP for flat defense.
- **Caster / Staff runs:** **Red-Hooded Rabbit** for regen, or **Wizard Bunny / Frog / Red-Clothed Cat** to lean into one element once you can hit the threshold.
- **Co-op support:** **Wing-Eared Rabbit.** Nothing else in the starter set helps the team.
- **Dash-reliant builds:** anything **except White Wolf** — losing dash i-frames changes what the dodge button means.

## FAQ

**Q: Do costumes change your stats?**
Yes. They are functional classes, and the developers' own patch notes refer to "the costume effect." Older summaries describing costumes as purely cosmetic are out of date. [Official]

**Q: Can I swap costumes during a run?**
No — you equip in the hub before descending, and the modifiers hold for the whole climb.

**Q: Do I have to re-unlock costumes on a new save?**
No. Unlocks are account-wide once earned, so a fresh save starts with everything you have already opened. [Editorial]

**Q: Why can't I see a costume in the menu?**
Locked costumes can stay hidden rather than showing a silhouette, which is why players hunting the Wingless Bat describe an apparently empty slot. [Community]

**Q: Is the Wing-Eared Rabbit worth it solo?**
No. Its headline effect buffs nearby allies and reduces revive time — with no allies in the room, you are paying the spell-cost and weapon-damage penalties for the starting Grimoire alone.
