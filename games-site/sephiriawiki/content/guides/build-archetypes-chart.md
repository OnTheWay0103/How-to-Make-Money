## Build Archetypes Chart — Sephiria 1.0

> Version 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026

Sephiria's builds are built from **six weapon styles**, layered with **Artifacts** (arranged and empowered with **Tablets** in the grid inventory), **Talents**, and **Miracle** abilities. Each weapon has **50+ unique upgrades**, so builds branch in dozens of directions — but they all collapse into the archetypes below.

---

## The Six Weapon Styles

| Weapon Style | Playstyle | Risk | Best For |
|--------------|-----------|------|----------|
| **Katana (melee)** | Fast, combo-heavy close combat — risk-taking, high damage | High | Players who want active, aggressive fights |
| **Heavy Greatsword** | Slow, heavy hits with big payoff | Medium-High | Big single hits and stagger pressure |
| **Magic** | Ranged casting, elemental damage | Medium | Safe distance play, AoE coverage |
| **Projectiles** | Ranged shots, kiting, sustained DPS | Low-Medium | Newer players, boss safety |
| **Summons** | Summoned allies fight alongside you | Medium | Crowd control, distraction, co-op support |
| **Elemental effects** | Status-focused stacking (burn, chill, etc.) | Medium | Clear-speed and setup for burst |

---

## Core Build Archetypes

| Archetype | Weapon Style | How It Plays | Key Systems |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Tanky Shield** | Katana or Greatsword + defensive Artifacts | Block, counter, outlast — the survivable close-range build | Defense-focused Artifacts, Tablet priorities on mitigation, Talents for sustain |
| **Meteor Mage** | Magic | Summon big AoE casts on groups; burst windows | Magic Artifacts, Tablet stacking on spell power, Miracle cooldown Talents |
| **Katana Glass Cannon** | Katana | Maximum DPS, zero margin for error | Damage Artifacts, crit/attack-speed Tablets |
| **Summoner** | Summons | Let allies tank and deal damage while you control the fight | Summon-strength Artifacts, Tablet priorities on summon count/duration |
| **Elemental Stacker** | Elemental effects | Stack status effects that multiply each other | Status-proc Artifacts, elemental Tablets |
| **Safe Ranged** | Projectiles | Kite everything from range, rarely touched | Ranged damage Artifacts, utility Tablets |

## How to Use This Chart

1. **Pick an archetype** that matches how you want to play (tanky, burst, safe, support)
2. **Match it to a weapon style** — each archetype maps to 1-2 styles above
3. **Layer systems in order**: weapon upgrade path → artifact/tablet setup → talents → miracles
4. **In co-op, cover two archetypes** — a tanky frontliner plus a ranged DPS handles every boss
