## What is Sephiria?

Sephiria is a **pixel-art action roguelite** developed by TEAM HORAY (the Dungreed studio). You play as a rabbit warrior climbing the **Tower of Sephiria** through 6 procedurally-arranged chapters against 10+ bosses. The game supports **1-4 player online co-op** and launched at 1.0 on July 31, 2026 after 484 days of Early Access (April 2025 → July 2026).

Each run is a fresh climb: choose from 6 weapons, collect artifacts, socket inscriptions, defeat the chapter boss, and die or reach the top. Death is part of the loop — permanent progression (talents, inscriptions, artifacts, weapon mastery) makes every run count.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **6 Weapons** — Sword & Shield, Greatsword, Dagger, Katana, Crossbow & Staff (200+ upgrades in 1.0)
- **6 Chapters** — The story ends at Chapter 6 with a proper ending
- **~300 Artifacts** — Per-run passive items with deep synergies
- **Talents & Inscriptions** — Permanent progression systems
- **Hard Mode** — Level 60 with 6 modifiers for veterans
- **Side Bag & Mystic Pot** — Inventory bag and artifact re-roll station
- **1-4 Player Co-op** — Full campaign playable solo or with friends
- **94% Positive** — Very Positive Steam rating (~5,700 reviews)
