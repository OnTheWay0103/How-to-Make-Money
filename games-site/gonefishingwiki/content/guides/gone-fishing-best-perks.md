GONE Fishing has **nine perks**, and you can bring **two**. They unlock by surviving seven consecutive days, and each player picks up to two before a run. That is the decision, and it is hard because the game explains almost none of them. This tier list ranks what we could verify and gives separate picks for solo and co-op.

## How Perks Work

| Rule | Detail | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Total perks | Nine | Community (Fandom) |
| Unlock condition | Survive seven consecutive days | Community (Fandom) |
| How many you bring | Up to two per player | Community (Fandom) |
| Who they affect | The player who selected them | Community (Fandom) |
| Exception | Midas Touch, because gold is shared | Community (Fandom) |
| Available from the first run | Sticky Hook, Tackle Box, Midas Touch | Community (Steam discussion) |
| Fish Whisperer effect | +5% average fish size | Community (Fandom) |

The seventh-day unlock is the reason perks belong to the mid-game, not the first hour. A new player should not plan a build around them; a player who has survived a week should not ignore them. Nine perks with a pick-two limit means the choice is about your role, not about raw power.

## The Tier List

This list ranks only the perks whose names and effects we could confirm. The remaining five are listed separately rather than guessed at.

| A | Midas Touch | Gold is shared, so the perk benefits the lobby | Co-op economy | Effect beyond shared gold `[Unconfirmed]` |

### Why Sticky Hook is S

If the community description is right, Sticky Hook is the only perk that directly protects the core minigame. Fishing in GONE Fishing is a tension management problem: keep the marker in the green, do not snap the line, land the fish. A perk that makes that window more forgiving is worth more than a percentage bonus, because a snapped line is a zero.

The caveat is honest: we could not verify the exact implementation. Treat the tier as "best if the reported effect is accurate", and test it on a low-stakes run before you build a strategy around it.

### Why Fish Whisperer is A

Fish Whisperer has the cleanest verified number in the entire perk list: +5% average fish size. That is a small multiplier, but size feeds bait score, value, and legendary odds. It is the perk you take when the quota is already solved and you are fishing for money or trophies.

In a lobby that is still failing quotas, Fish Whisperer is a luxury. In a lobby that clears the altar before dusk, it is the best use of a slot.

### Why Midas Touch is A in Co-op and B Solo

The community consensus is that perks are personal except Midas Touch, because gold is shared. If that is accurate, Midas Touch is a co-op perk: one player takes it and the whole lobby's economy improves. Solo, the shared-gold exception does not matter, and the perk has to justify itself on its own effect, which we could not verify.

Take it in a group. Skip it solo until you know what it does.

### Why Tackle Box is B

Tackle Box is confirmed to exist and to be available from the first run. Its effect is not confirmed in our sources. The name suggests a bait-related benefit, and bait is a real bottleneck, but we are not going to invent a number. B is a placeholder for "plausible, unverified".

## The Five Perks We Did Not Rank

Nine perks exist; four are named in our sources. The other five are not ranked here because we could not verify their current names or effects in the 1.0 build. Listing them from memory would be exactly the kind of fabrication this site exists to avoid.

What we can say is structural: with nine perks and two slots, the game is designed around specialisation. Expect at least one quota perk, one size perk, one economy perk and one night-survival perk in the full set. If you find a current, sourced list, compare it against the four above rather than replacing them blindly.

## Solo Picks

| Slot | Pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sticky Hook | Protect the minigame you repeat hundreds of times |
| 2 | Fish Whisperer | Size is the solo player's bottleneck after the quota is solved |

If you are still failing quotas, swap Fish Whisperer for Midas Touch and buy upgrades earlier. A solo player's economy is the run's ceiling.

## Co-op Picks

| Lobby role | Slots | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Angler | Sticky Hook + Fish Whisperer | Maximise landed fish and size |
| Cook / runner | Midas Touch + Tackle Box | Economy and bait supply for the group |
| Scout | Sticky Hook + Midas Touch | Survival plus lobby economy |

The principle is that **personal perks should match personal jobs**. A cook with Fish Whisperer is a wasted slot unless they also fish. An angler with Midas Touch is fine if nobody else took it, because gold is shared, but the angler is usually the player who benefits least from an economy perk they cannot stop to use.

## How to Unlock Perks Faster

The unlock condition is survival, not fishing. That changes the optimisation target.

1. **Do not chase size before day seven.** The quota is the only thing that extends the run.
2. **Do not play Hardcore while learning.** Hardcore is permanent death, and a wipe resets the streak you are trying to build.
3. **Sleep when the flame is blue.** The night is where streaks end.
4. **Play with a group that agrees on the dusk rule.** One player who stays out fishing can end everyone's streak.
5. **Treat day seven as a milestone, not a victory.** The perks unlock; the quota keeps growing.

Whether the seven-day counter is per save, per lobby or per player is `[Unconfirmed]`. Test it before you plan a seven-day push around a specific character.

## Common Questions

**Can I change perks mid-run?** Community reports describe selecting up to two at the start. We found no verified way to swap them mid-run.

**Do perks stack if two players take the same one?** `[Unconfirmed]`. The Fandom note that perks are personal suggests no, except for shared gold.

**Is there a best perk for boss fights?** We could not verify any boss-specific perk. Sticky Hook remains the safest general pick because it protects the action you perform under pressure.

**Are perks the same in 1.0?** The 1.0 update reworked progression and replaced the old upgrade path with trinkets. The perk list may have changed too, and the Fandom page may lag behind. Treat the nine-perk count as current-community, not patch-verified.

**Should I take Midas Touch solo?** Only if you have verified its effect yourself. In co-op, the shared-gold exception makes it a reasonable support pick.

Perks are a small system with an outsized effect on how a run feels. The right two do not make you better at fishing; they make the fishing you already do count for more.
