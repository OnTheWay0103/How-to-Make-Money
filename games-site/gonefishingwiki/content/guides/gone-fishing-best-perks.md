GONE Fishing has **nine perks** and you can bring **two**. The developer has never published a perk list — every name below was reconstructed from patch notes that mention perks one at a time — so this page separates what Paper Cult actually wrote from what the community assumes. Seven of the nine have a described effect. Two do not.

## How Perks Work

| Rule | Detail | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Total perks | Nine | Community count; the ninth (Alcoholic) was added 2026-09-23 [Official] |
| Unlock cadence | Day 7, then day 14, 21 and so on | [Official] |
| How many you bring | Up to two | [Official] |
| Available from the first run | Sticky Hook, Bigger Tacklebox, Midas Touch | [Community] |
| Who they affect | The player who picked them, except Midas Touch (gold is shared) | [Community] |

The cadence matters more than the count. Perks arrive on a **seven-day clock**, not a level curve, so a player who wipes on day six earns nothing from six days of work. The two-slot cap is enforced, not advisory: a January 2026 note reads "Fixed an exploit where players could stack perks and have more than 2 active at a time." [Official]

Every effect in that table is a developer quote, not a datamine. That is unusually strong for a game this quiet — and it is also the limit of what exists: no official page, store description or FAQ lists the perks together.

### Why Sticky Hook is S

Fishing in GONE Fishing is a tension problem: hold the marker in the red without snapping the line. Sticky Hook is the only perk the developer has described as directly widening that window, and the numbers are large. Before the perk the line ripped after 1.25 seconds in the red; the perk multiplied that by 1.5, and an October 2025 balance pass raised it to a flat three seconds. [Official]

Three seconds is not a small nudge — it is more than double the original grace period, on the action you repeat more than any other. A snapped line is a zero: no fish, no points, wasted bait. Every other perk improves a number; this one prevents a failure.

The honest caveat is that "time in the red" is the developer's phrasing, not a tutorial's. Test it on a cheap run before building a route around it.

### Why Fish Whisperer is A

Fish Whisperer's effect is easy to misquote, so here is the exact line: it "applies a 5% buff to point value and weight as the fish is caught instead of at fish upload to leaderboard." [Official] Two things are happening. You get +5% to both score and size, and the multiplier is applied at the moment of catch rather than at upload.

That timing detail is why the perk is better than a flat +5% sounds. Weight feeds size-based objectives and it is the number legendary attempts are judged against; point value feeds the quota. One slot improving both is efficient.

It is still a luxury while your lobby is failing quotas. Take it when the altar is already being cleared before dusk.

### Why Midas Touch is A in co-op and C solo

The developer has buffed this perk twice, which tells you both that it was weak and that they care about it: first from 1 gold every 5 minutes to 1 every 2, then to **3 gold every 2 minutes**. [Official] That is 90 gold an hour from a passive slot.

In co-op the case is obvious. Gold is shared, so one player's perk pays the whole lobby, and a support player who is not fishing can run the economy. Solo, you are giving up a fishing perk to earn money you could have earned by fishing — only worth it if you are deliberately banking for an upgrade tier.

### Why the B tier is bait, air and legs

**Bait Carepackage** is the one B-tier perk with a described effect and a clear use: it spawns extra bait in the cabin when you load into a level. [Official] On multi-day runs where bait is the binding constraint, a free resupply is a real economic effect — just smaller than a permanent multiplier.

**BUDS** doubles underwater time from 7 to 14 seconds. [Official] The perk only matters on routes with submerged loot or oxygen-gated escapes; when a map has those, it is worth more than its tier suggests, which is why it is a conditional B rather than a C.

**Trained Runner** takes sprint speed from 700 to 750 against a 600 baseline. [Official] That is a movement perk: it shortens the walk back to the cabin before dusk, which is the mistake that ends runs. It does not help you fish.

**Alcoholic** triples the duration of the beer bonus. [Official] It is the newest perk (added 2026-09-23) and the most situational: the value depends entirely on whether you keep beer stocked. No beer, no perk.

## The Two Perks Nobody Can Explain

**Bigger Tacklebox** and **Bug Net** exist — both appear by name in fix notes going back to 2025 — but no announcement describes what either does. [Official]

This is where most perk lists start inventing. The name suggests Bigger Tacklebox expands bait capacity and Bug Net lets you collect insects, and both guesses are plausible. They are still guesses. If you have one of these unlocked, the only reliable test is a controlled run: record your bait count or catch total with and without it.

One loose end worth flagging: two later fix notes mention an "extra tackle" perk and a "drowning" perk. They read like aliases of Bigger Tacklebox and BUDS respectively, but the developer never says so. That identification is inference, not fact. [Unconfirmed]

## Solo Picks

| Slot | Pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sticky Hook | Protects the action you repeat hundreds of times |
| 2 | Fish Whisperer | Score and size in one slot once quotas are stable |

Still failing quotas? Swap Fish Whisperer for Midas Touch and buy upgrades earlier — 90 gold an hour is worth more than 5% while your gear is behind. Diving-heavy solo runs should take BUDS instead of slot 2.

## Co-op Picks

| Lobby role | Slots | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Angler | Sticky Hook + Fish Whisperer | Land more fish, and land bigger ones |
| Support / cook | Midas Touch + Bait Carepackage | Lobby economy and bait supply |
| Runner / scout | Sticky Hook + Trained Runner | Survival plus speed across the map |

The principle is that **personal perks should match personal jobs**. A support player holding Fish Whisperer is burning a slot unless they also fish; an angler holding Midas Touch is not wasted, because gold is shared, but it is the slot a dedicated support player should have taken.

## How to Unlock Perks Faster

The unlock condition is survival on a seven-day clock, so the optimisation target is "do not wipe", not "fish better". [Official]

1. **Do not chase size before day seven.** The quota is the only thing that keeps the run alive.
2. **Do not play Hardcore while learning.** Permanent death resets the streak you are building.
3. **Sleep when the flame turns blue.** Night is where streaks end.
4. **Agree on a dusk rule in co-op.** One player still out fishing can end everyone's streak.
5. **Treat day 14 and 21 as the real goals.** The cadence is recurring, not one-off.

Whether the counter is tracked per save, per lobby or per player is `[Unconfirmed]`. Official notes confirm the 7/14/21 cadence but never the scope.

## Common Questions

**Can I change perks mid-run?** Community reports describe selecting up to two at the start. No official note describes swapping them later.

**Do perks stack if two players take the same one?** `[Unconfirmed]` for non-gold perks. Midas Touch is the clear case: it is shared gold, so duplicates add nothing.

**Is there a best perk for boss fights?** Nothing official is boss-specific. Sticky Hook remains the safest general pick because it protects the action you perform under pressure.

**Are the perk names the same as on the Fandom page?** Mostly. The community calls one "Tackle Box"; the developer writes "Bigger Tacklebox". Where they differ, this page uses the developer's spelling.

**Should I take Midas Touch solo?** Only while banking for an upgrade tier. Otherwise the slot is better spent on a fishing perk.

Perks do not make you better at fishing; they make the fishing you already do count for more.
