Legendary fish are the reason the bait system exists. GONE Fishing has **more than a hundred** catchable species, and a handful sit behind a **score threshold** rather than a location — Nessie is the community's best-known example, listed in the store material as Lochness. This guide separates what the developer confirms from what players report.

## What "Legendary" Means Here

GONE Fishing does not publish a rarity table. What it does publish, through the official FAQ, is the set of levers that raise your odds of a big catch. That matters because it reframes the hunt: you are not searching for a spawn point, you are raising a score until the rare table opens.

| Term | What we know | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Legendary / mythical | A rarity tier above normal catches | Community |
| Nessie | The community name for the Lochness catch | Community; store uses Lochness |
| Where it appears | Reported on all maps | Community `[Unconfirmed]` |
| Weight range | Reported 690–125,000 lbs | Community `[Unconfirmed]` |
| Bait score threshold | 60+ named by the official FAQ for big fish | Official |
| Depth threshold | 2,500+ named by the official FAQ | Official |
| Best bait value | Troll at 86 | Official |

The honest summary is that the **levers are official and the creature specifics are not**. Build the hunt around the levers.

## The Bait Score Levers, Verified

The official FAQ lists the factors that feed bait score. This is the most valuable table on this page because every row is developer-sourced.

| Lever | Official guidance | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Bait value | 60+ for big fish; Troll is the highest at 86 | Sets the ceiling of what can bite |
| Depth | At least 2,500 | Unlocks deeper species and size |
| Map depth access | 4,500+ available on every map | Means no map is locked out of deep fishing |
| Sinker | Higher sinker level raises score | A gear multiplier, not a bait substitute |
| Beer | Beer score contributes | Another stackable multiplier |
| Leviathan totem | Team-wide buff | Raises the whole lobby's odds |
| Days survived | Day 40 is the FAQ's fully stacked benchmark | Time is a lever, not just a grind |
| Weather | Storms are the friendly version of bad weather | A multiplier worth waiting for |

Two implications follow. First, **a legendary hunt is a gear-and-time project**, not a lucky cast. Second, **you can hunt on any map**, because the FAQ says every map has access to 4,500+ depth. The map choice is about convenience and danger, not about whether the fish exists.

## Nessie and Lochness: What Is Actually Confirmed

The store material names Lochness. The community calls the catch Nessie, which is the obvious nickname and the one most searches use. Fandom lists it as Very Rare and available on all maps, with a weight range that spans two orders of magnitude.

That weight range is the part to treat carefully. A community-compiled range of roughly 690 to 125,000 lbs is plausible as a min-max across many reports, but it is not developer data and we could not verify the endpoints. If you catch one, record the weight; the community table only improves when players do.

What we will not do is invent a spawn time, a bait name or a weather requirement for Nessie specifically. None of our sources support that level of detail.

## Building the Score: A Practical Order

If you want the highest chance per hour, improve the levers in the order that costs least.

1. **Bait value to 60+.** This is the entry ticket. Below 60, the official FAQ's big-fish guidance does not apply.
2. **Depth to 2,500+.** Fish the deepest water you can reach without snapping. If you cannot hold 2,500, fix the line before buying more bait.
3. **Sinker level.** A cheap, permanent multiplier. Upgrade it before chasing Troll bait.
4. **Beer score.** Stackable, and easy to forget. Treat it as part of the pre-cast checklist.
5. **Leviathan totem.** Team-wide, so buy it in co-op where it lifts every rod.
6. **Troll bait (86).** The top of the bait table, and expensive enough that it should be the last lever you pull, not the first.
7. **Day count.** The FAQ's day-40 benchmark exists because the levers compound. A day-20 player with perfect gear is still behind a day-40 player with the same gear.

The order is deliberately boring. Most players who complain that legendaries never bite are fishing at 1,800 depth with 40-value bait and a level-one sinker.

## Weather, Storms and Timing

The FAQ calls storms the friendly version of bad weather, which is a rare piece of unambiguous advice: fish the storm. The multiplier is real, and the downside is visibility and night safety, not catch quality.

| Condition | Effect on the hunt | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Clear day | Baseline | Farm quota and money |
| Storm | Multiplier on the score | Fish through it if the group is safe |
| Night | Threat window, not a bonus window | Sleep if the flame is blue |
| Jeremy Wade difficulty | +20% weight, higher legendary and mythical rates | Accept Hardcore if you want the bonus |

Jeremy Wade is the difficulty question. It gives the +20% weight bonus and, per community reports, raises legendary and mythical catch rates. It also forces Hardcore, which means a death is permanent and reloading destroys bait. Do not learn the hunt there.

## Gear and Route

| Slot | What to prioritise | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rod / line | Depth access without snapping | The 2,500 threshold is the gate |
| Sinker | Highest level you can afford | Permanent score multiplier |
| Bait | 60+ value; Troll for the top end | Sets what can bite |
| Totem | Leviathan, then Fish Frenzy | Team-wide value |
| Perks | Fish Whisperer for +5% size | Small multiplier, applies to every catch |
| Transport | Shortest route to deep water | Time at depth is the real resource |

A route that spends twenty minutes walking is a route that fishes for forty. Pick the map where deep water is closest to the altar, not the map that looks best in a screenshot.

## How Long It Actually Takes

Nobody can give you a cast count, and anyone who does is guessing. What can be said is that the official FAQ's day-40 benchmark implies a multi-week project for a normal player, and that the score levers are all permanent upgrades. Progress is visible in your gear and your depth, not in your catch log.

| Player state | Realistic expectation |
|---|---|
| Bait under 60, depth under 2,500 | Not hunting yet; fix the levers |
| Bait 60+, depth 2,500, no totem | Possible, slow |
| Full levers, day 40+, storm | Best odds the game offers |
| Jeremy Wade, full levers | Higher rates, permanent-death risk |

## Common Myths

**"Nessie only spawns at night."** No source supports this. Night is a threat window; the FAQ's levers are depth, bait, gear, totems, weather and days.

**"You need a specific map."** The FAQ says every map reaches 4,500+ depth. Use the map you can survive.

**"Troll bait guarantees a legendary."** It is the highest bait value, not a guarantee. It raises the ceiling; the roll still happens.

**"Cooking a legendary increases its value."** Cooking increases value by 33% for fish you sell or sacrifice. Whether a legendary can be cooked at all is `[Unconfirmed]`; do not risk a rare catch on an untested interaction.

**"Nessie is the only legendary."** The Fandom species list includes multiple rare entries. Nessie is simply the most searched.

## Common Questions

**What is the best bait for legendary fish?** Troll at 86 is the highest value named by the official FAQ. Bait value 60+ is the working threshold.

**How deep do I need to fish?** At least 2,500 for big fish; every map can reach 4,500+.

**Does the Leviathan totem help?** The official FAQ lists it as a bait-score lever, and it is team-wide, so yes.

**Can I catch Nessie solo?** Nothing in our sources says otherwise, but solo means slower score-building and no shared totems.

**Should I sell or sacrifice a legendary?** Sell it unless the quota is in danger. The money funds the next hunt; the altar only needs value.

**How much does Nessie weigh?** Community reports span roughly 690 to 125,000 lbs, which we could not verify. Record your own catch.
