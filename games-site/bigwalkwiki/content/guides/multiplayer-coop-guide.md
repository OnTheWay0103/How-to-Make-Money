# Big Walk Multiplayer & Co-op Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Big Walk is a **cooperative-only** game. There is no single-player mode, no AI teammates, and — importantly — **no matchmaking**. The entire experience is built around playing with friends. Here is everything confirmed about how multiplayer works at launch.

---

## Player Count: 2-12 Players

- **Minimum:** 2 players. You cannot play alone.
- **Maximum:** 12 players on the same island.
- **Puzzle scaling:** puzzles are designed to work for groups of 2, 3, or 4+, and larger groups naturally split up and help each other.

Practical squad sizes:

| Group Size | Best For |
|------------|----------|
| 2 players | Intimate, puzzle-by-puzzle play. Every puzzle is a two-person conversation. |
| 3 players | The classic "spotter / talker / doer" split. Great all-rounder. |
| 4 players | The most reliable co-op setup — pairs can split and solve two things at once. |
| 5-12 players | Party-scale walking. Groups split into sub-teams that shout updates via walkie-talkie and megaphone. |

There is no confirmed limit on how groups can be organized — the game simply scales puzzles so any group can contribute.

## Crossplay: Everything, Everywhere

**Full cross-platform play is supported** across:

- PC (Steam)
- Mac (Steam)
- PlayStation 5
- Nintendo Switch 2

Your friend's platform does not matter — invite them like you would for any game on your platform. Crossplay was confirmed in pre-release coverage and works at launch.

## Invites: No Matchmaking, Bring Your Friends

Big Walk has **no matchmaking** — there is no "play with randoms" button. The game is designed, in the developers' words, around playing with people you know.

To start a walk:

1. **Host a game** on your platform (Steam / PS5 / Switch 2).
2. **Invite friends** through your platform's party system (Steam Friends, PlayStation party, or Switch friend invites).
3. Up to 11 friends join your island.

**Tip for mixed-platform groups:** everyone should add each other on one platform if possible, or use Discord/voice-call fallbacks for coordinating the invite itself. In-game, your crossplay friends will appear as normal.

## Voice Chat: The Core Mechanic

Voice chat in Big Walk is **proximity-based and positional**:

- **Voices fade with distance** — walk too far away and your friend becomes a distant mumble, then silence.
- **Echoes down corridors** and through the tunnel network.
- **Muffles through walls** — you know someone is on the other side, but not what they are saying.
- **Crackles through walkie-talkies** — the tool that lets separated groups communicate.

This system *is* the game. The developers built Big Walk around the feeling of hearing your friends through a crackly walkie-talkie from across the island. Distance is a mechanic, not an inconvenience.

### Managing the chat system

- **Stick together** if you want easy conversation — the walk itself is the game.
- **Split up with walkie-talkies** — one per sub-team, minimum. The crackle is the point.
- **Use gestures when voice fails** — pointing, waving and flailing ("Arms!!") are a real communication layer, and some puzzles are designed around them.
- **Outdoor voices carry** — open areas let voices travel further than tunnels and structures.

## Crossplay Voice Chat Notes

- **In-game voice chat** works across platforms since it is part of the game, not the platform.
- Many groups still keep a **Discord or party chat open** for lobby coordination before the walk starts — the proximity system is a feature, but the invite screen is easier with a fallback.
- **Push-to-talk is available in the launch build** (default **V** on PC, **L3** on controllers) — see the [Controls Guide](/guides/controls-guide) for bindings and rebinding instructions.

## Getting the Most Out of a Group

### For 2 players
You are a **walker-talker duo**: one carries binoculars and reads the terrain, the other carries the walkie-talkie and does the talking. Swap when the puzzle demands it. Every puzzle is designed to be solved by exactly your kind of pair.

### For 3-4 players
Run the **spotter / relay / action split**:
- **Spotter:** high ground, binoculars, reads what the group needs.
- **Relay:** walkie-talkie, repeats and organizes instructions.
- **Actors:** everyone else, executing the physical parts of the puzzle.

### For 5-12 players
- **Split into sub-teams of 2-4** and assign each sub-team an area.
- Give **every sub-team a walkie-talkie** — the big-group experience is hearing the whole island chattering over the airwaves.
- A **megaphone** is genuinely useful at this scale for broadcasting to everyone outdoors.

## Common Co-op Questions

**Can my PS5 friend and my Steam friend play together with me on Switch 2?** Yes — full three-platform crossplay is supported.

**Do we all need to own the game?** Yes. Each player needs their own copy (or PS Plus claim). There is no confirmed guest/pass mechanic at launch.

**Can someone join mid-walk?** Mid-session joins have not been confirmed at launch. Plan for everyone to be present at the start.

**What happens if someone drops out?** Not officially documented yet — we will update this guide once the community has tested it.

**Is there couch co-op?** Not confirmed. Big Walk is an online multiplayer game at launch; local same-screen co-op has not been announced.
