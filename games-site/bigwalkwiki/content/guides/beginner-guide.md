# Big Walk Beginner's Guide — Your First Walk

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Welcome to the island. **Big Walk** is a cooperative open world puzzle adventure from House House (Untitled Goose Game) and Panic — a game about walking, talking, and solving puzzles together. There is no combat, no health bar, no timer and no fail state. The only requirement is at least one friend, because Big Walk is **co-op only**: there is no solo mode and no matchmaking.

This guide covers the first hour: getting into a game, the core loop, your first tools, and the habits that make a good walking group.

---

## Before You Start: The Golden Rule of Big Walk

Big Walk is a game about **communication**, and everything about it is designed to make talking to each other the challenge:

- Voice chat is **proximity-based** — voices fade as you get further apart, echo in tunnels, and muffle through walls.
- There is **no map menu and no objective markers** — you navigate by landmarks and each other.
- Many puzzles **separate players** and require one person to describe what they see to everyone else.

If you go in expecting a game to play *at*, you will be confused. If you go in expecting a walk with friends where you have to keep talking to figure things out, you will love it.

---

## Step 1: Get a Group Together

- Big Walk supports **2-12 players** with full crossplay between PC (Steam), Mac, PS5, and Nintendo Switch 2.
- There is **no matchmaking** — you invite your own friends through your platform (Steam friends, PlayStation parties, Switch friends).
- Puzzles are designed for groups of 2-4+, and bigger groups naturally split up and help each other across the island.

For a first session, **2-4 players is the sweet spot**. A pair experiences every puzzle intimately; a group of four has the most reliable "one person spots, one person describes, two people act" setup. You can still start a 12-player walk if you have the friend count — it is a party game at that scale.

Need more detail on invites, crossplay, and voice chat setup? See the [Multiplayer & Co-op Guide](/guides/multiplayer-coop-guide).

## Step 2: Your First Tools

When you spawn on the island you can pick up tools. The two you want first:

- **Walkie-Talkie** — the communication workhorse. Voices crackle through it over long distances, so separated groups can stay in touch. If your group is splitting up, someone on each side should carry one.
- **Binoculars** — your substitute for a map. From high ground you can read signs, spot landmarks, and relay directions to everyone else.

As you play you will find more: laser pointers, megaphones, flares, flashlights, whiteboards, cowbells, and the mysterious golden head. Full details in the [Tools Guide](/guides/tools-guide).

**Beginner tip:** before moving anywhere, establish a "spotter" (someone with binoculars on high ground) and a "talker" (someone with the walkie-talkie). This division of labor solves half of Big Walk's puzzles before they even start.

---

## Step 3: The Core Loop — Look, Describe, Act

The Big Walk loop is simple and it never changes:

1. **Look** — find a puzzle. It will usually be a bright, colorful structure that stands out against the natural island terrain.
2. **Understand** — figure out what the puzzle wants. Buttons, symbols, balls, tiles, relays.
3. **Describe** — tell your group what you see. This is the step most beginners rush, and it is the one that matters most.
4. **Act together** — execute the solution as a team. Many puzzles require players in different places, pressing buttons simultaneously or relaying information.

The game gives you almost no instructions on purpose. Every puzzle is a small "what are we looking at?" conversation. If you get stuck, the answer is almost always *talk about it more*, not *try harder alone*.

---

## Step 4: Gourds — The Reward System

Solving puzzles rewards you with **gourd-like red objects** (previewers called them "babies" or "nubbins" — small red gourd-shaped key items). Gourds are the game's progression currency:

- They **unlock towers and new areas** of the island.
- They **unlock fast travel** — ski lifts and the underground tunnel network.
- Some gourds are tied to hidden questlines and secrets (see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide)).

**Beginner tip:** if you see a gourd but cannot figure out what to do with it, carry it and keep exploring. Gourds have multiple uses, and half the fun is finding out where they go.

---

## Step 5: Surviving Your First Night

Big Walk has a day/night cycle, and **nights get very dark**. This is not a danger mechanic — nothing hunts you — but it is a navigation one: you will not see the landmarks you rely on.

- Carry a **flashlight** for night stretches, or plan your walk to reach shelter before dark.
- If you have flares, they double as night signal markers for regrouping.
- Nights are also when the game is at its coziest — previewers loved sitting and watching the sunset, which is a totally legitimate activity in Big Walk.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Not grabbing a walkie-talkie before splitting up.** Ten minutes later, you will be shouting into nothing.
2. **Everyone moving at their own pace.** Big Walk is a group activity. Set a "slowest walker" pace — the game is deliberately a walk, not a sprint.
3. **Forgetting that voices travel.** You can hear friends before you can see them. Follow the voices.
4. **Hoarding tools.** If you are not using the binoculars, hand them to someone who is. Tools circulate in good groups.
5. **Expecting a map.** There is no map menu. Landmarks, binoculars, and your friends are the navigation system — this is intentional.
6. **Skipping the gestures.** When voice is not an option (distance, tunnels), the point/wave/flail gestures ("Arms!!") are a real language. Use them.

---

## What to Do in Your First Hour

1. Spawn in, grab a walkie-talkie and binoculars.
2. Do a voice check with your group — hear how voices fade with distance.
3. Find your first puzzle and solve it **verbally** — describe everything out loud.
4. Collect your first gourd and find out where it goes.
5. Reach the first unlock (tower or tunnel) and watch the island open up.

That is the Big Walk loop, and it is the whole game. The good news: it does not get old.

## FAQ for Beginners

**Do I need to be good at games?** No. There are no reflex checks, no enemies, and no timing demands. The only skill is communication.

**Can I join random players?** No — there is no matchmaking. Big Walk is designed for playing with people you know (or at least people you can talk to).

**What happens if I walk away from my group?** You get lost, most likely — but you can always regroup via walkie-talkie or by finding your friends with binoculars. Nothing punishes you for it.

**Is there anything to fail?** No fail states confirmed at launch. The game is pressure-free by design.
