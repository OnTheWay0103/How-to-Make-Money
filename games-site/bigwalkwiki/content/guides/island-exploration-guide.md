# Big Walk Island Exploration Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Big Walk's world is a large open island — previews describe it as **roughly 1.4 km square**, inspired by Wilsons Promontory National Park in Australia. Realistic natural terrain (trees, hills, rivers, coastline) is dotted with **bright, cartoonishly colored structures** that stand out against it. That contrast is the design: the structures are your landmarks.

There is **no map menu and no objective markers**. This guide covers what we know about the island's layout and, more importantly, how to navigate it like the developers intend.

---

## The Navigation Principle: Landmarks, Not Maps

Big Walk deliberately removes the map. You navigate by:

1. **Landmarks** — towers, colored structures, distinctive terrain. The island's architecture is designed to be read from a distance.
2. **Voices** — proximity voice chat means you can hear your group before you see them. Follow the talking.
3. **Binoculars** — the long-range tool for reading signs, spotting structures, and relaying directions.
4. **The sun and moon** — the day/night cycle is also a compass; landmarks look different in different light, and nights are very dark.

**The golden rule of Big Walk navigation:** never move somewhere you cannot describe. If you cannot say "toward the red tower, past the yellow arch," your group cannot follow you.

---

## Known Island Features

### Natural Terrain
- **Hills and ridges** — high ground is the best spotting position; binoculars from a ridge cover huge distances.
- **Rivers** — waterways break up the terrain and are useful reference lines when describing routes.
- **Coastline** — the island edge; previews mention secret alcoves and the famous "throw things in the ocean" pastime.
- **Forests** — dense tree cover blocks sightlines and muffles voices; stay close in forests.

### Man-Made Structures
- **Towers** — the island's main unlock structure. Gourds unlock towers, and towers unlock new regions. If a tower is dark, it wants a gourd.
- **Colorful puzzle structures** — the bright, playground-plastic buildings that are the game's puzzle stations.
- **Ski lifts** — fast travel between high points, unlocked via gourds.
- **Tunnel entrances** — the underground network unlocks as you progress.

### The Tunnel Network
Previews confirmed an **underground tunnel system** that unlocks as you progress through the game:

- Tunnels function as **fast travel** between distant regions.
- Voices **echo down corridors** — tunnels are excellent relay points for groups.
- Reports suggest **branches off the main tunnel routes** lead to secrets (see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide)).

## Fast Travel: How It Works

Big Walk's fast travel is unlocked, not free:

- **Ski lifts and tunnels open as you earn gourds** and unlock towers.
- Unlocked fast travel points stay available for the whole group.
- The unlock loop is: **solve puzzle → earn gourd → unlock tower → open new region/fast travel → repeat.**

This makes exploration a spiral: every new unlock reveals more island, which contains more puzzles, which earn more gourds.

## Exploration Strategies

### The Spotter System
Always have a **spotter with binoculars** on high ground when moving into unknown territory:

1. Spotter climbs a ridge and describes what they see: structures, paths, colors.
2. The group votes on the next landmark to walk toward (yes, vote — it is a walking game).
3. Spotter rejoins or stays as a fixed relay with a walkie-talkie.

### The Splinter Strategy (4+ players)
Big groups explore faster by splitting:

- **Sub-teams of 2-3** fan out toward different landmarks.
- **Every sub-team carries a walkie-talkie** — the report-back chatter is the map.
- First sub-team to find something announces it; everyone converges via landmarks and flares.

### Night Navigation
- **Carry flashlights** — nights are very dark.
- **Flare the destination** — a flare placed at a landmark makes it visible from anywhere.
- **Move early, explore late** — do discovery walks in daylight, use nights for cozy landmark-watching and inside areas.
