# Big Walk Trophies & Achievements Guide — Full List (PS5, Steam)

> Launch Version / Updated: August 5, 2026

Big Walk launched August 4, 2026 on PS5 (day-one on PlayStation Plus), PC (Steam), Mac, and Nintendo Switch 2. Like most multi-platform releases, it ships with a **trophy set on PS5** and an **achievement set on Steam** — and at launch, the full public list is still being compiled by the community. This page is the tracker.

**Honesty note:** We will not invent trophy names. What follows is (1) what early players have confirmed popping, (2) what the game's systems make *likely*, clearly labeled as expected-but-unconfirmed, and (3) what remains completely unknown. The list will be finalized as reports come in.

---

## Trophy / Achievement Overview

- **PS5:** Standard trophy tiers (Bronze / Silver / Gold / Platinum). No DLC trophies confirmed at launch.
- **Steam:** Achievements with global percentage tracking. Steam has no platinum equivalent — 100% is the completion goal.
- **Switch 2:** No formal achievement system (Nintendo platform limitation) — nothing to collect there.
- **Cross-save note:** Whether trophies "autopop" on a second platform is **unconfirmed**; Big Walk's crossplay does not necessarily include cross-save. Do not buy twice expecting instant completion.

## Expected Trophy Categories (Based on Game Systems)

The game's design makes these categories near-certain, and we expect official trophies to cover them. **None of these are confirmed names** — this is a roadmap of what the set will almost certainly contain:

1. **Story / progression** — completing each act and the ending.
2. **Puzzle trophies** — solving N puzzles; solving specific puzzle types (relay, symbol, ball, tower).
3. **Collection trophies** — collecting gourds; collecting a large share of all gourds; possibly an "all gourds" trophy.
4. **Tool trophies** — using each tool; specific tool moments (e.g., a megaphone broadcast, a walkie-talkie conversation across the island).
5. **Co-op / social trophies** — the heart of Big Walk: playing with 2 / 4 / 12 players, exchanging items, gesture communication, voice-related moments.
6. **Secret trophies** — the gourd-to-cutter machine questline, the golden head, and hidden areas. See the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide).

## Hidden Trophy Speculation

House House loves a gag (Untitled Goose Game's achievements are almost entirely jokes). Expect the hidden slots to be **mischievous**:

- Throwing a friend's item into the ocean.
- Stealing an item and carrying it across the whole island.
- Watching a full sunset with your group (a confirmed favorite activity).
- Getting a friend lost, or losing yourself and finding your way back.
- Anything involving the cowbell.

**Status: pure speculation based on developer personality.** Hidden trophies stay hidden in the in-game list until earned.

## Difficulty & Time to Platinum / 100%

### Difficulty: Very Low

- **No combat, no fail states, no timers** — nothing in Big Walk can end a run. See the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide).
- The only real challenge is **organizing 2-12 humans** and finding every collectible on a 1.4 km² island with no map.
- Expect the true bottleneck to be **secret/hidden trophies** (machine questline, all gourds), not skill.

### Time to 100%: ~25-35 hours (estimate)

| Target | Estimated time | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Platinum / 100% base | 15-20 hours | Playing normally, no hunting |
| Completionist (all gourds + secrets) | 25-35 hours | Gourd map being built — see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide) |
| Minimum (story only) | 8-12 hours | See [How Long to Beat](/guides/how-long-to-beat) |

*These are estimates from preview playtime reports and launch-week player sessions — expect them to tighten over the first month.*

## Tips for Specific Trophies

1. **Plan your big-group sessions early.** If a 12-player trophy exists, 12-player sessions are the hardest thing to organize in Big Walk — there is no matchmaking. Book friends in advance.
2. **Use every tool once, deliberately.** If a tool trophy exists, it will not pop by accident for the whiteboard and cowbell. Do a "tool day" with the group.
3. **Take the golden head everywhere.** If it is a trophy trigger, nobody knows what it does — treating it as a collectible tour item covers your bases.
4. **Feed the cutter machine.** The machine questline is the most likely secret-trophy source. Log every gourd you feed it — [contact us](/contact) with findings.
5. **Do not rush collectibles.** Playthrough one naturally; gourd locations (and any "all gourds" trophy) will be fully mapped long before you finish.
6. **Watch the Steam global stats.** Steam's percentage tracker is the fastest way to discover hidden achievements — a sudden uptick in an achievement's rarity means the community found something.

## PS5 vs Steam Differences

| Aspect | PS5 | Steam |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Structure | Bronze/Silver/Gold + Platinum | Flat achievements, 100% goal |
| Tracking | Trophy levels & rarity | Global % completion per achievement |
| Hidden trophies | Hidden until earned, then visible | Hidden until earned, then visible |
| Crossplay | Yes — play with Steam/Switch 2 friends | Yes — play with PS5/Switch 2 friends |
| Cross-save | Unconfirmed | Unconfirmed |
| PS Plus | Required for online play (day-one title) | Not applicable |

**One confirmed difference:** PS5 players need an active **PlayStation Plus subscription** to play co-op online, while Steam players do not. Everything else is identical — same game, same content.
