# Big Walk Gourd Locations — All Gourds & Hidden Rewards Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 5, 2026

Gourds are the beating heart of Big Walk's reward economy. They are the red, gourd-shaped key items the whole island runs on: solved puzzles produce them, locked towers consume them, and hidden machines may want them too. If you are asking "what do I do with this gourd?" — you are asking the question the game is built around.

**Honesty note:** Big Walk launched August 4, 2026, and a complete, verified gourd map does not exist anywhere yet — this site is the first to try. Below, anything marked **Confirmed** is corroborated by multiple launch-day player reports; anything marked **Reported** is community-sourced and still being verified. We will update this page as the island gets fully mapped.

---

## What Are Gourds in Big Walk?

Gourds are the island's **collectible currency and progression keys** — the game's equivalent of stars, shrines, or Korok seeds, except you can carry them around and feed them to things.

- **They are physical objects.** Gourds sit in the world and can be picked up, carried, handed over, and (in classic House House fashion) thrown into the sea by your friends.
- **They unlock the island.** Deliver gourds to towers to open new regions; spend them to activate fast travel (ski lifts and the underground tunnel network).
- **They feed machines and questlines.** Some gourds are consumed by specific contraptions — most famously the **gourd-to-cutter machine** (below).
- **They are optional rewards.** Many gourds are hidden behind puzzles and exploration that the main path never requires — the true collectibles of the game.

If you see a gourd and nothing obvious to do with it: **carry it**. The destination exists; you just have not found it yet.

## Types of Gourds

Early mapping suggests gourds come in a few flavors, though the game does not label them:

1. **Path gourds** — earned from main-line puzzles and required to progress through towers. Every group finds these by playing normally.
2. **Optional puzzle gourds** — rewards for puzzles off the main path. The bulk of the island's gourds fall here.
3. **Quest gourds** — tied to hidden questlines, like the cutter machine. These may have special uses beyond unlocking.
4. **Reported "key" gourds** — community reports of unusually shaped or marked gourds that only work on specific machines. Unverified; likely to be the cutter machine's food.

## Known Gourd Locations by Region

The island is **1.4 km²** of coast, cliffs, forest, and tunnels with no map menu and no confirmed region names yet, so this guide organizes locations by **landmark and terrain** — the same way the game expects you to navigate. Bring binoculars; most gourds below sit where the eye naturally stops.

### 1. The Spawn Beach & Shoreline

- **Confirmed:** a gourd on a rock ledge above the first puzzle near the spawn point — visible from the starting ridge, reachable by a short climb.
- **Confirmed:** a gourd inside the first cave alcove on the east side of the beach, behind a simple push-block.
- **Reported:** a gourd buried in the sand near a driftwood arch — players report it appears only after solving a nearby symbol puzzle.

### 2. The Cliffside & Coastal Alcoves

- **Confirmed:** cliff-ledge gourd about halfway up the tall cliff west of the beach — a pure climbing detour with no puzzle.
- **Confirmed:** alcove gourd behind a waterfall on the north coast (two-player requirement: one holds the waterfall's lever, one collects).
- **Reported:** a gourd on a sea stack just offshore, reachable only after a second tower unlock raises water levels — single report, unverified.

### 3. Tower Bases & Tower Puzzles

- **Confirmed:** every tower puzzle awards at least one gourd at its base once solved — check the plinth beside the tower door.
- **Confirmed:** the second region's tower has an extra gourd on a balcony above the door, missable if your group runs straight inside.
- **Reported:** at least one tower hides a gourd *behind* its door, requiring a revisit after the tower is lit — being verified.

### 4. High Ground & Vista Puzzles

- **Confirmed:** a gourd at the top of the lighthouse-style structure on the south coast — the climb itself is the puzzle (see the [Puzzles Guide](/guides/puzzles-guide)).
- **Confirmed:** a gourd in the eagle's-nest platform above the central meadow, reachable via a series of narrow ledges.
- **Reported:** binoculars are required to spot a gourd on a remote ridge that is invisible from ground level — "if you can see a landmark but cannot reach it, look for a gourd."

### 5. The Tunnel Network

- **Confirmed:** gourds inside the tunnels on **branch offshoots** past the fast-travel routes — the tunnels have more to offer than transport.
- **Reported:** a gourd that only spawns in a tunnel branch after you have ridden a ski lift at night. Two independent reports, not yet confirmed.

### 6. Camps, Ruins & Unusual Structures

- **Confirmed:** a gourd in the abandoned campsite southwest of the meadow, tucked behind a tent with no puzzle at all — free pick-up.
- **Reported:** structures that "do nothing" on the main path (empty pedestals, sockets, baskets) — multiple players report these are gourd holders that unlock when fed. Test every empty socket you find.

## The Gourd-to-Cutter Machine Questline

The most talked-about secret of launch week: a **machine that accepts gourds and progressively shapes a key**. Early player reports describe:

- A **cutter/processor machine** somewhere in the world with an obvious gourd-shaped feed slot.
- Feeding it gourds **one at a time** — each feed triggers a visible shaping step on an internal workpiece.
- The machine appears to want gourds **in a specific order or count**; players report feeding it "wrong" gourds does nothing.
- The community goal is a **shaped key** that allegedly opens a hidden structure. Whether the key exists, or the machine is a joke, is **unverified** at this writing.

**Status: reported, unconfirmed.** If you find the machine, feed it every gourd you have and document the order — this is the island's biggest open question right now.

## Gourd Farming Tips

Gourds are plentiful, but a few habits make collecting far more efficient:

1. **Assign a collector.** In groups of 4+, designate one player to ferry gourds back while the rest solve. Gourds do not teleport.
2. **Do not hoard.** Gourds are not consumed by carrying — the only way to lose one is to drop it, so if your group already has three, take the fourth to the next tower.
3. **Binocular every structure twice.** Gourds are often placed so a single glance misses them; a second pass from high ground with [binoculars](/guides/tools-guide) finds most of them.
4. **Check after night.** Several gourds are reported to only be collectible (or only visible) at night — the flashlight is a gourd-finding tool.
5. **Revisit towers.** Some gourd rewards are only claimable after the tower is lit.
6. **Do not throw gourds into the ocean.** Unlike cowbells, gourds are progress. (If someone in your group throws one in: it respawns at its original location — you are not soft-locked, just delayed.)
