# Big Walk Walkthrough — The Main Progression Path

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Big Walk is a hand-crafted cooperative adventure with a beginning and an ending. There are no quest markers and no checklist — the story is discovered through walking, puzzle-solving, and unlocking the island piece by piece.

**Honesty note:** Big Walk launched August 4, 2026, and a full step-by-step walkthrough does not exist yet — nobody has published one, and we will not invent one. What this guide provides is the **confirmed progression structure** from previews and launch-day play, so you always know what to do next without spoiling the surprises.

---

## The Progression Loop

Big Walk's main path runs on one loop that never changes:

1. **Walk** — explore the island and find the next puzzle or landmark.
2. **Solve** — complete cooperative puzzles (see the [Puzzles Guide](/guides/puzzles-guide)).
3. **Earn gourds** — puzzle rewards in the form of red gourd-shaped keys.
4. **Unlock towers** — deliver gourds to towers to open new regions.
5. **Expand** — new regions contain more puzzles, more gourds, and new fast travel (ski lifts, tunnels).
6. **Repeat until the island is open** — the main questline resolves through this progression.

If you are ever unsure what to do next, the answer is almost always: **find the next locked tower or unlit structure and bring it a gourd.**

## Act-by-Act Overview (Spoiler-Free)

Previews and early reviews describe the structure in phases. Exact boundaries are unconfirmed, but the shape is consistent:

### Act 1: The First Walk
- You and your group arrive on the island with basic tools.
- Learn the core loop: find a puzzle, solve it verbally, earn your first gourd.
- Unlock your first tower and gain access to the next region.
- **Goal check:** you have solved 2-3 puzzles and unlocked 1-2 towers.

### Act 2: The Island Opens Up
- Gourds start flowing; ski lifts and the tunnel network unlock.
- The group can now split up meaningfully — sub-teams explore different regions.
- Hidden questlines begin appearing alongside main objectives (see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide)).
- **Goal check:** you have unlocked the majority of the island's regions.

### Act 3: The Ending
- Reviewers describe the finale as a **surprise on a familiar game-design maxim** — without spoiling anything: the ending reportedly rewards how well you and your group have learned to understand each other.
- The ending is co-op: the whole group experiences it together.

## Progression Tips

1. **Gourds are always the answer.** A locked tower wants a gourd. A machine wants a gourd (see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide)). An unlit structure probably wants a gourd. If in doubt, carry a gourd there.
2. **Unlock fast travel early.** Ski lifts and tunnels dramatically shrink the map — prioritize towers that reveal transport.
3. **Bring the whole group to towers.** Tower unlocks are a group moment, and the region they open deserves all eyes on it.
4. **Do not rush the main path.** The game's best content (and its most rewarding puzzles) sits off the main line. Previews consistently praised detours.
5. **Let the slowest walker lead.** Big Walk is a walking game; the group that walks together solves together.

## The Ending (Spoiler-Free)

What reviewers have said without spoiling:

- The ending is **widely praised** — multiple outlets called it a highlight and even emotional.
- It is described as a **"subtle but ingenious twist"** on a familiar game-design maxim.
- It reportedly **reflects your co-op journey** — the skills your group built along the way are the point.

If you are reading this before finishing: **do not search for ending details.** The surprise is the reward, and it is best experienced with your group in voice chat.
