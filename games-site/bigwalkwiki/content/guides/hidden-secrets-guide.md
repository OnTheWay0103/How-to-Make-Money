# Big Walk Hidden Secrets & Quests Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Big Walk hides more than the main path. Pre-release coverage confirmed that the island is packed with **hidden questlines, secret areas, and bonus objectives** that reward patient groups with gourds and unlocks. Because the game launched August 4, 2026, the community is still mapping these out — this guide collects what is confirmed and what is being discovered right now.

**Honesty note:** Big Walk is one day old. Anything below marked "reported" or "unverified" is community-sourced at launch and needs confirmation. We will update this page as findings are verified.

---

## Gourds: The Currency of Secrets

Gourd-like red objects — previewers called them "babies" or "nubbins" — are the game's reward items. The community's launch-day understanding:

- **Solved puzzles and completed quests give gourds.**
- Gourds **unlock towers, new areas, and fast travel** (ski lifts, tunnels).
- Some gourds are **optional rewards** — the game's equivalent of collectible treasure.
- Some gourds appear to feed **specific machines and questlines** rather than standard unlocks.

**Practical rule at launch:** if you see a gourd and nothing obvious to do with it, carry it. The destination exists — you just have not found it yet.

---

## The Gourd-to-Cutter Questline (Reported)

The most-talked-about secret at launch is a questline involving **feeding gourds into a cutter machine**. Early player reports describe:

- A **machine that accepts gourds** somewhere in the world (described as a cutter/processor).
- Feeding it gourds appears to trigger **repeatable or escalating rewards**.
- Whether the machine has a **final payoff** (a special area, an ending-related unlock) is **unverified** at this writing.

**Status: reported by early players; not yet independently confirmed by us.** If you find the machine, feed it a gourd and tell us what happens.

## Secret Areas & Off-Path Content

House House has a history of hiding things in plain sight (see: every hidden task in Untitled Goose Game). Early reports and preview details suggest:

- **Secret caves and alcoves** along the island's cliffs and coastline.
- **Structures that do nothing on the main path** but clearly accept input — try buttons, symbols, and (yes) gourds.
- **High-ground rewards:** climbing puzzles with no obvious goal often lead to a gourd or a vista with a clue.
- **Tunnel network offshoots:** the underground tunnels (unlocked with gourds) reportedly have branches beyond the fast-travel routes.

**How to find secret areas:**
1. **Binocular everything.** Read every sign, banner, and symbol from every angle — some messages only make sense from high ground.
2. **Follow the weird.** A structure that looks different from the others, or a patch of ground that is too flat, is usually a hint.
3. **Throw things into the ocean.** Previews confirmed you can steal friends' items and kick them into the sea — but also that items sometimes *matter* where you least expect.
4. **Test the golden head** on every structure. The mystery item may be a universal key or a complete joke — nobody knows yet.

## Hidden Questlines: What We Know

Previews confirmed the island contains **hidden questlines** beyond the main objectives, but specific quest structures are still being discovered. What is safe to say:

- Quests are **discovery-driven** — no quest log confirmed; you find them by exploring and interacting.
- Rewards are **gourd-based** and feed the unlock economy.
- At least one reported questline involves **the cutter machine** (above).
- The game's **ending** has been praised by reviewers as a surprise on "a familiar game design maxim" — strongly hinting that what you find off the path matters for the finish.
