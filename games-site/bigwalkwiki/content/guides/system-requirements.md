# Big Walk System Requirements — PC, Steam Deck & Console

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Everything we know about running Big Walk, from the official Steam listing to console facts.

---

## PC Minimum Requirements (Official)

From the Big Walk Steam page:

| Component | Minimum |
|-----------|---------|
| **OS** | Windows 10 |
| **Processor** | Intel Core i7-7700K or AMD Ryzen 5 1600X |
| **Memory** | 12 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti (2GB), AMD Radeon R7 370 (2GB), or Intel Arc A380 (6GB) |
| **Other** | 64-bit processor and operating system required |

## PC Recommended Requirements (Official)

| Component | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| **OS** | Windows 11 |
| **Processor** | Intel Core i5-10600K or AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| **Memory** | 16 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB), AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT (8GB), or Intel Arc A750 (8GB) |
| **Other** | 64-bit processor and operating system required |

### What the specs mean

- Big Walk is a **stylized open world with realistic terrain** — the requirements are moderate and most gaming PCs from the last 8 years should be fine at minimum settings.
- The **recommended tier** (GTX 1070-class) is a comfortable mid-range machine — no flagship GPU needed.
- **12 GB minimum RAM** is slightly higher than typical for a stylized game; if your machine has 8 GB, expect to close background apps or wait for community performance reports.

*Note: OS requirements can vary by store region; the numbers above are from the official English Steam listing.*

## Performance Notes & Fixes

At launch, the standard advice applies:

- **Update GPU drivers** before the first session.
- On **Windows 11**, ensure game mode/overlays (Discord, GeForce Experience) are not interfering with the network stack — Big Walk is voice-heavy.
- If you experience **stutter on minimum-spec machines**, lower resolution first (the stylized art scales well), then foliage quality — open-world terrain rendering is usually the cost.
- **Crossplay sessions** are most stable when the host has the best upstream connection — have the player with the fastest upload host.
