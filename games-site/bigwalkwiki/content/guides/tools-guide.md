# Big Walk Tools Guide — All Tools & Toys Explained

> Launch Version / Updated: August 5, 2026

Big Walk has no weapons — it has **tools and toys**, and they are the whole game. Tools extend your senses and your voice across the island: the walkie-talkie defeats distance, the binoculars defeat the absent map, and everything else exists to make communication clearer or sillier.

This guide covers every tool confirmed in pre-release coverage and the launch build, where to find each one, and how sharing and respawning work. Where a tool's full behavior is still being figured out, we say so.

## Walkie-Talkie

**The heart of Big Walk.** When voice chat is proximity-based, the walkie-talkie is how you defeat distance: your voice **crackles through it over long range**, letting separated groups stay in conversation.

- **How to use:** pick it up from tool spots around the island; carry it in hand or talk into it to broadcast.
- **Best practice:** every sub-team gets one. If your group is splitting up, the walkie is the minimum kit.
- **Tactical note:** the crackle is intentional and charming — do not expect crystal-clear audio; half of the fun is the radio aesthetic.

## Binoculars

**Your map, your scope, your everything.** Big Walk has no map menu and no objective markers — navigation is landmark-based. Binoculars give you the long-range eyes to:

- Spot **landmarks and structures** on the horizon.
- Read **signs and symbols** that matter for puzzles.
- Relay **directions to your group** ("the yellow tower, left of the ridge...").

Best used from high ground with a "spotter" player dedicated to reading the terrain.

## Laser Pointer

The precision instrument. When you need to point at **a specific button, tile, or symbol** across a distance, a laser dot beats shouting "no, the one on the left — MY left."

- Excellent for **symbol relay puzzles** where one player describes what another must press.
- Also useful for directing **ball hauling** and **tower-building** puzzles from a distance.

## Flashlight

The island has a **day/night cycle**, and nights get **very dark**. The flashlight is your tool for:

- Continuing exploration after sunset.
- Reading details that are only visible with a light source.
- Staying found at night (the beam is visible to your group).

Previews also mention **lanterns** as light sources in the world. Bring at least one flashlight per sub-team when the sun is low.

## Megaphone

The broadcast tool. The megaphone projects your voice **loud and clear over distance** — the opposite of the walkie-talkie's private crackle.

- **Best for big groups (5-12):** one broadcast reaches everyone outdoors.
- **Best outdoors:** open terrain carries the sound further than tunnels and buildings.
- Less useful indoors and in the tunnel network, where it mostly annoys (but sometimes that is the goal).

## Flare

A **bright, visible signal** that works across the whole island.

- Mark a location for regrouping ("flare on the beach — come to the beach").
- Sign your position at night.
- **Sit down, then flare:** they are a limited resource — save them for when the group is split and lost.

*Note: pre-release coverage lists "flares" as tools; a dedicated flare gun has not been confirmed. We will update this entry if one exists in the launch build.*

## Whiteboard

For the note-takers. The whiteboard lets you **write things down** — puzzle solutions, symbol matches, directions.

- Situational: most groups prefer voice and pointing.
- Valuable for **long multi-step puzzles** and for **matching symbol puzzles** where you have to remember what you saw elsewhere.
- Also genuinely funny to leave messages for your friends. Use it.

## Cowbell

A noisemaker. Confirmed as a tool/toys roster item in preview coverage.

- Great for **keeping a big group together at night** — follow the clang.
- The correct use is mostly to ring it near your friends.
- Expect it to be thrown into the ocean at least once per session. That is part of the deal.

## The Golden Head

A **big golden head** appears on the confirmed tools-and-toys list, and no preview fully explained it. Community theories at launch:

- A key or puzzle piece for a specific structure.
- A purely ceremonial joke item (very House House).
- Something to do with the hidden questlines.

**Status: unconfirmed.** We will document it the moment someone figures it out.

## Where to Find Every Tool

Tools sit in the world at **tool spots**: racks, crates, camps, and landmarks where equipment naturally accumulates. There is no shop and no unlock screen — you find them on the island, pick them up, and carry them.

The locations below are from launch-day play and community reports; "Confirmed" means multiple reports, "Reported" means unverified. The island is 1.4 km² with no map menu, and no complete tool map has been published anywhere yet.

### Walkie-Talkie — Essential

- **Confirmed: the spawn area tool rack.** Every group's first tool spot — two walkie-talkies sit on a wooden rack next to the first puzzle, impossible to miss.
- **Confirmed: campsite tables.** The abandoned campsite southwest of the meadow has one on a table.
- **Confirmed: tower plinths.** Lit towers display a walkie-talkie beside their doors — a natural resupply as you progress.
- **Reported:** a walkie-talkie inside a tunnel branch near the northern lift.

**Rule of thumb:** if a location has chairs, a table, or a rack, check it — walkie-talkies are the most common tool on the island for a reason.

### Binoculars — Essential

- **Confirmed: the lookout platform above the spawn beach.** Binoculars on a post, facing the horizon — the game's way of teaching you the "spotter" role in the first ten minutes.
- **Confirmed: high-ground lookouts** on the cliff trail west of the beach.
- **Confirmed: the south lighthouse summit**, next to the vista puzzle.
- **Reported:** a pair at the eagle's-nest platform above the central meadow.

**Rule of thumb:** binoculars spawn at places you'd want binoculars — high ground, overlooks, and summits. If you can see the whole island from a spot, check it for a pair.

### Laser Pointer — High priority

- **Confirmed: the tool shed near the first tower.** A small shed with a "TOOLS" sign (the only sign that says it plainly) holds a laser pointer on a shelf.
- **Confirmed: workshop benches** in the mid-island workshop area (the structure cluster with gears and pulleys).
- **Reported:** a laser pointer on a bench at the south coast pier.

### Megaphone — Medium

- **Confirmed: the central meadow stage.** A small stage/bandstand structure holds a megaphone — appropriately, the island's loudest tool lives where a crowd would gather.
- **Confirmed: picnic area tables** near the meadow's north edge.
- **Reported:** a megaphone in the abandoned campsite (one report).

### Flares — Medium

- **Confirmed: coastal supply crates.** Crate clusters along the shoreline (especially east of the spawn beach) hold 1-2 flares each.
- **Confirmed: the pier at the south coast.**
- **Reported:** flares in the tunnel entrance cache near the central meadow.

**Rule of thumb:** flares are found where supplies would be stored — crates, caches, and the pier. They are limited: each spot holds one or two.

### Flashlight — High priority

- **Confirmed: the campfire sites.** Three campfire clusters (spawn area, meadow edge, cliff trail) each have a flashlight propped by the fire — the game putting light where the dark will come.
- **Confirmed: the tunnel entrance cache** at the north tunnel mouth.
- **Reported:** a flashlight on the lighthouse structure's landing halfway up.

**Rule of thumb:** flashlight = night content. If a spot is near a tunnel, a cave, or a campfire, check it before sunset.

### Whiteboard — Situational

- **Confirmed: the schoolhouse-style building** in the mid-island ruins (the building with desks and a blackboard) — whiteboards and markers sit on the teacher's desk.
- **Confirmed: puzzle sites with symbol relays** — several relay puzzles have a whiteboard staged nearby as a hint (see the [Puzzles Guide](/guides/puzzles-guide)).
- **Reported:** a whiteboard at the gourd-to-cutter machine's reported location (unverified — and if true, extremely useful).

### Cowbell — Fun

- **Confirmed: the pasture** — the fenced field with animals, on a fencepost at the gate.
- **Confirmed: the bandstand stage** next to the megaphone (the tool rack beneath the stage).
- **Reported:** a cowbell hanging from a tree near the cliff trail.

**Rule of thumb:** cowbells are where animals or music would be. They are the easiest tool to lose (friends throw them in the sea), which is why they respawn so reliably.

### Golden Head — Mystery

- **Reported (single location, multiple reports):** the golden head appears at **a small shrine-like structure on the island's north coast**, on a pedestal that looks made for it. Reports of additional golden heads elsewhere are **unconfirmed**.
- **What it does: unknown.** Community theories range from a universal key to a complete joke — see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide) for the ongoing investigation.

**This is the community's #1 open question.** If you find a golden head anywhere else, [contact us](/contact) immediately.

## How Tools Are Shared & Respawn

**Tools are per-player and physical.** Each tool exists as a single object at a time:

- Pick up a tool and it's **in your hand** — nobody else can use it until you drop it, hand it off, or throw it.
- Groups share by **handing tools around** — the game's quiet design push toward communication ("can I have the binoculars back?").
- When a tool is dropped, it stays where it dropped (briefly) before respawning at its spot.
- The first-pick-up notification (the game names a tool on screen the first time your group finds it) is per-group, not per-player — everyone has technically "found" it once one player has.

**Practical result:** a 12-player group does not have 12 walkie-talkies. Tool scarcity is a feature, and splitting sub-teams means allocating gear — see the [Multiplayer & Co-op Guide](/guides/multiplayer-coop-guide).

**Respawn behavior:**

- **Respawn timer:** dropped tools return to their home spot within a few minutes (community-measured; exact timing varies by spot).
- **Restart respawn:** restarting a walk resets every tool to its home spot.
- **Ocean rule:** tools thrown into the sea do *not* respawn at the water's edge — they return to their home spots. No tool is permanently lost.
- **Gourds vs tools:** gourds follow similar rules but are not classified as tools — see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide).

**Progression gating:** most tools are free world pickups — no gourd cost, no puzzle required, find-and-grab. Some tool spots appear to be gated by progression (reported): the lighthouse binoculars and the pier flare crates are only reachable after towers open those regions. No tool is permanently missable.

## Tool Kit Recommendations by Group Size

**Duo (2 players):** Walkie-Talkie + Binoculars minimum. Add a Flashlight for evening walks.

**Trio (3 players):** Spotter carries Binoculars, talker carries Walkie-Talkie, third carries Flashlight + Flare. Laser Pointer if the group splits.

**Full party (4+):** One Walkie-Talkie per sub-team, Binoculars for each spotter, one Megaphone, and all the Flares you can carry. Cowbells mandatory for morale.

## Tools & Puzzles

Tools are not just equipment — several puzzle types exist *because* of tools:

- **Relay puzzles:** one player spots with binoculars, describes a symbol sequence, another replicates it (laser pointer makes it precise).
- **Long-distance coordination:** walkie-talkie relay chains — one player describes a landmark to another, who describes it to a third.
- **Night sections:** flashlight-required exploration and reading (see the [Island Exploration Guide](/guides/island-exploration-guide)).
- **Symbol matching:** whiteboard note-taking shines here — write down what you see at site A to match at site B.
- **Grouping puzzles:** megaphone calls gather a scattered group to one trigger point.
- **The golden head question:** every unusual structure should be tested with the head — if it is a key, the puzzles it opens are the island's deepest secret.

Full puzzle mechanics are in the [Puzzles Guide](/guides/puzzles-guide).
