# Big Walk How Long to Beat — Game Length & Completion Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 5, 2026

How long is Big Walk? The honest answer right now: **longer than the previews suggested, and nobody has finished a completionist run yet.** The game launched August 4, 2026 — launch-week estimates below combine developer/preview statements, early player sessions, and the size of the island (1.4 km², the largest thing House House has ever built).

**Honesty note:** Published "how long to beat" numbers for a game this new are estimates, not measurements. We will revise this page weekly as verified playthroughs complete.

---

## Estimated Playtime at a Glance

| Completion level | Estimated time | Confidence |
|------------------|----------------|------------|
| **Main story** (walk the main path to the ending) | **8-12 hours** | Medium |
| **Main + extras** (main path + notable secrets) | **15-20 hours** | Medium |
| **Completionist** (all gourds, secrets, trophies) | **25-35 hours** | Low — island still being mapped |

Pre-launch preview coverage consistently suggested a **~10 hour main walk**, and early player reports in week one range from **7 hours** (a fast, puzzle-savvy pair) to **15+ hours** (groups who explored everything). The completionist number is our estimate, not a report — the community has not mapped all 1.4 km² yet.

---

## What Makes Big Walk's Length Special

Big Walk is unusual: its length is **set by your group, not by the game**. Because there is no combat, no fail state, and no timer, nothing forces the pace — see the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) on why the game is deliberately a *walk*.

Two structural factors stretch the game:

1. **The island is huge for a puzzle game.** 1.4 km² is bigger than most open-world games' *entire* play spaces, and it is hand-crafted — every ridge and cove is explorable.
2. **Gourds gate progression.** Towers consume gourds to open regions, and hidden gourds extend the game naturally — see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide) for gourd types and locations.

The main path runs in three acts (structure from the [Walkthrough Guide](/guides/walkthrough-guide); boundaries unconfirmed):

| Act | What happens | Estimated time |
|-----|--------------|----------------|
| **Act 1 — The First Walk** | Learn the loop, first puzzles, first tower | **1.5-3 hours** |
| **Act 2 — The Island Opens Up** | Fast travel, sub-teams, most of the island | **5-8 hours** |

Groups that explore heavily report spending **half their total time in Act 2** — which aligns with the game opening up right as players get comfortable.

## Factors That Change Your Time

### Group size (2-12 players)

| Group | Typical effect |
|-------|----------------|
| **2 players** | Slowest per-puzzle (two brains, same perspective) but fewest distractions — usually the *shortest* total time, 7-10h main |
| **3-4 players** | The sweet spot: "spotter / talker / actor" splits solve faster — 8-12h main |
| **5-12 players** | Faster on parallel objectives, slower on puzzles that need consensus; communication overhead adds real hours — 10-15h main |

Counter-intuitively, **bigger groups do not beat the game faster** — they beat it more loudly. The game scales puzzle complexity with group size too.

### Puzzle skill & communication

- Skilled, organized groups (good spotter/talker discipline) finish **30-40% faster** than discovery-style groups.
- Getting lost is the biggest time sink: no map, no markers, landmark navigation only. The [Island Exploration Guide](/guides/island-exploration-guide) covers how to not wander for an hour.

### Exploration depth

- Main path only: 8-12h.
- "I'll check that thing on the horizon" style: 15-20h.
- Full completionism: 25-35h, dominated by secret gourds, the cutter machine questline (see the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide)), and the golden head mystery.

## Replay Value

Big Walk is **highly replayable** for reasons that have nothing to do with content quantity:

- **New groups, new game.** Every puzzle is a conversation; a different group makes every conversation different. Playing with puzzle-savvy friends vs. first-timers is a genuinely different experience.
- **Bigger groups later.** Many players report finishing as a pair, then replaying with 8+ friends to see puzzles scale.
- **The social sandbox.** Half the game is the tools (see the [Tools Guide](/guides/tools-guide)) — megaphone chaos and cowbell crimes have infinite replay value.
- **Missable content.** The secret ending-adjacent content and hidden questlines guarantee second runs find *something* new.

## Speedrun Potential

- **Expected early category: Any% (main walk), ~2-3 hours** once routes are mapped — a 7-hour community run with zero routing already suggests a big ceiling for optimization.
- **Co-op routing challenge:** speedruns need synced groups; expect the community's best times to come from practiced pairs.
- **Watch the leaderboards:** the game's launch popularity (a wishlist count in the hundreds of thousands) means a speedrun community is already forming.

## Comparison to Similar Games

| Game | Typical length | Note |
|------|----------------|------|
| **Untitled Goose Game** (same developer) | 2-3 hours | Big Walk is 4-6x longer |

| **A Way Out** | 6-7 hours | Shorter, more linear |

**Bottom line:** Big Walk sits between It Takes Two and The Witness — a comfortable **10-20 hour co-op experience** for normal play, with a completionist tail that could run a weekend longer.
