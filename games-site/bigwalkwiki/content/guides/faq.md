# Big Walk FAQ — Everything You Need to Know

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

The quick answers to the questions everyone is asking about Big Walk. For details, follow the linked guides.

---

## Release Date & Platforms

**When did Big Walk release?**
Tuesday, **August 4, 2026**, at 6am PDT / 9am EDT / 1pm UTC / 2pm BST.

**What platforms is Big Walk on?**
**Steam (PC and Mac), PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2.** It is not on Xbox.

**Who made it?**
**House House**, the ~10-person Melbourne studio behind Untitled Goose Game and Push Me Pull You, published by **Panic** (Firewatch, Thank Goodness You're Here!).

---

## Price & Purchase

**How much does Big Walk cost?**
**$19.99 USD** on Steam and Switch 2, with a **25% launch discount** at launch. Regional pricing may vary.

**Is Big Walk free on PS5?**
For PS Plus subscribers, yes — it is the **PlayStation Plus Essential day-one free game for August 2026** (available across all PS Plus tiers from **August 4 to September 1, 2026**).

**Do I need PS Plus to play?**
To claim it for free, yes. Whether an online subscription is required for crossplay sessions follows the normal platform rules.

## Co-op & Multiplayer

**How many players?**
**2-12 players.** Co-op only — there is no single-player mode.

**Is there matchmaking?**
**No.** Big Walk is designed for playing with friends; you invite them yourself.

**Is there crossplay?**
**Yes, full crossplay** between PC (Steam), Mac, PS5, and Nintendo Switch 2.

**Can I play Big Walk solo?**
**No.** You need at least two players.

**Is there couch co-op / local multiplayer?**
Not confirmed at launch; Big Walk is an online co-op game.

---

## Gameplay

**Is there combat in Big Walk?**
**No.** No combat, no enemies, no health bars, no timers, no fail states. It is a relaxed co-op adventure about walking and talking.

**How does communication work?**
**Proximity voice chat** — voices fade with distance, echo in tunnels, muffle through walls, and crackle through walkie-talkies. Pointing, waving and flailing gestures ("Arms!!") cover the rest.

**What tools are in the game?**
Walkie-talkies, binoculars, laser pointers, megaphones, flares, flashlights, whiteboards, cowbells, and a big golden head. See the [Tools Guide](/guides/tools-guide).

**What do you do in Big Walk?**
Explore a large island, solve co-op puzzles, earn gourd keys, unlock towers, tunnels and ski lifts, and follow hidden questlines. See the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide).

## Length & Content

**How long is Big Walk?**
**Official playtime is not confirmed yet.** It is a hand-crafted adventure with a beginning and an ending (not an endless sandbox). We will publish community average playtimes once they exist.

**Does Big Walk have an ending?**
**Yes.** Reviewers have praised the ending as an ingenious surprise. Avoid spoilers until you finish.

**Is there replay value?**
Yes — hidden questlines, secret areas, and the inherently social nature of the game give groups reasons to walk again. See the [Hidden Secrets Guide](/guides/hidden-secrets-guide).

**Is there DLC or post-launch content planned?**
Nothing officially announced at launch.
