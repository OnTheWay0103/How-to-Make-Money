## What is Big Walk?

Big Walk is a 2-12 player cooperative open world puzzle adventure developed by House House — the ~10-person Melbourne studio behind Untitled Goose Game and Push Me Pull You — and published by Panic (Firewatch, Thank Goodness You're Here!). It launched on August 4, 2026.

You and your friends explore a large open island (roughly 1.4 km square, inspired by Wilsons Promontory National Park in Australia) dotted with bright, cartoonishly colorful structures. There is no combat, no health bars, no timers and no fail states. The entire game is built around walking, talking, and solving puzzles together — the developers call it a "cooperative online walker-talker."

Communication is the core mechanic. Voice chat is proximity-based: voices fade with distance, echo down corridors, muffle through walls, and crackle through walkie-talkies. The game has no map menu and no objective markers — you navigate by spotting landmarks on the horizon and relaying directions to each other.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **2-12 Players, Co-op Only** — No single-player mode and no matchmaking; bring your own friends. Puzzles scale for groups of 2-4+
- **Proximity Voice Chat** — Voices travel through the world; walkie-talkies extend your reach; gestures ("Arms!!") cover the rest
- **Gourd Rewards** — Solving puzzles earns gourd-like red objects that act as keys to unlock towers, new areas, and fast travel (ski lifts, tunnels)
- **No Map, No Markers** — Landmark-based navigation; binoculars are your long-range eyes
- **Day/Night Cycle** — Nights get very dark; flashlights and lanterns matter
- **Tools & Toys** — Walkie-talkies, binoculars, laser pointers, megaphones, flares, whiteboards, cowbells, and a big golden head
- **Hidden Quests & Secrets** — Secret questlines and areas beyond the main path, being discovered at launch
