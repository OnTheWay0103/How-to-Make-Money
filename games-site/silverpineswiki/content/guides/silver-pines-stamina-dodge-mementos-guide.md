**Silver Pines has a stamina bar, and it is short. Two things drain it: dodging, and swinging a melee weapon. Running does not drain it at all.**

That combination is unusual, and it tells you exactly what kind of combat the game wants. Stamina is not a traversal meter you manage while moving around the town — it is a **combat-only resource**, which means it functions as a second ammunition pool. When it is empty, you cannot dodge, and in a horror game a character who cannot dodge takes damage.

> **Sourcing note.** The Steam store page does not describe the stamina system. Everything below is **Community-reported** from demo players and preview coverage, which is consistent on the mechanics but has published no numbers. Exact stamina values, regeneration rates and Memento effects are **[Unconfirmed]**.

The fact that running is free is the most important detail here, and it is the design choice that separates Silver Pines from a Soulslike. Stamina does not gate your movement through the world. It gates your **actions inside a fight**.

## What That Means in Practice

### Distance is always available to you

Because running costs nothing, **disengaging is always an option**. You can always leave a fight — the limitation is not stamina, it is whether the thing chasing you is faster, and whether the route behind you is clear.

This lines up with the official store description, which frames the game as *"fight or avoid the horrors of the town"*. Avoiding is a supported answer, and the stamina design is what makes it mechanically viable.

### An empty bar is a countdown, not a cooldown

An empty stamina bar means no dodge. If you are still in range of something that attacks, you are going to take the hit. The practical rule: **never spend your last stamina on offence.** Melee swings consume the same pool you need to escape with.

### Fights have a natural rhythm: burst, disengage, recover

Because both attacking and dodging draw on the same short bar, sustained aggression is impossible by design. The loop the systems push you toward is:

1. **Close and swing** until the bar is low
2. **Disengage** — running is free, so this costs you nothing but positioning
3. **Let stamina recover** while you are out of range
4. **Re-engage**

Preview coverage describes fights as requiring you to judge whether you are in a position to dodge and counter, which is exactly this loop.

### You cannot tank hits

Player reports describe Walker as fragile — taking only a few hits before dying, and healing items being scarce enough that every one is *"maximized"*. Stamina is not a buffer you can absorb damage with. It is the resource that prevents damage, and damage is expensive.

## Coffee: The Stamina Item With a Catch

Preview coverage reports that **drinking coffee restores stamina**, and that the action **takes real time** — leaving you vulnerable while you do it [Community-reported].

That is a deliberate trap, and it mirrors the reload mechanic: the thing you consume to keep fighting leaves you stationary while you consume it. Sources describing it consistently warn against using it with an enemy nearby.

The relationship between coffee and the game's other consumables is worth noting — coffee is a consumable you buy with the same coins that fund healing items, tape, ammunition and your saves. See the [coin economy guide](/guides/silver-pines-coins-vending-machines-economy).

What is useful and *is* consistently reported: **Mementos are not puzzle keys.** Preview coverage makes a point of this — they are ability buffs rather than the odd knick-knacks you collect for locks. That matters for how you treat them: a Memento is permanently useful, so it is worth a detour in a way that an unidentified key is not.

## Combat Rules That Follow From the Systems

These are conclusions rather than official instructions, but they follow directly from how the systems are described.

1. **Never empty the bar on offence.** Keep enough stamina for one dodge at all times
2. **Running is free — use it as your reset.** There is no stamina cost to repositioning, only to the actions you take once you are there
3. **Fight in bursts.** Attack, break off, recover, return
4. **Avoid fights you do not need.** The store page supports it and the stamina economy rewards it
5. **Do not drink coffee with something in the room.** The restore costs you the same exposure a reload does
6. **Treat healing items as scarce.** Every healing item matters — that is on the store page, not just in player reports
7. **Do not repair or reload mid-fight if you can avoid it.** Both are real-time actions that leave you stationary, on top of a bar you need for dodging
