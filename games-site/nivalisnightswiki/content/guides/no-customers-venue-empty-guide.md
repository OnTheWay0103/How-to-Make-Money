# Nivalis Nights No Customers: Why Your Venue Is Empty

An empty Nivalis Nights venue is usually not random. A customer only considers a venue that is open, has a cookable menu, has cooking and serving staff with more than an hour of shift overlap left, and has a free seat. Popularity and distance then decide who wins. Check those gates in order before blaming RNG.

## The five gates every customer checks

| Gate | What must be true | What it looks like when it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Open + menu | Venue switched on; at least one dish is cookable from current stock | Menu shows missing ingredients; kitchen cannot make anything |
| 2. Staff overlap | Cook **and** server on shift, with more than 1 hour left in the overlap | Staff are present but orders are not taken or not served |
| 3. Free seat | At least one free seat; covered on rain or storm days | People walk past a room that looks half empty |
| 4. Popularity + distance | Venue weight is above 0 | Venue is eligible but always loses to rivals |
| 5. Hunger + timing | Customer is at least half hungry; rush window is open | Dead mid-afternoon, busy around opening |

This page diagnoses empty venues. For the opening loop, see the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide); for stock that never arrives, see [Fill Venue Inventory Stuck](/guides/fill-venue-inventory-stuck-guide).

## Gate 1: The venue is open, but the menu is not cookable

A non-empty menu is not a cookable menu. The community reference says a dish the kitchen cannot cook is treated like an out-of-stock item and excluded. [Community] A menu full of missing ingredients can leave the kitchen with nothing a customer may order.

This is the failure mode behind many "no customers all day" reports from the early build: when manager deliveries failed, the menu became a wall of missing ingredients, nothing could be served, and no customers turned up. [Community, retired mod] Update #3 fixed customers not leaving when you take orders without required processors, and Update #4 made abandoned-order ingredients return to storage. [Official]

Check each dish: ingredients in **venue storage**, the right processor, and a menu short enough to cook.

## Gate 2: Cooking and serving shifts must overlap

The visit rule is stricter than "someone is on shift". Cooking and serving staff must both be on shift, with more than one hour left in the overlap; the venue stops taking new customers in that final hour. [Community] A cook alone or a waiter alone does not qualify.

Update #2 agrees: "Single staff venues will likely leave many customers unserved if there are too many seats." [Official, 2026-10-02] The task order also puts serving and processing above taking a new order, so one worker can spend the shift in the kitchen. [Official]

Check overlap length, and whether there are more seats than the team can serve. Player reports describe the symptom: "Picking up one plate as a waiter is laughable", and a reviewer returned to "a slew of red-faced customers still waiting for their orders". [Player; Third-party]

## Gate 3: A free seat, and a covered one when it rains

A venue needs a free seat. On rain or storm days it must be covered - under a roof or an umbrella seat - unless the customer is very hungry; snow does not impose the same restriction. [Community]

That makes traffic collapse only in bad weather while the room, menu and staff look fine. The fix is physical. Update #7 also fixed seating and door problems at venues such as Octane & Olive and Yummy Canteen, so check for a broken seat after a patch. [Official, 2026-10-29]

Do not overbuild seats: Update #2 warns that too many seats for a single worker leaves customers unserved. [Official]

## Gate 4: Popularity and distance decide the winner

Passing the first three gates only enters the draw. The community reference describes each venue's weight as its popularity with that customer group plus a distance adjustment from +0.1 adjacent to -0.1 far away. A weight of 0 or less means the venue is never chosen; among positive weights, selection is proportional to weight. [Community]

Ranking is relative. Update #5 made "a venue's tier, popularity and income now count equally", compares income with the city's best earners, and says "earnings alone no longer decide the order". [Official, 2026-10-15] It also fixed a bug that pushed smaller rivals' popularity to zero: "This affects how venue visits are decided and could influence (and potentially reduce) your customer activity." [Official]

Owner reputation became a stronger ranking factor, and the 16 October patch fixed Dagmar's Sabotage popularity debuff not resetting. [Official] If traffic dropped after a story choice, check the rank screen.

## Gate 5: Hunger cycles and rush windows

Customers are not always hungry. The community reference says a townsperson searches when at least half hungry, waits two hours after a sit-down visit, and twenty minutes after a failed search. [Community] The 16 October patch changed the clock: "Everyone restores their hunger every 6 game hours now", with "a big morning rush when nearly everyone is fully hungry." [Official, 2026-10-16]

An empty room at 3 p.m. can be correct. Judge traffic across a full day. If the morning rush is also empty, return to gates 1-4.

## When it is a bug, not a design problem

Several official fixes touch traffic directly, so version matters:

| Patch | What changed | If you still see it |
|---|---|---|
| Update #2 (2026-10-02) | Task order; single-staff venue warning | Fix shifts and seat count |
| Update #3 (2026-10-05) | Orders without the required processors | Confirm the processor exists |
| Update #4 (2026-10-09) | Cancelled reviews; abandoned-order ingredients | Recheck the menu |
| Update #5 (2026-10-15) | Rival popularity bug; ranking rebalance | Check the rank screen |
| Small Patch (2026-10-16) | Hunger every 6 game hours; Dagmar debuff | Expect a morning rush |
| Update #6 (2026-10-22) | Venues run while you sleep | Check the end-of-day report |
| Update #7 (2026-10-29) | Managers do delivery runs | Confirm a manager is on shift |

If all five gates pass, stock is correct, popularity is positive, and the room is still empty through a full day on the latest build, report it with version, venue, menu, roster and screenshots.

## The five-minute diagnostic

1. **Venue overview.** Is it switched on, and is the menu non-empty?
2. **Every dish.** Ingredients in venue storage? Right processor?
3. **Roster.** Do cooking and serving staff overlap by more than one hour?
4. **Seats.** Is there a free seat, and is it covered in rain or storms?
5. **Rank screen.** Is popularity positive, or did a story event apply a debuff?
6. **Clock.** Are you judging during a rush or a dead hour?
7. **Update and retest.** Run one full in-game day on the latest patch.

## What actually raises traffic long-term

Traffic follows reputation; reputation follows operations. The community guide groups satisfaction into service speed, freshness and atmosphere; bare venues get less traffic. [Community] Update #2 confirms freshness affects "your customer reviews", and Update #4 rebalanced negative reviews from cancelled guest visits. [Official]

Invest in this order: **service first** (a cook and server who overlap for the rush beat another decor item), **freshness second** (buy closer to what you sell; see [Cooking, Recipes & Rotten Food](/guides/cooking-recipes-rotten-food-guide)), **atmosphere third** (decor cannot rescue a kitchen that never serves). [Community]

Do not over-automate early: managers handle delivery after Update #7 but drain money, and players report them waiting for you to solve problems. [Player] Read the [Manager & Restock Guide](/guides/manager-restock-guide) before hiring one.

The mood icon and star review can disagree. The 16 October patch fixed a visual bug where customers showed an unhappy smiley after eating and then no review, calling it "entirely visual". [Official] A Steam review reports the same confusion: customers "are always unhappy, but can still leave a 4-5* review." [Player] Trust the written review and service record, not one face.

## FAQ

**Why is my restaurant empty at 3 p.m.?**
Customers search when at least half hungry, and hunger restores every 6 game hours, creating a large morning rush. A quiet afternoon is often timing, not a bug. [Official; Community]

**Do I need a manager to get customers?**
No. Managers help with delivery after Update #7, but visits depend on menu, staff overlap, seats and popularity. [Official]

**Why did traffic drop after a patch?**
Update #5 rebalanced ranking and fixed rival popularity; the 16 October patch changed hunger. Recheck the rank screen and judge a full day. [Official]

**Can bad reviews stop customers?**
Popularity affects visits, and reviews are one input the community associates with reputation. The exact formula is unpublished. [Official; Community]
