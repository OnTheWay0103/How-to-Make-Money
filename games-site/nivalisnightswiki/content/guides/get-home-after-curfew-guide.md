# Nivalis Nights: How to Get Home After Curfew & Sleep

The curfew in Nivalis Nights shuts the city down in the small hours, and being outside when it hits costs you a fine and an evening. Sleep in your own bed, rent a second apartment, or use the emergency apartment that opens in each district — and plan the trip home first.

This guide covers that one moment: it is late, you are in the wrong district, and the streets are about to stop being yours. For the wider time system, read the [Day & Night Cycle Guide](/guides/day-night-cycle-guide); for the districts themselves, see the [City Guide](/guides/city-districts-guide).

## The curfew is a rule with teeth

The developer describes the curfew as a boundary between two kinds of play, not a punishment screen. The August 2025 community blog puts it plainly: *"As long as you follow the rules, respect the curfew, and stay within your home at night, you'll be safe."* The March 2026 Q&A repeats the idea when asked whether the day/night cycle affects gameplay: *"day and night life offer different opportunities (after all there's a curfew for a reason)."*

Two things follow, and both matter:

- **The night is a second game.** Day and night content are deliberately different, so the curfew is the seam between them.
- **Being caught is survivable.** Update 6 shipped a fix reading *"Getting caught during curfew no longer traps you"* — which means getting caught is a normal, expected event, and before that patch it could leave you stuck.

## Why the first nights go wrong

The complaint is not that the curfew exists. It is that the game gives you almost no help finding your way back before it lands. A single launch-week thread on the Quarter To Three forum contains four players hitting the same wall:

| Player | What they reported |
|---|---|
| Enidigm | "navigating these streets is almost impossible without an overhead map"; "I keep getting lost trying to get home and keep getting fined for being out too late" |
| Enidigm | "I still don't actually understand where th I live" |
| Papageno | "once I'd bought them I had no idea how to get back home"; "When I did it the second time I memorized the route" |
| Gordon_Bleu | "I almost wish you could turn off curfew mode and just give the player energy/sleep bars" |

Nivalis is a vertical city of stacked districts, there is no overhead map to orient by, and the quest journal tracks objectives rather than your bed. The game assumes you have learned the walk home. On day one you have not.

## Four places to sleep

There is more than one answer to "where do I sleep", and the right one changes as your business spreads across districts.

| Option | What it is | Best for | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your own apartment** | The starting home you inherit | Nights when you finish near home | Official — apartments are rented housing you can furnish |
| **A second rented apartment** | Another home in another district | Working a venue across town | Official — "rent as many as your wallet allows", and "one in a strategic location can make your movements around the city much more convenient" |
| **District emergency apartment** | A temporary place that opens during curfew | Getting caught far from home | [Community] — players report "every district has an emergency apartment or two that opens up during curfew" |
| **Staying out** | No bed; you keep playing the night | Deliberate night runs | [Community] — players describe getting "fined for being out too late" |

The middle two are the ones most players never use. The official blog is explicit that multiple apartments are allowed and that location is the point — a cheap studio in the district you actually work in does more for your evenings than a bigger flat you cannot reach. The emergency apartment solves the opposite problem: you did not plan, lockdown caught you, and you need a door that opens. Treat it as a safety net rather than a strategy.

## How to get home before lockdown

The fix is boring and it works. Build the trip into the day instead of reacting to the clock.

1. **Decide where you are sleeping before you leave in the morning.** If you end the day in a district you do not live in, the walk home is already lost.
2. **Do your last errand in that district.** Late furniture shopping is the classic way to end up across town with a full inventory.
3. **Travel while travel still works.** Enidigm's version: "maybe the idea is to fast travel directly to your apartment before curfew hits ... rather than try to take a taxi and manually walk back."
4. **Learn one route properly.** Papageno was lost the first time and memorised the route the second. Pick the walk between your venue, your bed and the market, and stop improvising it.
5. **Leave slack.** If the warning catches you mid-trip, you want enough evening left to finish on foot.

## What happens if security catches you

Being spotted is a cost, not a death. Players in the thread above report being fined for being out too late rather than losing progress, and Update 6's "no longer traps you" fix confirms the developers expect you to reach that state routinely.

Plan for three consequences:

- **A fine.** The amount is community-reported rather than published, so do not budget around a number. [Community]
- **A lost night.** Time spent avoiding security is time not spent on your venue, quests or ingredients.
- **A worse morning.** Whatever you were doing when lockdown hit is unfinished, and morning is when the city reopens.

The bug that could leave you stuck is gone as of Update 6, but the fine did not go with it. Getting caught is still the expensive way to end an evening.

## When staying out is the right call

Every so often the answer really is "do not go home". Players describe the night layer as the point of the system: the curfew is when the game's seedy options open up, and going to bed early means skipping them. [Community]

The judgement call is simple if you make it in advance:

| You are doing this tomorrow | Stay out tonight? |
|---|---|
| Restocking a venue, opening a business, a quest with a timed step | No — sleep, keep the morning clean |
| A night-only objective, or a run specifically for curfew content | Yes — plan the emergency apartment as your exit |
| Nothing in particular | No — there is no upside to an accidental fine |

If you do stay out, decide where the night ends before it starts: not surviving until morning, but reaching a specific door.

## Sleeping early still earns — check your venues first

Update 6 changed the calculus of going to bed. The patch notes state it directly: *"Venues keep running while you sleep. When you go to bed before curfew, the evening plays out: customers are served, pay and leave reviews, and staff are paid for those hours."*

An early night is not lost revenue; it is how your venues settle the evening. It is also why sleeping is the wrong moment to discover a bad restock budget — the venue now trades while you are unconscious. Set the manager's daily budget and supply target before you sleep, not after. The [Manager Restock Guide](/guides/manager-restock-guide) covers those settings, and the [Cooking & Rotten Food Guide](/guides/cooking-recipes-rotten-food-guide) covers stock that sits too long.

## Evening checklist

- [ ] Know which bed you are ending the day in
- [ ] Finish shopping and deliveries before the warning, not after
- [ ] Use fast travel while it is available
- [ ] Confirm the venue's supply target and daily budget before bed
- [ ] If you are staying out, know which emergency apartment you are aiming for
- [ ] If you get caught, note the fine and move on

## What is not confirmed

ION LANDS has documented *that* the curfew exists and *that* getting caught is a normal state. It has not published the rules around it, so these are player-reported and should be treated as **[Unconfirmed]** for your build:

- **The exact start time.** Community reports overwhelmingly put lockdown at 2:00 AM with a warning beforehand, but at least one review describes an earlier start. Watch your own clock for two nights.
- **The size of a fine,** and whether it scales with repeat offences.
- **Whether emergency apartments are free,** or charged like rented housing.
- **Whether rent is billed per property per day** once you own several homes.
- **Whether the boat cabin works as a bed.** It is widely repeated in community guides; we have not verified it.

## FAQ

**Can I fast travel during the curfew?**
Players report that crossing districts during curfew is the hard part, and that the reliable approach is to travel before it starts. Do not plan a cross-city trip as your last action of the night.

**What happens if I go to bed before the curfew?**
Your evening still resolves. Update 6 made venues run while you sleep, so customers are served, pay and review, and staff are paid for those hours.

**Do I have to go home at all?**
No. The apartment is one option among several — the emergency apartment exists precisely for nights when home is out of reach, and staying out is a legitimate way to play the night layer.

**Why do I keep getting fined?**
Almost always because the trip home started too late. Shopping in one district and sleeping in another is the usual cause, and the fix is choosing your bed before you leave in the morning.

The curfew is not there to punish exploration. It is there to make the last hour of every day a decision — and once you make that decision in the morning instead of at midnight, the fines stop.
