# Nivalis Nights vs Cloudpunk — Same City, Very Different Game

If you played *Cloudpunk* — ION LANDS' beloved neon-noir delivery adventure — you already know the city of Nivalis. The rain. The voxel glow. The skylanes over the canyon streets. Nivalis Nights returns to that city, and Cloudpunk fans are the biggest audience the new game has — one of the most-wishlisted upcoming games on Steam, many of those wishlists from players who flew Rania's HOVA through the same rain.

But here's the honest headline: **Nivalis Nights is not Cloudpunk 2.** It's a different genre set in the same universe — a life simulation where you live in the city instead of delivering across it. This guide compares the two games system by system, so series fans know exactly what's familiar, what's new, and what to expect.

## The Big Picture

| | Cloudpunk (2020) | Nivalis Nights (Sept 29, 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **Genre** | Story-driven adventure / "deliver 'em up" | Life simulation / business management |
| **Perspective** | Third-person HOVA flight + on-foot sections | First-person (confirmed by developer) |
| **Protagonist** | Rania, a delivery driver | You — an heir to a noodle bar |
| **Core loop** | Pick up packages, fly, deliver, talk | Open your business, serve customers, live your life |
| **Combat** | None | None |
| **The city** | Nivalis, the voxel megacity | The same city, rebuilt from street level |
| **Time scale** | A single rain-soaked night | Full day-night cycles, seasons of play |
| **Length** | ~10-12 hours main story | Dozens of hours, ongoing (see [How Long to Beat](/guides/how-long-to-beat-guide)) |
| **Developer** | ION LANDS | ION LANDS |

The developer's own framing is blunt: Cloudpunk was the story of one night's deliveries; Nivalis Nights is the story of a life lived in the same city.

## The Shared Universe — What's the Same

### The City of Nivalis
This is the most important continuity. Nivalis Nights is set in the same rain-soaked, neon-lit, voxel-art city as Cloudpunk — a city built in the sky, its districts stacked above a dark sea. Series fans should expect the same architectural language: the glowing skylanes, the vertical canyons, the support pillars plunging into the water below.

**The perspective shift is the real difference:** Cloudpunk viewed Nivalis from a flying car at speed; Nivalis Nights puts you on the streets, in the markets, and — via the boat — beneath the pillars in the waterways. The same city, seen from angles Cloudpunk never showed. Our [Hidden Locations Guide](/guides/hidden-locations-guide) details the under-city layer that's already confirmed.

### The HOVA
The flying hovercar is the icon of the franchise, and it carries over. In Cloudpunk, the HOVA was the whole game — skylanes, fuel stops, repair bills, parking discipline, and a full upgrade path (speed, durability, cosmetics). In Nivalis Nights, a **personal HOVA is confirmed as a travel option** — a purchase goal that grants the same vertical freedom. The [Transportation Guide](/guides/transportation-guide) covers what Cloudpunk's HOVA mechanics tell us about the new game's implementation.

### The Weather and the Night
Cloudpunk's defining aesthetic was rain-soaked night flight. Nivalis Nights keeps the atmosphere but makes it systemic: a **realistic weather system** (sunshine, rain, snow, fog, thunderstorms) with gameplay effects on traffic and energy prices, and a **day-night cycle** with the famous 2 AM curfew. See the [Day & Night Cycle & Weather Guide](/guides/day-night-cycle-guide).

### The Lore
The universe's deep history carries over: the CorpSec security presence, the city's corporate power structure, the AI entities that shaped Nivalis, and the ocean's darker depths — community coverage of Nivalis Nights already references "ancient, God-like AIs dreaming dark dreams" beneath the waves, squarely in Cloudpunk's CORA lineage.

## The Key Differences — What's New

### Genre: The Delivery Run vs. The Life
Cloudpunk's loop was simple and cinematic: take a package, fly across the city, have conversations that revealed the story. Nivalis Nights replaces that loop with simulation:

- **Business management** — seven business types from noodle bar to nightclub, with menu design, pricing, staffing, and inventory (see the [Best Business Guide](/guides/best-business-guide)).
- **Economy** — a dynamic, neighborhood-by-neighborhood market with a daily newspaper, arbitrage, and a black-market energy trade (see the [Best Business Guide](/guides/best-business-guide)).
- **Living systems** — apartment customization, fishing, chess, and relationships with 135 voiced citizens, each on their own schedule.

Cloudpunk fans should expect a much slower, denser rhythm. The question "what do I do next?" has dozens of answers at any moment — that's the point.

### Story Structure: One Night vs. Every Day
Cloudpunk told a tight, branching story across a single night — Rania's deliveries revealed a conspiracy, and your choices at each stop shaped the ending.

Nivalis Nights' official premise is different in shape but familiar in mood: the city is "pushed to the brink by CorpSec crackdowns and a mysterious serial killer," and you start humble with a noodle stand before climbing to the city's top restaurants and premier nightclubs. Expect:

- **A personal story** — inheriting the noodle bar from the retiring cook Thaddeus Carmunis is your entry point (see the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide)).
- **Living stories, not cutscenes** — the game's pitch is discovering "the stories of the people you meet... your friends, your customers, and maybe even find love." That's the life-sim story model: narratives you surface by living, not by triggering.
- **Choice everywhere** — "every day is a choice" is the game's own slogan, in explicit contrast to Cloudpunk's scripted night.

### Perspective: Third Person vs. First Person
This is confirmed and important: **Nivalis Nights is first-person.** ION LANDS has stated the game revolves around *you*, not a virtual avatar — you see only "gender-neutral arms and hands" when interacting. Cloudpunk fans used to watching Rania and Camus from behind will be seeing the city through their own eyes instead. For more on what this means for customization, see the [Character Customization Guide](/guides/character-customization-guide).

### Conflict: Flight Hazards vs. Sabotage and the Black Market
Cloudpunk's tension was driving — fuel, damage, AI traffic that doesn't yield. Nivalis Nights translates that tension into economic risk:

- **The black-market energy trade** — CorpSec polices it, and fines escalate (see the [Best Business Guide](/guides/best-business-guide)).
- **Rival sabotage** — under cover of the 2 AM curfew, you can sabotage competing businesses while avoiding security systems (see the [Day & Night Cycle & Weather Guide](/guides/day-night-cycle-guide)).
- **A serial-killer plot** — the official premise introduces a crime story Cloudpunk never had at street level.

## Characters — Who Carries Over

Series fans will want to know: do Cloudpunk's characters appear? As of pre-release, **no returning characters have been officially confirmed for Nivalis Nights.** Here's the honest state of knowledge:

### Cloudpunk's Cast (for reference)
- **Rania** — the delivery driver protagonist of Cloudpunk, who returned as a playable character in the City of Ghosts DLC.
- **Camus** — her canine companion.
- **Control** — the AI dispatcher who was Rania's radio companion through the night.
- **CORA** — the city-scale AI whose fate shaped Cloudpunk's ending.
- **Hayse** — the gambler introduced as a playable character in City of Ghosts.
- **Morpho** — tied to late-game choices in the DLC's branching story.

### What to Expect in Nivalis Nights
- **New cast, new stories** — the confirmed cast is a fresh set: Thaddeus Carmunis, Banor, and the city's 135 voiced citizens. The [NPC Relationships Guide](/guides/npc-relationships-guide) profiles who we know.
- **Cameos are plausible, not confirmed.** ION LANDS demonstrated with City of Ghosts that it weaves continuity between its Nivalis stories. A world this dense would be the perfect place for a familiar voice on the radio or a familiar face at a market stall — but nothing has been announced.
- **The lore will connect even if the characters don't.** The city, CorpSec, the AI history, and the ocean's mysteries are shared DNA. Expect continuity of place more than continuity of cast.

## Should You Play Cloudpunk First?

This is the question series-curious players ask most. The honest answer:

**Not required — but recommended for two reasons:**

1. **Atmosphere and worldbuilding.** Cloudpunk is a 10-12 hour game, and it's the best possible introduction to the city you're about to live in. Every district, landmark, and institution Nivalis Nights references will land harder if you've flown it once. Cloudpunk regularly goes on deep discount; it's the cheapest way to enrich Nivalis Nights.

2. **Genre whiplash protection.** Cloudpunk is fast, cinematic, and complete-in-one-night. Nivalis Nights is slow, systemic, and open-ended. Playing the first game calibrates you for the shared universe — so the second game's different rhythm is a welcome change of pace rather than a disappointment.

**The case for skipping:** if you bounce off delivery gameplay and just want the life sim, you can start fresh at Nivalis Nights with zero lost context. The games share a universe, not a save file or a required reading list. Nivalis Nights' own [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) assumes no prior knowledge.

**Also worth knowing:** the *City of Ghosts* DLC sits between the two games in release history and is the most Nivalis Nights-adjacent Cloudpunk content — it added street racing, HOVA customization, and a longer, branching campaign. It's the best sample of ION LANDS' ambitions for the city.

## Common Questions

### Is Nivalis Nights a sequel to Cloudpunk?
Not a direct sequel — it's a life-sim spin-off in the same universe and the same city. Think of it as the same world, a new genre, and a new story. The City of Ghosts DLC is the actual narrative continuation of Cloudpunk's story.

### Will Rania or Camus appear in Nivalis Nights?
No returning characters have been confirmed as of August 2026. Cameos are plausible given ION LANDS' continuity style, but unconfirmed — treat any "Rania in Nivalis Nights" claim as rumor until the studio confirms it.

### Do I need to play Cloudpunk to understand Nivalis Nights?
No. The games share a universe but have independent stories. Playing Cloudpunk enriches the worldbuilding; skipping it costs nothing narratively.

### Is the HOVA in Nivalis Nights?
Yes — a personal HOVA is confirmed among the game's travel options, described as a purchase goal. Its mechanics will likely echo Cloudpunk's (fuel, repairs, parking, upgrades), per the [Transportation Guide](/guides/transportation-guide).

### Which game is longer?
Cloudpunk's main story is roughly 10-12 hours (about 20+ with the DLC and side content). Nivalis Nights is a life sim with seven business types, 135 NPCs, and ongoing simulation — expect dozens of hours or more, with estimates in the [How Long to Beat Guide](/guides/how-long-to-beat-guide).

### Will Cloudpunk fans like it?
Different experience, same soul. If you loved Cloudpunk for the rain, the neon, and the melancholy beauty of Nivalis — yes, it's the same city, and the [Day & Night Cycle & Weather Guide](/guides/day-night-cycle-guide) promises more of it than ever. If you loved it specifically for the delivery-run pacing, adjust expectations: Nivalis Nights is a marathon, not a night ride.

## Bottom Line

Nivalis Nights and Cloudpunk are siblings, not twins: one city, one studio, one universe — and two very different games. Cloudpunk was the cinematic night flight; Nivalis Nights is the daily life beneath and beyond the skylanes. Series fans get the best of both: the city they fell in love with, rebuilt from street level, with the depth of a full life sim on top. Play Cloudpunk first if you want the full tour — then move in.
