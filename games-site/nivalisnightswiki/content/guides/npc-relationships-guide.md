# NPC Relationships Guide — Key NPCs, Friends, Rivals & Romance in Nivalis Nights

The city of Nivalis is alive — more than 100 voiced citizens (135 by some counts) with their own schedules, routines, and stories walk its streets while you run your business. Relationships aren't a side feature: they shape your business, unlock content, and drive the life-sim half of the game.

This guide covers how relationships work, the key NPCs we know about, and how to pursue friends, rivals, and romance.

## How Relationships Work

Nivalis Nights features a full social system with three axes:

- **Friendship** — daily interactions, shared activities, and gifts deepen bonds.
- **Rivalry** — competing with or antagonizing NPCs creates rivals, including business rivals who open competing venues.
- **Romance** — the game's marketing promises you can "find love" in Nivalis. Romantic relationships unlock new storylines and gameplay options.

Every NPC has:
- **A schedule** — they live their own lives, and you'll find them in different places at different times.
- **A story** — personalities, backstories, and quests that develop based on your choices.
- **A reaction to you** — relationships evolve based on how you treat them.

## The Key NPCs We Know About

Pre-release info is limited — most of the cast's stories will be discovered at launch. Here's who we know:

### Thaddeus Carmunis — The Retiring Cook
The **92-year-old** cook who hands you the noodle bar in Meridian Market. He's your origin story: a lifetime in the kitchen, and he's decided you're the one to carry it on. Expect his story — and the business's legacy — to be a through-line of the early game.

### Banor — Your First Employee
A **64-year-old former restaurant owner**, Banor becomes your first employee and your tutor in the management systems. He's a mentor figure with real expertise — listen to him, and he pays for himself many times over in lessons about staffing and service.

### The Citizens of Meridian Market
Your starting district is full of regulars: customers, neighbors, and small business owners. Early friendships here matter disproportionately — these are the people who funnel customers to your noodle bar in your fragile first week.

## Why Relationships Matter for Business

The social system and the business system are connected:

- **Word of mouth.** NPCs and factions can funnel customers your way. A friend in the right neighborhood sends traffic to your venue.
- **Business rivals.** Competing businesses are dynamic — if you dominate a niche, expect imitators. NPC rivalries can turn into competing venues opening across the street.
- **Staffing.** Employees are NPCs with relationships too. A happy staff performs better; a betrayed one walks.
- **Access.** Some areas, stories, and business opportunities unlock through relationship milestones.

## How to Build Friendships

### Daily Interactions
Talk to NPCs regularly. Their schedules matter — catch them at the right time and place for the best conversations.

### Shared Activities
The game's activities are also social tools:

- **Fishing together / gifting catches** — high-value seafood makes excellent gifts (see the [Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide)).
- **Minigames** — challenging NPCs to minigames builds rapport and can win prizes.
- **Chess** — the chess tournament scene is social as well as competitive; players and spectators are characters worth knowing.

### Gifts
Gift-giving is a core relationship mechanic. The best gifts are tailored to the NPC — their tastes, their story, their needs. Generic gifts are fine early; personalized ones are what move relationships.

### Help Them
NPCs have problems. Helping with theirs is the fastest path to friendship — and often leads to quests, favors, and unlocks.

## How Rivalries Work

Not everyone will like you — and not everyone should. Rivalries are a designed feature:

- **Business rivalry:** Dominate a niche and competitors open nearby. This is the economy's way of keeping you honest.
- **Personal rivalry:** Choices that harm or disrespect NPCs create enemies who can work against you — sabotaging your reputation, poaching staff, or tipping off the Corps.

**Managing rivals:** Rivalries aren't automatically bad — in some neighborhoods, a feared reputation is useful. But choose your enemies deliberately, and never let a rival know your energy-trading routes.

## Romance — Finding Love in Nivalis

Romance is confirmed, with the developers promising you can "find love" — and even "maybe find love" — in the city. What we know:

- **It's part of the relationship system**, not a separate minigame. Friendship deepens into romance through meaningful interactions and shared experiences.
- **Romance unlocks new storylines and gameplay options** — partners appear in your story, affect your days, and can change how you play.
- **Your home matters.** [Apartment customization](/guides/apartment-customization-guide) includes decorating spaces for friends — and romantic partners are the most likely beneficiaries of your design skills.

**Practical advice:** Don't rush romance. Build the friendship first — daily interactions, tailored gifts, shared activities. The romantic path opens through depth, not volume.

## Relationship-Focused Playstyle Tips

If the social side is your main draw:

1. **Live near your people.** Apartment location makes daily visits practical.
2. **Keep fishing.** Fish are the universal gift — valuable, personalizable, and always useful.
3. **Learn schedules.** A relationship grows through consistency, and consistency needs a calendar.
4. **Balance with business.** Relationships need time, but they feed your business too — word of mouth is real income.
5. **Accept imperfection.** Rivalries are part of the story. A city where everyone likes you is a city with no drama.
