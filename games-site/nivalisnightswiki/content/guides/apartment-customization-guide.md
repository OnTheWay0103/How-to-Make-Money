# Apartment Customization Guide — Decorating, Upgrades & Location Choices in Nivalis Nights

Nivalis Nights is a life sim as much as a business sim — and your home is where that life happens. Apartments in Nivalis are buyable, customizable, and more than decoration: where you live and how you furnish it shapes your character's mood, social standing, and how your story unfolds.

This guide covers apartment buying, decorating, upgrades, and how to choose where to live.

## Can You Customize Your Apartment?

Yes. Apartment customization is a confirmed core feature of Nivalis Nights. You can:

- **Buy apartments** in different locations across the city
- **Decorate** with furniture, lighting, and other decor
- **Upgrade** your living space over time
- **Customize homes for friends** — the game lets you decorate spaces for other characters too

## How Customization Affects Gameplay

Decoration isn't just cosmetic:

- **Mood.** Your living environment affects your character's state. A thoughtfully furnished, well-lit home keeps you in better shape for work, relationships, and long days on the boat.
- **Social standing.** Your home is a signal to the citizens of Nivalis. Where you live and how you present it affects how NPCs perceive you — which feeds into the [relationship system](/guides/npc-relationships-guide).
- **Relationships.** You can decorate homes for friends. Well-chosen gifts of furniture and decor for an NPC's space can strengthen bonds in ways simple conversation can't.

## Choosing Where to Live

Location matters in Nivalis Nights. Key considerations when buying:

### Proximity to Your Business
Time is money. Living near your main venue means shorter commutes, easier rush-hour coverage, and more time for everything else. In the early game, the **Meridian Market** area — home of your inherited noodle bar — is the practical choice.

### Neighborhood Economy & Safety
Different districts have different economies — and different levels of CorpSec presence. Some areas are quieter and pricier; some are rougher but cheaper. If you trade on the black market, your home neighborhood's security posture matters in ways you'll feel later.

### Ambition Alignment
Your home should match your goals:

- **Business-focused:** live near your empire's core, minimize travel time.
- **Relationship-focused:** live near the NPCs you're courting or befriending — proximity makes daily interaction easier.
- **Explorer/life-sim-focused:** pick a district that puts you near the canals (fishing), markets, and the chess scene.

## The Decorating Process

While exact furniture lists are pre-release, the confirmed decorating systems are:

- **Furniture:** Beds, chairs, tables, shelving, and full furnishing sets. Some furniture is purely aesthetic; some provides functional benefits (rest quality, mood, hosting).
- **Lighting:** Light choices change your apartment's feel and your mood. Neon-accented cyberpunk lighting is very Nivalis — and very effective.
- **Layout:** Arranging furniture isn't just about looks — usable space and flow affect how the apartment *feels* and functions.
- **Personalization:** Small personal touches — decor items tied to your hobbies (fishing trophies, chess sets, mementos from NPC relationships) — make the space yours and signal your story to visitors.

## Upgrading Your Home

Upgrades operate on two tracks:

1. **Apartment-level upgrades:** Bigger, better, or better-located properties. Each neighborhood tier offers a step up in quality — and price.
2. **Content upgrades:** New furniture, lighting options, and decor unlock as you progress — through Achievement Points, wealth, and relationship milestones.

**Strategy:** Don't overspend on the first apartment. Buy functional and close to your business early; invest in your dream home once passive income (managers, nightclubs) is flowing.

## Decorating for Friends

The friend-decoration system is a nice depth layer: as you build relationships, you may earn the chance to help furnish an NPC's home. This is a high-value relationship investment — decor for a friend's space is remembered.

## Budgeting for Your Home

Your apartment competes with business expansion for every nivalis. Suggested priorities:

| Stage | Home Strategy |
|-------|---------------|
| Day 1 | Take the practical option near the noodle bar. Furnish minimal. |
| Week 1 | Small upgrades: lighting, functional furniture. Priority stays on venue level 3 + Manager. |
| Mid-game | Consider the upgrade: better district, better size. Decorate to your identity. |
| Late game | Dream home. Full furnishing, friend homes, premium decor. |

The golden rule: **a home is an asset, not a trap.** If buying the nicer apartment delays your Manager unlock by three days, it's the wrong purchase.

## Tips Summary

- Location beats square footage early — live near your business.
- Functional furniture (rest, mood, hosting) beats purely aesthetic buys.
- Lighting is the cheapest way to transform a space — and mood.
- Decorate friends' homes to deepen relationships.
- Upgrade your home as your income compounds, not before.
