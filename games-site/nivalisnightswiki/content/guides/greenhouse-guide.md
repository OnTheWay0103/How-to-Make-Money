# Greenhouse Guide — Growing Your Own Ingredients in Nivalis Nights

Ingredients are the leak in every venue's profit: buy them at market and your margins shrink with every dish. **Greenhouses are the fix.** Rent a greenhouse near the docks, master its control console, and your noodle bar, ramen bar, and restaurant can source food at near-zero cost — weather and market swings stop touching your margin. This guide covers how greenhouses work and how to run them profitably.

## Why Grow Your Own Food

| Sourcing Method | Cost | Risk |
|-----------------|------|------|
| **Market ingredients** | Highest — full retail price | Prices shift with supply, demand, and weather |
| **Fishing** | Free, time-based | Limited variety; weather affects catches |
| **Greenhouse crops** | Setup cost, then near-free | Requires time and module management |

The [Business Management Guide](/guides/business-management-guide) calls greenhouse supply the long-term cost killer for a reason: once your beds produce, ingredient price spikes (snow days, shortages) stop hurting you.

## Getting Started

- **Rent a greenhouse near the docks** — the confirmed greenhouse location is dock-side, close to fishing access and the ingredient markets.
- **Use the control console** to manage the three core growing conditions:
  - **Temperature** — too hot or cold stalls growth
  - **Humidity** — dry air slows crops; humidity swings risk damage
  - **Light** — light levels drive growth speed

The console is your daily touchpoint: check conditions each day, adjust for weather (rain and fog change ambient conditions), and harvest.

## Advanced Modules & Seeds

Greenhouses support upgrades that expand what you can grow:

- **Advanced modules** unlock new growing capabilities as you invest
- **New seed varieties** unlock advanced crops — which in turn enable premium menu items that ordinary market sourcing can't supply

Expect the module and seed unlock order to follow your venue progression: basic staples first, premium ingredients as your restaurant's menu demands them.

## Greenhouse + Weather = Timing

Weather is a real factor in greenhouse management:

- **Rain** — ambient humidity changes; you'll spend less on water, but check the console's humidity balance
- **Snow** — heating demand and temperature management matter more
- **Clear days** — most stable growing conditions

The [Weather Guide](/guides/weather-guide) has the full climate breakdown — pair it with a daily greenhouse check and you'll never lose a harvest to a cold snap.

## Profitable Greenhouse Routines

1. **Pick crops your venues use.** Growing ingredients you actually cook with beats growing for resale — double margin on every dish.
2. **Check the console at day start.** One minute of adjustments prevents a stalled harvest.
3. **Prioritize premium seeds.** Advanced crops enable premium menu items — the highest-margin dishes in the game.
4. **Weather-proof your staple crops.** Grow enough buffer stock that a snow week never forces you back to full-price markets.
