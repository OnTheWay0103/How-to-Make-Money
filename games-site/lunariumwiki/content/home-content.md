## What is Lunarium?

Lunarium is a hand-drawn isometric action RPG developed by Lunarium Game Studio, a nine-person debut team based in Shanghai. You play as Ave, a light-armored swordmaster, accompanied by Lune, a mysterious Starfarer sorceress who acts as an AI companion.

The game features parry-based combat, deep skill trees for both characters, an Embroidery Charm crafting system, three interconnected regions, and a branching story with three distinct endings. It launches July 29, 2026 on Steam for $17.99.

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Parry Combat** — Time blocks just before enemy attacks for perfect parries that stagger foes and build Resonance
- **Resonance Points** — Shared resource between Ave and Lune; spend on coordinated attacks, healing, and ultimates
- **Ave's Skill Tree** — Three branches: Sword Arts (offense), Parry Arts (defense), Resonance Arts (hybrid)
- **Lune's Skill Tree** — Three branches: Starfire (damage), Moonlight (healing), Constellation (crowd control)
- **Embroidery Charms** — Craft and equip passive bonuses; up to 5 charm slots
- **Cloak System** — Mobility and utility bonuses from different cloak types
- **Three Regions** — Forest, Dilapidated Castle, and Archipelago, each with unique enemies and bosses
- **Three Endings** — Two standard endings plus a hidden True Ending
