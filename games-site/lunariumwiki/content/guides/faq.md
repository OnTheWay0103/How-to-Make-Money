# Lunarium FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: July 25, 2026**

---

## General Questions

### What is Lunarium?
Lunarium is a hand-drawn isometric action RPG developed by Lunarium Game Studio, a nine-person debut team based in Shanghai, and published by Imperfect Games. You play as Ave, a light-armored swordmaster, accompanied by Lune, a mysterious Starfarer sorceress who acts as an AI companion.

### When does Lunarium release?
Lunarium launches on **July 29, 2026** on Steam for PC.

### How much does Lunarium cost?
**$17.99 USD / €17.99 / £15.99** on Steam.

### What platforms is Lunarium on?
PC via Steam at launch. Console versions (PlayStation, Xbox, Switch) have not been announced but may follow based on sales performance.

### What languages are supported?
English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese (interface and subtitles).

## Gameplay Questions

### Is Lunarium a soulslike?
Not exactly. While it shares some DNA — parry-primary combat, bench save points, limited healing — the developers have emphasized that the difficulty is designed to **teach rather than punish**. The tone is intimate and relationship-focused rather than hostile.

### How long is Lunarium?
A first playthrough takes approximately **12-18 hours** depending on exploration and difficulty. Completionist runs (all items, both endings) take **25-30 hours**.

### Can I play as Lune?
You control Ave directly in combat. Lune operates as an AI-controlled companion who supports in combat and exploration. You CAN upgrade Lune's skills, direct her with D-pad commands, and unlock her ultimate abilities.

### Is there multiplayer?
No. Lunarium is a single-player experience focused on the relationship between Ave and Lune.

### How does saving work?
You save at **bench Waypoints** (similar to bonfires in Dark Souls). Resting at a bench restores HP, replenishes healing items, and respawns regular enemies.

### Can I respec my skills?
Yes. Respeccing is available at any Temple save point. Your first respec is free; subsequent respecs cost Stardust.

### Is there a New Game Plus?
Yes. After completing any ending, NG+ unlocks with carried-over charms, cloaks, and Stardust. Character levels and story progress reset. Harder enemy variants appear.

## Story Questions

### Who is Lune?
Lune is a "Starfarer" — a celestial being from beyond the stars drawn to the dying world of Lunarium. Her true nature and origins are revealed on Starfall Island in the Archipelago.

### How many endings are there?
Three: Ending A (Star's Return), Ending B (Together), and the hidden True Ending (Shared Light). See the [Endings Guide](/guides/endings-guide) for details.

### Are there romance options?
The relationship between Ave and Lune is central to the story, but the game focuses on their **bond and partnership** rather than explicit romance mechanics. The True Ending represents the deepest version of their connection.
