# Lunarium FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Answers are based on official sources, press coverage, and community walkthroughs of the released game. Where we don't know, we say so.

---

## General Questions

### What is Lunarium?
Lunarium is a hand-drawn isometric action RPG developed by Lunarium Game Studio (a small debut team based in Shanghai) and published by Imperfect Games. You play as **Ave**, a light-armored swordmaster, alongside **Lune**, a Starfarer sorceress who acts as an AI companion. The pair investigates a corrupting darkness called **Nihilus** across four regions. Combat is parry-focused (often compared to Sekiro).

### When did Lunarium release?
**July 29, 2026** on Steam (PC).

### How much does Lunarium cost?
**$17.99 USD** at launch, with a **10% launch discount** (~$16.19) available during the opening weeks. Regional pricing applies.

### What platforms is Lunarium on?
PC via Steam at launch (some sources also list the Epic Games Store). Console versions have not been announced.

### What languages are supported?
Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese are confirmed on the Steam page, alongside English. Japanese was reported by launch coverage — check the Steam page for the current full list.

### Is there a demo?
A demo/preview build existed during the March 2026 preview period. **Current demo availability is unconfirmed** — check the Steam page.

## Gameplay Questions

### Is Lunarium a soulslike?
It's a "Souls-lite": parry-primary combat, bench save points, and demanding bosses, but with a gentler tone and an **Easy Mode** added at launch based on player feedback. It's not an open-world game — four connected regions, linear story.

### How long is Lunarium?
Press reports put a first playthrough at roughly **12 hours**. Full completion (both endings + optional content) is estimated around **20 hours** — that figure is a community estimate, unconfirmed.

### Can I play as Lune?
No. You control **Ave** directly. Lune is an AI companion you can direct with D-pad commands and upgrade through her skill tree.

### Is there multiplayer or co-op?
**No.** Lunarium is single-player only — no co-op, no PvP, no multiplayer of any kind. Lune is the AI companion. See [Is Lunarium Co-op?](/guides/coop-guide) for details.

### How does saving work?
You save by **resting at benches** (Souls-like save points). Resting restores HP and respawns regular enemies.

### Can I respec my skills?
**Unconfirmed.** No reliable source documents a respec feature. If you find one, we'll update this answer.

### Is there a New Game Plus?
**Unconfirmed.** No official announcement of NG+ exists. Claims that NG+ unlocks after the ending are unverified.

### How many endings are there?
**Two distinct endings** — confirmed by official press materials. The specific requirements for each are **not yet documented**; see the [Endings Guide](/guides/endings-guide). There is no confirmed "true" or secret third ending.

### What is the Resonance system?
A **shared gauge** between Ave and Lune built through combat. Reviews confirm Lune uses it to build enemy stagger meters. **Astral Shards** (found in Cyanstone) raise the resonance cap. Exact costs and rates are unpublished.

### Does Lunarium have daily quests or weekly content?
**No.** It's a single-player game with no daily/weekly/live-service systems. See the [Progression Guide](/guides/daily-weekly-guide).

## Technical Questions

### What are the system requirements?
Official minimum and recommended requirements are published on the Steam store page. Per the Steam store page, specs can change with updates — **check there for the current official minimum and recommended requirements.**

### Does Lunarium support Steam Deck?
Not officially confirmed. Community testing is ongoing.

### Is there controller support?
Yes — full controller support is confirmed; reviews generally recommend controller over keyboard/mouse (the short dodge and camera behavior feel better on gamepad).

## Story Questions

### Who is Lune?
A "Starfarer" — an ancient guardian being wielding white/sky magic, drawn to the dying world. Her nature and history unfold across the story, and her relationships (with Ave, with the corrupted Lilia in Cyanstone, with Ryuu and Doro) are the emotional core of the game.

### Does my playthrough have choices that matter?
The game's press materials confirm the story "converges toward two distinct endings," implying meaningful narrative decisions — but **no verified source documents which choices matter**. Play naturally, and keep a save before the final boss.

### Are there romance options?
The story focuses on the **bond and partnership** between Ave and Lune rather than explicit romance mechanics (per press coverage). There are no documented romance systems.
