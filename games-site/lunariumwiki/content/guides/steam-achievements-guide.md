# How to 100% Lunarium — All 35 Steam Achievements Guide

Lunarium has **35 Steam achievements**. The capstone is **Stellar Lector**, awarded for earning every other achievement. The list is a mix of story bosses, optional fights, collectible sets, stat maximums, and a handful of hidden or vaguely described trophies.

This guide organizes all 35 by category, gives the public unlock description where one exists, and marks the ones Steam does not fully explain. It also lays out a practical 100% route so you are not replaying regions because you missed a bench or a souvenir.

**Accuracy note:** achievement names and descriptions below come from the public Steam list as compiled by GameCast.it and cross-checked against community achievement pages. Where Steam only provides flavor text or no description at all, the requirement is marked **[Unconfirmed]**. Do not treat a guessed trigger as fact.

## How the Achievement List Is Structured

The 35 achievements fall into four practical groups:

| Group | What it tracks | Why it matters for 100% |
|-------|----------------|-------------------------|
| **Story and boss trophies** | Defeating named bosses and reaching story beats | Mostly automatic if you finish the campaign |
| **Optional and hidden fights** | Side bosses and scripted encounters | Easy to miss if you rush the critical path |
| **Collection and system completion** | Capes, embroidery, souvenirs, benches, skills, HP, Resonance, Junonia | The real 100% grind; requires thorough exploration |
| **Capstone** | Stellar Lector | Unlocks only after all 34 others |

The important consequence: you cannot clean up the collection achievements after the credits if you have not been tracking them during the story. Lunarium lets you resume from the last bench after the ending, but it does not give you a chapter-select screen. Missing a bench in an early region means walking back, not reloading a chapter.

### What Each Collection Actually Requires

**Capes (Fashionista).** Capes are equipment with cosmetic and ability effects. You find them through progression and exploration, not a single shop. Check every region's side paths and chests before moving on.

**Embroidery (A Brilliant Mind).** Embroidery is the charm-like passive system. The first confirmed blueprint is in a chest in Cyanstone, in the room to the left of the first bench. Treat that as the pattern: blueprints hide in optional rooms, not on the main road.

**Souvenirs (Know It by Heart).** Souvenirs are placeable keepsakes. The achievement requires placing all of them, not merely owning them. If your count is stuck, check your hub display and place anything sitting in inventory.

**Benches (A Seat to Rest).** Benches are save points. Activating all of them is a traversal completion task; it rewards you for exploring each region's optional routes. This is the achievement most likely to send you back to an early region.

**Skills (Born with It).** Skill trees develop through combat and exploration, powered by threads of hope. There is no level system, so "unlock all skills" is a completion goal rather than a max-level grind. Play thoroughly and spend threads as they come.

**HP and Resonance (Full of Spirit!, Evergreen Bond).** These are maximum-stat achievements. Ave's HP and Lune's Resonance cap grow through progression and collectibles such as Astral Shards. Exact caps are **[Unconfirmed]**; use the in-game stat screen as the source of truth.

**Junonia (Weapon Master).** Junonia is a weapon you forge to its highest tier. The number of tiers is not stated in the achievement description. Keep forging as materials allow and check the forge menu for the current cap.

## A Practical 100% Route

The efficient route is not "finish the story, then clean up." It is "clean each region before you leave it."

1. **Firleaves:** Activate every bench you pass, take the optional paths, and beat the Rearguard Warrior when you reach Cyanstone's outskirts. Confirm Lune's bracelet achievement early so you know the story trophies are registering.
2. **Cyanstone:** This is the collection region. Find the first embroidery blueprint near the first bench, collect the Astral Shards, defeat Lilia for the Blade of Rutila, and fight the optional Rearguard Warrior if you have not already.
3. **White Reef Isles:** Sweep for capes and souvenirs before the point of no return. The Shipyard key and Messenger's Key gate optional areas; use them rather than rushing the main path.
4. **Paradise:** Clear the Dreampath content, free Doro, and finish the story. Before the final boss, confirm your collection counters. After the credits, resume from the last bench and finish anything left.
5. **Ending cleanup:** If you are missing **Story's End** or **Shared Finale**, replay toward the other ending. The exact triggers are **[Unconfirmed]**, so keep a save before the final boss if the game allows it.

### Bench and Souvenir Discipline

The two easiest achievements to fail are **A Seat to Rest** and **Know It by Heart**, because both depend on actions you can walk past. Rest at every bench you see, even if you do not need the heal. Place every souvenir as soon as you get it. Neither action costs a resource, and both remove a later backtrack.

## Boss Tips That Also Protect Achievements

Most boss achievements are unavoidable, but a few optional fights are easy to skip. The Rearguard Warrior is the confirmed optional boss; defeating it is both an achievement and a good test of the parry timing you need for the finale.

Three habits keep boss attempts productive:

- **Parry white telegraphs, dodge red flashes.** Red-flash attacks cannot be parried. This rule applies to every named boss achievement.
- **Shoot Nihilus orbs and cores with Lune.** Enemies with orbs take reduced damage until the orb is destroyed; core-blocked paths need Lune's arrow. A boss that seems unkillable is usually an orb problem.
- **Use the Blade of Rutila for interrupts.** It is the marquee unlock from Lilia and is required for at least one late-fight interrupt. If you skipped Lilia, go back.

## Missables and Point of No Return

The public achievement list does not classify any achievement as missable, and no official point of no return is documented. Community coverage also says there is not enough verified data to confirm missability.

The practical stance: **assume collection achievements can become inconvenient, not impossible.** Lunarium lets you resume after the ending, and the four regions are connected. You may have to walk back, but the game does not appear to lock you out of regions. The two ending achievements are the exception in practice — if the game autosaves past the final choice, you may need a second run, and the exact trigger is **[Unconfirmed]**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many achievements does Lunarium have?**
35, including Stellar Lector.

**What unlocks Stellar Lector?**
Earning all other 34 achievements.

**Are there multiplayer achievements?**
No. Lunarium is single-player only; there is no co-op or PvP.

**Do I need to collect everything in one run?**
No, but you should collect per region before leaving. Post-credits cleanup means walking back, not chapter select.

**Does the Animus Bracelet do anything?**
