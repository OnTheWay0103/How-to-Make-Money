# Lunarium Ability Unlock Guide — Skills, Tools & How to Get Them

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

"Unlocking" in Lunarium is not a menu of skill points — it's a mix of **progression-gated skills**, **collectible resources**, and **story-earned tools**. This guide maps every unlock system that is confirmed, in the order you'll meet it, and answers the questions players actually search for — including the one we can't answer yet.

---

## 1. The Big Picture (Confirmed)

| Unlock type | How it works |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Skill trees** (Ave + Lune) | Develop **through combat and exploration** (Steam page) |
| **Threads of hope** | Collected throughout the journey; used to **boost strength and learn new skills/magic** (Steam page) |
| **Story tools** | Key abilities earned from bosses/story (Blade of Rutila, Dreampath butterfly) |
| **Stat-less upgrades** | Astral Shards raise the resonance cap; embroidery blueprints expand crafting |
| **Hub crafting** | Sanctuary (bench, Fungal Mortar, Loom) + upgrades at the Temple |

There are **no levels, no XP bars, no stat points** — progression is driven by play, collection, and story.

## 3. Threads of Hope — the Skill Currency

The Steam page states: collect **"threads of hope"** throughout the journey to **boost strength and learn new skills and magic**.

**What we know:**
- It's the game's core skill-upgrade resource.
- Sources: combat and exploration (the trees "develop through combat and exploration").
- It powers **both** Ave's sword techniques and Lune's suppressive magic.

**What we don't know:** exact amounts, per-skill costs, and whether threads are consumed or earned-per-act. No reliable source has documented the economy. Treat any guide quoting "500 threads per skill" as unverified.

**Practical advice:** because specifics are unpublished, don't hoard threads — spend them as the trees allow, and prioritize parry-related skills (see the [Skill Tree Guide](/guides/skill-tree-guide)).

## 4b. How Unlocks Chain Together (The Dependency Map)

Unlocks in Lunarium are not independent — each region's tools are needed to solve the next region's obstacles. Understanding the chain tells you what to prioritize:

| Stage | Tool you gain | What it enables later |
|-------|---------------|-----------------------|
| Firleaves | **Lune's light arrows** | Destroying Nihilus orbs everywhere — including the Longhorn Troll rematch |
| Firleaves | **Sanctuary stations + Wayfarer's Cloak** | The crafting loop and your first cloak effect |
| Cyanstone | **Astral Shards** (resonance cap) | Bigger Resonance gauge for the boss gauntlet ahead |
| Cyanstone | **Blade of Rutila** | Interrupting red-flash attacks — required for Enid's sword-slam in the finale |
| White Reef Isles | **Shipyard key → Messenger's Key** | Gate unlocks that lead to the Dreamsinger Priestess and the third lantern |
| Paradise | **Doro's Stellar Core + butterfly** | Permanent Dreampath safety — makes post-game exploration painless |

**The golden rule:** if a path is blocked, you are missing a tool from an earlier step — a key, a core to shoot, or a boss you haven't beaten. Lunarium's unlocks are strictly sequential in this way.

## 5. How to Unlock Skills — Step by Step

1. **Play.** Skill trees develop through combat and exploration — there is no skill shop or trainer NPC documented.
2. **Collect threads of hope** — chests, enemy drops, and story moments (sources per Steam page; exact locations unpublished).
3. **Return to the hub** — craft and spend at the Sanctuary; upgrade at the Temple.
4. **Beat the bosses** — several "skills" are boss-earned: Lune's arrow (story), the Blade of Rutila (Lilia), the Dreampath butterfly (Doro).
5. **Explore thoroughly** — Astral Shards and embroidery blueprints are the hidden unlockables; the first confirmed blueprint is in Cyanstone.

### Common "unlock" Questions

**"Why can't I open this door?"**
Almost every locked door in Lunarium is key-item gated (Souls of the Created, Shipyard key, Messenger's Key, four bells). Check your key items, then look for the boss or puzzle that drops the key.

**"I beat the boss but nothing unlocked?"**
A few unlocks are story-gated rather than boss-gated — resting at the Sanctuary or a bench triggers them (e.g., the meeting that sends you to the White Reef Isles after Cyanstone). If a path still won't open, rest, then revisit.

**"Why does my damage feel flat in Cyanstone?"**
Two likely causes: (1) you are hitting enemies with Nihilus cores without shooting the cores, and (2) you haven't collected enough Astral Shards to raise the resonance cap. Both are unlock-progression issues, not a build problem.

**"Do I need to find every blueprint to finish the game?"**
No — blueprints (and most collectibles) make the game easier, but the story only requires the boss-earned unlocks (Blade of Rutila, Stellar Cores, keys). 100% collection is optional and can be done post-credits.

**"Why does this enemy take no damage?"**
It has a **Nihilus orb or core** — Lune's arrow destroys it. This is the most common "I can't progress" moment.

**"When do I get the cool interrupt ability?"**
The **Blade of Rutila**, from beating Lilia in Cyanstone. It's the game's marquee unlock.
