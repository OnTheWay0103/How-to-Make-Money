# Is Lunarium Co-op? — Multiplayer Explained

**Short answer: No. Lunarium has no co-op and no multiplayer of any kind.** It is a strictly single-player game, and the companion who fights beside you — **Lune** — is an AI character, not a second human player.

This page exists because "lunarium co op" and "lunarium multiplayer" are common searches. If you came here hoping to play with a friend, here is everything you need to know.

---

## 1. The Facts (Confirmed)

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does Lunarium have online co-op? | **No.** No multiplayer mode exists |
| Does Lunarium have local co-op / split-screen? | **No** |
| Can a friend play Lune? | **No.** Lune is always AI-controlled |
| Is there PvP? | **No.** No arena, no duels, no ranked play |
| Is the game online-only? | No — it's a fully offline-capable single-player ARPG |

This is confirmed by official sources: Lunarium is described consistently as a single-player experience, and press coverage describes Lune as an AI companion.

---

## 2. Why People Think It's Co-op

Lunarium looks duo-friendly, and that's by design:

1. **Two protagonists** — Ave and Lune fight together constantly. It *feels* like a partner game.
2. **D-pad commands** — you can direct Lune's attention in combat (press-confirmed), which feels like commanding a second player.
3. **Shared resources** — the Resonance gauge is shared between the two characters.
4. **Reviews describe the "chemistry"** — reviewers consistently praise the Ave/Lune dynamic, which reads like a buddy-game pitch.

None of this is multiplayer. It's a well-designed AI companion system.

## 4. Frequently Searched Co-op Questions

### "Can I play Lunarium with friends?"
No. There is no way to play with other humans. You can share the experience by watching, or play side-by-side with one person on keyboard/mouse and taking turns — but the game itself is single-player.

### "Will co-op be added later?"
There is **no announcement** of co-op or multiplayer features from the developer (Lunarium Game Studio) or publisher (Imperfect Games). Assume single-player-only unless an official update says otherwise.

### "Is there remote play together support?"
Steam Remote Play Together works with *some* single-player games, but it requires the game to support multiple local players — Lunarium has no local co-op, so **Remote Play Together will not work** for it.

### "Is Lunarium like 'It Takes Two' or other co-op games?"
Thematically it has a duo, but mechanically it is a solo Souls-like ARPG. The closest comparison is games where an AI companion joins your single-player journey.
