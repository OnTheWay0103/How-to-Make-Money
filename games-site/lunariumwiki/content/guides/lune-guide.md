# Lunarium Lune Guide — Starfarer Abilities & Synergy

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: July 25, 2026**

Lune is the mysterious Starfarer who accompanies Ave throughout Lunarium. While AI-controlled, understanding her abilities and how to maximize her potential is key to mastering the game.

---

## 1. Who Is Lune?

Lune is a sorceress from beyond the stars — a "Starfarer" drawn to the dying world of Lunarium. She wields celestial magic and supports Ave in both combat and exploration.

| Trait | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Role** | AI Companion / Support Caster |
| **Combat Style** | Ranged magic, debuffs, healing, RP generation |
| **Element** | Celestial / Star magic |
| **Control** | AI-controlled; D-pad commands for direction |

---

## 2. Lune's Abilities

### Active Spells
Lune automatically casts these based on the combat situation:

| Spell | Effect | Trigger Condition |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| **Star Bolt** | Single-target magic damage | Always available — basic attack |
| **Starfall** | AoE damage in target area | 3+ enemies grouped together |
| **Celestial Bind** | Root a single enemy for 3s | Elite/Boss enemy detected |
| **Lunar Veil** | Shield Ave for 15% max HP | Ave below 50% HP |
| **Nova Burst** | Massive AoE around Lune | Surrounded by 4+ enemies |

### Passive Abilities (Unlocked via Skill Tree)

| Passive | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Starlight's Grace** | +10% healing effectiveness |
| **Celestial Attunement** | Spell cooldowns reduced by 15% |
| **Guiding Star** | Reveal hidden items on minimap |
| **Astral Link** | +20% RP generation when near Ave |
| **Cosmic Ward** | Lune takes 30% less AoE damage |

---

## 3. Lune Skill Tree

### Offensive Path (Starfire)
Focus on Lune's damage output:
- **Star Bolt II** → +25% Star Bolt damage
- **Starfall II** → Starfall radius +30%
- **Nova Burst II** → Nova Burst applies burn DoT
- **Cosmic Rain** (Ultimate) → Channel a rain of stars for 5s (costs 100 RP)

### Support Path (Moonlight)
Focus on healing and protection:
- **Lunar Veil II** → Shield strength +50%
- **Healing Light** → New ability: targeted heal for 25% HP
- **Purify** → Remove all debuffs from Ave
- **Moon's Embrace** (Ultimate) → Full party heal + invulnerability for 3s (costs 100 RP)

### Utility Path (Constellation)
Focus on crowd control and debuffs:
- **Celestial Bind II** → Root duration +2s
- **Gravity Well** → Pull enemies toward a point
- **Starlight Shroud** → Blind enemies in area (50% miss chance)
- **Event Horizon** (Ultimate) → Black hole pulls all enemies + slow (costs 100 RP)

## 4. D-Pad Commands

You can direct Lune with the D-pad during combat:

| Command | Input | Effect |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **Attack Target** | D-Pad Up | Lune focuses spells on locked target |
| **Defend Position** | D-Pad Down | Lune stays close and prioritizes shielding |
| **Spread Out** | D-Pad Left | Lune maintains distance for ranged casting |
| **Go All Out** | D-Pad Right | Lune uses highest-damage spells, ignores defense |

---

## 5. Best Synergy Combos

### Combo 1: Parry into Nova
1. Ave parries an enemy → generates RP + stagger
2. Press D-Pad Down → Lune moves close
3. Activate Resonance Strike (50 RP)
4. Ave and Lune execute a coordinated finisher

### Combo 2: Gravity into Starfall
1. Lune casts Gravity Well (pulls enemies together)
2. Lune immediately follows with Starfall (AoE on grouped enemies)
3. Ave cleans up survivors with melee

### Combo 3: Celestial Bind Boss Strategy
1. Lune roots the boss with Celestial Bind
2. Ave uses heavy attacks on the immobilized boss
3. Lune follows with Star Bolt spam during the root window
4. Repeat when Celestial Bind comes off cooldown
