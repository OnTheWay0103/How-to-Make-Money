# Lunarium Lune Guide — Starfarer Abilities & Support Tactics

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Lune is the mysterious Starfarer who accompanies Ave through all four regions of Lunarium. She is an **AI-controlled companion** — you never directly control her — but she is far from passive: her abilities are woven into the game's core combat, exploration, and puzzle design. Understanding her is the difference between struggling and cruising through boss fights.

---

## 1. Who Is Lune?

| Trait | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Role** | AI companion / magic support |
| **Nature** | A "Starfarer" — an ancient guardian being wielding white/sky magic, drawn to the dying world (per press materials) |
| **Combat style** | Ranged light magic, arrows, support casting |
| **Control** | AI-controlled; D-pad commands direct her attention (per press coverage) |

Story-wise, Lune is also the emotional core of the game: her friend **Lilia** (Cyanstone), her relationships with other Starfarers (**Ryuu**, **Roan**, **Doro**), and her bond with Ave drive the narrative — and the game's **two endings** are a major payoff for that relationship.

---

## 2. Lune's Confirmed Abilities

| Ability | What It Does | Where It Matters |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Light arrows** | Destroy **Nihilus orbs**; stagger enemies | The Longhorn Troll rematch; every orb-protected enemy; the Sea Monster's eye core; Lilia's thorn trap |
| **Attack interruption** | Can stop specific boss attacks | Shadow of Firleaves' Phase 2 shockwave; Fintan's wide-area attack and charge |
| **Stagger pressure** | Keeps boss stagger bars from draining; builds stagger via Resonance (per reviews) | The Red Dancer fight is explicitly designed around it |
| **Combat support** | Fights alongside Ave with magic attacks | Every fight |
| **Healing** | Heals Ave in combat (confirmed by reviews) | Survival, especially early game |
| **Puzzle/environmental support** | Interacts with environmental magic (gates of light, bells, lanterns) | Cyanstone paths, White Reef Isles lanterns |

> **How to get her best use in practice:** keep her alive and in line of sight of the boss. Her arrow works when she can fire — don't kite enemies away from her line of fire in fights that need interrupts.

## 3. Lune's Skill Tree

The Steam page confirms Lune has her own **skill tree of suppressive magic**, unlocked as the pair progresses (skill growth comes from combat and exploration, powered up by collecting **threads of hope**).

What is known:
- Upgrades **her magic** — damage and utility.
- The tree exists alongside Ave's sword-technique tree.
- Specific skill names, tiers, and effects are **unconfirmed** — no reliable source has published the full tree.

### Priority Advice (Sensible, Marked as Unverified)
| Situation | Recommended Focus |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Struggling to survive | Healing / support upgrades first |
| Bosses stalling | Stagger/arrow effectiveness upgrades |
| Wants faster clears | Offensive magic upgrades |

Treat any guide that lists exact Lune skill names with effects as **unverified** for now.

---

## 4. The D-Pad Commands

Press coverage confirms you can **direct Lune with D-pad commands** in combat. The exact command set is unconfirmed, but the documented intent is directing her focus (attack this target, hold position, etc.).

**Practical use:**
- **Boss fights:** keep her pointed at the boss so her arrow is ready for core destruction and interrupts.
- **Exploration:** point her at orb-blocked paths so she clears them.
- **Puzzle areas:** let her work the environmental magic while you handle the platforming/fighting.

## 5. Lune Strategies per Fight (Confirmed Patterns)

| Fight | Lune's Role |
|-------|-------------|
| **Longhorn Troll (rematch)** | Her arrows destroy the Nihilus orbs and stagger the risen troll — the win condition of the scripted fight |
| **Shadow of Firleaves** | Interrupt the Phase 2 area shockwave |
| **Red Dancer** | Hold the stagger bar when Ave can't land hits |
| **Fintan** | Shoot the Phase 2 back-core after the smash; stop the charge |
| **Sea Monster** | Shoot the eye-core when it appears — the cue for the damage window |
| **Lilia** | Shoot the Nihilus core of the thorn trap to free Ave before the explosion |
| **Shadow in the Dream / Enid** | Backup interrupt support while Ave handles parry and the Blade of Rutila |

---

## 6. Lune Myths to Ignore

- ❌ "You can play as Lune" — she is AI-controlled; you play Ave. (You can upgrade her, direct her, but not control her.)
- ❌ "Lune is optional" — she is mandatory and central to combat, exploration, and story.
- ❌ "Co-op lets a friend play Lune" — Lunarium is single-player; no co-op exists. Lune is always the AI.
- ❌ "Lune has a class build (DPS/support/etc.)" — one skill tree, no classes.
