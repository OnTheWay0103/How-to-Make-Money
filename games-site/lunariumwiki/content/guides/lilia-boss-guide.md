# How to Beat Lilia in Lunarium — Full Boss Strategy

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

**Lilia** is the climax boss of Cyanstone, Lunarium's second region — and the game's first real skill check. She is a Starfarer and Lune's dear friend, but by the time you reach her she has been **completely consumed by Nihilus**. She is the **first boss in the game with two health bars**, and her moveset is specifically designed to punish everything the earlier bosses taught you to do lazily.

This guide breaks down every confirmed part of the fight. All information is sourced from community walkthroughs of the released game.

---

## 1. Fight Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Location** | Cyanstone (after collecting the Souls of the Created) |
| **Health bars** | **Two** — the first boss with two bars in the game |
| **Signature mechanic** | Feint opener + deflect counter-combos |
| **Unparriable attacks** | Yes — multiple red-flash attacks |
| **Rewards** | **Lilia's Stellar Core** + the permanent **Blade of Rutila** skill |
| **Difficulty** | Hard — the region's (and so far the game's) hardest fight |

### What you need before the fight
- **Full heal/rest at the last bench** — you want to arrive fresh.
- **Lune alive and available** — her arrow is required for the thorn trap in Phase 2.
- **Comfortable parry timing** — you will parry a lot.
- **The optional Rearguard Warrior** is worth doing first as a warm-up for Nihilus-core fights, if you haven't already.

### What this fight is really testing
The Lilia fight is a checklist of everything Cyanstone taught you:

| Skill | How Lilia tests it |
|-------|--------------------|
| Reading feints | Her delayed opener punishes reaction parries |
| Parry discipline | The deflect follow-ups are always a parry-able two-hit combo |
| Red-flash awareness | Four different unparriable attacks, sometimes chained |
| Lune management | Only her arrow escapes the Phase 2 thorn trap |
| Stamina/pacing | Two full health bars demand a sustainable rhythm — you cannot burst her down |

If you are stuck, go back through this table one row at a time. The fight is a test, not a gear check — there are no levels to grind and no better sword to find in Cyanstone at this point.

## 2. Her Move Set (Confirmed)

### The Feint Opener
Lilia often **opens with a delayed attack** — the **first swing is a feint**, and the **real attack is the second swing**.

**Counter:** don't parry the first swing on sight. Wait for the second swing, then parry it. Players who react to the first swing get hit by the second, every time.

### Red-Flashing Unparriable Attacks
Like every boss from the Longhorn Troll onward, Lilia has red-flash attacks that **cannot be parried** — dodge them:

| Red-flash attack | Dodge advice |
|------------------|--------------|
| **Thrust** | Dodge sideways (not back) |
| **Nihilus ground AoE** | Keep moving; the ground effect lingers |
| **Projectile spam** | Run to the sides — do not try to parry through it |
| **Spear slam** | Dodge on the slam, then counter |

### Blocking & Deflecting
When you attack her, she **blocks** — and occasionally **deflects**:

- After a deflection, she **always follows with a two-hit combo** — either **two thrusts** or a **double swing**.
- **Parry both hits.** This is the fight's core rhythm: attack → she deflects → parry, parry → punish.

**Tip:** if you're not sure which follow-up is coming, stay defensive until you've seen the pair — both combos are parry-able, so a calm read beats a panic parry.

## 3. Phase 1 (First Health Bar)

**Goal:** survive, learn the rhythm, burn the bar.

- **Play patient.** Bait the feint opener, parry the second swing, and take your punish window.
- **Dodge every red flash** — the thrust and the AoE are the run-enders.
- **Let Lune work** — her arrows stagger and keep pressure while you focus on reads.
- **Spend Resonance when the bar is nearly full** — a full gauge during a parry-heavy fight is wasted potential.

**Phase 1 rhythm:** *wait for opener → parry the real hit → 2-3 attacks → expect deflect → parry the pair → repeat.*

---

## 4. Phase 2 (Second Health Bar)

When the first bar depletes, the fight changes:

- **She becomes more aggressive** — attacks come faster and combos run longer.
- **The Nihilus thorn trap:** she can **trap you in Nihilus thorns** while preparing an **explosion**.
  - **Do not panic-roll** — you can't escape the thorns by movement alone.
  - **Shoot the Nihilus core with Lune's arrow** — destroying the core destroys the thorns and frees you.
  - Keep Lune's line of fire clear during Phase 2; if she can't see you, she can't save you.
- Expect the red-flash attacks to come **in denser sequences** — the projectile spam phase can overlap with ground AoEs.

**Phase 2 rhythm:** *aggressive reads → parry what you can → dodge what you can't → arrow the thorn trap → punish.*

---

## 5. The Finisher: Blade of Rutila

After you deplete both health bars, the fight is **not over**:

- **Use the Blade of Rutila to finish her off.**
- This is a scripted finisher — after her defeat, you use the blade to deliver the final blow.

### What you get
- **Lilia's Stellar Core** (key item).
- The **Blade of Rutila becomes a permanent skill**: it **interrupts enemies during red-flashing unparriable attacks**, dealing **heavy stagger damage**.

**Why this matters for the rest of the game:** the Blade of Rutila is the only tool that works against red-flash attacks. From this fight onward, the game expects you to use it — the final boss **Enid** is designed around it (parry her thrusts, use the Rutila when she smashes her sword into the ground).

## 7. Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Parrying the feint's first swing | Wait for the second swing — it's the real attack |
| Attacking into her block | Expect the deflect → parry the two-hit follow-up |
| Parrying red flashes | Red = dodge. Always |
| Standing in Nihilus ground AoEs | Keep moving; the AoE lingers |
| Panicking in the thorn trap | Arrow the core — movement alone won't free you |

## 7b. If You're Stuck — A Troubleshooting Walkthrough

Work through these in order:

1. **"I keep getting hit by her opener."** — You are parrying the feint (the first swing). Wait for the *second* swing; that's the real attack. Count to one after she winds up.
2. **"She deflects me and I eat a combo."** — When she blocks your attack, immediately go into parry stance and hold until you see the first hit of the follow-up. Parry it, then immediately parry the second. The pair is always: two thrusts OR a double swing.
3. **"The red flashes are everywhere."** — Isolate them: the thrust is dodge-sideways, the ground AoE is dodge-away, the projectile spam is run-to-the-side, the spear slam is dodge-on-slam. Practice one at a time by staying defensive and only punishing after each red attack resolves.
4. **"Phase 2 thorns kill me."** — Do not try to roll out of the thorns. The instant you see them, check Lune's position — if she's not in line of sight, move so she can see you — and let her arrow hit the core. The arrow, not your dodge, is the escape.
5. **"I run out of healing before her second bar."** — You're taking chip damage from red flashes you should dodge. Slow down: in Phase 1, the only damage you should ever take is a single punish window you mistimed. Rework the rhythm before you blame resources.

## 8. After the Fight

- **Lune's arc:** this fight is the emotional peak of the Cyanstone story — Lune has to confront her corrupted friend. The story moves toward "seeing her dear friend's face for one last time" (official hub description).
- **Progression:** return to the **Sanctuary and rest** — this triggers the mysterious meeting that sends you to the White Reef Isles (see the [White Reef Isles walkthrough](/guides/white-reef-isles-walkthrough)).
- **Post-game:** if you miss anything in Cyanstone (Astral Shards, the optional Rearguard Warrior, the embroidery blueprint chest), you can return after the credits via resume-from-bench.
