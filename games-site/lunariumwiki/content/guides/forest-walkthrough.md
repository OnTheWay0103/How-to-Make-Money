# Lunarium Walkthrough — Forest Region

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: July 25, 2026**

The Forest is Lunarium's opening region — a lush, hand-drawn woodland that serves as the tutorial zone and first major exploration area.

---

## 1. Forest Entrance

### Walkthrough
1. **Starting Point:** You begin in a small clearing. Move forward along the dirt path.
2. **First Enemies:** Three Parasites (tutorial enemies). Use basic attacks — parry is taught next.
3. **Starfall Clearing:** Meet Lune in the cutscene. She joins your party permanently.
4. **Training Stone:** Interactive stone that teaches parry mechanics. Complete all 3 tutorials for bonus Stardust.

### Collectibles
| Item | Location |
|------|----------|
| Traveler's Wrap (Cloak) | Starting area, behind the large tree |
| Stardust x100 | Training Stone — complete all tutorials |
| Common Charm: Vitality +1 | Defeat all 3 tutorial Parasites |

---

## 2. Temple Grounds

### Walkthrough
1. Follow the cobblestone path uphill to the Temple
2. Activate the **Temple Waypoint** (first save point)
3. Speak with the **Temple Keeper** (unlocks Skill Tree and Equipment Upgrade systems)
4. Explore the Temple exterior before entering

### Temple Services Unlocked
| NPC | Service |
|-----|---------|
| Temple Keeper | Skill Tree, Respec |
| Forge Master | Weapon/Armor upgrades |
| Loom Keeper | Charm crafting |
| Star Merchant | Buy/Sell items |

### Hidden Areas
| Area | Location | Reward |
|------|----------|--------|
| Behind the Waterfall | Left of Temple entrance | Rare Charm: Wind Dancer |
| Rooftop Garden | Climb vines on Temple's right side | Stardust x300 |
| Crypt Entrance | Behind the Temple, hidden by bushes | Leads to optional mini-dungeon |

---

## 3. Forest Depths

### Walkthrough
1. Exit the Temple through the eastern gate
2. Follow the stream downhill (check both banks for loot)
3. Defeat the Wandering Wisps (first ranged enemies)
4. Cross the fallen log bridge
5. Reach the Ancient Grove (boss arena)

### Enemy Guide
| Enemy | HP | Attacks | Strategy |
|-------|-----|---------|----------|
| Forest Parasite | 50 | Bite, Lunge | Parry the lunge — very telegraphed |
| Wandering Wisp | 80 | Star Shot (ranged), Fade | Dodge projectiles, attack when visible |
| Thorn Hound | 150 | Charge, Bite Combo | Parry the charge, punish after |
| Ancient Treant | 400 | Root Slam, Vine Whip | Elite — use Resonance Strike |

### All Collectible Locations
| # | Item | Location |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | Stardust x200 | Stream bank, near broken cart |
| 2 | Common Charm: Iron Skin | Cave behind second waterfall |
| 3 | Rare Weapon: Verdant Blade | Defeat Ancient Treant (elite) |
| 4 | Stardust x500 | Hidden shrine behind Thorn Hound pack |
| 5 | Embroidery Thread x2 | Abandoned campsite (south of bridge) |
| 6 | Featherweave Cloak | Behind waterfall near Ancient Grove |

## 4. Ancient Grove (Boss: Warden of the Grove)

### Preparation
- **Recommended Level:** 8-10
- **Recommended Build:** Resonance Knight (safe) or Parry Specialist (fast)
- **Bring:** 3+ Healing items, full Resonance gauge

### Boss Strategy
See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) for the full Warden of the Grove strategy.

### Post-Boss
- Collect the **Grovekeeper's Charm**
- Activate the Ancient Grove Waypoint
- The path to the Dilapidated Castle opens
