# Lunarium Walkthrough — Firleaves (First Region)

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Firleaves is the opening region of Lunarium — a mountainous, forested homeland where Ave's journey begins and where she first meets Lune. It serves as the tutorial zone: parry timing, dodge basics, and Lune's support abilities are all introduced here. The region also contains the game's main hub, Starfall Village, which you will return to throughout the adventure.

---

## 1. How the Game Begins

Lunarium opens with **Ave** setting out from the Firleaves mountains. You are alone at first — a light-armored swordmaster with a sword, a dodge roll, and a parry button. The opening minutes teach the basics:

1. **Move and attack** against the first regular enemies (basic sword swings).
2. **Meet Lune** — a Starfarer who joins Ave in the Firleaves mountains. From this point she fights beside you as an AI companion.
3. **Learn the parry** — Lunarium's signature mechanic. Press block just before an enemy attack lands to negate damage and stagger the enemy.
4. **Learn the dodge** — used for the red-flashing attacks that cannot be parried.

> **Note on difficulty:** Lunarium launched with an Easy Mode that was added at release based on player feedback. The game's own marketing describes the difficulty as designed to "teach rather than punish," but the parry focus is still demanding — expect to spend time learning attack patterns in the first hour.

## 2. Firleaves Enemies

The Firleaves mountains are populated by corrupted wildlife and Nihilus-spawned creatures. Community walkthroughs describe:

| Enemy | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Regular corrupted creatures | Basic melee enemies — parry their telegraphed swings |
| **Vine Fungi** | A plant consumed by Nihilus. Occasional swings, a red-flash smash (unparriable — dodge), and it summons small plants that shoot spikes. Destroy the summoned plants quickly. This enemy type recurs as a mini-boss encounter in Firleaves. |
| Nihilus-spawned creatures | Enemies made of the dark corruption — some carry **Nihilus cores/orbs** (see Mechanics below) |

### Key Mechanic: Nihilus Orbs & Lune's Arrow
Certain enemies and environment hazards in Firleaves (and every later region) are protected by floating **Nihilus orbs** — the dark energy that corrupts the world. **Lune's light arrows** destroy these orbs. An enemy protected by a Nihilus orb takes heavily reduced damage until the orb is shot. Lune fires her arrow automatically when she can, but you can also direct her attention with D-pad commands (confirmed by press coverage of the companion controls).

**Practical rule:** whenever an enemy seems to take almost no damage, look for the orb and get Lune to shoot it.

## 3. First Boss: Longhorn Troll

The first major boss of Lunarium (the "Troll" at the end of the Firleaves opening).

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Location** | Firleaves mountains (opening area) |
| **Phases** | 1, plus a scripted second fight |
| **Parry-able attacks** | Yes — its basic swipes and smashes |
| **Difficulty** | Low (introduction boss) |

### Attack Pattern (Phase 1)
- **Two swipes** into a **smash attack** — the bread-and-butter combo. Parry the swipes; the smash is also parry-able in this first phase.
- **Below 40% HP:** the Troll starts **glowing red** before some attacks. These red-flashing attacks **cannot be parried — dodge them instead**.

### Scripted Second Fight
After the Troll is defeated, it is **consumed by darkness** and rises again in a scripted rematch. This is where Lune's support shines:

- **Lune's light arrows destroy the Nihilus orbs** that appear during this fight.
- Her arrows also **stagger enemies** — including the Troll — opening windows for Ave to attack safely.
- Defeat the risen Troll to complete the opening sequence and proceed deeper into Firleaves.

## 4. Second Boss: Shadow of Firleaves

The region's second major boss, encountered deeper in the Firleaves mountains. Community walkthroughs describe:

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Location** | Firleaves depths |
| **Phases** | 2 (becomes aggressive below 40% HP) |
| **Rewards** | **Echoes of Yore** |

### Phase 1
- **Glows red and pukes Nihilus** on the ground — the puddle is dangerous, stay out of it.
- **Summons ground spikes** — watch the ground telegraphs.
- Can **surround itself with spikes** when pressured — don't get cornered.

### Phase 2 (below ~40% HP)
- Becomes significantly more aggressive.
- **An eye appears on its forehead** — this is your visual cue for the phase change.
- Charges an **area-wide shockwave** — either dodge it, or **interrupt it with Lune's arrow**.

### Strategy Summary
1. Play patient — the Nihilus puddles and spike summons punish aggression.
2. Parry its standard swings when possible; dodge anything with a red flash.
3. Save Lune's arrow for the Phase 2 shockwave interrupt.

### Rewards
- **Echoes of Yore** (key item — community walkthroughs confirm this drop)

## 5. Starfall Village & the Sanctuary (Hub)

After the Firleaves bosses, Ave and Lune reach **Starfall Village** — the region's settlement and the game's main hub. Here you:

- Receive the **Wayfarer's Cloak** (a wearable cloak — the game's cloak system gives each cloak both a unique appearance and ability effects).
- Set up **Lune's Sanctuary** — the crafting hub where Lune works. It contains:
  - A **crafting bench**
  - The **Fungal Mortar**
  - The **Loom** (used for the embroidery system — see below)
- Rest at **benches** — Lunarium's save points, similar to bonfires in Dark Souls. Resting restores HP, and (per community reports) resting at the Sanctuary at key story moments triggers story events.

### The Hub Loop
The game is structured as four connected regions, but you will repeatedly return to the Sanctuary to craft, upgrade, and rest. Resources gathered during the adventure are used here — including **upgrading equipment at the Temple** (a sanctuary location).

### Embroidery (Charm-Like Passives)
- **Embroidery works like charms** — each piece of embroidery carries a different passive power that enhances a specific combat style.
- Swapping a single embroidery can dramatically change how Ave plays (a slow, tanky build can become a glass-cannon burst build).
- Confirmed: an **embroidery blueprint** is found in a **chest near the first resting bench in Cyanstone** (in the room to the left of the bench) — so blueprints/patterns are a collectible you'll hunt for.

> Exact embroidery names, effects, and slot counts are **unconfirmed** — treat community lists with caution until verified.
