# Lunarium Walkthrough — White Reef Isles (Third Region)

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

The White Reef Isles is Lunarium's third region — a strange island chain where Ave wakes up under mysterious circumstances after the events of Cyanstone. The region is a self-contained mystery: light **three lanterns**, uncover the secrets of the island's people (the **Dreamsingers**), and earn passage to **Paradise** from the Starfarer **Ryuu**.

Everything in this guide is sourced from community walkthroughs of the released game (July 2026). Details not confirmed are marked as such.

---

## 1. How You Arrive

After the Lilia fight in Cyanstone, return to the Sanctuary and rest — a mysterious meeting follows, and **Ave wakes up on the White Reef Isles**. You start with no clear objective. Shortly after exploring, you meet **Ryuu**, a Starfarer living on the island.

**Ryuu's offer:** help the local people by experiencing their stories and lighting **three lamps (lanterns)**. If you succeed, Ryuu will take you to Paradise.

**The deal structure:**
- **Three lanterns** are the region's main quest markers.
- Each lantern platform has a **riddle** that hints at how to light it — though progressing the story also lights them naturally.
- The region's many sub-quests (the sea beast, the lighthouse, the Dreamsinger laboratory, the priestess) all feed into the lanterns.

---

## 2. Region Mechanics: Gates of Light

The White Reef Isles introduces **gates of light** — environmental devices that **adjust the timeline**:

- Interacting with a gate **raises the water level drastically**, unlocking new paths while closing others.
- Expect to revisit areas as the water level changes — some collectibles are only reachable at certain water levels.

**Practical tips:**
- After passing through a gate, explore the newly opened routes before moving on.
- One path leads to a **bench** where a **ghost of a young man** appears — his quest involves a gigantic **sea beast** (see the Shipyard section). Take the conversation; it frames the region's big set piece.

---

## 3. The Lighthouse & Pale Tear Village

### Lighthouse
- Reach the **lighthouse** and light it — the dreamsingers that previously blocked the path **disappear once the lighthouse is lit**, opening the route onward.
- Lighting the lighthouse is also your first concrete step toward the lantern objectives.

### Pale Tear Village
- Reach **Pale Tear Village**.
- **Lower the drawbridge** to proceed toward the Shipyard.

### Shipyard
- Continue to the **Shipyard** where a ship is ready to sail.
- Note the **locked gate** in the Shipyard — it needs a key you won't have until after the sea voyage.

---

## 4. Boss: The Sea Monster (Ship Fight)

As the ship sails, a **huge Sea Monster** attacks. This is a scripted, mechanically unusual fight:

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Scripted ship fight |
| **Key rule** | **You cannot attack the monster directly at first** |
| **Difficulty** | Medium — pattern-based |
| **Drops** | A **key** that unlocks the locked gate in the Shipyard |

### How the fight works
1. **Avoid its attacks** — learn its tells and dodge.
2. When the monster **smashes a fin on the deck, strike the fin** — that's your damage window.
3. **Dodge the projectiles** it shoots toward the ship.
4. **Survive long enough** and one of its **eyes turns into a Nihilus Core** — at that moment the monster **rests its fin on the deck**: attack now for big damage.
5. Defeat it to claim the **key** for the Shipyard's locked gate.

**Tip:** this fight is a pure survival/pattern check — your build barely matters. Learn the fin tell and stay patient.

---

## 5. Harbor Edge & the Dreamsingers' Secret Laboratory

Return to land with the key: unlock the Shipyard gate and proceed to **Harbor Edge** — revealed to be the **Dreamsingers' headquarters**.

### Finding the Hidden Base
- Find the **cliffside pathway** leading to the Dreamsingers' hidden base.
- Inside, a **bell surrounded by ghostly Dreamsingers** — **ringing the bell drives the ghosts away**, clearing the way.

### The Four Bells (Main Puzzle)
To unlock the main gate on the north path, **four bells must be rung** in the laboratory:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Follow the ground **markings** — they lead to **levers** that light up paths |
| 2 | **Pull the first lever** to light the area; **slip through a crack in the glass** |
| 3 | Head southeast; find the **second lever** in a room with a resting bench |
| 4 | Return to the laboratory entrance and pass through the gate where the ground lines end |
| 5 | **Ring a bell** to clear the Dreamsinger ghosts |
| 6 | Head southeast through the now-unlocked gate |

### The Prison Lift Puzzle
In the depths, you find a **prison with a lift puzzle**:

- **Pull levers** to make one lift go down while the other goes up — you adjust them while crossing.
- The enemies here are challenging; **running past them is advised** (you can come back).
- A **third switch** sits at the end of a left path.
- A lift beyond the gate to its right **descends into a boss arena** — that's the Volcano Crab.

---

## 6. Boss: Volcano Crab

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Mandatory boss |
| **Difficulty** | Low-medium — slow, easy-to-avoid attacks |
| **Phase change** | After the **first health bar**, it divides the arena into **three parts** |

### Strategy
- **Phase 1:** its attacks are slow — read and dodge them; it **jumps twice** to attack with its pincers.
- **Phase 2:** the arena splits into three sections — move with the sections and don't get stranded on the wrong side.
- After victory, **cross the bridge** to reach the **fourth bell**.

### Post-Boss
- Ring the **fourth bell**; a lift leads back to the main gate in the hub.
- **Ringing the bell at the main gate lights the second lantern.**

---

## 7. The Dreamsinger Priestess

### Reaching Her
1. Leave the laboratory, return to **Harbor Edge**, and head **northwest to a ship**.
2. In the ship's **deepest chamber**, find the **Dreamsinger priestess**.
3. **Take the liquid from her and drink it** — this opens a door into the **priestess' dream**.

### Boss: Dreamsinger Priestess
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Mandatory boss (dream sequence) |
| **Key rule** | She fights alongside an **aggressive puppet** |
| **Drops** | **Messenger's Key** |

### Strategy
- **Focus the priestess first** — she has **low HP** and goes down quickly with a few hits.
- **Do NOT kill the puppet first** — if the puppet is defeated before the priestess, it **resurrects after she falls**.
- The puppet has **huge HP** but **easy-to-avoid attacks** — ignore it while you burn the priestess.

### After the Fight
- Find the **Messenger's Key**.
- Head north and **ring the final bell** — this lights the **third lantern**.

---

## 8. The Witch of the Abyss (Ryuu Boss Fight)

With all three lanterns lit, return to the **White Reef Isle starting area** and cross the **lantern bridge** to reach Ryuu's arena.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Mandatory boss — Starfarer duel |
| **Phase change** | Below **40% HP** |
| **Difficulty** | Medium — fast projectiles |

### Strategy
- **Phase 1:** Ryuu **primarily uses projectile attacks** — keep moving, close distance between volleys.
- **Phase 2 (below 40% HP):** the **arena changes** and her projectiles intensify. They're **fast but don't deal heavy individual damage** — the danger is being overwhelmed. Keep dodging and punish the gaps.
- After defeat, Ryuu keeps her promise: she **takes Ave and Lune to Paradise** — the final region.

> **Before you go:** the Messenger's Key also lets you return to **Pale Tear Village** and enter the priestess' abode to access a **dream path** (nothing can be done there yet at that point — but it's worth knowing it exists). Paradise awaits — see the [Paradise walkthrough](/guides/archipelago-walkthrough).
