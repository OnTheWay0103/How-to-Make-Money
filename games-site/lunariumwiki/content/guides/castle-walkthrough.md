# Lunarium Walkthrough — Cyanstone (Second Region)

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Cyanstone is the second region of Lunarium — home of **Lilia**, a Starfarer and a dear friend of Lune. By the time Ave and Lune arrive, Cyanstone is already heavily corrupted by **Nihilus**. The region is built around a single objective: gather the **four Souls of the Created** to open the locked gate to the palace, and confront what lies beyond.

This is also the region where Lunarium's difficulty noticeably steps up. Expect: enemies with Nihilus cores on their backs, unparriable red-flash attacks, multi-phase boss fights, and the game's first **two-health-bar boss**.

---

## 1. Arriving in Cyanstone

You travel to Cyanstone after completing Firleaves and setting up the Sanctuary. Community walkthroughs report the following:

### Region-Wide Mechanics
- **Astral Shards:** scattered through the region, they **increase your resonance cap** — the maximum size of the Resonance gauge shared by Ave and Lune. If you find one, grab it; resonance capacity is the closest thing Lunarium has to a mana-bar upgrade.
- **Nihilus Cores block paths:** many paths in Cyanstone are blocked by Nihilus corruption that must be **shot with Lune's arrow** to clear. If a path seems dead, look for a core to shoot.
- **Enemies with Nihilus Cores on their backs** take reduced damage until you shoot the core. Lune fires automatically when she can — keep her alive and she handles most of this.

### First Confirmed Collectible
Near the **first resting bench** in Cyanstone, in the room to the **left of the bench**, there is a **chest containing an embroidery blueprint**. Embroidery is the charm-like passive system — blueprints like this one expand what you can equip. This is one of the few explicitly documented collectibles in the game, so grab it early.

## 2. Cyanstone Outskirts

### Optional Boss: Rearguard Warrior
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Location** | South of the village |
| **Type** | Optional colossus infected by Nihilus |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |

- During some of its attacks, a **Nihilus core appears on the boss** — destroy it to interrupt the attack.
- It's optional, but it's a good warm-up for the region's mandatory colossus fights and likely drops useful materials (drops unconfirmed).

### Gate Boss: Sentinel Colossus — Tower Shield
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Location** | Main gate of Cyanstone proper |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |
| **Drops** | **Soul of the Created** (fragment of Lilia's Lumina) |

This is the region's first mandatory boss. Key behaviors:

- **Blocking:** if the colossus is blocking and you attack, your attack is deflected and it responds with a **counter shield bash** followed by a **delayed dash**. The bash and dash are parry-able — parry often.
- **Knockdown follow-up:** when the boss is staggered/knocked down, it performs **one final attack** after getting back up. Be careful about jumping on it — community walkthroughs warn that players get hit here.
- **Red glow:** a red glow means a **ground smash AoE** — dodge, don't parry.

**Strategy:** this fight rewards parry-heavy play. Keep the stagger pressure on, and treat every red glow as a dodge cue.

## 3. The Souls of the Created

The **Palace Entrance has a locked gate requiring four Souls of the Created**. You find them from bosses scattered around Cyanstone. Confirmed sources of Souls:

| # | Boss | Type | Notes |
|---|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Sentinel Colossus — Tower Shield | Mandatory gate boss | Drops Soul of the Created |
| 2 | **Red Dancer** | Mandatory | See below |
| 3 | **Fintan** | Mandatory | Colossus knight on horseback |
| 4 | **Hauler Colossus — Heavy** | Mandatory | Near-invulnerable — stagger fight |

### Red Dancer
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Difficulty** | Medium |
| **Drops** | Soul of the Created |

- **Very evasive** — but easy to parry/dodge once you have her rhythm.
- **Use Lune's arrow** to keep her stagger bar from draining when you can't land hits yourself.
- **At 40% HP:** she disappears, **the lights go out**, and she summons **four lesser colossus dancers**. Stagger and defeat each of them; then the fight resumes (and ends shortly after).

### Fintan
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Difficulty** | Medium-Hard |
| **Drops** | Soul of the Created (the third) |

- A **colossus knight on horseback** wielding a halberd. The horse also **tackles and kicks** — the horse can hit you in ways the knight's halberd can't.
- **Red glow:** throws its halberd into the ground, then **jumps and smashes** — dodge the jump.
- **Phase 2:** a **Nihilus Core appears on its back**. After the smash, the core is exposed — **shoot it with an arrow** to interrupt a wide-area attack.
- It may also **charge recklessly** — stop the charge with an arrow.

### Hauler Colossus — Heavy
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Difficulty** | Medium (unusual rules) |
| **Drops** | Soul of the Created |

- **Takes almost no damage** — don't grind your weapon against it. The fight is about building its **stagger bar**; staggering it **twice is enough to win**.
- **Red glow:** throws itself on the ground — small AoE, dodge away.
- **Phase 2:** the red glow causes **rocks to drop from above** — keep moving.

**Tip:** fights like this are why the game pushes you to learn the stagger system early. Parries, Lune's arrow, and heavy attacks all build stagger.

## 4. The Mysterious Visitor (Roan)

After unlocking the gate with the first three Souls of the Created, you meet the **Mysterious Visitor — Roan**:

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Difficulty** | Easier than the Sentinel fights |
| **Weapon** | A cane |

- **Three-hit combo** into a thrust.
- **Red-flash thrust** — dodge it.
- Some **projectiles**.

Roan is a breather fight before the region's centerpiece. Roan returns in the fourth region (Paradise) with a much harder two-phase rematch — see the [Paradise walkthrough](/guides/archipelago-walkthrough).

## 5. Lilia — the Region's Climax

**Lilia** is a Starfarer and Lune's dear friend — and by the time you reach her, she has been **completely consumed by Nihilus**. This is the hardest fight in the game so far and the **first boss with two health bars**.

See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) for the full breakdown. The essentials:

- **Feint opener:** she often opens with a **delayed attack** — the first swing is a feint, the second is the real attack.
- **Red-flashing unparriable attacks:** thrust, Nihilus ground AoE, projectile spam, and a spear slam. Dodge all of them; keep moving during the projectile phase.
- **Deflects:** when she blocks/deflects your attacks, she **always follows with a two-hit combo** — parry both hits.
- **Phase 2 (second health bar):** more aggressive. She can **trap you in Nihilus thorns** while preparing an explosion — **shoot the Nihilus core with an arrow** to destroy the thorns and escape.
- **The finisher:** after defeating her, use the **Blade of Rutila** to finish her off.

### Rewards
- **Lilia's Stellar Core** (key item)
- **Blade of Rutila** becomes a **permanent skill**: it lets you interrupt enemies during their red-flashing unparriable attacks, dealing heavy stagger damage. This is a game-changing tool — you will use it for the rest of the game, including the final boss.

---

## 6. After Lilia — Into White Reef Isles

After the Lilia fight, return to the Sanctuary and **rest** — resting triggers a mysterious meeting, and Ave wakes up in a foreign area. This is the transition into the third region, the **White Reef Isles**.
