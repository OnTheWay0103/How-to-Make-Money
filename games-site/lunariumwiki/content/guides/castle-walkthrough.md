# Lunarium Walkthrough — Dilapidated Castle

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: July 25, 2026**

The Dilapidated Castle is Lunarium's second major region — a vertical, multi-level fortress filled with armored enemies, traps, and two major boss encounters.

---

## 1. Castle Approach

### Walkthrough
1. Cross the bridge from the Forest (cutscene plays)
2. Defeat the gate guards (2 Armored Knights)
3. Enter through the main gate

### Enemies Introduced
| Enemy | HP | Key Trait | Strategy |
|-------|-----|-----------|----------|
| Armored Knight | 250 | Shield — blocks frontal attacks | Heavy attack to break guard, then combo |
| Crossbow Guard | 120 | Ranged, applies Bleed | Dodge bolts, close distance quickly |
| Patrol Hound | 180 | Fast, calls reinforcements | Kill before it howls |

---

## 2. Castle Courtyard

### Collectibles
| # | Item | Location |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | Stardust x400 | Behind the dried-up fountain |
| 2 | Rare Armor: Ironheart Plate | Defeat Courtyard Knight (elite) |
| 3 | Embroidery Thread x3 | Broken cart near stables |

### Hidden Area: Tower Spiral
From the courtyard, find the cracked wall (marked with faint blue glow):
1. Heavy attack the wall to break it
2. Climb the spiral staircase
3. At the top: **Assassin's Wrap Cloak** + Stardust x600
4. Use the zipline to return to the courtyard

---

## 3. Main Gate (Boss: Armored Golem)

### Preparation
- **Recommended Level:** 15-18
- **Key Tip:** The Golem has NO parry-able attacks — bring mobility charms
- **Recommended Build:** Blade Dancer (dodge-focused)

### Boss Strategy
The Armored Golem is a dodge-check fight. See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) for the complete strategy.

### Post-Boss
- Collect **Golem Core Charm**
- The main gate opens, revealing the castle interior
- Activate the Castle Interior Waypoint

---

## 4. Great Hall

### Walkthrough
The Great Hall is a multi-level room with balconies:

1. Clear the ground floor (4 Armored Knights)
2. Use the stairs to reach the upper balcony
3. Pull the lever to open the throne room door
4. Optional: explore the side rooms for loot

### Collectibles
| # | Item | Location |
|---|------|----------|
| 4 | Stardust x800 | Great Hall chandelier (shoot it down with ranged) |
| 5 | Rare Charm: Counterweight | Throne room antechamber (hidden alcove) |
| 6 | Embroidery Thread x5 | Kitchen storage room (side path) |

### Side Path: Dungeon (Optional)
Below the Great Hall stairs, there's a dungeon entrance:
- Contains 3 cells with loot
- Elite enemy: Tortured Knight (drops Epic crafting material)
- Exit leads back to the Great Hall

---

## 5. Chapel (Boss: Corrupted Priestess)

### Preparation
- **Recommended Level:** 18-22
- **Key Tip:** Save Resonance for Phase 3
- **Bring:** Healing items, Lune's Healing Light unlocked

### Walkthrough
1. Enter the Chapel through the throne room
2. Watch the cutscene (Ave and Lune discover the Priestess's corruption)
3. Boss fight triggers after dialogue

### Boss Strategy
See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) for the full Corrupted Priestess strategy.

### Post-Boss
- Collect **Priestess Tear Charm** and **Celestial Edge** (Legendary Sword)
- The Chapel altar reveals a portal to the Archipelago
- Lune gains a new dialogue option about the Priestess's fate (affects ending)
