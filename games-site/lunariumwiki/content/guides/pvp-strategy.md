# Lunarium PvP Strategy Guide — Arena Combat & Player Duels

**Lunarium 1.0 / Updated: July 27, 2026**

Beyond its story campaign, Lunarium features a dedicated PvP arena where players can test their builds against one another. This guide covers everything you need to climb the ranked ladder — from arena modes and class builds to advanced combat techniques and counter-strategies.

---

## 1. Arena Modes

| Mode | Players | Format | Win Condition |
|------|---------|--------|---------------|
| **Duel** | 1v1 | Best of 3 | First to 2 kills |
| **Tag Team** | 2v2 (Ave + Lune pairs) | Single elimination | Eliminate both opponents |
| **Free For All** | 4 players | Timed (5 minutes) | Most kills wins |
| **Ranked Duel** | 1v1 | Best of 5 | First to 3 kills (seasonal leaderboard) |

### Mode-Specific Rules

| Mode | Special Rules |
|------|---------------|
| **Duel** | No healing items allowed. Each round starts with full HP and Resonance. |
| **Tag Team** | Lune assists her paired Ave. If the Ave falls, Lune fights alone. |
| **Free For All** | Health pickups spawn periodically. Third-party kills are common. |
| **Ranked Duel** | Gear is normalized to equal stat caps. Only skill and build matter. |

## 2. Best PvP Builds

### Build 1: Parry Specialist (Ave)
**Best for:** Duel and Ranked Duel

| Slot | Item / Skill | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Swift Blade (fastest attack speed) | Quick parry recovery, punish windows |
| **Armor** | Light Leather Set | Maximum dodge roll distance |
| **Cloak** | Shadow Stalker Cloak | Invisibility on backstep |
| **Charm 1** | Parry Window Brooch | +30% parry frame duration |
| **Charm 2** | Counterstrike Gem | 50% bonus damage on parry follow-up |
| **Key Skill** | Riposte Mastery (max rank) | Parry stuns opponent for 2 seconds |

**Playstyle:** Wait for your opponent to attack. Parry their strike, then punish with the Riposte stun window. Swift Blade lets you land 3-4 hits during the stun. Do not initiate — force them to come to you.

### Build 2: Resonance Knight (Ave)
**Best for:** Tag Team and Free For All

| Slot | Item / Skill | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Resonance Blade | Builds Resonance faster on hit |
| **Armor** | Medium Plate Set | Balanced defense and mobility |
| **Cloak** | Energy Weaver Cloak | Lune's Resonance generation increased by 25% |
| **Charm 1** | Resonance Amplifier | +2 Resonance per hit |
| **Charm 2** | Lune's Favor | Lune auto-heals 30% faster |
| **Key Skill** | Resonance Cascade (max rank) | AoE burst when Resonance is full |

**Playstyle:** Build Resonance through sustained combat. Once Resonance is full, activate Cascade for massive area damage. In Tag Team, Lune's enhanced support keeps you alive longer.

### Build 3: Starfarer Assault (Lune — Tag Team Support)
**Best for:** Supporting a damage-focused Ave partner

| Slot | Ability | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| **Primary** | Astral Bolt (charged) | Long-range poke, interrupts enemy combos |
| **Secondary** | Healing Nova | Burst heal for both Ave and Lune |
| **Ultimate** | Starfall | Large AoE slow + damage, controls arena space |
| **Passive** | Resonance Link | Share 20% of generated Resonance with Ave |

**Playstyle:** Stay at range. Use charged Astral Bolts to disrupt enemy Ave approaches. Save Healing Nova for when your Ave is at 40% HP or below. Starfall controls the arena — drop it on the enemy's escape path.

## 3. Advanced Combat Techniques

### Parry Punish Strings

| Parry Result | Guaranteed Follow-Up | Damage |
|-------------|---------------------|--------|
| Successful parry (any weapon) | Light attack (x2) | 200-300 |
| Successful parry (Swift Blade) | Light (x3) + Heavy | 450-550 |
| Counterstrike Gem parry | Heavy (charged) | 500-700 |
| Riposte Mastery parry | Full combo (light x4 + finisher) | 800-1,000 |

### Resonance Management

| Situation | Action | Result |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Opponent has full Resonance | Back off and bait the Cascade | Waste their ultimate |
| You have full Resonance | Combo into Cascade confirm | Cannot be dodged mid-combo |
| Both players low HP (under 30%) | Save Resonance for defense | Use Cascade as a counter, not an opener |
| Tag Team: your Lune has ultimate | Pull enemy toward Lune | Starfall + Cascade wombo combo |

### Counter-Strategies

| Enemy Build | Counter | Why It Works |
|-------------|---------|--------------|
| Parry Specialist | Heavy weapon (Greatsword) | Heavy attacks cannot be parried. Trade hits — you win the trade. |
| Resonance Knight | Shadow Stalker Cloak | Reset combat on your terms. Never let them build a full combo. |
| Kite Mage (Lune-focused) | Speed Boost charm + gap closer | Close distance immediately. Mages lose in melee range. |
| Tank Armor build | Bleed charms + sustained damage | Tanks are weak to DoT effects. Hit-and-run until bleed stacks kill them. |

## 4. Ranked Play Tips

### Leaderboard Seasons

| Season Detail | Information |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Season Length** | 8 weeks |
| **Rank Tiers** | Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Lunarius |
| **Placement Matches** | 10 matches to determine initial rank |
| **Points per Win** | +15 to +25 (varies by opponent rank) |
| **Points per Loss** | -5 to -15 |
| **Season Rewards** | Exclusive weapon skin, title, Luminary Cloak |

### Climbing Strategy

1. **Master one build** before trying others. Switching builds every match prevents you from learning matchups.
2. **Learn the parry timing** for every weapon type. Each weapon has a unique attack rhythm.
3. **Grind Free For All** for warm-up — it teaches you to handle multiple opponents and third-party situations.
4. **Review replays** after losses. Identify the moment the fight turned against you.
