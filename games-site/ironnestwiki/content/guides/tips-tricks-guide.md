# Advanced Tips & Tricks — The Operator's Playbook

> Launch Version / Updated: August 11, 2026

The beginner guide teaches the loop; this playbook teaches the operator habits that separate smooth gold-medal runs from twenty-minute firefights. Everything here is drawn from community launch-week play — where a figure is community-measured rather than official, it is marked.

## The Twin Guns Are a Second Operator

The Iron Nest's two 800 mm barrels operate fully independently — the single biggest force multiplier in the game. The proven twin-gun rhythm:

| Barrel | Duty | Loading |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Left** | Target A — e.g. the bunker | AP, loaded while right gun is in flight |
| **Right** | Target B — e.g. the infantry screen | HE, loaded while left gun is in flight |

While one shell is in flight — which can take a long time at extreme range — the other barrel is loading. Missions where both barrels are idle are missions you are losing. Pre-load different shell types so a dictated change of targets never forces a partial mission with the wrong ordnance in the barrel — **you cannot unload**, so plan loads in pairs.

## Rotation Before Elevation, Always

Traversing 360° takes real time; setting elevation takes seconds. Sequence every shot: start rotation when the load completes, fine-tune elevation while the turret is still turning. Return the heavy rotation lever **smoothly** — sudden movements are the #1 cause of hydraulic damage and jams.

## The Recoil Buffer Flush — Cheapest Insurance in the Game

Recoil buffer fluid drains with every shot. Below 80% the calibration warning light flickers, dials drift and shots you solved start missing. **Flush the lines between salvos** — it is the cheapest, fastest maintenance action in the game and it prevents a whole class of frustrating near-misses. Treat it as part of the reload, not optional maintenance. (Safe ranges and failure states: [systems & failures](/guides/systems-failure-guide).)

## Pace the Barrel; Watch the Heat

Every shot heats the barrel; past **450 °C** the loaded round cooks off — instant game over. Twin guns double the heat problem when both fire. The pacing rule: two fast shots are fine; ten fast shots are a cook-off waiting. In multi-target salvos, alternate barrels so no single barrel eats a sustained burst.

## Counter-Battery Discipline

- **Mark every enemy battery before your first shot.** The counter-battery timer starts with your first round on enemy guns — unplotted batteries become a death sentence mid-fight. ([Full counter-battery guide](/guides/counter-battery-survival-guide))
- **Do not spend FDC pauses immediately.** Destroying a Fire Direction Center pauses counter-battery fire — it is a resource. Save it for the moment you need to work undisturbed: a long reload, a relocation, a final barrage.
- **Move after major engagements.** A dozen rounds from one position hands the enemy a firing solution. Emergency moves exist for this; verify your map token after every relocation.

## The Deafening Window (Community-Reported)

Firing deafens you for roughly **10 seconds** — during which radio corrections and teleprinter updates can be missed if you are watching the dials. The community practice:

- Keep an eye on the **comms transcription ticker** rather than audio alone after firing.
- The **acoustic dampening upgrade** (from the requisition tree) reportedly cuts the deafened window to ~4 seconds — one of the stronger early upgrades once you can afford it.

## Clipboard & Notepad Discipline

- Keep the clipboard visible at all times (**Caps Lock**); hold **Tab** to focus it with a movable cursor.
- **Clear stale lines** — a bearing from the last mission will happily overwrite your current one. Right-click removes lines.
- Community reviewers call the in-game scratchpad **essential**: track target IDs, shot results and FDC status on paper, because the map tokens only show a fraction of what you know.
- **Number row 1–4** quick-selects map tokens at the map table — muscle-memory token placement saves seconds per target.

## The Star Shell Timing Trick

S.T.A.R. illumination reveals a 12.74 km area and — critically — **does not trigger counter-battery fire**. The pro move: fire the star shell, solve while it burns, and have the kill shell loaded so it lands while the area is still lit. In night and fog missions this is effectively mandatory (see [reconnaissance](/guides/reconnaissance-guide)).

## Score Farming — The One-Shot Multi-Kill (Community-Reported)

Community score-runners found a fast requisition/score combo in challenge mode: triangulate using a **front-line infantry report (for distance) + observer #2 (for bearing)**, then land a single **AP shell** that destroys an **artillery position and its adjacent ammo depot together**. AP kills both because the depot is an underground/hardened target; HE would splash the guns and leave the depot standing. It is a demonstration of the general rule: *one shell, two confirmed kills* is the best score-per-round ratio in the game. Full details are in the [requisition & upgrade guide](/guides/requisition-upgrade-guide).

## The 30-Second Rule

Before pulling the calculator lever: clipboard range matches the map, bearing matches the target token, shell matches the load, charges match the chart, classification matches the orders. **Thirty seconds of verification saves five minutes of reloading.** (Details in the [ballistic calculator guide](/guides/ballistic-calculator-guide).)

## Mistakes That End Missions — The Killers

| Mistake | Why It Ends You | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Firing before all batteries are plotted | Counter-battery timer starts blind | Pre-fire map discipline |
| Wrong charge count in the barrel | Cannot unload; shot falls short/overflies | Verify every load twice |
| Riding the traverse stops | Hydraulic rupture, 90 s dead turret | Ease off before end of travel |
| Both guns rapid-firing | Barrel cook-off → game over | Alternate barrels, pace salvos |
| Hoarding requisition credits | Credits do not carry over sessions | Spend before major operations |
| Correcting before the round lands | Shooting at ghosts — long flight times at range | Wait for impact reports |

## The Operator's Morning Routine (Before Every Mission)

1. Read **both** teleprinters — every channel.
2. Place the Iron Nest token; verify it against the brief.
3. Check the weather readout (Chapter 3+) — wind changes shell choice.
4. Stock powder + the shells the orders dictate; never leave the depot with an empty charge locker.
5. Flush the recoil buffer.
6. Mark all artillery before the first shot.
7. Keep the clipboard clean and the coffee hot.
