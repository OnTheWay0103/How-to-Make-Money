# All Shell Types — Full Ammo List

> Launch Version / Updated: August 11, 2026

The game advertises **30 unlockable ammo types and abilities**. At launch, seven shells are confirmed. This page covers every confirmed shell: cost, blast radius, and exactly when to load it.

## The Confirmed Shell List

| Shell | Cost | Effect Radius | Best Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HE (High Explosive)** | 3 Requisition Credits | 0.27 km | The standard round — artillery batteries, infantry, mechanized, riots |
| **HCHE (High-Capacity HE)** | 5 Credits | 0.63 km | Clustered targets — more than double HE's radius |
| **AP (Armor Piercing)** | 3 Credits | 0.14 km | Bunkers, armored vehicles, hardened/underground targets |

## Shell-by-Shell Breakdown

### HE — High Explosive (S-Tier)

The workhorse. Cheap (3 credits), effective against infantry, artillery batteries, mechanized columns and civil riots — and the shell most mission orders will request. Its moderate 0.27 km radius means you can engage targets near friendlies with care. **Master HE first; it will carry most of your engagements.**

### HCHE — High-Capacity HE (A-Tier)

Five credits buys more than double the blast radius (0.63 km). Devastating against packed formations of infantry or tightly clustered batteries — and equally devastating to anything friendly in that radius. High Command will rarely dictate it; **you choose it when the intel promises a crowd.**

### AP — Armor Piercing (A-Tier)

The surgical round. A tight 0.14 km radius means it is safe near friendlies, but it exists for one job: penetrating bunkers, armored vehicles and hardened positions that HE merely splashes off. The developers advertise armor-piercing ammo for bunkers, armored vehicles, and underground or hardened targets. **Always carry AP when orders mention fortifications.**

### S.T.A.R. — Illumination (Utility)

The 1-credit recon flare. Non-combat — it lights up a massive 12.74 km area and does not trigger counter-battery fire the way combat shells do. Use it to confirm target positions at night, in fog, or when spotter intel is thin. It is a tool, not a weapon: it cannot kill anything.

### SMOKE (Situational)

Covers a large 1.86 km area in a smokescreen. Community guides rate it as currently limited in practical use — the core missions favor direct fire — but it shines for screening your own position during relocations and planned withdrawals. Buy it when the plan involves movement under observation.

### PHOSGENE — Gas (Situational)

Toxic gas that kills infantry while leaving infrastructure intact — the shell for when you need the ground afterwards (a bridge, a factory, a road junction). Expect it as a mission-dictated ordnance rather than a daily carry. **Wear the gasmask (G) when handling gas shells.**

### TEAR GAS (Curiosity)

Non-lethal crowd dispersal with a 1.86 km radius — thematically perfect for the civil-war setting, but of little use in standard military engagements. Revisit it in challenge modes where the scoring rewards restraint.

## Buying & Stocking Shells

- Shells are purchased with **Requisition Credits** at the requisition station — along with powder charges, recon planes and support.
- **Powder charges are non-negotiable**: a shell without charges is a very expensive paperweight. Never leave the depot without both.
- **Requisition credits do not carry over between sessions** — spend them, do not hoard them.
- Destroying **priority targets marked with stars** earns bonus requisition points. Prioritize them when choosing between engagements.
