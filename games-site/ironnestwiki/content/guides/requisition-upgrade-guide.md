# Requisition & Best Upgrade Path

> Launch Version / Updated: August 11, 2026

Requisition credits are the currency of the Iron Nest: they buy shells, powder charges, recon support and permanent upgrades. This guide explains how the economy works, every confirmed income source, the efficient earning patterns the community has settled on, and the upgrade order that wins.

---

## How Requisition Credits Work

- **Earn them by completing objectives** — and destroying **priority targets marked with stars** earns bonus points. Prioritize starred targets when planning engagements.
- **Spend them at the requisition station** on ammunition, powder charges, recon planes, forward observers and emergency moves.
- **Credits do NOT carry over between sessions.** Hoarding is strictly a mistake — a stockpile of credits is worthless the moment the mission ends. Spend them before major operations.
- **Powder charges are the base of the economy**: shells are useless without them, so charge purchases are never wasted. Never leave the depot with an empty charge locker.

---

## Where Credits Come From

| Source | Income | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Completing objectives** | Base income per objective | Reliable, per-mission |
| **Destroying priority targets (starred)** | Bonus credits | The single best income multiplier — starred targets are worth far more than standard ones |
| **Medal performance** | Indirect | Gold medals gate the best missions and skirmishes |
| **Skirmish challenges** | Score-based | The only repeatable high-income mode (see below) |

The income rule of the campaign: **when the intel marks a target with a star, it is your highest-value shell target on the board.** Plan the engagement order around starred targets first — a cheap HE round on a star is a better trade than an expensive AP round on a routine truck.

## Earning Credits Efficiently — Community Methods

### The One-Shot Multi-Kill (Community-Reported)

Score-runners found a repeatable high-value combo in challenge mode (community-reported):

1. **Triangulate with a front-line infantry report + observer #2** — infantry reports give distance, the observer gives a bearing; the intersection is precise without spending a single credit on recon.
2. **Line up an artillery position and its adjacent ammo depot in one solution** — the depot is an underground/hardened target.
3. **Fire ONE armor-piercing shell** — AP penetrates the hardened depot; HE would splash the guns and leave the depot standing. One shot, two confirmed kills, a very high score.

The general lesson: the best score-per-shell ratio in the game is **one AP round killing two adjacent hardened targets**. Whenever two high-armor targets sit close together, that is your money shot.

### Credit Budgets That Win Medals

The gold-medal system pays in prestige and unlocks — and every one of its requisition budgets teaches the same lesson: **efficiency, not volume.** Budget ceilings below are community-reported and still being verified:

| Mission | Budget Ceiling (Community-Reported) | What Fits |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Rock of Gibraltar | Very tight | 2× HE + powder — the cheapest medal in the game |
| Liberation / Counter-Battery / Dead Reckoning | Tight | HE + powder; skip recon; borrow star shells |
| Siege of Cartagena / Blind Fire | Moderate | HE + one HCHE for cluster shots |
| Skirmish – Chill | Volume — spending is the point | The one exception — buy everything |

Farming insight: missions with tight budgets force you to learn the star-shell-only recon pattern, which then becomes your low-cost default for the rest of the campaign.

### Challenge Mode — The Repeatable Farm

The two skirmish challenges are the only modes you can replay endlessly for score:

- **Skirmish – Challenge – Counter Battery Timer** — pressure from the first shot; rewards fast solutions and clean kill chains.
- **Skirmish – Chill – Uncontested Front** — no counter-battery fire; the volume farm. Its medals reward heavy spending and multi-kill shells — buy HCHE, wait for a packed battery, and let the credits flow.

## The Recommended Upgrade Order

The community's core philosophy: **"Build a machine you can trust before building one that simply hits harder."** Consistency and control beat raw firepower every time.

### 1. Precision Trajectory Systems
Improves shot-to-shot consistency. Every shot you fire benefits, on every target, for the rest of the game. This is the single best first investment.

### 2. Hydraulic Improvements
Faster, smoother turret control — and the hydraulic system is the one that fails under stress. Less time cranking and fixing means more time shooting.

### 3. Armor-Piercing Shells
Unlocks/improves the AP capability that bunker and armored missions demand. Without it, hardened targets become grind-fests.

### 4. High-Capacity Explosive Shells
HCHE turns clustered targets into one-shot problems. Grab it once clustered batteries and packed infantry start appearing.

### 5. Enhanced Map Equipment
Faster, more accurate targeting workflow — reduced plotting time and better measurement precision. You live and die by the map; improve the map.

### 6. Recon Aircraft
Superior battlefield awareness: reveals hidden positions and reduces counter-battery risk. The better you see, the less you get shot back at.

### 7. Utility Ammunition
Smoke, illumination and gas flexibility. Situational, but the missions that need them need them badly.

## Spending Strategy by Phase

| Phase | Priority |
|---|---|
| **First session** | Powder charges + HE shells, then Precision Trajectory |
| **Early campaign** | Hydraulic Improvements, then AP |
| **Mid campaign** | HCHE, Enhanced Map Equipment |
| **Late campaign** | Recon Aircraft, Utility Ammunition |

**Liquidity rule:** never spend down to zero before an operation — missions often dictate ordnance you do not have, and the requisition station is the only place to fix that.

## Common Economy Mistakes

| Pattern | Why It's a Trap |
|---|---|
| Hoarding credits "for later" | Credits evaporate at session end — spend before major operations |
| Spending down to zero mid-campaign | Missions dictate ordnance you may not own; keep a small buffer |
| Recon planes on every mission | Cheap star shells do most of the job; recon is for corridors that matter |
| Ignoring starred targets | You are leaving the highest-value credits on the board |
| HE on hardened targets | Splash damage, zero penetration — the shell is wasted either way; AP is the value round |
