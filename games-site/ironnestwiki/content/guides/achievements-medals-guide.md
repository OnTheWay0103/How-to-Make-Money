# Achievements & Medals Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 9, 2026

IRON NEST ships two parallel reward systems: **150 Steam achievements** tracked by Valve, and the in-game **medal system** with 100+ unlockable medals awarded per mission. The two overlap heavily — most Steam achievements are earned by collecting in-game medals. This guide explains both systems, what the community has confirmed so far, and the fastest reliable paths to the hard ones.

## The Two Systems

| System | Count | Where It Lives | What It Tracks |
|--------|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Steam Achievements** | 150 | Steam overlay / profile | Medal collection, story progression, campaign completion |
| **In-Game Medals** | 100+ | Mission end screen | Per-mission performance: accuracy, streaks, efficiency |

The full official Steam list is not yet documented anywhere (the game is three days old at the time of writing) — the community has confirmed the totals and the hardest entries from profile data. **Exact unlock requirements for individual achievements are community-reported.**

## Confirmed Achievement Facts

- **150 achievements total**, with a community-estimated median completion time around **10 hours**.
- The hardest confirmed achievement: **"Destroy 50 targets in one mission"** — only **~12% of players** have earned it. No regular mission reliably presents 50 targets; the campaign's biggest single-mission kill counts are The Gorge and Final Harvest at 30 kills each. Community consensus is that 50-kill runs happen in **Skirmish – Chill – Uncontested Front**, where you control the tempo and target density is highest.
- Achievements sync with Steam cloud saves and support Family Sharing (both confirmed on the store page).

## The Medal System — How Medals Are Awarded

Every mission awards medals across six confirmed categories:

| Medal Category | What It Measures | How to Farm It |
|----------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Ally kills = 0** | Friendly-fire discipline | Confirm every target with a second spotter before firing; check the Iris radius before HCHE |
| **Kill streak** | Consecutive kills without a miss/escape | One target lane at a time; twin-gun pre-loading |
| **Kills per shot** | Efficiency — multi-kills per round | HCHE on clustered batteries and packed infantry |
| **Requisition limit** | Budget discipline (≤10 / 30 / 50 credits) | Lean loadouts — HE + powder only (see the [requisition guide](/guides/requisition-upgrade-guide)) |
| **No recon planes** | Map and spotter skill | Substitute S.T.A.R. star shells — they are ammunition, not recon requisitions |
| **Counter-battery time left** | Survival speed (260+ seconds) | Mark all batteries pre-shot, HE them fast, save FDC pauses for the end |

Mission-specific medal requirements — from Calibration Fire's "1 shot fired" to the skirmish challenges — are catalogued in the [story walkthrough guide](/guides/story-walkthrough-guide).

## Known Medal Quirks (Community-Reported)

- The **Counter-Battery mission's 3-kill window medal** appears to have a typo in its data and is awarded for kills up to ~45 seconds apart.
- **High Tide's medal data** lists the kill-streak requirement twice — community testers believe this is a bug meaning one fewer medal requirement than listed.
- **White Shells** currently has no documented medal set at all — a mission still being tested.

## The 12% Club: How to Kill 50 Targets in One Mission

The community recipe for the hardest achievement:

1. Play **Skirmish – Chill – Uncontested Front** — no counter-battery pressure means you control the pace.
2. Budget for volume: this is the mission where the **600+ requisition spent** medal encourages buying everything anyway.
3. Stage twin-gun loadouts — one barrel cycling HE for infantry screens, one cycling AP for the armored lane.
4. Keep kills-per-shot above 1.5 with HCHE clusters (this also earns the skirmish's efficiency medal simultaneously).
5. Manage barrel heat — 50 kills means 50+ rounds; pace salvos and watch the 450 °C ceiling (see [systems & failures](/guides/systems-failure-guide)).

## Medal Strategy Notes

- **Medals are per-mission, not cumulative** — a streak resets when you miss, so a clean mission is worth more than a spectacular one with friendly-fire incidents.
- **Gold-tier medals reward restraint**, not volume: the highest medal sets require 0 ally kills and tight requisition budgets. Treat each mission as a score challenge rather than a survival sim, and the medals follow.
- The **two skirmish challenges** exist precisely for achievement hunters — one with counter-battery pressure for the "counter-battery time left" medals, one without for kill-volume medals.
