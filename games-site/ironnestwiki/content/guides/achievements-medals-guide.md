# Achievements & Medals Guide

> Launch Version / Updated: August 11, 2026

IRON NEST ships two parallel reward systems: **33 Steam achievements** tracked by Valve (per the official Steam store listing), and the in-game **medal system** with 100+ unlockable medals awarded per mission. The two overlap heavily — most Steam achievements are earned by collecting in-game medals. This guide explains both systems, what the community has confirmed so far, and the fastest reliable paths to the hard medals.

## The Two Systems

| System | Count | Where It Lives | What It Tracks |
|--------|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Steam Achievements** | 33 | Steam overlay / profile | Medal collection, story progression, campaign completion |
| **In-Game Medals** | 100+ | Mission end screen | Per-mission performance: accuracy, streaks, efficiency |

The store page confirms the achievement total, but the full official unlock list is community-documented rather than officially published. **Exact unlock requirements for individual achievements and medals are community-reported and still being verified.**

## Confirmed Achievement Facts

- **33 achievements total**, per the Steam store listing.
- Achievements sync with Steam cloud saves and support Steam Family Sharing (both confirmed on the store page).
- Most achievements are earned indirectly: play the campaign, collect medals, and the list fills itself. If you are chasing the full list, the fastest track is medal hunting — see below.

## The Medal System — How Medals Are Awarded

Every mission awards medals across six confirmed categories:

| Medal Category | What It Measures | How to Farm It |
|----------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Ally kills = 0** | Friendly-fire discipline | Confirm every target with a second spotter before firing; check the Iris radius before HCHE |
| **Kill streak** | Consecutive kills without a miss/escape | One target lane at a time; twin-gun pre-loading |
| **Kills per shot** | Efficiency — multi-kills per round | HCHE on clustered batteries and packed infantry |
| **Requisition limit** | Budget discipline | Lean loadouts — HE + powder only (see the [requisition guide](/guides/requisition-upgrade-guide)) |
| **No recon planes** | Map and spotter skill | Substitute S.T.A.R. star shells — they are ammunition, not recon requisitions |
| **Counter-battery time left** | Survival speed | Mark all batteries pre-shot, HE them fast, save FDC pauses for the end |

Mission-specific medal requirements — from Calibration Fire's extreme shot economy to the skirmish challenges — are catalogued in the [story walkthrough guide](/guides/story-walkthrough-guide).

## Fastest Medals (Do These First)

| Medal | Mission | Loadout | Effort |
|-------|---------|---------|--------|
| **2 kills, tiny budget** | Rock of Gibraltar | 2× HE + powder | Trivial |
| **Single shot fired** | Calibration Fire | 1× HE + powder | Trivial — the first mission's gold |
| **0 recons, 15 kills** | Siege of Cartagena | HE + one HCHE for clusters | Easy |
| **6+ streak, tight budget** | Liberation | HE, spotters only | Easy |
| **Long counter-battery survival** | Counter-Battery / Phantom Battery | Fast HE on batteries, late FDC pauses | Medium |
| **5 kills, one shell** | Skirmish – Chill | HCHE on a packed battery | Medium |

## Medal Strategy Notes

- **Medals are per-mission, not cumulative** — a streak resets when you miss, so a clean mission is worth more than a spectacular one with friendly-fire incidents.
- **Gold-tier medals reward restraint**, not volume: the highest medal sets require 0 ally kills and tight requisition budgets. Treat each mission as a score challenge rather than a survival sim, and the medals follow.
- The **two skirmish challenges** exist precisely for achievement hunters — one with counter-battery pressure for the "counter-battery time left" medals, one without for kill-volume medals.
