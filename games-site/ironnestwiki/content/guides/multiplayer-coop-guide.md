# Multiplayer & Co-op — Is IRON NEST Online?

> Launch Version / Updated: August 11, 2026

**Short answer: no.** IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator is strictly single-player at launch. There is no co-op, no multiplayer, no online lobby — and the developers' design choices explain why. This guide covers the answer in detail, what the game offers *instead* of multiplayer, and the honest state of community workarounds.

## The Confirmed State of Play

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is there online multiplayer? | **No** |
| Is there local co-op / split-screen? | **No** |
| Is there any PvP mode? | **No** |
| Is there cross-play? | **No — there is no online mode at all** |
| Does the store page list multiplayer features? | No — the store advertises achievements, cloud saves and Steam Family Sharing only |

Nothing in the developer's feature list (30 unlockable ammo types and abilities, 100+ medals, 15 regions, two challenge modes) mentions online play. At launch, the developers had made no concrete multiplayer announcement; some post-launch press coverage has reported multiplayer as a possible future update — treat any "coming soon" claim as unconfirmed for now.

## Why It Is Single-Player by Design

IRON NEST is built as a *one-operator* experience: a first-person simulation of a 5,000-ton walking turret where you physically walk between the teleprinters, map table, calculator, loading platform and trigger station. The whole game is the tension of one person doing the work of an entire artillery crew (see the [stations guide](/guides/crew-stations-guide)). Designers Nick Nieuwoudt and Dominik Latos described the game explicitly as solo operation — Japanese launch coverage described it as a "one-person operation" for exactly this loop.

Adding a second player would require rebalancing the entire simulation — who reads the map while someone else loads? — which is why even split-screen does not exist. The game is a solo meditation with a leaderboard, not a party game.

## What Replaces Multiplayer — The Challenge Modes

The solo-replayability layer is the **two skirmish challenges**, both single-player score runs with **online leaderboards** — the closest thing to competing against other players the game has:

| Challenge | Pressure | Best For |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Skirmish – Challenge – Counter Battery Timer** | Counter-battery fire from the start | Speed, precision and survival runs |
| **Skirmish – Chill – Uncontested Front** | No counter-battery pressure | Kill-volume and score records |

Leaderboards are score-based, so you *are* racing other operators worldwide — just asynchronously. If your itch is competition, this is where it gets scratched. Full medal requirements for both modes are in the [story walkthrough](/guides/story-walkthrough-guide).

## Steam Features You Do Get

- **33 Steam achievements** — the full completionist track (see the [achievements guide](/guides/achievements-medals-guide))
- **Cloud saves** — your campaign follows you between machines
- **Steam Family Sharing** — pass the game (and its saves) to family libraries
- **16 languages** — interface, audio and subtitles

## Co-op Workarounds — The Honest Version

**There are no multiplayer mods** for IRON NEST at launch — the simulation is single-seat by construction, and no community netcode or modding hooks were available in the launch build. What the community does instead:

- **Screenshot score runs** — players share skirmish leaderboard scores and gold-medal mission results in community hubs.
- **"Two-screens" co-op** — two operators on two machines, one runs the campaign, the other acts as spotter/radio from the wiki's spotter data and feed coordinates over voice. It is roleplay, not game code — but it genuinely mirrors the FDC-spotter relationship the game simulates.
- **Community translation duty** — the wiki's ongoing effort: documenting shell stats, medal requirements and correction tables so other operators don't have to rediscover them (every guide here is that work in progress).

## The Verdict

| If you want… | IRON NEST offers… |
|---|---|
| Online co-op artillery crews | **Nothing — it is solo** |
| Competitive artillery scoring | The two skirmish leaderboards |
| A shared hobby with friends | Community docs, screenshot races, gold-medal bragging rights |
| An offline solo sim you can pause any time | Everything — 15 regions, 100+ medals, 150 achievements |

Buy IRON NEST for the 5,000-ton machine and the slow, deliberate solo ritual. It will not become a co-op game overnight — but its two leaderboards make every shot a race against every other operator on the planet.
