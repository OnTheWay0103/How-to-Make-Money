# Story Mode Walkthrough — All Missions & Gold Medal Requirements

> Launch Version / Updated: August 11, 2026

The campaign is not a fixed level list — High Command issues procedurally generated objectives across 15 regions — but the mission *set* is consistent: a named sequence of operations that every operator plays through. This walkthrough covers the campaign phases, the core mission flow, every confirmed mission name with mission-by-mission tips, the special mission formats, and the gold medal requirements community testers have reverse-engineered. Medal criteria are **community-reported and still being verified** (the official medal documentation is incomplete at launch) — treat exact thresholds as a working draft.

## The Campaign at a Glance

| Phase | Missions | What's Tested | Gold Focus |
|-------|----------|---------------|------------|
| **Phase 1 — Training** | Calibration Fire → Liberation | Muscle memory: read, plot, load, fire | Shot economy (fewest shells) |
| **Phase 2 — Deviations** | Counter-Battery → Dead Reckoning | Spotter errors, MOA corrections, counter-battery | Speed + zero friendly fire |
| **Phase 3 — Multi-Target Salvos** | Fire on Call → Final Harvest | Three-target vectors, mixed loads, pressure | Kill streaks + kills-per-shot |
| **Final Siege** | Campaign end | Multi-stage defense against an armored division | Everything at once |

The community-recorded mission order below matches the walkthrough record — the game itself does not number missions on screen.

## The Core Mission Flow

Every mission follows the same sequence:

1. **Read the teleprinters.** High Command sends the day's orders plus spotter reports and frontline intel — check *every* channel; secondary channels carry threat warnings.
2. **Plot targets on the tactical map.** Mark known positions (your Iron Nest token, spotters, reference points) and triangulate.
3. **Calculate the firing solution.** Range into the ballistic calculator → elevation; bearing into the turret controls.
4. **Load and fire.** Correct shell, correct powder charge count, correct gun.
5. **Confirm the kill.** Second-spotter confirmation (or a star shell) before firing again saves ammunition and time.

## Phase 1 — Training Missions

### Calibration Fire
The very first mission: one ranging shot, one kill. The gold medal requires an extremely strict shot economy — the community record is a single shot fired — so do not "confirm" with a second shell. Plot carefully, trust the solution, and treat the single shot as the whole exam.

### Fire and Light
The recon-fire intro: illumination plus combat. Gold requires **4 or fewer shots fired total** — star shells do not count against the limit (community testing), so illuminate freely, plot fast, and make every kill shell count.

### Liberation
The first full battle, and the first mission with multiple medals: **0 ally kills, 6+ consecutive kills, 30 or less requisition spent** (plus an accuracy medal with an unverified threshold).

- Zero friendly-fire means **confirm every target with a second spotter** before firing — the blue skull is the medal-killer.
- The tight credit cap forbids expensive ordnance: budget HE plus powder, skip recon planes, and only bring what the orders dictate.

## Phase 2 — Deviations Missions

### Counter-Battery
The race mission. Medals: **3 kills within 30 seconds, 260+ seconds of counter-battery time left, 10 enemy kills, 30 or less requisition spent.**

- The counter-battery clock forces ruthless battery prioritization: mark all artillery before the first shot (see the [counter-battery guide](/guides/counter-battery-survival-guide)), kill batteries with fast HE, and save FDC pauses for when the timer runs low.
- The 3-kills-in-30-seconds medal is reportedly buggy — community testers report it is awarded for kills spread slightly wider than the listed window. Use twin-gun double-loading to chain kills in one window.

### Iron Road
Convoy interdiction. Medals: **0 ally kills, 20+ consecutive kills, 2+ kills per shot (10+ kills), 0 recon planes requisitioned.**

- No recons means spotters and star shells are your only eyes — spend the cheap S.T.A.R. round early.
- 20 consecutive kills demands target discipline: engage one lane of the road at a time and never leave a convoy half-killed while it escapes.

### Siege of Cartagena
A coastal city siege. Medals: **0 ally kills, 15 enemy kills, 0 recons, 50 or less requisition spent.**

- The credit budget is generous enough for HCHE — use it on clustered defenders to make each shell kill multiple targets, which both hits the kill count and respects the budget.

### The Gorge
The famous river-gorge mission. Medals: **0 ally kills, 10+ consecutive kills, 30 enemy kills, 2+ kills per shot (10+ kills).**

- The walkthrough trick stands: this mission can be completed **without the ballistic calculator** — a well-aimed ranging shell and impact corrections do the job.
- 30 kills is one of the largest kill counts in the campaign: prioritize clustered targets for the kills-per-shot medal, and expect the fight to run long — pace barrel heat.

### Rock of Gibraltar
A small, surgical mission: gold requires **only 2 enemy kills, 0 recons, and 10 or less requisition spent.** This is the cheapest medal in the game — two perfect HE shots and a minimal loadout (HE + charges, nothing else) takes it. Do not over-buy before this mission.

### Dead Reckoning
Blind-fire training: **0 ally kills, 6+ consecutive kills, 15 enemy kills, 30 or less requisition spent.** Dead-reckoning targets from vague intel — the "shoot the box" method from the [target-spotting guide](/guides/target-spotting-guide) earns the first-shot kills that feed the streak.

## Phase 3 — Multi-Target Salvos

### Fire on Call
Fire missions on demand: **0 ally kills, 30 enemy kills, 2+ kills per shot (10+ kills), 0 recons.** The kills-per-shot requirement makes HCHE the mission MVP — clustered batteries and packed infantry are worth waiting for a clean multi-kill solution instead of sniping singles.

### High Tide
Coastal defense. Medals: **6+ consecutive kills and 3 kills within 30 seconds** (an accuracy medal is also listed; the kill-streak requirement appears twice in the medal data, which community testers believe is a bug meaning one fewer requirement). Twin-gun pre-loading is the whole strategy: left barrel ready for target A, right barrel for target B, both firing inside one short window.

### Blind Fire
No-eyes artillery: **20 enemy kills, 6+ consecutive kills, 0 recons, 50 or less requisition spent.** The name is the hint — spotter data will be thin, so S.T.A.R. illumination (which does not count as a recon plane) carries the mission.

### Phantom Battery
Counter-battery under deception: **9 enemy kills, 260+ seconds of counter-battery time left, 0 recons, plus an accuracy medal.** A phantom battery fires and displaces — verify kills before re-engaging empty positions, and treat every round you send as advertising your own position.

### Final Harvest
The last regular mission before the siege: **30 enemy kills, 0 ally kills, 0 recons, first shot within 50 seconds.** The opening timer is the hard part — the moment the mission starts, plot from memory: your own position was in the brief, spotters were in the last field report, and the first target should be a star-shell confirm, not a guess. Pre-plot the most likely artillery positions before the mission if the previous briefings hint at it.

### White Shells
The only mission with **no medal list documented** — community testers are still working this one out. If you unlock it, record what you achieve; the wiki would welcome the data.

## Special Mission Types

Beyond the standard objectives, the campaign throws in distinctive mission formats:

### 1. Recon Mission
The campaign's training for intelligence warfare: **fire star shells to illuminate grid squares**, then use the revealed positions to build firing solutions. Timing the illumination shell with your loading sequence is the skill being tested — light up the area, plot fast, engage before the illumination fades.

### 2. FDC Counter-Battery Mission
A countdown timer runs while hostile artillery hunts your turret. The key mechanic: **destroying the enemy's Fire Direction Center (FDC) units pauses the countdown**, buying you breathing room. Locate FDCs by drawing bearings from known positions — where the lines intersect is the likely FDC location. Remember spotter data carries a margin of error, so treat intersections as estimates.

### 3. Emergency Reposition — Reverse Triangulation
The mission where you must figure out **where your own turret is**. You know the exact bearing, distance, and charges used for a calibration star shell, so you work backward from the detonation point to draw a line to your position. Failure here almost always comes from rushing the earlier coordinate entries — double-check every spotter coordinate before placing map markers.

## The Final Siege Defense

The campaign ends with a **massive, multi-stage siege defense**: you must hold off an entire armored division single-handedly. Expect waves of armored convoys and fortified targets coming from multiple bearings with little time between salvos. Preparation tips:

- **Stock mixed shells.** You will need AP for convoys and hardened targets in the same engagement (HE splashes off armor — see the [enemy types guide](/guides/enemy-types-guide)).
- **Protect your systems.** A hydraulic rupture or barrel overheat mid-siege is fatal — see the [Systems & Failure Guide](/guides/systems-failure-guide) for safe operating zones.
- **Counter-battery first.** Enemy artillery batteries should be silenced the moment they appear — you cannot survive sustained counter-battery fire while fighting the siege.

## High Command's Patience Meter

High Command tracks your efficiency: miss targets or take too long on vectors and their patience drops — enough failures can end in **court-martial**. Practical rules that keep patience stable:

- **Don't fire without a solution.** A confirmed second-spotter intersection beats a guess every time.
- **Use the right shell class.** Hitting a bunker with HE wastes shots and time.
- **Speed comes from map discipline.** Keep the map clean — yellow for spotting lines, red for firing solutions — so you never re-derive a vector you already solved.

## Skirmish Challenges — The Two Endgame Modes

Beyond story mode, two score-based challenges power the leaderboards:

| Challenge | Rules | Medal Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| **Skirmish – Challenge – Counter Battery Timer** | Counter-battery pressure from the start | First shot within 50 s; high kills-per-shot; long kill streaks; 0 recons |
| **Skirmish – Chill – Uncontested Front** | No counter-battery pressure | High kills-per-shot; long streaks; **very heavy requisition spending**; **multi-kill single shells** |

The Chill mode's heavy-spending medal inverts normal strategy — this is the one place hoarding and buying everything is the goal. The multi-kill-with-one-shell medal points straight at HCHE on a clustered formation, or a well-timed round on a packed artillery battery.

## General Gold-Medal Tactics

| Medal Type | How to Win It |
|---|---|
| **0 ally kills** | Second-spotter confirm before every shot; check the Iris radius against friendly positions |
| **Consecutive kills** | One target lane at a time; never leave a group half-killed; twin-gun pre-loading |
| **Kills-per-shot** | HCHE on clusters; wait for the multi-kill solution instead of sniping |
| **Requisition limit** | Budget loadout from the [requisition guide](/guides/requisition-upgrade-guide); skip recon planes; HE + powder only |
| **0 recons** | S.T.A.R. star shells instead — they are ammunition, not recon requisitions |
| **Counter-battery time left** | Mark all batteries pre-shot, HE them fast, spend FDC pauses late |
| **First shot within 50 s** | Pre-plot from the brief: own position, spotters, first target's lane |
| **Multi-kill one shell** | HCHE on a packed battery or formation; verify radius with the Iris first |
