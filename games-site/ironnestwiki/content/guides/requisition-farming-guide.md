# Requisition Points — How to Farm Credits Fast

> Launch Version / Updated: August 9, 2026

Requisition credits are the lifeblood of the Iron Nest — they buy every shell, charge, recon asset and upgrade, and **they do not carry over between sessions**. Farming them efficiently is the difference between a well-stocked machine and a paperweight with an empty charge locker. This guide covers every confirmed income source, the community's fastest score methods, and how to spend farmed credits for the best long-term return.

## Where Credits Come From

| Source | Income | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Completing objectives** | Base income per objective | Reliable, per-mission |
| **Destroying priority targets (starred)** | **Bonus credits** | The single best income multiplier — starred targets are worth far more than standard ones |
| **Medal performance** | Indirect | Gold medals gate the best missions and skirmishes |
| **Skirmish challenges** | Score-based | The only repeatable high-income mode (see below) |

The income rule of the campaign: **when the intel marks a target with a star, it is your highest-value shell target on the board.** Plan the engagement order around starred targets first — a 3-credit HE round on a star is a better trade than an expensive AP round on a routine truck.

## The Community's One-Shot Score Method (114 Points)

Score-runners found a repeatable high-score combo in challenge mode (community-reported):

1. **Triangulate with a front-line infantry report + observer #2** — infantry reports give distance, the observer gives a bearing; the intersection is precise without spending a single credit on recon.
2. **Line up an artillery position and its adjacent ammo depot in one solution** — the depot is an underground/hardened target.
3. **Fire ONE armor-piercing shell** — AP penetrates the hardened depot; HE would splash the guns and leave the depot standing. One shot, two confirmed kills, **~114 points**.

The general lesson: the best score-per-shell ratio in the game is **one AP round killing two adjacent hardened targets**. Whenever two Class 4/5 targets sit close together, that is your money shot.

## Credit Budgets That Win Medals

The gold-medal system pays in prestige and unlocks — and every one of its requisition budgets teaches the same farming lesson: **efficiency, not volume.**

| Mission | Budget Ceiling | What Fits |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Rock of Gibraltar | **≤10 credits** | 2× HE + powder — the cheapest medal in the game |
| Liberation / Counter-Battery / Dead Reckoning | **≤30 credits** | HE + powder; skip recon; borrow star shells |
| Siege of Cartagena / Blind Fire | **≤50 credits** | HE + one HCHE for cluster shots |
| Skirmish – Chill (600+ medal) | **≥600 credits spent** | The one exception — volume is the point |

Farming insight: missions with tight budgets force you to learn the star-shell-only recon pattern, which then becomes your low-cost default for the rest of the campaign.

## Challenge Mode — The Repeatable Farm

The two skirmish challenges are the only modes you can replay endlessly for score:

- **Skirmish – Challenge – Counter Battery Timer** — pressure from the first shot; rewards fast solutions and clean kill chains (first-shot-under-50-seconds, 1.5+ kills-per-shot, 20+ streaks).
- **Skirmish – Chill – Uncontested Front** — no counter-battery fire; the volume farm. Its medals literally require you to **spend 600+ requisition and kill 5+ targets with a single shell** — buy HCHE, wait for a packed battery, and let the credits flow.

## What to Spend Farmed Credits On (Short Version)

The full upgrade order lives in the [requisition & upgrade guide](/guides/requisition-upgrade-guide); the farming-angle shortlist:

| Purchase | Why It Pays for Itself |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Powder charges** | Nothing fires without them — the only never-wasted purchase |
| **HE shells** | The cheapest income per credit — more rounds = more objectives = more credits |
| **Precision Trajectory Systems** | Misses are the most expensive thing in the game; accuracy is income |
| **Acoustic dampening** | Cuts the post-firing deafness window from ~10 s to ~4 s (community-reported) — faster corrections, faster kills |
| **HCHE** | The multi-kill round — the only shell that converts one credit spend into several kills |

## The Farming Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Why It's a Trap |
|---|---|
| Hoarding credits "for later" | Credits evaporate at session end — spend before major operations |
| Spending down to zero mid-campaign | Missions dictate ordnance you may not own; keep a small buffer |
| Recon planes on every mission | 1-credit star shells do most of the job; recon is for corridors that matter |
| Ignoring starred targets | You are leaving the highest-value credits on the board |
| HE on hardened targets | Splash damage, zero penetration — the shell is wasted either way; AP is the value round |
