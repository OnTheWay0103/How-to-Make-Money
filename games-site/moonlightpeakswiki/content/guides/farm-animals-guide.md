# Moonlight Peaks — Farm Animals Guide: All 7 Animals, Barns & Fodder

> Launch Version / Updated: August 8, 2026

Animals are Moonlight Peaks' passive-income layer — and the ingredient engine behind the [Cooking & Recipes Guide](/guides/cooking-recipes-guide)'s best dishes. There are **7 farm animals**, all bought from **Luna's farm** (three rotate into her shop each day), all requiring a Barn first. This guide lists every animal, its product, and the care system that keeps them happy and productive.

## Barns First

- **Barns are bought from Ridge** — 4,000 Coins each, **hold 4 animals**, and take a day to build
- You'll need **at least three Barns** to house one of each animal type
- Buy a Barn before animals: most animals can't be purchased until one exists

## All 7 Animals — Quick Reference

| Animal | Cost | Product | Unlock |
|--------|------|---------|--------|
| **Cheeken** | 1,200 Coins | Eggs (and Golden Egg) | Early game — "Farm Animals For Sale" quest (Day 2) |
| **Rabbicula** | 2,800 Coins | **Luck Dust** dig spots | Spring, Year 2 (Luna's letter) |
| **Piggoat** | 3,500 Coins | Piggoat Milk (→ Hellfeta Cheese) | After building a Barn |
| **Draculamb** | 4,500 Coins | Draculamb Milk + Wool (→ Ghoulembert Cheese) | After building a Barn |
| **Cowcula** | 6,000 Coins | Cowcula Milk (→ Standard Cheese) | After building a Barn |
| **Stoney** | 9,000 Coins | Stone Hearts / Heart Stone (→ 20 Stone) | Winter, Year 1 |
| **Bumpkin** | 12,000 Coins | Plops (→ Moonshine via the Keg) | Fall, Year 1 — most expensive animal |

Animals also come in **color pattern variants** (e.g., spotted vs. non-spotted Cowculas) — cosmetic, but collectors take note.

## The Care Loop

### Feeding
Keep the **trough filled daily**:

| Feed | Recipe | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Fodder** | 2 Fiber = 1 Fodder | Prevents mood loss |
| **Premium Fodder** | 2 Fodder + 1 Mana Essence | **Boosts mood** — more production |

Fiber comes from the Refiner; Mana Essence is the premium multiplier.

### Mood & Happiness
- **Pet animals daily** — talking to them in **Hellkitten form** can replace petting and gives hints about their needs
- A **gramophone** improves friendship once obtained
- Happy animals produce more — for Rabbicula, happiness raises the **quantity of Luck Dust dig spots** (Luck Dust has no quality tiers)

### Pairing Rules
Animals have compatibility quirks — get them wrong and happiness suffers:

- **Rabbiculas pair well with Bumpkins**
- **Rabbiculas should NOT share a barn with Cowculas** (same applies to Cheekens)
- Plan barn assignments around the confirmed pairings

### Cleanliness
Clean out the **fertilizer** animals produce regularly so it doesn't overflow — neglected stalls hit mood.

## Rabbicula — The Luck Dust Machine

The standout animal (2,800 Coins, Spring Year 2). A well-fed, happy Rabbicula periodically creates **Luck Dust dig spots** around accessible farm areas — collect with a Shovel or in Hellkitten form. Luck Dust is:

- The **primary ingredient for Love Potions** (recipe from Sabrina at Webb of Wonders) — which boost friendship gained from gifts
- A "Liked" gift for almost every villager — except **Death**, who doesn't appreciate it
- Sellable for **110 Coins** each — steady passive income

**Warning:** Rabbicula **eat your crops** — they target seeds, early-stage growth, and carrots at any stage. Protect the garden:

- **Fence the crops** (or the barn) with Workshop-built fencing — they can't get through small gaps
- **Chase them away** — walking toward them pushes them out of the garden
- **Block off areas** — place them in the Southern part of the farm (down the steps) or the Western part (across the bridge by Viktor's house), and fence the steps/bridge

## Production Chains Summary

| Animal | Raw Product | Refined Into | Final Use |
|--------|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Cheeken** | Egg | — | Cooking (Pancakes, Fried Eggs, Eggnog, Shakshuka) |
| **Piggoat** | Piggoat Milk | Hellfeta Cheese | Cooking (Cheese Platter, Angry Pizza) |
| **Draculamb** | Milk + Wool | Ghoulembert Cheese | Cooking; wool for crafting |
| **Cowcula** | Cowcula Milk | Standard Cheese | Cooking (Margherita Pizza, Crimson Pudding) |
| **Stoney** | Heart Stone | 20 Stone | Crafting materials |
| **Bumpkin** | Plops | Moonshine (via Keg) | Money recipes (Bloody Mana Mary, Eggnog) |
| **Rabbicula** | Luck Dust | — | Love Potions, gifts, selling (110 Coins) |

**Early-game pick:** Cheeken (1,200 Coins, Day 2) — eggs feed the best starter recipes. **Mid-game priority:** Piggoat or Draculamb for the milk→cheese chains. **Late-game goals:** Bumpkin and Rabbicula for the Moonshine and Luck Dust economies.
