# Moonlight Peaks — Festivals & Events Guide: The Full Seasonal Calendar

> Launch Version / Updated: August 8, 2026

Moonlight Peaks runs on a real calendar with four seasons — and each season brings at least one town event worth planning around. Festivals are where the game's biggest rewards live: exclusive items, event currencies, rare fish, and relationship boosts you can't get anywhere else. This guide collects every confirmed festival, its dates, and how to make the most of each one.

## Event Calendar — Quick Reference

| Event | Dates | Location | Star Reward |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Loveage Festival** | Spring 20–21 | Town center (stage, Luna's stall, banquet table) | Secret Admirer romance event, bouquets |
| **Mastersoup** | Autumn 5th Night | Town (cooking competition) | Mastersoup Trophy decoration |
| **Evernight Festival** | Autumn 26–28 | Whole town + Midnight Market | Evernight Coins → Sabrina's shop |
| **Full Moon** | 15th of every season | Howling Marshes, Luna Bay, Forest | Moonflutter Fish, Moon Goddess, "Awooooo!" achievement |

## Loveage Festival (Spring 20–21) — The Romance Event

Hosted by **Llemi**, the Love Demon. The two-night event is the game's romance set-piece:

**Spring 20 (Saturday):**
- Talk to Llemi on his stage to secretly select up to **three residents** for the Secret Admirer Gift Exchange (talk to him multiple times to gift all three)
- Visit **Luna's bouquet stall** (outside Webb of Wonders, southeast corner of town) to craft custom flower bouquets — flowers cost 8–20 Coins each
- Contribute up to 3 dishes to **Mina's communal banquet table** (outside Third Eye Threads)

**Spring 21 (Sunday):**
- The gift exchange triggers automatically when you enter town — a cutscene reveals who gave gifts to whom

**Prep tip:** save your best dishes and have flowers picked before Spring 20. Event-only flowers from Luna make strong gifts in the exchange.

## Mastersoup (Autumn 5th Night) — The Cooking Competition

Hosted annually by **Mina** on the 5th Night of Autumn: three elimination rounds (Appetizers, Main Dishes, Desserts). You pick which dish to cook with **three attempts** per round — Mina hints at wrong ingredients after failures, and three failed attempts eliminate you. The reward is the **Mastersoup Trophy** (cosmetic), and participating boosts your relationship with Mina like a loved gift.

**Round recipes:** Appetizers — Mushroom Soup, Gazpacho Soup, Carrot Soup. Mains — Veggie Burrito, Margherita Pizza, Farmer's Stew, Orbis Nigiri, Mushroom Stew, Breakfast Burger. Desserts — Red Velvet Cupcake, Blood Orange Cupcake, Raspberry Cupcake, Ollebrod, Crimson Pudding.

See the [Cooking & Recipes Guide](/guides/cooking-recipes-guide) for every recipe's ingredients.

## Evernight Festival (Autumn 26–28) — The Longest Festival

A three-day Halloween-themed event hosted by **Pumpkin Head** — the game's biggest festival. Activities earn **Evernight Coins**, spent at Sabrina's Evernight Shop in the Midnight Market. **Unspent coins are lost when the event ends.**

| Activity | How It Works | Reward |
|----------|--------------|--------|
| **Luna's Twilight Showcase** | Submit up to 6 items at the pier north of Death's Shack; winner chosen on the final night | 1st place: **100 Evernight Coins** (items are lost forever) |
| **Jitterbug** | Match-3 minigame at Heart Pier, hosted by Elvira | 1 Evernight Coin per 10 score points |
| **Evernight Fashion Show** | Design outfits for three models at Third Eye Threads (Autumn 27) | Coins; outcome doesn't matter |
| **Pumpkin Dash** | Red-light/green-light at Peaksview Park | Participation: Pumpkin Beanie; 1st: 10 coins, 2nd: 5, 3rd: 1 — unlimited replays |
| **Candy Buckets** | Interact with buckets outside homes | Evernight Coins |

**Event-exclusive Vampsters** appear only during the festival (under the tree west of Coffee and Coffins, and behind the pumpkin decoration on the southeast patio of The Broken Lamp) — return them to the Cave of Echoes den before the event ends, or wait a year.

**Evernight Shop highlights (Sabrina):** Pumpkin Spice Latte (21 Coins), Pumpkin Pie (16), Pumpkin Stew (12), recipes (1 Coin each), plus gravestones, tombstones, festive tables, lanterns, scarecrow, tent, and wallpapers from 1–59 Coins.

**Showcase tip:** high-value items (2-star foods, Diamonds, Golden Eggs) improve your chances of winning the Twilight Showcase.

## Full Moon (15th of Every Month)

On the 15th day of every season, the full moon transforms the town:

- **Werewolves gather in the Howling Marshes** — Howling Hammer closes; no town business with Mayor Brook
- The **"Awooooo!" achievement** unlocks only by speaking to transformed werewolves at midnight
- The **Moonflutter Fish** can only be caught on the 15th in Luna Bay with a premium/enchanted rod — required for the museum Aquarium, sells for 330 Coins at 2-star
- The **Moon Goddess** can be met in the Forest and Deep Forest on the 15th (and the two days prior) — maxing her friendship yields a Chakra Tuner for the Deity Collection

**Prep tip:** keep a premium rod charged and reach Luna Bay before midnight — the Moonflutter is a once-a-month catch.
