## Advanced Guide — Time Management & Late-Game Tips

Moonlight Peaks looks relaxed, but its day-night cycle is a genuine optimization problem: crops, potions, relationships, and town events all compete for the same hours. This advanced guide covers the systems that multiply your time — the potion economy, shapeshifting utility, and schedule planning — so late game stays profitable instead of frantic.

### The Potion Economy

| Potion | Ingredient Cost | Sell Value | Profit |
|--------|-----------------|------------|--------|
| Minor Healing | 10 gold | 35 gold | 25 gold |
| Luck Potion | 30 gold | 85 gold | 55 gold |
| Love Potion | 60 gold | 180 gold | 120 gold |
| Alter Ego Elixir | 120 gold | 350 gold | 230 gold |

1. **Brew in bulk:** Cauldron runs are fixed time. Save ingredients and brew 5-10 potions per session instead of one-offs.
2. **Luck Dust matters:** Luck potions boost drop rates — brew them before mining or fishing sessions, not for sale.
3. **The Alter Ego Elixir:** The best late-game money maker. Lock down its ingredient supply early.

### Daily Schedule Optimization

1. **Morning:** Harvest and water crops; check quest boards.
2. **Afternoon:** Mining or fishing with a Luck Potion active — double-gather windows.
3. **Evening:** Town errands, relationships, and Nokturna matches (villagers are home).
4. **Night:** Brew potions and handle night-only events.

### Shapeshifting Utility

- **Bat form:** Fast travel between town and outlying farms — saves 10+ minutes daily.
- **Hellkitten form:** Fits through small gaps in caves, unlocking shortcut ores others can't reach.
- **Mana and stamina costs:** Keep one stamina potion on hand; running out mid-form drains the next morning's energy.
- **Form mastery:** Unlock every form's utility upgrades before their combat perks — utility pays daily.

### Relationships and Romance

1. **Gift strategy:** Give villagers their loved gifts (see the gift guide) once per week; daily gifts waste inventory space.
2. **Quest synergy:** Stack romance quests with town errands that visit the same villager.
3. **Marriage timing:** Complete the romance chain before winter — winter events have no romance content.
4. **Companion bonuses:** Married companions grant daily resource bonuses; choose by playstyle, not preference.

### Nokturna Deck Tips

- **Control the early game:** Win the first two rounds with cheap cards, save strong cards for the third.
- **Deck size:** Keep your deck lean — 12-15 cards is ideal for consistent draws.
- **Prize value:** Tournament matches pay more than casual play; always enter the weekly tournament.
