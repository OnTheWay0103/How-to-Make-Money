# Moonlight Peaks — Romance Guide: Hearts, Dates & Marriage

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Romance is one of the deepest systems in Moonlight Peaks — **~24 residents** across the town's **seven families** are fully dateable, and the game is **completely LGBTQIA+ friendly**: every romanceable character can be dated regardless of your character's appearance or identity, with no gender restrictions anywhere in the system. This guide explains the heart system, how to ask someone out, dates, proposals, weddings, and how divorce works.

## How Relationships Work

Every relationship is driven by **relationship points**, earned through:

- **Talking daily** — the slow, steady source of points
- **Gifts** — the fast track; **liked gifts** grant extra points and **loved gifts** grant even more
- **Dates and kisses** — only available once you're officially together

| Hearts | Unlock | What you can do |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **2 hearts** | **Hug** the character | Physical affection becomes available |
| **4 hearts** | **Ask the character out** | Unlocks **daily kisses** and **dates** |
| **8 hearts** | **Propose during a date** | You can officially propose marriage |
| **Married** | **Wedding** | Wedding happens **at least 2 days after you set the date** |

**Pro tip:** gifts are the difference between a slow burn and a fast courtship. A loved gift (see the Gift Guide) is worth dramatically more than a liked one, and the **Love Potion** — brewed at your cauldron — raises a resident's affinity faster than any normal gift when gifted.

## How to Ask Someone Out

1. **Build the relationship to 4 hearts.** Talk daily and gift liked/loved items. The Love Potion accelerates this substantially.
2. **Choose the "ask out" dialogue option** — at 4 hearts the option appears in your conversation menu.
3. **Accept the relationship.** From that point you get **daily kisses** and can schedule **dates**.

There's no rejection mechanic at the threshold — once you're at 4 hearts, the option is yours to take. If you're dating multiple people, each relationship is tracked independently; hearts don't decay when you're seeing someone else.

**Pro tip:** date-famous characters like Death take longer to reach because some (like Death) are tied to story progression — you need to unlock the south map via **"A Bridge Too Far"** before you can even find him.

## Dates

Dates are scheduled on nights you're together and function as boosted relationship sessions:

| Date activity | What it's for |
|---------------|---------------|
| Talking at venues around town | Points + flavor dialogue |
| Gifting on a date | Extra affinity on top of the normal gift bonus |
| Festival dates | Seasonal events double as date opportunities |

Once you're dating, **daily kisses** are the reliable point source — a quick kiss before you head out to farm keeps the relationship ticking upward even on busy nights.

## Proposals and Marriage

At **8 hearts**, a new option appears **during a date**: **propose**.

1. Reach **8 hearts** with your partner.
2. Initiate a **date**.
3. Choose the **proposal** option during the date.
4. **Set the wedding date** — the wedding itself happens **at least 2 days after** you set it, so plan around the calendar (and check for festival overlaps).
5. **Wedding night** — you move into married life with your spouse, who keeps their routine in town.

**Pro tip:** propose on a night when the calendar is clear — if a festival lands on your chosen wedding day you can't simply postpone without re-setting the date, and nobody wants a wedding competing with the town's festival celebrations.

## Divorce via Llemi

Divorce in Moonlight Peaks is deliberately painless:

- Handled by **an NPC named Llemi** — no court, no drama.
- **No social penalty** — your ex-spouse keeps their normal friendship level and interacts with you as usual.
- You're free to **date again immediately** (or pursue one of the other ~23 romanceable residents).

There is **no child-rearing feature** in the game — marriage is about your spouse and your life together, not heirs for the family line.

## Romance FAQ

**Can I date same-sex characters?**
Yes — every romanceable character can be dated by any player character, full stop. **LGBTQIA+ friendly** is a core design principle of the game, not a toggle.

**What happens if I date everyone?**
Nothing bad. Multiple simultaneous relationships carry no jealousy system or social penalty. Proposals, however, are exclusive — you can only marry one spouse.

**Does my appearance affect romance options?**
No. Romance is unrestricted regardless of your character's appearance or identity — including the **Alter Ego Elixir**, which completely changes your appearance, with no effect on your relationships.

**Can I remarry after divorce?**
Yes. Divorce via Llemi leaves you single and free to date and marry again.

**Which character should I marry first?**
If you want the easiest path: **Noel Webb** (loves easy-to-source items like Mana Coffee and Mana Chocolate, and **Goliath fish**), or **Ridge Logan** (loves Beer, Golden Egg, Hamburgers — all buyable or cookable). If you want the story event: pursue **Death** — finding him requires the "A Bridge Too Far" quest line, and his wedding is the most talked-about in the community.
