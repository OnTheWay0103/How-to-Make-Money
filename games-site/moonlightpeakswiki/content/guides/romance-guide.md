# Moonlight Peaks — Romance Guide: Hearts, Dates & Marriage

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Romance is one of the deepest systems in Moonlight Peaks — **23 residents** are fully dateable: 5 vampires, 4 witches, 3 werewolves, 3 seers, 3 mermaids/mermen, 3 humans, plus the standalone **Death** and **Llemi**. The game is **completely LGBTQIA+ friendly**: every romanceable character can be dated regardless of your character's appearance or identity, with no gender restrictions anywhere in the system. This guide explains the heart system, how to ask someone out, dates, proposals, weddings, and how divorce works.

## How Relationships Work

Every relationship is driven by **relationship points**, earned through:

- **Talking daily** — the slow, steady source of points
- **Gifts** — the fast track; **liked gifts** grant extra points and **loved gifts** grant even more
- **Dates and kisses** — only available once you're officially together

| Hearts | Unlock | What you can do |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **2 hearts** | **Hug** the character | Physical affection becomes available |
| **4 hearts** | **Ask the character out** | Unlocks **daily kisses** and **dates** (minigame dates) |
| **8 hearts** | **8-heart cutscene**, then **propose at the end of a date** | You can officially propose marriage |
| **Married** | **Wedding** | Wedding happens **at least 2 days after you set the date**; spouse moves in and two final hearts unlock (10 total) |

**Pro tip:** gifts are the difference between a slow burn and a fast courtship. A loved gift (see the Gift Guide) is worth dramatically more than a liked one, and the **Love Potion** — brewed at your cauldron — gives a bonus to affinity when gifted. Check the **relationship page** to see which characters are dateable (stacked hearts with "..." = dateable; four single hearts = friend-level cap).

## How to Ask Someone Out

1. **Build the relationship to 4 hearts.** Talk daily and gift liked/loved items. The Love Potion accelerates this substantially.
2. **Choose the "ask out" dialogue option** — at 4 hearts the option appears in your conversation menu.
3. **Accept the relationship.** From that point you get **daily kisses** and can schedule **dates**.

There's no rejection mechanic at the threshold — once you're at 4 hearts, the option is yours to take. If you're dating multiple people, each relationship is tracked independently; hearts don't decay when you're seeing someone else.

**Pro tip:** date-famous characters like Death take longer to reach because some are tied to story progression — Death's story introduction comes via the **Ambrosia Graveyard** cutscene, which requires **"A Bridge Too Far"** and the Crest Garden quest line (though his house by the docks is visible in town from early on).

## Dates

Dates are scheduled on nights you're together and function as boosted relationship sessions — and they're **minigames**:

| Date activity | What it's for |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Bouquet arranging** | A date minigame — do well and the points flow |
| **Marshmallow roasting** | Another date minigame — failing a minigame **loses points**, so focus |
| Talking at venues around town | Points + flavor dialogue |
| Gifting on a date | Extra affinity on top of the normal gift bonus |
| Festival dates | Seasonal events double as date opportunities |

Once you're dating, **daily kisses** are the reliable point source — a quick kiss before you head out to farm keeps the relationship ticking upward even on busy nights.

## Proposals and Marriage

At **8 hearts**, your partner's **8-heart cutscene** plays, and then a new option appears **at the end of a date**: **propose**.

1. Reach **8 hearts** with your partner and see their 8-heart cutscene.
2. Initiate a **date**.
3. Choose the **proposal** option at the end of the date.
4. **Set the wedding date** — the wedding happens **at least 2 days later**, so plan around the calendar (and check for festival overlaps). Optional white attire is available from **Third Eye Threads**.
5. **Married life** — your spouse moves in, **two final hearts unlock (10 total)**, and all other romance candidates are **locked at 4 hearts**.
6. Marrying a **human** spouse can turn them into a **vampire**; there is **no child-rearing feature** in the game.

**Pro tip:** propose on a night when the calendar is clear — if a festival lands on your chosen wedding day you can't simply postpone without re-setting the date, and nobody wants a wedding competing with the town's festival celebrations.

## Divorce via Llemi

Divorce in Moonlight Peaks is deliberately painless:

- A few days after your wedding, **Llemi** (the love demon) visits to tell you about it: "A clean slate. No bonds, no vows, no lingering what-ifs."
- **Visit Llemi's house in the Pink Grove** — a late-game area. Getting there requires unlocking **Luna Bay** (via the Museum and Ludo's **"A Curious Passage"** quest).
- Request the divorce, sleep until the next night, and your ex-spouse returns to **four hearts as a plain friend** — no "hurt" label, no hard feelings, and they'll happily date you again.
- **Remarriage** requires gifting and dating your way back to 8 hearts — marriage locks other candidates at 4 hearts, so divorce *unlocks* them again.
- Llemi also handles **breakups** with dating partners (resetting them to 4 hearts with a temporary "Hurt" status).

There is **no child-rearing feature** in the game — marriage is about your spouse and your life together, not heirs for the family line.

## Romance FAQ

**Can I date same-sex characters?**
Yes — every romanceable character can be dated by any player character, full stop. **LGBTQIA+ friendly** is a core design principle of the game, not a toggle.

**What happens if I date everyone?**
Nothing bad while dating — multiple simultaneous relationships carry no jealousy system. But marriage is exclusive: once you marry, **all other romance candidates are locked at 4 hearts** until you divorce via Llemi.

**Does my appearance affect romance options?**
No. Romance is unrestricted regardless of your character's appearance or identity — including the **Alter Ego Elixir**, which completely changes your appearance, with no effect on your relationships.

**Can I remarry after divorce?**
Yes. Divorce via Llemi leaves you single and free to date and marry again.

**Which character should I marry first?**
If you want the easiest path: **Noel Webb** (loves Mana items like Mana Coffee and Mana Chocolate, plus Golden Egg, Diamond and Common Mushroom), **Evan** (loves the coffee on his own menu), or **Ridge Logan** (loves Beer, Golden Egg, Hamburgers — all buyable or cookable). If you want the story event: pursue **Death** — his introduction follows the "A Bridge Too Far" and Crest Garden quest lines, and his wedding is the most talked-about in the community.
