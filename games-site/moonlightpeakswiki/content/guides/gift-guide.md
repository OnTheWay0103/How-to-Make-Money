# Moonlight Peaks — Gift Guide: Loved Gifts & Birthdays

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Gifting is the fastest way to raise any relationship in Moonlight Peaks. Every resident has **liked gifts** (solid point gains) and **loved gifts** (big point gains), and birthdays multiply the impact of a well-chosen present. This guide lists the loved gifts for every character we've documented, the universal favorites, and where to get everything.

## How Friendship and Gifting Work

- **Relationship points** come from daily conversation and gifts.
- **Liked gifts** grant extra points; **loved gifts** grant significantly more.
- Gift **once per day** per character for best efficiency — most players report that repeat-gifting the same item in one night wastes value.
- A **birthday gift** is the biggest single spike available — combine a loved gift with a birthday for the strongest gains in the game.
- The **Love Potion** is the exception to every rule: when gifted, it raises a resident's affinity **faster than any normal gift**, and it works on everyone.

**Pro tip:** build a two-tier gift economy. Keep a stock of universal loved gifts (Diamond, Golden Egg, Mana Coffee) for characters you haven't memorized, and farm character-specific loves (like **Goliath fish** for Noel) once you're courting someone.

## Universal Good Gifts

These items are loved by a wide swath of residents — a safe bet for anyone:

| Item | Loved by |
|------|----------|
| **Diamond** | Aras, Evan, Ludo, Noel, Orlock, Ridge, Samael, Tae, Winston — essentially everyone documented |
| **Golden Egg** | Aras, Ridge, Samael, Tae, Winston |
| **Mana Coffee / Mana Chocolate** | Sabrina, Noel, Evan |
| **Bloody Mana Mary** | Sabrina, Noel, Orlock |
| **Red Rose** | Orlock, Samael |
| **Moonlight Flowers** (blue/purple) | Aras, Sabrina, Ridge (blue) |

**Diamond** is the closest thing the game has to a universal loved gift — it appears on nearly every character's loved list, so it's the ultimate "don't know what they like" panic present.

## Gifts Fiona Hates (and Why It Matters)

Fiona Webb, the coven leader at Moonlit Pines, **hates honey**. This matters more than you'd think because honey is a common universal "safe" gift in other farming games — it is not one here. Gifting honey to Fiona is an active relationship loss. When in doubt with Fiona, bring **white tulips** or a bouquet.

**Pro tip:** a **Rabbicula from Luna's Farm (2800 Coins)** is one of the best early investments in the friendship economy — its spiral digging mounds yield **Luck Dust**, which doubles as both a Love Potion ingredient and a favored gift for Noel and Sabrina.

## Gift-Giving Tips Summary

1. **Talk first, then gift** — daily conversation points are free; never skip them.
2. **One gift per character per day** — space out the heavy hitters.
3. **Save loved gifts for birthdays** — the birthday multiplier is the biggest spike in the game.
4. **Diamond solves everything** — when unsure, Diamond.
5. **Brew Love Potions** — they outpace every normal gift; see the Potions Guide for the recipe (1x Hold-Me-Close, 1x Muse Nut, 1x Amour, 1x Suffrain Powder, 1x Luck Dust).
6. **Keep junk for Death** — Old Shoe and Rusty Bean Can are genuinely loved.
