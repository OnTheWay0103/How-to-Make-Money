# Moonlight Peaks — Gift Guide: Loved Gifts & Birthdays

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Gifting is the fastest way to raise any relationship in Moonlight Peaks. Every resident has **liked gifts** (solid point gains) and **loved gifts** (big point gains), and birthdays multiply the impact of a well-chosen present. This guide lists the loved gifts for every character, the universal favorites, and where to get everything.

> **Source note:** these gift tables are community-compiled (Pocket Tactics, Game Rant, and other launch-window guides). In-game, the **relationship page shows a star icon** on gifts a character likes/loves — when in doubt, check it before committing an expensive gift.

## How Friendship and Gifting Work

- **Relationship points** come from daily conversation and gifts.
- **Liked gifts** grant extra points; **loved gifts** grant significantly more.
- Gift **once per day** per character for best efficiency — most players report that repeat-gifting the same item in one night wastes value.
- A **birthday gift** is the biggest single spike available — combine a loved gift with a birthday for the strongest gains in the game.
- The **Love Potion** is the exception to every rule: when gifted, it raises a resident's affinity **faster than any normal gift**, and it works on everyone.

**Pro tip:** build a two-tier gift economy. Keep a stock of universal loved gifts (Diamond, Golden Egg, Mana Coffee) for characters you haven't memorized, and farm character-specific loves (like **Goliath fish** for Noel) once you're courting someone.

## Universal Good Gifts

Two items are safe bets for almost everyone:

| Item | Coverage |
|------|----------|
| **Diamond** | Liked by **everyone except Brook, Death, and Pumpkin Head** — and it's a *loved* gift for many (Evan, Orlock, Samael, Ridge, Ludo, Aras, Tae, Winston) |
| **Golden Egg** | Liked by almost everyone (exceptions: Brook, Death, Elvira, Evan, Mina, Orlock, Pumpkin Head, Sabrina, Samael) and *loved* by Aras, Ridge, Tae, Winston, and others |

| Item | Loved by |
|------|----------|
| **Mana Coffee / Mana Chocolate** | Sabrina, Noel, Evan, Fiona |
| **Bloody Mana Mary** | Sabrina, Noel, Orlock, Fiona, Death |
| **Red Rose** | Orlock, Samael |
| **White Tulip** | Fiona |
| **Black Azalea** | Death, Ludo |

**Diamond** is the closest thing the game has to a universal loved gift — it appears on many characters' loved lists and is *liked* by almost everyone else, so it's the ultimate "don't know what they like" panic present. **Trash items** are universally hated — except by **Death, Jada, and Llemi**, who love them.

## Gifts Fiona Hates (and Why It Matters)

Fiona Webb, the coven leader and florist at Moonlit Pines, is picky in specific ways: she **hates seeds of any kind, onions, seaweed, raw copper and ore, conches, insects, and trash**. Several guides list Copper, Seeds, and Onion Seeds as hated — so don't clear your inventory into her hands. When in doubt with Fiona, bring **white tulips** (a loved gift) or any Mana drink.

**Pro tip:** a **Rabbicula from Luna's Farm (2800 Coins)** is one of the best early investments in the friendship economy — its spiral digging mounds yield **Luck Dust**, which doubles as both a Love Potion ingredient and a favored gift for Noel and Sabrina.

## Gift-Giving Tips Summary

1. **Talk first, then gift** — daily conversation points are free; never skip them.
2. **One gift per character per day** — space out the heavy hitters.
3. **Save loved gifts for birthdays** — birthdays (and the Loveage festival) are the biggest spikes in the game.
4. **Diamond solves almost everything** — when unsure, Diamond (it's liked by everyone except Brook, Death, and Pumpkin Head).
5. **Brew Love Potions** — they outpace every normal gift; see the Potions Guide for the recipe (1x Hold-Me-Close, 1x Muse Nut, 1x Amour, 1x Suffrain Powder, 1x Luck Dust). Noel and Sabrina love both Love Potions and Luck Dust.
6. **Keep junk for Death** — Old Shoe, Old Canister, and Rusty Bean Can are genuinely loved by him (and Jada and Llemi).
