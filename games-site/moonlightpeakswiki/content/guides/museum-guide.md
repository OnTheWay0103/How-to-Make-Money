# Moonlight Peaks Museum Guide — All Five Rooms and Every Reward

The Museum is the largest long-term project in Moonlight Peaks, and it is easy to bounce off because the game never explains it in one place. It is not a single collection: it is **five rooms, 210 exhibits**, and a reward track that hands out some of the best blueprints and tools in the game. This guide covers how to unlock it, what each room actually wants, and the fastest path through the reward ladder.

**The short version:** befriend Jada, finish her museum questline, donate one of every eligible item, and collect a milestone reward every 5% (later every 10%) of total completion. The 10% reward alone — the Firefly Pollinator — changes how your farm runs.

## How the Museum Unlocks

The Museum is tied to **Jada Henderson**, not to a calendar date. Community guides place the chain around the start of **Fall in Year One**, after Persephone, Jada, and Winston settle into the abandoned house by the pier, but friendship is the real gate.

The flow looks like this:

1. **Build friendship with Jada.** Talk to her nightly and give gifts; the museum chain does not start until she trusts you.
2. **Complete the first museum quest.** Jada asks you to collect artifacts from four residents: **Brook, Dragan, Fiona, and Orlock**. Brook sends **Saga** in his place. Finish each favor and return the four artifacts to Jada.
3. **Use the scooter museum.** This first version is small but functional — it introduces donations before the full building opens.
4. **Raise Jada's friendship to her first Heart Event.** She then finds a rundown building **south of Town Hall**.
5. **Clean up the park, decorate, and hold the grand opening.** Most invited guests refuse to come; that is scripted, not a failure. After the opening, the full five-room Museum is open.

**Quest-name warning:** some guides list quest titles such as "A New Beginning," "Museum in the Making," and "Curating the Museum." We could not verify those exact names against an official source, so treat them as **[Unconfirmed]**. Follow Jada's dialogue and the objective markers instead of hunting for a specific quest title.

## The Five Rooms and What They Want

Every donation goes into one of five rooms. The room you need is determined by the item, not by your preference — the donation sign in each room only accepts its own collection.

| Room | Collections | Exhibit count | What you donate |
|------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Supernatural** | Witch, Werewolf, Vampire, Seer, Mermaid | 34 (5 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 8) | Family-themed relics, crafted items, foraged flowers, cooked dishes |
| **Deity** | Deity artifacts | 4 | Story-gated artifacts tied to Death, the Moon Goddess, and the Sun God |
| **Farming** | Crops, mushrooms, tree fruit | 55 | One of every harvestable crop across all four seasons |
| **Aquarium** | Fish | 22 | One of every fish species |
| **Critter** | Bugs | 95 | Butterflies, beetles, snails, and other net-caught critters |

That adds up to **210 exhibits**. The Critter Room is the longest checklist by a wide margin; the Farming Room is the longest *sustained* grind because it is season-locked.

### Supernatural Room details

Each family has its own sub-collection, and the items are deliberately mixed across systems:

- **Witch:** Mystic Cauldron, Cosmic Globe, Witch's Hat (dug up), a built Mana Extractor, cooked Googly Soup.
- **Vampire:** Red Wine from the Keg, Bloody Mana Mary, Gothic Mirror, foraged Black Azalea, Bat Skull.
- **Seer:** Moon Crystal from the Crystal Cave, Seer Board, Coil of Fate, Third Eye Hand, Blue Moonlight Flowers, White Roses.
- **Werewolf:** Farmer's Stew, Mana Moon Cupcake, Wolfsbane Powder, and dug items such as Wolf's Pray and the Moonreaver.
- **Mermaid:** Fishy Milkshake, Poseidon's Trident, Dried Seaweed, and dive-only finds such as the Shell Lantern and Seahorse Sculpture.

This is why the Museum feels scattered: it is a cross-section of every system in the game, not a fishing or farming sidequest.

## Donation Rules You Need to Know

Donations are **not** handed to Jada or left at a front desk. The flow is:

1. Enter the **portal in the main room**.
2. Go into the collection room that matches the item.
3. Interact with that collection's **signboard**.

Eligible items are marked with a **museum icon** in your inventory. If the icon is missing, the item is not part of any collection.

The quality rules are where most players waste trips:

- **Farming Room:** crops must be **2-star (silver) quality**. Do not sell your best harvests until you have donated one.
- **Aquarium:** launch-era community guides say fish must be **starless/regular quality**, and some species require a rod upgrade to catch. Patch 1.2.3 then changed the Museum to accept higher-quality items "including fish," so the current rule is **[Unconfirmed]** — check the donation sign. If a starred fish is rejected, keep a starless one.
- **Crafted donations:** some recipes must be made at **2-star** quality.
- **Color variants matter:** the **Fur Chair** must be **Brown**, and the **Aquatic Cabinet** must be **Blue**. A wrong-color version will not count.
- **Artifacts** come from **shiny spiral spots** and **water bubble spots**. They are single-copy finds and stay hidden until discovered.
- **Deity artifacts** are story- and friendship-gated; Llemi starts the relevant discussions after maximum friendship and story progress.

## Every Milestone Reward (5% to 100%)

Rewards trigger at 5% intervals up to 30%, then at 10% intervals. Jada meets you outside the collection rooms when a threshold is reached.

| Total completion | Reward | Why it matters |
|------------------|--------|----------------|
| **5%** | Vampsters Playing Poker painting | Decoration |
| **10%** | Firefly Pollinator + Firefly Sanctuary blueprints | Automated pollination; one of the best early farm unlocks |
| **15%** | Basic Fertilizer x32 | Free crop quality |
| **20%** | Rapid Reel Potion x2 | Fishing QoL |
| **25%** | Dracula Insignia Key | Opens the Twilight Catacombs |
| **30%** | Animal Feeder Temple + Pillow Cat House | Animal QoL and decoration |
| **40%** | Mana Essence x5 | Potion crafting |
| **50%** | Arcane Flame | Permanently raises maximum mana |
| **60%** | Seed Sequencer blueprint | Lets you craft your own seeds |
| **70%** | Mana Potion x3 | Consumable |
| **80%** | Greenhouse unlock (build through Ridge at the Howling Hammer) | Off-season farming |
| **90%** | Museum Relic Replica x13 | Decoration set |
| **100%** | "One Collection to Rule Them All" achievement | Completion proof |

The standout targets are **10%** (Firefly Pollinator), **25%** (Catacombs access), **50%** (permanent mana), **60%** (seed crafting), and **80%** (Greenhouse). Those five rewards are worth planning donations around even if you never intend to finish the Critter Room.

## The Fastest Route Through the Reward Track

You do not need to complete rooms in order, and you do not need to finish one room before starting another. Donate whatever is eligible as soon as you have it, then steer your play toward the next threshold.

**Priority order we recommend:**

1. **Rush 10%.** Donate every early crop, common fish, and easy critter you own. The Firefly Pollinator blueprint is the single highest-value early reward.
2. **Bank 2-star crops.** Once the Farming Room opens, set aside silver-quality harvests instead of selling them. This is the slowest collection to backfill because of season locks.
3. **Donate artifacts immediately.** Supernatural and Deity artifacts are one-copy finds; selling one by mistake can delay a room for an entire playthrough.
4. **Carry a net and check critter spawns nightly.** The 95-critter room looks intimidating, but it fills passively if you catch while foraging.
5. **Fish for the Aquarium on dedicated nights.** Use the [fishing guide](/guides/fishing-guide) for locations; do not donate your only Goliath if you still need it for a quest — check the icon first.
6. **Target 25% before the Catacombs.** The Dracula Insignia Key is required content access, not just a collectible.
7. **Push 50% before late-game mana upgrades.** The Arcane Flame is a permanent stat increase and cannot be bought cheaply elsewhere.

**Pro tip:** treat the Museum as a second Almanac. Every time you harvest, catch, dig, or cook something new, check for the museum icon before selling. The donation is almost always worth more long-term than the coin stack.

## What Patch 1.2.3 Changed

Patch 1.2.3 (August 20, 2026) made two museum-relevant changes:

- The Museum now accepts **higher-quality items, including fish**.
- The Museum, Player House, and Henderson House received distinct **map visuals** until repaired, and a soft lock in the **critter room** was fixed.

The quality change is good news for players who upgraded their rods or grew silver crops before donating. It does not remove the 2-star crop requirement for the Farming Room — that rule is separate and still reported by community guides.

## Common Mistakes

- **Selling your first artifact.** Artifacts are one-copy finds. Donate first, sell duplicates only if the game allows it.
- **Donating a wrong-color furniture variant.** Brown Fur Chair and Blue Aquatic Cabinet are specific.
- **Assuming the Museum is one room.** The portal and signboards are the system; Jada is not the donation point.
- **Ignoring the 10% reward.** The Firefly Pollinator is easy to miss and transforms crop management.
- **Waiting for Year Two.** The chain is friendship-gated, not calendar-gated; start gifting Jada early.

## FAQ

**Do I need to finish one room to unlock the next?** No. All five rooms are part of the same completion percentage, and you can donate to any open room.

**Does the Museum accept starred fish now?** Patch 1.2.3 says higher-quality items including fish are accepted, but launch guides still describe starless fish. Check the sign; the exact current behavior is **[Unconfirmed]**.

**What is the total number of exhibits?** 210 across the five rooms, based on the collection counts above.

**Is the 100% reward worth it?** The achievement is the only reward at 100%. The practical value is in the 10%-80% milestones, especially the Firefly Pollinator, Arcane Flame, Seed Sequencer, and Greenhouse.

## Final Verdict

The Museum is the best long-term money-and-time sink in Moonlight Peaks because it pays you back in permanent tools rather than coins. Unlock it through Jada, donate everything with a museum icon, and aim for 10% first. After that, let the 2-star crops and critter catches accumulate while you chase the 25% key and the 50% mana upgrade. The Critter Room can wait; the rewards cannot.
