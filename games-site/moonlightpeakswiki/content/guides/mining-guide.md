# Moonlight Peaks — Mining Guide: Cave of Echoes, Copper & Furnaces

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Mining in Moonlight Peaks is how you feed the game's crafting backbone. Everything from tools to quest progression runs through **Copper Ore** and the smelted **Copper Bar** — including a story quest that literally demands one. This guide covers unlocking the **Cave of Echoes**, finding copper, smelting bars, and what to spend them on.

## Unlocking the Cave of Echoes

The first mine isn't available at the start of the game. Here's the unlock path:

1. **Complete "A Bridge Too Far"** — talk to **Fiona Webb at Moonlit Pines** and lift the curse on the bridge.
2. The bridge unlocks the **south map** (Misty Shores, Moonlight Tower, Ambrosia Graveyard, Ambrosia Mansion).
3. The quest **reopens the estate** that contains the mine entrance — the **Cave of Echoes**.

| Unlock step | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| Accept "A Bridge Too Far" | Quest progress |
| Lift the bridge curse | South map opens |
| Estate reopened | **Cave of Echoes** entrance accessible |

**Pro tip:** if you haven't reached the Cave of Echoes, you're not behind — you're just pre-bridge. The mine, the furnace economy, and Death's quest line all sit behind the same quest, so finishing it early pays off in every direction at once.

## What You're Mining For: Copper Ore

- **Copper Ore** comes from **brown-tinted rocks**.
- Mine them with a **pickaxe**.
- Brown-tinted rocks are **commonly found in mines** — the Cave of Echoes is the reliable source, though scattered brown rocks can also show up elsewhere in the world.
- Not every rock is a copper rock: look for the **brown tint** specifically. Grey and dark rocks yield different materials or nothing.

| Rock type | Yield |
|-----------|-------|
| **Brown-tinted rock** | **Copper Ore** (with a pickaxe) |
| Other rocks | Various materials / stone |

**Pro tip:** carry a pickaxe on every mine trip, and don't waste stamina swinging at every rock — the brown-tinted ones are your copper income, and mining is stamina-hungry in the early game.

## Smelting Copper Bars

Copper Ore alone isn't what crafting wants — you need **Copper Bars**, and that means a **furnace**.

| Recipe | Ingredients | Result |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Copper Bar** | **4 Copper Ore + 1 Charcoal** | 1 Copper Bar |

1. Gather **4 Copper Ore** from brown-tinted rocks.
2. Source **1 Charcoal**.
3. Put both in a **furnace**.
4. Wait for the smelt to finish — bars take time in the furnace, so start smelts early in the night and collect before dawn.

**Pro tip:** smelt in batches. One Copper Bar per ore-run means constant furnace babysitting; stock 8–12 ore and smelt three bars at once so your furnace work is front-loaded and your nights stay free for everything else.

## What Copper Bars Are Used For

Copper bars are one of the most-used resources in the game. Known uses:

| Use | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **Crafting** | Copper bars are used **regularly in crafting** — tools, upgrades, and equipment |
| **Story quests** | The **Crest Garden quest** requires a Copper Bar: **Yabbis the frog** swallowed the Logan Crest and demands a Copper Bar to return it |
| **Tool/estate upgrades** | Bars feed the upgrade economy (upgrade costs are famously steep in this game) |

**Pro tip:** the Yabbis demand in **"Tension in the Crest Garden / A Croak and A Crest"** is a hard requirement — you cannot skip it. Keep one spare Copper Bar in storage at all times once you have the furnace going; the questline does not warn you well before it asks.

## What Else Lives in the Mine

Copper is the headline, but the Cave of Echoes pays out in other ways too:

| Material | Why it matters |
|----------|----------------|
| **Copper Ore** | The main event — smelt into bars |
| **Stone and rock drops** | Feed the furnace economy (Charcoal production) and basic crafting |
| **Rarer node finds** | Deeper cave areas hold better materials — progression rewards exploration |
| **Cave loot** | Chests and deposits can hold crafting materials that skip whole production steps |

**Pro tip:** mine with a goal, not a mood. If you need bars for the Yabbis quest, sweep the copper rocks on the accessible floors and leave. If you're upgrading tools, go deeper — the good material is past the first floor's brown-tinted rocks.

## The Copper Economy

Copper bars are the game's most-traded early material, and their economy works like this:

```
Copper Ore (brown rocks) → furnace (4 Ore + 1 Charcoal) → Copper Bar
                                                              │
        ┌───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
        ▼                               ▼
   Crafting & upgrades            Quest demands (Yabbis)
        │
        ▼
  Surplus bars → sell for coins
```

- **Crafting consumes bars regularly** — every tool and many workshop recipes want them, so demand never disappears.
- **The Yabbis quest demands one bar outright** — "A Croak and A Crest" requires delivering a Copper Bar to the frog before the Logan Crest comes back.
- **Surplus is sellable** — but only after your own crafting needs are covered; buying bars back later at a markup is the classic mining mistake.

**Pro tip:** track your bar stock like a budget. One bar for Yabbis, a few for the next upgrade tier, and only then consider selling. The furnace runs hot in this game — bars are inventory, not just money.

## Stamina and Mining Tips

- **Mining is stamina-heavy.** A full mine run can drain most of your early-game bar — bring cooked food.
- **Prioritize copper rocks.** In a new mine level, clear the brown-tinted rocks first; they're your actual income.
- **Don't ignore other nodes entirely** — non-copper materials feed the furnace (Charcoal) and crafting, but copper is the reason you're down there.
- **Exit before dawn.** You get returned to your coffin at sunrise regardless, so time your runs to finish at the mouth of the cave, not mid-shift.

**Pro tip:** the classic early-game mistake is mining until your stamina hits zero, then realizing you have no energy to cook or smelt. Stop at roughly a third of your bar, run home, smelt what you have, and bank the rest of the night for other systems.

## Mining FAQ

**When can I mine?**
After **"A Bridge Too Far"** reopens the estate containing the **Cave of Echoes** entrance. Before that, your only rocks are what you find in the overworld.

**Where do I find Copper Ore?**
**Brown-tinted rocks** mined with a **pickaxe**, commonly in mines — the Cave of Echoes is the main source.

**How do I make a Copper Bar?**
**4 Copper Ore + 1 Charcoal** in a **furnace**.

**Why does everyone keep asking for copper?**
Copper bars are used **regularly in crafting**, appear in story quests (Yabbis the frog demands one), and feed the upgrade economy. It's the game's most important early material.
