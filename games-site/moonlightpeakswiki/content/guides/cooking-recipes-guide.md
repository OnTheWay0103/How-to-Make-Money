# Moonlight Peaks — Cooking & Recipes Guide: Stamina, Mana & Money Dishes

> Launch Version / Updated: August 8, 2026

Cooking is Moonlight Peaks' workhorse system: it restores **Stamina** and **Mana**, earns coins, wins festivals, and feeds romance. You start with a Cooking Station in your home and 16 basic recipes — the rest comes from cookbooks. This guide covers how cooking works, where recipes come from, and which dishes are actually worth making.

## How Cooking Works

- **Cooking Station** — you start with one at home; each dish is made one at a time (no bulk cooking)
- **Quality mini-game** — select a recipe, choose desired quality, then hit the button when the puck is in the red zone
- **Star quality** — dishes come in No Star, 1 Star, and 2 Star versions; higher stars scale **Stamina restored and sale price** (Mana values stay fixed)
- **The Keg** — drinks, wines, and alcohols are made here instead of the Cooking Station

## Recipe Families

- **Juices** — typically 3x fruit: Blackberry, Blood Orange, Blueberry, Cranberry, Dark Strawberry, Blood Grape, White Grape, Lemon (3x Lemons), Melon, Moon (3x Moonfruit), Raspberry, Strawberry, Black Sun
- **Milkshakes** — all need Milk from farm livestock: Chocolate (Cowcula Milk), Fairy (Draculamb Milk + Lavender Powder), Fishy (Piggoat Milk), Mom's (Blood Orange + Draculamb Milk), plus Blueberry, Moon, Strawberry, Void
- **Pizzas** — Margherita (Blood Tomato, Flour, Cheese), Puk's (adds Onion), Angry (adds Angry Mandrake)
- **Soups & Stews** — Carrot (Carrot + Henbane Powder), Eggplant, Fishy (Glibby + Orbis), Googly, Mushroom (2x Common Mushroom + 2x Yellow Glowglammer), Potato, Mushroom Stew, Farmer's Stew (2x Blood Tomato + Eggplant)
- **Other notables** — Pancakes (1x Egg), Pumpkin Pie (Pumpkin + Egg), Fried Egg (1x Egg), Onigiri (Whisper + Rice), Crimson Pudding (Strawberry + Raspberry + Cowcula Milk), Pink Wedding Cake (Hold-Me-Close)

**Coffee Powder** is made by milling 2x Dried Coffee Beans in a **Mill** (8,000 Coins from Ridge; Coffee Bean Tree seeds 4,500 from Luna) — the base for the whole coffee family.
