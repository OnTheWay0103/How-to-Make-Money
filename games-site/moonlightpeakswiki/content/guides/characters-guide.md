# Moonlight Peaks — Characters Guide: NPCs & the Seven Families

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Moonlight Peaks is a town story, and the town runs on its residents. Every character belongs to one of the **seven families** — Dracula, Ambrosia, Logan, Webb, Khazan, Horseus, and Henderson — with a handful of standalone characters (Death, Sabrina, Llemi, and others) beyond the family structure. This guide maps the families, the notable NPCs, and the shops that keep the town running.

## The Seven Families

| Family | Who we know | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Dracula** | **Count Dracula** (your father), **Viktor** (old relative turned into a bat, lives in a crypt) | The family you're born into — you've left the castle to build your own life |
| **Ambrosia** | **Orlock, Mina, Evan, Samael, Elvira, Vlad** | Vampires, town commerce: Orlock is the family head, Mina and Evan co-run **Coffee & Coffins**, Samael runs the **The Broken Lamp** tavern, Elvira is the treasure-hunting artist, Vlad the protective father figure |
| **Logan** | **Ridge, Ludo, Saga, Brook** | Werewolf family: Ridge is the carpenter/blacksmith, Ludo the laid-back musician, Saga keeps the town running, Brook is the town mayor |
| **Webb** | **Fiona, Noel, Sabrina, Luna** | The coven: Fiona leads at Moonlit Pines and runs the flower shop, Noel is the flirt who works the forge, Sabrina runs **Web of Wonders**, Luna is the sea witch with plant stalls and cursed livestock |
| **Khazan** | **Dragan, Alina, Aras** | Seers: Dragan invented the card game **Nokturna**, Alina is the fiery truth-teller, Aras is the tailor at **Third Eye Threads** |
| **Horseus** | **Kim, Tae, Ray** | **Mermaids/mermen** who arrive in town later in Year 1; Tae lives in Luna Bay |
| **Henderson** | **Persephone, Jada, Winston** | The **human** family — they move in mid-year; Persephone loves astrology, Jada collects relics, Winston is the town skeptic |

The **secrets of the seven families** are a story thread running through the whole game — the quests, the graveyard cutscenes, and the town's history all connect back to who these families are and what they've done.

**Pro tip:** the families are also a romance map — **23 residents are dateable across the seven families** plus standalones, with no gender restrictions anywhere. See the Romance Guide for the full courting breakdown.

## Who to Talk to When

A quick-reference table for the quest-gated and system-gated NPCs:

| Goal | NPC to find | When |
|------|-------------|------|
| Farming intro (Blood Grapes / Blood Wine) | **Orlock Ambrosia** | Night 1–2 (his "Wine Scheme" quest) |
| Cauldron + potions | Cauldron arrives from your **mother** → **Fiona** (repair, 2 Mana Essences) | As soon as "Mend it with Magic" appears |
| South map unlock | **Orlock** (bar tab) → **Mina** → **Fiona** → **Noel, Luna, Sabrina** | "A Bridge Too Far" — Day 11 or so |
| Death | Introduces himself in town early; story intro in the **Ambrosia Graveyard** | After the Crest Garden quest |
| Crest Garden mystery | **Alina, Aras, Dragan** → meeting at **The Broken Lamp** | Talk to all three to trigger |
| Howling Marshes search | **Ludo** and **Saga** | During the crest quest |
| Fishing rod | **Noel Webb** | Night 2–3, near Moonlit Pines |
| Potion recipes | **Sabrina** at the **Web of Wonders** | Whenever her stock refreshes |
| Tool upgrades | **Ridge Logan** at the **Howling Hammer** | Open 18:00–00:00 |
| Divorce / breakups | **Llemi** (Pink Grove) | After marriage; needs Luna Bay unlocked |
| Luck Dust | **Luna's Farm** (Rabbicula, 2800 Coins) | After you can afford it |

**Pro tip:** the game never holds your hand on trigger conditions — quests like the Crest Garden chain start only after you've *talked* to the right people. When stuck, the answer is almost always "talk to the characters whose names you saw in the quest description."

**Pro tip:** memorize the shop loop: **Coffee & Coffins** for coffee gifts, **Web of Wonders** for potion recipes and Sabrina's loves, **Luna's Farm** for seeds and the Rabbicula that powers Luck Dust. That triangle covers the town's economy almost by itself.

## Characters FAQ

**Who is the player character?**
The **child of Count Dracula**, who moves to Moonlight Peaks to build a wholesome life away from the family castle. Your appearance is fully customizable (and the **Alter Ego Elixir** can change it completely later).

**Are all characters romanceable?**
Not all — but **23 of them are**, spanning every family, with no gender restrictions.

**Where do I find Death?**
Death's house is by the **docks in the center of town**, and he introduces himself early in the story (he gives you the Net for catching Soul Blobs). His main story introduction happens at the **Ambrosia Graveyard**, which requires completing **"A Bridge Too Far"** and the Crest Garden quest line.

**Who should I befriend first for gameplay?**
**Orlock** (farming intro), **Noel** (fishing rod), **Fiona** (cauldron repair + quests), **Sabrina** (potion recipes), and **Luna** (seeds + the Rabbicula for Luck Dust). Those five unlock most of the game's systems.
