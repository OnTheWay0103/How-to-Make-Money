# Moonlight Peaks — Characters Guide: NPCs & the Seven Families

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Moonlight Peaks is a town story, and the town runs on its residents. Every character belongs to one of the **seven families** — Dracula, Ambrosia, Logan, Webb, Khazan, Horseus, and Henderson — with a handful of standalone characters (Death, Sabrina, Tae Hosu, and others) beyond the family structure. This guide maps the families, the notable NPCs, and the shops that keep the town running.

## The Seven Families

| Family | Who we know | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Dracula** | **Count Dracula** (your father) | The family you're born into — you've left the castle to build your own life |
| **Ambrosia** | **Orlock, Evan, Samael** | Vampires, town commerce: Orlock gives the farming quests, Evan runs Coffee & Coffins, Samael runs the bar |
| **Logan** | **Ridge, Ludo** | Werewolf family; Ridge is the carpenter, Ludo teams with you on the Crest Garden quest |
| **Webb** | **Fiona, Noel** | The coven: Fiona leads at Moonlit Pines, Noel is the warlock with a Mana-food obsession |
| **Khazan** | **Aras** | His loved-gift list (soups, stews, Beret, Diamond) marks him as one of the town's food-and-finery characters |
| **Horseus** | — | One of the seven families; full member roster still being documented |
| **Henderson** | **Winston** | The **human** family — Winston proves humans and supernaturals share the town |

The **secrets of the seven families** are a story thread running through the whole game — the quests, the graveyard cutscenes, and the town's history all connect back to who these families are and what they've done.

**Pro tip:** the families are also a romance map — **~24 residents are dateable across the seven families**, with no gender restrictions anywhere. See the Romance Guide for the full courting breakdown.

## Who to Talk to When

A quick-reference table for the quest-gated and system-gated NPCs:

| Goal | NPC to find | When |
|------|-------------|------|
| Farming intro (Blood Grapes / Blood Wine) | **Orlock Ambrosia** | Night 1–2 |
| Cauldron + potions | **Viktor** (delivers cauldron) → **Fiona** | As soon as "Mend it with Magic" appears |
| South map unlock | **Fiona Webb** at **Moonlit Pines** | Early priority |
| Death's quest line | **Fiona** (bridge) → then the south map | After "A Bridge Too Far" |
| Crest Garden mystery | **Alina, Aras, Dragan** → meeting at **The Broken Lamp Inn** | Talk to all three to trigger |
| Howling Marshes search | **Ludo** and **Saga** | During the crest quest |
| Potion recipes | **Sabrina** at the **Web of Wonders** | Whenever her stock refreshes |
| Divorce | **Llemi** | Whenever you need it |
| Luck Dust | **Luna's Farm** (Rabbicula) | After your first 2800 Coins |

**Pro tip:** the game never holds your hand on trigger conditions — quests like the Crest Garden chain start only after you've *talked* to the right people. When stuck, the answer is almost always "talk to the characters whose names you saw in the quest description."

## Characters FAQ

**Who is the player character?**
The **child of Count Dracula**, who moves to Moonlight Peaks to build a wholesome life away from the family castle. Your appearance is fully customizable (and the **Alter Ego Elixir** can change it completely later).

**Are all characters romanceable?**
Not all — but **~24 of them are**, spanning every family, with no gender restrictions.

**Where do I find Death?**
On the **south map**, which requires completing **"A Bridge Too Far"** (talk to **Fiona Webb at Moonlit Pines**).

**Who should I befriend first for gameplay?**
**Fiona** (quests + cauldron repair), **Orlock** (farming intro), **Sabrina** (potion recipes), and **Luna's Farm** (Rabbicula for Luck Dust). Those four unlock most of the game's systems.
