# Moonlight Peaks — Potions Guide: Cauldron, Recipes & Ingredients

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Potion brewing is one of Moonlight Peaks' most rewarding systems — it feeds your money economy, your romance, and your character customization all at once. This guide covers how to unlock the **cauldron**, every recipe we've confirmed, where ingredients come from (including **Luck Dust**), and how brewing time and selling values work.

## Unlocking the Cauldron: "Mend it with Magic"

The entire potion system hangs off one quest:

1. **Accept "Mend it with Magic"** — available early in your story progression.
2. **Your mother's broken cauldron arrives** at your front door — Viktor, an old relative, points out it broke during travel.
3. **Bring Fiona two Mana Essences** — Fiona, the coven leader at **Moonlit Pines**, recognizes it as your mother's cauldron and repairs it.
4. **Craft Mana Essence** at the **Mana Extractor** (a station unlocked by helping Sabrina) — it extracts essence from **magical crops**.

From that point you can brew at your cauldron at home — but you must **purchase recipes from Sabrina** at the **Web of Wonders** magic shop first (you can't just input random ingredients). Check her stock regularly, because the **Love Potion recipe (4,000 Coins)** is one of the best purchases in the game.

**Pro tip:** don't stall this quest. It unlocks with early story, and the payoff — a functioning cauldron — is the single biggest early-game money multiplier available.

## How Brewing Works

- Brewing happens at your **cauldron** (home) with the ingredients listed on the recipe.
- **Potions take time to brew** — like juice and wine, they aren't instant. Start a brew early in the night and collect the finished potion later in the same night (or the next night).
- Recipes are one-use-per-ingredient-set: you need the full ingredient list every time you brew.
- The cauldron blueprint from Fiona lets you craft the cauldron itself; recipes give you what to put in it.

**Pro tip:** treat the cauldron like a night-time production line. Set a brew going the moment you wake, do your farming and chores, then collect the finished potion before you hit the shops. Dead brewing time is wasted money.

**Batch brewing:** because brews take time to finish, the cauldron pays you for forethought — prep ingredient sets for several batches ahead of time (a chest drawer per recipe works well). When one brew finishes, the next is one interaction away, so the cauldron is never idle during the night hours.

## Known Recipes

### Alter Ego Elixir

- **Effect:** completely changes your character's appearance — a full re-roll.
- **Recipe (reported):** **1x Quartz Dust, 1x Nightshade Powder, 1x Honey**; the recipe itself sells for around **1,300 Coins** at the magic shop, and the elixir sells for about **1,000 Coins**.
- **Source:** available from **Sabrina at the Web of Wonders** (she sells the elixir; whether Fiona hands you this exact recipe first is reported differently by different guides).
- **Notes:** the effect is cosmetic and total. It has **no effect on relationships** — romance is unrestricted regardless of appearance, so use it freely. (Some guides list it as a gift Sabrina enjoys, but that's unconfirmed.)

### Love Potion

| Ingredient | Qty | Source |
|------------|-----|--------|
| **Hold-Me-Close** | 1 | A rare magical **plant** — matures in **13 days** |
| **Muse Nut** | 1 | Foraged |
| **Amour** | 1 | Foraged flower / plant |
| **Suffrain Powder** | 1 | Processed from **Suffrain**, a regular **autumn crop** (7 days) |
| **Luck Dust** | 1 | **Dig spiral spots where fed Rabbiculas have left mounds** |

- **Effect:** when **gifted to a resident, gives a bonus to friendship/romance affinity** — the best romance accelerant in the game.
- **Sells for:** **2200 Coins**.
- **Bonus:** the Love Potion is a **favored gift for Noel and Sabrina** — combined with Luck Dust, their affinity can grow at "immense speeds".

**Pro tip:** the Love Potion is the rare item that's simultaneously the best romance accelerant, a 2200 Coin sell, and a loved gift. Once you can reliably source **Luck Dust**, brew Love Potions every night and route them toward whichever goal you need: coins, a partner, or Sabrina's friendship.

## Luck Dust: The Critical Ingredient

**Luck Dust** is the ingredient that gates your Love Potion production, and it has a very specific source:

1. **Buy a Rabbicula from Luna's Farm** — **2800 Coins** (resells for **2100 Coins**).
2. **Feed your Rabbicula daily** — Luck Dust only seems to appear when it's well fed.
3. **Dig the spiral spots** where it leaves mounds — but each mound it makes takes **one of your crops** as compensation, so keep it snacking on something you can spare.
4. **Harvest Luck Dust** from the dig sites.

| Fact | Value |
|------|-------|
| Rabbicula price | **2800 Coins** at Luna's Farm |
| Mound mechanic | Fed Rabbiculas occasionally leave mounds; dig the **spiral spots** |
| Catch | Each mound costs you **one crop** — keep it fed and happy |
| Use | Love Potion ingredient |
| Gifting | **Favored gift for Noel and Sabrina** |

**Pro tip:** the Rabbicula is an investment, not an expense — 2800 Coins buys a Luck Dust source that fuels both your potion income (Love Potions at 2200 Coins each) and your best friendship farming (Noel and Sabrina both love Luck Dust). Two Love Potions pay the Rabbicula back.

**Pro tip:** if your goal is coins, potions out-earn raw crops in most cases — a Love Potion's 2200 Coins beats a stack of early-game vegetables, and it takes the same hands-off brewing time as wine. See the Money Guide for the full ranking.

## Potion FAQ

**When do I get the cauldron?**
Through **"Mend it with Magic"** — your mother's broken cauldron arrives at your front door (Viktor points out it broke in transit), and Fiona repairs it after you bring her **2 Mana Essences** (crafted at the Mana Extractor from magical crops).

**Where do I get new recipes?**
**Sabrina at the Web of Wonders** magic shop sells extra recipes. Check her inventory regularly.

**How long does brewing take?**
Potions take time to brew, like **juice and wine** — set them going early and collect later.

**Can I change my appearance back?**
The **Alter Ego Elixir** changes your appearance completely; brewing another lets you re-roll again. Your relationships are unaffected either way.
