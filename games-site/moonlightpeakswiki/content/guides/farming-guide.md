# Moonlight Peaks — Farming Guide: Crops & Moonlight Mechanics

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Farming in Moonlight Peaks is the game's foundation — and it runs on a rule no other farming sim teaches you: **crops grow under moonlight, not sunlight**. Every seed you plant grows during the night hours, and because you're returned to your coffin at sunrise, the night IS your growing season. This guide covers the core mechanics, **Blood Grapes**, mystical crops, watering, harvest timing, and which crops are actually worth your stamina.

## The Night Farming Rule

- **All farming happens at night.** You wake at dusk, water and harvest through the night, and are automatically returned to your coffin at sunrise.
- **Crops grow while you sleep the day away** — the day passes instantly, and your fields advance their growth cycle during it.
- The practical upshot: **plant late-night, harvest next dusk**. A crop planted tonight is ready in a fixed number of nights regardless of what you do during the day, because the day is a cutscene, not gameplay time.

**Pro tip:** the day-night split makes Moonlight Peaks farming more forgiving than it looks. You can't "waste a day" — every day is just a growth tick. The real constraint is your **stamina** budget at night, not calendar days.

## Soil, Watering and Harvesting

| Action | What it does | Stamina note |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Tilling** | Prepares soil for seeds | Costs stamina |
| **Planting** | Places seeds in tilled soil | Cheap |
| **Watering** | Required for growth each night (and over the day cycle) | One of your biggest **stamina drains** in the early game |
| **Harvesting** | Collects mature crops | Moderate stamina per tile |
| **Processing** | Turns crops into products (e.g. **Blood Grapes → Blood Wine**) | Uses cauldron/processing equipment, takes time like juice and wine |

**Pro tip:** water in the evening right after dusk, harvest what's mature first, then plant — the order matters because every unwatered crop that "sleeps" through the day costs you a growth tick. Mature crops that sit unharvested also stop the growth clock on that tile.

## Blood Grapes and Blood Wine

**Blood Grapes** are your introduction to the whole system — **Orlock's Wine Scheme** quest hands you your first Blood Grape seeds and a shovel on Spring 1, Year 1:

| Step | Task | Detail |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | Accept Orlock's Wine Scheme | Grow grapes, make wine |
| 2 | Plant **Blood Grapes** | Spring/Summer crop; seeds are 20 Coins at Luna's Seed Stall in Moonlit Pines |
| 3 | Water through the growth cycle | Grapes mature in **5 nights**, then **regrow every 4 nights** — a multi-harvest crop |
| 4 | Harvest | Collect the grape bunches |
| 5 | Process into **Blood Grape Wine** | Put grapes in a **Keg** (crafted from 10 Wood) |
| 6 | Deliver wine to Orlock | Quest complete |

**Pro tip:** raw Blood Grapes sell for only **5 Coins** — never sell them unprocessed. The wine sells for **360 Coins** (390 for one-star quality), which makes grapes one of the best early money-makers. **White Grapes** work identically (same 20 Coins / 5+4 nights) and their wine sells for even more: **460 Coins** (490 one-star). Wine is also a loved gift for **Orlock and Samael**, and **Mana Wine** is a favorite of Noel's.

## Mystical Crops and the Moonlight Bonus

Beyond mundane vegetables, Moonlight Peaks offers **magic crops** — seeds that lean into the game's supernatural theme. What we know about how they work:

- **Magic seeds are watered with the Aquaflux Spell** instead of a watering can. Because the spell costs **mana** (your pool is small — reported max 3), keep magic plots small.
- The payoff is big: **Nightshade** and **Skunktail** sell for **180 Coins** and **Gobbler** for **550 Coins** — far above mundane crops.
- **Moonlight Flowers** (blue and purple variants) are a real flower family — **Purple Moonlight Flower** is a loved gift for **Aras**, **Blue Moonlight Flower** for **Aras and Ridge** (gift lists are community-compiled; confirm with the relationship page).
- **Amour, Hold-Me-Close, and Muse Nut** feed directly into the **Love Potion** recipe — Hold-Me-Close is a rare magical plant (matures in **13 days**), and **Suffrain** (a regular autumn crop, 7 days) becomes **Suffrain Powder** (see the Potions Guide).

**Pro tip:** magic plots are a mid-game income spike, not an early-game habit. Until your mana pool grows, spend your mana on quests and keep magic fields to a few tiles.

## Stamina Management for Farmers

The early game's **restrictive stamina** is the main farming bottleneck — here's how to farm without running dry:

1. **Cook food.** Dishes restore stamina and convert foraged scraps into fuel for your fields.
2. **Prioritize watering over everything.** An unwatered crop costs you a full growth tick; a skipped forage trip costs nothing.
3. **Scale your field to your stamina**, not your ambitions. A field you can't fully water is a field that's losing value every night.
4. **Upgrade tools first.** Tool upgrades reduce stamina per tile and are the highest-value purchase in the game, ahead of cosmetics (upgrade costs are notoriously high, so spend wisely).
5. **Don't be afraid to leave some soil fallow** on busy nights — missed growth ticks are recoverable; a bone-dry stamina bar is not.

## Farming FAQ

**Can crops grow during the day?**
Yes — the day passes instantly while you're in your coffin, and growth ticks happen across it. You only *work* the fields at night.

**What happens if I don't water?**
The crop misses a growth tick and delays its harvest. Severe neglect can dry out the soil entirely.

**What should I grow first?**
**Blood Grapes** (quest + wine), then a small mix of everyday vegetables for cooking, then potion ingredients once the cauldron unlocks.

**Are mystical crops better than normal crops?**
Usually — but only if you use them correctly. Mystical crops shine as **gift and potion ingredients**, not as raw sales.
