# Moonlight Peaks — Fishing Guide: Spots, Bait & Fish Uses

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Fishing in Moonlight Peaks is the quiet workhorse of the early game — low risk, decent coins, and fish that double as gifts and ingredients. Like everything else, it happens at night: rivers and shores are at their liveliest after dusk, and your catch list shifts as you unlock new areas. This guide covers where to fish, how bait works, and what to actually do with your catch.

## Where to Fish

Fishing spots in Moonlight Peaks are water-dependent. The two reliable categories:

| Location | Availability | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **Rivers** | From the start | Town river spots are the first fish you'll ever catch |
| **Misty Shores** | After **"A Bridge Too Far"** | Coastal water; the more interesting catch list, including **Goliath fish** territory |

**Pro tip:** the river spots are for stamina-to-coins conversion early on; the **Misty Shores** catches are for gifts and cooking. If you're fishing for money, rivers are fine — if you're fishing for Noel's favor, get the bridge quest done and head south.

## How Bait Works

- **Bait** improves your odds and unlocks better catches — fishing without bait works, but bait upgrades your pool of available fish.
- Different spots and water types respond to different bait; if a cast comes up empty or "nibbles only," try switching bait.
- Bait is cheap to source and craft — stock up before long sessions so you're not burning night hours traveling back and forth.

**Pro tip:** keep two bait types in your inventory at all times. One for rivers, one for coastal water — that covers every spot you can reach in the early and mid game.

## Gear and Progression

Fishing has a light gear track that follows the same upgrade economy as everything else in Moonlight Peaks:

| Upgrade | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Rod upgrades** | Better rods cast further, reach better spots, and reduce the stamina cost of each cast |
| **Bait quality** | Higher-tier bait opens the rarer catches at existing spots |
| **Skill practice** | Catch volume matters — the more fish you land, the better your catch rate on the same spots |

**Pro tip:** upgrade the rod before the bait. A better rod touches every catch you'll ever make; better bait only touches the spots you're already standing at. And since upgrade costs are notoriously high, tools-first is the rule.

## Fish by Location at a Glance

| Location | What it gives | Best use |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| **River spots** | Volume catches, early-game coins | Money and cooking basics |
| **Misty Shores** | The notable catches — **Goliath fish** and coastal species | Gifts and Mana dishes (Noel's loves) |
| **Night-hour variation** | Some spots fish better at specific night hours | Rotate spots across a session rather than standing still |

**Pro tip:** don't chain one spot all night. Moonlight Peaks' waters shift with the night hours — a river that's quiet pre-midnight can be busy after it. Cover two or three spots per session.

## What Fish Are Good For

Fish are three things in Moonlight Peaks: money, food, and gifts. The uses ladder:

| Use | Detail | Fish that matter |
|-----|--------|------------------|
| **Gifts** | Several residents love specific fish | **Goliath fish** — loved by **Noel Webb** |
| **Cooking** | Fish become ingredients for cooked dishes | **Twilight Mana Maki** and **Fishy Mana Quiche** (both loved by Noel) need fish |
| **Selling** | Raw fish sells; cooked fish sells for more | Common catches, early-game coins |

**Pro tip:** the **Goliath fish is the standout catch** of the game's gift economy — it's Noel Webb's loved gift and fish-driven cooking (Fishy Mana Quiche, Twilight Mana Maki) feeds his entire loved-gift list. Every Goliath you catch is effectively a relationship point bank deposit.

## Best Fish to Keep

Based on early-game reports, the practical ranking:

| Catch | Keep for | Sell? |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Goliath fish** | Noel's gifts + Mana dishes | Never sell raw — gift or cook it |
| **Cooking fish** (any fish that shows an ingredient tag) | Meals, stamina recovery, Mana dishes | Cook before selling |
| **Common river fish** | Immediate early-game income | Sell or cook |

**Pro tip:** never sell a fish raw if you can cook it first — the cooked version sells for more and the same fish might be a loved ingredient for Noel's dishes. Process, then sell.

## The Goliath Fish Economy

**Goliath fish** deserve their own callout because they're the rare catch that sits at the center of three systems at once:

1. **Gifting** — the Goliath fish is a **loved gift for Noel Webb**, the strongest single-fish relationship item we've documented.
2. **Cooking** — Goliath and other quality fish feed the dishes Noel loves: **Fishy Mana Quiche** and **Twilight Mana Maki** both need fish, and both are loved gifts for him.
3. **Selling** — a cooked, giftable fish outsells a raw one every time; keep one or two for Noel and cook the surplus for the stall.

**Pro tip:** every Goliath fish has a "default destination" in your inventory: gifts for Noel first, Mana dishes second, the stall last. That order converts one catch into relationship points, cooking XP, and coins — in that priority, never reversed.

## Fishing Tips

1. **Fish after your chores.** Fishing is a stamina-tidy activity — do it after watering and mining, when you still have enough bar for casts but not enough for heavy work.
2. **Time your runs.** Start a long fishing session early enough to walk back before sunrise; the auto-coffin return doesn't care how deep you are in a bite.
3. **Unlock Misty Shores early.** The bridge quest ("A Bridge Too Far") gates the best fishing, same as it gates mining and Death's story.
4. **Feed the gift chain.** Fish → cooking → Mana dishes → Noel's loves → relationship points. That's the full value chain of a single catch.
5. **Don't ignore fishing for money.** It's a low-skill, steady coin source that pairs well with the rest of the night — see the Money Guide for where it ranks.

## Fishing FAQ

**Where do I get the best fish?**
**Misty Shores**, unlocked by completing **"A Bridge Too Far"**. The river spots stay useful for volume, but the notable catches (Goliath fish and friends) are coastal.

**Do I need bait?**
No, but it's strongly recommended — bait widens your catch pool and improves the quality of what bites.

**What's the best fish?**
**Goliath fish** — it's Noel's loved gift and the key to his Mana-dish loved items. For money, processed/cooked fish beats raw fish every time.

**Can I fish at any time of night?**
Yes — but plan around the sunrise auto-return to your coffin. Time your sessions to finish at a walkable distance from home.
