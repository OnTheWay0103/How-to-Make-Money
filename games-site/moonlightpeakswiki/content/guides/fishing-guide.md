# Moonlight Peaks — Fishing Guide: Spots, Bait & Fish Uses

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

Fishing in Moonlight Peaks is the quiet workhorse of the early game — low risk, decent coins, and fish that double as liked gifts and cooking ingredients. You get your rod from **Noel Webb** on your second or third night (a cutscene by the water near Moonlit Pines), after which he challenges you to catch three different species in the **"Outfish the Fisherman"** quest (250 Coins reward). Like everything else, it happens at night. This guide covers where to fish, how the minigame works, and what to actually do with your catch.

## Where to Fish

Fishing spots in Moonlight Peaks are water-dependent. There are **22 fish species** in total, spread across the map:

| Location | Availability | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **Moonlit Pines / town waters** | From your first rod | Whisper (common, 40 Coins), Violet (65 Coins), and the **Super Rare Goliath** (500 Coins) |
| **Silverveil Lake** | Early-mid | Splotch (55), Mouthout (100), Orbis (75); Goldy in rivers nearby (Spring–Summer only) |
| **Misty Shores** | After **"A Bridge Too Far"** | Coastal catches; the bridge quest gates the south map |
| **Howling Marshes** | Mid-game | Brickle (80) |
| **Pink Grove** | Late-game | Armour (280); unlocked with Llemi's area |
| **Underground waters** | Late-game | Leftsee (140) |

**Pro tip:** the town waters are for stamina-to-coins conversion early on; the rare catches (Goliath, Armour) are for selling and the Museum's aquarium collection. If you're fishing for money, town spots are fine — if you're chasing the rare list, get the bridge quest done and keep exploring.

## How the Fishing Minigame Works

- **Spot the shadow:** fish appear as **fish-shaped shadows** in the water. Cast your line nearby — the base rod can only catch **small** shadows.
- **Wait for the bite:** the bobber/float dips under the water when a fish bites.
- **Reel it in:** press and hold the reel button to pull the catch in. Miss the timing and the fish gets away.
- No bait mechanic has been confirmed by community guides — the variables that matter are **where** you fish, **which rod** you hold, and **which season/night** it is (some fish are seasonal).

**Pro tip:** don't cast blind — only cast where you can see a shadow. Each wasted cast is wasted night time, and the night is short.

## Gear and Progression

Fishing has a short gear track that follows the same upgrade economy as everything else in Moonlight Peaks:

| Upgrade | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Fishing Rod** | Gifted by Noel — catches small fish shadows only |
| **Premium Fishing Rod** | **16,000 Coins + 1 Fishing Rod + 3 Gold Bars**, from Ridge at the **Howling Hammer** (open 18:00–00:00). Required to catch **large** fish shadows — including the top-value fish |

**Pro tip:** the Premium Rod is a big mid-game investment, but the large-fish catches (Goliath at 500 Coins) pay it back if you fish regularly. Until then, the base rod is plenty for quests and early income.

**Pro tip:** don't chain one spot all night. Cover two or three spots per session — the rare fish (like Goliath) are reported from specific waters, so fish where the species list says they live.

## What Fish Are Good For

Fish are three things in Moonlight Peaks: money, food, and gifts. The uses ladder:

| Use | Detail | Fish that matter |
|-----|--------|------------------|
| **Gifts** | Fish are **liked** gifts for Noel; no specific fish is confirmed as a loved gift | Any catch works in a pinch |
| **Cooking** | Fish feed cooked dishes, including Noel's loved **Mana dishes** (e.g. **Fishy Mana Quiche**, **Twilight Mana Maki**) | Quality fish and cooking fish |
| **Selling** | The rare fish are the big money — **Goliath sells for 500 Coins** raw | Goliath, Armour (280), Leftsee (140) |
| **Museum** | The aquarium collection wants one of each of the 22 species | Donate duplicates, sell the rest |

**Pro tip:** the **Goliath fish is the standout catch** of the money economy — a Super Rare fish reported in Moonlit Pines waters, selling for **500 Coins**. It also contributes to the Museum aquarium, which is worth more to completionists than the stall price.

## Best Fish to Keep

Based on early-game reports, the practical ranking:

| Catch | Keep for | Sell? |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Goliath fish** | Museum aquarium + 500-Coin sale | Sell or donate — it's a money fish, not a gift fish |
| **Rare location fish** (Armour, Leftsee, Mouthout) | Museum slots and high-value sales | Sell after donating one |
| **Cooking fish** (any fish that shows an ingredient tag) | Meals and stamina recovery | Cook before selling |
| **Common fish** (Whisper, Violet) | Immediate early-game income | Sell or cook |

**Pro tip:** donate the first of each species to the Museum, then sell the rest. A fish in the aquarium keeps its value forever; a fish in your chest is just waiting to be sold.

## The Goliath Fish Economy

**Goliath fish** deserve their own callout because they're the game's headline rare catch:

1. **Rarity** — Super Rare, reported from **Moonlit Pines** waters, with a **Very Large** shadow (you'll need the Premium Fishing Rod to land it).
2. **Value** — **500 Coins** raw, one of the best single-catch sales in the game.
3. **Collection** — one Goliath goes in the Museum's aquarium; the rest are pure profit.

**Pro tip:** every Goliath has a "default destination": donate one, sell the rest. That order converts one rare catch into collection progress and a fat coin stack — never hold rare fish waiting for a gift that was never confirmed.

## Fishing Tips

1. **Fish after your chores.** Fishing is a stamina-tidy activity — do it after watering and mining, when you still have enough bar for casts but not enough for heavy work.
2. **Time your runs.** Start a long fishing session early enough to walk back before sunrise; the auto-coffin return doesn't care how deep you are in a bite.
3. **Unlock Misty Shores early.** The bridge quest ("A Bridge Too Far") gates the south map, the mine, and the rarer waters.
4. **Feed the cooking chain.** Fish → cooking → Mana dishes → Noel's loved gifts → relationship points. That's the full value chain of a quality catch.
5. **Don't ignore fishing for money.** It's a low-skill, steady coin source that pairs well with the rest of the night — see the Money Guide for where it ranks.

## Fishing FAQ

**Where do I get the best fish?**
The **Goliath** (Super Rare, 500 Coins) is reported from **Moonlit Pines** waters. Other high-value catches are spread across the map — **Armour** (280 Coins) in the Pink Grove, **Leftsee** (140) in underground waters, and solid mid-tier fish at **Silverveil Lake**. There are 22 species in total.

**Do I need bait?**
No bait mechanic has been confirmed — the variables that matter are the spot, the rod, and the season. The **Premium Fishing Rod** (16,000 Coins + 1 rod + 3 Gold Bars, from Ridge at the Howling Hammer) is required for large fish.

**What's the best fish?**
**Goliath fish** — the most valuable catch, at 500 Coins, and a Museum aquarium centerpiece. (Note: despite some early guides claiming otherwise, no fish is confirmed as a *loved* gift — Noel's loved gifts are Mana dishes and Mana drinks; fish are only *liked*.)

**Can I fish at any time of night?**
Yes — but plan around the sunrise auto-return to your coffin. Time your sessions to finish at a walkable distance from home.
