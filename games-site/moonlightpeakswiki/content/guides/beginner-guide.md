# Moonlight Peaks — Beginner's Guide (First 5 Nights)

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Welcome to Moonlight Peaks, fledgling. You play the child of **Count Dracula**, who leaves the family castle behind to build a wholesome life in a supernatural town where nothing — not farming, not foraging, not friendship — happens before the sun goes down. This guide covers your first five nights: the core loop, what to unlock first, how to manage your **stamina** and **mana**, and the mistakes that slow down most new players.

**Moonlight Peaks** is a gothic cozy vampire farming and life sim by Dutch indie studio **Little Chicken Game Company**, published by **XSEED Games / Marvelous USA**. It launched **July 7, 2026** on **Steam (PC), macOS, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and Android (Google Play Games)** at **$34.99** (standard), **$39.99** (Switch 2 Edition), or **$44.97** (Digital Deluxe with art book and soundtrack), with a **15% launch discount** and a free demo on Steam. It has sold **200,000+ copies** in about three weeks and sits at **89% "Very Positive"** on Steam.

## The Core Loop: Everything Happens at Night

The single most important thing to internalize: **you live at night**. You wake in your coffin at dusk, farm, forage, fish, mine, brew potions, and socialize through the night hours, and at sunrise you are **automatically returned to your coffin**. You cannot "wait out" the day — the day belongs to sleep, and the town (mostly) sleeps with you.

A standard night looks like this:

| Time block | Activity |
|-----------|----------|
| **Dusk** | Wake in coffin, check quests and mail, plan the night |
| **Early night** | Water and harvest crops, brew potions in your cauldron |
| **Midnight** | Forage, fish, mine, visit shops (Web of Wonders, Coffee & Coffins, Luna's Farm) |
| **Late night** | Talk to residents, deliver gifts, progress quests, play Nokturna |
| **Pre-dawn** | Sell goods, process ores in the furnace, finish quests before sunrise |
| **Sunrise** | Automatically returned to your coffin; day passes instantly |

**Pro tip:** treat dusk as a planning moment. Spend 30 seconds reading quests and deciding your goal for the night — the night is finite and the stamina bar decides how much of it you actually spend working.

## Your First 5 Nights: Priority Checklist

The first nights are slower than the mid-game — that's a known critique of Moonlight Peaks, and it's fixable if you prioritize correctly. Don't try to do everything; follow this order instead.

| Night | Priority | Why |
|-------|----------|-----|
| **Night 1** | Explore town, meet residents, accept **Orlock's quest** | Orlock's task — growing **Blood Grapes** and making **Blood Wine** — teaches you farming. Town layout knowledge pays off all game |
| **Night 2** | Plant Blood Grapes, water them, start foraging | You need grapes before you can make wine; forage anything you see for early cash |
| **Night 3** | Harvest grapes, process into Blood Wine, deliver to Orlock | Completes your first real quest chain and unlocks further story content |
| **Night 4** | Start **"A Bridge Too Far"**: talk to **Fiona Webb at Moonlit Pines** | This quest lifts the curse on the bridge and unlocks the south map — the biggest early expansion |
| **Night 5** | Save up for a **Rabbicula (2800 Coins) from Luna's Farm** or an early tool upgrade | Rabbicula mounds produce **Luck Dust**, which feeds into the Love Potion recipe and gift economy |

**Pro tip:** the two quest lines that matter most early are **Orlock's Blood Grapes / Blood Wine chain** and **"A Bridge Too Far"**. The first teaches you farming; the second opens the map. Everything else can wait a few nights.

## Stamina and Mana Management

Moonlight Peaks gives you two separate pools, and both are **deliberately restrictive** in the early game — another common critique. Understanding them saves you hours.

| Pool | What it powers | How to recover |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **Stamina** | Farming (watering, harvesting), mining, fishing, foraging | Food (cooked dishes, snacks), sleep cycle reset at dusk |
| **Mana** | Spells, **shapeshifting**, some potion-related actions | Mana-restoring food and drinks like **Mana Coffee** and **Mana Chocolate** |

The core rule: **don't waste mana early.** Spells and shapeshifting (bat form, hellkitten) are fun, but in the first week your mana is better saved for what unlocks progression. Use bat form only for long treks, not for hovering across town.

**Pro tip:** keep a small stash of cooked food with you at all times. It converts otherwise-dead end-of-night time into productive time and is cheaper than relying on shops.

## Unlock Priorities: What to Chase First

Not every system unlocks on night one. Here's the order that gets you the most content fastest:

1. **Farming tools + Blood Grapes** — via Orlock's early quest. Farming is your steady income.
2. **Cauldron and potion brewing** — via **"Mend it with Magic"**: Viktor delivers your mother's broken cauldron, you bring **Fiona** two **Mana Essences**, and she repairs it and teaches you the cauldron blueprint plus your first recipe, the **Alter Ego Elixir**. Extra recipes come from **Sabrina at the Web of Wonders** magic shop.
3. **"A Bridge Too Far"** — unlock the south map: **Misty Shores, Moonlight Tower, Ambrosia Graveyard, and Ambrosia Mansion**. It's also the prerequisite for eventually finding **Death**.
4. **Mining in the Cave of Echoes** — the first mine becomes available once **"A Bridge Too Far"** reopens the estate containing its entrance. Copper is needed constantly.
5. **Fishing** — cheap stamina-to-coins conversion, and fish are great gifts (Noel loves **Goliath fish**).

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why it hurts | What to do instead |
|---------|--------------|--------------------|
| **Wasting mana on shapeshifting all night** | Leaves you dry for quests and potion work | Reserve mana; shift only for real shortcuts |
| **Ignoring the cauldron quest** | Potions are your best early money and gift items (Love Potion sells for **2200 Coins**) | Do "Mend it with Magic" as soon as it's available |
| **Skipping the bridge quest** | The south map holds shops, fish, ore, and story content | Talk to Fiona Webb at Moonlit Pines early |
| **Selling everything** | You'll re-buy ingredients at a markup | Keep at least a few of each foragable and crop |
| **Overspending on upgrades** | Upgrade costs are famously high in this game | Upgrade tools before cosmetic upgrades |
| **Sleeping through the night schedule** | Festivals and character birthdays are date-specific | Check the calendar; birthdays matter for gifts (see the Gift Guide) |

**Pro tip:** the early-game pacing complaints are real — progression is slow and upgrade costs are steep. The fix is simple: pick one money system (farming or potions) and stick with it for the first two weeks rather than dabbling in everything.

## What to Know About Town Life

- **All socializing happens at night** — residents are awake on roughly the same clock as you.
- **~24 residents are romanceable** across the town's **seven families** (Dracula, Ambrosia, Logan, Webb, Khazan, Horseus, Henderson), and romance is **fully LGBTQIA+ friendly** — no gender restrictions on any relationship.
- **Nokturna**, the in-game card game with **67 cards**, is a great way to pass late nights and earn money — opponents can be found around town.
- **Divorce is handled by an NPC named Llemi** with no social penalty, so you never need to worry about being "stuck" in a marriage.
- Your **haunted cottage** is fully customizable — but cosmetics come after tools in the upgrade order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I stay awake during the day?**
No. At sunrise you are **automatically returned to your coffin**. Plan every night around that hard cutoff.

**Is there a demo?**
Yes — a **free demo is available on Steam**, covering the opening nights of the game.

**How long does the slow early game last?**
Most players report the game opens up noticeably after the bridge quest and the cauldron unlock. Roughly the first week of in-game nights is the tightest.

**Which platforms can I play on?**
PC (Steam), macOS, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and Android via Google Play Games, with full support at launch.
