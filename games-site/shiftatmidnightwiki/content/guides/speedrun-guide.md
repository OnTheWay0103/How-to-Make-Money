## What Is a Shift At Midnight Speedrun?

Shift At Midnight (Bun Muen / Kwalee, released July 22, 2026) is a co-op horror game about running a rural 1993 gas station while identifying doppelganger impostors across 13 shifts. It looks like a survival game, but speedrunners treat it as a **money-management puzzle with a fixed enemy roster** — and that framing is the whole trick.

Every campaign runs on the same 13-shift skeleton with a **fixed doppelganger list per shift**, so a speedrun is route discipline: run the optimal shift loop, screen known fakes fast, skip optional entity fights, and bank **$250+ in personal cash by the end of Shift 12** — the True Ending requirement. Reach Shift 13 with the money in hand, survive the swarm, and do not call Clyde.

## Speedrun Fundamentals: The Optimal Shift Loop

Every shift runs the same six steps. The speedrun version compresses steps 1-3 so you spend maximum time at the register, where money and fakes both live:

1. **Clean the store** — use the **sledgehammer and hose** instead of the mop; it's faster and the cleanup pays.
2. **Open the garage door** — leave it open for the delivery truck first, so restock isn't waiting on you.
3. **Restock the front shelves** — keep **15+ items stocked**. The stock quota and nightly money goal must be hit every shift; missing them wastes time on punishment.
4. **Read the noticeboard** — objectives, plus clues that disprove customer stories (a customer claims to work somewhere the board says burned down). Ten seconds saves minutes of questioning.
5. **Screen at the register** — scan purchases, scan the ID, ask questions (up to 5 per customer), and cross-reference the NET database. Run the **alive/dead check first** — the fastest filter in the game.
6. **Kill confirmed doppelgangers before they pay** — the run's most important habit. A fake that completes its purchase triggers a **60-second lock-up alert** and returns in monster form; killing before payment skips it entirely (more below).

Do steps 1-3 at a dead run and treat steps 5-6 as the whole game, and you'll cut each shift down to its minimum.

## Money Optimization

The run is won at the register and at the wheel, not in combat. These are the highest-yield money actions:

- **Token farm before every shift** — tokens found before each shift feed the **prize wheel**. Aim for a **$10 win**; it's the fastest single payday and takes seconds once you route the spawns.
- **Never shoot innocent humans** — a shot at a real customer docks your pay and can wipe out a shift's profit.
- **Clean with the sledgehammer and hose** — faster than the mop, and the cleanup pays.
- **Do the paid bonus tasks** — Shift 4's rat bonus (throw rats in the trash before the next customer) and Shift 9's roach bonus (cleared with the mop) are free income.
- **Upgrade the shopfloor** — early store-money upgrades raise earning power for every shift after.
- **Keep store money and personal funds separate** — the True Ending check reads *personal* cash only. Traps, planks, and Arsenal weapons come from store money.

Check your personal balance every shift. You need **$250+ by the end of Shift 12**; if the trajectory misses, prioritize the wheel and bonus tasks.

## Entity Skip Strategies

Entity fights are the biggest time sink in the game, and almost all of them are avoidable:

1. **Kill doppelgangers before they complete their purchase.** A fake that pays triggers the 60-second lock-up alert and returns in monstrous form — a fight you chose to take. Killing before payment prevents the spawn and skips the alert; this one habit removes most optional encounters.
2. **Pre-place traps from the store computer.** Scatter them in advance for later nights — mid-hunt there may be no window. Traps shorten whatever fight happens.
3. **Fight rather than hide once the Arsenal is stocked.** The entity's patience bar depletes as you damage it, and it can be gunned down with everything it spawns. From mid-campaign, fighting is the *fast* option; hiding is for early nights with an empty Arsenal.
4. **Keep the mic on push-to-talk.** The entity tracks audio — one wasted hunt to a noisy mic is a run-killer.
5. **From Shift 5, board the correct bus.** An **empty driver's seat means safe; a seated driver is a doppelganger**, and boarding that bus introduces one of the fastest monsters in the game. Check every time.

Blood-rain nights (first around Shift 5) make the Arsenal unreliable — the pistol must carry you, so budget traps and planks from store money instead.

## Route to the True Ending

The True Ending route is a three-phase plan:

- **Shifts 1-4 — build the routine.** Hit the nightly money goal and stock quota every shift and start the personal-cash habit immediately. Pay the pet's medicine bill on Shift 3, take the Shift 4 rat bonus — small savings compound across 13 shifts.
- **Shifts 5-8 — protect the accounts.** Traps and Arsenal upgrades come from store money only. **Upgrade the Arsenal before Shift 7, when the Dentist arrives** — the confirmed reason to save store funds early. Keep personal cash untouched.
- **Shifts 9-12 — the final push.** Mop the Shift 9 roach bonus and upgrade weapons again. At Shift 10 the **$250 surgery requirement is revealed** — check your balance the moment it is; Shift 11 is about keeping that cash. At Shift 12 the sheriff's email offers to cover it — **it's a bluff. Do not call Clyde.**

**Night 13 — survival only.** The finale is a swarm of all types; screening is no longer the point. **Exhaust all 5 questions per customer** if you need breathing room, focus on survival until the Dentist cutscene triggers, and the post-shift screen confirms the surgery. The ending was decided by your money discipline — the finale is the finish line.

## Time-Saving Tips

- **Skip optional tasks that don't pay.** No money goal, quota, or confirmed fake attached? Not on the route.
- **Prioritize high-value actions.** A doppelganger about to pay outranks a perfectly arranged shelf.
- **Manage inventory in bulk.** Plan purchases at shift start, scatter traps in advance, and learn where the gun and sledgehammer live.
- **Budget your 5 questions.** Screen against the database first; two or three stacked contradictions are enough — don't interrogate someone you've already caught.
- **When in doubt, let them leave.** A missed fake costs one hunt; a shot at an innocent costs money and the shift — always the worse loss.
