## Shift At Midnight Endings Guide

*Shift At Midnight* has **three endings**, decided by a single decision at the end of Night 12 plus one number: **whether you call Sheriff Clyde**, and **whether your personal cash hits $250** by the finale. Multiple published guides (GameRant, IGN, GamesHorizon, AllThings.How and others) now document the same three outcomes consistently — the True Ending, Grave Decision, and Empty Home.

This guide covers the confirmed conditions for each ending, the checklist for the True Ending, and the mistakes that lock you out of it. For what the endings *mean* — the Dentist's identity, the story theories, and the unresolved questions — see the [Endings Explained guide](/guides/endings-explained-guide).

## The Three Endings at a Glance

| Ending | Call Clyde after Night 12? | Personal cash at the vet check | Outcome |
|--------|----------------------------|--------------------------------|---------|
| **True Ending** | No | $250 or more | You pay for the pet's surgery yourself. Clyde and your pet both survive — the only ending with no death |
| **Grave Decision** | Yes | Any amount | Clyde covers the surgery. Your pet survives, but Clyde is killed by the Dentist — a sacrifice ending |
| **Empty Home** | No | Under $250 | The surgery can't be paid. Clyde survives, but your pet dies — the worst ending |

The check happens at the **vet after Night 13**, and the Shift 13 payout lands *before* the vet checks your balance — so a small gap can still close on the final night.

## What's Confirmed About the Ending Mechanics

1. **The True Ending requires $250+ in personal cash** by the finale — the money pays for the pet's surgery. The requirement is revealed after Shift 10.
2. **Only personal cash counts.** Store money (revenue, quota payouts, store balance) is a separate account and does not count toward the $250. Mixing the two is the most common way runs fail.
3. **The Night 12 call is the other switch.** Call Clyde and he arrives on the final night at fatal risk (Grave Decision). Decline and the ending depends entirely on your cash (True Ending or Empty Home).
4. **The ending achievements are mutually exclusive** — you need multiple playthroughs to see all three.

## The Finale (Shift 13)

The ending conditions play out in the final shift's cutscenes:

1. Survive the **swarm of all doppelganger types**
2. Shoot until the **Dentist cutscene** triggers
3. **Run in the opposite direction** until the second cutscene catches you
4. In that cutscene, **Clyde finishes off the Dentist** — in the True Ending and Empty Home, Clyde survives the encounter; in Grave Decision he doesn't

The finale is cinematic rather than a boss fight with a health bar. What matters for the ending is what you did *before* Shift 13: the $250 check and the call decision.

**A note on the cutscene**: earlier community versions described "a stranger" killing the Dentist in the finale. Current published guides consistently describe **Clyde** in that role — the earlier wording is believed to be an early-report variation. Either way, the outcome for your ending is unchanged.

## The True Ending Checklist

- [ ] Track personal cash separately from store funds from Shift 1
- [ ] Bank **$250+ in personal cash** before the finale (requirement revealed at Shift 10)
- [ ] Skip spending personal funds on store supplies (traps, weapons, boards come from store money)
- [ ] At the end of Night 12, read the sheriff's message — and **decline the call**
- [ ] Make sure Clyde survives the campaign — killing the real Clyde locks out every ending (see below)
- [ ] Watch the vet scene after Night 13 for the surgery confirmation

## Grave Decision: The Sacrifice Ending

**How to get it**: call Clyde at the end of Night 12. He visits the store on the final night, covers the $250 surgery, your pet survives — and Clyde is killed by the Dentist. A funeral scene plays.

This is not a failure state: it's the "save your pet at any cost" path. If you want the True Ending, don't take it — calling Clyde when you already have $250 saved gives you nothing extra; you simply lose Clyde.

## Empty Home: The Worst Ending

**How it happens**: decline the call at Night 12 but come up short on personal cash. Clyde never visits the store and survives, but the surgery can't be paid and your pet dies. Overspending on pet medicine earlier in the campaign is the most commonly reported cause.

## Sheriff Clyde: The Call Decision

| Scenario | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| $250+ personal cash, don't call | True Ending — surgery paid, both survive |
| Call Clyde (any cash) | Grave Decision — Clyde covers it, dies |
| Under $250, don't call | Empty Home — pet dies, Clyde survives |
| Real Clyde killed earlier in the run | **All three endings become unreachable** — there's no one to call, and the ending achievements are locked out |

## The Clyde Warning (Most Important Rule)

Clyde Dawson visits the store during earlier shifts as a **human** — and on **Night 9, a doppelganger wearing his name** walks in. Killing the *real* Clyde (mistaking him for a fake) permanently kills him, removes the Night 12 call option entirely, and **locks out all three ending achievements** — there is no ending at all after that, just the finale. Verify Clyde's identity with the NET Database like any customer, and never shoot on name recognition alone. Full fake list by shift: [All Doppelgangers](/guides/all-doppelgangers-list).

## The Pet Subplot

The pet's story runs through the whole campaign:

- **Shift 3**: pay the pet's **medicine bill**
- **Shift 10**: the **$250 surgery** requirement is revealed
- **Shift 11**: keep enough cash for surgery
- **Night 12**: the call decision — the sheriff's offer vs. your own savings
- **Vet check after Night 13**: the $250 personal-cash check decides whether the surgery happens

Community guides describe the pet as central to the True Ending's emotional payoff — this is the character the money actually goes to.

## Money Guidance for the Ending

For full income strategy, see the [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide). The short version:

- Keep store and personal funds separate from the start
- Personal cash is the ending currency — spend from store money whenever possible
- Personal income is roughly a **$10 daily wage plus ~10% of store revenue** when you beat the quota (community-reported figures; the exact formula is not published)
- The stock quota (15+ items on front shelves) and the nightly money goal keep store income flowing without touching personal funds

## Common Ending Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | The Fix |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Mixing store and personal money | The check reads personal cash only | Two mental accounts from Shift 1 |
| Spending personal cash on gear | Traps and boards come from store funds | Budget supplies from store income |
| Calling Clyde "to be safe" | Turns a True Ending run into Grave Decision | Decline the call if you have the money |
| Shooting the real Clyde | Locks out all three endings permanently | Verify identity every visit — Night 9's Clyde is the fake |
| Ignoring the Shift 10 reveal | You learn the $250 target too late | Save personal cash from Shift 1 |
