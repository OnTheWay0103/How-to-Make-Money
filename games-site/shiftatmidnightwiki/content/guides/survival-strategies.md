## Survival Strategies — Core Mechanics, Resource Management, and Nighttime Tactics

Surviving a shift at the gas station requires more than just spotting doppelgangers. The survival mechanics in Shift at Midnight form a complex web of resource management, threat assessment, and environmental awareness. This guide covers the core survival systems and the strategies that keep you alive through Night 13.

---

## Core Survival Mechanics

### Sanity System

Your sanity is your most important resource. At full sanity (100%), you have clear vision and normal audio. As sanity decreases, the game becomes progressively more dangerous.

| Sanity Level | Effects | Recovery Methods |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| 80-100% | Normal gameplay, clear audio | None needed |
| 60-79% | Minor screen flicker, occasional whispers | Stay in lit areas |
| 40-59% | Auditory hallucinations, false footsteps | Use sanity pills (+15) |
| 20-39% | Visual distortions, fake doppelganger warnings | Prayer candles (+25) |
| 0-19% | Constant hallucinations, Entity attracted | Flashbang + hiding |
| 0% | Character incapacitated (game over) | N/A (requires teammate revive) |

**Sanity drain sources**:
- Being near an undisguised doppelganger (-2/sec)
- Entity patrols within 20 meters (-5/sec)
- Failed ID checks (-10 per failure)
- Dark areas without flashlight (-1/sec)
- Witnessing a teammate's death (-20 instant)

### Stamina System

Stamina governs your ability to sprint, melee attack, and perform special actions. Unlike sanity, stamina regenerates naturally.

| Action | Stamina Cost | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Sprint | 15/sec | Regens 5/sec while walking |
| Melee attack (bat) | 10 per swing | Regens fully in 4 seconds |
| Melee attack (sledgehammer) | 20 per swing | Longest recovery |
| Climb over obstacle | 15 | Quick action |
| Push heavy object | 25 | Only when barricading |

### Health System

Health is straightforward but limited. You cannot regenerate health naturally — only through items and teammate abilities.

| Healing Item | HP Restored | Availability | Cost |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|------|
| Bandage | 20 | Stockroom cabinets | Free (limited) |
| First Aid Kit | 50 | Shop Terminal | $15 |
| Medkit | 100 | Rare loot, boss drops | N/A |
| Teammate CPR | 40 | Co-op only | N/A |

## Survival Priority System

When multiple threats emerge simultaneously, use this priority table to decide what to handle first.

| Priority | Threat | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| 1 (Critical) | Entity patrol | Instant kill. Hide immediately. |
| 2 (High) | Gnome spotted | Escalates rapidly. Destroy within 30 seconds. |
| 3 (High) | Aggressive doppelganger | Will attack. Use weapon or alarm. |
| 4 (Medium) | Stock quota expiring | Reduces bonus but not lethal. |
| 5 (Medium) | Phantom stalking | Dangerous but slow. Set trap. |
| 6 (Low) | Customer queue growing | Missed sales but no immediate danger. |
| 7 (Low) | Store stains | Affects bonus only. Clean when safe. |

## Resource Management

### Income and Spending

Your daily income determines what tools and weapons you can afford. Optimize your spending for long-term survival.

**Daily income sources**:
- Base salary: $10 per shift
- Sales commission: 10% of total sales
- Urgent task rewards: $0.50-$30 per task
- Doppelganger loot: $5-$25 per kill

**Priority spending by phase**:

| Night Range | Priority Purchase | Budget | Reason |
|-------------|------------------|--------|--------|
| 1-3 | Baseball bat + 2 bear traps | $32 | Biggest early survivability upgrade |
| 4-6 | Pistol + ammo + motion alarms | $35 | Ranged threat response |
| 7-9 | Shotgun + wooden boards | $70 | Entity defense preparation |
| 10-12 | SMG + electrical traps | $55 | Swarm management |
| 13 | Flamethrower + full ammo | $95 | Final boss preparation |

### Inventory Management

You have 6 inventory slots. Every item must justify its slot.

**Recommended base loadout** (1 weapon + 2 tools + 2 consumables):
1. Primary weapon (bat or better)
2. Bear trap (critical for multiple threats)
3. Second tool (motion alarm or wooden boards)
4. Healing item (first aid kit)
5. Sanity item (sanity pills)
6. Emergency weapon (brick or pistol)

## Stealth vs. Combat Decision Guide

Not every threat requires a fight. Knowing when to engage and when to hide is the most important survival skill.

### When to Fight

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| Normal doppelganger confirmed | Fight (2 bat hits) |
| Aggressive doppelganger | Fight immediately (SMG or shotgun) |
| Gnome (fresh, under 30 seconds) | Fight (destroy instantly) |
| Phantom (trapped) | Fight (sledgehammer finish) |
| The Dentist (Night 13) | Fight (final boss, unavoidable) |

### When to Hide

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| Entity patrolling | Hide in locker or back room |
| Phantom (not trapped) | Hide until it passes, then set traps |
| Gnome (escalated beyond 60s) | Hide until it resets |
| Multiple doppelgangers | Hide and thin them with ranged weapons |
| Low health, no healing items | Hide until teammates arrive |

### When to Flee

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| Entity chase phase | Sprint to opposite side of store |
| Demon possession ritual failed | Leave the store, wait for reset |
| Sanity below 20% | Run to the back office (brightest area) |
| Weapon broken/no ammo | Retreat to stockroom for supplies |

## Shelter and Safe Zones

### Safe Zone Tiers

| Location | Safety Level | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Back office (lights on) | Very Safe | Sanity recovery, Entity avoidance |
| Locker (inside) | Safe | Hiding from Entity |
| Stockroom (lights on) | Safe | Healing, inventory management |
| Behind register counter | Moderate | Doppelganger ID, quick retreat |
| Bathroom stall | Moderate | Emergency hiding spot |
| Outdoors (lit area) | Risky | Urgent tasks, resource gathering |
| Outdoors (dark area) | Very Dangerous | Only during emergencies |

### Creating Safe Zones

Use tools to create safe zones in high-traffic areas:

1. **Place bear traps** at all entrance points to the back office
2. **Board up windows** in the stockroom (minimum 2 boards)
3. **Install motion alarms** in hallways connecting to the register area
4. **Keep lights on** — replace burnt-out bulbs immediately
5. **Designate a rally point** in co-op (usually the back office)

## Nighttime Strategy Framework

### Pre-Midnight Preparation (30 minutes before shift)

1. Check Shop Terminal emails for threat warnings
2. Review delivery manifest for stock quota
3. Check inventory and restock from Shop Terminal
4. Place tools in strategic locations (traps, alarms)
5. Coordinate roles with teammates in co-op

### Early Shift (12:00 AM - 1:30 AM)

- **Focus on register duty** — Early customers are most likely to be doppelgangers
- **Stock shelves between rushes** — The first stock quota is usually generous
- **Do not explore** — Threats are minimal but the Entity may still patrol

### Mid Shift (1:30 AM - 3:00 AM)

- **Balance register and maintenance** — Customer frequency increases
- **Respond to urgent tasks** — The forest meat run is best done now
- **Reinforce defenses** — Board up windows before the Entity becomes active
- **Check on trapped enemies** — Collect loot from defeated doppelgangers

### Late Shift (3:00 AM - 6:00 AM)

- **Maximum threat level** — Doppelganger spawns peak, Entity activity increases
- **Stay near safe zones** — Do not venture far from the back office
- **Save healing items** — The final hour is the most dangerous
- **Prioritize survival over tasks** — Missed stock quotas are better than death

## Common Survival Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Is Dangerous | The Fix |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| Hoarding money | Tools save lives. Cash is worthless when dead. | Spend $30-40 per shift on essentials |
| Ignoring sanity | Low sanity causes hallucinations that mask real threats | Use sanity items when below 50% |
| Fighting the Entity | The Entity has 750 HP and cannot be killed | Hide. Always hide. |
| Wandering without a flashlight | Dark areas drain sanity and hide threats | Keep flashlight on at all times |
| Using guns on weak enemies | Ammo is expensive and limited | Use melee on normal doppelgangers |
| Not communicating (co-op) | Silent players cannot coordinate threat response | Use push-to-talk for callouts |
