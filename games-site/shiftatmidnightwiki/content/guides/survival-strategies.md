## Survival Strategies — Core Mechanics, Preparation, and Nighttime Tactics

Surviving a shift at the gas station requires more than spotting doppelgangers. Shift At Midnight is a preparation game: the shift's survival phase is won or lost by what you did *before* it started. This guide covers the confirmed survival mechanics and the routine that keeps you alive through Night 13.

**An honesty note up front**: some third-party guides describe sanity meters, stamina systems, and health bars with exact numbers. The community has **not confirmed** those systems. This guide covers the mechanics that walkthroughs and community guides actually document — and flags what's unverified.

## The Confirmed Survival Loop

Every shift runs the same loop:

1. **Read the noticeboard** — objectives, warnings, and news that can disprove customer stories
2. **Clean up** — mop the blood, bag the limbs, dump them outside
3. **Open the garage** — leave the door open for the delivery truck
4. **Stock the shelves** — 15+ items on the front shelves is the community-cited quota; restock after each sale
5. **Serve and screen** — scan IDs, cross-reference the NET Database, ask questions, kill fakes before payment
6. **Survive the entity phase** — barricade, trap, hide, stay quiet
7. **Catch the bus** — from Shift 5, board the correct one (empty driver's seat = safe)

Master this loop and the campaign mostly plays itself. Break it and the night compounds: escaped fakes, failed quotas, and unprepared barricades all feed each other.

## Preparation Priority System

Preparation is the survival mechanic that matters most. Before the shift's threats arrive:

| Priority | Action | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Buy traps and planks from the store computer in advance | The survival phase assumes they're already in place |
| 2 | Upgrade the Arsenal before Shift 7 and Shift 9 | Confirmed milestones: the Dentist arrives, the campaign escalates |
| 3 | Check the Arsenal on blood-rain nights | It's unreliable during blood rain — the pistol must carry those shifts |
| 4 | Set your mic to push-to-talk | Audio tracking is a confirmed mechanic |
| 5 | Track personal cash separately from store funds | The True Ending checks personal money |

## Screening Discipline (The Prevention Layer)

The best survival strategy is preventing the survival phase from getting worse:

- **Kill confirmed fakes before payment** — every fake that pays triggers the 60-second lock-up alert on top of the shift's normal entity attack
- **Stack the clues** — two or three contradictions (ID, database, behavior) confirm a fake; one odd answer is a misread risk
- **Watch the name traps** — Shift 7's first Louisa Mari is fake (second is real); Shift 11's first Andal Khan is real (second is fake); the Driver is a recurring fake from Shift 4
- **Remember the Norbert exception** — a failed scan alone isn't a kill order; Norbert the Gnome is harmless (see the [Norbert guide](/guides/norbert-the-gnome-guide))

## Stealth vs. Combat (The Survival Phase)

When the entity phase begins (end-of-shift attack or the 60-second alert), the decision is straightforward:

### When to Fight

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| Stocked Arsenal, entity spawning | Gun it down with everything it spawns (the confirmed speedrun tactic) |
| 60-second alert | Lay traps in the garage and near the desk, then fight from position |
| Blood Moon night with prep | Expected — fight with traps and planks in place |

### When to Hide

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| Empty Arsenal, early campaign | Hide — vents work but only temporarily; keep moving |
| Audio pressure | Stay silent; the entity tracks sound |
| Walls broken (final shift) | Hide in back-room lockers until sunrise (community walkthrough advice) |

### The Audio Rule

The most consistently reported mechanic: **the entity tracks audio.** Open-microphone noise draws it to you. Push-to-talk, muting, or silence during hunts is the difference between a free win and a dragged-out chase. The Dentist punishes noise too — see the [Dentist Guide](/guides/dentist-guide).

## The Shift-End Bus (Your Most Forgotten Death)

From Shift 5, the shift ends with a bus choice: **an empty driver's seat means it's safe; someone sitting behind the wheel is a doppelganger**, and boarding introduces one of the fastest monsters in the game. Check the driver's seat every single shift. It's a two-second habit that prevents one of the campaign's most common deaths.

## Nightly Routine Framework

### Pre-Shift (At Shift Start)

1. Read the noticeboard and check the store computer (warnings, Arsenal availability, purchases)
2. Clean up and open the garage
3. Stock shelves to quota
4. Place traps in strategic locations
5. Confirm the Arsenal plan (and its blood-rain fallback)

### Early Shift

- **Register duty** — customers arrive steadily; screening is the priority
- **Stock between rushes** — the quota refreshes; restock after each sale
- **Don't wander** — the scripted events (generator sabotage on Shift 2, thieves on Shift 7) come when they come

### Mid Shift

- **Balance register and maintenance** — customer flow peaks
- **Handle scripted events** — Shift 4's rat task, Shift 6's forest cleanup, Shift 7's Dentist arrival
- **Reinforce defenses** — top up planks and traps before the entity phase

### Late Shift / The Entity Phase

- **Maximum threat** — the shift's finale
- **Barricade the three doors, lay traps, go quiet**
- **Prioritize survival over tasks** — a missed quota hurts; death ends the run

## Common Survival Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Is Dangerous | The Fix |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| Letting fakes pay | 60-second alerts stack on top of the entity phase | Kill before payment, every time |
| Buying nothing until the hunt | The survival phase assumes pre-placed traps | Buy traps and planks at shift start |
| Open mic during hunts | Audio tracking is a confirmed mechanic | Push-to-talk or mute |
| Boarding the wrong bus | One of the fastest monsters boards | Check the driver's seat |
| Mixing store and personal money | The True Ending checks personal cash | Two accounts from Shift 1 |
| Trusting third-party stat tables | Sanity/stamina/health numbers are unconfirmed | Stick to the documented loop |

## What the Community Has NOT Confirmed

- **Sanity meters** with drain rates and recovery items
- **Stamina costs** for actions
- **Health bars and healing items** with exact values
- **Named trap types** with damage and stun numbers
- **Boss health pools** and phase transitions

If a guide cites specific numbers for any of the above, treat them as unverified.

## Final Checklist

- [ ] Shelves at quota (15+ items)
- [ ] Traps and planks in place
- [ ] Arsenal plan set (blood-rain fallback ready)
- [ ] Mic on push-to-talk
- [ ] Personal cash tracked separately
- [ ] Bus-check habit active from Shift 5
