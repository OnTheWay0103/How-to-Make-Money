# Shift At Midnight Shop Rating Guide — Cleaning, Decor, and the Reviews Tab

Cleaning in Shift At Midnight is not cosmetic. Community-tested reports say your store rating, shown in the computer's Reviews tab, affects how many customers walk in and how much they spend, while blood, body parts, trash, rats, and roaches drag it down. This guide covers what each mess pays, the fastest nightly cleaning order, and how decor raises the rating without touching your personal cash.

## Does Cleaning Actually Matter?

Players disagree, and the disagreement is worth understanding. In an itch.io feedback post, one player calls restocking "tedious" and argues there is not much reason to keep the store clean "apart from your own comfort in a not blood stained gas station." In a Steam discussion thread, another player asks the developer not to cut stocking or mopping, because those chores have "a good reward system with the store rating."

The second view matches the official store page, which lists keeping the store clean as part of the management loop and tells you to clean up after executing a suspected doppelganger. Community guides go further: they report that the rating controls customer traffic and spending, and that leaving gore or trash out lowers it. So cleaning matters, but not every square inch matters equally. Prioritize the mess customers can see and the mess that persists into the next shift.

## How the Store Rating Works

The rating is checked on the store computer under the **Reviews** tab. Community sources agree on the inputs even when they disagree on the exact scale:

| Input | Effect on rating | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Clean blood and spills | Positive | Community and editorial guides |
| Remove rats and cockroaches | Positive | Community and editorial guides |
| Place decor on the sales floor | Positive | Community and editorial guides |
| Fast, correct customer service | Positive | Community-reported, exact values unconfirmed |
| False accusation or slow service | Negative | Community-reported, exact values unconfirmed |
| Leaving gore, trash, or pests overnight | Negative | Community guides and player reviews |

The precise scale is **[Unconfirmed]**. Some community pages describe a hidden 0-100 score displayed as stars; others talk about four stars or five stars. No official source publishes thresholds, so treat any exact number you see as an estimate. What is consistent across sources is the direction: a clean store and placed decor raise the rating, and a higher rating means more customer spending.

One consistency check: the 1 September 2026 update ("30 NEW CUSTOMERS + MORE") added customers, the chainsaw, and Endless Mode changes, but its changelog lists no change to cleaning, decor, or rating. The next official post was a 1 October Autumn Sale notice. As of 26 October 2026, the system described here is the current one.

## What Each Mess Pays

Community guides report the cleanup payouts below. This site's [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide) already warns that exact dollar figures are not officially published, so treat every number in this table as **[Unconfirmed]** and verify it against your own end-of-shift report.

| Mess | Tool | Reported pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blood puddle | Mop | ~$0.10 per puddle | Small but constant |
| Bathroom mess | Mop | ~$0.40 per mess | Easy to miss |
| Roach | Mop | ~$3 each | Tied to the Shift 9 roach bonus |
| Live rat | Carry to dumpster | ~$3 each | Shooting reportedly pays ~$0.10 |
| Body parts / limbs | Trash bag, then dumpster | Not confirmed | Mandatory before customers |
| Litter / trash | Trash bag | Small, not confirmed | Lower priority than gore |

Two practical conclusions follow. First, live rats are worth far more than dead ones, so carry them out when the store is calm instead of shooting them in a panic. Second, blood and bathroom messes pay almost nothing per unit. You clean those for the rating and for a store that does not look abandoned, not for the cash.

## The Fast Cleaning Routine

Use the same order every shift. It front-loads the mess that affects customers and leaves low-value work for quiet gaps.

1. **Mop blood in customer-facing aisles first.** Customers react to visible gore; a spill behind the counter can wait a few seconds longer.
2. **Bag limbs and body parts immediately.** Carry them straight to the dumpster. Do not stack bags by the door where customers path.
3. **Clear litter and the bathroom.** These are quick mop and bag jobs that protect the rating without much travel time.
4. **Handle rats and roaches when the queue is empty.** Live rats go to the dumpster; roaches get mopped.
5. **Restock during the lull after cleanup.** A bare shelf means fewer sales, and sales are what the nightly goal reads.
6. **Do a final sweep before the entity phase.** Gore left when the shift ends carries into the next night's rating.

In co-op, split the work instead of duplicating it. One player owns the mop and bags while another stocks and runs the register; the third screens customers. The [Co-Op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide) covers the role split in more detail. Solo players should clean first, when the store is empty, then stock; the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) has a priority list for that.

## How to Raise Your Rating With Decor

Decor is the rating lever that costs no personal cash. The loop, as described by community guides:

1. Open the store computer and go to the **Shop** tab.
2. Buy decor items. Each listing shows how many **Decor Points** it adds. Cheap plants and posters are efficient; use **Refresh Shop** if the selection is bad.
3. Pay from **store funds**, not personal savings. This is the same two-account rule that protects your $250 ending target; see the [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide).
4. Wait for the **delivery truck**. The order lands in the back room.
5. Carry each item out and **place it on the sales floor**. Decor still in the box does not count.
6. Recheck the **Reviews** tab after placing.

Two warnings. Decor raises the ceiling but does not cancel dirt: a decorated store with blood on the floor still loses rating. And because purchases come from store money, do not drain the account you need for traps and planks before the Dentist shift. The [Resource Management Guide](/guides/resource-management) covers that budget split.

## What Does Not Affect the Rating

Community reports say customers ignore unactivated traps and scattered boards, so you do not need to tidy your defenses before opening. What they do react to is gore, limbs, and trash. One Japanese user review adds an important detail: dirt and trash left **outside** the store can also keep dragging the rating down, so the dumpster area and forecourt are part of the job, not a safe dumping ground.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Leaving blood until the end of the shift | Customers see it and the rating drops | Mop visible aisles first |
| Shooting every rat | Shooting reportedly pays a fraction of the live-rat payout | Carry live rats out when calm |
| Buying decor with personal cash | Eats into the $250 surgery target | Use store funds only |
| Buying decor but leaving it boxed | Unplaced decor adds no points | Carry and place it the same shift |
| Cleaning during a rush | Customers queue, service slows, rating suffers | Clean before the first wave or between waves |
| Trusting exact dollar figures | Community numbers vary and are not official | Check your own end-of-shift report |

## Nightly Rating Checklist

- Mop every blood puddle in customer-visible areas
- Bag and dump all limbs and body parts
- Clear litter, bathroom mess, and any trash outside
- Remove rats and roaches; carry live rats to the dumpster
- Restock shelves before the queue builds
- Place any delivered decor on the sales floor
- Check the computer's Reviews tab before the entity phase
- Leave the store clean enough that tomorrow starts at the same rating

## FAQ

### Is five stars actually possible?

**[Unconfirmed]**. Community sources disagree on whether the rating is a star scale or a hidden 0-100 score rendered as stars, and no official source publishes a cap. Aim for steady improvement rather than a specific number.

### Does cleaning pay into personal funds or store funds?

Community guides claim cleanup payouts count toward the $250 personal-cash target, but the exact routing is not officially documented. Check your balance after a cleaning-heavy shift and compare it with the end-of-shift report instead of assuming.

### Do I need to clean in Endless Mode?

The 1 September 2026 patch reworked several Endless Mode systems but did not mention cleaning or rating. The safest assumption is that the same rating logic applies until the Q4 Endless update says otherwise, but that is **[Unconfirmed]**.

## Bottom Line

Clean for the rating, not for the pennies. Mop the blood customers can see, bag the limbs, carry live rats out, and place cheap decor using store funds. That routine protects your customer traffic, your nightly sales goal, and your $250 personal-cash target at the same time.
