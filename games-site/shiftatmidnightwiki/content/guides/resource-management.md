# Shift at Midnight Resource Management — Every Supply, Spent Right

**Shift At Midnight / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Shift at Midnight is a resource game with a jumpsuit on. Stock quotas, delivery boxes, traps, planks, arsenal weapons, and two separate bank accounts — the players who survive all 13 shifts are the ones who budget. This guide covers the resource rules: the store/personal cash split, quota discipline, supply priorities, and the bonus tasks that quietly fund the game's most important purchase.

---

## 1. The Two-Account Rule (The Most Important Rule)

Community walkthroughs are unanimous: **store money and personal funds are separate accounts, and the ending check reads your personal account.**

- **Store money** pays for supplies: traps, planks, Arsenal weapons, and store upkeep.
- **Personal cash** is what you take home — and what the **$250 surgery check** counts for the True Ending.
- **Mixing them** (spending personal cash on store gear) is the most common reason runs fall short.

**The habit:** at the start of every shift, decide which account pays for what. Store expenses come out of store money; personal cash only leaves your pocket for the pet's bills.

---

## 2. Stock and Quota Discipline

| Resource Rule | What's Confirmed |
|---------------|------------------|
| Stock quota | At least **15 items on the front shelves** is the community-cited minimum |
| Nightly money goal | The store enforces a money goal each shift — hit it or face consequences |
| Restock timing | Restock only after sales; delivery boxes arrive via the garage door |
| Overstock | Shelves full of slow sellers block quota progress — stock fast movers first |

**The discipline:** stock once at the start, restock only after sales, and keep the fast-moving items front-facing. The [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide) documents the nightly goals by shift; treat the quota as a timer — every shift under quota is a shift that makes later shifts harder.

---

## 3. The Supply Budget (Store Money)

| Supply | Priority | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Traps | High | Bear traps / tripwires win the entity phase — placement guide in the [Stealth Guide](/guides/stealth-guide) |
| Planks (barricades) | High | Block the entity's routes during the lock-up alert |
| Arsenal weapons | Medium | The gun is your main weapon all campaign — upgrade it in the arsenal, but only when store money allows |
| Ammo | Medium | Runs out at the worst moments; restock after every shift |
| Cleanup supplies | Low | Mop and bags are mandatory; extra cleaning supplies are not |

**The budget rule:** traps and planks first, weapons second. A full arsenal with empty trap slots loses to a boarded-up garage with one good gun.

---

## 4. The Bonus Tasks (Free Money, Everyone Misses Them)

| Shift | Task | Payout |
|-------|------|--------|
| Shift 3 | Pet medicine bill appears — personal cash only | Required for the campaign |
| Shift 4 | **Rat bonus** — throw rats in the trash before the next customer | Steady income |
| Shift 9 | **Roach bonus** — cleared with the mop | Bonus cash |
| Shift 10+ | $250 surgery check revealed | The True Ending gate |

**Why they matter:** the bonus tasks are the difference between a $200 and a $300 personal account — and the $250 check is binary. See the [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide) for the full income strategy by campaign phase.

---

## 5. The Per-Shift Resource Checklist

1. **Before opening:** check the noticeboard, check store money vs. personal cash (Section 1).
2. **Cleanup:** mop, bag, dump, garage door decision.
3. **Stock:** quota minimum (15+ items), fast movers front-facing.
4. **Bonus tasks:** rats and roaches first — they expire with the shift.
5. **Supplies check:** traps placed, planks stocked, ammo counted.
6. **After the entity phase:** restock supplies from store money only.

---

## 6. Common Resource Mistakes

1. **Paying for store gear with personal cash.** The #1 run-killer — the surgery check never forgives.
2. **Ignoring the rat and roach bonuses.** They're free income with a time limit; skipping them is leaving the True Ending short.
3. **Overstocking slow sellers.** Ten boxes of a slow item is quota space you can't use; stock what sells.
4. **Buying arsenal upgrades before traps.** The gun helps, but the entity phase is won with traps and barricades (see the [Survival Strategies Guide](/guides/survival-strategies)).
5. **Hoarding supplies for a "better" shift.** Supplies don't carry value across shifts the way cash does — use them.

## 7. How Resources Carry Into Endless Mode

The Endless Mode beta (see the [Endless Mode Guide](/guides/endless-mode-guide)) keeps the same loop — stock, screen, survive — with survival milestones replacing story milestones. The resource discipline in this guide is what Endless Mode is built on: the two-account rule, the quota rhythm, and the trap budget all carry over unchanged.
