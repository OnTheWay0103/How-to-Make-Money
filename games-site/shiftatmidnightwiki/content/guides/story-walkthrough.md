## Shift At Midnight Story Walkthrough — 13 Nights

Welcome to the night shift. *Shift At Midnight* unfolds across **13 shifts** at a rural 1993 American gas station in Midnight Town. Shifts are randomly generated, so customer names, stories, and layouts vary between runs — but the campaign's scripted beats and its per-shift doppelganger lists are consistent enough to plan around.

This walkthrough covers every shift, the fakes to watch for, the scripted events, and the path to the True Ending. Shift-by-shift details are community-documented and may drift with updates; unconfirmed items are marked.

## How Many Nights Does Shift At Midnight Have?

**13 shifts (nights).** The story campaign always runs exactly 13 randomly generated shifts, and the ending is decided at the vet check after Night 13 — see the [Endings Guide](/guides/endings-guide).

## The Daily Routine

Every shift follows a fixed loop (confirmed by walkthroughs):

1. **Read the noticeboard** for objectives (a gas-pump note joins it from Shift 4)
2. **Clean the previous night's mess** — mop the blood, bag the limbs, dump them outside
3. **Leave the garage door open** for the delivery truck
4. **Stock shelves** from the delivery boxes; restock again after each sale
5. **Serve customers** — scan IDs, bag items, screen for doppelgangers
6. **Survive the entity attack** after the last customer
7. **Catch the bus** — and from Shift 5, board the *correct* one

## Shift 1 — The First Shift

**Fakes**: Nathan Calloway, Julietta Granger

- Real customers **Clyde Dawson** and **Bonnie McCree** arrive — treat them as human
- A **road car lures a monster** — run inside when the event triggers
- **First entity attack** at shift end
- Pick up the **gun** early — it's your main weapon all campaign
- Learn the loop: noticeboard, cleanup, stock, serve, screen

## Shift 2 — Generator Sabotage

**Fakes**: Natasha Lin, Emilia Carter, Norbert

- **Generator sabotage** event: check the generator armed, then shoot the doppelganger waiting back inside
- **Norbert the Gnome appears** for the first time — do not shoot him (see the [Norbert guide](/guides/norbert-the-gnome-guide))
- Cleanup and stocking routine continues

## Shift 3 — The Medicine Bill

**Fakes**: Tommy Delgado (first appearance only)

- Heavier customer flow
- **Pay the pet's medicine bill** — the pet subplot begins
- Good night to practice the full ID check routine; tells are still relatively simple

## Shift 4 — Gas Pumps and Rats

**Fakes**: Keith Hargreaves, Driver (2nd), Martha Collins, Ray Rowland

- **Gas pumping starts** — now customers arrive at the pumps too; the noticeboard gains a pump note
- **Rat bonus task**: throw rats in the trash before the next customer for steady income
- Note the Driver is already a fake — the recurring "Driver" slot is a campaign pattern

## Shift 5 — Blood Rain

**Fakes**: Driver, Anita Varma, Elena Park

- **Blood rain** night — community walkthroughs report the Emergency Arsenal is **unreliable** during blood rain, so the pistol must suffice
- **Break-room painting** event
- **Buy and scatter traps** — you'll need them for the end-of-shift phase on later nights
- From this shift, **board the correct bus** at shift end (empty driver's seat = safe)

## Shift 6 — Forest Limbs

**Fakes**: Jade Sinclair, Ramona Delgado, Harold Brant, Driver

- **Forest limb cleanup** — head out with your **flashlight** to bag the limbs
- **Norbert shows up again** — still don't shoot him

## Shift 7 — The Dentist Arrives

**Fakes**: Driver, Louisa Mari (1st), Herbert, Richard McCree — and the bear is also fake

- **Name trap**: the *first* Louisa Mari is a doppelganger; the *second* one is real
- **Thieves try to skip payment** — shooting beats chasing them
- **The Dentist arrives** — upgraded weapons drive him and the entity off, so have your Arsenal stocked before this shift (see the [Dentist Guide](/guides/dentist-guide))

## Shift 8 — Volume

**Fakes**: Driver, Daria Groves, David Kennedy, Lisa Cannon

- More customer volume, no new entity tricks — a solid night to bank money before the campaign gets expensive

## Shift 9 — The Fake Clyde

**Fakes**: Aria Romero, Clyde Dawson (a fake Clyde)

- **Roach bonus** — cleared with the mop
- **Norbert on a motorbike** — yes, really
- **Upgrade your weapons** — the finale is approaching and threats get faster

## Shift 10 — The Grave Task

**Fakes**: Rudnick Wex, Vivien Marrow, Net Pongsak

- **Blood rain again** — weapons are harder to reach mid-fight; prep the Arsenal before the shift
- **Forest grave task**: fill a bucket and pour liquid into the grave
- **A missing dog poster for "Guillermo"** appears on the noticeboard — **do not chase it**: community investigation confirms the dog cannot be found anywhere, the number can't be called, and it's a red herring (see the [Characters Guide](/guides/characters-guide))
- **After this shift, the pet's $250 surgery is revealed** — the True Ending requirement enters play

## Shift 11 — Cash for Surgery

**Fakes**: Dylan Pike, Agnes Wells, Ethan Voss, Andal Khan (2nd)

- **Name trap**: the *first* Andal Khan is human; the *second* is fake
- **Keep enough cash for surgery** — your personal funds need to clear the $250 line before the finale

## Shift 12 — Doppelgangers of You

**Fakes**: Yasmin Carvalho, Maya Davila, Diana Volkov, Barry Aoki

- **Self-doppelgangers attack** near shift's end — the campaign turns your own face against you
- A **sheriff email arrives offering to cover the surgery** — remember this for the finale (it's a trap)

## Shift 13 — The Swarm

**Fakes**: Swarm of all types

- The final shift throws a **swarm of all doppelganger types** at you
- Shoot until the **Dentist cutscene** triggers, then **run in the opposite direction** until a second cutscene catches you
- In the second cutscene, **Clyde finishes off the Dentist** — whether Clyde survives that encounter is what separates the endings (some early community versions described "a stranger" in this role; current published guides consistently name Clyde)
- Endless Mode beta runs on this same loop if you want more after the credits

## The True Ending

The True Ending requires **$250+ in personal cash**, **kept separate from store funds**, and **no call to Clyde** in the finale — the most common failure is reaching the vet check short on cash. Track your personal balance from Shift 1 and avoid bleeding it into store expenses. Full conditions and checklist: [Endings Guide](/guides/endings-guide).

## Walkthrough Pro Tips

- **Kill fakes before payment** — a completed purchase triggers the 60-second lock-up alert; shooting before payment skips it entirely
- **Learn the name traps** — Shift 7 (Louisa Mari) and Shift 11 (Andal Khan) punish pattern-matching; verify identities on repeat customers
- **The Driver is a recurring fake** — expect a fake Driver most shifts from Shift 4
- **Read the noticeboard every shift** — it can disprove customer stories (a diner the paper says burned down)
- **Stock the Arsenal before Shift 7 and Shift 9** — the walkthroughs recommend upgraded weapons for the Dentist and the late-campaign threats
- **Check the bus every shift from Shift 5** — a driver in the seat is a doppelganger; boarding is one of the fastest deaths in the game
