## Shift At Midnight Story Walkthrough -- 13 Nights

Welcome to the night shift. *Shift At Midnight* unfolds across 13 nights in a single location -- a remote 1990s gas station -- but the threats escalate dramatically as the campaign progresses. This walkthrough covers every night, the key enemies you will face, and strategies to survive until dawn.

The campaign splits into 4 distinct phases, each introducing new mechanics and escalating danger.

## Campaign Phase Overview

| Phase | Nights | Theme | New Threats | Key Milestone |
|-------|--------|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Phase 1 | 1-4 | Onboarding | Basic Doppelgangers, Roaches, Mice | Learn NET Database |
| Phase 2 | 5-8 | Escalation | Silent Doppelgangers, Phantom, Gnomes | Fuel pumps unlocked, first Entity encounter |
| Phase 3 | 9-12 | Siege | Blood Moon events, Demented, Marionette | Multiple threats per night |
| Phase 4 | 13 | Finale | The Dentist | Final survivor encounter |

## Phase 1: Onboarding (Nights 1-4)

The first four nights are the tutorial phase. The game teaches you the basics of store management, customer interaction, and the NET Database system. Threats are minimal, and you can survive with just the brick and baseball bat.

### Night 1 -- The First Shift

**Time**: 10:00 PM -- 6:00 AM
**Threats**: None scripted, 1-2 normal Doppelgangers
**Goals**: Learn register operation, stock shelves, complete first ID check

Night 1 is pure onboarding. Sheriff Clyde stops by to introduce himself and explain the basics. You learn how to:

- Operate the **register** (ring up items, handle cash, calculate change)
- Stock shelves (3 deliveries arrive throughout the night)
- Use the **NET Database** (first ID check tutorial -- the system flags the imposter)
- Interact with the **Shop Terminal** (you cannot afford anything yet)

There are no forced encounters on Night 1. The Doppelganger that appears is scripted to be obvious -- its ID photo does not match the customer's face. Pass the check and you learn the core mechanic.

### Night 2 -- First Real Customer

**New Mechanics**: Prize wheel, urgent tasks unlocked
**Threats**: 2-3 normal Doppelgangers
**Key Event**: Cockroach infestation at 2:00 AM

Your first urgent task spawns at 2:00 AM: kill 8 cockroaches before they spread to the food shelves. Use the brick or your hands to squish them. A failed cockroach task reduces your commission bonus.

The prize wheel appears in the back corner. You get one free spin per night starting Night 2. Prizes range from $1 cash to a free bear trap.

### Night 3 -- Mice Problem

**New Mechanics**: Cleaning station, hose unlocked
**Threats**: 3 normal Doppelgangers, mice infestation
**Key Event**: Mice outbreak at 1:00 AM

Mice are faster than cockroaches. You need the **hose** to flush them out from under shelves. Kill 10 mice in 60 seconds. The hose costs $8 from the Shop Terminal if you did not buy it yet.

Budget tip: Buy the hose on Night 2 to have it ready.

### Night 4 -- Meat Collection

**New Mechanics**: Forest run unlocked
**Threats**: 4 Doppelgangers (2 normal, 1 aggressive)
**Key Event**: Meat collection in the forest (60 seconds, up to $30 bonus)

Night 4 introduces the forest run. At 3:00 AM, a prompt appears: "Something in the woods." You have 60 seconds to run to the forest edge (outside the gas station, past the fuel pumps), collect 5 meat packages, and return. Each package is worth $6, for a maximum bonus of $30.

**Warning**: The first aggressive Doppelganger appears on Night 4. It lunges if you take too long at the register. Flag it quickly in the NET Database.

## Phase 2: Escalation (Nights 5-8)

The difficulty curve steepens. New enemy types appear, the fuel pumps become operational, and you encounter The Entity for the first time.

### Night 5 -- Fuel Pumps

**New Mechanics**: Fuel pump management unlocked
**Threats**: 5 Doppelgangers (varied), first Silent Doppelganger
**Key Event**: Gas delivery at 11:00 PM

Fuel pumps now have customers. You must manage both inside customers and pump customers. Silent Doppelgangers appear -- they do not speak into the microphone. If playing with proximity voice chat, the silence is your clue.

Solo tip: Prioritize inside customers first. Pump customers can wait, but unattended inside customers become aggressive faster.

### Night 6 -- Save the Cat

**New Mechanics**: None
**Threats**: 5-6 Doppelgangers, 1 aggressive variant
**Key Event**: Back room fire at 1:30 AM, cat rescue

This is a critical night for ending outcomes. At 1:30 AM, smoke appears from the back room. You have 60 seconds to rescue the cat inside. Enter the room, grab the cat, and use the hose to extinguish the fire.

Saving the cat affects your endgame epilogue. It does not change the ending type, but the cat's presence or absence changes the emotional tone significantly.

### Night 7 -- The Phantom Arrives

**New Threats**: Phantom (first encounter)
**Key Event**: Phantom stalks the store at 3:00 AM

Your first Phantom encounter is scripted. At 3:00 AM, the lights flicker and a player reports hearing breathing. The Phantom is invisible and attacks from behind.

**Crucial**: Place a bear trap in a narrow hallway. Bait the Phantom to chase you through it. The trap reveals the Phantom for 10 seconds. Hit it with a sledgehammer (one shot) or shotgun (one shot) to kill it.

### Night 8 -- First Entity Encounter

**New Threats**: The Entity (first appearance)
**Key Event**: Blood Moon visual effect, Entity patrols the store

Night 8 is a major milestone. At approximately 2:00 AM, the sky outside turns red (Blood Moon visual). The Entity -- a giant black spider-like creature -- descends from the roof and begins patrolling the store.

#### Entity Survival Checklist for Night 8
- [ ] Board up the back room door
- [ ] Place bear traps at main store entrances
- [ ] Turn on all lights (replace burnt-out bulbs)
- [ ] Assign one player to watch cameras (Janitor role)
- [ ] Keep quiet on proximity voice chat
- [ ] Hide in the back room when Entity approaches

The Entity patrols for approximately 10 real-time minutes before retreating. Survive this encounter and you are ready for Phase 3.

## Phase 3: Siege (Nights 9-12)

The gas station transforms into a siege zone. Multiple threats appear per night, and Blood Moon events become more frequent.

### Night 9 -- The Gnome Arrives

**New Threats**: Gnome, increased Entity activity
**Key Event**: Gnome spawns in the stockroom

A Gnome spawns in the stockroom at the start of Night 9. **Destroy it immediately**. It takes 3 baseball bat hits or 1 sledgehammer hit. If you let it sit for 30 seconds, it starts moving; at 60 seconds, it attacks; at 90 seconds, it calls reinforcements.

Destroying the Gnome drops a **Gnome Key** which unlocks the storage room containing bonus supplies (ammo, bear traps, cash).

### Night 10 -- Double Trouble

**New Threats**: Demented (weeping angel type), Marionette
**Key Threats**: 2 major enemies + Entity possible + Doppelgangers

Night 10 introduces the Demented and the Marionette. You may face both in the same night. This is the first night where the Shop Terminal should stock advanced weapons.

Priority list for Night 10:
1. Kill Gnome instantly (if one spawns)
2. Spot and immobilize Demented (do not look away)
3. Shoot the Marionette string (pistol or SMG)
4. Handle Doppelgangers at the register
5. Survive Entity if it appears

### Night 11 -- Blood Moon Siege

**New Threats**: Max aggression
**Key Event**: Extended Blood Moon (entire night)

Night 11 has an extended Blood Moon that lasts the entire shift. The Entity is active for longer periods. Multiple Doppelgangers arrive simultaneously. Stock up on wooden boards and bear traps before this night.

Co-op tip: Split into teams of 2. One team manages the register and customers; the other handles monster control and repairs.

### Night 12 -- Calm Before the Storm

**New Threats**: None (preparation night)
**Key Event**: Sheriff Clyde calls with a warning

Night 12 is deliberately easier. Fewer enemies, shorter Entity patrols, and more prize wheel tokens. The game is giving you room to prepare for Night 13.

**Use this night to:**
- Stock up on ammo (shotgun shells priority)
- Buy wooden boards and bear traps
- Ensure you have $250+ if aiming for True Ending
- Coordinate with your co-op team on final night strategy

## Phase 4: The Final Shift (Night 13)

### Night 13 -- The Dentist

**New Threats**: The Dentist (boss encounter)
**Duration**: 10:00 PM -- approximately 4:00 AM (shorter shift)

Night 13 is the final encounter. No customers arrive. No Doppelgangers. The only event: The Dentist comes for you.

#### Night 13 Timeline

| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| 10:00 PM | Shift starts. Store is empty. Eerie silence. |
| 10:30 PM | The lights in the parking lot go out |
| 11:00 PM | A car is heard approaching. Then silence. |
| 12:00 AM | The power flickers. Backup generator activates. |
| 1:00 AM | Footsteps on the roof. |
| 2:00 AM | The front doors shake. Barricades take damage. |
| 3:00 AM | The Dentist breaks through. Encounter begins. |

#### The Dentist Encounter

The Dentist is an 8-foot-tall serial killer in a blood-stained dental coat with 1000 HP. He has two phases:

**Phase 1 (75% HP remaining)**: Slow, deliberate movement. He swings a bone saw in a wide arc. Attacks knock off 80% of your health. Kite him through the store while your team shoots from a distance. Shotgun headshots deal the most damage.

**Phase 2 (25% HP remaining)**: Enraged. He moves 40% faster and charges at players. The flamethrower is invaluable here -- the burn damage slows him significantly.

#### Dentist Kill Strategy (Co-op)

1. **Lead Clerk** kites The Dentist through the aisles
2. **Stocker** unloads shotgun from behind shelves
3. **Janitor** watches flanks and places bear traps as fallback
4. All players coordinate on the call prompt (do NOT call if aiming for True Ending)

## Walkthrough Pro Tips

- **Save $250+** to unlock the True Ending path. Spend conservatively on Nights 1-8 and you will reach this threshold naturally
- **Stockpile wooden boards** before Night 9 -- you will need them for Entity defense
- **The prize wheel is worth playing** every night after Night 2. Free money and tools
- **Co-op communication is critical** for Night 8 and Night 13. Use callouts for enemy positions
- **Your first playthrough should aim for Grave Decision** -- the story payoff is better than optimal resource management allows for the True Ending
