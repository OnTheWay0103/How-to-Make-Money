## Shift At Midnight Patch Notes & Update History

*Shift At Midnight* had a famously bumpy road to release — announced for **May 28**, delayed to **June 17**, and finally launching **July 22, 2026**. The developer (solo Australian indie **Bun Muen**, published by **Kwalee**) responded to launch-week problems fast, shipping three updates in the first three days. If you hit a bug that a guide or the Steam discussions still talk about, check whether it was already fixed here — the answer is often yes.

This guide tracks every publicly documented update since launch, what each one changed, and what's coming next.

## July 22, 2026 — Launch & Network Issues Patch

The game launched to strong reviews but immediate **lobby connection problems**. Some players simply could not join friends' sessions, which is fatal for a co-op horror game.

The developer's response was a temporary Steam beta branch so affected players could play immediately while a permanent fix was prepared:

### How to Opt Into the Network Fix Beta Branch

1. Open Steam → right-click *Shift At Midnight* → Properties
2. Go to **Betas** (Game Versions & Betas)
3. Enter the access code: **`networkissues`**
4. Select the **`network-issues-patch`** branch from the dropdown

**Important**: everyone in your co-op group must opt into the same branch for it to work. Mixing versions breaks lobbies — this remains the most common cause of "can't connect" reports even after the official fixes.

This branch is still the community's first recommendation when nothing else fixes connection issues. The permanent fixes shipped in the later patches below.

## July 23, 2026 — Day-One Patch: 6-Player Lobbies

The biggest functional change came one day after launch, in direct response to community feedback:

### 6-Player Lobbies (Previously 3)

- Lobby size expanded from **3 to 6 players** maximum
- The developer was explicit that the game was designed and marketed around **3 players** — playing above that is "explicitly not recommended for a first playthrough" and will likely be chaotic
- The game already supported a 6-player queue for Endless Mode, which made the 3-player cap feel arbitrary

For advice on running larger groups, see the [Co-Op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide) — most of its role-splitting advice still applies, but with 6 players you can afford dedicated jobs for everyone.

### Balance Changes

- **Marionette HP lowered** — the Shift 9 threat is easier to put down, which matters for players who missed the jack-in-the-box rewind timing (see the [Hunt Special Monsters Guide](/guides/hunt-special-monsters-guide))
- **Jack-in-the-box audio made louder** — the game's most infamous ambush is now harder to miss hearing

### Other Changes

- **Matchmaking fix** for Russian players unable to create joinable lobbies
- **Profanity filter removed** from text settings
- The developer confirmed a follow-up patch was already planned for players who crashed and lost the ability to interact with the cursor or buttons

## July 24, 2026 — Hotfix: Lobby Physics & Soft Locks

Two days after launch, a broader hotfix landed addressing the most-reported bugs:

### Stability Fixes

- **Lobby physics**: object physics in good-ping lobbies are no longer laggy
- **Crash rejoin**: players who crash are no longer trapped in the main menu when they try to rejoin
- **Customer conversation soft-lock**: a progression-halting bug while conversing with customers is fixed
- **Shift 10 black screen**: players freezing on a black screen when entering Shift 10 — the campaign's most crash-prone transition — is fixed

### Enemy Fixes

- Monsters no longer get **stuck on indestructible barricades**, closing an exploit where the end-of-shift entity could be permanently kited around a wall
- Specific **monster health values were adjusted/rebalanced** to better fit their encounters (exact numbers were not disclosed)

### Endless Mode

- Fixed the issue preventing **6 players from queueing and playing Endless Mode** properly

If you're playing on an older version, updating fixes all of the above automatically — Steam applies the patch on next launch. For persistent technical problems even after updating, see the [PC Performance & Issues Guide](/guides/pc-performance-guide).

## How to Stay Updated and Report Bugs

- **Steam**: updates install automatically; check the game's announcement feed for patch notes and beta branches
- **Discord**: the official server is the developer's stated channel for bug reports and patch feedback
- **Steam Community Hub**: patch threads are usually pinned shortly after each update

A quick version check before a co-op session saves headaches: make sure everyone is on the same build — mixed Steam/Game Pass versions and one player on the beta branch are both common lobby killers.

The launch-week patch cadence shows how quickly the game has stabilized. For the big-picture question of what the final version will look like, keep an eye on the Q4 2026 roadmap — and for tips on what's already in the game, start with the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide).
