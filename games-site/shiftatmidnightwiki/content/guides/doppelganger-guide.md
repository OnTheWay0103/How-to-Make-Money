## The Art of Detection

In Shift At Midnight, a doppelganger can walk through your gas station doors at any moment. These shapeshifting creatures perfectly mimic human appearance — but they cannot perfectly mimic human behavior. Your job as a night-shift clerk is to spot the cracks in their disguise before they strike.

This guide covers the complete doppelganger identification system, from the 5-question screening protocol to the NET database cross-referencing method.

---

## The 5-Question System

Every customer you serve can be questioned using the register's built-in screening interface. You have exactly **5 questions** per customer before the transaction must complete. Choose wisely.

### Best Question Order

| Priority | Question | Why It Works |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | "Can I see your ID?" | The first and most important check — IDs are the hardest for doppelgangers to fake reliably |
| 2 | "What brings you in tonight?" | Doppelgangers often give vague, rehearsed answers |
| 3 | "Do you have a rewards card?" | A surprisingly effective trap — real regulars say yes |
| 4 | "Is the weather treating you okay?" | Casual small talk throws doppelgangers off script |
| 5 | "Are you from around here?" | Doppelgangers often claim they are new in town regardless of what their ID says |

**Do not** ask questions in the same order every time. Doppelgangers learn and adapt. Vary your sequence.

## Red Flags Reference Table

Memorize these red flags. Each one is a reason to escalate your screening:

| Category | Red Flag | Confidence Level |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| **ID Mismatch** | Name on ID does not match verbal introduction | High |
| **ID Mismatch** | Photo looks slightly different from the person | High |
| **ID Mismatch** | Birth year feels wrong (recent or ancient) | Medium |
| **Occupation** | Claims a job that doesn't exist or sounds absurd | Medium |
| **Purchase History** | Buying only one of every item | Low |
| **Purchase History** | Buying things that don't go together (gas + cereal only) | Medium |
| **Purchase History** | Paying with exact change counted perfectly | Low |
| **Photo** | Eyes in photo look dead/blank | Medium |
| **Photo** | Photo quality doesn't match ID issue date | High |
| **Appearance** | Skin tone or eye color changes during conversation | High |
| **Appearance** | Flickering/glitching visual effect around edges | Very High |
| **Behavior** | Staring at you silently for too long | Medium |
| **Behavior** | Repeating your questions back to you | High |
| **Behavior** | Asking what year it is or what town this is | Very High |
| **Vehicle** | Car outside does not match any known customer | Low |
| **Vehicle** | No vehicle at all (how did they get here at 2 AM?) | Medium |

## NET Database Cross-Referencing

The **NET Database** is your most powerful tool for identifying doppelgangers. Access it from the **Shop Terminal** in the back office. The database cross-references customer information against multiple registries:

### How to Use the NET Database

1. **Start the transaction** at the register and note the customer's name
2. **Ask for their ID** — take note of the ID number
3. **Excuse yourself** — tell them you need to check something in the back
4. **Run time-sensitive**: You have roughly 30 seconds before the customer becomes suspicious. Walk (do not run — running triggers suspicion) to the Shop Terminal.
5. **Enter the ID number** into the NET Database search
6. **Review the results**:
   - **Green result** — ID matches all registries. Customer is likely human.
   - **Yellow result** — Minor discrepancies. Proceed with caution and ask more questions.
   - **Red result** — Major discrepancies. The customer is almost certainly a doppelganger.
7. **Return and decide** — proceed with the sale, initiate panic protocol, or prepare to fight

### Database Fields Checked

- **DMV Records** — Does the photo match their appearance?
- **Employment Registry** — Does their stated job exist and match?
- **Criminal Database** — Any prior incidents at this gas station?
- **Local Resident List** — Have they been seen in town before?

## The Emotion Detector

Unlocked around **Night 5-6**, the Emotion Detector upgrade for your register displays a customer's emotional state as colored waves:

| Color | Emotion | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Green | Calm | Normal customer |
| Blue | Neutral/Sad | Normal range |
| Yellow | Nervous | Suspicious — escalate screening |
| Orange | Agitated | High suspicion — consider panic button |
| Red | Aggressive/Hostile | Almost certainly a doppelganger — prepare for combat |

The Emotion Detector is not foolproof. Some doppelgangers learn to mask their emotional signature after Night 8. Trust the NET Database over the Emotion Detector in the late game.

## Visual Tells

If you are watching carefully, doppelgangers reveal themselves through subtle visual glitches:

- **Brief pixelation** around the edges of their body
- **Slight desync** between their mouth movement and spoken words
- **Facial features shifting** between eye blinks (look away and back quickly)
- **Shadow not matching** their body shape
- **Reflection missing or delayed** in glass surfaces (windows, the register screen)

## Common Doppelganger Tricks

Experienced doppelgangers use specific tactics to evade detection:

- **The Distraction Act** — One doppelganger creates a commotion while another slips through
- **The Regular Routine** — They copy a real regular's appearance and order
- **The Emergency Ploy** — They claim a medical emergency to rush you through the transaction
- **The Quiet One** — They say nothing, answer questions with nods, and avoid eye contact

## Decision Flowchart

Follow this priority when screening a suspicious customer:

1. **ID matches everything** — Complete the sale. They are safe.
2. **Minor flags (1-2 low confidence)** — Ask 5 questions. If answers are consistent, complete sale. If not, use the panic button.
3. **Multiple flags (3+)** — Use NET Database immediately. Prepare for combat.
4. **Red NET result** — Do not engage. Hit the panic button. Use your weapon.
5. **Aggressive Emotion Detector** — Skip the database. Fight or hide. Trust the detector.

## Doppelganger Combat

When identification fails and a doppelganger reveals itself:

- **Night 1-3**: Use a Brick or Baseball Bat. Doppelgangers at this stage are weak.
- **Night 4-6**: Switch to the Shotgun. Doppelgangers gain health and speed.
- **Night 7+**: The Flamethrower is your best friend. Doppelgangers become aggressive and numerous.
- **Always carry a Bear Trap** as backup. A trapped doppelganger cannot attack.

Every doppelganger you correctly identify and neutralize earns a bonus at shift end. Every one you miss puts the store — and your life — at risk.
