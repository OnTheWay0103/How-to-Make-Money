## Item Locations — Weapons, Tools, and Supplies in the Gas Station

A note before anything else: **Shift At Midnight's shifts are randomly generated, and the community has not documented fixed loot spawn tables.** Any guide that lists exact spawn points with percentages and respawn timers is inventing detail the community has not confirmed. What we *do* have is a confirmed list of the items themselves and where the game reliably puts the essentials.

This guide covers every confirmed item, where it comes from, and how the store's supply loop actually works.

## Where Items Come From (Confirmed)

Shift At Midnight has two confirmed item sources:

1. **Fixed stations in the store** — the campaign's essentials live in predictable places
2. **The store computer (Shop Terminal)** — consumables and equipment are *bought*, not looted

Beyond that, randomly generated shifts mean item positions vary run to run. Treat "I found it behind the counter once" stories as useful hints, not spawn tables.

## Confirmed Items and Their Sources

| Item | Where It Comes From | Confirmed Use |
|------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Gun** | Picked up early on **Shift 1** (community reports: kept under the counter) | The campaign's main weapon; kill-before-payment; survives the end-of-shift entity |
| **Mop** | Part of the store's cleanup station | Clean blood; clears the Shift 9 roach bonus |
| **Trash bags** | Part of the store's cleanup station | Bag limbs and dump them outside (every-shift routine; Shift 6 forest cleanup) |
| **Flashlight** | Store supplies | Dark areas and outdoor tasks (Shift 6 forest limbs, Shift 10 grave task) |
| **Traps** | **Bought from the store computer** | Slow or stop the entity and its spawns; used during the 60-second lock-up alert |
| **Planks / wood boards** | Store supplies | Barricade the three doors before the end-of-shift attack |
| **Cola bottles** | Store shelves | Lure enemies with noise (community-reported use) |
| **Arsenal weapons** | **Bought from the store computer** before the night begins | Upgraded weapons; unreliable during blood rain |

## The Store Computer (Shop Terminal)

The computer is your supply depot. Confirmed facts:

- **Traps are bought from the computer** — walkthroughs advise buying them in advance and scattering them around the store
- **Arsenal weapons are bought from the computer** — before the night begins, and needed by Shift 7 (the Dentist) and Shift 9 (escalation)
- Check it at the start of every shift, along with the noticeboard

**Prices and stock rotation are not publicly documented.** Don't trust specific dollar figures from other guides.

## Fixed Stations in the Store

These are the confirmed "always there" parts of the loop:

| Station | What's There | When You Use It |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| **The register** | Purchase scanning, ID scanning, question prompts | Every customer |
| **The store computer** | NET Database, Shop Terminal purchases | Every shift |
| **The noticeboard** | Shift objectives, warnings, news that can disprove customer stories | Start of every shift |
| **Cleanup station** | Mop, trash bags | Every shift's cleanup routine |
| **The garage** | Delivery truck arrives; leave the door open | Every shift (deliveries) |
| **The gas pumps** | Pump duty with vehicle checks | From Shift 4 |
| **The bus stop** | Shift-end departure | Every shift from Shift 5 (check the driver's seat) |

## The Delivery Loop (Your Real "Loot")

The most reliable item flow in the game is the delivery loop, not floor spawns:

1. **Leave the garage door open** at shift start
2. The **delivery truck** arrives with stock
3. **Stock the shelves** from the boxes (community-cited minimum: **15 items on the front shelves**)
4. **Restock after each sale** to keep the shelves full

Deliveries and restocking keep store income flowing — which matters because store money (not personal cash) pays for traps, planks, and Arsenal weapons. The supply loop and the money loop are the same loop. See the [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide) for the fund-separation rules.

## Pro Tips for Item Gathering

1. **Buy early, buy often** — traps bought from the computer in advance are the single best prep investment; don't wait until the hunt starts
2. **Blood rain changes the rules** — the Arsenal is unreliable during blood rain (first around Shift 5), so stock traps and planks before those shifts
3. **Carry the gun everywhere** — from Shift 1, it's the confirmed workhorse; the whole survival phase assumes you have it
4. **Flashlight for outdoor shifts** — Shift 6 (forest limbs) and Shift 10 (grave task) are confirmed flashlight tasks; check you have it before heading out
5. **Co-op sharing** — in co-op, the store computer's purchases benefit the whole team; coordinate who buys what at shift start

## Unconfirmed Claims to Ignore

- **Fixed spawn tables with percentages** — no reliable public documentation exists
- **Respawn timers** (per-item cooldowns) — not documented
- **Crafting materials and workbenches** — no confirmed crafting system
- **Quest items with specific unlock requirements** (keys, folders, safe codes) — no reliable documentation
- **"Better loot on later nights"** — unverified
