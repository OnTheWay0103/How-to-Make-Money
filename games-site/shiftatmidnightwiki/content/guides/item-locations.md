## Item Locations — Weapons, Ammo, Crafting Materials, and Consumables

Knowing where to find items is as important as knowing how to use them. Shift at Midnight features a dynamic loot system where most item spawns are fixed but availability changes based on the night, player count, and cumulative actions. This guide maps every key item location in the gas station and surrounding area.

---

## Item Spawn Point Categories

Items in Shift at Midnight fall into three spawn categories:

| Category | Behavior | Examples |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Static** | Always in the same location every shift | Bricks, register items, stockroom supplies |
| **Rotating** | Cycles between 2-3 fixed locations per night | Ammo boxes, healing items, tools |
| **Dynamic** | Spawns based on player actions or threat level | Gnomes, phantom drops, boss loot |

## Consumable Farming Routes

### Optimal Route 1: Interior Loop (3 minutes)

Best for: Quick supply run at shift start

1. Start at **back office** — Check cabinet for sanity pills, first aid box for bandages
2. Move to **stockroom** — Check tool bench, top shelf for ammo, bottom shelf for bricks
3. Enter **janitor's closet** — Check shelf for tools
4. Cross to **bathroom** — Check trash can for ammo, toilet area for brick
5. End at **register** — Check under counter for brick and ammo drawer

**Expected yield**: 2-3 consumables, 1 tool, 1-2 bricks

### Optimal Route 2: Exterior Loop (5 minutes)

Best for: Mid-shift supply run when store is calm

1. Start at **gas pumps** — Check for gas can, metal scraps
2. Walk to **dumpster** — Collect bricks (3), metal scraps (2-3)
3. Check **red mailbox** — Flashlight battery
4. Investigate **blue car trunk** (Night 4+) — Tire iron
5. Check **broken vending machine** — Rare ammo spawn
6. Search **wood pile** — 4-6 wood planks
7. End at **red car trunk** — Rare First Aid Kit chance

**Expected yield**: 3-4 bricks, 2-3 metal, 4-6 wood, optional rare items

### Optimal Route 3: Harvest Loop (8 minutes)

Best for: Late-shift when urgent tasks are active

1. Respond to **cockroach spawns** — $0.50 each (locations: stockroom, bathroom, back office)
2. Check **mouse spawns** — $1.00 each (locations: stockroom shelves, behind register)
3. Complete **meat collection** in forest (60-second timer) — $30 bonus
4. Clean **blood stains** in store — $0.10 each
5. Return to **stockroom workbench** — Craft tools from collected materials

**Expected yield**: $10-40 bonus income, crafting materials

## Pro Tips for Item Gathering

1. **Learn the sound cues** — Items like ammo boxes and tools have distinct audio when nearby. A metallic clink indicates metal scraps; a paper rustle indicates ammo.
2. **Check behind stockroom shelves** — Items often clip through shelves visually. Walk behind and press E on every surface.
3. **Night determines loot quality** — Later nights (9+) have better loot tables. Save rare materials for boss preparation.
4. **Co-op item sharing** — Items picked up by one player are visible to all. Coordinate who picks up what.
5. **Do not hoard materials** — Craft tools at the workbench immediately. Materials in your inventory can be lost on death.
