# How to Get the Chainsaw in Shift At Midnight (Locked and Loaded)

The **Chainsaw** is the newest weapon in Shift At Midnight, added in the **September 1, 2026** patch (Build 25057748). It is not a hidden pick-up and it is not a reward for a secret ending — it is a **purchasable melee weapon** that appears in the Weapons Arsenal, and it is now **required for the LOCKED AND LOADED achievement**. That combination has made it the most-asked-about item in the game since the update.

This guide covers where to buy it, what it is good for, what it is bad against, and how to plan the full LOCKED AND LOADED checklist without wasting shifts.

**Accuracy note (October 2026):** the Chainsaw's existence, its September 1 patch, its purchasable status and its requirement for LOCKED AND LOADED are confirmed by the official update announcement. The purchase location and combat behaviour are community-documented. Its price, keybinds and exact damage values are **not** published and are marked **[Unconfirmed]**.

---

## What the September 1 Update Added

The September 1 patch was the largest content drop since launch. The official announcement headline is **"30 NEW CUSTOMERS + MORE,"** and the patch also added:

- **30 new customers** to Story and Endless modes.
- A **purchasable Chainsaw**, now required for LOCKED AND LOADED.
- **Endless Mode security cameras** and pet purchasing.
- **Restored patience** in Endless Mode.
- **2 bear traps and 2 planks** for the first shift.
- Three rather than two additional purchasable bear traps.
- Endless-mode report and email changes, plus bug and crash fixes.

The Chainsaw is therefore part of a broader quality-of-life patch, not a standalone weapon DLC. If your game is up to date, it is already in the build.

---

## Where to Buy the Chainsaw

The Chainsaw is bought, not found. The community-documented route is:

1. **During regular store hours**, walk to the **in-store computer**.
2. Open the **Weapons Arsenal** section.
3. Buy the **Chainsaw** with in-game currency.
4. It is a **one-time purchase** — no ammunition is required.

Two details matter:

- **It appears in the Weapons Arsenal from Shift 1** after the patch. You do not need to reach a late shift to buy it.
- **If you buy it before a Hunt starts**, it is distributed through the **Emergency Arsenal** along with your other pre-purchased weapons.

### How much does it cost?

No official price has been published. [Unconfirmed] The community guide does not give a number either. Treat any exact dollar figure as unverified. The important budgeting question is not the number but the account: purchases can come from personal funds or the store budget depending on timing, and the exact rule is **[Unconfirmed]**. Keep the two-account discipline from the money guide — store gear should come out of store money whenever possible, because the True Ending check reads personal cash.

### What if it is not in the Arsenal?

- Confirm the game is updated to the September 1 build or later.
- Check the **Weapons Arsenal** section, not the consumables or traps tab.
- Try a fresh shift; the Arsenal inventory is presented at shift start.
- If you are in Endless Mode, remember that mode's Arsenal and report screens differ from Story Mode.

## How the Chainsaw Works

The community description is specific:

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Type | Melee weapon |
| Ammo | None — one-time purchase |
| Damage | High, close range |
| Targets | Ground-based enemies during Hunts |
| Weak against | Floating enemies, notably the Marionette |
| Best use | Finishing enemies that break barricades, or targets held by a Bear Trap |

The intended combo is simple: **trap first, chainsaw second**. A Bear Trap holds a ground enemy in place, and the Chainsaw delivers high close-range damage without consuming ammo. Against an Entity or Demented that has broken through a barricade, the Chainsaw gives you a close-range answer that does not compete with your firearm ammo.

The limitation is equally important. The **Marionette ignores ground-based attacks**, so the Chainsaw cannot carry that fight. Keep a firearm for floating enemies and treat the Chainsaw as a ground-threat tool, not a replacement for your gun.

Controls, attack speed, range and exact damage values are **[Unconfirmed]**. Practice the swing timing in a low-risk shift before relying on it during a Hunt.

## Is the Chainsaw Worth Buying?

Yes, for two reasons.

**First, the achievement.** LOCKED AND LOADED requires the Chainsaw. If you are chasing 100%, the purchase is mandatory.

**Second, the ammo economy.** A no-ammo melee weapon that deals high damage to ground enemies is a useful backup when your firearm is empty or when you are saving ammo for a floating target. It is not a primary weapon, but it fills a real gap in the survival phase.

It is less useful if you already run a stocked Arsenal and never run dry. In that case, buy it for the achievement and keep it as a barricade-break backup.

## The Full LOCKED AND LOADED Checklist

The official patch note confirms the Chainsaw is required. The full checklist below is community-documented and should be verified against your in-game achievement tracker:

| Category | Items |
|----------|-------|
| **Firearms** | Pistol (default), SMG, Shotgun, Flamethrower |
| **Melee** | Brick, Baseball Bat, Sledgehammer, **Chainsaw** |
| **Traps** | Bear Trap, Stun Mine, Flame Trap |

Notes from the community guide:

- The **Brick, Baseball Bat and Sledgehammer are collectibles**, not purchases. Search the map thoroughly for them.
- The **Chainsaw is the only melee weapon you must buy.**
- **Traps are purchased over multiple shifts** — do not try to buy them all in one night.
- One source reports the achievement tracks melee weapons only, while others include firearms and traps. The official patch note only confirms the Chainsaw requirement. Treat the full list as community-reported and check your tracker. [Unconfirmed]

### A realistic purchase plan

1. **Shift 1:** buy the Chainsaw if you can afford it; it is available from the start.
2. **Shifts 2–4:** buy one trap per shift, starting with the Bear Trap.
3. **Shifts 5–7:** finish the trap set and pick up any missing melee collectibles.
4. **Mid-campaign:** buy the remaining firearms when store budget allows.
5. **Before the final shifts:** confirm every item is owned, not just unlocked in the shop.

The achievement is a budgeting challenge more than a skill challenge. Spreading purchases across shifts keeps your trap and plank supply intact for the Hunts.

## Using the Chainsaw in Co-Op

Co-op adds a coordination question: does each player need their own Chainsaw, or does one purchase arm the team? The Emergency Arsenal distributes pre-purchased weapons to players, but whether the Chainsaw is shared or per-player is **[Unconfirmed]**.

Practical approach: have one player buy it first and confirm whether it appears for everyone in the Emergency Arsenal. If it does not, the other players buy their own. Do not assume a single purchase covers a three-player team.
