## Tips for the Hardest Achievements

### Silenced (Kill Shrieking Doll Before It Screams)

The Shrieking Doll appears randomly during night shifts. It looks like an ordinary doll on the shelf but animates when approached. **You have roughly 3 seconds** before it screams and alerts nearby enemies.

**Strategy:** Carry the **Baseball Bat** as your default melee. When you spot a doll that was not there before, equip the bat immediately and **heavy attack** — do not wait to confirm it is moving. The heavy swing kills it before its scream animation finishes.

### Freed (Bear Trap a Demented or Weeping Angel)

Both the Demented and Weeping Angel enemies are fast. You must **predict their path** rather than react.

**Strategy:** Place the bear trap in a **doorway or corridor choke point**. Then make noise (drop an item or speak briefly) to attract the enemy. Back through the trapped doorway. The enemy pathing leads them directly into the trap. This works best on Night 5-7 when these enemies start appearing regularly.

### Relentless (Survive a Hunt in Under 30 Seconds)

When a hunt begins, the game enters a survival phase. You must avoid taking damage for 30 seconds while the enemy actively searches for you.

**Strategy:** The back room is your safest bet. When you hear the hunt music start, **immediately sprint to the storage area** and crouch behind the tallest shelf stack. Do not move, do not speak (the Entity hears microphone input), and do not use items. Wait for the music to fade. This achievement triggers as soon as 30 seconds pass without damage.

You can also use **tripwires** to slow the pursuing enemy, giving you more time to reach a hiding spot.

### Last Performance (Kill the Marionette)

The Marionette is a late-game boss enemy controlled by strings. It attacks in predictable patterns but hits hard.

**Strategy:** Use the **Shotgun** for this fight. The Marionette pauses between attack strings — during these pauses, fire 2-3 shots at its center mass. **Bear traps** placed before the fight can immobilize it for easy damage windows. Do not use the Flamethrower here — it deals too little burst damage and the Marionette closes distance fast.

### Overachiever (All Urgent Tasks in One Night)

This is one of the hardest achievements because urgent tasks appear **randomly** and you cannot control which ones spawn.

**Strategy:** Play on **Night 2 or 3** when enemy threats are lower. Skip all non-urgent activities. The moment an urgent task appears, drop everything and complete it. You need 3-4 tasks to spawn in a single night, which is luck-dependent. Save before the shift and reload if you get fewer than 3 tasks.

### Silent Night (No Speaking)

The Entity hears microphone input, so this achievement is easier than it sounds. Simply **mute your microphone** at the start of the shift. The achievement tracks voice input — if your mic picks up any background noise, it does not count. Use push-to-talk or a hardware mute switch to guarantee success.

### Ghost (Survive Entity Without Detection)

This requires **zero interaction** with the Entity. Use **motion alarms** to track its position. When the alarm triggers, move to the opposite side of the store and crouch. The Entity patrols a set route — learn the pathing patterns on your current night and stay clear.

## 100% Completion Order

If you are aiming for full achievement completion, tackle them in this order:

1. **Playthrough 1 (Normal)** — Focus on the 13-night story. Unlock all ending-related achievements, Night Shift Veteran, and basic combat achievements naturally.
2. **Playthrough 2 (Money Focus)** — Go for True Ending ($250+ saved). Unlock Shopkeeper, ID Expert, Clean Sweep, and prize wheel achievements.
3. **Cleanup Run** — Target specific hard achievements: Overachiever, Silenced, Relentless. Use Night 2-3 for easier conditions.
4. **Pet Run** — Focus entirely on pet ailments and rat collection for Animal Lover, Veterinarian, and Rat King.

## Achievement Tracker Tips

- **Use the in-game ledger** to track your savings progress toward True Ending
- **Prize wheel progress** carries between playthroughs — you do not need all 3 jackpots in one run
- **Enemy kill counts** are cumulative across all playthroughs
- **You can replay individual nights** after completing the campaign to farm specific achievements

Good luck, and remember — **the Entity is always listening**.
