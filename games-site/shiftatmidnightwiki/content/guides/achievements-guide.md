## Shift At Midnight Achievements Guide

Shift At Midnight launched on **July 22, 2026** on Steam and Xbox Game Pass with a full achievement/trophy suite. Here's the honest state of the documentation: **the official achievement list is not yet reliably documented in public sources.** Some third-party guides publish named lists — treat those names and unlock conditions as community-reported and unverified until they're confirmed against the in-game list.

What this guide can do reliably: tell you the *kinds* of goals the community reports the achievements tracking, and how to plan a 100% run around the game's confirmed mechanics. Before chasing anything, check the in-game achievement tracker (and your platform's list) to confirm the current names.

## Planning a 100% Run

Without a confirmed list, plan a completion run around the confirmed mechanics — you'll cover most goals naturally:

### Run 1: Story + True Ending (Normal)

- Play the full 13-shift campaign
- Track personal cash separately from store funds from Shift 1 (True Ending discipline)
- Kill fakes before payment (skips alerts — faster and cleaner)
- This run covers story, ending, screening, and survival goals simultaneously

### Run 2: Goal Farming

- Speedruns are a viable farming pattern: the community's fast loop is register, one restock, minimal cleaning, screen only flagged customers
- Replaying specific shifts (Shift 2's generator sabotage, Shift 7's thieves and Dentist, Shift 12's self-doppelgangers) hits the scripted-event goals
- If a goal is count-based (doppelgangers killed, traps used), shift replay is the reliable way to grind it

### Cleanup: The Tricky Ones

- **False-positive goals** (if they exist) need deliberate mistakes — do them in a throwaway run so you don't jeopardize the True Ending playthrough
- **Silent goals** (if they exist): play with your mic muted — audio discipline is a confirmed mechanic, and muting is the reliable way to satisfy anything tied to it
- **Luck-based goals** (if any): the 60-second alert and Blood Moon events are the confirmed random-ish events; replay until they appear

## What NOT to Trust

- **Named achievement lists with exact conditions** — no reliable public source exists yet; names vary between guides
- **Achievement-tied rewards** (unlocks, skins, bonuses) — nothing official documented
- **Difficulty claims** ("Hard", "Easy" labels) — invented by guide authors

## Achievement Tracker Tips

- **Use the in-game ledger/save info** to track your personal cash toward the True Ending — confirmed as important, and it doubles as progress tracking
- **Campaign progress carries the story** — the game's save structure (13 shifts) means you can replay individual shifts to farm specific goals without losing the run
- **Co-op counts**: goals completed in co-op sessions are reported to count, though official confirmation is pending — coordinate with your team if a goal needs specific events
