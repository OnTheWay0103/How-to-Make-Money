# Shift At Midnight Endless Mode — What's Live Now and What's Coming

The 13-shift story campaign is the heart of Shift At Midnight, but it is not the only way to play. The developers have been running an **Endless Mode beta** since launch, and a **major free update planned for Q4 2026** will fully release it alongside new content. This guide collects everything currently confirmed about Endless Mode, separates it from what's still unconfirmed, and explains what it means for your playtime.

## The Current Situation

As of the launch version (released **July 22, 2026**), here is the confirmed state of Endless Mode:

- **Endless Mode is in beta.** The mode exists and is playable, but the developers describe it as not yet finished.
- **Full release is planned for Q4 2026** as part of a **major free update**.
- The update will add **new customers, weapons, traps, and monsters** alongside the full Endless Mode release.

That is the official roadmap in a nutshell: Endless Mode now (beta), Endless Mode fully released plus new content later this year. Beyond that, public documentation of the beta's exact systems is thin, so this guide is honest about what's verified and what isn't.

## How Endless Mode Works (Confirmed + Community Reporting)

The mode's core premise is straightforward and confirmed: **Endless Mode removes the 13-shift story arc and keeps the gas station loop going for as long as you can survive it.**

What that loop looks like, based on how the main game works (and community reporting on the beta):

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Core loop | The same as the campaign: screen customers, scan IDs, cross-reference the NET database, restock shelves, handle deliveries |
| Threats | Doppelgangers keep coming; the end-of-shift entity attack and lock-up alerts remain part of the loop |
| Progression | Replaces story milestones with survival milestones — how many shifts you complete, how many doppelgangers you catch |
| Player count | Same as the main game: solo or up to 3 players in online co-op |
| Randomization | Each shift is randomly generated in the base game, and this carries into the endless format |

**What is not yet documented** (treat as unconfirmed): the exact unlock conditions for the beta, the difficulty curve, whether there are wave-based segments, and whether Endless Mode has its own score or leaderboard system. Community reports vary, so we are not printing specifics we can't verify.

## What the Q4 2026 Update Adds (Confirmed Roadmap)

The developers have publicly stated that the full Endless Mode release comes with:

- **New customers** — new faces (and new fakes) to screen at the register.
- **New weapons** — additions to the Emergency Weapons Arsenal you buy from the store computer.
- **New traps** — more options for the barricade-and-trap phase of end-of-shift attacks.
- **New monsters** — new threats for both the customer-screening and survival phases.

The exact names and details of these additions have not been announced in detail, so this guide deliberately avoids listing them.

## Why Endless Mode Matters for Your Progress

Even if you are a campaign-focused player, Endless Mode is worth understanding:

1. **It's a free update, not DLC.** If you own the game (or play via Game Pass), the Q4 2026 update costs nothing.
2. **It reuses skills you already have.** The screening routine you build in the campaign — five questions, ID cross-reference, kill-before-payment — is the same routine the endless format tests at higher repetition.
3. **It's the best practice ground.** With the story pressure gone, Endless Mode is a low-stakes place to learn the doppelganger tells and the entity-survival loop. See the [Doppelganger Guide](/guides/doppelganger-guide) and [Entity Survival Guide](/guides/entity-survival-guide) for those fundamentals.

## Tips for Playing the Endless Format Now (Beta)

Since the beta runs on the same core loop, the campaign strategies carry over. A few priorities that matter even more when there is no story ending to reach:

- **Build a routine, not a reaction.** In an endless format, exhaustion sets in after several shifts. The players who survive longest are the ones who run the same checklist every shift: noticeboard → clean up → leave the garage door open → stock → serve → scan every ID → barricade before the entity phase.
- **Kill doppelgangers before payment.** Completing a fake's purchase triggers the 60-second lock-up alert. Shooting before payment skips the alert entirely — a habit that saves you shift after shift.
- **Keep store money and personal funds separate.** Even in endless play, managing your personal cash matters if the mode inherits the campaign's pet-surgery check. Until the full release, treat fund separation as good hygiene rather than a confirmed requirement.
- **Upgrade the Arsenal when you can.** Weapons are bought from the store computer before the night begins. A stocked Arsenal makes the end-of-shift entity phase dramatically easier — and community walkthroughs note the Arsenal is unreliable during blood-rain nights, so prepare before those shifts.

## How the Endless Loop Differs From the Campaign

The campaign gives every shift a scripted skeleton: known fake lists, known set-piece events, a known ending checkpoint. Endless Mode removes that skeleton, and the difference changes how you play:

- **No story milestones.** There's no Shift 7 Dentist to prepare for, no Shift 10 reveal, no $250 ending check (at least, none confirmed). The targets become your own: how many clean shifts in a row, how many fakes caught, how long the store survives.
- **Pressure is cumulative.** In the campaign, a bad shift is a setback on a 13-shift arc. In an endless format, every missed fake and every failed quota raises the baseline for the next shift — community reports on the beta describe a snowball effect where the store gets progressively harder to keep stocked and funded.
- **The routine becomes the content.** The campaign hides variety behind scripted beats; Endless Mode exposes the loop itself. If you don't enjoy the screening-and-stocking rhythm for its own sake, the endless format will not change your mind.

That last point is worth being honest about: Endless Mode is for players who love the loop, not the story.

## What to Practice Now for the Q4 Release

The full Endless Mode release in Q4 2026 adds new customers, weapons, traps, and monsters — but the confirmed roadmap doesn't change the fundamentals. Whatever ships, these are the skills that will carry you:

1. **The screening routine under fatigue.** The ID check loop (scan, inspect, cross-reference, question, decide) is the core skill. Practice running it identically every customer — in an endless format, consistency beats cleverness.
2. **Kill-before-payment as reflex.** Every fake that pays adds a 60-second lock-up alert. In an endless run, a shift that racks up alerts snowballs into a store you can't defend. Make the no-alert shift the baseline.
3. **Trap economy.** Traps are bought from the store computer and scattered in advance. The campaign teaches you to buy them; an endless run teaches you to budget them across many shifts. Track what you placed where.
4. **Audio discipline.** The entity tracks audio; the Dentist punishes noise too. In a long session, push-to-talk habits are the difference between a quiet night and a discovered one.
5. **Fund separation.** Store money vs. personal cash matters for the campaign's ending. Until the Q4 update's economy is documented, keeping the two accounts separate is the safe habit regardless of what Endless Mode's own systems turn out to be.
