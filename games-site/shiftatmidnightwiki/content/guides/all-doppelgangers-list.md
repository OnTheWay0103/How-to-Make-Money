## The Complete Doppelganger List

Every night at the Midnight Town gas station, the NET Database and the community have flagged a roster of repeat offenders. Some of these creatures are so memorable that players have given them names — the same ones, walking in through the same door, shift after shift.

This guide collects the **named doppelgangers** documented by community guides and walkthroughs, the **specific tell** that exposes each one, and the shift each tends to appear on. A warning before the list: **the game mixes real customers in with fakes.** A name on this list is not a kill order — the doppelganger wearing the name will always slip on the detail below, and the human customer won't. Verify the tell before you shoot; killing an innocent person docks your pay.

For the full identification system — the question pool, the NET Database routine, and the general visual tells — see the [Doppelganger Guide](/guides/doppelganger-guide). This guide is the roster.

### Notes on the table

- **Tommy Delgado** appears in his own walkthroughs under two guises — the sister tell is the one players report missing most often, because it requires actually reading the database entry for Catherine rather than just checking the customer's own file.
- **Harold Brant's** age trick is the classic "numbers contradiction" — it's why the [Doppelganger Guide](/guides/doppelganger-guide) tells you to cross-check **occupation and birth date** against the database before anything else.
- **The Anomaly Lens** customers are the reason some community guides recommend keeping the lens equipped as a second tool even after the Emotoscope unlocks — fakes that pass every ID check can still be caught on camera.

## The Patterns Behind the List

Three recurring patterns are worth internalizing:

### 1. The Driver is a recurring fake

From Shift 4 onward, expect a **fake Driver** most shifts. The recurring Driver slot is one of the campaign's most reliable tells — when a Driver walks in, run the full check on them without assuming either way, but expect the fake.

### 2. The name traps (Shift 7 and Shift 11)

- **Shift 7 — Louisa Mari:** the *first* Louisa Mari is a doppelganger; the *second* one is the real customer.
- **Shift 11 — Andal Khan:** reversed — the *first* Andal Khan is human; the *second* is the fake.

These exist to punish pattern-matching. If you kill on the first sighting of a name, the game will show you exactly why that's wrong.

### 3. The deceased and the impossible

The single most common tells in the roster are **dead people claiming to be alive** (Nathan Calloway, Tommy Delgado's sister, Agnes Wells's adult disguise) and **facts that cannot be true** (Harold Brant's 40 years of work at 39, Elise Hart's sunny beach at midnight). Run the alive/dead check first on every customer — it's the fastest filter in the game.

## What the List Does NOT Cover

- **Norbert the Gnome** fails his scan with "Fake ID. Scan Unsuccessful." but is *not* a doppelganger — he's a harmless prankster, and shooting him escalates his pranks. See the [Norbert Guide](/guides/norbert-the-gnome-guide).
- **Self-doppelgangers** (Shift 12) attack near shift's end and aren't customers at the register — they're covered in the [Story Walkthrough](/guides/story-walkthrough).
- **Unlisted fakes** appear every shift thanks to random generation. The named roster is the *known* part of the pool; the general identification routine still applies to every stranger who walks in.

## Final Checklist Before You Shoot

1. **Read the database entry** — alive/dead check first, then occupation, appearance, birth date
2. **Find the specific tell** — for the names above, one of the tells in the table *will* be wrong
3. **Stack your evidence** — the tell plus at least one more contradiction (a scan, an answer, a physical detail) before you draw
4. **Shoot before payment** — a completed purchase triggers the 60-second lock-up alert and the monster form; killing before payment skips it entirely
5. **When in doubt, let them leave** — a missed fake costs you a hunt; a shot at an innocent costs you money and the shift

Master the roster and the game's most dangerous customers stop being strangers. Want the survival side of the equation? See the [Entity Survival Guide](/guides/entity-survival-guide) for what happens when one slips through anyway.
