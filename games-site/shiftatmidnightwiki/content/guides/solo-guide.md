## Shift At Midnight Solo Guide: Tips & Strategy for Playing Alone

Shift At midnight supports **1-3 player co-op**, and playing solo is a completely different experience from running with a team. You shoulder every responsibility — stocking shelves, checking IDs, cleaning messes, and fighting monsters — with no backup. But solo play also means **all the income is yours** and there is zero risk of miscommunication. This guide covers everything you need to survive and thrive alone.

## Solo Difficulty Overview

Playing Shift At Midnight solo is **harder than co-op**, but very much doable. Here is what changes:

- **No role specialization** — you must do every job yourself
- **Every doppelganger is your problem** — you are the only ID checker
- **Entity encounters are more dangerous** — no teammate to distract or rescue
- **Task management is relentless** — customers, stock, cleaning, all on you
- **Death ends the shift** — no revival from teammates

However, solo play has one major advantage: **every dollar earned is yours**. No splitting commission, no shared prize wheel tokens. A solo playthrough that masters the systems below actually hits the $250 True Ending threshold faster than most co-op runs.

## The Solo Prioritization System

You cannot do everything at once. Here is the priority order that works for solo play:

1. **Customers first** — serve them before anything else
2. **Identify doppelgangers** — use the NET Database on every ID
3. **Stock shelves** — full shelves drive commission income
4. **Clean messes** — lowest priority

### Why Customers Come First

Every customer is potential commission income. **Ignoring a customer means losing $1-2 in commission** and risking a complaint. In co-op, the Lead Clerk handles this. Solo, you must drop whatever you are doing to serve.

The trick is to **serve in batches**. Let 2-3 customers queue, serve them all, then run to stock shelves while the next batch forms. You waste less time running back and forth.

## Solo Doppelganger Detection

In co-op, the Lead Clerk runs the register while other players watch for suspicious behavior. Solo, **you are the only ID checker**. You cannot afford to miss a single doppelganger.

Use the **NET Database** on **every single customer**. The 5-question system is your only reliable tool:

| Question | What to Check |
|----------|---------------|
| Identity Match | Does the photo match the customer? |
| Purchase History | Recent purchases consistent with profile? |
| Behavior Flag | Any unusual behavior reported? |
| Emotion Reading | Use the emotion detector |
| Cross-Reference | Check against known doppelganger patterns |

**Pro tip:** Doppelgangers are slightly more likely to appear during **later shifts (Night 7+)** and after **power outages**. Be extra vigilant during these windows.

## Entity Survival Solo

The Entity — a giant black spider-like creature — is the most dangerous threat in solo play. Without a teammate to draw aggro or rescue you, survival depends entirely on preparation.

### Entity Countermeasures

- **Stay silent** — The Entity hears microphone input. Do not speak.
- **Use tripwires** — Place them in corridors to buy running time
- **Bear traps** — One good trap can stop the Entity in its tracks
- **Motion alarms** — Early warning gives you time to hide
- **Hide in the back room** — The Entity does not patrol the storage area

### Weapon Priority for Entity Encounters

| Weapon | Effectiveness | Why |
|--------|---------------|-----|
| Shotgun | High | Stopping power, one blast buys escape time |
| SMG | Medium | Sustained fire keeps the Entity staggered |
| Baseball Bat | Medium | Reliable melee no ammo needed |
| Sledgehammer | Medium | Slow but high damage per hit |
| Pistol | Low | Better for doppelgangers than the Entity |

## Task Management Alone

With no Janitor or Stocker, you must triage tasks constantly:

- **Cockroaches** — Kill only if they are in your path. Do not hunt them.
- **Mice** — Low priority unless they are near the register area
- **Spills** — Clean only during lulls between customer rushes
- **Meat collection (forest)** — High reward but **extremely dangerous solo**. Only attempt if you have tripwires set between the store and the forest.

### When to Call It Quits

Some nights are not worth saving. If you lose **more than $30 from penalties** (shooting humans, failing urgent tasks, getting robbed), consider accepting death and restarting. A bad save file snowballs — better to restart Night 1 fresh than limp into Night 13 under $250.

## Solo Advantages

Playing alone is not all bad. Here is what you gain:

- **All income is yours** — $10 salary + 10% commission + bonuses, no splits
- **No miscommunication** — You know exactly what you have checked
- **Perfect information** — No teammate accidentally serving a doppelganger
- **Consistent pacing** — You control the speed of every interaction
- **No voice chat needed** — The Entity cannot hear you if you never speak

## Best Solo Weapons

Without teammates to cover your back, weapon choice matters more:

1. **Shotgun** — Best all-rounder for solo. Kills doppelgangers quickly and staggers the Entity.
2. **Baseball Bat** — Silent, infinite use, good for cockroaches and small threats.
3. **Sledgehammer** — Slow but devastating. Ideal if you prefer bait-and-smash tactics.
4. **Pistol** — Backup weapon. Keep holstered for emergency doppelganger kills.
5. **SMG** — High ammo consumption. Only use in co-op; solo you run out too fast.

**Do not use the Flamethrower solo.** It is too easy to set the store on fire, and with no teammate to call for help, a burning store is a run-ending disaster.

## No AI Companions

Shift At Midnight does **not** provide AI companions for solo players. There are no bots, no NPC assistants, no automated clerks. Every task is yours alone. This is a deliberate design choice — the game wants you to feel the weight of running a gas station by yourself at midnight.

If you find solo too stressful, the game supports **1-3 player co-op**, and even one additional player dramatically reduces the workload. But for those who want the challenge — and the full paycheck — solo mode is a rewarding experience that tests your prioritization, detection, and survival skills to their limit.

## Final Solo Checklist

- [ ] Serve customers in batches (2-3 at a time)
- [ ] Run NET Database checks on every ID
- [ ] Stock shelves during lulls
- [ ] Place tripwires before Night 7
- [ ] Stay silent during Entity encounters
- [ ] Save shotgun ammo for doppelgangers and the Entity
- [ ] Track savings against the $250 target every night
- [ ] Do urgent tasks but skip forest runs without tripwires

Master these habits, and solo mode becomes not just survivable but profitable.
