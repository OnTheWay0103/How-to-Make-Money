## Special Monsters in Shift At Midnight

The [Enemy Bestiary](/guides/enemy-bestiary) covers the main cast: doppelgangers, the Entity, the Dentist. But during the campaign's later shifts, the Entity is not the only thing hunting you. Community guides and walkthroughs consistently document **three special monsters** that join the hunt phase — the **Marionette**, the **Demented**, and the **Shrieking Doll** — each with its own rule, and each tied to an achievement.

A note on sourcing: these three were initially poorly documented (our own bestiary listed some as unconfirmed). They are now covered consistently by multiple community guides and the IGN wiki, and the details below reflect that consensus. The game is still being updated, so specific numbers may drift.

---

## Shrieking Doll — the distraction swarm

**Where you meet it:** during Entity hunts, from the mid-campaign onward. The Shrieking Doll is a small, childlike creature that crawls along the floor alongside the main Entity.

**What it does:** it has **better sight than the Entity** — it can spot you while you're hiding in a way the blind Entity can't. When it sees you, it crawls toward you, the camera zooms in, and it screams before leaping at you. Several can appear at once, and their constant harassment will slow you down and ruin your positioning against the real threat.

**How to deal with it:**
- **Shoot it from a distance** — a few shots from any weapon kill it, so they're a distraction, not a boss
- **If one jumps, dodge sideways** — left or right, never backward. The creature leaps straight ahead and will miss
- **Deal with them first** — clear the dolls before re-engaging the Entity; fighting both at once is how players die in hunts

**Achievement:** killing a Shrieking Doll unlocks **Silenced**.

## Demented — look away and it moves

**Where you meet it:** during Entity hunts, from the late campaign. One of the **rarest** monsters in the game — an incredibly fast creature with long limbs.

**What it does:** it behaves like a Weeping Angel or SCP-173. **It only moves when you are not looking at it**, and while you have it in view it freezes in place and cannot be damaged. Look away for even a moment and it closes the gap terrifyingly fast.

**How to deal with it:**
- **Never break eye contact** while it's closing distance
- **Place a Bear Trap in its path**, then deliberately look away — it moves into the trap, which kills it automatically
- **Watch the trap placement** — the Entity can stumble into traps first. If the Entity takes the trap, the Demented survives, so place multiple traps or lure it deliberately
- Do **not** try to outrun it — it is the fastest thing in the store

**Achievement:** killing a Demented unlocks **Freed**.

## Marionette — rewind the box before the melody ends

**Where you meet it:** the Marionette is a jack-in-the-box-style creature that can appear during Entity hunts **from Shift 9 onward**. In Shift 9 you receive an email from N.E.T. warning you about the Jack-in-the-Box.

**How to prevent it:** during a hunt, **if you hear the box's music start playing, find the box and rewind it before the melody plays three times.** Fully rewinding it prevents the Marionette from appearing at all. One honest warning from community guides: **rewinding the box draws the Entity and other creatures to your location** — it's a tradeoff, so time the rewind when you have a clear escape.

**How to fight it:** if you fail to rewind in time, the Marionette appears. It is **tougher than the regular Entity** — community guides recommend bringing real firepower, and the fight is significantly easier in co-op, where one player can keep the box rewound while another engages. Kill it with whatever your Arsenal has.

**Achievement:** killing the Marionette unlocks **Last Performance**.

## How These Fit Into the Hunt

The three special monsters don't replace the Entity — they join it. A typical late-campaign hunt plays out like this:

1. **Prep before the lockdown** — barricade the three doors, place traps, and stock the Emergency Arsenal from the store computer (see the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide))
2. **Kill fakes before payment** — a doppelganger completing a purchase is what starts the hunt in the first place; the [Doppelganger Guide](/guides/doppelganger-guide) covers the screening routine
3. **When the hunt starts, listen** — the Marionette's music is the only warning you get; the Shrieking Dolls make noise before they lunge
4. **Prioritize threats** — dolls first (they give away your position), then the Entity, and keep the Marionette's box in mind at all times
5. **The Demented changes your movement** — you can't just run from everything when the fastest thing in the store only moves when you blink
