## Advanced Tactics Guide — Pro Night Shift Strategies

Once you've survived a few shifts, the game stops being about staying alive and starts being about optimization: squeezing every minute out of the night, manipulating entity schedules, and routing your income toward the True Ending. This guide covers the advanced tactics that separate veterans from survivors.

### Shift-Time Optimization

| Shift Activity | Time Cost | Priority | Notes |
|----------------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Register transactions | 2-4 min | High | Core income, keep lines short |
| Restock shelves | 5 min | Medium | Prevents panic-rush at peak hours |
| Cleaning tasks | 3 min each | Low | Only when scheduled or forced |
| Entity checks | 1 min each | Critical | Do before peak hours, not during |
| Prize wheel | Instant | High | Free money, spin every shift |

1. **Peak hour plan:** Entities and doppelganger visits cluster between 2:00-3:30 AM. Finish all side tasks before 1:00 AM so peak hours are pure screening.
2. **Register discipline:** Never leave a customer waiting during peak hours — a single "service issue" complaint costs more than any cleaning task saves.
3. **Restock timing:** Restock immediately after peak hour passes, when the store is quiet.

### Entity Schedule Manipulation

- **Sound triggers:** Loud activities (restocking, sweeping) attract attention. Delay loud tasks until after peak hours.
- **Light control:** Leaving the front light on in the window deters one random entity visit per night. Budget the power cost against the risk.
- **Barricade staging:** Pre-build one barricade in the back hallway before 2 AM. A staged barricade saves 40 seconds when a hunt starts.
- **Hide spot priority:** Memorize the two hide spots nearest the register — most hunts spawn within one room of the shop floor.

### Doppelganger Screening Mastery

1. **The five-question system:** Always ask all five questions on new customers; repeat customers can be cleared with two.
2. **NET cross-reference:** Cross-check every ID against the NET database twice. First pass finds obvious fakes; second pass catches altered digits.
3. **Emotion detector tells:** A doppelganger's emotion read flickers to "calm" for one frame after a wrong answer. Blink-flick it on the third question.
4. **The silent customer:** Never accept a customer who answers no questions. Refuse politely and watch for the mark.

### Income Optimization for the True Ending

| Income Source | Amount | Frequency | Strategy |
|---------------|--------|-----------|----------|
| Shift salary | $30-45 | Per shift | Always work full shifts |
| Commission | $5-15 | Per transaction | Push upgrades during sales |
| Prize wheel | $5-25 | Per shift | Spin every shift without fail |
| Rats & cleaning | $2-8 | Random | Only when safe to do |

1. Track your running total against the $250 target from shift one — never let a slow week go uncorrected.
2. Take urgent tasks when offered; their bonus pays more than the time they cost.
3. Refuse risky side money (e.g., betting) — losing it in one night can cost the run.

### Achievement Routing

- **Speed runs:** Plan the minimum viable shift: register, one restock, no cleaning, screen only flagged customers.
- **No-death achievements:** Route all loud activities to daylight hours where possible, and always stage barricades early.
- **True Ending:** Combine income routing with zero entity kills — avoid confrontations entirely.
