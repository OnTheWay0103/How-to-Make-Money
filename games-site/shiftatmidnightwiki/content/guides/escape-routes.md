# Shift at Midnight Escape Routes — Get Out Alive, Every Night

**Shift At Midnight / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Every shift ends the same way: survive the entity, then get out. But "get out" is a set of routes, not one door — the correct bus from Shift 5 onward, the garage door timing, the interior kiting loops, and the exits that look safe and aren't. This guide maps every escape route in the gas station and the rules that keep the night from ending early.

---

## 1. The End-of-Shift Sequence

1. Last customer served (and screened — see the [Doppelganger Guide](/guides/doppelganger-guide))
2. Entity attack — survive the patience bar
3. Lock-up check: garage door state, traps triggered or not
4. **Catch the bus** — from Shift 5, check the driver's seat first

The campaign's escape rule is simple and lethal: **from Shift 5 onward, the Driver is a recurring fake.** Boarding the wrong bus ends the run. The check takes one second and the walkthroughs list it as mandatory (see the [Story Walkthrough Guide](/guides/story-walkthrough)).

---

## 2. Station Interior Routes (The Entity Phase)

During the entity attack, the station has three reliable routes:

| Route | Path | Best For |
|-------|------|----------|
| Counter loop | Register → shelving → back-room doorway → register | Solo players — continuous line-of-sight breaks |
| Garage route | Garage → dump area → counter | The fastest loop; the open garage is also the entity's sightline |
| Back-room loop | Back room → bathroom → register | The safest loop — most cover, slowest movement |

**Routing rules:**

1. **Never end a loop in a corner.** The entity catches players who stop to "wait it out."
2. **Use the counters as cover, not the walls.** Walls have no line-of-sight breaks.
3. **Garage door closed before the phase.** An open garage is a sightline the entity uses (see the [Stealth Guide](/guides/stealth-guide)).
4. **Traps change the route.** Once traps are laid, route *through* them — that's where the damage lands.

---

## 3. The Bus Check (From Shift 5)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Approach the bus door, don't board |
| 2 | Check the driver's seat — is the Driver in the seat? |
| 3 | Driver present → fake → don't board (kill or avoid per your loadout) |
| 4 | Empty seat / confirmed human → board |

The Driver is the recurring fake from Shift 4 onward; by the late shifts, checking the seat is as automatic as the register routine. Community walkthroughs emphasize it because it's the one step that kills players who have already "won" the night.

---

## 4. Co-op Split Routes (The 3-Player Escape)

| Player | Role | Route |
|--------|------|-------|
| Player 1 | Kite | Counter loop — keeps the entity moving |
| Player 2 | Traps | Garage route — triggers traps into the entity's path |
| Player 3 | Watch | Back-room doorway — calls the entity's position, covers the bus approach |

**Co-op rule:** the entity tracks one target at a time; the kiter holds aggro on the counter loop while the trapper routes the entity through the garage traps and the watcher keeps the bus approach clear. Splitting routes is how three players extract without the 60-second scramble — see the [Co-op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide) for full squad tactics.

## 5. The Exits That End Runs Early

| Exit | The Trap | The Safe Version |
|------|----------|------------------|
| Bus (Shift 5+) | The Driver fake | Check the seat, always |
| Garage door | Open during entity phase = sightline | Close it before the phase |
| Back door | Locks behind you mid-phase | Only leave through it if the entity is on the counter loop |
| "Free" street exit | The street is not an exit — the shift isn't over | The bus is the only real exit until the shift ends |

**The rule behind all of them:** the shift ends at the bus, not at the door. Leaving through any door before the bus sequence is an early-exit death — the [Survival Strategies Guide](/guides/survival-strategies) has the full phase breakdown.

---

## 6. Worst-Case Routes (The Alert Playbook)

When a lock-up alert fires anyway (a fake paid), the escape route changes:

1. **Use the 60-second window to place traps** — garage and near the desk (see the [Entity Survival Guide](/guides/entity-survival-guide))
2. **Route the monster through the traps** — the counter loop now works *with* the traps
3. **Finish with the gun, then bus** — the bus check still applies; don't skip it in the rush

The alert costs a minute; the bus check costs a second. Players who skip the second to save the first lose the night.

---

## 7. The Escape Route Checklist

1. Garage door closed before the entity phase
2. Traps placed, route planned through them
3. Kite loop chosen (counter loop for solo, split routes for co-op)
4. Entity patience bar drained — survive or damage
5. **Bus: driver's seat checked before boarding**

That's the entire escape game: one loop, one door decision, one seat check. The [Endings Guide](/guides/endings-guide) covers what boarding the right bus leads to, and the [Item Locations Guide](/guides/item-locations) marks the safe spots worth passing on the way out.
