# Shift At Midnight Norbert Guide — Don't Shoot the Gnome

Every gas station clerk learns the same lesson eventually: when a garden gnome shows up in your store, the worst thing you can do is reach for your gun. **Norbert the Gnome** is one of Shift At Midnight's most beloved recurring characters — and one of the most misunderstood. New players routinely shoot him on sight because his ID scan fails. That is a mistake.

This guide covers who Norbert is, why his fake ID is a trap, every prank he pulls, and how to handle his appearances across the 13-shift campaign.

## Who Is Norbert?

Norbert is a doppelganger — but unlike the hostile fakes you screen at the register, he is a harmless prankster. He first appears around **Shift 2** and then keeps showing up at random points through the campaign (confirmed appearances include Shift 6 and Shift 9, where he rides a motorbike into the store).

When you try to scan his ID at the register, the scan fails with a message along the lines of **"Fake ID. Scan Unsuccessful."** — the same error you get for other fakes. That is exactly why so many players shoot him: the game's own identification system flags him as a doppelganger. But Norbert is the exception to every rule you learn at the register.

## The Golden Rule: Do Not Shoot Norbert

Community guides and the game's own design agree: **do not shoot Norbert.** Treat him like a regular customer, let him do his thing, and ignore him.

Here is what happens if you do shoot him:

- He is **not dead**. He returns later in the shift or on a future shift.
- He **retaliates with escalating pranks** — and some of his pranks are actively dangerous.
- You waste ammunition and attention on a threat that was never going to hurt you.

Players who shoot him once often end up shooting him again when he reappears, which just compounds the pranks. The developers clearly built Norbert as a "don't shoot the harmless weirdo" test — pass it and the game treats you better.

**The exception:** if you already shot him and he keeps coming back, some players report that shooting him on his reappearances eventually ends the prank cycle. The safest and simplest route, however, is to just ignore him from the start.

## Every Norbert Prank (Confirmed)

Across the campaign, Norbert messes with you in a few well-documented ways:

| Prank | What Happens | When |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Bike rides** | Norbert rides a bicycle through the store, weaving around shelves and customers | Random shifts, especially after his introduction |
| **Parked cars** | Strange cars appear parked around the store that weren't there before | Random shifts |
| **Customer disguise** | Norbert uses the Anomaly Lens to disguise himself as a customer, so you may screen him again at the register | From Shift 2 onward |
| **Lemonade stand** | Norbert sets up a lemonade stand; drinking from it **poisons you** | Random shifts |
| **Motorbike entrance** | Norbert crashes into the store on a motorbike | Confirmed around Shift 9 |

The pranks are mostly comic relief — the bicycle loops and surprise cars are pure slapstick. The **lemonade stand is the one to take seriously**: the drink actively poisons you, so if you see a stand go up, do not drink from it. Just ignore it and move on.

### The Anomaly Lens Detail

Community guides note that Norbert disguises himself as a customer using an in-game item called the **Anomaly Lens**. Practically speaking, this means that even if you manage your screening routine perfectly, you can occasionally meet a "customer" whose details simply don't behave like any other doppelganger you've seen. If the strange customer turns out to be Norbert, the correct response is still the same: let him buy his items and leave. He is not the threat.

## How to Handle Norbert Step by Step

1. **Shift 2 onward, watch for gnome appearances** — a gnome in the store means Norbert is around.
2. **Never act on the scan failure alone** — "Fake ID. Scan Unsuccessful." is normally a kill signal, but for a gnome-shaped customer it's a prank flag. Cross-check: does this customer match a known doppelganger name from your shift? (Norbert is his own category — treat him as harmless.)
3. **If he sets up the lemonade stand, don't drink** — the poison is the only real damage he can do.
4. **Keep serving customers normally** — Norbert doesn't interrupt the store flow beyond the pranks.
5. **If you already shot him once** — decide whether to keep ignoring him or shoot him on reappearance. Most guides recommend ignoring him permanently.

## Why Players Get It Wrong

The trap is understandable. Shift At Midnight's whole core loop trains you to trust the scan: a failed scan is a doppelganger, a doppelganger must die before it completes its purchase, or it escapes and returns as a monster. Norbert breaks that loop on purpose:

- His ID **does** fail to scan.
- He **does** look like a doppelganger.
- He **is** a doppelganger — but a benign one.

The game deliberately creates a false positive to test whether you apply rules blindly or think about context. The best detection routine in the world will flag Norbert; the best players are the ones who recognize the exception.

## Norbert and the True Ending

Norbert is not tied to the ending requirements — the True Ending depends on banking **$250 in personal cash** for the pet's surgery and **not calling Clyde** in the finale. Shooting or sparing Norbert doesn't change that. He's pure spice: a reminder that the store at midnight has a sense of humor between the horror.

## Norbert vs. Real Doppelgangers

To be clear about the difference:

| Check | Real Doppelganger | Norbert |
|-------|-------------------|---------|
| ID scan | Fails — and so does the rest of the cross-reference (occupation, appearance, date of birth, expiration) | Fails on scan only |
| Other tell categories | Stack 2-3 contradictory clues (wrong job + mismatched ID + odd behavior) | No other tells; behaves like a normal (if mischievous) customer |
| After completing a purchase | Escapes and returns as a monster, triggering the 60-second lock-up alert | Just leaves, maybe sets up a prank later |
| Recommended action | Kill before payment | Ignore |

If you are unsure whether a customer is a doppelganger or Norbert, run the full routine from our [Doppelganger Guide](/guides/doppelganger-guide): check the ID against the NET database, cross-check occupation and appearance, and use your questions. A real doppelganger will stack contradictions in at least two or three categories. Norbert won't.

## Norbert in Co-Op

Norbert creates a special coordination problem for teams. The screening instinct is strongest when you have company: someone at the register, someone watching the line, everyone primed to call out a failed scan. When a gnome-shaped customer fails the scan in co-op, the whole team tends to converge and the newest player usually shoots first.

The team protocol that works:

1. **Tell new teammates about Norbert before the shift** — one sentence is enough: "If a gnome customer fails the scan, don't shoot — it's Norbert."
2. **One callout, no debate** — the register player announces "Norbert" and everyone stands down. The discussion time you save is time for the actual screening.
3. **If someone shoots him anyway** — don't compound it. Keep serving normally; his pranks play out either way, and shooting him again mid-shift just adds more chaos.
4. **The lemonade stand rule applies to everyone** — if the stand appears, tell the whole team not to drink from it. One poisoned player is a distraction the team doesn't need.

Co-op also makes Norbert easier to identify confidently: the register player can watch his behavior while a teammate cross-checks the scan message. A real doppelganger stacks other tells (wrong occupation, mismatched appearance, contradictions under questioning); Norbert fails the scan and nothing else — the kind of pattern a second pair of eyes confirms quickly.

## Why the Game Includes Him

Norbert is a deliberate design decision. Shift At Midnight's entire core loop trains you to treat a failed scan as a kill order — and then the game hands you a failed scan that is not a kill order. He tests whether you're applying rules by context or by reflex.

There's also a lore dimension the community has noted: doppelgangers are said to be born from people's negative emotions, and Norbert is the rare doppelganger whose behavior is playful rather than predatory. Where the others mimic humans to get close enough to hurt you, Norbert mimics a customer to get close enough to prank you. The same identification system that catches killers also catches the one fake who just wants to ride a bike through your store.

## Norbert FAQ

**Can I actually kill Norbert?** No confirmed method. Community guides report he survives shooting and returns — the game treats him as persistent.

**Does shooting him change the ending?** No confirmed connection. The ending is decided by personal cash and the Clyde call, not by gnome policy.

**Does he appear in Endless Mode?** Unconfirmed — the Endless Mode beta's content is still being mapped by the community (see the [Endless Mode Guide](/guides/endless-mode-guide)).

**Is his appearance guaranteed every playthrough?** No — shifts are randomly generated, and his appearances vary run to run. Confirmed sightings cluster around Shifts 2, 6, and 9, but not every run sees all of them.

**Is the lemonade stand lethal?** The poison damages you, but community reports describe it as survivable — the real cost is the distraction, not death.

## Community Consensus and Unconfirmed Details

What's confirmed by multiple community sources and walkthroughs:

- Norbert appears from around Shift 2 and recurs randomly.
- Shooting him leads to prank retaliation rather than a kill.
- The lemonade stand poisons you.
- He uses a disguise (Anomaly Lens) to appear as a customer.

What is **not** confirmed (treat as unverified):

- Whether he appears in every playthrough (shifts are randomly generated, so his appearances vary run to run).
- Whether shooting him on every reappearance truly stops the pranks.
- Whether there are additional unlisted pranks beyond the ones documented here.

## Final Word

Norbert is one of the best-known running jokes in Shift At Midnight, and he's a genuine knowledge check for new players: **the fake ID screen is not always a kill order**. Spare the gnome, skip the lemonade, and get back to the register — the actual threats will be waiting there.
