# Shift At Midnight Rakes Guide — How to Stop the Endless Mode Threat

Rakes are Endless Mode monsters that walk out of the forest toward your shop, and the developer's own rule for them is blunt: shoot them before they reach the playable area. They ignore the Hunt system entirely, so a quiet shift is not a safe one. Here is how to spot one and stop it in time.

## What the developer actually said

The Rake was introduced on **29 July 2026**, and the announcement is short enough to quote in full — it is also the only primary description that exists:

> "In ENDLESS MODE / POST-STORY MODE, monsters called "Rakes" will slowly emerge from the forest. You must shoot them before they reach the playable area, or bad stuff will happen :)" [Official]

Everything else in a Rake guide — how fast it moves, how much health it has, whether it can be trapped — is player observation rather than developer statement. We mark which is which.

## When and where Rakes appear

Four things are settled by that one sentence:

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| They exist in **Endless / post-story mode** | Developer announcement, 29 July 2026 [Official] |
| They **emerge from the forest** around the gas station | Same announcement [Official] |
| They walk toward the **playable area** | Same announcement [Official] |
| The counter is to **shoot them before they arrive** | Same announcement [Official] |

Community write-ups add that Rakes do not appear in the 13-shift story campaign at all — you have to be in the post-story loop to meet one. [Community] That matches the announcement's pairing of "Endless Mode" with "Post-Story Mode": it is the mode that continues after the campaign ends, not a separate menu.

The detail that matters most is the one the announcement implies without saying: **a Rake is not an event in the Hunt chain.** The Entity only wakes up after a doppelganger clears the register, and the whole shop is braced for that by then. A Rake needs no trigger you can see. It can arrive during an ordinary, calm shift, which is why it catches players who are still treating "quiet" as "safe". [Community]

## What a Rake does to a normal shift

Community documentation describes a four-legged predator that sprints, glows red, and goes for your customers rather than for you. [Community]

| What you see | What it means for the shift |
|---|---|
| A shape coming out of the trees | Your usual scan-the-register attention is pointed the wrong way |
| Running on all fours | It closes the distance quickly, so the reaction window is short |
| A red glow in the dark | Your visual cue at night; it is meant to be visible |
| Customers shrieking | Your audio cue, and the fastest alert you get |
| It heads for the shop, not for you | The loss is economic — customers scared off or killed — not a death screen |
| It dodges and kites when you shoot | Chipping at it wastes the window; commit to the burst |

That last row is the part people get wrong. A Rake is not a damage race you win by taking pot shots between customers. Community guides describe it breaking sideways when fired at, so the correct line is a committed burst, not a dribble of shots. [Community]

## The response, in order

**1. Treat the shriek as an alarm.** Customers screaming is the earliest signal you get, and it comes before you can see anything. Stop scanning. [Community]

**2. Find the red glow at the treeline.** Scan the forest edge around the forecourt rather than the aisles. It glows for exactly this reason. [Community]

**3. Leave the counter.** The register will hold. A customer queue is recoverable; a Rake reaching the shop is not, by the developer's own wording. [Official]

**4. Commit to the shot.** One player, one target, full burst. Do not split fire between a doppelganger and a Rake — if both are live, the Rake is the one that expires. [Community]

**5. Confirm before you go back inside.** If you cannot confirm the kill, assume it is still coming and keep someone watching the treeline while the other finishes the shift. The announcement says "or bad stuff will happen" and never specifies what that is, so do not assume a wounded Rake walks away. [Official]

## Security cameras, added for exactly this

The **1 September 2026** update added cameras to Endless Mode, and the developer's description points straight at the forest:

> "Added Security Cameras in ENDLESS MODE. These can be viewed on the computer and let you keep an eye on anything that might emerge from the forest." [Official]

That is a treeline watch you can run from inside the store, which is the one thing a Rake otherwise denies you: you cannot stare at the forest and stock shelves at the same time. The practical routine is to check the camera feed between customers instead of only when something already feels wrong. [Editorial]

Be precise about what the announcement claims. It says the cameras let you *look*. It does not say they detect, alarm, or damage anything, and no source we found shows a camera stopping a Rake. Treat them as eyes, not as a defence. [Official]

## Traps and planks — what is and is not confirmed

The same 1 September update also changed Endless Mode supplies:

- **2 bear traps and 2 planks** are placed in the storage room for the **first shift** of Endless Mode. [Official]
- You can now **buy 3 additional bear traps instead of 2**. [Official]

It is tempting to read that as anti-Rake equipment. Neither announcement says a bear trap affects a Rake, and the community wiki that covers the monster explicitly refuses to claim it. [Community] Bear traps are documented in this game as an anti-Entity and anti-intruder tool — see the [Entity Survival Guide](/guides/entity-survival-guide) — and until someone tests a trap on a Rake, using one as your forest answer is a guess. The confirmed counter remains shooting it before it reaches the lot.

## Co-op: put one pair of eyes on the treeline

A Rake punishes a team that is all facing the same direction. The clean division is:

| Role | Job when the shriek hits |
|---|---|
| Watcher | Stops scanning, takes the treeline and the shot |
| Counter staff | Keeps the queue moving and calls out if a second threat appears |
| Runner | Handles restock and the door, so the Watcher does not have to break off |

That is the same shape as the wider co-op doctrine in our [Co-Op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide): one player owns a threat, the rest keep the shift running. The mistake is everyone turning to look — you lose the register and gain nothing, because only one of you needs to shoot.

## Rakes vs the other threats

The roster below comes from a community threat list that covers all four in one table. [Community]

| Threat | When it shows up | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Entity** | After a doppelganger completes a purchase and leaves | Barricade, traps, guns — see the [Enemy Bestiary](/guides/enemy-bestiary) |
| **Marionette** | Later shifts, as a Hunt add | Rewind the Jack-in-the-Box, or shoot it |
| **Dentist** | Shift 13 finale | Run the chase; do not stand and trade |
| **Rake** | Endless / post-story shifts, without a Hunt trigger | Shoot it in the forest before it reaches the lot |

The distinction worth internalising: every other threat in that table is announced by a system. The Rake is not. It is the only entry where the correct trigger for your response is a customer scream rather than a game event.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for a Hunt warning.** There is none. The shriek is the warning. [Community]
- **Treating it as a melee problem.** The developer's counter is explicitly to shoot it before arrival, not to fight it in the aisles. [Official]
- **Chasing it into the forest.** Your job is to stop it reaching the shop, not to win an argument with the treeline.
- **Splitting fire.** Kiting behaviour punishes partial damage. [Community]
- **Assuming a trap did the job.** No source confirms traps work on a Rake. [Community]

## What is not confirmed

- **Health, damage and speed figures.** No reliable source exists; the wiki that covers the monster says so outright. [Community]
- **Whether a bear trap or plank stops one.** Not stated in either official announcement.
- **Whether a Rake can enter the shop interior.** The developer's wording stops at "the playable area".
- **Spawn cadence and whether it scales with shift number.** Community accounts vary.
- **What "bad stuff" is.** The announcement declines to say.

## FAQ

**Which mode do Rakes appear in?**
Endless / post-story mode, added in the 29 July 2026 update. Not the 13-shift campaign. [Official]

**Can I just hide from a Rake?**
No source describes hiding as a counter. The developer's instruction is to shoot it before it reaches the playable area. [Official]

**Do security cameras help against Rakes?**
They let you watch the forest from the computer, which is how you get warning. They are not documented as a defence. [Official]

**Should I buy the extra bear trap for Rakes?**
Only if you want it for the threats it is documented against. Nothing official ties bear traps to Rakes.
