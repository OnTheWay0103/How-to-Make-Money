## Shift At Midnight Multiplayer FAQ

*Shift At Midnight* is an **online co-op horror game** — but the multiplayer details are the source of more confusion than any other topic, mostly because the answers changed after launch. This FAQ answers the questions players actually type: how many players, crossplay, couch co-op, inviting friends, voice chat, and the join errors. For team roles and tactics once you're in a lobby, see the [Co-Op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide).

## How Many Players Can Play Shift At Midnight?

**Up to 3 players in online co-op** — one host plus two friends. That's the officially documented number on the Steam store page, and it's the number most guides confirm.

**The 6-player question**: a patch around **July 23, 2026** reportedly lets the Steam lobby host raise the lobby cap toward **6 players**. This is community-documented — the developer has stated anything above 3 is outside the intended balance and not recommended. Treat 6-player lobbies as possible but unofficial: expect janky balance, and note the crossplay restrictions below still apply.

Solo play is fully supported — the campaign scales for one player.

## Does Shift At Midnight Have Crossplay?

**Partially — it's split into two separate pools:**

| Pool | Who Can Play Together |
|------|----------------------|
| **Microsoft pool** | Xbox Series X\|S ↔ PC Game Pass / Microsoft Store — these share one matchmaking pool (Xbox Play Anywhere-style crossplay) and play together freely |
| **Steam pool** | Steam ↔ Steam only — Steam players match only with other Steam players |

**Steam ↔ Xbox / PC Game Pass: no.** The two pools don't connect — there's no shared lobby between them. Some early/pre-launch guides say "no crossplay at all"; the accurate post-launch picture is that crossplay exists within the Microsoft ecosystem, and Steam is isolated.

A full Steam-Xbox crossplay bridge has been mentioned as a possible future update — no date is confirmed.

## Does Shift At Midnight Have Couch Co-Op or Split-Screen?

**No.** There is no local co-op, couch co-op, split-screen, or LAN/system-link play on any platform. Multiplayer is **online only** — every player needs their own device, account, and copy of the game, even in the same household. If you're sharing one console or PC, the game is single-player for you.

## How to Play Shift At Midnight With Friends

1. **Steam**: add each other as Steam friends, then host or join through the **Steam invite** — the host's lobby is a private session, and friends join from the invite. (A public server browser exists on Steam for open lobbies.)
2. **Xbox / PC Game Pass**: invite through the standard **Xbox party/invite flow**.
3. Use **proximity voice chat** in-game to coordinate — it's positional, so distance and walls actually affect how well your team hears you.

Note: **"how to play with 4/5/6 players"** — the game officially supports 3. Anything above that relies on the community-reported lobby-cap change on Steam, not the base design.

## Voice Chat, Muting, and Mic Discipline

- **Proximity chat** is built in and positional — teammates sound quieter the farther they are, and the entity can hear you through open mics
- **How to mute players**: the in-game session/player list has per-player mute options (the exact menu location varies by platform build — if you can't find it, check the pause menu during a shift)
- **Best practice**: push-to-talk or a muted default. The entity tracks audio, and an unmuted teammate is how most co-op runs die — see the [Entity Survival Guide](/guides/entity-survival-guide)

## Fixing "Failed to Join Session" and Lobby Errors

The most common reported causes, with community-tested fixes:

| Error / Symptom | Likely Cause | Community-Reported Fix |
|-----------------|--------------|------------------------|
| Failed to join session | Host and guest on different pools (Steam vs. Microsoft) | Both players must own the game on the **same** platform pool |
| Failed to create lobby | Network restrictions / NAT | Restart the game, check firewall, host from a stable connection |
| Can't see friend's invite | Privacy settings | Set Steam/Xbox profile to allow invites from friends |
| Join fails at 6-player cap | Unofficial 6-player lobby | Drop back to 3; the cap change is community-reported and unstable |

If errors persist after a restart and a network check, the game's official Discord is the best place for current reports (the [tech & mods questions](/guides/pc-performance-guide) page covers performance-side causes).

## What Platforms Is Shift At Midnight On?

Launched **July 22, 2026** on:

**Not available**: PlayStation 5, Switch, Mac, or mobile — the "coming to PS5" searches you may have seen are unanswered hopes, not announcements.
