# Weapons Guide — All 5 Weapon Families Explained

> Launch Version / Updated: July 28, 2026

The Relic: First Guardian offers five distinct weapon families, each with 12 skill trees that dramatically alter your combat approach. Unlike traditional soulslikes, every weapon you find is unique — there is no random loot, no repeated drops. This guide breaks down each weapon family's playstyle, strengths, weaknesses, and the skill tree archetypes available at launch.

---

## Sword & Shield (The Balanced Guardian)

The most versatile weapon family, Sword & Shield offers the widest range of defensive options without sacrificing damage output. This is the only weapon family with a native block ability.

### Core Mechanics

- **Block:** Hold L1/LB to raise your shield. Blocks all physical damage but chip damage (10-15%) still passes through. Blocking costs no stamina.
- **Guard Counter:** Press R1/RB immediately after blocking an attack. A well-timed guard counter deals 1.5x damage and heavy posture damage.
- **Shield Bash:** Heavy attack while blocking lunges forward and staggers humanoid enemies.

### Best Skill Trees

| Skill Tree | Focus | Best For |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Iron Wall | Pure defense, guard counter damage | Beginners, learning boss patterns |
| Radiant Crusader | Holy damage, self-healing on block | Sustain-focused players |
| Storm Wall | Lightning-infused counters, speed boost after block | Aggressive defensive play |
| Twilight Guard | Dark damage, parry that inflicts fear | Advanced players seeking high risk/reward |

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:** Safest playstyle, excellent posture damage, forgiving for learning patterns.
**Cons:** Lower raw DPS than other families, shield discourages learning perfect dodge timing.

### Best Rune Synergies

Fragment of Vigor (stamina recovery) is essential for maintaining blocking uptime. Iron Bulwark (increases guard stability) reduces chip damage to 5%, making it one of the best defensive pairings in the game.

## Dual Daggers (The Agile Assassin)

The fastest weapon family with the shortest reach. Dual Daggers excel at applying status effects and exploiting brief attack windows through rapid hit-and-run tactics.

### Core Mechanics

- **Quick Step:** Costs only 15 stamina (versus 25 for a standard dodge). This lower cost enables aggressive weaving between attacks.
- **Bleed Application:** Every third consecutive hit applies a bleed stack. At 5 stacks, the target takes 15% of their max health as damage.
- **Backstab Multiplier:** Attacks from behind deal 2.5x damage versus 1.5x for other weapons.

### Best Skill Trees

| Skill Tree | Focus | Best For |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Shadow Dance | Invisibility on dodge, backstab damage | Stealth-focused clearing |
| Viper's Kiss | Poison damage, DoT stacking | Boss fights with long engagement windows |
| Bloodletting | Bleed amplification, attack speed on kill | Mob clearing, crowd control |
| Moonlit Stalker | Night/darkness damage bonus, teleport backstab | Positioning specialists |

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:** Highest DPS potential, lowest stamina costs, excellent status effect application.
**Cons:** Short range requires precise positioning, no block, steep learning curve against aggressive bosses.

## Sword (The Versatile Duelist)

The Sword family sits between Sword & Shield and Dual Daggers — offering excellent mobility with moderate range and reliable damage. Two-handed sword stance trades the shield for a powerful parry that transitions directly into a counterattack.

### Core Mechanics

- **Two-Handed Stance:** Press Triangle/Y to two-hand your sword. This removes your off-hand options but boosts damage by 20% and changes your moveset.
- **Parry & Riposte:** The sword parry has a wider active window than Dual Daggers but narrower than Sword & Shield's guard counter. Successful parries open enemies for a riposte dealing 3x damage.
- **Momentum System:** Landing successive attacks builds momentum stacks (max 5). Each stack increases damage by 6%. Taking damage resets momentum.

### Best Skill Trees

| Skill Tree | Focus | Best For |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Wind Blade | Speed-focused, attack speed per momentum stack | Aggressive duelists |
| Flamebrand | Fire damage, burning ground on heavy attacks | Area denial, crowd control |
| Frost Warden | Ice damage, slow application, defensive parry | Control-focused players |
| Void Edge | Dark damage, life-steal on critical hits | Sustain duelists |

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:** Solid damage output, rewarding parry mechanic, momentum system incentivizes aggressive play.
**Cons:** No native block, momentum resets on hit (punishing against multi-hit combos).

## Two-Handed Weapon (The Heavy Hitter)

The slowest but hardest-hitting weapon family. Two-Handed Weapons excel at staggering enemies, breaking posture, and dealing massive single-hit damage.

### Core Mechanics

- **Hyper Armor:** Heavy attacks have poise frames that cannot be interrupted. You will trade hits, but your hit deals significantly more damage.
- **Posture Break:** Two-Handed Weapon heavy attacks deal 3x posture damage compared to other weapons. Breaking an enemy's posture opens them for a critical hit.
- **Charge Attacks:** Hold the attack button to charge. Fully charged attacks deal 2x damage and knock down most humanoid enemies.

### Best Skill Trees

| Skill Tree | Focus | Best For |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Earth Splitter | AoE ground slams, tremor damage | Crowd control, mob clearing |
| Colossus | Defense boost while charging, hyper armor extension | Trading hits with bosses |
| Executioner | Bonus damage against enemies below 30% health, execution threshold | Boss finisher builds |
| Stormbreaker | Lightning-infused overhead slams, attack speed on kill | Improving weapon speed |

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:** Highest raw damage per hit, best posture damage, hyper armor enables aggressive trades.
**Cons:** Slowest attack speed, high stamina costs for sprinting/dodging due to weight penalty, punishable whiffs.

## Staff (The Ranged Battlemage)

The Staff is the only weapon family with native ranged capabilities. Staves scale with memory fragments collected rather than stats, making them a late-blooming powerhouse.

### Core Mechanics

- **Ranged Projectile:** Light attack fires a magic bolt that deals moderate damage at range. Bolts have a travel time and can be aimed manually.
- **Staff Stance Switch:** Triangle/Y toggles between Combat Stance (faster casting, close range) and Focus Stance (slower but more powerful spells).
- **Channeling:** Holding the attack button charges spells. Fully channeled spells deal 1.5x damage but lock you in place during casting.

### Best Skill Trees

| Skill Tree | Focus | Best For |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Arcane Bolt | Pure projectile damage, piercing shots | Ranged DPS specialists |
| Frost Weaver | Ice spells, slowing fields, defensive barriers | Area control and utility |
| Flame Cascade | Fire AoE, residual burn zones | Crowd control, boss damage |
| Void Scholar | Dark magic, mana leech, spell refund | Sustain casters, long engagements |

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:** Only ranged option, excellent for safe boss damage, powerful AoE options.
**Cons:** Weak early game (need multiple runes to scale), vulnerable during channeling, no defensive option beyond dodge.

## Weapon Switching During Combat

You can equip two weapon families simultaneously and switch between them with D-Pad Left/Right. This enables powerful combinations:

- **Staff + Sword:** Chip at range, finish in melee
- **Dual Daggers + Two-Handed:** Apply bleed with daggers, execute with heavy weapon
- **Sword & Shield + Staff:** Defensive safety with ranged pressure

Mastering weapon-swap combos is an advanced technique that significantly expands your tactical options.

## Weapon Rarity Tiers

Every weapon in the game is unique, but they fall into general quality tiers:

| Tier | Color | How to Find |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Common | White | Standard enemy drops, early zones |
| Exceptional | Blue | Mini-boss rewards, hidden alcoves |
| Legendary | Gold | Main boss drops, complex side quests |
| Relic | Red | Secret bosses, NG+ exclusive areas |

Each weapon also has a unique passive effect — for example, the Legendary sword "Sorrow's Edge" (dropped by the Weeping Knight) applies a stacking despair debuff that reduces enemy attack power.
