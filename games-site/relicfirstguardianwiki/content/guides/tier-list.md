# Weapon Tier List — Best Weapons Ranked (July 2026)

This tier list ranks all 5 weapon families in The Relic: First Guardian based on their overall effectiveness, skill ceiling, and build diversity. The game launches July 31, 2026 — rankings will be updated as the meta develops.

---

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **S-Tier** | Highest potential. Dominant in skilled hands. Top-tier in both DPS and utility. |
| **A-Tier** | Excellent performance. Slightly lower ceiling or more situational than S-tier. |
| **B-Tier** | Solid and reliable. Great for specific playstyles or beginners. |
| **C-Tier** | Viable but outclassed. Requires specific builds to match higher tiers. |

---

## Overall Rankings

### S-Tier: Dual Daggers, Sword

**Dual Daggers** — The highest DPS ceiling in the game. Bleed stacking, critical hit synergy, and the fastest attack speed make Daggers the top choice for experienced players. The Bloodletter + Shadow Step skill tree combination melts bosses. Risk: fragile, punished hard by mistakes.

**Sword** — The most versatile weapon. Balanced damage, excellent parry windows, and the Blade Dancer combo system rewards mastery without the fragility of Daggers. Iaijutsu skill tree provides burst damage that rivals Two-Handed weapons.

### A-Tier: Two-Handed Weapon

**Two-Handed Weapon** — Massive burst damage and the best stagger potential. Colossus Strike + Earthshatter can stun-lock most non-boss enemies. The Unstoppable skill tree provides hyper armor, letting you trade hits. Weakness: slow recovery, difficult against fast bosses.

### B-Tier: Sword & Shield, Staff

**Sword & Shield** — The safest and most forgiving weapon. Perfect for beginners and survivability-focused builds. Guardian's Resolve + Shield Counter creates an nearly unkillable tank. Weakness: lower DPS ceiling, slower boss kills.

**Staff** — Excellent ranged damage and elemental crowd control. The only weapon that can safely damage from distance. Elemental Attunement provides versatility against different enemy types. Weakness: struggles at close range, squishy.

**Q: Is this tier list final?**
A: No. This is based on pre-launch preview and demo data. The meta will shift as players discover optimal builds post-launch.

**Q: Can off-meta weapons clear all content?**
A: Absolutely. All 5 weapon families are viable for the entire game. Tier lists reflect optimization ceiling, not viability.

**Q: Should I switch weapons based on this list?**
