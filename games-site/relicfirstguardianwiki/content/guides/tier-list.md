# Weapon Tier List — Early Impressions (Data Still Unconfirmed)

**Honest warning**: no verified tier-list data exists for The Relic: First Guardian at launch (August 2026). The game released July 31, 2026, and there is no community-verified meta yet. Any site ranking "S/A/B/C" weapons with confidence right now is speculating — many such lists are AI-generated with invented skill-tree names.

What this guide offers instead: **early impressions grounded in confirmed information** — official material and launch reviews describe each family's playstyle and trade-offs. Treat every placement here as a hypothesis, not a ranking.

## What We Actually Know (Confirmed)

| Family | Confirmed Playstyle | Confirmed Trade-off |
|--------|--------------------|---------------------|
| **Sword & Shield** | Defensive, parry-focused; only native block | Lower damage ceiling; can teach passivity |
| **Longsword** | Balanced, versatile | No block; relies on parry/dodge |
| **Dagger (dual blades)** | Fast, aggressive, bursty | Short range, no block, punishing mistakes |
| **Two-Handed** | Heavy damage, slow | Slow recovery; heavy-feeling attacks per previews |
| **Battle Staff** | Skill-heavy, mage-like ("skill-spamming" per reviews) | Needs relic support; weak early per previews |

Official material also confirms: **all five families are viable** — the game is designed around each weapon's two skill trees, and there is no stated "best" weapon.

## Early-Impression Placements (Hypotheses, Not Verified)

### Strong Early Impressions: Sword & Shield, Dagger

- **Sword & Shield** — the safest family in the game's defense-heavy economy. The confirmed generous parry window plus native block makes it the lowest-risk pick for learning the 70-80 Brutals. For most players, the best first weapon.
- **Dagger (Dual Blades)** — the best fit for the game's aggressive design: zero-stamina attacks mean speed is pure upside. High ceiling, high skill requirement.

### Solid Early Impressions: Longsword, Battle Staff

- **Longsword** — the reliable all-rounder. Nothing it does badly, nothing it does best. A safe second pick.
- **Battle Staff** — potentially the strongest *late-game* family (cooldown-reduction relics are a confirmed effect type), but previews say it starts weak until relics support it. A build-around pick, not a starter.

### The "It Depends" Placement: Two-Handed

- **Two-Handed** — big damage, slow rhythm. Reviews note heavy-feeling attacks; players who dislike trading will struggle. Fine if it fits you; not an objective winner.

## Why There Is No Real Tier List Yet

1. **No verified data** — relics, skill trees, and weapon stats are still being documented post-launch.
2. **The game resists tiers** — every weapon is unique and some carry Drawbacks; "the best weapon" is conditional on what you found and can live with.
3. **Builds, not weapons, win** — the confirmed loop is adapting relics and gear per boss. A "C-tier" weapon with the right relics can outperform a "S-tier" one in the wrong fight.
4. **Fabricated lists are everywhere** — lists naming invented skill trees ("Iron Wall," "Wind Blade," etc.) are fake on their face.

**Tier-list literacy**: if a list assigns specific skill-tree names, relic names, or percentages, it is not verified. If it gives no sources and appears within days of launch, it is speculative.
