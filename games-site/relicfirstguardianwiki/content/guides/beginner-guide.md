# Beginner Guide — Getting Started in Arsiltus

> Launch Version / Updated: July 28, 2026

The ruined world of Arsiltus is unforgiving. As the last First Guardian, you wake in the Ashen Fields with nothing but a fragmented memory and a choice of weapon. This beginner guide covers everything you need to survive your first 5 hours, master the stamina-based combat, and begin collecting the memory fragments that define your power.

---

## Choosing Your First Weapon

Your first major decision comes after the opening cutscene. In the Hollow Refuge, you find five weathered weapon stands. Each represents one of the game's five weapon families. You can find or purchase the others within the first 2-3 hours, so do not agonize too long.

### Best Starting Weapon for Beginners: Sword & Shield

The **Sword & Shield** is the most forgiving first weapon for several reasons:

- **Blocking costs no stamina**, unlike dodging — giving you a safety net against unfamiliar attack patterns.
- The base skill tree, **Iron Wall**, includes a reliable guard counter that deals respectable posture damage.
- Early memory fragments that boost defense and health synergize naturally with this playstyle.
- Your "gap closer" skill (Shield Charge) is unlocked at skill tree level 3, giving you both offense and positioning control.

### Alternative Starter: Sword (Two-Handed)

If you prefer a more aggressive approach, the **Sword** weapon family offers excellent mobility and damage output. Its starting skill tree, **Wind Blade**, focuses on rapid slashes and a parry that transitions directly into a counterattack. The trade-off is that you have no block — only dodge and parry for defense.

**We recommend against starting with Dual Daggers or Staff.** Dual Daggers demand intimate knowledge of enemy attack windows due to their short range, while Staff requires collecting several runes before its damage output becomes competitive.

## Understanding the Stamina-Only Defense System

The Relic: First Guardian has a unique combat economy. Unlike most soulslikes, **your attacks cost zero stamina**. Stamina is reserved exclusively for defensive actions:

| Action | Stamina Cost |
|--------|-------------|
| Dodge Roll | 25 stamina |
| Quick Step (Dual Daggers) | 15 stamina |
| Block (Sword & Shield) | 0 stamina (chip damage still passes through) |
| Parry | 30 stamina (successful parry refunds 15) |
| Sprint | 15 stamina per second |
| Guard Counter | 20 stamina |

**Critical tip:** Never let your stamina drop below 20. A single dodge could mean the difference between life and death. Develop the habit of managing stamina like a resource that regenerates slowly — base recovery takes about 3 seconds from empty to full, but memory fragments and artifacts can significantly improve this.

## Rune Collection Basics

There is no traditional leveling system in The Relic: First Guardian. You grow stronger by collecting **Memory Fragments (Runes)** — remnants of the shattered relic scattered across Arsiltus.

### How Runes Work

- Each rune grants a passive effect when equipped to one of your **Rune Slots**.
- You start with 3 rune slots. Additional slots are unlocked by finding **Rune Vessels** — special artifacts hidden in tombs and guarded by mini-bosses.
- Runes are divided into categories: Combat, Resilience, Utility, and Legendary.
- You can swap runes at any **Brazier of Reflection** (checkpoint). Experiment freely.

### Your First Essential Runes

Within your first 5 hours, prioritize these runes:

1. **Fragment of Vigor** (Found in an alcove behind the first waterfall in Ashen Fields) — +15% stamina recovery speed. This is the single most impactful early rune.
2. **Ember of Persistence** (Dropped by the Hollow Knight mini-boss in the Ruined Chapel) — Revive with 30% health when killed, once per bonfire rest. A literal life-saver.
3. **Shard of Clarity** (Quest reward from "The Mourning Widow" side quest in Hollow Refuge) — Reduces all skill cooldowns by 8%.

## Your First 5 Hours: Step by Step

### Hour 1: The Ashen Fields
- Complete the tutorial area. The game teaches basic movement, one light attack combo, and one dodge.
- Pick up your first weapon from the stands in Hollow Refuge.
- **Do not skip the tutorial messages** — The Relic uses several unique mechanics (skill cooldowns, rune equipping) that are not repeated.
- Find the first Brazier of Reflection and rest to unlock fast travel.

### Hour 2: Ruined Chapel and Your First Boss
- Navigate the Ruined Chapel. This is a linear gauntlet designed to teach you enemy variety.
- You will encounter your first mini-boss, the **Hollow Knight**, around the 90-minute mark.
- **Boss tip:** The Hollow Knight has a three-swing combo. Block the first two, dodge the third (it has a wider arc). After his combo ends, you have a 2-second window for 2-3 light attacks or 1 heavy attack.
- After defeating him, you earn your first artifact slot upgrade and the Ember of Persistence rune.

### Hour 3: The Whispering Woods
- A true semi-open area with branching paths. This is where exploration becomes rewarding.
- Find the **Memory Fragment: Wind Walker** behind a destructible wall near the Woods entrance — this rune gives +5% dodge distance and is excellent for all weapons.
- The Woods contain 3 side quests. "The Lost Flute" in particular rewards an early artifact (Empty Vessel).
- Do not rush to the area boss. Explore thoroughly.

### Hour 4: Underground Reservoir
- A vertical dungeon with multiple levels. This is where the game's platforming elements appear.
- You will find your first optional boss, the **Drowned Pilgrim**, at the bottom. He is entirely skippable but drops the **Tide Ward** artifact (reduces incoming damage by 12% when below 30% health).
- **Save at the Brazier before his arena.** There is no checkpoint between entering and the boss fight.

### Hour 5: The Gate of Whispers — First Story Boss
- The Gatekeeper Velrik, Warden of Whispers, is your first true story boss. His moveset has 5 distinct attacks.
- Strategy: Stay close to his left side (your right). Three of his five attacks cannot hit you there. His sweeping flame attack has a 4-second wind-up — sprint behind him when you see the orange glow.
- After this fight, you earn a new rune slot and the **Rune of Passage**, which opens the path to the Sunken Citadel.

## Early Game Tips Summary

- **Explore every corner.** Unique weapons and armor pieces are hand-placed, not randomly dropped. If you see a suspicious ledge, there is almost always something there.
- **Talk to NPCs twice.** Many quest givers have additional dialogue after their initial conversation, revealing hidden quests or item locations.
- **Do not sell your starting weapon artifacts.** Some are used in crafting recipes later.
- **Use your skills.** Skills are on cooldown timers, not resource pools. There is no reason to hoard them. Spam them when available.
- **Rest at every new Brazier.** They serve as fast travel points, and the game does not autosave mid-dungeon.
- **Read memory fragment descriptions.** Each rune tells part of the story of Arsiltus. Piecing them together unlocks bonus lore entries.
