# Beginner Guide — Getting Started in Arsiltus

The ruined world of Arsiltus is unforgiving. As the **last Guardian**, you wake with fragmented memories, collect **relic fragments** imbued with the dead's emotions, and fight to seal the Void. The Relic: First Guardian is a soulslike with one crucial difference from the classics: **no XP and no levels** — your growth comes from relics, skills, and unique gear. This guide covers what is confirmed about the opening hours and the systems you need to master.

---

## Choosing Your First Weapon

The game has **five weapon families**, each with its own playstyle and skill trees:

| Family | Playstyle (confirmed by official/preview material) |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Sword & Shield** | Defensive, parry-focused; the only family with a native block |
| **Longsword** | Balanced; the versatile middle ground |
| **Dagger (dual blades)** | Fast, aggressive; quick attacks and status play |
| **Two-Handed** | Heavy damage; slow but hits hard |
| **Battle Staff (combat staff)** | Mage-like; skill-spamming and ranged options |

**Best starter for most players: Sword & Shield.** The game's defense economy makes block and parry the most forgiving defensive tools, and reviews describe the parry window as generous. If you are new to soulslikes, start here.

**For veterans**: daggers (fast, aggressive) or two-handed (heavy trading) reward pattern knowledge. Staff is a stronger investment later — it needs relic support to shine, per preview coverage.

## The Combat Economy You Must Learn

This is the game's signature twist, confirmed across official material and reviews:

- **Attacks cost ZERO stamina** — swing freely; offense is unlimited.
- **Stamina is reserved for defense** — dodging, blocking, and parrying spend it.
- **Skills run on cooldown timers** — no mana, no resource bar; use them whenever they are up.

| Action | Stamina Cost (Confirmed) |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Light / heavy attacks | **0** (confirmed) |
| Dodge / block / parry | Costs stamina (exact values **unconfirmed**) |

**Rules of thumb:**
1. Never let your stamina hit empty near a boss — empty stamina means no dodge.
2. Attack constantly when safe — it costs nothing and pressure wins fights.
3. Learn parry timing early — the window is generous, and parry chains stagger bosses (reviews report chains of up to 15 parries vs some Brutals).

## The Relic System — Your Real "Leveling"

There are **no levels**. Your power comes from:

1. **Relics (Runes / Memory Fragments)** — over 70 unique passive fragments scattered across the world. Equip them, swap them freely, and adapt them to each boss.
2. **Relic slots** — unlock more via **Shards of Awakening**, brought to **Elisa** at any **camp**.
3. **Relic Energy** — earned from defeating **Brutals** (bosses) and **Ancient Relic Blessings**; spent on **skill trees** (12 total, two per weapon family). Buy **HP and stamina nodes first** — they are the cheapest survivability in the game.
4. **Unique equipment** — every weapon and armor piece appears once; some carry **Drawbacks** (e.g., stamina loss or a weakness) that you must weigh.
5. **Potion capacity** — find **Shards of Esser** on temple altars (e.g., in the **Faded Mist Forest**) to raise your health potion count above the starting **3**.

## Your First Hours — What to Expect

The game is **semi-open**: six large, connected regions (burning villages, ruined castles, void swamps, frozen mountains, underground catacombs, relic temples). Official material confirms **minimal guidance** — you are expected to explore, read letters and murals, and find things yourself.

### Confirmed loop for the opening hours:

1. **Learn the tutorial's unique mechanics** — stamina-for-defense, cooldown skills, and relic equipping are not retaught.
2. **Explore your starting region thoroughly** — hidden paths hold relics, gear, and secret bosses; the game's difficulty assumes you loot.
3. **Bring Shards of Awakening to Elisa at camps** as you find them — every slot matters.
4. **Clear "Events"** (the game's side quests, from NPCs or the environment) — they reward valuable gear.
5. **Fight your first Brutals** — bosses gate progress and drop special items, upgrades, and Relic Energy.

### What is NOT confirmed about the opening:
- The starting area's name, the identity of the first boss, and the exact order of the opening regions are **unconfirmed** at launch. Guides that name a "tutorial boss" or chapter areas are speculative.

## Early Priorities Checklist

- [ ] Pick Sword & Shield to learn defense first (or a weapon you love — all families are viable)
- [ ] Learn to parry — the window is generous, and parries stagger enemies
- [ ] Buy HP/stamina nodes in your weapon's skill tree with Relic Energy
- [ ] Collect Shards of Esser for more potions (start with 3)
- [ ] Unlock relic slots via Shards of Awakening + Elisa at camps
- [ ] Explore everything — uniques are hand-placed and never drop twice
- [ ] Swap relics before each boss — it is the game's intended prep loop
- [ ] Read letters, murals, and item text — that is the story

## Common First-Hour Mistakes

1. **Holding attacks back** — attacks cost zero stamina; passivity loses fights. Be aggressive.
2. **Panic-dodging** — stamina is your defense pool; every wasted dodge is a future death.
3. **Ignoring exploration** — the world is semi-open and difficulty assumes you looted it.
4. **Selling or discarding unique gear** — items never re-drop; every piece is one-of-a-kind.
5. **Skipping Events** — side quests carry gear the critical path does not offer.
6. **Trusting "level"-based guides** — there are no levels; such guides are fabricated.
