# Best Builds Guide — Build Frameworks for Every Weapon

Building a character in The Relic: First Guardian is about **synergy** — combining a weapon family, skill tree purchases, relic fragments, and unique gear into a cohesive combat style. Since there are **no levels or stat points**, your build is defined entirely by what you collect and equip.

**Honesty note**: at launch (August 2026), verified meta data does not exist yet. This guide gives you build **frameworks** built on confirmed mechanics — not named "best builds" with invented relic and artifact names. When the community documents real relic names and effects, this page will be updated with concrete loadouts.

---

## How Builds Work (Confirmed)

| Build Component | What It Does | How You Get More |
|-----------------|--------------|------------------|
| **Weapon family** | Your moveset + 2 skill trees (12 total across 5 families) | Chosen at start; every weapon found is unique |
| **Relics (Runes / Memory Fragments)** | Over 70 unique passive fragments; effects can reshape skills, alter combat tempo, buff weapons, improve survivability, or reduce cooldowns. **Freely swappable.** | Found in exploration |
| **Relic Slots** | Expanded with **Shards of Awakening** (brought to **Elisa** at any camp). More slots = more fragments = more build depth | Exploration |
| **Skills** | Bought with **Relic Energy** (from Brutals and Ancient Relic Blessings). Some nodes raise **HP or stamina**; others buff specific relics | Defeating Brutals, Ancient Relic Blessings |
| **Unique Gear** | Every weapon/armor is one-of-a-kind; some pieces carry **Drawbacks** (stamina loss, a weakness). Forge your favorites at the **blacksmith** | World drops, boss rewards |

Because relics are swappable and gear is unique, builds are **flexible but personal**: you adapt to the fight, and you build around the uniques you found.

## The Confirmed Damage Economy

Damage in The Relic: First Guardian works differently from most soulslikes:

| Rule | What It Means for Damage |
|------|--------------------------|
| Attacks cost zero stamina | DPS is limited by how long you can safely stay in range — never hold back attacks to save stamina |
| Skills run on cooldowns | Fire every skill the moment it comes off cooldown; there is never a reason to save one |
| Stamina is for defense only | Every point of stamina spent attacking is a point you cannot spend dodging or parrying |
| 12 skill trees, two per family | Your damage toolkit grows as you spend Relic Energy on skill nodes |
| Every weapon is unique | Your damage ceiling depends on which uniques you found — not on grinding better drops |

The confirmed takeaway: **damage uptime beats burst planning** — attack constantly between defenses, never ration a resource.

## Pick Your First Weapon

| Family | Beginner Rating | Why |
|--------|-----------------|-----|
| **Sword & Shield** | ★★★★★ | Native block + the game's generous parry = the safest kit in the game |
| **Longsword** | ★★★★ | Balanced; the versatile middle ground |
| **Battle Staff** | ★★★ | Skill-spamming ranged play, but no block — it needs relics first |
| **Dagger (dual blades)** | ★★★ | Fast and aggressive, but fragile |
| **Two-Handed** | ★★ | Big damage, slow swings — punishes new timing |

**Start with Sword & Shield.** The game's defense economy makes block and parry the most forgiving tools, and the parry window is confirmed generous by launch reviews. You can graduate to faster weapons once you know enemy timing.

## The 5 Build Frameworks

### 1. The Guardian (Sword & Shield) — Defense-First

**Confirmed synergy**: the family's native block + the game's generous parry = the safest framework in the game.

- **Skills**: buy block/parry-supporting nodes and **HP/stamina nodes** first.
- **Relics**: prioritize survivability fragments (defense, sustain, stamina) and any that buff defensive play.
- **Gear**: favor high-stability shields/armor; accept Drawbacks only if they do not tax stamina.
- **When to use**: learning the game, parry-gate boss fights, any boss where you are dying fast.

**Play pattern:** block or parry every attack, punish with free attacks (they cost no stamina), and never let the bar hit zero mid-combo. This framework survives every boss long enough for you to learn it.

### 2. The Assassin (Dagger / Dual Blades) — Speed & Burst

**Confirmed synergy**: the fastest family + the game's zero-stamina-attacks economy = constant pressure.

- **Skills**: buy attack-speed and burst skills; consider cooldown-reduction nodes (skills are your finishers).
- **Relics**: fragments that speed attacks, amplify your first hits, or shorten cooldowns; a survivability fragment covers the family's fragility.
- **Gear**: low weight and mobility; skip Drawbacks that cost stamina (dodging is your only defense).
- **When to use**: dodge-gate boss fights, humanoid enemies, players who know attack patterns.

**Damage style:** never stop attacking — dodge through combos and stay glued to the boss, weaving skills between flurries.

### 3. The Duelist (Longsword) — Balanced Parry Play

**Confirmed synergy**: balanced stats + parry = the "learn the game" framework that still deals damage.

- **Skills**: parry-tied skills and general damage nodes.
- **Relics**: hybrid fragments — a mix of damage and survivability; cooldown reduction is strong here.
- **Gear**: any unique that fits; this framework adapts to whatever you find.
- **When to use**: first full playthrough, unknown bosses, all-round content.

**Damage style:** constant pressure between parries — the family with the easiest rhythm for sustained DPS.

### 4. The Juggernaut (Two-Handed) — Heavy Damage

**Confirmed synergy**: big per-hit damage for players who trade deliberately.

- **Skills**: heavy-attack and stagger-oriented nodes (though enemies rarely stagger — see below).
- **Relics**: damage amplifiers and stamina recovery; **stamina management is critical** since your recovery windows are long.
- **Gear**: heavy armors if Drawbacks allow; otherwise accept the speed trade-off.
- **Caveat**: enemies **rarely stagger** — do not build around stun-locking; build around punishing openings.
- **When to use**: players who love deliberate combat and big numbers.

**Damage style:** patience — wait for the big recovery windows and land one heavy hit (or charged skill) per opening.

### 5. The Arcanist (Battle Staff) — Skill-Heavy Casting

**Confirmed synergy**: the staff is described by reviews as the "skill-spamming" family — built for the cooldown system.

- **Skills**: buy as many skills as your Relic Energy allows; the staff wants options.
- **Relics**: **cooldown-reduction fragments are the staff's most valuable relics** (confirmed effect type: relics can reduce cooldowns). Also prioritize damage fragments.
- **Gear**: survivability uniques — the staff has no block, and previews note it needs relic support before it shines.
- **When to use**: players who want ranged/skill-based play; mid-game onward once relics are collected.

**Damage style:** cooldown rotation — stand at range, fire skills as they come up, reposition between casts.

## Maximizing Damage Output

### 1. Attack in Every Dodge Gap
Because attacks are free, attack whenever you are not dodging, blocking, or parrying. Dodge through a combo, then immediately punish the recovery — gaps between boss attacks are your DPS windows.

### 2. Keep Skills on a Cooldown Rotation
Skills cost nothing, so a skill sitting off-cooldown is wasted damage. Weave each skill into the next opening the moment it is ready. The staff family is built around this; the others still benefit from firing skills between weapon swings.

### 3. Punish Boss Combos, Not Boss Wind-Ups
The confirmed boss design (70-80 Brutals split into **parry-gate** and **dodge-gate** fights) punishes greedy players. Attack after a combo fully resolves, not while the boss is still mid-chain. Your defense choice must match the gate type — see the [Boss Strategies guide](/guides/boss-strategies) before optimizing damage.

### 4. Break the Parry Chain Before Bursting
Against **parry-gate** bosses, commit to the parry chain (up to 15 parries per reports) before dumping your highest-damage skills into the broken guard. Stamina spent on offense is stamina you cannot spend on defense.

### 5. Relic Pairings for Damage

There are **70+ relics**, each granting its own **independent passive effect** — the game's main multiplier for damage:

- **Attack relics**: damage-boosting passives are the universal pick for every family.
- **Skill relics**: passives that improve skills pair best with the Battle Staff's cooldown rotation.
- **Survivability relics**: with cursed weapons equipped, healing passives keep the trade profitable.
- Relic **slots** are unlocked with Shards of Awakening (Elisa at camps); relics are **freely swappable**, so tune your loadout to each boss.

**Cursed weapons** are the confirmed risk/reward layer: they trade **damage for health** — you hit harder and lose life doing it. Pair them with healing relics or only equip them in fights where you are confident in your dodges. Whether a curse can be removed or mitigated is **unconfirmed** — plan healing around it either way.

## Skill Purchase Priority (Relic Energy)

1. **HP nodes first** — the confirmed way to raise survivability (the [How to Increase Health Guide](/guides/how-to-increase-health) covers the four methods in full).
2. **Stamina nodes second** — stamina is your only defense currency; a bigger bar is a longer life.
3. **Skill tree damage nodes third** — damage scales your clear speed, not your survival.
4. **Cooldown nodes last** — they matter most once your skills are already strong.

**Rule of thumb:** if a Brutal is killing you, your next skill purchase should be survival, not damage. The Relic Energy you spend on dying is wasted anyway.

## Gear and Drawbacks: The Beginner Rules

Unique gear is one-of-a-kind — there are **no duplicate drops**, so you cannot farm a "better version." That makes gear decisions permanent-ish:

| Gear Rule | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Forge favorites | The blacksmith is the only way to push a favorite weapon's damage up |
| Read Drawbacks | Powerful items can carry drawbacks (stamina loss, a weakness) — always read before equipping |
| Never tax stamina | For beginners, any Drawback that costs stamina is a no-go |
| Swappable relics cover the rest | Resistances, cleanses, and sustain all live on relics — swap, don't suffer |

---

## Universal Build Principles (Confirmed-System-Based)

1. **Buy HP and stamina nodes early in any build** — skill tree nodes raise these stats directly; it is the cheapest survivability in the game.
2. **Expand relic slots before obsessing over individual relics** — slots are permanent adaptivity; every shard of awakening matters.
3. **Adapt per fight** — relics are freely swappable; the game's intended loop is rebuilding between bosses.
4. **Build around your uniques, not against them** — since items never re-drop, a Drawback you can live with is often better than an item you cannot find.
5. **Do not trust named "meta" builds yet** — invented relic/artifact names circulate; verified loadouts come from in-game documentation, not pre-launch guides.

## Build Checklist Before a Boss

- [ ] Weapon forged to a decent level at the blacksmith
- [ ] HP/stamina nodes bought with Relic Energy
- [ ] Relic loadout swapped for the fight's archetype (parry-gate vs dodge-gate)
- [ ] Potion capacity boosted with Shards of Esser where possible
- [ ] Drawbacks on gear checked — none that kill your stamina economy
- [ ] Using 4+ relics with complementary effects (not random fragments)
- [ ] Skills on rotation — nothing sitting off-cooldown during the fight

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the best build in the game?**
A: **Unconfirmed** at launch. All five frameworks are viable; pick by playstyle and adapt per fight.

**Q: Can I change my build?**
A: Yes — relics are freely swappable, weapons keep their tree progress, and there is no respec cost.

**Q: Should I build for stagger?**
A: Be careful — enemies **rarely stagger** and have no visible stagger bar. Parries create openings, not heavy attacks.

**Q: What should I spend Relic Energy on first?**
A: HP/stamina nodes, then skills for your weapon's identity, then cooldown/damage nodes.

**Q: Which weapon has the highest DPS?**
A: **Unconfirmed.** No verified damage numbers exist. Dagger and staff have the highest potential uptime by design; sword & shield offers the safest realized damage.

**Q: Do cursed weapons stay cursed forever?**
A: The damage-for-health trade is confirmed; whether it can be removed or mitigated is **unconfirmed** — plan healing around it either way.

**Q: Are there build videos claiming "one-shot" setups?**
A: Treat them skeptically — no verified launch-meta data exists. Verified builds require documented relic names and effects.
