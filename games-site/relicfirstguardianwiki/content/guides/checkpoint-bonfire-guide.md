# Checkpoints & Camps Guide — Tibelle's Blessing, Camps and Resting

The Relic: First Guardian is a soulslike — so players expect bonfires. The truth is close, but different in one critical way: **resting here does not reset the world.** This guide covers everything confirmed about the game's checkpoints (the glowing **Tibelle's Blessing** stones), the camps where **Elisa** unlocks your relic slots, and the strategy that falls out of how resting actually works.

---

## The Two Landmark Types

| Landmark | What It Is | Confirmed Functions |
|----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Tibelle's Blessing** | Glowing stone checkpoints | Rest point; relics are swappable at rest points; resting does **not** respawn enemies |
| **Camps** | Scattered rest areas | Elisa is here; explosive barrels are set for a reason; Shards of Awakening are spent here |

---

## Tibelle's Blessing: The Checkpoint Stone

The glowing Tibelle's Blessing stones are your rest points — the closest thing the game has to Souls bonfires.

### What Is Confirmed

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Resting does not respawn enemies** | Confirmed. A cleared area stays cleared |
| **Relics are swappable at rest points** | You can rebuild your loadout between fights |
| **What's unconfirmed** | Exact heal/restore effects at the stone, respawn-on-death behavior, and fast travel between stones |

**The big difference from Souls:** in Dark Souls, resting resets the world — the price of a full heal is a full respawn. In The Relic: First Guardian, a cleared path **stays cleared**. That single rule changes the whole game's rhythm: you can grind forward safely, and backtracking is never punishment.

### The Checkpoint Strategy That Follows

1. **Clear deliberately** — because enemies do not respawn, every kill is a permanent advantage. There is no "farm the same mobs" loop; progress is progress.
2. **Swap relics at every stone** — rest points are free re-specs. Check the next area's threat (curses, poison, parry-gate bosses) and swap resistance relics in before you push.
3. **Never skip a stone you find** — an unactivated checkpoint is a longer death-walk.

## Camps: Elisa and the Relic Slots

Camps are the second landmark type — and the home of the game's most important NPC, **Elisa** (the woman who awakens you at the start).

### What Camps Do (Confirmed)

| Function | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Relic slot unlocks** | Bring **Shards of Awakening** (found through exploration) to Elisa at any camp — each shard unlocks a new relic slot |
| **Potion capacity** | Shards of Esser (found on temple altars, e.g. in the Faded Mist Forest) raise your potion count above the starting 3 |
| **Combat tools** | Camps are set with explosive barrels — confirmed terrain tools for clearing packs |

**The slot economy:** more relic slots = more passive fragments equipped = more build depth. Every Shard of Awakening should reach Elisa the moment you find it — the slot is permanent, and its value compounds with every relic you collect.

### Camp Strategy

- **Route camps into your clears** — every camp is a safe hub; clear from camp to camp rather than deep into unknown territory.
- **Explosive barrel discipline** — barrels delete packs but also delete you: pull the pack, roll past, detonate from range.
- **No-rest runs** — for challenge runs, skipping camps and checkpoints entirely is a viable (and brutal) strategy; see the [Difficulty Guide](/guides/difficulty-guide) for the details.

## Checkpoint vs. Bonfire: The Comparison Table

| System | Dark Souls Bonfire | Tibelle's Blessing Stone |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Respawns enemies on rest | Yes | **No — cleared stays cleared** |
| Full heal on rest | Yes | Unconfirmed at launch |
| Loadout changes at rest | Yes | Yes (relics) |
| Permanent upgrades at rest | Level-ups / estus | Elisa's slot unlocks are at camps, not stones |

## Resting Rules for Beginners

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Clear, then rest | Since resting never costs you the cleared world, rest freely — but only after the clear |
| Swap before boss gates | Use the last stone before a boss arena to match relic loadout to the fight |
| Hoard Shards of Esser until needed | Potions are finite per rest; save the capacity for boss runs |
| Check the enemy type first | The [Enemy Types Guide](/guides/enemy-types-guide) tells you what the next area throws — swap relics to match |
