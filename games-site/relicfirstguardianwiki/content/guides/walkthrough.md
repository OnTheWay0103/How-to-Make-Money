# Walkthrough — Main Story & Critical Path Guide

This walkthrough covers the critical path through The Relic: First Guardian's main story. Follow this guide to progress efficiently, avoid missing key items, and defeat bosses in the optimal order.

---

## Prologue: Awakening in Arsiltus

**Starting Area**: Ruined Outpost

The game opens with the First Guardian awakening in the ruined outskirts of Arsiltus. You have no memory of the cataclysm — only a burning fragment of the shattered relic embedded in your chest.

**Objectives**:
1. Learn basic movement and combat from tutorial prompts
2. Defeat the **Withered Gatekeeper** (tutorial boss)
3. Unlock your first Memory Rune slot
4. Proceed to the Forsaken Garrison

**Key Items**: Rune of Fortification (Gatekeeper drop), First Relic Fragment (story item, auto-acquired)

**Missable**: Bulwark Ring artifact — breakable wall behind the first bonfire.

---

## Chapter 1: The Garrison and the Sentinel

**Area**: Forsaken Garrison

A once-proud fortress now occupied by Void-corrupted soldiers. This area introduces group combat and the parry mechanic in earnest.

**Objectives**:
1. Fight through garrison courtyard (3-5 soldier groups)
2. Find the garrison key in the commander's quarters
3. Defeat the **Corrupted Sentinel** (boss)
4. Obtain the Sentinel's Helm artifact

**Key Items**: Garrison Key, Sentinel's Helm (artifact), Rune of Retribution

**Missable**: Shadowstep Boots artifact — rooftop chest accessible via ladder puzzle on the east wall.

---

## Chapter 2: Ruins of Elara

**Area**: Ruins of Elara

The first major open area. Multiple paths, optional mini-bosses, and the game's first real exploration challenge.

**Objectives**:
1. Explore the ruins and activate 3 bonfire equivalents
2. Optional: Defeat the Elara Gravekeeper (mini-boss) for Titan's Ring
3. Find the Void Step tutorial shrine
4. Defeat **Husk of the Betrayer** (story boss)
5. Unlock 3rd artifact slot

**Key Items**: Rune of Evasion, Betrayer's Blade Shard (key item), Rune of Momentum (hidden behind breakable wall)

**Missable**: Arcane Focus artifact — behind illusory wall near the Void Step tutorial area.

---

## Chapter 3: The Cathedral and the Saint

**Area**: Cathedral of the Hungry

A haunting cathedral where prayers went unanswered. This area introduces environmental storytelling at its finest.

**Objectives**:
1. Navigate the cathedral's multi-level interior
2. Solve the choir puzzle to unlock the catacombs
3. Optional: Defeat the secret catacomb boss for Rune of Vampirism
4. Defeat **The Starving Saint** (story boss)
5. Unlock 4th artifact slot (hidden altar in catacombs)

**Key Items**: Rune of Preservation, Saint's Tear (artifact), Rune of Vampirism (optional secret boss)

**Missable**: The secret catacomb boss is permanently missable if you defeat the Saint first. Explore the catacombs BEFORE engaging the Saint.

---

## Chapter 4: The Scorched Battlefield

**Area**: Scorched Battlefield

A war-torn wasteland haunted by the ghosts of a burned army. Drakhan awaits.

**Objectives**:
1. Cross the battlefield, fighting through ash soldier patrols
2. Defeat 3 Ash Commander mini-bosses for Juggernaut Plate
3. Activate the siege engines to open the general's camp
4. Defeat **Drakhan, the Ashen General** (story boss)

**Key Items**: Rune of Devastation, Ashen General's Pauldron, Rune of Fortitude (burned armory), Juggernaut Plate (defeat all 3 Commanders)

---

## Chapter 5: Twin Lakes

**Area**: Twin Lakes

A serene but deadly area. The twin lakes reflect the sky but hide a tragic secret beneath.

**Objectives**:
1. Navigate between the two lakes, using bridges and boats
2. Solve the water-level puzzle to drain the central island
3. Defeat **The Weeping Twins** (dual boss)
4. Unlock 5th rune slot

**Key Items**: Rune of Swiftness, Twins' Locket, Frostheart Talisman (lakebed mini-boss)

---

## Chapter 6: The Shattered Relic Chamber

**Area**: Shattered Relic Chamber

Deep beneath Arsiltus lies the chamber where the relic first shattered. The Void is strongest here.

**Objectives**:
1. Descend through 3 layers of the chamber
2. Collect 4 Fragment of the First Relic pieces (required for final boss)
3. Defeat the **Void-Touched Guardian** (story boss)
4. Unlock 7th rune slot

**Key Items**: Rune of the Arcanist, Crown of Storms (artifact), Rune of Chaos, Fragment of the First Relic ×4

---

## Chapter 7: Throne of Ashes (Mid-Game Finale)

**Area**: Throne of Ashes

The seat of the Forgotten King. All paths converge here.

**Objectives**:
1. Ascend the throne room, fighting royal guard elites
2. Solve the crown puzzle to weaken the King's defenses
3. Defeat **The Forgotten King** (mid-game finale boss)
4. Unlock 8th rune slot

**Key Items**: Rune of the Void, King's Shattered Crown, Rune of Immortality (hidden chamber post-boss), Sword Saint's Sash (rafters jump puzzle)

---

## Post Mid-Game

After defeating the Forgotten King:
- The remaining world areas unlock (late-game regions)
- NG+ becomes available
- Secret bosses become accessible
- The "Relic Collector" quest for the 5th artifact slot becomes completable

- [ ] Ruined Outpost → Gatekeeper → First Relic Fragment
- [ ] Forsaken Garrison → Sentinel → Sentinel's Helm
- [ ] Ruins of Elara → Betrayer → Betrayer's Blade Shard
- [ ] Cathedral → Saint → Saint's Tear (EXPLORE CATACOMBS FIRST)
- [ ] Scorched Battlefield → Drakhan → Ashen General's Pauldron
- [ ] Twin Lakes → Weeping Twins → Twins' Locket
