# Walkthrough — Main Story & Progression

This walkthrough covers what is **confirmed** about The Relic: First Guardian's main story and world structure at launch (August 2026). Because the game released on July 31, 2026, step-by-step chapter guides with named bosses and exact objectives do not yet exist in verified form — this guide gives you the confirmed skeleton, the real progression systems, and honest markers for what is still unconfirmed.

---

## The Confirmed Premise

The game opens with a classic soulslike setup, confirmed by official material:

- You are the **last Guardian** in **Arsiltus**, a once-prosperous land destroyed when the **great Relic shattered**.
- The land is being **swallowed by a Void**, and the world's people have been twisted into monsters.
- You collect **relic fragments imbued with the memories and emotions of the dead**.
- Your goal: seal the Void and restore the world.

Storytelling is fragmented — the game tells its story through **letters, murals, and environmental details** rather than cutscenes. Reading item descriptions and world text is the intended way to piece the story together.

---

## The Confirmed World: 6 Regions

Arsiltus is a **semi-open world** with **six seamlessly connected regions**, separated by natural obstacles (mountains, valleys) rather than corridor gates:

| Region | Confirmed Theme |
|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Burning villages** |
| 2 | **Ruined castles** |
| 3 | **Void swamps** |
| 4 | **Frozen mountains** |
| 5 | **Underground catacombs** |
| 6 | **Relic temples** |

One named area is confirmed by launch coverage: the **Faded Mist Forest** (a location where Shards of Esser — potion capacity upgrades — can be found on temple altars). Exact zone names, order, and the critical path through the regions are **unconfirmed** at launch. Any walkthrough that names specific chapters, bosses, and key items is speculative.

## How Progression Works (No Levels)

The critical path is not about XP — the game has **no traditional leveling system**. You progress in four confirmed ways:

1. **Relics** — over 70 unique fragments with passive effects; equip them as you find them (more slots unlock via Shards of Awakening at camps — see the [Upgrade System guide](/guides/upgrade-system)).
2. **Relic Energy** — earned from defeating **Brutals** and finding **Ancient Relic Blessings**; spent on skills in the 12 weapon skill trees.
3. **Unique gear** — every weapon and armor piece is unique; some carry Drawbacks (e.g., stamina loss or a weakness). Power comes from what you find and forge.
4. **Potions** — health potion capacity grows via **Shards of Esser** found on temple altars.

A practical walkthrough mindset: **exploration is the walkthrough**. The critical path gates you through regions, but the power to clear it comes from looting, not grinding.

## The Confirmed Loop: Brutals + Events

- **Brutals** (the game's 70-80 bosses) sit along and off the critical path. Defeating them yields special items, upgrades, and Relic Energy — the game expects you to engage with them.
- **Events** (the game's term for side quests) are optional missions obtained from **NPCs or discovered through the environment** — fetch quests, defending locations, and the like. They reward valuable gear given the equipment-driven progression.

**Suggested route**: as you enter each region, clear its events and explore its hidden paths before pushing toward the story boss. This mirrors the game's designed difficulty curve.

## Practical Walkthrough Tips (Confirmed Systems)

1. **Talk to NPCs and read everything** — events and lore are delivered through NPCs, letters, and murals; the game gives minimal guidance.
2. **Explore every region before its boss** — hidden paths, secret bosses, and rare relics live off the main road. The game openly rewards this.
3. **Collect Shards of Esser in temples** — more potions = more attempts per run; the Faded Mist Forest is one confirmed source.
4. **Bring Shards of Awakening to Elisa at camps** — each one is a permanent relic slot.
5. **Spend Relic Energy on HP/stamina nodes early** — skill tree nodes raise these stats directly.
6. **Adapt your build to each Brutal** — the intended loop is swapping relics and gear between fights, not brute-forcing.

## Common Questions

**Q: Where does the game start?**
A: You awaken as the last Guardian in the ruined world of Arsiltus. The exact starting area name is unconfirmed.

**Q: Is the world open or linear?**
A: **Semi-open** — six large, seamlessly connected regions separated by natural obstacles.

**Q: How do I get stronger without XP?**
A: Relics, relic slots, skills bought with Relic Energy, unique gear (forged at blacksmiths), and potion capacity. See the [Upgrade System guide](/guides/upgrade-system).

**Q: What are "Events"?**
A: The game's optional side quests — missions from NPCs or the environment (fetch quests, defense objectives) that reward gear.

**Q: Is there New Game Plus?**
A: **Unconfirmed** — no official source has confirmed NG+.
