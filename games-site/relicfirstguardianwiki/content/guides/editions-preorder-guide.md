# Editions & Pre-Order Guide — Bonuses, Platforms & How to Claim

> Last updated: August 11, 2026

This guide covers what each edition of The Relic: First Guardian includes, how the pre-order bonus works, and what to do if your bonus didn't appear.

## Editions

> **⚠️ Verification note:** The **physical release date (September 4, 2026)**, the **Forgotten Stories Edition**, and the itemized bonus contents below are **retailer-reported and not confirmed in official material**. Project Cloud Games and Perp Games have only officially confirmed the digital Standard edition and the PS5 pre-order bonus pack. Verify physical listings with your local retailer before relying on them.

| Edition | Platforms | Price (US) | Includes |
|---------|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Standard** (Digital) | PS5, Steam | $49.99 | Base game |
| **Standard** (Physical, retailer-listed) | PS5 | $49.99 | Base game (retailer-listed release September 4, 2026 — unconfirmed) |
| **Forgotten Stories Edition** (Collector's, retailer-listed) | PS5 (Physical) | TBA | Base game + physical collector's items (retailer-listed September 4 — unconfirmed) |

Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2 versions are confirmed but not yet dated.

## Pre-Order Bonus: The Night of the Unextinguished Flame

Pre-ordering **any digital edition** (PS5 or Steam) before the July 31, 2026 launch includes this bonus pack at no extra cost. The item names below are **retailer/community-reported** — official material confirms the pack contains a **unique Relic, exclusive armor and weapons, and a new dungeon**, without itemizing them:

| Item (reported) | Type | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| Sanctuary of the Forgotten Light | Bonus Dungeon | A brutal bonus dungeon with unique encounters |
| Sealstone of the Undying Ember | Unique Relic (Sealstone) | Exclusive relic providing a unique passive effect |
| Armor of the Unextinguished Vigil | Armor Set | Exclusive armor set |
| Unextinguished Vigil | Weapon Set | Exclusive sword and shield set |
| The Night of the Unextinguished Flame | Event | Bonus in-game event |

## How to Claim

**Digital pre-orders (PS5/Steam)**: The bonus is applied **automatically** — there is no separate code or manual claim step. The items should appear in your inventory upon starting the game.

**If your bonus is missing**:

| Platform | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Steam | Right-click the game → Properties → DLC → ensure the bonus pack is checked. If missing, verify game files or contact Steam Support |
| PS5 | Highlight the game → Options → Manage Game Content → ensure the bonus is installed. Try restoring licenses (Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses) |
| Both | Ensure you purchased a **pre-order** copy, not a post-launch purchase. Post-launch purchases (after July 31, 2026) do not include the bonus |

**Physical edition (September 4)**: The physical release is **not confirmed** to include the pre-order bonus. If you pre-ordered a physical copy, check with your retailer.
