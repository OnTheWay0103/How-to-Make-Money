# Late-Game Guide — Endgame Progression & Optimization

The late game of The Relic: First Guardian is about **squeezing every passive out of your build**: completing your relic collection, forging your gear, hunting the optional Brutals, and adapting to the hardest fights. This guide covers what is **confirmed** about the endgame at launch (August 2026) — and warns against the fabricated "NG+ superboss" guides circulating online.

---

## The Confirmed Endgame Loop

Since there are no levels, the late game is collection and optimization:

1. **Complete the relic collection** — over 70 unique fragments exist; hunting the missing ones is a genuine endgame goal.
2. **Expand relic slots** — Shards of Awakening (via Elisa at camps) power up your loadout depth.
3. **Forge your final gear** — blacksmith upgrades make your chosen uniques endgame-viable.
4. **Hunt optional Brutals** — most of the 70-80 boss roster is optional; secret bosses hide in the world.
5. **Raise potion capacity** — Shards of Esser on temple altars (e.g., Faded Mist Forest) are worth chasing to the last one.

## Relic Optimization (Confirmed System, Real Advice)

- **Specialize, don't spread** — relics have confirmed effect families (skill reshaping, combat tempo, weapon buffs, survivability, cooldown reduction). Stacking fragments that support one playstyle is stronger than covering everything thinly. (Strategy advice grounded in the confirmed design.)
- **Adapt per boss** — the game's explicit intended loop; late-game bosses demand the discipline of swapping loadouts between fights.
- **Cooldown reduction scales late** — with skills on cooldown timers and relics confirmed to shorten them, late builds can run skill-heavy rotations, especially with the battle staff.
- **Slots beat any single relic** — a new slot is permanent adaptivity; prioritize shard hunting over chasing one specific fragment.

---

## Forging & Gear Late Game (Confirmed System)

- **Your uniques are final** — every item appears once; the blacksmith is the only way to push a favorite weapon further. Exact costs and max levels are **unconfirmed**.
- **Drawbacks compound** — late-game uniques with Drawbacks (stamina loss, weaknesses) demand build discipline: check each piece's tax before equipping, especially stamina taxes in the defense economy.
- **Do not sell uniques** — no confirmed merchant/dismantle value; items never re-drop.

## The Optional-Brutal Hunt

Most of the 70-80 bosses are **optional** — the confirmed endgame activity is hunting them:

1. Explore every region's hidden paths and secret areas (see the [Secret Areas guide](/guides/secret-areas)).
2. Use the confirmed design split — parry-gate vs dodge-gate — to prep (see the [Boss Strategies guide](/guides/boss-strategies)).
3. Boss kills are a primary source of **Relic Energy** — the endgame is self-funding.

---

## Late-Game Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Trusting "NG+ prep" advice** — with NG+ unconfirmed, "stockpile for NG+" is fabrication. Spend your materials on the confirmed endgame.
2. **Selling or discarding uniques** — permanent loss; never re-drop.
3. **Ignoring survivability nodes** — even late, HP/stamina nodes bought with Relic Energy remain the cheapest defense.
4. **Skipping temple altars** — Shards of Esser are the last confirmed "free" power: potion capacity.
5. **Rushing the critical path** — the endgame's value is in the optional content; the world is the reward.
