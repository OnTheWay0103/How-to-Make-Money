## Late-Game Guide — Optimization & Secret Content

The late game in The Relic: First Guardian is about squeezing every passive out of your build: optimizing runes, completing secret areas, and stacking upgrades before New Game+ scales everything up. This guide covers the post-story loop and how to prepare for the hardest content.

### What Opens After the Story

| Unlock | Requirement | Value |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Secret Areas | Post-story key items | Exclusive artifacts and runes |
| NG+ | Clear the final boss | Replay with scaled enemies and better drops |
| Rune Forging | Story completion | Craft the strongest runes |
| Boss Rematches | Arena unlock | Upgrade materials on repeat kills |
| Artifact Vault | Full artifact set | Set bonus triggers |

### Secret Area Checklist

1. **The Overgrown Crypt:** Behind the statue in the second region — requires the Garden Key from the hidden shrine.
2. **The Collapsed Mine:** Enter via the breakable wall left of the mine entrance; contains the Obsidian Rune.
3. **The Sunken Temple:** Accessible only at night cycle; rewards the Tide Artifact.
4. **The Guardian's Vault:** Requires 3 Vault Fragments from boss rematches; contains the final artifact piece.

### Rune Optimization

- **The 70+ catalog:** Late-game builds rely on stacking two or three rune families; spread picks are weak picks.
- **Best late-game runes:** Berserker (damage at low HP), Vampiric (lifesteal), and Focus (cooldown) form the core of most endgame builds.
- **Farming locations:** Memory fragments drop from secret area bosses and NG+ elites — never pass a fragment node.
- **Rune slots:** Unlock the fourth rune slot via the Artifact Vault before NG+; it's the single biggest power jump.

### Upgrade Material Farming

| Material | Best Source | Use |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Relic Shards | Boss rematches | Level 5-8 upgrades |
| Pure Essence | Secret area bosses | Level 9-10 upgrades |
| Guardian Cores | NG+ elites | Level 11+ upgrades |
| Vault Fragments | Arena rematches | Vault key pieces |

### Late-Game Build Priorities

1. Finish your artifact set — the 4-piece set bonus outweighs any single upgrade.
2. Lock your rune families before NG+; rune drops get rarer relative to enemy scaling.
3. Push upgrades to at least +8 before NG+ — enemy scaling starts at your final story power.
4. Complete all secret areas first; their exclusive runes and artifacts define late-game builds.

### Late-Game Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Entering NG+ with uncompleted secret areas:** Secret area rewards are locked to story difficulty; NG+ scaling makes them far harder to earn.
2. **Enhancing artifacts over runes:** Runes provide permanent passives; artifacts only last one build. Rune upgrades compound across every future run.
3. **Skipping boss rematches:** Rematch materials are the cheapest source of high-level upgrade stones — rematches are the late-game daily loop.
4. **Ignoring the fourth rune slot:** The Vault's fourth slot is the largest power increase available; prioritize it over a fifth upgrade level on gear.
