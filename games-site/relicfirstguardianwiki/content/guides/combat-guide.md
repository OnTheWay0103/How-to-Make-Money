# Combat Guide — Mechanics, Parry & Stamina Deep Dive

The Relic: First Guardian's combat system sets itself apart from traditional soulslikes with one key innovation: **attacks cost zero stamina**. This creates a uniquely aggressive combat flow where offense and defense use separate resources. Master these mechanics to dominate every encounter.

---

## The Stamina Revolution

### Core Rules
- **Attacks**: Zero stamina cost — swing freely
- **Dodging**: Costs stamina based on equipment weight
- **Blocking**: Costs stamina per hit blocked (varies by shield stability)
- **Skills**: Use cooldown timers, not stamina or mana

### Stamina Management Strategy

Unlike traditional soulslikes where you need to budget stamina for both attacking and dodging, The Relic: First Guardian lets you go all-out on offense while keeping a full stamina bar for defense. This means:

1. **Never stop attacking** — There's no stamina penalty for aggression.
2. **Save stamina exclusively for defense** — Your bar is your "get out of jail" resource.
3. **Regen is fast** — Stamina recovers quickly when not sprinting or blocking (faster with Rune of Patience).
4. **Don't panic dodge** — Every unnecessary dodge wastes stamina you might need seconds later.

## Parry System

### Basic Parry Mechanics
- Press block at the moment an enemy attack lands
- Successful parry: enemy is staggered, open to riposte (critical counter-attack)
- Failed parry (too early): You take partial damage and lose stamina
- Failed parry (too late / missed): Full damage

### Parry Windows by Weapon

| Weapon | Parry Window | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Sword & Shield | 18 frames (easiest) | Shield has largest parry window |
| Sword | 14 frames | Standard parry timing |
| Dual Daggers | 10 frames (hardest) | Small window, but riposte deals +50% damage |
| Two-Handed | 8 frames | Can't parry — must dodge instead |
| Staff | 6 frames | Spell parry reflects projectiles |

### Parry Tips
1. **Watch the enemy's weapon hand**, not their body
2. **Press block when the weapon begins its forward motion**, not during windup
3. **Duelist's Charm artifact** adds +6 frames to all parry windows
4. **Rune of Riposte** increases riposte damage by +40%
5. **Practice on the Withered Gatekeeper** — its attacks are the slowest and most telegraphed

## Dodge System

### Dodge Types
- **Quick Dodge** (light equipment): Fast sidestep, 12 i-frames, low stamina cost
- **Standard Dodge** (medium equipment): Roll, 14 i-frames, medium stamina cost
- **Heavy Dodge** (heavy equipment): Slow roll, 10 i-frames, high stamina cost

### Dodge Tips
1. **Dodge into attacks**, not away — you pass through hitboxes faster
2. **Rune of Evasion** adds +4 i-frames, making dodging significantly easier
3. **Don't spam dodge** — waiting half a second between dodges prevents being roll-caught
4. **Sprint for AoE attacks** — ground-targeted AoEs are easier to run from than dodge through

## Skill Cooldown System

Skills in The Relic: First Guardian use cooldown timers instead of mana or stamina:

- **Fast skills**: 3-5 second cooldown (basic combos, quick spells)
- **Medium skills**: 8-12 second cooldown (gap closers, AoE attacks)
- **Heavy skills**: 20-30 second cooldown (ultimates, transformation skills)

### Cooldown Optimization
1. **Open with heavy skills** — Use your longest cooldown first so it starts recharging
2. **Weave skills between attacks** — Normal attacks during skill cooldowns maintain DPS
3. **Don't hold skills "for the right moment"** — If it's off cooldown, use it
4. **Rune of Channeling** reduces staff skill cooldowns by 20%

## Enemy Telegraphing

Every enemy attack has a tell. Here's what to watch for:

| Tell Type | Visual Cue | Response |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Weapon glow | White flash on weapon | Parry (physical attack incoming) |
| Purple aura | Void energy around enemy | Dodge (unblockable attack incoming) |
| Eye flash | Enemy eyes turn red | Sprint away (grab attack incoming) |
| Ground indicator | Red circle/line on ground | Move out of area (AoE incoming) |
| Audio cue | Distinct sound before attack | Prepare to dodge/parry based on sound |

## Advanced Combat Techniques

### Animation Canceling
Certain skills can cancel the recovery animation of normal attacks:
- **Dodge cancel** — Dodge immediately after a light attack to abort the recovery animation
- **Skill cancel** — Use a quick skill to cancel a heavy attack's recovery

### Stagger Locking
Build up stagger on enemies with heavy attacks and specific skills. When an enemy's stagger meter fills:
- They are stunned for 3-5 seconds
- All attacks during stagger deal +30% damage
- Rune of Overwhelm increases stagger buildup by 30%

### Back Attacks
Attacking enemies from behind deals +50% damage and guarantees critical hits with Rune of Lethality. Dual Daggers with Assassin's Mark skill tree deals +100% damage on back attacks.

### Elemental Interactions
Staff wielders can exploit elemental combinations:
- **Fire + Oil** (from environment): Explosion and burn DoT
- **Ice + Water**: Frozen solid for 3 seconds
- **Lightning + Wet**: Chain lightning to nearby enemies

## Combat Checklist for New Players

1. Attack constantly — there's no stamina cost
2. Save stamina ONLY for dodging and blocking
3. Learn enemy telegraphs — white flash = parry, purple = dodge
4. Practice parrying on the first boss until it's instinctive
5. Open boss fights with your longest cooldown skill
6. One or two hits per opening, then reset
7. Swap runes before every major boss fight
