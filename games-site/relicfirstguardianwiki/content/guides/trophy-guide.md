# Trophy & Achievement Guide — Full List and Platinum Route

The Relic: First Guardian ships with **40 trophies on PS5** (1 Platinum, 3 Gold, 12 Silver, 24 Bronze) and the equivalent achievement set on Steam. The Platinum is a serious commitment — expect 60-80 hours, because it demands the completionist run: every boss, every rune, every ending. This guide breaks down the full list, flags the missables, and gives you the fastest route to 100%.

*Trophy names reflect the launch build (1.0) and may vary slightly by region. Requirements are identical across PS5 and Steam.*

## Missable Trophies — Read Before You Play

- **Vampiric (secret catacomb boss)** — The secret catacomb boss in the Cathedral of the Hungry is **permanently missable** if you defeat The Starving Saint first. Clear the catacombs before the Saint, every run.
- **Chronicle Complete (all endings)** — The secret ending requires all 24 Memory Fragments plus all 12 Journal Pages. Several fragments sit in areas you cannot return to, so collect as you go.
- **Relic Ascended** — Requires Primeval Crystals from NG+ bosses and the Void Walker. Not missable, but plan for a second playthrough.
- **Untouchable** — Easiest on the Withered Gatekeeper (tutorial boss) before you outlevel its damage. Do it on a fresh character or in NG+.

## Fastest Platinum Route

1. **Playthrough 1**: Complete the critical path, clearing optional bosses as you reach each region (before story bosses where the guide warns). Collect every rune, fragment, and journal page. Unlock both base endings via save backup before the final boss.
2. **Relic tier 10**: Farm Void Shards in Whispering Woods/Sunken Citadel daily resets; dismantle all gear for Ember Dust; save every Primeval Crystal.
3. **NG+**: Speedrun to the superbosses, mop up co-op trophies with a friend (scaled NG+ bosses count), and clear any missed regional bosses.

**Estimated total**: 60-80 hours, matching the completionist estimate in the [Game Length guide](/guides/game-length-guide).

## Tips

1. **Do the Untouchable trophy first** — it only gets harder as you get stronger weapons.
2. **Use the boss kill counter** in the pause menu to verify Elder Slayer progress.
3. **Co-op counts for every boss trophy** — but the host and guest must both land the killing blow on the same encounter for progress to count.
4. **Keep a save backup before the final decision** — it halves the ending grind.
