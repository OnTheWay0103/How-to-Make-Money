# Trophy & Achievement Guide — What's Confirmed and What's Not

The Relic: First Guardian launched on **PS5 and Steam on July 31, 2026**, so both platforms have trophy/achievement support. This guide is an honest look at what is **confirmed** about the trophy setup at launch and what is **not** — because several circulating "full trophy lists" are fabricated.

## The Honest Status at Launch

| Question | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Does the PS5 version have trophies? | **Yes** — it is a native PS5 release |
| Does the Steam version have achievements? | **Yes** — Steam releases have achievement support; the exact set is being verified |
| Is the full trophy list documented? | **No** — exact trophy names, counts, and rarities are **unconfirmed** at launch |
| Is there a Platinum trophy? | **Very likely** (standard for PS5 titles) but **not verified** |
| Are there missable trophies? | **Unknown** — cannot be assessed until the real list is documented |

**Important warning**: several websites publish "The Relic: First Guardian — full trophy list" pages with invented trophy names (e.g., story trophies named after bosses like "The Forgotten King," co-op trophies, and collection trophies). The game has **no co-op**, and almost no boss names are confirmed — a trophy list containing such names is fabricated. The game launched only days ago; a verified list takes time.

## What the Real Completion Goals Look Like

While the trophy list itself is unverified, the game's confirmed systems tell us what a 100% run would plausibly involve. Use these as *likely* categories, not confirmed trophy requirements:

### Bosses (Platinum-style commitment)
- The roster is **70-80 Brutals**, most optional. Any "defeat all bosses" achievement would be the largest grind in the game.
- See the [All Bosses List](/guides/all-bosses-list) for what is confirmed about the roster.

### Collections
- **Over 70 unique relic fragments** to collect.
- **Every weapon and armor piece is unique** — a "collect all equipment" goal would demand thorough exploration.
- **Shards of Esser** (potion capacity) and **Shards of Awakening** (relic slots) are spread through the world.

### Combat Feats
- **Parry chains** — reviews report chains of up to 15 parries against some bosses; a parry-count achievement is plausible.
- **Boss kills** — with 70-80 Brutals, kill-count achievements are likely.

### Story
- **Endings** — the number of endings is **unconfirmed**. If the game has multiple endings (pre-launch coverage hinted at 2-3), an "all endings" trophy is plausible. See the [Endings guide](/guides/endings-guide).

## What to Check Before Trusting a Trophy Guide

1. **Does it mention co-op trophies?** — If yes, it is fabricated: the game is single-player only.
2. **Does it name specific bosses?** — Almost no boss names are confirmed; named lists are invented.
3. **Does it reference "levels" or "NG+"?** — The game has no levels, and NG+ is unconfirmed.
4. **Does it cite exact trophy counts?** — Counts are unverified; any "40 trophies, 1 Platinum, 3 Gold, 12 Silver, 24 Bronze" style breakdown is invented.

## How to Track Your Own Completion

Until verified lists exist, track the confirmed systems yourself:

- **Brutal kills** — the game encourages killing all bosses; note which regions you cleared.
- **Relic fragments** — count your collection against the 70+ confirmed fragments.
- **Shards of Esser** — potion capacity is a simple, trackable progression metric.
- **Events (side quests)** — complete every event you find; they gate unique rewards.
- **Skill trees** — 12 trees (2 per weapon family) fully bought is a concrete completion goal.
