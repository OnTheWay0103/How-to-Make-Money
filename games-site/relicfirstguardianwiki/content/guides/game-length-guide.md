# Game Length & Content Overview — How Long to Beat

The Relic: First Guardian is a substantial soulslike with 70+ bosses, a semi-open world, and deep build customization. Here's what to expect in terms of playtime and content volume.

---

## Playtime Estimates

| Playstyle | Estimated Hours | What's Included |
|-----------|:--------------:|-----------------|
| **Main Story Only** | 30-40 hours | Critical path bosses, minimal exploration |
| **Main + Side Content** | 45-55 hours | Story + optional bosses, some exploration |
| **Completionist** | 60-80 hours | All 70+ bosses, all runes, all artifacts, all side quests |
| **Speedrun (any%)** | TBD | Post-launch speedrun community will optimize |

---

## Content Breakdown

### Main Story (30-40 hours)
- 8 major story bosses (Gatekeeper → Forgotten King)
- 7 main areas (Ruined Outpost through Throne of Ashes)
- Core narrative: recover the 4 Relic Fragments and seal the Void
- Mid-game finale at Throne of Ashes; late-game continues beyond

### Optional Content (additional 15-25 hours)
- 60+ optional and secret bosses
- Hidden areas and exploration zones
- Side quests (Korean folklore-inspired tales)
- Rune and artifact collection
- Mini-boss gauntlets and challenge areas

### Post-Game and NG+ (additional 10-20 hours)
- New Game Plus with scaled difficulty
- Potential new enemy placements and boss variations
- Secret ending(s) requiring specific conditions
- Post-game superbosses

## What Affects Playtime?

### Factors That Increase Playtime
- **Thorough exploration** — The semi-open world rewards checking every corner
- **Boss difficulty** — Expect to die and retry. Soulslike veterans may clear faster, newcomers slower.
- **Build experimentation** — Switching weapons and rune setups adds hours
- **Side quest completion** — Korean folklore quests add significant story content
- **Rune hunting** — Finding all 70+ runes takes dedicated exploration

### Factors That Decrease Playtime
- **Soulslike experience** — If you've beaten Elden Ring, Lies of P, or similar games, you'll progress faster
- **Following a guide** — This walkthrough can save 5-10 hours of wandering
- **Sticking to one build** — Less experimentation = faster progression
- **Skipping optional bosses** — You can complete the story with only ~15 bosses

---

## Replayability

The Relic: First Guardian offers strong replay value through:

1. **5 Weapon Families** — A full playthrough with a different weapon feels substantially different
2. **Build Diversity** — 70+ runes and dozens of artifacts enable countless combinations
3. **NG+ Scaling** — Enemies scale, potentially with new moves and placements
4. **Multiple Endings** — Preview information suggests at least 2-3 endings
5. **Missable Content** — Some bosses, artifacts, and quests are permanently missable

---

## Content Value Assessment

At **$39.99**, The Relic: First Guardian offers excellent value:
- **$1.00/hour** for main story only
- **$0.57/hour** for completionist runs
- Compares favorably to similar soulslikes (Lies of P: ~30-40 hrs, Elden Ring: ~50-130 hrs)

## Tips for Time-Efficient Play

1. **Unlock rune slots early** — More runes = more power = faster boss kills
2. **B-line critical path first** — Do optional content after unlocking fast travel
3. **Use Sword & Shield for blind runs** — It's the safest weapon for unfamiliar encounters
4. **Don't farm** — Boss drops provide enough progression. Exploration finds > grinding
5. **Read boss guides** — Knowing attack patterns saves 2-10 attempts per boss
