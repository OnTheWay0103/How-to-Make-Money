# Game Length — How Long to Beat

**Honest answer**: there are **no verified playtime numbers** for The Relic: First Guardian at launch (August 2026). The game released July 31, 2026 — no reliable "hours to beat" data (e.g., from HowLongToBeat or wide community reporting) exists yet. These facts are confirmed and directly drive playtime:

| Content | Confirmed |

| Boss roster | **70-80 Brutals**, most optional |

## What Makes Playtime Vary

- **Exploration depth** — the semi-open world rewards thorough looting; a thorough player spends far longer per region.
- **Boss difficulty** — deaths and retries dominate time. Reviews note the roster is demanding; expect practice time.
- **No leveling grind** — there is no XP grind to pad or shorten; progression is exploration-driven.
- **Build experimentation** — 12 skill trees and freely swappable relics invite replay; committed players will spend hours per build.
- **Completionist goals** — over 70 relics + every unique item + 70-80 bosses is a long checklist by design.

## Time-Efficient Play Tips (Confirmed Systems)

1. **Explore as you go, not after** — backtracking later costs time; clear regions fully while the path is fresh.
2. **Buy HP/stamina nodes early** — fewer deaths = less retry time.
3. **Collect Shards of Esser** — potion capacity reduces failed-attempt churn.
4. **Swap relics per boss** — the game's intended prep loop; adapted builds kill faster than stubborn ones.
5. **Skip the grind mentality** — there is no XP to farm; exploration is the only real farming, and it pays.
