# Relic First Guardian Parry Timing Guide — The Window Is Generous, the Discipline Isn't

**The Relic: First Guardian / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Launch reviews are unanimous on one point: The Relic: First Guardian's parry window is **generous** compared to most soulslikes. That's the good news. The bad news is that parrying isn't the skill — *reading tells* is. This guide covers the confirmed parry rules, the tell-reading method, the parry-chain discipline that breaks boss guards (chains of **up to 15 parries** are reported), and the stamina math that turns parries into a resource, not a reflex.

---

## 1. The Confirmed Parry Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Window | Generous — confirmed by multiple launch reviews |
| Press timing | Block/parry input as the enemy attack lands |
| Success | Parries **stagger enemies** and build toward breaking a boss's guard |
| Parry-gate bosses | Some need **repeated parries — up to 15 in a row** — before an attack window opens |
| Failure | A failed parry leaves you vulnerable — **you cannot parry mid-swing** |
| Unblockable attacks | Must be dodged — blocking and parrying fail |

**The system context:** attacks cost **zero stamina**; stamina is defense-only (dodge, block, parry). This is what makes parrying affordable — you're never choosing between attacking and parrying, only between defenses.

---

## 2. Reading Tells (The Real Skill)

The #1 confirmed tip from strategy guides: **watch the weapon, not the body.**

| Tell Category | What to Read | Response |
|---------------|--------------|----------|
| Wind-up length | The attack limb's full swing arc | Parry as the weapon reaches its extension, before impact |
| Chain position | Which hit of a combo is coming | Parry the last hit of a chain — mid-chain parries build the meter, end-of-chain parries create the window |
| Heavier tells | Tells that look heavier than the rest of the chain | **Dodge** — that's the unblockable woven into the combo |
| Rhythm breaks | A boss that pauses mid-combo | The pause is bait — don't parry a dead window |

**The two-second rule:** if a tell looks ambiguous, dodge. A missed parry leaves you vulnerable; a dodge always moves you. When in doubt, the game's generous window favors parrying — but only when the attack is *definitely* parryable.

---

## 3. Parry-Chain Discipline (The 15-Parry Fights)

Parry-gate bosses work on a two-phase loop:

| Phase | What You Do | Mistakes |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Chain phase | Land deliberate parries to fill the guard meter (up to 15) | Parrying a rhythm you assume instead of the attack coming |
| Window phase | Guard breaks — the boss is staggered and open | Wasting the window on weak attacks; burst with your highest-damage skills (skills run on cooldowns, not resources — spend them here) |

**Chain rules:**

1. **Parry the attack that is coming, not the beat you expect.** Parry-gate bosses vary their rhythm deliberately.
2. **Count the chain.** Knowing you're at parry 11 of 15 keeps you from breaking early.
3. **Never parry an unblockable.** In mixed fights, bosses weave unblockable hits into combos — a "heavier" tell is the dodge signal.
4. **Preserve stamina for the chain.** Every failed parry costs stamina; treat the bar as chain ammo.

---

## 4. Stamina Discipline (Parry as a Resource)

Since attacks are free, the stamina economy is purely defensive:

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Never engage with an empty bar | Most deaths come from engaging at zero stamina |
| Reset distance to regen | When the bar is low, back off and wait — recovery is faster than respawn |
| Don't panic-dodge | Wasted dodges are the #1 new-player stamina leak — dodging *out of* a parryable fight costs twice |
| Guard stamina for parry chains | In parry-gate fights, stamina *is* the win condition |

**Exact costs are unconfirmed** at launch — guides quoting "25 stamina per roll" are speculative. The confirmed shape: parrying spends stamina, attacks don't, and the bar is defense-only.

---

## 5. The Practice Routine

1. **Start on regular enemies, not bosses.** Normal enemies telegraph generously — perfect parry practice (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide) for the full basics).
2. **Learn one attack at a time.** Fight the same enemy repeatedly, parrying only its wind-up attack, until it's automatic.
3. **Then learn chain-parrying.** Parry every hit of a three-hit combo, ending on the last.
4. **Then take it to a parry-gate Brutal.** The chain phase is where the discipline pays — count to the window, then burst.
5. **Check the controls.** Confirm your parry input binding in the [Controls & Settings Guide](/guides/controls-settings-guide) — a bad binding costs frames.

---

## 6. Common Parry Mistakes

1. **Parrying the body, not the weapon.** Tells are read off the attacking limb — that's the confirmed method.
2. **Breaking the chain early.** At parry 12 of 15, quitting resets the meter — the boss wins by attrition.
3. **Parrying unblockables.** Wasting stamina on a dodge-gate attack is how dodge-gate bosses kill parry-happy players.
4. **Mid-swing parries.** You cannot parry mid-swing — a failed parry locks you into vulnerability.
5. **Skipping the practice.** The window is generous, but generosity only helps if you're reading the tells; practice is the difference.

If a fight isn't responding to parries (probe: one early parry), it's a dodge-gate fight — switch immediately and stop spending stamina.
