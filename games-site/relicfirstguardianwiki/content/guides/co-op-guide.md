# Co-Op & Multiplayer Guide — How to Play with Friends

The Relic: First Guardian supports **2-player online co-op at launch**, letting you summon a friend to face the 70+ bosses side by side. It is a pure co-op experience — there is no PvP, and all rewards are shared fairly between players. This guide covers how summoning works, the rules of co-op, and how to farm effectively with a partner.

---

## How to Summon a Friend

1. **Unlock co-op**: The ability to summon unlocks after defeating the Withered Gatekeeper (tutorial boss) and resting at the Hollow Refuge Brazier of Reflection.
2. **Set a password**: Both players set the same multiplayer password in the Network section of the settings menu. Matching passwords guarantee your friend's sign appears — use one even if you want random partners.
3. **Host places the summon**: At any Brazier of Reflection, open the menu and activate **"Call Ally"**. Your friend's sign appears near the Brazier in your world.
4. **Guest joins**: The guest selects "Answer Call" from their menu to enter your world. The guest needs to have reached the same Brazier in their own world.

**Note**: Both players must have the latest patch installed, and you must be online. Summoning is disabled during boss fights, and you cannot summon in the tutorial area.

## Co-Op Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Party size | 2 players (host + guest) |
| Boss scaling | Bosses gain roughly +40% HP and +20% damage per additional player |
| Shared drops | Materials, gold, and runes dropped in co-op are shared among all players within range |
| Personal rewards | Boss artifacts and key items are granted to both players |
| Guest progression | Guests earn all loot and trophy progress, but do not advance the host's story flags |
| Friendly fire | Off — you cannot damage your partner |
| Revival | The Ember of Persistence rune revives you with 30% HP; otherwise a fallen ally waits at the last rested Brazier |

**Key insight**: Because bosses scale, co-op does not automatically make fights easier — but splitting aggro and covering each other's stamina windows makes most encounters far more forgiving.

---

## What Carries Over

- **Guest side**: Every rune, artifact, material, and gold drop you pick up in the host's world is kept when you return to your own world. This makes co-op an extremely efficient farming method.
- **Host side**: Story checkpoints, boss kills, and area unlocks persist for the host only. The guest must re-clear those bosses in their own world to progress their story.

---

## Is There PvP or Crossplay?

- **PvP**: Not at launch. The Relic: First Guardian is a cooperative experience; there are no invasions or duels in the 1.0 build.
- **Crossplay**: Not supported at launch. PS5 players can only co-op with PS5 players, and Steam players with Steam players. Crossplay is being evaluated by the developers for a post-launch patch.
- **Platforms**: Co-op requires the same platform (Steam or PS5). The upcoming Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2 versions will match their own player pools.

## Co-Op Strategies

**Tank + DPS (best for learning bosses)**
The Sword & Shield player holds aggro and blocks, while the Dual Daggers or Two-Handed player punishes openings. The tank never needs to attack — block costs zero stamina, so they can hold the boss indefinitely while the DPS wears it down.

**Stagger Lock**
Two-Handed (Colossus Strike + Earthshatter) builds stagger extremely fast with a partner layering in heavy attacks. Most bosses can be chain-staggered by alternating charges, effectively removing their moveset.

**Ranged Support**
A Staff build can kite adds and apply elemental statuses while the melee partner focuses the boss. Fire + oil and lightning + wet combos from the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide) are much easier to coordinate with two players.

**Material Farming**
Co-op is the fastest way to farm rare materials:
- Farm the Drowned Pilgrim and Elder Wyrm loops with a partner — both players get the drops
- Clear the Whispering Woods and Sunken Citadel daily resets together, doubling the shard intake
- Use the "Fortune's Favor" rune — rare material drop buffs apply to both players

## Common Co-Op Questions

**Q: Can I summon a friend for the tutorial boss?**
A: No — co-op unlocks after the Withered Gatekeeper is defeated.

**Q: What happens when the host dies?**
A: The session ends and the guest returns to their own world with all loot collected. Restart the summon at the last Brazier.

**Q: Do bosses drop double artifacts?**
A: Boss artifacts and key items are granted to both players. Regular drops are shared per pickup.

**Q: Can we share rune slots?**
A: No — each player equips their own runes and artifacts. Builds are personal.
