# Enemy Types Guide — Brutals, Field Mobs & How to Fight Them

The Relic: First Guardian is a **boss-heavy soulslike** — developers describe up to **70-80 "Brutals"** as the backbone of the game, with regular enemies comparatively rare. But the enemies you meet between bosses are not filler: every one is a piece of the world's story, a tragedy made monster by the Void. This guide breaks down what is **confirmed** about enemy categories, behavior, and loot — and what the community has not verified.

---

## The Big Picture: A World of Transformed People

The lore, confirmed by official material: the destruction of the **Great Relic** plunged the land of Arsiltus into the **Void**, transforming people into monsters called **Brutals**. That framing matters for gameplay — these are not faceless mobs:

- **Enemies are storytelling first** — bandits, knights, and mad villagers are the remnants of the world, and their placement tells you what happened to each region.
- **No XP, no levels** — regular enemies drop **gold and materials**, not experience. They are not worth farming for power; see the [Resources & Loot Guide](/guides/resource-loot-guide) for why.
- **Cleared areas stay cleared** — resting at checkpoints (the glowing **Tibelle's Blessing** stones) does **not** respawn enemies. This is confirmed and changes how you play: a cleared path stays safe.

## Boss Brutals: The Game's Real Enemy Roster

Brutals are the core of the experience. Confirmed facts from developer deep dives and previews:

- **Each Brutal is a story** — their existence comes from tragedy, desire, regret, or obsession. Defeating one lets you inherit its tragic story plus **special items and upgrades essential for progression**.
- **Every Brutal is a "combat puzzle"** — bosses have multiple varied attacks, extensive mobility, and are designed to test a specific skill (parrying, dodging, stamina discipline, or skill usage).
- **Some Brutals parry you back** — confirmed: certain bosses can parry the player's own attacks. Time your swings, or your aggression feeds their counter.
- **No stagger bar** — enemies and bosses rarely stagger and there is no visible stagger meter; openings come from parries and dodges, not stun-locks.

## Field Enemies: What You Fight Between Bosses

Because the game is boss-heavy, field enemies are less frequent — but still dangerous in groups. Confirmed behavioral details:

- **Enemies live in the world** — they loot corpses, sit at campfires, and can be damaged with **explosive barrels** left near their camps. Use the environment; it is placed deliberately.
- **Swarming is real** — the cemetery approach to Arden's fight features troublesome enemies that can swarm you if you are careless. Draw groups carefully.
- **They are aggressive and rarely stagger** — review coverage is consistent: field enemies attack relentlessly and do not flinch. There is no stagger-lock cheese.
- **Archetypes vary** — confirmed boss variety spans lumbering, powerful enemies that kill in a few hits; magic users; and rapid, quick enemies that close distance fast. Expect the same spread in elite field enemies.

## Fighting by Category

### vs. Human Enemies (Bandits, Mad Villagers, Fallen Knights)

1. **Use the free-attack economy** — attacks cost zero stamina, so press hard when the opening exists.
2. **Watch for group pulls** — human enemies often appear in packs; let them come to you instead of walking into all of them.
3. **Exploit barrels and terrain** — confirmed tool: camps are set with explosive barrels for a reason.
4. **Parry the telegraphed swings** — the generous parry window applies to regular enemies too; chained parries break guards.

### vs. Field-Level Brutals

- **Treat them as mini-bosses** — same rules as boss Brutals: learn the tell, dodge red-tinted unblockables, conserve stamina for defense.
- **Do not trade blows** — they hit much harder than human mobs and rarely stagger.
- **Swap relics before engaging** — the build-swap loop is the game's intended prep; see the [Runes Guide](/guides/runes-guide).

### vs. Boss Brutals

- Identify the gate type early: **parry-gate** (break stance with repeated parries) vs **dodge-gate** (unblockable red attacks).
- Use skills on cooldown — they cost nothing and recharge mid-fight.
- Full tactics: [Boss Strategies](/guides/boss-strategies).

## Common Questions

**Q: Are there many regular enemies in this game?**
A: **No** — it is intentionally boss-heavy. Regular enemies are comparatively rare and serve world-building and material drops more than combat challenge.

**Q: Do enemies respawn when I rest?**
A: **No** — resting at Tibelle's Blessing checkpoints does not respawn enemies. Cleared areas stay cleared.

**Q: Can I farm enemies for levels?**
A: **No** — there is no XP system. Enemies drop gold and materials; your power comes from uniques, relics, and skill trees.

**Q: Can enemies parry me?**
A: **Yes** — some Brutals are confirmed to parry the player's attacks. Watch your swing timing against them.

**Q: Why don't enemies stagger?**
A: Confirmed design — enemies rarely stagger and have no visible stagger bar. Openings come from parries and dodges, not stun-locks.
