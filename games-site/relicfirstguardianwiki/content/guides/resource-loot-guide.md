# Resources & Loot Guide — Uniques, Relics & Progressing Without Levels

The Relic: First Guardian has **no traditional level system** — no XP, no character levels, no stat points. Growth comes from three confirmed systems instead: **one-of-a-kind equipment**, **70+ relic fragments** with independent passives, and **12 skill trees** (two per weapon family) whose skills run on **cooldowns**. This guide explains how loot feeds that growth — and marks what the community has not verified.

---

## How You Grow Without Levels

| Growth Source | What It Does | Confirmed |
|---------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Unique gear** | Every weapon/armor piece is one-of-a-kind — your stats come from the uniques you found and forged | Yes |
| **Relic fragments (70+)** | Each grants an independent passive effect; more slots = more passives | Yes |
| **Skill trees (12)** | Buy nodes with Relic Energy; skills fire on cooldowns with no cost | Yes |
| **Boss rewards** | Brutals (70-80 fights) drop powerful items and Relic Energy | Yes |

In practice: **exploration is your leveling**. Every hidden path, secret area, and temple holds a unique item, a relic, or a shard — and because items never re-drop, each one is a permanent, one-time power gain.

---

## The Unique Loot System: No Duplicates, Ever

This is the game's signature system, and it shapes everything about resource management:

- **Every weapon and armor piece is unique** — there are no duplicate drops and no random loot tables. What you find is the only copy that exists.
- **Exploration is the loot system** — items are hand-placed in the world, including hidden paths and secret areas. See the [Secret Areas guide](/guides/secret-areas) for where uniques hide.
- **No farming for a "better version"** — you cannot grind a stronger copy; you forge instead.
- **Cursed weapons add a gamble** — some uniques trade **damage for health** (risk/reward): harder hits, lost life. Equipping one is a build decision, not a straight upgrade.

Because copies never re-drop, **every loot decision is permanent** — where you explore, what you equip, and what you forge all matter.

---

## Collecting the 70+ Relics

Relics are the game's main multiplier — **70+ fragments, each with its own independent passive effect** (damage boosts, resistances, utility, and more):

- **Each relic is its own passive** — effects are independent, so stacking the right combination is the core build loop.
- **Slots are unlocked with Shards of Awakening** — found through exploration, brought to **Elisa at any camp**; each shard unlocks a new relic slot.
- **Relics are freely swappable** — you can rebuild between fights, making a growing collection a growing set of options.
- **Collection is power** — every new fragment can be slotted in at any time. See the [Runes Guide](/guides/runes-guide) for the fragment system in depth.

---

## The 12 Skill Trees: Cooldowns, Not Costs

There are **12 skill trees — two per weapon family** — and the confirmed economy behind them is unusual:

- Skills are bought with **Relic Energy**, earned from defeating Brutals and finding Ancient Relic Blessings.
- **Skills run on cooldowns with no resource cost** — once bought, every skill is free to use; its only limit is the timer.
- Trees cover offense, defense, and utility — some nodes raise Health or Stamina, some buff specific relics.
- The two-tree structure per family is the game's "class system" — blending defines your build.

Because skills cost nothing to cast, buying nodes is pure upside: your rotation grows longer with every Relic Energy purchase.

## What to Save vs Spend: Resource Priorities

- **Shards of Awakening** — spend immediately: relic slots are the highest-value progression in the game.
- **Shards of Esser** — spend immediately: potion capacity is survival (start with 3 potions).
- **Relic Energy** — spend on your main family's trees; HP/stamina nodes first, then damage.
- **Forge materials** — spend selectively: they upgrade uniques that never re-drop, so commit to long-term weapons.
- **Unused uniques** — hold on to them: only one copy exists, and no verified selling/dismantling system exists.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is there a level system?**
A: **No.** There are no character levels or XP. You grow by collecting unique gear, stacking relic passives, and buying skill tree nodes.

**Q: Can I farm for better loot?**
A: **No.** Every weapon and armor piece is unique — there are no duplicate drops. Exploration and bosses are the only sources.

**Q: How do I get more relic slots?**
A: Find **Shards of Awakening** through exploration and bring them to **Elisa at a camp** — each unlocks a new slot.

**Q: What are the exact drop rates?**
A: **Unconfirmed.** No verified drop tables exist at launch. The only confirmed rule is that nothing drops duplicates.

**Q: Are cursed weapons worth using?**
A: They trade **damage for health** — strong if you have healing relics and confident dodges, a trap otherwise. See the [Weapon Damage Guide](/guides/weapon-damage-guide) for pairing advice.
