# Relic First Guardian Relic Combinations — Build the Right Set for the Right Brutal

**The Relic: First Guardian / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Relics are the passive fragment system that defines every build in The Relic: First Guardian — and the game's design is explicit about how they're meant to be used: **"freely swap them to adapt your build to each boss."** A static relic loadout is a misunderstanding of the game. This guide covers the confirmed relic effects, the four combo archetypes, weapon-family pairings, and the drawback math that turns a powerful set into a build decision.

---

## 1. What Relics Do (Confirmed Effects)

Official descriptions confirm five effect families:

| Effect Family | What It Does |
|---------------|--------------|
| Reshape skills | Change how an unlocked skill behaves |
| Alter combat tempo | Change attack rhythm or stamina behavior |
| Buff specific weapons | Amplify a weapon family or playstyle |
| Improve survivability | Health, defense, sustain effects |
| Reduce cooldowns | Skills run on cooldowns — fragments shorten them |

**The economy (confirmed):** **Relic Energy** (from Brutals and Ancient Relic Blessings) buys skills in weapon skill trees; **Shards of Awakening** (found in exploration) unlock new relic slots via **Elisa** at any camp. Power = fragments equipped + fragments collected + skills bought — all combat/exploration driven, none XP driven.

## 2. The Four Combo Archetypes

| Archetype | Core Idea | Confirmed Relic Effects to Combine | Best Against |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------------------------|--------------|
| **Parry Chain** | Maximize parry uptime and stagger building | Stamina regen + parry-enhancing relics + skill reshapes | Parry-gate Brutals (see the [Boss Strategies Guide](/guides/boss-strategies)) |
| **Dodge Flow** | Mobility and recovery for unblockable-heavy fights | Cooldown reduction + movement/evasion effects | Dodge-gate Brutals |
| **Sustain Wall** | Outlast every fight | Health, defense, sustain effects | Mixed fights where chip damage accumulates |
| **Cooldown Engine** | Skill spam as the damage core | Cooldown reduction + skill-reshaping relics | Any fight where your weapon's skill is the win condition |

**Why archetypes beat item lists:** with over 70 unique fragments and a roster of 70-80 Brutals, memorizing "the best relic" is useless — knowing *which archetype a boss demands* is the actual skill. Probe the fight (one early parry tells you the archetype), then match your combo.

## 3. Weapon-Family Pairings

Each weapon family has 2 skill trees (12 total) — the relic combo should amplify what the family wants to do:

| Weapon Family | Playstyle | Relic Effects That Amplify It |
|---------------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Two-Handed | Big wind-ups, big windows | Stamina economy + cooldown reduction |
| Dagger (dual blades) | Pressure, multi-hits | Weapon damage buffs + tempo changes |
| Battle Staff | Distance control | Skill reshapes + survivability |

**The pairing rule:** fragments that buff your weapon family first, survivability second, and skill reshaping third — unless the fight demands otherwise (dodge-gate fights flip that order). See the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide) for the full family breakdown.

## 4. Drawback Math (The Build Decision)

Some of the strongest **gear** (unique artifacts) carry **Drawbacks** — a confirmed mechanic: losing stamina, gaining a weakness, or similar costs. Combined with relics, the math works like this:

| Situation | The Decision |
|-----------|--------------|
| Drawback drains stamina | Not for parry-chain fights — stamina is defense ammo (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)) |
| Drawback grants a weakness | Match relics to cover it, or skip the item for that boss |
| Drawback fits the archetype | Build around it — the game wants you to manage trade-offs, not ignore them |
| Powerful item, wrong boss | Swap it out — official design is explicit that loadouts are per-fight |

**The rule:** every strong piece is a *build decision*, not a pure upgrade. The [Artifacts Guide](/guides/artifacts-guide) documents the unique-gear rules (no duplicates, hand-placed, drawbacks) that make these choices permanent.

## 5. The Swap Discipline (Pre-Boss Routine)

1. **Identify the fight** — probe: parry connects = parry-gate; parry fails = dodge-gate.
2. **Choose the archetype** — Parry Chain, Dodge Flow, Sustain Wall, or Cooldown Engine.
3. **Match relics** — weapon family buffs first, then the archetype's needs.
4. **Check the drawbacks** — does any equipped gear fight your stamina plan?
5. **Swap at camp between fights** — relic swapping is free and intended; never walk into a Brutal with last fight's set.

**The mistake the game punishes:** treating relics as a "build" you set once. The [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide) has full archetype loadouts, but every one of them expects adaptation — the game's hardest fights are tests of your swapping, not your collection.

---

## 6. Common Relic Mistakes

1. **Static builds.** The #1 mistake — the game literally tells you to swap per boss.
2. **Ignoring cooldown reduction.** Skills are your highest-damage tools; cooldown relics multiply the whole kit.
3. **Relic Energy hoarding.** Relic Energy buys skill-tree nodes (including HP and Stamina nodes) — spending it is how you survive the late roster.
4. **Skipping Elisa.** Shards of Awakening are worthless until you visit Elisa at a camp — unlock slots early and often.
5. **Gear over relics.** Unique artifacts are powerful, but relics are the adaptable half of the build — and adaptability is the game's core demand.

## 7. The Full Build Loop

Find fragments and Shards through exploration → unlock slots at camp → earn Relic Energy from Brutals → buy skill nodes → assemble an archetype combo → probe the next Brutal → swap and adapt.
