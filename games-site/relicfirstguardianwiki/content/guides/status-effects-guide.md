# Status Effects Guide — Curse, Poison & Debuffs Explained

Soulslikes live and die by their status effects, and The Relic: First Guardian is no exception — with one confirmed twist: **Curse is a death sentence**. The game's signature ailment builds a gauge that can kill you outright, and at least one boss is built around it. This guide covers the **confirmed** statuses and debuffs, the difference between Curse the status and "cursed" the gear property, and the resistance-swap strategy the game expects from you.

---

## The Two Confirmed Status Effects

| Status | Effect | Confirmed |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Curse** | Builds a gauge; maxing it deals massive damage — up to instant death against certain bosses | Yes |
| **Poison** | Damage-over-time ailment applied by specific weapons (e.g., a poison-infused dagger) | Yes |

**What is NOT confirmed:** the exact gauge thresholds, poison tick damage, ailment durations, and whether a dedicated curing item exists beyond your standard healing. Do not trust guides that quote "Curse kills at 100 gauge" or "poison ticks for 15 damage" — those numbers are not verified.

---

## Curse: The Instant-Death Mechanic

Curse is the game's most dangerous status and the one previews consistently call out:

- **Confirmed: Curse can kill outright.** The fairy boss's Curse gauge is described in IGN's preview as causing **instant death** when filled. This is not a debuff you can out-heal once it pops — you must manage the gauge before it fills.
- **Arden, the Mad Vindicator** inflicts Curse, using a red-tinted **unblockable** attack — a circular pulse of Curse damage followed by a swipe. Red tells always mean dodge, never block.
- **Curse resistance gear exists** — IGN's preview specifically recommends equipping **curse resistance armor** against the fairy boss. Gear-based resistances are the game's intended answer.

### How to Survive Curse Bosses

1. **Check your gear before the fight** — the build-swap loop is confirmed design; equip resistance pieces and relics for the matchup.
2. **Dodge every red-tinted attack** — unblockable Curse pulses must be avoided; a block eats both the damage and the gauge.
3. **Watch the gauge, not your health bar** — one slip on a full gauge ends the fight instantly.
4. **Keep a full heal ready** — if you take a hit that pushes the gauge toward max, reset and heal before re-engaging.
5. **Learn the boss's Curse sources** — once you know which attacks build the gauge, the fight becomes a dodge pattern (see [Boss Strategies](/guides/boss-strategies)).

## Poison: The Weapon-Born Ailment

Poison is confirmed to exist as a weapon property rather than a widespread hazard:

- **Specific weapons apply it** — previews confirm at least one dagger trades raw attack power for **poison application**. It is a build choice, not a random drop.
- **It is damage-over-time** — poison chips away at enemies while you reposition or defend. Exact tick values are unconfirmed.
- **It pairs with the free-attack economy** — since attacks cost zero stamina, a poison weapon lets you apply the ailment constantly; see the [Weapon Damage Guide](/guides/weapon-damage-guide) for pairing advice.

**Unconfirmed:** whether enemies poison the player, whether poison resistance gear exists, and whether poison can stack. Treat any claims about "poison builds" beyond the confirmed dagger as community speculation.

## "Cursed" Weapons vs. Curse the Status — Don't Confuse Them

This is the game's most common point of confusion, and the two are **not the same thing**:

| | Curse (status) | Cursed (gear property) |
|--|----------------|------------------------|
| **What it is** | An ailment applied during combat that builds a kill gauge | A permanent **Drawback** baked into a unique weapon |
| **Effect** | Instant death risk when the gauge fills | Trade-off such as **damage for health** (hits harder, you lose life) |
| **How to handle** | Resistance gear + dodging the red tells | A build decision — pair it with healing relics or skip it |

Unique gear in general can carry **Drawbacks** — confirmed examples include stamina loss and elemental weaknesses. These are permanent debuffs on your own build: equipping one is a conscious trade, not a hidden trap. Full coverage: [Artifacts Guide](/guides/artifacts-guide).

## The Debuff on the Player's Side: Stance Break (Groggy)

Not all statuses hurt you. The confirmed stagger system works in your favor:

- **Parrying fills an invisible "Groggy Gauge"** on enemies and bosses.
- **Breaking stance staggers them** and **massively increases damage** — confirmed.
- **Some bosses require it** — parry-gate fights are effectively puzzles: land repeated parries (up to ~15 per one launch review) before an attack window opens.

This is the closest thing the game has to a "stun" status, and it is your main tool against enemies that otherwise never stagger. Timing detail: [Parry Timing Guide](/guides/parry-timing-guide).

## Common Questions

**Q: What does Curse do in The Relic: First Guardian?**
A: Curse builds a gauge that, when filled, causes massive damage — confirmed **instant death** against the fairy boss. Dodge red-tinted attacks and equip Curse resistance.

**Q: Is there poison in the game?**
A: **Yes** — at least one dagger applies poison (a damage-over-time ailment) at the cost of raw attack power.

**Q: What is the difference between "cursed" weapons and the Curse status?**
A: Curse is a combat ailment. "Cursed" is a gear **Drawback** — uniques that trade one stat (e.g., health) for power. Different systems, easily confused.

**Q: Can I cure status effects?**
A: Standard healing (Breath of Esser potions) covers damage; a dedicated cure item for Curse is **unconfirmed**.

**Q: How do I build the Groggy gauge?**
A: **Parry** — successful parries fill the invisible Groggy Gauge; breaking stance staggers the enemy and massively increases damage.
