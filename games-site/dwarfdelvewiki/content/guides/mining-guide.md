## Mining in Dwarf Delve

Mining is the foundation of everything in Dwarf Delve. Every structure, weapon, trap, and upgrade begins with ore pulled from the earth. Efficient mining is not just about digging straight down — it is about planning your tunnels, choosing the right tools, and understanding what each depth layer offers.

This guide covers every aspect of mining, from basic techniques to advanced tunnel network planning.

## Mining Basics

### Core Mining Mechanics

- **Pickaxe Required** — You cannot mine ores without an equipped pickaxe. Fists can break dirt and loose stone, but nothing valuable.
- **Stamina Costs** — Each swing costs 5 stamina. Mining without stamina deals 1 damage per swing.
- **Vein Density** — Ores appear in veins. Larger veins yield more ore per swing but require more stamina to fully extract.
- **Collapse Risk** — Mining without support beams can cause cave-ins. Always reinforce tunnels wider than 2 blocks.

| Mid-depth | 50-100 | Iron, Silver | Gold, Mithril | Sapphire | Damp tunnels, poison gas |

## Efficient Tunnel Layouts

### The Spiral Ramp

The most efficient design for reaching deep layers. A spiral ramp descends at a 45-degree angle around a central pillar.

**Advantages**:
- Easy to navigate (always turning the same direction)
- Central pillar prevents collapses
- Cart tracks can be laid on the outer wall

**Disadvantages**:
- Wide footprint (uses more space than straight shafts)
- More total blocks to dig

### The Central Shaft with Branch Tunnels

A vertical shaft at the center of your base with horizontal branch tunnels at each depth layer.

**Advantages**:
- Fastest vertical travel (ladder or elevator)
- Branch tunnels cover maximum area per depth
- Easy to defend (choke points at shaft entrances)

**Disadvantages**:
- High collapse risk at the shaft
- Requires significant support beam investment

### The Grid System

An organized grid of horizontal tunnels at regular intervals, connected by short vertical shafts.

**Advantages**:
- Maximal ore coverage (no area is missed)
- Easy navigation (coordinates are predictable)
- Simple to expand

**Disadvantages**:
- Most labor-intensive layout
- High material cost for supports
- Complex without coordinate mapping

### Recommended Layout by Playstyle

| Playstyle | Recommended Layout | Why |
|-----------|-------------------|-----|
| Solo Miner | Spiral Ramp | Simple, safe, manageable workload |
| Group with Cart | Central Shaft + Branches | Fast travel, easy cart routing |
| Completionist | Grid System | Leaves no ore unfound |
| Speedrunner | Straight Shaft | Minimum digging to reach core |
| Builder/Architect | Custom (any) | Aesthetic priority over efficiency |

## Mining Tips and Tricks

- **Always carry extra support beams**. A cave-in at depth 200+ can trap or kill you.
- **Use torches every 5 blocks** to prevent mob spawns in your tunnels.
- **Listen for ore sounds**. Each ore type makes a distinct resonance when you are near it.
- **Mark explored tunnels** with colored flags or signs. Getting lost in deep tunnels is a common cause of death.
- **Never mine directly above yourself**. Gravity-affected blocks (gravel, sand) will fall on you.
- **Build rest stops** every 50 meters of depth with a campfire, chest, and bed.
- **Coordinate mining with defense planning**. Your mining tunnels are also potential invasion routes for enemies.
