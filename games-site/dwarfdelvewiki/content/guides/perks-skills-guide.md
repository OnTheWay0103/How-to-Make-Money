## The Perk System Explained

Between runs, you spend coins earned from extracted resources to unlock permanent perks for your Delveling. These perks persist across all future runs and are the primary form of meta-progression. You cannot lose perks once unlocked.

Perks are organized into three skill trees:
- **Might** (combat and mining efficiency)
- **Resilience** (survival and endurance)
- **Fortune** (resource gathering and luck)

## Might Skill Tree

The Might tree makes you hit harder, mine faster, and fight better.

### Tier 1 (Unlocks at Perk Level 1)
| Perk | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Heavy Swings** | 50 Coins | +10% Battlemallet damage |
| **Efficient Miner** | 50 Coins | +15% mining speed |
| **Battle Ready** | 75 Coins | Start each run with +1 Flare |

**Priority**: Efficient Miner > Heavy Swings > Battle Ready

### Tier 2 (Unlocks at Perk Level 5)
| Perk | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Berserker Rage** | 150 Coins | +25% damage when below 30% health |
| **Demolition Expert** | 150 Coins | 20% chance to break adjacent blocks when mining |
| **Critical Strike** | 200 Coins | 10% chance for double damage |

**Priority**: Demolition Expert > Critical Strike > Berserker Rage

### Tier 3 (Unlocks at Perk Level 10)
| Perk | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Dwarven Fury** | 400 Coins | +40% attack speed for 10s after taking damage |
| **Mine Sweeper** | 350 Coins | Destroyed blocks have 15% chance to drop bonus resources |
| **Executioner** | 500 Coins | One-shot enemies below 10% health |

**Priority**: Mine Sweeper > Dwarven Fury > Executioner

## Resilience Skill Tree

The Resilience tree keeps you alive longer and deeper into the mines.

| **Steady Grip** | 50 Coins | Lantern battery drains 20% slower |

**Priority**: Steady Grip > Tough Hide > Deep Breath

### Tier 2 (Perk Level 5)

**Priority**: Collapse Survivor > Dark Vision > Iron Stomach

### Tier 1

## Optimal Perk Unlock Order

### Phase 1: Foundation (Perk Levels 1-5)
1. **Efficient Miner** (Might T1) — Mining speed is everything
2. **Pack Mule** (Fortune T1) — More loot per successful run
3. **Steady Grip** (Resilience T1) — Longer lantern = deeper runs
4. **Keen Eye** (Fortune T1) — Easier to find valuable veins
5. **Heavy Swings** (Might T1) — Combat + mining boost

### Phase 2: Efficiency (Perk Levels 5-10)
1. **Demolition Expert** (Might T2) — Breaks adjacent blocks, massive efficiency boost
2. **Coin Collector** (Fortune T2) — +25% coin value from all runs
3. **Collapse Survivor** (Resilience T2) — Insurance against the #1 cause of death
4. **Critical Strike** (Might T2) — Free double damage
5. **Gem Sniffer** (Fortune T2) — Audio cues for gems

### Phase 3: Power Spike (Perk Levels 10+)
1. **Phoenix Heart** (Resilience T3) — A second life every run
2. **Mine Sweeper** (Might T3) — Bonus resources from mining
3. **Relic Hunter** (Fortune T3) — Relics are the endgame, this accelerates them
4. **Unbreakable** (Resilience T3) — Support beams last longer = deeper runs
5. **Midas Touch** (Fortune T3) — Passive gold income while mining

## Perk Synergies

- **Demolition Expert + Mine Sweeper**: Demolition Expert breaks adjacent blocks; Mine Sweeper makes those blocks drop bonus resources. Together, every swing is a jackpot
- **Collapse Survivor + Phoenix Heart**: Two separate survival mechanisms — survive a collapse AND revive from death in the same run
- **Gem Sniffer + Keen Eye + Relic Hunter**: Keen Eye helps you see veins, Gem Sniffer helps you hear gems, Relic Hunter boosts chamber rewards. You become a treasure-detecting machine

## Respeccing Perks

Currently, Dwarf Delve does not offer a perk respec option. Choose your path carefully — especially in the early game when coins are scarce. The recommended order above is safe and efficient for most playstyles.
