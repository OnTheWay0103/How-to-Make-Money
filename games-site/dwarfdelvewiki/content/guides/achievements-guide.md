## How Achievements Fit the Game Loop

Dwarf Delve is a first-person extraction roguelite: you descend into procedurally generated mines as a Delveling, mine ores and find relics, and extract before the collapsing mine gets you. The elevator leaves on a schedule, and anything not extracted is lost. Achievements in a game like this are designed around the loop:

- **Survival milestones** — extractions, deep descents, surviving collapses
- **Economy milestones** — ore refined, coin earned, upgrades purchased
- **Collection milestones** — relics, treasures, quarters decorations
- **Crafting milestones** — beams, flares, ladders, rails built
- **Combat milestones** — likely present given the Battlemallet and the Horror tag, though no enemy roster is confirmed

## Achievement-Hunting Strategy (Based on Confirmed Systems)

Until the full list is verified, these strategies maximize your chances of popping achievements organically:

### 1. Extract Consistently
The game's economy and progression both run on successful extractions. Every confirmed system (refining, coin, perks, upgrades) rewards completing runs. If "Greenbeard" is a first-extraction achievement — the most likely design — you'll get it naturally. If it's something deeper, consistent extraction is still the fastest route to every other milestone.

### 2. Play the Meta-Progression Loop
"Pack Mule" is almost certainly tied to carrying capacity or hauling — the game's confirmed theme is literally "carry more loot." Stack capacity upgrades (confirmed purchasable with coin from refined ore) and the minecart system (confirmed to "shift and store those heavy ores for you"). A full cart at the elevator is the kind of event achievement designers target.

### 3. Craft Everything
"D.I.Y" points at crafting. The confirmed craftables: support beams, platforms, ladders, flares, floodlights, and minecart rails. Don't hoard materials — use them. Beams on every unstable tunnel, flares in every dark corner, rails on every heavy haul. If D.I.Y has tiers (craft N items), volume matters.

### 4. Go Deep, Safely
"Legend" is very likely the endgame milestone. The confirmed depth premise — "the deeper you dig, the richer the haul" — means deep runs are the design's endgame content. Build up: Battlemallet upgrades (mining + combat), lantern upgrades (darkness), support beams (collapse), then push depth with a hard extraction trigger.

### 5. Bank Relics
Relics are the confirmed endgame collectible — "long-lost relics" unlock unique perks and quarters decorations. Achievement lists in this genre typically include relic-collection milestones. Every relic extracted is progress.

### 6. Decorate the Quarters
Quarters customization is confirmed ("Everything from carpets to furnishings to wall art!") and press materials confirm some furnishings "may have gameplay consequences beyond cosmetics." Decoration-related achievements are common in games with housing systems — and even if none exist, the quarters are where collection achievements (relics on display) would land.

## How Achievement Hunting Interacts With the Loop

Achievements in Dwarf Delve sit on top of a game that punishes greed. The confirmed loop — descend, mine, extract, upgrade — means most achievements will reward exactly what the game already rewards: successful extraction. The hunting implication is important: **achievement-oriented play should not mean reckless play.** A dead Delveling banks no progress toward either the meta-game or the achievement list.

### The Dedicated-Run Concept
The cleanest hunting strategy is to dedicate specific runs to specific goals, rather than chasing everything at once:

- **Crafting runs** — descend with the goal of crafting (beams, flares, ladders, rails). Spend materials on tools instead of hoarding them; if "D.I.Y" tracks crafting volume, volume runs are the fastest route
- **Carrying runs** — push capacity upgrades (confirmed "carry more loot" theme) and minecart rails, then run a heavy-ore loop. This is the likely "Pack Mule" territory
- **Relic runs** — ignore strip-mining, push depth, extract the moment a relic is found. Collection achievements are a genre staple
- **Learning runs** — shallow, fast, high-frequency runs that bank small hauls and build the "extract consistently" habit that every other milestone feeds

### The Greed Trap
The game's official slogan — "One wrong boot could cost you your life — or worse, your loot!" — applies to achievement hunters more than anyone. An achievement is not worth a full inventory. Extract first, unlock later: every milestone in the confirmed loop is reachable through consistent survival.
