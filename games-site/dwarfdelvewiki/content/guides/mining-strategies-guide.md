## Mining: The Heart of Dwarf Delve

Mining is the core activity in Dwarf Delve — it's how you gather resources, find relics, and fund your progression. This guide covers efficient mining strategies, resource priorities, and depth-based decision making to maximize your loot per run.

## Understanding Depth Levels

The mines in Dwarf Delve are divided into distinct depth zones, each with different rewards and risks:

| Depth Zone | Rock Type | Common Resources | Danger Level |
|------------|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Upper Mines** (0-50m) | Brown stone, crumbling walls | Iron, Copper, Coal | Low |
| **Middle Depths** (50-150m) | Dense rock, mineral veins | Silver, Gold, Gems | Medium |
| **Deep Mines** (150-300m) | Dark stone, crystal formations | Relics, Rare Gems, Ancient Ore | High |
| **The Abyss** (300m+) | Obsidian, glowing veins | Legendary Relics, Mythril | Extreme |

Each zone is exponentially more dangerous but also exponentially more rewarding.

## What to Mine: Resource Priority Tiers

### S-Tier (Always Take)
- **Mythril Ore** — Found only in The Abyss. Used for the best gear upgrades
- **Ancient Relics** — Unlock unique perks and quarters decorations
- **Large Gold Nuggets** — High sell value for coin

### A-Tier (High Priority)
- **Gems (Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald)** — Good sell value and used in crafting
- **Silver Ore** — Required for mid-game lantern and flare upgrades
- **Relic Fragments** — Combine 5 fragments into a full relic

### B-Tier (Fill Inventory)
- **Iron Ore** — Used for all basic upgrades; always useful
- **Copper** — Lantern and tool upgrades
- **Coal** — Powers minecart furnaces and crafting stations

### C-Tier (Skip If Inventory Full)
- **Stone** — Abundant, low value. Only take if you have space
- **Common Gems** — Low sell value. Leave for better finds

## Mining Efficiency Techniques

### The Spiral Method
When entering a new cavern, mine in a spiral pattern outward from the center. This maximizes surface area coverage and ensures you don't miss veins hidden in corners.

### Vein Following
When you find a mineral vein, follow it to its end. Veins often connect to larger deposits or hidden chambers. The shimmer gets brighter as you get closer to the core deposit.

### Wall Tapping
Tap walls with your Battlemallet before committing to a full swing. The sound tells you what's behind:
- **Dull thud** — Solid rock, low chance of resources
- **Hollow echo** — A cave or chamber lies beyond
- **Metallic ring** — Mineral vein nearby
- **Crumbling sound** — Unstable rock, high collapse risk

### The 3-Hit Rule
For efficient mining, use the 3-hit pattern: two horizontal swings then one vertical. This breaks rock faster than random swinging and covers a wider area per strike.

## Depth Strategy: When to Go Deeper

Push deeper when:
- Your inventory is less than 50% full and you're in Upper or Middle Mines
- You hear metallic ringing (indicating richer veins below)
- You have plenty of support beams and flare charges remaining
- The tunnel structure is stable (no creaking or dust)

Extract when:
- Your inventory is 80%+ full with valuable resources
- You've found a relic (secure the win rather than risk it)
- Support beams are running low (less than 3 remaining)
- Tunnels are becoming unstable (frequent creaking, falling dust)
- Your lantern battery is below 25%

## Hidden Chamber Detection

Hidden chambers contain the best loot in Dwarf Delve. Look for these signs:

- **Unusual wall textures** — Slightly different color or pattern from surrounding rock
- **Air currents** — Particles or dust moving in a consistent direction (indicates an opening)
- **Sound cues** — Water dripping or wind whistling through nearby cracks
- **Enemy behavior** — Creatures often guard entrances to hidden chambers

Mine toward these signs and you'll often break through into treasure rooms.

## Minecart Resource Transport

Once you unlock minecart rails, use them strategically:
- Build rail lines from resource-rich areas back toward the elevator
- Prioritize rail connections to gold and gem deposits
- A fully loaded minecart holds 3x what you can carry
- Minecarts move faster than walking, saving precious time in deep runs

## Risk Management by Depth

The golden rule of Dwarf Delve mining: **Your most valuable resource is a successful extraction.** A dead Delveling brings home nothing. Balance your ambition against the tunnel stability meter, your remaining supplies, and — most importantly — your gut feeling about whether to push deeper or head home.
