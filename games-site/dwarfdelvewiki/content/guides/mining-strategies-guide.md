## Mining: The Heart of Dwarf Delve

Mining is the core activity in Dwarf Delve — you gather **ores, treasures, and long-lost relics** with your Battlemallet, extract them before the mine collapses, and convert them into permanent power at the surface. This guide covers the strategy layer: depth decisions, resource priorities, and run management for maximum profit.

## Understanding Depth (Confirmed Premise, No Official Units)

The game's official premise is the whole depth model:

> "The deeper you dig, the richer the haul but the farther you are from safety."

Confirmed by that sentence:
- Depth increases reward value
- Depth increases risk (distance from the elevator + more collapse exposure)

No official depth units (meters, floors, levels) have been published. Community depth tables with specific numbers are speculation.

| Depth Band | Reward (Confirmed Premise) | Risk (Confirmed Premise) |
|------------|---------------------------|--------------------------|
| Shallow | Modest finds | Low — close to elevator |
| Mid | Richer finds | Moderate |
| Deep | "The richer the haul" | High — farthest from safety |

## Resource Priority: The Honest Tier List

Official materials name three reward categories. Priority follows from confirmed mechanics:

### Top Priority: Relics
- **Confirmed unique**: unlock perks and quarters decorations
- **Irreplaceable**: if you find one, the run's purpose is achieved — extract

### High Priority: Treasures
- Named alongside relics in the official pitch ("ores, treasures, and long-lost relics")
- High value per inventory slot — bank them

### Core Priority: Ores
- The economy's backbone: refined at home, traded for coin
- **Heavy ores**: the confirmed cargo for minecart rails — "shift and store those heavy ores for you"
- Common ore is everywhere; it's the last thing to prioritize when inventory fills

**Honest note:** no ore type names or coin values are published. Any "mithril = X coins" table is fabricated.

## Mining Efficiency Techniques (Safe Practice)

Without published game math, these are general voxel-mining practices that hold up in any similar game — use them, but don't treat them as official mechanics:

### 1. Sweep, Don't Tunnel
Mine in expanding sweeps around your position rather than one long tunnel. Sweeps reveal veins on multiple walls and keep you oriented. Procedural mines hide value anywhere — coverage beats depth-per-minute.

### 2. Listen and Look
In voxel-style games, veins often reveal themselves through texture or color differences in the rock. When you find a vein, follow it — veins frequently connect to larger deposits.

### 3. Depth Budgeting
Set a depth budget before descending: where are you going, and what's the extraction trigger? Every meter down is a meter back, plus accumulated collapse time. The confirmed trade-off is explicit — richer haul, farther from safety.

### 4. Rails for the Heavy Stuff
When you find heavy ore far from the elevator, build rail. The cart system is confirmed to exist precisely for this. A cart ride beats a dozen backpack trips through a collapsing mine.

### 5. Pre-Craft for the Return
Beams and flares cost nothing to carry in terms of inventory pressure (they're tools, not loot). Always keep enough for the return trip — the way home is where runs are lost.

## When to Push Deeper vs. Extract

The confirmed decision inputs:

| Push Deeper When | Extract Now When |
|------------------|------------------|
| Inventory has room | Inventory meaningfully full of valuables |
| Tunnels stable (no creaking/dust) | Tunnels unstable |
| No relic found yet | Relic found (bank it) |
| Beams and light in reserve | Beams/light running low |
| Elevator route known and short | Route long or unmarked |

The community rule (honestly labeled, not official): the first instability warning is the extraction signal. Greed is the game's designed killer — "One wrong boot could cost you your life — or worse, your loot!"

## The Mining Loop, Optimized

1. **Descend with a plan** — depth budget, extraction trigger, loadout check
2. **Sweep the first rich floor** — fill inventory efficiently, not heroically
3. **Rail the heavy ore** — build toward the elevator when the haul justifies it
4. **Extract on schedule** — the elevator doesn't wait
5. **Refine, trade, upgrade** — Battlemallet first, lantern second, capacity third
6. **Repeat deeper** — permanent upgrades compound run over run

## Risk Management by Depth

The golden rule of Dwarf Delve mining: **your most valuable resource is a successful extraction.** A dead Delveling brings home nothing.

- **Shallow**: learn the loop, push your luck safely
- **Mid**: start the return earlier than feels necessary
- **Deep**: set a hard trigger (relic, full cart, first warning) before descending — and honor it

Balance ambition against the state of the mine, your remaining supplies, and — most importantly — your honest read of whether this run is still winnable.
