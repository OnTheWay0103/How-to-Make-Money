## Solo Play Overview

Dwarf Delve is designed for co-op but is fully playable solo. Playing alone is harder — you have no one to revive you and must handle everything yourself — but it's also more rewarding. This guide covers everything you need to thrive as a solo miner.

## Is Solo Viable?

**Yes — but it's hard mode.** Enemies don't scale down for solo players. You face the same threats as a full team, but with only one set of hands. The trade-off: all loot is yours.

## Best Solo Build

### The "Self-Sufficient Miner"
- **Primary Weapon**: Pickaxe (upgraded for combat + mining)
- **Secondary**: Crossbow (for ranged threats)
- **Armor**: Heavy Plate (maximum survivability)
- **Perks**: 
  - **Iron Gut** — Less food/water drain
  - **Lone Wolf** — +15% damage when no teammates nearby
  - **Pack Mule** — +30% carry capacity
  - **Night Vision** — See in dark tunnels without torches

### Why This Build
- Pickaxe serves dual purpose (mining + melee), saving inventory slots
- Heavy armor compensates for no revives
- Lone Wolf perk is designed specifically for solo play
- Extra carry capacity means fewer extraction runs

## Solo Strategy by Biome

### Early Biomes (1-2)
- **Playstyle**: Aggressive mining, cautious combat
- **Strategy**: Clear a safe zone first, then mine it thoroughly
- **Danger**: Getting surrounded — always have an escape route
- **Tip**: Place torches to mark cleared areas

### Mid Biomes (3-4)
- **Playstyle**: Hit and run
- **Strategy**: Enter → grab specific resources → extract quickly
- **Danger**: Elite enemies — avoid them unless you're well-geared
- **Tip**: Know exactly what you're going in for

### Late Biomes (5-6)
- **Playstyle**: Surgical strikes
- **Strategy**: Rush to specific nodes, ignore everything else, extract
- **Danger**: Everything. One mistake = death.
- **Tip**: Only attempt with max gear and full consumables

## Solo Survival Tips

1. **Always bring 2× healing items** — No teammate to share theirs
2. **Stability meter is your lifeline** — Leave at 50%, not 10%
3. **Sound cues are critical** — Wear headphones, listen for enemy footsteps
4. **Traps are your best friend** — Set trap lines before mining a rich node
5. **Know when to run** — Some fights aren't worth it solo
6. **Upgrade Storage Vault first** — More carry capacity = more loot per extraction
