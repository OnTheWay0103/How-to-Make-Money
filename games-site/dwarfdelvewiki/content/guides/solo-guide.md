## Solo Play Overview: The Game IS Solo

Here's the most important fact about "solo play" in Dwarf Delve: **the game is single-player only.** The Steam listing confirms no co-op and no multiplayer — solo isn't a hard mode or a choice, it's the entire design. Any guide that frames Dwarf Delve as a co-op game "playable solo" is wrong about the game.

That's good news for solo players: everything in the game is balanced for one Delveling. This guide covers how to play alone, efficiently and safely.

## Solo Viability: Yes, By Design

Every system in Dwarf Delve is built for one player:

- **The loop**: descend, mine, extract — all solo
- **The collapse**: a one-dwarf race against the mine
- **The economy**: all extracted value is yours; no splits, no shares
- **The quarters**: your personal home between runs

The confirmed trade-off of solo play is actually a solo benefit: **100% of what you extract is yours.** Every upgrade, every coin, every relic.

## The Solo Build (Honest Version)

No official perk or gear tier lists exist (see the Perks guide for why), but the confirmed systems give a clear solo priority order:

### 1. Battlemallet — Your Everything Tool
It's the confirmed tool for mining AND combat. Solo, you have no one to cover you — mining speed and combat damage in one upgrade is the foundation of every solo run.

### 2. Lantern & Light
Darkness is a confirmed hazard that hides "unseen dangers and long-forgotten traps." Light upgrades (lantern, flares, floodlights) are solo survival tools — you can't watch every angle, but you can light it.

### 3. Carry Capacity
"Carry more loot" is a confirmed upgrade theme. Solo, inventory efficiency is your entire economy — more per trip, fewer trips, less exposure.

### 4. Support Beams
Collapse is the confirmed #1 killer. Beams are your insurance policy, and solo there's nobody to share the load — always carry more than you think you need.

### 5. Minecart Rails
Rails are confirmed to "shift and store those heavy ores for you." Solo, heavy ore is a nightmare to hand-carry; the cart system is your one-man logistics team.

## Solo Run Strategy

### The Consistent Extractor (Recommended First Style)
1. Descend shallow — learn the run's mine character before going deep
2. Sweep a defined area near the elevator
3. Bank modest but consistent hauls
4. Upgrade Battlemallet → lantern → capacity
5. Only then push depth

**Why:** early reviews (Mostly Negative, ~25 reviews) say the game's onboarding is rough. Consistent extractions fund the upgrades that make deep runs survivable.

### The Relic Hunter (Mid Game)
1. Upgrade mining speed and light first
2. Push deeper each run, marking your route with flares
3. Skip strip-mining; hunt for relics and treasures
4. The moment a relic is in hand, extract — relics are the confirmed endgame prize

### The Deep Hauler (Endgame)
1. Max carry capacity and Battlemallet
2. Rail a route from a deep deposit to the elevator
3. Fill carts, ride back, extract, refine, reinvest
4. Run the loop until the meta-progression compounds

## Solo Survival Tips (Confirmed Mechanics Only)

1. **Extract early, not late** — the elevator leaves on a schedule, with or without you
2. **Beams before creaking** — place supports on your return route, not just your mining face
3. **Light every dark corner** — flares are confirmed to uncover "secrets hidden in the shadows"
4. **Set your retreat threshold before descending** — "One wrong boot could cost you your life — or worse, your loot!" (official)
5. **Bank relics immediately** — a relic in hand means the run is done
6. **Upgrade between runs, not during** — permanent upgrades (perks, skills, gear) are the solo progression curve

## Common Solo Mistakes

- **Tunneling without a route back** — solo, getting lost is fatal; mark your path
- **Going deep before upgrading** — upgrades are permanent; depth is not
- **Hand-carrying heavy ore** — that's what rails are for
- **Fighting over extracting** — extraction is the win condition, not the boss you haven't confirmed exists
- **Ignoring the quarters** — some furnishings "may have gameplay consequences beyond cosmetics" (official press)

## Solo FAQ

**Is Dwarf Delve hard solo?** It's balanced for one player — the difficulty is the game's own (reviews call it rough around the edges, especially onboarding).

**Can I pause solo?** Unconfirmed — no official statement.

**Is a "solo build" different from a normal build?** No — every build in Dwarf Delve is a solo build. There is no multiplayer build.

**What's the fastest way to progress solo?** Consistent extraction → upgrade Battlemallet and capacity → push depth gradually. The meta-progression loop is the confirmed growth engine.
