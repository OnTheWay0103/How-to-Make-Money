## What is Grain Rot?

Grain Rot is a horror co-op extraction builder developed by Beck & Branch Games and published by Neem Interactive. You play as a **Living Spark** — a fragment of soul possessing fragile wooden vessels. You and up to 3 friends descend via a cursed elevator into procedurally shifting underground ruins to scavenge loot, fight or avoid the **Corrupted**, and extract before the Rot consumes you.

Between runs, you rebuild the **Sanctuary Outpost** — unlocking new vessels, constructs, stat upgrades, and rescued survivors. If your vessel is destroyed, your Spark escapes to possess a new body. But if everyone dies, all unextracted loot is lost.

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Vessels** — Fragile wooden bodies with different abilities that your Spark can possess and swap between
- **20+ Constructs** — Weapons, tools, and utilities to unlock and bring into runs
- **9 Enemy Types** — The Corrupted react to sound, movement, and each other
- **5 Biomes** — 3 demo biomes (community-named The Shallows, The Warrens, The Depths) plus 2 new at 1.0
- **Outpost Base Building** — Upgradeable rooms, stat upgrades, interactive furniture, and rescued survivors at the Sanctuary Outpost
- **Body Corruption** — The deeper you go, the more the Rot changes you
- **Tactical Carpentry** — Smash furniture for resources, or feed it to the Grinder for gold
