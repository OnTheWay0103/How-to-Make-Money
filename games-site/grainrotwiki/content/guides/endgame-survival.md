## Endgame Survival Guide — Deep Layers, Corruption & Hard Mode

Endgame Grain Rot is a survival economics problem: how deep do you push before corruption eats your loot, and how much risk is worth how much reward. This guide covers deep-layer hazards, the corruption system, extraction math, and the vessel loadouts that survive both.

### Deep-Layer Hazard Tiers

| Layer Range | Hazard | Threat Level | Endgame Loot |
|-------------|--------|--------------|--------------|
| The Shallows (1-5) | Spore clouds | Low | Starter materials |
| The Warrens (6-12) | Burrower ambushes | Medium | Molded tools |
| The Depths (13-20) | Corruption storms | High | Corrupt Cores |
| The Abyss (21+) | Blighted elites | Extreme | Abyss-only vessels |

### Corruption System Explained

1. **What it is:** Every minute below the Depths builds Corruption, a stacking debuff that drains max HP and damages your carried loot.
2. **Cleansing:** Corruption tokens cleanse one stack each. Stock at least 5 before any Abyss run.
3. **Loot damage:** Corrupted loot loses quality tiers on extraction. Never carry Abyss loot with 3+ stacks.
4. **The risk curve:** Past 30 minutes of Corruption, expected loot value drops — extraction beats greed past that point.

### Best Endgame Vessels & Loadouts

| Vessel | Strength | Best Loadout | Hard Mode Rating |
|--------|----------|--------------|------------------|
| Bulwark Vessel | Damage reduction | Shotgun + barricade tools | S |
| Stalker Vessel | Stealth, ambush | Scythe + trap kit | A |
| Hive Vessel | Pet swarm, aggro split | Crossbow + swarm upgrade | A |
| Berserker Vessel | Raw DPS | Greatblade + life leech tool | B (risky) |

### Extraction Math

- **Golden rule:** Extract when carried value reaches 60% of your theoretical max, or when Corruption hits 3 stacks — whichever comes first.
- **Chest discipline:** Skip unguarded starter chests below the Warrens; every chest opened is time building Corruption.
- **Route efficiency:** A straight line from entrance to target node beats a full-clear path. Depth bonuses don't compensate for corruption stacks.
- **Hard mode runs:** Hard mode multiplies loot values by 1.5x but adds 20% Corruption build rate — adjust your extraction threshold down to 50%.

### Endgame Checklist

1. Enter deep runs with at least 5 Corruption tokens and 2 cleansing herbs.
2. Confirm your vessel upgrade tree matches your loadout before descending.
3. Set a hard extraction point — communicate it in co-op so the team doesn't split.
4. Farm the Warrens for Corruption tokens between deep runs; tokens are the real gate resource.
5. Save blighted elite kills for timed events — event multipliers stack with endgame loot.
