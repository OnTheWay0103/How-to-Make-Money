# Endgame Survival Guide — Deep Layers, Corruption & Risk Management

Grain Rot's late game is the **Deep Layers** — the floors below the demo's range, where the Rot is strongest and the loot richest. This guide covers what is confirmed about deep play, the risk-reward math of deep runs, and honest preparation for launch. Note: with the game releasing August 7, 2026, **no verified endgame data exists yet**; every specific claim in older guides (named endgame vessels, corruption tokens, layer numbers) was unverified speculation.

## What Is Confirmed About the Endgame

- **The Deep Layers exist and are the danger zone.** Official copy: the Deep Layers are "filled with Corrupted vessels, reacting to sound, movement, and each other."
- **Deeper means worse Rot.** "The deeper you go, the worse the Rot becomes. Bodies warp. Voices distort. Emotes become…worse."
- **Deeper means better loot.** The official pitch is "Go deeper. Extract more."
- **Survivors are a deep-layer reward.** "Rescue survivors trapped in the Deep Layers" is an official feature.
- **There is no confirmed layer cap, boss list, or endgame system.** Anyone publishing a layer-by-layer guide before launch is guessing.

## The Deep-Run Risk Equation

Endgame Grain Rot is a survival economics problem built from confirmed mechanics:

**Reward** = loot value × depth multiplier (deeper floors pay better)

**Cost** = corruption buildup + Corrupted pressure + distance from the elevator

Every extra floor adds to both sides. The skill is knowing when the cost curve crosses the reward curve — and that point is different every run, because the ruins shift procedurally.

## Corruption: The Endgame Clock

Corruption is the confirmed throttle on deep play:

- It builds as you descend and spend time below.
- At high corruption, your body warps, your voice distorts, and your emotes degrade — you become visibly, audibly compromised.
- High corruption makes you a liability in co-op (your team can hear and see your state) and harder to control in solo.

**Rule of thumb (framework, not verified numbers):** treat corruption like a timer on your greed. The moment your degraded state threatens the extraction, the run is over — the loot is already lost if you don't bank it.

## The Deep-Run Checklist

Pre-flight for any serious descent, built from confirmed systems:

1. **Know your extraction route.** The elevator is the only bank. Scout its location first.
2. **Carry the team's value in the right body.** Designate a Keeper whose vessel is best positioned to survive and extract with the haul.
3. **Convert before committing.** Grinders turn junk into compact gold — do it before going deep, not after.
4. **Set a corruption threshold and honor it.** Decide in advance when the team bails, and say it out loud so greed can't argue.
5. **Respect fire.** Vessels catch fire; the deep layers of a wasteland "where everything burns" will test that.

## Preparing for the Endgame Now

The demo is your training ground for deep play:

- Practice extraction under pressure — full bag, enemies converging, timer anxiety.
- Learn the sound model cold: how the Corrupted chain-react, how far noise carries, when to fight vs. flee.
- Test every construct unlock; deep runs are won by tool versatility.
- Find your crew. Deep co-op runs need players who communicate.
