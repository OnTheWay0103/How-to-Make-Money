## Expected Price: $19.99–24.99 (Estimate)

The official price hasn't been published, and **any number you see before launch day is an estimate** — including ours. Here's the reasoning behind the $19.99–24.99 band:

- **Comparable indie co-op titles** — horror co-ops, extraction games, and team-based indies — overwhelmingly launch between **$15 and $25**. That's the band our own [Release Date & FAQ](/guides/release-date-faq) has cited for Grain Rot's comparables.
- **Scope points up, not down.** The 1.0 package is large for the category: 5 biomes (3 demo + 2 new), 9+ enemy types with elite variants and a boss, 20+ constructs, player leveling, a second Outpost floor, an enchantment/repair machine, and a complete quest flow. That's more content than most $15 titles.
- **A two-person studio.** Beck & Branch Games is a two-person indie team — small teams rarely price at the top of the range on a debut title; the publisher (Neem Interactive) typically runs a value-leaning launch playbook.
- **Where it could land outside the band:** below $15 (if they mirror Lethal Company-style pricing to convert the 350,000 demo players) or at $24.99 (if the 250K+ wishlist momentum supports it).

**Bottom line:** expect to pay roughly $20-25, budget for $25, and check the Steam page on August 7 for the official number. We'll update this page within hours of the price going live.
