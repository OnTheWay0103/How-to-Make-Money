## The Price: $9.99

The full game costs **$9.99 USD** on Steam, and it launched with a **10% launch-week discount to $8.99**. That's a deliberately value-leaning price for the category: comparable indie co-op extraction titles have launched between $15 and $25, and a two-person studio debut at $9.99 is squarely aimed at converting the demo's audience.

For context on the content you get at that price:

- **5 biomes** (3 from the demo + 2 new at 1.0), 9+ enemy types with elite variants and a boss, 20+ constructs, player leveling, a second Outpost floor, an enchantment/repair machine, and a complete 1.0 quest flow.
- **Full co-op for up to 4 players** — the shared extraction loop is where the replay hours go.
- The **free demo** remains on Steam, so you can try the core loop before spending anything.

## Available Editions

**Only one edition is indicated — a standard edition.** No deluxe, collector's, or DLC-bundle editions have been announced. What is confirmed:

- **No pre-purchase.** The full release had no pre-purchase and no preload; you bought on or after August 7.
- **No DLC or season pass** announced — and no post-launch roadmap at all. The confirmed feature set is the launch package.
- **No microtransactions** have been announced; the confirmed feature list includes none.

If a deluxe edition appears, it will be announced on the Steam page — that's the only authoritative source. We'll update this guide the moment one is listed.

## The Free Demo Is Your Value Test

Before spending a dollar, remember what you can already get for free:

- The demo (on Steam since June 12, 2026) contains the **complete core loop**: 3 underground biomes, 9 enemy types, 20+ unlockable constructs, upgradeable Outpost rooms, dungeon modifiers, and full co-op.
- It's the **exact same game**, just smaller — most players report several hours of content when chasing unlocks (individual mileage varies).
- Reception was strong: **Very Positive** on Steam, one of the **Top 15 most-played demos** at the June Steam Next Fest, and **350,000+ downloads**.

**The one caveat:** demo progress did not carry over to the full game — the demo is a separate standalone build. Your *skill* carried over, which is the part that matters. Everything about the demo is covered in the [Demo Guide](/guides/demo-guide).

This was an unusual situation for a launch: you could play the full core loop for free *before* deciding to buy. That's still true — if you're on the fence, the demo makes the decision easy.

## Is It Worth It? An Honest Analysis

**For $9.99 (or $8.99 during launch week), the content math is strongly favorable:**

- 5 biomes, a boss, elite enemies, leveling, an expanded Outpost, and a full quest flow — at roughly half the price of comparable indie co-op extraction titles.
- Co-op replay value is the game's core strength: the shared extraction loop (protect the haul, call the elevator, never all break at once) is where the hours go. Games like this live or die on whether you have a crew — see the [Co-op Guide](/guides/co-op-guide).

**The honest risks:**

**The verdict:** at $9.99, this is an easy buy for anyone who enjoyed the demo — and *if* you haven't tried the demo, that's the correct first step regardless of price. Play it free, then decide.
