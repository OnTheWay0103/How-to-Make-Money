# Grain Rot Spark Mode Guide — What Happens When Your Vessel Breaks

Grain Rot does not give you a health bar. It gives you a body, and the body is consumable: *"Death is just a change of scenery."*

But the state you enter when that body breaks — **Spark mode** — is the least explained part of the game, and it is where runs are won or lost. A reviewer with 2,657 hours put it plainly: *"there is no real explanation about like going into spark mode."*

> **Scope note:** this page covers the **break-and-recover state**. Using a doomed body as a deliberate escape is the [Enemy Chase Guide](/guides/survive-enemy-chase-guide); choosing which bodies to run is the [Best Vessels Guide](/guides/best-vessels-guide); banking the haul is the [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide).

---

## The Confirmed Rules

Straight from the Grain Rot Steam store page (app 4450620), read September 17, 2026:

- **You are a Living Spark**, surviving "inside fragile wooden vessels that splinter, collapse, and catch fire."
- **"When your vessel breaks, the Spark escapes"** — and **"you stay in the run as a Spark,"** where you "guide your team through the dark and search for another host with a new set of abilities."
- **"Force your way back in before you fade out completely."**
- **"Possess and swap between fragile wooden vessels"** is a listed feature.
- **"If everyone breaks, your loot is lost."**

Those lines describe **three states**, not two:

| State | In the run? | Can act on loot? |
|-------|:--:|:--:|
| In a vessel | Yes | Yes |
| A Spark, out of a body | Yes | Player-reported **no** — [Unconfirmed] |
| Faded out | **No** | No |

A broken vessel does not remove you from the run — the official copy says you *stay in it*. The fade does. The model is not "I died and respawned," it is **"I entered a timed recovery state, and the run is still live while I am in it"** — so because the loss condition is team-level, one Spark still floating is one player who can walk the loot out.

---

## The Fade Window

Official copy confirms the window exists and closes: you must *"force your way back in before you fade out completely."* No duration has ever been published. Two independent reviewers say it **feels short**: a 1,367-hour player reports "spark glow runs out to fast so you cannot get in to another body on the map" (review 232668015), and a 204-hour player reports "Glow doesn't last long enough" (review 232477767).

**[Unconfirmed — player-reported]** There is no official number and this guide will not invent one. Both reports point at the same implication: the timer is not generous enough to spend on exploration. **Do not scout as a Spark.** Move body to body.

---

## Spare Bodies Are the Scarce Resource

The window is only half the problem; the other half is that there may be no body to reach:

- *"they put barely any bodies on each level and none can actually collect things"* — review 232477767 (204 h)
- *"there are no robot bodies that are useful. If you die you are [finished] anyway until your next run"* — review 234119603 (214 h)
- *"when left as sparks... there is no way to win the game. You are just stuck forever"* — review 232922050 (387 h)

**[Unconfirmed — player-reported]** Spawn counts are undocumented, but three independent reviewers describing the same shortage is enough to plan around.

### The habit that fixes it: the body census

1. **Count the vessels you pass on arrival** — before you start smashing furniture, and actually count them.
2. **Call the count out loud in co-op.** A teammate who breaks in an unscouted wing is a teammate who fades.
3. **Remember the nearest one to the elevator** — that is your recovery anchor.
4. **Treat a body as part of the haul.** A spare vessel beats a mid-tier piece of furniture.

---

## Two Official Fixes That Landed on This Problem

**Patch 1.08 (August 26, 2026):** *"Added a new small Vessel named Boing"*, *"Small Vessels can now carry and hold up to one item"*, plus a fix for eye and badge changes on small vessels.

**Patch 1.09 (August 28, 2026):** *"Fixed a bug where items saved on an uncontrolled character would end up floating in the air."*

Players had been reporting that scavenged bodies "can't actually collect things." Whether or not 1.08 was aimed at that complaint — the developer has not said — its note is the first official statement that small bodies have carry capacity at all, and it sets the number at **one item**. Repossess into a small vessel and you can still carry what matters: the fuse, the gold, the last piece of the haul.

The 1.09 note confirms something structural: the game tracks **items stored on characters you are not controlling**, and it had a bug that left those items floating. That matches what a 667-hour reviewer described as *"losing all the resources in the pocket of your idle (not currently possessed) robots in the elevator"* (review 234558485, one player, **[Unconfirmed]**). Safe read of both: **an unpossessed body is not a storage chest you can trust.**

## What Happens to Your Loot

**Confirmed:** *"If everyone breaks, your loot is lost"* — a **full team break**, not an individual death. **Not documented:** whether a faded Spark counts toward "everyone breaks," and what happens to the contents of the body you left behind.

Assume the worst on both. **Treat a fade as a wipe for you** — you are out, and your carried loot went with the old body. **Treat your old body as a container you may never reopen.** If you were carrying the run's best item, reaching that body matters more than reaching any body. The instinct after breaking is *find the nearest vessel*; the correct play is *the nearest vessel on the way back to your old body*.

---

## The Recovery Protocol

Ordered, because the order is the point.

1. **Break on your terms, near a body.** If the vessel is finished and a spare is close, stop running and let it happen where you can recover — never in a corridor with a committed pursuer, or you repossess under the chase you just lost.
2. **As a Spark: go straight to a body.** No looting, no scouting, no detours. — [Unconfirmed]
3. **Call it out in co-op.** "I am out, heading for the body by the south door" turns a personal emergency into a team task. Silence is how Sparks fade.
4. **Cover the Spark, do not follow it.** Teammates who run to a Spark lead the Corrupted to it. Hold the room between them instead.
5. **Never let the last body break in a dead end.** If you are the only player still in a vessel, the haul rides on you — leave, do not explore.
6. **Extract the value, not the players.** One player back at the elevator with the loot is a successful run, even if the rest ended as Sparks.

Two mistakes undo all of this: treating Spark mode as a second life (it is a countdown with a body hunt inside it), and splitting the team up to hunt for bodies.

## FAQ

**Q: What is spark mode in Grain Rot?**
The state you enter when your vessel is destroyed. Official copy: "When your vessel breaks, you stay in the run as a Spark," and you search for another host before you "fade out completely."

**Q: How long do you have as a Spark?**
The window exists — you must force your way back in before fading. No duration has been published; community reports describe it as short. — [Unconfirmed]

**Q: Can you pick up items as a Spark?**
Players report a Spark cannot interact with loot, and that scavenged bodies "can't actually collect things." Player-reported, not documented. — [Unconfirmed]

**Q: How do you find a new vessel?**
Bodies spawn in the ruins and are the only host available; spawn rules and counts are undocumented, and players consistently report they are scarce. Patch 1.08 added a small Vessel named **Boing** and confirmed small Vessels can carry up to one item.
