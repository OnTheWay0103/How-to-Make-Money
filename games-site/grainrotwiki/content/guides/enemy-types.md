## Enemies of Grain Rot

The world of Grain Rot is filled with horrors. The Corrupted — once-human vessels twisted by the Rot — now roam the ruins, hunting any Spark they detect. Understanding each enemy type is essential for survival. This guide covers every enemy, their attack patterns, weaknesses, and how to defeat them.

## Enemy Classification

Enemies in Grain Rot are classified into categories based on their behavior and threat level.

### Classification System

- **Trash** — Low HP, low damage, slow. Can be ignored or killed easily.
- **Standard** — Moderate HP and damage. Require attention but are manageable.
- **Elite** — High HP, special attacks. Require strategy and resources to defeat.
- **Boss** — Very high HP, unique mechanics. Require preparation and teamwork.
- **Environmental** — Not technically enemies, but dangerous environmental threats.

## Detailed Enemy Analysis

### Wanderer

The most common enemy in Grain Rot. Wanderers are slow, weak, and predictable — the perfect enemy to learn the combat basics on.

**Appearance**: Humanoid figure, tattered clothing, milky white eyes, shuffling gait.

**Behavior**:
- Patrols aimlessly until it detects a player (within 15 meters)
- Once aggroed, shuffles toward the player at a slow walk
- Attacks with a two-handed claw swipe (slow, telegraphed)
- Will pursue the player indefinitely until line of sight is broken for 30+ seconds

**Attack Pattern**:
1. Raises both arms (1 second wind-up)
2. Swipes down with both claws
3. Stumbles forward after missing (1.5 second recovery)

**Strategy**:
- A single headshot with any firearm kills a Wanderer instantly
- Melee: Wait for the swipe, sidestep, attack during recovery
- Can be completely ignored if you move quietly (crouch-walk within 5 meters)

### Stalker

Stalkers are faster, more dangerous enemies that actively hunt the player.

**Appearance**: Hunched, elongated limbs, no visible face, moves on all fours.

**Behavior**:
- Spawns in forests and ruins, almost always at night
- Stalks the player from a distance (20-30 meters) before attacking
- Makes distinctive clicking sounds while stalking — listen for it
- Attacks when the player's back is turned or when isolated

**Attack Pattern**:
1. Pounces from a distance (can cover 10 meters instantly)
2. Follows with three quick claw swipes
3. Retreats and circles before another pounce

**Strategy**:
- Carry a light source at night — Stalkers are repelled by bright light
- When you hear clicking, find a wall — put your back to it
- Wait for the pounce, dodge sideways, attack during the recovery
- Fire damage deals bonus damage and causes the Stalker to flee briefly

### Brute

Brutes are heavily armored elites that require significant resources to bring down.

**Appearance**: Massive humanoid, fused with metal armor, one arm replaced with a club-like growth.

**Behavior**:
- Patrols high-corruption zones
- Can break through wooden walls and doors
- Aggro range is short (10 meters) but once aggroed, pursuit is relentless
- Roars before charging, alerting nearby enemies

**Attack Pattern**:
1. Charge (linear, 15-meter range, destroys obstacles)
2. Overhead slam (AoE, 3-meter radius, staggers)
3. Ground pound (360-degree AoE, knocks back)
4. Enrage (below 25% HP: double attack speed, +50% damage)

**Strategy**:
- Do not fight a Brute inside your base. Lure it away.
- Use fire damage and explosives — physical weapons deal reduced damage
- The charge attack leaves the Brute stunned for 2 seconds if it hits a wall
- Focus on dodging, not trading hits. One mistake costs half your HP.
- Bring at least 3 medkits and 2 explosive traps

### Screamer

Screamers are support enemies that summon reinforcements.

**Appearance**: Thin, tall figure with an oversized, distended jaw.

**Behavior**:
- Does not attack directly
- When threatened or when it detects a player, it emits a piercing scream
- The scream alerts all enemies within a 50-meter radius and causes nearby enemies to attack
- Screamers flee when approached, trying to maintain distance

**Strategy**:
- Kill Screamers first, before anything else
- A single headshot silences the Screamer instantly
- If you hear a scream, expect reinforcements within 15 seconds
- Use suppressed weapons to avoid triggering Screamers

### Phantom

Phantoms are supernatural enemies that only appear when your sanity is low.

**Appearance**: Translucent humanoid shape, flickering, no distinct features.

**Behavior**:
- Only spawns when sanity is below 40
- Cannot be damaged by physical weapons (phases through them)
- Inflicts sanity damage (15 per hit) rather than physical damage
- Can phase through walls and doors
- Disappears when sanity is restored above 50

**Strategy**:
- Restore sanity (use Blue Herbs, find light, rest) — the best counter
- Holy weapons (crafted with blessed materials) damage Phantoms normally
- Fire damage deals reduced damage but still works
- Do not run from Phantoms — they are faster than you. Stand and fight with appropriate weapons.

### The Harvestman (Boss)

The Harvestman is one of the most dangerous bosses in Grain Rot.

**Appearance**: A towering figure in a tattered cloak, wielding a massive scythe. No visible face under the hood.

**Behavior**:
- Spawned by a specific ritual in the final biome
- Teleports short distances
- Summons lesser Corrupted during the fight
- Has three distinct phases

**Phase 1 (100-66% HP)** :
- Scythe sweeps (wide horizontal arcs)
- Teleport slash (teleports behind a player and attacks)
- Summons 2 Wanderers every 30 seconds

**Phase 2 (66-33% HP)** :
- All Phase 1 attacks
- Scythe slam (AoE shockwave)
- Harvest (pulls all players toward it, then AoE)
- Summons 2 Stalkers every 30 seconds

**Phase 3 (33-0% HP)** :
- All previous attacks, faster
- Enrage (attack speed +100%, damage +50%)
- Summons 1 Brute (once)
- Final Reaping (room-wide AoE, 10 second cast, must line-of-sight block)

**Strategy**:
- Bring a full squad (4 players recommended)
- Holy weapons are effective against all phases
- Assign one player to handle summoned adds
- During Final Reaping, hide behind pillars or walls — line of sight blocks the damage
- Stockpile healing items: you will need at least 10 medkits per player

## Combat Tips Summary

- **Headshots** deal 3x damage to all humanoid enemies
- **Fire** is effective against most biological enemies
- **Holy/Light damage** is best against supernatural enemies (Phantoms, Harvestman)
- **Stealth** is always an option. Crouch-walk reduces enemy detection range by 80%
- **Suppressed weapons** reduce the chance of alerting nearby enemies
- **Running away** is sometimes the best strategy. Not every enemy needs to be killed.
