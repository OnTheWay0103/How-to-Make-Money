# Grain Rot Patch Notes & Update History — What Changed in 1.01–1.09 and What's Coming Next

Grain Rot has shipped **nine patches since its August 7, 2026 launch** — and the two that matter most to how you actually play (a public server browser and a batch of new buildables) landed *after* most reviews and guides were written. This page is the full, dated update history taken from the developer's own Steam announcements, plus the official roadmap for what is still coming.

> **Scope note:** this page is the *version history* — what changed and when. For playing with other people now that public servers exist, see the [Multiplayer & Matchmaking Guide](/guides/multiplayer-matchmaking-guide); for what shipped at 1.0, the [Full Release Guide](/guides/full-release-guide).

## The Patch That Changed the Most: 1.07 and the Public Server Browser

This is the single most important line in the whole history, and the one most guides and review summaries still get wrong:

> *"Public servers are now available to join via an in-game browser."* — Patch 1.07, August 12, 2026 (official Steam announcement)

For the first five days of launch, co-op really was invite-only, and the Steam community was loud about it — *"as of right now it's invite only making this hard to play,"* one poster wrote, calling a *"matchmaking/lobbylist a must have."* Review aggregators from that window still list **"No public matchmaking available"** as a top negative ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/4450620/grain_rot/stats), data stamped August 8).

Alongside the browser, 1.07 added **password-protected servers**, **more host settings** under the multiplayer options tab, **friends-of-friends joining** (the friends of *anyone* in the server, not just the host's), and a new **hat** hidden in the Deep Layers.

If you read that Grain Rot has no way to play with strangers, you are reading launch-week coverage. The feature shipped a month ago — the [Multiplayer & Matchmaking Guide](/guides/multiplayer-matchmaking-guide) has the walkthrough.

## The Content Era: 1.08 and 1.09

After the near-daily launch-week hotfixes, the developer slowed down and started shipping features instead of fixes.

### Patch 1.08 — August 26

The first real content patch, and the one the roadmap promised ("a batch of new constructs, and a new vessel"):

- **Boing**, a new **small Vessel**
- **7 new buildable constructs** — 3 common, 1 rare, 1 epic, 2 legendary
- **Small Vessels can now carry one item** — a meaningful change to how small bodies play
- **Grief protection** is now a per-player setting you can switch off, limiting harmful actions outside the Deep Layers
- **Persistent bans**, plus a search filter and a lock icon for password-protected servers
- **Wailer** got tweaked attack patterns *and an extra melee attack*; cosmetic spawns in dungeons were cut from 2 to 1
- A long tail of collision and networking fixes (citadel walls, bunkbeds, treasures, construct placement replication)

### Patch 1.09 — August 28

The current version, and a smaller quality-of-life patch:

- **The compass can now be recalibrated to point toward the Deep Layer grinder** — the headline change, and a genuine navigation aid for deep runs
- **Vibe Modulator** fixed (wrong scrap value) and its visual effect made cheaper to run
- **Health and stamina from resting on constructs is now percentage-based** rather than a flat amount — which matters more the larger your vessel's pool is
- Fixes for wall-clipping using chairs, and for items saved on an uncontrolled character floating in mid-air

The compass change updates how you plan a descent — see the [Weapons & Tools Guide](/guides/weapons-tools-guide) for where it fits in a loadout.

## What's Coming Next: The Official Roadmap

The developer published a post-launch plan on **August 12, 2026** that still frames where the game is heading. Grain Rot is made by a **two-person team** (Miche and Nikko, Beck & Branch Games), who explicitly said they prefer goals per month over fixed dates:

| Window | Plan | Status |
|--------|------|--------|
| Next month (from Aug 12) | A batch of new constructs + a new vessel | ✅ Delivered as **Patch 1.08** (Aug 26) |
| **September** | **"The Deep Layers Update"** — one new biome and several new enemies | ⏳ Not shipped as of Sep 11 |
| **October** | **"The Halloween Update"** — opens the **third floor of the Outpost**, adds a new questline and seasonal visual updates | ⏳ Planned |
| Ongoing | Fixes, performance work, balancing and quality-of-life updates between the larger drops | ✅ Continuous (1.09 was the latest) |

The roadmap also confirmed the direction behind Patch 1.07: the team was *"testing a public lobby browser, alongside improvements to the way friends can join a session,"* while keeping friends-only and invite-only modes.

**Treat the September and October windows as plans, not dates.** The developer stays flexible to react to feedback, and as of September 11 no Deep Layers Update had appeared on the announcement feed.

## How to Check Your Version and Update

1. **Steam updates Grain Rot automatically.** If you have played recently you are almost certainly on 1.09; to force it, right-click the game in your library → **Properties → Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files**.
2. **Version mismatches break co-op joins.** Everyone updates, then the host restarts the lobby — faster than debugging networking.
3. **Watch the official Steam announcements** for the Deep Layers Update. Every patch is posted there first, with the authoritative change list.

### Why some guides look out of date

Third-party patch trackers lag: one widely cited tracker still lists **1.07 as the newest version**, and review-summary sites are still surfacing complaint lists built from **August 8** data — before the server browser existed. When a guide and the official announcement disagree about Grain Rot, the announcement is right.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Grain Rot still being updated?**
Yes. Nine patches shipped between August 8 and August 28, 2026, with 1.09 as the current version. The developer has a published plan through October.

**Q: What is the newest Grain Rot update?**
Patch 1.09 on August 28, 2026 — the compass recalibration plus Vibe Modulator and resting fixes.

**Q: Did Grain Rot add a server browser?**
Yes. Patch 1.07 on August 12, 2026 added public servers joinable through an in-game browser, along with password-protected servers and extra host settings. Coverage saying otherwise reflects the pre-1.07 launch build.

**Q: When is the Grain Rot Deep Layers Update?**
The developer plans it for September 2026 — one new biome and several new enemies. No exact date has been announced, and it had not shipped as of September 11, 2026. The Halloween Update (third Outpost floor, a new questline) is planned for October.
