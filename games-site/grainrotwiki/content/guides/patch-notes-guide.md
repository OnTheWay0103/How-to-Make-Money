# Grain Rot Patch Notes & Update History — Every Patch from 1.01 to 1.13

Grain Rot has shipped **more than a dozen patches since its August 7, 2026 launch**, including the 1.1 content update on September 24 and Patches 1.11–1.13 through September 30. The two that matter most to how you actually play — a public server browser and a batch of new buildables — landed *after* most reviews and guides were written. This page is the dated update history taken from the developer's own Steam announcements, plus the official roadmap for what is still coming.

> **Update (October 24, 2026):** the latest version is **Patch 1.13 (September 30)**. Update **1.1** landed on **September 24** with **Expedition Mode**, the **Rainbow Ward** biome and colour-pattern cosmetics, followed by **Patches 1.11 (September 28), 1.12 (September 29) and 1.13 (September 30)**. The mode and its patch changes are covered in the [Grain Rot Expedition Mode Guide](/guides/grainrot-expedition-mode-guide).

> **Scope note:** this page is the *version history* — what changed and when. For playing with other people now that public servers exist, see the [Multiplayer & Matchmaking Guide](/guides/multiplayer-matchmaking-guide); for what shipped at 1.0, the [Full Release Guide](/guides/full-release-guide); for what the locked third Outpost floor means for the end of the current content, see [Why the Third Floor Is Locked](/guides/third-floor-locked-guide).

## The Patch That Changed the Most: 1.07 and the Public Server Browser

This is the single most important line in the whole history, and the one most guides and review summaries still get wrong:

> *"Public servers are now available to join via an in-game browser."* — Patch 1.07, August 12, 2026 (official Steam announcement)

For the first five days of launch, co-op really was invite-only, and the Steam community was loud about it — *"as of right now it's invite only making this hard to play,"* one poster wrote, calling a *"matchmaking/lobbylist a must have."* Review aggregators from that window still list **"No public matchmaking available"** as a top negative ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/4450620/grain_rot/stats), data stamped August 8).

Alongside the browser, 1.07 added **password-protected servers**, **more host settings** under the multiplayer options tab, **friends-of-friends joining** (the friends of *anyone* in the server, not just the host's), and a new **hat** hidden in the Deep Layers.

If you read that Grain Rot has no way to play with strangers, you are reading launch-week coverage. The feature shipped a month ago — the [Multiplayer & Matchmaking Guide](/guides/multiplayer-matchmaking-guide) has the walkthrough.

## The Content Era: 1.08 and 1.09

After the near-daily launch-week hotfixes, the developer slowed down and started shipping features instead of fixes.

### Patch 1.08 — August 26

The first real content patch, and the one the roadmap promised ("a batch of new constructs, and a new vessel"):

- **Boing**, a new **small Vessel**
- **7 new buildable constructs** — 3 common, 1 rare, 1 epic, 2 legendary
- **Small Vessels can now carry one item** — a meaningful change to how small bodies play
- **Grief protection** is now a per-player setting you can switch off, limiting harmful actions outside the Deep Layers
- **Persistent bans**, plus a search filter and a lock icon for password-protected servers
- **Wailer** got tweaked attack patterns *and an extra melee attack*; cosmetic spawns in dungeons were cut from 2 to 1
- A long tail of collision and networking fixes (citadel walls, bunkbeds, treasures, construct placement replication)

### Patch 1.09 — August 28

The last patch before the September content drop, and a smaller quality-of-life patch:

- **The compass can now be recalibrated to point toward the Deep Layer grinder** — the headline change, and a genuine navigation aid for deep runs
- **Vibe Modulator** fixed (wrong scrap value) and its visual effect made cheaper to run
- **Health and stamina from resting on constructs is now percentage-based** rather than a flat amount — which matters more the larger your vessel's pool is
- Fixes for wall-clipping using chairs, and for items saved on an uncontrolled character floating in mid-air

The compass change updates how you plan a descent — see the [Weapons & Tools Guide](/guides/weapons-tools-guide) for where it fits in a loadout.

### Update 1.1 — September 24

The biggest drop since launch, and a new mode rather than a fix list:

- **Expedition Mode** — a hardcore mode with **permadeath**: if a run fails, it is over. It starts at a separate Expedition Outpost on the dark side of the planet, where cold replaces heat, frost damage replaces scorch damage, and you keep **Peter the Heater** running by feeding it scrap and constructs
- **The Rainbow Ward** — a new biome
- **Colour-pattern cosmetics**, unlocked by playing Expedition
- **Five save slots**, plus the ability to rename save files
- Shop items now persist during a session; starting the elevator and renaming signs/rooms became anti-grief-protected actions
- Withered Manor and the Frozen Vaults now spawn one loot room instead of two

The developer marked the milestone with an announcement noting **over 250,000 players**. The mode itself is covered in the [Grain Rot Expedition Mode Guide](/guides/grainrot-expedition-mode-guide).

### Patches 1.11–1.13 — September 28–30

Three quick follow-ups to 1.1, mostly stability and quality of life:

- **1.11** added a new enemy, the **Corrupted Builder**, plus **unstuck logic** when leaving a chair or bed and when possessing a character with your spark — two of the most common ways players got trapped
- **1.12** fixed a crash in the Grain Rot infection system and added a **dungeon-run timer** with run info visible in the build menu
- **1.13** fixed gamepad navigation when selecting a game mode and when deleting a save, made room-upgrade cosmetics available in the Expedition wardrobe, and let small vessels change colour patterns

## What's Coming Next: The Official Roadmap

The developer published a post-launch plan on **August 12, 2026** that still frames where the game is heading. Grain Rot is made by a **two-person team** (Miche and Nikko, Beck & Branch Games), who explicitly said they prefer goals per month over fixed dates:

| Window | Plan | Status |
|--------|------|--------|
| Next month (from Aug 12) | A batch of new constructs + a new vessel | ✅ Delivered as **Patch 1.08** (Aug 26) |
| **September** | **"The Deep Layers Update"** — one new biome and several new enemies | ✅ A new biome (the **Rainbow Ward**) and a new mode (Expedition) shipped in **1.1** on Sep 24; the developer did not use the "Deep Layers Update" name |
| **October** | **"The Halloween Update"** — opens the **third floor of the Outpost**, adds a new questline and seasonal visual updates | ⏳ Planned — not shipped as of Oct 24 (the latest announcement, Oct 23, is Twitch Drops) |
| Ongoing | Fixes, performance work, balancing and quality-of-life updates between the larger drops | ✅ Continuous (1.13 is current) |

The roadmap also confirmed the direction behind Patch 1.07: the team was *"testing a public lobby browser, alongside improvements to the way friends can join a session,"* while keeping friends-only and invite-only modes.

**Treat the October window as a plan, not a date.** The developer stays flexible to react to feedback, and as of October 24 no Halloween Update had appeared on the announcement feed.

## How to Check Your Version and Update

1. **Steam updates Grain Rot automatically.** If you have played recently you are almost certainly on 1.13; to force it, right-click the game in your library → **Properties → Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files**.
2. **Version mismatches break co-op joins.** Everyone updates, then the host restarts the lobby — faster than debugging networking.
3. **Watch the official Steam announcements** for the Halloween Update. Every patch is posted there first, with the authoritative change list.

### Why some guides look out of date

Third-party patch trackers lag: when this page was first built, one widely cited tracker still listed **1.07 as the newest version**, and review-summary sites were still surfacing complaint lists built from **August 8** data — before the server browser existed. When a guide and the official announcement disagree about Grain Rot, the announcement is right.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Grain Rot still being updated?**
Yes. Thirteen patches shipped between August 8 and September 30, 2026, with 1.13 as the current version. The developer has a published plan through October.

**Q: What is the newest Grain Rot update?**
Patch 1.13 on September 30, 2026 — gamepad fixes, Expedition wardrobe cosmetics and small-vessel colour patterns. The last content drop was **1.1** on September 24 (Expedition Mode and the Rainbow Ward).

**Q: Did Grain Rot add a server browser?**
Yes. Patch 1.07 on August 12, 2026 added public servers joinable through an in-game browser, along with password-protected servers and extra host settings. Coverage saying otherwise reflects the pre-1.07 launch build.

**Q: When is the Grain Rot Deep Layers Update?**
The developer planned a September update — one new biome and several new enemies. The **1.1** drop on September 24 delivered a new biome (the Rainbow Ward) and Expedition Mode, though the developer never used the "Deep Layers Update" name. The Halloween Update (third Outpost floor, a new questline) is planned for October and had not shipped as of October 24, 2026.
