# Grain Rot Full Release Guide — What's New in 1.0 vs the Demo

Grain Rot launches **August 7, 2026** on Steam as a **full 1.0 release** — not Early Access. The launch build is considerably larger than the demo that racked up 350,000+ downloads at the June Steam Next Fest. This guide lists every confirmed 1.0 addition from the publisher's launch announcement, and clearly marks what is still unconfirmed.

## The Quick Answer

If you played the demo, you know the core loop: ride the cursed elevator into shifting underground ruins, smash furniture for loot, dodge the Corrupted and the Rot, and extract with your haul to upgrade the Outpost. At 1.0, that loop gets:

- **2 new biomes** (on top of the demo's 3)
- A **boss encounter** and **elite enemies**
- **Dedicated Spark abilities** — active powers usable while disembodied after death
- **Player leveling**
- A **second Outpost floor**
- An **enchantment and repair machine**
- **Elevator modifiers**
- **Gamepad support** and **difficulty rebalancing** for solo and small groups

All of the items below come from the publisher's launch announcement (covered by the official launch trailer premiere and multiple outlets). Anything not in that announcement is marked **unconfirmed**.

## What's Confirmed to Carry Over

- **The demo's 3 biomes** — the full game builds on, not replaces, what you played (the 1.0 additions are described as *new* biomes on top of the demo's).
- **The demo's 9 enemy types** — the Corrupted roster remains the baseline.
- **The Outpost upgrade loop** — a second floor is an *addition*, so the demo's room upgrades carry into the design.
- **Sound discipline, extraction rhythm, Grinder economy** — every skill you learned transfers; nothing about the core systems changes.
- **11 languages, co-op for up to four players, single-player** — unchanged from the Steam page.

**Demo progress carry-over:** still not confirmed, and not expected. Plan for a fresh start on August 7 — treat the demo as training, not a head start.

## How to Prep for 1.0

1. **Play the demo again before August 7.** The rebalancing means day-one runs will feel different — a fresh feel for the base loop smooths the transition.
2. **Don't overplan for the new systems.** Spark abilities and leveling are confirmed, but the specifics aren't published. Expect to learn them in-game.
3. **Check your PC against the requirements.** Minimum: i5-4590 / Ryzen 5 2600, 8 GB RAM, GTX 970 / R9 390. Recommended: i5-10600 / Ryzen 5 3600, RTX 2060 / RX 5700. 10 GB storage, broadband required.
4. **Line up your crew.** If you played the demo solo because of tuning, the difficulty rebalance is aimed at you — but the game is built for squads.

## FAQ

**Is Grain Rot 1.0 free to existing demo players?** The demo is a separate free build; the full game is a paid release. Price is unannounced (comparable indie co-op titles have launched in the $15–25 range — that's an estimate, not a fact).

**Do the demo's new-biome and boss additions have names?** Not published as of August 3, 2026. This page will be updated the moment official materials name them.

**Was the Discord free-access event a beta?** No — verified Discord members ("Scrappies") got 24 hours of the complete 1.0 build on August 1, ahead of the public launch. Top scrap-collectors received free copies of the game.

**What changed for solo players?** Difficulty rebalancing for solo and small-group play is confirmed; exact numbers aren't published. The demo's tuning leaned toward full squads.

**When exactly does it unlock?** The calendar date (August 7) is confirmed; no exact unlock hour has been announced. No preload has been announced either.
