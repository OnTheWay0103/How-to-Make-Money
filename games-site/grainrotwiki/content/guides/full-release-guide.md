# Grain Rot Full Release & Launch Guide — What's New in 1.0, First 24 Hours & FAQ

Grain Rot launched **August 7, 2026** on Steam as a **full 1.0 release** — not Early Access. The launch build is considerably larger than the demo that racked up 350,000+ downloads at the June Steam Next Fest, and the game arrived with roughly **200,000 wishlists** behind it. This guide lists every confirmed 1.0 addition from the publisher's launch announcement, the verified launch facts, and a first-24-hours plan for the full game.

## The Quick Answer

If you played the demo, you know the core loop: ride the cursed elevator into shifting underground ruins, smash furniture for loot, dodge the Corrupted and the Rot, and extract with your haul to upgrade the Sanctuary Outpost. At 1.0, that loop gets:

- **2 new biomes** (on top of the demo's 3)
- A **boss encounter** and **elite enemies**
- **Dedicated Spark abilities** — active powers usable while disembodied after death
- **Player leveling**
- A **second Outpost floor**
- An **enchantment and repair machine**
- **Elevator modifiers**
- **Gamepad support** and **difficulty rebalancing** for solo and small groups

All of the items below come from the publisher's launch announcement (covered by the official launch trailer premiere and multiple outlets). Anything not in that announcement is marked **unconfirmed**.

## What's Confirmed to Carry Over

- **The demo's 3 biomes** — the full game builds on, not replaces, what you played (the 1.0 additions are described as *new* biomes on top of the demo's).
- **The demo's 9 enemy types** — the Corrupted roster remains the baseline.
- **The Outpost upgrade loop** — a second floor is an *addition*, so the demo's room upgrades carry into the design.
- **Sound discipline, extraction rhythm, Grinder economy** — every skill you learned transfers; nothing about the core systems changes.
- **11 languages, co-op for up to four players, single-player** — unchanged from the Steam page.

**Demo progress carry-over:** never confirmed, and no carry-over was provided — the demo is a separate standalone build. Treat the demo as training, not a head start.

## The Demo Head Start

If you played the demo, you have a real head start — on *skill*, not progression:

- The demo contained the full core loop: 3 underground biomes, 9 enemy types, 20+ unlockable constructs, upgradeable Outpost rooms, and dungeon modifiers.
- **Demo progress did not carry over.** The demo is a separate build; your unlocks stayed there.
- Everything you learned — sound discipline, Grinder economy, extraction rhythm, co-op calls — transfers directly.

## Before Your First 1.0 Run

1. **Check your PC against the requirements.** The full spec tables are in the [System Requirements Guide](/guides/system-requirements): minimum is an Intel Core i5-4590 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600, 8 GB RAM, and a GTX 970 / R9 390; recommended is an i5-10600 / Ryzen 5 3600 with an RTX 2060 / RX 5700. The game needs 10 GB of storage and broadband for online play.
2. **Line up your crew.** Co-op is the center of gravity — the game supports up to 4 players, and the difficulty rebalancing means solo and small-group play are more viable than in the demo.
3. **If you haven't recently, play the demo once more.** A refresher on the mechanics makes day-one runs smoother.

## Your First 24 Hours: The Plan

### Hour 0: Descend

- Update the game, invite your crew, and take the elevator for the first time.
- **Don't optimize — experience.** The first run is about feeling the confirmed systems: possession, sound, corruption, extraction.

### Hour 1-2: Foundation Runs

- Complete 2-3 extractions, however small. **Consistency beats greed on day one.**
- Expect tuning differences from the demo — the demo was more generous by design, and 1.0 rebalanced the difficulty.
- Find the Grinder rhythm: smash, convert, extract.

### Hour 2-6: Build the Outpost

- Spend extracted resources on the confirmed unlocks: constructs, stat upgrades, and Outpost rooms.
- **Prioritize unlocks that improve runs** (tools, stats) over cosmetics — "questionable cosmetics" are confirmed, but they don't extract loot.
- Rescue survivors when you reach them — "rescue survivors trapped in the Deep Layers" is official.

### Hour 6-24: Go Deeper

- Push past demo depth. The Deep Layers are where the game's real rewards live.
- **Extract with something.** "If everyone breaks, your loot is lost" — never risk a full wipe on day one.
- Check the community hubs (Discord, Reddit) for verified post-launch data — and be skeptical of day-one "tier lists": no verified vessel or construct rankings existed at launch.

## Day-1 Pitfalls

1. **Expecting demo progress to carry over.** It didn't — plan around a fresh start.
2. **Trusting pre-launch tier lists.** No verified vessel/weapon data existed; test and learn.
3. **Going too deep too fast.** The Rot escalates with depth — respect the clock.
4. **Splitting the squad.** Launch-week chaos hits everyone; stay together until the team knows the new content.
5. **Ignoring the requirements.** A machine below minimum specs will struggle — check before you descend, not during.

## What About Post-Launch Content?

The developers have not announced a roadmap, DLC, seasons, or live events. The confirmed launch package is the game itself, and no microtransactions have been announced. We'll update this page (and the rest of the wiki) with verified post-launch data as it appears.

## FAQ

**Is Grain Rot 1.0 free to existing demo players?** No — the demo is a separate free build; the full game is a paid release at **$9.99** (10% launch-week discount to $8.99 at launch).

**Do the new-biome and boss additions have names?** Not published in official materials as of this writing. This page will be updated the moment official materials name them.

**Was the Discord free-access event a beta?** No — verified Discord members ("Scrappies") got 24 hours of the complete 1.0 build on August 1, ahead of the public launch. Top scrap-collectors received free copies of the game.

**What changed for solo players?** Difficulty rebalancing for solo and small-group play is confirmed; exact numbers aren't published. The demo's tuning leaned toward full squads.

**Does Grain Rot support controllers?** Yes — gamepad support is a confirmed 1.0 feature. See the [Controller Guide](/guides/controller-guide) for what's confirmed about supported controllers and layouts.

**When exactly did it unlock?** The calendar date (August 7) was confirmed; no exact unlock hour was pre-announced. The game is live on Steam now.
