## What is Dressmaker?

Dressmaker is a **cozy dressmaking simulation** developed by **Cozy Lives** and published by **Free Lives**. Rather than dressing a character, you make the garment: you pick the fabric, lay out and cut the pattern pieces, sew them together, and decorate the result — then hand it to a customer or sell it from your shop window.

The official store description frames the whole game around control:

> Choose fabric, arrange and cut out patterns, sew garments and add decorations. Dressmaker is a cozy simulation game where every part of making a dress is under your control.

That control extends down to the cloth itself. **Whether a pattern is aligned to the grain or cut on the bias changes what appears on the final dress**, and you can choose a different fabric for every individual panel of a garment. The game is a simulation of real dressmaking rather than a dress-up toy — real sewing concepts are the mechanic, not the theme.

You are also running a business. A whole town of people wants custom dresses for important events, each with their own tastes, and the official description is explicit that you can **satisfy — or sabotage — them**. Turning dresses in earns **reputation and coin**; so does displaying your own designs in your shop window and selling pre-made dresses off the rack.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Fabric** — five officially named fabrics: cotton, linen, wool, silk and velvet, bought from a wide range of suppliers
- **Grain and bias** — pattern alignment is part of the simulation, not decoration; the official description says it determines what appears on the finished dress
- **Per-panel fabric choice** — take full control over which fabric goes into each panel of a garment
- **Pattern layout and cutting** — arranging and cutting the pattern is a decision, not a formality
- **Sewing** — feed your fabric through the sewing machine to stitch it together
- **Decoration** — buttons, bows, appliqué, lace trims and accessories, placed with complete creative freedom
- **Sketchbook** — design and colour a dress, adding fabric samples and appliqué before you commit cloth
- **Mannequin preview** — check the dress before it leaves the table
- **Customers** — a whole town ordering custom dresses for important events and individual tastes — satisfy them or sabotage them
- **Shop window** — display your own designs and sell pre-made dresses off the rack
- **Reputation and coin** — the two confirmed rewards for turning in finished work
- **Accessibility** — Steam lists a Mouse Only Option and Playable without Timed Input
