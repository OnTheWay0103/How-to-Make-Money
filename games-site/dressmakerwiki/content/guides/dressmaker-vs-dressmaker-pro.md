**If you searched "Dressmaker" and found something that is not a cozy game about sewing dresses, you are not imagining it.** There are three separate products sharing nearly the same name on Steam, plus a 2015 film and an entire real-world trade using the same word.

This page tells you which one you want — and, more practically, how to check that you are on the right store page before you spend money.

## Which One Do You Want?

**You want Dressmaker (4019220) if** you are looking for a cozy simulation about making dresses — choosing fabric, arranging and cutting patterns, sewing panels together, decorating, and dressing a town of customers. Developed by Cozy Lives, published by Free Lives, releasing September 21, 2026 on Windows and macOS.

**You want DressMaker (1487080) or DressMaker Pro (1599300) if** you are working with **VRoid/VRM 3D avatars** and need a tool that applies one-piece textures to a model's clothing. These are authoring utilities for virtual-avatar creators, not games. They are Windows-only, published in 2021, and unrelated to Cozy Lives or Free Lives.

Most people searching for "Dressmaker game" want the first one. Most people who land on the wrong store page were searching for "DressMaker" in capitals, or clicked the wrong result on Steam's search page.

## The Two Other Things Called "Dressmaker"

Steam is not the only source of confusion. The word itself is heavily overloaded:

**The real-world trade.** A dressmaker is a person who makes custom clothing — a profession with centuries of history. A large share of "dressmaker" searches are about tailoring services, sewing patterns, courses and shops, not about any software at all. If you arrived here from a search about garment construction, the concepts overlap more than you might expect: Dressmaker the game deliberately simulates real dressmaking, including grain direction and bias cutting.

**The 2015 film.** *The Dressmaker* is a 2015 Australian film starring Kate Winslet, based on Rosalie Ham's novel. It shares the name and nothing else. Searches for the film, its cast and its plot are a significant portion of "dressmaker" traffic.

**Sewing machines.** "Dressmaker" is also a brand name used on sewing machines and sewing equipment. Product searches for those machines land in the same result pool.

None of these have anything to do with Cozy Lives or Free Lives.

## Where Do "Free Lives" and "Cozy Lives" Fit?

If you arrived via a search for *Free Lives Dressmaker* or *Cozy Lives Dressmaker*, here is the relationship, stated precisely:

- **Cozy Lives** is the **developer** of Dressmaker
- **Free Lives** is the **publisher** of Dressmaker
- Cozy Lives is Free Lives' cozy-games label, and **Dressmaker is its first release**

Free Lives is the South African studio behind titles including *Broforce*, *GORN* and *Terra Nil*. Dressmaker is a deliberate step into cozy games for them, and Cozy Lives is the label created to publish it.

### A correction worth making

A number of pre-release articles and fan pages describe Dressmaker as "developed by Free Lives." That is not what the official store data says. Steam lists:

- `developers: ["Cozy Lives"]`
- `publishers: ["Free Lives"]`

The correct phrasing is **"developed by Cozy Lives and published by Free Lives."** The distinction matters because credit is the one thing a small studio reliably controls, and it is also the detail most often copied incorrectly from one article to the next.

## Which Version Should You Actually Buy?

There is nothing to choose between them, because they do different jobs:

- **Want a game about making dresses?** Dressmaker, App ID 4019220, from September 21, 2026.
- **Want a VRoid/VRM avatar texturing tool?** DressMaker or DressMaker Pro, App IDs 1487080 and 1599300, available now.

The only real decision is between DressMaker and DressMaker Pro, and that is a question for avatar creators, not for players. If you are here for the cozy dressmaking game, neither of those is relevant to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is DressMaker the same game as Dressmaker?**
No. DressMaker (1487080) is a VRoid/VRM avatar texturing tool released in 2021. Dressmaker (4019220) is a cozy dressmaking simulation releasing September 21, 2026.

**Why do they have the same name?**
They do not, strictly — Dressmaker uses a lowercase "m", DressMaker capitalises the "M". That is a very thin distinction for a search engine and an even thinner one for a shopper, which is exactly why this page exists.

**Is DressMaker Pro the deluxe edition of Dressmaker?**
No. DressMaker Pro is the Pro edition of the *avatar texturing tool*, not of the game.

**I want the game — what do I search for?**
Search "Dressmaker release date" or use the App ID 4019220 directly. Searching the bare word "dressmaker" will surface the trade, the film and the tools alongside the game.

**Is the Dressmaker game by Free Lives?**
Published by Free Lives. Developed by **Cozy Lives**. Both statements are true and they are not interchangeable.

**Are there other games called Dressmaker?**
Not on Steam. The other similarly-named Steam products are utilities, not games.

**Is there a Dressmaker on itch.io too?**
Yes — the original prototype of the Cozy Lives game is hosted on itch.io on a name-your-price basis. That is the same project as the Steam release, just an earlier build. See [Is Dressmaker Free?](/guides/dressmaker-free-steam-vs-itch-prototype)
