## Biomes of the Forgotten World

Dinoblade's world is divided into six distinct biomes, each with its own ecosystem, environmental hazards, and secrets. Progressing through the game requires you to survive each biome's unique challenges, from scorching heat fields to toxic swamps.

This guide covers every biome, what you will find there, and how to prepare before entering.

## Detailed Biome Breakdown

### Verdant Meadows

The starting biome. Lush grasslands with gentle hills, scattered forests, and crystal-clear streams. This is your training ground — the enemies are forgiving, and resources are plentiful.

**Environmental Hazards**: None. The only danger is falling from cliffs.

**Native Creatures**:
- Velociblade (common)
- Compy Swarm (common, day only)
- Trike Charger (uncommon, territorial)
- Raptor Claw (rare, night only)

**Resources**:
- **Herbs** (Healing, Antidote crafting) — Found near water sources
- **Wood** (Campfires, basic structures) — From trees and fallen logs
- **Iron Ore** (Basic weapon and armor upgrades) — Rocky outcroppings near the eastern cliffs

**Hidden Areas**:
1. **Waterfall Grotto** — Behind the waterfall at the northeast edge of the map. Contains a weapon upgrade bench and a chest with a rare weapon blueprint.
2. **Rabbit Burrow Cave** — Hidden under a large tree root in the central forest. Contains an NPC vendor who sells unique items.

**Recommended Gear**: Basic iron equipment, any weapon type. No special preparations needed.

### Scorched Canyon

A vast canyon network with exposed rock, geothermal vents, and rivers of lava. The heat here is relentless.

**Environmental Hazards**:
- **Heat Damage** — Without heat resistance gear, you lose 1 HP every 5 seconds while exposed. Wearing at least one piece of Fire-resistant armor negates this entirely.
- **Lava Pools** — Instant death on contact. Some narrow paths require precise jumping.
- **Geyser Eruptions** — Random steam vents that knock you back. Watch for the ground rumbling indicator.

**Native Creatures**:
- Carno Stalker (common)
- Ptera Screamer (common, spawns from high perches)
- Ankylo Smasher (uncommon)
- Stego Defender (rare)

**Resources**:
- **Fire Crystals** (Fire weapon upgrades, heat resistance potions)
- **Obsidian** (High-durability weapons and armor)
- **Bone** (Tool handles, decorative armor)

**Recommended Gear**: At least one piece of Fire-resistant armor. A weapon with Ice damage is highly effective here.

### Misty Swamp

A low-visibility swamp filled with toxic pools and dense fog. Navigation is the real challenge here.

**Environmental Hazards**:
- **Poison Pools** — Standing in poison water deals 2 HP per second. Clean water is safe, but murky water is toxic.
- **Low Visibility** — The fog reduces your effective range by 60%. Use torches or the "Sharp Eyes" skill to improve vision.
- **Quicksand** — Patches of unstable ground that slow you to a crawl. Enemies will attack while you are stuck.

**Native Creatures**:
- Spino Tyrant (boss-class, central area)
- Raptor Claw (common, uses fog for ambushes)
- Compy Swarm (uncommon)
- Ptera Screamer (rare, perched on dead trees)

**Resources**:
- **Venom Glands** (Poison coating for weapons, antidote crafting)
- **Ancient Wood** (High-durability crafting material)
- **Gold Ore** (High-tier currency crafting)

**Recommended Gear**: Poison resistance consumables, a light source (torch or lantern), and a weapon with Lightning damage to deal with the swamp's creatures.

### Crystal Highlands

A frozen mountain range with glittering crystal formations. The cold here is as dangerous as the creatures.

**Environmental Hazards**:
- **Cold Exposure** — Drains stamina 2x faster. Cold resistance clothing is available from the vendor in the Meadows.
- **Slippery Surfaces** — Ice patches reduce traction. Dodging slides farther than intended.
- **Avalanche Risk** — Loud noises (explosions, roars) can trigger avalanches that deal massive area damage.

**Native Creatures**:
- Raptor Queen (boss, summit area)
- Velociblade (common, white-fur variant)
- Trike Charger (uncommon, ice variant with freeze breath)

**Recommended Gear**: Cold resistance armor. Ice resistance is not needed — the creatures deal physical damage primarily.

### Ash Wastes

A volcanic wasteland where ash storms reduce visibility and the ground is scorching hot.

**Environmental Hazards**:
- **Ash Storms** — Periodic storms that reduce visibility to near zero. Take shelter in caves or behind rock formations.
- **Hot Ground** — Certain patches of ground are superheated. They glow orange — avoid them.
- **Falling Ash** — Ash accumulation slows movement. Shake it off by dodging.

**Native Creatures**:
- Ankylo Smasher Elite (boss-class)
- Stego Defender (common, heat-adapted variant)
- Carno Stalker (uncommon)

**Recommended Gear**: Fire resistance armor, a weapon with Ice or Lightning damage.

### Forgotten Valley

The final biome, where all previous hazards combine. This is the endgame area, and only the well-prepared survive.

**Environmental Hazards**: All hazards from previous biomes can appear. Patches of the valley cycle through different states.

**Native Creatures**: Every enemy type in the game appears here, including elite variants of all species.

**Hidden Areas**: The Valley has more secrets than all other biomes combined. Exploration is heavily rewarded.

**Recommended Gear**: Full endgame equipment, multiple weapon types, full inventory of consumables.

## Biome Preparation Checklist

Use this checklist before entering a new biome:

- [ ] Craft or purchase appropriate resistance gear
- [ ] Stock healing items (at least 5)
- [ ] Equip a weapon matching the biome's elemental weaknesses
- [ ] Clear your inventory for new resources
- [ ] Visit the biome's vendor if available
- [ ] Learn the biome's specific dodge timings (hazards are on predictable cycles)
