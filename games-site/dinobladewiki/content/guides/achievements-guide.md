# Dinoblade Achievements Guide — Full Trophy List and Unlock Requirements

Dinoblade features 42 achievements covering combat challenges, exploration milestones, collection goals, and hidden secrets. This guide lists every achievement, its unlock requirement, and tips for earning the most difficult ones efficiently.

---

## 1. Combat Achievements

These achievements are earned through defeating enemies, mastering combat mechanics, and surviving difficult encounters.

| Achievement | Requirement | Tips |
|-------------|-------------|------|
| First Blood | Defeat your first enemy | Automatic during tutorial |
| Raptor Slayer | Defeat 100 Raptors | Farm the first zone's open plains |
| Titan Bane | Defeat all 4 zone boss variants | Must kill each boss type at least once per save |
| Perfect Parry | Parry 50 consecutive attacks without damage | Practice on slow raptors in zone 1 |
| No Hit Run | Complete any boss fight without taking damage | Use ranged build + full stamina investment |
| Overkill | Deal 5,000+ damage in a single hit | Obsidian Greatsword + Strength build + rage potion |
| Unstoppable | Defeat 20 enemies without resting at a bonfire | Light armor + health regen accessories |

## 6. Efficiency Tips for Completionists

- **Start with lore tablets.** Collect all 30 before fighting the final boss — they unlock dialogue options that affect ending availability.
- **Save egg hatching for last.** Eggs are RNG-dependent. Hatch them while doing other tasks; check periodically.
- **Use a notepad for map fragments.** The game does not track which fragments you have found. Manual tracking saves hours.
- **Speed Demon and True Ending are incompatible.** Speed Demon requires ignoring optional content. Do them on separate saves.
- **No Hit Run is easiest on the first boss.** Its attack patterns are the slowest and most predictable.
