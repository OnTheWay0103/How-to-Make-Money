# How Long Is Dinoblade? — Length, Replay Value & 100% Time

Dinoblade is a **short, focused Soulslike** — and the developer says the length is intentional. This guide covers the confirmed figures (from creator Jean Nguyen's own statements), what each piece of content takes, and an honest estimate for full completion.

**Honesty note:** only the main-story figure below is officially confirmed. Completion times, Boss Rush clear times, and 100% estimates are estimates based on confirmed content structure — we label them as such.

## The Confirmed Numbers

| Content | Time | Source |
|---------|------|--------|
| **Main story (single playthrough)** | **~7 hours** on average | Creator Jean Nguyen, based on ~30 playtesters — varies with Soulslike experience |
| **Free demo** | **~1 hour** (Dry Canyons route + first boss) | Steam demo coverage |
| **Bosses in the game** | **16** (mini-bosses + main bosses) | Creator statement at launch |

The developer's own framing: ~7 hours for players with Soulslike experience, more if the parry-based combat is new to you. Dinoblade is deliberately a **concise experience** — it tops out far below the hundreds-of-hours benchmarks of the genre.

## What's in Those 7 Hours

The campaign is structured in three parts with three confirmed main bosses (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)):

- **Part 1 — The Cavern:** tutorial corridor, Kira the Exile (first boss, teaches the Instinct Counter)
- **Part 2 — The Northern Paths:** waterfalls, the giant skull landmark, hidden SP pickups, boss **Axe**
- **Part 3 — The Mountain Caves:** hammer-wielding dino waves, final boss **Kasei the Tyrant** (two phases)

The remaining 13 bosses are **mini-bosses** — the creator's completionist description ("defeat all the mini bosses and main bosses to unlock skills") confirms they're a real part of the content, though their exact roster is still being documented by the community.

## After the Main Story

### Boss Rush (Confirmed)

**Boss Rush mode unlocks after beating the game** — the confirmed post-game mode. It's the game's answer to "what do I do now": replay the boss gauntlet back-to-back. See the [Boss Rush Guide](/guides/boss-rush-guide).

### Replay for Skills (Confirmed Direction)

The creator confirmed you **won't be able to max out all skills in a single playthrough** — the intended loop is starting a new game to max out different skills. Note: this is *not* a confirmed "New Game+ mode" — no official New Game+ has been documented (see the [New Game Plus Guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide)). What's confirmed is that replays exist to complete the skill set.

### Achievements

**29 achievements** are confirmed (see the [Achievements Guide](/guides/achievements-guide)) — the completionist checklist.

## 100% Completion Estimate (Honest Guesswork)

No official completion-time figure exists. Based on the confirmed content structure, a reasonable estimate for a full clear (main story + all bosses + Boss Rush + achievements) is:

- **Story + all bosses:** ~8–10 hours (the extra mini-bosses and exploration add 1–3 hours on top of the 7-hour story)
- **+ Boss Rush and achievements:** ~12–15 hours for most players
- **+ Maxing all skills across replays:** longer — this is the open-ended tail, since skill maxing spans multiple playthroughs

**These are estimates, not official figures.** They will be updated when community data (e.g., a HowLongToBeat crowd figure) confirms real-world times.

## Is It Worth the Price for the Length?

At **$19.99** (first-week 10% discount bringing it to $17.99), a ~7-hour main story with a Boss Rush endgame and multi-playthrough skill progression is on the short side for the genre — but the demo is free and rated **Overwhelmingly Positive**, so the easiest answer is: **play the ~1 hour demo first**. If the parry loop clicks for you, the length is a feature, not a flaw — this is a game built to be replayed, not grinded.
