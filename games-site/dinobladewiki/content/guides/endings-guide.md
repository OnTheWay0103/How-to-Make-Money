# Dinoblade Endings Guide — What's Confirmed About the Ending(s)

"dinoblade endings" and "dinoblade how to get all endings" are popular search terms — but Dinoblade's ending system is **not officially documented** as of August 2026. This guide separates what is confirmed about the campaign's structure from what remains unknown.

**The short answer:** it is not yet confirmed whether Dinoblade has multiple endings. Treat any guide that walks you through "Ending A / Ending B / True Ending" requirements with step-by-step item lists as fabricated — none of that is sourced.

## What Is Confirmed

### The Campaign Structure

Dinoblade's story is a survival tale: a cataclysm has armed every creature in the prehistoric world with a blade, and you — a young Spinosaurus with a colossal greatsword in your jaws — fight rival dinosaurs and story bosses to survive the extinction that follows.

The campaign progresses through three parts:

1. **Part 1 — the cavern:** ends with **Kira the Exile**, the first main boss. Defeating him unlocks the Summoning system and Soul Arts (Boss SP).
2. **Part 2 — the northern paths:** ends with **Axe**. Absorbing his soul unlocks the path forward.
3. **Part 3 — the mountain caves:** ends with **Kasei the Tyrant**, the final boss. Absorbing his soul unlocks the path forward, and defeating him unlocks **Boss Rush mode**.

### The Boss-Soul Progression

A confirmed story mechanic: **absorbing defeated bosses' souls unlocks the path forward to the next area**. This soul-progression loop — not item-collection quests — is the campaign's confirmed spine.

### Story Premise Details (Confirmed)

- The game began as a viral animation meme of a Spinosaurus swinging a sword; developer Jean Nguyen spent roughly three years turning it into a full game.
- Official description: a cataclysm changed the prehistoric world; dinosaurs carry their own weapons; you fight to prevent extinction.
- The now-famous trailer gag — parrying the meteor that wiped out the dinosaurs — is the tone-setter: the game is hard but playful.

## What Is Unconfirmed (TBD)

- **The number of endings** — one, two, or several: unconfirmed.
- **Ending unlock conditions** — any requirements (items, choices, optional bosses, collectibles) are unconfirmed.
- **Whether choices exist** — dialogue or behavior-based branching has not been documented.
- **Post-game state** — what happens after the credits (beyond the confirmed Boss Rush unlock) is unconfirmed.
- **New Game+** — see the [New Game Plus Guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide); it is not officially confirmed.

## What to Do If You Care About Endings

1. **Beat the campaign** — the confirmed first step regardless of how many endings exist.
2. **Explore thoroughly** — confirmed hidden SP pickups (Part 2's rocks, giant skull, and main path) prove the game rewards exploration, and legendary weapons are confirmed to exist.
3. **Do optional content before assuming it's done** — Boss Rush unlocks after the final boss, and the [Secrets Guide](/guides/secrets-guide) tracks what hidden content is verified.
4. **Ignore unsourced "ending requirement" lists** — if a guide's requirements involve items and areas you've never seen in verified coverage, it's invented.
