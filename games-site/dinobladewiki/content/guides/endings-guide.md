# Dinoblade Endings & Secrets Guide — Campaign, Hidden Content & What's Confirmed

"dinoblade endings," "dinoblade all endings," and "dinoblade secrets" are popular search terms — but Dinoblade's ending system is **not officially documented** as of August 2026. This guide separates what is confirmed about the campaign and its hidden content from what remains unknown.

**The short answer:** it is not yet confirmed whether Dinoblade has multiple endings. Treat any guide that walks you through "Ending A / Ending B / True Ending" requirements with step-by-step item lists as fabricated — none of that is sourced.

## What Is Confirmed About the Campaign

### The Structure

Dinoblade's story is a survival tale: a **cataclysm changed the prehistoric world**, arming rival dinosaurs with brutal weapons of their own, and you — a **young Spinosaurus with a colossal Great Sword in your jaws** — fight through biome after biome to **prevent a looming extinction event**.

- The world is handcrafted, with **dry canyons, dense mist-shrouded jungles, frozen mountain basins, and forgotten ruins**.
- Each region is ruled by an **Alpha apex predator** that guards the path forward — for example, a T-Rex wielding a smaller dinosaur as a weapon, and tyrannosaurs armed with battleaxes and halberds.
- The campaign builds to the game's signature moment from the trailer: **preparing to fight the meteor** that wiped out the dinosaurs.

### Progression (Confirmed)

- **Every victory unlocks strength** — attribute and skill upgrades, Soul Arts, and boss summons.
- **Legendary weapons are hidden in the world** and change how your Spinosaurus plays — exploration is a real part of progression.
- Defeating the **final boss unlocks Boss Rush Mode**.

### Hidden Content (What's Confirmed)

- **Legendary weapons** — confirmed to exist and be hidden in the world. Their names, locations, and effects are **undocumented** as of August 2026.
- **Exploration rewards** — the official description frames world exploration as a core activity, and hidden legendary weapons are the confirmed payoff.
- **Boss summons** — defeating bosses lets you summon them as AI allies; for completionists, every boss kill adds a combat tool.

## What Is Unconfirmed (TBD)

- **The number of endings** — one, two, or several: unconfirmed.
- **Ending unlock conditions** — any requirements (items, choices, optional bosses, collectibles) are unconfirmed.
- **Whether choices exist** — dialogue or behavior-based branching has not been documented.
- **Post-game state** — what happens after the credits (beyond the confirmed Boss Rush unlock) is unconfirmed.
- **Secret bosses and secret areas** — no hidden boss or area with a confirmed name exists in official material; the full bestiary and map remain undocumented.
- **New Game+** — not officially confirmed. See the [New Game Plus Guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide).

## What to Do If You Care About Endings and Secrets

1. **Beat the campaign** — the confirmed first step regardless of how many endings exist.
2. **Explore thoroughly** — legendary weapons are confirmed to be hidden in the world; sweeping each biome is the natural approach.
3. **Hunt legendary weapons before assuming the world is done** — they change how your Spinosaurus plays, and their locations are the community's biggest open discovery project.
4. **Ignore unsourced "ending requirement" lists** — if a guide's requirements involve items and areas you've never seen in verified coverage, it's invented.
5. **Check the Steam Community hub** — as the community documents the full game, verified findings surface there first.
