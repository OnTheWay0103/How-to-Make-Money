# Dinoblade Boss Rush Guide — Unlock, Format & Strategy

Boss Rush is Dinoblade's endgame gauntlet — a back-to-back sequence of boss fights. It is one of the few confirmed post-game features, and it's the mode most likely to carry the game's toughest achievements.

**Honesty note:** the *existence* of Boss Rush is confirmed (it unlocks after clearing the final boss). The scoring system, ranks, and rewards below are **TBD** — no verified source has documented them as of August 2026. Don't trust specific "S-rank requires 850 points" claims.

## What Is Confirmed

- **Unlock:** Boss Rush unlocks after defeating the final boss, **Kasei the Tyrant**.
- **Content:** a continuous sequence of boss fights, following the game's standard posture-parry combat.
- **Boss roster:** the three main bosses — **Kira the Exile, Axe, and Kasei the Tyrant** — are the campaign bosses. Whether Boss Rush includes additional or remixed fights is unconfirmed.
- **Access:** reached from the main menu after clearing the campaign.

## What Is Unconfirmed (TBD)

- **Scoring / ranks** — no verified scoring system or S/A/B rank thresholds have been documented.
- **Rewards** — cosmetic rewards, weapon enchantments, or mounts are all unconfirmed. (Any guide claiming a "Brachiosaur Mount" or "Eternal Flame enchantment" reward is speculating.)
- **Healing rules** — whether healing charges persist between fights or are reset is unconfirmed.
- **Difficulty scaling** — whether the mode scales with your level or New Game+ state is unconfirmed (and New Game+ itself is unconfirmed — see the [New Game Plus Guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide)).

## How to Prepare (Confirmed Mechanics Only)

### 1. Master the Instinct Counter

Every boss fight in Dinoblade is won by filling the boss's Resolve bar with parries and landing the **Instinct Counter** — the massive counterattack triggered by perfectly parrying a boss when its Resolve bar is full. In a gauntlet with no downtime, this is your fastest damage and your stamina economy: perfect parries restore stamina and Resonance. See the [Parry Guide](/guides/parry-guide).

### 2. Know the Three Fights Cold

- **Kira the Exile:** three-hit combo and charging attacks. Telegraphed — the easiest fight to do "clean."
- **Axe:** spinning combos, diagonal slashes, jumping attacks with landing AoE. Parry the **final swing of the spin** — it's the confirmed easy parry window.
- **Kasei the Tyrant:** two phases; below one-third health he swaps to a greatsword with gap-closers, sword throws, and parryable jump slashes. Stay at his side, dodge unblockables, parry the jump slashes.

Detailed breakdowns in the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

### 3. Build for Sustain

- **Spend Boss SP on Soul Arts that give sustain or mobility** — e.g., a dash art for closing gaps or creating space. All five confirmed Soul Arts (Meteor Slice, Meteor Dash, Alpha Roar, Meteor Zone, Survival Instinct) are bought with Boss SP; pick what covers your weakness. See the [Soul Arts Guide](/guides/soul-arts-guide).
- **Bring the Axe summon** — the Axe summon's stamina recovery boost is the most consistently useful confirmed summon effect for long fights.

### 4. Practice Discipline

- **Don't spam parries.** Wait for a real attack animation; mistimed blocks cost stamina and leave you vulnerable.
- **Dodge unblockables.** Some attacks (Kasei's phase 2 kit) cannot be parried — treat them as dodge-only.
- **Heal deliberately.** With healing rules unconfirmed, assume charges are limited and heal only in safe windows.

## Run Checklist

1. Clear the campaign (Boss Rush requires Kasei's death).
2. Refill and memorize your healing charge count.
3. Warm up your parry on regular enemies.
4. Route order Kira → Axe → Kasei and rehearse each individually.
5. Time your Instinct Counters — they're the difference between a slog and a clean run.
