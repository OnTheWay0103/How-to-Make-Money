# Dinoblade Demo Guide — What's In It and Should You Try It?

The Dinoblade free demo on Steam is the best way to try the game before buying — and its reception is one of the game's biggest success stories. This guide covers what's confirmed about the demo and what's still unknown.

## Demo Basics (Confirmed)

- **Availability:** free on **Steam** since **October 2025** (the full game launched July 23, 2026).
- **Rating:** the demo holds an **"Overwhelmingly Positive"** rating — roughly **97% positive across 4,200+ reviews**, with players consistently praising the combat. It's the strongest signal that Dinoblade's parry-and-posture combat clicks.
- **Size:** roughly 8.7 GB.
- **Languages:** the demo launched **English-only**; the full game supports many languages.
- **Its role:** the demo helped the game pass **500,000 Steam wishlists** before launch, and the full game hit No. 1 on Steam's Popular Releases chart at launch.

## What's In the Demo (Partial Confirmation)

The demo covers the **opening of the game** — the dry canyons where you learn the core toolkit: light and heavy attacks, blocking, parrying, dodging, lock-on, and the parry-and-posture loop. The exact extent of the demo (which areas and bosses it reaches) is **not officially documented** — coverage is conflicting, so treat any guide that maps the demo's full content as speculative.

## Save Transfer (Unconfirmed)

**This is the big open question.** Team Spino has not published an official statement covering whether demo saves carry into the full game. Treat any guide that confidently details a save-transfer process (including "save file locations" and transfer tables) as unverified.

**What to do anyway:**

1. Play the demo — worst case you lose some early-game progress; best case it carries over.
2. **Don't delete the demo until you've launched the full game** once, if you want to maximize the chance of transfer.

## Demo-First Strategy (Works Either Way)

Whether or not your save transfers, the demo is the ideal practice arena:

### 1. Learn the Parry Loop

The demo's smaller dinosaur packs are perfect training targets. Get comfortable with parrying to build posture and land guard-break counterattacks — the loop that carries the whole game. See the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide).

### 2. Learn the Dodge Discipline

Some attacks can't be parried. In the demo, practice recognizing which attacks must be dodged so you build the dodge-first reflex early.

### 3. Test the Water on Difficulty

The demo tells you whether the parry timing clicks for you — the game's combat is the entire pitch, and the demo shows it in full.

### 4. Time Your Buy

If you like the game, the demo makes the decision easy: the full game is **$19.99** on Steam, with a 10% launch discount through July 30, 2026 (the discount has ended; the standard price is $19.99).

## Reception Honesty

The demo's Overwhelmingly Positive rating reflects the combat. Launch reviews of the full game added caveats: **bugs, performance problems, imprecise hitboxes, janky UI, and inconsistent hit registration**. The demo is the best version of the pitch — the full game has more content and more rough edges.
