# Dinoblade Demo Guide — What's In It and Whether Progress Carries Over

The Dinoblade free demo on Steam is the best way to try the game before buying — and the demo's reception is one of the game's biggest success stories. This guide covers what's confirmed about the demo, and what's still unknown.

## Demo Basics (Confirmed)

- **Availability:** free on Steam since **October 2025** (the game launched July 23, 2026).
- **Length:** roughly **one hour** of gameplay.
- **Content:** the opening **Dry Canyons** route — the tutorial area with the game's standard enemies (small dinosaurs, a larger enemy) and the core combat toolkit (blocking, parrying, light/heavy combos, sprinting, dodging, lock-on, healing charges, charge attacks).
- **Reception:** the demo holds an **"Overwhelmingly Positive"** rating — around 97% positive across roughly 4,400+ reviews at last check. Players consistently praise the combat.
- **Its role:** the demo helped the game pass **500,000 Steam wishlists** before launch (the developer cites 600,000+).

**How far the demo goes (conflicting reports):** the demo covers the opening Dry Canyons route with mini-bosses and a main boss. Some coverage describes the demo extending through more of the early game — the exact boss roster in the demo is **TBD**. If you want to see the game's signature mechanic in action, the demo shows the parry-and-Resolve loop and the tutorial-installed Instinct Counter concept before your first real boss.

## Save Transfer (Unconfirmed)

**This is the big open question.** Community discussion of demo save transfer for Dinoblade is mixed, and Team Spino has not published an official statement covering whether demo saves carry into the full game. Treat any guide that confidently details a save-transfer process (including "save file locations" and transfer tables) as unverified.

**What to do anyway:**

1. Play the demo — worst case you lose an hour of progress; best case it carries over.
2. **Don't delete the demo until you've launched the full game** once, if you want to maximize the chance of transfer.
3. If you own both, launch the full game before uninstalling the demo.

## Demo-First Strategy (Works Either Way)

Whether or not your save transfers, the demo is the ideal practice arena:

### 1. Learn the Parry Release-Timing

The parry is **hold block, release right before the strike lands**. The demo's small dinosaur packs are the perfect training targets. Get comfortable with perfect parries — they restore stamina and Resonance and fill enemy Resolve bars. See the [Parry Guide](/guides/parry-guide).

### 2. Learn the Unblockable Tell

Coverage confirms unblockable attacks exist. In the demo, learn to recognize which attacks can't be parried so you build the dodge-first reflex early.

### 3. Test the Charged Heavy Projectile

The charged heavy attack fires a **ranged projectile for 5 Resonance plus stamina**. The demo is a great place to internalize the cost — it's the game's only confirmed ranged tool and fights against reach-weapon enemies depend on it.

### 4. Time Your Run

If you like the game, the demo's one hour is long enough to reach its main boss and decide if the $19.99 full price is worth it (a 10% launch discount brought it to $17.99 through July 30, 2026).
