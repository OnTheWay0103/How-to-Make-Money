# Dinoblade New Game+ & Endgame Replay — Status, Challenge Runs & Speedrunning

**The direct answer: New Game+ is NOT confirmed for Dinoblade.** As of August 2026, no official source (Steam store page, developer announcements, or verified launch coverage) documents a New Game+ mode. This guide explains what that means, what *is* confirmed about the post-game, and how players are extending the game regardless.

## Why "Dinoblade NG+" Gets Searched

"dinoblade new game plus" is a popular search term — Dinoblade is a Soulslike, and NG+ is the genre's standard endgame. But popularity isn't confirmation:

- The Steam store page does not list NG+ among features.
- No verified walkthrough or coverage mentions NG+.
- Any guide describing "NG+ enemy multipliers," "NG+ exclusive weapons," or "NG+ boss movesets" for Dinoblade is **invented**.

**Bottom line:** plan for a single-playthrough experience. If Team Spino adds NG+ in a patch (as many Soulslikes do post-launch), this guide will be updated.

## What IS Confirmed for Post-Game

### Boss Rush Mode

The one confirmed endgame feature: defeating the **final boss** unlocks **Boss Rush** — a continuous gauntlet of boss fights. This is the game's official replay venue. See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) for the full breakdown.

### Skill Progression Across Runs

Official material confirms you **learn new skills and build your own combat style** as you progress — and that **every victory unlocks strength**. The game's confirmed progression layers (attribute/skill upgrades, Soul Arts, legendary weapons, boss summons) give replays distinct paths: a second run can pursue different Soul Arts, hunt different legendary weapons, and play a different style.

### The Campaign Loop Itself

The core loop is worth replaying: parry-and-posture combat, weapon-wielding rival dinosaurs, Alpha predators ruling each region, and a finale that sets up the fight against the meteor.

## Community Extensions (Not In-Game Systems)

The game does not track these — they are community formats built on confirmed mechanics:

- **Challenge runs** — parry-only runs, no-hit runs, no-heal runs. Community-defined rules that test mastery of the posture loop. Any guide claiming in-game rewards ("titles," "skins") for challenge runs is fabricating.
- **Speedrunning** — the campaign's region-by-region structure is route-friendly. Specific record times are **community-sourced and change constantly**; treat any "world record" figure online as folklore, not fact.
- **Boss Rush time trials** — the confirmed mode gives speedrunners a pure-execution venue with no exploration.

## What Carries Between Playthroughs

**Unconfirmed.** With no documented NG+ mode, there is also no documented carry-over list. What's known:

- Your **save** is a normal single-run save.
- **Boss Rush** unlocks per save once the final boss is down.
- Nothing about carrying gear, stats, or unlocks into a second cycle has been officially described.

## How to Plan Your Endgame

1. **Clear the campaign** — this unlocks Boss Rush, the confirmed endgame.
2. **Pursue different builds on replays** — Soul Arts, legendary weapons, and skill upgrades make each run distinct. See the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide).
3. **Set a Boss Rush target** — clean clears first, then faster clears.
4. **Watch official channels** — Team Spino's announcements and the Steam store page are where an NG+ patch would be announced.
