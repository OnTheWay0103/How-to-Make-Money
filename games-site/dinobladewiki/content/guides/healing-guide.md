# Dinoblade Healing Guide — Healing Charges, Save Stones & Survival

"dinoblade how to heal" is one of the most searched Dinoblade questions — and the answer is simpler than in most Soulslikes: **you have a fixed number of healing charges, and you refill them at Save Stones**. This guide covers the confirmed healing and survival systems, how the resource economy actually keeps you alive, and what is still unverified.

**Honesty note:** the confirmed healing system is healing charges + Save Stones. Any guide describing crafting potions, healing items on a hotbar, or "+heal charm" builds is unverified — no such systems appear in verified coverage as of August 2026.

## The Confirmed Healing System

### Healing Charges

- **You start with 3 healing charges** — confirmed by the verified Part 1 walkthrough: "You should have 3 Healing Charges as designated on the HUD."
- Each charge restores health when used — the exact heal amount is **undocumented** (no official numbers published).
- Charges are your **only confirmed heal**. There is no flask-crafting, no passive regen confirmed, and no healing item economy.

### Save Stones — The Refill Point

- **Save Stones** are the game's rest points (not bonfires, not campfires).
- **Resting at a Save Stone refills your healing charges** — this is the core recovery loop.
- **Resting respawns enemies** — the standard soulslike trade: recovery in exchange for a reset area.
- Light every Save Stone you find; they're your respawn anchors and your heal refills.

### The Death Penalty Makes Healing Forgiving

- **You do not drop currency when you die** — confirmed. Dying costs you time, not progression.
- Practical effect: you can spend healing charges aggressively to learn a fight. A failed attempt with no charges left costs nothing but a trip back.

## The Hidden Healers: Parry and Dodge

Dinoblade's survival system is bigger than the heal button. The **resource economy** is where fights are actually won:

| Mechanic | What It Does | Survival Value |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| **Perfect parry** | **Restores stamina AND Resonance** (the blue bar under your health) | Free sustain — defense literally refills your resources |
| **Block (held)** | Costs stamina to hold | Cheap survival for attacks you won't parry |
| **Blocking late** | Consumes stamina | The punish for mistimed blocks — this is why the parry is a release, not a press |
| **Dodge / roll** | I-frames during the roll | The **only** answer to unblockable attacks |
| **Charged heavy attack** | Ranged fire projectile, costs **5 Resonance + stamina** | Kills threats at range before they touch you |

### The Rules of Staying Alive

1. **Don't block everything.** Holding block drains stamina — and with no stamina, you can't dodge the unblockables. Block to learn, parry to sustain, dodge to survive.
2. **Unblockable attacks must be dodged.** Confirmed: some attacks cannot be parried. Roll through them with i-frames; blocking them is how packs and bosses punish you.
3. **Parry first, heal after.** A perfect parry restores stamina and Resonance for free — the best "heal" in the game is a well-timed parry that prevents the damage *and* refunds your resources.
4. **Use charges deliberately.** The confirmed beginner advice: don't heal at full safety. Learn the boss's pattern first, then heal to survive the *second* time through the pattern (see the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide)).

## Healing Through a Run: The Save Stone Rhythm

A typical healthy run follows a loop:

1. **Light the Save Stone** at the area start — refills charges, anchors your respawn.
2. **Fight forward** — parry to restore stamina/Resonance, dodge unblockables, spend charges only when a hit is coming.
3. **Backtrack to the stone when low** — resting refills charges but respawns enemies; accept the respawn as practice.
4. **Enter the boss at full charges** — 3 charges + the parry economy is the confirmed survival kit for every fight.

### Boss Fights Specifically

- **Start with 3 charges** — that's the budget. The boss isn't a war of attrition; it's a test of how little damage you take.
- **The Instinct Counter is survival**: a full Resolve bar + perfect parry triggers the massive "clash" counterattack — the best way to end a fight *before* your charges run out (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)).
- **Kira the Exile** (first boss) is the charge-economy exam: his telegraphed three-hit combo and charging attacks are exactly what the parry system is for.
