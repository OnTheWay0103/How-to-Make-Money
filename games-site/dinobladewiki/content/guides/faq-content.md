# Dinoblade FAQ — Common Questions Answered

Answers to the most common Dinoblade questions, verified against the Steam store page and launch coverage. Where information isn't documented, this FAQ says so instead of guessing.

---

## 1. What is Dinoblade?

**A single-player, dinosaur-themed Soulslike action RPG** developed by **Team Spino LLC**, the studio of veteran animator **Jean Nguyen** (formerly at Sucker Punch Productions). You play a **young Spinosaurus gripping a colossal Great Sword between its teeth**, fighting weapon-wielding rival dinosaurs across a prehistoric world to prevent a looming extinction event — the finale sets up the fight against the meteor. Press shorthand: "Dark Souls with dinosaurs," with a Sekiro-style parry-and-posture combat system.

## 2. When is the release date?

**July 23, 2026 on Steam (PC).** The game hit No. 1 on Steam's Popular Releases chart at launch. A free demo has been available since October 2025.

## 3. How much does it cost?

**$19.99 USD** on Steam, with a **10% launch discount** (bringing it to $17.99) during the first week, through July 30, 2026. No microtransactions are documented.

## 4. Is there a demo?

**Yes — free on Steam since October 2025.** The demo is rated **"Overwhelmingly Positive"** (~97% positive across 4,200+ reviews), covers the opening of the game, and helped the game pass 500,000 Steam wishlists. Whether demo saves transfer to the full game is **unconfirmed** — see the [Demo Guide](/guides/demo-guide).

## 5. What platforms is it on?

**PC only — Steam on Windows.** No console versions exist, and Team Spino has not announced any console ports. The game is **single-player only** (see question 9).

## 6. Is it a Soulslike?

**Yes.** It has the standard Soulslike toolkit — stamina-based combat, light and heavy attacks, dodging with i-frames, timed parrying, and challenging boss fights that require pattern learning:

| Mechanic | How It Works in Dinoblade |
|----------|--------------------------|
| **Parrying** | Well-timed parries build enemy **posture** — a Sekiro-style posture system |
| **Posture breaks** | Full posture = guard break = counterattack/finishing blow window |
| **Saving** | Save points restore healing and respawn enemies |
| **Progression** | Attribute and skill upgrades, Soul Arts (special attacks), boss summons, legendary weapons |
| **Bosses** | Each region ruled by an Alpha apex predator; Boss Rush unlocks after the final boss |

## 7. How do healing and saving work?

- **Healing is limited and restored at save points** — the game's rest points. Resting restores healing and respawns enemies, the standard Soulslike trade.
- **Exact mechanics are undocumented** — charge counts, heal amounts, and any additional healing systems appear in no official source. See the [Advanced Combat Guide](/guides/combat-advanced-guide).

## 8. What are Soul Arts?

**Special attacks** — part of the game's confirmed progression ("powerful weapon skills," "learn new skills, build your own combat style"). **No official list of Soul Art names or effects has been published** — treat any guide naming specific arts as unverified. See the [Soul Arts Guide](/guides/soul-arts-guide).

## 9. Is there multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** Dinoblade is a confirmed **single-player** game. There is no co-op, no PvP, and no invasion system. The "summons" you use are AI versions of defeated bosses, not other players. See the [Multiplayer Status Guide](/guides/multiplayer-pvp-guide).

## 10. What weapons can I use?

Your confirmed weapon is the **Great Sword** held in your Spinosaurus's jaws. **Legendary weapons are hidden in the world** and change how your Spinosaurus plays — their specifics are undocumented. No official weapon list with stats exists. See the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide).

## 11. What are the system requirements?

**See the official Steam store page.** We do not reproduce spec values here because they change with patches — the store page is the authoritative source.

## 12. Is there New Game+ or endgame content?

**New Game+ is unconfirmed.** The confirmed endgame feature is **Boss Rush Mode**, which unlocks after defeating the final boss. See the [New Game Plus Guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide).

## 13. How well reviewed is it?

At launch, Dinoblade sat at **89% Very Positive** on Steam (~400 reviews), with the demo rated **Overwhelmingly Positive** (~97% across 4,200+ reviews). Launch reviews praised the combat and reported **bugs, performance problems, imprecise hitboxes, janky UI, and inconsistent hit registration**.

## 14. Is it Steam Deck compatible?

The game is rated **Playable** on Steam Deck by Valve, and has **full controller support**.

## 15. Is it worth buying?

The signals are strong: a demo rated Overwhelmingly Positive, 500,000+ wishlists, a day-one top spot on Steam's Popular Releases chart, and a combat system widely praised in coverage. The launch price of $19.99 is modest for the genre — and the free demo is the easiest way to decide.
