# Dinoblade FAQ — Common Questions Answered (Verified)

Answers to the most common Dinoblade questions, verified against the Steam store page and launch coverage. Where information isn't documented, this FAQ says so instead of guessing.

---

## 1. What is Dinoblade?

**A single-player, third-person "Soulslite" action RPG** (a Soulslike with a more forgiving death penalty) by solo developer **Jean Nguyen** (former senior gameplay animator at Sucker Punch) under **Team Spino LLC**, with Boltray Games credited as publisher. Built in Unreal Engine 5, you play a young **Spinosaurus gripping a colossal greatsword in its jaws**, fighting through a world where a cataclysm armed every dinosaur with a blade. It's often described as "Dark Souls with dinosaurs," with a parry system compared to Sekiro and Lies of P.

## 2. When is the release date?

**July 23, 2026 on Steam (PC).** The game topped Steam's Popular Releases chart on day one. A free demo has been available since October 2025.

## 3. How much does it cost?

**$19.99 USD** at launch, with a **10% launch discount** (bringing it to $17.99) during the first week (through July 30, 2026). Regional pricing: ¥44 in mainland China, HK$78 in Hong Kong, NT$278 in Taiwan. No microtransactions are documented.

## 4. Is there a demo?

**Yes — free on Steam.** The demo covers roughly **one hour** of gameplay in the opening Dry Canyons route, including the game's core combat (blocking, parrying, light/heavy combos, dodging, lock-on, healing charges, charge attacks). The demo is rated **"Overwhelmingly Positive"** (~97% positive across thousands of reviews) and helped the game pass 500,000 Steam wishlists. Whether demo saves transfer to the full game is **unconfirmed** — see the [Demo Guide](/guides/demo-guide).

## 5. What platforms is it on?

**PC only — Steam on Windows.** No console versions exist, and Team Spino has not announced any console ports. The game is single-player only.

## 6. How many bosses are there?

**Three main bosses are confirmed** — **Kira the Exile** (first boss, teaches the Instinct Counter), **Axe** (melee fighter with spinning combos), and **Kasei the Tyrant** (two-phase final boss). Whether additional optional or secret bosses exist is **TBD**. See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

## 7. Is it a Soulslike?

**Yes, with a twist.** It has the standard Soulslike toolkit — stamina-based combat, light and heavy attacks, dodging with i-frames, parrying, boss fights — but it's deliberately more forgiving ("Soulslite"):

| Mechanic | How It Works in Dinoblade |
|----------|--------------------------|
| **Parrying** | Hold block, release right before the strike lands. Perfect parries **restore stamina and Resonance** and fill enemy Resolve bars |
| **Resolve/Instinct Counter** | Full Resolve bar + perfect parry = massive counterattack "clash" |
| **Saving** | **Save Stones** serve as rest points (restore healing charges, respawn enemies) |
| **Death penalty** | **You do NOT drop currency when you die** — no retrieval run needed |
| **Leveling** | Collect souls/SP; **leveling is partly automatic**; you can adjust attributes before boss fights |
| **Progression** | Defeat bosses to absorb their souls; earn Boss SP for **Soul Arts**; summon defeated bosses |

## 8. How do healing and saving work?

- **Healing charges** are your heal — refilled at **Save Stones**, the game's rest points.
- **Save Stones** (not bonfires) are where you rest, respawn enemies, and anchor your save. They're confirmed in walkthrough coverage of all three parts.
- **Death is forgiving** — no currency drop, so dying is a learning event, not a setback.

## 9. What are Soul Arts?

**Abilities purchased with Boss SP** (earned from boss victories). The five confirmed Soul Arts are **Meteor Slice, Meteor Dash, Alpha Roar, Meteor Zone, and Survival Instinct**. Soul Arts run on **Resonance** — the blue bar under your health that perfect parries restore. See the [Soul Arts Guide](/guides/soul-arts-guide).

## 10. Is there multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** Dinoblade is a confirmed **single-player** game. There is no co-op, no PvP, and no invasion system. The "summons" you use are AI versions of defeated bosses, not other players.

## 11. What is the Ex-Mod system?

**TBD.** "Ex Mod" is a widely searched term for Dinoblade, but the mechanic is not officially documented in any verified source as of August 2026. Treat any site describing Ex-Mod tiers, forges, and modifier effects as speculative until the community verifies it.

## 12. What are the system requirements?

**Minimum (official):** Windows 10 64-bit, Intel Core i5-7500 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600, 4 GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 1050 (4 GB) / AMD Radeon RX 560, DirectX 12, 13 GB storage (the demo needs 6 GB). Full details in the [Controls Guide](/guides/controls-guide).

## 13. Is there New Game+?

**Unconfirmed.** No official source documents New Game+ for Dinoblade. What *is* confirmed as post-game content is **Boss Rush mode**, which unlocks after the final boss. See the [New Game Plus Guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide).

## 14. Is the game violent?

**Yes — with an option.** The game contains frequent blood effects and cutscene violence and carries a mature-content note, but includes an option to **reduce or disable gore**.

## 15. Is it worth buying?

That's up to you — but the signals are strong: a demo rated **Overwhelmingly Positive** (~97% positive), 500,000+ wishlists, a day-one top spot on Steam's Popular Releases chart, and a combat system widely praised in early coverage. The launch price of $19.99 with a first-week discount is modest for the genre. Try the free demo first.
