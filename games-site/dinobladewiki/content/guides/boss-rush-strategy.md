## Boss Rush Strategy Guide — S-Rank Scoring & Speed Tactics

Boss Rush is Dinoblade's true endgame gauntlet: a continuous sequence of bosses where your score, kill speed, and damage taken all feed into a final rank. Most players can clear the mode, but S-ranks demand deliberate routing, perfect parries, and loadouts built around sustain. This guide breaks down the scoring math and the strategies that reliably produce S-rank runs.

### How Boss Rush Scoring Works

Every boss kill grants score based on four factors, evaluated the moment the boss dies.

| Factor | Contribution | How to Maximize |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Kill Time | Up to 400 points | Shorter fights score more; speed matters most |
| Parries | Up to 300 points | Perfect parries grant bonus per successful riposte |
| Damage Taken | Up to 200 points | Full points at 0 hits taken, scales down from there |
| Style Combo | Up to 100 points | Chaining attacks without taking damage builds combo |

S-rank requires roughly 850 points per boss on average across the full run. Missing the parry bonus on two consecutive bosses will usually drop you to A-rank.

### Optimal Boss Order

1. **Carnotaurus first** — fastest kill, lets you bank score and momentum early.
2. **Utahraptor second** — its dashes punish panic; parry the third dash in each chain.
3. **Triceratops third** — the only boss with a shield phase; break the shield with two charged heavies.
4. **Allosaurus fourth** — save your full Style Combo for this fight; it has the widest parry windows.
5. **Rex final** — the hardest fight; enter with full health and both healing items available.

### Parry-Focused Speed Tactics

- **Parry the tell, not the attack.** Every boss telegraphs with a distinct sound cue before its fastest move — react to the audio, not the animation.
- **Riposte canceling:** A perfect parry riposte can cancel into a dash attack, saving roughly 0.8 seconds per exchange. Use this on every parryable attack.
- **Learn the two-hit rhythm:** Rex's claw combo has a parry window on both hits. Parrying both triggers an extended stun worth massive style points.
- **Don't chase parries on unparryable attacks:** The Triceratops charge cannot be parried — dodging it and continuing your combo is worth more than a failed parry attempt.

### Loadout Choices for S-Rank Runs

| Loadout | Strength | Weakness | Best Bosses |
|---------|----------|----------|-------------|
| Dual Claws + Agile Vambraces | Fastest combo build-up | Low burst | Utahraptor, Allosaurus |
| Greatsword + Parry Charm | Huge riposte damage | Slow start | Triceratops, Rex |
| Spear + Elemental Core | Range and sustain | Lower style ceiling | Carnotaurus, Allosaurus |
| Balanced (Sword + Shield) | Safe damage reduction | Mid-tier score | Full run consistency |

### Run Checklist

1. Equip at least one healing accessory before entering — there is no rest between bosses.
2. Warm up your parry timing on the Carnotaurus; it shares attack rhythms with later bosses.
3. Chain parries into ripostes for the first 20 seconds of every fight to build the Style Combo.
4. Never over-commit: taking a hit costs up to 200 points, more than any extra combo swing gains.
5. If your first boss goes badly, restart — a single lost bonus cascades through the whole run.
