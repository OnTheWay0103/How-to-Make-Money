# Dinoblade Boss Rush Strategy — Clean Runs, Parry Routing & Sustain

Boss Rush in Dinoblade is a continuous gauntlet of the campaign's bosses, unlocked after defeating **Kasei the Tyrant**. Because no verified scoring system exists yet (see the [Boss Rush Guide](/guides/boss-rush-guide)), this strategy guide focuses on what actually wins fights: **parry routing, Instinct Counter timing, and sustain**. A "clean run" — minimal damage, no spammed parries, controlled healing — is the baseline every player should aim for before chasing ranks or times.

## The Strategy Core: Parry Routing

Dinoblade rewards patience over aggression. In a boss gauntlet, every hit you take is a resource drain, so route the fights around your parries:

1. **Parry the real attacks, not the tells.** Every boss telegraphs its fastest moves. Wait for the attack animation to actually begin, then release block right before impact.
2. **Perfect parries pay for themselves.** A perfect parry restores stamina and Resonance — the more perfect parries you land, the more resources you have for Soul Arts and charged attacks.
3. **Fill Resolve, then trigger Instinct Counters.** When a boss's Resolve bar is full, perfectly parrying one of their attacks triggers the Instinct Counter "clash" — massive damage. Route each fight around hitting that window; don't waste parries when the bar is empty of value.
4. **Dodge unblockables.** Some boss attacks cannot be parried. Learn which ones they are (Kasei's phase 2 kit is the big example) and treat them as dodge-only.

## Per-Boss Routing

### Boss 1: Kira the Exile

- **Route:** block his three-hit combo once or twice to learn it, then parry the swings to fill his Resolve bar. Charge attacks are dodge-or-parry; the ground explosion after the charge is a dodge-only.
- **Clean-run note:** Kira is the most telegraphed fight in the game — this is where you bank a low-damage opener. Spend the fight practicing perfect parries rather than racing.

### Boss 2: Axe

- **Route:** his spinning combos are the fight's parry centerpiece — the **final swing of the spin is the confirmed easy parry window**. Parry it, fill Resolve, land your finishing blow.
- **Dodge the jumping attacks** — their landing AoE punishes players who stand their ground. Punish the recovery after whiffed heavies.

### Boss 3: Kasei the Tyrant

- **Phase 1:** fire attacks, tail sweeps, charges, and slams. Stay at his flank, parry what you can, dodge tail sweeps.
- **Phase 2 (below one-third HP):** greatsword kit — gap-closers, sword throws, heavy combos, and **parryable jump slashes**. Parry the jump slashes, dodge everything else, and punish the missed sword throws.
- **Clean-run note:** phase 2 is where runs die. Be defensive: side-positioning and patience beat aggression here.

## Sustain Choices

- **Spend Boss SP on a Soul Art that fits the gauntlet.** The five confirmed Soul Arts are Meteor Slice, Meteor Dash, Alpha Roar, Meteor Zone, and Survival Instinct — exact effects are partially documented, so test each on regular enemies first. A mobility art (a dash or zone tool) generally carries more gauntlet value than pure damage. See the [Soul Arts Guide](/guides/soul-arts-guide).
- **Use the Axe summon.** The Axe summon's stamina recovery boost is the confirmed summon effect with the most sustain value for consecutive fights.
- **Heal in safe windows only.** With healing rules unconfirmed, assume charges are finite. Heal after a boss transitions or during long recovery animations — never mid-pressure.

## Clean-Run Checklist

1. Refill healing charges before entering.
2. Warm up parry timing on regular enemies.
3. Kira: bank a clean opener; focus on perfect parries.
4. Axe: parry the final spin swing; dodge landing AoEs.
5. Kasei: flank in phase 1; parry jump slashes in phase 2; dodge unblockables.
6. Spend Boss SP before the run — sustain arts and the Axe summon are the best confirmed investments.
