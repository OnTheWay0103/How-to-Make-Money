## Challenge Runs in Dinoblade

After completing Dinoblade's main story, many players turn to challenge runs to extend the experience. Challenge runs are self-imposed rulesets that drastically increase difficulty, forcing you to master the game's mechanics. The community has developed several popular challenge types, and the game even rewards some of them with exclusive cosmetics and titles.

This guide covers every popular challenge run type, how to prepare for them, and strategies for success.

## Challenge Run Types

| Challenge | Rules | Difficulty | Reward | Recommended For |
|-----------|-------|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| No-Hit Run | Complete the game without taking any damage | Extreme | "Untouchable" title, Ghost weapon skin | Parry masters |
| Level 1 Run | Never level up, use starting equipment only | Very Hard | "Determined" title, Rusted weapon skin | Veterans seeking difficulty |
| Speedrun (Any%) | Complete the game as fast as possible | Hard | Leaderboard ranking, "Swift" title | Route optimizers |

| Broken Weapon | Use only broken/rusted weapons | Very Hard | "Scavenger" title | Resource management experts |

## No-Hit Run Strategies

The no-hit run is the most prestigious challenge in Dinoblade. Every single hit you take — even a tiny scratch from a Compy — means restarting.

### Preparation

- **Practice on every boss** in the Boss Rush mode until you can defeat each one without taking damage consistently.
- **Learn all enemy attack patterns** from the Enemy Guide. You need to recognize every wind-up animation instantly.
- **Choose your route carefully**. The Verdant Meadows are easy enough, but the Misty Swamp's poison pools make it especially dangerous.
- **Back up your save file** — not to cheat, but to avoid losing progress to bugs or glitches.

### Key Skills to Master

1. **Perfect Parry** — Parrying at the exact moment of impact negates all damage and staggers the enemy. This is your most important tool.
2. **Dodge Rolling** — Invincibility frames during rolls are your backup. Learn the timing for every attack in the game.
3. **Stamina Management** — Never let your stamina drop below 30%. You need reserves for emergency dodges.
4. **Positioning** — Many enemies have attacks that can be avoided simply by standing in the right spot.

### Biome-Specific No-Hit Tips

| Biome | Biggest Threat | Mitigation |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| Verdant Meadows | Falling off cliffs | Slow down, watch your footing |
| Scorched Canyon | Heat damage (passive) | Heat resistance armor is mandatory |
| Misty Swamp | Poison pools (passive) | Antivenom consumable before entering water |
| Crystal Highlands | Cold + Avalanches | Cold resistance gear, avoid explosive barrels |
| Ash Wastes | Ash storm visibility | Memorize enemy positions, use audio cues |
| Forgotten Valley | Everything combined | Perfect preparation, no shortcuts |

## Level 1 Run Strategies

In a level 1 run, you never spend XP to level up. You use the starting weapon and armor throughout the entire game. Your stats remain at their base values, meaning every enemy is a serious threat.

### Essential Tips

- **Upgrade your starting weapon**. You cannot change weapons, but you can upgrade the one you have. Fully upgrade it using materials from all biomes.
- **Use consumables aggressively**. Since you cannot improve your stats, consumables are your main source of buffs. Stack damage and defense buffs before every major fight.
- **Learn to perfect parry**. Without leveling, you cannot afford to trade hits. Parrying is your only reliable damage window against bosses.
- **Prioritize Stamina**. In a normal run, you might invest in Strength or Vitality. In a level 1 run, Stamina is your only stat that matters — level up Endurance whenever you can.
- **Skip optional content**. Side bosses and elite enemies are not worth the risk. Stick to the critical path.

## Speedrun Routes

### Any% Route

The goal is to reach the final boss and defeat it as quickly as possible. Current world record is approximately 22 minutes.

**Route Overview:**

1. **Verdant Meadows** (3 min) — Sprint to the exit. Kill only the Trike Charger blocking the path. Skip all optional content.
2. **Scorched Canyon** (4 min) — Take the upper path to avoid most enemies. Use the shortcut through the magma cavern (requires 1 Fire Crystal).
3. **Misty Swamp** (4 min) — Follow the right wall to navigate the fog. The Spino Tyrant is mandatory. Use Lightning damage for a faster kill.
4. **Crystal Highlands** (4 min) — Climb directly to the summit. Ignore the ice cavern network. The Raptor Queen is vulnerable to Fire.
5. **Ash Wastes** (3 min) — Take the lava tube shortcut to skip half the biome. The Ankylo Smasher Elite can be parry-killed in 3 parries if you have practiced.
6. **Forgotten Valley** (4 min) — Direct path to Kasei the Tyrant. Use the highest DPS build you have. This fight determines your final time.

### 100% Route

The goal is to complete all content — every boss, every side quest, every secret area. Current world record is approximately 4 hours.

**Key Differences from Any%**:
- Visit every hidden area in each biome (13 total)
- Defeat all optional bosses and elite variants
- Complete all NPC quest lines
- Collect all unique weapons and armor pieces
- Unlock all skills in the skill tree

## Rewards for Challenge Runs

The game tracks most challenge runs automatically and awards cosmetic rewards upon completion:

- **Cosmetic Titles** — Displayed in multiplayer and on the main menu
- **Weapon Skins** — Visual only, no stat changes
- **Armor Skins** — Change your appearance without affecting stats
- **Profile Badges** — Shown on your player profile
- **Score Multiplier** — Some challenges unlock a permanent score multiplier for Boss Rush mode
