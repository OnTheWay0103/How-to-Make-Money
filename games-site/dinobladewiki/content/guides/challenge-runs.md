# Challenge Runs Guide — Self-Imposed Rules & Speedrun Routes

After clearing Dinoblade's campaign, many players extend the game with self-imposed challenge runs. The game's forgiving death penalty (you don't drop currency when you die) makes these far less punishing than in traditional Soulslikes.

**Honesty note:** challenge runs are a community activity — the game does not track them or award titles/cosmetics for them. Any guide claiming in-game rewards ("Untouchable" titles, weapon skins) is fabricating. What follows is how to run the popular community challenges with confirmed mechanics.

## Popular Challenge Run Types

| Challenge | Rules | Difficulty | Why It Works |
|-----------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **Parry-Only Run** | No light/heavy attacks — damage only via parries, finishing blows, and Instinct Counters | High | The parry → Resolve loop is the game's core; this forces full mastery |
| **No-Hit Run** | Complete a boss fight (or the game) without taking damage | Extreme | Requires perfect parry routing and unblockable discipline |
| **No-Heal Run** | Never use healing charges | Very High | Makes every block/parry decision matter |
| **Any% Speedrun** | Beat the campaign as fast as possible | Hard | Short campaign (3 bosses), routing-friendly |
| **Boss Rush Time Trial** | Clear Boss Rush as fast as possible | Hard | Confirmed mode, no exploration required |
| **Level-1 Style Run** | Never spend SP/levels (if the game permits it — TBD) | High | Tests base-kit mastery |

## Parry-Only Run (The Signature Challenge)

Dinoblade's design makes this the defining community challenge — it's the one run that fully uses the game's identity.

**How to play:**

- **Fill Resolve bars** on regular enemies with parries, then use the heavy finishing blow for kills.
- **Against bosses:** route every fight around the **Instinct Counter** — when the Resolve bar is full, perfect-parry a real attack for the massive counter damage.
- **Dodge unblockables** — they're the only attacks you can't parry; the run lives or dies on recognizing them.
- **Spend Boss SP on sustain arts** (Survival Instinct-type tools) since you can't chip-damage your way through fights.

## No-Hit Run

**Preparation:**

- Practice each boss individually until you can do the fight with zero damage taken (Boss Rush is an ideal rehearsal venue — see the [Boss Rush Guide](/guides/boss-rush-guide)).
- Learn every enemy's wind-up animation. You need instant recognition, not just parry skill.
- Route around environmental hazards — Part 2's hidden SP pickups sit in risky spots; decide whether they're worth it.

**Key skills:**

1. **Perfect parry timing** — the primary survival tool.
2. **Unblockable recognition** — the only way to avoid them is dodge i-frames.
3. **Stamina discipline** — never exhaust yourself; exhausted stamina is how "clean" runs end.
4. **Positioning** — many attacks are beaten by standing in the right place, not reacting.

## Speedrun Routes (Any%)

The campaign has three bosses (Kira → Axe → Kasei), so the route skeleton is simple: **learn each boss as fast as possible, minimize travel, skip optional content.**

**Routing principles:**

- **Skip hidden SP pickups** unless they're directly on the path.
- **Know the parry windows before the run starts** — the fights, not the map, determine your time. See the [Parry Guide](/guides/parry-guide).
- **Kira is the first gate** — a clean Instinct Counter routing on Kira saves the most time because the fight is the longest of the three to learn.
- **Kasei phase 2 is the run-killer** — dodge his unblockables, parry his jump slashes, and don't greed for damage.
- **Times are player-dependent.** Any "world record" figure you see online is community-sourced and changes constantly — treat numbers like "22 minutes" as folklore, not fact. See the [Speedrun Guide](/guides/speedrun-guide).

## General Challenge Run Tips

1. **Back up your save** — not to cheat, but to avoid losing a long run to a crash or update.
2. **Use Boss Rush as a training ground** — it gives you back-to-back boss practice without the travel.
3. **Watch your stamina like a health bar** — in every challenge run, stamina is the first resource that fails.
4. **Adjust attributes per boss** — the game allows attribute tuning before major fights; use it to fit the challenge (e.g., more health for no-heal runs).
