# Dinoblade Beginner Guide — Survive Your First Hunt

Welcome to Dinoblade, a dinosaur-themed **Soulslike action RPG** developed by **Team Spino LLC**, the studio of veteran animator **Jean Nguyen** (formerly at Sucker Punch Productions). You play a **young Spinosaurus gripping a colossal Great Sword between its teeth**, fighting weapon-wielding rival dinosaurs across a prehistoric world to prevent a looming extinction event.

The game **launched July 23, 2026 on Steam (PC) at $19.99**, with a 10% launch discount through July 30, 2026. A free demo has been available on Steam since October 2025. Dinoblade is **single-player only** — there is no co-op or PvP.

This guide covers the confirmed core mechanics: the parry-and-posture loop, stamina management, what to do in your first hours, and the mistakes every new player makes.

---

## The Core Loop: Parry → Posture Break → Counterattack

Dinoblade uses a classic Soulslike combat model with a **Sekiro-style posture system** at its heart:

1. **Block or dodge** to survive attacks you do not want to parry. Blocking and dodging both cost stamina.
2. **Parry** incoming strikes with well-timed timing. Parries **build enemy posture** — every enemy and boss has a posture meter that fills as you deflect their attacks.
3. When an enemy's posture breaks (**guard break**), they are staggered and vulnerable to a **counterattack or finishing blow**. This is the game's main damage window, especially in boss fights.
4. Manage your **stamina** — attacking, blocking, dodging, and sprinting all consume it. If you exhaust your stamina, you cannot defend yourself.

The game's combat is built around this loop: defense feeds offense. The better your parry timing, the faster you break enemy posture and land counterattacks.

### Confirmed Combat Basics

- **Light and heavy attacks** with combo strings — the in-game tutorial covers the basics.
- **Dodge/roll with invincibility frames** — your answer to attacks that cannot be parried or blocked.
- **Parry timing is the skill that matters most.** Not every attack can be parried; when in doubt, dodge.
- **Soul Arts** — special attacks that add to your toolkit (see the [Soul Arts Guide](/guides/soul-arts-guide)).
- **Summons** — defeated bosses can be summoned to fight alongside you as AI allies.
- **Legendary weapons** are hidden in the world and change how your Spinosaurus plays.
- **Attribute and skill upgrades** — every victory unlocks strength; you build your own combat style.

### Healing and Save Points

- Healing in Dinoblade is **limited and restored at save points** — the game's rest points. Resting restores healing and respawns enemies, the standard Soulslike trade.
- Exact healing mechanics and charge counts are not officially documented; treat detailed healing tables in other guides as unverified (see the [Advanced Combat Guide](/guides/combat-advanced-guide)).

## Your First Expedition

### Where You Start

The game opens in the **dry canyons** — the world's first biome — where you learn the basics: movement, light and heavy attacks, blocking, parrying, dodging, and lock-on. Take your time here. The smaller dinosaurs you meet are the game's intended parry practice; use them to drill the timing before you face your first Alpha boss.

### First Priorities

1. **Learn to parry early.** The posture loop is the game's identity — parries build posture, posture breaks open counterattacks, and against bosses this is your biggest damage window.
2. **Respect stamina.** Every action costs stamina. Exhausting yourself mid-combo is how packs of smaller dinosaurs kill new players.
3. **Explore.** Legendary weapons are hidden in the world, and the game rewards thorough exploration of its biomes.
4. **Spend upgrades deliberately.** Attribute and skill upgrades matter — tune your character toward the fight ahead.
5. **Don't fear death.** Dying is part of the Soulslike loop: learn the pattern and try again.

## What to Expect Next

- **Region by region**, each biome is ruled by a ferocious **Alpha predator** that guards the path forward — for example, a T-Rex that wields a smaller dinosaur as a weapon, and tyrannosaurs armed with battleaxes and halberds. See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).
- **The finale** sets up the game's signature moment: preparing to fight the meteor that caused the dinosaurs' extinction.
- After the final boss, **Boss Rush Mode** unlocks — a back-to-back gauntlet of boss fights.

## Known Issues & Reception (Honest Note)

Dinoblade launched at **89% Very Positive** on Steam (~400 reviews). Players praise the parry combat — but launch reviews also reported **bugs, performance problems, imprecise hitboxes, janky UI, and inconsistent hit registration**. If a fight feels unfair, it may be a technical issue rather than a design one; report bugs to Team Spino via the Steam Community hub.
