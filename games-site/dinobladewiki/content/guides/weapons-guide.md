# Dinoblade Weapons & Builds Guide — The Great Sword, Playstyles & Tier Honesty

Dinoblade's weapons are held in your dinosaur's jaws — you play a **young Spinosaurus biting down on a colossal Great Sword**. That single fact defines the game's combat identity, and it is the one confirmed player weapon in the game.

**Honesty note:** no official weapon list with stats has been published as of August 2026. Any guide printing base damage numbers, scaling grades, or a "six weapon types" table for Dinoblade is unverified. This guide tracks what official material and verified coverage confirm — and stays honest about what is undocumented.

## The Confirmed Weapon: The Great Sword

- **Confirmed:** your signature weapon — a colossal Great Sword gripped in your Spinosaurus's jaws. It is the centerpiece of the game's official description, marketing, and every walkthrough.
- **Playstyle:** heavy, wide-arc swings built around the game's parry-and-posture loop — light and heavy attacks, combos, and the timing game that the combat system is known for.
- **Moveset details:** light and heavy attack combos are confirmed; exact frame data, stamina costs, and reach values are undocumented.

## Legendary Weapons (Confirmed to Exist)

Official material states you can **"hunt down legendary weapons"** hidden in the world that **change how your Spinosaurus plays**. That confirms:

- Legendary weapons exist and are hidden in the world.
- They meaningfully change playstyle.
- Their **names, locations, and effects are undocumented** as of August 2026.

Any guide naming specific legendary weapons ("Fossil Greatsword," "Volcanic Hammer," and similar) with stats or locations is **unverified fan content** — see the [Endings & Secrets Guide](/guides/endings-guide) for what is confirmed.

## Build Direction (What's Confirmed)

There is **no published stat-point system** with named attributes and soft caps. What the official material and coverage confirm about progression:

- **Attribute and skill upgrades** — every victory unlocks strength; you build your own combat style.
- **Soul Arts** — special attacks that add distinct playstyle options (see the [Soul Arts Guide](/guides/soul-arts-guide)).
- **Summons** — defeated bosses fight alongside you as AI allies.
- **Legendary weapons** — playstyle-changing finds hidden in the world.

Any guide printing "Strength 30 / Dexterity 45" tables, soft caps, or per-stat breakpoints for Dinoblade is working from an unverified source. Build "spreads" are not how the game's confirmed progression works.

## Playstyle Frameworks (Advice, Not Facts)

With a single confirmed weapon, playstyle differences come from the systems *around* it:

### The Aggressive Parry-User

- **Core loop:** parry relentlessly to build enemy posture, break guards, and land counterattacks.
- **Fits:** players who enjoy Sekiro-style deflection — the game's posture system is directly comparable.

### The Patient Defensive Player

- **Core loop:** block and dodge first to learn attack patterns, then parry the safe windows.
- **Fits:** players who prefer learning fights before committing; a strong way to handle the game's imprecise-hitbox launch issues.

### The Spacing Player

- **Core loop:** control distance with positioning and punish whiffed attacks from reach-weapon enemies.
- **Fits:** players who like fighting tyrannosaurs with battleaxes and halberds on their own terms.

## An Honest Tier List

Tier lists for Dinoblade are mostly **opinion** — the verified weapon set is small, and exact values are undocumented. Here is the honest version:

| Tier | Weapon | Status |
|------|--------|--------|
| **S** | **The Great Sword** | The confirmed primary — a colossal blade held in your Spinosaurus's jaws. |
| **?** | **Legendary weapons** | Confirmed to exist and change playstyle — everything about them is TBD. |
| **?** | **Enemy weapons** (battleaxes, halberds, swords, scythes) | Confirmed in rival dinosaurs' hands — no verified source documents them as player weapons. |

**The real "tier list" is the playstyle list above.** In a game whose confirmed progression is attribute/skill upgrades plus Soul Arts, "best weapon" is less meaningful than "best loop for you."

## Weapon-Specific Practice

1. **Great Sword:** practice the parry → posture-break → counterattack rhythm until it is reflexive — every boss fight in the game runs through it.
2. **Vs. reach weapons:** tyrannosaurs with halberds and battleaxes out-range you in neutral; use dodges and spacing instead of trading hits.
3. **Hunt legendary weapons:** exploration is a confirmed part of progression — sweep each biome thoroughly.
