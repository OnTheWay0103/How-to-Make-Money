# Dinoblade Speedrun Guide — Routing, Boss Timing & Pace

Dinoblade is a strong speedrun game: a short, linear campaign (three parts, three bosses), forgiving death (no currency drop), and a combat system where skill, not gear, determines clear time. This guide covers the route skeleton, per-boss timing, and honest notes on record times.

**Honesty note:** Dinoblade's speedrun community is young (the game launched July 23, 2026). Any specific record times you see online — "22-minute world record" and similar — are community-sourced, change constantly, and should not be treated as official. This guide covers *how* to get fast, not what the fastest is.

## The Route Skeleton (Confirmed Structure)

The campaign is strictly linear, which makes routing simple:

1. **Part 1 — the cavern:** tutorial; the **flaming sword** pickup at the start; end with **Kira the Exile**.
2. **Part 2 — the northern paths:** waterfalls and the giant skull landmark; end with **Axe**.
3. **Part 3 — the mountain caves:** waves of hammer-wielding dinosaurs; end with **Kasei the Tyrant**.
4. **Post-game:** **Boss Rush** unlocks.

The path forward only opens by **absorbing each boss's soul** — there are no sequence breaks documented, so the route is: fight every boss, as fast as possible.

## Speedrun Categories

| Category | Objective | Route Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Any%** | Beat Kasei the Tyrant as fast as possible | Skip optional SP pickups, fight all three bosses |
| **Boss Rush** | Clear the Boss Rush gauntlet fast | No travel, pure boss execution |
| **No-hit** | Any% with zero damage taken | Parry routing perfection (see the [Challenge Runs Guide](/guides/challenge-runs)) |

## Per-Boss Routing

### Boss 1: Kira the Exile

- **Where the time is:** the fight is telegraphed and forgiving — a clean run here banks momentum.
- **Route:** learn his three-hit combo and charging attacks; fill his Resolve bar with parries; trigger the **Instinct Counter** on a full bar for the massive damage window.
- **Pace tip:** don't rush into phase-pushing — Kira punishes greed less than the later bosses, so play clean and fast rather than reckless.

### Boss 2: Axe

- **Where the time is:** his spinning combos are the fight's parry core.
- **Route:** parry the **final swing of his spinning combo** (the confirmed easy window), fill Resolve, land the finishing blow. Dodge his jumping attacks' landing AoE — standing in it costs you the clean run.
- **Pace tip:** the AoE on his jumps is the #1 run-killer; respect it and the fight is faster than any damage race.

### Boss 3: Kasei the Tyrant

- **Where the time is:** phase 2 (below one-third health) is where runs die.
- **Route (phase 1):** fire attacks, tail sweeps, charges, and slams — stay at his side, parry what's safe, dodge the rest.
- **Route (phase 2):** greatsword kit — dodge the unblockable gap-closers and sword throws, **parry his jump slashes**, punish the recovery.
- **Pace tip:** greed in phase 2 costs more time than it saves. Two clean parry-punish cycles beat five reckless trades.

## Time-Saving Principles (Not Route Breaks)

1. **Move during cutscene/transition moments** — reposition while the game transitions between boss phases.
2. **Skip optional SP pickups** unless they're directly on the path — every detour costs more than the pickup returns.
3. **Warm up before the run** — a 10-minute parry warm-up on raptors saves more time than any route optimization.
4. **Restart discipline** — if Kira takes more than a few attempts, reset the run; the early game is short.
5. **Boss Rush as practice** — run Boss Rush as a pure-boss rehearsal; it isolates execution from travel. See the [Boss Rush Strategy Guide](/guides/boss-rush-strategy).

## Practice Drills

1. **Kira drill:** fight him with parry-only routing — no light attacks until his Resolve bar is full. This teaches the Instinct Counter timing that carries the whole run.
2. **Axe spin drill:** block one full spinning combo, then parry only the final swing until it's automatic.
3. **Kasei phase 2 drill:** focus only on phase 2 — dodge unblockables, parry jump slashes — and restart the fight when you die.
4. **Boss Rush rehearsal:** chain all three fights back-to-back to build the stamina management and healing discipline the gauntlet demands.
