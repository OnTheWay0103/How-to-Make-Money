# Probably Stolen Starting Scenarios: All 9 Explained

> Last verified: October 28, 2026 · Compiled from official 1.0 material published before the 14:00 CET unlock

Probably Stolen ships nine starting scenarios in 1.0, and three more arrive after launch. Each one changes what you open with and which constraint you spend the run solving. You pick a scenario first, then one of four difficulties. This page lists every official brief verbatim, then says what each start means in practice.

## How the selection screen works

The 1.0 announcement is explicit about the order: "After picking a starting scenario, you can choose between 4 difficulty options." Both are set before the shop opens. The scenario is also the closest thing the game documents to a character choice: no classes or character builds are announced.

Two details are easy to miss. Rock Bottom is a scenario whose own description is "Maximum difficulty", so one scenario is already a hard mode. Separately, patch notes name Hard Mode as a condition that changes an ending rule, and whether Hard Mode is one of the four options is [Unconfirmed].

What the screen does not tell you is which scenario earns fastest. No per-scenario income data, starting cash figure or buy-out time has been published by the developer, so every "best scenario" claim online at this point is either a demo impression or a guess.

## All nine launch scenarios

These briefs are quoted from the official wishlist announcement, published 2026-10-27.

| Scenario | Official brief | Pick it if |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | "Generalist. Best for beginners." | You want the developers' own recommended first run |
| Water Merchant | "Test, purify, re-label or don't. Liability is yours." | You want the appraisal and liability loop as your core |
| Fence | "Stolen goods." | You want the contraband route stated plainly |
| Rock Bottom | "You start with nothing. Maximum difficulty." | You know the systems and want the hardest economy |
| Hydroponic Farmer | "Grow crops in small spaces. Finish with genetically modified breeds people pay a fortune for." | You want a production chain inside a small footprint |
| Gunsmith | "Weapons and mods. High margins, security inspections." | You want high margin and accept inspection pressure |
| Chemist | "Profitable chemicals, closely guarded recipes, and customers who come back." | You want recipes, repeat customers and a supply bottleneck |
| Rat Wrangler | "Can you grow a healthy rat?" | You want the strangest brief in the list |
| Aquafarmer | "Breed fish. Manage their tanks and water quality." | You want animal husbandry and water chemistry |

The Apprentice brief has a longer official version: "Best for beginners. Be a generalist. Upgrade as you go. Learn the game at your own pace." It is the only scenario the developers rank against the others, and they rank it as easiest to learn, not most profitable.

## Rat Wrangler, in detail

Rat Wrangler carries its own search demand and almost no documentation. Patch 48.3.12 confirms that the scenario, previously secret, "is now visible alongside the other starting scenarios" — so for a long stretch of the playtest it existed but could not be selected from the menu.

The official brief, in full, is a question: "Can you grow a healthy 2x3 rat?" The 1.0 announcement uses the same line. The "2x3" is the only measurement attached to any scenario in any official text, and what it measures is [Unconfirmed]. It could describe a pen, a cage footprint or the animal itself; nobody at the studio has explained it.

What that means for a first run: this is a husbandry route, and the developer is signalling that the goal is a healthy animal rather than a profitable one. Whether it uses the same growth, feeding and quality systems as the Aquafarmer is [Unconfirmed]. If you want a documented production chain, start with Aquafarmer instead.

## Aquafarmer, Water Merchant and Rock Bottom

Three scenarios have more official detail than the rest, because each got its own announcement when it was added.

**Aquafarmer** is the best documented. You "begin with a basic water circulation system, a stock of tilapia, and a supply of fish food." Tilapia comes first, then "more valuable species, such as eels and sturgeons", each with its own habitat requirements. That means managing water quality, filtration and the energy cell. Fish may reproduce by laying and fertilising roe; eggs hatch into fry and grow into mature fish. A knife handles slaughtering and butchering, some fish give resources beyond meat, and Surgery Tools on sturgeons harvest caviar.

**Water Merchant** arrived with the September demo and is defined by one sentence: testing and re-labelling are optional, and the liability is not.

**Rock Bottom** was added in August as "hardcore mode, you start with nothing" and is labelled "Maximum difficulty" in the 1.0 list. It is the only scenario that is also a difficulty statement.

## The three post-launch scenarios

The 1.0 announcement lists three scenarios under a post-launch heading. They are not selectable at launch, and any guide that treats them as live is describing a future build.

| Scenario | Official note | Status at 1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Organ Trader | "For the Butcher fans" | Post-launch, not selectable |
| Junker | Listed among post-launch scenarios only | Post-launch, not selectable |
| Street Food Vendor | Listed among post-launch scenarios only | Post-launch, not selectable |

No release window, price or mechanic detail has been published for any of the three. Two of them carry no description at all beyond their names, so anything you read about Junker or Street Food Vendor mechanics is inference.

## Which scenario fits which player

This table is editorial. It maps the official briefs onto play styles, not onto measured outcomes.

| If you are | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New to shop sims | Apprentice | The only scenario the developers recommend for learning |
| New to the genre but confident | Water Merchant | One clear system, one clear risk |
| A production-chain player | Hydroponic Farmer or Chemist | Both briefs describe a chain with a bottleneck |
| A risk player | Gunsmith | High margins plus security inspections |
| A completionist | Aquafarmer | The most documented scenario, with breeding, water quality and caviar |
| A veteran who wants the wall | Rock Bottom | "You start with nothing. Maximum difficulty" |

One rule of thumb from the briefs: most scenarios pair a reward with a cost. Gunsmith pairs margins with inspections, Chemist pairs profit with guarded recipes, Hydroponic Farmer pairs valuable breeds with small spaces. Choose the constraint you find interesting, not the reward.

## Demo saves, and what carries over

Your demo save is compatible. The launch announcement also says the team "greatly recommend starting a new game to avoid potential bugs", and that the demo is removed once 1.0 is live. Demo mods will likely not work with the full game.

A new game also means a fresh scenario selection, which is the argument for starting over: the nine-scenario list is new content.

## Starting scenario questions

### How many scenarios are in 1.0?

Nine: Apprentice, Water Merchant, Fence, Rock Bottom, Hydroponic Farmer, Gunsmith, Chemist, Rat Wrangler and Aquafarmer. Organ Trader, Junker and Street Food Vendor arrive post-launch.

### Is Rat Wrangler a hidden scenario?

It was. Patch 48.3.12 made it visible alongside the others. At 1.0 it is a normal menu choice.

### What is the best starting scenario for money?

Nobody has published per-scenario income, so there is no sourced answer. The only official ranking is Apprentice as best for beginners, which is a learning recommendation rather than a profit claim.

### Do scenarios change the difficulty?

Rock Bottom is explicitly "Maximum difficulty" as a scenario, so at least one does. Whether the other eight vary in difficulty, and how that interacts with the four difficulty options, is [Unconfirmed].

### Can I change scenario mid-run?

[Unconfirmed]. No official statement covers switching, restarting with a new scenario or carrying progress between scenarios.

## The short version

Take Apprentice to learn, Water Merchant for one clean risk, Aquafarmer for the most documented systems, and Rock Bottom for the wall. Rat Wrangler is the strange one, and its brief — a healthy 2x3 rat — is the only detail the developer has given about it.
