# Probably Stolen Beginner Guide: First Night and Money

> Last verified: October 28, 2026 · Compiled from official 1.0 material published before the 14:00 CET unlock

Probably Stolen is a shopkeeping sim, not a survival game: your first night is about stock, prices and closing time. The loop is to pick a scenario, learn what customers accept, sell only what you can stand behind, then put leftover stock to work overnight. Here is the order that keeps rent from deciding for you.

## What the game asks of you

> "Earn enough to buy out your shop before rising rent forces your hand."

That line from the store description is the design brief. The 1.0 "Game Ending" is the buy-out itself: you "purchase and own the store", and finishing it shows THE STATS — most profitable sale, days survived, total worth, perks. Money and days are the scoreboard.

Three systems follow, each with a beginner trap:

- **Stock has an origin problem.** "Goods of questionable origin" sit beside crafting and trading, so some inventory can be verified and some is contraband.
- **Reputation gates access.** Security and Black Market reputation decide which customers and services you keep.
- **Time runs on days.** The Toolbox appears from Dr. Jackson "after day 14", so the day count is the real clock.

## Your first night, in order

No official launch-day walkthrough exists as of 2026-10-28, so this ordering is ours. Each step removes a problem the next one would hit.

| Step | What to do | Why it belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick a scenario, then a difficulty | Apprentice is labelled "Best for beginners. Be a generalist" |
| 2 | Walk the shop before buying anything | Display case, smuggler bay and furnace carry different risks |
| 3 | Check what each customer tier accepts | Premium Spring Water is refused by non-Upper Level customers and tourists |
| 4 | Price for those customers, then haggle | Bargaining affects customer budgets, so it is a pricing tool |
| 5 | Close up and let stock sell overnight | Vending Machines "sell goods overnight after your close shop" |

Two caveats. Step 5 assumes Vending Machines exist on day one, and no announcement says when they unlock, so the timing is [Unconfirmed]. Starting stock is never quantified either, so "buy this item first" advice is guesswork.

## The rent loop, and what has not been published

Developer site copy describes rent and the station's cut both climbing over time: a fixed cost and a percentage cost, pressing from two directions. Confirmed is the shape of the loop, never the numbers.

| Question | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rent rises over time | Confirmed | Store description; developer site |
| Buying out the shop ends the run | Confirmed | 1.0 Game Ending announcement |
| Losing the shop is possible | Implied, mechanics [Unconfirmed] | "before rising rent forces your hand" |
| Starting rent, increase rate, grace period | [Unconfirmed] | Never published |
| Buy-out price and any payment terms | [Unconfirmed] | Never published |

So you cannot plan against a number, only a direction: treat each day's profit as paying for the next day's rent. One confirmed edge case is that at -40 reputation with the Black Market you keep the janitorial service, but its cost is doubled, so reputation loss taxes you instead of cutting you off.

## Appraisal: what you can verify

"Goods of questionable origin" is not decoration. The Water Merchant description is blunt: "Test, purify, re-label or don't. Liability is yours." That is the appraisal system in miniature — the game offers the choice to skip verification and keeps the consequence.

**The display case is a window with a paper trail.** Patch notes describe T3 buyers from the display case scaling their budget, so the case is where higher-tier money looks. It is also where contraband gets noticed: showing contraband produces an inspection, and passing that inspection no longer grants Security reputation — a change made to stop players farming reputation by displaying contraband and then hiding it.

**The smuggler bay is a hiding place, not a licence.** Contraband goes there to pass inspection, and using it costs the reputation you would otherwise earn. Hiding is safe, but not free.

**Verification has no published checklist.** No official list of inspection steps, thresholds or penalties for mislabelled goods exists as of 2026-10-28, so treat appraisal as [Unconfirmed] in detail.

## Four ways to make money

| Channel | What is confirmed | Best for | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counter sales | Customer tiers reject specific goods; Chemist customers "come back" | Verified stock you can price honestly | Liability on mislabelled goods |
| Black Market | The Thief's stock "scales with Black Market reputation" | High-margin goods you cannot sell openly | Inspections, reputation loss |
| Vending Machines | "Sell goods overnight after your close shop to generate more profit" | Surplus you cannot clear in a day | Limits and power draw [Unconfirmed] |
| Bargaining | "Adjusted how bargaining affects customer budgets" | Big-ticket items and T3 buyers | Patch changed the effect, not the values |

Counter sales are your baseline, because every other channel depends on reputation or an unlock. The Black Market is a margin tool, not an escape hatch: the Thief's stock improving with reputation means the channel rewards use, and it is also the channel that gets you inspected. Outside those four, reporting a criminal customer pays a credit bonus — Model Citizen raised it from 20% to 50%.

Bargaining deserves a note, because beginners skip it. Patch notes say bargaining affects customer budgets — budgets, not prices — which is why it pairs with the display case and its budget-scaling T3 buyers.

## Tools and the numbers that changed

Patch 049 is the most useful beginner document tinyBuild has published, because it lists before-and-after values.

| Change | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory windows open at once | 10 | 25 |
| Ingot size upgrade | No durability component | +20 maximum and +20 current durability |
| Repairing with scrap | Flat 50 points | 50% of maximum durability |
| Toolbox source | Not stated | Dr. Jackson, after day 14 |
| Model Citizen credit bonus | 20% | 50% |
| Intimidating evidence reduction | 25%, starting cost 3 | 33%, starting cost 2 |
| Addiction perks trading penalty | 5% | 8% |

Read the first three rows together. The inventory cap is quality of life, but ingot and repair interact: if scrap repair returns half of maximum durability, every point of maximum durability you buy is worth half a repair, repeatedly. Upgrading ingot size before repairing is cheaper than the reverse. The notes give values, not costs, so do not over-read this into a formula.

## Upgrade priority for the first week

This ordering is editorial; it follows the documented numbers, not play data.

1. **Durability on the tool you use daily.** With scrap repair returning 50% of maximum durability, maximum durability multiplies every repair instead of buffering one.
2. **Perks that pay per transaction.** Intimidating costs 2 starting points instead of 3 and cuts evidence generation by 33%; Model Citizen at 50% turns criminal customers into a credit trickle.
3. **The Toolbox, the day it unlocks.** Plan cash for day 14 rather than discovering it empty-handed.
4. **Bargaining practice before big stock.** Learn the dialogue on low-value stock, so the habit is in place before the display case fills.
5. **Vending Machines as soon as they are offered.** Overnight selling is the only confirmed way to earn while closed.

At the bottom: extra product lines. Most scenario briefs pair a reward with a constraint — inspections for the Gunsmith, guarded recipes for the Chemist, habitat requirements for the Aquafarmer. A second line adds a constraint before it adds income.

## Beginner questions

### Is Apprentice really the easiest start?

The developers say so: "Best for beginners. Be a generalist. Upgrade as you go." Whether it reaches a buy-out fastest is a different question, and no official material answers it.

### How much is rent, and when does it rise?

[Unconfirmed]. Rent rises and the station takes a cut, and no starting figure, increase rate or grace period has been published. Anyone quoting a number is quoting their own save.

### Do the four difficulties change the economy?

[Unconfirmed]. The 1.0 announcement confirms four options and nothing about what they modify. Patch notes mention Hard Mode in the context of one ending condition, so at least one difficulty is named, but its effects are unpublished.

### Does my demo save carry over?

Yes, demo saves are compatible, though the launch announcement says "we greatly recommend starting a new game to avoid potential bugs". The demo is removed once 1.0 is out, and demo mods will likely not be compatible.

## The short version

Learn what each customer tier accepts, verify before you price, and sell safe stock at the counter. Spend profit on durability and the day-14 Toolbox before a second product line. Rent is the clock and the buy-out is the finish line; the rent and margin numbers in between are still [Unconfirmed].
