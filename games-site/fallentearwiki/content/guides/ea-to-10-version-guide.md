# Fallen Tear: The Ascension EA vs 1.0 Guide

**Fallen Tear: The Ascension** launched in Steam Early Access on **March 17, 2026** and leaves Early Access with its **1.0 launch on September 16, 2026** [Official]. The update **roughly doubles the game's content** [Official].

This guide is the complete version comparison — what the EA build offered, what 1.0 adds, and what changes for players.

## The EA Baseline

The Early Access build covered about **35% of the full game** and offered 15–20 hours of content [Official][Community]:

| Content | EA Build |
|---------|----------|
| **Regions** | 10 interconnected major regions |
| **Main bosses** | 6 |
| **Optional bosses** | 3 |
| **Fated Bonds** | 10 |
| **Ascensions** | 3 |
| **Hunter Skills** | 3 |
| **Mastery Skills** | 16 (Tiers I–II) |
| **Elemental Imbuements** | 0 |
| **Playtime** | ~15–20 hours |

[Source: Official]

## What 1.0 Adds

The 1.0 update adds the following on top of the EA content [Official]:

| Content | 1.0 Addition | 1.0 Total |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| **Regions** | +10 major regions | ~20 |
| **Main bosses** | +5 | 11 |
| **Optional bosses** | +19 | 22 |
| **Fated Bonds** | +11 | ~21 |
| **Ascensions** | +3 | (see note) |
| **Hunter Skills** | +6 | (see note) |
| **Mastery Skills** | +16 (Tiers III–IV) | (see note) |
| **Elemental Imbuements** | +4 | 4 |
| **Playtime** | — | 35–40h story / 80h+ completion |

**Note on totals:** The press release states what 1.0 *adds* (e.g., "3 new Ascensions"), and the EA build already had 3 Ascensions and 16 Mastery Skills. Simple addition suggests 6 Ascensions, 9 Hunter Skills, and 32 Mastery Skills at 1.0 — but the press release does not state final totals, so treat those as inferred [Editorial].

## Multiple Endings & The Full Story

The EA build stopped mid-story — reviews noted the ending was missing [Community]. The 1.0 release completes the narrative with **multiple possible endings shaped by player choices and bonds** [Official]. This is the single biggest change for story-focused players: the EA act is now a complete campaign.

## Does Progress Carry Over?

**No official statement had been published as of August 31, 2026** on whether Early Access saves, upgrades, or bonds carry into 1.0 [Unconfirmed].

What to do:

- **Don't assume.** If you have a strong EA save you want to keep, back it up (Steam Cloud is on for the game, but the carry-over question is separate) [Editorial].
- **Treat EA as practice.** Even if progress resets, the EA build is a perfect training ground for the full campaign.
- **Watch the official announcements** near September 16 for a definitive answer [Editorial].

## Price Changes at 1.0

- **EA price:** $19.99
- **1.0 price:** $24.99 / €24.99 / £21.99, with a **10% launch discount for the first 14 days** [Official].

See the [Price & Platforms Guide](/guides/price-platforms) for full purchasing details.

## How to Approach the Transition

**For EA players:**
1. Decide what to finish before launch — sidequests, bond collections, and boss clears you want "done" in the EA state.
2. Expect the map to roughly double. Revisit regions you thought were complete; new paths open with new abilities [Official].
3. Prepare for the expanded skill system — 3 new Ascensions, 6 Hunter Skills, 4 Imbuements, and 16 Mastery Skills change the upgrade meta [Official].

**For new players at 1.0:**
1. Start with the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide).
2. Know you're buying the complete 35–40 hour game, not an EA slice.
3. The full 33-boss roster and 20-region map is the real deal [Official].

## What Doesn't Change at 1.0

Not everything is new at 1.0. The confirmed constants [Official]:

- **The core combat loop.** Slash, parry, dodge, and Overgrowth remain the foundation [Official].
- **The Fated Bonds recruitment model.** Allies with combat and traversal abilities stay the heart of the game [Official].
- **The Temple of Oras hub.** It was in EA and remains the hub at 1.0 [Official].
- **The world of Raoah.** The same hand-drawn world, now roughly doubled in scope [Official].

The 1.0 update is an **expansion of the same game**, not a redesign — EA skills and combat habits transfer directly [Editorial].

## What 1.0 Changes for Story Players

For players who bought EA for the story, 1.0 is the payoff:

- **The EA build had no ending** — reviews noted it stopped mid-story [Community].
- **1.0 completes the campaign** with multiple endings shaped by choices and bonds [Official].
- **The four corrupted gods** (Haslan/Ymara/Siv/Evan) get their full arcs [Community].

If you were waiting for "the real game," 1.0 is it [Editorial].

## What 1.0 Changes for Completionists

The completion surface roughly doubles [Official]:

- **Map:** 10 → ~20 regions
- **Main bosses:** 6 → 11
- **Optional bosses:** 3 → 22
- **Fated Bonds:** 10 → ~21
- **Skill tiers:** I–II → I–IV

A player who 100%'d the EA build at 15–20 hours faces a much larger 80+ hour completion target at 1.0 [Official].

This guide will be updated with the confirmed 1.0 totals, save carry-over confirmation, and any launch-window patches. Until September 16, the EA baseline and announced additions above are what's verified; totals and carry-over are [Unconfirmed].
