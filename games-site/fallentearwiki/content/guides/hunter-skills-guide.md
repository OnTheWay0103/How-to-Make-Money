# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Hunter Skills & Elemental Imbuements Guide

Fallen Tear: The Ascension's combat power comes from **Hunter Skills** (active combat abilities) and — new at 1.0 — **Elemental Imbuements** [Official]. Early Access shipped with **3 Hunter Skills** and no Imbuements; the 1.0 update on September 16, 2026 adds **6 more Hunter Skills** and **4 Elemental Imbuements** [Official].

Because individual skill names and effects have not been published, this guide covers the confirmed structure and how to think about building a combat loadout [Editorial].

## What Are Hunter Skills?

Hunter Skills are Hira's **active combat abilities** — the triggered skills that sit on top of the base Korr Blade attacks [Official]. The Steam page's framing of the **Ascension and Hunter skill trees** puts them at the core of how Hira fights [Official].

Confirmed structure:

- **3 Hunter Skills in Early Access.** These are live and testable now — the skills you unlock early in the skill tree.
- **6 more at 1.0.** The 1.0 update expands the Hunter tree with new active abilities [Official].

Think of Hunter Skills as your combat "buttons": a gap-closer, a heavy strike, a defensive counter — the exact roster is [Unconfirmed].

## What Are Elemental Imbuements?

**Elemental Imbuements are brand new at 1.0** — Early Access had none, and the press release announces **4 added** at launch [Official]. There is no live gameplay to confirm how they work.

Based on the confirmed worldbuilding, the likely shape [Editorial]:

| Imbuement (inferred) | Element | God |
|----------------------|---------|-----|
| Earth | Earth | Haslan [Community] |
| Fire | Fire | Ymara [Community] |
| Wind | Wind | Siv [Community] |
| Water | Water | Evan [Community] |

The four gods of Raoah are **Haslan** (Earth), **Ymara** (Fire), **Siv** (Wind), and **Evan** (Water) [Community]. Four Imbuements and four elemental gods is a strong signal that Imbuements let you **imbue the Korr Blade with an element** for weakness-based damage [Editorial]. This is inference — [Unconfirmed].

## Building a Hunter Skill Loadout (Pre-Release)

Until skills are documented, use this framework [Editorial]:

### 1. One Gap-Closer, One Burst, One Defensive
A well-rounded Hunter loadout covers three jobs:
- **Gap-closer** — close distance to bosses and enemies.
- **Burst** — a damage skill for punish windows after parries.
- **Defensive** — a counter or escape for when dodge isn't enough.

### 2. Match Imbuements to the Zone
At 1.0, if an area is fire-themed, a water Imbuement is likely the counter [Editorial]. Elemental coverage turns hard regions into easy ones.

### 3. Upgrade in Tiers
Hunter Skills sit in the Ascension skill tree [Official]. Spend on one skill to its tier unlock before spreading points — a single upgraded skill beats three weak ones [Editorial].

## Hunter Skills vs Mastery Skills

- **Hunter Skills** = active abilities you trigger in combat [Official].
- **Mastery Skills** = passive stat and capability upgrades [Official].
- **Ascensions** = the structural progression layer [Official].

A good build balances all three: **Hunter Skills for options, Mastery Skills for stats, Ascensions for structure** [Editorial].

## Pre-Release Expectations

- **EA players:** the current 3 Hunter Skills are testable; use them to learn which playstyle fits (aggressive, defensive, or utility).
- **1.0 players:** expect 9 total Hunter Skills (3 EA + 6 new — arithmetic from the confirmed counts) and 4 Imbuements to experiment with [Editorial].
- **Build guides:** nothing is finalized until launch — don't over-commit to a "meta" that may not exist [Editorial].

## How to Test the 3 EA Hunter Skills Now

Early Access is live, and its **3 Hunter Skills** are the one part of this system you can test before 1.0 [Official]. To get the most out of them:

- **Unlock all three** in the EA skill tree and try each in a variety of fights — mobs, a miniboss, and a main boss.
- **Note the cooldown rhythm.** Active skills usually have a cooldown or resource cost; learn the cadence now so 1.0 skills feel familiar [Editorial].
- **Pair them with parry windows.** The strongest use of a burst skill is inside a parry punish — practice that timing with the EA kit [Editorial].

This EA practice directly transfers: the 1.0 tree adds **6 more Hunter Skills** on the same framework [Official].

## Synergy With Elemental Imbuements

At 1.0, Hunter Skills and Elemental Imbuements will likely interact [Editorial]:

- **Imbuements change your basic attacks** (fire/earth/wind/water on the Korr Blade), while **Hunter Skills stay active abilities** [Editorial].
- A burst Hunter Skill **after an Imbuement** could deliver elemental damage at range or in AoE — the classic "imbue then unload" loop [Editorial].
- Bosses tied to a god (Haslan/Ymara/Siv/Evan) likely resist their own element, so **match the Imbuement to the zone, not the god** [Editorial].

This is inference from the confirmed systems — [Unconfirmed].

## Common Build Mistakes to Avoid

Pre-release, the frequent errors are [Editorial]:

1. **Ignoring the Ascension tree.** Hunter Skills sit under Ascensions [Official]; skipping the structural layer starves your active skills.
2. **Over-specializing one element.** With four Imbuements and four gods, a one-trick element leaves you weak in mismatched zones [Editorial].
3. **Neglecting Mastery Skills.** Stats win long fights; active skills alone cannot carry a 35-hour campaign [Official].
