# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Game Length Guide

**Fallen Tear: The Ascension** is a large Metroidvania. The official 1.0 press release confirms the game offers **roughly 35–40 hours of story content** and **80+ hours for completion-focused players** [Official]. The Early Access build was about **35% of the game** at 15–20 hours [Official].

Here is the full picture of how long the game takes.

## The Confirmed Numbers

| Playstyle | Playtime | Source |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Main story** | 35–40 hours | Official 1.0 press release |
| **Completion / 100%** | 80+ hours | Official 1.0 press release |
| **Early Access build** | 15–20 hours (~35% of game) | Official + community |
| **A single region** | ~1–3 hours each (roughly 20 regions) | Editorial estimate |

The 35–40 hour story figure assumes a focused path through the main campaign. The 80+ hour completion figure assumes **everything**: all 20 regions, all 33 bosses (11 main + 22 optional), all 21 Fated Bonds, all bond sidequests, all skill trees, and multiple endings [Official].

## Why It's This Long

Fallen Tear is a **Metroidvania** (exploration and backtracking) plus a **JRPG-style party system** (Fated Bonds) [Official]. That combination adds time two ways:

1. **Backtracking.** Traversal abilities open old regions; re-exploring them is built into the loop [Official].
2. **Bond sidequests and story.** Every Fated Bond carries sidequest and story content [Official]. Completionists spend hours on bond content alone.

## How Long Is the Early Access Build?

Early Access covered roughly **35% of the full game** and took **15–20 hours** [Official][Community]. If you played EA, you have seen the opening act — the 1.0 map roughly doubles, and the story completes with multiple endings [Official].

## What Adds Time at 100%?

The 80+ hour completion run is mostly the optional content [Official]:

- **19 optional bosses** (1.0 adds 19 on top of the 3 EA optional bosses) — optional boss hunts are a major time sink.
- **10 new regions** at 1.0 — new map to fully explore.
- **11 new Fated Bonds** — new recruitment and sidequests.
- **Skill tree completion** — all Ascension, Hunter, Mastery, and Imbuement tiers.
- **Multiple endings** — replaying the final act for different outcomes.

## How to Budget Your Time

- **Story-first players:** expect ~35–40 hours. Follow the corruption trail (the four gods) and recruit bonds as you pass them [Editorial].
- **Completionists:** plan for 80+ hours. Add a full-bond sweep and optional-boss circuit after the main campaign [Editorial].
- **EA veterans:** your 15–20 hour EA run counts as roughly the first third; the remaining 20+ hours of story and 60+ hours of completion content are new at 1.0 [Official].

## How the Length Breaks Down

A rough estimate of where your hours go across the 1.0 campaign [Editorial]:

| Activity | Estimated Hours |
|----------|----------------|
| Main story (regions + main bosses) | 25–30 |
| Fated Bond recruitment and sidequests | 8–12 |
| Optional bosses (22 at 1.0) | 6–10 |
| Exploration, backtracking, secrets | 8–15 |
| Multiple-endings replays | +2–5 |

These are editorial estimates based on the official total ranges — not verified numbers [Editorial]. The point is that the 80+ hour completion figure is mostly **optional content** (bonds, optional bosses, endings), not a padded main story.

## Comparing to Similar Games

To calibrate expectations, compare Fallen Tear to other hand-drawn Metroidvanias:

- **Hollow Knight**: ~25–35 hours main, ~50–60 hours completion. Fallen Tear's confirmed 35–40 / 80+ is **larger** [Editorial].
- **Ori and the Will of the Wisps**: ~12–15 hours main, ~25 completion. Fallen Tear is roughly triple [Editorial].
- **Dead Cells / roguelite Metroidvanias**: not directly comparable — Fallen Tear is a fixed campaign, not a run-based game [Editorial].

If the 35–40 hour figure holds, Fallen Tear is one of the **longest hand-drawn Metroidvanias in recent memory** — a real selling point for the genre [Editorial].

## Factors That Change Your Playtime

- **Skill level.** Parry-focused combat means a player comfortable with timing will clear bosses faster than one who relies on dodging and retries [Editorial].
- **Exploration habits.** Completionists who sweep every room will naturally drift toward the 80+ figure; focused players stay near 35–40 [Editorial].
- **Bond prioritization.** Recruiting and completing all Fated Bonds adds hours of sidequest content — skipping them keeps you in the lower range [Official].
- **Version.** EA players who completed the EA build have already spent 15–20 of their hours; the 1.0 content beyond that is the remaining 20+ story hours and 60+ completion hours [Official].

## How to Plan Your Playthrough

- **Short on time:** follow the corruption trail (the four gods), recruit bonds you pass, and skip optional bosses. Expect ~35–40 hours [Editorial].
- **Want the full experience:** after the main campaign, run a full-bond sweep and an optional-boss circuit, then replay the final act for alternate endings. Budget 80+ hours [Editorial].
- **Casual pace:** split the game into region-sized sessions — roughly one region per sitting at the start [Editorial].
