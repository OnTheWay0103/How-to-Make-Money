# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Progression & Ascension Guide

Progression in **Fallen Tear: The Ascension** is built around skill trees that make Hira stronger in four confirmed categories: **Ascensions, Hunter Skills, Mastery Skills, and Elemental Imbuements** [Official]. The Early Access build covered **Tiers I–II**; the 1.0 update on September 16, 2026 expands into **Tiers III–IV** [Official].

This guide explains the confirmed progression structure and how to think about upgrades ahead of 1.0.

## The Four Progression Systems

### 1. Ascensions
Ascensions are the meta-progression layer of Hira's skill trees [Official]. The Early Access build shipped with **3 Ascensions**, and the 1.0 update **adds 3 more** [Official].

What exactly an Ascension is — a tier reset, a new tree, or a class-like specialization — is not fully documented [Unconfirmed]. The safest read: Ascensions are the big structural upgrades that gate the rest of the skill tree [Editorial].

### 2. Hunter Skills
Hunter Skills are Hira's **active combat abilities** — the offensive skills you trigger in fights [Official]. Early Access had **3 Hunter Skills**; 1.0 adds **6 more** [Official].

These are the buttons you press in combat beyond the base Korr Blade attacks. See the [Hunter Skills Guide](/guides/hunter-skills-guide) for the full breakdown.

### 3. Mastery Skills
Mastery Skills are passive upgrades that improve Hira's stats and capabilities [Official]. The Early Access build shipped with **16 Mastery Skills** (Tiers I–II), and 1.0 **adds 16 more** across Tiers III–IV [Official].

Reviews describe improving Hira's stats via **Hira Skills and Mastery Skills** at the Temple of Oras [Community] — confirming that mastery upgrades are purchased/spent at the hub.

### 4. Elemental Imbuements
Elemental Imbuements are new in 1.0 — **4 added** at launch [Official]. Early Access had none, so there is no live information about how they work.

Based on the name and the game's elemental gods (Haslan/Earth, Ymara/Fire, Siv/Wind, Evan/Water) [Community], Imbuements likely let you imbue the Korr Blade with elements for weakness-based damage [Editorial]. This is inference — [Unconfirmed].

## The Tier Structure

The skill trees are organized into **Tiers I through IV** [Official]:

| Tier | Version | Content |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Tier I** | Early Access | Base Ascensions, first Hunter/Mastery skills |
| **Tier II** | Early Access | Expanded Mastery Skills (16 total in EA) |
| **Tier III** | 1.0 | New Ascensions, Hunter Skills, Imbuements |
| **Tier IV** | 1.0 | New Mastery Skills (16 added at 1.0) |

The progression pattern is: **earn resources → unlock skills → unlock higher tiers → repeat** [Editorial].

## Where Upgrades Happen

The **Temple of Oras** is the central hub where you "improve Hira's stats via Hira Skills and Mastery Skills" [Community]. This makes the Temple more than a cosmetic base — it is the **upgrade station** for the whole progression system [Official].

Expect a loop of: explore → gather resources → return to the Temple → spend on skills → head back out [Editorial]. See the [Temple of Oras Guide](/guides/temple-of-oras-guide) for details.

## Pre-Release Upgrade Priorities

Because individual skill names and effects are [Unconfirmed], this is a framework, not a build [Editorial]:

1. **Mobility skills first.** In a Metroidvania, movement upgrades (dash upgrades, double jump) open the map and accelerate everything else.
2. **One damage skill.** Pick a single Hunter Skill and upgrade it enough to clear bosses comfortably.
3. **Survivability second.** Mastery Skills that add health or recovery make long expeditions safer.
4. **Elemental Imbuements when bosses resist you.** At 1.0, if a boss shrugs off physical damage, an Imbuement is likely the answer [Editorial].

## Early Access vs 1.0 Skill Math

A quick reference for what changes at launch [Official]:

- **Ascensions:** 3 (EA) → 6 (1.0, +3)
- **Hunter Skills:** 3 (EA) → 9 (1.0, +6)
- **Mastery Skills:** 16 (EA) → 32 (1.0, +16)
- **Elemental Imbuements:** 0 (EA) → 4 (1.0, +4)

Note: these are the *announced additions*, not necessarily the final totals — the press release states what 1.0 *adds*, and totals may differ [Editorial].

## What Resets vs What Persists

A key progression question in any skill-tree game is what carries over if you respec or restart [Unconfirmed]. As of August 31, 2026, nothing official has been published about respecs or resets [Unconfirmed].

The safe assumptions [Editorial]:

- **Skill points spent are likely permanent** — standard for Metroidvania-style trees.
- **Respec options may or may not exist** — some games offer a paid or gated respec at the hub; Fallen Tear has not confirmed one [Unconfirmed].
- **Save carry-over from EA to 1.0** is separately unconfirmed [Unconfirmed] — see the [EA vs 1.0 Guide](/guides/ea-to-10-version-guide).

Until confirmed, **spend conservatively**: if respecs are absent, a wasted Ascension point is permanent [Editorial].

## A Skill-Spending Order That Ages Well

A conservative pre-release upgrade order [Editorial]:

1. **Mobility Mastery Skills first.** Dash/double-jump upgrades are never wasted — they speed every future hour [Community].
2. **One Hunter Skill to its tier.** Active damage that reliably clears bosses [Official].
3. **Survivability next.** Health and recovery Mastery Skills make the 35-hour campaign forgiving [Official].
4. **Elemental Imbuements when a boss resists you.** Buy the element that matches your current zone [Official].

This order keeps you flexible if the 1.0 skill names change the meta — movement and survivability are evergreen [Editorial].
