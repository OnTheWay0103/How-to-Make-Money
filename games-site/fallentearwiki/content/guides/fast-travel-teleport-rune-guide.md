# How Fast Travel Works in Fallen Tear

Fast travel in **Fallen Tear: The Ascension** changed twice in a week. Patch **1.0.2** on October 16 added a **Teleport Rune** that lets Hira warp from anywhere, along with better map indicators. Patch **1.0.3** on October 23 went further: **Korr Shards are no longer lost when Hira dies**, and the **maximum teleport cost was halved**.

If you played at launch, the traversal rules you remember are out of date. This guide covers how fast travel works now, what each patch changed, and how to plan routes so you are not burning shards on backtracking.

## The Short Version

- Fast travel is paid for with **Korr Shards**, the same currency dropped by enemies and found in the world [Community].
- Patch 1.0.2 added the **Teleport Rune**, which allows warping from anywhere rather than only from a fixed point [Official].
- Patch 1.0.3 **halved the maximum teleport cost** and removed the death penalty on Korr Shards [Official].
- Map pins now start at **40** and rise to **100** as Rins join, and rare hunt bosses have map markers [Official].
- The exact shard cost per teleport, and how you obtain the Teleport Rune, are **not stated in the patch summaries** [Unconfirmed].

## Fast Travel Basics

Fallen Tear is a Metroidvania with a large, interconnected map — roughly **20 major regions** at 1.0 [Official]. Backtracking is part of the design: new traversal abilities open routes you have already passed, and the game expects you to return.

Fast-travel points are tied to **Temples** [Community]. Finding a Temple gives you a node you can return to, and the Temple also hosts the bond-point upgrades used for movement abilities. The Cubed3 review notes that fast-travel locations double as the place where bond points are spent on upgrades such as double jump and dash.

The review is also blunt about the launch-state problem: there was a **lack of fast-travel points**, and in some biomes the points were **clustered too closely together**, leaving large areas without one. That is the complaint the October patches are responding to.

## What Patch 1.0.2 Added

The October 16 patch added three traversal and navigation changes [Official]:

### The Teleport Rune

The **Teleport Rune** lets you warp **from anywhere** [Official]. Before it, fast travel was tied to fixed points; the Rune removes the walk back to a Temple before you can leave.

The patch notes do not say how the Rune is obtained. It may be a story unlock, a vendor item, or a quest reward — that detail is **not in the patch summary** [Unconfirmed]. Check your key items after major story beats, and check vendors in the hub if you have not found it.

### Early quest map indicators

Early quests now have **map indicators** [Official]. This is a smaller change than the Rune but matters for new players: the first few hours no longer depend on remembering an NPC's directions.

### Rare hunt boss markers

Rare hunt bosses now appear on the map [Official]. If you are hunting optional bosses for rewards or completion, this removes a large amount of blind searching.

The same patch also changed **map pins**: they start at **40** and rise to **100** as Rins join [Official]. The practical effect is that you can mark more points of interest early, and your marking capacity grows with the story rather than being fixed.

## What Patch 1.0.3 Changed

The October 23 patch is the more important one for day-to-day travel [Official]:

### Korr Shards are no longer lost on death

Before 1.0.3, dying could cost you Korr Shards. That made every death a double penalty: lost progress and a currency setback that could leave you unable to fast travel back to the boss you were learning. The patch removes that loss.

This is a significant quality-of-life change. It means you can attempt a hard boss repeatedly without watching your travel budget drain. The [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) covers the 33 bosses; the death penalty change makes learning those fights much less punishing.

### Maximum teleport cost halved

The patch halves the **maximum teleport cost** [Official]. The notes do not state the old cap or the new cap in the summary, so the exact numbers are [Unconfirmed]. What matters is the direction: long-distance warps are cheaper at the top end, which makes cross-map travel more affordable.

The change does not make fast travel free. Korr Shards are still the currency, and you still need to earn them.

### More map markers in the early progression guide

The early progression guide was expanded with **more map markers** [Official]. Combined with the 1.0.2 quest indicators, the early game is now much more directed than it was at launch.

The patch also merged two achievements into **"Got the Chef"** and added **"Beyond the Fallen Tear"** [Official]. Neither changes traversal, but they are part of the same update.

## How to Plan Routes Around Raoah

[Editorial] The patches make fast travel cheaper and more flexible, but they do not remove the cost. A good route still saves shards and time.

### Bank a Temple before exploring a new biome

When you enter a new region, find the Temple first. It gives you a return node, a place to spend bond points, and a safe point to teleport back to if a boss or a traversal section goes wrong.

### Use map pins deliberately

With 40 pins at the start and up to 100 as Rins join, you have room to mark more than boss rooms. Mark:

- Temples and fast-travel nodes.
- Rare hunt boss locations.
- Ability-gated paths you cannot open yet.
- Resource clusters you want to revisit.
- Quest NPCs you will need to return to.

The [Map & Regions Guide](/guides/map-regions-guide) covers the region structure and traversal gates.

### Clear in loops, not lines

Fallen Tear rewards looping routes: enter a region, clear the side paths, hit the Temple, then warp out. Walking back through cleared rooms is the main time sink. If you are going to warp anyway, finish everything in the area first.

### Do not hoard shards for emergencies

Korr Shards are a travel currency, not a score. Spending them to reach a boss, a quest turn-in, or a rare hunt is usually better than walking for several minutes. The one exception is early game, when your income is low and every shard matters.

### Fast travel before a boss attempt

If a boss is far from your current position, warp to the nearest Temple before you engage. The walk back after a death is exactly what fast travel is for.

## Why Fast Travel Felt Bad at Launch

The Cubed3 review identifies the structural problem: fast-travel points were **not evenly distributed**. In some biomes they were clustered, leaving large stretches of map without a node. That makes backtracking a chore in exactly the places where Metroidvania design asks you to backtrack most.

The review also highlights the **Rin Map Tower** system: each of the 20-plus biomes has a tower that reveals the whole biome map once found. That is a separate navigation layer from fast travel — the tower tells you where to go, the Temple gets you there. If you are lost in a region, look for the tower before you look for a teleport node.

The October patches do not add new fast-travel points. They make the existing network cheaper and give you better map information. If a biome still has a sparse network, the fix is route planning rather than waiting for a patch [Editorial].

## What Is Still Unconfirmed

As of October 23, 2026, these details are **not confirmed** [Unconfirmed]:

- How the **Teleport Rune** is obtained.
- The exact **Korr Shard cost per teleport**, and the old and new maximum costs.
- Whether the Teleport Rune works in boss arenas, dungeons, or story-locked areas.
- Whether there is a fast-travel point in every region.
- Whether the death penalty removal applies to all difficulty modes.

The patch notes confirm the existence and direction of each change, but not the numbers. Do not plan a shard budget around a specific cost you saw in a pre-1.0.3 guide.

## FAQ

**Do I lose Korr Shards when I die now?**
No. Patch 1.0.3 removed the loss [Official].

**What does the Teleport Rune do?**
It lets you warp from anywhere, rather than only from a fixed fast-travel point [Official].

**Where do I get the Teleport Rune?**
Not confirmed in the patch notes [Unconfirmed]. Check story progress, key items, and hub vendors.

**Did the maximum teleport cost change?**
Yes — it was halved in 1.0.3 [Official]. The exact old and new caps are not stated in the patch summary.

**Do map pins increase?**
Yes. They start at 40 and rise to 100 as Rins join [Official].

**Are rare hunt bosses marked on the map now?**
Yes, since 1.0.2 [Official].

**Can I fast travel from inside a boss fight?**
Not confirmed [Unconfirmed]. Assume you cannot until tested, and plan your route so you do not need to.
