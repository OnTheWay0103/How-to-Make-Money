# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Early Game Walkthrough

This walkthrough covers the **opening arc** of Fallen Tear: The Ascension — the confirmed first steps in Raoah and a framework for routing the 20-region map. Because the full 1.0 launches September 16, 2026, this is a **pre-release framework** rather than a step-by-step route [Editorial]. Exact region order beyond the opening is [Unconfirmed].

## The Opening: What Happens First

The confirmed story frame: you play as **Hira**, a mysterious child raised by **Miah**, the healer who adopted him [Community]. The world of **Raoah** is being corrupted as its gods fall from grace, and Hira is fated to stand against them [Official].

The EA opening flow, confirmed by Early Access coverage:

1. **Start in the jungle-adjacent regions.** **Paupao Falls** — the confirmed jungle biome — is your first taste of Raoah's hand-drawn world [Community].
2. **Learn combat fundamentals.** Slash, parry, dodge — the early enemies are built to teach timing.
3. **Meet your first Fated Bonds.** Early recruitment opens the bond system and its traversal abilities.
4. **Reach the Temple of Oras.** The hub becomes your base for upgrades [Official].

## Suggested Early Route

A routing framework based on confirmed systems [Editorial]:

### Phase 1 — The Jungle (Hours 1–3)
- Explore the opening regions (Paupao Falls and neighbors).
- **Goal:** master parry timing, collect the first movement abilities (dash, double jump [Community]).
- **Recruit:** at least one traversal Fated Bond.

### Phase 2 — First Boss Gate (Hours 3–6)
- Clear side paths before the first main boss.
- **Goal:** return to the Temple, spend resources on skills and stats [Community].
- **Fight:** the first main boss tests whether you learned parry fundamentals.

### Phase 3 — Bond Expansion (Hours 6–12)
- Follow sidequests to recruit more Fated Bonds.
- **Goal:** build a balanced roster — one combat ally, one healer, one traversal ally [Editorial].
- **Explore:** new traversal abilities unlock previously gated paths.

### Phase 4 — The Corruption Trail (Hours 12+)
- Pursue the corrupted gods — **Haslan, Ymara, Siv, Evan** [Community].
- **Goal:** each god's domain is likely a major region with a main boss [Editorial].
- **Optional content:** weave in optional bosses as you pass their arenas.

## Boss Routing Tips

- **Optional bosses scale up.** The 1.0 adds 19 optional bosses; many are meant to be retried later. If one is too hard, mark it and come back [Editorial].
- **Main bosses gate story.** The 11 main bosses [Official] are the campaign's spine — route through them in region order.
- **Never enter a boss without Temple upgrades.** Even one skill tier changes the fight [Community].

## When to Backtrack

Backtracking is core to the Metroidvania loop [Official]. After each new traversal ability, sweep:

1. **Traversal-gated doors** — bond-symbol paths and high ledges.
2. **Cracked walls** — the rock-breaking uppercut opens them [Community].
3. **Water/fire areas** — if Elemental Imbuements interact with the environment at 1.0 [Unconfirmed].

## When to Take on Optional Bosses

The 1.0 roster adds **19 optional bosses** on top of the 3 EA optional bosses [Official]. A routing rule of thumb [Editorial]:

- **Try every optional boss once.** If it falls within two or three attempts, clear it — the reward funds your next upgrades.
- **Mark and move on if it's a wall.** Optional bosses are not required for the main story. A boss you cannot beat now becomes trivial after one or two more traversal upgrades.
- **Sweep them at the end.** The most efficient completion route is: main campaign → full exploration sweep → optional-boss circuit → ending replays [Editorial].

## Side Content Priorities

Not all side content is equal. In order of payoff [Editorial]:

1. **Fated Bond sidequests** — they unlock allies whose traversal/combat abilities open the map and shape endings [Official].
2. **Traversal-gated treasures** — hidden chests behind new abilities, which fund Temple upgrades [Community].
3. **Optional bosses** — large resource and reward payouts.
4. **Minigames** — a reliable secondary income once the Temple unlocks them [Official].

## What to Do If You're Stuck

A structured unstick routine for any wall in the game [Editorial]:

1. **Backtrack for abilities.** If you cannot reach a path or beat a boss, you are probably missing a traversal upgrade. Sweep regions you have cleared for new routes.
2. **Return to the Temple.** Spend all resources on skills and stats before retrying [Community].
3. **Swap your active Fated Bond.** A different ally changes your combat and traversal options mid-run [Official].
4. **Attempt a different region.** The map is non-linear — the "next" area may be optional until you are stronger.
5. **Only then consider grinding.** Farming is a last resort, not a default [Editorial].
