## How to Buy

1. **Wishlist now.** The Steam page is live at App ID 1446900. Wishlisting gets you a launch notification.
2. **Buy in the first 14 days** for the 10% launch discount [Official].
3. **Consider the bundle.** Steam lists an **Afterimage + Fallen Tear: The Ascension** bundle — useful if you also want the other hand-drawn Metroidvania [Official].

## Is It Worth the EA Price Difference?

The EA build was $19.99 and covered ~35% of the game [Official]. The 1.0 price is $24.99 for the **complete game** — 20 regions, 33 bosses, 21 Fated Bonds, multiple endings, and roughly double the EA content [Official]. For new buyers, $24.99 at 1.0 is the full-package price; the EA discount is history.

## Regional Pricing Note

Steam regional pricing means USD/EUR/GBP list prices do not necessarily match your local storefront. The official press release states the USD/EUR/GBP figures above; your local price is set by Steam's pricing tiers [Editorial].

## Should You Buy Now or Wait?

For a $24.99 Metroidvania, the main question is whether to buy at launch or wait for a deeper sale.

**Buy at launch if:**
- You want to experience the story and multiple endings while they are new — spoilers spread fast in a story-driven game [Editorial].
- You value the 10% launch discount. Waiting for a bigger sale means waiting months; indies of this size rarely discount more than 20–25% within the first year [Editorial].
- You want to be part of the post-launch discussion. A story-driven Metroidvania rewards being current.

**Wait if:**
- Your backlog is long. The game will still be $24.99 or cheaper in a few months, and patches may improve performance [Editorial].
- You are on the fence about the genre. The free demo (if still available) and Steam reviews can help you decide [Unconfirmed].

A reasonable middle path: **wishlist now, buy in the first 14 days** to lock in the 10% discount without committing months ahead [Editorial].
