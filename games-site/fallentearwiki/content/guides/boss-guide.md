# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Boss Guide

**Fallen Tear: The Ascension** ships 1.0 on September 16, 2026 with roughly **33 bosses** — **11 main bosses and 22 optional side bosses** [Official]. Early Access had **6 main and 3 optional**; the 1.0 update adds **5 main and 19 optional** [Official].

Here is the honest state of boss knowledge: the **counts** are confirmed, but **individual boss names and attack patterns have not been published** [Unconfirmed]. This guide covers the confirmed roster structure and a pre-release strategy framework you can apply to any boss. After launch, it will be updated boss-by-boss.

Optional bosses are the classic Metroidvania side content — harder versions, secret arenas, and reward-dense fights tucked behind traversal-gated paths [Editorial].

## The Corrupted Gods

The story spine of Raoah is the gods who have **fallen from grace** [Official]. The confirmed pantheon [Community]:

- **Haslan** — Earth God
- **Ymara** — Fire Goddess
- **Siv** — Wind Goddess
- **Evan** — Water God

Whether each god is a main boss, and whether there are boss fights beyond the four gods, is not confirmed [Unconfirmed]. What is safe to say: the corruption of these gods is the reason Raoah is falling apart, and defeating them (or their champions) drives the main campaign [Editorial].

## Pre-Release Boss Strategy Framework

Since specific patterns aren't published, use this universal framework built on the confirmed combat systems [Editorial]:

### 1. Learn the Parry Rhythm First
Fallen Tear combat is "based on millimetric dodges and parries" [Community]. Bosses telegraph their attacks — the first phase of any boss fight should be spent **learning which attacks can be parried**:

- **Parry the single, telegraphed strikes.** These stagger the boss and open a punish window.
- **Dodge the unparryable.** Grab attacks, AoE pulses, and multi-hit flails usually can't be parried — dodge them.
- **Expect a speed-up at low health.** Many bosses become more aggressive in their final phase; tighten your timing.

### 2. Use Your Full Toolkit
A boss fight is not just the Korr Blade:

- **Fated Bond bursts** — summon an ally's combined attack for burst damage and disruption [Community].
- **Overgrowth** — unleash the bestial form in the boss's danger phase to close the fight [Community].
- **Blink-Strike backstabs** — use the teleport backstab when the boss's pattern leaves its back exposed [Community].

### 3. Prepare Before You Enter
Bosses sit at the end of region paths. Before attempting:

- **Upgrade at the Temple of Oras.** Even one stat or skill upgrade changes the fight [Community].
- **Rotate your active bond.** Bring a healer for endurance, a damage ally for DPS checks [Editorial].
- **Stock recovery items.** Long boss fights are resource fights.

### 4. Know When to Back Off
Optional bosses especially are designed to be retried later. If you're hitting a wall:

- **Go find a traversal upgrade** — new map access often means new gear and stats.
- **Grind one upgrade tier.** A single Mastery Skill can swing a fight [Editorial].
- **Come back with Overgrowth charged.** Walk in with the ultimate ready for the danger phase.

## Main vs Optional Boss Rewards

Why fight optional bosses at all? The reward philosophy in Metroidvania-JRPG hybrids usually looks like this [Editorial]:

- **Main bosses** gate story progression and typically reward large resource payouts plus access to new regions [Official].
- **Optional bosses** are the highest-value risk/reward — tougher fights with rare materials, unique bond-gift items, or stat boosts that the main path does not provide [Editorial].

The 1.0 count — **19 new optional bosses** [Official] — signals a heavy optional-boss focus, matching games like Hollow Knight's optional gauntlets. Completionists will treat them as a dedicated circuit [Editorial].

## Pre-Boss Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Recovery items stocked
- [ ] Parry timing refreshed on nearby trash mobs
- [ ] Overgrowth ready for the final phase

Metroidvanias sometimes hide content behind boss conditions — like beating a boss without taking damage, or with a specific bond active [Unconfirmed]. If post-launch data reveals such conditions, this guide will document them. For now, treat "clear" as simply defeating the boss [Editorial].
