# Fallen Tear: The Ascension FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about **Fallen Tear: The Ascension** — the hand-drawn 2D Metroidvania by **Winter Crew Studios** / **CMD Studios**.

## General

**What is Fallen Tear: The Ascension?**
A hand-drawn, story-driven 2D Metroidvania set in the corrupted high-fantasy world of **Raoah**. You play as **Hira**, a mysterious child fated to stand against ancient gods. It blends the party-building of classic JRPGs with the freeform exploration of Metroidvanias, with full voice acting [Official].

**Who develops and publishes it?**
Developed by **Winter Crew Studios** (Manila, Philippines, founded 2020) and published by **CMD Studios** [Official].

**What are the corrupted gods?**
The confirmed pantheon is **Haslan** (Earth), **Ymara** (Fire), **Siv** (Wind), and **Evan** (Water) — gods who have fallen from grace and corrupted Raoah [Community].

## Release & Price

**When does it release?**
Early Access launched **March 17, 2026**. The 1.0 full release is **September 16, 2026** [Official].

**How much does it cost?**
$24.99 / €24.99 / £21.99 at 1.0, with a **10% launch discount for the first 14 days**. Early Access was $19.99 [Official].

**What platforms?**
PC via Steam at launch, **Steam Deck playable**. PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch are planned for later [Official][Community].

**What languages are supported at 1.0?**
English, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese at launch; French, Italian, Spanish, Turkish, Korean, and Japanese planned post-launch [Official].

## Gameplay

**What are Fated Bonds?**
Recruitable allies — mages, swordsmen, tricksters, healers — whose combat and traversal abilities change how you fight and explore. **10 in EA, ~21 at 1.0** (Steam says "up to 22" [Unconfirmed]) [Official].

**How many bosses?**
**~33 at 1.0** — 11 main and 22 optional. EA had 6 main + 3 optional [Official].

**How many regions?**
**~20 regions at 1.0** — 10 EA + 10 new [Official].

**Does it have multiple endings?**
Yes — multiple endings shaped by player choices and Fated Bond relationships [Official]. Unlock requirements are [Unconfirmed].

**What is the Temple of Oras?**
A customizable personal hub above Raoah where you build a castle, strengthen bonds, unlock minigames, and improve Hira's stats [Official].

**What is Overgrowth?**
Hira's ultimate ability — a transformation into a faster, stronger bestial form unleashed in combat [Community].

**Is there multiplayer?**
No — strictly single-player [Official].

## Practical

**How long is the game?**
35–40 hours for the main story, 80+ hours for completion. EA was ~35% of the game at 15–20 hours [Official].

**Does EA progress carry to 1.0?**
No official statement as of August 31, 2026 [Unconfirmed].

**What are the system requirements?**
Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit, i5-4460/Ryzen 3 1200, 8 GB RAM, GTX 950/R7 370, DirectX 11, 15 GB. Recommended: Windows 10/11, i5-6600K/Ryzen 5 1400, 16 GB RAM, GTX 1060 3 GB/RX 580 4 GB [Official].

**Is there an official wiki?**
No dedicated wiki exists as of August 31, 2026. This site is an unofficial fan resource [Editorial].

## Art, Voice & Presentation

**What is the art style?**
Traditional hand-drawn 2D animation — lush forests, lost cities, and forgotten ruins rendered frame by frame [Official]. The style is heavily inspired by anime and JRPG presentation [Community].

**Does the game have voice acting?**
Yes — **full voice acting** is a confirmed feature [Official]. The 1.0 launch supports English audio [Official].

**Who is the protagonist?**
**Hira**, a mysterious child fated to stand against ancient gods, raised by Miah, the healer who adopted him [Community].

**Who composed the music?**
The soundtrack has not been formally credited in official material reviewed as of August 31, 2026 [Unconfirmed]. The game's tone is JRPG-inspired, matching the hand-drawn storybook presentation [Editorial].

## Characters & Story

**Who are the Fated Bonds allies?**
The confirmed roster includes Trystan, Lenela, Nesta, Cadel, Magnus, Dagoth, Primorin, Sendrin, Korin, Durin, Zozrin (cartographer), Kinoro, Demir, Enid, Gwinri, Gunnar, Runa, Idris, and Annie (puppeteer) [Community].

**What is the story about?**
Raoah is a world "balanced by its gods." When the gods fall from grace, the world is plunged into chaos. Hira must restore order by confronting the corruption — with the bonds they form along the way shaping the outcome [Official].

**Are there collectibles or achievements?**
The EA build lists **12 Steam Achievements** [Official]. The 1.0 achievement list has not been confirmed to expand [Unconfirmed]. Achievements guide collectors to hidden content once the full list is published [Editorial].

## Performance & Features

**Does the game run on Steam Deck?**
Yes — the store page lists it as **Steam Deck playable** [Official].

**Is there cloud saving?**
Yes — **Steam Cloud** is supported [Official].

**Can the whole family share the game?**
Yes — **Family Sharing** is supported [Official].

**What about the Afterimage bundle?**
Steam lists an **Afterimage + Fallen Tear: The Ascension bundle** — a combined purchase for both hand-drawn Metroidvanias [Official].

## Getting Started

**Where do I start?**
Read the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide), then the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide) for parry and dodge fundamentals. Recruit a traversal Fated Bond early to open the map [Editorial].

**Is there anything I should know before 1.0?**
If you played Early Access, decide whether to finish EA content before September 16 — save carry-over is not officially confirmed [Unconfirmed]. New players can buy at 1.0 with the 10% launch discount [Official].
