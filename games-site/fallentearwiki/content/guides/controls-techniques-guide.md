# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Controls & Advanced Techniques Guide

Fallen Tear: The Ascension is a fast, skill-based combat game where **timing beats mashing** [Official]. Early Access reviews describe combat as built on "millimetric dodges and parries" [Community]. This guide covers the confirmed moveset and the advanced techniques that turn survival into domination.

## Core Techniques

### Parry Timing
The parry is the highest-skill, highest-payoff defensive tool [Community].

- **Trigger on the wind-up, not the strike.** Most enemies telegraph; parry as the attack begins moving toward you, not when it lands.
- **Parry to stagger.** A successful parry staggers the attacker, opening a punish window — usually enough for a full combo.
- **Learn per-enemy rhythms.** Different enemies have different tell-to-impact speeds. Practice on basic mobs before bosses.

### Dodge Discipline
Dodging is your universal escape [Editorial].

- **Dodge through attacks**, not away from them — i-frames cover the swing.
- **Dodge-cancel attack recovery** to keep pressure up. Attack → dodge → attack is the bread-and-butter rhythm.
- **Save dodges for unparryable attacks.** If you can parry it, parry it; save the dodge for grabs and AoE.

### Blink-Strike Backstabs
The **Blink-Strike** teleports Hira behind an enemy for a backstab [Community].

- **Best use:** opening an encounter by blinking behind the strongest enemy and deleting a chunk of its health.
- **Positioning:** land it from outside the enemy's facing arc; enemies turn toward you, so re-position between strikes.
- **Not a panic button:** the teleport has an animation commitment — use it from a safe angle.

### Aerial Combos & Juggling
Some attacks **launch enemies into the air**, and Hira can follow up with aerial strings [Community].

- **Launch on parry-stagger** for free aerial damage.
- **Juggle single enemies** to keep them out of the fight while you deal with adds.
- **Don't juggle bosses** unless their pattern is broken — aerial commitment is risky against fast bosses [Editorial].

## Movement Techniques

- **Dash upgrade:** the dash can be upgraded to leave a **trail of fire** [Community] — useful for both damage and (potentially) environmental interaction.
- **Double jump:** confirmed by early reporting as part of Hira's movement set [Community].
- **Slam attack:** a downward strike for platforming and crowd control [Community].
- **Rock-breaking uppercut:** opens cracked walls and stuns enemies [Community].

These movement tools are exactly what make a Metroidvania map open up — see the [Map & Regions Guide](/guides/map-regions-guide).

## The Overgrowth Form

**Overgrowth** transforms Hira into a **faster, stronger bestial form** [Community].

- **Use it for burst windows** — boss danger phases, dense mobs, or when you need to end a fight fast.
- **Don't waste it on trash.** Overgrowth is a resource; basic combos clear normal enemies.
- **Pair it with Fated Bond burst.** Summon a bond's combined attack and Overgrowth together for a massive damage window [Editorial].

## Building Good Habits

1. **Parry small, dodge big.** Parry telegraphed single hits; dodge everything with a lingering hitbox.
2. **Move after every string.** Never finish a combo and stand still — dodge-cancel into repositioning.
3. **Blink to open, juggle to control.** Open with a backstab, juggle adds, and keep the boss's parry rhythm in your head.
4. **Spend Overgrowth on the phase, not the fight.** Save it for the boss's final, most aggressive phase [Editorial].

## Practicing the Tech

Technique practice is best done in a low-stakes area [Editorial]:

1. **Parry window drill** — find a basic enemy and parry only, no attacks, until you can deflect three in a row.
2. **Dodge-cancel rhythm** — chain attack → dodge → attack in a loop against a training dummy or slow enemy.
3. **Blink-strike positioning** — practice teleporting behind enemies that are already turning, to learn the animation commitment.
4. **Overgrowth timing** — save the ultimate for the second half of a fight and practice activating it mid-combo.

These drills map directly onto boss fights — the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) explains why.
