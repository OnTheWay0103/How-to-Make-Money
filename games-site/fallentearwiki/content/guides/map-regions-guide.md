# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Map & Regions Guide

**Fallen Tear: The Ascension** takes place in the corrupted high-fantasy world of **Raoah** — lush forests, lost cities, and forgotten ruins rendered in hand-drawn animation [Official]. At 1.0 launch on September 16, 2026, the map spans roughly **20 major regions** [Official].

This guide covers the confirmed structure of the map: what Early Access players explored, what the 1.0 update adds, and how traversal abilities shape your route through Raoah.

## The Confirmed Region Count

The 1.0 press release confirms the map roughly doubles at launch:

| Version | Regions | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **Early Access** | 10 interconnected major regions | ~35% of the full game, 15–20 hours |
| **1.0 addition** | 10 additional major regions | New biomes, cities, and ruins |
| **1.0 total** | ~20 regions | 35–40 hour story, 80+ hours completion |

[Source: Official]

## Confirmed Regions So Far

Only a few region names are confirmed in official and community sources. The rest of the map is known by count, not by name — individual names are [Unconfirmed] until the game ships.

### Paupao Falls (Confirmed)
**Paupao Falls** is the jungle biome confirmed in Early Access coverage [Community]. It showcases the game's hand-drawn animation and is the kind of lush, vertical area where Metroidvania traversal abilities shine. Early Access players will recognize it as a starting-adjacent region with dense foliage, platforming, and hidden paths.

### Temple of Oras (Confirmed)
The **Temple of Oras** is not a region in the traditional sense — it is a **customizable personal hub above Raoah** [Official]. You return to it between expeditions to strengthen bonds, unlock minigames, and improve Hira's stats. Think of it as your castle and home base [Community].

### The Four Gods' Domains (Partially Confirmed)
The world of Raoah is balanced by gods who have "fallen from grace" [Official]. The confirmed pantheon is **Haslan** (Earth God), **Ymara** (Fire Goddess), **Siv** (Wind Goddess), and **Evan** (Water God) [Community]. It is reasonable to expect each god's corruption to be tied to a themed region, but which regions correspond to which god is [Unconfirmed] [Editorial].

## How Metroidvania Traversal Opens the Map

Fallen Tear is a Metroidvania — the map is a web of interconnected paths that open up as you gain abilities [Official]. The confirmed traversal tools:

- **Fated Bond traversal abilities** — allies grant new ways to move, and these are central to exploration [Official].
- **Dash** — the dash is upgradable to leave a trail of fire, suggesting environmental interaction [Community].
- **Double jump, slam attack, and rock-breaking uppercut** — early reporting lists these as part of Hira's movement set [Community, may be outdated].

The pattern is classic: **you see a locked path, find the ability that opens it, then return.** Backtracking is expected and rewarded [Editorial].

## Suggested Routing (Pre-Release)

Because the full map is not yet documented, treat this as a framework rather than a precise route [Editorial]:

1. **Start in the jungle-adjacent regions** (Paupao Falls and neighbors) to learn combat and movement.
2. **Recruit a traversal Fated Bond early.** Map-opening abilities should come before raw combat power.
3. **Return to the Temple of Oras** whenever you hit a wall — upgrades there may be the intended unlock.
4. **Pursue the corruption trail.** The gods' corruption is the story spine; following it leads to main bosses and regions [Editorial].

## Backtracking Checklist

When you gain a new traversal ability, sweep back through:

- **Fate-locked doors** — paths marked by bond symbols.
- **High ledges** — once double jump or a traversal bond is available.
- **Cracked walls** — for the rock-breaking uppercut [Community].
- **Water or fire-gated paths** — if elemental abilities interact with the environment [Unconfirmed].

## Biome Variety to Expect

The official material describes "lush forests, lost cities, and forgotten ruins" [Official] — a strong signal of biome diversity across the ~20 regions [Editorial]:

- **Jungle/forest** — confirmed by Paupao Falls [Community].
- **Lost cities** — likely ruin-style regions tied to the corrupted gods' domains [Official].
- **Forgotten ruins** — underground and decayed areas, classic Metroidvania back-half territory [Official].
- **Divine/domain regions** — the four gods (Haslan/Ymara/Siv/Evan) will likely each anchor a themed zone [Editorial].

Expect the hand-drawn art style to give each biome a distinct palette — that is what the "living world" framing promises [Official].

## Secrets, Hidden Paths & the "Living Reflection"

Two design signals point to dense secrets [Official][Community]:

- **Backtracking is core** — the map is built around re-exploring old regions with new abilities [Official].
- **The Temple is a "living reflection of Hira's journey"** — a hub that grows with what you find implies collectible-driven discovery [Official].

In practice, expect: hidden walls, ability-gated treasure rooms, optional-boss arenas tucked off the main path, and bond-gift items scattered through side rooms [Editorial]. A completionist run will spend its 80+ hours mostly here [Official].
