# Fallen Tear: The Ascension Beginner's Guide

**Fallen Tear: The Ascension** is a hand-drawn, story-driven 2D Metroidvania set in the corrupted world of **Raoah**. You play as **Hira**, a mysterious child fated to stand against ancient gods. The game blends the party-building and emotional weight of classic JRPGs with the freeform exploration of Metroidvanias.

The 1.0 launch arrives on **September 16, 2026**. This guide covers the confirmed core loop, what Early Access players already know, and how to start strong at launch.

## The Core Loop (Step by Step)

The game revolves around a simple four-phase loop:

| Phase | What Happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **Explore** | Traverse interconnected regions of Raoah. Unlock new traversal abilities to reach previously closed paths. |
| **Fight** | Combat is fast and skill-based — slash with the Korr Blade, parry and dodge, and unleash Overgrowth. [Editorial] |
| **Recruit** | Befriend **Fated Bonds** — allies whose abilities change how you fight and explore [Official]. |
| **Upgrade** | Spend resources at the **Temple of Oras** hub and in skill trees to improve Hira [Official]. |

Early Access covered roughly 35% of the full game (about 15–20 hours). The 1.0 release roughly doubles the content, adding 10 regions, 5 main bosses, 19 optional bosses, 11 new Fated Bonds, and more [Official].

## Combat Fundamentals

Fallen Tear combat is built around **slashing, parrying, and dodging** [Official]. Reviews of the Early Access build describe it as "fast and based on millimetric dodges and parries" [Community]. Key principles:

- **Slash** with Hira's blade as your basic attack string. Pressing into combos keeps pressure on enemies.
- **Parry** to deflect enemy strikes. Timing is strict — the window is narrow, and whiffed parries leave you open [Community].
- **Dodge** through or away from attacks. Movement is your primary survival tool, especially in multi-enemy rooms.
- **Overgrowth** is Hira's ultimate — a bestial form that is faster and stronger, unleashed in combat when you need burst damage [Community].

For a full breakdown, see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide).

## When to Recruit Fated Bonds

Fated Bonds are recruitable allies — mages, swordsmen, tricksters, and healers — whose unique combat and traversal abilities change how you fight and explore [Official]. Early Access had 10; the 1.0 update adds 11 more for roughly 21 total [Official].

Recruit early for two reasons:

1. **Traversal first.** Some allies unlock new ways to move around Raoah, which opens the map and gets you to more content faster [Editorial].
2. **Combat support.** Allies join fights with burst damage, buffs, or enemy disruption. Having one combat ally and one healer makes the first major bosses far more forgiving [Editorial].

See the [Fated Bonds Guide](/guides/fated-bonds-guide) for the confirmed roster.

## Common Early Mistakes

### 1. Ignoring Parry Timing
**The Mistake:** Mashing attack and dodging randomly through every fight.
**Why It Hurts:** The game's combat is balanced around deflection. Relying on dodges alone works early but falls apart against fast bosses [Community].
**Fix:** Learn the parry window on basic enemies first, then carry that timing into boss fights.

### 2. Skipping Fated Bonds
**The Mistake:** Beelining for the objective and ignoring recruitable allies.
**Why It Hurts:** Bond abilities gate both combat strength and map traversal. Missing them means missing regions, items, and story.
**Fix:** Explore side paths, talk to every named character, and complete bond sidequests as they appear [Editorial].

### 3. Not Using the Temple of Oras
**The Mistake:** Treating the hub as decoration.
**Why It Hurts:** The Temple is where you improve Hira's stats, unlock minigames, and strengthen bonds [Official]. Skipping upgrades makes mid-game content much harder.
**Fix:** Return to the Temple regularly and spend your resources.

### 4. Forgetting That 1.0 Doubles the Map
**The Mistake:** Assuming the Early Access map is the whole game.
**Why It Hurts:** Ten new regions arrive at 1.0. Reaching them requires the full set of traversal abilities — backtracking is expected.
**Fix:** Treat Early Access knowledge as the first act, not the whole story.

## How to Prepare for the 1.0 Launch

If you played Early Access:

- **Complete what you want from the EA build now.** The developer has not published an official statement on whether saves or upgrades carry into 1.0 as of August 31, 2026 [Unconfirmed].
- **Refresh your combat fundamentals.** The 1.0 update adds new skills and bosses; the old rhythm will still matter.
- **Expect the map to roughly double.** Plan for 35–40 hours of story and up to 80 hours for full completion [Official].

If you are new at 1.0:

- Start with the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide), then read the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide).
- Recruit at least one combat ally and one traversal ally before the second major region [Editorial].
- Upgrade at the Temple of Oras whenever you return to the hub.

## Parting Advice

Fallen Tear rewards patience and exploration over rushing. Parry instead of panic-dodging, recruit bonds instead of beelining, and treat the Temple of Oras as your basecamp. The world of Raoah is large — and at 1.0 it gets twice as large. Take your time.
