## What is Fallen Tear: The Ascension?

Fallen Tear: The Ascension is a hand-drawn, story-driven 2D Metroidvania set in the corrupted high-fantasy world of Raoah. You play as **Hira**, a mysterious child fated to stand against ancient gods. The game blends the emotional weight and party-building of classic JRPGs with the freeform exploration of Metroidvanias, and features full voice acting and multiple endings shaped by your choices.

Developed by **Winter Crew Studios** (Manila, Philippines) and published by **CMD Studios**, the game entered Early Access on March 17, 2026 at $19.99 and leaves Early Access on **September 16, 2026** at $24.99 / €24.99 / £21.99 (10% launch discount for the first 14 days).

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Fated Bonds** — Recruit allies (mages, swordsmen, tricksters, healers) whose combat and traversal abilities change how you fight and explore. 10 in EA, 21 at 1.0.
- **Skill Trees** — Ascensions, Hunter Skills, Elemental Imbuements, and Mastery Skills across Tiers I–IV. 1.0 adds 3 Ascensions, 6 Hunter Skills, 4 Elemental Imbuements, and 16 Mastery Skills.
- **Temple of Oras** — A customizable personal hub above Raoah where you strengthen bonds, unlock minigames, and improve Hira's stats.
- **Multiple Endings** — The story outcome is shaped by player choices and the bonds you form.
- **Overgrowth** — Hira's ultimate bestial transformation, unleashed in combat.
- **Boss Roster** — ~33 bosses at 1.0: 11 main + 22 optional across 20 regions.
- **Full Voice Acting** — English, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese at launch.
