# Bonehold Spells Guide — Spell Scrolls and Magic Systems

Spell scrolls add a whole dimension to Bonehold's charge-based combat system. Unlike your weapon, spells operate on cooldowns and resource costs, giving every class access to magic regardless of their primary kit. This guide covers how scrolls work, every spell type, class synergies, and when to cast instead of charge.

## How Spell Scrolls Work

Spell scrolls are consumable items you find in dungeon chests, dropped by enemies, or purchase from between-floor merchants. Each scroll teaches a one-time-use spell for that run only. You cannot learn spells permanently — scrolls are always per-run consumables.

**Scroll mechanics:**

- **Consumable:** Each scroll has 1-3 charges depending on rarity (Common: 1 charge, Uncommon: 2 charges, Rare/Legendary: 3 charges). Once charges are depleted, the scroll is destroyed.
- **Cooldown:** After casting, the scroll goes on a 15-30 second cooldown depending on the spell's power. You cannot cast the same scroll twice in quick succession.
- **Cast time:** Most spells have a 0.5-1.5 second cast animation. You can be interrupted during casting.
- **Resource cost:** Spells cost a percentage of your stamina bar — typically 15-25% for basic spells, 30-40% for powerful ones. This prevents spell spam.
- **Inventory limit:** You can carry up to 5 scrolls at once. Managing which scrolls to keep and which to drop is a key decision.

**Key rule:** Using a scroll spends your stamina, which means you cannot dodge or charge attack immediately after casting. Only cast when you have a safe window.

## Spell Types by Element

Bonehold's spell scrolls fall into five elemental categories, each with distinct effects and situational uses.

### Fire Spells

Fire spells deal high damage over time (DoT) and have moderate AoE. Enemies hit by fire take 4-6 seconds of burn damage.

**Notable fire scrolls:**

- **Fireball (Common)** — Launches a slow-moving projectile that explodes on contact. Deals 80 base damage + 15/sec burn for 4 seconds. Good all-purpose damage.
- **Flame Wall (Uncommon)** — Creates a wall of fire that lasts 6 seconds. Enemies walking through it take 25 damage per tick. Excellent for chokepoint defense.
- **Meteor (Rare)** — Calls a meteor that deals 200 damage in a wide radius and leaves a burning zone for 8 seconds. Use on boss adds or crowded rooms.

**Best class:** Scorcher (passive fire damage bonus + burn extension)

### Ice Spells

Ice spells focus on crowd control and survivability. They deal lower damage but apply chill (30% slow) or freeze (full immobilization).

**Notable ice scrolls:**

- **Frost Bolt (Common)** — A fast projectile that deals 50 damage and chills for 3 seconds. Good for slowing dangerous melee enemies.
- **Ice Shield (Uncommon)** — Surrounds you with a frost barrier that absorbs 100 damage and chills attackers. Lasts 8 seconds.
- **Blizzard (Rare)** — Area denial spell that deals 30 damage/sec and freezes enemies after 3 ticks. Excellent for controlling boss arenas.

**Best class:** Knight (ice shield compensates for limited mobility; slow effect helps land charge attacks)

### Lightning Spells

Lightning spells are the fastest-casting spells in the game. They deal moderate damage but have very short cast times (0.3-0.5 seconds), making them ideal for interrupting enemy attacks.

**Notable lightning scrolls:**

- **Spark (Common)** — Instant-cast bolt dealing 40 damage. Staggers most enemies for 0.5 seconds. Use to interrupt skeleton commander overhead slams.
- **Chain Lightning (Uncommon)** — Hits up to 4 enemies. Each bounce deals less damage (100-75-50-25). Strong in packed rooms.
- **Thunder Strike (Rare)** — Delayed AoE that hits after 1 second for 150 damage in a large radius. Requires predictive placement.

**Best class:** Barbarian (lightning's fast cast fits aggressive play; staggers let you land rage attacks)

### Dark Spells

Dark spells have high base damage but come with drawbacks or self-damage. They are high-risk, high-reward options for experienced players.

**Notable dark scrolls:**

- **Life Drain (Common)** — Deals 60 damage and heals you for 50% of the damage. Slow cast time (1.5 seconds). Use when low on health and clear of immediate danger.
- **Curse (Uncommon)** — Applies a debuff that increases all damage the target takes by 25% for 8 seconds. Excellent before boss fights.
- **Soul Explosion (Rare)** — Deals 300 damage in a small radius but costs 30% of your current HP. Use only as a finishing move.

**Best class:** Spellblade (dark spells benefit from magic damage scaling; curse synergizes with weapon combos)

### Support and Healing Spells

These spells do not deal direct damage but provide sustain, utility, or buffs.

**Notable support scrolls:**

- **Heal (Common)** — Restores 40% of your max HP. Cast time 2 seconds. The longest cast in the game — only use when enemies are dead or controlled.
- **Blink (Uncommon)** — Short-range teleport in your movement direction. Breaks enemy targeting. Stamina cost: 20%. Essential for deep floors.
- **Haste (Uncommon)** — Increases movement speed and charge speed by 30% for 8 seconds. Pop before boss fights for faster charge windows.
- **Barrier (Rare)** — Negates the next 3 instances of damage. Lasts until consumed or 15 seconds pass. Saves runs against boss combos.

**Best class:** Any — support spells are universally useful. The Knight benefits most from Barrier due to its frontline positioning.

## Spell Synergies by Class

While any class can use any spell scroll, each class has built-in synergies that make certain spells much more effective.

### Scorcher — Fire Focus

The Scorcher deals 25% more fire damage passively and extends burn duration by 2 seconds. The Scorcher's class ability, Ignite, applies a stacking burn effect on charge attacks that can stack with fire scroll DoTs.

**Best spells:** Fireball, Flame Wall, Meteor
**Strategy:** Apply Ignite stacks with charge attacks, then cast Fireball to consume the stacks for 3x damage. This combo deletes elite enemies.

### Spellblade — Weave Magic Between Strikes

The Spellblade has a unique passive: each spell cast empowers your next charge attack with bonus magic damage equal to 30% of the spell's base damage. This creates a cast-attack-cast rhythm that out-damages pure melee or pure casting.

**Best spells:** Frost Bolt, Chain Lightning, Curse
**Strategy:** Cast Frost Bolt (chill) → charge attack (bonus magic damage + chill effect makes charge safe to land) → weapon combo → repeat. Use Curse before boss phases, then land 2-3 empowered charge attacks.

### Knight — Defensive Casting

The Knight's heavy armor does not penalize cast speed, and its shield block covers the slow recovery after casting.

**Best spells:** Ice Shield, Barrier, Heal
**Strategy:** Use Ice Shield before charging into a group. Cast Barrier before boss encounters. Heal during block — hold block, then cast Heal; the block animation shortens the cast window by about 0.3 seconds.

### Barbarian — Aggressive Casting

The Barbarian has a rage mechanic that builds as you take and deal damage. At full rage, your charge attacks explode in an AoE. Spell scrolls do not consume rage.

**Best spells:** Spark, Life Drain, Haste
**Strategy:** Use Spark to stagger enemies during rage combos. Life Drain sustains through the Barbarian's low defense. Haste makes the Barbarian an unstoppable blender.

## Spell Scroll Farming Strategies

Spell scrolls are renewable but limited resources per run. Optimizing your scroll collection can make or break a deep run.

**Best farming methods:**

- **Chest rooms** on floor 3+ have the highest scroll drop rate (about 60% contain at least one scroll). Prioritize optional chest rooms.
- **Elite enemies** drop scrolls about 25% of the time. If you see an elite flame symbol on the door preview, enter for the scroll chance.
- **Between-floor merchants** sell scrolls for 200-500 gold. Always buy Heal and Blink if available — they are worth more than any weapon upgrade.
- **Shrine rooms** occasionally have a spell pedestal that grants a random scroll for free. Always check shrine rooms for this.

**Inventory management:** With only 5 scroll slots, you must choose carefully. A good loadout is one heal spell, one mobility spell (Blink), one AoE damage spell, one CC spell (ice/curse), and one flex slot for whatever the floor needs.

## When to Use Spells vs Charge Attacks in Boss Fights

Boss fights demand precise resource management. The wrong spell at the wrong time means you cannot dodge a telegraphed attack.

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Boss is in a recovery animation | Charge attack (no stamina cost for spells this window) |
| Boss is about to use a heavy attack | Save stamina for dodge — do not cast |
| Boss spawns adds | AoE spell (Fireball, Chain Lightning) — quick add clear saves HP |
| Boss enters enrage phase | Barrier or Ice Shield for survival; do not charge attack |
| You are below 30% HP with no clear window | Run and create distance, then cast Heal — do not risk it |
| Boss has obvious 2+ second wind-up | Full charge attack (more damage per stamina than spells) |

**General rule:** Spells are for crowd control and burst moments. Charge attacks are for sustained DPS. If you find yourself casting more than twice per boss phase, you are spending too much stamina on spells and will run out of dodge stamina.
