# Bonehold System Requirements Guide — Minimum & Recommended PC Specs

Bonehold is a modestly demanding game. The 3D roguelike dungeon crawler from Pixel Jackal (and co-developer SaikingS) uses a lightweight retro aesthetic — hand-painted textures blended with pixel art — and its published requirements go back to hardware from the early 2010s. This guide covers the official minimum and recommended specs, what they mean for your machine, controller support, and the Steam Deck question.

**Honesty note:** the specifications below come straight from the official Steam store page, checked on August 6, 2026. Where the store page is missing or inconsistent (it lists RAM and storage in megabytes, which is clearly a typo), we say so plainly instead of guessing.

## Minimum Requirements (Confirmed, Steam Store Page)

Bonehold's minimum specs are remarkably forgiving. These are the hardware requirements listed on the official Steam page:

| Component | Minimum Requirement |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **OS** | Windows 7 |
| **Processor** | AMD Phenom 8450 Triple-Core (~2.1 GHz) or Intel Core 2 Duo E8400 @ 3.0 GHz |
| **Memory** | Listed as "4 MB RAM" (see note below) |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470 (1 GB VRAM) or ATI Radeon HD 6870 (1 GB VRAM) |
| **Storage** | Listed as "1 MB available space" (see note below) |
| **Sound Card** | DirectX 11 compatible sound device |

## Recommended Requirements (Confirmed, Steam Store Page)

| Component | Recommended Requirement |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| **OS** | Windows 10 |
| **Processor** | Intel Core i5-8400 @ 4.0 GHz |
| **Memory** | Listed as "8 MB RAM" (see note below) |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 |
| **Storage** | Listed as "2 MB available space" (see note below) |
| **Sound Card** | DirectX 11 compatible sound device |

## What These Specs Actually Mean

**The game is light on hardware.** A minimum GPU from 2010 (GTX 470) and a Core 2 Duo CPU from 2008 are the official entry point. Any reasonably modern PC or laptop will clear the recommended tier. This fits the game's art direction — stylized, hand-painted, pixel-textured visuals are far less taxing than realistic 3D rendering.

**The RAM and storage numbers are store-page typos.** The Steam page literally lists "4 MB RAM" and "1 MB available space" — those are placeholder errors, not real figures. The actual RAM requirement and install size are **not officially published as of August 6, 2026** (unverified). For reference, the demo installs in seconds and the full game's retro art style suggests a small download, but we will not invent a number.

**Windows only — confirmed.** The Steam store page lists Windows support only. **There is no macOS or Linux version** as of August 6, 2026. Linux users would need to run it through a compatibility layer, which is unverified.

**No official framerate or performance numbers exist.** The store page publishes no expected FPS, resolution support, or benchmark results. Given the modest hardware floor, most players on recommended-tier hardware should have no trouble, but treat any specific "runs at 60 FPS" claims you see in community posts as unverified.

## Controller Support (Confirmed)

Bonehold ships with **full controller support** — a fact confirmed directly on the Steam store page. That means you can play start to finish with a gamepad, no keyboard required.

Store-listed controller and accessibility details, all confirmed:

- **Full controller support** (game can be played entirely with a controller)
- **DualShock 4 controller support** (PlayStation 4)
- **DualSense controller support** (PlayStation 5)
- **Camera Comfort** — a Steam store label indicating camera behavior designed to reduce motion discomfort
- **Playable without Timed Input** — a Steam label confirming the game never demands quick-reaction timed inputs
- **Custom Volume Controls** and **Subtitle Options** — audio and readability options
- **Stereo Sound** and **Surround Sound** — audio output options

For a combat game built around charge timing, the "Playable without Timed Input" label is notable: it means no part of the game demands frame-perfect reaction input, which is good news for slower, methodical play styles. The existing guides on this site confirm that charging, dodging, blocking, and parrying are the core of combat — all controller-friendly mechanics. See our [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide) for the tactics.

*Specific button mappings (which button charges, which dodges) are not officially documented anywhere as of August 6, 2026 — you will need to check the in-game settings menu.*

## Steam Deck Status — Not Yet Officially Rated (unverified)

As of August 6, 2026, **Bonehold has no official Steam Deck Verified or Playable badge** on its store page. Here is what we can say:

- The store page does not display a Deck compatibility rating.
- The **Bonehold Demo** page shows the community indicator **"Played Mostly on Steam Deck"** — evidence that players have been running it on Deck, but this is a community signal, not an official rating.
- The game's official store labels **"Camera Comfort"** and **"Playable without Timed Input"** are the kind of accessibility features Steam associates with comfortable handheld play.
- The very light system requirements (GTX 470 minimum) and full controller support make the game a plausible Deck candidate — but **official verification status is unconfirmed** as of August 6.

If Deck compatibility matters to you, the safest test is the free demo (see below). If it runs well in handheld mode there, that is a strong real-world signal.

## Languages (Confirmed)

The Steam page confirms **10 supported languages**: English (with full audio), French, Spanish (Spain), Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Greek, Hungarian, and Japanese. English is the only language listed with full audio support; the rest are interface/subtitle options per the store listing. Simplified Chinese being supported is a notable plus for players outside the English-speaking audience.

## How to Test Performance Before Buying — The Free Demo

The **Bonehold Demo** is free on Steam and was released May 5, 2026 — before the full game's July 28, 2026 launch. It is the most reliable way to answer "can I run Bonehold?" for your specific machine:

1. Install the demo from the [Bonehold Demo Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4643980/Bonehold_Demo/).
2. Play the first few floors with your intended input method (keyboard or gamepad).
3. Pay attention to framerate during combat — charge attacks and crowd fights are the most demanding moments.
4. If the demo runs comfortably on your hardware, the full game will too.

The demo also lets you try the class selection and combat feel before committing money — see our [Review & Worth-It Guide](/guides/review-guide) for whether the full game is worth it at its ~$10 price.

## Quick Answers

- **Can I run Bonehold on a laptop without a dedicated GPU?** Possibly — the minimum is a GTX 470 (2010). Integrated graphics are unverified territory; test with the free demo.
- **Is Bonehold on Mac or Linux?** No. Windows only, confirmed.
- **Does Bonehold support controllers?** Yes — full controller support, including DualShock 4 and DualSense, confirmed on the Steam page.
- **How big is Bonehold's install?** Not officially published (store page lists placeholder "MB" values). Unconfirmed as of August 6.
- **Is Bonehold Steam Deck Verified?** No official badge as of August 6. Community reports of Deck play exist for the demo, but official status is unconfirmed.
