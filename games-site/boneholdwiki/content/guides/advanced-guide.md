## Advanced Guide — Deep Dungeon Tactics & Prefix Stacking

Bonehold's depth system punishes comfort: past floor 10, enemy affixes stack with dungeon depth, and survival becomes a build question as much as a skill question. This guide covers deep-run tactics, the prefix stacking theory that defines late builds, and the risk decisions around legendary items.

### Deep Dungeon Scaling (Floor 10+)

| Floor Range | Enemy Affixes | Loot Multiplier | Survival Notes |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|----------------|
| 10-15 | Poison, Armor | 1.5x | Bring cleanse potions |
| 16-20 | Explosive, Leech | 2.0x | Kite explosive enemies |
| 21-25 | Multi-affix, Enrage | 2.5x | Boss floors every 5 |
| 26+ | Full affix stacks | 3.0x | Maxed build required |

1. **Affix priority order:** Poison first, Explosive second, Leech third — poison kills through armor and healing both.
2. **Floor rhythm:** Save ultimate cooldowns for the 5th floor of each tier — bosses spawn there guaranteed.
3. **The exit is the trap:** Past floor 15, exits can spawn elite ambushes. Check exit rooms before committing.

### Prefix Stacking Theory

| Prefix Family | Stack Rule | Best Class | Example Build |
|---------------|------------|------------|---------------|
| Damage prefixes | Same-family stacks additive | Barbarian, Scorcher | Ruthless + Crushing |
| Defense prefixes | Diminishing past 3 | Knight | Stone + Bulwark |
| Element prefixes | One element max | Spellblade | Frost + Frigid |
| Utility prefixes | One slot only | Any | Swift boots |

- **The rule of families:** Stack within one family; cross-family prefixes waste slots.
- **Element focus:** Pick one element and stack it — mixed elemental prefixes split your spellblade scaling.
- **Legendary rule:** A legendary item's primary effect doubles your strongest prefix family — build the family first, hunt the item second.

### Boss Pattern Counters

| Boss | Pattern | Counter |
|------|---------|---------|
| The Bone Warden | Bone wall + charge | Break wall from the side, dodge charge laterally |
| The Grave Singer | AoE scream | Interrupt window after third verse |
| The Hollow Knight | Armor phase | Stack armor-piercing prefixes before this fight |
| The Reaper | Execute under 30% HP | Save heals for the execute window |

### Risk Management for Legendary Items

1. **Carry discipline:** Legendary items drop physically; you must survive to keep them. Bank items at floor 10 before pushing deeper.
2. **Enhancement risk:** Enhancing a legendary past +5 risks breaking it. Use the safe upgrade path on legendaries only.
3. **The one-run rule:** Never push 3+ floors deeper than your best clear with a legendary in the bag — extract value, not pride.
