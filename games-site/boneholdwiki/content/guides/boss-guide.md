# Bonehold Boss Guide - Boss Strategies and Tips

Boss fights are among the highlights of Bonehold — the game's description promises "mighty bosses," and launch reviews describe them as **set-piece encounters that punish greedy players**. This guide covers what is confirmed about boss fights, the general strategy framework that applies to all of them, and — honestly — what is still unknown about the boss roster.

## What Is Confirmed About Bosses

Let's start with what we actually know from official materials and launch reviews:

- **Bosses are set-piece encounters.** Reviews describe boss fights as scripted, designed events rather than random spawns — each boss is a crafted fight with its own arena.
- **Bosses punish greed.** The most consistent review theme: trying to squeeze in one extra hit is the fastest way to die. Boss fights reward patience over aggression.
- **Parries and rolls are rewarded.** Launch reviews describe well-timed parries and dodges in boss fights as "glorious" — the defensive toolkit is the boss-fighting toolkit.
- **Traps remain a threat.** Reviewers note dying to traps even in serious situations — don't assume arena hazards disappear in boss rooms.
- **The boss roster is unconfirmed.** The full list of bosses, their names, the floor they appear on, and their attack patterns have **not been officially documented**. Any specific boss names or attack lists you see elsewhere (including older versions of this guide) are unverified.

## The General Boss Strategy Framework

Because the confirmed information is about *how* bosses fight, not *what* each one does, the most useful guide is a framework that works regardless of which boss you face.

### 1. Watch Before You Attack

Spend the first part of any new boss fight dodging and observing. You are looking for:

- **Tells** — the animation that precedes each attack. Every attack in Bonehold's charge-based combat has a windup; learn to read it before you commit to anything.
- **Openings** — the recovery period after the boss's attack or combo where it cannot act.
- **Phase changes** — boss behavior usually shifts at health thresholds (new attacks, faster patterns, summoned adds). Do not assume a boss you've seen half of is a boss you've seen all of.

### 2. Defend First, Damage Second

Bonehold's combat loop — charge attacks, dodges, blocks, parries — applies to bosses more strictly than anywhere else:

- **Dodge into the rhythm.** Rolls and well-timed parries are confirmed to be the rewarded defensive tools. When in doubt, dodge.
- **Block what you can't dodge.** Shields and blocking cover mistakes and multi-hit patterns, but some attacks will punish holding block. Learn which.
- **Parry for openings.** A successful parry staggers the enemy and creates a guaranteed window for a full charge attack — the highest-value single exchange in the game.

### 3. Charge Only During Safe Windows

The charge system is what makes boss fights dangerous for greedy players:

- A **full charge** is the big damage tool, but it locks you in place during the windup.
- Only start charging when the boss is in a recovery animation, staggered from a parry, or busy with a summons animation.
- A **quick attack or partial charge** is often the correct answer when your window is small — landing damage matters less than not getting hit.

### 4. Manage Your Resources

Boss fights are marathons, not sprints:

- **Stamina is survival.** In Bonehold, dodge and charge both consume stamina. Reaching a boss fight with low stamina means you cannot escape the first punish combo.
- **Hold healing for emergencies.** If the game gives you healing items, they are worth the most at low health in a boss arena — not after the first scratch.
- **Save your strongest consumables for phase changes.** The moment a boss transitions (adds spawn, new attacks unlock) is when players die most.

### 5. Use the Arena

Boss arenas are designed spaces:

- **Environmental hazards cut both ways.** Traps in boss arenas can damage you, but positioning can often put them between you and the boss.
- **Pillars and obstacles break line of sight** against ranged attacks — use them to create breathing room while your stamina recovers.
- **Don't back yourself into corners.** Keep an escape path open so you can always dodge sideways or through the boss, never into a wall.

## What to Expect From Boss Fights by Run Progress

We cannot confirm the exact boss roster, but the game's structure — a fortress you descend through with increasing difficulty — implies a pattern consistent across roguelikes:

| Run Stage | What to Expect (Unconfirmed) |
|-----------|------------------------------|
| Early floors | Skill-check bosses teaching the basics: telegraphed attacks, block/parry timing, dodge discipline |
| Mid floors | Bosses with more attack variety, adds, and arena mechanics |
| Deep floors | Multi-phase fights combining everything: elements, summons, fast combos, enrage-style behavior |

Treat this as a general expectation, not a specific boss list. The game's difficulty curve and reviews ("punish greedy players" applies at every stage) support the framework, but the specifics are TBD.

## Boss-Fight Checklist

Before every boss door, run through this checklist:

- [ ] Stamina as full as possible — never enter a boss fight at low stamina
- [ ] Healing options available and mentally mapped to keys/slots
- [ ] Best damage tools ready (full charge timing fresh in your mind)
- [ ] Arena scanned for hazards and useful cover
- [ ] A plan for adds if the boss summons them (they usually do at some point)
- [ ] Escape routes identified — where you will dodge to
- [ ] Greed checked — the fight is won by patience, not extra hits

## Final Verdict

Bonehold's boss fights are confirmed to be set-piece encounters that reward patience, parries, and dodges while punishing greedy charging. The specific boss roster and their attack patterns are **not yet officially documented** — treat any named boss list as unverified until the community or developer publishes the real one.

When you meet a new boss, apply the framework: watch, defend, punish safe windows, manage stamina, and never get greedy. That framework is confirmed to work by the reviews that praise the game's tight combat.
