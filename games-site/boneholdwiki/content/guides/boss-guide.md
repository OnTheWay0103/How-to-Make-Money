# Bonehold Boss Guide - All Boss Strategies and Tips

Boss fights in Bonehold are set-piece encounters at the end of each dungeon floor. Unlike regular rooms, bosses have unique mechanics, multiple attack phases, and no escape. This Bonehold boss guide covers general strategies, how bosses punish different playstyles, and specific tactics for every boss you will face.

## Boss Fight Structure

Every boss encounter follows the same basic structure:

1. **Entry phase:** The boss spawns with a dramatic animation. You have 3-5 seconds to position yourself before combat begins.
2. **Phase 1 (100%-60% HP):** The boss uses its basic moveset. This is your learning window -- patterns are slower and more telegraphed.
3. **Phase 2 (60%-30% HP):** New attacks unlock. The boss becomes more aggressive. Environmental hazards may activate.
4. **Phase 3 (30%-0% HP / Enrage):** Maximum aggression. The boss gains attack speed and may summon adds. This phase tests everything you have learned.

Bosses do not have random attack patterns -- they follow scripted sequences based on positioning and HP thresholds. Learn the sequence, and you can predict the next move.

## General Boss Tips

**Do not be greedy.** The number one cause of boss deaths in Bonehold is trying to squeeze in one extra hit. The charge system punishes greed -- if you commit to a level 3 charge and the boss starts its attack animation, you either cancel (wasting the opening) or get hit.

**Learn attack patterns by watching, not charging.** Spend the first 30 seconds of any new boss fight just dodging. Watch for tells:
- Weapon glow = heavy attack incoming
- Boss looks at you and pauses = charge attack (dodge sideways)
- Boss raises arms = AoE ground slam (back away)
- Boss summons adds = kill adds immediately

**Charge during openings, not randomly.** Safe windows are:
- After the boss finishes a combo (1-2 seconds)
- After successfully parrying a boss attack (full charge window)
- During add-summon animations (the boss is idle)
- After the boss misses a charge attack (recovery frames)

**Stamina management is critical.** Never let your stamina drop below 30% during a boss fight. One dodge at low stamina means you are taking the next hit.

**Use the environment.** Many boss rooms contain traps, pillars, or environmental hazards. Some traps damage bosses. Kite the boss into spike traps or explosive barrels for free damage. Pillars block line-of-sight against ranged attacks.

**Save spell scrolls for phases 2 and 3.** Using scrolls early wastes their impact window. Save burst scrolls for the 30% enrage phase when the boss is most dangerous.

## How Bosses Punish Different Playstyles

Bonehold bosses are designed to challenge specific approaches.

- **Aggressive players:** Bosses have counter-attack moves that trigger when you stay in melee range too long. The floor 3 boss punishes constant attacking with a retaliation strike.
- **Defensive players:** Several bosses have unblockable attacks or grabs that bypass shields. The floor 2 champion's charge attack cannot be blocked -- only dodged.
- **Ranged players:** Bosses close distance quickly. The Scorcher's advantage of range shrinks when the boss dashes across the room.
- **Summoner players:** Bosses with AoE clear can wipe your minions instantly. The final boss has a room-wide blast that kills all summons.

The solution is adaptability. No single approach works for every boss -- switch tactics between phases if your current strategy is failing.

## Early Bosses (Floors 1-2)

### Skeleton Commander (Floor 1)

The first boss serves as a skill check. It teaches the fundamentals of boss combat without being overly punishing.

**Attacks:**
1. **Overhead slam** -- Slow (1.5-second windup), easily telegraphed. The boss raises its greatsword overhead. Dodge sideways, never backward (the shockwave has forward range).
2. **Horizontal sweep** -- Medium speed, wide arc. Block or parry if you have a shield. Dodge through if you do not.
3. **Summon skeletons** -- At 50% HP, the boss summons 2-3 basic skeletons. Kill them immediately to avoid being mobbed.

**Strategy:** Stay at mid-range. Bait the overhead slam. Dodge sideways. Full charge the boss during its recovery. When skeletons spawn, kill them with one level 1-2 charge and return focus to the boss. Repeat until dead.

**Dodge timing:** The overhead slam connects 0.5 seconds after the boss's arm peaks. Dodge at the peak.

### Bone Construct (Floor 2)

A massive animated pile of bones with an armored shell. Tankier than the Skeleton Commander but slower.

**Attacks:**
1. **Arm sweep** -- Wide 180-degree arc. Telegraph is the construct leaning back. Jump over or dodge through.
2. **Bone volley** -- Fires a spread of bone shards. Projectiles can be blocked but drain stamina. Hide behind pillars if available.
3. **Ground pound** -- At 60% HP, the boss slams the ground creating a shockwave. Jump to avoid.
4. **Enrage charge** -- At 30% HP, charges across the room. Unblockable. Must be dodged.

**Strategy:** Use a mace if available -- maces deal bonus damage to armored enemies. Stay behind the construct as much as possible; its back has no armor reduction. Dodge the bone volley by strafing, not rolling.

## Mid-Game Champion (Floor 3-4)

### Wraith Lord (Floor 3)

A ghostly spellcaster that teleports around the arena. The first boss that requires strong positioning.

**Attacks:**
1. **Soul bolt** -- Homing projectile. Can be blocked but breaks guard in one hit. Better to dodge at the last moment.
2. **Teleport strike** -- Boss teleports behind you and attacks. Immediately roll forward after the teleport flash.
3. **Soul storm** -- At 60% HP, creates a vortex that pulls you toward the boss. Hold sprint away from the vortex center.
4. **Phantom adds** -- At 30% HP, summons ghost copies that mirror the boss's attacks for 10 seconds.

**Strategy:** This fight is about positioning. Stay near the arena edges so you can see the teleport flash clearly. Save your highest-damage scroll for the phantom phase. Kill the adds before returning to the boss.

**Dodge timing:** Soul bolt has a homing curve. Dodge perpendicular to its trajectory at the very last moment, not early.

### Ebon Knight (Floor 4)

A dark knight boss with a flamberge and shadow magic. Fast, aggressive, and relentless.

**Attacks:**
1. **Flurry strike** -- 4-hit combo. Block the first hit, parry the second, dodge the third and fourth.
2. **Dark lunge** -- Charges forward with extended reach. Telegraph is the boss crouching. Dodge sideways.
3. **Shadow zone** -- At 50% HP, creates a dark pool that damages you if you stand in it. The pool expands over time.
4. **Execution** -- At 20% HP, a telegraphed overhead slam that deals lethal damage if not parried.

**Strategy:** Parry is essential here. The flurry strike becomes manageable if you parry the second hit -- it interrupts the remaining combo. Stay out of the shadow zone at all costs; it deals percentage-based damage.

## Final Boss

### Lich King Aethel (Floor 5)

The ruler of the necromantic fortress and the final challenge in Bonehold. This fight has four distinct phases and no room for error.

**Phase 1 (100%-70%):** The Lich fights with frost magic.
- **Frost bolt** -- Fast projectile. Block or dodge.
- **Ice floor** -- Creates a slippery ice patch. Move to the other side of the arena.
- **Summon skeleton** -- One elite skeleton. Kill it before returning to the boss.

**Phase 2 (70%-45%):** Switches to fire magic. Arena edges become lava pools.
- **Fire pillar** -- Ground erupts under you. Keep moving.
- **Flame wave** -- Horizontal fire wave. Jump over.
- **Meteor** -- Large AoE targeting your position. Run, do not roll.

**Phase 3 (45%-20%):** Uses both elements. Attack patterns combine.
- **Frost-fire combo** -- Ice floor followed by flame wave. The ice makes it harder to reach the wave jump point.
- **Dual element adds** -- Both frost and fire skeletons spawn. Prioritize fire skeletons (they have ranged attacks).

**Phase 4 (20%-0% / Enrage):** The Lich enters its true form. All attacks are enhanced.
- **Apocalypse** -- Room-wide blast that kills all summons and deals massive damage. The only way to survive: stand behind one of the three pillars that glow before the blast.
- **Relentless assault** -- Continuous attack combos with no pause. You must parry to create openings.
- **Desperation** -- At 5% HP, the boss heals 10% and gains attack speed. Do not panic -- you have won. Stay focused, execute the same patterns.

**Final strategy:** Phase 4 is the hardest encounter in Bonehold. Memorize the pillar positions before the fight. When the Lich starts the Apocalypse cast animation, get behind a pillar immediately -- this is the only survival check in the fight. If you reach phase 4, you can win.

## Quick Reference: Boss Checklist

Before every boss fight, run through this mental checklist:

- [ ] Stamina at maximum
- [ ] Health potions hotkeyed (1-2 minimum)
- [ ] Best spell scrolls ready (put them in first two inventory slots)
- [ ] Weapon charges mentally mapped (3 second full charge)
- [ ] Arena scanned for traps and environmental hazards
- [ ] Escape route planned (where to dodge)
- [ ] Adds expected (scroll ready for clear)
