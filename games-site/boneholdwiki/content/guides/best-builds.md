# Bonehold Best Builds - Top Build Archetypes and Synergies

Buildcrafting is where Bonehold truly shines. With 7 classes, dozens of weapon prefixes, and universal spell scrolls, the number of viable combinations is vast. This guide covers the top 5 build archetypes that consistently deliver results through deep dungeon floors. Each build includes weapon choices, ideal prefixes, scroll synergies, and adaptation tips.

## 1. Spellblade Hybrid Caster

The Spellblade hybrid caster balances sword strikes with magic for relentless pressure. This build excels at applying debuffs through melee attacks while finishing enemies with spells.

**Core concept:** Melee applies Arcane Weave stacks; spells consume stacks for bonus damage.

- **Weapon:** One-handed sword (Arcane prefix preferred).
- **Secondary:** Staff or orb with Echoing prefix.
- **Ideal prefixes:** Arcane (sword), Echoing (staff), Quick (armor), Mana Font (accessory).
- **Spell scrolls:** Frost Lance (slow), Arcane Bolt (stack builder), Temporal Shift (emergency reset).
- **Playstyle:** Build Arcane Weave stacks with 2-3 sword swings, then consume them with a charged spell for massive burst.

**Prefix stacking tip:** Arcane and Echoing share a hidden synergy -- each stack of Arcane Weave increases spell projectile count by 1 when Echoing is equipped. At 5 stacks, your spells fire 6 projectiles instead of 1.

**When to use:** Any run where you find an Arcane weapon. This build scales well through all floor depths.

## 2. Knight Tank Fortress

The Knight tank fortress build is about becoming immovable. Stack defense, block chance, and recovery effects until nothing can threaten you.

**Core concept:** Maximum survivability lets you face-tank bosses while steadily wearing them down.

- **Weapon:** One-handed mace (Sturdy or Deflecting prefix).
- **Shield:** Tower shield with Fortress prefix (increases block angle).
- **Ideal prefixes:** Sturdy (all gear), Deflecting (shield), Fortress (chest), Regenerating (boots).
- **Spell scrolls:** Stone Skin (temporary armor), Sanctuary (heal zone), Taunt (force aggro).
- **Playstyle:** Hold block during dangerous attacks, parry when safe, punish with charged mace swings. Let defensive procs do the work.

**Prefix stacking tip:** Fortress + Sturdy on 3+ gear pieces triggers the unbreakable set bonus: damage reduction cap increases from 60% to 80%. Combined with block, you take roughly one-third of normal damage.

**When to use:** Always viable, but especially strong when learning new bosses or floors.

## 3. Barbarian Rage Machine

The Barbarian rage machine converts damage taken into raw offensive power. The goal is to maintain maximum rage as long as possible while lifestealing through incoming damage.

**Core concept:** High rage = high damage = high lifesteal = sustainable aggression.

- **Weapon:** Two-handed axe (Vampiric or Berserker prefix).
- **Ideal prefixes:** Vampiric (weapon), Berserker (weapon or gloves), Bloodthirsty (accessory), Wrathful (chest).
- **Spell scrolls:** Blood Rage (rage multiplier), Leeching Strike (single-target heal), War Cry (AoE fear).
- **Playstyle:** Dive into groups, spam charge attacks, use Blood Rage to double rage generation. As long as you keep hitting, you keep healing.

**Prefix stacking tip:** Berserker + Bloodthirsty creates a feedback loop -- dealing damage generates rage, and high rage increases lifesteal percentage. At maximum rage with both prefixes, you heal for 15% of all damage dealt.

**When to use:** Best when you have Vampiric on your weapon. Avoid if you find no sustain -- Barbarian without healing burns through health potions too fast.

## 4. Scorcher Firestorm

The Scorcher firestorm build turns entire rooms into infernos. This is the fastest room-clearing build in Bonehold but demands perfect positioning.

**Core concept:** Ignite enemies, spread fire through kills, stack burn damage exponentially.

- **Weapon:** Staff (Flaming or Volatile prefix).
- **Ideal prefixes:** Flaming (staff), Volatile (staff or gloves), Wildfire (chest), Cinder (accessory).
- **Spell scrolls:** Fireball (AoE), Combustion (detonate all burns), Inferno (room-wide fire zone).
- **Playstyle:** Open with Inferno, spam Fireball from range, detonate burns with Combustion when enemies cluster. Never stop moving.

**Prefix stacking tip:** Wildfire causes burning enemies to spread fire to nearby enemies when they take damage. In dense rooms, one well-placed Fireball can ignite an entire group, which then chain-reaction kills itself.

**When to use:** Excellent for farming lower floors quickly. Less effective against single targets unless you have single-target scrolls as backup.

## 5. Death Knight (Knight + Cross-Class Hybrid)

The Death Knight hybrid combines Knight's durability with necromantic spell scrolls for a unique summoner-tank playstyle. This build requires finding the right scrolls but rewards with unmatched battlefield control.

**Core concept:** Summon minions to tank while you debuff enemies with necromantic magic.

- **Weapon:** One-handed sword (Cursed prefix for debuff on hit).
- **Shield:** Any with Deflecting prefix.
- **Ideal prefixes:** Cursed (weapon), Necromantic (accessory), Fortress (chest), Siphoning (gloves).
- **Spell scrolls:** Raise Skeleton (summon), Bone Armor (defense), Decaying Touch (enemy debuff), Soul Harvest (heal from nearby deaths).
- **Playstyle:** Summon skeletons to draw aggro, curse dangerous enemies with Decaying Touch, fight alongside your minions. Replace skeletons as they fall.

**Prefix stacking tip:** Siphoning + Necromantic causes your minions' attacks to heal you. A full skeleton squad provides constant passive healing, freeing you to focus on positioning and debuff application.

**When to use:** Whenever you find Raise Skeleton early. This build only works if you get the right necromantic scroll drops.

## Adapting Builds to What Drops

Roguelikes demand flexibility. Here is how to pivot when the RNG gods offer something unexpected:

- **Found a legendary item?** Build around it. Legendary items in Bonehold are rare power spikes with unique effects that can redefine your run. Even if it does not match your class, the legendary bonus often outweighs class synergy.
- **No good weapon prefix?** Focus on armor prefixes instead. A Sturdy + Fortress defensive setup works with any weapon.
- **Too many scrolls of one school?** Pivot to that school. Spell scroll scaling in Bonehold is good enough that a scroll-focused build can carry you to the final boss.
- **Early floor offering poor options?** Save your gold. Shops on later floors offer better gear, and dying with unspent gold contributes nothing to meta-progression.

The best Bonehold players are not the ones with perfect build plans -- they are the ones who adapt fastest to what the dungeon provides.
