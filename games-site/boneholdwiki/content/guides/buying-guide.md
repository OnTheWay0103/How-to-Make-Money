# Bonehold Price & Buying Guide - Cost, Sales, Bundles, and DLC

Bonehold is a budget-priced roguelike with a well-organized store page — and knowing how the pricing works can save you real money. This guide covers the base price, the confirmed bundles on the Steam store page, what DLC actually exists, and the honest buying math for 2026.

**Honesty note:** prices change. The figures below reflect launch-period pricing (July–August 2026) and the store-page bundles confirmed at launch. Always check the current Steam page before buying — regional pricing means your price may differ.

| Item | Price | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| Bonehold (full game) | **~$9.99 USD** / ¥41.86 (CN listing) | Confirmed at launch; regional pricing varies by store |

**What that means:** at under $10, Bonehold sits in the impulse-buy band of the Steam catalog — the same territory as many popular roguelikes. The 10% launch discount has ended, but the game's price point is low enough that the difference is small.

## The Bundles (Confirmed on the Steam Store Page)

Bonehold's store page lists **19 bundles** — verified by our launch coverage. Buying one of these with the game is often cheaper than buying Bonehold alone, and several bundle partners are genuinely great games. The standouts:

| Bundle | Partner Game | What You Get |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Baronehold** | Barony | Bonehold + Barony — the classic roguelike pairing; reported 10–16% off |
| **Mortal Bone** | Mortal Sin | Bonehold + Mortal Sin — dark fantasy pair |
| **Mask and Bone** | Verho – Curse of Faces | Bonehold + Verho — both dark-fantasy dungeon crawlers |
| **Eyes of the ElderWood bundle** | Eyes of the ElderWood | Bonehold + Pixel Jackal's other game, 10% off both — the same-studio bundle |

**Bundle math:** if you want *any* of the partner games, the bundle is the cheapest way to own both. If you only want Bonehold, bundles can still come out ahead on Steam's bundle pricing (you pay the game's price minus the discount). Check the bundle prices — they are listed per-bundle and change with sales.

## DLC: What Actually Exists

The DLC situation is refreshingly simple (confirmed by the site FAQ):

- **No microtransactions.** No battle passes, no in-game purchases, no cosmetics store.
- **One DLC: the official soundtrack.** The only paid add-on for the game.
- **No expansion packs, season passes, or content DLC** as of August 2026 (unverified) — future updates can always change this).

If you love the game's dark, hand-painted-meets-pixel atmosphere, the soundtrack is the natural companion purchase. If you only care about gameplay, the base game is the complete gameplay package.

## How to Buy at the Best Price

1. **Check deal trackers.** Bonehold appears on deal aggregators; wishlist it and wait for a sale if the timing is bad. Roguelikes in this price band go on sale regularly, and the store page's 19 bundles mean frequent bundle-triggered discounts.
2. **Watch for bundle-triggered discounts.** When any of the 19 bundle partners goes on sale, the bundle price adjusts — buying Bonehold *through* a discounted bundle can beat its standalone sale price.
3. **Try the demo first.** It is free, and a few hours in the fortress answers the "should I buy" question better than any guide — see the [Demo Guide](/guides/demo-guide).
4. **Use Steam's refund policy if unsure.** Steam's standard 2-hours-played / 14-day refund window is a platform fact that applies here: buy, play, and refund if the charge combat or trap pacing isn't for you.
