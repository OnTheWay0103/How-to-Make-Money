## What is Bonehold?

Bonehold is a 3D roguelike dungeon crawler ARPG developed and published by Pixel Jackal, released on July 28, 2026 on Steam. You choose from 7 unique classes and descend into an ancient necromantic fortress filled with skeletons, ghosts, and magical abominations. Each run features procedurally generated dungeons, weapons, spells, and armor — no two descents are the same.

The game combines fast-paced hack-and-slash combat with roguelike permadeath tension. Weapons use a charge mechanic that rewards timing and positioning over button mashing. Items have randomized prefixes that can dramatically alter playstyles, and legendary drops can turn an ordinary run into something extraordinary. With 98 Steam achievements and deep build variety, Bonehold offers dozens of hours of replayability.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **7 Classes** — Knight, Barbarian, Scorcher, Spellblade are officially named; the other 3 are unconfirmed at launch.
- **Charge Combat** — Weapons use charge mechanics. Timing and positioning matter more than speed.
- **Procedural Generation** — Dungeon layouts, loot, enemies, and traps are randomized every run.
- **Item Prefixes** — Weapons, spells, and armor roll random prefixes that can transform playstyles.
- **Legendary Items** — Rare drops with game-changing effects. Each one is a potential run-definer.
- **Spells** — Collectible spell loot that adds magic abilities to any class build.
- **Single-Player** — No co-op or multiplayer; Windows only, no controller support at launch.
- **98 Achievements** — Confirmed Steam achievement count (the list itself is not yet officially published).
