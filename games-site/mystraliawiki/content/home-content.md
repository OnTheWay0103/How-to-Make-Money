## What is Echoes of Mystralia?

Echoes of Mystralia is an action-heavy spellcrafting ARPG roguelite developed by Borealys Games, the studio behind Mages of Mystralia (2017). You play as Mazarim, a Watcher protecting the Cycle of Memories from a mysterious Rift threatening to consume the world.

The game's defining feature is the spell crafting system — combine the game's 7 elements (fire, ice, and lightning among them) with Memories harvested from defeated enemies. The placement of components within a spell's grid determines the output, enabling millions of unique combinations.

Each run sends you through procedurally varied regions filled with enemies, culminating in Guardian boss fights. Between runs, return to the Azimuth Temple to unlock permanent Lotus upgrades, customize your spell loadout, and build relationships with fellow Watchers.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Spell Crafting** — 16 starting spells × 7 elements × 100+ Memories = millions of combinations
- **100+ Memories** — harvested from enemies and combined into spells (50 in the demo)
- **40+ Relics** — passive items confirmed (per the official Steam page)
- **Lotus Progression** — permanent upgrade system with 50+ Altar upgrades
- **3 Regions** — the Ash Forest is the only one named so far
- **Guardian Bosses** — one guarding each region heart; Therakan is the one confirmed
- **Hybrid Spell Crafting** — component placement changes what a spell does (fire next to ice becomes steam, per official material)
