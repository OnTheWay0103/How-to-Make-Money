## Dungeon Guide — Echoes of Mystralia

Echoes of Mystralia's dungeons are handcrafted challenges that blend combat, puzzle-solving, and exploration. Each dungeon has unique mechanics, multiple boss encounters, and hidden rooms containing rare rewards. This guide covers every major dungeon, their puzzle mechanics, boss strategies, and the optimal spell loadouts for each.

### Azimuth Temple (Tutorial Dungeon)

The first dungeon serves as a tutorial for Mystralia's core mechanics:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** The temple uses elemental switches that must be hit with the matching element. Fire switches need fire spells, ice switches need ice spells, and lightning switches need lightning spells.
  - **Tip:** The final puzzle room requires hitting three switches simultaneously. Use the Orb spell form to maintain pressure on one switch while you manually hit the others.
- **Boss: Guardian Construct**
  - A stone golem with two attack patterns: a slow punch and a ground slam.
  - **Strategy:** The Construct is weak to lightning. Equip a Lightning Bolt with Amplify and Hasten for this fight. Dodge the ground slam by jumping — the shockwave travels along the ground.
  - **Reward:** Fire Element Memory (first Memory unlock)
- **Hidden Rooms:**
  - Room 1: Behind a false wall in the first puzzle room. Contains a Common Mana Potion.
  - Room 2: After the boss room, to the left of the exit. Contains a second spell form scroll.

### Whispering Catacombs

An underground necropolis filled with undead enemies:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** Pressure plates that must be weighed down. Use the Orb spell form to hold plates while you cross. Some plates require multiple orbs or physical objects.
  - **Tip:** The Mine spell form can also trigger pressure plates. Plant a Mine on the plate and it will stay active for 10 seconds.
- **Boss: Bone Lord**
  - A skeletal mage that summons undead adds and fires bone projectiles.
  - **Phase 1:** Standard caster fight. Dodge bone projectiles and kill adds.
  - **Phase 2:** The Bone Lord becomes a giant skeleton with melee attacks. The room fills with darkness — use fire spells to create light.
  - **Recommended Loadout:** Fire Nova (for adds and light), Ice Wall (to block projectiles), Lightning Bolt (boss DPS)
  - **Reward:** Persist Memory, Bone Key (opens locked chest in dungeon)
- **Hidden Rooms:**
  - Room 1: A cracked wall in the third corridor. Destroy with any spell to reveal a chest with a Rare Memory.
  - Room 2: Behind a pressure plate puzzle — requires holding two plates simultaneously while a third player (or a well-placed Orb) hits the door switch.
  - Room 3: After the boss, a hidden passage to the left of the exit stairs.

### Frozen Depths

An ice cavern with slippery floors and freezing hazards:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** Ice bridges that shatter after a few seconds. You must cross quickly using speed-boosting spells or chain ice blocks together with the Wall form.
  - **Tip:** The Wall spell form with Ice element creates permanent ice platforms that can extend bridges.
- **Boss 1: Frost Troll**
  - A large troll with a freeze aura. Stay at range. Fire spells deal 40% bonus damage.
- **Boss 2: Ice Queen**
  - A spellcaster that creates ice storms and freezing chains. The chains link players together — spread out to minimize chain damage.
  - **Phase 1:** Ranged combat. Dodge ice spikes and break chains.
  - **Phase 2:** The Queen freezes the floor — movement becomes slippery. Use the Rift teleport to reposition.
  - **Recommended Loadout:** Fire Nova (boss damage), Ice Wall (platform building), Lightning Arc (chain clear)

### Magma Core

A volcanic dungeon with rising lava mechanics:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** Lava flow management. Some rooms have lava that rises over time. You must activate cooling crystals to lower it. Other rooms have lava rivers that must be crossed using ice platforms.
- **Boss 1: Lava Elemental**
  - Immune to fire, weak to ice. A large elemental that splits into smaller elementals at 50% HP. Use Ice Nova to clear the split copies.
- **Boss 2: Fire Drake**
  - A dragon that breathes fire and creates lava pools. Stay mobile and use ice spells to remove lava pools.
- **Recommended Loadout:** Ice Shard (boss DPS), Ice Wall (platforming and lava removal), Lightning Bolt (secondary DPS)

### Storm Spire

A vertical dungeon climbing through a storm-battered tower:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** Electrical conduit puzzles. You must connect power conduits by placing conductive Memories in the correct sequence. Lightning spells charge conduits faster than other elements.
- **Boss 1: Storm Elemental**
  - Immune to lightning, weak to ice. A flying enemy that summons lightning strikes. Stay spread out.
- **Boss 2: Thunder Giant**
  - A giant that creates shockwaves and calls lightning strikes on random players. Requires precise positioning.
- **Recommended Loadout:** Ice Lance (boss DPS), Lightning Arc (conduit activation), Fire Vortex (add clear)

### Shadow Keep

A haunted castle with complex illusion mechanics:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** Illusion walls and mirrors. Some walls are illusions that can be walked through — the Glimmer Moth effect (Light + ice) reveals them. Mirrors must be rotated to reflect light beams onto switches.
- **Boss 1: Phantom Knight**
- **Boss 2: Shadow Mage**
- **Boss 3: The Dark Lord**
- **Recommended Loadout:** Light-based spells (fire + ice hybrids), Arc (for mirror puzzles), Nova (close-quarters combat)

### Celestial Archive

A library floating among the clouds:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** Constellation matching — align star patterns by placing Memories in specific grid positions. Some puzzles require creating specific spell effects to match the constellation's element.
- **Boss 1-3:** Three celestial guardians, each representing an element.
- **Recommended Loadout:** Bring spells of every element. The Archive demands versatility.

### The Void Rift

The final dungeon with reality-shifting mechanics:

- **Puzzle Mechanics:** The arena shifts between three reality states (Fire Realm, Ice Realm, Lightning Realm). Your spells change element to match the current realm.
- **Boss 1-4:** Four void aspects. The final boss is the Void Entity.
- **Recommended Loadout:** Balanced all-element build. The Rift spell form is particularly effective here.

### General Dungeon Tips

1. **Adapt your loadout.** Each dungeon has different elemental strengths and weaknesses. Check the dungeon's theme before entering.
2. **Explore thoroughly.** Hidden rooms contain Memories and gear that cannot be found anywhere else.
3. **Save before bosses.** Campfires before boss rooms let you change your loadout. Use them.
4. **Learn puzzle patterns.** Puzzles follow consistent logic. Once you understand the pattern, subsequent puzzles become trivial.
5. **Bring a healing Memory.** At least one Leech Memory per loadout ensures you sustain through attrition.
