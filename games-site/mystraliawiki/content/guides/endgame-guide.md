# Endgame Guide — What Comes After the Three Regions

What is the endgame of Echoes of Mystralia? At Early Access, the honest answer is: **the confirmed content is the 3 regions plus Guardian and Elite encounters, and the endgame is the loop itself** — collection, crafting mastery, and permanent progression — until Borealys ships the post-launch content it has planned. This guide explains what "endgame" means for this game at Early Access, what's confirmed, and what's planned for later.

Echoes of Mystralia entered **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026** with a confirmed scope of **3 regions, 7 magical elements, 16 starting spells, 40+ Relics, 100+ Memories, and 25 Steam achievements** (per the official Steam page).

## What's Confirmed: The Core Loop Is the "Endgame"

For a roguelite, the endgame isn't a separate mode — it's the loop itself, repeated at higher power:

1. **Run through regions** — 3 at launch, more planned during Early Access
2. **Harvest Memories** — 100+ at launch; the crafting resource drives all progression
3. **Collect Relics** — 40+ at launch; passive items that shape your build
4. **Craft better spells** — the developer describes the space as "millions of possible combinations"; your build improves with every Memory
5. **Defeat Guardian and Elite encounters** — the boss content of the launch regions
6. **Spend the Lotus** — 50+ Altar upgrades at the Azimuth Temple grow permanently between runs
7. **Earn the 25 Steam achievements** — a confirmed collection goal for the launch build

In other words: the endgame at launch is **mastery** — discovering the real spellcrafting meta, completing your Memory and Relic collections, earning the achievements, and pushing your builds as far as the systems allow.

## The Realistic Early Access Endgame

Based on confirmed facts, here's what post-region play looks like at launch:

### 1. Memory & Relic Collection

With 100+ Memories and 40+ Relics at launch, completing your collections is the natural long goal. Harvest from every enemy type, compare notes with other players, and identify which Memories and Relics make the strongest builds. (Specific drop systems are being mapped from the launch build.)

### 2. Build Refinement

The crafting system's combination space is the deepest part of the game. Post-region play is when players map it: documenting what each Memory does, which arrangements produce which spells, and what the strongest loadouts are. This guide network will cover those findings as they're verified in the launch build.

### 3. Speed and Mastery

Roguelite players set their own goals: faster clears, no-death runs, one-spell challenges. The demo's **4 fight challenges** preview the self-improvement loop that post-launch content will extend.

### 4. Achievements

**25 Steam achievements** ship with Early Access (per the official Steam page) — a confirmed checklist for completionists.

### 5. Content Updates

Borealys has promised gameplay-focused updates throughout Early Access. New regions, Memories, Relics, spells, puzzles, and boss fights will keep arriving — the endgame at launch is the foundation for what comes next.

## How to Prepare

1. **Beat everything in the launch build** — clear the Ash Forest, defeat Therakan, finish the fight challenges, and push through all 3 regions
2. **Learn the crafting system** — endgame mastery is crafting mastery
3. **Set realistic expectations** — don't believe claims about endgame modes Borealys hasn't announced
4. **Plan to iterate** — the first EA patch may rebalance what you learned; the studio already changed progression once
