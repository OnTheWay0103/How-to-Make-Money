# Crafting & Materials Guide — Memories, Consumables & Upgrades

Every ARPG player eventually asks the same three questions: what can I craft, what materials do I need, and where do I find them? In Echoes of Mystralia the honest answers are: **one confirmed crafting system (spellcrafting), one confirmed material family (Memories), and a farming map built on the Ash Forest, the fight challenges, Elites, and Guardians.** Potions, consumables, and gear-upgrade crafting are **not announced** — this guide marks exactly what is confirmed, what is honest expectation, and what to test on day one of Early Access (**August 11, 2026**).

> **EA Launch note:** Anything marked **TBD** has not been confirmed by Borealys Games, and anything marked **[EA — subject to change]** depends on the launch build.

## The Only Confirmed Crafting System: Spellcrafting

When Borealys talks about crafting, they mean **spell crafting** — the confirmed systems are:

1. **Harvest Memories** from defeated enemies
2. **Forge and customize spells** from those Memories
3. **Reorder and combine** Memories to change spell behavior
4. **Place components deliberately** — where you place each component in your spell grid changes what the spell does (fire next to ice becomes steam; lightning behind a homing component chases enemies)
5. **"Millions of possible combinations"** — the developer's own description of the design space

There is **no confirmed item-crafting bench** for weapons, armor, or trinkets. If you see a "crafting recipe" list online before launch, it is speculative — the only crafting with an official source is spellcrafting. See the [Spell Crafting Guide](/guides/spell-crafting-guide) for the full system breakdown.

## The Materials: Memories

If spellcrafting is the crafting, **Memories are the materials** — the raw resource of everything you build:

| Memory fact | Detail | Status |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| Acquisition | Harvested from defeated enemies — every enemy type can drop them | ✅ Confirmed |
| Demo pool | 50 Memories | ✅ Confirmed |
| EA launch pool | 110 Memories | ✅ Confirmed |
| Rarity tiers | Unannounced — treat "rare Memory" lists as fiction | **TBD** |
| Individual effects | No official Memory list exists | **TBD** |
| Drop locations | Not published; will be mapped from the EA build | **TBD** |

One material warning carries over from the demo: **Echo Fragments, the demo currency, reset to zero at launch.** Anything you hoarded is gone by design — the launch economy is unannounced, so plan for currency costs to be **[EA — subject to change]**.

## Potions & Consumables — The Honest TBD

Do potions exist in Echoes of Mystralia? **Borealys has not published any healing or consumable system.** The [How to Heal Guide](/guides/how-to-heal-guide) covers this in depth; the short version is that healing items, regeneration, and pickups are all unconfirmed. What is confirmed is the survival framework: dodging and positioning are the core defense, and the **Azimuth Temple** hub is the safe space between runs where resources reset.

**Test these in the first session of EA:**

1. Do enemies or the environment drop healing items? (**TBD**)
2. Can Memories produce healing or defensive spells? (**TBD**)
3. Does the hub fully restore HP between runs? (Expected: yes — **TBD**)
4. Does out-of-combat regeneration exist? (**TBD**)

Until those answers land, treat survivability as a crafting problem — a control spell that keeps enemies away is as valuable as any potion.

## Gear & Equipment Upgrades — Also TBD

There is **no equipment to upgrade** — no armor, no accessories, no enhancement system has been announced. "Upgrading gear" in Echoes of Mystralia means two confirmed things instead:

- **Permanent skill upgrades at the Azimuth Temple** — the confirmed power sink between runs
- **Better crafted spells** — upgrading your loadout through Memory combinations

For the full honest picture of item systems, see the [Legendary Gear Guide](/guides/legendary-gear) — and expect the first week of EA to reveal whether Borealys added any item-adjacent system that was not announced ([EA — subject to change]).

## Where the Materials Come From (Farming Locations)

The confirmed farming map at launch:

| Source | What it gives | Reliability |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **The Ash Forest** (region one) | Memory drops from every enemy; full-region clears | ✅ Confirmed source, safe farm |
| **The 4 fight challenges** (demo, carry structure into EA) | Repeatable, scripted encounters; steady Memory income | ✅ Confirmed in demo |
| **Elite fights** (EA launch) | Multiple confirmed at launch; tougher enemies expected to reward better | ✅ Confirmed to exist; rewards **TBD** |
| **Guardians** (Therakan + 2) | The biggest single targets; first-kill rewards not itemized | ✅ Confirmed to exist; tables **TBD** |

The farming principles from the [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide) apply directly: kill everything (every enemy is a material opportunity), clear speed beats grinding duration, diversify early, and bank progression between runs.

## Material Tips That Survive Launch

1. **Craft, don't hoard** — unused Memories do nothing; invested Memories teach you the system
2. **One spell per element** — fire, ice, and lightning coverage farms more situations than a single element
3. **A/B test in the fight challenges** — compare two Memory arrangements on the same encounter and keep the winner
4. **Spend upgrades between runs** — the hub is the confirmed power sink that makes farming pay off
5. **Keep notes on drops** — you will be among the first to map the launch economy; your notes have real value
