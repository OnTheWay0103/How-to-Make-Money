# FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Echoes of Mystralia is now live in **Steam Early Access** (launched **August 11, 2026 at 15:00 UTC**). This FAQ covers the questions players ask most — release, price, content, progression, and the spell crafting system. Answers are drawn from official sources (the Steam page and developer announcements); genuinely unconfirmed items are marked as such.

## General Questions

### What is Echoes of Mystralia?

Echoes of Mystralia is a **spellcrafting ARPG roguelite** developed and published by **Borealys Games** (Montreal, founded 2014), the studio behind *Mages of Mystralia* (2017). You play as **Mazarim, a Watcher**, protecting the **Cycle of Memories** from a mysterious **Rift** that threatens to consume Mystralia. Its defining feature is **memory-based spell crafting** — harvest Memories from enemies, then reorder and combine them to create your own spells, in a space the developer describes as "millions of possible combinations."

### When is the release date?

**Steam Early Access launched August 11, 2026** (15:00 UTC). Early Access is planned to last roughly a year before the 1.0 release (per developer announcements), with more content, spells, puzzles, boss fights, a new region, and the final narrative act arriving during that period.

### What platforms will it be on?

**PC via Steam.** It is a single-player game. Additional platforms have not been announced.

### What is the price?

Echoes of Mystralia is a **paid Early Access title on Steam**. The price was announced alongside the Early Access announcement (July 17, 2026), and the developer has said the price is **expected to rise at the 1.0 release** (per developer announcements). Check the Steam page for the current price.

### Is there multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** Echoes of Mystralia is a single-player game; no co-op or multiplayer has been announced. The hub features fellow Watchers, but as characters you bond with, not co-op partners.

### How long is the game?

**No official playtime figures exist.** Based on the confirmed scope — 3 regions, Guardian and Elite encounters, 16 starting spells, 100+ Memories — a multi-session Early Access experience is expected, but concrete hour counts depend on your playstyle and are being documented by players.

## Spell Crafting Questions

### How does spell crafting work?

The confirmed fundamentals: defeated enemies drop **Memories**; you **forge and customize spells** from them by **reordering and combining**; the result is a spell loadout of your own design — from lightning bolts to freezing tornadoes and fire vortices. The game ships with **7 magical elements, 16 starting spells, and 100+ Memories** (per the official Steam page); the demo taught the basics with 4 spells and 50 Memories.

### How many spell combinations are possible?

Borealys Games describes the space as **"millions of possible combinations."** With 16 starting spells, 7 elements, and 100+ Memories, the combination space is enormous — the exact math is being explored by players in the Early Access build.

### What are the best spells for beginners?

No legitimate full tier list exists yet — rankings need real play data, and balance will change during Early Access. Start with the 4 spells you know from the demo, and keep **elemental coverage** (the game has 7 elements) until real match-ups are documented.

## Progression Questions

### What carries over from the demo to Early Access?

**Demo save data transfers to Early Access** (per developer announcements). Borealys reworked progression systems between the demo and EA, so not everything behaves identically — the exact itemized transfer list has not been published. See the [Demo Save Transfer Guide](/guides/demo-save-transfer) for details.

### Do I lose everything when I die?

As a roguelite, runs reset, but **permanent progression exists**: the **Lotus** at the Azimuth Temple hub provides permanent upgrades (**50+ Altar upgrades** at launch, per the official Steam page) between runs.

### Is the "Lotus" real?

**Yes.** The Lotus is the game's confirmed permanent progression system (per the official Steam page) — the meta-progression that survives failed runs, spent at the Azimuth Temple hub. (Earlier drafts of this wiki denied the Lotus existed; that was an overcorrection and has been corrected.)

### Are there "Relics"?

**Yes.** **40+ Relics** ship with Early Access (per the official Steam page) — passive items that modify your build alongside your crafted spells. See the [Relics Guide](/guides/relics-guide) for details. (Earlier drafts of this wiki claimed Relics were unconfirmed; that was an overcorrection and has been corrected.)

## Content Questions

### How many regions are there?

**3 regions at Early Access launch** (per the official Steam page). The Ash Forest is the only one officially named; it was the demo region. Additional regions are expected to arrive during Early Access.

### How many bosses are there?

The Early Access launch includes **Guardian and Elite encounters** across its 3 regions (per the official Steam page). **Therakan** — the game's first Guardian, from the demo — is the only boss officially named so far. Other Guardian and Elite encounters are being documented from the build.

### What content ships with Early Access?

Per the official Steam page: **3 regions, 7 magical elements, 16 starting spells, 40+ Relics, 100+ Memories, 50+ Altar upgrades, hidden puzzles**, the **Lotus** permanent progression system, and **25 Steam achievements** — plus Guardian and Elite encounters.

### Will there be more content after Early Access?

**Yes.** The developer has committed to roughly a year of Early Access with gameplay-focused updates — more content, spells, puzzles, boss fights, a new region, and the final narrative act before 1.0, when the price is expected to rise (per developer announcements). Specific update contents and dates are announced by the developer as they land.

### Is the demo progress carried over to Early Access?

**Yes — demo save data transfers to Early Access** (per developer announcements). See the [Demo Save Transfer Guide](/guides/demo-save-transfer) and the [EA Launch Guide](/guides/ea-launch-guide).

## Early Access Policy Questions

### Can I refund it if I don't like it?

Steam's standard Early Access refund policy applies: **refundable within 14 days of purchase if you have under 2 hours of playtime**. This is Steam policy, not a Borealys commitment.

### Will the price change for 1.0?

**Expected to rise** — the developer has stated the Early Access price is expected to increase at the 1.0 release (per developer announcements).

### How do I follow announcements?

The **Steam store page** (wishlist + news), the **official Discord** (linked from the Steam page), and the **Borealys newsletter** are the developer's confirmed channels. Patch notes and community events are announced there.
