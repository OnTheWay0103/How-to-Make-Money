# Exploration Guide — Regions, Secrets & What's Confirmed

Echoes of Mystralia is a game about exploring the lands of Mystralia — a world the developers describe as **haunted by the past, with peoples scarred by war and exploitation**. This guide covers what's confirmed about exploration in the game, what the demo teaches, and what remains honestly unknown.

> **Pre-release note:** Echoes of Mystralia enters Steam Early Access on **August 11, 2026**. Anything marked **TBD** has not been confirmed by Borealys Games.

## Confirmed Exploration Structure

- **Runs through regions** — exploration happens inside runs, through procedurally varied territory
- **3 regions at launch** — the Ash Forest is the only one named and playable (demo)
- **Regions end in Guardian fights** — the boss gate at each region's heart
- **The Azimuth Temple hub between runs** — the safe space where runs are prepared
- **Enemies drop Memories** — the exploration economy: everything you fight feeds crafting

## What the Demo Confirms About Exploration

The Ash Forest demo is the only playable map, and it demonstrates the exploration fundamentals:

1. **Regions are looped spaces, not straight lines** — expect branching paths and choices
2. **Combat and exploration interleave** — enemies guard the space between points of interest
3. **The run structure rewards efficiency** — what you find and harvest shapes how far you go
4. **The boss arena is the endpoint** — Therakan's arena closes the loop

## Secrets and Hidden Areas — The Honest State

Players love hidden content, and this wiki previously listed elaborate "secret rooms" with specific rewards — **that content was fabricated and has been removed.** What's actually known:

- Borealys has **not documented any secret rooms, hidden bosses, or optional areas** for Echoes of Mystralia
- The demo *may* contain hidden content — nothing official has been said either way (**TBD**)
- Region secrets will be discovered and documented by players after launch — this guide will be updated with verified findings

## Exploration Tips That Apply Regardless

These are general principles that hold for any region until specifics are confirmed:

1. **Check off-path routes** — roguelite regions routinely hide rewards in dead ends
2. **Harvest from every enemy** — Memories drop from defeated foes; skipping fights skips crafting material
3. **Learn the region before optimizing** — first clear is for learning; later clears are for speed
4. **Watch the environment** — the Ash Forest's burnt terrain is alive with hazards and cues
5. **Use the hub between runs** — upgrades make exploration safer, which makes it faster
6. **Keep element coverage** — one spell per element keeps you ready for anything
