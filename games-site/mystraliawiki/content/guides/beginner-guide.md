# Beginner Guide — Getting Started in Echoes of Mystralia

Welcome to **Echoes of Mystralia**, the spellcrafting ARPG roguelite from Borealys Games, the studio behind the award-winning *Mages of Mystralia* (2017). The game is now live in **Steam Early Access** (launched **August 11, 2026**). This guide covers what the game is, how its core systems work, what the free demo taught players, and a plan for your first hours in the Early Access build.

## What Is Echoes of Mystralia?

Echoes of Mystralia is a **spellcrafting action-RPG roguelite** set in the same world as *Mages of Mystralia*. You play as **Mazarim, a Watcher**, sworn to protect the **Cycle of Memories** — and the World's Memory itself — from a mysterious **Rift** that threatens to consume the land of Mystralia.

The key facts:

- **Developer / publisher:** Borealys Games (Montreal, Canada; founded 2014)
- **Sequel to:** Mages of Mystralia (2017), whose award-winning spellcrafting system returns in evolved form
- **Release:** Steam Early Access launched **August 11, 2026** (announced July 17, 2026, after the game passed 175,000 Steam wishlists); the game was first announced at gamescom 2024
- **Platform:** PC via Steam (single-player)
- **Early Access plan:** roughly a year of content updates before 1.0, with the price expected to rise at full release (per developer announcements)

### The Core Gameplay Loop

Between runs you return to the **Azimuth Temple**, the game's hub. There you level up and spend the **Lotus** — the permanent progression system with **50+ Altar upgrades** — and bond with fellow Watchers. Each run sends you through regions of Mystralia fighting **Rift-spawned foes** and "**Shadows of the Past**," culminating in Guardian and Elite encounters.

### The Memory Spellcrafting System

The defining feature of the game is **memory-based spell crafting**:

- You **harvest Memories from defeated enemies**
- Memories are used to **forge and customize spells** — you reorder and combine them to create your own arcane skills
- The developer describes the space as **"millions of possible combinations"** — from lightning bolts to freezing tornadoes and fire vortices
- A single Memory's placement and combination can completely change how a spell behaves

The Early Access build ships with **16 starting spells, 7 magical elements, and 100+ Memories** (per the official Steam page). The demo contained 4 spells, 3 of the elements, and 50 Memories.

## Your First Steps in Early Access

### 1. Re-Establish Your Foundation

If you played the demo, your save transfers (per developer announcements) — but the launch sandbox is much bigger. Re-clear the Ash Forest once to re-learn movement, dodging, and harvesting rhythm with the full system.

### 2. Understand the Three Layers of a Spell

Everything about spellcrafting fits into three layers:

| Layer | Role | Status |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Starting spells** | The base templates of your loadout (16 at EA launch) | Demo showed 4; full names/behaviors being mapped |
| **Elements** | 7 magical elements at launch, including the fire, ice, and lightning shown in the demo | Exact interactions being mapped |
| **Memories** | Modifiers harvested from enemies, reordered to change spell output | 50 in demo, 100+ at launch; effects being mapped |

### 3. Collect Memories and Experiment

Kill enemies and collect their Memories, then try every combination you can. Because Memories are **reordered and combined** into spells, the same set of Memories can produce very different spells depending on arrangement — experimenting is the fastest way to learn the system.

### 4. Bank Progression at the Azimuth Temple

The hub is where Watchers **level up, bond with fellow Watchers, and spend the Lotus** — the permanent progression system (50+ Altar upgrades). Upgrade after every run: banked progression does nothing; invested progression does.

### 5. Beat Therakan

Therakan is the first Guardian and the Ash Forest's capstone. Demo-verified advice: learn the tells (his attacks are telegraphed), manage space, bring your best-crafted spells, and don't overcommit.

## 10 General Tips for New Players

1. **Play the demo first** — it's free on Steam, the save transfers (per developer announcements), and it teaches the fundamentals
2. **Experiment with Memory placement** — reordering Memories changes spells; try every arrangement
3. **Harvest Memories from every enemy type** — all enemies can drop Memories
4. **Collect Relics as you go** — the 40+ Relics add a passive layer to your build
5. **Use the Azimuth Temple between runs** — the Lotus is where long-term power lives
6. **Learn boss tells** — Therakan's fight challenges your pattern recognition; watch the wind-ups
7. **Keep elemental coverage** — with 7 elements at launch, broad coverage beats narrow specialization while match-ups are unmapped
8. **Join the community** — Borealys is running a community-first Early Access with active feedback channels
9. **Read official sources for updates** — the Steam page and Borealys' channels announce changes during Early Access
10. **Don't trust unverified wikis that overclaim** — verify specifics in-game or against official announcements (this guide does)
