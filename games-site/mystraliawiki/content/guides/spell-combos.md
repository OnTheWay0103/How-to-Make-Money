# Spell Combos & Memory Arrangement — The Crafting Depth Guide

The spellcrafting system of Echoes of Mystralia is the game's identity: **harvest Memories from enemies, then reorder and combine them to forge and customize your own spells.** Borealys Games describes the combination space as **"millions of possible combinations"** — which makes combo-crafting the deepest skill in the game. This guide covers what's confirmed about how combos work and gives you a framework for discovering the real ones at launch.

> **Pre-release note:** Echoes of Mystralia enters Steam Early Access on **August 11, 2026**. Specific combo recipes, "fusion" mechanics, and Memory effects are **TBD** — earlier recipe tables on this wiki were fabricated and removed.

## Confirmed Combo Fundamentals

Three facts anchor everything:

1. **Memories are reordered and combined** — the same set of Memories can produce different spells depending on arrangement
2. **Elements shape outcomes** — fire, ice, and lightning are the element trio, and showcased spells include **lightning bolts, freezing tornadoes, and fire vortices** — the same form system producing very different results
3. **The space is huge** — 12 spell forms and 110 Memories at launch (4 and 50 in the demo) with reorderable arrangements = the developer's "millions"

## What the Demo Confirms by Play

The demo is a 4-spell, 50-Memory lab. Verified-by-play observations:

- **Arrangement changes behavior** — rebuilding one spell with the same Memories in different orders produces noticeably different results
- **Elements are loadout choices** — fire, ice, and lightning loadouts feel meaningfully different in combat
- **Defensive crafting is possible** — the demo's Memory pool supports control- and survival-oriented spells alongside pure damage
- **The 4 fight challenges reward combo testing** — repeatable encounters make A/B testing cheap

## Showcased Spell Directions (Official Material)

Borealys' own examples give three combo families to expect:

| Family | Showcase example | Direction |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Lightning combos** | Lightning bolts | Fast, direct damage |
| **Ice combos** | Freezing tornadoes | Control, area denial |
| **Fire combos** | Fire vortices | Sustained area damage |

These are *examples*, not meta — the true combination space will be mapped by players after launch.

## A Framework for Discovering Combos at Launch

When Early Access goes live, use this experiment loop:

### 1. Change One Variable at a Time
Start from a spell you understand, then change one Memory (or its position) and test. This isolates what each change does — the fastest way to learn any crafting system.

### 2. Test in the Fight Challenges
Repeatable, low-stakes encounters are the perfect combo lab. Bring candidate spells, compare performance, iterate.

### 3. Keep Loadout Notes
The combination space is too large for memory alone. Document: form + Memories + arrangement → observed behavior. Community spreadsheets will likely emerge within the first week.

### 4. Respect the Resource Economy
A combo is only as good as its cost. Test whether a flashy spell is sustainable across a full region run, not just one fight.

### 5. Share and Compare
Borealys is running a community-first Early Access with active feedback channels — the fastest combo discoveries will come from player discussion.
