# Secret Areas & Hidden Content — What's Confirmed

Secret areas are a beloved part of the *Mages of Mystralia* heritage — the original game was full of hidden paths and optional content. For **Echoes of Mystralia**, the honest state of secrets is: **Borealys Games has not announced any hidden areas, secret bosses, or optional zones.** Earlier versions of this guide described six "secret areas" with puzzles, coordinates, and legendary rewards — **that content was fabricated** and has been removed.

> **Pre-release note:** Echoes of Mystralia enters Steam Early Access on **August 11, 2026**. Anything marked **TBD** has not been confirmed.

## What's Confirmed

- The game world is the **lands of Mystralia**, described by the developer as **haunted by the past** — a strong foundation for hidden lore content, but nothing specific
- Runs explore **3 regions at launch** (the Ash Forest being the only named one)
- Enemies include **"Shadows of the Past"** — narrative echoes that could pair naturally with secret content (speculation, not confirmation)
- The demo's Ash Forest is playable **now** — the only place real secrets could exist today

## What the Demo Teaches (Verified)

Play the demo with a secret-hunter mindset and you'll practice the skills that matter:

1. **Check off-path rooms** — roguelite regions regularly hide rewards in dead ends
2. **Look for environmental oddities** — broken walls, unusual terrain, and out-of-place objects
3. **Replay with different loadouts** — some features only appear with specific spells or Memories (a common design pattern; not confirmed here)
4. **Listen and observe** — audio and visual cues mark points of interest in well-designed levels

If the demo contains hidden content, players will find and document it — and this guide will be updated with verified findings.

## Why Pre-Launch Secret Guides Are Unreliable

Any guide that lists named secret areas with exact locations and rewards before launch is inventing content:

- Borealys has published no map, no area list beyond the Ash Forest, and no secret documentation
- The EA build may differ from the demo build (already true for progression systems)
- Secret content is, by definition, discovered after release — not announced before it

Trust only findings you can verify in-game.
