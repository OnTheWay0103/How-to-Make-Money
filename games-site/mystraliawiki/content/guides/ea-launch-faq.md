# EA Launch FAQ — Price, Content & Roadmap Questions for August 11

Echoes of Mystralia hits **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**, four days from this guide's update. If you're deciding whether to buy, what to expect on day one, or how Early Access works, this is the launch-focused FAQ: price status, confirmed launch content, roadmap plans, save transfer, and Steam's rules. Answers are drawn from official announcements only — anything unconfirmed is marked **TBD**.

> **Pre-release note:** Borealys Games has not yet published a price, an exact launch time, or a dated roadmap. This FAQ will be updated the moment those details land.

## Launch Basics

### When exactly does Early Access launch?

**August 11, 2026**, on Steam. The exact unlock time of day has **not been announced (TBD)** — Steam typically unlocks at a single global time, but nothing is confirmed. Wishlist the page and Steam will notify you.

### How much does it cost?

**Not announced (TBD).** The demo is free. Borealys has not confirmed whether the Early Access price will be discounted versus the eventual 1.0 release — many studios raise the price for full release, but that is not confirmed here. Watch the Steam page and official channels.

### Can I refund it if I don't like it?

Steam's standard Early Access refund policy applies: **refundable within 14 days of purchase if you have under 2 hours of playtime**. This is Steam policy, not a Borealys commitment — the game itself is single-player with no confirmed special refund terms.

### Is it multiplayer?

**No.** Echoes of Mystralia is a single-player game; no co-op or multiplayer has been announced. The hub's fellow Watchers are characters, not partners.

### What platforms?

**PC via Steam** at launch. Console versions haven't been announced, though Borealys has expressed interest in expanding post-launch.

## What's Included at Launch

### What content ships on August 11? (Confirmed)

| Content | Early Access launch | Demo |
|---------|---------------------|------|
| Regions | **3** | 1 (The Ash Forest) |
| Guardians (bosses) | **3**, one per region | 1 (Therakan) |
| Spell forms | **12** | 4 |
| Memories | **110** | 50 |
| Elite fights | ✅ Multiple (press release) | The 4 fight challenges |

The three launch regions are confirmed to progress in difficulty. Only the first — **The Ash Forest**, with Guardian **Therakan** — has been named and shown.

### What's NOT in Early Access at launch?

- **More than 3 regions** — additional regions are expected during Early Access (TBD)
- **Console versions** — unannounced
- **The full story** — the final narrative act is planned for the 1.0 update (TBD details)
- **A finished endgame** — Borealys has said completing the end-game experience is part of the Early Access plan

### What is the story about?

You play **Mazarim, a Watcher**, tasked with protecting the **Cycle of Memories** — the World's Memory itself — from a **Rift** that threatens to consume everything. You explore the lands of Mystralia, meet its lost souls, and face the **Shadows of the Past** across three regions patrolled by primordial Guardians.

## Save Transfer & Progression

### Does my demo save carry over?

**Yes — confirmed.** Demo save data transfers to Early Access. One confirmed exception: **Echo Fragments, the demo currency, reset to zero** because the progression systems were reworked between demo and EA. Exactly what else transfers (Memories, spells, region progress) has not been itemized (**TBD**). See the [Demo Save Transfer Guide](/guides/demo-save-transfer) for details.

### Is it a roguelike or roguelite?

**Roguelite.** You lose progress on death but keep some upgrades and Memories, making each run a step toward a stronger build. Permanent progression lives at the **Azimuth Temple** hub between runs.

### How long is Early Access?

**TBD.** Borealys has said the core experience and main adventure will be fully playable at launch, with Early Access used to finalize development alongside the community — gameplay-focused updates, balance tuning from player data, and the endgame completion are the stated priorities. No 1.0 date has been announced.

## Roadmap & Updates

### What is the roadmap?

There is **no dated roadmap** — Borealys has committed to a direction, not a schedule:

1. **Gameplay-focused updates** throughout Early Access — new content and system improvements
2. **Balance tuning** based on player feedback and data (the studio has already reworked progression once, between demo and EA)
3. **Completing the endgame** experience during Early Access
4. **The final narrative act**, arriving with or before the 1.0 release (TBD)
5. **Community-driven development** — official Discord, private playtests, and feedback channels

### Will the price change for 1.0?

**TBD.** No pricing policy has been announced. The studio's stated approach — Early Access as the "foundation" with a 1.0 that adds content — leaves room for a price change, but nothing is confirmed. Buying at EA launch is the only price you can rely on today.

### How do I follow announcements?

The **Steam store page** (wishlist + news), the **official Discord**, and the **Borealys newsletter** are the developer's confirmed channels. Patch notes and community events are announced there.
