# First 5 Hours — Beginner Progression Plan for Early Access

Echoes of Mystralia launches on **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**, and your first session sets the tone for everything after: the demo save transfer, the expanded crafting sandbox (12 spell forms, 110 Memories), and the confirmed progression systems. This guide is a hour-by-hour plan for your **first five hours** — what to do, what to skip, and what to bank. It's written for launch-day reality, where some details (exact drop rates, Memory effects) are still **TBD** and will be discovered by you and everyone else in week one.

> **Pre-release note:** Hour estimates are based on the confirmed launch scope and demo pacing. If you played the demo, your save transfers — so hours 1–2 compress dramatically.

## Before You Start (5 Minutes)

1. **Confirm your demo save is ready** — the save transfer prompt appears at first launch; accept it. One confirmed exception: Echo Fragments reset to zero (by design).
2. **Set your expectations** — launch has 3 regions, 3 Guardians, 12 spell forms, and 110 Memories. Region one is the familiar Ash Forest.
3. **Don't look for a meta** — no legitimate tier lists exist yet (see the [Tier List guide](/guides/tier-list)). Your first hours are for learning, not optimizing.

## Hour 1 — Re-Establish Your Foundation

**Goal: get comfortable with the full combat and crafting sandbox.**

- **Re-clear the Ash Forest once** — even with a transferred save, one familiar run re-establishes movement, dodging, and harvesting rhythm
- **Open the crafting interface and compare** — the demo taught you 4 forms and 50 Memories; launch adds 8 forms and 60 new Memories. Rebuild your demo loadouts with the new options
- **Harvest from every enemy type** — Memories are the resource that fuels everything; treat every kill as a pickup
- **Try the 4 fight challenges** — they're repeatable, safe, and the best way to test the new forms without risk

**Skip:** farming currency (the demo currency reset; the launch economy is TBD). **Bank:** Memory knowledge and the feel of the new forms.

## Hour 2 — Master the Crafting Loop

**Goal: understand how placement changes behavior.**

The confirmed core: **"where you place each component in your spell grid changes what the spell actually does"** — fire next to ice becomes steam, lightning behind a homing component chases enemies.

- **Run one-variable experiments** — take a spell you understand, move one Memory, and test. Record the difference
- **Push one spell in offense, then defense** — feel how the same form changes with different Memories
- **Keep element coverage** — one spell per element (fire, ice, lightning) in your loadout while match-ups are unknown
- **Spend Memories, don't hoard them** — unused Memories do nothing; invested Memories teach you the system

**Skip:** hunting for "optimal" builds. **Bank:** notes on what each Memory change does — you'll be among the first to map the system.

## Hour 3 — Bank Permanent Progression

**Goal: turn runs into lasting power at the Azimuth Temple.**

The hub is where Watchers **develop their skills, change their combat approach, and consult a millennia-old dragon** — it's the confirmed home of permanent progression.

- **Upgrade after every run** — banked progression does nothing; invested progression does
- **Prioritize what stops you** — dying to bosses? Prefer defensive/survival upgrades. Running out of steam? Prefer clear-speed options
- **Spend broadly early** — before the systems are mapped, broad upgrades beat narrow specialization
- **Talk to the characters** — the hub has a confirmed character layer with fellow Watchers; interaction may unlock options (details TBD)

**Skip:** grinding one region for hours before upgrading. **Bank:** the habit of upgrading between every run — it's the roguelite muscle you'll rely on all game.

## Hour 4 — Push Into Harder Content

**Goal: test yourself against elites and the region's pressure points.**

- **Re-run the fight challenges at full loadout** — elite-style pressure with safe retries
- **Explore the Ash Forest thoroughly** — secrets, hidden areas, and world events reward the curious (see the [Exploration Guide](/guides/exploration-guide))
- **Meet the region's Elite fights** — multiple Elite fights are confirmed at launch; they're the gate between you and the Guardian
- **Watch your death economy** — failed runs still bank upgrades and Memories, so aggressive attempts are fine

**Skip:** save-scumming or resetting on death — the roguelite loop is designed for runs to end. **Bank:** pattern knowledge of elites.

## Hour 5 — The Therakan Milestone

**Goal: beat the first Guardian and unlock the path to region two.**

Therakan is the first primordial Guardian (demo boss, confirmed for launch). Demo-verified advice:

1. **Learn the tells** — his attacks are telegraphed; watch the wind-ups, then dodge
2. **Manage space** — keep distance when unsure, close in only during openings
3. **Bring your best-crafted spells** — a tuned loadout beats a default one every time
4. **Don't overcommit** — greedy casting after a dodge ends runs

If you beat him early: you've confirmed the Guardian loop and opened region two (name TBD). If not: the hub upgrades and Memory collection from hours 3–4 are the exact levers the game expects you to pull.

**Skip:** obsessing over first-kill rewards — Guardian drop tables are **TBD**. **Bank:** the Guardian pattern template — it applies to every boss after this.

## The First-Week Arc

| Hours | Focus | Milestone |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1–2 | Combat + crafting fundamentals | Loadout rebuilt with launch forms |
| 3 | Hub progression habit | First permanent upgrades banked |
| 4 | Elites and full-region clears | Region one pressure points mapped |
| 5 | Guardian attempt | Therakan down (or a plan to return) |
| 6–10 | Region two + deeper crafting | Second region opened and explored |

## Common First-Session Mistakes

1. **Hoarding Memories** — the system rewards experimentation, not accumulation
2. **Playing with default spells** — crafting IS the game; ignoring it leaves most of your power on the table
3. **Sticking to one element** — keep coverage until real match-ups are documented
4. **Skipping the hub** — permanent upgrades are the confirmed answer to every wall
5. **Trusting pre-launch tier lists** — week-one community rankings will be wrong somewhere; your own notes are worth more
