# Challenge & Difficulty Modes — What We Know So Far

Echoes of Mystralia is a **spellcrafting ARPG roguelite** from Borealys Games, entering Steam Early Access on **August 11, 2026**. As a roguelite, it has an inherent loop of escalating runs — but the game does **not** currently have a confirmed "Challenge Mode," leaderboards, or any official difficulty system announcement. This guide covers what's confirmed, what's reasonable to expect, and what remains TBD.

> **Pre-release note:** Earlier drafts of this guide described a challenge mode with mutators and leaderboards. That content was **not supported by any official source and has been removed**. Borealys has announced nothing about challenge systems.

## What Is Confirmed About Difficulty and Replay

### The Roguelite Loop (Confirmed)

The game's structure itself is a form of challenge progression:

- Runs are **procedurally varied** through the regions of Mystralia
- Between runs you return to the **Azimuth Temple** hub to upgrade **permanent skills**
- Defeated enemies drop **Memories** — the crafting resource that makes you stronger over time
- Each region ends in a **primordial Guardian** boss fight

That means difficulty growth comes from two confirmed directions: **your permanent progression** (hub upgrades) and **the escalating regions** (3 at Early Access launch).

### The Demo's "Fight Challenges" (Confirmed)

The free demo includes **4 fight challenges** — repeatable, scripted combat encounters that function as a practice/arena mode. These are the closest thing to a confirmed "challenge" feature today:

- They are repeatable and free to retry
- They train elite-level combat and boss-style discipline
- They are an excellent way to test Memory combinations

## How to Create Your Own Challenges (Today)

Until official modes exist, the demo lets you self-impose challenges — useful for prepping for Early Access:

1. **Low-Memory runs** — complete the Ash Forest using a minimal Memory loadout
2. **No-damage Guardian attempts** — beat Therakan without being hit
3. **Speed clears** — time your Ash Forest full runs and try to improve
4. **One-spell runs** — clear the region using a single crafted spell
5. **Fight-challenge gauntlets** — clear all 4 challenges in one session without using the hub between them

These mirror the self-imposed challenges the roguelite community loves and cost nothing to try.
