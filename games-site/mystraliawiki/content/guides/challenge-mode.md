# Challenge Mode Guide — Mutators, Rewards & Leaderboards

Challenge Mode is Mystralia's endgame gauntlet. Unlocked after completing the main story (Chapter 10), it lets you replay any zone or dungeon with **mutators** that modify enemy behaviour, your stats, and the rules of engagement. The harder the mutators you stack, the higher your score multiplier — and the better your rewards.

---

## How Challenge Mode Works

- **Access:** Speak to **Grandmaster Vex** at the Challenge Hub in the capital city (any chapter after the main story).
- **Format:** Select a zone, then choose up to **5 mutators**. You have 30 minutes to clear the zone.
- **Scoring:** Base score + kill count × multiplier. Bonus points for speed, no deaths, and full exploration.
- **Leaderboards:** Separate weekly and all-time leaderboards for each zone. Rewards are distributed every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

## Mutator Table

| Mutator | Effect | Score Multiplier | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glass Cannon** | Your HP is reduced to 1. Any hit is lethal. | ×3.0 | Extreme |
| **Time Warp** | Enemies move and attack 40% faster. | ×2.0 | Hard |
| **Elemental Storm** | Random elemental damage strikes the arena every 10 seconds. | ×1.5 | Medium |
| **Armor Lock** | You cannot wear any armor or amulet. Only weapons and rings allowed. | ×2.5 | Hard |
| **No Healing** | Healing flasks and regeneration effects are disabled. | ×2.0 | Hard |
| **Boss Rush** | Only boss rooms appear; standard enemies removed. | ×2.5 | Hard |
| **Darkness** | Visibility reduced to a 5-meter radius around your character. | ×1.5 | Medium |
| **Cursed Ground** | You take 5% max HP damage per second while standing still. | ×1.5 | Medium |
| **Reverse Controls** | Movement and camera controls are inverted. | ×2.0 | Hard |
| **One Life** | If you die, the run ends immediately. No revives. | ×2.5 | Extreme |

## Best Mutator Combinations

### For Score (Leaderboard Push)

Maximum multiplier without making the run impossible:

| Combo | Mutators | Total Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed Demon** | Time Warp + Glass Cannon + Boss Rush | ×7.5 |
| **Survivor** | One Life + No Healing + Armor Lock | ×7.0 |
| **Elementalist** | Elemental Storm + Cursed Ground + Darkness | ×4.5 |

**Speed Demon** (×7.5) is the highest-scoring viable setup. It requires:
- A fully upgraded **Stormbringer** or **Primeval Catalyst**
- **Tidecaller's Boots** for the dodge bonus
- **Band of Endless Mana** for infinite skill spam
- Memorisation of every boss pattern (one hit = death)

### For Farming (Consistent Rewards)

Maximise repeatable runs with minimal risk:

| Combo | Mutators | Total Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| **Easy Farm** | Darkness + Cursed Ground | ×3.0 |
| **Steady Gains** | Elemental Storm + No Healing | ×3.5 |
| **Safe Rush** | Time Warp (only) | ×2.0 |

**Easy Farm** (×3.0) is the recommended starter combo. Use it to farm Legendary Shards and upgrade materials until you outgear the difficulty.

## Weekly Challenge Rotation

The game cycles through **5 weekly challenge types**:

| Week | Theme | Modifier | Bonus Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | **Assassination** | All enemies are elite versions; bosses have double HP | 3× Legendary Shards |
| Week 2 | **Survival** | Infinite enemy waves; survive 15 minutes | **Exclusive Weapon Skin** |
| Week 3 | **Speed Run** | 10-minute time limit; enemies have 50% less HP | **Mount Speed Boost** (permanent) |
| Week 4 | **Elemental Fury** | All damage converted to random element each minute | **Elemental Reroll Token** |
| Week 5 | **Gauntlet** | All 5 mutators are pre-selected (random pool) | **Legendary Gear Voucher** |

> Check the **Challenge Board** in the capital every Monday to see the current week's theme.

## Leaderboard Tips

1. **Run the shortest zone** — The **Arcane Ruins** zone can be cleared in 4 minutes with Boss Rush + Glass Cannon. It's the most efficient leaderboard farm.
2. **Mana sustain is everything** — Without Band of Endless Mana, runs longer than 5 minutes run out of steam. Prioritise mana regen relics and rings.
3. **Learn enemy spawn patterns** — Every Challenge Mode zone has fixed spawn points. Memorise them for Time Warp runs where hesitation costs you HP.
4. **Save your 30-minute window** — Practice the route without mutators first. Only use the 30-minute attempt when you can consistently clear the zone in under 2 minutes clean.
5. **Don't ignore defensive items** — Even in Glass Cannon runs, the **Seal of the Guardian** ring gives you one mistake buffer per 90 seconds. It's worth the ring slot.
