# EA Roadmap & What to Expect — Early Access Launch

Echoes of Mystralia enters **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026** — the spellcrafting ARPG roguelite from Borealys Games, the studio behind Mages of Mystralia. With over 175,000 wishlists and a free demo already out, it's one of the most anticipated indie launches of late summer. Here's everything we know about what ships at Early Access, what comes after, and how the demo save transfers.

## What Ships on August 11

At Early Access launch, the game includes the full core experience:

| Content | EA Launch | Demo |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Regions | **3** (Ash Forest, Frozen Depths, Stormspire) | 1 (Ash Forest) |
| Guardians (bosses) | **3** (one per region) | 1 (Therakan) |
| Spell forms | **12** | 4 (Bolt, Shard, Arc, Nova) |
| Memories | **110** | 50 |
| Relics | **30+** | 15 |
| Endless / post-region modes | Included per region | Limited |

The three launch regions progress in difficulty: **Ash Forest** (burning woodland, Guardian: Therakan) is the beginner region, **Frozen Depths** (icy caverns) the intermediate, and **Stormspire** (lightning-charged tower) the hardest. Each has its own enemy roster, elemental flavor, and a Guardian guarding its heart.

## Demo Save Transfer — What Carries Over

Borealys Games has confirmed **demo save data transfers to Early Access**. There's one catch:

- ✅ **Your demo save carries over** — collected Memories, unlocked spell forms, Relics, Lotus upgrades, and progress through Ash Forest all transfer.
- ⚠️ **Echo Fragments reset to zero**. The demo's Echo Fragment currency is being retired because the progression systems changed between demo and EA. Don't hoard them expecting value at launch — the reset is by design.

**Practical advice**: finish the demo run you're on before August 11 if you want the cleanest transfer, and don't spend hours farming Echo Fragments in the last week — they won't come with you.

## What's NOT in Early Access (Yet)

- **More than 3 regions** — additional regions are planned for later in Early Access.
- **Full release content** — the EA roadmap includes new Memories, spell forms, Relics, and Guardians throughout the cycle.
- **Console versions** — currently PC (Steam) only. PS5, Xbox, and Switch 2 remain unconfirmed, and would follow the full PC release if greenlit.

## The Early Access Roadmap

Borealys Games has committed to **gameplay-focused updates throughout Early Access**. Based on developer statements, expect:

1. **Post-launch content patches** — new Memories and Relics to expand the crafting pool, plus balance tuning based on player data.
2. **More spell forms** — the 12 at launch are the foundation; additional forms are expected as the game evolves.
3. **Region and Guardian additions** — new regions follow the existing three, each with its own Guardian.
4. **Difficulty and endgame systems** — expect deeper endgame content as the meta matures.
5. **A full release** — no date has been announced yet. The studio has said Early Access will run until the vision is complete, with the full release expected later in 2026.

## Early Access Price & Platform

- **Price**: not officially announced; expect $15–$25 USD based on comparable indie ARPG roguelites. No microtransactions have been mentioned — it's a buy-once title.
- **Platform**: Windows PC via Steam, built on Unity, with **full controller support** (Steam Deck playable).
- **Languages**: English, French, Italian, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazilian), Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish (Latin America) — a strong sign of the studio's international ambitions.

## How to Prepare Before August 11

1. **Play the demo** — your save transfers. Time in Ash Forest is time you don't have to repeat at launch.
2. **Collect Memories, not Echo Fragments** — Memories persist; the currency resets.
3. **Learn the crafting system now** — the demo teaches Memory positioning with 4 spell forms; launch doubles the forms and adds 60 Memories. The skill transfers, even if the save didn't need to.
4. **Beat Therakan** — the first Guardian is in the demo; defeating him before launch means your first EA session can start on Frozen Depths prep instead of Ash Forest grinding.
