# Difficulty & Settings Guide — The Real Curve, No Menu

Does Echoes of Mystralia have difficulty options? **No difficulty selector has been announced** — no Easy/Normal/Hard menu exists in any official material. Instead, the difficulty is **designed into the structure**: an action-heavy combat model, three regions that progress in difficulty, and a roguelite loop where death resets a run but permanent progression (the **Lotus**) at the Azimuth Temple makes each subsequent run easier. This guide covers what is confirmed about difficulty and settings, the real difficulty knobs you control, and the self-imposed challenge runs players use.

Echoes of Mystralia is live in **Steam Early Access** (launched **August 11, 2026**).

## Does It Have Difficulty Options? (Honest Answer)

**No difficulty levels have been announced** — no Easy mode, no difficulty slider, and no accessibility difficulty options. The game's difficulty is instead communicated through its design:

- The combat is officially described as **"action-heavy"** — rapid, fast-paced, and tactical, rewarding quick reflexes and precise movement
- **The three launch regions progress in difficulty** — the first (the Ash Forest) is the tutorial-shaped entry, and the later regions are harder by design
- **Death is a loop, not a failure** — runs end, you return to the Azimuth Temple hub, and Lotus upgrades there make future runs easier

Whether Borealys adds difficulty settings during Early Access is unannounced — nothing has been promised.

## The Real Difficulty Knobs (Confirmed)

Since there is no difficulty menu, these are the levers the game actually gives you:

1. **The Lotus** — spend permanent progression at the **Azimuth Temple** between runs; this is the confirmed answer to any wall (50+ Altar upgrades at launch)
2. **Spell crafting quality** — your crafted loadout determines your effective power more than anything else; tuned Memory combinations outperform default spells
3. **Elemental coverage** — the game has 7 elements at launch; broad coverage handles more situations while match-ups are unmapped
4. **Practice in the fight challenges** — the 4 repeatable challenges from the demo are a zero-risk place to learn elite-style fights and test loadouts
5. **Route discipline** — entering a boss arena at full HP with a clean run is a huge advantage; treat earlier fights as an HP economy

In roguelite terms: **the game's difficulty is tuned around your progression**, so a player who invests Lotus upgrades and crafting knowledge experiences a much gentler curve than one who rushes.

## Self-Imposed Challenges (Until Official Modes Exist)

Until official challenge systems are announced, players create their own:

1. **Low-Memory runs** — complete the Ash Forest using a minimal Memory loadout
2. **No-damage Guardian attempts** — beat Therakan without being hit
3. **Speed clears** — time your Ash Forest full runs and try to improve
4. **One-spell runs** — clear the region using a single crafted spell
5. **Fight-challenge gauntlets** — clear all 4 challenges in one session without using the hub between them

## Performance: What You Can Verify

- **Minimum specs** are published on the Steam store page (see the [System Requirements Guide](/guides/system-requirements) for the full table, attributed to the Steam page)
- **Particle-heavy spell effects** are the main GPU stressor — dense spell stacks are exactly where frame dips appear, so test the fire vortices and lightning-heavy fights in the demo
- If the demo runs comfortably on your machine, the EA build's baseline should behave similarly — with the caveat that the launch build adds content and may change performance

## Steam Deck Status

**Steam Deck compatibility is not officially confirmed.** The positive signs: full controller support is confirmed, and the minimum specs on the Steam page sit within Steam Deck hardware territory. The definitive test is the same as any PC — run the free demo in Desktop Game Mode and watch the particle-heavy fights. No verification badge has been published.

## Difficulty Will Change During EA

Two confirmed reasons the difficulty feel will shift after launch:

1. **Balance tuning is a stated Early Access priority** — Borealys will tune from player data and feedback
2. **The progression systems have already been reworked once** — between the demo and Early Access; expect further changes to how quickly you power up

Plan accordingly: what feels hard in week one may not be what feels hard in month three. Guides like this one will be updated as the curve moves.
