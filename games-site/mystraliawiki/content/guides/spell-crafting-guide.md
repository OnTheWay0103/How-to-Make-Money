# Spell Crafting Guide — Elements, Starting Spells & Memory Crafting

The **spell crafting system** is the heart of Echoes of Mystralia. Instead of choosing spells from a list, you **build your own spells from components** — harvesting Memories from enemies and combining them into custom spells. This guide covers everything confirmed about the system at Early Access: the 7 magical elements, the 16 starting spells, how Memories work, and the hybrid spell directions shown in official material.

Echoes of Mystralia entered **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**. Details below that come from official sources (the Steam page and developer announcements) are stated directly; genuinely unconfirmed mechanics are marked as such.

## What's Confirmed About the System

Borealys Games has officially described:

1. **You harvest Memories from defeated enemies** — Memories are the raw material of all magic
2. **You forge and customize spells from Memories** — spells are player-constructed, not selected
3. **You reorder and combine Memories** — arrangement is part of the design space
4. **The developer describes the design space as "millions of possible combinations"** — the studio's own marketing language
5. **Showcased examples** — lightning bolts, freezing tornadoes, and fire vortices show the range one system can produce
6. **It evolves the award-winning spellcrafting of Mages of Mystralia** (2017) — the studio's own description

## The 7 Elements

Echoes of Mystralia launches with **7 magical elements** (per the official Steam page). The elements are woven through the Memories you craft: a Memory carries an element property, and how you arrange Memories changes what your spells do. **Fire, ice, and lightning** are the elements shown in the demo and in official trailers — showcased as **fire vortices**, **freezing tornadoes**, and **lightning bolts** — but the launch build ships with seven elements total. The full list of seven, and exactly how each element behaves in combat, is being mapped by players in the Early Access build.

**Do NOT assume a rock-paper-scissors counter system.** Borealys has not published an elemental match-up table. Earlier drafts of this wiki claimed "fire beats ice enemies, ice beats lightning, lightning beats fire" — **that table was never confirmed and has been removed.** Until elemental weaknesses are verified in the EA build, treat element choice as a playstyle decision, not a counter-picking minigame.

## The 16 Starting Spells

Early Access launches with **16 starting spells** — the base templates your crafted loadout is built on (the demo contained 4). These are the skeletons: each one is a starting point, and the Memories you combine onto a spell determine its behavior.

The developer's description is explicit about how components combine: **"where you place each component in your spell grid changes what the spell actually does."** Confirmed examples from official material:

- Move an ice component next to a fire component and the spell becomes **steam**
- Place a lightning component behind a **homing** component and the attack **chases enemies**
- Showcased results include **thunderbursts, freezing tornadoes, and fire vortices** — the same templates producing radically different spells

Individual spell names and exact behaviors beyond the showcased examples are being documented from the EA build — treat any full list of "16 spells with exact stats" with suspicion until verified in-game.

## Memories: The Crafting Resource

Memories are the components you harvest and combine:

- **Acquisition:** defeated enemies drop Memories — every enemy type can drop them
- **Arrangement matters:** the same set of Memories in different arrangements produces different spells
- **Launch pool:** **100+ Memories** (the demo contained 50)
- **Rarity tiers:** the game's rarity structure is being documented from the launch build; treat pre-launch "rare Memory" lists as fiction

Individual Memory names, effects, and drop locations are not published by the developer in a single catalog — the community is mapping them from the Early Access build.

## Hybrid Crafting Directions (Official Material)

Borealys' own examples give three crafting families to expect:

| Family | Showcase example | Direction |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Lightning crafting** | Lightning bolts / thunderbursts | Fast, direct damage |
| **Ice crafting** | Freezing tornadoes | Control, area denial |
| **Fire crafting** | Fire vortices | Sustained area damage |

These are *examples*, not a meta — the true combination space is being mapped by players now that the game is live.

## How to Learn the System

1. **Start with the 4 demo-era spells you know** — if you played the demo, your muscle memory transfers
2. **Run one-variable experiments** — take a spell you understand, change one Memory (or its position), and test in the fight challenges
3. **Keep notes** — the combination space is too large to remember; document form + Memories + arrangement → observed behavior
4. **Test in the 4 fight challenges** — repeatable, low-stakes encounters are the perfect combo lab
5. **Balance damage and survival** — the demo rewarded rounded loadouts, and Early Access launch balance is still being tuned

## The Mages of Mystralia Connection

For players of the original: *Mages of Mystralia* (2017) let you combine runes with magical properties into custom spells — a system that earned honors at SXSW and PAX South 2017 and was written by **Ed Greenwood**. Borealys explicitly describes Echoes of Mystralia as **evolving that award-winning spellcrafting**. Fans of the original will recognize the philosophy: **the player is the designer.**

## Crafting Tips That Survive Patches

1. **Document your experiments** — the combination space is too large to remember; take notes
2. **Build around what you find** — early discovery beats meta-chasing in a fresh sandbox
3. **Expect balance changes** — Borealys has already reworked systems once (between demo and EA) and will tune during Early Access; adapt
