# Spell Crafting Guide — The Confirmed System Breakdown

The **spell crafting system** is the heart of Echoes of Mystralia. Instead of choosing spells from a list, you **build your own spells from components** — and the developer's marketing promise is "millions of possible combinations." This guide covers everything Borealys has actually confirmed about the system, what the demo teaches, and what remains honestly unknown.

> **Pre-release note:** Echoes of Mystralia enters Steam Early Access on **August 11, 2026**. Specific mechanics marked **TBD** are not confirmed — earlier version tables on this wiki (form grids, slot positions, exact modifier effects) were fabricated and removed.

## What's Confirmed About the System

Borealys Games has officially described:

1. **You harvest Memories from defeated enemies** — Memories are the raw material of all magic
2. **You forge and customize spells from Memories** — spells are player-constructed, not selected
3. **You reorder and combine Memories** — arrangement is part of the design space
4. **The result is "infinite variety"** — the developer cites **"millions of possible combinations"**
5. **Showcased examples** — lightning bolts, freezing tornadoes, and fire vortices show the range one system can produce
6. **It evolves the award-winning spellcrafting of Mages of Mystralia** (2017) — the studio's own description

## The Three Layers

Everything fits into three layers:

| Layer | Role | Confirmed details |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| **Spell forms** | The base template of a spell | 12 at EA launch; 4 in the demo. Names/behaviors TBD |
| **Elements** | The element of the spell | Fire, ice, lightning shown across official material. Interactions TBD |
| **Memories** | The components you harvest and combine | 50 in demo; 110 at launch. Individual effects TBD |

## What the Demo Teaches (Verified by Play)

The demo is a complete mini-sandbox of the system: **4 base spells, 50 Memories, 4 fight challenges, one region.**

### 1. Harvesting Is the First Skill
Every enemy can drop Memories. The demo trains the habit: fight, harvest, rebuild.

### 2. Arrangement Changes Behavior
The single most important lesson — **the same Memories in different arrangements produce different spells.** Experiment with every combination the demo allows.

### 3. Elements Shape Playstyle
Building fire, ice, and lightning loadouts feels genuinely different in the demo. Try all three before launch.

### 4. Crafting Is Iterative
The demo's repeatable challenges make rebuilding cheap. The workflow — cast, observe, adjust — is the real skill that transfers to the full game.

## How to Learn the System Before Launch

1. **Play the demo** — it's free, and your save transfers
2. **Keep one spell per element** — coverage while match-ups are unknown
3. **Push arrangements** — deliberately rebuild spells in unusual orders and note the differences
4. **Test in the fight challenges** — they're designed for exactly this
5. **Read official channels** — Borealys' Steam page and Discord are the reliable sources for system reveals

## What's TBD (Not Confirmed)

- **Form grids and slot mechanics** — how Memory placement is visualized (grids, sequences, or something else) is unannounced
- **Memory effects** — no official Memory list exists
- **Hybrid element crafting** — mixing fire/ice/lightning within one spell is implied by "millions of combinations" but not documented
- **Rarity systems** — unannounced
- **Crafting costs** — no launch currency economy is published (Echo Fragments reset at launch)
- **Loadout saving** — expected, unconfirmed

## The Mages of Mystralia Connection

For players of the original: *Mages of Mystralia* (2017) let you combine runes with magical properties into custom spells — a system that won awards (Best of PAX South 2017, among others) and was written by Ed Greenwood. Borealys explicitly describes Echoes of Mystralia as **evolving that award-winning spellcrafting**. Fans of the original will recognize the philosophy: **the player is the designer.**

## Crafting Tips That Will Survive Launch

1. **Document your experiments** — the combination space is too large to remember; take notes
2. **Build around what you find** — early discovery beats late meta-chasing in a fresh sandbox
3. **Balance damage and survival** — the demo rewards rounded loadouts; expect the full game to as well
4. **Expect balance changes** — Borealys has already reworked systems between demo and EA; adapt
