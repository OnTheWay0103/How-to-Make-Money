# Farming Guide — Memories, Relics & Efficient Grinding in Early Access

"Gearing up" in Echoes of Mystralia means **growing your Memory collection, collecting Relics, and spending Lotus progression** — there is no weapon or armor loot layer (see the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide)). The confirmed resources at Early Access are: **100+ Memories** for spellcrafting, **40+ Relics** as passive items, and the **Lotus** permanent progression system with **50+ Altar upgrades** at the Azimuth Temple hub. This guide covers the early-game farming loop: where confirmed resources come from, which sources are worth repeating, and how to turn everything you gather into lasting power.

Echoes of Mystralia entered **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**.

## What You're Actually Farming (Confirmed)

| Resource | What it is | Confirmed source |
|----------|------------|------------------|
| **Memories** | Components that forge and customize spells | Dropped by defeated enemies — "harvest them from every enemy type" |
| **Relics** | Passive items that modify your build | 40+ at launch; sources being mapped from the EA build |
| **Crafting knowledge** | The arrangement skill that turns Memories into power | Experimentation; nothing replaces it |
| **Permanent upgrades** | Lotus Altar upgrades at the Azimuth Temple | Earned by runs; spent at the hub between runs |

Note: the demo and the Early Access build differ — Borealys reworked progression systems between them (per developer announcements), so anything you learned in the demo sandbox should be re-verified in the launch build.

## Where to Farm in the Early Game

### 1. The Ash Forest (Region One)

The first region is your reliable baseline farm — familiar, tutorial-shaped, and full of enemies:

- **Full-region clears** — every enemy is a Memory opportunity; clear deliberately, not blindly
- **Re-run it with purpose** — once your loadout outpaces the region, you can farm it fast while it's still the safest source of drops
- **Harvest everything** — all enemies can drop Memories; there's no confirmed reason to skip any enemy type

### 2. The 4 Fight Challenges

The demo's fight challenges are the single best repeatable source of practice and steady income:

- **Repeatable and safe** — scripted, retryable, zero run-loss risk
- **Elite-style pressure** — they preview the Elite fights confirmed for launch; the skill you gain IS farm value
- **Perfect A/B testing ground** — compare two Memory arrangements on the same encounter and keep the winner

### 3. Elite Fights

**Elite encounters are confirmed at Early Access launch.** The expectation — not yet confirmed — is that tougher enemies reward better:

- **Kill every elite you meet** — they're the highest-value single targets in the early game
- **Prepare before engaging** — elites are the gate between you and region progress; bring a tuned loadout
- **Re-fight them** — roguelite structure typically rewards repeated clears; exact rewards are being mapped

### 4. Guardian Fights (Therakan and Beyond)

The first Guardian is the region's capstone and the biggest single target in the early game:

- **First-kill rewards are not itemized** — but Guardian kills are the confirmed path forward
- **Failed attempts still pay** — enemies on the way to the arena drop Memories you keep; a dead run is not a wasted run
- **Pattern knowledge is the real drop** — the Guardian loop applies to every boss encounter after this

## Crafting & Materials — The Honest State

When Borealys talks about crafting, they mean **spell crafting** — the confirmed systems are:

1. **Harvest Memories** from defeated enemies
2. **Forge and customize spells** from those Memories
3. **Reorder and combine** Memories to change spell behavior
4. **Place components deliberately** — where you place each component in your spell grid changes what the spell does (fire next to ice becomes steam; lightning behind a homing component chases enemies)

There is **no confirmed item-crafting bench** for weapons, armor, or consumables. **Potions and healing consumables have not been announced** — see the [How to Heal Guide](/guides/how-to-heal-guide) for the honest state of HP recovery. What is confirmed as "crafting" is spellcrafting, and its materials are Memories.

## Farming Principles (Demo-Verified, Safe at Launch)

1. **Kill everything, always** — every enemy is a Memory opportunity; there's no confirmed reason to skip any
2. **Clear speed beats grinding duration** — a fast, comfortable region clear farms more per hour than slow, risky clears of harder content
3. **Diversify early** — you don't know which Memories and Relics matter yet; sample widely before specializing
4. **Keep notes on drops** — you'll be among the first to map drop behavior in Early Access; your notes have real value
5. **Bank progression between farms** — spend Memories on crafting and Lotus upgrades as you go; the hub is the power sink that makes farming pay off
6. **A/B test in the fight challenges** — verify which loadout farms faster before committing

## How to Convert Farmed Memories Into Power

The loop that makes farming worthwhile:

1. **Craft, don't hoard** — unused Memories do nothing; invested Memories teach you the system
2. **Tune one spell at a time** — change one component (or its position) per test; the confirmed rule is that placement changes behavior
3. **Keep elemental coverage** — the game has 7 elements at launch; broad coverage farms more situations than a single-element loadout
4. **Upgrade at the hub after every farm run** — the Lotus is the point of the grind
