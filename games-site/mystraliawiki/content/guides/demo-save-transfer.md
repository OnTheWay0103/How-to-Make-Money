# Echoes of Mystralia Demo Save Transfer — Does Progress Carry to Early Access?

The short answer: **yes — your demo save data transfers to Early Access**, which launches on **August 11, 2026**. There is one confirmed catch: **Echo Fragments, the demo's currency, reset to zero**. Everything else about exactly what carries over has not been itemized by Borealys Games yet.

This guide breaks down what's confirmed, what's still **TBD**, and how to make sure your save transfers cleanly on launch day.

> **Pre-release note:** This page is based on developer statements made before launch. We will verify every point against the live Early Access build after August 11, 2026 and update this guide accordingly.

## The Confirmed Facts

Two things are confirmed by Borealys Games:

1. **Demo save data transfers to Early Access.** Time spent in the demo — including your spells, builds, and hub upgrades — is not wasted.
2. **Echo Fragments reset to zero at launch.** This is by design: progression systems changed between the demo and Early Access, so the demo currency does not carry over.

What else transfers — Memories, spell forms, region progress — **has not been itemized (TBD)**. The developer confirmed the save transfer but hasn't published a line-by-line list of what's included.

## What Carries Over From Demo to EA

### Confirmed

- **Your save data, as a whole** — Borealys states demo save data transfers to Early Access

### Confirmed NOT carrying over

- **Echo Fragments** — the demo currency resets to zero at launch by design (progression systems changed)

### TBD (not yet itemized by Borealys)

- **Memories** — the demo has 50 unique Memories; Early Access launches with 110. Whether your demo-collected Memories import into the larger pool has not been specified
- **Spell forms / crafted spells** — the demo has 4 spell forms; Early Access has 12. How (or whether) your demo-built loadouts map onto the new forms is unannounced
- **Region progress** — the demo's Ash Forest is one of three launch regions; progress state on transfer is unannounced
- **Hub upgrades at the Azimuth Temple** — permanent skill upgrades exist in both, but the transfer behavior is unannounced

The practical stance: **expect your save to transfer as a whole, but plan to re-earn anything that doesn't** — everything is quickly re-obtained in region one (Ash Forest).

## How to Make Sure Your Save Transfers — Step by Step

Borealys hasn't published a transfer procedure, so these steps cover what any Steam player can do to maximize the odds of a clean transfer:

1. **Play the demo on the same Steam account** you'll use for Early Access. The demo and EA build share your Steam identity; switching accounts is the most common way saves go missing.
2. **Finish your demo run and return to the hub** — quit from a normal state rather than a crash or hard kill, so the save file is written cleanly.
3. **Confirm Steam Cloud sync completed.** The demo uses Steam Cloud saves; check your library's demo entry for a green cloud-sync icon before closing Steam. If sync is pending, let it finish before launch day.
4. **Don't uninstall the demo before EA launch** — or if you must, uninstall via Steam (which preserves cloud saves) rather than deleting files manually.
5. **Stop farming Echo Fragments now.** They reset by design — any grinding in the final week before launch is wasted effort.
6. **On launch day, accept the save-transfer prompt.** When the EA build boots for the first time, it will offer to import your demo save — accept it and let the import finish before touching anything.
7. **Verify after loading in.** Check your hub upgrades, spell list, and Memory count against what you had in the demo. If something is missing, the EA Launch Guide's step-by-step plan covers re-earning it fast.

## What Happens to Unlocked Memories and Spell Forms

This is the most common question after "does the save transfer?" — and the honest answer is **TBD until launch**.

What's confirmed about the systems themselves:

- The demo sandbox contains **50 Memories and 4 spell forms**
- Early Access launches with **110 Memories and 12 spell forms**
- Memories are the raw material of spellcrafting — harvested from enemies, reordered and combined into custom spells
- Spell forms are the base templates those crafted spells are built on

What's unconfirmed is the *mapping*: whether your 50-Memory demo collection appears in your EA save's Memory pool, whether your demo-crafted spells survive as loadouts, and how the 4 demo forms relate to the 12 EA forms. Borealys confirmed "save data transfers" without itemizing these components (**TBD**).

Plan for the reasonable case: your progress imports, but treat your demo builds as **starting points, not endgames** — the EA sandbox doubles your options, and the [Spell Crafting Guide](/guides/spell-crafting-guide) will be updated with the full 12-form ruleset after launch.

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Steam only (confirmed).** Early Access launches on PC via Steam; other platforms have not been announced. There is no cross-platform save situation to worry about.
- **Steam Cloud saves** are the demo's save mechanism — they're tied to your Steam account, so the transfer stays within the same account ecosystem.
- **Back up manually if you want belt-and-braces safety:** locate the demo's save folder (typically under `%USERPROFILE%\Documents` or the Steam `userdata` folder) and copy it before launch. This is optional — Steam Cloud should handle it — but a manual copy costs nothing.
