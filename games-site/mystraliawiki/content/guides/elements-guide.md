# Elements Guide — Fire, Ice & Lightning in Echoes of Mystralia

Echoes of Mystralia's combat revolves around three elements: **fire, ice, and lightning**. They appear throughout the official material — in trailers, screenshots, and the demo — and they are the element trio of the game's predecessor, *Mages of Mystralia*. What exactly each element does in the new game is **partly confirmed, partly TBD**. This guide separates the two honestly.

> **Pre-release note:** Echoes of Mystralia enters Steam Early Access on **August 11, 2026**. Mechanics marked **TBD** are not confirmed by Borealys Games.

## What's Confirmed

- **The element trio is fire, ice, and lightning** — confirmed by the developer's own showcase examples: **lightning bolts, freezing tornadoes, and fire vortices**
- **Elements are combined through Memory crafting** — Memories carry element properties; reordering and combining them changes your spells
- **Element choice shapes your playstyle** — the showcased directions (fast lightning chains, control-focused ice, damage-over-time fire) map to very different builds
- **The demo contains all three elements** — with 50 Memories and 4 base spells, you can already experiment with fire, ice, and lightning loadouts

## The Mages of Mystralia Heritage (Historical Context)

The original game gives useful context — clearly labeled as *history*, not confirmation of the sequel's mechanics:

- In *Mages of Mystralia* (2017), runes carried elemental properties that combined into spells
- Fire runes produced burning effects and area damage
- Ice runes produced freezing and slowing effects
- Lightning runes produced arcing, chaining effects
- The game's magic system was written by **Ed Greenwood** (creator of the Forgotten Realms) and won awards — and Borealys has said Echoes of Mystralia evolves that award-winning spellcrafting

The *expectation* that fire burns, ice freezes, and lightning chains is reasonable — but the exact mechanics of Echoes of Mystralia are **TBD** until players verify them in the EA build.

## Honest Guidance for the Demo (Verified by Play)

What you can verify today in the demo:

1. **Try all three elements** — the demo's 4 spells cover the trio; feel each one
2. **Test element-specific Memories** — the 50 demo Memories let you push one element or mix them
3. **Watch enemy behavior** — Rift-spawned foes and Shadows of the Past react differently to different spells; observe before assuming counters
4. **Compare in the 4 fight challenges** — repeatable encounters are perfect for A/B testing elements

**Do NOT assume a rock-paper-scissors counter system.** Earlier drafts of this guide claimed "fire beats ice enemies, ice beats lightning, lightning beats fire." **That table was never confirmed by Borealys** and has been removed. If the game has elemental match-ups, they will be discovered and documented after launch.
