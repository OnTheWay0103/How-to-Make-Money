# Spell Forms Guide — The 12 Forms & How "Classes" Work in Mystralia

Echoes of Mystralia has **no character classes** — no warrior, mage, or rogue to pick at a character screen. Your "class" is defined by **spell forms**: the base templates your spells are built on. Early Access launches with **12 spell forms** (the demo contains 4), and each one is a starting point for a different way to play. This guide explains the form system, what the demo proves about it, and how to evaluate and rank forms the moment Early Access goes live on **August 11, 2026**.

> **Pre-release note:** Individual form names, behaviors, and balance data are **TBD** — Borealys has not published them. What's below is the confirmed framework plus a framework for ranking forms honestly at launch.

## How the Form System Works (Confirmed)

The crafting loop is confirmed in three layers:

| Layer | What it is | Launch scope |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| **Spell forms** | The base template of a spell — the "skeleton" your class is built on | 12 at EA (4 in demo) |
| **Elements** | Fire, ice, and lightning (and more per official material) — the flavor and behavior of the form | Trio shown so far |
| **Memories** | Components harvested from enemies, reordered and combined onto a form | 110 at EA (50 in demo) |

The developer's description is explicit about how these combine: **"where you place each component in your spell grid changes what the spell actually does."** Confirmed examples from official material:

- Move an ice component next to a fire element and the spell becomes **steam**
- Place a lightning rune behind a **homing** component and the attack **chases enemies**
- Showcased results include **thunderbursts, freezing tornadoes, and fire vortices** — the same form system producing radically different spells

This is why "class" is the wrong mental model: two players with the same 12 forms can play completely differently based on how they arrange components.

## The 4 Demo Forms — Your Pre-Release "Class Trials"

The demo caps you at **4 base spells** — a deliberate sampler. Treat each one as a class trial:

1. **Try all four in the demo** — the demo forms cover different roles (single-target, area, control, and a fourth to discover yourself)
2. **Push each form with the 50 Memories** — the same form tuned for damage, then for control, feels like a different class
3. **Test in the 4 fight challenges** — repeatable encounters make form comparison cheap and honest
4. **Note what feels right** — your demo save transfers to Early Access, and the forms you enjoyed are the ones to expand first at launch

The 8 forms beyond the demo set are unnamed and unshown (**TBD**) — part of the launch-day discovery.

## How to Rank Forms at Launch (The Honest Tier-List Method)

The [Tier List guide](/guides/tier-list) explains why no pre-launch ranking can be legitimate. When EA goes live, this is how to build the first real form rankings:

### 1. Rank by context, not globally
A form can be S-tier for Guardian fights and C-tier for farming. Evaluate each form in at least four contexts:

- **Single-target DPS** — Guardian damage
- **Area clear** — pack speed
- **Control / survival** — safety and zone denial
- **Economy** — Memory cost to make the form good

### 2. Change one variable at a time
Test a form with one Memory changed (or moved), record the difference. One-variable tests are the only reliable way to map 12 forms × 110 Memories.

### 3. Track version numbers
Borealys has already rebalanced systems once (between demo and EA) and will tune during Early Access. A ranking without a game version is stale on arrival.

### 4. Aggregate, don't trust impressions
Individual runs skew. Real rankings come from many players' results — community spreadsheets and cleared-run data, not one opinion.

### 5. Update as balance lands
Expect the first week's rankings to be wrong somewhere. The studio has committed to balance changes from player data.

## Picking Your Starting Form at Launch

- **New to the game?** Start with the demo's 4 forms — they're the tutorial-safe picks, and your demo save means you've already practiced them
- **Playstyle-driven?** Match your archetype preference (burst, control, clear speed, survival) to the showcase family above, then verify in-game
- **Experimenter?** Pick one form you haven't seen and make it work — the 8 unrevealed forms are where launch-week discoveries live
- **Practical rule:** keep **one spell per element** (fire, ice, lightning) until real match-ups are documented — coverage beats specialization while the form meta is unknown
