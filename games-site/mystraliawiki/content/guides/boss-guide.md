# Guardian Boss Guide — Therakan & What We Know About Bosses

**Guardians** are the major bosses of Echoes of Mystralia — massive, primordial creatures guarding the heart of each region. Early Access launches with **3 Guardians**, one per region, and only the first has been publicly shown: **Therakan**, who appears in the free demo. This guide covers everything confirmed about Guardians and gives honest, demo-verified advice for Therakan's fight.

> **Pre-release note:** Attack patterns, phases, HP values, and drop tables are **TBD** — Borealys has not published them, and the demo build may differ from Early Access. This guide will be updated after launch.

## Therakan — The First Guardian

Therakan is the boss of the Ash Forest and the only Guardian fightable today. Here's what you can verify by playing the demo, and how to approach the fight.

### What the Demo Confirms

- Therakan is a large, ancient, elemental-aspected creature (the "primordial Guardian" archetype)
- His arena is the end of the Ash Forest run
- The fight is preceded by the rest of the region — your spell and Memory choices determine your state entering the arena
- Beating Therakan is the demo's capstone; he exists to teach the Guardian loop

### Demo-Verified Combat Advice

1. **Learn the tells.** Therakan's attacks are telegraphed. Watch for the wind-up, then dodge — reaction beats prediction at first.
2. **Manage space.** Guardian fights in the demo play out in a large arena; keep distance when unsure, close in only during openings.
3. **Bring your best-crafted spells.** The fight tests your Memory combinations. A spell you've tuned with harvested Memories outperforms a default loadout.
4. **Don't overcommit.** Greedy casting after a dodge is how most first attempts end. One safe hit pattern is better than a dead run.
5. **Use the fight challenges.** The demo's 4 fight challenges are excellent Guardian-style practice — scripted, repeatable, and free to retry.

> Specific attack patterns, phase transitions, elemental weaknesses, and drop tables are **TBD**. We will document them from the Early Access build (and re-check after balance patches, since the developer has already changed systems between demo and EA).

## General Boss-Fighting Principles (Safe to Apply at Launch)

These hold regardless of unconfirmed specifics:

1. **Learn the attack rotation before optimizing damage** — survival first, DPS second
2. **Watch the environment** — arenas in the demo include hazards; bosses often interact with them
3. **Harvest Memories between attempts** — failed Guardian runs still yield enemy drops you keep
4. **Upgrade at the Azimuth Temple between runs** — permanent progression is the real answer to a wall
5. **Re-fight Guardians** — roguelites typically reward repeated Guardian kills; exact rewards are TBD

At Early Access, the likely path is: **Ash Forest → Therakan → Region 2 → Guardian 2 → Region 3 → Guardian 3**. The second and third regions and their Guardians are not yet named or shown — we will expand this guide the moment Borealys reveals them.
