# Enemy & Guardian Boss Guide — Every Foe in Mystralia

What enemies does Echoes of Mystralia have? Borealys Games has confirmed **four enemy layers** — Rift-spawned foes, Shadows of the Past, Elites, and **Guardian boss encounters** — but has not published an official bestiary. This guide collects the confirmed roster structure, the demo-verified combat behavior, and everything known about the game's Guardian bosses, including **Therakan** from the free demo.

Echoes of Mystralia entered **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**. Individual enemy names, attack patterns, and drop tables are being documented from the Early Access build; anything below that comes from official sources is stated directly.

## The Four Confirmed Enemy Layers

| Enemy layer | Confirmed details | Status |
|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Rift-spawned foes** | The general enemy population of the regions; drops Memories | ✅ Confirmed as a type; individual entries being mapped |
| **Shadows of the Past** | Called out on the Steam page — "memories of Mystralia's horrors given form"; your spells' primary target | ✅ Confirmed as a type; individual entries being mapped |
| **Elites** | Elite fights confirmed at Early Access launch; the demo's 4 fight challenges preview elite-style pressure | ✅ Confirmed to exist; rewards being mapped |
| **Guardians** | Massive, ancient, pattern-based boss encounters guarding region hearts; **Therakan** was playable in the demo | ✅ Confirmed; Therakan's demo fight verified |

There is **no official bestiary** — treat any "complete enemy list" that cannot point to the game itself or an official announcement with suspicion.

## Rift-Spawned Foes: The General Population

The **Rift-spawned foes** are the everyday enemies of Mystralia — the standard population you clear through every region. Confirmed facts:

- They are the basic Memory source — **every enemy type can drop Memories**
- They fill the regions and are cleared during runs
- The demo shows telegraphed melee and ranged behaviors; a full catalog is being built from the EA build

## Shadows of the Past: The Primary Target

The Steam description is explicit about who you are fighting: **"Memories are your only weapon against the Shadows of the Past."** These are the named antagonists of the combat loop — **memories of Mystralia's horrors given form**, born from the world's wounded memory. In lore terms they are the direct symptom of the Rift's corruption (see the [Story & Lore Guide](/guides/story-lore-guide)); in combat terms they are what your crafted spells are for.

## Elites: The Mid-Tier Pressure

**Elite encounters are confirmed at Early Access launch** (per the developer's announcements). What we know:

- The demo's **4 fight challenges** are the confirmed preview — scripted encounters with elite-style pressure, repeatable and safe
- Elites sit between standard foes and Guardians in difficulty; the expectation (not yet confirmed) is that tougher enemies reward better
- Elite layouts and reward tables at launch are being documented from the EA build

## Guardian Boss Encounters

**Guardians** are the major boss encounters of Echoes of Mystralia — massive, primordial creatures guarding the heart of each region. Early Access ships with **Guardian and Elite encounters across its 3 regions** (per the official Steam page), and only the first Guardian has been publicly shown: **Therakan**, who appears in the free demo and is the game's confirmed first Guardian.

| Guardian | Region | Status |
|----------|--------|--------|
| **Therakan** | The Ash Forest (demo region) | ✅ Confirmed — playable in the demo and the EA launch build |

Confirmed facts:

- Guardians guard the regions of Mystralia, which are threatened by the **Rift**
- Therakan is described as the **"first primordial Guardian"** — the demo's boss fight
- The demo's save transfers to Early Access (per developer announcements), so your Therakan knowledge carries over

## Therakan — The First Guardian

Therakan is the boss of the Ash Forest and the Guardian fightable today. What the demo confirms, verified by play:

- Therakan is a large, ancient, elemental-aspected creature (the "primordial Guardian" archetype)
- His arena is the end of the Ash Forest run
- The fight is preceded by the rest of the region — your spell and Memory choices determine your state entering the arena
- Beating Therakan is the demo's capstone; he exists to teach the Guardian loop

### Demo-Verified Combat Advice

1. **Learn the tells.** Therakan's attacks are telegraphed. Watch for the wind-up, then dodge — reaction beats prediction at first.
2. **Manage space.** Guardian fights in the demo play out in a large arena; keep distance when unsure, close in only during openings.
3. **Bring your best-crafted spells.** The fight tests your Memory combinations. A spell you've tuned with harvested Memories outperforms a default loadout.
4. **Don't overcommit.** Greedy casting after a dodge is how most first attempts end. One safe hit pattern is better than a dead run.
5. **Use the fight challenges.** The demo's 4 fight challenges are excellent Guardian-style practice — scripted, repeatable, and free to retry.

> Specific attack patterns, phase transitions, elemental weaknesses, and drop tables are being documented from the Early Access build. Balance will change during Early Access — the developer has already reworked systems once between demo and EA.

## Demo-Verified Enemy Behavior (Safe to Practice)

The demo confirms how enemies actually fight, and these behaviors are safe to drill:

1. **Attacks are telegraphed** — Rift-spawned foes and Shadows of the Past wind up their attacks; reading the tell beats panic-dodging
2. **Movement is the counter** — every enemy attack in the demo can be avoided with movement; dodging and positioning are your core defense
3. **Arenas matter** — fight-challenge arenas and Therakan's arena include hazards and space constraints; watch the environment, not just the enemy
4. **Standing still is punished** — the demo rewards moving after casting
5. **Every enemy is a resource** — all enemy types drop Memories; dead enemies are also farming opportunities, so clear deliberately

## Weaknesses & Elemental Match-Ups — The Honest State

**No enemy weaknesses have been confirmed.** There is no official elemental match-up system and no vulnerability table published by the developer. Until the EA build is fully mapped:

- Do **not** trust "fire beats X enemy" lists — none are official
- Keep **elemental coverage** in your loadout (the game has 7 elements at launch) so you are prepared no matter what the real match-ups turn out to be
- Expect match-ups and status effects to be documented here as the community verifies them in-game

## General Boss-Fighting Principles (Safe to Apply)

These hold regardless of unconfirmed specifics:

1. **Learn the attack rotation before optimizing damage** — survival first, DPS second
2. **Watch the environment** — arenas in the demo include hazards; bosses often interact with them
3. **Harvest Memories between attempts** — failed Guardian runs still yield enemy drops you keep
4. **Upgrade at the Azimuth Temple between runs** — permanent progression (the Lotus) is the real answer to a wall
