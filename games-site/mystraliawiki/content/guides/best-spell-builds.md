# Best Spell Builds & Combinations — Early Access Build Planning

Echoes of Mystralia is live in **Steam Early Access** (launched **August 11, 2026**). "Best build" guides that claim exact rankings are usually unreliable this early — Borealys has not published damage numbers or balance data, and balance will shift during Early Access. What we *can* do is explain the confirmed crafting fundamentals, the combinations the developer has explicitly showcased, and a framework for evaluating builds yourself as the meta forms.

## Confirmed Crafting Fundamentals

The system Borealys has described works like this:

1. **You harvest Memories from enemies** — Memories are the raw material of every spell
2. **You forge and customize spells from Memories** — reordering and combining them creates your spell loadout
3. **The combinatorial space is enormous** — the developer describes it as **"millions of possible combinations"**
4. **Showcased examples** include **lightning bolts**, **freezing tornadoes**, and **fire vortices** — evidence that the same base spell can become very different spells through Memory arrangement

The three building layers:

| Layer | Confirmed | Details |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Starting spells** | 16 at EA launch (4 in the demo) | The base templates your loadout is built on; names and exact behaviors being mapped |
| **Elements** | 7 at launch; fire, ice, and lightning shown | Element interactions being mapped |
| **Memories** | 100+ at EA launch (50 in the demo) | Individual Memory effects being mapped |

## What the Demo Taught About Building

The demo capped you at **4 spells and 50 Memories** — a deliberately small sandbox. Within it you could already test the core build question: **how does Memory arrangement change a spell?** Practical experiments:

- **Same Memories, different order** — build two spells from identical Memories in different arrangements and compare them in the fight challenges
- **Memory density** — test whether stacking many Memories on one spell beats spreading them thin
- **Element focus** — try one-element and mixed-element loadouts and feel the difference
- **Defense vs offense** — some players prefer survival tools; test what the game offers and decide your preference

Whatever you learned transfers directly: per developer announcements, the demo save carries to Early Access, and the crafting *skill* transfers even where specifics changed.

## Showcased Build Directions (from Official Material)

Borealys has shown three spell archetypes in trailers and screenshots:

| Direction | Example shown | Likely strength |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Lightning** | Chain-style lightning bolts | Pack clearing |
| **Ice** | Freezing tornadoes | Crowd control |

These are *showcased examples*, not confirmed endgame builds — and with 7 elements at launch, the full elemental picture is broader than the trio shown. Treat them as inspiration for your own experiments, not as a meta.

## A Framework for Build Evaluation

Evaluate any build with these questions:

1. **What does the spell actually do?** — a "S-tier" label means nothing without a described behavior
2. **What does it cost?** — every build has resource costs; a combo you can't sustain isn't a combo
3. **Against what?** — single-target boss DPS, area clear, and survival are different jobs; one build rarely does all three
4. **What's the Memory requirement?** — builds that need specific Memories from the 100+ pool take time to assemble
5. **What Relics support it?** — with 40+ Relics at launch, the best builds pair crafted spells with complementary passive items

## Build Types to Expect (Being Documented)

Based on the confirmed systems, players are specializing in directions like these — each being validated in the launch build:

- **Boss-killer builds** — single-target damage focused on Guardian fights
- **Clear-speed builds** — area and chain focus for fast Memory farming
- **Survival builds** — defensive spell arrangements for learning new content
- **Hybrid builds** — mixing elements to cover more situations

## Common Early Access Pitfalls

1. **Trusting "leaked" tier lists** — no legitimate rankings existed before launch; verify anything in-game
2. **Ignoring the demo lessons** — the 4-spell/50-Memory sandbox teaches the fundamentals that still apply
3. **Over-planning** — balance changes between demo and EA are confirmed (the developer reworked progression once); expect more tuning
4. **Assuming element match-ups** — community theories about fire/ice/lightning counters are not developer-confirmed; the game has 7 elements
