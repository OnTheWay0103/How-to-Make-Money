# Early Access Launch Guide — What to Do First

Echoes of Mystralia is **live in Steam Early Access** (launched **August 11, 2026**), and the launch build is substantially bigger than the demo: **16 starting spells** instead of 4, **100+ Memories** instead of 50, **3 regions** instead of 1, and **40+ Relics** added. If you played the demo, **your save transfers** (per developer announcements) — but Borealys reworked progression systems between demo and EA, so a plan helps. Here's your first-session checklist.

## Before You Start

1. **Confirm your demo save is ready** — per developer announcements, demo save data transfers to Early Access. The launch build may prompt you to import it at first boot.
2. **Set your expectations** — the launch build ships with 3 regions, Guardian and Elite encounters, 7 magical elements, 16 starting spells, 40+ Relics, 100+ Memories, 50+ Lotus Altar upgrades, and 25 Steam achievements (per the official Steam page).
3. **Decide your starting direction** — see the [Character & Playstyle Guide](/guides/character-playstyle-guide). You don't need a plan more detailed than "keep elemental coverage" until the launch meta is understood.

## Your First Session: Step by Step

### Step 1: Load Your Demo Save

Launch the game and accept the save transfer prompt if it appears. What exactly carries over beyond the confirmed "save data transfers" — Memories, spells, region progress — has not been itemized by Borealys. If something doesn't transfer, it's quick to re-earn in region one.

### Step 2: Explore the Full Crafting System

The demo capped you at 4 spells and 50 Memories. Early Access expands the sandbox to **16 starting spells and 100+ Memories**:

- Open the crafting interface and experiment — Memory arrangement is the core skill
- Compare your demo-built spells with the new starting spells and Memories
- Rebuild your loadout with the new options — the demo's 4-spell builds are starting points, not endgames

### Step 3: Re-Clear the Ash Forest (Fast)

Even with a transferred save, one familiar Ash Forest run re-establishes the feel of combat:

- Harvest Memories from enemies — they're the resource that fuels everything
- Collect any Relics you find — the 40+ Relic pool is part of the new power layer
- Complete the region's fights to bank early Lotus progress
- Don't rush the Guardian — warm up first

### Step 4: Take on Therakan

With a transferred save and hub upgrades intact, Therakan is a comfortable first-session goal:

- Bring the strongest spells you crafted in the demo
- Remember his tells from the demo — pattern knowledge carries over
- First-kill rewards are not itemized, but a Guardian kill is the milestone that unlocks the path forward

### Step 5: Open Region Two

The second region unlocks after the first region's Guardian (expected structure). Before you descend:

- **Cover elements** — with 7 elements in the game, keep broad coverage until real match-ups are known
- **Bank Lotus upgrades** — permanent progression is the safety net for harder content
- **Expect harder elites** — Elite encounters are confirmed at launch, and region two is advertised as a step up from region one

### Step 6: Region Three and Beyond

Region three is the hardest of the three launch regions. After clearing it, the loop continues: farm Memories and Relics, push your crafting, spend the Lotus, and wait for Borealys' post-launch content updates. Full-release details are being announced by the developer as Early Access progresses.

## First-Week Priority Order

| Day | Focus | Why |
|-----|-------|-----|
| Day 1 | Load save, explore the crafting system, re-clear Ash Forest | Re-learn the game with the full sandbox |
| Day 2 | Defeat Therakan | Milestone + region two access |
| Day 3–5 | Clear region two, harvest Memories and Relics | Mid-game power from the new pools |
| Day 5–7 | Region three attempts, build experimentation | Endgame prep and the final launch region |

## Common Launch-Week Mistakes

1. **Ignoring the new crafting options** — the demo taught 4 spells; launch adds 12 more starting spells plus 40+ Relics
2. **Trusting pre-launch tier lists** — no legitimate rankings existed before launch; verify in-game
3. **Sticking to one element** — with 7 elements, keep coverage until the real match-ups are discovered
4. **Skipping the hub** — Lotus upgrades at the Azimuth Temple are the core of long-term progress
5. **Assuming the demo taught you everything** — progression systems changed between demo and EA; re-verify

## After the First Week

Borealys has committed to **gameplay-focused updates throughout Early Access** — expect new regions, Memories, Relics, spells, puzzles, and boss fights over time (see the [EA Roadmap Guide](/guides/ea-roadmap-guide)). A strong start positions you for everything the studio adds next.
