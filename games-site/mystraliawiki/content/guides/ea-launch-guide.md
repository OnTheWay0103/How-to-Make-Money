# Early Access Launch Guide — What to Do First on August 11

Echoes of Mystralia hits **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**, and the launch build is confirmed to be substantially bigger than the demo: **12 spell forms** instead of 4, **110 Memories** instead of 50, **3 regions** instead of 1, and **3 Guardians** instead of one boss. If you played the demo, **your save transfers** — but the progression systems changed between demo and EA, so a plan helps. Here's your first-session checklist.

> **Pre-release note:** Where the developer has confirmed details, they're stated directly. Everything else is marked **TBD**.

## Before Launch Day

1. **Finish your demo run** — demo save data transfers to Early Access, so time in the demo isn't wasted.
2. **Stop farming Echo Fragments** — the demo currency **resets to zero at launch by design** (progression systems changed). Any grinding in the final week is wasted.
3. **Wishlist and watch the Steam page** — wishlisting supported the game (175,000+ wishlists so far) and Steam will notify you about launch, pre-loads, and any pricing announcement.
4. **Decide your starting direction** — see the [Character & Playstyle Guide](/guides/character-playstyle-guide). You don't need a plan more detailed than "keep one spell per element" until launch builds are understood.

## Your First Session: Step by Step

### Step 1: Load Your Demo Save

Launch the game and accept the save transfer prompt. What exactly carries over beyond the confirmed "save data transfers" — Memories, spell forms, region progress — has not been itemized by Borealys (**TBD**; the confirmed exception is that **Echo Fragments reset**). If something doesn't transfer, it's quick to re-earn in region one.

### Step 2: Explore the Full Crafting System

The demo capped you at 4 spells and 50 Memories. Early Access expands the sandbox to **12 spell forms and 110 Memories**:

- Open the crafting interface and experiment — Memory arrangement is the core skill
- Compare your demo-built spells with the new forms and Memories
- Rebuild your loadout with the new options — the demo's 4-form builds are starting points, not endgames

### Step 3: Re-Clear the Ash Forest (Fast)

Even with a transferred save, one familiar Ash Forest run re-establishes the feel of combat:

- Harvest Memories from enemies — they're the resource that fuels everything
- Complete the region's fights to bank early progress
- Don't rush the Guardian — warm up first

### Step 4: Take on Therakan

With a transferred save and hub upgrades intact, Therakan is a comfortable first-session goal:

- Bring the strongest spells you crafted in the demo
- Remember his tells from the demo — pattern knowledge carries over
- First-kill rewards for Guardians have not been itemized (**TBD**), but a Guardian kill is the milestone that unlocks the path forward

### Step 5: Open Region Two

The second region unlocks after the first region's Guardian (expected structure; official confirmation of the unlock condition is **TBD**). Before you descend:

- **Cover elements** — keep fire, ice, and lightning spells available until real match-ups are known
- **Bank hub upgrades** — permanent progression is the safety net for harder content
- **Expect harder elites** — region two is advertised as a step up from region one (the demo's "4 fight challenges" preview this difficulty)

### Step 6: Region Three and Beyond

Region three is the hardest of the three launch regions (**TBD** details). After clearing it, the confirmed loop continues: farm Memories, push your crafting, and wait for Borealys' post-launch content updates. Full-release details are **TBD**.

## First-Week Priority Order

| Day | Focus | Why |
|-----|-------|-----|
| Day 1 | Load save, explore the crafting system, re-clear Ash Forest | Re-learn the game with the full sandbox |
| Day 2 | Defeat Therakan | Milestone + region two access |
| Day 3–5 | Clear region two, harvest Memories | Mid-game power from the new Memory pool |
| Day 5–7 | Region three attempts, build experimentation | Endgame prep and the final launch Guardian |

## Common Launch-Week Mistakes

1. **Hoarding Echo Fragments** — they reset at launch; this is confirmed
2. **Ignoring the new crafting options** — the demo taught 4 forms; launch rewards the other 8 (details TBD)
3. **Trusting pre-launch tier lists** — no legitimate rankings exist before launch
4. **Sticking to one element** — keep coverage until the real match-ups are discovered
5. **Skipping the hub** — permanent upgrades at the Azimuth Temple are the core of long-term progress

## After the First Week

Borealys has committed to **gameplay-focused updates throughout Early Access** — expect new regions, Memories, spell forms, and Guardians over time (see the [EA Roadmap Guide](/guides/ea-roadmap-guide)). A strong start positions you for everything the studio adds next.
