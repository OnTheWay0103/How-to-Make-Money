# Early Access Launch Guide — What to Do First on August 11

Echoes of Mystralia hits **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026**, and the launch build is substantially bigger than the demo: 12 spell forms instead of 4, 110 Memories instead of 50, 3 regions instead of 1, and 3 Guardians instead of one boss. If you played the demo, your save transfers — but the game you're stepping into is different enough that a plan helps. Here's your first-session checklist.

## Before Launch Day

1. **Play or finish the demo** — demo save data transfers to Early Access. Memories, spell forms, Relics, and Lotus progress carry over.
2. **Don't farm Echo Fragments** — the demo currency resets to zero at launch by design (progression systems changed). Any time spent grinding them in the final week is wasted.
3. **Wishlist and pre-load** — Steam pre-loads typically open in the final days before launch; download early so you're playing at release time, not downloading.
4. **Decide your starting archetype** — see the [Character & Playstyle Guide](/guides/character-playstyle-guide). If you're unsure, plan to start with the survivable Blizzard King template (Ice Nova with Barrier + Leech).

## Your First Session: Step by Step

### Step 1: Load Your Demo Save

Launch the game and accept the save transfer prompt. Your demo progress imports — you should start with your Memories collection, unlocked spell forms, Relics, and Lotus tiers intact. Then immediately **spend any banked Memory Shards** on Lotus upgrades before your first run; a failed run shouldn't leave shards sitting idle.

### Step 2: Rebuild Your Loadout With All 12 Forms

The demo capped you at 4 spell forms. Early Access adds 8 more — Vortex, Barrage, Wall, Mine, Orb, Wave, Lance, Rift. Before your first run:

- Open the spellcrafting grid and **experiment with the new forms** in the Temple training area (there's a practice dummy).
- **Vortex** and **Arc** are the meta damage forms; **Lance** is the boss killer; **Rift** is the mobility tool.
- Rebuild your saved loadouts to use the new options — the demo's 4-form builds are now incomplete.

### Step 3: Re-Clear Ash Forest (Fast)

Even with a transferred save, do one quick Ash Forest run to re-learn the combat feel. Targets:

- Kill elites for their Memory drops — they're the fastest source of new Memories.
- Open golden chests for Rare-tier drops.
- Don't rush Therakan yet; use this run to warm up and farm shards.

### Step 4: Take on Therakan

With a transferred save and Lotus upgrades intact, Therakan is very beatable in your second session. Bring:

- **Ice or Lightning spells** (the counter element for fire enemies)
- A **Barrier or Leech** defensive Memory
- The **Phoenix Feather** Relic if you have it — the safety net is worth the slot for your first kill

First Guardian kills guarantee a **Legendary Memory** drop — this is the single most valuable moment in the early game.

### Step 5: Open the Frozen Depths

The second region (ice caverns, intermediate difficulty) unlocks after Therakan. Before you descend:

- Confirm your loadout has a **fire spell** — fire beats ice enemies.
- Check your Lotus: Vitality and Resilience tiers pay for themselves in a new region.
- Expect harder elites and a new Guardian at the end. Farm Ash Forest between attempts if you hit a wall — shards and Memories from region one fund region two.

### Step 6: Stormspire, Then Endgame Farming

Stormspire (lightning-charged tower) is the hardest launch region — bring ice, and bring patience. After clearing it, the loop opens up:

- **Speed-farm Ash Forest** on hard difficulty with the Storm Weaver build for Memory Shards.
- **Stormspire endless mode** (waves) is the best shard rate in the game for veterans.
- **Guardian re-fights** drop Legendary Memories on first kill only, but still pay well in shards.
- **Target the rare Memories** you need for your endgame build from the [Memories Guide](/guides/memories-guide).

## First-Week Priority Order

| Day | Focus | Why |
|-----|-------|-----|
| Day 1 | Load save, rebuild loadouts, re-clear Ash Forest | Re-learn the game with all 12 forms |
| Day 2 | Defeat Therakan | Legendary Memory + unlocks Frozen Depths |
| Day 3–5 | Clear Frozen Depths, farm Memories | Mid-game power spike from Rare Memories |
| Day 5–7 | Stormspire attempts + endless mode | Endgame resources and the final launch Guardian |

## Common Launch-Week Mistakes

1. **Ignoring new spell forms** — the demo taught you Bolt/Shard/Arc/Nova. Launch rewards the new 8. Experiment early.
2. **Skipping loadout saves** — save a loadout per region before descending. Swapping at a shrine mid-run beats re-crafting from memory.
3. **Hording Memory Shards** — spend them. Lotus tiers are permanent; a dead run with unspent shards is pure waste.
4. **Grinding one region** — progress needs both Memories (region-specific drops) and shards (any region). Rotate.
5. **Expecting the demo economy** — Early Access costs and drop rates are tuned for the full loop; the demo was generous. Plan for slower progression.

## After the First Week

The endgame opens: challenge modes, higher difficulties, secret areas, world events, and build min-maxing for the next content patch. The roadmap promises new regions, Memories, and Guardians through Early Access — so a strong start positions you for everything Borealys adds next.
