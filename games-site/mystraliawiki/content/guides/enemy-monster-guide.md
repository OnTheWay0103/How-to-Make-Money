# Enemy & Monster Guide — Every Foe in Mystralia (Confirmed + TBD)

What enemies does Echoes of Mystralia have? Borealys Games has confirmed **four enemy layers** — Rift-spawned foes, Shadows of the Past, Elites, and primordial Guardians — but has **not published an official bestiary.** Individual enemy names, behaviors, weaknesses, and drop tables are **TBD** until Early Access launches on **August 11, 2026**. This guide collects the confirmed roster structure, the demo-verified combat behavior, and the honest state of weaknesses and farming.

> **EA Launch note:** Anything marked **TBD** has not been confirmed by Borealys Games. The demo build and the EA build may differ — Borealys has already changed systems once between them, so expect roster details to be **[EA — subject to change]**.

## The Four Confirmed Enemy Layers

| Enemy layer | Confirmed details | Status |
|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Rift-spawned foes** | The general enemy population of the regions; drops Memories | ✅ Confirmed as a type; individual entries TBD |
| **Shadows of the Past** | Called out on the Steam page — "memories of Mystralia's horrors given form"; your spells' primary target | ✅ Confirmed as a type; individual entries TBD |
| **Elites** | Multiple Elite fights confirmed at EA launch; the demo's 4 fight challenges preview elite-style pressure | ✅ Confirmed to exist; rewards TBD |
| **Primordial Guardians** | The 3 region bosses — massive, ancient, pattern-based fights | ✅ Confirmed; Therakan playable in the demo |

There is **no official bestiary** — do not trust pre-launch "complete enemy list" posts; no such list exists from the developer.

## Rift-Spawned Foes: The General Population

The **Rift-spawned foes** are the everyday enemies of Mystralia — the standard population you clear through every region. Confirmed facts:

- They are the basic Memory source — **every enemy type can drop Memories**
- They fill the regions and are cleared during runs
- Their individual designs and attack patterns are **TBD** (the demo shows telegraphed melee and ranged behaviors, but nothing is officially cataloged)

## Shadows of the Past: The Primary Target

The Steam description is explicit about who you are fighting: **"Memories are your only weapon against the Shadows of the Past."** These are the named antagonists of the combat loop — **memories of Mystralia's horrors given form**, born from the world's wounded memory. In lore terms they are the direct symptom of the Rift's corruption (see the [Story & Lore Guide](/guides/story-lore-guide)); in combat terms they are what your crafted spells are for.

## Elites: The Mid-Tier Pressure

**Multiple Elite fights are confirmed at Early Access launch** (press release). What we know:

- The demo's **4 fight challenges** are the confirmed preview — scripted encounters with elite-style pressure, repeatable and safe
- Elites are expected to sit between standard foes and Guardians in difficulty — the expectation (not yet confirmed) is that tougher enemies reward better
- Elite layouts, rewards, and spawn patterns at launch are **TBD**

## Primordial Guardians: The Bosses

The **3 Guardians** are the capstone of each region — massive, ancient, pattern-based boss fights. The first, **Therakan**, is playable in the demo. Everything confirmed about them — including demo-verified combat advice — is in the [Guardian Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide). The shorthand for week one: **learn the tells, manage space, bring your best-crafted spells.**

## Demo-Verified Enemy Behavior (Safe to Practice Now)

The demo confirms how enemies actually fight, and these behaviors are safe to drill before launch:

1. **Attacks are telegraphed** — Rift-spawned foes and Shadows of the Past wind up their attacks; reading the tell beats panic-dodging
2. **Movement is the counter** — every enemy attack in the demo can be avoided with movement; dodging and positioning are your core defense
3. **Arenas matter** — fight-challenge arenas and Therakan's arena include hazards and space constraints; watch the environment, not just the enemy
4. **Standing still is punished** — the demo rewards moving after casting
5. **Every enemy is a resource** — all enemy types drop Memories; dead enemies are also farming opportunities, so clear deliberately

## Weaknesses & Elemental Match-Ups — The Honest TBD

**No enemy weaknesses have been confirmed.** There is no official elemental match-up system, no status-effect chart, and no vulnerability table — earlier counter tables on this wiki were fabricated and removed. Until the EA build is verified:

- Do **not** trust pre-launch "fire beats X enemy" lists
- Build **one spell per element** (fire, ice, lightning) so you are covered no matter what the real match-ups turn out to be
- Expect match-ups and status effects (burn, freeze, stun) to be documented here after launch — **TBD**

## Farming Spots by Enemy Type

Where to fight each layer at launch:

| Layer | Best farming spot | Notes |
|-------|-------------------|-------|
| Rift-spawned foes | Full **Ash Forest** clears | The safest, most reliable Memory source |
| Shadow of the Past packs | Region clearing during runs | Primary targets of your loadout; drops Memories |
| Elite-style fights | The **4 fight challenges** (demo structure) | Repeatable, scripted, zero run-loss risk |
| Elites (EA) | Elite fights across the 3 regions | Highest-value single targets (expected); rewards TBD |
| Guardians | Therakan + Guardians #2/#3 arenas | Biggest rewards, hardest fights; failed runs still bank drops from the approach |
