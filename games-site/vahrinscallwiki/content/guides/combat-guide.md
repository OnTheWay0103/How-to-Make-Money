# Combat Guide — Tips, Mechanics & Advanced Techniques

Combat in Vahrin's Call is deliberate, punishing, and deeply rewarding once mastered. Every weapon swing, dodge, and block requires commitment — you can't animation-cancel your way out of mistakes.

---

## Core Combat Mechanics

### The Commitment Rule
**Every action has a recovery animation.** When you swing a greatsword, you're locked into the full swing animation. When you dodge, you can't attack until the roll completes. This means:

- Don't swing unless you know you'll hit (or can safely miss)
- Don't dodge preemptively — dodge **reactively**
- Blocking is faster to recover from than dodging, but costs stamina

### Stamina Management
Stamina is the hidden second health bar:

| Action | Stamina Cost |
|--------|-------------|
| Light attack | 10-15 |
| Heavy/charged attack | 25-40 |
| Dodge roll | 20 |
| Block (per hit) | 15-30 (varies by shield) |
| Sprint | 5/sec |

**Golden rules**:
- Never let stamina drop to zero (staggered state = death)
- Always reserve enough stamina for one dodge (~20)
- Blocking consumes stamina proportional to the blocked damage — heavy attacks will guard-break you if stamina is low

## Defensive Techniques

### Blocking
- **Regular block**: Hold block. Negates 70-100% of physical damage (depends on shield). Costs stamina.
- **Perfect block**: Tap block just before impact. Negates 100% damage, costs 50% less stamina, and briefly staggers the enemy.
- **Guard break**: If an attack depletes your remaining stamina, you're staggered for 1.5 seconds — enough time for enemies to land 2-3 free hits.

**When to block**: Against fast, multi-hit combos. One perfect block can stop an entire chain.

### Dodging
- **Roll**: Full invulnerability frames (i-frames) during the middle portion of the animation. Covers distance.
- **Side-step**: Faster than roll, shorter i-frames. Best for quick attacks.
- **Back-step**: Shortest distance, medium i-frames. Creates space without turning your back.

**i-Frame Data**:
| Dodge Type | i-Frames (base) | i-Frames (50 Agility) |
|------------|----------------|----------------------|
| Roll | 0.3s | 0.5s |
| Side-step | 0.2s | 0.35s |
| Back-step | 0.25s | 0.4s |

**When to dodge**: Against heavy/unblockable attacks, AoE slams, grab attacks.

### Parrying
Parrying is a high-risk, high-reward technique. Timed correctly, it deflects the enemy's attack and opens them for a critical counter.

- **Parry window**: ~0.2 seconds (roughly 12 frames at 60 FPS)
- **Cannot parry**: Two-handed heavy attacks from large enemies, boss grab attacks, magical projectiles
- **Successful parry**: Enemy staggers, next attack deals 2× damage
- **Failed parry**: You take full damage + stagger

**When to parry**: Against humanoid enemies with predictable attack patterns. Don't attempt on first encounters — learn the timing first.

## Offensive Techniques

### Basic Combos
| Weapon | Light Combo | Heavy Finisher |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Greatsword | Horizontal → Horizontal → Vertical | Overhead Slam |
| Sword & Shield | Slash → Thrust → Shield Bash | Charged Thrust |
| Dual Wield | Left → Right → Left → Right → Cross | Spin Slash |
| Hammer | Overhead → Side → Overhead | Ground Pound |
| Bow | Quick shot → Quick shot → Aimed shot | Piercing Arrow |

### Charged Attacks
Hold the attack button to charge. Release at the right moment for maximum damage:
- **Level 1** (0.5s charge): +20% damage
- **Level 2** (1.0s charge): +50% damage, adds stagger
- **Level 3** (1.5s+ charge): +100% damage, adds elemental effect if weapon is imbued

### Status Effects

|--------|--------|--------|

| Poisoned | Alchemy, traps, certain enemies | 3% max HP/sec for 8s |

## Enemy Types & Strategies

### Humanoid (Soldiers, Bandits, Mercenaries)
- **Strengths**: Organized, block and dodge your attacks
- **Weaknesses**: Predictable patterns, parry-able
- **Strategy**: Parry → critical counter. Bait their attacks and punish recovery.

### Beast (Khraiax drones, wild animals)
- **Strengths**: Fast, swarm tactics, hard to hit
- **Weaknesses**: Low HP, vulnerable to AoE, fire
- **Strategy**: AoE attacks (Cleave, Fireball, Flame Wave). Don't let them surround you.

### Heavy (Golems, armored enemies)
- **Strengths**: High HP, high armor, stagger-resistant
- **Weaknesses**: Slow, elemental weaknesses
- **Strategy**: Hammer for armor break, elemental damage. Dodge — blocking their attacks is a stamina trap.

### Caster (Mages, Librarians)
- **Strengths**: Ranged, high damage, buffs allies
- **Weaknesses**: Low HP, long cast times, interruptible
- **Strategy**: Rush them down. A single charged attack interrupts their spells. Kill casters first in any group fight.

### Boss
- **Strengths**: Unique patterns, high HP, multiple phases
- **Weaknesses**: Predictable once learned, elemental weaknesses
- **Strategy**: See [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) for individual strategies.

## Advanced Techniques

### 1. Animation Canceling (Limited)
Vahrin's Call intentionally limits animation canceling — you can't roll out of a whiffed swing. But there are exceptions:
- **Block cancel**: You can raise your shield during the recovery frames of a light attack
- **Spell weave**: Casting a spell resets your combo — Light Attack → Spell → Light Attack is faster than Light → Light

### 2. Enemy Baiting
Most enemies have a "leash range" — they'll chase you only so far. Use this to:
- Pull single enemies from groups with a bow shot
- Retreat to choke points where only one enemy can reach you
- Reset boss aggro by breaking line of sight

### 3. Environmental Kills
- **Ledges**: Many enemies can be knocked off. Hammer's Seismic Slam near a ledge = instant kill.
- **Explosive barrels**: Found in bandit camps and some dungeons. Shoot with fire arrows.
- **Falling objects**: Chandeliers, loose rocks, icicles — shoot them down on enemies below.

### 4. The Quick Swap
You can equip two weapon sets and swap between them with one button. Use this:
- Open with bow → swap to greatsword when enemies close
- Apply Frost debuff with ice magic → swap to hammer for shatter damage
- Place traps with utility set → swap to main weapon for combat

### 5. Potion Buffering
You can queue a potion use during an attack animation. The potion activates the moment your animation ends — no downtime.

## Build-Specific Combat Tips

### Heavy Warrior (Strength)
- **Stagger is your friend**: A staggered enemy deals zero damage. Chain charged heavy attacks for perma-stagger on non-boss enemies.
- **Block → Counter**: Block the first hit, counter-attack during enemy recovery. Don't try to out-dodge — your roll is slow.
- **Spacing**: Stay at medium range. Greatsword has reach advantage — hit enemies before they can hit you.

### Ranger (Agility/Bow)
- **Kite or die**: Never let enemies reach melee range. Frost Arrows (crafted) or Frost Spear maintain distance.
- **Headshots**: 2× damage multiplier. Worth the extra aiming time against slow enemies.
- **Trap placement**: Before pulling enemies, place traps between you and them. They'll walk right into it.

### Mage (Intelligence)
- **Mana discipline**: Don't spam your biggest spell. Use cheap spells for trash, save mana for elites and bosses.
- **Element matching**: Fire on beasts, Ice on fast enemies, Earth on groups. Matching elements doubles your damage.
- **Positioning**: Stay behind melee allies (or traps). Your casting animation makes you vulnerable.

### Hybrid (Mixed Stats)
- **Open with ranged, finish with melee**: Soften enemies from distance, then swap to melee for the kill. This conserves both mana and HP.
- **Flexibility is your strength**: You handle situations pure builds struggle with. Lean into versatility.
