# Combat Guide — Mechanics & Tips

Vahrin's Call is a real-time action-RPG, and its combat is the most debated part of the game. Steam reviews at launch earned the game a "Mixed" rating largely because of the feel of combat: multiple reviewers describe it as "muddy" or "sluggish," with enemies that don't always stagger or react visibly to hits. This guide sticks to what is confirmed by the developers and launch reviews, and flags the rest as unconfirmed.

## Confirmed Core Mechanics

### Real-Time Action with Commitment

Attacks, dodges, and spells all take time and cannot simply be cancelled. The developers designed combat around deliberate timing — which is why khraiax swarms demand precise dodge timing and boss fights emphasize **pattern recognition**.

### Stamina System

A **stamina system** exists and, notably, limits prolonged magic use. Manage your stamina as a resource: you cannot spam spells or dodges forever. The exact costs of individual actions have not been officially published.

### Blocking and Hitboxes

Reviews note that **blocking works and hitboxes are acceptable** — a positive among otherwise mixed feedback. Timing your guard against attacks is a viable defensive tool.

### No Finishers, but Backstabs Exist

The developers confirmed there are **no traditional finishers** (no cinematic kill moves). Instead:

- You can **sneak up on human enemies and stab them in the back** for significant damage.
- Reviewers warn the stealth system is clunky: there is **no prompt for sneak attacks**, and you must have your melee weapon **manually equipped** for the backstab to work.

### Traps Deploy While Moving

Trap deployment was reworked during development. Previously, placing a trap locked your character in a 1.15-second kneeling animation. Now (March 2026 update) **traps can be deployed while moving**, with a short upper-body animation of just under one second. Traps are therefore viable mid-combat, not just pre-combat.

### Throwing Potions

A new alchemy mechanic lets you **throw potions at enemies — or yourself** — once you level up your alchemy skill and obtain the specific perk. This makes alchemy an offensive combat system, not just healing.

### Magic and the Environment — Know the Limits

Titan Roc has been explicit about magic's limits:

- Spells can be used to **trigger things like traps**.
- Spells **do not fully interact with the environment** — no setting trees on fire, no water spells creating mud.
- **Combining spells (fire + ice) is not planned** for this title (the developers would consider it for a sequel).
- **Day/night and weather are purely cosmetic** — they do not affect spells or combat.

### Spell Customisation

Spells can be customised to increase **damage, range, and casting speed** — the three spell attributes the developers have confirmed.

## How to Fight Khraiax

The khraiax are the main threat: grotesque, insect-like creatures that swarmed and overwhelmed Vahrin, killing the king and thousands of soldiers from the Free Cities who marched to aid the city. Practical advice based on confirmed behavior:

- They come in **swarms** — positioning matters more than raw DPS.
- **Dodge with precise timing**; holding down the attack button against a swarm will get you surrounded.
- Use the environment — choke points, corridors, and your own traps to thin the swarm.
- Fire-based options and thrown potions are strong tools for groups (elemental balance data is unconfirmed, but area denial is widely effective).

## Practical Combat Tips from Launch Feedback

1. **Set expectations for impact feedback.** Enemies may not stagger or react when hit. This feels wrong at first; it is a consistent trait of the game, not a bug on your end. The developers have acknowledged the feedback.
2. **Pace your attacks.** Since every action has commitment, over-swinging against swarms is the fastest way to eat damage.
3. **Use traps mid-fight.** The reworked deployment animation means you can drop a trap while backing away from a pursuer — use it.
4. **Throw potions offensively.** Once you have the alchemy perk, damaging potions give ranged options to any build.
5. **Learn boss patterns.** Boss fights are pattern-recognition checks: watch wind-ups, punish recovery windows, and don't greed.
6. **Keep a melee weapon equipped for stealth.** If you want backstabs, don't rely on the game to switch weapons for you.

## What Is NOT Confirmed

The following are commonly repeated online but **not confirmed** by any official source — do not build your strategy around them:

- Exact stamina costs per action (dodge, block, sprint)
- Parry timing windows or i-frames (whether a parry mechanic exists at all)
- Animation-cancel options
- Status effects (burning, frozen, bleeding) and their exact values
- Elemental weakness/resistance charts

When Titan Roc publishes detailed combat documentation — or when we verify mechanics in-game with video evidence — this guide will be updated.
