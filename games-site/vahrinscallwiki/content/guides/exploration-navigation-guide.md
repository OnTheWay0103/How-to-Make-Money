# Exploration & Navigation Guide — Shrines of Sol, Fast Travel & Checkpoints

Vahrin's Call is a zone-based, non-linear world, and moving through it efficiently matters as much as fighting well. Since launch, players have documented the game's travel systems in detail — including facts our [Zones & Areas Guide](/guides/zones-areas-guide) had to mark unconfirmed at launch. This guide collects what is now community-verified about checkpoints, fast travel, death, and the map, and keeps the honest "unconfirmed" ledger for the rest.

## Shrines of Sol — Your Checkpoint System

**Shrines of Sol** are the game's checkpoints, confirmed by launch-era guides and mapped throughout the world:

- Dying (or reloading the game) **respawns you at the last Shrine of Sol you activated**.
- The **closest shrine auto-activates** when you pass it — you do not need to interact with every shrine, just reach it.
- They are treated as a **distinct map marker category** from fast travel points, so you can always tell them apart on the map.

### Verified Shrine Counts by Zone

Community maps document Shrines of Sol in every confirmed zone:

| Zone | Shrines of Sol |
|---|---|
| South Vale | 13 (including one underground) |
| Forest Edge | 8 (including one on the 1st floor) |
| Lower City | 7 |
| Market | 2 |
| Petrified Forest | 2 |
| South Vale Hamlet | 1 |

That is **33 documented shrines** across six zones. South Vale and Forest Edge — the game's largest early zones — are the most generously checkpointed, which matches their size and backtracking-heavy design.

### Practical Shrine Strategy

1. **Never skip a shrine.** Auto-activation means passing one costs nothing, and death can come from a surprise swarm or boss — the last shrine is your recovery point.
2. **Clear toward the next shrine.** Before pushing into a boss-shaped room or unknown dungeon section, make sure a shrine is behind you. If not, backtrack to the nearest one first.
3. **Note the underground shrine in South Vale.** The only documented underground shrine sits in the largest zone — a hint that deep exploration there is expected to be punishing.

## Fast Travel — The Verified Network

Fast travel exists, and the community has named and mapped the network. Our Zones guide flagged this as unconfirmed at launch; **it is now verified by player-mapped data**. Fast travel is done via **signposts** found in most zones, each with a proper name:

| Fast travel point | Zone |
|---|---|
| South Vale Hamlet | South Vale Hamlet |
| Market | Market |
| Lower City | Lower City |
| Petrified Forest | Petrified Forest |
| South Vale: Road | South Vale |
| South Vale: Lumbermill | South Vale |
| Forest Edge: South | Forest Edge |
| Forest Edge: North | Forest Edge |

**Eight documented signposts across six zones.** Large zones get two (South Vale, Forest Edge), smaller zones get one. The classic use is returning to **South Vale Hamlet** — the first town and the game's upgrade and brewing hub — to sell loot, upgrade gear, and brew potions before heading back out.

### Practical Fast Travel Tips

1. **Use signposts after clearing, not before.** Fast travel returns you to town; the journey out is where exploration income lives.
2. **Plan around the Lumbermill.** "South Vale: Lumbermill" is the only documented South Vale point besides the road — if a quest sends you deep into South Vale, the Lumbermill is likely the closer drop.
3. **Check every zone for a signpost.** All six mapped zones have at least one; if a new area lacks one, that is information about its size.

## Death, Respawn, and Save Files

- **Death respawns you at the last activated Shrine of Sol.** No community source documents a confirmed penalty beyond respawning — no dropped loot or lost XP has been verified, though specific death penalties remain officially unconfirmed.
- **Save files** are stored per-player at `C:\Users\<USERNAME>\AppData\Local\VahrinsCall\Saved\SaveGames` (community-reported; the exact path may vary by Steam library setup). Backing up this folder preserves your progress across machines or before experimenting with mods.
- **Quicksaving**: no confirmed quicksave or manual save options have been documented — the shrine system is the checkpoint mechanism.

## The Map and Discovery Tracker

The in-game map is one of the game's quiet strengths, and community maps confirm it tracks more than terrain:

- **Points of interest markers** for exits, fast travel points, chests, and skill books.
- A **discovery tracker** that records collectible progress — including **destroyed Genomy**, the gnome-like creatures from the side quest.
- Community documentation describes the maps as **unusually detailed** — a reliable tool rather than a decorative afterthought.

Practical map habits:

1. **Use the discovery tracker for completion.** If a zone shows undiscovered chests or books, the tracker is your shopping list.
2. **Mark locked doors mentally (or literally).** The Lower City's Sewer Key gates and the Market's vault entrances are documented map features you may need to return to.
3. **Cross-reference with landmarks.** Quest tracking is Morrowind-style (minimal HUD, no trackers by default) — read quest text and use map features to navigate; quest markers can be enabled if you prefer.

## Movement, Encumbrance, and Inventory

Travel isn't just about distance — how you carry and wear things changes how you move:

- **No maximum carrying capacity.** The inventory does not cap your total items (community-confirmed), so hoarding is free — but see encumbrance below.
- **Lootable items glow**, and the glow color is adjustable in settings.
- **Encumbrance is real**: exceeding your weight limit hurts running, jumping, and general movement. Heavy armor does the same — standard armor keeps your dodge fast and long, while heavy armor shortens it to a "simple side-step."
- The **Armour skill** improves dodging regardless of armor weight, so investment in it is a travel quality-of-life upgrade too.

## South Vale Hamlet — Your Home Base

The first town (South Vale Hamlet) is where the travel network funnels you:

- **Blacksmith upgrades** for gear (coin plus crafting materials — the community advice is to invest in armor over weapons, since weapons are replaced quickly).
- **Brewing** for potions and the alchemy systems.
- **Selling and dismantling** — old gear dismantles into crafting materials rather than selling for pennies.

With one shrine and one signpost, it is the smallest mapped hub — but every other zone's signpost connects back to it. Treat it as home base and the fast travel network as your commute.

## Performance Note for Travelers

Launch reports consistently describe **stutters when loading the overworld** — crossing zone boundaries can drop frames for a moment. This is a known launch issue the developers have committed to fixing. Practical mitigation: give big transitions a second before moving, and don't judge a zone's performance from its loading screen.

## What Is NOT Confirmed

- **Any death penalty** beyond respawning at the last shrine (XP loss, gold loss, item loss — none verified)
- **Manual/quicksave options** and their behavior
- Whether shrines **heal you** or provide bonuses on activation (no effect has been documented)
- The **exact save file path** on non-Windows setups and cloud-save behavior
- How fast travel **unlocks** (from the start, or gated by discovery — unverified)
- Whether zones **reset** enemies and loot on revisit
