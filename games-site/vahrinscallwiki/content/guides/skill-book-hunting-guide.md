# Skill Book Hunting Guide — The "for Oafs" Series and Every Confirmed Location

**A state-of-knowledge update**: at launch, no official skill book location list had been published — and none has been published since. What changed is the community's documentation: since launch, community maps and location guides have documented a substantial set of skill books by name and zone — including full walkthrough locations for Forest Edge and Barathrum Forest. This guide collects every location we can verify as of **August 6, 2026**, plus everything confirmed about how the book system works. Individual book-to-spot pairings are marked clearly where the data is thinner.

## Why Skill Books Matter (Refresher)

- Skills — including every weapon family and every magic school — grow **through use**, Elder-Scrolls-style.
- **Skill Books scattered through the world are required for full upgrades** of abilities (confirmed by developer updates). Books cannot be bought or earned purely through leveling.
- The result: hunting books is a direct power path, and the community's documented books confirm the **skill families** the game actually contains — great swords, dual weapons, great axes, shields, bows, armour, traps, alchemy, and the elemental magic schools.

## The "for Oafs" Series

Nearly every confirmed book belongs to a single in-universe series: **"X for Oafs"** — an instructional line of books ("Poke," "Traps Do Not Hurt," "Ice to See You!"). The series is how the world teaches you, and the titles are worth reading for their flavor alone. Books follow a consistent naming pattern that tells you exactly which skill they upgrade:

- **"Great Swords for Oafs," "Dual Weapons for Oafs," "Great Axes for Oafs," "Bows for Oafs," "Shields for Oafs," "Skill Book: Shield," "Armour for Oafs"** — weapon and defence skills
- **"Fire Magic for Oafs," "Ice Magic for Oafs," "Earth Magic for Oafs," "Alteration Magic for Oafs"** — magic schools
- **"Alchemy for Oafs," "Trap Making for Oafs"** — crafting skills

## Confirmed Books by Zone

### Barathrum Forest (2 books) — the starting zone

The community's clearest data. **Barathrum Forest** is the first area (it contains the game's Starting Spawn and an exit into Forest Edge), and it is unusually generous: **7 Shrines of Sol** — the most documented in any single mapped zone. The books here are also the easiest to collect:

- **Alchemy for Oafs: A Guide to Things That Are Edible More Than Once**
- **Shield Skill Book**

Both books — and the zone's two chests — sit at the **very end of the area, near the gate with friendly NPCs who warn you about venturing further**. Explore left and right of the gate; books and chests are marked by a golden sparkle.

### Forest Edge (5 books) — the first big area

Forest Edge is the largest early zone (12 chests, 12 Genomy statues, 4 notes on the community map) and the best-documented skill book location in the game. The five mapped locations:

1. **Hunter Cabin** — enter the area, hang left, continue straight until you see a hut with a lone hunter inside; the book is in the hut.
2. **Abandoned Cart Intersection** — mid-area intersection with tents and abandoned wagons **guarded by enemy soldiers**; defeat them and take the book from the table on the left.
3. **The Tower** — after the intersection, head past the abandoned carts, take the first left between rocks, and look for a hole with a warning sign. Jump into the underground pit — it leads to the tower's lower level, where a **Snatcher enemy wielding a great sword** waits. The book is on a table.
4. **Overgrown Cabin** — after unlocking the gate, return to the intersection from book #2 and look right. Behind a well is a damaged, overgrown cabin; the book is on a bedside table inside.
5. **Forest Edge Exit** — just before leaving Forest Edge, take a right before the Hamlet sign to find the last book.

The five named books documented for this zone:

| Book | Name |
|---|---|
| Skill | "Trap Making for Oafs: Traps Do Not Hurt" |
| Skill | "Alchemy for Oafs: So You've Chosen to Study Alchemy" |
| Skill | "Armour for Oafs: How to Be Harder to Kill" |
| Skill | "Great Swords for Oafs: Start to Finish — Mastering Attack Follow Through" |

The name-to-location pairing (which book sits in which spot) has not been fully published — collect all five and the zone is complete regardless.

### South Vale Hamlet (3 books)

The first town holds three books (plus 7 chests and 4 Genomy statues):

- **Bows for Oafs: Barbed Arrows for the Morally Flexible**
- **Armour for Oafs: Plate Expectations** — documented on the 2nd floor
- **Great Axes for Oafs: Jumping in Hammer First**

### South Vale (7 books)

The largest zone on the community maps (11 chests, 7 books, 5 Genomy statues) holds the most documented books in the game:

- **Great Swords for Oafs: Sweeping Arcs — Hitting Multiple Enemies Efficiently** (Underground)
- **Earth Magic for Oafs: So, You've Chosen to Study Earth Magic**
- **Fire Magic for Oafs: Fire Has Its Own Plans**
- **Alteration Magic for Oafs: So, You've Chosen to Study Alteration Magic** (2nd Floor)
- **Ice Magic for Oafs: Ice to See You!**
- **Skill Book: Shield**
- **Trap Making for Oafs: So You've Chosen to Study Trap Making**

The map pins only two of these with level hints (Underground, 2nd Floor) — the rest need standard thorough exploration. This zone is also where the only documented **underground Shrine of Sol** sits, so plan a deep dive.

### Garrison Road (3 books)

Garrison Road is a small corridor zone (7 mapped points of interest) connecting the Market to a Garrison area, with fast travel at both ends and one Shrine of Sol. It has no boss and only 2 chests — but 3 books:

- **Great Swords for Oafs: Cleaving with Confidence**
- **Fire Magic for Oafs: Your Burning Questions Answered**
- **Bows for Oafs: Making Murder Twigs**

### The Colosseum (4 books)

The Colosseum (reached from the Market) is the most marker-dense mapped area — 29 points of interest including 7 shrines, 6 locked doors, 1 hidden door, a lift, 6 ladders, and Rafters Access. It hides 4 books:

- **Shields for Oafs: Conditioning for the Uneven Shield Bearer**
- **Armour for Oafs: So, You've Decided to Wear Armour**
- **Earth Magic for Oafs: So, You're Buried in the Aftermath**
- **Alteration Magic for Oafs: The Necessary Agony — Everything Hurts**

The Colosseum also contains the solved **Colosseum Tomb puzzle** (unique sword and shield — see our [Puzzles & Secrets Guide](/guides/puzzles-secrets-guide)) and a documented basement boss, **Dirk the Flayer** — expect the deepest book to be guarded.

## Hunting Tips That Work Everywhere

1. **Golden sparkles mark books and chests** — when you enter a room, sweep for the glow before looting containers.
2. **Books share locations with quest-critical loot.** The Forest Edge tower book sits behind a Snatcher enemy — the same enemy family tied to the Snatcher's Vault password hunt.
3. **Watch level hints.** "2nd Floor," "Underground," and "Basement" labels on the community maps are the only breadcrumbs some books get — check vertical space.
4. **Do the gate check.** Barathrum Forest's books are at the end of the zone near friendly NPCs — early-game habit of sweeping the end of every area pays off.
5. **Track completion via the map.** The in-game discovery tracker records collectible progress; community maps confirm chest and book counts per zone so you know when a zone is done.
6. **Prioritise your primary skills.** Books are the gate to full upgrades — if you are a magic main, hit South Vale (fire, ice, earth, alteration) before garrisoning elsewhere.
