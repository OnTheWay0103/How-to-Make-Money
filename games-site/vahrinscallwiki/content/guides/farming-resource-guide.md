# Farming & Resource Guide — What's Confirmed

**Correction first**: earlier versions of this guide described specific farming routes through named zones (Barathrum Forest, Quarry Depths, Frozen District) with exact yields per run. **Those zone names and yields are not confirmed** — no official area list exists, and exact drop/yield numbers have never been published. This guide covers what is confirmed about earning gold, gear, and materials, plus honest farming principles that hold regardless of the map.

## Confirmed: How You Earn in Vahrin's Call

### Exploration Is the Economy

The game's core pitch is a mercenary "seeking to liberate the city, whilst claiming your fair share of gold and riches along the way" — looting is baked into the fantasy. Launch reviews confirm:

- The hand-crafted semi-open world is packed with **hidden treasures**.
- **Exploration is the game's strongest feature** — reviewers consistently describe it as genuinely rewarding.
- There is a **"whole huge area" hidden behind a side quest**, reportedly rich in content and rewards.

### Bounty Hunting

Official material confirms the Vale of Vahrin is populated by rival mercenaries, bandits, and monstrous beasts **that players can hunt for bounties**. Bounties are a confirmed income source — the specific bounty board locations and payouts are unconfirmed.

### Quest Rewards and Loot

Quests pay gold and gear (the main quest pays for contract work; side quests are described as surprisingly generous with content). Enemy loot and treasure are core — sell what you don't use.

## Honest Farming Principles (Work on Any Map)

Since no official farm routes exist, use principles that hold anywhere:

1. **Fully clear new areas once.** First-clear loot (chests, hidden treasures, books) is the biggest single income spike. The non-linear map means you'll backtrack — combine clearing with revisits.
2. **Hunt bounties during travel.** Stack bounty targets with your route; don't make dedicated bounty trips.
3. **Do side quests for material rewards.** Side quests reportedly open hidden content — including the huge hidden area.
4. **Loot containers thoroughly.** Crates, chests, and caches are the game's treasure language — check everything.
5. **Don't hoard low-value gear.** With no confirmed enhancement system, gear progression is find-based; sell duplicates.
6. **Stock up on ingredients when you find them.** Alchemy ingredients have no published spawn lists — when you find a cluster, take it all. You never know when you'll see it again.
7. **Craft with the perk in mind.** The potion-throwing perk needs leveled alchemy, so ingredients are an investment in your combat toolkit.

## Merchant and Economy Status

**Unconfirmed**: merchant inventory rotation, restock timers, buy/sell price ratios, and vendor specializations. Reviews do not document a robust player-driven economy, and no official source details merchant mechanics. If you see "merchants restock daily" advice online, it is unverified.

## A Realistic Time Budget

- Average playthrough: **15–30 hours** (official FAQ).
- Aggregated launch data (PlayPile): average playtime ~**22 hours**, with about 20% of players exceeding 35 hours through side content.
- Completion rate at ~45% — meaning half of players finish the main story, and the rest play heavily without completing it.

Farm with that in mind: the game's value is in exploration, not repetitive grinding — and with no confirmed respawn or reset mechanics, "grinding a route" may not even be possible.
