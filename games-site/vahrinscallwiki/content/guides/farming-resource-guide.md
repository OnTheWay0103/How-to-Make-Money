# Farming & Resource Guide

Whether you're stockpiling potions for a tough boss or grinding levels before the final dungeon, efficient farming saves hours. Here are the best routes, methods, and locations.

---

## Gold Farming

### Method 1: Bandit Camp Clear (Best Early Game)
- **Location**: Quarry Depths — bandit camp (southeast corner)
- **Enemies**: 8-12 bandits, 1 bandit leader
- **Gold per run**: 300-500 (drops + sellable gear)
- **Time per run**: 5-8 minutes
- **Additional loot**: Iron, Volatile Oil, Cross Slash Skill Book (first clear only)

**Strategy**: Clear the camp, loot all corpses and chests, sell unwanted gear to the Quarry merchant. Enemies respawn when you leave the zone and return.

### Method 2: Catacombs Loot Run (Best Mid Game)
- **Location**: Vahrin Catacombs
- **Enemies**: Undead, skeletons, one mini-boss (respawns)
- **Gold per run**: 600-900
- **Time per run**: 10-12 minutes
- **Additional loot**: Crystal Dust, Moonpetal, Azure Moss

**Strategy**: Full clear of the first two catacomb levels. Skip the third level (boss room — not efficient for farming). Undead drop valuable trinkets that sell well. The mini-boss on level 2 drops a guaranteed Rare item each clear.

### Method 3: Material Flipping (Passive)
- Buy **Volatile Oil** from merchants (15-25 gold each)
- Craft into **Fire Bombs** (requires Ember Root)
- Sell crafted Fire Bombs for 60-80 gold each
- **Profit margin**: ~200% return on Volatile Oil

## Experience (XP) Farming

### Best XP Spot: Temple Ruins
- **Location**: Temple Ruins (mid-game area)
- **Enemies**: Undead priests, animated armor, wraiths
- **XP per run**: 2,000-3,500
- **Time per run**: 8-10 minutes

**Why Temple Ruins?**
- High enemy density — no downtime between fights
- Multiple enemy types = varied combat = less boring
- Wraiths give bonus XP (1.5× standard)
- Blizzard Skill Book location = double value

### Alternative: Barathrum Forest Loop
- **XP per run**: 1,000-1,800
- **Time per run**: 4-6 minutes
- **Best for**: Levels 1-15, alchemy material farming simultaneously

### XP Boosts
- Rest at campfires for "Well Rested" buff: +20% XP for 30 minutes
- Intelligence Elixir grants +15% XP from magic kills
- Certain armor pieces have "+X% experience gained" as a bonus stat

## Material Farming Routes

### Route 1: The Beginner's Loop (Barathrum Forest)
**Best for**: Bloodleaf, Azure Moss, Wind Thistle, early levels

1. Enter Barathrum Forest from Vahrin Outskirts
2. Follow the main path, checking each tree stump for **Bloodleaf** (red flowers)
3. At the first fork, go left to the cliff edge — **Wind Thistle** spawns here
4. Enter the small cave on the right path — **Azure Moss** on walls
5. Clear the ruined camp (3-5 enemies, loot chest)
6. Exit and repeat

**Yield per 10-minute loop**: 12-18 Bloodleaf, 6-10 Azure Moss, 2-4 Wind Thistle, 200-400 gold

### Route 2: The Cavern Run (Quarry Depths + Crystal Cavern)
**Best for**: Earth Shard, Crystal Dust, Iron, Ore

1. Enter Quarry Depths
2. Clear the upper level mining nodes (4-6 nodes, **Earth Shard** + **Iron**)
3. Take the elevator to the deepest shaft
4. Find the hidden Crystal Cavern entrance (cracked wall — use Hammer charged attack)
5. Inside Crystal Cavern: **Crystal Dust** nodes (4-8), Crystal Golem mini-boss
6. Exit and repeat

**Yield per 15-minute run**: 8-14 Earth Shard, 10-16 Iron, 4-8 Crystal Dust, 500-700 gold (golem drops)

### Route 3: The Night Harvest (Old Vahrin Walls)
**Best for**: Moonpetal, Nightshade, Fallen Mercenary boss

**Important**: Moonpetal only blooms at night! Use the campfire to wait until night.

1. Enter Old Vahrin Walls
2. Follow the wall path, checking every flower patch — **Moonpetal** glows blue at night
3. Enter the graveyard section for **Nightshade** (purple plants near tombstones)
4. Optionally fight the Fallen Mercenary hidden boss (night only, drops legendary greatsword)
5. Exit and repeat

**Yield per 10-minute run**: 8-12 Moonpetal, 5-8 Nightshade, optional boss loot

### Route 4: The Frozen Run (Frozen District)
**Best for**: Frost Petal, Frost Spear Skill Book, mid-game XP

1. Enter Frozen District
2. Check frozen plant clusters for **Frost Petal** (white-blue flowers)
3. Clear enemy packs (ice wraiths, frozen soldiers) for XP + gold
4. Visit the ice cave for Frost Spear Skill Book (first visit only)
5. Visit the watchtower for Elemental Riposte Skill Book (first visit only)
6. Exit and repeat

**Yield per 12-minute run**: 6-10 Frost Petal, 1,500-2,500 XP, 400-600 gold

## Merchant Stock Rotation

Understanding merchant mechanics saves hours of farming:

### Restocking
- All merchant inventories refresh every **in-game day** (wait at campfire)
- Each merchant has a fixed pool of items they can stock
- Rare items have a 10-20% chance to appear each restock

### Best Merchants by Category

| Merchant | Location | Specializes In |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| Alchemist Elara | Merchant Quarter | Potion ingredients, recipes |
| Blacksmith Torvin | Smoldering Quarter | Ore, metal, weapon upgrades |
| Fletcher Sera | Upper Vahrin | Arrows, bow mods, leather |
| Curio Merchant | Merchant Quarter (rotates) | Random rare items, trinkets |
| Apothecary | Temple District | Potions, elixirs, Amnesia Elixir |

### Flipping Strategy
1. Buy Volatile Oil whenever available (all merchants, 15-25g)
2. Craft into bombs/traps at Alchemy Station
3. Sell crafted items for 2-3× material cost
4. Use profits to buy rare materials when they appear

## Inventory Management

### What to Always Keep
- **Bloodleaf**: Always. You can never have enough healing potions.
- **Azure Moss**: Mana potions are essential for mage builds.
- **Crystal Dust**: Rare, used in the best recipes. Never sell.
- **Volatile Oil**: Buy whenever available, even if you don't need it now.

### What to Sell
- **Common weapons/armor** (white): Sell to any merchant
- **Duplicate uncommon gear**: Sell unless it has a better bonus stat
- **Excess Iron**: Keep a stack of 20, sell the rest

### Storage
Use the **Storage Chest** at any settlement campfire to offload materials. Items in storage are accessible from any storage chest (shared inventory).

## Efficient Farming Principles

1. **Combine objectives**: Never farm one thing. Always combine material farming + XP + gold on the same route.
2. **Skill Books first**: Each farming area has at least one Skill Book. Prioritize finding it — subsequent runs are pure farming.
3. **Night vs. Day**: Moonpetal and Nightshade only spawn at night. Plan separate day and night routes.
4. **Respawn mechanics**: Enemies and nodes respawn when you leave and re-enter a zone. Fast travel resets count.
5. **Haste Potion for farming**: The Haste Potion (+30% movement speed) pays for itself in time saved on repeated routes.
6. **Inventory upgrades**: Certain quests and merchants sell bag upgrades. Prioritize these — more inventory = fewer trips back to town.
