# NPCs & Characters Guide — Every Confirmed Character in the Vale of Vahrin

Vahrin's Call is fully voiced and full of names, but at launch almost nothing about its characters was officially documented — which is why no character guide existed on this wiki. Since then, community maps have pinned the game's named characters in place, and developer posts have explained how the game introduces them. This guide collects every character we can confirm as of **August 6, 2026**: who they are, where they stand, and what role the map itself assigns them. Where a character's purpose is unknown, we say so — no invented backstories.

## How the Game Introduces Characters (Confirmed)

A developer post (by Robin_TitanRoc on Steam) confirms the game's presentation system:

- **Cutscenes are used in exactly two places**: to introduce a **new boss**, and in **NPC conversations that progress the main story**.
- These conversation cutscenes are built in **UE4's Sequencer** — full dialogue lines, character animations, facial expressions, and camera shots, with the ability to skip to the next line.
- Everything is **voiced by real voice actors**, while your mercenary protagonist stays silent.

Practical takeaways: if you see a cinematic start for a character, that character is a boss or a main-story figure — treat them as important. And because only main-story conversations get cutscenes, side-quest characters are introduced more casually.

That is a remarkable density for a hub the community map describes as the smallest zone (1 shrine, 1 signpost). What the roles tell us:

- **Two merchants** (Devisha Harroway, Rosyn Felux) plus the **Blacksmith** — a full service town.
- **A tavern** (Adien Tudbury) — the classic place for gossip, quest hooks, and the game's brewing system (see our [Exploration & Navigation Guide](/guides/exploration-navigation-guide) for the town's brewing station).
- **Two boss-tier residents**: Dallen Bellmore and Talia Almath are both flagged as Boss/Miniboss encounters **inside the hamlet itself**. The first town contains fights, not just shops — clear the hamlet's named characters before treating it as home base.
- **Artor Catell is documented as Deceased** — a death the map itself records. The community notes his body as a point of interest; whether he dies in a quest, an ambush, or by player choice is not documented.

## Named Minibosses Across the Vale

Community maps now document **four named boss-tier encounters** in the mapped zones. Fight details (health, phases, movesets, drops) are **not** published for any of them — but the names and arenas are verified:

| Boss | Zone | Map Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sylvia Lynderly** | Barathrum Forest | Only boss in the starting zone |
| **Dirk the Flayer** | Colosseum (basement) | The Colosseum's only documented boss; shares the map with the Colosseum Tomb puzzle |
| **Dallen Bellmore** | South Vale Hamlet | Also listed as a resident NPC |
| **Talia Almath** | South Vale Hamlet | Boss/Miniboss in the first town |

Two patterns worth noting: bosses are tied to the **mapped dungeon-style areas** (Colosseum basement) and to **hub characters** (the hamlet pair), and the starting zone already has one. Also remember the dev post: every boss gets a cinematic introduction.

## The Snatcher — Enemy Type and Vault Keeper

"The Snatcher" appears twice in confirmed content, and the two are connected:

- **Enemy type**: a **Snatcher enemy wielding a great sword** is documented guarding the tower pit in Forest Edge (it drops a skill book — see our [Skill Book Hunting Guide](/guides/skill-book-hunting-guide)).
- **Vault**: **Snatcher's Vault** in the Market requires **password notes collected across multiple zones** (Forest Edge, South Vale, Market — see our [Puzzles & Secrets Guide](/guides/puzzles-secrets-guide)).

The vault's namesake is likely the enemy family (or a specific individual) that hoards what it steals. Whether the vault's password hunt ends in a character confrontation is unconfirmed — but the pattern fits a named "boss" reveal.

## The Characters Behind the Story (Lore Figures)

These figures are confirmed through official lore and in-world documents rather than map pins:

- **King Aldous Symme** — led the Combined Order's armies into the depths beneath the city against the khraiax; the king of Vahrin lies dead after the invasion. The campaign's fallen hero figure. (Full story: [Lore & Story Guide](/guides/lore-story-guide).)
- **The Combined Order of Humankind** — not a character but the banner under which the Free Cities' soldiers marched; the surviving factional landscape of the Vale is defined by its collapse.
- **Lady Devellian** — her **journal page** is a documented clue item in the Market, tied to the Vault of Vahrin mystery.
- **The Taxman** — the **Taxman's Journal** is the other documented clue item in the Market; whether he is a character we meet, a dead man's papers, or the vault's owner is unverified.
- **Artor Catell** — the hamlet's documented deceased resident (above).

The in-world note economy (journal pages, password notes, letters) is the game's character ledger for everyone who is not a main-story figure.

## Practical Character Advice

1. **Talk to every named NPC in the hamlet.** With two merchants, a tavern keeper, a blacksmith, and two hidden boss-tier encounters, the hub is a full content node — and boss-flagging means some conversations may turn hostile.
2. **Expect cutscenes at big moments.** A sudden cinematic means a boss or a story milestone — put your hand on the dodge button rather than the skip button.
3. **Clear Barathrum Forest's boss early.** Sylvia Lynderly is in the starting zone — one of the earliest named fights in the game.
4. **Collect journals and notes as evidence.** Lady Devellian's Page and the Taxman's Journal are clue items; characters we cannot pin to a location are still documented in their papers.
5. **Treat the Snatcher hunt as a character hunt.** The Forest Edge Snatcher and the Snatcher's Vault passwords point at the same thread — follow it to its (unconfirmed) conclusion.
