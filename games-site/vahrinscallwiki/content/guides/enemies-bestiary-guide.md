# Enemies & Bestiary Guide

Vahrin's Call is a war story, and its enemies come in two confirmed halves: the **khraiax** — the insect-like horde that destroyed the city — and the **human and beast threats** that fill the Vale while the city burns. This guide covers every enemy type confirmed by official sources and launch reviews, with honest combat advice built from confirmed mechanics. Individual enemy names, health values, and drop tables have **not** been officially published; nothing below invents them.

## The Khraiax — The Main Threat

The khraiax are described officially as **grotesque, insect-like creatures** that swarmed and overwhelmed the city of Vahrin. Lore context:

- They killed the **king of Vahrin**.
- Thousands of soldiers from the **Free Cities**, who marched to aid the city under the banner of the **Combined Order**, died against them.
- And the twist: while the khraiax were being defeated, **"a far greater danger rose from beneath the city's streets."** The khraiax are not the worst thing in this game.

### How Khraiax Fight

Launch reviews give us a clear behavioral picture:

- **They swarm.** Khraiax attacks come in numbers — reviewers describe needing "precise timing to dodge khraiax swarms."
- **They punish greedy offense.** With the game's commitment-based combat (no animation cancelling, stamina-limited magic), standing still to DPS is how you get surrounded.
- **They are the tutorial enemy.** The opening hours teach you to read their attacks before anything else.

### Fighting Khraiax — Confirmed-Strategy Advice

1. **Move constantly.** Swarm fights are won by positioning, not trading blows.
2. **Use area control.** Traps deploy while moving (confirmed rework), and thrown potions (confirmed alchemy perk) give you area denial in swarm fights.
3. **Use chokepoints.** The game's corridors, streets, and dungeon spaces funnel swarms — fight where only one or two can reach you.
4. **Fire options are widely reported effective** against insect-type enemies by community players — **unconfirmed officially**, but consistent with genre expectations and worth trying.
5. **Don't expect visible reactions.** Enemies may not stagger or flinch when hit — the most common combat criticism in Steam reviews. Track progress by health bar, not reaction.

## Confirmed Enemy Categories

Beyond the khraiax, official materials confirm the following categories populate the Vale of Vahrin:

### Bandits

- **Confirmed via**: official description (the Vale is home to "bandits" players can hunt).
- **Where**: city outskirts, ruins, roads — the classic bandit territories.
- **Fighting them**: they are humanoid — meaning the **backstab** is confirmed to work on them (the developers confirmed sneak backstabs against **human enemies** for significant damage). Reviewers note stealth is clunky (no prompt, melee must be manually equipped), but the damage payoff is real.

### Rival Mercenaries

- **Confirmed via**: official description — the Vale is populated by **rival mercenaries**.
- **Context**: you are not the only soldier-for-hire in a city full of contracts and gold. Rival mercs are the dangerous human encounters — armed, organized, and in it for the same payday as you.
- **Fighting them**: human enemies with weapons mean blocking is valuable (blocking is confirmed functional); parry-style precision is unconfirmed, so rely on timed guard and dodge.

### Monstrous Beasts

- **Confirmed via**: official description — **monstrous beasts** roam the Vale.
- **Where**: bogs, caverns, and wilder zones (see [Zones & Areas Guide](/guides/zones-areas-guide)).
- **Fighting them**: beast patterns are less readable than human ones — reviews suggest learning each beast's wind-ups before committing to offense.

### "Other Hideous Creatures"

- **Confirmed via**: official description (the Vale is full of "other hideous creatures").
- **Note**: no names, no appearances, no behaviors officially documented. What lurks in the murky bogs and magical caverns is TBD — and the "far greater danger" beneath the city is the biggest unknown of all.

### Bounty Targets

- **Confirmed via**: official description — these enemies **can be hunted for bounties**.
- **Practical meaning**: gold income confirmed from killing; specific bounty boards, target lists, and payouts are unconfirmed.

## The War So Far — Enemy Lore Timeline

Official lore gives the khraiax a proper history, worth knowing because it explains where every enemy you meet comes from:

1. **The khraiax swarm the city.** Grotesque, insect-like creatures overwhelm Vahrin, which was already "on the brink of collapse."
2. **The king falls.** Vahrin's king is killed in the invasion.
3. **The Free Cities answer the call.** Thousands of soldiers from the Free Cities march to aid the city under the banner of the Combined Order.
4. **The army breaks.** Thousands of those soldiers die against the khraiax. The survivors are the ruined, war-torn city you arrive in.
5. **The khraiax are (mostly) defeated...** and the city is now a battleground of leftovers: khraiax nests, looters, and mercenaries.
6. **...but something worse stirs.** Official lore: **"a far greater danger rose from beneath the city's streets."** Every zone underneath the city is a question mark with teeth.

Your enemies, then, are the shattered pieces of this war: the remnants of the swarm, the humans who prey on the ruin, and the unknown that took advantage of it all.

## Fighting by Category — Matching Your Approach to the Enemy

Different confirmed enemy families demand different confirmed tools:

| Enemy family | Confirmed behavior | Best confirmed approach | Avoid |
|--------------|--------------------|------------------------|-------|
| **Khraiax (swarms)** | Precise dodge timing required; come in numbers | Traps while kiting, thrown potions, chokepoints, area damage | Standing still, greedy combos |
| **Bandits** | Humanoid — organized, blockable | Timed block, counter; backstab when sneaked | Trading blows head-on without guard |
| **Rival mercenaries** | Humanoid, armed and dangerous | Guard discipline, stamina management, spells to trigger traps | Fighting in their prepared positions |
| **Monstrous beasts** | Aggressive, less readable patterns | Learn wind-ups first, then punish; dodge over block | Attacking before seeing the pattern |
| **Bounty targets** | Huntable for gold | Treat as bosses — come prepared, collect the pay | Under-gearing a known-hard fight |

The confirmed universal rule: **the game's combat is commitment-based**. Every swing, dodge, and cast has weight, and stamina gates prolonged magic. Against every family, the rhythm is the same: read the pattern, avoid the hit, punish the recovery.

## The Bounty Economy — What Hunting Means

The bounty system is confirmed in the game's official description: the Vale is populated by threats that "players can hunt for bounties." Practical implications:

- Bounties are a **confirmed income source** — gold for kills, on top of loot.
- Bounty targets sit alongside the main enemy families — bandits, rival mercenaries, and beasts are all fair game.
- **Unconfirmed**: where bounties are posted, who pays, whether there are difficulty tiers, and whether bounties are repeatable.

Strategy until confirmed: treat every notable kill as a potential bounty — clear camps fully, remember named or tougher enemies, and check every hub NPC for payouts.

## Defensive Toolkit by Enemy Type

Confirmed defense works differently per enemy family:

- **Against humanoids (bandits, mercenaries)**: **blocking** is confirmed functional and shines here — human attacks are readable and guardable. Backstabs also work on human enemies for significant damage (stealth is clunky: no prompt, manually equipped melee — but the payoff is confirmed).
- **Against swarms (khraiax)**: **dodging with precise timing** is the confirmed tool — you cannot block your way out of being surrounded. Movement, not guard, is the answer.
- **Against beasts**: mix both — watch the wind-up, block or dodge to match, and punish.
- **Against everything**: **traps deploy while moving** (confirmed) — a trap dropped mid-kite is damage you didn't pay in stamina; **thrown potions** (confirmed alchemy perk) are the same idea at range.

## Enemy FAQ

**Are the khraiax the final enemy?**
No — official lore says a "far greater danger" rose from beneath the city. The khraiax are the visible threat; the true endgame antagonist is something else entirely.

**Can I sneak past enemies?**
Stealth exists but is confirmed clunky (no sneak-attack prompt, melee must be equipped manually). Sneaking for backstabs is viable against human enemies; expect to fight through khraiax swarms.

**Do enemies drop loot?**
Loot is a core system and enemies are part of it — exact drop tables are unconfirmed.

**Do enemies respawn?**
Unconfirmed — no official statement. Treat farming guides that promise "enemies respawn on zone reset" as unverified (see [Farming & Resource Guide](/guides/farming-resource-guide)).

## Enemy Design — The Honest Criticism

Steam's "Mixed" launch rating and multiple reviews highlight **repetitive enemy design** — the same creature archetypes resurface throughout the game with limited variation. This is the flip side of a hand-crafted game built by seven people over twelve years. Expectations set: the khraiax swarms are excellent in the opening hours and get familiar by mid-game.
