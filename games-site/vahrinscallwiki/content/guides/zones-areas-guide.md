# Zones & Areas Guide — The Vale of Vahrin

Vahrin's Call is set in the **Vale of Vahrin** — a hand-crafted, semi-open fantasy world built around the war-torn city of the same name. This guide collects everything officially confirmed about the game's zones and areas. It is deliberately shorter on invented detail than most wiki guides, because Titan Roc has not published a full area list — and we refuse to make one up. What is confirmed is rich enough to plan your exploration around.

## The Confirmed World Structure

Official materials describe the world as **zone-based** with **semi-open areas** connected to **more structured dungeon-style areas**. In plain terms:

- The world is not a single open map (like a modern Elder Scrolls game) and not instanced levels (like a classic dungeon crawler). It sits between: larger connected zones you can roam, with dungeons layered inside them.
- Each semi-open area contains **enemies, quests, puzzles, loot, and NPCs** — the full game loop happens inside each zone.
- The map is **non-linear** and hand-crafted (not procedurally generated) — launch reviews praise this: forests feel dense, towns feel lived-in.

**A caution from reviews**: the overworld can cause **performance drops when loading** (a known launch issue the developers have committed to fixing). Expect stutters when crossing zone boundaries until optimization patches ship.

## The World of Leyda

Vahrin's Call takes place in the world of **Leyda**, which is split between endless darkness and endless sun. Those hostile extremes are the "**Forsaken Realms**" of the title — the wastelands beyond the settled lands. The game itself is set in the Vale of Vahrin, the war-torn region where the story unfolds.

## Confirmed Regions and Environments

These are the environments explicitly confirmed by official materials (Steam page, FAQ, press releases) — no names beyond these should be trusted:

### 1. The City of Vahrin

The centerpiece: a **war-torn city on the brink of collapse**. The city was overwhelmed by the khraiax; its king was killed, along with thousands of soldiers from the Free Cities who marched to aid it under the banner of the Combined Order. As a mercenary you fight through the city's streets district by district, reclaiming ground.

- Expect: urban combat, ruined buildings, streets full of khraiax and bandits, quest hubs with NPCs.
- The city's **underground** is the story's biggest mystery — see below.

### 2. Overgrown City Streets

Official descriptions of the Vale mention **overgrown city streets** — the mark of a settlement that has fallen to nature (and monsters) since the khraiax invasion. These are the classic ARPG "urban ruins" spaces: exploration-heavy, with buildings to enter and hidden corners.

### 3. Magical Caverns

The Vale includes **magical caverns** — underground spaces where the game's fantasy elements surface. Expect puzzles, glowing environments, and the kind of hidden treasure rooms reviewers praise. Caverns are also where the underground threat narrative points.

### 4. Murky Bogs

Swamp territory: **murky bogs** with slow terrain, ambush-friendly visibility, and the game's murkier enemies. Bog areas are the classic place for the "other hideous creatures" the game promises — see our [Enemies & Bestiary Guide](/guides/enemies-bestiary-guide).

### 5. Dungeon-Style Areas

Beyond the open zones, the game features **more structured dungeon-style areas** — tighter, encounter-designed spaces with the traditional dungeon rhythm: puzzles, locked doors, boss-shaped rooms, and concentrated loot.

### 6. The Hidden Area (Behind a Side Quest)

Launch reviews confirm the existence of a **"whole huge area" hidden behind a side quest** — a region-sized chunk of the world that players following only the main story will never see. Reportedly one of the game's best zones, it is the single most important exploration target in the game. Finding it requires doing side quests — which is exactly why the side content is worth your time.

## The Threat Beneath the City

The story's central mystery is confirmed in official lore: while the khraiax were being defeated, **"a far greater danger rose from beneath the city's streets."** The deepest zones of the game are connected to this underground presence. What it is, where exactly it lives, and what it looks like are not confirmed — but the deepest caverns and any "depths" areas are where the endgame story lives.

## The Forsaken Realms Beyond the Vale

The title refers to more than just the war. Official lore divides the world of **Leyda** into two hostile extremes:

- **Lands of endless darkness** — where the sun never rises.
- **Lands of endless sun** — where it never sets.

These extremes are the "Forsaken Realms" proper — the wastelands that flank the settled world. The game is set entirely in the **Vale of Vahrin**, so you will not trek into the endless extremes in this title, but the lore frames why the world feels isolated and embattled: the Vale is a thin band of survivable land between two impossible regions, now torn apart by war from within.

## Semi-Open Zones vs. Dungeon Areas — How to Play Each

The FAQ's zone structure ("semi-open areas" plus "more structured dungeon-style areas") implies two different play rhythms. Reviews give us enough to plan for both:

### Semi-Open Zones

- **What they are**: large, free-roaming spaces with enemies, quests, puzzles, loot, and NPCs — the game's "alive" spaces. Towns, streets, forests, bogs.
- **How to play them**: pace yourself. Reviewers praise these spaces for density — poke into every building, follow every side path. They are where the game's exploration magic lives.
- **The catch**: non-linear layouts and backtracking mean routes cross themselves. Use landmarks; the game's Morrowind-style quest tracking (minimal HUD, no trackers by default) expects you to navigate by description. Quest markers can be enabled if you prefer.

### Dungeon-Style Areas

- **What they are**: tighter, structured spaces with encounter design — puzzles, locked doors, concentrated loot, and boss-shaped rooms. The "more structured" half of the map.
- **How to play them**: go prepared. Dungeons concentrate threats (see [Enemies & Bestiary Guide](/guides/enemies-bestiary-guide)) and are the natural home of the game's deeper combat checks. Bring healing, traps, and a stock of thrown potions — the confirmed consumable toolkit.
- **The catch**: the underground storyline — from the magical caverns to whatever rises beneath the city — points at dungeons as the story's spine. Expect the biggest reveals underground.

## First-Visit Checklist for Any New Zone

1. **Find the quest hub.** Semi-open zones have NPCs — they hold the quests (including side quests, which is where the hidden area lives).
2. **Scan for puzzle hooks.** The game's puzzles are confirmed environmental — unusual statues, locked doors, pressure plates.
3. **Clear every building.** Loot and Skill Books hide in the spaces you could skip.
4. **Check the edges.** Hidden treasures concentrate on out-of-the-way paths; reviewers consistently found the best stuff off the beaten track.
5. **Note the exits.** Non-linear maps mean you will return — remember where each path goes so backtracking pays off.
6. **Watch the load.** The overworld can stutter when loading (confirmed launch issue, optimization patches announced) — give big transitions a moment.

## Exploration Strategy for the Vale

1. **Follow side quests first.** The hidden area is the confirmed payoff of side content. Treat any side quest as a potential gateway to a new zone.
2. **Clear zones fully before moving on.** The non-linear map means you'll return anyway — first clears are where the loot lives.
3. **Push into the underground.** The story's confirmed endgame threat is beneath the city; the deepest zones hold the biggest answers.
4. **Expect to backtrack.** Reviews call the map layout backtracking-heavy. Budget travel time and keep your loadout ready for already-cleared routes.
5. **Mind performance at zone boundaries.** Loading the overworld can stutter at launch — the developers have confirmed optimization work is coming.
6. **Explore caverns and bogs thoroughly.** These are the environments where hidden treasures concentrate per official description ("hidden secrets" across the Vale).
