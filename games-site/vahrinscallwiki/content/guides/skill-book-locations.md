# Skill Book Locations Guide

Skill Books are the most valuable collectibles in Vahrin's Call. They unlock new active abilities and passive bonuses that **cannot be obtained** through leveling alone. Missing them means missing entire parts of your build's potential.

---

## How Skill Books Work

- Found hidden throughout the world (no quest markers)
- Read to permanently unlock the skill for that character
- Some require minimum weapon proficiency level to use
- Can be found in any order (not level-gated)
- **Detect Magic** (Alteration spell) reveals nearby books within 30m

### Always Use Detect Magic
The Alteration spell **Detect Magic** is your most important exploration tool. Cast it every time you enter a new room or area. Skill Books glow gold when detected.

## Greatsword Skill Books

### Cleave
- **Location**: Vahrin Garrison, armory room (second floor)
- **Direction**: From the main hall, take the right staircase. Break the weakened wall in the back corner.
- **Effect**: Horizontal arc attack hitting all enemies in front

### Heavy Slam
- **Location**: Smoldering Quarter, collapsed building
- **Direction**: Enter the building with the collapsed roof. Crawl through the gap in the debris. Book is on a corpse in the basement.
- **Effect**: Overhead smash with 2× stagger damage

### Whirlwind
- **Location**: Temple Ruins, center chamber
- **Direction**: Solve the statue puzzle (align all four statues facing inward). The floor opens to a hidden room.
- **Requirement**: Greatsword proficiency level 20
- **Effect**: 360° spin attack

## Sword & Shield Skill Books

### Fortify Stance
- **Location**: Vahrin Garrison, training yard
- **Direction**: Behind the training dummies. Break the wooden barricade.
- **Effect**: Reduced stamina cost while blocking

### Elemental Riposte
- **Location**: Frozen District, abandoned watchtower
- **Direction**: Climb to the top of the watchtower. Book is on a frozen corpse near the signal fire.
- **Effect**: After a perfect block, counter with elemental damage matching your equipped magic

---

## Dual Wield Skill Books

### Flurry
- **Location**: Merchant Quarter, thieves' den
- **Direction**: Enter through the basement of the abandoned warehouse. Navigate past traps (use Detect Magic to see tripwires). Book is in the loot chest.
- **Effect**: Rapid 5-hit combo

### Cross Slash
- **Location**: Quarry Depths, bandit camp
- **Direction**: Clear the bandit camp in the southeast corner of Quarry Depths. Book is in the camp leader's tent.
- **Effect**: X-shaped attack with bleed chance

### Shadow Dance
- **Location**: Upper Vahrin, rooftop garden
- **Direction**: Access the rooftops via the fallen crane near the Observatory. Follow the rooftops east to find a hidden garden. Book is under the gazebo.
- **Requirement**: Dual Wield proficiency level 20
- **Effect**: Increased dodge i-frames while dual wielding

## Hammer Skill Books

### Armor Break
- **Location**: Quarry Depths, mining foreman's office
- **Direction**: Enter the mining complex. The foreman's office is on the upper level, accessible by repairing the elevator (requires 3 Iron).
- **Effect**: Next attack ignores 60% armor

### Seismic Slam
- **Location**: Smoldering Quarter, forge basement
- **Direction**: In the main forge building, pull the hidden lever behind the anvil. The basement contains the book.
- **Effect**: AoE ground pound, knocks down nearby enemies

---

## Bow Skill Books

### Piercing Shot
- **Location**: Barathrum Forest, hunter's lodge
- **Direction**: Follow the forest path east until you find an abandoned wooden lodge. Book is on the second floor, near a broken window overlooking the forest.
- **Effect**: Arrow penetrates armor and multiple enemies

### Rain of Arrows
- **Location**: Old Vahrin Walls, archer's tower
- **Direction**: Climb the tallest intact tower on the eastern wall. Book is at the top, next to a skeleton with a bow.
- **Effect**: AoE arrow volley

### Eagle Eye
- **Location**: Observatory, upper dome
- **Direction**: Reach the Observatory (mid-game area). Climb to the upper dome via the spiral staircase. Book is on the telescope platform.
- **Requirement**: Bow proficiency level 20
- **Effect**: Slows time while aiming

## Fire Magic Skill Books

### Flame Wave
- **Location**: Barathrum Forest, burning grove
- **Direction**: In the southeast corner of the forest, find the permanently burning tree grove. The book is on a stone altar in the center (immune to fire damage while near the altar).
- **Effect**: Cone AoE fire attack

### Fireball
- **Location**: Vahrin Catacombs, ritual chamber
- **Direction**: Deep in the Catacombs, find the circular ritual chamber with braziers. Light all four braziers (use Firebolt or Flame Imbued weapon). The central sarcophagus opens.
- **Effect**: Explosive AoE projectile

### Meteor
- **Location**: Quest reward — "Heart of the Forge"
- **How to start**: Speak to the blacksmith in Smoldering Quarter after completing the Captain Theron fight
- **Effect**: Massive AoE with long cast time

---

## Ice Magic Skill Books

### Frost Spear
- **Location**: Frozen District, ice cave
- **Direction**: Enter the ice cave in the northern part of Frozen District. Navigate through the frozen tunnels (watch for falling icicles). Book is trapped in ice — melt with Firebolt.
- **Effect**: Piercing projectile with strong slow

### Blizzard
- **Location**: Temple Ruins, frozen chapel
- **Direction**: In the northwest corner of Temple Ruins, find the frozen chapel. Defeat the Ice Wraith inside. Book is on the altar.
- **Effect**: Persistent AoE slow zone

---

## Earth Magic Skill Books

### Stone Spikes
- **Location**: Quarry Depths, deepest shaft
- **Direction**: Take the mining elevator to the deepest level. Follow the tunnel to a dead end with glowing earth veins. Use a Hammer charged attack on the cracked wall.
- **Effect**: Ground AoE that entangles enemies

### Earth Shield
- **Location**: Old Vahrin Walls, fortification room
- **Direction**: Enter the wall fortifications from the main gate. The room with maps and tactical displays has the book on a desk.
- **Effect**: Absorbs 30% incoming damage for 20 seconds

### Petrify
- **Location**: Quest reward — "Stone and Bone"
- **How to start**: Find the petrified soldier statue in the Quarry Depths (use Detect Magic to reveal the quest item)
- **Effect**: Single-target stun (slows bosses)

---

## Alteration Magic Skill Books

### Elemental Weapon
- **Location**: Mage Guild Ruins, enchanting chamber
- **Direction**: The ruins are in the northern part of the city. Find the room with enchanting tables. Book is in a locked chest (key dropped by a mage enemy in the same ruins).
- **Effect**: Adds chosen element to weapon for 90 seconds

### Arcane Ward
- **Location**: Grand Library, restricted section
- **Direction**: In the Grand Library, find the locked gate to the restricted section. The key is held by a librarian enemy on the upper floor. Book is on a lectern in the restricted section.
- **Effect**: Magic damage absorption shield

### Haste
- **Location**: Quest reward — "Race Against Time"
- **How to start**: Find the dying messenger on the road between Vahrin Garrison and Merchant Quarter
- **Effect**: +30% movement and attack speed for 15 seconds

### Telekinesis
- **Location**: Observatory, planetarium
- **Direction**: In the Observatory's planetarium room, rotate the orrery to align all planets (solution: Sun → Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars). A hidden compartment opens.
- **Effect**: Pull distant items, trigger switches
