# Skill Book Locations — What's Confirmed

**Correction first**: earlier versions of this guide listed 25 specific Skill Books with exact room-level directions (e.g., "Vahrin Garrison, armory, break the weakened wall"). **No official Skill Book list or locations exist.** Those entries were invented and have been removed. What we know for certain about Skill Books is actually simple and important.

## Confirmed: Skill Books Exist and Matter

Titan Roc's official updates and the game's Steam materials confirm:

- **Skill Books are scattered throughout the world.**
- They are **needed for full upgrades** of your abilities — using a skill alone is not enough to max it out.
- This applies across the classless system: weapon abilities and magic alike.

In other words: **exploration is a progression system**. The player who finds books gets access to upgrade paths the leveler never sees.

## How to Find Books Without an Official Map

Since no locations are published, book hunting comes down to confirmed game behavior:

1. **Explore everything.** The world is hand-crafted and "genuinely rewarding" to explore per launch reviews — books hide in the kinds of places treasure hides: basements, ruins, hidden rooms, behind puzzles.
2. **Solve environmental puzzles.** Puzzles are a confirmed part of the world's structure; they typically guard the best finds.
3. **Do side quests.** The "whole huge area" hidden behind a side quest is exactly the kind of place books live.
4. **Clear hidden areas.** Dungeon-style areas and secret corners are where "scattered" collectibles concentrate.
5. **Revisit with new abilities.** The non-linear map rewards backtracking — a ledge or door you couldn't reach earlier may hide a book.
6. **Prioritise books for your primary systems.** Since skills improve through use, pair each book with the skill you're already practicing.

## Community Claims — Marked Unverified

Some launch discussions report:

Treat these as rumors until confirmed.

- A "detect magic"-style reveal ability (any such spell name is invented)
