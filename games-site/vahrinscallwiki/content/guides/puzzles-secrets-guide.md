# Puzzles & Secrets Guide — Colosseum Tomb Solution and Hidden Rewards

Environmental puzzles are a confirmed pillar of Vahrin's Call's world: the Steam description promises hidden secrets and puzzles across the Vale of Vahrin, and launch reviews consistently describe puzzles guarding the game's best finds. Since launch, players have documented the game's puzzle structures — and verified one full solution, the **Colosseum Tomb**, with exact rewards. This guide collects what is verified, what is structurally known but unsolved, and how to spot secrets the game hides in plain sight.

## The Colosseum Tomb Puzzle (Verified Solution)

The Colosseum Tomb is the first major puzzle the community fully solved, and it is worth solving: it is the earliest reliably documented source of a free unique weapon and shield pair.

### Where It Is

The puzzle sits inside the **Colosseum**, reached right after the Market. It is on the far end of the first floor, roughly three-quarters of the way through the Colosseum — expect to cross multiple floors, fight through enemies, and navigate rubble via alternate routes to reach the room.

### How It Works

Six sarcophagi line the room, each with an inscription carved on top and a **dial with six symbols** below it. Read the inscription, identify the creature it references, and set the dial to the matching symbol. The dial symbols correspond to creatures in the inscriptions, so the room is solvable with careful reading — no order matters, only correct matches.

### The Full Solution

| Tomb Inscription (who the tomb belongs to) | Set dial to |
|---|---|
| Jarson faced the relentless **Mundagar** | Mundagar |
| Alysia dueled the legendary gladiator **Orkwood** | Gladiator |
| Wylde confronted the formidable **greti** | Greti |
| Chrysha stood against the relentless **krat** mischief | Krat |
| Harlik was torn asunder by a ravenous **wolfhound** | Wolfhound |
| Dantis met his fate in a duel with a **molten wyrm** | Molten Wyrm |

### The Reward

Set all six correctly and the back wall opens, revealing two pieces of equipment:

- **Colosseum Victor Sword** — Level 1, **55.8 Slash damage**, sells for 56 Coins. Dismantles into Iron, Alder Wood, and Aurochs Hide.
- **Colosseum Victor Shield** — Level 1, **16.2 Block Rating**, sells for 56 Coins. Dismantles into the same materials.

Both are Level 1 equipment — strong for when you reach the Colosseum, and useful as an early source of Iron and Aurochs Hide for upgrading your preferred gear. The shield pairs naturally with a Sword & Shield or defensive playstyle; the sword is a solid Slash-damage option with no skill prerequisite.

## Other Confirmed Puzzle Structures

The community has mapped several more puzzle-bearing locations. The structures are verified; the solutions are **not yet published** — treat anything you see online with the exact answers as unverified until video or screenshot evidence exists.

### The Vault of Vahrin (Market)

In the Market district, a locked **Vault of Vahrin entrance** (plus a side entrance reached by a ladder) is guarded by **four button/lever mechanisms** (mapped as Buttons #1–4). The room is clearly a code- or order-based puzzle — the exact sequence is unverified. If you reach it, experiment with the four buttons and note any inscriptions or journal pages in the room: the Market also holds clue items such as **Lady Devellian's Journal Page** and the **Taxman's Journal**.

### Snatcher's Vault (Password Notes)

A vault tied to a character called "the Snatcher" requires **password notes collected across multiple zones**. Community maps document the notes' existence:

- **Snatcher's Vault Password** — found in **Forest Edge**, near a **Cave to Snatcher Cellar** and a locked door.
- Additional password notes (**#2, #3, #4**) are documented in **South Vale** and the **Market**.

The passwords themselves are not published anywhere we can verify. The structure is clear though: this is a multi-zone scavenger hunt — check every readable note in Forest Edge, South Vale, and the Market, and keep the collection together.

### Sewer Key Gates (Lower City)

The Lower City map documents **Sewer Key** items and locked gates that require them, with paths leading into the sewers and a route to the "Northern High Ground." No key location list is confirmed, but the pattern is the same as the Snatcher's Vault: locked content gated by an item you find elsewhere.

## Reading the World for Secrets

Beyond set-piece puzzles, the game hides rewards in the environment. Community-documented storytelling clues that signal hidden content:

- **Bloody traces** — often lead to bodies, ambushes, or hidden caches.
- **Destroyed structures** — rubble frequently conceals passages or treasure.
- **Weird signs and markings** — can indicate puzzle-relevant objects or concealed paths.
- **Crumbled statues** — a recurring marker for interactable secrets.
- **Mysterious lighting** — unusual light sources mark points of interest.
- **Bizarre placements of objects** — a lone crate, a single torch, an out-of-place barrel.

Similarly, the community's rule of thumb for map geography: **mysterious cliffs, single buildings, dead end roads, and secret valleys** are all worth investigating. Off-path rewards consistently include Skill Books, spells, better gear, talismans, hidden bosses, and unique equipment found nowhere else.

## The Genomy Side Quest

One of the game's most persistent secret-hunting loops is the **Genomy** — small, gnome-like creatures tied to a side quest that asks you to destroy dozens of them. Verified facts:

- **Genomy appear throughout the zones** — the Forest Edge alone is mapped with **9 Genomy locations**.
- Your **map tracks destroyed Genomy** in its discovery tracker, so progress is visible.
- The quest's reward and total count are unconfirmed; the map tracking suggests completionists are expected to clear them zone by zone.

Since Genomy are tied to a side quest and the maps track them, treat every zone's Genomy as part of one long-term objective rather than a distraction.

## The Hidden Area Behind the Side Quest

Launch reviews confirmed the existence of a **"whole huge area" hidden behind a side quest** — one of the game's best zones, entirely missable on a main-story run. The community's documented puzzle structures (Snatcher's Vault, the Market's vaults, the Sewer Key chain) are the kind of content that leads toward it. If a side quest asks you to collect notes, kill creatures, or solve a vault, follow it to the end.

## Practical Puzzle-Hunting Checklist

1. **Read everything.** Inscriptions, journal pages, and notes are the game's puzzle language — the Colosseum Tomb is solvable purely from text.
2. **Interact with statues and dials.** Crumbled statues and dial mechanics are confirmed interactable puzzle elements.
3. **Collect password notes across zones.** Snatcher's Vault spans at least three zones — don't assume a note belongs to the area you found it in.
4. **Use the map's POI markers.** Community maps and the in-game discovery tracker flag chests, skill books, locked doors, and destroyed Genomy.
5. **Return with keys and new abilities.** Sewer Keys and vault entrances are documented in areas you may pass early — note the gate, come back later.
6. **Solve the Colosseum Tomb when you reach it.** The Victor Sword and Shield are the first fully documented unique-gear reward in the game.
7. **Check dead ends and single buildings.** Community exploration reports consistently find the best loot off the main path.
