# Vahrin's Call Bounty Hunts Guide: Bounty Board, Jimet Korb & Charagoth

Bounty hunting is the most under-documented profitable activity in **Forsaken Realms: Vahrin's Call**. The official store page confirms that the Vale is full of mercenaries, bandits and monstrous beasts you can hunt for bounties, and launch reviews call bounty hunts and monster contracts the best part of the side content. What those sources do not tell you is where the board is, who runs the hunt chain, or why the final hunt seems to disappear.

This guide answers those questions using the official store page, a Steam Community thread documenting the Charagoth chain, and a launch review describing what the hunts pay. Where the game does not document a number — payouts, enemy levels, exact chapter gates — we mark it **[Unconfirmed]** instead of guessing.

**Spoiler note:** this page names the final hunt and its reward. The mechanics are more useful than the surprise, but stop here if you want to find Charagoth blind.

## What Bounty Hunts Are

Vahrin's Call is a classless action-RPG about a mercenary reclaiming the war-torn city of Vahrin. The economy runs on contracts: you take work, kill the target, and get paid in gold and gear. Bounty hunts are the clearest expression of that fantasy.

Confirmed: the Vale contains rival mercenaries, bandits and monstrous beasts you can hunt for bounties (official store page); bounty hunts and monster contracts are real side activities alongside optional bosses and hidden areas (GamesCreed); and they reward better equipment, gold, spells and crafting materials (GamesCreed).

Not confirmed: a complete bounty list, exact gold payouts, target levels, or whether boards restock. Any guide with a payout table is inventing numbers. [Unconfirmed]

## Where the Bounty Board Is

The bounty board is in **South Vale Hamlet**. That location is community-documented, not named on the official store page, so treat the exact building position as a landmark hunt rather than a map marker.

How to use it:

1. **Reach South Vale Hamlet.** If you have not unlocked the region's fast-travel signpost yet, follow the main road out of the starting area and look for the hamlet's cluster of buildings.
2. **Find the bounty board.** It is the contract hub for the region's hunt quests.
3. **Accept every hunt you can.** The board is how the hunt chain begins; skipping hunts delays the final contract.
4. **Read the quest description before leaving.** Vahrin's Call uses minimal quest tracking by default. Quest markers can be enabled in the options if you prefer them, but the descriptions tell you which region the target is in.

If the board appears empty, progress the main story or return after other side content. The review notes that returning to hubs can reveal newly unlocked quests, so an empty board early is not necessarily a bug. [Unconfirmed for restock rules]

## Jimet Korb and the Hunt Chain

**Jimet Korb** is the hunt quest-giver. He is located in his **cabin in Forest Edge**, according to the Steam Community thread that documents the chain. Forest Edge is a community-mapped region connected to South Vale Hamlet.

The chain works like this:

- Hunts are obtained from the **Bounty Board in South Vale Hamlet**.
- Jimet Korb oversees the hunts from his Forest Edge cabin.
- You must **complete all of Jimet Korb's hunt quests** before the final contract appears.
- After those hunts are done, **talk to Jimet Korb again**. He gives you **Hunt: Charagoth** and the **Old City Key**.

The important detail is order: finishing the hunts is not enough. You must return to Jimet Korb and speak to him again to trigger the final quest and the key. Players who clear the board and walk away are the ones who later report that Charagoth "doesn't exist."

## How to Unlock Hunt: Charagoth

Charagoth is the capstone hunt, and it is gated twice: once by the hunt chain and once by story progress.

### Step 1: Complete Every Hunt

Clear all of Jimet Korb's hunt quests. The hunts span multiple regions, and some targets sit in areas **past the Old City**, so you may need to advance the main story before the last hunts become reachable.

### Step 2: Return to Jimet Korb

Talk to him again in his Forest Edge cabin. He gives you **Hunt: Charagoth** and the **Old City Key**. If he has nothing new to say, you are missing a hunt somewhere.

### Step 3: Reach the Old City

The Old City unlocks after clearing **Chapter 5**, according to the Steam thread; one commenter reports Chapter 8 instead. Treat the exact chapter as **[Unconfirmed]** and use the quest's own description as the authority. If the gate is closed, keep progressing the main story.

### Step 4: Find the Locked Gate

From the **Old City North fast-travel point**, head **northwest**. Charagoth is behind a **locked gate near the beginning of the Old City area**. The Old City Key from Jimet Korb opens it.

### Step 5: Prepare for a Real Fight

Charagoth is a named hunt, not a normal elite. Treat it like a boss: bring healing, traps, thrown potions if you have the alchemy perk, and a build you have actually tested. The combat guide's core rule applies — every action has commitment, so do not over-swing.

## What Hunts Pay

Confirmed reward categories are **gold, better equipment, spells and crafting materials** (GamesCreed). That makes bounty hunting a legitimate alternative to dungeon clearing for funding upgrades.

- **Hunts fund gear enhancement.** Gold and materials feed the blacksmith.
- **Hunts can drop spells.** Worth doing even when the target is out of your way.
- **Hunts stack with exploration.** Targets live near chests, Skill Books and optional bosses, so make each bounty trip a full exploration trip.
- **Do not expect a fixed payout.** Exact amounts are not published. [Unconfirmed]

The farming guide's advice holds: clear new areas once, loot thoroughly, and stack bounty targets onto routes you were already travelling.

## Hunt Tactics That Actually Apply

Hunts reuse the game's enemy families — bandits, rival mercenaries and beasts — with tougher stats and a named target. The rules are the same, with less room for error.

- **Fight the target, not the camp.** Clear adds first; a hunt that becomes a 1v5 is how under-geared players lose the reward.
- **Set traps before the pull.** Trap deployment works while moving, so you can retreat into a fallback line.
- **Backstab human targets if you can.** Sneak attacks require a manually equipped melee weapon and have no prompt.
- **Throw potions once alchemy is levelled.** The perk gives any build a ranged answer.
- **Upgrade armour before the named fight**, not after.

If a hunt feels impossible, the problem is usually progression, not tactics. Some targets sit past the Old City for a reason.

## A Clean Bounty Route

1. **Check the South Vale Hamlet board** each session.
2. **Pick up every hunt**, including ones in regions you are not visiting yet.
3. **Group hunts by region** so one trip covers multiple contracts and exploration.
4. **Clear nearby side content** while you are there.
5. **Return to Jimet Korb** after each batch.
6. **Save before Charagoth** and treat it as a boss attempt.

This route is our recommendation, not an official quest order. The confirmed structure is board → hunts → Jimet Korb → Charagoth.

In **South Vale Hamlet**, per community documentation.
