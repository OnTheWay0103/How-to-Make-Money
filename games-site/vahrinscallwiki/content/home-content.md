## What is Vahrin's Call?

Vahrin's Call is a narrative-driven, classless action-RPG developed by Titan Roc, a self-funded 7-person indie studio from England. After 12 years of development, it launched on Steam on July 27, 2026, with over 200,000 wishlists.

You play as a mercenary arriving in the war-torn city of Vahrin. The city has been overrun by insect-like creatures called the **khraiax**, and a greater threat stirs beneath. With no preset classes, your character grows through use — swing greatswords to master them, cast fire magic to become a pyromancer, or blend disciplines into a unique hybrid build.

---

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Classless Progression** — No classes. Use any weapon or magic, improve through practice
- **5 Attributes** — Strength, Agility, Intelligence, Constitution, Focus — free respecs at Mentor NPCs
- **6 Weapon Types** — Greatsword, Sword & Shield, Dual Wield, Hammer, Bow, and 4 Magic schools
- **4 Magic Schools** — Fire (damage), Ice (crowd control), Earth (defense), Alteration (buffs/utility)
- **Alchemy & Traps** — Craft potions, bombs, and traps. Alchemy is a combat system, not a side activity
- **25 Skill Books** — Hidden throughout the world, unlock abilities not available through leveling
- **8+ Bosses** — Story bosses and optional hidden bosses with unique mechanics
- **Handcrafted World** — No procedural generation, no AI content. Every area designed by humans
