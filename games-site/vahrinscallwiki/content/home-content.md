## What is Vahrin's Call?

Vahrin's Call is a narrative-driven, classless action-RPG developed by Titan Roc, a self-funded seven-person indie studio from South-West England. Conceived in 2014 and built in Unreal Engine 4 with no generative AI, it launched on Steam on July 27, 2026 at $34.99 / £27.99 / €29.99, passing 100,000 wishlists before release.

You play as a mercenary arriving in the war-torn city of Vahrin, which was overrun by insect-like creatures called the **khraiax**. The king was killed, thousands of soldiers from the Free Cities (banner of the Combined Order) died defending it, and — while the khraiax were being defeated — a far greater danger rose from beneath the city's streets.

With no preset classes, your character grows through use: swing a sword to master it, cast fire magic to grow it, brew potions to unlock potion-throwing. You can adapt, alter, or completely switch playstyles at any point.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Classless Progression** — No classes. Skills improve through use, Elder Scrolls-style
- **Skill Books** — Scattered through the world; needed for full upgrades
- **Spell Mastery** — Spells upgrade through use; customisable damage, range, and casting speed
- **Stamina System** — Limits prolonged magic use; paces combat
- **Alchemy** — Confirmed combat system: throw potions at enemies or yourself
- **Traps** — Deploy while moving; some spells can trigger them
- **Magic Limits** — No environmental effects, no fire/ice combinations (confirmed by devs)
- **Co-op** — Cut during development; single-player only
- **Handcrafted World** — Semi-open zones and dungeons in the Vale of Vahrin; 15–30 hours
