## What is Doloc Town?

Doloc Town is a cozy, side-scrolling post-apocalyptic farming simulator developed by **RedSaw Games** and published by **Logoi Games**. You play a young scavenger who builds a vertical farm in the settlement of Doloc Town — a sanctuary built from the remnants of trains and collapsed bridges.

The game blends *Stardew Valley*-style farming with *Fallout*-inspired survival: acid rain erodes crops, heat bakes the earth, and thunderstorms roll through. You can turn the weather to your advantage — harnessing lightning to power the farm and using rain for irrigation — while restoring the environment and uncovering the town's mystery of a vanished leader and "Eden" technology.
