# Combat & Exploration Guide — Surviving the Wasteland

Doloc Town is primarily a farming game, but the wasteland beyond the farm is **full of hostile creatures** — exploring the Rivervalley, caves, and Old City Ruins means fighting (per [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/doloc-town-launches-august-6) and the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)). This guide covers what's verified about the combat layer.

## How Combat Works

The official materials describe **fighting hostile creatures** during exploration, and your **Health** bar is the combat resource — at zero you **die and lose money** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).

What's verified:

- **Hostile creatures** exist in exploration areas (per [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/doloc-town-launches-august-6))
- **Death costs money** — like passing out from zero Energy, dying at zero Health loses Goldcoin (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks))
- **Health restores** via food, sleep, and farm equipment (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks))

## Combat Drones (1.0)

The 1.0 patch introduced **combat drones and accessories** (per the [1.0 patch notes via VGSpoilers](https://www.vgspoilers.com/game/doloc-town/patches/1.0)). These are separate from the farming automation drones — combat drones help you fight. Details:

- How to obtain combat drones: [Unconfirmed]
- Whether they fight alongside you or as equipment: [Unconfirmed]
- Community guides discuss **drone combat** as part of the late-game loop (per [Mejoress](https://www.mejoress.com/how-to-master-doloc-town-early-upgrades-resource-loops-drone-combat/))

## Exploration Readiness Checklist

Before you head into hostile areas:

1. **Bring stat food** — Deli Omurice (+300 SP/+75 HP) and Hot Cocoa (+320 SP/+110 HP) keep you alive on long trips (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
2. **Budget Energy** — every cast of a tool costs Energy, and you need it for the trip back
3. **Wear the welder's mask** in acid rain — corrosion damage stacks with creature damage (per [Huijiwiki](https://doloctown.huijiwiki.com/wiki/%E7%8A%B6%E6%80%81))
4. **Keep a health buffer** — don't explore at half Health; death costs money

## Tools vs. Weapons

The three core tools (Scythe, Pickaxe, Axe) are for gathering (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)). Whether they double as combat weapons is [Unconfirmed — not documented]. Dedicated weapons have not been documented in the English sources we verified; the 1.0 combat drones are the confirmed combat addition.
