# Doloc Town FAQ — Quick Answers to Common Questions

Short, verified answers to the questions players ask most. Every answer cites its source; anything unconfirmed is marked [待确认].

## How long is Doloc Town?

**30+ hours of main story, 100+ hours with additional content** (per the official [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)). The game has **80 achievements** for completionists.

## How does saving work?

**Saving is automatic.** Doloc Town saves as you play — you can close the game and continue where you left off, including farm layout, inventory, and story progress. (Based on the game's single-player structure; there is no manual save-slot menu documented [待确认].)

## How much does Doloc Town cost?

Check the **Steam store page** for the current price. Early Access purchasers received the full 1.0 release free, and the game launched at a 20% discount until **August 20, 2026** (per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)). The price has been adjusted since — see the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/).

## Is there multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** Doloc Town is a single-player game (per the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)).

## What platforms is it on?

**PC (Steam)** with a **"Playable"** Steam Deck rating (per the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)). No console versions announced.

## What are the system requirements?

Minimum: **Windows 10, Intel Core i3 6100, 8 GB RAM, GTX 460, 2 GB storage** (per the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)). See the [Steam Deck & System Requirements Guide](/guides/steam-deck-guide).

## How do I get the Golden Fish?

The **Golden Fish** is found only in the **Deep/Forest Pond** and sells for **1,000 Goldcoin** (with a guild quest paying **2,000** for one) (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/)). See the [Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide).

## Is there romance in Doloc Town?

The official materials describe **befriending townsfolk with personal storylines**, but full romance mechanics are **[待确认]** (per [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/doloc-town-launches-august-6)). See the [Gifts & Friendship Guide](/guides/gifts-romance-guide).

## What did the 1.0 update add?

**Old City Ruins** area, **main story conclusion**, **farming automation (drones)**, **new tools**, and languages: **Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese** (per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)).

## Is Doloc Town any good?

At the time of writing, Doloc Town holds a **95% "Overwhelmingly Positive"** rating on Steam (per the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)), and press coverage reported a **critic average of 80/100** with 100% critic recommendation (per [one press summary](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)). We don't publish our own score — read the player reviews for the full picture.

## Does Doloc Town have mods?

**Yes** — Steam Workshop integration was added during Early Access (per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)). See the [Mods & Steam Workshop Guide](/guides/mods-guide).

## What languages are supported?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (since Early Access), plus **Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, German, and Brazilian Portuguese** added at 1.0 (per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)).

## How do I stop passing out from energy loss?

Nap, sleep, or eat cooked food before your Energy hits zero — passing out costs Goldcoin (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)). See the [Energy Guide](/guides/energy-guide).
