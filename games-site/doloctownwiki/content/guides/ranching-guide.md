# Ranching Guide — Animals & Animal Feed in Doloc Town

Ranching and aquaculture (fish farming) were added to Doloc Town during Early Access in the **"Pastoral Waves"** update (per the official [Pastoral Waves announcement](https://www.gamespress.com/nl/Community-Acclaimed-Doloc-Town-Expands-with-First-Major-Update-Pastora)). This guide covers what's verified about raising animals and fish in the current 1.0 build.

## What the Pastoral Waves Update Added

The first major Early Access update added (per the official [Pastoral Waves press release](https://www.gamespress.com/nl/Community-Acclaimed-Doloc-Town-Expands-with-First-Major-Update-Pastora)):

- **Ranching** — raising land animals on your farm
- **Aquaculture** — fish farming (raising fish in farm ponds)
- New animal products used in cooking

## Why Bother with Ranching

- **Animal products feed the cooking profit engine** — milk enables cheese (see the [Cooking & Recipes Guide](/guides/cooking-recipes-guide)), and meat is a processed-goods source
- **Kasia sells meat** — players buy meat from her and turn it into jerky for ~50 net profit per piece (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
- **1.0 added animal production improvements** alongside balance changes (per the [1.0 patch notes via VGSpoilers](https://www.vgspoilers.com/game/doloc-town/patches/1.0))

## Drones Automate Livestock in 1.0

The 1.0 Farming Automation system extends to ranching: at the advanced **Large-Scale Drone Hub** stage, drones manage **livestock rearing** on ranches and in fish farms, freeing you to explore (per the official [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews)). See the [Automation & Drones Guide](/guides/automation-drone-guide) for the full unlock path.
