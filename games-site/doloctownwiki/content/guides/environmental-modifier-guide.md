# How to Restore the Environment in Doloc Town — Environmental Modifier Walkthrough

Doloc Town's store page makes a big promise: **restore the environment piece by piece, drive back the extreme weather, and watch the wasteland bloom once more**. That promise is not background flavour. It is a late-game quest chain built around a terraforming machine called the **Environmental Modifier**, and it gates some of the game's later regions.

This guide walks through the repair, the materials, the wiring puzzle and Phase 1. It is a progression guide, not a story spoiler dump. For the weather hazards themselves, see the [Weather Survival Guide](/guides/weather-guide).

## What the Environmental Modifier is

The Environmental Modifier is a large machine in a hidden cave, encircled by four pillars. Its front terminal is dead when you find it; the rear panel is the part you can actually repair. Restoring it starts a chain that includes river dredging, phased terraforming, and eventually the wetlands and Old City content.

It is **not** the same thing as the "Weather Regulator" mentioned in some weather guides. The Environmental Modifier is the terraforming machine tied to the Eden investigation; the Weather Regulator's exact identity and unlock path remain **[Unconfirmed]** in the English sources we checked. Do not go looking for one device using the other's name.

## Prerequisites: what you need before the cave

The quest does not start from the machine. It starts from the Eden investigation and a short chain of town steps:

- Complete **Night Assembly** and attend the treaty port between **18:00 and 22:00**.
- Break the **River Valley rockfall** with a **copper pickaxe**. Merlin sells it for roughly **1,500 G**, plus **five copper ingots** and **three old pickaxes**.
- Deliver the **tattered layout blueprint** to **Mayor Claud**.
- Show the blueprint to **Mira** at the Doloc Valley outpost.
- Investigate the **Core Extractor** in the mining area on the valley's left side.
- Have a **jetpack with sprint and air dash**. Rubber for the dash must be researched and crafted.
- Report to **Alchemy** at the botanical lab, then ask **Mody** about the power repair. The wiring hint is under **Archives → terraforming circuit guide**.

If the cave entrance is still solid rock, you are not far enough along. Story progress clears it; there is no alternate entrance to brute-force.

## Step 1: Take the Core Sample

At the Core Extractor, take the sample from its storage compartment. This gives you the **Core Sample** and reveals the coordinates of the next location. Keep the sample. You will need it again after the electrical repair, and the quest does not give you a second one.

## Step 2: Find the machine in the secret cave

The community directions are: from the farm, head toward Ridge Valley — **down, left, up**. The entrance is associated with a former landslide, with a **small pond and a trampoline** in front of it. Inside, look for the large machine ringed by four pillars.

Walk around to the **rear panel**. The front terminal does not respond, and interacting with it repeatedly will not advance the quest. The rear panel reveals the damaged wiring that is the actual blocker.

## Step 3: Get Mody's repair plan

Report the damaged circuit and speak with **Mody** (spelled "Modi" in some guides). Mody provides a written repair plan and identifies the energy components you need. The relevant archive entry is under **Archives → terraforming circuit guide** if you want to read the original diagram.

## Step 4: Build the repair kit

The confirmed kit is:

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Small Battery** | 2 | Must be the **placeable** Small Battery equipment, not an ordinary inventory Battery |
| **Electric Wire** | 5 | Crafted separately |
| **Core Sample** | 1 | From the Core Extractor; do not consume or sell it |

The battery trap is the most common failure point. Ordinary Batteries are not a substitute for the placeable **Small Battery** equipment. If the recipe is missing, unlock the **Industrial → Basic Power Storage** technology node.

The exact Small Battery recipe is disputed between community sources. One guide lists **2 Copper Ingots + 2 Rubber + 1 Battery** per unit; another lists **2 Copper Ingots + 5 Rubber + 1 Charger**. Because those cannot both be right, treat the recipe as **[Unconfirmed]** and read your workbench tooltip before farming materials. For two batteries, the safer shopping list is **4 Copper Ingots, 4 Rubber, 2 Batteries**, plus whatever rubber or charger your build actually asks for.

## Step 5: Solve the wiring puzzle

The archive gives a pairing map, but the cave terminals reportedly do not match the archive image one-to-one. The community-recorded starting map is:

1. Left battery **blue** → right battery **red**
2. Left battery **red** → machine **green**
3. Right battery **blue** → damaged **yellow**
4. Machine **black** → **white**
5. Join the two opposite wires at the bottom of the machine
6. Power the panel

This is a starting point, not a guaranteed solution. The source that documented it explicitly warns that the cave has "a twist" and that the terminals do not match the archive exactly. Wrong choices fail the minigame but **return the materials**, so experiment rather than reloading a save after every attempt.

## Step 6: Insert the Core Sample and start Phase 1

With the circuit repaired:

1. Insert the **Core Sample** into the Environmental Modifier.
2. Let the machine accumulate **30 Energy**.
3. Activate **Phase 1**.

Phase 1 is a start button, not an instant climate fix. It clears the blocked riverway and shifts progression back toward the dock and Treaty Port. A **5,000 G port bill** follows the dredging, so do not spend your last coin on wiring materials.

Alchemy also grants the **Eden Fruit**, a permanent stat item. Community guides are unanimous on one point: **do not cook it**. It is a one-time upgrade, not an ingredient.

## What comes after Phase 1

The later restoration stages are community-mapped rather than fully documented in patch notes. They involve four energy tracks:

- **Plant energy** — seed research and the crop database, with Alchemy and Sakko.
- **Animal energy** — fish and livestock records; described as the slowest track.
- **Machine energy** — the monster catalog from combat.
- **Environment energy** — outdoor resource collection across the regions.

Later progress includes **wetlands restoration** with an **Axolotus / lotus prototype** and **four pipe kits** from Industry assembly tech, then the **Old City power districts**. Community guides state that this chain is required for the 1.0 ending and that the Old City Ruins and finale are gated behind terraforming progress. Treat that as community-reported endgame structure.

## Patch changes that affect the grind

- **1.00.04** increased Environmental Modifier point gains, making full restoration easier than in 1.00.03.
- **1.00.07** fixed several Old City Ruins progression problems, including a power-station quest that could get stuck if repaired in an unconventional order.
- **1.00.08** fixed severe weather not refreshing immediately after environmental modifying — if the weather looks stuck after a phase change, update first.
- **1.00.09** made the **Blank Node Carrier** sellable and fixed several interface issues, though none are specific to the modifier.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Cave entrance is solid rock | Story not far enough | Finish Night Assembly, the rockfall and the blueprint handoff |
| Front terminal does nothing | Wrong panel | Inspect the rear panel |
| Battery recipe missing | Tech not unlocked | Research Industrial → Basic Power Storage |
| Batteries will not insert | Ordinary Battery instead of placeable Small Battery | Craft the placeable equipment version |
| Wiring keeps failing | Archive map does not match the cave | Use the map as a starting point; materials are refunded on failure |
| Phase 1 will not start | Core Sample missing or under 30 Energy | Recover the sample; wait for the energy bar |
| Weather does not change after modifying | Pre-1.00.08 behaviour | Update the game |
| Guardian fight soft-lock after a loss | Community-reported issue | Save before the fight and reload if the battle does not resume |

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] Night Assembly complete and treaty port attended.
- [ ] Copper pickaxe bought and River Valley rockfall cleared.
- [ ] Blueprint delivered to Mayor Claud and shown to Mira.
- [ ] Core Sample taken from the Core Extractor.
- [ ] Jetpack with sprint and air dash unlocked.
- [ ] Industrial → Basic Power Storage researched.
- [ ] Two placeable Small Batteries and five Electric Wires crafted.
- [ ] Save made before entering the cave and before the Guardian fight.
- [ ] 5,000 G reserved for the port bill.

The Environmental Modifier is one of Doloc Town's longest chains, but it is also one of the few that changes the world rather than just your farm. Repair the rear panel, solve the wiring, and Phase 1 turns a blocked river into the route to the rest of the wasteland.

*Quest steps and wiring map are community-documented and cross-checked against two independent guides plus a Steam discussion. The Small Battery recipe is contradictory across sources and is marked [Unconfirmed]; exact costs and later-phase requirements may change with patches.*
