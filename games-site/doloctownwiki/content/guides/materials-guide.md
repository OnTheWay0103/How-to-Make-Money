# How to Get Ore & Crafting Materials in Doloc Town

Every device on your farm — from lightning rods to drone hubs — needs crafting materials, and most of them are found in specific parts of the map. This guide collects the verified material locations from the sources below; anything unconfirmed is marked [Unconfirmed].

## The Core Tools for Gathering

Per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks):

| Tool | Used for |
|------|----------|
| **Scythe** | Cutting grass (yields Fiber) |
| **Pickaxe** | Rocks, soil, sand, and demolition |
| **Axe** | Trees (yields Wood and Resin) |

Using tools grants **Field Study, Tech Research, or Tool Operation points**, which you spend in the **Tech Tree** (per [Neoseeker](https://www.neoseeker.com/doloc-town/walkthrough)).

## Material Locations (Verified)

Per [TechRaptor's Crafting Materials Guide](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-crafting-materials-guide-resource-locations):

| Material | Where to find it |
|----------|------------------|
| **Bamboo Tube** | Bamboo at the **Eastern Pond** |
| **Coal / Copper / Iron Ore** | The **Rivervalley**, or buy from the blacksmith |
| **Fiber** | Cutting grass with the Scythe |
| **Old Batteries / Plastic Bottles / Rubbish** | **Garbage piles** (also via Lank's Waste Exchange trades) |
| **Resin** | Trees, or tree taps |
| **Rockwood** | The **western map / Ridge Valley** — requires **double-jump or dash** upgrades to reach |

## Early Priorities

1. **Copper → Lightning Rods.** When the Rivervalley opens, gather copper for lightning rods before upgrading tools — storm protection pays for itself (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).
2. **Fiber is the sneaky bottleneck.** Everything from cloth to looms needs Fiber from grass — scythe every patch you pass.
3. **Recycle your junk.** Scrap and "trash" items (bottles, scrap metal) can be recycled at the **junk recycler** for resources (per [Cheatbook](https://www.cheatbook.de/files/doloctown.htm)).

## How to Get Sand

"How to get sand" is one of the most-searched player questions (per Google search suggestions). Sand comes from **digging soil/sand patches with the Pickaxe** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks), which lists "rocks, soil, sand" as Pickaxe targets). The specific sand-rich locations are [Unconfirmed — not yet documented in English guides].

## How to Get Coal Fast

Coal is primarily found in the **Rivervalley** or purchased from the **blacksmith** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-crafting-materials-guide-resource-locations)). It's the fuel for the **Smelter**, which you unlock through the Tech Tree (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).

## Ore & Smelting

- The **Smelter** (Tech Tree unlock) processes raw ore into usable metal (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks))
- Gold ore exists in the game (per player questions) — the exact gold ore locations are [Unconfirmed]
