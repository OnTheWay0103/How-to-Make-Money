# Fishing Guide — Rods, Fish & Locations in Doloc Town

Doloc Town has **40+ fish** (per the official [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)), and fishing is a strong early income source that needs **no bait** — just energy (10 per cast) and the right rod. The game's fishing is based on **ponds, rods, and weather** (per the official Steam description).

## All Fishing Rods (Verified Prices)

Per [Raiderking's 100% Fishing Guide](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/):

| Rod | Price | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| **Old Rod** | 100 G | Tutorial rod |
| **Basic Rod** | 350 G | Starter purchase |
| **Bamboo Rod** | **1,000 G** | **Completion-critical** — the first rod that catches the full range |
| **Fiberglass Rod** | 3,000 G | Mid-tier upgrade |
| **Carbon Fiber Rod** | 6,000 G | Top-tier rod |

**Community advice:** get the **Bamboo rod as soon as possible** — it's the minimum for serious fishing and unlocks the completion-critical catches (per [Raiderking](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/) and [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/)).

## Where to Fish

- **The pond by the elk/deer statue** has a **100% fish catch rate** — the safest early fishing spot (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/))
- **Golden Fish** are found only in the **Deep/Forest Pond** (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/)); exact unlock requirements [Unconfirmed]
- Different ponds hold different fish — the **Eastern Pond** and other water bodies are tied to specific catches (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items))

## How the Fishing Mini-Game Works

- Each cast costs **10 Energy** (per [Raiderking](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/))
- **No bait needed** — you can fish anytime with the right rod
- Some fish are **time-gated** (Catfish at night, Pike in the day) and others are **season/weather-gated** (Sturgeon in mild seasons)

## The Lucky Fishhook

The **Lucky Fishhook** is obtained from the **Kon-Tiki faction mission** and can **double your catches** (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/)). It's a priority unlock for anyone planning to fish for profit.

## Fishing for Profit: The Numbers

- A good day of fishing can earn ~**2,000 Goldcoin** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
- **Grass carp and perch** cook into Delicious Steamed Fish (210 G) — fishing feeds your cooking profit engine (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
