# Beginner Guide — Getting Started in Doloc Town

Welcome to **Doloc Town**, the cozy post-apocalyptic farming simulator from **RedSaw Games**, published by **Logoi Games**. The game entered Steam Early Access on **May 8, 2025** and launched its full **1.0 version on August 6, 2026**, with a **95% "Overwhelmingly Positive"** review rating on Steam. This guide covers the essentials: what the game is, how its stats work, and a plan for your first week.

## What Is Doloc Town?

Doloc Town is a **side-scrolling, pixel-art farming simulation** set in a post-apocalyptic world. You play a young scavenger who stumbles on the settlement of **Doloc Town** — a sanctuary built from the remnants of trains and collapsed bridges — and decides to build a farm from scratch. It has been described as a blend of *Stardew Valley*, *Fallout*, and *Terraria* (per [Neoseeker](https://www.neoseeker.com/doloc-town/walkthrough)).

The key facts:

- **Developer / publisher:** RedSaw Games / Logoi Games
- **Release:** Steam Early Access May 8, 2025; **1.0 on August 6, 2026** (per the [official press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-))
- **Rating:** 95% "Overwhelmingly Positive" on Steam (at time of writing)
- **Content:** 30+ crops, 80+ recipes, 40+ fish, 80 achievements, 30+ hours main story (100+ with side content) — per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)
- **Platform:** PC via Steam, single-player, rated "Playable" on Steam Deck

### The Core Gameplay Loop

Every day you wake up on your farm with a set amount of **Energy**. You farm crops, fish, gather materials, complete villager tasks, and explore the wasteland. When Energy runs out, you pass out and **lose Goldcoin** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)). The game uses real weather hazards — acid rain, heat, and thunderstorms — that affect your crops, which makes your farm layout and timing matter more than in a typical cozy farming game.

## The Three Stats You Must Manage

Per [Grindnstrat's beginner guide](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/) and [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks):

| Stat | What it does | What happens at zero |
|------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Energy** | Spent by almost every action (farming, fishing, exploring) | You **pass out** and lose Goldcoin |
| **Health** | Your life bar | You **die** and lose money |
| **Stamina** | Spent by tool use (chopping, mining, fishing) | You **can't perform actions** |

Restore them by **sleeping, napping, or eating cooked food** — cooked meals are far more efficient than raw ingredients.

## Your First Week — What to Do

### Day 1–3: Set Up Your Farm Properly

1. **Collect soil before wood.** Soil is a limited daily resource and is needed for upgraded planters. Community guides recommend gathering all available soil first, then using leftover energy on wood (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).
2. **Don't waste resources on the Plant Basin – Basic.** This first planter decays after a single use. Complete villager tasks to unlock the reusable **Plant Basin – Wood** recipe instead (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/) and [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).
3. **Plant Endyams.** They're the top early money crop: they grow in Light Rain and Heavy Rain months, take 2 days to first harvest, and sell for **40 Goldcoin** each (per the [crop database via Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)). They're also **not harmed by acid rain**, making them ideal for outdoor planting.
4. **Build vertically.** Farm space is limited — build platforms upward. One community guide suggests ~10 Plant Basin – Wood planters in a row, expanded upward with wooden platforms (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

### Day 4–8: Upgrade and Explore

- **Upgrade your backpack early.** Talk to **Hult** at the general store (east of your farm): **20 slots = 500 Goldcoin, 30 slots = 4,000, 40 slots = 10,000** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks) and [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).
- **Do villager tasks.** The Rivervalley isn't open for the first few days, so complete tasks for rewards, recipes, and progression (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).
- **Expect the Day 8 event.** On around day 8, a theft event occurs where **Lank reclaims his pickaxe** from you (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).
- **Unlock cooking early** in the Tech Tree so you can process whole harvests at once (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

### When the Rivervalley Opens

The **Rivervalley** unlocks after the first few days. Prioritize getting **copper** there for **Lightning Rods** to protect your crops from storms — before upgrading your tools (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

## 10 Tips for New Players

1. **Endyams are your early money-maker** — cook them before selling for +5 Goldcoin each (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks))
2. **Never plant without checking the weather** — acid rain erodes most crops
3. **Skip the single-use Plant Basin – Basic** — the Wood planter is the real investment
4. **Nap or sleep rather than pushing through at zero Energy** — passing out costs Goldcoin
5. **Fishing costs 10 Energy per cast** — don't empty your bar on the pond (per [Raiderking](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/))
6. **Check the pond by the elk/deer statue** — it has a 100% fish catch rate (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/))
7. **Complete villager tasks early** — they unlock recipes and reusable tools
8. **Get the Bamboo fishing rod (1,000 Goldcoin) before the fiberglass** — it's completion-critical (per [Raiderking](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/))
9. **Save copper for Lightning Rods** before tool upgrades (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
10. **Don't trust guides that overclaim** — verify specifics in-game or against official announcements (this guide does)
