# Weather Survival Guide — Acid Rain, Heat & Storms

Weather is not decoration in Doloc Town — it's the core challenge. The post-apocalyptic world features **acid rain that erodes crops, scorching heat that bakes the earth, and thunderstorms that roll through** (per the official [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)). You can turn the weather to your advantage, though: harness lightning to power the farm and use rain for irrigation.

## The Three Weather Hazards

### 1. Acid Rain

- **Erodes crops** planted outdoors (per the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/))
- **Corrodes your character**: exposure builds up a corrosion status that damages you over time
- **Protection** (per the [Huijiwiki status page](https://doloctown.huijiwiki.com/wiki/%E7%8A%B6%E6%80%81)): stay indoors, use a **shower**, or wear the **welder's mask**
- **Endyams are not harmed by acid rain** — the only verified acid-rain-resistant crop (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- **Chinese cabbage fears acid rain** — plant it in tents or protect it (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

### 2. Heat Waves

- **Scorching heat bakes the earth**, reducing soil quality and stressing crops (per the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/))
- The **dry season** (e.g., April) is when crops are hardest to grow — which is why **item prices spike then** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

### 3. Thunderstorms

- Lightning can **destroy unprotected crops**
- **Lightning Rods** (crafted from **copper**) protect your plants (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- You can also **harness lightning to power the farm** (per the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)) — the exact lightning-harvesting devices are [待确认]

## Using Weather to Your Advantage

- **Rain for irrigation** — heavy rain months reduce how much you need to water manually
- **Endyams in the rainy months** — the crop database lists them as growing in Light Rain and Heavy Rain months (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- **Mild seasons for Sturgeon** — fishing is weather-gated: Sturgeon only appears in mild seasons (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/))

## The Weather Regulator

"How to get the weather regulator" is a commonly searched question (per Google search suggestions). The **Weather Regulator** appears to be a device that controls or mitigates weather effects — its exact unlock path is [待确认 — not yet documented in English sources we could verify].

## Weather Safety Checklist

- [ ] Lightning Rods on all outdoor crop rows (copper from Rivervalley)
- [ ] Welder's mask equipped for outdoor work during acid rain
- [ ] Shower/indoor space to clear corrosion status
- [ ] Tents or covered planting for rain-fearful crops
- [ ] Hoard goods for the dry-season price spike
