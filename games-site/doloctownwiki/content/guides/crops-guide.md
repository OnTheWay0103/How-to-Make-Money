# All Crops & Seeds Guide — What to Plant in Doloc Town

Doloc Town ships with **30+ crops** (per the official [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)), each with its own seasons, growth times, yields, and **gene traits** that you can modify through the gene system. This guide covers the verified facts and the community's best crops per phase.

## How Crop Seasons Work

Doloc Town's year is divided into **weather months** — Light Rain, Heavy Rain, and Dry months — and different crops grow in different months (per the crop database reported by [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)). The weather also damages crops: acid rain erodes most of them, so check the forecast before planting outdoors.

## The Verified Early Crops

### Endyams — the starter money crop

| Property | Value (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)) |
|----------|------|
| Grows in | Light Rain and Heavy Rain months |
| Days to first harvest | 2 |
| Regrowth | None |
| Yield | 1 |
| Sell price | 40 Goldcoin |
| Acid rain | **Resistant** — safe outdoors |

### Chinese Cabbage — high profit, rain-fearful

- Net ~12.5 per pot per day — one of the best early earners
- **Fears acid rain** — plant in tents or protect it (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

### Pumpkins — the mid-game star

- Seeds cost **100**, fruit sells for **340**, **9-day maturity**, ~26.7 net per pot per day (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
- **Save them for April** — they sell for 412 each during the dry-season price spike
- Pumpkin seed stock unlocks after the salmon festival (25 salmon, days 25–27 of month 1)

## Planters: The Bottleneck

| Planter | Status (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)) |
|---------|--------|
| **Plant Basin – Basic** | Single-use — decays after one planting. Don't waste resources on it |
| **Plant Basin – Wood** | Reusable — unlocked via villager tasks. The real starter planter |

**Soil is the limiting resource** — it's limited daily, so gather it first every morning (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

## The Gene System

The gene system lets you **modify crop traits** — growth speed, yield, and more (per the official [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)). ## Sprinklers

Manual sprinklers automate watering. One community guide uses **4 manual sprinklers to cover 32 pots** (~320 Goldcoin/day with Endyams) (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)). Sprinklers are unlocked through the Tech Tree (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).
