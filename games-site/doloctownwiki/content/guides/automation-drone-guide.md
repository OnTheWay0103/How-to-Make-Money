# Automation & Drones Guide — How the 1.0 System Works

The **Farming Automation** system was the headline feature of the Doloc Town **1.0 release** (August 6, 2026): robotic drones that handle your farming chores — and eventually your ranching, crafting, and logistics (per the official [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews)). This guide walks through what's confirmed about the system.

## How to Unlock Drones

- Drones are a **late-game mechanic**, unlocked by researching the **Automation node on the Tech Tree** (per the official [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews))
- Two NPCs guide drone automation research through **roughly two phases**: **Mechanic Mody** and **Gunsmith Cod** help you upgrade and modify drones (per the [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews))

## Phase 1: Basic Agricultural Drone + Indoor Mini Hub

The entry point gives you **simple, single-purpose agricultural drones** (per the [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews)):

- Drones can **sow seeds, water crops, fertilize fields, and lay mulch** in an order you assign
- They **automatically fetch required supplies from different crates**
- Paired with the **mini hub**, this automates the entire **indoor planting and crop-care workflow**

## Phase 2: Large-Scale Drone Hub

The advanced stage scales everything up (per the [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews)):

- Plan and deploy **multiple drones with different roles** for automated tasks
- Drone operating range expands to **both indoor and outdoor spaces**, and drones can **travel freely between separate buildings**
- A **wide variety of drones** is available. Beyond planting, fertilizing, and harvesting, they manage:
  - **Refilling, processing, and transportation**
  - **Livestock rearing** (ranches and fish farms)
  - **Recipe crafting**
  - **Item sorting and storage**

## What the 1.0 Patch Added Around Drones

Per the [1.0 patch notes via VGSpoilers](https://www.vgspoilers.com/game/doloc-town/patches/1.0) and [GameGrin's 1.0 report](https://www.gamegrin.com/news/doloc-town-reveals-more-about-update-1-0/):

- The Farming Automation system ships with an **Industrial Tech Tree and tutorial quests**
- **New tool tiers** and **combat drones** and accessories
- **Display Stands** improved to display complete drones with their components
- Gameplay adjustments improving **animal production** alongside resource gathering, crafting, and combat balance

## Automation Tips

1. **Design your farm for drones early** — drones fetch from crates and travel between buildings; keep storage and planter rows accessible (see the [Farm Layout Guide](/guides/farm-layout-guide))
2. **Follow the tutorial quests** — 1.0 ships with automation tutorial quests that teach the two phases
3. **Automation frees time for exploration** — the 1.0 story concludes in the Old City Ruins, and drones are meant to let you go there
