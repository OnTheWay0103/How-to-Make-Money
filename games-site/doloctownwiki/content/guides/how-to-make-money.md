# How to Make Money Fast in Doloc Town

Goldcoin is the lifeblood of Doloc Town — it buys backpack slots, fishing rods, seeds, and upgrades. This guide collects the verified money-making strategies used by the community, with the numbers from the sources that report them. Numbers are per the sources cited; anything unconfirmed is marked [待确认].

## Phase 1: Endyams Are the Top Early Crop

Multiple guides agree: **Endyams are one of the best early-game crops for making Goldcoin**, and you should start growing as many as possible (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/) and [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).

From the crop database (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)):

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Grows in | Light Rain and Heavy Rain months |
| Days to first harvest | 2 |
| Regrowth | None |
| Yield | 1 |
| Sell price | **40 Goldcoin** each |
| Acid rain tolerance | **Not harmed** — ideal for outdoor mass planting |

**Key Endyam strategies:**

1. **Cook them before selling.** Once you unlock cooking in the Tech Tree, cooked Endyams sell for **+5 Goldcoin more each** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks) and [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)). Cook in batches using multiple cooking pots.
2. **Profit math:** Raw Endyams net about **7.5 Goldcoin per pot per day**; cooked (Roasted Endyam) profit rises to about **10 per pot per day** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).
3. **Mass planting:** One community guide suggests four manual sprinklers covering 32 pots, earning ~320 Goldcoin/day (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).

## Phase 2: Cooking Is the Value-Add Engine

Unlocking **cooking early in the Tech Tree** lets you process whole harvests at once, which is where real profit comes from (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)). Verified examples:

- **Delicious Steamed Fish** — e.g., catfish (85 G) + Endyam (30 G) sells for **210 Goldcoin** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
- **Delicious Stir-Fried Meat combos** — netting 95–140 profit per dish (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
- **Cabbage + shrimp stir-fry** — sells for **240 Goldcoin** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

**Chinese cabbage** is also a strong early earner (~12.5 net per pot per day), but it **fears acid rain** — plant it in tents or protect it (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).

## Phase 3: Fishing for Big Paydays

Fishing needs **no bait** — you just need energy (10 per cast) and a rod (per [Raiderking](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/)):

- **Golden Fish** sell for **1,000 Goldcoin each**, and there's a guild quest paying **2,000** for one (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/)). They're found only in the Deep/Forest Pond [待确认 exact pond — see the [Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide)].
- The pond by the **elk/deer statue** has a **100% fish catch rate** (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-fishing-guide/)).
- A good day of fishing can earn ~2,000 Goldcoin (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).
- **Grass carp and perch** cook into Delicious Steamed Fish (210 G) for solid profit (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).

## Phase 4: Processing Stations Run 24/7

Processing adds 10–20% value with no energy cost once set up (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)):

| Setup | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Pickle jars + blueberries | Processed goods sell for more |
| Drying boxes + fish | Dried fish value-add |
| Looms + fibers | Higher-value cloth goods |
| Buy meat from **Kasia** → jerky | Net ~50 per piece; 20 boxes ≈ 1,000/day |

## The Salmon Festival (Days 25–27 of Month 1)

On days 25–27 of the first month, **salmon can be caught in any water** — but it requires an upgraded rod (a 1,000 Goldcoin investment). Catching **25 salmon** unlocks an **electric dryer** and **pumpkin seed stock** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).

## Pumpkins: The Mid-Game King (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

- Pumpkin seeds cost **100**, fruit sells for **340**, 9-day maturity — netting ~26.7 per pot per day
- **Save pumpkins to sell in April** — they go for **412 each** during the dry-season price spike

## Sell During the Dry Season

**Item prices rise in April** when crops are hard to grow, so hoard goods and sell then (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)). Also collect scrap and recycle "trash" items (bottles, scrap metal) at the **junk recycler** for resources (per [Cheatbook](https://www.cheatbook.de/files/doloctown.htm)).
