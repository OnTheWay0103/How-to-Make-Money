# How to Manage Energy, Health & Stamina in Doloc Town

Doloc Town tracks three separate stats, and mismanaging any of them costs you Goldcoin. This guide explains how each one works and how to keep them topped up. The stat definitions below are per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks) and [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/).

## The Three Stats

| Stat | What it does | What happens at zero |
|------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Energy** | Decreases with almost every activity | You **pass out** and **lose Goldcoin** |
| **Health** | Your life bar; drops when you take damage | You **die** and **lose money** |
| **Stamina** | Consumed by tool use (chopping, mining, fishing) | You **can't perform actions** |

### Energy

Energy is the resource that governs your whole day. Farming, fishing, exploring, and crafting all drain it. When it hits zero, you pass out and lose Goldcoin (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).

**How to restore Energy:**

- **Sleeping** — ends the day and fully restores you
- **Napping** — a shorter rest; community guides recommend it over pushing through (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- **Eating** — cooked food restores far more than raw ingredients

### Health

Your Health bar drops when hostile creatures or hazards damage you. At zero you die and lose money (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).

**How to restore Health:**

- Food (cooked meals restore both Health and Energy)
- Sleep
- Farm equipment — some gear restores health over time [待确认 — exact items]

### Stamina

Stamina is consumed specifically by **tool use** — chopping with the Axe, mining with the Pickaxe, and fishing. At zero you can't perform those actions until it recovers (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).

## Energy-Saving Habits

1. **Don't fish your Energy dry.** Fishing costs **10 Energy per cast** (per [Raiderking](https://raiderking.com/doloc-town-100-fishing-guide-all-locations/)) — budget it like any other activity.
2. **Prioritize daily-limited resources.** Soil is limited daily, so gather it first while your Energy is fresh (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).
3. **Nap instead of passing out.** Pushing to zero Energy costs Goldcoin — the nap investment pays for itself (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks)).
4. **Batch cooking pays off.** Cooking multiple dishes at once keeps you fed without repeated Energy costs on individual actions.

## What About the Acid Rain Corrosion Status?

Separate from the three stats, exposure to **acid rain** builds up **corrosion** on your character. It can be removed by **staying indoors, using a shower, or wearing the welder's mask** (per the Chinese fan wiki [Huijiwiki](https://doloctown.huijiwiki.com/wiki/%E7%8A%B6%E6%80%81)).
