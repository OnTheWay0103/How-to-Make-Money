# Cooking & Recipes Guide — How to Unlock All 80+ Recipes

Doloc Town ships with **80+ recipes** (per the official [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)), and cooking is the game's best **value-add engine**: cooked food sells for more than raw ingredients and restores far more stats. This guide covers every verified way to unlock recipes and the dishes worth making.

## How to Unlock Recipes

Per [TechRaptor's Recipe Guide](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items), recipes unlock through **five channels**:

| Channel | How it works |
|---------|--------------|
| **Villager tasks** | Completing tasks rewards recipes (and is how you get the reusable Plant Basin – Wood) |
| **Mail** | Recipes arrive by mail as you progress |
| **Zenis' Tavern cookbooks** | Buy cookbooks for **500 G** each |
| **Lank's Waste Exchange** | Trade Rubbish for recipe unlocks |
| **Kuma the traveling trader** | Buy recipes when he visits |
| **Tech Tree** | Research cooking nodes to unlock cooking and specific recipes (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)) |

## The Best Profit Dishes (Verified Values)

Per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/):

| Dish | Ingredients (example) | Sell price | Net profit |
|------|----------------------|------------|------------|
| **Delicious Steamed Fish** | Catfish (85 G) + Endyam (30 G) | **210 G** | ~95 G |
| **Delicious Stir-Fried Meat** | Meat + vegetables | — | 95–140 G per dish |
| **Cabbage + Shrimp Stir-Fry** | Chinese cabbage + shrimp | **240 G** | — |
| **Roasted Endyam** | 1 Endyam | 45 G (40 + 5) | +5 G per crop |

**The principle:** cook before you sell. Roasting a full Endyam harvest adds +5 Goldcoin per crop — the profit compounds across the season (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items) and [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

## Frequently Asked Cooking Questions

**How do I make cheese?** — "How to make cheese" is a common player question (per Google search suggestions). Cheese requires milk from farm animals, which means unlocking **ranching** first. The exact cheese recipes are [待确认 — not yet documented in English sources we could verify].

**Can I cook in bulk?** — Yes. Community guides recommend **multiple cooking pots** so you can process whole harvests at once (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/)).

## Cooking Tips

1. **Unlock cooking early in the Tech Tree** — it's the highest-ROI research early on (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
2. **Cook Endyams before selling** — +5 G each with no extra ingredients
3. **Buy the Zenis' Tavern cookbooks** (500 G) as soon as you can afford them — they're a permanent recipe source (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items))
4. **Save stat foods for exploration days** — Hot Cocoa and Deli Omurice carry you through long trips
