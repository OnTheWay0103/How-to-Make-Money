# How to Set Up Your Farm — Layout & Building Guide

Doloc Town is a **vertical farm simulator** — you stack planters on platforms instead of laying out endless fields (per the official [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/)). Your layout directly affects your income and your survival. This guide covers the verified building facts and community layout strategies.

## The Vertical Farm System

- Farm space is limited, so the game expects you to **build upward with platforms** (Platform – Wooden structures) (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- One community layout: a row of ~10 **Plant Basin – Wood** planters, expanded upward (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- Crops are planted in **pots stacked across platforms** — convenient layout matters for automation efficiency later (per the [1.0 overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews))

## Layout Priorities by Phase

### Early game: function over looks

1. **Collect soil first, then wood** — soil is the daily-limited bottleneck (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
2. **Skip the Plant Basin – Basic** (single-use) and rush the reusable Plant Basin – Wood from villager tasks
3. **Group planters in rows** so sprinklers cover them — 4 manual sprinklers cover ~32 pots (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
4. **Keep a tent or indoor zone for acid-rain-fearful crops** like Chinese cabbage (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

### Mid game: lightning protection

- The **Rivervalley** opens after the first few days — prioritize **copper** there for **Lightning Rods** to protect crops from storms, before tool upgrades (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- Thunderstorms strike during storm weather; rods are your verified protection (per the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/))

### Late game: plan for drones

- The 1.0 **Farming Automation** system sends drones to sow, water, fertilize, and mulch in an **assigned order** — drones fetch supplies from crates, so keep storage near planters (per the [1.0 feature overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews))
- The advanced **Large-Scale Drone Hub** lets drones work both indoors and outdoors and travel between buildings — keep pathways open between farm buildings (per the [1.0 feature overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews))
- **Display Stands** can display complete drones with their components (per the [1.0 patch notes via VGSpoilers](https://www.vgspoilers.com/game/doloc-town/patches/1.0))

## Can You Move Buildings?

"How to move buildings" is one of the most-asked player questions (per Google search suggestions). At the time of writing, we haven't found an official statement confirming whether existing buildings can be relocated — [Unconfirmed]. Community discussion suggests planning your layout carefully rather than relying on moving structures later.

## Farm Checklist

- [ ] Plant Basin – Wood unlocked (villager tasks)
- [ ] Soil collected daily
- [ ] Sprinklers placed to cover planter rows
- [ ] Lightning Rods on outdoor crops (copper from Rivervalley)
- [ ] Indoor/tented zone for rain-fearful crops
- [ ] Storage crates near planters (for drone automation later)
