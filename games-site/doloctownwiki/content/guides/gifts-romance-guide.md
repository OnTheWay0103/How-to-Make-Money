# Gifts & Friendship Guide — Befriending the Townsfolk

Doloc Town's settlement is populated by **quirky townsfolk with hidden stories** — befriending them unlocks tasks, recipes, and progression (per [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/doloc-town-launches-august-6) and [Neoseeker](https://www.neoseeker.com/doloc-town/walkthrough)). This guide covers what's verified about the social systems.

## Why Befriend the Townsfolk

- **Villager tasks are the game's early progression engine** — they reward recipes, Goldcoin, and the reusable Plant Basin – Wood (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- **The Rivervalley isn't open for the first few days** — use that time to complete tasks for rewards (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
- Key villagers with known roles:
  - **Hult** — general store, backpack upgrades (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks))
  - **Zenis** — runs the Tavern, sells cookbooks (500 G) (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items))
  - **Lank** — runs the Waste Exchange; also the Day 8 pickaxe event (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items) and [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
  - **Kuma** — the traveling trader who sells recipes (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items))
  - **Mechanic Mody** and **Gunsmith Cod** — the 1.0 drone guides (per the [1.0 content overview](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2285550/allnews))
  - **Kasia** — sells meat for processing (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

## Festivals

- **Seasonal festivals** are part of the game (per the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2285550/Doloc_Town/))
- The **Salmon Festival** (days 25–27 of month 1) is the best-documented one: salmon appear in any water, and catching **25 salmon** unlocks the **electric dryer** and **pumpkin seed stock** (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))

## The Town's Mystery

Beyond the daily life systems, the town's **vanished leader** and the remnants of **"Eden" technology** drive the main story, which concludes in the 1.0 **Old City Ruins** content (per [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/doloc-town-launches-august-6) and the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-)).

## Social Tips

1. **Do tasks daily while the Rivervalley is locked** — free progression and recipes (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/))
2. **Save up for Zenis' cookbooks** — 500 G each for permanent recipe unlocks (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items))
3. **Watch for Lank on day 8** — he reclaims his pickaxe from you in a scripted event (per [Prodigygamers](https://prodigygamers.com/2026/08/10/doloc-town-beginners-guide-4th-month-cash-strategy-farming-meta/))
4. **Check for Kuma the trader** — his visiting-stock recipes rotate (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-recipe-guide-unlock-new-items))
