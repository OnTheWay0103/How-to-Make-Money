# Backpack Upgrades Guide — Slot Costs & When to Buy

Your backpack is your only inventory — and in a game where soil and materials are daily-limited, carrying capacity directly limits how much you can collect per day. The upgrade path is simple and verified: **talk to Hult at the general store, east of your farm** (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks) and [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

## When Each Upgrade Is Worth It

### 500 G (30 slots) — buy on day 2–3

This is the best value in the game. 500 G is one good fishing day, and 10 extra slots make every gathering trip meaningfully longer. Community guides recommend upgrading the backpack **early** (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

### 4,000 G (40 slots) — first week

By the time you're processing crops and visiting the Rivervalley, 4,000 G is realistic within a week. The 40-slot pack is the point where a full day of gathering fits in one trip.

### 10,000 G — mid-game

Buy it when your money engine (cooking + processing) is stable. Prioritize it **after** copper lightning rods and tool upgrades, per community advice (per [Grindnstrat](https://grindnstrat.com/doloc-town-1-0-beginner-guide/)).

## Tips

1. **Upgrade before big gathering days** — soil and materials are daily-limited; more slots means more of them per trip
2. **Don't upgrade before your first Endyam harvest** — the 500 G pack should be funded by your first income wave, not by skipping it
3. **Hult's shop is east of the farm** — check it early so you know the route (per [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/doloc-town-guide-beginner-tips-and-tricks))
