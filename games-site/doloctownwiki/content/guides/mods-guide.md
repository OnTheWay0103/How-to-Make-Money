# Mods & Steam Workshop Guide — How to Install Mods

Doloc Town has **official Steam Workshop integration**, added during Early Access in the "Steam Workshop" update (per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-), which lists Workshop integration among the major Early Access updates). This guide covers how the mod system works.

## What's Confirmed

- **Steam Workshop integration** was added during Early Access (per the [1.0 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Doloc-Town-Leaves-Early-Access---Beloved-Post-Apocalyptic-Farming-Sim-))
- The game runs on **Steam**, so Workshop mods are the standard delivery method

## How to Install Mods

1. **Open Steam** and navigate to Doloc Town's **Workshop** page (Community → Workshop, or via the store page)
2. **Browse and subscribe** to the mods you want — subscribing downloads them automatically
3. **Launch the game** — Workshop content is usually applied on startup
4. **Manage mods** through the Workshop's "Your Files" page (unsubscribe to remove)

## Mod Safety Tips

1. **Back up your save** before installing gameplay-changing mods — see the [FAQ](/guides/faq) for save-file locations
2. **Check the mod's last-update date** — the 1.0 patch changed many systems, so old mods may break
3. **Report broken mods** to their authors via the Workshop
