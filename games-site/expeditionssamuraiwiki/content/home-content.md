## What is Expeditions: Samurai?

Expeditions: Samurai is a party-based historical CRPG set in **Sengoku-era Japan, 1600 AD** — the final year of a brutal civil war that will end at the **Battle of Sekigahara**. In an alternate history, you play **Captain Flynn**, an English privateer on a mission from Queen Elizabeth who arrives in Japan aboard the battered Dutch frigate **De Albatros** — beating William Adams to the punch and becoming the first Protestant to set foot in Japan.

What begins as a shipwrecked privateer's survival story becomes a bid for power: navigate civil war politics through **stealth, diplomacy, or violence**, earn the trust of **8 companions**, and eventually rise to become a **samurai lord (Hatamoto)** in service to Tokugawa Ieyasu — with a village of your own to rule.

The game is built in **Unreal Engine 5** with dynamic 3D character portraits in dialogue, full controller support, and the series' first **2-player co-op campaign**.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Three Pillars** — Stealth (new to the series), Diplomacy, and Violence. Most situations can be resolved with any of them.
- **8 Companions** — Each with a personal story and quest chain; every one can become a friend, rival, or lover.
- **Simultaneous Turn-Based Combat** — Movement, attacks, and interruptions play out at the same time.
- **East Meets West Arsenal** — Flintlocks, cutlasses, and grenades alongside katanas, naginatas, and longbows, with custom gear crafting.
- **World Map** — 45+ levels and 100+ events/encounters, quests in any order.
- **2-Player Co-op** — Full campaign, drop-in/drop-out, twin captains, shared dialogue checks.
- **Village Management** — Become Hatamoto and rule a village (arriving during Early Access).
- **Custom Difficulty** — Combat and resource management tuned independently.
