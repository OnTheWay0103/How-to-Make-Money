# Expeditions: Samurai — Romance Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Romance in Expeditions: Samurai is not a side minigame — it is the deepest level of the companion relationship system. **All 8 companions are romanceable**, and each relationship can develop into a meaningful friendship, a lifelong rivalry, or "possibly even a new love." This guide covers how the system works, how to steer relationships toward romance, and the unique twist: in co-op, you can compete for a companion's affections.

## How the Relationship System Works

The official materials describe the system in three tracks:

- **Friendship** — Companions you treat well become loyal allies.
- **Rivalry** — Companions you cross, dismiss, or contradict become rivals — a state with real consequences.
- **Romance** — The deepest bond, reached by consistently building trust and choosing romantic dialogue options.

Every companion can be steered down any of the three tracks. The same companion who becomes your lover in one playthrough can become your bitter rival in another.

## The Core Principles of Building Romance

From the official materials and series precedent, romance follows these rules:

1. **Earn trust first.** Companions only open up to people they trust — "earn their trust and an ally becomes a friend — or something more," as the official trailer puts it. Complete their personal quest chains.
2. **Be consistent.** The relationship meter is shaped by every interaction. Flirty dialogue followed by dismissive choices sabotages progress.
3. **Play their personal quests.** Each companion's dedicated quest chain is the backbone of their story — and of your relationship. Never skip them.
4. **Respect their values.** The trailer emphasizes that "they may not share the same values" — companions respond to choices that align with their worldview.
5. **Romance is a long game.** Expect romance beats to unlock across Act One's story chapters, with full payoffs as the campaign continues into Acts Two through Four.

## The Five Named Companions — What We Know About Their Stories

- **Antje (burglar)** — Practical, stealthy, and independent. Expect her quest chain to revolve around her past as a thief and what she's running from.
- **Johan (swordsman)** — Proud and arrogant, with blood ties to your predecessor as captain. His story is about legacy — and his ego needs handling with care.
- **Tora-Hime (onna-musha)** — Seeking her place in history in a warrior culture that denies women that place. Supporting her ambition is the obvious key to her heart.
- **Étienne Durand (priest-diplomat)** — The crew's conscience and negotiator. Expect faith, duty, and the tension between them to define his arc.
- **Piet Ruisch (brawler)** — Sword and pistol, no-nonsense. His arc is likely built around loyalty and the rough world of privateers.

The three unnamed companions will be detailed here as soon as official materials reveal them.

## Romance vs. Rivalry: The Stakes

The game explicitly allows **every** relationship to go either way — "friends, rivals, or even lovers." The rivalry track is not a failure state; it's a different story:

- **Romantic companions** stand with you, gain loyalty bonuses, and unlock personal endings.
- **Rival companions** can oppose you in story-critical moments — dangerous in a game about civil war factions.
- **Some events may be romance-gated.** Expect exclusive dialogue, scenes, and potentially endings tied to your deepest relationships.

## Romance in Co-op: Competing for Affection

Expeditions: Samurai adds a twist unique to the series: in **2-player co-op**, both captains share dialogue scenes — and can **compete for a companion's affections**. The official line: players can "wrestle for control of important decisions or compete for a companion's affections."

In practice, expect:

- **Shared dialogue scenes** where both players' dialogue choices count toward the same relationship.
- **Competition mechanics** — when you both pursue the same companion, your choices in shared scenes decide who wins their heart.
- **Division of labor** — the smart co-op play is to each romance a different companion, avoiding direct competition entirely.

## Practical Romance Tips

1. **Keep romanceable companions in your active party.** Relationships only grow through interaction.
2. **Do their quests early.** Loyalty quests compound — doing them early gives every later interaction better footing.
3. **Save before big conversations.** If you're chasing a specific romance, keep a save at chapter boundaries.
4. **Don't romance everyone at once** (if you want stable relationships). Focus on one or two companions to avoid loyalty conflicts.
5. **In co-op, coordinate.** Decide who's pursuing whom before the game starts — competing for the same companion can split the party's focus.
