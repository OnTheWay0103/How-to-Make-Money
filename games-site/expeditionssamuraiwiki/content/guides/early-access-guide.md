# Expeditions: Samurai Early Access Guide — Act One Content, Roadmap & What's Missing

**Expeditions: Samurai** — the party-based tactical CRPG from **Campfire Cabal** (the Danish studio founded in 2022 by veterans of Expeditions: Conquistador, Viking, and Rome), published by **THQ Nordic** — launches into **Steam Early Access on August 7, 2026**. This guide lays out exactly what ships on day one, what arrives later, and how the Early Access roadmap to the 1.0 release works. Everything here is drawn from official materials (the Steam page, press releases, the companion trailer coverage, and developer diaries); anything not officially confirmed is marked **TBD**.

## Release Date and Platforms

- **Early Access launch:** August 7, 2026, on **PC via Steam** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).
- **Full release (1.0):** targeted for **Q1 2027**.
- **Language:** English interface, full audio, and subtitles at launch.
- **Price:** the Steam page confirms Early Access is "at a slightly discounted price compared to the full version" — the exact price will be confirmed on the store page at launch.
- The developers have said they will publish a **detailed roadmap before launch** covering what's in the release build and what follows.

## What's in the Early Access Launch (Confirmed)

The official Early Access description promises a "satisfying slice of the story" that shows off **almost every feature of the final game**, including:

- **Act One** — roughly **8 hours** of quests with full high-quality voice-over.
- **Full-scope combat** — the fast, freeing simultaneous turn-based system where movement, attacks, and interruptions play out at the same time.
- **Exploration** — real-time movement through levels.
- **Stealth** — the series' first full stealth pillar: sneaking in real time, splitting your party, setting ambush positions, and opening fights with devastating surprise attacks. Firecrackers are officially confirmed as a distraction gadget.
- **World map travel** — the open map structure the series is known for.
- **Character creation** — create Captain Flynn (the surname is fixed, everything else is yours: first name, appearance, and a choice of four classes).
- **The companion cast** — all 8 companions exist from the start with personal stories and dedicated quest chains; five are officially named (Antje the burglar, Johan the swordsman, Tora-Hime the onna-musha, Étienne Durand the priest-diplomat, and Piet Ruisch the brawler), with three more revealed at or around launch.

The official launch list — combat, exploration, stealth, world map, and quests — is the definition of what "Act One, nearly feature-complete" means in practice.

## What's NOT in the Launch Build

Being clear about what's missing is half the point of an Early Access guide:

- **Village management and the Hatamoto title** — confirmed to arrive **during Early Access development**, not on day one. The full release includes "rulership of your own village in Japan," unlocked through the Hatamoto rank. See the [Village Management Guide](/guides/village-management-guide) for everything known about the feature.
- **Act Two** — arrives via a dedicated story update later in Early Access (~10 more hours of quests).
- **Acts Three and Four** — part of the full 1.0 release.
- **Crafting (timing TBD)** — official materials describe custom gear crafting as part of the full game ("craft custom gear, experiment with tactical abilities, and leverage weapon skills"), and the Steam page lists crafting among the **full-version additions**, while Early Access "adds quests, weapons, and armor over time." Whether any crafting system is in the EA launch build is **TBD** — don't plan an Act One endgame around it until launch confirms it. Our [Weapons & Armor Guide](/guides/weapons-armor-guide) covers the arsenal and crafting expectations.
- **More character build options** — the full release adds build customization depth on top of the launch classes.
- **The complete companion roster** — three of the eight companions are still unnamed; expect reveals in the launch-week media cycle.

## The Early Access Roadmap (Confirmed)

The confirmed path from launch to 1.0:

| Milestone | What arrives |
|-----------|--------------|
| **EA Launch — Aug 7, 2026** | Act One (~8 hours), full combat / exploration / stealth / world map, fully voiced quests |
| **Content Update 1** | New content for existing systems |
| **Content Update 2** | New content for existing systems |
| **Story Update** | Act Two unlocked (~10 more hours of quests) |
| **1.0 — Q1 2027** | Four acts, ~60+ hours total, 45+ levels and 100+ events/encounters, fully voiced, village management + Hatamoto, crafting, more build options |

The team has also promised a more detailed roadmap document before the August 7 launch — check it (and this page) for exact update timing once it lands.

## Co-op: Coming, But Launch Timing Is TBD

Two-player drop-in/drop-out co-op is the first in the series' history, with twin captains, shared dialogue checks, and simultaneous combat resolution. **However**: the Steam page's Early Access notes list the **co-op mode among features being added during Early Access**, and the official launch-features list (combat, exploration, stealth, world map) does not mention it. That means:

- Co-op is a confirmed feature of Expeditions: Samurai.
- Whether it's playable **on day one** is **TBD** — it may arrive with one of the first content updates.

See the [Co-op Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide) for the full confirmed details of the mode itself.

## How the Studio Runs Early Access

The Steam page confirms the studio's EA approach: the developers will **personally participate in Steam forum discussions and the Expeditions Discord server**, and the team wants community feedback during the Early Access period. Campfire Cabal also confirmed no generative AI was used in the game's development. This is a veteran team (the Expeditions series has sold over 1.3 million copies across its entries) using Early Access to finish and polish rather than to test an unproven concept.

## Should You Buy the Early Access Build?

An honest buying framework based only on confirmed information:

**Buy at launch if** you want the tactical CRPG experience the series is known for (simultaneous combat, stealth ambushes, world map freedom) and are comfortable with an 8-hour act while the rest ships over the next year. The launch build is described as "almost feature complete" in systems — it is not an empty shell.

**Wait for 1.0 if** you want the complete story (four acts, ~60 hours), village management, the full crafting and build-customization suite, and the complete companion roster with all reveals done. Early Access pricing is discounted versus the full version, but the full experience only exists at 1.0.

**Unknowns to watch:** whether **saves carry from Early Access to 1.0** (not officially confirmed — treat it as TBD until the roadmap or launch materials say otherwise), the exact **co-op availability date**, and the **class names** and build details (revealed at launch).
