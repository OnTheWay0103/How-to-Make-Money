# Expeditions: Samurai — Best Builds Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Expeditions games live and die by builds. With four player classes, eight companions, three gameplay pillars, and an East-meets-West arsenal, Expeditions: Samurai gives you more build space than any prior entry in the series. Because the full class trees ship with Early Access on **August 7, 2026**, this guide gives you the build **philosophy** and **framework** that will carry through launch week — plus the archetypes that work in every Expeditions game.

## The Three Build Pillars

Every build in Expeditions: Samurai leans on one (or more) of the three pillars:

| Pillar | Core fantasy | Key stats/gear | Best companions |
|--------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Stealth** | Open every fight on your terms | Agility/movement, silence, gadgets (firecrackers, smoke bombs) | Antje |
| **Diplomacy** | Win without fighting | Social skills, dialogue bonuses, reputation | Étienne Durand |
| **Steel** | Overwhelm with force | Damage, armor, weapon skills | Johan, Piet Ruisch |

Pure builds are viable; hybrid builds are *stronger*. A stealth opener followed by a combat clean-up wins fights a pure-combat party would bleed for.

## The Five Archetypes to Build Toward

### 1. The Shadow Captain (Stealth-first)

- **Class pick:** the stealth-leaning class at creation
- **Build:** movement and ambush bonuses first; gadget proficiency (firecrackers, smoke) early; silent takedown upgrades as soon as available
- **Playstyle:** split the party, scout with your Captain, position everyone, open combat with a surprise attack
- **Why it works:** combat is "cerebral" and rewards setup. A surprise attack opener is the single biggest advantage available in the game.

### 2. The Silver Tongue (Diplomacy-first)

- **Class pick:** the social-skilled class (pair with Étienne)
- **Build:** dialogue skill line, reputation bonuses, follower-buffing auras
- **Playstyle:** talk past fights entirely; when forced into combat, win with debuffs and control
- **Why it works:** diplomacy resolves encounters with zero casualties and zero supply burn. In a resource-strained Act One, that matters enormously.

### 3. The Kenshi (Katana duelist)

- **Build:** katana weapon skills, armor penetration, counter/interrupt talents
- **Playstyle:** front-line duelist — kill priority targets before they act
- **Why it works:** the fast simultaneous combat system rewards interrupt-heavy duelists who can punish enemy actions.

### 4. The Ranger (Naginata / longbow)

- **Build:** reach weapons (naginata) and ranged (longbow), zone control, elevation/positional bonuses
- **Playstyle:** control space — keep enemies off your squishier characters while whittling them down at range
- **Why it works:** the world map and encounter design favor positioning; reach weapons thrive in chokepoints.

### 5. The Gunslinger (Flintlock hybrid)

- **Build:** pistol skills, grenades, quick-draw talents, close-mid range flexibility
- **Playstyle:** burst damage at the start of every fight, then clean up with melee
- **Why it works:** East-meets-West gear means powder and steel synergize — open with a flintlock volley, finish with a blade.

## Build Progression Priorities

Regardless of archetype, follow this order through Act One:

1. **Starter gear, not starter skills.** Your first purchases should be weapons and armor — crafted gear scales with weapon skills, so a good weapon makes every skill point better.
2. **One pillar to 70%.** Commit to your primary pillar first. Jack-of-all-trades builds underperform in Expeditions.
3. **Weapon skills over utility.** Skill lines that boost your chosen weapons beat generic utility early; the game's "weapon skills" system rewards focus.
4. **Companion synergy at every level.** Your build is four characters, not one. Level companions into roles that complement your Captain's pillar.
5. **Crafting after Act One's midpoint.** By mid-Act-One you'll have recipes and resources for custom gear — invest then, not before.

## Common Build Mistakes

1. **Spreading points across all three pillars.** You end up mediocre at everything.
2. **Ignoring injuries.** A debuffed build is a broken build. Tend wounds between missions.
3. **Building for late game in Act One.** Early Access is Act One only — a build that comes online in Act Three is wasted until 1.0. Build for the content that exists.
4. **Not checking enemy resistances.** "Leverage weapon skills to tackle diverse enemy types" is the game telling you loadouts are matchup-dependent. Carry two weapon families.

## Build Reset Options

Expeditions games have historically provided respec options — the studio knows players experiment. Check the launch build for respec availability early; if respec is cheap, experiment freely in your first run.
