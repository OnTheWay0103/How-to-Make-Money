# Expeditions: Samurai — Crafting Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

A samurai's blade is an extension of the self — and in Expeditions: Samurai, the armory is yours to control. **Custom gear crafting is confirmed for the full release** of the game, letting you forge and modify the East-meets-West arsenal (katanas and naginatas alongside flintlocks, cutlasses, and grenades). What remains **TBD** is how much of the crafting system ships in the Early Access launch on August 7, 2026 — official materials describe crafting as part of the full release, and the launch build's scope is still being confirmed.

## What Is Confirmed

- **Custom gear crafting exists.** Official materials confirm you can craft and customize gear in the full game.
- **The arsenal is East-meets-West.** Crafting sits on top of a weapon roster spanning Japanese and European arms — expect recipes for both families.
- **Materials matter.** The world map's events and encounters are the source of the resources you'll spend at the forge.
- **EA timing is TBD.** Whether the August 7 build includes crafting is unconfirmed — the walkthrough and beginner guides plan for both outcomes.

## The Expected Crafting Loop

Based on official framing and the series' design DNA (Expeditions: Rome had a workshop and gear upgrades at its Roman-era camp):

| Stage | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| **Gather** | Collect materials from world map events, exploration, loot, and purchases |
| **Unlock** | Acquire recipes — from quests, vendors, and blacksmiths |
| **Craft** | Build new weapons and armor at the forge |
| **Customize** | Add mods and upgrades to tailor gear to your party |
| **Equip** | Load out your four active companions |

Treat the stages above as **informed expectation** — the exact structure will be confirmed with the launch build.

## Materials Gathering

The confirmed backbone of any crafting system is a steady material income:

- **World map events generate resources.** The 100+ events/encounters across the full game are the official source of the supplies and materials that keep your crew going.
- **Exploration rewards.** Searching levels and optional corners pays in crafting components — check every building, body, and shelf.
- **Loot and purchases.** Weapons and armor you find can be upgraded even before you can craft from scratch — the [Weapons & Armor Guide](/guides/weapons-armor-guide) covers loadout management.
- **A village economy (1.0).** Village management arrives in the full release, and a domain of your own is a natural production hub — workshops and storehouses were the classic roster in the series' earlier base-building.

## Forge, Blacksmith & Upgrades

Where crafting happens is still **TBD** — the series has used a camp/workshop model in the past, and village management (added during Early Access development) is a likely home for the full forge:

- **Weapon customization** — expect to modify blades, barrels, grips, and scopes rather than only whole weapons.
- **Armor upgrades** — tailoring, padding, and plate modifications to raise protection and weight tolerance.
- **Mods** — slots on gear for add-ons that shift stats: damage, accuracy, armor, and utility.
- **Blacksmith NPCs** — likely vendors of both services and recipes, wherever they appear in the world.

## Recipes Known From Act One

**None are officially confirmed yet** — the recipe list will be published here the moment official materials or the launch build reveal it. What we can say in advance:

- Recipes are likely tied to **quest rewards, vendor stocks, and discovery** — keep an eye on every trader and every side quest reward.
- Expect recipes for **both weapon families**: Japanese (katana, naginata, longbow, tanto) and European (flintlock, cutlass, grenade, pike).
- Crafting material scarcity is a likely **difficulty lever** — the game's resource-management settings (see the [Difficulty Guide](/guides/difficulty-guide)) may interact with how freely you can afford to forge.

## Early Access Strategy: With or Without the Forge

Because crafting's EA status is TBD, plan for both worlds:

**If crafting is in the EA build:**
- Hoard materials from the first hours — mid-Act is when the recipes worth crafting appear.
- Prioritize one upgrade per companion rather than spreading materials thin.

**If crafting is NOT in the EA build:**
- Invest in strong looted and purchased gear instead — the walkthrough plan covers this route.
- Bank materials anyway — resources are expected to carry into the full release, where the forge awaits.
