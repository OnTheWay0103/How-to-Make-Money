# Expeditions: Samurai Launch Day Guide — Release Time, Price & First 24 Hours

Expeditions: Samurai — the party-based tactical CRPG from **Campfire Cabal**, published by **THQ Nordic** — enters **Steam Early Access on Friday, August 7, 2026**. This is the game's first-ever Early Access release for the series, and the launch window is where the community gets shaped. This page collects every confirmed launch-day fact, from the exact unlock time to the price status, plus an honest first-24-hours plan for Act One.

## When Does It Unlock?

The Early Access unlock time is **14:00 UTC on August 7, 2026** (SteamDB-listed):

- **7:00 AM** — Pacific Daylight Time
- **10:00 AM** — Eastern Daylight Time
- **4:00 PM** — Central European Summer Time
- **11:00 PM** — Japan Standard Time

If the page shows a countdown, treat the Steam store as authoritative — unlock times occasionally shift by minutes around big launches.

## Price: Confirmed at Launch

The price is now on the store page: **US$29.99 / €29.99** (¥4,389 in Japan), with a **34% Early Access launch discount to US$19.79 / €19.79** valid until **August 14** (August 15 for the Japanese store). What else is worth knowing:

- The discount matches the Steam page's promise that Early Access would be "at a **slightly discounted price** compared to the full version" — a thank-you to early supporters. The full 1.0 release will carry a higher price.
- **No pre-purchase existed**, so there was no pre-order discount mechanism — you buy at or after launch.
- The **THQ Nordic Digital Showcase ran the same day** (see below) as the launch — the announcement cycle around it carried the final numbers.
- Always treat the Steam store page as the authoritative source for current regional pricing — discounts rotate.

## What's Actually in the Day-One Build

Everything below is confirmed from the Steam page, the "Behind the Early Access" developer diary (August 5), and official announcements:

### In at launch
- **Act One: five main quests and three side quests** — roughly **6–8 hours** of fully voiced quests.
- **Full-scope simultaneous turn-based combat** — movement, attacks, and interruptions resolve at the same time.
- **Exploration** — real-time movement through levels.
- **Stealth** — the series' first full stealth pillar: real-time sneaking, split-party positioning, ambush setups, and surprise attacks; firecrackers confirmed as distraction gadgets.
- **World map travel** — the open map structure the series is known for.
- **Character creation** — Captain Flynn's first name, appearance, and one of **four classes** (class names revealed at launch).
- **All 8 companions exist** — with personal stories and dedicated quest chains; five are officially named (Antje, Johan, Tora-Hime, Étienne Durand, Piet Ruisch), with the rest revealed at or around launch.
- **Full 2-player online co-op** — confirmed for day one: twin captains, shared dialogue checks, drop-in/drop-out.
- **Save compatibility with 1.0** — confirmed: your Early Access progress carries to the full release.

### Not in at launch
- **Village management and the Hatamoto title** — arrive during Early Access development, not day one.
- **Act Two** — arrives via a dedicated story update later in Early Access (~10 more hours).
- **Acts Three and Four** — part of the full 1.0 release (Q1 2027, ~60+ hours total).
- **Crafting** — official materials tie custom gear crafting to the full version; whether any crafting is in the EA build is still TBD.
- **Three of the eight companions** — still unnamed until the launch-week reveal cycle.

## Your First 24 Hours: The Plan

### Hour 0: Create Captain Flynn
- Update the game, then spend real time in character creation — the four classes decide your approach (stealth, diplomacy, or combat lean), and your choice shapes Act One.
- **Don't over-plan the build.** Class names and ability trees reveal at launch; the series' own history says your first run should be exploratory, not optimized.

### Hours 1–2: The Opening Beats
- Follow the main-quest line to learn the three-pillar systems (stealth, diplomacy, steel) in a safe context.
- **Test the stealth pillar early.** It's new to the series — firecrackers, split-party ambushes, and surprise-attack openers are the signature systems.
- If you're playing co-op: set up the twin-captain dynamic now, and **agree on romance targets** before the campaign gets going (competing is supported — and messy).

### Hours 2–6: Push the Side Quests
- The three side quests are where the companion content lives — recruit early and start loyalty chains.
- Save often and experiment with both East and West weaponry (flintlocks and katanas alike).
- **Expect tuning changes.** Early Access builds get balance patches quickly — treat every "meta" claim from day one as provisional.

### Hours 6–24: The World Map
- Once Act One's main path is stable, use the world map freedom to hit encounters in any order — the map structure rewards wandering.
- If you finish the 6–8 hours: that's the entire launch act. The rest arrives via updates — don't expect a 60-hour campaign on day one.

## Day-One Pitfalls

1. **Trusting pre-launch "build guides" and tier lists.** No verified class/ability data exists before the launch build is playable — any detailed build advice from before August 7 is speculation. Test and learn.
2. **Expecting the full 60 hours.** The launch act is 6–8 hours by design. That's the Early Access model, not a missing feature.
3. **Assuming crafting or village management is in.** Both are confirmed for later in Early Access — neither is day one.
4. **Skipping the requirements check.** The game is Unreal Engine 5-based; verify your PC against the [official specs](/guides/system-requirements-guide) before launch, not during.
5. **Ignoring the save-carry guarantee.** Your progress carries to 1.0 — so invest in your Act One character without worrying about a wipe.
