# Expeditions: Samurai — Johan Companion Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 5, 2026

Johan is one of the **five officially named companions** in Expeditions: Samurai — officially described as "a swordsman and blood relative of your late captain — just as arrogant as he is deadly with a blade." He is the crew's blade: the strongest pure damage option in melee, a fighting man carrying the family legacy of the captain you replaced, and the personality most likely to test your captaincy. This page collects everything known about him from official materials — details marked **based on pre-release information** will be confirmed or corrected after the Early Access launch on August 7, 2026.

## Who Is Johan?

- **Background:** a swordsman and **blood relative of your late captain** — the man whose command you inherited. That bloodline is the engine of his character: a legacy he fights under and, perhaps, must reconcile with *(based on pre-release information)*.
- **Confirmed role:** "A swordsman and blood relative of your late captain — just as arrogant as he is deadly with a blade" *(official)*.
- **Place in the crew:** the family name. Where you inherited the command, Johan inherited the reputation that came with it — and he expects both to be honored.
- **Role in the party:** frontline melee damage — the strongest pure damage option in melee.

## How to Recruit Him

**Recruitment details are TBD** — the specific quest and location are not confirmed. Based on pre-release information and the general companion structure:

- He is expected to be one of the **earliest recruits** — the walkthrough's Act One plan assumes all five named companions join early, and Johan is a core frontline option from the first hours.
- His recruitment likely involves **his family legacy** — a man whose blood ties to your late captain are his defining fact will care how you honor (or discard) that inheritance.
- Expect his recruitment to be **story-gated** on the world map rather than a simple hire — companion quest chains in Expeditions: Samurai are dedicated and personal.

## Personality and Values

- **Proud and arrogant** — "just as arrogant as he is deadly with a blade" is the official word; the beginner guide calls him "as arrogant as he is dangerous."
- **Legacy-driven** — the romance guide frames his story around "blood ties to your predecessor as captain": his arc is about legacy.
- **Ego with teeth** — "his ego needs handling with care," and the companions guide warns that provoking him gives the rivalry system real bite.
- **A warrior of the blade** — deadliness is his native language; expect him to measure people by their courage and their competence.

## Loyalty Triggers

Based on pre-release information and the official companion framing:

| What builds his loyalty | What damages it |
|-------------------------|-----------------|
| **Engaging with his legacy** — his quest chain revolves around the late captain and his bloodline | Mocking his lineage, his pride, or the captain you replaced |
| Letting him fight — frontline combat suits him best | Benching him through his personal quest arc |
| Choosing honorable, decisive captaincy | Choosing paths he reads as cowardice or disrespect |
| Completing his dedicated quest chain | Rivalry flags from repeated disrespect |

The general loyalty rules apply: every companion has a **dedicated quest chain**, and your choices in dialogue, combat, and captaincy decisions move him toward **friendship, rivalry, or romance**. Loyalty has mechanical weight — those who trust you stand with you when it matters.

## Best Party Role

Johan is the **frontline damage dealer** — the roster's strongest pure melee option:

| Slot | Role | Why Johan |
|------|------|-----------|
| Frontline | Melee damage | The official word: "just as arrogant as he is deadly with a blade" — expect his damage output to lead the roster |
| Steel pillar | Combat core | The [Best Builds Guide](/guides/best-builds-guide) slots Johan under Steel — the "overwhelm with force" pillar |
| Intimidation presence | Pressure for checks | The [Diplomacy Guide](/guides/diplomacy-guide) lists Johan alongside Piet as the muscle that gives intimidation checks teeth |

Practical notes:

- **Pair him with a talker.** Étienne handles persuasion; Johan supplies the threatening presence that makes the same conversations go your way — the diplomacy pillar's strongest combo.
- **Rotate him with Piet.** Both are steel-pillar frontline options — the [Companions Guide](/guides/companions-guide) treats them as interchangeable slots, and their personal quests both need airtime.
- **Handle his ego.** Provoke him and you feed the rivalry track; honor his blade and his lineage and you have the most reliable damage in the party.

## Romance Path

The official romance system treats every companion as a potential partner — "an ally becomes a friend — or something more." For Johan specifically:

- **His legacy is the door.** The romance guide flags his blood ties to your predecessor as the engine of his story — expect his romance to run through the moments where that legacy is at stake.
- **Respect over flattery.** A proud swordsman responds to being taken seriously, not to being charmed — and "his ego needs handling with care."
- **Romance competes with loyalty.** Friendship, rivalry, and romance are branches of the same relationship system — every choice feeds one of them.
