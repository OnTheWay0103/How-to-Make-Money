# Expeditions: Samurai — World Map Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Between the story beats of Expeditions: Samurai lies Japan itself. The world map is the game's hub: a free-roam structure where you pick destinations, trigger events, and decide when to push the story forward. The full release promises **45+ levels and 100+ events/encounters**, with quests doable in any order — and because the game never hard-locks you, the map is also your difficulty valve: if a fight is too hard, go elsewhere, level up, and come back.

## How the World Map Works

The confirmed structure:

- **A hub of destinations.** Pick your next location, travel there, and play through its level in real time.
- **Non-linear quests.** Quests are tackleable in any order — there is no single "correct" route through Act One.
- **Exploration in real time.** Movement through levels is real-time, which is what makes the stealth pillar work — you scout and position as you move.
- **Events and encounters everywhere.** The 100+ events/encounters of the full game are scattered across the map, and they're the engine of your resources: supplies, materials, and companion moments.
- **No hard locks.** Progress is never fully blocked — a failed or skipped encounter is a detour, not a wall.

## The Core Exploration Loop

| Stage | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| **Plan** | Read the map, weigh quest locations against your supplies and party state |
| **Travel** | Move to a destination (and watch for events along the way) |
| **Scout** | Explore the level in real time — stealth makes this a choice, not a chore |
| **Engage** | Resolve encounters via stealth, dialogue, or combat |
| **Reap** | Collect loot, materials, and event rewards; tend the party afterward |

## Level Types in Act One

The map's levels are built around the three pillars. Expect each region to favor one approach (level-type details are **TBD** — this is the expected shape from official framing):

| Level type | What it's for | Best pillar |
|------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Stealth grounds** | Patrols, locked doors, loot behind guards | Stealth |
| **Diplomatic hotspots** | Faction seats, negotiations, court scenes | Diplomacy |
| **Battlefields** | Set-piece fights, siege-ready strongholds | Combat |
| **Scavenge zones** | Materials and supplies, lightly defended | Any — bring the fast approach |

**Rotate destinations.** Different regions offer different encounter types — a session of pure stealth grounds will not keep you supplied the way a diplomatic city will.

## Events and Encounters: The Resource Engine

The 100+ events/encounters are how the map feeds you:

- **They generate supplies.** Food, medicine, and ammunition come from resolving events, not just buying them.
- **They generate materials.** Crafting components (for the full-release forge) are event rewards.
- **They generate companion moments.** Some events are companion-specific — another reason to rotate your active four and check in with everyone.
- **They're replayable resources.** Events reset or respawn on the map, making them a grind-free source of income between story chapters.

## Exploration Rewards: Why the Corners Matter

- **Loot beyond the main path.** The best gear in Expeditions games has historically lived off the beaten track.
- **Bonus dialogue and lore.** Optional encounters feed the diplomacy pillar with knowledge-based options later.
- **Sneak bonuses.** A fully cleared stealth section pays in both loot and the ambush opener advantage when combat does trigger.
