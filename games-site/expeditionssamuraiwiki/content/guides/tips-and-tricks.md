# Expeditions: Samurai — Tips & Tricks

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Beyond the basics, Expeditions: Samurai rewards the kind of thinking the studio has built its reputation on: **planning over luck, positioning over raw stats, and preparation over improvisation.** This guide collects the advanced tactics and pro moves — built from officially confirmed mechanics and the proven Expeditions series formula.

## The Setup Economy: Win Before Turn One

The single biggest skill ceiling in the game is the **setup phase** — everything you do before combat starts. The confirmed design quote says it directly: "everything is set up for your perfect victory."

- **Every fight worth doing deserves a scouting pass.** Know the enemy layout before you commit.
- **The surprise attack opener is your strongest ability.** A party positioned for ambush starts every fight with a free round of advantage.
- **Thin before you fight.** Silent takedowns on isolated sentries compound into overwhelming numbers advantage.
- **In co-op, choreograph the opener.** Firecracker pulls + double takedown = two free kills before the first turn.

## Resource Economy: The Real Main Quest

Expeditions games are resource games wearing RPG clothes. In Act One, supplies are the real difficulty knob:

- **Heal injuries early and often.** An untreated injury is a compounding debuff — and in a game where "keeping party members alive" is an explicit design pillar, injuries can end runs.
- **Steal and negotiate before buying.** Your diplomacy pillar earns discounts and freebies; your stealth pillar earns loot.
- **Craft, don't hoard.** Materials do nothing sitting in storage — crafted gear is the Act One endgame.
- **Tune the knobs honestly.** Combat and resource management difficulty are separate sliders. If resources stress you out, ease that slider and keep combat spicy — or vice versa.

## Pillar Synergy: Mixing Is Winning

Pure playthroughs work. Mixed playthroughs dominate:

- **Stealth + Combat:** Thin the enemy, then fight with numbers.
- **Stealth + Diplomacy:** Get leverage (hostage, document, position), then negotiate from strength.
- **Diplomacy + Combat:** Talk past the weak encounters, fight only the ones worth fighting.
- **The Ghost run:** skip fights entirely — "players may avoid combat entirely in many situations."

## Dialogue Checks: Who Talks Matters

Dialogue is confirmed to draw on character skills (and in co-op, **both characters' skills count**):

- **Send the right speaker.** Keep Étienne (the diplomatic superhero) in the active party for negotiation-heavy missions.
- **Build a backup talker.** If your main talker is injured or absent, a secondary social character saves the day.
- **In co-op, split the social load.** One twin invests in dialogue, the other in combat — the shared checks pass more often.

## Companion Management: Loyalty Is a Currency

"Loyalty is worth more than gold" is the official tagline — spend it wisely:

- **Rotate companions through missions** to progress all 8 personal quests.
- **Personal quests are time-sensitive to story chapters** in the series' tradition — don't leave them for the end of Act One.
- **Every companion can go rival.** One dismissive streak can lock off a story route — save before big companion conversations if you care about outcomes.
- **Romance is the deep loyalty track.** See the [Romance Guide](/guides/romance-guide) for the full system.

## Loadout Science

- **Two weapon families minimum.** "Leverage weapon skills to tackle diverse enemy types" is the game telling you matchups are real — carry a secondary family.
- **Weapon skills reward focus.** Mastery comes from use; your crafted gear should match your mastered families.
- **Mobility beats armor.** In the simultaneous combat system, positioning is king — don't armor-tank your whole party.
- **Grenades are a cheat code** for clustered enemies — craft them.

## World Map Mastery

- **45+ levels, 100+ events, quests in any order.** The map is your ally — you can never be hard-locked out of progress.
- **Events are supply engines.** Run easy events when your stores are low.
- **The map is faction territory.** Where you go and who you help there shapes your political standing heading into Sekigahara.

## Difficulty: Use the Knobs

The confirmed customization is one of the most underrated features: combat and resource management tune **independently**. Play the game you want:

- **Story-forward players:** ease resource pressure, keep combat at a level that engages you.
- **Tacticians:** crank combat, ease resources, focus purely on the battlefield.
- **Survivalists:** easy combat, brutal resources — the expedition fantasy.

## The Co-op Commandments

1. **Coordinate romance targets** before the campaign — or compete on purpose.
2. **Share the pillars.** Two players on the same pillar waste half the toolbox.
3. **Time the firecrackers.** The coordinated distraction is the best ability in co-op.
4. **Use twin captains for dialogue.** Double skill checks beat solo builds every time.

## Pro Moves Checklist

- [ ] Scout every fight before committing
- [ ] Takedown isolated sentries on approach
- [ ] Position the party before triggering combat
- [ ] Heal injuries between every mission
- [ ] Rotate companions to keep all quests alive
- [ ] Carry two weapon families, crafted for your mastered skills
- [ ] Use difficulty knobs to tune your ideal game
- [ ] In co-op: coordinate pillars, romance, and openers
