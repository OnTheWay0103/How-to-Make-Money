# Expeditions: Samurai — Beginner Guide (First Hours)

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Welcome to Sengoku-era Japan, Captain. **Expeditions: Samurai** — the party-based tactical CRPG from **Campfire Cabal** (the ex-Logic Artists team behind Expeditions: Rome, Viking, and Conquistador), published by **THQ Nordic** — launches into **Steam Early Access on August 7, 2026** with Act One (~8 hours of quests). This guide covers your first hours: the core loop, the three gameplay pillars, party basics, and the mistakes that slow down most new players.

## Your Starting Situation

You are **Captain Flynn** — surname fixed, first name and appearance yours to choose. In an alternate history, you are an English privateer on a mission from Queen Elizabeth whose battered Dutch frigate, **De Albatros**, arrives in Japan in 1600 AD, weeks before the **Battle of Sekigahara** decides the fate of the nation. You beat the real-life navigator William Adams to Japan and become the first Protestant to set foot there — a fact with real consequences for the story.

Your crew is small, injured, and short on supplies. What you do next is up to you: sneak, talk, or fight your way into the politics of a dying warlord era.

## The Three Pillars: Stealth, Diplomacy, and Steel

Every situation in Expeditions: Samurai can be approached through three pillars, and the best players combine them:

- **Stealth** — New to the series. Sneak in real time, split your party, set up ambush positions, distract guards with firecrackers and smoke bombs, and perform silent takedowns. A well-placed surprise attack can win a fight before the first turn is rolled.
- **Diplomacy** — Talk your way through. Skill checks in dialogue draw on your characters' abilities, and a diplomatic solution costs no lives and no supplies.
- **Steel** — Frontal assault into fast, simultaneous turn-based combat. Sometimes the fastest answer is the loudest one.

None of the pillars is a "wrong" path — the game is built so stealth, diplomacy, or violence can each carry you through most of the story. Mixing them, however, is almost always more effective than going all-in on one.

## The Core Loop

A typical session looks like this:

| Stage | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| **World map** | Pick your next destination from 45+ levels and 100+ events/encounters — quests can be tackled in any order |
| **Exploration** | Move in real time, scout with stealth, find loot and optional events |
| **Engagement** | Resolve encounters via stealth, dialogue, or turn-based combat |
| **Aftermath** | Tend injuries, craft gear, level up skills, manage resources |
| **Story beats** | Talk to companions, make choices that reshape the political landscape of Japan |

The world map is your hub. Because quests are non-linear, you are never hard-locked out of progress — if a fight is too hard, go elsewhere, level up, and come back stronger.

## Build Your Party Around All Three Pillars

You recruit **8 companions**, each with a personal story and a dedicated quest chain. Five have been named in official materials:

- **Antje** — a burglar who excels at stealth and opening locks
- **Johan** — a deadly swordsman, blood relative of your late captain, and as arrogant as he is dangerous
- **Tora-Hime** — an onna-musha (female samurai) seeking her place in history
- **Étienne Durand** — the crew's priest and "diplomatic superhero"
- **Piet Ruisch** — a brawler handy with both sword and pistol

Early on, make sure your party covers all three pillars: keep a stealth specialist (Antje), a talker (Étienne), and a frontline fighter (Johan or Piet) active. Companions you leave behind still contribute through their personal quests, but your active four determine what approaches are available in any given situation.

## Resource Management Matters More Than You Think

Expeditions games are famous for punishing careless leaders. Keep an eye on:

- **Injuries** — A wounded companion fights worse and can die permanently if ignored. Tend injuries early and often.
- **Supplies** — Food, medicine, and ammunition are finite. Stealing or negotiating them beats buying them.
- **Loadouts** — Experiment with the East-meets-West arsenal: flintlocks, cutlasses, and grenades alongside katanas, naginatas, and longbows. Craft custom gear to fit each companion's role.

## Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Rushing into combat without scouting.** A surprise attack opener is the single biggest swing in any fight. Scout first.
2. **Ignoring injuries.** An untreated injury compounds into a permanent debuff. Rest and heal between missions.
3. **Putting all points into one pillar.** A party that can only fight gets out-talked and out-sneaked.
4. **Skipping companion quests.** Every companion quest chain rewards loyalty, gear, and often unlocks better endings.
5. **Forgetting your difficulty knobs.** Combat and resource management difficulty are customizable separately — tune them to your skill level rather than suffering.

## What's Coming After Act One

Early Access ships with Act One (~8 hours), and the roadmap includes **two content updates** plus a **story update unlocking Act Two**, ahead of the full 1.0 release in early 2027 — four acts, ~60 hours total, plus village management (become a **Hatamoto** and rule a village of your own). Whatever you do in Act One sets the stage for all of it.
