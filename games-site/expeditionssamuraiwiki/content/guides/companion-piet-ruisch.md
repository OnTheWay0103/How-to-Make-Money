# Expeditions: Samurai — Piet Ruisch Companion Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 5, 2026

Piet Ruisch is one of the **five officially named companions** in Expeditions: Samurai — officially described as "a brawler handy with both a sword and pistol." He is the crew's flexible fighter: at home at close range with a blade and at medium range with a flintlock, a no-nonsense combatant whose arc is expected to run through loyalty and the rough world of privateers. This page collects everything known about him from official materials — details marked **based on pre-release information** will be confirmed or corrected after the Early Access launch on August 7, 2026.

## Who Is Piet Ruisch?

- **Background:** a brawler — and the full name and trade fit the privateer world of the *De Albatros*, where being "handy with both a sword and pistol" is a career description *(based on pre-release information)*.
- **Confirmed role:** "A brawler handy with both a sword and pistol" *(official)*.
- **Place in the crew:** the practical fighter — the romance guide's sketch is short and to the point: "sword and pistol, no-nonsense."
- **Role in the party:** melee / pistol hybrid — the flexible combatant, at home on either side of the engagement range.

## How to Recruit Him

**Recruitment details are TBD** — the specific quest and location are not confirmed. Based on pre-release information and the general companion structure:

- He is expected to be one of the **earliest recruits** — the walkthrough's Act One plan assumes all five named companions join early, and Piet is a frontline option from the first hours.
- His recruitment may well involve **the privateer world** — a man of the rough seas is expected to be won in a situation that matches his world.
- Expect his recruitment to be **story-gated** on the world map rather than a simple hire — companion quest chains in Expeditions: Samurai are dedicated and personal.

## Personality and Values

- **No-nonsense** — the romance guide's word for him; expect direct speech and direct solutions.
- **Loyal** — "his arc is likely built around loyalty and the rough world of privateers."
- **Practical** — a working fighting man who judges people by what they do.
- **A product of the privateer world** — rough edges expected; he knows how loyalty works among people who live by the blade and the gun.

## Best Party Role

Piet Ruisch is the **flexible combatant** — the only confirmed melee-and-ranged hybrid among the named five:

| Slot | Role | Why Piet Ruisch |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Hybrid fighter | Melee / pistol flex | "Handy with both a sword and pistol" — blade for close quarters, pistol for medium range |
| Frontline | Backup melee | Rotates with Johan for the steel-pillar slot; the [Companions Guide](/guides/companions-guide) treats them as interchangeable frontline options |

Practical notes:

- **Use him as your fill-in.** His hybrid nature makes him a good fit for any combat-heavy mission — the companions guide's own recommendation.
- **Rotate him with Johan.** Both are steel-pillar frontline options, and their personal quests both need airtime — alternate them to progress both stories.
- **Respect his range.** Close quarters ask for the blade, medium range for the pistol; expecting him to fight on one range alone wastes half his kit.
