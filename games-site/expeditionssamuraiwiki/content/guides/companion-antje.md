# Expeditions: Samurai — Antje Companion Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Antje is one of the **five officially named companions** in Expeditions: Samurai — officially described as "a skilled burglar who stealthily moves around while rendering pesky locks blocking the way forward obsolete." She is the crew's infiltrator: the key to the stealth pillar, the one who makes locked doors a non-issue, and a practical soul on a ship of idealists and fighters. This page collects everything known about her from official materials — details marked **based on pre-release information** will be confirmed or corrected after the Early Access launch on August 7, 2026.

## Who Is Antje?

- **Background:** a burglar — her craft is moving unseen and opening what others have locked. Her name and role suggest Dutch origins, consistent with the crew of the *De Albatros*, the privateer frigate at the heart of the story *(based on pre-release information)*.
- **Confirmed role:** "A skilled burglar who stealthily moves around while rendering pesky locks blocking the way forward obsolete" *(official)*.
- **Place in the crew:** the practical specialist. Where the captain dreams and the swordsmen fight, Antje gets the job done quietly and gets out.
- **Role in the party:** stealth, infiltration, and locks — the operational backbone of the stealth pillar.

## How to Recruit Her

**Recruitment details are TBD** — the specific quest and location are not confirmed. Based on pre-release information and the general companion structure:

- She is expected to be one of the **earliest recruits** — the beginner guide's party plan assumes you have her in the active four during the first hours of Act One.
- Her recruitment may well involve **her old profession** — a burglar's loyalty is rarely won through formal ceremony.
- Expect her recruitment to be **story-gated** on the world map rather than a simple hire — companion quest chains in Expeditions: Samurai are dedicated and personal.

## Personality and Values

The romance guide's character sketch is the best short summary:

- **Practical** — she is a working professional in a crew of dreamers; expect her to value results over rhetoric.
- **Stealthy** — comfortable in the shadows, both literally and socially.
- **Independent** — a burglar answers to herself; her trust is earned, not owed.
- **Haunted by a past** — "her past as a thief and what she's running from" are flagged as the engine of her quest chain *(based on pre-release information)*.

The general loyalty rules apply: every companion has a **dedicated quest chain**, and your choices in dialogue, combat, and captaincy decisions move her toward **friendship, rivalry, or romance**. Loyalty has mechanical weight — those who trust you stand with you when it matters.

## Best Party Role

Antje is the **infiltrator** — there is no other character on the roster confirmed for her role:

| Slot | Role | Why Antje |
|------|------|-----------|
| Infiltrator | Stealth, locks, scouting | Officially the burglar — locks "blocking the way forward" become obsolete in her hands |
| Ambush architect | Setup for surprise attacks | She scouts, places the party, and thins sentries — the pre-combat setup phase is where battles are won |

Practical notes:

- **Always bring her for stealth missions.** She is the pillar's specialist; the [Stealth Guide](/guides/stealth-guide) explains why that setup phase matters more than any combat skill.
- **Pair her with a talker.** A silent approach + a negotiation from strength is the strongest pillar combination in the game — Antje acquires the leverage, Étienne wields it.
- **She's not your frontline.** Keep her out of open brawls; a burglar's place is the flank and the shadows.

## Romance Path

The official romance system treats every companion as a potential partner — "an ally becomes a friend — or something more." For Antje specifically:

- **Her past is the door.** Her quest chain — "her past as a thief and what she's running from" — is the expected engine of her romance path *(based on pre-release information)*.
- **Earn her trust through competence.** An independent professional responds to being trusted with the work, not to being charmed.
- **Practicality over promises.** Expect her romance to reward actions, not speeches.
