# Expeditions: Samurai — Étienne Durand Companion Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 5, 2026

Étienne Durand is one of the **five officially named companions** in Expeditions: Samurai — officially described as "a priest who serves as the crew's diplomatic 'superhero,' leading negotiations and talking the party through difficult situations." He is the crew's voice: the talker who wins dialogue checks, the conscience of the ship, and the man whose faith and duty the story is expected to weigh against each other. This page collects everything known about him from official materials — details marked **based on pre-release information** will be confirmed or corrected after the Early Access launch on August 7, 2026.

## Who Is Étienne Durand?

- **Background:** a priest serving aboard the *De Albatros* — the privateer frigate at the heart of the story. A man of the cloth on a ship of privateers and mercenaries is a contrast the narrative is built to use *(based on pre-release information)*.
- **Confirmed role:** "A priest who serves as the crew's diplomatic 'superhero,' leading negotiations and talking the party through difficult situations" *(official)*.
- **Place in the crew:** the conscience and negotiator — the romance guide frames him as the voice that steadies the crew when talk is needed.
- **Role in the party:** diplomacy pillar — the lead negotiator, and the only confirmed talker among the named five.

## How to Recruit Him

**Recruitment details are TBD** — the specific quest and location are not confirmed. Based on pre-release information and the general companion structure:

- He is expected to be one of the **earliest recruits** — the walkthrough's Act One plan assumes a diplomat in the active four early so the diplomacy pillar is fully available.
- His recruitment likely involves **a difficult situation** — a man who "talks the party through difficult situations" is expected to join in one.
- Expect his recruitment to be **story-gated** on the world map rather than a simple hire — companion quest chains in Expeditions: Samurai are dedicated and personal.

## Personality and Values

- **Faithful** — he is a priest first; the romance guide expects faith to define his arc.
- **Dutiful** — the tension between faith and duty is officially expected to be the core of his story: "expect faith, duty, and the tension between them to define his arc."
- **A talker, not a fighter** — his value is in dialogue checks and negotiations, not the frontline.
- **The crew's conscience** — the man who argues for the harder, more humane path.

## Loyalty Triggers

Based on pre-release information and the official companion framing:

| What builds his loyalty | What damages it |
|-------------------------|-----------------|

The general loyalty rules apply: every companion has a **dedicated quest chain**, and your choices in dialogue, combat, and captaincy decisions move him toward **friendship, rivalry, or romance**. Loyalty has mechanical weight — those who trust you stand with you when it matters.

## Best Party Role

Étienne is the **talker** — there is no other character on the roster confirmed for his role:

| Slot | Role | Why Étienne Durand |
|------|------|--------------------|
| Talker | Diplomacy, negotiations | Officially the crew's "diplomatic superhero" — the [Diplomacy Guide](/guides/diplomacy-guide) names him the lead negotiator |
| Conscience | Story weight | His faith-and-duty arc is expected to anchor key story decisions and open peaceful routes |
| Intimidation support | Pressure for checks | Pair him with Piet or Johan — their presence gives his negotiations teeth |

Practical notes:

- **Keep him in the active party for negotiation-heavy missions.** The [Diplomacy Guide](/guides/diplomacy-guide) is explicit: the talker rides in your active four when talks matter.
- **Pair him with a burglar.** A silent approach plus a negotiation from strength is the strongest pillar combination in the game — Antje acquires the leverage, Étienne wields it.
- **He's not your frontline.** Protect the talker; a dead diplomat is a closed story route.

## Romance Path

The official romance system treats every companion as a potential partner — "an ally becomes a friend — or something more." For Étienne specifically:

- **Faith and duty are the door.** The romance guide expects the tension between them to define his arc — expect his romance to run through the moments where his vows and his loyalties pull him apart.
- **Win him through conviction.** A priest responds to principle over flattery — stand by the harder right thing and he will see it.
- **Romance competes with loyalty.** Friendship, rivalry, and romance are branches of the same relationship system — every choice feeds one of them.
