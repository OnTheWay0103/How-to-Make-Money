# Expeditions: Samurai — Difficulty Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Expeditions: Samurai is a game designed to be tuned. Press coverage has described **combat difficulty and resource-management difficulty as separately adjustable** — meaning you can have a brutal tactical battlefield with generous supplies, or gentle combat under a starvation economy, or anything in between. The exact settings available are **TBD until the launch build** on August 7, 2026, but the design intent is clear: the challenge you play should be your choice.

## What Is Confirmed

- **Separate combat and resource settings.** The two difficulty axes are independently adjustable — confirmed by press coverage, with the exact setting names and ranges unconfirmed.
- **A tuned game, not a fixed one.** The design philosophy across official materials is a game you can shape: tactical depth for those who want it, accessibility for those who don't.
- **The core systems stay the same.** Difficulty tuning doesn't remove the pillars — stealth, diplomacy, and steel remain viable at every setting.

## The Two Axes: What Each Setting Likely Changes

Exact definitions are **TBD**; what follows is the expected shape based on official framing and the series' DNA (Expeditions games have historically punished careless resource management harder than careless tactics):

### Combat Difficulty

| Likely changes | What it feels like |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Enemy stats (health, damage, armor) | Higher settings make every enemy a threat |
| Enemy numbers and composition | More frequent and better-formed ambushes |
| Enemy AI aggression | Smarter patrols, better interrupt usage, less wasted turns |
| Interruption pressure | Your actions get canceled more often — the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide) explains why that hurts |

### Resource Difficulty

| Likely changes | What it feels like |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Supply scarcity (food, medicine, ammunition) | Fewer supplies found, more consumed |
| Injury severity and healing cost | Wounds compound faster, treatment costs more |
| Economy pressure | Less money, worse prices, more incentive to steal or negotiate |
| Injury/mortality stakes | Higher resource pressure raises the cost of every careless fight |

## Recommended Settings

Since the exact ranges are TBD, these are starting points to tune from — treat them as philosophy, then adjust to taste:

| Player type | Combat | Resources | Why |
|-------------|--------|-----------|-----|
| **Total newcomer to the genre** | Low | Low | Learn the three pillars before the economy punishes you |
| **CRPG veteran, first time in this series** | Medium | Medium | The flagship Expeditions experience: real fights, real costs |
| **Expeditions series veteran** | High | High | The full survival pressure — this is the intended hardcore loop |
| **Tactical purist** | High | Low | Pure battlefield challenge with a generous supply line |
| **Survival masochist** | Low | High | Gentle fights, brutal economy — the true resource-management test |

**Tune mid-campaign, not just at start.** If a chapter is grinding you down, adjust rather than suffering — the game is explicitly designed to be tuned.

## Ironman and Hardcore Considerations

Permanent consequences are part of the Expeditions identity:

- **Companions can die.** Expeditions games are unforgiving — a dead companion is a lost story, not a reload screen. The [Companions Guide](/guides/companions-guide) warns to protect your party.
- **Injuries compound.** An untreated injury becomes a permanent debuff — resource difficulty directly controls how fast this bites.
- **A true ironman mode is TBD.** Whether the launch build includes a no-saves or permadeath mode has not been confirmed. Even without one, the series' culture is "play with your mistakes" — many veterans self-impose single-save runs.
- **High resource difficulty is the "soft" ironman.** Even with saves, a starvation economy makes every decision cost — it is the closest confirmed difficulty lever to the classic Expeditions experience.

## Settings and the Three Pillars

Your difficulty choices change how much each pillar matters:

- **Low combat, high resources:** stealth and diplomacy become optimization, not necessity — fine for story-first players.
- **High combat:** stealth's pre-combat setup (see the [Stealth Guide](/guides/stealth-guide)) becomes nearly mandatory — ambushes, not brawls, win high-difficulty fights.
- **High resources:** diplomacy stops being a bonus and becomes survival — negotiating for supplies beats buying them, and the [Diplomacy Guide](/guides/diplomacy-guide) covers how.

**Can I change settings mid-campaign?** Likely yes — the "tuned game" framing suggests settings are adjustable; exact UI behavior TBD at launch.

**Do settings affect co-op?** Co-op runs the full campaign with a partner; expect shared settings (details TBD).

**Is there a separate story mode?** Not confirmed — but the two-axis system covers most of what a story mode would offer: low combat and low resources.

**Does difficulty affect achievements or endings?** Unknown — check the launch build and this page.
