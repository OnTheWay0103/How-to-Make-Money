# Expeditions: Samurai — Tora-Hime Companion Guide

> Early Access Build (Act One) / Updated: August 1, 2026

Tora-Hime is one of the **five officially named companions** in Expeditions: Samurai — an **onna-musha** (a female samurai of the feudal era) officially described as "seeking her place in history." In a game set in the year 1600, at the climax of Japan's civil war, that search is a story with real stakes: a warrior culture that denies women the warrior's path, and a nation about to be decided by the sword. This page collects everything known about her from official materials — details marked **based on pre-release information** will be confirmed or corrected after the Early Access launch on August 7, 2026.

## Who Is Tora-Hime?

- **Background:** an onna-musha — the historical class of Japanese women trained in martial arts, often defending homes and castles while the men were at war.
- **Goal:** seeking her place in history. Her ambition is the core of her character — she wants to be remembered, in an era and a culture that does not expect women in the warrior lineage.
- **Connection to the setting:** her story ties directly into the game's Sekigahara-era politics — the era of 1600 was both the end of the Sengoku period and a hinge for how warrior identity was defined.
- **Role:** warrior and story bridge — she fights on the frontline and carries story weight between the crew and Japan.

## How to Recruit Her

**Recruitment details are TBD** — the specific quest and location are not confirmed. Based on pre-release information and the general companion structure:

- She is expected to be one of the **earlier recruits** — the five named companions are expected to join during the first half of Act One.
- Her recruitment likely involves a **demonstration of respect** — an onna-musha "seeking her place in history" is unlikely to join someone who dismisses her ambition.
- Expect her recruitment to be **story-gated** on the world map rather than a simple hire — companion quest chains in Expeditions: Samurai are dedicated and personal.

## Personality and Values

- **Ambitious** — her entire character arc is about earning a place that history has not granted her.
- **Proud** — expect rivalry flags if you treat her ambition as a joke or a side quest.
- **Honorable in the samurai mold** — loyalty, duty, and reputation likely matter deeply to her.

## Loyalty Triggers

Based on pre-release information and the official companion framing:

| What builds her loyalty | What damages it |
|-------------------------|-----------------|
| **Supporting her ambition** — the romance guide flags this as the obvious key to her heart | Mocking or dismissing her goals |
| Letting her fight — frontline combat suits her character | Benching her through her personal quest arc |
| Choosing honor and reputation in story decisions | Choosing cowardly or dishonorable paths |
| Completing her dedicated quest chain | Rivalry flags from repeated disrespect |

The general loyalty rules apply: every companion has a **dedicated quest chain**, and your choices in dialogue, combat, and captaincy decisions move her toward **friendship, rivalry, or romance**. Loyalty has mechanical weight — those who trust you stand with you when it matters.

## Best Party Role

Tora-Hime is a **frontline warrior** with story utility:

| Slot | Role | Why Tora-Hime |
|------|------|---------------|
| Frontline | Melee damage / tank | An onna-musha is a warrior first — expect polearm or blade specialization |
| Story anchor | Faction politics | Her quest ties into the Sekigahara-era politics and is likely to open unique story routes |

Practical notes:

- **Pair her with a talker.** Her story is driven by reputation — a diplomacy-capable party (Étienne's specialty) gives you the options that honor her path.
- **Rotate her in for faction-heavy chapters.** Her personal quest is expected to be the bridge to unique political content.
- **She's not your infiltrator.** Lockpicking and scouting are Antje's lane — see the [Companions Guide](/guides/companions-guide) for the full roster.

## Romance Path

The official romance system treats every companion as a potential partner — "an ally becomes a friend — or something more." For Tora-Hime specifically:

- **Support her ambition.** The romance guide's assessment: "Supporting her ambition is the obvious key to her heart." Her romance is expected to run through her quest chain and the moments where her place in history is at stake.
- **Respect over flattery.** A proud onna-musha is more likely to respond to respect for her warrior path than to generic charm.
- **Romance competes with loyalty.** Friendship, rivalry, and romance are branches of the same relationship system — every choice feeds one of them.
