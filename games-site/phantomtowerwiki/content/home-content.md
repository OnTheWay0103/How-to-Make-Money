## Why Phantom Tower?

- **8 Unique Classes** — Each with distinct combat styles and elemental affinities. Find the adventurer that matches your playstyle.
- **Elemental Combat System** — Master IGNIS, AQUA, TERRA, and VENTI. Swap mid-battle to trigger 16 elemental reactions including freeze, erosion, and shockwaves.
- **128 Weapons Across 6 Types** — Collect and forge an arsenal with the Soulbinder system. Permanently lock stats and push gear to level 99.
- **47 Tower Blessings** — Game-changing abilities hidden across floors. Stack blessings for run-defining synergies like Artemis Dive + Chain Quake.
- **Two Game Modes** — Climb Mode (100-floor campaign with progression carryover) and Survival Mode (permadeath infinite hordes).

## What is Phantom Tower?

Phantom Tower is a fast-paced roguelike action RPG developed and published by Horien Studio, released on July 13, 2026 on Steam Early Access. Adventurers gather at the foot of a mysterious tower that no one has ever reached the top of, climbing floor by floor in search of glory and power. Inside are forgotten relics, precious resources, boss-exclusive weapons, and 47 Tower Blessings that dramatically reshape every run.

The game combines frenetic hack-and-slash combat with deep roguelike progression. Two modes offer different challenges: Climb Mode (the main 100-floor campaign where you forge equipment and carry progress between runs) and Survival Mode (permadeath endless hordes). With 8 classes, 4 swappable elements triggering 16 reactions, 128 weapons, and 200+ gear pieces, Phantom Tower offers dozens of hours of build experimentation and replayability.
