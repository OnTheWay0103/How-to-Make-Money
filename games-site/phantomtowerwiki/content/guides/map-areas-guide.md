# Phantom Tower Map & Areas — What Is Inside the 100-Floor Tower

Phantom Tower is a game about a single building — so the "map" question is really: what is inside the tower, and what surrounds it? The official record is unusually specific about scale and deliberately silent about names. This guide covers every area the game officially confirms: the tower itself, the base outside it, the two modes' arenas, and the honest map of what nobody knows yet.

## The Tower: 100 Floors of Escalation (Confirmed)

The tower is the entire map of Climb Mode. Confirmed facts from official materials:

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Height | **100 floors** — the summit exists, and nobody has reached it |
| Premise | "Many have tried; none have reached the summit" |
| Difficulty curve | Floors are "increasingly dangerous" with tougher enemies and greater rewards |
| Bosses | Bosses appear during the climb and drop Boss Unique Weapons |
| Loot | Materials, resources, and equipment are collected on the way up |

There are no officially named regions, districts, or floor groups within the tower. Any guide naming zones ("The Ash Levels," "The Hollow Floors") is inventing them.

## The Floor Bands — What Each Stretch Tests

Based on the confirmed difficulty curve and the run structure, the climb breaks into three broad bands. These are descriptive, not official names:

**Early floors (1–30ish).** Fundamentals: dodge timing, element swapping, basic horde management. This is where your first materials come from and where the game teaches you how to extract. Push fast, gather everything, learn patterns.

**Mid floors (30–70ish).** Reaction play: dense packs that punish static builds, more varied enemy types from the 60+ roster, and preparation for boss encounters. This is where the element-swap system earns its keep — Freeze for control, Erode for crowd chains, Shockwaves for reach.

**Deep floors (70–100).** Sustained horde pressure plus the toughest boss encounters and the best loot. Runs that reach here are the ones that justify the whole economy: Boss Unique Weapons and the materials that only deep floors produce.

The exact floor thresholds for these bands are **not official** — the bands describe the confirmed difficulty curve, not published level design.

## Boss Floors — The Confirmed Danger Spots

Bosses guard the climb — "unique monsters and challenging bosses" is the official description — and Steam bosses drop **Boss Unique Weapons**, gear only bosses can drop.

- **Boss floor placement is unpublished.** The official record does not say how many bosses, on which floors, or whether bosses repeat.
- **Expect boss floors to be where runs end.** Plan your extraction decision *before* the boss floor, not after.
- **The rooms before a boss are part of the fight.** They preview the enemy mix the boss will summon or share the arena with.

## The Base Outside the Tower — The Between-Run Area

You do not live in the tower. The confirmed structure puts a preparation space between runs:

- **"Prepare at camp"** is how the game's own loop describes it — the official beginner materials frame the Blacksmith as the place you forge and upgrade gear between climbs.
- The **Blacksmith** is the confirmed crafting hub: "Forge your equipment, develop your combat style."
- The **Soulbinder** — the confirmed system for permanently locking stats onto gear — lives in this between-run layer.

So the practical map of the game has two areas: the tower (where you risk everything) and the camp (where you convert what you risked into permanent power). Whether the camp is a town, a single screen, or has other facilities is **not officially detailed** `[待确认]`.

## Survival Mode — The Other Arena

Survival Mode is the game's second confirmed area: an endless arena where you choose your equipment, face **infinite hordes** of monsters, and death is permanent.

- **It is a separate area from the tower climb.** You do not "reach" Survival Mode by climbing — it is a distinct mode with its own structure.
- **Loadout is chosen up front.** Your best-forged, Soulbound gear is what you test here — see the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide).
- **No floor structure is confirmed.** The mode is described as endless waves; whether it uses tower floors, arenas, or generated rooms is unpublished `[待确认]`.

## Hidden Areas & Secrets — The Honest Map

This is where most "map" guides lie. The confirmed record on hidden content:

- **Blessings are officially "hidden across its floors."** Thorough room search is confirmed value — the 47 Tower Blessings are placed in the tower somewhere, and finding them is the design intent.
- **Secret rooms, shrines, elite floors, and hidden encounters are NOT officially documented.** Claims about them in other guides have no official source.
- **The developer has said nothing about a map or fast travel.** Treat any guide describing minimaps, floor shortcuts, or teleporters as unverified.

The honest map: search every room because blessings are hidden, but do not plan your run around secret rooms that no official source confirms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many floors does Phantom Tower have?**
A: 100, per the official description. No one has reached the summit — that is the game's premise.

**Q: Are there different areas in the tower?**
A: The tower escalates in difficulty bands, but no areas have official names. Treat named regions in other guides as invented.

**Q: Where do I forge gear?**
A: At the Blacksmith — the confirmed crafting hub, accessed in the camp/preparation space between runs.

**Q: Are there hidden rooms?**
A: Tower Blessings are officially "hidden across its floors," so searching thoroughly is worth it. Specific secret rooms are not officially documented `[待确认]`.

**Q: Does Survival Mode have floors?**
A: The mode is described as endless hordes with chosen equipment and permanent death. Its internal structure is unpublished.
