# Phantom Tower FAQ — Honest Answers to the Questions Players Actually Ask

Phantom Tower is a fast-paced roguelike action RPG from Horien Studio, a two-person Italian team, and it launched into Steam Early Access on July 13, 2026. Because the game is new, a lot of the questions around it have no official answer yet — and too many guides fill those gaps with invented numbers. This FAQ answers the questions players actually ask, with every answer grounded in official sources and every unverified detail marked `[Unconfirmed]`.

## Buying, Platforms & the Demo

**What is Phantom Tower, and how much does it cost?**

Phantom Tower is a roguelike action RPG about climbing a mysterious 100-floor tower, fighting hordes of monsters, and bringing materials, resources, and equipment back out to power your next run. It costs **$12.99 (€12.99 / £11.75)** on Steam, per the official store listing, with a 24% launch discount for the first two weeks (July 13–26, 2026).

**Is there a demo?**

Yes — a free demo is available on Steam, per official press materials. The demo's exact content cap (which floors it lets you reach, which modes it includes) has not been published in verifiable official material — treat any specific "demo goes up to floor X" claim as `[Unconfirmed]`.

**Is Phantom Tower on console or mobile?**

The Steam Early Access version is PC-only as of August 2026. The game also exists on mobile as a free-to-play version with ads — the Steam version has no ads, and the two versions are separate products. Console releases have not been announced.

**Does the price go up?**

Yes. The developers have stated the price will increase when the game leaves Early Access, as a thank-you to early buyers.

## Gameplay & Mechanics

**How many classes are there?**

Eight classes launch with the game, per official press materials. Only four are officially named so far: the **Warrior** (the starting class), **Mage**, **Archer**, and **Assassin** (a "Shadow Specialist" using Shadow Clones). The other four exist but have not been named — treat any guide that names them as unreliable.

**How do elements work?**

Combat is built on four elements — **IGNIS (fire), AQUA (water), TERRA (earth), and VENTI (wind)** — which you can swap mid-fight to trigger **16 elemental reactions**. Only three reactions are officially named so far: **Freeze** (AQUA-based), **Erode** (TERRA-based, spreads between enemies), and **Shockwaves** (VENTI-based, chain across the arena). The full reaction table has not been published — any guide showing all 16 with exact effects is filling in the blanks itself.

**What are Tower Blessings?**

Blessings are run-shaping modifiers hidden across the tower's floors — **47 total** per the official count. Only two are named: **Artemis Dive** (evasion summons tracking arrows) and **Chain Quake** (shockwaves chain between enemies).

**What happens when I die?**

It depends on the mode. In **Climb Mode**, materials, resources, and equipment you collected *and brought back out* persist — what you were still carrying when you died is lost. In **Survival Mode**, death is permanent: no checkpoint, no carry-over.

**What is the best class?**

There is no official tier list, and no official class stats have been published. The honest answer based on the confirmed class descriptions: the **Warrior** is the intended starting point (the game teaches its mechanics around it), **Mage** and **Archer** are the strongest confirmed picks for ranged/reaction play, and the **Assassin** is powerful but demanding. See our [honest Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) for the full breakdown.

**How long is a run?**

Not officially published. Runs scale with how deep you push; Early Access players report that the compounding loop punishes greed — extract with a full haul instead of dying one floor deeper. Any guide quoting specific run lengths or "hours to complete the game" is giving you unverified numbers `[Unconfirmed]`.

## Progression & the Endgame

**What carries over between runs?**

Confirmed carry-over: materials, resources, and equipment you bring out of the tower (Climb Mode's official design), and **Soulbinder stats** — stats you permanently lock onto gear, developable up to level 99. Which specific currencies and materials persist has not been officially detailed `[Unconfirmed]`.

**How do I get better gear?**

Kill monsters and harvest materials (the game has **60+ enemy types**), then forge gear at the **Blacksmith** — the official line is "Forge your equipment, develop your combat style." Upgrade the pieces you keep, then lock the best stats with the Soulbinder. There are **128 weapons across six types** and **200+ equipment pieces** overall. Bosses on Steam also drop **Boss Unique Weapons** — gear only bosses can drop.

**Are there achievements?**

Yes — the Steam page lists **8 achievements**, with more planned during Early Access. Four have been revealed by name: **The Artisan**, **Sanctum conqueror**, **Billionaire**, and **Grand Achiever**. Their requirements have not been published.

**Is there multiplayer?**

Not yet. The game is single-player only at Early Access launch; the official roadmap says multiplayer co-op is **under investigation** — a possibility, not a commitment.

## Game Modes & the Roadmap

**What are the two game modes?**

**Climb Mode** is the main campaign: climb the 100-floor tower, collect materials, resources, and equipment to bring back outside, and compound progress run after run. **Survival Mode** is the endless endgame: you choose your equipment, face infinite hordes, and death is final.

**How long will Early Access last?**

The developers have stated they target a full **1.0 release in the first half of 2027**, with content updates along the way. Planned additions include new bosses, more playable characters, more gear, and localization for French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, and Korean (per the official Steam page).

**What is the endgame?**

Confirmed endgame targets: push Soulbinder stats toward the level-99 ceiling, farm bosses for Boss Unique Weapons, extend your personal best on the 100-floor climb, and test builds in Survival Mode's permadeath.

**Bottom line:** Phantom Tower's official record is precise about its systems (8 classes, 4 elements, 47 blessings, 128 weapons, 100 floors) and silent about the details (reaction tables, rarity, drop rates, boss patterns). Play the confirmed systems, and treat every unverified claim — including the ones in this FAQ marked `[Unconfirmed]` — as provisional until the official wiki catches up.
