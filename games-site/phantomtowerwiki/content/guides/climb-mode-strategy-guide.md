# Phantom Tower Climb Mode Strategy Guide — Floor-by-Floor Run Tactics

Climb Mode is Phantom Tower's main campaign: you enter a tower that is **100 floors tall**, fight its hordes, and — the part that defines the game — **bring materials, resources, and equipment back out**. Nothing else about the run matters as much as the extraction math: every run feeds the next, so the best players optimize what they take home, not just how far they get. This guide covers run structure, floor phases, and the decisions that make runs compound.

## The Confirmed Run Structure

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Tower height** | 100 floors per the official description; "many have tried; none have reached the summit" |
| **Run goal** | Collect materials, resources, and equipment to bring back outside |
| **Carry-over** | Collected resources persist between runs — the mode's defining feature |
| **Failure** | Death ends the run, but collected-and-extracted gains persist |
| **Boss floors** | Bosses appear during the climb and drop Boss Unique Weapons |

The practical shape: **materials → Blacksmith forging → gear → Soulbinder stats → a higher ceiling on the next climb**. Your first runs are scouting; your tenth run starts at a higher floor's difficulty because your gear is genuinely better.

## Floor Phases — What Each Stretch of the Tower Tests

The tower escalates in three broad bands (based on the climb structure and the game's confirmed difficulty curve):

| Phase | What It Tests | Run Strategy |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **Early floors** | Fundamentals — dodge timing, element swapping, horde basics | Speed over caution: push fast, gather everything, learn patterns |
| **Mid floors** | Reaction play — pack management, elemental crowd control | Prioritize resource nodes; plan your loadout for the boss stretch |
| **Deep floors** | Sustained horde pressure and boss encounters | Extract decisively once your haul outweighs your survival odds |

## The Core Run Decision: Push or Extract

Every Climb Mode run ends in one of two ways: you **die**, or you **leave with your haul**. Since extraction is what compounds, the key decision each run is *how deep to push before calling it*.

**Push another floor when:**
- Your current loadout is still strong (gear condition, blessing charges, consumables)
- The floor's resource value is high — materials you haven't banked before
- You have a Soulbinder-ready piece of gear that needs testing (see the [Soulbinder Guide](/guides/soulbinder-guide))

**Extract when:**
- You're low on recovery resources and the hordes are still scaling
- You've filled your inventory with materials/equipment you'd be sad to lose
- A boss floor is near and your build isn't boss-ready (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide))

Rule of thumb: **the run that ends with a full extraction is better than the run that dies one floor deeper.** Early Access players report the compounding loop punishes greed — losing a full inventory sets you back a whole run cycle.

## Preparing for Boss Floors

- **Check your element matchup.** Bosses are where the [Elements Guide](/guides/elements-guide)'s reaction play matters most — freeze windows and erode chains are your damage multipliers.
- **Bring a boss-focused loadout.** Boss Unique Weapons are the confirmed chase items; you can't farm them if you can't survive the encounter.
- **Swap gear before the fight.** The Blacksmith and Soulbinder exist so you can prepare; use them between floors, not during.

## Run Preparation Checklist

- [ ] **Review your permanent gear** — Soulbound stats carry across runs; enter every climb with your strongest locked gear equipped
- [ ] **Set a floor target** — decide your extraction floor before you enter, adjust it based on loot quality
- [ ] **Pack for your class** — see the [Classes Guide](/guides/classes-guide) for what each class needs
- [ ] **Plan your blessings** — a [Tower Blessings](/guides/blessings-guide) loadout built for the floors you're targeting (horde-heavy vs. boss-heavy)

## How Runs Compound — The Endgame Loop

1. **Climb and extract** — bank materials, resources, and equipment
2. **Forge** — turn materials into gear at the Blacksmith
3. **Soulbind** — lock the best stats onto gear permanently, pushing toward the level-99 ceiling
4. **Climb higher** — your new ceiling is the next run's starting difficulty
5. **Farm bosses** — Boss Unique Weapons are the confirmed endgame chase items

When a run stops contributing to this loop, it's a failed run — even if you didn't die. Climb with intent, extract with discipline, and the [Progression Guide](/guides/progression-guide)'s meta-layer does the rest.
