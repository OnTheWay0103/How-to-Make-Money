# Phantom Tower Items & Equipment Catalog — Verified Item Types and the Unverifiable Ones

Search for "Phantom Tower relic," "expansion fragments," or "guild license" and you will get results that look like item guides. Here is the honest problem: **none of those terms appear in any official Phantom Tower material we could verify.** This catalog lists every item type that is actually confirmed, then deals with each unverified search term directly — what it probably is, and why you should not trust guides that list it as a Phantom Tower item.

## The Verified Catalog (Official)

These item types are confirmed in official press materials and store descriptions:

| Item type | What it is | Confirmed detail |
|-----------|------------|------------------|
| **Weapons** | 128 weapons across **six weapon types** — the count is official, the full type list has not been published | Official press materials |
| **Equipment pieces** | 200+ total, including weapons, armor, and artifacts | Official press materials |
| **Boss Unique Weapons** | Weapons only bosses can drop — exclusive to the Steam version | Official press materials |
| **Materials** | Harvested from the game's **60+ enemy types**, used to forge gear at the Blacksmith | Official press materials |
| **Glyphs** | Unlockable hidden powers in the mobile version — a distinct system from gear | Mobile store description |
| **Forged gear** | Weapons, armor, and artifacts created at the Blacksmith from monster materials ("Forge your equipment, develop your combat style") | Official tagline |

One more mobile-only item type appeared in community-tracked update records: **Scythes**, a weapon type added in a mobile update, with Necromancer characters gaining unique bonuses when equipping them `[Community]` — not confirmed on the Steam version.

**What the catalog does not include:** rarity tiers (Common/Uncommon/Epic/Legendary), drop rates, or named legendary items. None of that has been officially published. Any guide showing you a rarity table with floor ranges is filling in the blanks itself.

## The Search Terms We Could Not Verify

Players search for these items constantly, so here is the direct verdict on each — based on the complete absence of official sources, not on silence:

| Search term | Verdict |
|-------------|---------|
| **Relic** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official material defines a "relic" item class in Phantom Tower. (The phrase "forgotten relics" appears in the game's flavor text about the tower, but no relic *item system* is published.) |
| **Expansion fragments** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official source. This term strongly resembles currencies from other games; likely a name-collision keyword rather than a Phantom Tower item. |
| **Guild license** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official source, and no guild system has been announced. |
| **Crystal** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official item of this name; already flagged as a low-confidence keyword in our research. |
| **Fractal** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official source; low-confidence keyword. |
| **Masamune** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official weapon of this name; the game's named content is currently limited to Boss Unique Weapons, Blessings (Artemis Dive, Chain Quake), and reactions (Freeze, Erode, Shockwaves). |
| **Code** | `[Unconfirmed]` — no official redeemable-code program has been announced. |

**Why this matters:** there are at least 25 unrelated products called "Phantom Tower" (a Minecraft mod, a Jump King quest, a NZXT case, a speaker, a shopping mall, and more). Item terms like "expansion fragments" and "guild license" are far more likely to belong to one of those products — or to a different game entirely — than to Horien Studio's roguelike. A guide that lists them as Phantom Tower items with effects and drop locations is inventing content.

## How the Real Items Work

The verified item loop, in one paragraph: you climb, kill monsters from the **60+ enemy types**, and collect **materials**; back at the Blacksmith you forge those materials into **weapons, armor, and artifacts** from the **200+ piece** pool; upgrades and the **Soulbinder** (permanent stat locking, developable to level 99) turn a chosen piece into a long-term investment; and on Steam, bosses add **Boss Unique Weapons** as the chase drops. Full system detail lives in the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide) and the [Soulbinder Guide](/guides/soulbinder-guide).

## What to Do When You See an Item List Elsewhere

1. **Check the name.** Does the list name weapons or items that Horien Studio has never published? If it names more than the two Blessings (Artemis Dive, Chain Quake), the three reactions (Freeze, Erode, Shockwaves), or the four classes (Warrior, Mage, Archer, Assassin), it is inventing.
2. **Check the numbers.** Official numbers are 128 weapons / 6 types / 200+ gear / 60+ enemies / 47 Blessings / 8 classes. A "complete item database" that does not match those bounds is not official.
3. **Check the source.** If the guide cannot point to a store page, press release, or the official wiki, treat every entry as speculation.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there a relic in Phantom Tower?**
A: No official material defines one `[Unconfirmed]`. "Relic" appears only as flavor text about the tower's contents, not as an item system.

**Q: What are expansion fragments?**
A: No official Phantom Tower source mentions them `[Unconfirmed]`. If you saw them in another game, they are probably from that game — not this one.

**Q: How many items does the game actually have?**
A: Officially: **128 weapons across six types** and **200+ equipment pieces** overall, plus materials from 60+ enemy types and, on Steam, Boss Unique Weapons.

**Q: Where can I find a full item list?**
A: Nowhere official yet. The developer has published counts, not a database. Until the official wiki publishes one, treat any complete item list as unverified.
