## What Press Coverage Actually Says

Early-access coverage is factual rather than scored, and the consistent through-line is genre comparison:

- **COGconnected** covered the Early Access debut, describing it as intense roguelike gameplay
- **RPG Gamers** framed the launch around the game's climb-and-extract loop
- **DroidGamers** covered the Android release, describing an action-heavy roguelike
- Multiple outlets compare it to **Hades-style action roguelikes** — the most common descriptive frame

One Chinese-language outlet's preview adds a useful data point and a conflict: it describes the game as a 2.5D single-player title **without controller support**, while SteamDB flags the demo as having **full controller support** — we could not resolve this conflict on the official store page, so controller support stays `[Unconfirmed]` (see our [Platforms Guide](/guides/platforms-purchase-guide)).

**What this adds up to:** press coverage describes a competent, action-dense roguelike with a strong core loop — not a breakout phenomenon, and not a broken launch. For a two-person studio's first title, that is the honest ceiling of what reviews say so far.

## What Players Are Actually Asking

The most common player questions right now, from search behavior and store Q&A patterns:

1. **"Is it worth buying in Early Access?"** The honest answer: the game is priced below its eventual 1.0 price, the demo is free, and the roadmap is public — if you like Hades-style roguelikes, the demo answers this better than any review.
2. **"Is it multiplayer?"** No — single-player at launch, co-op "under investigation" per the official roadmap.
3. **"Is the iOS version good?"** The iOS version is the free-to-play mobile product (ads + IAPs), a *separate* game from the Steam version — iOS reviews you find are for that version, not the PC game. See the [Platforms Guide](/guides/platforms-purchase-guide).
4. **"How is the early game?"** The [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) covers the first floors; player-reported early-game experience matches the official loop — short climbs, material extraction, compounding runs.
