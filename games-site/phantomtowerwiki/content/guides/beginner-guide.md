# Phantom Tower Beginner Guide — Essential Tips for Getting Started

Phantom Tower drops you at the base of a mysterious 100-floor tower with nothing but your chosen adventurer and whatever weapons you can scavenge. As a fast-paced roguelike action RPG from Horien Studio, every climb is a fresh test of reflexes, strategy, and elemental mastery. Whether you are dying on floor 3 or wondering what Tower Blessings actually do, this Phantom Tower beginner guide covers everything you need to survive your first hours.

## Best Starting Class for Beginners

Phantom Tower features 8 unique classes at launch. For new players, your first class choice matters more than anything else.

**The Warrior is the best class for beginners.** Here is why:

- **High base HP and defense** — You can tank mistakes that would kill lighter classes
- **Straightforward combos** — No complex elemental rotations; learn the basics first
- **Balanced stats** — Warrior works well with all weapon types, letting you experiment
- **Forgiving playstyle** — Blocking and heavy armor give you room to learn enemy patterns

**Alternative beginner-friendly classes:**
- **Guardian** — If you prefer a defensive, tank-heavy playstyle with blessing stacking
- **Ranger** — If you prefer ranged combat and kiting safely from a distance

**Classes to avoid as a beginner:**
- **Elementalist** — Requires knowing all 16 elemental reactions to be effective
- **Rogue** — Fragile melee class that demands perfect dodge timing

## Understanding the Core Loop

Every Phantom Tower run follows this rhythm:

1. **Prepare at camp** — Forge weapons, upgrade gear, choose your loadout
2. **Enter the tower** — Start from your highest unlocked floor or a new descent
3. **Clear rooms** — Defeat enemies, collect loot, discover Tower Blessings
4. **Fight the floor boss** — Each floor ends with a boss encounter
5. **Return or continue** — Cash out your loot or push deeper for better rewards

The key tension: the deeper you go, the better the rewards — but the more you risk losing if you die.

## Elemental Combat Basics

Phantom Tower's combat revolves around four elements you can swap between mid-fight:

| Element | Color | Specialty | Best For |
|---------|-------|-----------|----------|
| **IGNIS** (Fire) | Red | Damage over time, AoE burns | Clearing groups, boss DPS |
| **AQUA** (Water) | Blue | Freezing, control, healing | Crowd control, survivability |
| **TERRA** (Earth) | Green | Defense, shields, erosion | Tanking, debuff spreading |
| **VENTI** (Wind) | Yellow | Speed, mobility, chain attacks | Kiting, multi-target damage |

**Elemental Reactions — The Core Mechanic:**

Swapping elements mid-attack triggers powerful reactions. The most important ones for beginners:

- **IGNIS + AQUA = Steam Burst** — AoE damage + blind nearby enemies
- **AQUA + VENTI = Frost Storm** — Freeze + slow in a wide area
- **TERRA + IGNIS = Magma Eruption** — High single-target damage
- **VENTI + IGNIS = Firestorm** — Chain damage between enemies

**Beginner tip:** Start with IGNIS for damage, swap to AQUA when enemies close in. This two-element rotation will carry you through the first 20 floors.

## Survival Tips for Your First 10 Floors

**1. Do not be greedy.** The number one cause of early deaths is trying to squeeze in one extra attack. Learn enemy attack patterns and only commit when you have a clear opening.

**2. Check every room corner.** Tower Blessings are hidden throughout rooms. Even if you clear a room quickly, take 5 seconds to check corners and alcoves for hidden blessings.

**3. Prioritize weapon upgrades.** A level 5 common weapon outperforms a level 1 rare weapon. Visit the Blacksmith between floors and upgrade your best weapon consistently.

**4. Learn enemy tells.** Every enemy in Phantom Tower has a visual "tell" before their attack. Skeleton archers draw their bowstring fully. Slimes pulse before exploding. Watch for these tells and dodge accordingly.

**5. Save materials for forging.** Do not waste rare materials on early-floor gear. Save mythril, elemental crystals, and boss drops for forging weapons after floor 20.

**6. Use the environment.** Many rooms have explosive barrels, crumbling pillars, and spike traps. Bait enemies into these hazards — environmental damage scales independently of your weapon level.

## Death and Meta-Progression

Death in Phantom Tower works differently depending on your mode:

**Climb Mode (Main Mode):**
- You keep all forged equipment and Soulbinder upgrades
- You lose items and materials collected during that specific run
- You restart from your checkpoint floor (every 10 floors)

**Survival Mode (Permadeath):**
- Death is final — you lose everything
- Designed for experienced players testing optimized builds
- Great for farming rare materials if you can survive long enough

**What persists permanently across all runs:**
- Unlocked classes
- Soulbinder stats on gear
- Discovered Tower Blessings (unlocked for future runs)
- Steam achievements

## Your First Boss Strategy

The Floor 10 boss (The Warden) is your first major skill check. Here is how to beat it:

**Warden Attack Patterns:**
1. **Slam** — Raises weapon overhead, slams after 1.5s. Dodge sideways.
2. **Charge** — Runs toward you in a straight line. Dodge perpendicular.
3. **Summon** — At 50% HP, summons 3 skeleton adds. Kill them quickly.
4. **Enrage** — At 25% HP, attacks 30% faster. Save your element swap for this phase.

**Recommended strategy:**
- Use IGNIS element for damage in phase 1
- When adds spawn, swap to VENTI and use AoE to clear them
- During enrage, swap to AQUA and freeze the boss between attack windows
- Bring at least 3 health potions; you will need them

**If you wipe on Warden:** This is expected. Focus on upgrading your weapon to level 8+ and collecting defensive Tower Blessings before trying again.

## Tower Blessings: What to Look For

Tower Blessings are game-changing abilities you discover on each floor. As a beginner, prioritize these blessing types:

1. **Defensive blessings** — "Stone Skin" (damage reduction), "Vitality Boon" (max HP up)
2. **Healing blessings** — "Life Leech" (heal on kill), "Regeneration" (passive HP regen)
3. **Mobility blessings** — "Wind Step" (dodge distance up), "Quick Recovery" (shorter dodge cooldown)

Avoid offensive blessings early on — they scale with gear that you do not have yet. Survivability wins runs.

- [ ] Save rare materials for post-floor-20 forging
- [ ] Practice Warden's attack patterns on Floor 10
- [ ] Stack defensive blessings for your first deep run
