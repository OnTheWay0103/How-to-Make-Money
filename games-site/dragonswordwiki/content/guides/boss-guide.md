## DragonSword: Awakening Boss Guide

DragonSword: Awakening has a substantial boss roster across story chapters, open-world World Bosses, and the endgame Origin Dragons. This guide lists the bosses whose existence is **verified** — from official material and a complete full-bosses gameplay compilation — and is honest about which attack patterns are documented versus unconfirmed.

**Accuracy note:** earlier drafts of this guide published detailed attack patterns, break tips, and World Boss names. Most of that was not verified and has been removed. This guide now only states what the community has actually documented.

### General Strategy Principles (Confirmed Mechanics)

Rather than inventing per-boss attack patterns, here is what applies to every boss from the confirmed combat system:

1. **Stack ailments before switching.** Boss fights revolve around the Apply → Extend → Finish loop — build your team's ailment stacks, then switch for Signal Skill triggers (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)).
2. **Break Super Armor.** Elites and bosses have a white Super Armor bar; breaking it opens finisher prompts and staggers the boss. Castella (Break + Stun) is the confirmed breaker.
3. **Use the Bleed Chain team.** Theresia + Lute + Castella is the community-standard boss team — Bleed sustains damage, Stun locks the boss, Break opens finisher windows (see the [Team Compositions Guide](/guides/team-compositions)).
4. **Practice via Hunts.** Re-fighting story bosses at higher difficulty is a confirmed endgame activity and the best way to learn patterns without story consequences.

### The Grassland Lesser Dragon

The best-documented mid-story fight — a full-bosses video covers it and players frequently cite it as the first difficulty spike.

- It is a **dragon fight** — expect fire-based attacks and flight
- Community reports describe leg-breaking as the key mechanic: breaking the dragon's legs topples it for a damage window. The exact timing and damage multipliers are **unconfirmed**
- The [Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough) covers its story context

### Barduk

A corrupted dragon rider who is the most complex confirmed story boss. Player footage shows a **human phase and a dragon transformation phase**. Detailed pattern breakdowns are still being documented — marked **TBD**.

### Ursula — Final Boss

The confirmed final boss of the main story, described by players as a **three-phase fight**. Community reports describe escalating arena-wide mechanics in the later phases, but exact patterns are **unconfirmed**.

## World Bosses

World Bosses are a **confirmed endgame system** — **9 open-world bosses per the official description**, on a rotation, farmed for materials and gear. What is not confirmed:

- The names of individual World Bosses. **Names like Pakirus, Gallore, or Andras that appear in other guides are not verified** and are not repeated here
- Their locations and spawn timers
- Whether they rotate daily or on another schedule

**Farming pattern (community-confirmed):** check which World Boss is active, form a co-op party, repeat kills. Exact drop tables are TBD. See the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide).

**Origin Dragons** are a confirmed endgame concept — the "origin dragon" appears in the full-bosses compilation, and players describe them as the pinnacle endgame challenge. Unconfirmed details:

## Boss-Related Systems (Confirmed)

- **Hunts** — re-fight story bosses at higher difficulty tiers; a confirmed endgame activity and material source
- **Co-op raids** — 3-player raid encounters extend the boss content into multiplayer (see the [Raids Guide](/guides/dungeon-raids))
- **Super Armor finishers** — breaking a boss's Super Armor opens special finisher prompts (cinematic grabs, heavy finishers)

## Frequently Asked Questions

The full count is unconfirmed. Verified story bosses number six, plus World Bosses and Origin Dragons whose exact rosters are TBD.

Ursula (final boss) is the community consensus, with Origin Dragons as the endgame escalation — but detailed difficulty comparisons are still being written.
