## DragonSword: Awakening Boss Guide

DragonSword: Awakening features a wide variety of boss encounters — story bosses that advance the narrative, World Bosses on daily rotation, and the ultimate Origin Dragons. This **boss guide** covers every boss, their attack patterns, and the best strategies to defeat them.

## Story Bosses (In Order)

### Orc Warrior (Prologue)

Your first boss fight. The Orc Warrior is a tutorial encounter designed to teach basic combat.

- **Attack patterns**: Slow overhead slam, wide sweep, charge attack
- **Strategy**: Dodge the charge (telegraphed by a red line), punish with 3-hit combos
- **Break tip**: Two E skills break its Super Armor
- **Team**: Lute only (solo fight)

### Mysterious Swordsman (Chapter 1)

A humanoid duelist who tests your fundamentals.

- **Attack patterns**: Quick slashes, parry stance, lunging thrust
- **Strategy**: Do not spam attacks — he parries and counters. Wait for his lunge, dodge, then punish
- **Break tip**: Bait the lunge, dodge sideways, counter with heavy attacks
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Castella + Aria

### Furcas (Chapter 1)

A demonic knight with fire attacks.

- **Attack patterns**: Flame slash, fire pillar AoE, charge with burning trail
- **Strategy**: Side-step the charge. When he raises his sword, back away — a fire pillar erupts at your feet
- **Break tip**: Frost status effects from Kalien (if unlocked) double break damage
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Castella + Aria

### Vanak (Chapter 2)

A giant armored beast weak to aerial attacks.

- **Attack patterns**: Ground pound (AoE), tail sweep, charging headbutt
- **Strategy**: Stay behind him at all times. His front attacks are devastating, but his rear is vulnerable
- **Break tip**: Launch skills work wonders here — Reina is MVP
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Reina + Aria

### Grassland Lesser Dragon (Chapter 2)

The first major difficulty spike. This dragon tests everything you have learned.

- **Attack patterns**:
  - **Fire breath** — Wide cone, long wind-up (run to the side)
  - **Tail sweep** — 180-degree behind attack (jump over it)
  - **Wing gust** — Knocks you back (dodge through it)
  - **Aerial fire barrage** — Below 50% HP, flies up and rains fire (run in a circle)
- **Strategy**: Break its legs (front or back) to topple it. A toppled dragon takes double damage for 8 seconds
- **Break tip**: Break one leg first, then focus the other. Two broken legs = instant topple
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Theresia + Aria

### Barduk (Chapter 3)

A corrupted dragon rider who transforms into a dragon mid-fight. This is the most complex story boss.

- **Phase 1 (Human)**: Fast sword combos, fire slashes, summon minions
  - Strategy: Stay aggressive, break his armor quickly before he summons too many minions
- **Phase 2 (Dragon)**: Massive AoE attacks, flight phases, fire breath
  - Strategy: Use the mounting mechanic after breaking Super Armor to ground him
- **Phase 3 (Berserk)**: All attacks faster, new fire tornado attack
  - Strategy: Save all Signal Skills for this phase. Burn him down before the fire tornado fills the arena
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Theresia/Kalsion + Aria

### Lich (Chapter 3)

An undead mage with powerful ranged magic.

- **Attack patterns**: Homing skulls, AoE curse circles, summon undead, teleport
- **Strategy**: Kill the summoned undead immediately — they empower the Lich. Save dodges for the homing skulls
- **Break tip**: Physical damage heroes break the Lich faster than magic users
- **Recommended team**: Kalsion + Theresia + Charlotte

### Ogre Slaughterer (Chapter 3)

A massive berserker ogre with high damage but slow attacks.

- **Attack patterns**: Ground slam (AoE), charge, grab attack (instant kill if at low HP)
- **Strategy**: All attacks are heavily telegraphed. Patience is key — dodge, punish 2-3 hits, repeat
- **Break tip**: Constant Super Armor, requires sustained pressure to break
- **Recommended team**: Castella (tank) + Theresia (DPS) + Aria (heals)

### Gauron (Chapter 3)

A fallen hero corrupted by dark energy. Fast, aggressive, and unforgiving.

- **Attack patterns**: Dark energy blasts, teleport slashes, shadow clone attack
- **Strategy**: When he creates shadow clones, hit the real one (slightly darker aura). Perfect dodges are crucial here
- **Break tip**: Parry his teleport slash for a massive break window
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Ornette + Aria

### Ursula (Final Boss — Chapter 3)

The final story boss. A goddess of destruction with three phases.

- **Phase 1**: Giant energy swords, light beams, gravity wells
- **Phase 2**: Summons angelic minions, arena-wide light attacks
- **Phase 3**: Transforms into a winged colossus — screen-wide attacks, limited safe zones

- **Strategy**: Memorize the safe zones in Phase 3 (indicated by glowing blue platforms). Save all resources for Phase 3
- **Break tip**: Only breakable during specific attack windows. Watch for the tell — she glows red before each vulnerable moment
- **Recommended team**: Max-level team with best gear. Bring Kalsion for survival, Theresia for damage, Aria for healing

## World Bosses

World Bosses appear on a daily rotation. You need Mercenary Rank C or higher to challenge them.

| Boss | Location | Weakness | Strategy |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Pakirus** | Whispering Woods | Frost | A giant turtle. Attack the shell until it cracks, then hit the exposed weak point |
| **Gallore** | Frostpeak Mountains | Fire | Ice golem. Break its arms to disable its slam attacks |
| **Horg** | Burning Steppes | Frost | Fire giant. Dodge his lava pools and attack from behind |
| **Karon** | Skyspire Highlands | Bleed | Aerial beast. Ground it with stuns, then go all out |
| **Brack** | Sunken Ruins | Shock | Water serpent. Stay mobile, avoid the whirlpool traps |
| **Octavia** | Coral Reef | Poison | Giant kraken. Destroy tentacles first, then attack the head |
| **Lavanis** | Volcanic Core | Frost | Lava elemental. Bait its charge into walls to stun it |
| **Barpedin** | Sky Fortress | Bleed | Flying fortress construct. Destroy the orbs powering its shields |
| **Andras** | Abyss Gate | All | Endgame World Boss. Requires full S-tier team and max gear |

### World Boss Tips

- **Check daily rotation** — World Bosses cycle every 24 hours. Plan your farming around the boss you need
- **Co-op recommended** — Use the in-game Party Finder for World Bosses. Solo is possible but time-consuming
- **Break parts for loot** — Breaking specific body parts (wings, horns, shells) drops exclusive crafting materials

## Origin Dragons

The ultimate endgame challenge. Three Origin Dragons are available at launch.

| Dragon | Level Req | Rewards | Strategy |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Vaeldris** | 40 | Origin relic set | Fire dragon. Requires fire resistance gear and precise positioning |
| **Frostmaw** | 45 | Frost relic set | Ice dragon. Stay moving — standing still for 3 seconds freezes you |
| **Void** | 50 | Void relic set | Dark dragon. Phase-based fight with DPS checks. Highest difficulty |

All Origin Dragons require a full party of 3 and Mercenary Rank A or higher. They are the hardest content in the game at launch.
