## Cerese Guide — Frost Crowd Control & the Magic Knight Order Commander

Cerese is the **commander of the Orbis Magic Knight Order** — the third member of the **Orbis Kingdom trio** (with Reina and Kalsion) introduced in the June 2026 hero teaser. In gameplay terms she is the roster's **frost crowd-control specialist**: community documentation describes a kit built around ice that controls enemy movement and turns encounters around. Community tier lists place her in **B-tier** — strong in frost teams, narrower outside them.

> **Accuracy note:** Cerese's identity and remodel are officially confirmed (the developer AMA). Her kit details are **community-documented** — HOUND13 has not published an official skill sheet. The community tier lists that detail her kit are the source for the skill names below; treat them accordingly.

## Who She Is (Confirmed)

- **Commander of the Orbis Magic Knight Order** — "Ceris, Captain of the Orbis Magic Order of Knights" per the HOUND13 AMA; the wiki roster notes list her as the order's commander.
- **Name spelling:** "Cerese" (community and this wiki's roster notes) vs. "Ceris" (the AMA's transliteration) — same character.
- **The remodel (confirmed):** HOUND13 redesigned her ahead of launch — her original **bun hairstyle was changed to a bob**, and the **bulky hood of her cloak was removed**. The AMA frames this as a visual redesign; no kit changes were mentioned.

## Community-Documented Kit

Per community documentation, Cerese's kit centers on **frost-based crowd control**:

| Skill | Community-Documented Effect |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **Ice Storm** | The kit highlight — controls enemy movement and can turn encounters around |
| **Ice Turn** | Frost-type attack, associated with the Frost ailment and Airborne states |
| **Frozen Spike** | Frost-type attack, associated with the Frost ailment and Airborne states |

**The playstyle:** pack enemies together with ice, then let teammates punish the clump. Her value is **encounter control** — freezing and grouping turns deadly swarms into a single, safe target. The tradeoff, per community tier lists, is that she "doesn't slot naturally into as many compositions as the characters above her" — she wants a frost-themed team.

### How She Fits the Combat Loop

Cerese plays the **setup role** in the Apply → Extend → Finish loop (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)):

1. **Apply** — Ice Storm groups and controls enemy movement; frost stacks build
2. **Extend** — frozen enemies stay helpless while teammates set up
3. **Finish** — switch out under the controlled state to trigger a **Signal Skill** on the packed group

## Team Synergy (Community-Documented)

### The Frost Team

**Cerese + Reina + Othello** — the community-documented rotation:

1. **Cerese** groups enemies together with icy storms
2. **Reina** juggles the packed group with Airborne (see the [Reina Guide](/guides/reina-guide))
3. **Othello** finishes survivors — his Ice Spears accumulate Cold stacks for powerful follow-ups

**Caveat:** Othello's playable status at launch is disputed — this wiki's roster notes flag him as pre-launch material with an unfinished model. The frost team is community-suggested; verify his availability in your build before planning around him.

### Pairing Notes

- **With Reina:** the two teaser sisters-in-arms share the frost-team identity — her grouping sets up Reina's best-case scenario (a packed, helpless clump).
- **With any airborne hero:** frost control + any launch keeps groups juggled for the whole chain.
