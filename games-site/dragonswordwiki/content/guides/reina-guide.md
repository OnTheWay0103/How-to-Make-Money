## Reina Guide — Airborne DPS & the Royal Guard Captain

Reina is the **Captain of the Royal Guard** of the Kingdom of Orbis — one of three heroes introduced in the **Orbis Kingdom Hero Teaser** (June 2026) alongside Cerese and Kalsion. In gameplay terms, she is the roster's defining **airborne combat specialist**: a highly mobile melee fighter built around launching enemies and finishing them in the air. Community tier lists place her in **S-tier**.

> **Accuracy note:** HOUND13 has not published an official skill sheet for Reina. Her identity is officially confirmed (the Orbis teaser and the developer AMA), while her kit details below are **community-documented** — consistent across the community tier lists and team guides that cover her. Treat the skill names as community-documented rather than official, and everything gear-related as TBD.

## Who She Is (Confirmed)

- **Captain of the Royal Guard** — confirmed in HOUND13's own AMA coverage (the teaser introduces "Reina, Captain of the Royal Guard"). The wiki roster notes also list her as a captain of the Orbis Royal Castle's 8th Knight Order.
- **Orbis Kingdom trio:** she debuted alongside **Cerese** (Magic Knight Order commander) and **Kalsion** (the king) in the Kingdom of Orbis teaser.
- **Role in lore:** the king's first line of defense — fitting for a hero whose gameplay is all about taking the fight into the air.

## Community-Documented Kit

Per community documentation (tier lists and team guides), Reina's kit centers on **Airborne**, the status ailment that launches enemies:

| Skill | Community-Documented Effect |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **Rising Cutter** | Deals damage and inflicts **Airborne** — the launch |
| **Wind Slayer** | Deals damage and inflicts **Airborne** — a second launcher |
| **Tempest** | A powerful move — best used against a strong enemy directly in front of her |

**The playstyle:** she chains attacks while enemies are airborne, enabling extended offensive sequences and juggling. Her potential depends heavily on learning how her attacks interact — community tier lists consistently rate her as **difficult to master and not recommended for newcomers**, and best suited for airborne teams, combos, and mobility-focused play.

### How She Fits the Combat Loop

Reina slots into DragonSword's **Apply → Extend → Finish** loop (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)) as the **Apply and Extend** engine:

1. **Apply** — Rising Cutter or Wind Slayer launches the target (Airborne status)
2. **Extend** — follow up with Tempest and air attacks while the enemy is helpless
3. **Finish** — switch to a teammate under the airborne state to trigger their **Signal Skill** — the game's biggest single-hit damage source

Because Airborne leaves enemies unable to defend, she is a natural setup hero for the whole party.

## Team Synergies (Community-Documented)

### The Airborne Team

**Reina + Lute + Castella** (Aria can substitute for Lute) — the community-documented rotation:

1. **Reina** launches the enemy (Rising Cutter or Wind Slayer)
2. **Reina** follows up with Tempest
3. **Lute** extends the juggle (and heals the party when needed)
4. **Castella** slams the enemy back down and breaks Super Armor for the finisher

### The Frost Team

**Cerese + Reina + Othello** — Cerese's icy storms group enemies together, Reina's Airborne application juggles the packed group, and the frost damage dealer finishes survivors. *Caveat:* Othello's playable status at launch is disputed in the wiki roster notes (flagged as pre-launch material) — treat this team as community-suggested, not verified.

### What to Avoid

- **Newcomer parties.** Her skill ceiling means a team built around her underperforms until the pilot is practiced — community tier lists say this plainly.
- **Pure ground teams.** A party with no airborne extenders wastes her launches; she wants at least one hero who can follow up in the air.
