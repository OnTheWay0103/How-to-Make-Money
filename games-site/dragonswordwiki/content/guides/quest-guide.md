# Quest Guide — Main Quests, Side Quests & Hidden Quests

Dragonsword features **80+ quests** spanning a main storyline of 12 chapters, 30+ side quests, and a handful of hidden quest chains that reward legendary items and exclusive achievements. This guide covers every quest type, priority order, and the rewards that make certain quests essential.

---

## Quest Types Overview

| Type | Count | Typical Rewards | Time per Quest |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main Story** | 60 (12 chapters × 5 quests) | Skill points, rare gear, story progression | 30–60 min |
| **Side Quest** | 35+ | Gold, XP, consumables, reputation | 15–30 min |
| **Daily Quest** | 8 (rotation) | PvP Tokens, crafting materials | 10–15 min |
| **Weekly Quest** | 4 | Legendary Shards, exclusive cosmetics | 60–90 min |
| **Hidden Chain** | 5 chains | Legendary items, titles, achievements | 2–4 hours per chain |
| **Reputation Quest** | 6 factions | Faction gear, mounts, recipes | Ongoing |

## Hidden Quest Chains

### Chain 1: The Lost Dragon

**Starts:** Read the "Ancient Dragon Tome" in the Spire Peak library (coordinates 22, 15).  
**Requirements:** Level 30+, Chapter 6 completed.

| Step | Quest | Objective | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Faded Script | Collect 3 pages of the Dragon Tome | Spire Peak (scattered around library) |
| 2 | The Whistling Cavern | Find the hidden cave entrance | Frostmarch, frozen waterfall (32, 40) |
| 3 | The Guardian | Defeat the Frost Wyrm (level 35 elite) | Inside Whistling Cavern |
| 4 | The Hatchling | Feed the dragon egg 5 Fire Crystals | Quest item; Fire Crystals drop from Burning Fields elites |
| 5 | Loyal Companion | Complete the bonding ritual | Return to Spire Peak |

**Final Reward:** **Draconic Companion** (permanent mount, +40% movement speed), Achievement: *Dragonfriend*

### Chain 2: The Phantom Blades

**Starts:** Defeat 100 enemies in Ironhold Arena (any level). An NPC named "Shade" appears.  
**Requirements:** Level 40+ (unlock 3v3 arena).

| Step | Quest | Objective | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Whisper | Talk to Shade in Ironhold Arena | Ironhold |
| 2 | The Test | Win 3 duels without taking damage | Any 1v1 arena |
| 3 | The First Blade | Collect the Phantom Dagger in Deepmire | Deepmire, hidden chest (12, 8) |
| 4 | The Rival | Defeat the Phantom Assassin (hidden boss) | The Rift, north wing |
| 5 | The Legacy | Equip Phantom Dagger and win 10 consecutive 3v3 matches | Any 3v3 arena |

**Final Reward:** **Phantom Assassin's Cloak** (Legendary, +25% stealth movement speed, +10% backstab damage)

### Chain 3: The Forgemaster's Legacy

**Starts:** Speak to Master Forgegnome in Ironhold (after reaching level 45).  
**Requirements:** Level 45+, Chapter 10 completed.

| Step | Quest | Objective | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Lost Hammer | Find the Forgemaster's Hammer in Dragon's Peak | Dragon's Peak, behind the waterfall |
| 2 | The Eternal Flame | Collect 10 Ember Shards (from Fire Elementals) | Burning Fields or Dragon's Peak |
| 3 | The Perfect Ingot | Mine 10 Mithril Ore (rare node) | Deepmire, underground tunnels |
| 4 | The Masterwork | Craft an item with the Forgemaster's Hammer | Any forge (consumes hammer) |
| 5 | The Legacy Continues | Return to Master Forgegnome | Ironhold |

**Final Reward:** **Forgemaster's Blessing** (permanent buff: +15% crafting speed, +1 item quality level on all crafted gear)

### Chain 4: The Celestial Pilgrim

**Starts:** Find the "Star Map" in Celestial Realm (random drop from Celestial Guardians).  
**Requirements:** Level 50+, Chapter 11 completed.

| Step | Quest | Objective | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Star Map | Decode the map (interact at Celestial Observatory) | Celestial Realm |
| 2 | The First Constellation | Activate the Dragon constellation (solve light puzzle) | Spire Peak at night |
| 3 | The Second Constellation | Activate the Phoenix constellation (defeat 3 Phoenix elites) | Burning Fields |
| 4 | The Third Constellation | Activate the Wolf constellation (survive 5 waves of wolves) | Frostmarch clearing |
| 5 | The Celestial Gift | Return to the Observatory | Celestial Realm |

**Final Reward:** **Celestial Crown** (Legendary helmet, all stats +5, +1 skill level to all Core abilities)

### Chain 5: The Shadow of the Void

**Starts:** Defeat Overlord Xyclos in The Rift (endgame boss). A void portal opens.  
**Requirements:** Level 55+, endgame gear.

| Step | Quest | Objective | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Portal | Enter the void portal after Xyclos fight | The Rift |
| 2 | The Wasteland | Survive the Shadow Wasteland (5 minutes, constant damage) | Shadow Wasteland |
| 3 | The Void Lord | Defeat the Void Lord (level 60+ boss, 2M HP) | Shadow Wasteland, throne room |
| 4 | The Choice | Take the Void Crown or destroy it | Throne room |
| 5 | The Aftermath | Return to the capital; your choice affects NPC dialogue permanently | Capital city |

**Final Reward:** **Void Crown** (if kept: Legendary, +30% dark damage, -20% holy damage) **OR** **Pureheart's Blessing** (if destroyed: permanent +10% all resistances)

## Quest Priority Route

1. **Main story first** — Complete all 12 chapters before farming side quests. Story unlocks zones, skills, and endgame content.
2. **Essential side quests** — The Lost Artisan, A Hunter's Pride, and The Alchemist's Apprentice give permanent bonuses you want early.
3. **Daily quests** — Do Arena Brawler and Material Run daily. They take 10 minutes and the materials / tokens add up.
4. **Hidden quest chains** — Tackle these at the recommended levels. The rewards are best-in-slot items that carry through multiple level ranges.
5. **Weekly quests** — Complete all 4 weekly quests before the weekly reset. The Legendary Shards and PvP Tokens are essential for endgame gearing.
