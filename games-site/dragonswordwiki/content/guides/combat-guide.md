## DragonSword: Awakening Combat Guide

DragonSword: Awakening's combat is built around one loop: **apply status ailments, extend the combo, and finish with Signal Skills triggered by switching heroes**. This guide explains every confirmed mechanic of that loop — and clearly marks what is still unconfirmed.

## The Core Loop: Apply → Extend → Finish

HOUND13 describes the combat as heroes that "weave in and out of battle in endless combo chains through **Switching Signals**." The loop is:

1. **Apply** — hit enemies with Active Skills that stack Status Ailments (Bleed, Stun, Burn, and so on)
2. **Extend** — chain more skills, juggle enemies in the air, and build toward the switch
3. **Finish** — switch to another hero to trigger their **Signal Skill** — the game's biggest single-hit damage source

There is **no traditional ultimate skill bar**. The Signal Skill trigger replaces it.

## Status Ailments — The Confirmed List

Each of the 19 heroes specializes in one or two ailments. Only these pairings are confirmed by official reveals:

| Ailment | Confirmed Users | Effect |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| **Stun** | Lute, Castella, Kalien, Theresia | Locks the target in place |
| **Break** | Castella | Drains Super Armor / breaks armor |
| **Bleed** | Theresia | Damage over time |
| **Burn** | Aria | Damage over time (fire) |
| **Airborne** | Aria | Launches the enemy into the air |
| **Electrocution (Shock)** | Ornette | Interrupts enemy actions |
| **Knockdown** | Lute (secondary), Ornette (secondary) | Knocks the enemy prone |

Ailments claimed for heroes like **Poison do not exist in any verified source** — treat guides that use them as unreliable. **Frost** appears in community documentation for some heroes (see the [Cerese Guide](/guides/cerese-guide)) but is not officially confirmed. The remaining heroes' ailments are **unrevealed** as of August 2026.

## How Signal Skills Trigger

This is the confirmed trigger sequence:

1. **Stack ailments.** Each time your active hero lands Active Skills on an enemy, a hidden ailment layer builds up on the target. Stack it to the max limit.
2. **Watch for the prompt.** When a benched team member has a trigger available, a sign flashes near their face and a new input prompt appears over the game view.
3. **Press the tag hotkey.** Switch to the benched hero — they enter with their high-damage Signal Skill while the target stays locked in the ailment state.
4. Sometimes the active hero can trigger their own Signal Skill simultaneously for even more damage.

Ailments applied by one hero are consumed by skills from any hero — so **party composition determines which ailment chains are possible**.

## The Tag-Team System

You control one hero while two others wait in reserve.

**Confirmed mechanics:**

- **Swapping mid-combo** is the heart of the system — the old hero's lingering animation finishes while the new hero starts setting up the next status effect
- **Persistent field skills** — multi-hit status moves finish their animation even if you swap quickly, so an old hero keeps adding debuff stacks during the new hero's turn
- **Linking skills** — chaining an Active Skill into a teammate's Signal Skill keeps pressure on without downtime

**Unconfirmed:** reserve hero HP/SP regeneration rates, swap cooldowns, and tag-attack bonuses. Do not trust specific numbers published elsewhere.

## Super Armor and Breaking

Elite monsters and bosses have a **white armor bar underneath their health bar** — their Super Armor. While it is full, they ignore stagger and knockback.

- **Break the bar** with heavy attacks and Break-type skills (Castella is the confirmed specialist)
- A broken bar drops the enemy's Super Armor and **opens special finisher prompts** — cinematic grabs, huge sword swings, environmental throws
- **Stealth openings exist:** sneaking up on sleeping or unaware monsters lets you open with an instant heavy-impact skill to clear camps fast

The white-bar/Super Armor system is confirmed; the exact "50% more damage" type numbers published elsewhere are **not**.

## Aerial Combat

Airborne is a confirmed ailment — Aria's kit launches enemies, and the community describes aerial chains as a major damage source.

**Confirmed techniques:**

- Launch an enemy (Airborne ailment or a launched broken-armor state), jump after them, and continue the combo in the air
- Airborne combos are described as ignoring ground defenses

**Unconfirmed:** whether every hero has air strings, exact air damage multipliers, and "infinite air" claims.

## Team Composition Rules (Community-Confirmed)

From how the trigger system works, these rules follow:

1. **Mono-stacking is efficient.** Teams focused on one or two matching ailments fill the stack meter fastest. Three different ailments build too slowly.
2. **Two matching + one breaker.** Two heroes sharing a main effect, plus a third who breaks frames and Super Armor, is the recommended pattern.
3. **Avoid three identical ailments.** You want a chain — hero A's skill leads into hero B's Signal, which prepares the enemy for hero C. Three identical kits waste stacks.
4. **Stun is the most common confirmed ailment** (four heroes), making lock-down teams easy to build.

For concrete teams, see the [Team Compositions Guide](/guides/team-compositions).

## Example Confirmable Combo Directions

**Bleed Pressure:** use two Bleed-focused heroes whose Signal Skills trigger each other — against high-HP bosses this maintains near-constant damage. (Theresia is the only confirmed Bleed hero at launch, so this needs a second Bleed hero — **TBD**.)

**Stun-Lock:** Castella + Theresia + Lute — three confirmed Stun sources mean the target is locked for most of the fight, and every switch triggers a Signal Skill.

**Aerial Pressure:** Aria launches with Airborne; follow up with air attacks while her Burn ticks, then swap to a ground hero to reset.

## Practice Advice

- Learn the prompt timing in the prologue and early chapters — the tutorial introduces switching in the first hours
- Practice breaking Super Armor on the Orc Warrior (prologue) and Furcas — the first two confirmed bosses
- Use [Hunts](/guides/endgame-guide) in the endgame to re-fight story bosses and perfect your loops

## What Does NOT Exist

To keep you away from misinformation:

- **No PvP combat** — there is no PvP at launch
- **No ultimate skill bar** — Signal Skills replace it
- **No skill trees or stat points** — heroes have fixed kits
- **No confirmed controls list** — earlier drafts published keyboard layouts that were never verified; the game supports controllers and the tutorial teaches the real inputs
