## DragonSword: Awakening Combat Guide

DragonSword: Awakening's combat system is deeper than it first appears. While the tutorial covers the basics, mastering the **tag-team system**, **Signal Skills**, and **status ailments** is the difference between scraping by and dominating. This combat guide breaks down every system.

## Core Combat Mechanics

### Basic Attacks and Skills

Every hero has:
- **Light attack chain** (Left Click) — Up to 5 hits, builds combo meter
- **Combo skill** (Q) — Contextual follow-up after light attacks
- **Area skill** (E) — AoE or heavy attack, consumes SP
- **Signal Skill** (R) — Ultimate ability, requires full Signal Gauge
- **Dodge** (Shift) — Invincibility frames, cancels current animation

SP (Skill Points) regenerate over time. Each hero has about 100 SP. Skills cost 15-30 SP. Managing your SP across three heroes is a key skill.

### The Tag-Team System

The **tag-team system** is the heart of combat. Here is how it works in detail:

### Swapping Characters

Press **Tab** to swap to your next hero. The swap order is determined by your party setup screen. Key mechanics:

- **Swap cancels animation** — You can dodge by swapping instead of rolling
- **Reserve heal** — Off-battle heroes regenerate 5% HP and 10% SP per second
- **Tag attack** — Swapping during a combo triggers a tag attack (the incoming hero performs a dive-in strike)
- **Swap cooldown** — 3 seconds between swaps (2 seconds if you perform a tag attack)

### Signal Gauge

The **Signal Gauge** fills as you deal and receive damage. It fills faster when:
- Performing aerial combos
- Breaking enemy Super Armor
- Applying status ailments
- Swapping frequently (each swap gives a small gauge bonus)

Once full (100%), press **R** to activate your active hero's **Signal Skill**. These are game-changing ultimate abilities with unique effects.

## Status Ailments

Eight **status ailments** exist in DragonSword: Awakening. Understanding them is critical for high-level play.

| Ailment | Effect | Best Applied By |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **Bleed** | Damage over time, stacks up to 5 times | Theresia, Roxy |
| **Stun** | Enemy cannot act for 2-4 seconds | Kalsion, Castella |
| **Airborne** | Enemy launched into the air, takes extra aerial damage | Reina, Lute |
| **Poison** | Damage over time, reduces healing received | Cerese |
| **Burn** | Damage over time + reduces enemy attack power | Eileen |
| **Frost** | Slows enemy movement and attack speed by 40% | Kalien |
| **Shock** | Interrupts enemy actions, deals minor damage | Sion, Alex |
| **Knockdown** | Enemy falls prone, vulnerable to ground attacks | Kalsion, Charlotte |

### Status Synergies

Some ailments combo together:
- **Bleed + Poison** — Both DOTs stack independently for massive passive damage
- **Frost + Stun** — Frost extends Stun duration by 1.5 seconds
- **Burn + Bleed** — Burn refreshes Bleed duration
- **Airborne + Shock** — Shocked enemies stay airborne longer

## Super Armor and Breaking

Every enemy (including bosses) has a **Super Armor bar** — the white gauge below their health bar.

### How Super Armor Works

- **Full bar** — Enemy ignores stagger, knockback, and most interrupts
- **Breaking the bar** — Use heavy attacks, break skills, and status ailments
- **Broken state** — Enemy is staggered, takes bonus damage, can be launched

Best Super Armor breakers:
1. **Castella** — Shield bash skills do massive break damage
2. **Kalsion** — Heavy melee combos shred armor
3. **Theresia** — Bleed stacks deal bonus break damage

### Super Armor Break Priority

For story bosses, break the armor before going all out on damage. A broken boss takes 50% more damage for 5-8 seconds. Time your Signal Skills to land during this window.

## Aerial Combos

Aerial combat is a major DPS source. Once an enemy is **Airborne** (launched by a skill or broken armor), jump and continue attacking.

### Basic Air Combo String

1. Launch enemy (Q or E skill that causes Airborne)
2. Press Space to jump
3. Left Click x3 (air light chain)
4. Q (air combo skill)
5. E (air AoE - slams enemy back to ground)
6. Tab swap during landing animation for tag attack

### Advanced Air Techniques

- **Double launch** — Launch -> air combo -> swap -> second launch before enemy lands
- **Infinite air** — Reina can stay airborne indefinitely with her unique float mechanic
- **Air dodge** — You can dodge in mid-air. Use this to reposition during air combos
- **Wall bounce** — Knock enemies into walls for a short stun even with active Super Armor

## Advanced Tips

### Skill Lingering

Many skills have a **linger** period — a few frames where the hitbox remains active even after the animation ends. Use this to:
- Hit dodging enemies
- Cover your swap
- Create space while retreating

### Mounting Mechanic

After breaking a large enemy's Super Armor, you can **mount** them by pressing Space near their staggered body. Mounted enemies:
- Cannot attack
- Take 100% more damage
- Are controlled by your movement inputs (steer them into walls or off cliffs)

Mounting is especially effective against World Bosses and large story enemies.

### Perfect Dodge

Dodging at the exact moment an attack hits triggers a **Perfect Dodge** (character glows blue). This:
- Refunds the SP cost of the dodge
- Slows time for 1 second
- Guarantees the next attack is a critical hit
- Builds significant Signal Gauge

Practice Perfect Dodges against the Grassland Lesser Dragon — its tail sweep has a very generous timing window.

### Emergency Swap

If your active hero is about to take a fatal hit, press Tab to swap. The swap animation has invincibility frames (i-frames) identical to a dodge. This works even after an enemy's attack has started its animation — the swap cancels the incoming damage.

## Team Combat Strategy

### Rotation Example

Here is a sample combat rotation for a Lute / Theresia / Aria team:

1. **Open with Lute** — Apply buff, start light combo chain
2. **Q combo skill** — Launch enemy into air
3. **Tab swap to Theresia** — Tag attack into the airborne enemy
4. **Theresia combo** — Apply Bleed stacks (3-5 stacks)
5. **E skill** — Break Super Armor
6. **R (Signal Skill)** — Blood Moon, massive damage on broken armor
7. **Tab swap to Aria** — Heal if needed, apply defense buff
8. **Repeat** — Cycle through heroes as SP and Signal Gauge allow

The key is to never stay on one hero longer than two full combos. Constant swapping keeps pressure on enemies and builds your Signal Gauge fastest.

## Practice Makes Perfect

The best place to practice combat is the **Training Ground** in Skyspire Town (unlocked after Chapter 1). It features:
- Dummy enemies with infinite HP
- Damage meters
- Combo recording
- No SP or Signal Gauge restrictions

Spend 15 minutes here getting comfortable with swaps before taking on the first story boss.
