## Endgame Content Guide — The Post-Story Loop

After the main story, DragonSword: Awakening opens into a structured endgame. The confirmed activities are **World Bosses, the Tower of Trials, Hunts, Raids, and familiar collection**, all feeding into gear progression toward **Karma gear**. This guide explains what is confirmed about each activity and marks the rest as TBD.

## The Confirmed Endgame Activities

These systems are confirmed to exist by keyword research, official descriptions, and player reports:

| Activity | Confirmed Scale | Confirmed Role |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Tower of Trials** | Floor-based gauntlet (details TBD) | Vertical challenge + rewards |
| **World Bosses** | Open-world bosses (co-op supported) | Rotation-based boss farming |
| **Hunts** | Re-fight story bosses at higher difficulty | Material and practice farming |
| **Raids** | Up to 3-player co-op | Endgame boss encounters |
| **Familiar collection** | Collectible companions | Passives and traversal |
| **Rift System** | Post-launch feature | Announced, not yet released |

## World Bosses

World Bosses are a confirmed endgame system — players report a daily rotation of large open-world bosses and "world boss farming" as a core activity. What is **not** confirmed:

- The names and count of World Bosses (earlier drafts of this wiki published names like Pakirus or Andras — **these are not verified** and have been removed)
- Their locations and spawn mechanics
- Whether they require a Mercenary Rank gate to access
- Their drop tables (familiars, Karma gear, materials)

Farming pattern that is community-confirmed: check the daily rotation, form a co-op party, and repeat kills for materials. Exact drop rates are unknown.

## Tower of Trials

The Tower of Trials is a confirmed floor-based challenge. Unconfirmed details include:

- The number of floors (25 has been claimed by players, but is not officially verified)
- Floor modifiers and scaling
- Weekly reset rules
- Exact rewards (players associate it with high-tier gear)

## Hunts

Hunts let you re-fight story bosses at increased difficulty. They are community-described as the best source of certain crafting materials and boss-practice. Confirmed boss roster for Hunts comes from the main story: **Furcas, the Grassland Lesser Dragon, Barduk, a Lich, and Ursula** (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)). Difficulty tiers, XP values, and drop tables are TBD.

## Raids

Raids are 3-player co-op endgame encounters — the full breakdown is in the [Raids Guide](/guides/dungeon-raids).

## Karma Gear and the Gear Loop

**Karma gear** is a confirmed high-end gear tier that players associate with endgame sources like World Bosses and the Tower of Trials. The full endgame gear loop looks like this:

1. Clear the story (see the [Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough))
2. Farm World Bosses and Hunts for materials
3. Climb the Tower of Trials for high-tier gear
4. Run raids for co-op-exclusive rewards (details TBD)
5. Chase Karma gear as the endgame target

Exact drop sources, upgrade paths, and enhancement systems are **unconfirmed** — see the [Equipment Guide](/guides/equipment-guide) and [Gear Progression Guide](/guides/gear-progression) for what is known.

## The Rift System (Upcoming)

The **Rift System** is announced as a post-launch endgame feature. Confirmed: it exists on the roadmap. Unconfirmed: its mechanics — claims about procedurally generated floors, leaderboards, and multiplayer scale are **player speculation**, not verified information. This wiki will document it when HOUND13 publishes details.

## Optimal Endgame Routine (Based on What Is Confirmed)

1. **Finish the story** — endgame systems unlock after the main quest line.
2. **Check the World Boss rotation daily** — farming the active boss is the community standard for materials.
3. **Push the Tower of Trials** — the deeper you climb, the better the rewards (exact loot TBD).
4. **Run Hunts** — the fastest way to practice boss patterns and farm crafting materials.
5. **Raid with friends** — 3-player co-op for the hardest confirmed content.
6. **Collect familiars** — including the free Abyssal Direwolf DLC if you do not have it (see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/familiars-guide)).

## Endgame Team Recommendations

Endgame content favors focused ailment teams. Confirmed-safe recommendations:

- **Bleed Chain team:** Theresia (Bleed/Stun) + Lute (Stun/Knockdown) + Castella (Break/Stun) — works for World Bosses and Tower floors
- **Stun-Lock team:** Castella + Theresia + Lute — maximum control while you learn endgame patterns
- **Swap in Ornette** (Electrocution/Shock, scythe) once her kit's exact behavior is documented — she adds a third confirmed ailment type to your options

For more lineups, see the [Team Compositions Guide](/guides/team-compositions) and the [Tier List](/guides/tier-list).

## Common Endgame Mistakes

1. **Farming without a plan.** World Boss rotation matters — check which boss is active before committing time.
2. **Ignoring Hunts.** Story bosses at higher difficulty are the community's main source of crafting materials.
3. **Chasing unconfirmed gear.** If a guide lists specific relic set names or drop rates, treat it as speculation until verified.
4. **Forgetting co-op.** Raids are built for 3 players — a full party stacks ailments far faster than solo play.

## Bottom Line

The endgame loop is: World Bosses → Hunts → Tower of Trials → Raids → Karma gear, with familiar collection along the way. Every specific number in the endgame (floor counts, drop rates, lockouts) is still TBD, and this guide will be updated as the community verifies them.
