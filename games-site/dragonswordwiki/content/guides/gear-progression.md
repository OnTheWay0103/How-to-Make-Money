## Gear Progression in DragonSword: Awakening

Gear is the backbone of your character's power in DragonSword: Awakening. While stats and skills determine what you can do, your equipment determines how well you can do it. This guide covers the entire gear progression journey, from your first iron sword to legendary raid equipment.

## Equipment Tier System

Equipment in DragonSword: Awakening follows a tier system. Higher tiers have higher base stats and can be upgraded further.

### Tier Overview Table

| Tier | Name | Level Range | Stat Range (Weapon ATK) | Stat Range (Armor DEF) | Source |
|------|------|------------|------------------------|----------------------|--------|
| 1 | Iron | 1-20 | 10-25 | 5-12 | Story quests, shop |
| 2 | Steel | 20-35 | 25-45 | 12-22 | Dungeons, blacksmith |
| 3 | Mythril | 35-50 | 45-70 | 22-35 | World bosses, craftsmen |
| 4 | Enchanted | 50-65 | 70-100 | 35-50 | Expert dungeons, raids |
| 5 | Celestial | 65-80 | 100-140 | 50-70 | Raids, high-level crafting |
| 6 | Primordial | 80-95 | 140-190 | 70-95 | Endgame raids, Legendary quests |
| 7 | Legendary | 95-100 | 190-250 | 95-130 | The Hollow Heart raid, world events |
| 8 | Mythic | 100+ | 250-320 | 130-170 | Mythic raids, PvP ranking rewards |

### Rarity within Tiers

Each tier has four rarity levels that multiply the base stats:

| Rarity | Color | Stat Multiplier | Upgrade Cap |
|--------|-------|----------------|-------------|
| Common | White | 1.0x | +3 |
| Uncommon | Green | 1.15x | +5 |
| Rare | Blue | 1.3x | +7 |
| Epic | Purple | 1.5x | +10 |
| Legendary | Orange | 1.8x | +15 |
| Mythic | Red | 2.0x | +20 |

## Upgrade System

Gear can be upgraded at any blacksmith NPC. Each upgrade level adds a flat stat bonus and, at certain thresholds, unlocks additional effects.

### Upgrade Costs by Level

| Upgrade Level | Success Rate | Materials Required | Gold Cost |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| +1 | 100% | 5 Iron Ore | 500 |
| +2 | 100% | 10 Iron Ore | 1,000 |
| +3 | 95% | 5 Steel Ingot | 2,000 |
| +4 | 85% | 10 Steel Ingot | 4,000 |
| +5 | 75% | 5 Mythril Ingot | 8,000 |
| +6 | 60% | 10 Mythril Ingot | 15,000 |
| +7 | 45% | 5 Enchanted Essence | 25,000 |
| +8 | 35% | 10 Enchanted Essence | 40,000 |
| +9 | 25% | 5 Celestial Shard | 60,000 |
| +10 | 15% | 10 Celestial Shard | 100,000 |

**Important**: Upgrade attempts at +7 and above can downgrade your gear by 1 level on failure. Use **Protection Scrolls** (bought from the cash shop or earned from events) to prevent downgrade.

## Recommended Progression Order

### Level 1-20: The Story Sets

Do not worry about gear optimization yet. Use whatever drops from story quests. The Iron Sentinel set is more than sufficient for this phase.

### Level 20-35: First Upgrades

Craft or buy the Steel Stalker set from the auction house. Upgrade your weapon to +5. Focus on weapons before armor.

### Level 35-50: Transition to Endgame Prep

Farm the Earth Golem world boss for Mythril Ingots. Craft the Mythril Mage set if you are a magic build, or mix-and-match Steel/Mythril for physical builds. Upgrade your weapon to +7.

### Level 50-65: First Raid Gear

Start running Dragon's Maw weekly. The Dragon's Wrath set will carry you through the next tier. Joining a raiding guild at this stage is highly recommended.

### Level 65-80: Raid Progression

Progress through Serpent's Coil and Skyreach Citadel. The Serpent's Grace and Skyreach Armor sets offer massive power spikes. Upgrade your weapon to +9.

### Level 80-95: Endgame Gearing

Run The Abyssal Throne and Primordial Forge. Start saving Celestial Shards and Protection Scrolls for +10 attempts. Begin thinking about which endgame set you want to invest in long-term.

### Level 95-100: Mythic Preparation

Clear The Hollow Heart raid for Legendary gear. Save all rare materials for Mythic crafting. This is the final gear tier — choose wisely.

### Level 100+: Mythic Endgame

Run Mythic difficulty raids for Tier 8 gear. Compete in PvP ranking for unique rewards. Optimize your set bonuses for specific content types.
