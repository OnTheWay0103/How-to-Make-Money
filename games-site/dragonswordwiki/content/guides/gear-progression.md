## Gear Progression in DragonSword: Awakening

Gear in DragonSword: Awakening is earned entirely through gameplay — there is no gacha, no cash shop, and no paid gear. This guide lays out the confirmed gear progression path and is honest about what the community has not yet documented.

**A note on accuracy:** earlier drafts of this site (and many external guides) published detailed tier tables, enhancement success rates, and upgrade materials. None of that is verified. HOUND13 has not published an official gear system breakdown, so this guide only states what is confirmed and marks the rest TBD.

## The Confirmed Gear Path

The progression arc, confirmed by the game's design and player reports, is:

1. **Starting gear** — quest and story rewards carry you through the prologue and early chapters
2. **Dungeon and exploration drops** — hidden dungeons, treasure, and side content yield better equipment
3. **World Boss and endgame drops** — the strongest confirmed gear sources, tied to the endgame loop
4. **Karma gear** — the confirmed high-end tier, associated with World Bosses and the Tower of Trials

There is no confirmed "tier list" of gear names (Iron, Steel, Mythril, etc.) — those were invented in earlier drafts and have been removed.

## Karma Gear

**Karma gear** is the most important confirmed gear concept:

- It is a distinct, high-end equipment tier
- Players associate it with endgame sources — World Bosses and the Tower of Trials are the commonly cited origins, but exact drop locations are **unconfirmed**
- Its unique effects are described by players as changing how hero skills behave, but no verified list of Karma effects exists

When we have verified sources for Karma gear sources and effects, this section will be expanded.

## Weapons — Fixed to Heroes

Unlike class-based RPGs, weapons in DragonSword: Awakening are **fixed to each hero** — you do not equip a different weapon type; you find stronger versions of your hero's weapon. The confirmed hero weapons (from official reveals):

| Hero | Confirmed Weapon |
|------|------------------|
| **Lute** | Greatsword |
| **Castella** | Frontline vanguard (Break/Stun kit) |
| **Theresia** | Twin sacred maces |
| **Ornette** | Scythe |
| **Aria** | Bombs / ranged (firepower specialist) |
| **Roxy** | Bow |
| **Kalien** | Commands the fire fox spirit Sur (spirit-based) |

Weapon names (e.g. "Dragonfang Blade" or "Bloodletter Set") that appear in other guides are **not verified** and are not listed here.

## Relics

**Relics** are a confirmed gear category (players refer to "relics" and "relic sets" in endgame discussions), but:

- No official relic set names are confirmed
- No official set bonuses are confirmed
- Sources are unconfirmed

Any guide that names relic sets like "Berserker," "Guardian," or "Sage" is speculating. This wiki will publish relic details when they are verified.

## Upgrades — What We Know and Don't Know

- **Upgrades exist** — the game's blacksmiths and upgrade systems are referenced in player discussions, and upgrading gear before story bosses is common advice.
- **The mechanics are unconfirmed** — enhancement levels, success rates, failure penalties, and materials have not been verified by official sources.

Practical advice that holds regardless of the exact numbers: upgrade your **weapon first** (damage is the strongest lever in an action RPG), and only upgrade gear you expect to keep for several chapters.

## Where to Farm Gear

| Stage | Confirmed Sources |
|-------|-------------------|
| Prologue – Chapter 1 | Main quest rewards, exploration, first dungeons |
| Chapter 2 – 3 | Story quests, side quests, hidden dungeons, treasure hunting |
| Endgame | World Bosses, Tower of Trials, Hunts, 3-player raids |

The game's official description explicitly lists "treasure hunting" and "dungeon exploration" as core activities, so exploration is a first-class gear source — not an afterthought.

## Gear-Related Systems That Do NOT Exist

To avoid misinformation, these systems are **confirmed absent** at launch:

- **No gacha or paid gear** — all gear is earned in-game
- **No cash-shop upgrade items** — "protection scrolls from the cash shop" are an invention from other games' guides
- **No PvP gear rewards** — there is no PvP
- **No 8-player raid gear** — raids are 3-player co-op

## Upgrade Priority (Sensible Defaults)

While official numbers are pending, the community consensus order is:

1. **Weapon** — direct damage increase, always first
2. **Offensive accessories/relics** — set bonuses matter more than raw stats
3. **Defense** — only as needed; dead enemies deal no damage

## Bottom Line

Gear progression in DragonSword: Awakening is: story gear → dungeon/exploration drops → endgame sources → Karma gear. The exact tier names, upgrade numbers, and drop rates are still being documented — this guide will be updated as they are verified.
