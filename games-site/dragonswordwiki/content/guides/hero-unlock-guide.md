## Unlock Every Hero in DragonSword: Awakening

The best thing about DragonSword: Awakening's hero system: **there is no gacha, no loot boxes, and no paid hero packs**. All 19 launch heroes are earned through gameplay — story progression, quests, exploration, and hidden content. This guide covers everything confirmed about recruitment and is honest about what is still undocumented.

**Accuracy note:** earlier drafts of this guide published specific quest names, locations, and unlock methods (e.g. "The Crimson Blade" quest, "Skyspire Town," hidden arena duels). None of that was verified and it has been removed. Exact unlock methods for most heroes are still being documented by the community (marked TBD).

## What Is Confirmed

### The Starting Trio

| Hero | Confirmed Info |
|------|----------------|
| **Lute** | Protagonist — your first controlled character in the prologue |
| **Castella** | Joins the party early — the Elf vanguard of the mercenary group |
| **Aria** | Part of the early roster — bomb-using firepower specialist |

The game's official material and the demo both present this trio as the early party.

### Johnny — Companion, Not Confirmed Playable

**Johnny**, the mercenary who travels with Lute, is a confirmed companion character — but he is **not confirmed among the 19 playable heroes**. Sources that list him as playable are speculating.

### All 19 Heroes Earnable In-Game

HOUND13's official messaging confirms that the western release was redesigned so that **all heroes and equipment can be obtained through story progression and in-game activities** — this is the core promise of the buy-to-play model. Every hero has a gameplay path; exact methods per hero are TBD.

### Hero Roster (Confirmed Members)

The confirmed roster of 19 includes: **Lute, Castella, Aria, Kalien, Theresia, Ornette, Cerese, Reina, Kalsion, Charlotte, and Roxy** — plus the demo-exclusive hero (playable in the June 2026 demo but **not in the full roster**). The remaining launch heroes' identities have not been officially catalogued by verified sources yet.

Note: heroes named **Eileen, Astria, Sion, Alex, and Tarte** in other guides have **no official confirmation** — treat those guides as unreliable. **Dana** is confirmed as Kalien's companion but is not confirmed playable in the main roster.

## The Four Post-Launch Free Heroes (Confirmed)

HOUND13's roadmap confirms four additional heroes, all free, arriving in the second half of 2026:

| Hero | Confirmed Kit |
|------|---------------|

| **Logan** | Tank with a massive shield who protects allies |

## How Unlock Methods Likely Work (Based on Confirmed Design)

While specific unlock quests are TBD, the game's confirmed design implies these sources:

- **Main story progression** — several heroes join through the story chapters (the starting trio is confirmed this way)
- **Side quests** — the official description confirms side content with "local residents' problems"; hero recruitment is a common reward type
- **Exploration and hidden content** — treasure hunting and hidden dungeons are confirmed activities
- **Mercenary Rank promotions** — rank gates are a confirmed progression system, and content unlocks ride on them

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I unlock all heroes without paying?**
Yes — officially confirmed. No hero requires real money.

**Is anyone missable?**
The game's single-player, no-live-service design makes permanently missable heroes unlikely, but this is not officially confirmed.

**Does the demo unlock anything in the full game?**
No — the demo's save data does not carry over to the full game.

**When do Ryza, Jerome, Veronica, and Logan arrive?**
Second half of 2026, per the official roadmap. Exact patch timing is TBD.
