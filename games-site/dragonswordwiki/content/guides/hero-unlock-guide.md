## Unlock Every Hero in DragonSword: Awakening

One of the best things about DragonSword: Awakening is that every hero is earnable through gameplay. There is no gacha, no loot boxes, and no paid hero packs. This complete **hero unlock guide** shows you exactly how to recruit all 19 characters and the 4 upcoming free heroes.

## Starting Heroes (Automatic)

These three heroes are given to you during the prologue and first story chapter. You cannot miss them.

| Hero | When You Get Them |
|------|-------------------|
| **Lute** | Prologue — your first controlled character |
| **Castella** | End of prologue — joins after defending the town |
| **Aria** | Chapter 1 completion reward |

## Side Quest Unlocks

Several heroes require completing specific **side quests** or quest chains. These are missable if you skip side content.

### Charlotte
Start the "Shadow of the Assassin" chain from an NPC in the Hunter's Guild (Chapter 2). The chain has 5 quests culminating in a duel against Charlotte. Defeat her to unlock.

### Kalien
Found in the Frostpeak Mountains. An NPC there starts "The Frozen Heart" quest. Complete the 3-part chain and clear the Frostpeak Hollow dungeon to recruit Kalien.

### Eileen
Eileen's quest "Flames of Rebellion" is given by a refugee in the Burning Steppes camp (Chapter 3). It requires defeating 3 fire elemental mini-bosses spread across the zone.

### Astria
A hidden quest giver near the Great Library in Skyspire City starts "The Guardian's Duty." This is a lore-heavy chain with a boss fight at the end. Astria is a powerful buffer worth the effort.

### Alex
Alex is unlocked by completing the "Underground Brawl" tournament in the Slums district. Win three consecutive fights without swapping heroes for bonus rewards.

### Tarte
Tarte is the only hero that requires a specific item. Find the "Lost Shield of Tarte" in a hidden chest in the Whispering Woods (Chapter 2 area). Bring it to the armor merchant in Skyspire Town.

### Cerese
Cerese requires collecting 3 Poison Vial drops from specific enemies:
1. **Toxic Slime** — Whispering Woods (10% drop)
2. **Venomous Wyrm** — Frostpeak Caverns (15% drop)
3. **Corrupted Alchemist** — Burning Steppes (25% drop)

Bring all three to the Alchemist in Skyspire Town.

## Exploration Unlocks (Hidden Locations)

These heroes require finding **secret locations** in the open world.

### Ornette
Ornette is hidden in a secret arena beneath the Collapsed Temple in the Whispering Woods. You need to find a cracked wall (marked by a faint glow), break it with a heavy attack, and descend into the arena. Defeat Ornette in a 1v1 duel to unlock her.

### Reina
Reina is found atop the **Skyspire Spire** — the highest point in the game world accessible via a series of platforming challenges starting from Skyspire Rock Mountain. The path requires precise jumping and aerial combat skills. Reina joins you after you prove your aerial mastery.

## Secret Dungeon Unlocks

### Eileen (Alternate Method)
If you miss the side quest, Eileen also drops as a clear reward from the **Burning Sanctum** secret dungeon (found in the Burning Steppes, hidden behind a waterfall).

If you want to optimize your hero collection:

2. **Roxy** — Hunting Lodge in Chapter 2 (ranged DPS)
3. **Reina** — Skyspire Spire exploration (top-tier aerial fighter)
4. **Ornette** — Secret arena duel (burst damage)
5. **Kalsion** — Chapter 3 story boss (tank/DPS hybrid)

## Can You Miss Heroes?

Most heroes are permanently available. However:
- **Charlotte's quest chain** is only available during Chapter 2 before the Grassland Lesser Dragon fight
- **Cerese's poison vials** can be farmed at any time — nothing is permanently missable
- **Ornette's arena** remains accessible after clearing the game
