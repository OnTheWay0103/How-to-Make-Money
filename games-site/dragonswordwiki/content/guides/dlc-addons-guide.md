## Familiar: Abyssal Direwolf — Free Claim (Limited Time)

The **Abyssal Direwolf familiar DLC** launched with the game as a limited-time promotion: **free for a claim window, permanently kept once claimed.**

### Claim Deadline

The free window ends around **August 30–31, 2026**:

- Reported deadline: **August 30, 2026, 10 PM UTC** (Automaton West)
- Alternate reporting: **August 31, 2026, 7 AM KST** — that is 6 PM EDT / 3 PM PDT on August 30 (KeenGamer/NewsBreak)

**Whatever the exact minute, the window is over by the end of August 2026 — claim it now if you have not.**

### What It Costs After the Window

The post-promo price is **reported inconsistently — $7.99** per one outlet and **$3.99** per another. Whichever is accurate, the free claim saves you real money, and the DLC is a **permanent account addition** once claimed.

### How to Claim (Confirmed Steps)

1. **Own the base game** — required to use the DLC.
2. Go to the Steam store page for **"DragonSword : Awakening - Familiar : Abyssal Direwolf"** and add it to your library while it shows **"Free."**
3. Download it (it appears as owned DLC).
4. **Launch the game** and claim it in-game:
   - Press **ESC** (or the gamepad "≡" button) to open the Main Menu
   - Go to **Codex → Familiar**
   - Select **Abyssal Direwolf** → **Claim/Obtain**
   - Equip it to a hero from the **Familiar** menu

**Troubleshooting:** if the Claim button does not appear, **restart the game** after the DLC download completes — a known first-time step.

### Abyssal Direwolf Abilities (Confirmed by Coverage)

| Ability | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Boost Jump** | Jump farther when using a Ground Boost — no cooldown |
| **Boost Jump unlock (all familiars)** | All familiars gain access to Boost Jump |
| **Stamina efficiency** | 20% less stamina consumption during Ground Boost, for all familiars |
| **Movement speed** | 10% extra movement speed on land, for all familiars |

**Lore:** the Abyssal Direwolf is a wolf "living in pure darkness" that loves children despite its eerie appearance; its howl is said to drive away nightmares and ghosts.

**Why it matters:** familiars provide passive bonuses (elemental stat bonuses, damage-over-time triggers) and traversal help — see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/familiars-guide) for the system.

Yes — all add-ons require DragonSword: Awakening.
