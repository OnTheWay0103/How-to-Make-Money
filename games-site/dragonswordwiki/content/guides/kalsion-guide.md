## Kalsion Guide — Fire Melee DPS & the King of Orbis

Kalsion is the **King of the Orbis Royal Castle** — the ruler the kingdom's knights serve, introduced in the **Orbis Kingdom Hero Teaser** (June 2026) alongside Cerese and Reina. In gameplay terms he is an **aggressive fire-based melee powerhouse**: he charges straight into combat, burns through enemies at close range, and builds his own attack power as the fight goes on. Community tier lists place him in **S-tier** for aggressive play.

> **Accuracy note:** Kalsion's identity is officially confirmed (the Orbis teaser and the developer AMA — which transliterates his name "Calcion"). His kit details below are **community-documented**, consistent across community tier lists and build guides. HOUND13 has not published an official skill sheet, so treat skill names as community-documented and gear recommendations as community-suggested.

## Who He Is (Confirmed)

- **King of Orbis** — "Calcion, King of Orbis" per the HOUND13 AMA; community coverage calls him the "Sun of the Kingdom."
- **Orbis Kingdom trio:** he debuted alongside **Reina** (Royal Guard captain) and **Cerese** (Magic Knight Order commander) — the kingdom's royal trio.
- **Name spelling:** "Kalsion" (community and this wiki's roster notes) vs. "Calcion" (the AMA's transliteration) — same character.

## Community-Documented Kit

Per community documentation, Kalsion's kit centers on **fire-based melee offense**:

| Skill | Community-Documented Effect |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **Blaze** | Fires bursts of fire energy at enemies — his ranged option |
| **Flame Wave** | Deals damage while **increasing his attack power** — he gets more threatening as offense ramps |
| **Charge / shockwave attack** | Charges directly into combat and generates shock waves that finish surrounding enemies |
| **Alternative modes** | Give him flexibility instead of a single repetitive approach |

**The playstyle:** sustained offensive pressure. He's built for players whose idea of fun is charging straight into enemies and overwhelming them — the community tier lists describe him as one of the best picks for exactly that.

### How He Fits the Combat Loop

Kalsion is the **damage engine** of the Apply → Extend → Finish loop (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)):

1. **Apply** — his fire attacks build burn-style pressure while Flame Wave ramps his attack power
2. **Extend** — shock waves clear the crowd around him, keeping the pressure on
3. **Finish** — switch out under the stacked state to trigger a teammate's **Signal Skill**, then charge back in

His attack-power ramp makes him a **sustained-damage hero**: longer fights make him scarier, which is exactly what boss phases demand.

## Team Role (Community-Documented)

Community build guides (including the fire-team meta) slot Kalsion as the **burn sub-DPS**:

- **Fire team:** Kalsion (burn sub-DPS) + **Kalien** (burst main DPS with her fire fox Sur) — the community-documented fire core, using burn and airborne states for burst damage.
- **Aggressive teams:** his charge-and-shockwave kit wants a team that can follow his lead — a breaker (like Castella) to open windows, and a support (like Lute) to cover the damage he trades while charging in.

### Community Gear Direction

From the community fire-team build guide (community-suggested, not official):

- **Equipment:** Earth Smasher 3-piece set + Fire 2-piece set
- **Main stat:** ATK
- **Substat priority:** Ignore Defense > Attack > Crit Rate > Crit DMG > Max HP > Defense
