## Dungeon Raids in DragonSword: Awakening

Dungeon raids are the pinnacle of DragonSword: Awakening's PvE content. These multi-boss encounters require coordinated groups, specialized builds, and detailed knowledge of boss mechanics. Successful runs reward the best gear in the game.

This guide covers every raid dungeon, its bosses, mechanics, recommended group compositions, and loot tables.

## Raid Overview

Raids are 8-player instanced dungeons with multiple boss encounters. Each raid has a minimum level requirement and recommended gear score. Raids are on a weekly lockout system — you can clear each raid once per week for rewards.

## Raid Details

### Dragon's Maw

The entry-level raid, designed to teach raid mechanics. Three bosses with straightforward patterns.

**Boss 1 — Emberfang the Smoldering**
- **Mechanics**: Flame breath (frontal cone), Tail sweep (rear AoE), Ember pools (floor hazard)
- **Tank Strategy**: Keep Emberfang facing away from the group. Move when ember pools appear.
- **DPS Strategy**: Attack from the sides. Avoid the frontal cone and rear tail.
- **Healer Strategy**: Stack with the group. Emberfang's damage is consistent but low.

**Boss 2 — Cinderwing the Ashen**
- **Mechanics**: Flight phase (periodic), Ashen rain (AoE projectiles), Swoop attack (targets random player)
- **Tank Strategy**: Cannot be tanked during flight. Collect adds during ground phase.
- **DPS Strategy**: Save burst cooldowns for ground phase. During flight, focus on dodging ashen rain.
- **Healer Strategy**: Spread out during flight to minimize AoE damage overlap.

**Boss 3 — Ignis the World-Ender**
- **Mechanics**: Three phases (100-70%, 70-30%, 30-0%), Lava floor phase, Meteor call, Enrage timer (8 minutes)
- **Tank Strategy**: Swap at 3 debuff stacks. Position Ignis away from lava pools.
- **DPS Strategy**: Phase 2 is the most dangerous — kill the lava spouts quickly. Phase 3 is a burn phase.
- **Healer Strategy**: Save group healing cooldowns for meteor impact.

### Serpent's Coil

A four-boss raid set in an underwater cavern. Introduces movement-based mechanics.

**Boss 1 — Tidal Guardian**
- **Mechanics**: Water jets (knockback), Bubble traps (root), Tidal wave (room-wide AoE, jump to avoid)
- **Key Tip**: The tidal wave can be dodged by jumping at the right moment. Practice the timing.

**Boss 2 — Venomfang**
- **Mechanics**: Poison pools, Venom spit (targeted DoT), Submerge (untargetable, spawns adds)
- **Key Tip**: Assign one player to cleanse poison debuffs. Kill adds quickly during submerge phase.

**Boss 3 — Kraken's Grasp**
- **Mechanics**: Tentacle grabs (grab random player), Ink clouds (blindness), Whirlpool (pull toward center)
- **Key Tip**: Free grabbed players by attacking the tentacle holding them. Save movement skills for whirlpool.

**Boss 4 — Leviathan, the Deep Serpent**
- **Mechanics**: Electric orbs (spawned periodically), Chain lightning (shared damage), Enrage at 10 minutes
- **Key Tip**: Spread out for chain lightning. Assign orb collectors to prevent orbs from reaching the boss.

### Skyreach Citadel

The first "hard" raid. Mechanics require precise coordination.

**Boss 1 — Storm Knight**
- **Mechanics**: Static field (AoE on ground), Lightning dash (line attack), Storm summon (adds)
- **Key Tip**: Do not stand in static fields. The Storm Knight's dashes follow a predictable pattern.

**Boss 2 — Wind Witch**
- **Mechanics**: Tornadoes (knockback, reposition), Wind shield (immune until adds killed), Gust (push toward edges)
- **Key Tip**: Kill adds to break the wind shield. Use gap closers to avoid being pushed off edges.

**Boss 5 — Council of Skyreach (3 simultaneous mini-bosses)**
- **Mechanics**: Three bosses must be killed within 15 seconds of each other or they revive
- **Key Tip**: Bring all three to low HP, then execute them simultaneously. Coordinate with voice chat.

### The Abyssal Throne

Endgame raid for players approaching the level cap.

**Boss — The Void Tyrant**
- **Mechanics**: Void zones (death zones on floor), Reality tear (splits arena), True Sight (reveals hidden adds)
- **Key Tip**: Save dashes for void zone placement. Kill reality tears quickly to prevent arena loss.

## Recommended Group Composition

A balanced raid group should include:

- **2 Tanks** (Paladin or tank-built Lute) — One main tank, one off-tank for adds or tank swap mechanics
- **2 Healers** (Aria or support-built Castella) — Primary healer and backup healer
- **4 DPS** — Mix of ranged and melee. At least 2 ranged for mechanics that require range
- **1 Flex** — Can fill any role depending on the raid's specific needs

## Weekly Lockout and Reset

Raids reset every Tuesday at server reset time (04:00 server time). You can only loot each boss once per week. If you have already cleared a raid this week, you can still join others but will not receive loot.
