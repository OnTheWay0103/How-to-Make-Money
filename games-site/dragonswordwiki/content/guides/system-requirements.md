## DragonSword: Awakening System Requirements & PC Specs Guide

DragonSword: Awakening (HOUND13's gacha-free action RPG, released July 22/23, 2026 on PC via Steam) is a fast, anime-style combat game — and it has a hard requirement many players miss: **an SSD**. This guide covers the minimum and recommended specs published on the game's **Steam store page**, the SSD rule, and what they mean in practice.

## Official Minimum Requirements

| Component | Minimum |
|-----------|---------|
| **OS** | Windows 10 (64-bit) |
| **Processor** | Intel i5-9400F / AMD Ryzen 5 3600 |
| **Memory** | 8 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT |
| **DirectX** | Version 12 |
| **Storage** | 25 GB available space — **SSD strictly required** |

## Official Recommended Requirements

| Component | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| **OS** | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) |
| **Processor** | Intel i7-9700F / AMD Ryzen 7 5800X |
| **Memory** | 16 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT |
| **DirectX** | Version 12 |
| **Storage** | 25 GB available space (SSD) |

## The SSD Requirement — Read This First

**An SSD is strictly required for a smooth experience.** Community and review reporting is consistent: installing DragonSword on an HDD can cause extended loading times, texture pop-in, frame drops, and crashes. If your system drive is an HDD, budget for an SSD — this is the single most common performance mistake new players make.

## Storage Note — 25 GB vs 60 GB

Most sources list **25 GB**; one third-party wiki lists 60 GB. The 25 GB figure is the widely reported store-page number. If your free space is tight, expect at least 25 GB — the higher figure may reflect post-launch updates (DLC and content patches are confirmed coming).

DirectX 12 is required — if you are on Windows 10, ensure your GPU drivers are current; DX12 on a GTX 16-series card at minimum spec is exactly the territory where driver updates matter most.

## Performance Advice (Based on Confirmed Specs)

1. **SSD first, GPU second.** The confirmed HDD failure modes (stutter, pop-in, crashes) are worse than a graphics drop.
2. **Minimum spec = medium settings.** The GTX 1660 minimum maps to medium-ish settings at 1080p in the launch build; the RTX 3060 recommended tier is the 1080p high / 1440p target.
3. **Co-op raids add load.** 3-player raid encounters (see the [Raids Guide](/guides/dungeon-raids)) stream more actors — a machine at minimum spec should expect the worst frame dips in raids.
4. **Keep 25 GB free beyond the install.** Patches and the announced post-launch content (Rift System, 4 free heroes, DLC) will use the space.

## Bottom Line

DragonSword: Awakening is not a demanding game by 2026 standards — a GTX 1660 and 8 GB RAM meet minimum. The requirement that actually changes your experience is the **SSD**. Meet the SSD rule and the published specs, and performance issues become rare.
