## DragonSword: Awakening Endgame Guide

After the main story, DragonSword: Awakening opens into a structured endgame of repeatable activities. The confirmed systems are the **Tower of Trials, World Bosses, Hunts, and 3-player Raids**, with the **Rift System** announced for post-launch. This guide covers what is confirmed about each — and is explicit about what the community has not yet documented.

## The Confirmed Endgame Activities

| Activity | Confirmed Facts | Details Status |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Tower of Trials** | 50-floor gauntlet (per the official description) | Modifiers & rewards TBD |
| **World Bosses** | 9 open-world bosses on a rotation, farmed for materials/gear | Roster & timers TBD |
| **Raids** | Up to 3-player co-op endgame encounters | Mechanics TBD |
| **Hunts** | Re-fight story bosses at higher difficulty | Tiers TBD |
| **Familiar collection** | Collectible companion creatures | Count & functions TBD |
| **Rift System** | Announced post-launch feature | Not released |

## Tower of Trials

The Tower of Trials is the community's most-cited vertical challenge — a **50-floor gauntlet** (per the official game description) with rising difficulty and endgame rewards, frequently mentioned alongside **Karma gear** as a reward source.

**Unconfirmed (TBD):**

- Floor modifiers and mechanics
- Whether it is solo-only or supports co-op
- Weekly reset rules
- Exact reward tables

**Community advice that holds regardless:** climb as high as your Mercenary Rank and gear allow, and treat Tower rewards as a major progression lever. Details will be added here as they are verified.

## World Bosses

World Bosses are confirmed open-world endgame bosses — **9 in total per the official description** — on a **rotation schedule**, farmed in co-op for materials, gear, and (per player reports) familiar-related content.

**Unconfirmed:** individual boss names, locations, spawn timers, and drop tables. Names like Pakirus, Gallore, or Andras that appear in other guides are **not verified** and are not repeated here. Whether the bosses require a Mercenary Rank gate is also unconfirmed.

**Farming pattern (community-confirmed):** check the rotation, form a co-op party, repeat kills. See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

## Hunts

Hunts let you **re-fight story bosses at higher difficulty tiers**. The confirmed story boss roster (Orc Warrior, Furcas, Grassland Lesser Dragon, Barduk, Lich, Ursula) forms the huntable set.

**Unconfirmed:** the number of difficulty tiers, XP and material values, and leaderboard claims.

**Why Hunts matter:** they are the community's recommended way to learn boss patterns without story consequences and a cited source of crafting materials.

## Raids

Raids are **3-player cooperative boss encounters** — the game's biggest multiplayer content. HOUND13 confirms co-op for up to 3 players in Raid, host-based and without regional restrictions.

**Unconfirmed:** raid names, boss rosters, mechanics, and loot. No verified source has published them — treat any list of raid names as speculation. Full coverage in the [Raids Guide](/guides/dungeon-raids).

## Karma Gear and the Gear Loop

**Karma gear** is the confirmed high-end gear tier, associated with World Bosses and the Tower of Trials (player-reported sources, not officially confirmed). Players describe it as having unique effects that change how skills work — no verified list of effects exists.

The endgame gear loop looks like this:

1. Clear the story (see the [Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough))
2. Farm World Bosses and Hunts for materials
3. Climb the Tower of Trials for high-tier gear
4. Run raids for co-op-exclusive rewards (details TBD)
5. Chase Karma gear as the endgame target

Exact drop sources, effects, and upgrade paths are **unconfirmed** — see the [Equipment Guide](/guides/equipment-guide) for what is known.

## The Rift System (Upcoming)

The **Rift System** is announced for post-launch — beyond that, nothing is verified. Claims about procedurally generated floors, leaderboards, or multiplayer scaling are **player speculation**. This guide will document the Rift when HOUND13 publishes details.

## Optimal Endgame Routine (Based on What Is Confirmed)

1. **Finish the story** — endgame systems unlock after the main quest line.
2. **Check the World Boss rotation daily** — farming the active boss is the community standard for materials.
3. **Push the Tower of Trials** — the deeper you climb, the better the rewards (exact loot TBD).
4. **Run Hunts** — the fastest way to practice boss patterns and farm crafting materials.
5. **Raid with friends** — 3-player co-op for the hardest confirmed content.
6. **Collect familiars** — including the free Abyssal Direwolf DLC if you do not have it (see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/familiars-guide)).

## Endgame Progression Path (Confirmed Structure)

1. **Clear the story** — endgame activities unlock after the main quest line (see the [Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough))
2. **Push Mercenary Ranks** — promotions gate content unlocks (see the [Leveling Guide](/guides/leveling-guide))
3. **Farm World Bosses** — the daily rotation is the community-standard loop
4. **Climb the Tower of Trials** — the vertical challenge and Karma gear source
5. **Run Hunts** — materials and boss practice
6. **Raid with friends** — the 3-player co-op capstone
7. **Collect familiars** — including the free Abyssal Direwolf DLC (see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/familiars-guide))

## Endgame Team Recommendations

The confirmed endgame-viable core:

- **Bleed Chain:** Theresia (Bleed/Stun) + Lute (Stun/Knockdown/heal) + Castella (Break/Stun) — the community-standard all-around team, works for World Bosses and Tower floors
- **Stun-Lock:** Castella + Theresia + Lute — control-heavy for learning hard fights
- **Ranged Pressure:** Aria (Burn/Airborne) slots in where distance helps
- **Swap in Ornette** (Electrocution/Shock, scythe) once her kit's exact behavior is documented — she adds a third confirmed ailment type to your options

More lineups in the [Team Compositions Guide](/guides/team-compositions).

## Common Endgame Mistakes

1. **Farming the wrong rotation** — always check which World Boss is active before committing time
2. **Skipping Hunts** — boss practice and materials come cheap here
3. **Trusting unverified gear tables** — specific relic names, drop rates, and floor rewards are mostly unconfirmed; this guide will publish them when verified
4. **Soloing raids unnecessarily** — raids are built for up to 3 players; a full party stacks ailments faster

## Bottom Line

The endgame is: Tower of Trials + World Bosses + Hunts + 3-player Raids + Karma gear, with the Rift System coming later. The systems are confirmed; the numbers are not. This guide will be updated as the community verifies specifics. For gear, see the [Equipment Guide](/guides/equipment-guide).
