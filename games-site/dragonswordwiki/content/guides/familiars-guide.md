## DragonSword: Awakening Familiars Guide

Familiars are more than just mounts in DragonSword: Awakening. They provide passive bonuses, combat assistance, and fast traversal across the open world. This **familiars guide** covers every obtainable familiar, how to unlock them, and which ones are worth your time.

## What Are Familiars?

Familiars are creatures you can summon as mounts and combat companions. Every familiar provides:

- **Rapid traversal** — Faster than running, some can fly or swim
- **Passive bonus** — Always active stat boost (even when not summoned)
- **Active skill** — Usable in combat on a cooldown
- **Cosmetic prestige** — Rare familiars are status symbols

You can equip up to 3 Familiars at once (summoned with 1, 2, 3 keys). Only one is active as a mount at a time.

## All Familiars at Launch

Approximately 30 **all familiars** are available in the launch version. Here is every one sorted by unlock method.

### Free Launch Rewards

| Familiar | Effect | How to Get |
|----------|--------|------------|
| **Abyssal Direwolf** | +8% movement speed, +5% ATK | Free launch reward (log in before August 22, 2026) |
| **Forest Sprite** | +5% HP regen | Complete the prologue |

The **Abyssal Direwolf** is the best launch reward. It is a fast ground mount with a useful combat active (howl that stuns nearby enemies for 2 seconds). If you bought the game at launch, you already have this — check your in-game mailbox.

### Deluxe Edition Exclusive

| Familiar | Effect | How to Get |
|----------|--------|------------|
| **Celestial Griffin** | +12% ATK, +15% gliding speed | Deluxe Edition ($44.99) |

The Celestial Griffin is exclusive to the Deluxe Edition. It can fly, making it the best traversal familiar in the game. If you did not buy the Deluxe Edition, you cannot obtain this one — but the gap between it and free alternatives is small for ground content.

## Best Familiars by Use Case

### Best for Combat

1. **Abyssal Direwolf** — Free, good stats, useful stun active
2. **Abyssal Phantom** — Best combat stats, but extremely rare (3% from Andras)
3. **Storm Eagle** — Excellent for aerial combat builds (Reina, Lute)
4. **Ember Fox** — Best for Burn-focused teams

### Best for Exploration

1. **Celestial Griffin** — Flight, fastest traversal (Deluxe Edition only)
2. **Wind Hawk** — Extended gliding, great for reaching high areas
3. **Tidal Serpent** — Water breathing, essential for underwater areas
4. **Crystal Moth** — Emits light in dark areas (extremely useful in caves)

### Best for Farming

1. **Fortune Cat** — Increases drop rate by 10% (best farming familiar)
2. **Golden Finch** — Increases gold drops by 15%
3. **Firefly Swarm** — Increases material drops by 10%

## Familiar Progression

Familiars have their own level and rarity system:

- **Common** (white) — Basic passives
- **Uncommon** (green) — 2 passives
- **Rare** (blue) — 2 passives + active skill
- **Epic** (purple) — 2 passives + strong active
- **Legendary** (orange) — 3 passives + powerful active

Familiars gain XP when summoned. Higher-level familiars have stronger passives. Focus on leveling one combat familiar and one exploration familiar first.

### Familiar Fusion

You can fuse duplicate familiars to increase their rarity:
- 3 copies of the same familiar = upgrade to next rarity tier
- Fusion preserves level
- Max rarity is Legendary

Start farming World Boss duplicates early if you want Legendary familiars.

## Quick Tips

- **Always have a familiar summoned** — even a basic one provides passives
- **Swap familiars by situation** — use combat mounts for bosses, exploration mounts for traversal
- **Feed your familiar** — Cooking items can temporarily boost familiar stats
- **The Abyssal Direwolf is enough** — for most content, the free launch reward is competitive with paid options
- **Familiars cannot be traded** — every familiar is account-bound on unlock
