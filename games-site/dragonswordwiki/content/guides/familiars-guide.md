## DragonSword: Awakening Familiars Guide

**Familiars are collectible companion creatures** in DragonSword: Awakening. HOUND13's official material describes them as companions that accompany your heroes — and the free **Abyssal Direwolf** DLC confirms they are a real, shipped system.

**Accuracy note:** earlier drafts of this guide (and many external guides) published lists of 30 familiars, drop rates, fusion systems, and cooking recipes. **None of that is verified.** This guide covers only what is confirmed and marks the rest TBD.

## What Is Confirmed

### Familiars Exist as a System

- The Steam page describes collectible companions called **Familiars**
- HOUND13 has shipped a free familiar DLC — the **Abyssal Direwolf** (a separate Steam app, installable from the store)
- Familiar content is planned post-launch — the free DLC model suggests more to come

### The Abyssal Direwolf (Free DLC)

The **Abyssal Direwolf** is the only familiar with verified official documentation:

- **How to get it:** it is a **free DLC** on the Steam store page for DragonSword: Awakening — you install it from Steam (search the game's store page for the DLC entries, or the Steam app page). It is a launch-period free add-on; reported in-game claim steps are in the [DLC & Add-Ons Guide](/guides/dlc-addons-guide).
- **What it does:** officially described as a familiar with documented traversal abilities (Boost Jump, stamina efficiency, movement speed) per the DLC's Steam store page; passive stat bonuses and whether it is a mount remain **unconfirmed**.
- **Who should get it:** everyone — it is free and adds familiar functionality to your account.

### Familiars in Gameplay

Unconfirmed specifics — this wiki will not publish invented numbers:

- Whether familiars serve as **mounts** (claimed but not verified)
- Whether they grant passive stat bonuses (claimed but not verified)
- How many familiars exist at launch (the "30 familiars" figure is not verified)
- How new familiars are obtained — World Boss drops, quests, exploration, and paid cosmetic DLC are all plausible sources, but none are officially confirmed

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I get the Abyssal Direwolf?**
Install the free "Familiar: Abyssal Direwolf" DLC from the game's Steam store page. If the game is already installed, it downloads with the DLC addition; the familiar becomes available in-game. Reported in-game claim steps are in the [DLC & Add-Ons Guide](/guides/dlc-addons-guide).

**Are familiars pay-to-win?**
No — the game has no gacha and no microtransactions for power. The Abyssal Direwolf is free, and any paid familiar DLC would be cosmetic-tier, per the game's design principles. Whether paid familiars exist at all is unconfirmed.

**Can I ride familiars?**
Mounting has not been officially confirmed. The game is an open-world action RPG where traversal is mostly on foot with gliding/exploration mechanics — treat "mount" claims as unverified.

**Do familiars fight?**
Combat assistance has not been officially confirmed. Treat this as TBD.

**Is the Abyssal Direwolf available forever?**
The DLC itself remains installable; whether it later becomes paid is unconfirmed. Install it now while it is free.

**What about the demo?**
The demo does not carry save data to the full game, so any familiar content earned in the demo does not transfer.

## Bottom Line

Familiars are a real, confirmed system — with one confirmed member: the free Abyssal Direwolf DLC. Everything else about the system (count, functions, acquisition) is still being documented, and this guide will be updated as information is verified.
