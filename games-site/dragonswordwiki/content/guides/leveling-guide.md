## DragonSword: Awakening Leveling Guide

Reaching max level and climbing the Mercenary Ranks efficiently requires knowing the right path. This **leveling guide** covers the fastest XP farming methods, promotion quests, and the optimal progression route from start to endgame.

## The Mercenary Rank System

Your overall character progression is measured through **Mercenary Ranks**, not traditional character levels. There are 9 ranks: F, E, D, C, B, A, S, SS, and SSS.

| Rank | Level Cap | Unlocks | Promotion Quest |
|------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| F | 10 | Basic skills | Tutorial (auto) |
| E | 15 | Relic slot, campfire cooking | Defeat 3 mini-bosses |
| D | 20 | Relic Dungeons, second accessory slot | Clear a Relic Dungeon |
| C | 25 | World Boss access, third Familiar slot | Defeat a World Boss |
| B | 30 | Tower of Trials Floor 1-10 | Clear Tower Floor 5 |
| A | 35 | Hunts, all gear slots | Complete 5 Hunts at difficulty III |
| S | 40 | Origin Dragons, Tower 11-25 | Clear Tower Floor 15 |
| SS | 45 | Rift System (future) | Clear Tower Floor 20 |
| SSS | 50 | Max level cap | Clear all content |

Each promotion quest is a solo challenge. You cannot swap heroes mid-quest, so prepare accordingly.

## Fastest XP Farming Methods

### 1. Main Story Quests (Levels 1-25)

Main story quests give the most XP per hour by a wide margin in the early game. Do not skip them.

- **Prologue + Chapter 1**: Takes 2-3 hours, gets you to Rank E (level 15)
- **Chapter 2**: Takes 4-5 hours, gets you to Rank D-C (level 20-25)
- **Chapter 3**: Takes 6-8 hours, gets you to Rank B (level 30)

### 2. World Boss Rotation (Levels 25-35)

Once you hit Rank C, World Bosses are the best XP source. Each kill gives roughly 10% of a level at Rank C, scaling down as you progress.

**Optimal route**: Check which World Boss is active. Form a party (Party Finder works well here). Farm 5-10 kills per session. Each kill takes 3-5 minutes with a good team.

### 3. Relic Dungeon Runs (Levels 20-30)

Relic Dungeons give excellent XP for their duration. A full clear takes 10-15 minutes and gives about 15% of a level.

**Best route**: Run the highest-level Relic Dungeon you can clear in under 15 minutes. Push difficulty as soon as you can handle it.

### 4. Hunts (Levels 30-40)

Hunts let you re-fight story bosses at higher difficulties. This is the fastest XP farm from Rank A onward.

- **Hunt Difficulty I**: 5% of a level (5 minutes)
- **Hunt Difficulty II**: 10% of a level (5-7 minutes)
- **Hunt Difficulty III**: 15% of a level (8-10 minutes)
- **Hunt Difficulty IV**: 20% of a level (10-15 minutes)
- **Hunt Difficulty V**: 30% of a level (15-20 minutes)

**Best Hunt for XP**: Re-fight the Grassland Lesser Dragon on Difficulty III. It is the fastest clear for the XP reward.

### 5. Tower of Trials (Weekly, Levels 35+)

The Tower of Trials resets weekly and gives massive one-time XP per floor. Do not clear floors early — wait until you are near the recommended level for maximum efficiency.

- Each floor gives 5-8% of a level on first clear
- Floors 1-10 are doable at Rank B (level 30)
- Floors 11-25 require Rank S (level 40+)

### Daily Routine for Maximum XP

| Activity | XP | Time | Frequency |
|----------|----|------|-----------|
| 3x Daily Dungeon Runs | ~30% of a level | 30 min | Daily |
| World Boss (daily rotation) | ~10% per kill | 15-30 min | Daily |
| Promotion Quest (if available) | 1 full rank | 20-30 min | One-time |
| Hunts (3-5 runs) | ~50-75% of a level | 30-45 min | Any time |
| Tower of Trials (weekly) | Huge burst | 1-2 hours | Weekly |

## Cooking and Crafting Buffs

Do not overlook consumables for faster leveling. These recipes significantly speed up XP gain:

| Recipe | Effect | Ingredients |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Warrior's Stew** | +15% XP for 30 min | Meat + Vegetable + Herb |
| **Sage's Tea** | +20% XP for 15 min | Herb x2 + Honey |
| **Elixir of Growth** | +30% XP for 10 min | Rare Herb + Magic Crystal + Gold |

Stack Warrior's Stew with Sage's Tea for a 35% XP bonus. Save Elixir of Growth for boss kills.

## Goddess Statues

Goddess Statues are permanent stat upgrades found throughout the world. Each statue gives +1 to a random stat (ATK, DEF, HP, or SP). They are worth seeking out.

### All Statue Locations

| Statue | Zone | Bonuses |
|--------|------|---------|
| Statue of Courage | Whispering Woods (near first boss arena) | ATK +1 |
| Statue of Wisdom | Skyspire City (library roof) | SP +2 |
| Statue of Fortitude | Frostpeak Mountains (cave entrance) | DEF +1, HP +5 |
| Statue of Valor | Burning Steppes (volcano base) | ATK +2 |
| Statue of the Sky | Skyspire Rock Mountain (summit) | All stats +1 |
| Statue of the Deep | Sunken Ruins (underwater chamber) | HP +10 |
| Statue of the Ancients | Secret area (requires climbing all statues) | All stats +3 |

Save the Statue of the Sky for last — activating it after all others grants an extra bonus.

## Exploration Rewards

Exploration gives flat XP bonuses the first time you discover each area. The bonus scales with the danger level of the zone.

| Zone Type | XP Bonus | Estimated Time |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Town/Outpost | 5% of level | 2 minutes |
| Field zone | 10% of level | 5-10 minutes |
| Hidden area | 15-25% of level | 10-20 minutes |
| Secret dungeon | 30-50% of level | 20-30 minutes |

Exploring every zone fully can give you 2-3 free levels worth of XP.

## Recommended Progression Path

### Fresh Start (0-2 hours)
1. Complete the prologue
2. Unlock Lute, Castella, Aria
3. Clear Chapter 1
4. Reach Rank E

### Early Game (2-8 hours)
1. Complete Chapter 2 main story
2. Unlock Theresia and a second DPS hero
3. Run each available dungeon once for first-clear bonuses
4. Reach Rank D

### Mid Game (8-20 hours)
1. Complete Chapter 3 main story
2. Start World Boss rotation farming
3. Unlock Relic Dungeons and farm your first full set
4. Reach Rank B

### Late Game (20-40 hours)
1. Unlock Hunts and farm for gear
2. Climb Tower of Trials floors 1-15
3. Unlock all 19 heroes (optional but recommended)
4. Reach Rank S

### Endgame (40+ hours)
1. Farm Origin Dragons for best gear
2. Climb Tower of Trials floors 16-25
3. Max out all Mercenary Ranks
4. Prepare for post-launch content (Rifts, new heroes)

## Leveling Tips Summary

- **Main story first** — it is the fastest XP until Rank C
- **Stack XP buffs** — cooking consumables are cheap and effective
- **Do your dailies** — daily dungeon runs are the most time-efficient XP
- **Join a party** — World Boss and dungeon parties give bonus party XP
- **Explore between quests** — discovery XP adds up significantly
- **Do not grind mobs** — they give minimal XP. Focus on quests and bosses
- **Push promotion quests** — each rank unlocks new content that gives more XP
