## DragonSword: Awakening Leveling Guide

Progression in DragonSword: Awakening is tracked through **Mercenary Ranks** — a rank ladder with **promotion quests** that gate new content. This guide covers what is confirmed about the system and the fastest reliable ways to progress, without inventing XP numbers.

**Accuracy note:** earlier drafts of this guide published a nine-rank ladder (F through SSS), level caps, XP-per-activity values, and cooking XP recipes. None of that is verified. This guide covers confirmed structure only.

## The Mercenary Rank System

**Confirmed:**

- Your overall progression is measured through **Mercenary Ranks**
- Each promotion requires completing a **promotion quest** — a solo challenge that tests your combat mastery
- Higher ranks unlock new abilities, gear access, and content (World Bosses, Tower of Trials, and other endgame systems are commonly cited as rank-gated)

**Unconfirmed:**

- The exact number of ranks and their names (F→S ladders published elsewhere are unverified)
- The specific requirements of each promotion quest
- Whether a numeric character level exists alongside ranks

The keyword research behind this wiki confirms strong search demand for "mercenary rank" and "promotion quest" — we will publish the verified ladder as soon as the community documents it.

## Fastest Progression Methods (Community-Confirmed Structure)

### 1. Main Story Quests

The main story is the fastest reliable progression in the early game — it delivers quest rewards, new regions, hero unlocks, and rank promotions in one stream. Do not skip it; the [Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough) maps the confirmed story beats.

### 2. Push Promotion Quests When Available

Each promotion quest is a one-time gate. Do them as soon as they appear — every rank up expands what you can do, which compounds your progression speed.

### 3. World Boss Rotation (Endgame)

Once endgame unlocks, the **World Boss rotation** is the community-standard farming loop — check which boss is active, farm it in co-op (see the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide)).

### 4. Tower of Trials and Hunts

The **Tower of Trials** (floor gauntlet) and **Hunts** (story boss re-fights at higher difficulty) are the other confirmed repeatable progression sources. Exact XP values are TBD.

## Cooking and Buffs

**Cooking is a confirmed side activity** — meals prepared at campfires from gathered ingredients. Whether cooking grants XP buffs or only combat/economy buffs is **unconfirmed**. Specific recipes and percentages published elsewhere are speculation.

## Exploration and Side Content

The official description confirms **treasure hunting**, **dungeon exploration**, and **solving residents' problems** as core activities. Exploration is a real progression vector: gear, materials, and hero recruitment live in the world, not just the quest log.

## What Does NOT Exist

- **No stat points** — heroes have fixed kits (see the [Character Builds Guide](/guides/character-builds))
- **No skill trees** — no confirmed skill point system
- **No paid boosts** — no gacha, no XP boosters, no cash shop
- **No "Goddess Statue" stat upgrades** — claims about statues granting permanent stats are unverified

## Recommended Progression Path

1. **Prologue + Chapter 1** — complete the tutorial, learn the switch loop, keep the starting trio
2. **Chapters 2–3** — follow the main story, do side quests as you pass them, push promotions when available
3. **Endgame** — clear the story, then run the World Boss rotation, Tower of Trials, Hunts, and raids

## Leveling Tips Summary

- **Story first** — it is the fastest and unlocks everything else
- **Promote early** — don't sit on a completed promotion quest
- **Explore between quests** — gear and materials come from the world
- **Farm the rotation** — endgame speed comes from matching the World Boss schedule
- **Learn the loop** — combat mastery (ailment stacking + Signal Skill timing) is the real XP multiplier; see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)

## Bottom Line

Progression = Mercenary Ranks + promotion quests + the story path + endgame farming. The ladder's exact shape is still being documented, but the structure is confirmed. For what unlocks at the end of the road, see the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide).
