## DragonSword: Awakening — Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ is **fact-checked against verified sources**. Where the answer is not yet known, it says so honestly (TBD) instead of guessing.

## Game Basics

### What is DragonSword: Awakening?

A buy-to-play, anime-style **open-world action RPG** developed by South Korean studio **HOUND13** (creators of *Hundred Soul*), built on **Unreal Engine 5**. You play **Lute**, a young boy who joins a mercenary band on the continent of **Orbis**, tracing the footsteps of the six Heroes of Old to stop a dragon that awakened after 60 years. It launched on Steam on **July 22, 2026**.

### How much does it cost?

**$29.99** for the base game, **$44.98** for the Deluxe Edition. The game is buy-to-play: **no gacha, no loot boxes, no microtransactions, no season pass**. Paid DLC is planned.

### What platforms is it on?

**PC via Steam** at launch. **PlayStation and Nintendo versions are officially underway** (a PS5 port has been specifically reported); release dates are TBD. Xbox has **not** been announced.

### Is DragonSword: Awakening pay-to-win?

No. There is no gacha, no premium currency for power, and no paid heroes. All **19 heroes** are earned through story progression and gameplay. The only paid content is the Deluxe Edition and cosmetic DLC (a beach costume pack has already shipped in patch 1.0.6).

### What are the system requirements?

The verified **minimum** specs: Windows 10, Intel Core i5-9400F, 8 GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 1660, DirectX 12, and ~60 GB of storage (SSD recommended). Recommended specs have not been officially published.

### Does it run on Steam Deck?

The **demo** is playable on Steam Deck but "may have technical issues," per the store listing. "Steam Deck Verified" status is **not confirmed**.

### Is there a demo?

Yes — a free demo is on Steam. **Save data does not carry over** to the full game.

## Heroes

### How many heroes are there at launch?

**19 playable heroes**. Four more free heroes (Ryza, Jerome, Veronica, Logan) are confirmed for the second half of 2026.

### Can I unlock all heroes without paying?

Yes — officially confirmed. All heroes and equipment are obtainable through story progression and in-game activities. See the [Hero Unlock Guide](/guides/hero-unlock-guide).

### Who is the best hero?

Early community consensus: **Theresia** (Bleed + Stun) is the strongest confirmed DPS, **Lute** is the most flexible support (confirmed ally heals), and **Castella** is the essential breaker. See the [Tier List](/guides/tier-list) for the full early-meta ranking.

### Who are the starting heroes?

**Lute, Castella, and Aria** — the confirmed starting trio. Note that Aria is a bomb-using firepower specialist, **not a healer** (a common misconception).

## Combat and Progression

### How does the tag-team system work?

You control one hero while others wait in reserve, and you **switch mid-combo** to trigger **Signal Skills** — the game's biggest damage source. Switching while a target is under a status ailment triggers the incoming hero's Signal Skill; there is no ultimate skill bar. See the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide).

### What are status ailments?

The confirmed ailments are **Stun, Break, Bleed, Burn, Airborne, Electrocution (Shock), and Knockdown**. Poison and Frost are **not confirmed** — guides that list them are unreliable.

### What is the max level?

Unconfirmed. Progression runs through the **Mercenary Rank** system with promotion quests; the exact ladder and cap are TBD (see the [Leveling Guide](/guides/leveling-guide)).

### How long is the main story?

Unconfirmed (community estimates of 15–20 hours are reasonable but unverified). The story spans the continent of Orbis across three commonly cited chapters, ending with the **Ursula** boss fight.

### Is there a New Game Plus?

**Not confirmed.** Post-story content (World Bosses, Tower of Trials, Hunts, raids) serves as the repeatable endgame instead.

### Can I respec my character?

There is no stat allocation or skill tree to respec — heroes have fixed kits (see the [Character Builds Guide](/guides/character-builds)).

## Familiars

### What are Familiars?

Collectible companion creatures. The **Abyssal Direwolf** is confirmed as a free DLC. Most system details (count, combat role, mounting) are **unconfirmed** — see the [Familiars Guide](/guides/familiars-guide).

### How do I get the Abyssal Direwolf?

Install the free "Familiar: Abyssal Direwolf" DLC from the game's Steam store page.

## Multiplayer and Co-op

### Is DragonSword: Awakening single-player?

The main story is single-player. **Co-op** exists for **Battle (up to 2 players)** and **Raid (up to 3 players)** — host-based sessions with no regional restrictions. See the [Co-op & Multiplayer Guide](/guides/pvp-guide).

### Is there PvP?

**No.** No PvP modes exist at launch, and none have been announced.

### Is there cross-play?

Cross-play with the upcoming console versions has **not been announced**.

## Technical and Support

### Is the game optimized?

It runs on Unreal Engine 5 and holds a "Very Positive" rating on Steam (~90% positive across 1,600+ English reviews). Individual performance reports vary by hardware.

### Can I play offline?

Not confirmed either way. The game's online features (co-op) require a connection; official documentation on offline play has not been published.

### What anti-cheat does the game use?

**Unconfirmed.** Claims about Easy Anti-Cheat are not verified.

### Will there be post-launch content?

Yes — confirmed roadmap: **four free heroes** (Ryza first, then Jerome, Veronica, Logan), **DLC plans**, and ongoing patches (a beach costume pack and patch 1.0.6 have already shipped). The **Rift System** has been announced as a post-launch feature; its details are unconfirmed.

## Community Questions

### Is the game worth buying?

Most players say yes: 200,000+ copies sold in under two weeks, "Very Positive" reviews, praised combat, and a no-gacha design. Common criticisms: no PvP and a story some players found shorter than expected.

### How does it compare to Genshin Impact or Wuthering Waves?

DragonSword: Awakening is buy-to-play with no gacha — all content is earned in-game. Combat is built around tag-team switching and Signal Skills rather than elemental reactions, and the open world is a single-player story package rather than a live service.

### Is there controller support?

Controller support exists (the game was shown with gamepad play), but an official supported-controller list is unconfirmed.

### Where can I find the community?

The Steam Community Hub is the most active hub; player-written guides there were the basis for parts of this wiki. HOUND13 also publishes news and patch notes on the official site and Steam.

### Does the game have achievements?

Steam achievements exist, but the count is **unconfirmed** — "50 achievements" claims are not verified.
