## DragonSword: Awakening — Frequently Asked Questions

This **FAQ** covers the most common questions about DragonSword: Awakening. If you are looking for something specific, use the categories below to jump to the right section.

## Game Basics

### What is DragonSword: Awakening?

DragonSword: Awakening is a buy-to-play anime open-world action RPG developed by Hound13. It features 19 unique heroes, a tag-team combat system, Signal Skills, Familiars, and no gacha mechanics. It was released on July 22, 2026.

### How much does DragonSword: Awakening cost?

The base game costs **$29.99** on Steam. A **Deluxe Edition** is available for **$44.99** which includes the Celestial Griffin familiar, an exclusive weapon skin set, and a digital art book.

### What platforms is it available on?

At launch, DragonSword: Awakening is available exclusively on **PC via Steam**. Console versions have been announced but no release date has been confirmed. Hound13 has stated that PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S versions are in development.

### Is DragonSword: Awakening pay-to-win?

No. There are no gacha mechanics, no loot boxes, and no premium currency for power. All 19 heroes are earnable through gameplay. The only paid items are cosmetic — the Deluxe Edition skin set and the Celestial Griffin familiar (which has a minor traversal advantage but no combat advantage over free alternatives).

### What are the system requirements?

**Minimum**:
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- CPU: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 3 3300X
- RAM: 8 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA GTX 1060 (6GB) / AMD RX 580 (8GB)
- Storage: 50 GB SSD

**Recommended**:
- OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit
- CPU: Intel Core i7-10700 / AMD Ryzen 5 5600X
- RAM: 16 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX 2070 Super / AMD RX 6700 XT
- Storage: 50 GB SSD

### Does it run on Steam Deck?

Yes. DragonSword: Awakening is **Steam Deck Verified**. It runs at 30-40 FPS on Medium settings at 800p. Expect occasional dips during heavy combat with multiple status effects. The default controller mapping works well.

### What languages are supported?

The game supports **English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, German, Spanish, and Portuguese** (text). Voice acting is available in **English, Japanese, and Korean**.

## Heroes

### How many heroes are there at launch?

**19 heroes** are available at launch. Hound13 has announced 4 additional free heroes coming in post-launch updates (Ryza, Jerome, Veronica, and Logan).

### Can I unlock all heroes without paying?

Yes. Every hero in the base game is earned through gameplay — main story, side quests, exploration, and secret dungeons. There are no paid hero unlocks.

### Who is the best hero?

**Theresia** is widely considered the best overall DPS hero. **Lute** is the most flexible support. **Reina** dominates aerial combat. See the [Hero Tier List](/guides/tier-list) for a full ranking.

### Can I change my starting hero selection?

You are not locked into a starting selection. The game gives you three starter heroes (Lute, Castella, Aria) automatically. You can unlock and switch to any other hero as you progress. There is no penalty for changing.

### How do I unlock more heroes?

Heroes are unlocked through:
- Main story progression (6 heroes)
- Side quest chains (5 heroes)
- Exploration and hidden locations (3 heroes)
- Secret dungeons (2 heroes)
- Other methods (rank achievement, item collection — 3 heroes)

For full details, check the [Hero Unlock Guide](/guides/hero-unlock-guide).

## Combat and Progression

### How does the tag-team system work?

You control one hero at a time with two in reserve. Press Tab to swap instantly. Swapping cancels animations, provides invincibility frames, and builds the Signal Gauge. Off-battle heroes regenerate HP and SP.

### What are Signal Skills?

Signal Skills are ultimate abilities activated when the Signal Gauge reaches 100%. Each hero has a unique Signal Skill. The gauge fills by dealing damage, breaking Super Armor, and swapping heroes.

### What is the max level?

The max Mercenary Rank is **SSS** (equivalent to level 50). Most story content can be cleared at Rank A (level 35). Endgame content requires Rank S (level 40) and above.

### How long does it take to beat the main story?

The main story (Prologue through Chapter 3) takes approximately **15-20 hours** for a first playthrough. Completing all side content adds another 10-15 hours. Endgame activities add 50-100+ hours of repeatable content.

### Is there a New Game Plus?

No New Game Plus mode is available at launch. However, you can replay story boss fights through the **Hunt** system at higher difficulties. Hound13 has indicated that New Game Plus is being considered for a future update.

### Can I respec my character?

Yes. Respeccing is available at any Goddess Statue for a small gold fee (500 gold at early levels, scaling up to 5000 at high levels). You can respect individual heroes or your entire roster.

## Familiars

### What are Familiars?

Familiars are mountable creatures that provide passive stat bonuses and an active combat skill. You can equip up to 3 and summon them with the 1, 2, 3 keys.

### How many Familiars are there?

Approximately **30 Familiars** are available at launch, earned through World Boss drops, exploration, cooking, and special events.

### How do I get the Abyssal Direwolf?

The **Abyssal Direwolf** is a free launch reward for logging in before **August 22, 2026**. Check your in-game mailbox after completing the prologue.

### Are any Familiars exclusive to the Deluxe Edition?

Yes. The **Celestial Griffin** is exclusive to the Deluxe Edition ($44.99). It provides faster traversal (flight) but has no combat advantage over free alternatives.

## Multiplayer and Co-op

### Is DragonSword: Awakening a single-player game?

The main story is fully playable solo. However, **World Bosses, Raids, and Tower of Trials** support up to 3-player co-op. You can use the in-game Party Finder to match with other players.

### Is there PvP?

No PvP modes are available at launch. Hound13 has not announced any plans for PvP content.

### Can I play the whole game co-op?

Only specific activities support co-op: World Bosses, Raids, and Tower of Trials. The main story is single-player only.

### Is there cross-play?

Since the game is PC-only at launch, cross-play is not applicable. Cross-play with future console versions has not been announced.

## Technical and Support

### Is the game optimized for PC?

DragonSword: Awakening runs on **Unreal Engine 5** and is generally well-optimized. Performance issues are rare but some users with minimum-spec GPUs report frame drops in the Burning Steppes zone. Hound13 has released a day-one patch addressing most launch-week issues.

### What anti-cheat does the game use?

The game uses **EAC (Easy Anti-Cheat)** which runs on the kernel level. It is installed automatically with the game.

### Can I play offline?

DragonSword: Awakening requires a persistent internet connection. There is no offline mode. The game saves progress to cloud servers.

### Will there be post-launch content?

Yes. Hound13 has announced a roadmap including:
- **Patch 1.1** (August 2026): New hero Ryza, balance adjustments, bug fixes
- **Patch 1.2** (September 2026): Rift System (infinite dungeon), hero Jerome
- **Patch 1.3** (October 2026): Hero Veronica, new World Bosses
- **Patch 1.4** (November 2026): Hero Logan, first story expansion

### Is there a physical edition?

No physical edition has been announced. The game is digital-only on Steam.

### How do I report a bug?

Use the in-game feedback tool (Escape menu -> Support -> Report Bug) or post on the official Hound13 Discord server. The developers are responsive during launch week.

## Community Questions

### Is the game worth buying?

Based on current Steam reviews (85% Very Positive), most players recommend it. The combat system is widely praised, the buy-to-play model is appreciated, and the content volume is considered fair for the $29.99 price point. Common criticisms include the lack of PvP and the relatively short main story.

### How does it compare to Genshin Impact or Wuthering Waves?

DragonSword: Awakening is a buy-to-play game with no gacha, unlike Genshin Impact and Wuthering Waves which are free-to-play with gacha mechanics. The combat system is more focused on tag-team swapping and Signal Skills. The open world is smaller but denser. The lack of PvP and limited co-op may disappoint players coming from those games.

### Is there controller support?

Yes. Full controller support is available. The game auto-detects controllers and provides on-screen button prompts. Xbox, PlayStation, and Switch Pro controllers are supported.

### Where can I find the community?

- **Official Discord**: Largest community hub, developer announcements, LFG channels
- **Steam Community Hub**: Guides, discussions, screenshots
- **Reddit**: r/DragonSwordAwakening — active community
- **Official Hound13 Website**: News and patch notes

### Does the game have achievements?

Yes. Steam has **50 achievements** ranging from story completion to challenging endgame tasks. Unlocking all heroes is an achievement.
