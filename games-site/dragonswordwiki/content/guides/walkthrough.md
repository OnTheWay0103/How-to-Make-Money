## DragonSword: Awakening Main Story Walkthrough

This **walkthrough** covers the entire main story of DragonSword: Awakening — from the Prologue to the final Chapter 3 boss. Follow this guide for boss strategies, key decision points, and all missable content.

## Prologue — The Awakening

### Opening Sequence

The game opens with Lute waking from a strange dream. A mysterious voice speaks of a "dragon's bloodline" and a "coming storm." These are not throwaway lines — they foreshadow the entire story.

### Tutorial Combat

Your first objective is to fight through a short forest path.

- **Enemies**: Forest Slimes (harmless) and Goblin Scouts (slow)
- **Objective**: Reach the town gate
- **Collectible**: 3x Basic Herb on the right side of the first clearing

### Orc Warrior Boss

The first boss is an Orc Warrior attacking a town gate.

**Phase 1**: Slow swings. Dodge the overhead slam (telegraphed by 2-second wind-up). Punish with 3-hit combos.

**Phase 2** (50% HP): Enrages. Faster attacks, new charge move. Side-step the charge.

**Reward**: 100 Gold, Basic Armor, Lute's first weapon upgrade

### Meet Castella and Johnny

After the fight, you meet **Castella**, a shield-maiden, and **Johnny**, a merchant who serves as the comic relief. Castella joins your party.

### Prologue Missables

- **Hidden chest** behind the collapsed building in town (right after the Orc fight) — contains 500 Gold
- **Johnny's first side quest** — "Delivery Run" — accept it immediately (limited time before Chapter 1)

## Chapter 1 — A New Beginning

### Skyspire Town

You arrive in **Skyspire Town**, the main hub for the first half of the game. Explore thoroughly:

- **Blacksmith**: Upgrade your weapon to +2 before proceeding
- **Inn**: Save at the Goddess Statue (unlocks fast travel)
- **Merchant**: Buy healing potions (carry at least 10)
- **Training Ground**: Optional tutorial for advanced combos

### Key Quests

**"The Missing Scouts"** — Main quest. Enter the Whispering Woods to find lost scouts.
- Enemies: Wolves, Poison Flowers, Bandits
- Mini-boss: Bandit Leader (easy, dodge his charge)
- **Missable**: North of the bandit camp, a glowing tree contains a Fairy Tear (permanent HP +5)

**"Aria's Request"** — Meet Aria at the forest shrine.
- She asks you to collect 3 Sacred Herbs
- These are on the ground near the shrine — do not leave without them
- After collecting, Aria joins your party
- **Tip**: There is an ambush after the cutscene — three wolf packs spawn. Keep Aria alive.

### Mysterious Swordsman Boss

A duel against a masked swordsman.

- **Phase 1**: Standard sword combos. He parries often — wait for his lunge, dodge, punish
- **Phase 2**: Adds throwing knives (blockable) and a spinning slash
- **Strategy**: Let him attack first. He is designed to punish aggression

**Reward**: Lute's unique Skill "Dragon Slash"

### Chapter 1 Missables

- **Fairy Tear** (+5 HP) — glowing tree north of bandit camp
- **Rusted Key** — behind the waterfall in Whispering Woods (opens chest in Skyspire Town)
- **Johnny's Inventory Expansion** — complete his 3-part side quest chain before leaving for Chapter 2

## Chapter 2 — Trials and Truths

### The Promotion Exam

You must pass the **Mercenary Promotion Exam** to advance. This is a solo challenge — no tag-team.

- **Fight 1**: 3 waves of wolves — conserve SP
- **Fight 2**: Shield Knight — learn to perfect dodge
- **Fight 3**: Examiner (human NPC) — aggressive duelist
- **Tip**: Focus on dodging, not attacking. The Examiner has a pattern you can exploit after 3 dodges

After passing, you meet **Saintess Ysera**, a mysterious figure who hints at Lute's dragon heritage.

### Meeting Lord Ewald

**Lord Ewald** is the ruler of Skyspire territory. He tasks you with investigating reports of a dragon sighting.

- **Choice**: Accept immediately or ask for more details (no gameplay impact, but asking reveals lore)
- **Side quests available**: 5 new quests — complete "Shadow of the Assassin" to unlock Charlotte

### The Fury of Furcas

**Furcas** is a demonic knight blocking the path to the Grassland.

- **Attack patterns**: Flame slash (dodge sideways), fire pillar (back away when sword raises), charge (side-step)
- **Strategy**: Frost effects from Kalien double break damage
- **Recommended team**: Lute + Theresia (unlocked in this chapter) + Aria

### Vanak — The Armored Beast

A giant beast in the Frostpeak Mountain pass.

- **Stay behind him** — his frontal attacks are devastating
- **Break his back legs** — this topples him for free damage
- **Watch for the tail sweep** — jump over it

### Skyspire Rock Mountain — Compass Pieces

To find Barduk, you need to assemble a compass from 3 pieces:

1. **Compass Piece 1**: Frostpeak Caverns — in a chest guarded by Frost Wyrms
2. **Compass Piece 2**: Whispering Woods — Collapsed Temple basement
3. **Compass Piece 3**: Hidden alcove on Skyspire Rock Mountain — platforming section with collapsing platforms

### Grassland Lesser Dragon Boss

The **Grassland Lesser Dragon** is the hardest boss so far.

**Phase 1** (100-50% HP):
- Fire breath (wide cone — run sideways)
- Tail sweep (jump over)
- Wing gust (dodge through)

**Phase 2** (50-0% HP):
- Aerial fire barrage (run in circles)
- Ground slam with shockwave (jump timing)
- Enrage at 20% (faster attacks, less downtime)

**Critical Strategy**: Break both front legs to topple the dragon. Each leg has its own Super Armor bar. While toppled, the dragon takes double damage for 8 seconds.

**Reward**: Dragon Scale (crafting material), Lute's half-dragon transformation teased

### Chapter 2 Missables

- **Charlotte's quest chain** — available only during Chapter 2. Start it before the Grassland Lesser Dragon fight
- **Reina's unlock** — Skyspire Spire is accessible after getting the compass. The climb is optional but worth it
- **Hidden Goddess Statue** — Frostpeak Caverns secret room (break a cracked wall)

## Chapter 3 — The Dragon's Legacy

### Finding Barduk

Barduk is hiding in the **Burning Steppes**, a volcanic region north of Skyspire.

- **First**: Talk to the refugee camp leader (gives context)
- **Second**: Clear the Burning Sanctum (optional but recommended for fire resistance gear)
- **Third**: Confront Barduk at the Volcanic Caldera

### Barduk Boss Fight

**Phase 1 — Human**:
- Fast sword combos with fire enhancements
- Summons fire minions every 30 seconds
- **Strategy**: Kill minions immediately, break armor quickly

**Phase 2 — Dragon Transformation**:
- Barduk turns into a corrupted dragon
- Massive AoE attacks
- Flight phases where he is untouchable
- **Strategy**: Wait for him to land, break Super Armor, mount him

**Phase 3 — Berserk**:
- Fire tornado attack fills the arena
- All attacks 30% faster
- **Strategy**: Save Signal Skills for this phase. Burn him down

After the fight, **Lute undergoes his half-dragon transformation**, unlocking new abilities and a permanent stat boost.

### Lich

An undead mage in the **Sunken Ruins**.

- **Strategy**: Kill summons quickly. Physical damage breaks the Lich faster than magic
- **Watch for**: Curse circles on the ground. They do massive damage if you stand in them

### Ogre Slaughterer

A berserker ogre in the **Crimson Valley**.

- **Enrage mechanic**: Deals increasing damage over time. Bring Aria
- **Strategy**: Patience. Only hit 2-3 times between his attacks
- **Grab attack**: Instant kill at low HP. Dodge sideways

### Gauron

A fallen hero corrupted by dark energy.

- **Shadow clones**: Attack the one with the darker aura
- **Teleport slash**: Perfect dodge for a massive opening
- **Toughest humanoid fight in the game**

### Ursula — Final Boss

The goddess of destruction awaits at the **Celestial Spire**.

**Phase 1** — Giant energy swords, light beams, gravity wells
- Stay mobile. Gravity wells pull you in — dodge away immediately

**Phase 2** — Angelic minions, arena-wide light attacks
- Prioritize the healers in the minion wave

**Phase 3** — Winged colossus transformation
- Blue platforms are safe zones
- Everything else deals damage
- DPS check — if you cannot kill her in 3 minutes, the enrage wipes you

**Reward**: Lute's full dragon power unlocked, Origin Relic access, Mercenary Rank S title

### Chapter 3 Missables

- **All 5 Dragon Shrine puzzles** — these unlock the Tiny Dragon familiar. Spread across the zone
- **Eileen's side quest** — available in the refugee camp only before the Barduk fight
- **Cerese poison vials** — farmable from specific enemies in this chapter

## Post-Story Content

After clearing the story, the full endgame unlocks:

- **Tower of Trials** (all 25 floors)
- **Hunts** (re-fight bosses at higher difficulties)
- **Origin Dragons** (Vaeldris, Frostmaw, Void)
- **All World Bosses** become available

The game also hints at future content — a post-credits scene shows a shadowy figure collecting dragon blood, setting up the first major story expansion.
