## DragonSword: Awakening Main Story Walkthrough

DragonSword: Awakening tells the story of **Lute**, a young boy who falls in with a mercenary band and ends up tracing the footsteps of the **six Heroes of Old** across the continent of **Orbis** — the legendary figures who drove back the dragon 60 years before the game's events. This walkthrough covers the **confirmed story structure**: verified characters, verified boss encounters, and the narrative framework. Specific quest names, side objectives, and zone layouts are still being documented and are marked **TBD** where unknown.

**Accuracy note:** earlier drafts of this guide (and many external guides) published chapter-by-chapter quest names, locations like "Skyspire Town," and missable-item lists. **None of that was verified** and has been removed. This walkthrough only states what the community has actually confirmed, and will grow as documentation matures.

## Prologue — The Awakening

**Confirmed beats:**

- The game opens with **Lute** and the tutorial combat — the first hours teach the basics of the tag-team system
- You meet **Johnny** (a mercenary) and **Castella** (an Elf) — Lute's companions and traveling party
- Your first boss is an **Orc Warrior** attacking a town gate — a tutorial-style encounter
- The prologue sets up the premise: the dragon has awakened after 60 years, and the party will trace the six Heroes of Old

**TBD:** exact tutorial objectives, item pickups, and optional content in the opening area.

## Chapter 1 — The Path Begins

**Confirmed beats:**

- The party joins the mercenary life on the continent of **Orbis**
- The roster expands toward the starting trio — **Aria** (bomb-using firepower specialist) joins the party early
- A boss encounter with **Furcas**, a demonic knight, is confirmed in the early story

## Chapter 2 — Trials

**Confirmed beats:**

- The **Grassland Lesser Dragon** boss fight — the first dragon encounter and the community's cited first difficulty spike. Players describe a leg-breaking mechanic: break the dragon's legs to topple it for a damage window (details TBD)
- The **Mercenary Rank** promotion system enters the picture, gating further progress (see the [Leveling Guide](/guides/leveling-guide))

**TBD:** chapter quest structure, exploration content, and hero recruitment specifics.

## Chapter 3 — The Dragon's Legacy

**Confirmed beats:**

- **Barduk**, a corrupted dragon rider, is the late-story boss — player footage shows a human phase and a dragon transformation phase
- A **Lich** boss is confirmed in the late story
- The story concludes with **Ursula** — the final boss, a three-phase fight that community reports describe as the hardest encounter in the main story

**TBD:** the exact route, intermediate bosses, and story cutscene details between these confirmed encounters.

## The Six Heroes of Old

A key narrative structure: the six Heroes of Old are the legendary figures who defeated the dragon 60 years ago. **They are not playable** — they exist as a lore framework that Lute's party traces across Orbis. Expect their histories, trails, and legacy to structure the chapter flow. (Which heroes they correspond to, if any, is TBD.)

- **World Bosses** — open-world bosses on a rotation

- **Hunts** — re-fight story bosses at higher difficulty

## Honest Guidance for Players

Because the detailed quest structure is still being documented, the best walkthrough advice right now is:

1. **Follow the story marker** — the main quest chain is linear; the game points you where you need to go
2. **Explore between story beats** — the official description confirms treasure hunting, hidden dungeons, and residents' problems as core content; the world is where gear and extra heroes live
3. **Master the combat loop before bosses** — the Apply → Extend → Finish loop (see the [Combat Guide](/guides/combat-guide)) matters more than any quest detail
4. **Bring the confirmed boss team** — Theresia + Lute + Castella covers Bleed, Stun, Break, and healing for every verified boss fight (see the [Team Compositions Guide](/guides/team-compositions))
