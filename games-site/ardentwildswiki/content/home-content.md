# Ardent Wilds Wiki — Complete Guide Hub

**Everything you need to survive, build, and conquer in the open-world survival crafting RPG by Spellware Studios.**

Ardent Wilds launched its free demo on July 30, 2026 on Steam. Described by playtesters as "3D Terraria," this game combines a fully destructible voxel world, a unique world-built skill tree, deep base building, arcade combat, and 6-player co-op into one ambitious indie package. This wiki covers all core systems for the demo build — and will expand as the full game releases.

## Why Ardent Wilds?

- **World-Built Skill Tree** — Skill nodes are physical locations in the world. Restore ancient spires and forgotten facilities to unlock powers, passive bonuses, and fast-travel points.
- **Fully Destructible Voxel World** — Dig, flatten, tunnel, and reshape the entire terrain. Bombs and Pyroblasts leave craters. The Tome of Ice creates frost bridges over canyons.
- **Deep Base Building** — Grow crops, cook meals, brew elixirs, craft armor and weapons, and build your dream fantasy home. Fortify against Eldran attacks.
- **Arcade Combat with No Classes** — Play as a fireball wizard, deadly warrior, or precision marksman — or mix all three. Rare Artifacts unlock unique abilities.
- **6-Player Co-op** — Build together, fight together, survive together. Full online and LAN support with shared bases.
- **Full Modding Support** — Lua API with all game scripts accessible. The community can create anything.

## What is Ardent Wilds?

Ardent Wilds is an open-world survival crafting RPG developed and published by Spellware Studios, a small indie team based in Ghent, Belgium. Players must cleanse the land of the Eldran, an encroaching eldritch hivemind, and ultimately challenge their god: Yorgoroth, the Devourer of Worlds.

The game stands out with its "world-built" skill tree — instead of opening a menu to spend points, you physically explore the map to find and restore ancient spires that unlock your powers. Combined with a fully destructible voxel world, deep crafting and base building, fast arcade combat with artifact-based builds, and co-op for up to 6 players, Ardent Wilds aims to be what playtesters call "the 3D Terraria we always wanted."
