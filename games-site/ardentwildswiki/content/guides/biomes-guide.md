# Biomes & Resource Locations Guide — Every Region Mapped

Ardent Wilds features five distinct biomes in the demo build, each with unique resources, enemies, environmental hazards, and visual identity. Knowing which biome to visit for which resource is essential for efficient progression.

## Biome Overview

| Biome | Difficulty | Key Resources | Key Enemies | Hazard |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-------------|--------|
| **Verdant Basin** | ★ | Wood, Stone, Fiber, Copper, Flint | Corrupted Wildlife, Wisps | None (safest biome) |
| **Crystal Caverns** | ★★ | Crystal, Iron, Coal, Gold | Crystal Golems, Alchemical Horrors | Darkness, cave-ins |
| **Mountain Pass** | ★★★ | Obsidian, Gold, Iron, Coal | Lava Serpents, Frozen Colossi | Extreme temperatures |
| **Deep Forest** | ★★★ | Ancient Wood, Hardwood, Herbs, Mushrooms | Shadow Stalkers, Ancient Treants | Permanent darkness |
| **Eldran Wastes** | ★★★★★ | Eldran Shards, Primal Essence, Crystal | Void Knights, all Eldran types | Constant Eldran presence |

## Verdant Basin (Starting Biome)

The safest and most forgiving biome. This is where you spawn and where you should build your first base.

### Resources

| Resource | Abundance | Best Locations |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Wood | Very High | Everywhere — trees are abundant |
| Stone | High | Riverbanks, hillsides |
| Fiber | Very High | Open fields, bushes everywhere |
| Flint | Medium | Riverbeds, gravel patches near water |
| Copper | Medium | Hills on the northern and eastern edges |
| Seeds | High | Gather from wild vegetables scattered throughout |

### Enemies

- **Corrupted Wildlife** — Wolves, bears, boars. Aggressive at night, passive during day unless approached.
- **Eldran Wisps** — Spawn at night near water. Ranged attackers.
- **Root Horrors** — Stationary, found near corrupted trees. Easy to avoid.

### Notable Locations

- **Spires #1, #2, #3** — The first three Spires are all in this biome
- **Abandoned Farm** — Forgotten Facility that boosts crop yield
- **River Delta** — Excellent base location, abundant fish

### Best Gathering Route

Start at the river in the center. Follow it east → collect Stone + Flint from riverbed. Loop north through the hills → collect Copper. Return through the western fields → collect Fiber + Seeds. This 5-minute loop yields all basic materials.

## Crystal Caverns (Underground Biome)

Accessed through cave entrances in Verdant Basin and Mountain Pass. This biome is entirely underground — bring Torches or Night Vision potions.

### Resources

| Resource | Abundance | Best Locations |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Crystal | High | Large open chambers, Spire ruins |
| Iron | Very High | Cave walls, concentrated in deeper sections |
| Coal | Very High | Dark stone deposits everywhere |
| Gold | Low | Deepest chambers, near lava pools |
| Stone | High | Everywhere — you are inside a mountain |

### Enemies

- **Crystal Golems** — High defense, slow. Weak to Fire and Lightning. Guard the best Crystal deposits.
- **Alchemical Horrors** — Split at 50% HP. AoE attacks are essential.
- **Cave Crawlers** — Small, fast, swarm in groups. Use sweeping weapons.

### Environmental Hazard: Cave-ins

Mining too aggressively (removing 5+ connected tiles in a short time) can trigger cave-ins. Falling rocks deal 40 damage. Leave support pillars when mining extensively.

### Notable Locations

- **Spires #4, #5** — Deeper in the caverns
- **Forgotten Forge** — Boosts crafting speed
- **Crystal Grove** — The densest Crystal concentration, but guarded by 3 Crystal Golems

## Mountain Pass (High-Altitude Biome)

Accessed from the northern edge of Verdant Basin. Vertical terrain — bring the Grappling Hook or be prepared to build extensively.

### Resources

| Resource | Abundance | Best Locations |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Obsidian | Medium | Near volcanic vents at the summit |
| Iron | High | Exposed cliff faces |
| Gold | Medium | River deposits at the base |
| Coal | High | Throughout, especially near vents |

### Enemies

- **Lava Serpents** — Fire-immune. Use Ice or Void weapons. Spawn near volcanic vents.
- **Frozen Colossi** — Slow, devastating melee attacks. Weak to Fire. Found near the summit.
- **Harpies** — Flying enemies. Use ranged weapons or Lightning. Nest on high cliffs.

### Environmental Hazard: Extreme Temperatures

- **Near lava:** Constant Fire damage (5/sec). Fire Resistance Potion required for extended stays.
- **Near summit:** Constant Cold damage (3/sec), movement speed reduced 20%. Bring Warm Clothing (crafted at Loom: 5 Fiber + 2 Leather).

### Notable Locations

- **Spires #6, #7** — Two of the most powerful Spires
- **Old Observatory** — Reveals all Spire locations (priority restoration)
- **Summit Peak** — Highest point in the demo. Amazing base location if you can reach it.

## Deep Forest (Dark Biome)

Accessed from the eastern edge of Verdant Basin. The darkest biome — permanent twilight even during daytime.

### Resources

| Resource | Abundance | Best Locations |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Ancient Wood | Medium | Ancient Trees (massive, glowing trees scattered throughout) |
| Hardwood | High | Dense forest sections |
| Herbs | Very High | Forest floor, especially near water |
| Mushrooms | Very High | Dark corners, fallen logs, cave entrances |

### Enemies

- **Shadow Stalkers** — Invisible until they attack. Use AoE or Lantern of Revealing to detect them. Most dangerous enemy outside of Eldran Wastes.
- **Ancient Treants** — The most durable non-boss enemies. Extremely slow — you can outrun them easily.
- **Corrupted Wildlife (enhanced)** — Stronger versions of Verdant Basin enemies. +50% HP and damage.

### Environmental Hazard: Permanent Darkness

Vision is reduced to ~10 tiles. Without a light source, enemies are nearly invisible. Always bring:
- Torches (cheap, short range)
- Lantern of Revealing artifact (reveals invisible enemies)
- Night Vision Elixir (best option — full vision for 20 min)

### Notable Locations

- **Spires #8, #9** — Shadow Step and Regeneration — both extremely valuable
- **Ancient Library** — XP gain boost
- **The Ancient Tree** — Largest landmark in the demo. Visible from afar at night (it glows). Surrounded by Ancient Wood.

## Eldran Wastes (Endgame Biome)

The most dangerous biome. Accessed from the far north, past Mountain Pass. Do not enter without full Iron gear, multiple potions, and ideally a co-op partner.

### Resources

| Resource | Abundance | Best Locations |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Eldran Shards | Medium | Dropped by all Eldran enemies |
| Primal Essence | Very Low | Near Void Rifts (rare spawns), boss drops |
| Crystal | High | Corrupted crystal formations |

### Enemies

All enemies here are Eldran-type and deal +30% damage compared to their normal counterparts. Key enemies:

- **Void Knights** — Teleport, heal from Eldran pools on the ground. Use Void weapons to interrupt their healing. The most dangerous non-boss enemy.
- **Eldran Colossi** — Massive, slow, devastating. Same attack patterns as Frozen Colossi but void-empowered.
- **Corrupted Wisps (Eldran)** — Faster, more aggressive versions of normal Wisps.

### Environmental Hazard: Constant Eldran Presence

- Eldran enemies spawn continuously — you cannot "clear" an area
- Eldran corruption slowly damages non-Eldran structures (your base will decay here)
- Void Rifts occasionally open, spawning waves of enemies

### Notable Locations

- **Spire #10 (Spire of the Void)** — The final and most powerful Spire
- **Ruined Shrine** — Once-per-day full heal
- **Yorgoroth's Gate** — Boss arena. The ultimate challenge in the demo.

## Biome Transition Tips

1. **Gear check before entering a new biome** — Make sure your tools and weapons are appropriate for the tier
2. **Build outposts at biome borders** — A small base with a bed, chest, and campfire gives you a safe retreat point
3. **Use Spire fast-travel to hop between biomes** — The distance between biomes becomes negligible once you have 3+ Spires
4. **Bring the right potions** — Each biome has a specific environmental hazard. Prepare accordingly.
