# Biomes & Resource Locations Guide — Every Region Mapped

**Please read this first:** Spellware Studios has **not released an official biome list** for Ardent Wilds. The world is **procedurally generated** — the April 2026 playtest switched to fully custom world generation, so no two worlds are the same. Any guide (including older versions of this one) that names specific regions like "Verdant Basin" or lists exact Spire locations is describing community-made terms or assumptions, not official game content. Those names will almost certainly not match your world.

What we do know for certain: the game world includes **surface wilderness, ancient caves, and underground mines** with deadly traps, fierce enemies, hidden secrets, loot, and rare materials — all confirmed by the developers. This guide covers how to explore that world efficiently and safely, without pretending to have a map we don't have.

## How Ardent Wilds Worlds Work

Before planning any route, understand the confirmed mechanics:

- **Custom world generation:** every world is generated fresh. Seeds produce different terrain layouts (confirmed by the April 2026 playtest update).
- **Fully destructible voxel terrain:** you can dig, tunnel, and reshape everything. Terrain is a tool, not a barrier — if you cannot find a route, you can make one.
- **Underground is content:** ancient caves and underground mines hold the rare materials, artifacts, and loot — and the traps and fierce enemies that guard them.
- **Fast travel changes everything:** restoring **Spires** (part of the world-built skill tree) unlocks fast-travel points on your map, traveled via Aether Rays. The map's geography matters less with every Spire you restore.

## What to Expect from Surface Regions

While the exact biome roster is unconfirmed, the demo and playtest footage consistently show a few region archetypes. Treat these as general expectations, not named locations:

| Region Type | Expect | Typical Resources |
|-------------|--------|-------------------|
| **Starting wilderness** | The safest area; where you begin and where your first base should be | Wood, stone, fibers, basic food sources |
| **Deep forest** | Denser trees, darker canopy, stronger wildlife | Hardwoods, herbs, mushrooms |
| **Highlands / mountains** | Vertical terrain, tougher creatures, harsher conditions | Ore, stone, high-altitude materials |
| **Caverns / underground mines** | Traps, enemies, darkness — the high-risk/high-reward zone | Rare ores, artifacts, loot, rare materials |
| **Corrupted zones** | Heavy Eldran presence, constant pressure, dangerous ground | Eldran-tied materials (if the current build has them) |

## The Underground: Caves and Mines

Caves and underground mines are the confirmed endgame of exploration. What the developers have said: they contain **deadly traps, fierce enemies, hidden secrets**, and rewards of **loot, rare materials, and artifacts**.

### Preparing to Go Underground

1. **Light.** Darkness is a killer. Bring torches or whatever light sources the current build offers, and know how to craft them before you descend.
2. **Tools.** Digging tools for clearing blocked passages, and a weapon you trust.
3. **Food and potions.** Long expeditions drain supplies; bring enough to retreat comfortably.
4. **Mark your route.** Tunnel entrances and landmarks are easy to lose in the dark. With the voxel dig tool, consider digging a straight escape tunnel back to daylight.
5. **A respawn point nearby.** If there is a bed you can place, a surface camp near the cave entrance saves a long walk after a death.

### In the Mines

- **Trap discipline:** the developers confirm traps exist. Move carefully in unexplored passages — watch the floor and walls.
- **Loot is the reward:** rare materials and artifacts are the reason to be down here. Check dead ends and suspicious rooms; the good stuff is often guarded.
- **Do not mine everything:** in voxel games, aggressive excavation can destabilize terrain. Leaving support pillars while strip-mining is cheap insurance — exact cave-in behavior in Ardent Wilds is unconfirmed, but the habit costs almost nothing.
- **Eldran presence:** corrupted zones underground are worse than on the surface. If the corruption density spikes, expect stronger enemies.

## Finding Rare Materials

Confirmed: rare materials exist and are found in the deep places. Unconfirmed: the full material list and exact spawn patterns. The reliable method is:

1. **Follow the terrain.** Ore and rare materials cluster in specific terrain types (caves, mines, highlands). Learn to read your world.
2. **Depth pays.** In most voxel survival games, deeper = rarer. Push downward (carefully).
3. **Guardians guard.** Valuable deposits are often protected by tougher enemies. Gear up before clearing a promising vein.
4. **Check every structure.** Ruins, shrines, and facilities (the "forgotten facilities" of the skill-tree system) are placed by design and usually hide something good.
5. **Ask your map.** Restore Spires to reveal more of the world and plan efficient loops.

## Gathering Route Strategy

Because every world differs, memorize principles instead of routes:

- **One loop per resource.** Plan a circuit that collects one resource type efficiently rather than wandering for everything at once.
- **Surface basics near base.** Wood, stone, and fiber should never require a long trip — keep them within minutes of home.
- **Rare stuff on expeditions.** Treat rare-material runs as planned expeditions: full supplies, daytime start, clear goal, known exit.
- **Outposts for distant regions.** When you start farming a distant area, build a small outpost there: bed, chest, campfire, and a crafting station. It converts a dangerous trip into a quick one.

## Biome Transition Tips

1. **Gear check before entering new territory** — make sure your tools and weapons are appropriate for the difficulty you expect
2. **Build outposts at region borders** — a small base with a bed, chest, and campfire gives you a safe retreat point
3. **Restore Spires for fast travel** — the distance between regions becomes negligible once you have several fast-travel points
4. **Bring the right consumables** — each region type has hazards (cold at altitude, darkness underground, corruption in Eldran zones). Prepare accordingly, and treat any specific hazard numbers you see online as unconfirmed
