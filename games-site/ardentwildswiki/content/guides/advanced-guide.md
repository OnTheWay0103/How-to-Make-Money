## Advanced Guide — Late-Game Survival & Raid Defense

Late-game Ardent Wilds is defined by one event: the Eldran raid waves that assault your base on a schedule. Surviving them requires defensive design, prepared resource stockpiles, and a team that knows its role. This guide covers raid defense, spire completion, and the advanced crafting that keeps a base alive.

### Eldran Raid Waves

| Wave | Night | Composition | Threat |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|
| Scout Party | Night 7 | 3 raiders | Low |
| Raid Party | Night 14 | 6 raiders + 1 elite | Medium |
| Siege Wave | Night 21 | 10 raiders + 2 elites | High |
| Warchief Assault | Night 28+ | 15 raiders + Warchief | Extreme |

1. **Wave schedule:** Eldran attacks land every 7 nights after the first scout party. Prepare stockpiles the day before.
2. **The Warchief:** The Warchief ignores walls and targets players — assign a dedicated kiter for it.
3. **Elite types:** Siege elites break walls; a single wall gap lets the whole wave pour in.

### Base Defense Layouts

| Layout | Strength | Weakness | Best For |
|--------|----------|----------|----------|
| Ring Fort | All-round defense | Expensive | Solo bases |
| Choke Point | Cheap, funnel kills | Vulnerable flanks | Co-op bases |
| Tower Stack | Max DPS output | Fuel hungry | Late-game bases |
| Hidden Core | PvP and raid safe | Slow expand | Endgame |

- **Wall priority:** Upgrade the walls facing the raid approach path first — waves always approach from the same direction.
- **Turret spacing:** Place turrets 3 tiles apart; AoE elite attacks hit clustered turrets simultaneously.
- **Repair kit stockpile:** Keep 20+ repair kits per player before wave 21.

### Ancient Spire Completion

- **Restoration order:** Restore the fast-travel spire first, then the defense spire, then combat spires — travel pays off every day.
- **Completed spire bonuses:** Fully restored spires grant permanent global buffs (damage, defense, gathering speed).
- **Forgotten Facilities:** These contain the highest-tier crafting recipes; clear them between raid waves.
- **Skill tree synergy:** Spire bonuses stack with skill tree passives — plan both around your playstyle.

### Advanced Crafting

1. **Fuel economy:** Fuel upgrades on furnaces cut smelting costs by up to 40% — prioritize before mass crafting.
2. **Set crafting:** Endgame gear sets (from Forgotten Facilities) beat random epics in every slot.
3. **Consumable rotation:** Battle potions and repair kits share a slot — stock both, use potions only during waves.

### Co-op Role Strategy

| Role | Duties | Best Skills |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Defender | Walls, turrets, aggro control | Defense tree, taunt |
| Hunter | Elite kills, Warchief kiting | Combat tree, mobility |
| Gatherer | Resources between waves | Gathering tree, speed |
| Builder | Repairs, upgrades mid-wave | Building tree, auto-repair |
