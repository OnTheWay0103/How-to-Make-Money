# Story & Quests Guide — How the Ardent Wilds Campaign Works

Ardent Wilds has a clear campaign spine, even though the game presents it through world exploration rather than a traditional quest journal: **push back the Eldran, restore the ancient spires, and grow strong enough to challenge their god.** If you are asking "what am I supposed to do next?", this guide lays out the confirmed progression path from first steps to the endgame confrontation.

**Honesty first:** Spellware Studios has not confirmed a classic quest system — no official quest log, journal, or mission list has been announced. The story structure below comes from the official game description, developer announcements, and the closed-alpha content list. Treat any detailed "main quest walkthrough" you find elsewhere with suspicion; there is no confirmed quest-by-quest breakdown yet.

## The Story Setup

The premise, straight from the official description: the world of Ardent Wilds is being consumed by the **Eldran**, "an encroaching eldritch hivemind" that spreads corruption across the land. Your job is to cleanse it — and eventually challenge the god of the Eldran: **Yorgoroth, the Devourer of Worlds**.

The setting has a clear past: the world was built on the **Aetherium**, an ancient civilization whose structures — spires and forgotten facilities — are scattered across the map. The Eldran's corruption covers this legacy, and the campaign is about taking it back.

## The Confirmed Campaign Goal

The endgame and the story are the same thing. Confirmed from the official description and developer materials:

1. **Restore the ancient spires.** The full-game goal is rebuilding the **four ancient magical spires** to push back the Eldran (community reports cite a count of four; treat exact numbers as provisional)
2. **Explore forgotten facilities.** Spires and facilities together form the world-built skill tree — each restored node grants a passive bonus
3. **Reclaim territory.** Every spire you restore pushes the corruption back and opens new areas
4. **Challenge Yorgoroth.** Growing strong enough to fight the Devourer of Worlds is the confirmed endgame confrontation

The skill tree guide covers the mechanical side; the story side is simple: **the campaign is a reclamation war, and each objective is a location on the map.**

## The Progression Loop (What You Actually Do)

There is no confirmed main-quest list, but the closed-alpha content and the demo give a clear picture of how the campaign opens. The alpha was announced to cover: completing the intro, building your base, crafting and discovering better gear, and ending with a boss fight. That maps to a loop you will repeat all game:

| Stage | What Happens | Why It Matters |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Intro & tutorial** | Learn combat, gathering, and survival basics | Confirmed alpha content |
| **First base** | Establish a shelter with crafting stations | The hub of the whole game |
| **Gear up** | Craft and loot weapons/armor, unlock artifacts | Survival in harder zones |
| **Clear a region** | Fight Eldran enemies, dungeons, and a regional boss | Opens the next spire/facility |
| **Restore a spire** | Activate the structure, gain a passive and a fast-travel node | The story advances |
| **Repeat, deeper** | Escalate into harder biomes and threats | Leads to Yorgoroth |

Story progression and survival progression are fused: you do not "unlock the next quest" by talking to an NPC — you grow strong enough to clear the next corrupted region.

## Zyrrhak and His Minions — The First Confirmed Boss Fight

The closed alpha was announced to conclude with a boss fight against **Zyrrhak and his minions**. This is the only named early-game boss confirmed so far:

- Zyrrhak is the first boss players will face in the current build — a mini-boss-tier encounter capping the early game
- He fights with minions, meaning crowd control matters as much as single-target damage
- He was the intended "you made it past the intro" milestone for alpha testers

Expect a similar structure in the full game: each early region caps with a regional boss before you can meaningfully push into the next area. Preparation principles for Zyrrhak are the same as for every boss — see the [Boss Guide](boss-guide.md) for the full checklist.

## Side Content: Dungeons and Secrets

Between spire objectives, the world is full of confirmed side content:

- **Caves and underground mines** — the game's dungeons, with traps, enemies, hidden secrets, loot, and rare materials (confirmed)
- **Forgotten facilities** — Aetherium ruins that function as skill-tree nodes and exploration targets
- **Rare material runs** — the underground is the confirmed source of high-tier crafting materials

For players in the current build, dungeon crawling is both the side quest and the gear grind — the [Artifacts & Loot Guide](artifacts-loot-guide.md) explains why.

## Fast Travel Is Story Progression

Restoring spires does double duty: each restored node is a **fast-travel point**, and you travel between them via **Aether Rays** (confirmed). This means your fast-travel network grows exactly as your story progress does. In practice:

- Unlock new travel points by physically reaching and restoring them — there is no taxi system
- Your restored network makes later regions more practical, since you can hop between unlocked spires
- The more of the map you reclaim, the more mobile you become — the campaign literally widens your map

## Co-op and the Campaign

The full campaign is playable with up to 6 players (confirmed). Things that work differently in co-op story progression:

- **One host's world, everyone's progress** — confirm how your group handles shared progression, since worlds are player-hosted
- **Division of labor** — one player farms while another scouts the next spire location
- **Boss walls fall faster** — early campaign bosses like Zyrrhak are far easier with a team

See the [Co-op Guide](coop-guide.md) for multiplayer-specific strategy.
