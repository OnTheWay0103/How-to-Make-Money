# Boss Guide — How to Beat Every Boss in Ardent Wilds

Bosses in Ardent Wilds are skill checks — they test your understanding of combat mechanics, your gear progression, and your preparation. This guide covers every boss in the demo, from the first Spire Guardian to Yorgoroth himself.

## Preparation Checklist (Every Boss)

Before attempting any boss, have:

- [ ] Weapon at the appropriate tier (Copper minimum, Iron recommended for later bosses)
- [ ] 5+ Health Elixirs
- [ ] 2+ relevant potions (Strength Tonic for melee, Fire Resistance for fire bosses, etc.)
- [ ] Food buff active (Warrior's Feast for damage, Hearty Stew for survivability)
- [ ] Armor equipped (crafted at Loom)
- [ ] Spare weapon (in case yours breaks mid-fight)
- [ ] Bed nearby (set respawn point close to the arena)

## Spire Guardians (Mini-Bosses)

Spire Guardians scale in difficulty based on how many Spires you have already restored. The order below assumes you are fighting them in the recommended sequence.

### Corrupted Treant (Spire #1)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~800 |
| **Weakness** | Fire (+50% damage) |
| **Resistance** | Ice (immune to freeze) |
| **Location** | Verdant Basin — Spire of Awakening |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Root Slam** — Raises roots, slams ground after 1.5s windup. Dodge sideways.
2. **Vine Sweep** — 180° sweep with vines. Jump over or dodge backward.
3. **Spore Cloud** (below 50% HP) — Releases spores in a circle. Stand outside the cloud.

**Strategy:** Circle-strafe and attack between Root Slams. This boss is designed to teach you dodge timing. When it raises roots, count "one-one-thousand" then dodge. Fire Staff or fire-enchanted weapons make this fight trivial.

### Eldran Wisp Swarm (Spire #2)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | 5 × ~200 each |
| **Weakness** | Lightning (chain damage hits all) |
| **Resistance** | Single-target attacks (you waste damage overkilling) |
| **Location** | Verdant Basin — Spire of Endurance |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Energy Bolt** — Each Wisp fires a slow-moving projectile. Easy to dodge individually, overwhelming in groups.
2. **Merge** (when 3+ remain) — Remaining Wisps merge into a Super Wisp with combined HP.

**Strategy:** Kill Wisps evenly — do not let one survive while others die. The Super Wisp from merging is stronger than the sum of its parts. Lightning Wand absolutely wrecks this fight — chain lightning hits all Wisps simultaneously. Without Lightning, use Bow and focus them one at a time.

### Corrupted Alpha Wolf (Spire #3)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~1,200 |
| **Weakness** | Ice (freeze interrupts its combos) |
| **Resistance** | None |
| **Location** | Verdant Basin — Spire of the Hunt |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Pounce** — Leaps at you after a brief crouch. Dodge toward the wolf (you pass under it).
2. **Frenzy** (below 60% HP) — 5 rapid claw attacks. Block or dodge backward multiple times.
3. **Howl** — Summons 2 Corrupted Wolves. Kill them quickly or they overwhelm you.

**Strategy:** The Alpha Wolf is fast and aggressive — the first real skill check. Freeze it with Ice attacks, unload damage, repeat. During Frenzy, do not try to trade hits — you will lose. Dodge backward 3 times, then re-engage. Kill summoned wolves immediately — ignoring them is how most players die.

### Crystal Golem (Spire #4)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~2,000 |
| **Weakness** | Fire, Lightning |
| **Resistance** | Ice (immune), Physical (50% reduction) |
| **Location** | Crystal Caverns — Spire of Resilience |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Crystal Barrage** — Fires 3 crystal shards in a spread. Strafe to avoid.
2. **Ground Pound** — Slams ground, creating crystal spikes in a line. Move perpendicular to the golem.
3. **Crystal Armor** (below 50% HP) — Golem encases itself in crystal, gaining 80% damage reduction for 10 seconds. Attack during this phase is wasted — use the time to heal and reposition.

**Strategy:** Physical weapons are nearly useless — bring Fire or Lightning damage. When Crystal Armor activates, stop attacking entirely. Use the 10 seconds to drink potions, reapply buffs, and create distance. The fight is a war of attrition — play patiently.

## Biome Bosses

### Lava Serpent (Mountain Pass)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~3,500 |
| **Weakness** | Ice (2x damage), Void |
| **Resistance** | Fire (immune) |
| **Location** | Mountain Pass — near volcanic vents |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Fire Breath** — Cone attack, 3 second channel. Dodge to the side — do not try to outrange it.
2. **Lava Pool** — Spits lava on the ground, creating a hazard zone (10 sec duration). Do not stand in it.
3. **Burrow** (below 40% HP) — Burrows underground, emerges under you after 2 seconds. Sprint in one direction when it burrows.
4. **Eruption** — Causes volcanic vents to erupt, dealing AoE damage. Stand on non-cracked ground.

**Strategy:** This is a Fire-immune boss — Pyromancers, bring an Ice or Void weapon. Keep the Lava Serpent on solid ground (not over lava cracks) — it heals when touching lava. Freeze it with Ice, unload damage, reposition when it burrows. Bring Fire Resistance Potions.

### Shadow Stalker (Deep Forest)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~2,800 |
| **Weakness** | AoE attacks (reveal it), Fire (Light) |
| **Resistance** | Single-target (hard to hit what you cannot see) |
| **Location** | Deep Forest — Spire of Shadows |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Ambush** — Invisible until it attacks. First strike deals 3x damage. Listen for footsteps.
2. **Shadow Slash** — Quick melee attack. Blockable.
3. **Vanish** — Re-enters invisibility. Can be interrupted with AoE or Lantern of Revealing.
4. **Shadow Clone** (below 30% HP) — Creates 2 clones with 1 HP each. The real one has a subtle glow.

**Strategy:** The hardest Spire Guardian due to invisibility. Bring the Lantern of Revealing artifact or Night Vision Elixir. Use AoE attacks (Pyroblast Core, Fire Staff) to reveal and damage it simultaneously. When it Vanishes, count to 3 and dodge — it almost always attacks from behind exactly 3 seconds after vanishing.

### Void Knight (Eldran Wastes — Spire #10)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~5,000 |
| **Weakness** | Void (interrupts healing) |
| **Resistance** | Physical (30% reduction) |
| **Location** | Eldran Wastes — Spire of the Void |

**Attack Patterns:**
1. **Void Slash** — Teleports to you, slashes. Dodge the moment it reappears.
2. **Eldran Drain** — Channels a beam that heals it for 200 HP/sec. MUST interrupt — hit it with Void damage or deal 500+ damage in 3 seconds.
3. **Void Rift** (below 50% HP) — Opens rifts that spawn Eldran Wisps. Close rifts by standing in them for 2 seconds.
4. **Desperation** (below 15% HP) — Constant Eldran Drain. If you cannot out-DPS the healing, you lose.

**Strategy:** The Eldran Drain is the key mechanic — you MUST interrupt it every time. Void Scepter is purpose-built for this fight and makes it significantly easier. Close Void Rifts immediately — ignoring them for even 20 seconds means fighting 8+ Wisps alongside the boss. For Desperation phase, save all artifact abilities and burst everything.

## Yorgoroth, the Devourer of Worlds (Final Boss)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **HP** | ~10,000 |
| **Weakness** | All elements equally |
| **Resistance** | Status effects (reduced duration) |
| **Location** | Eldran Wastes — Yorgoroth's Gate |
| **Recommended Party** | 3+ players or highly optimized solo build |
| **Minimum Gear** | Full Iron or better, 3 artifacts, 8+ Spires restored |

### Phase 1 (100% - 70% HP): The Awakening

**Attacks:**
1. **Tentacle Slam** — 3 tentacles slam in sequence. Dodge sideways through them.
2. **Void Breath** — Wide cone, channeled. Run behind Yorgoroth.
3. **Summon Wisps** — Spawns 4 Eldran Wisps. Kill them — they do not stop spawning otherwise.

**Strategy:** Conservative phase. Learn the timing of Tentacle Slams. Kill Wisps as priority. Save potions for later phases.

### Phase 2 (70% - 40% HP): The Corruption

**Attacks (adds to Phase 1):**
1. **Corrupt Ground** — The floor in sections becomes corrupted (purple glow). Standing in corrupted ground deals 15 damage/sec and slows you.
2. **Eldran Beam** — Targets one player with a tracking beam. Keep moving — the beam accelerates.
3. **Tentacle Grab** — A tentacle grabs one player, dealing damage over 3 seconds. Other players must attack the tentacle to free them.

**Strategy:** Control the arena — corrupted ground limits your space. Lead Yorgoroth to clean sections. In co-op, the grabbed player calls for help immediately. Solo, you must break the tentacle yourself — keep a high-damage weapon for this.

### Phase 3 (40% - 0% HP): The Devouring

**Attacks (adds to Phase 2):**
1. **Devour** — Yorgoroth opens its maw and begins pulling everything toward it. Sprint away. If you are pulled in, you die instantly.
2. **Void Rift Storm** — Opens 3 Void Rifts simultaneously. Close them or be overwhelmed.
3. **Desperate Fury** (below 15%) — All attacks become 30% faster. This is the final burn phase.

**Strategy:** Devour is the run-killer. When you see the maw open, immediately sprint to the arena edge. Do not try to get "one more hit" — you will die. During Void Rift Storm, prioritize closing rifts over damaging the boss. At 15%, pop every remaining potion, use every artifact ability, and burn him down before the accelerated attacks overwhelm you.

### Recommended Builds for Yorgoroth

| Build | Role | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| **Pyromancer** | AoE DPS | Clears Wisps, consistent boss damage, terrain control for corrupted ground |
| **Frost Tank** | Support | Guardian Shield blocks Devour pull (!), Tome of Ice freezes adds |
| **Berserker** | Single-target DPS | Highest damage output, but risky — needs Frost Tank support |
| **Storm Archer** | Ranged DPS | Safe, consistent damage, can damage boss during Devour from range |

### Co-op Strategy

- **3 players:** Frost Tank + Berserker + Storm Archer is the ideal trio
- **4+ players:** Add a second DPS (Pyromancer) and a dedicated healer (Healing Staff)
- Whoever Yorgoroth targets should focus on survival while others DPS
- During Devour, the Frost Tank uses Guardian Shield to block the pull (this is a hidden interaction — the shield negates forced movement)

## Boss Farming

After defeating a boss, you can re-fight it by interacting with its arena entrance (bosses respawn after 1 in-game day). Farming bosses yields:

- **Eldran Shards** — Consistent drops from all bosses
- **Primal Essence** — Low drop rate (10-20%) from Yorgoroth, guaranteed from first kill
- **Unique Artifacts** — Each boss has a unique artifact in its loot table (low drop rate on repeat kills)
