# Full Release & Roadmap Guide — What Is Coming to Ardent Wilds

Ardent Wilds is a **pre-release game in active development** — the free demo has been out since July 30, 2026, the full game has no release date, and the developer has shipped multiple public playtest builds with significant changes. If you are deciding whether to follow the game, when to buy, or what to expect next, this page is the status reference.

**Honesty first:** everything here is drawn from the official [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2275010/Ardent_Wilds/), the official [demo page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5007130/Ardent_Wilds_Demo/), and the official playtest patch notes (April 2026 build, June 10 2026 v0.5.9). Nothing below is a rumor — and where the developer has not said anything, we say so instead of guessing. Release dates are especially volatile in pre-release games: treat any date you see elsewhere as unconfirmed unless it appears on the official page.

## Confirmed Development History

The public build history tells you where development actually is:

| Build | Date | Confirmed Highlights |
|-------|------|----------------------|
| **Alpha playtest build** | April 2, 2026 | Custom world generation rebuilt from the ground up (pre-generated worlds removed); experimental dedicated server executable (`ArdentWilds_Server.exe`); new tutorial system with "[On Victory]"/"[On Play]" mechanics and a restart function; female player character model; reworked intro and Points of Interest; new items and abilities; underground mines added; base-building improvements with many new pieces; music in starting areas |
| **v0.5.9** | June 10, 2026 | Pre-generated worlds returned for faster startup; runtime worldgen temporarily disabled (older hardware issues); main-menu world selection UI; improved **Plains floating islands with new rewards**; **new boss, armor sets, weapons, and building materials**; **hold-to-cast abilities**; **sleeping in beds to fast-forward nights**; audio, UI, and bug fixes |
| **Demo** | July 30, 2026 | Free demo released on Steam — the current public playable build; see the [Demo Guide](demo-guide.md) for content details |

Pattern worth noting: development is **content-adding and course-correcting in parallel** — new content landed in v0.5.9 (boss, gear, building materials) while the worldgen system was walked back for stability. That is normal pre-release rhythm, and it means features you read about today can be removed, renamed, or reworked tomorrow.

## Confirmed Features for the Full Game

These are stated on the official store page as the game's design — they are confirmed *goals* for the full release:

- **World-built skill tree** — restore ancient Spires and Forgotten Facilities to unlock powers; every node grants a passive bonus and serves as a fast-travel point (see [Skill Tree & Spire Guide](skill-tree-guide.md))
- **The campaign structure** — rebuild the four ancient magical spires to push back the Eldran and challenge their god, **Yorgoroth, the Devourer of Worlds** (see [Story & Quests Guide](story-quests-guide.md))
- **Fully destructible voxel world** — dig, sculpt, explode; bombs and pyroblasts leave craters; the Tome of Ice creates frost bridges
- **Base building with furniture and decorations** — plus farming, cooking, and elixir brewing (see [Base Building Guide](base-building-guide.md) and [Farming & Cooking Guide](farming-cooking-guide.md))
- **Caves and underground mines** — deadly traps, fierce enemies, hidden secrets, rare materials
- **Arcade-style classless combat** — fireball wizard, warrior, marksman, or a mix; rare artifacts unlock unique abilities (see [Combat Guide](combat-guide.md))
- **Co-op for up to 6 players** — online via Steam and LAN, with the player count expandable beyond 6 (unbalanced) (see [Co-op & Multiplayer Guide](coop-guide.md))
- **Full Lua modding** — every game object, creature, ability, and combat effect is programmable via the Lua API; all script files ship with the game (see [Modding Guide](modding-guide.md))
- **Character customization** — hair styles, beards, colors
- **English only** — currently the only listed language on the store page

## What Is Confirmed "In Development" (From Playtest Notes)

These shipped in playtest builds but are not yet announced for the full game — treat them as direction, not promises:

- **Dedicated server hosting** — experimental, being worked on; see the [Dedicated Server Guide](dedicated-server-guide.md)
- **Custom world generation** — rebuilt, then temporarily shelved in v0.5.9 pending stability fixes; see the [World Generation & Seeds Guide](world-generation-seeds-guide.md)
- **Sleep-in-bed time skip** — confirmed in v0.5.9 (fast-forward nights)
- **Hold-to-cast ability controls** — confirmed in v0.5.9
- **Floating islands with rewards** — confirmed in v0.5.9 for the Plains biome

## Should You Buy Now or Wait?

Honest decision framework:

- **You want the game today** → the demo is free and is the full preview of the systems. Play it, decide, and the demo is your benchmark — see the [Demo Guide](demo-guide.md)
- **You want to follow development** → playtest builds and patch notes are the confirmed channel; they appear via the Steam page's news feed
- **You want the finished experience** → wait for the release date announcement. The game is in active flux (worldgen was rebuilt and then shelved within three months — the June v0.5.9 notes alone reworked core systems)
- **You plan to mod** → development flux affects mods too; the Lua API is confirmed, but mods written for one build may break in the next (see [Modding Guide](modding-guide.md))
