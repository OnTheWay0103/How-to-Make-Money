# Multiplayer & Co-op Guide — Play With Up to 6 Players

Ardent Wilds supports online and LAN co-op for up to 6 players. This guide covers everything from setting up your first multiplayer session to advanced team strategies that will keep your whole group alive against the Eldran.

## Setting Up Multiplayer

### Online Co-op (Steam)

1. **Host:** Start a single-player world, then open the pause menu → "Open to Friends" or "Open to Public"
2. **Friends:** Open Steam overlay (Shift+Tab) → right-click the host → "Join Game"
3. The host's world saves progress for everyone — only the host needs to be online

### LAN / Direct IP

1. **Host:** Start a world, enable "LAN Mode" in settings
2. **Players:** From the main menu → "Join LAN Game" → enter the host's local IP address
3. All players must be on the same local network

### Server Settings

The host can configure:

- **Max Players:** 2-6 (can be increased beyond 6 via config files, not officially balanced)
- **Difficulty Scaling:** Off (enemies do not scale) / Linear (enemies gain +15% HP per player) / Adaptive (enemies gain +15% HP and +1 new attack pattern per 2 players)
- **Friendly Fire:** Off / Reduced (50%) / Full
- **Resource Sharing:** Individual (each player gathers separately) / Shared (all resources go into a shared pool)

**Recommended settings for beginners:** Adaptive scaling, Friendly Fire Off, Individual resources. This provides the intended challenge without punishing coordination mistakes.

## Team Roles and Composition

With up to 6 players, specialization becomes powerful. Here are the recommended team compositions:

### 2-Player Team

| Role | Build | Responsibilities |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Vanguard** | Berserker or Frost Tank | Combat, boss tanking, exploration |
| **Quartermaster** | Any + focus on crafting artifacts | Base building, farming, crafting, cooking |

### 3-Player Team

| Role | Build | Responsibilities |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Vanguard** | Berserker | Frontline DPS, boss aggro |
| **Ranger** | Storm Archer | Ranged DPS, pulling enemies, covering fire |
| **Quartermaster** | Frost Tank + crafting artifacts | Base management, support, crafting |

### 4-Player Team

| Role | Build | Responsibilities |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Vanguard** | Berserker or Pyromancer | Primary DPS |
| **Sentinel** | Frost Tank | Tanking, crowd control, teammate protection |
| **Ranger** | Storm Archer | Ranged DPS, exploration, spire hunting |
| **Quartermaster** | Flex + all crafting artifacts | Base, farming, gear production for team |

### 6-Player Team

| Role | Build | Count |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Vanguard** | Berserker or Pyromancer | 2 (rotate aggro on bosses) |
| **Sentinel** | Frost Tank | 1 (dedicated tank) |
| **Ranger** | Storm Archer or Marksman | 1 (ranged DPS, weak point targeting) |
| **Support** | Healing Staff + Guardian Shield | 1 (healer/buffer, stays back, keeps team alive) |
| **Quartermaster** | Any + crafting artifacts | 1 (full-time base and gear manager) |

**Critical insight:** In teams of 4+, dedicate one player to full-time base management. One player handling all farming, cooking, crafting, and smelting while others explore means the team always has top-tier gear, buffs, and potions. This specialization alone can double your team's effective progression speed.

## Shared Base Strategies

### The Communal Base Model

All players contribute to and live in one large base. Pros: efficient resource use, easier to defend. Cons: crowded, resource competition, one Eldran siege threatens everyone.

### The Hub-and-Spoke Model (Recommended for 4+)

One central "Hub Base" with shared workstations, storage, and farm. Each player (or pair) also builds a small "Spoke Base" near a different biome for specialized resource gathering. Spokes connect to the Hub via Spire fast-travel.

**Hub Base contains:** All workstations, bulk storage, main farm, best defenses
**Spoke Base contains:** Bed, 2-3 chests, Campfire, biome-specific gathering tools

### Resource Management in Co-op

Resource sharing mode dramatically affects your strategy:

**Individual Resources (recommended for beginners):**
- Each player is self-sufficient
- No arguments over who "took all the iron"
- Slower overall progression — duplicated effort

**Shared Resources (recommended for experienced teams):**
- Dramatically faster progression — gatherers gather, crafters craft
- Requires trust and communication
- One player hoarding can cripple the team

**Hybrid approach (what most experienced teams use):**
- Basic materials (Wood, Stone, Fiber) are shared
- Rare materials (Crystal, Gold, Eldran Shards) are distributed by need
- The Quartermaster manages distribution

## Combat Synergies in Co-op

### Combo Attacks

Certain artifact and weapon combinations create powerful synergies when used by different players:

| Player 1 | Player 2 | Combo Effect |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Tome of Ice (freeze enemy) | Pyroblast Core (fire attack) | **Thermal Shock** — 3x damage multiplier |
| Venom Dagger (20 poison stacks) | Lightning Wand | **Toxic Discharge** — consumes all poison stacks for burst damage |
| Guardian Shield (taunt) | Berserker Axe (max stacks) | **Protected Rampage** — Berserker deals full damage with zero risk |
| Marksman Rifle (weak point) | Greatsword | **Exposed Weakness** — weak point shots mark targets, Greatsword deals 2x to marked |

### Boss Fight Coordination

Against major bosses:

1. **Phase 1:** Everyone DPS, learn patterns, Sentinel practices aggro timing
2. **Phase 2 (50% HP):** Most bosses gain new attacks. Sentinel holds aggro. Others switch to ranged and play safe.
3. **Phase 3 (25% HP):** Desperation phase. Use all potions, all artifact abilities. Support player focuses exclusively on healing.

**Death protocol:** If someone dies during a boss fight, they respawn at their bed. The remaining players' job is to **survive**, not to finish the boss. A dead boss with 3 dead teammates is a bad trade — resurrections are not free (you lose gathered resources).

## Communication Tips

Ardent Wilds does not have built-in voice chat. Use Discord or Steam voice. Here is what to communicate:

- **Spire discoveries** — "Found Spire #6, marking on map. Guardian is Lava Serpent — bring Ice weapons."
- **Resource calls** — "Found a rich iron vein at [location], marking. Bring Iron Pickaxes."
- **Raid warnings** — "Major Eldran attack incoming from the east! Everyone back to base!"
- **Artifact trades** — "Got a spare Berserker Axe. Who wants it?"
- **Food/potion status** — "We have 2 Warrior's Feasts left. Cooking more now — wait 5 minutes before the boss."

## Troubleshooting Common Co-op Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| **Can't join friend's game** | Both restart Steam. Host toggles world privacy to "Friends" and back. |
| **Lag / desync** | Host should reduce max players. Limit base decorations (too many items causes lag). |
| **Enemies not taking damage** | Desync issue — the joining player should leave and rejoin. |
| **Shared chest items disappeared** | Known demo bug. Do not store valuables in shared chests — use individual chests. |
| **One player always dies first** | That player is under-geared. The Quartermaster should prioritize crafting gear for them. |
