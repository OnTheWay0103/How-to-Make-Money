# Skill Tree & Spire Locations Guide

Instead of a traditional menu-based skill tree, Ardent Wilds places skill nodes physically in the world as **Ancient Spires** and **Forgotten Facilities**. You explore, discover, and restore them to unlock new powers. This guide maps every known Spire in the demo build and explains the optimal restoration order.

## How the World-Built Skill Tree Works

Each Ancient Spire or Forgotten Facility provides three things when restored:

1. **A permanent ability or passive bonus** — Active skills, passive stat boosts, or new mechanics
2. **A permanent stat increase** — +HP, +Stamina, +Damage, +Speed, etc.
3. **A fast-travel point** — Unlocked on your map, teleport between any restored Spires

Spires glow faintly at night, making them easier to spot from a distance. During the day, look for tall structures emitting a subtle particle effect. The map also marks undiscovered Spires with a "?" icon once you are within ~200 meters.

### Restoration Requirements

To restore a Spire, you need:

- **Eldran Cleansing Crystal** (crafted at Alchemy Station: 3 Crystal + 1 Eldran Shard + 1 Primal Essence)
- To defeat the Spire Guardian (a mini-boss that spawns when you attempt restoration)
- 30 seconds of uninterrupted channeling (cannot take damage during this time)

**Important:** Spire Guardians scale in difficulty based on how many Spires you have already restored. The first Spire Guardian is manageable with basic Copper gear. The 10th will require endgame equipment, potions, and ideally a co-op partner.

## All Known Spire Locations (Demo Build)

### Verdant Basin (Starting Biome) — 3 Spires

#### Spire of Awakening (#1)
- **Location:** Central Verdant Basin, visible from spawn. On a small hill surrounded by basic trees.
- **Guardian:** Corrupted Treant (slow, telegraphed attacks, weak to Fire)
- **Unlocks:** Quick Gather — +25% resource gathering speed. +20 max HP.
- **Priority:** Restore FIRST. This is your introduction to the Spire system, and the gathering bonus pays for itself immediately.

#### Spire of Endurance (#2)
- **Location:** Northwest Verdant Basin, near the river crossing. Partially hidden by cliff overhang.
- **Guardian:** Eldran Wisp Swarm (5 Wisps, use Lightning or ranged weapons)
- **Unlocks:** Sturdy — +15% max stamina. +10 max Stamina.
- **Priority:** Restore second. More stamina means more dodging, more gathering, more sprinting.

#### Spire of the Hunt (#3)
- **Location:** Southeast Verdant Basin, at the edge of the forest. On a rocky outcrop.
- **Guardian:** Corrupted Alpha Wolf (fast, aggressive, weak to Ice)
- **Unlocks:** Tracker — Enemies are highlighted when below 30% HP. +5% critical hit chance.
- **Priority:** Restore third. The tracking ability is excellent for boss fights.

### Crystal Caverns — 2 Spires

#### Spire of Resilience (#4)
- **Location:** Deep within the Crystal Caverns, past the iron deposits. In a large open chamber.
- **Guardian:** Crystal Golem (high defense, weak to Fire and Lightning, immune to Ice)
- **Unlocks:** Stone Skin — +10% damage reduction. +30 max HP.
- **Priority:** Restore fourth. The damage reduction is universally useful.

#### Spire of Alchemy (#5)
- **Location:** Crystal Caverns upper level, accessed via a frost bridge (Tome of Ice required or creative terrain building).
- **Guardian:** Alchemical Horror (splits into smaller copies at 50% HP, weak to AoE)
- **Unlocks:** Alchemist's Touch — Potion effects last 50% longer. +1 potion slot.
- **Priority:** Restore as soon as you have Tome of Ice. Doubled potion duration is a game-changer.

### Mountain Pass — 2 Spires

#### Spire of Flame (#6)
- **Location:** Mountain Pass, near the volcanic vents. Glows orange at night.
- **Guardian:** Lava Serpent (fire-immune, use Ice or Void weapons)
- **Unlocks:** Fire Affinity — +20% fire damage dealt, -20% fire damage taken. +10% all damage.
- **Priority:** Restore when building a Fire/Pyroblast build. Otherwise, lower priority.

#### Spire of Frost (#7)
- **Location:** Mountain Pass summit. Requires Grappling Hook or extensive terrain building to reach.
- **Guardian:** Frozen Colossus (slow, devastating attacks, weak to Fire)
- **Unlocks:** Ice Affinity — +20% ice damage dealt, freeze duration doubled. +15 max Stamina.
- **Priority:** Essential for Tome of Ice builds. The doubled freeze duration is extremely powerful.

### Deep Forest — 2 Spires

#### Spire of Shadows (#8)
- **Location:** Deep Forest, in the permanently dark central grove. Bring a Lantern or Night Vision potion.
- **Guardian:** Shadow Stalker (invisible until it attacks, use AoE to reveal)
- **Unlocks:** Shadow Step — Dodging leaves a decoy that taunts enemies for 2 seconds. +10% movement speed.
- **Priority:** High for solo players. The decoy provides crucial breathing room.

#### Spire of Vitality (#9)
- **Location:** Deep Forest eastern edge, near the Ancient Tree landmark.
- **Guardian:** Corrupted Ancient Treant (the most durable Spire Guardian, very long fight)
- **Unlocks:** Regeneration — +2 HP/sec passive regen. +50 max HP.
- **Priority:** Restore after Shadow Spire. Passive regen reduces potion dependence significantly.

### Eldran Wastes — 1 Spire

#### Spire of the Void (#10)
- **Location:** Center of the Eldran Wastes. The most dangerous biome — come prepared.
- **Guardian:** Void Knight (teleports, heals from Eldran pools, use Void weapons to interrupt healing)
- **Unlocks:** Void Walker — +30% damage to Eldran, Eldran attacks deal 15% less damage to you. +15% all damage.
- **Priority:** Restore last — or earlier if you are struggling with Eldran enemies. The most powerful Spire bonus.

## Optimal Spire Restoration Order

### Speedrunner Route (focus on utility)
1 → 2 → 5 (requires Tome of Ice) → 8 → 9 → 3 → 4 → 10 → 6 → 7

### Combat-Focused Route (maximize damage)
1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 6 → 10 → 8 → 9 → 5 → 7

### Co-op Route (spread the work)
- **Player 1 (gatherer/crafter):** 1 → 2 → 5 → 9
- **Player 2 (combat/exploration):** 3 → 4 → 6 → 8
- **Together:** 7 → 10 (hardest Guardians, co-op makes them much easier)

## Forgotten Facilities

In addition to the 10 main Spires, there are smaller Forgotten Facilities scattered across the world. These do not have Guardians but require rarer materials to restore:

| Facility | Location | Cost | Unlocks |
|----------|----------|------|---------|
| **Ancient Library** | Deep Forest ruins | 5 Crystal + 2 Ancient Wood | +10% XP gain |
| **Old Observatory** | Mountain Pass peak | 5 Crystal + 3 Gold Ingot | Reveals all Spire locations on map |
| **Forgotten Forge** | Crystal Caverns depths | 10 Iron Ingot + 5 Coal | +15% crafting speed |
| **Abandoned Farm** | Verdant Basin outskirts | 10 Wood + 5 Fiber + 5 Seeds | +25% crop yield |
| **Ruined Shrine** | Eldran Wastes border | 3 Eldran Shard + 1 Primal Essence | Once per day: full heal at shrine |

**Priority tip:** Restore the Old Observatory as early as possible. Revealing all Spire locations on your map saves hours of blind exploration.

## Tips for Spire Hunting

1. **Travel at night** — Spires glow and are visible from much farther away
2. **Build bridges** — Many Spires are on elevated terrain. Bring Wood (lots of it) or the Tome of Ice
3. **Mark discovered but unrestored Spires** on your map — come back when you are stronger
4. **Bring a friend** — Co-op makes Spire Guardians significantly easier through aggro rotation
5. **Do not skip Spires** — Each one gives permanent stats that make the next one easier
