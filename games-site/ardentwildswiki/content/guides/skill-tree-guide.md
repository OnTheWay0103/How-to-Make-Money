# Skill Tree & Spire Guide — The World-Built Skill Tree

Ardent Wilds does something unusual: **the skill tree exists in the world itself.** Instead of opening a menu, you explore, find, and restore ancient structures — **Spires** and **Forgotten Facilities** — and each one you restore grants you power. This is the game's signature system, confirmed in detail by the developers, and it is the single most important progression mechanic to understand.

**Honesty note:** the game is pre-release (demo July 30, 2026; full game TBA). No official list of Spire locations, names, or unlocks has been published. Older guides — and much of the internet — claim exact locations for "Spire #1 through #10" with named bonuses; that is fabricated and contradicts the official description of the full-game goal. This guide covers the confirmed system and honest strategy.

## How the World-Built Skill Tree Works — Confirmed

From the official description:

1. **The skill tree exists in the world itself.** There is no traditional menu-based talent tree
2. You restore **ancient Spires and forgotten facilities** — Aetherium-era structures scattered across the map
3. **Every node you unlock grants a passive bonus**
4. **Every unlocked node serves as a fast-travel location** — travel between them via **Aether Rays**
5. In the full game, the goal is to **rebuild four ancient magical spires** to push back the Eldran — and grow strong enough to challenge **Yorgoroth, the Devourer of Worlds**

So progression, exploration, and travel are the same activity. The more of the world you reclaim from the Eldran's corruption, the more powerful and mobile you become.

## What This Means in Practice

### Progression Is Exploration

There is no skill menu to grind. Your build grows by **reading the world**:

- Look for tall structures on elevated ground — Spires are landmarks by design
- Watch for ruined facilities — the "forgotten facilities" of the Aetherium era are second-tier nodes, often tied to rare materials
- Explore the dangerous places — nodes in corrupted zones and deep underground grant the biggest bonuses (this is the genre-consistent pattern; exact placement is unconfirmed)
- Follow the corrupted zones — the Eldran spread defines where the threats are, and the structures you need are often near them

### Fast Travel Is Earned, Not Given

Every restored node is a fast-travel point. The practical consequence:

- **Your first restored node changes the game** — a fast-travel point near your base cuts travel time permanently
- **Travel nodes are priorities** — restoring a node near a resource-rich region turns every future gathering trip into a teleport
- **Build the network early** — the map's geography stops mattering once you have several nodes

### Passives Stack

Every node grants a **passive bonus** — and because it is a passive, it applies permanently. This is the game's answer to character progression: your character is literally built out of the world you have restored. Choose deliberately — bonuses are permanent once unlocked (per the confirmed "passive bonus" design; respeccing is unconfirmed).

## Restoration Strategy

### What Restoration Involves

Confirmed: you restore the structures. Unconfirmed: the exact process — whether restoration requires materials, a fight, time channeling, or all three. Community reports describe guarded sites and a restoration interaction, but details vary between builds. Expect to **prepare before you go**: bring supplies, and be ready to fight for the site.

### Restoration Order Principles

Since locations are unconfirmed, use principles, not a fixed route:

1. **Restore the nearest node first.** Your first fast-travel point should be close to where you live
2. **Prioritize travel over stats in the early game.** A second node near a different region is worth more than an extra stat point — mobility compounds
3. **Time dangerous nodes around boss readiness.** If a node sits in high-difficulty territory, treat it like a boss trip: gear up, stock consumables, bring help
4. **Balance passive bonuses with your build.** If the confirmed passives in your world lean toward combat, gathering, or utility, match them to your playstyle
5. **Restore in co-op, share the reward.** Teams should split node-hunting across the map — every restored node becomes team fast travel (shared fast travel is the genre standard and implied by the design; exact rules unconfirmed)

### The Full-Game Goal: Four Spires

The official goal is **rebuilding four ancient magical spires** to push back the Eldran. Keep that scale in mind: the campaign is structured around a handful of major restoration projects, not dozens of minor nodes. The final challenge — Yorgoroth — comes after the spires are restored (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)).

## Forgotten Facilities

In addition to Spires, **forgotten facilities** are the second confirmed node type. Differences from Spires are unconfirmed, but the design pattern suggests:

- Facilities may be **smaller nodes** with narrower bonuses than major Spires
- They are often **tied to rare materials** — restoring them may cost what you mine from the deep places
- They sit in **dangerous and remote spots** — the rewards justify the trip

Treat facilities as mid-tier goals between major Spire projects.

## Tips for Spire Hunting

1. **Read the skyline** — Spires are tall structures on elevated ground; they are landmarks by design
2. **Explore toward the corruption** — the most valuable structures sit near the Eldran's territory (community pattern; unconfirmed)
3. **Mark discovered but unrestored nodes** on your map — come back when you are stronger
4. **Bring a friend** — sites are often guarded; teams restore faster and safer (co-op is confirmed for up to 6 players)
5. **Do not skip nodes** — each one gives a permanent passive that makes the next one easier
6. **Do not trust location guides** — worlds are procedurally generated (confirmed in the April 2026 playtest notes); nobody else's map is your map

## What Is Still Unknown (Unverified)

- The full node catalog: how many Spires and facilities exist in the full game
- Official node names and exact bonuses
- Restoration requirements (materials, combat, time)
- Whether nodes can be lost or re-corrupted by the Eldran
- Whether passives can be respec'd

This guide will be updated with confirmed information as the game approaches release. For now: restore everything you find, travel for free, and stack those passives.
