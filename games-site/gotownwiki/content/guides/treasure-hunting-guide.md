# Go-Go Town! Treasure Hunting Guide — Fossils, Artifacts & Hidden Relics

**Treasure Hunting** is one of the two flagship features of the 1.0 update (July 16, 2026), alongside Creative Mode. It adds a full collection layer to the town builder: bugs, gems, fossils, artifacts, relics, and rare fish hidden across the map. Collect them, display them in the Town Hall, and sell your duplicates for coins.

## What You're Looking For

| Collectible | Found In | Tool Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Ancient Artifacts | Buried treasure spots | Shovel |
| Fossils | Desert, forests, caves | Shovel |
| Rare Gems | Mines and rocky cliffs | Pickaxe |
| Hidden Relics | Secret rooms and underground zones | Drill / Shovel |
| Rare Fish | Special fishing spots | Fishing Rod |
| Unique Bugs | Regional biomes | Capture on sight |

**Always carry**: shovel, pickaxe, drill, fishing rod, and a bubble blower (for slime-covered passages). Nothing kills a treasure run faster than finding a dig spot and realizing the tool is back in town.

## Best Hidden Areas

- **Deep Forest** — winding paths with fossilized leaves, forest-themed artifacts, and rare insects. Unlock the Pill Bug vehicle to make the maze easier to navigate.
- **Mine Tunnel & Hidden Tomb** — deep in the tunnel network is a **cracked wall** that looks slightly different from the others. Use an **upgraded drill** to reveal a hidden Tomb containing ancient relics, historical artifacts, and the secret Townie **Dusty**, who joins your town after the associated questline.
- **Desert & Far Desert** — the fastest way to fill the Collections app: prehistoric fossils, dinosaur remains, buried relics, and uncommon collectibles. The area is enormous — bring a fast vehicle.
- **Petal Grove** — higher-value collectibles: floral fossils, pink insects, colorful crystals, and decorative items. Check the elevated cliff pathways — some entrances are easy to miss.
- **The Sewers** — use the bubble blower to clear slime blockages, then find industrial relics, forgotten equipment, and unusual insects.
- **Hedgewarrens & Bunker Network** — late-game areas with the rarest collectibles; needed to complete every collection category.

**Pro tip**: changing locations dramatically changes what you find — an hour in the mines yields a completely different collection than exploring the desert or forest.

## How the System Works

Finds come from **diggable treasure spots**, artifact troves, special fishing spots, and wandering bugs. Interact with the right tool and you get a triumphant reveal animation. Your phone tracks what you've found and how many items remain undiscovered in each category, so it doubles as a completion checklist.

## The Town Hall and Arizona Smythe III

The new **Town Hall** building in 1.0 is your collection hub:

- **Arizona Smythe III**, the troll NPC, buys duplicate finds for coins — a reliable income source that beats selling basic crafted goods
- **Display cases** — aquariums, bug stands, and plinths show off your collection (more displays are sold from the vending machine in the back with EGO)
- Displays can be placed indoors or outdoors, and the Town Hall interior can be decorated and expanded like your home

## What to Do With Your Finds

- **Display them** — a museum-style collection attracts sightseeing visitors and indirectly benefits nearby businesses
- **Sell duplicates** — Arizona buys extras, so never hoard doubles
- **Complete categories** — the Collections app tracks every category; finishing one is the real endgame goal for hunters

## Treasure Hunting Efficiency Tips

1. **Use fast travel** — critters and diggable treasures hide across all maps, including external zones; fast travel makes sweeps viable
2. **Spend resources before expeditions** — go in with an empty backpack so finds aren't wasted
3. **Let Townies work** — hire workers to gather common materials while you explore
4. **Add the minimap and clock via the Widgets App** — both help you track hunting time and location
5. **Grab the Pill Bug vehicle** — several areas (Deep Forest, Petal Grove) are much easier with its maneuverability
