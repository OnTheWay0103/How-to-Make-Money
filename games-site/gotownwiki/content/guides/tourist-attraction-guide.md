# How to Attract and Satisfy Tourists in Go-Go Town!

Tourists are the heart of Go-Go Town!. They buy from your shops (coins), their happiness generates EGO, and some of them move in and become your workforce. Understanding what makes them come — and what makes them happy once they're here — is the single most important skill in the game.

## How Tourists Arrive

Tourists arrive by **train every day** — which is why the train station is the most important early building. Once your town's deeds spread by word of mouth, more visitors flock in. Building and repairing structures, adding shops and food stalls, and decorating all encourage higher tourist numbers. The train station's tourist slider lets you control arrival volume; max it out when you're ready to scale sales.

## What Makes Tourists Happy

Tourist satisfaction is driven by several systems:

- **Decor** — benches, statues, streetlights, trees, and planters all raise happiness. More decor = happier tourists = more EGO.
- **Cleanliness** — tourists litter constantly. Trash bins, dumpsters, bubble machines, and a hired sanitation crew keep satisfaction from decaying.
- **Food and shopping** — tourists expect somewhere to eat and buy. Star-rated food and shopping systems tier recipes; items that are "too fancy or too cheap" draw complaints, so match your menus to what visitors can afford.
- **Amenities** — rest benches, entertainment, and beautiful scenery all matter. Interactive decor like spring riders and drinking fountains boosts engagement.
- **Entertainment** — theatres, landmarks, and attractions raise the ceiling on what your town can support.

## Tourist Desires (Tourist Trap Update)

The Tourist Trap update added **Tourist Desires**: each visitor has unique preferences for entertainment, rest, hunger, health, exploration, and shopping that directly impact your town rating. You can now see exactly what visitors want through **Thought Bubbles** and the **Tourist Tracker app** — check it regularly and address complaints to keep ratings climbing. Some visitor types (clowns, robots, aliens, dinosaurs, ghosts, mummies) can be welcomed or banned via **Transit Terminals** once unlocked — ban types your town can't satisfy.

## Town Rating: The Progression Loop

Tourists leave reviews after visits, visible in the Tourist Tracker app. Good reviews raise your **town ranking**, and each tier unlocks the next set of EGO Tech bundles (houses, shops, tools, automation). Advancing tiers also **raises your coin and EGO caps** and unlocks new money sources — markets, statues, and attractions.

## Major Landmarks

Late-game, build **Major Landmarks** to draw bigger crowds: the **Lighthouse, Conservatory, Stadium, and Hot Springs** are massive attractions that bring visitors in bulk. Landmarks are expensive, so wait until your supply chains are stable — a landmark that can't be stocked or maintained is a wasted investment.

## Converting Tourists to Townies

Tourists with a **"+"** above their head are ready to move in. Talk to them, fulfill their request, and invite them — but **build a house or apartment first**. Once they become residents (Townies), you can assign them to work in mines, forest, farm, shops, and more, which is how the town starts running itself.

## Quick Satisfaction Checklist

1. Train station built and tourist slider tuned
2. Trash bins in every high-traffic area + sanitation workers
3. Decor scattered through the town square
4. Shops and stalls stocked near the station
5. Menus matched to tourist budgets (star-rated recipes)
6. Tourist Tracker checked daily for complaints
7. Houses available for "+" tourists ready to move in

When in doubt, remember the loop: **happy tourists → more EGO → higher town rank → more unlocks → bigger and stranger crowds**.
