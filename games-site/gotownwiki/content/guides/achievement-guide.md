# Go-Go Town! Achievements Guide — All 56 and How to Unlock Them

Go-Go Town! shipped 1.0 on July 16, 2026 with **56 total achievements** — the 26 that existed during Early Access plus **30 brand-new ones**. The original 26 are fully documented; the new 30 are still being mapped by the community (names and conditions below are what has been confirmed so far).

## The Original 26 Achievements — Fully Documented

### Tech Tree Progression (9 achievements)

| Achievement | How to Unlock |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Tier 1 — Tier 9 Tech Tree** | Unlock each respective tier of the EGO Tech tree (nine separate achievements) |

## The 30 New 1.0 Achievements

The 1.0 update added **30 achievements covering the new systems** — Treasure Hunting, Creative Mode, Garbirds, and the reworked progression. The full list is still being compiled post-launch. What to expect based on confirmed 1.0 systems:

- **Treasure Hunting collection milestones** (bugs, fossils, gems, relics, artifacts, rare fish)
- **Creative Mode** activities
- **Garbird** encounters
- **New tech tree tiers** (the tree was revamped in 1.0)

Specific names and conditions are **awaiting confirmation** — this section will be filled in as the community documents them.

## 100% Completion Strategy

- **Tech tree tiers come naturally** — they follow normal progression; see the [Building Unlock Guide](/guides/building-unlock-guide) for the EGO Tech structure.
- **Vehicle achievements need deliberate driving** — find a Pill Bug early (it's hidden on the map — the [Treasure Hunting Guide](/guides/treasure-hunting-guide) covers map exploration) and put miles on it for the distance/drift/run-over achievements.
- **Grumbo is a story character** — join him to the town during the main storyline; you can't miss him if you follow the story beats.
- **The grindy ones (200 trees, 150 trash, 50 fish + 50 potatoes)** — combine with normal play: chainsaw as you clear land, route trash collection through your [Automation Guide](/guides/automation-guide) workers, and farm fish while doing the [Treasure Hunting Guide](/guides/treasure-hunting-guide)'s rare-fish circuit.
- **Co-op counts** — achievements progress across players in co-op sessions on the same save, so a four-player town hits the big milestones four times faster.

**Next steps:** Hunt the new 1.0 achievements' targets with the [Treasure Hunting Guide](/guides/treasure-hunting-guide), and see what else 1.0 changed in the [1.0 Update Guide](/guides/one-point-zero-update-guide).
