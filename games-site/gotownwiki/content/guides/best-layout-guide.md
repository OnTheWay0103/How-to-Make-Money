# Best Town Layout Guide for Go-Go Town!

A good layout in Go-Go Town! isn't about looks — it's about **keeping couriers moving, workers fed, and tourists happy**. A badly placed road or a storage bin outside a zone boundary can silently strangle your entire economy. Here are the layout principles that keep a town running smoothly.

## Roads: Double Lanes, Clear Paths

The most important layout rule: **keep roads clear for delivery vehicles**. Couriers use vehicles to deliver materials to shops, food trucks, and construction sites — if there's no road or the road is blocked, they run on foot and efficiency collapses.

Use **double-lane (two-lane) main roads** for high-traffic corridors. Single lanes technically allow vehicles to turn around, but two vehicles traveling simultaneously get stuck and cause traffic jams that cascade through the whole delivery network. The truck also only drives on roads — not brick or tile surfaces — so any zone you want truck-served needs road access.

## Zone Organization: Each Zone Is Its Own Bubble

Zones are **independent bubbles**: each zone's workers only operate within their own zone and won't transfer materials across zone boundaries. That means every production zone needs its own storage and processing setup — a farm zone without its own storage will jam the moment its workers fill up.

Practical consequences:

- **Split large zones.** After the "Build and Bustle" update the map is bigger and every zone can be resized and relocated. One player recommended splitting one big farm into three micro-farms to reduce crowding.
- **Scatter zones deliberately.** Zones don't need to be side-by-side. Place them near resources, then connect them with roads or **subway stations** for fast courier travel.
- **Storage inside the zone.** Tools and storage must be inside the zone boundary — workers won't fetch tools dropped outside it. This is the #1 cause of "my workers aren't working."
- **Leave clear pathways** between machines and storage trays so workers can deposit items without pathfinding around clutter.

## Building Spacing: 3–4 Tiles

Buildings can't be rotated — shops especially — so plan your layout left-to-right and remember the map has more vertical space than horizontal. Space buildings **3–4 tiles apart** rather than 1–2. Closer spacing works mechanically but blocks the camera view of entrances and details, and makes it hard to spot what's happening in high-traffic areas. Shops facing the road sell better because tourists can reach them.

## Trash Bins and Sanitation Coverage

Tourists litter, and trash lowers satisfaction. Place **trash bins in high-traffic areas** — near the train station, food shops, and stalls. Build a **Sanitation Bureau** and hire 1–3 workers so trash disposal and street cleaning are automated. When purple stains appear on the ground (they damage nearby equipment), have bubble machines or sanitation workers clean them before repairs pile up.

## Tourist Zones: Cluster Around the Station

Tourists cluster near the train station and don't disperse much. Place dessert shops, souvenir stalls, and benches in the station square to capture sales before visitors wander off. Decorative interactive items — benches, spring riders, drinking fountains, streetlights — increase engagement, and decorative items can now be placed outside zone boundaries, so decorate freely.

## Production Zone Notes

- **Mining zone** has very little outdoor space — plan to place storage and machines inside the mine shaft.
- **Forestry zone** is obstructed by trees and bushes that can't be removed; your setup may be partially hidden — keep tool paths clear anyway.
- **Fishing zone** benefits from separate crab cages vs. rod fishing areas — workers will otherwise focus on one and ignore the other.

## When to Finalize Your Layout

The best advice from experienced players: **finish the main quests first** to unlock all tech and shops before planning your final town layout. Early-game layouts get reworked anyway — unlock the full building list, then lay out your dream town once. Moving buildings is free (build menu → move mode → press F), so don't be afraid to iterate.
