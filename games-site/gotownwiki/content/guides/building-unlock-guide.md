# Go-Go Town! Building Unlock Guide — All Buildings and How to Unlock Them

Buildings in Go-Go Town! unlock through a mix of story progression, **EGO Tech bundles** (the phone's tech tree app), and **town rank**. Nothing is locked behind cash — just progression. Here's the complete picture of what exists and how to get it.

## How Unlocks Work

Three progression paths feed into buildings:

1. **Story/Boss challenges** — the Boss calls with objectives that unlock specific buildings and tools directly
2. **EGO Tech bundles** — themed packages of buildings, tools, and upgrades bought with EGO in the phone app
3. **Town rank** — tourist reviews raise your rating; each tier raises coin/EGO caps and unlocks new EGO Tech bundles

## Tier 0: Starter Unlocks (Story)

These come from early story progression and need no purchases:

- **Industrial Entry** — pickaxe (mining), brick kiln (stone → bricks), mine lamp, chainsaw (logging), sawbench (wood → planks), small storage crate (16 slots)

## Starter Purchases (15 Blue Coins)

- **New Town** — small house (houses 2 residents), small shops (takeout/fast food, craft corner, book corner, hat shop)
- **Basic City Appearance** (10 EGO) — town benches, explorer statue, water pipes (these generate EGO over time)
- **Basic Municipal** (5 EGO) — garbage disposal, trash bins, bubble box (cleaning)

## Shop Sizes and Types

Shops come in three sizes — cycle through sizes with up/down keys when placing:

- **Small shops** — fast food, craft corner, book corner, hat shop
- **Medium shops** — bistro and similar sit-down food
- **Large shops** — cinema, barbershop, and other big-ticket venues

Each shop needs staff, a menu matching your supply chain, and supplied materials. The **bookstore** is a popular second shop because paper reams come from the forest table saw.

## Housing

- **Small house** — houses 2 residents
- **Apartments** — larger capacity housing for bigger populations

Housing is required before a tourist with a "+" can move in and become a worker — it's the bottleneck that limits your workforce.

## Infrastructure and Services

- **Train station** — daily tourist arrivals; the economic lifeline (build early)
- **Logistics building** — hire couriers to deliver materials to shops, food trucks, and construction sites
- **Sanitation Bureau** — hire workers for trash disposal, street cleaning, and repairs
- **Transit Terminals** — control which special tourist types (clowns, robots, aliens, dinosaurs, ghosts, mummies) are welcomed or banned

## Landmarks and Attractions

Late-game **Major Landmarks** draw crowds in bulk:

- **Lighthouse**
- **Conservatory**
- **Stadium**
- **Hot Springs**

Landmarks are expensive — build them once your supply chains can keep them stocked and maintained.

## Production Buildings (by Zone)

| Zone | Machines |
|------|----------|
| **Forest** | sawbench (planks, paper), table saw (paper reams), woodworking machine (frames, fiberboard), loom (fluff → cloth) |
| **Mine** | brick kiln (stone → bricks, metal → ingots), assembly machine (concrete, metal beams), magic extractor (raw magic on purple spots), gem crusher (gem powder → glass) |
| **Farm** | food processor (flour, sugar, protein, juice), milk/soy machine (milk, cheese, butter, chocolate), bean roaster (coffee, bread, popcorn) |
| **Fishery** | seafood converter (fish → sausages, protein), crab pots, rice paddies |

## Where to Spend EGO First

Priority order used by experienced players:

1. **New Town** (15 EGO) — housing + first shops
2. **Basic Municipal** (5 EGO) — trash bins keep tourists happy
3. **Automation unlocks** — zone clocks, logistics, sanitation workers
4. **Landmarks** — once the economy is stable

## Key Takeaway

There are no missable unlocks — everything remains available once its tier is reached. Finish the main quests to unlock all tech and shops before planning your final town layout, then buy EGO bundles in the order above to keep the economy growing.
