# Go-Go Town! FAQ — Common Questions, Answered

Go-Go Town! keeps most of its systems hidden behind a deliberately hands-off tutorial, so the same questions come up over and over. This guide collects the answers that actually matter for new mayors — each one is sourced from the Steam store page, official announcements, or this wiki's detailed guides.

## What Is Go-Go Town! and What Does It Cost?

Go-Go Town! is a co-op city builder and life simulation game developed and published by **Prideful Sloth**, an Australian indie studio. The 1.0 full release launched **July 16, 2026** on Steam, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2 after two years of Early Access. The standard price is **$27.99 USD** per the Steam store page (a 25% launch discount to $20.99 ran through July 30, 2026). PlayStation and Xbox versions were announced and are planned, but no release dates have been confirmed.

## Is There a Free Demo?

Yes. The **Go-Go Town! Demo** is a separate free listing on Steam (App ID 2445480, "Free on Demand" per SteamDB). It was first released during the game's Early Access period and has been updated since — the 1.0 launch announcement notes that new mayors can get a head start with the free demo available on Steam. The demo listing supports 10 languages and full controller support, per the store listing. Demo progress and the full game's relationship are separate saves, so trying the demo is safe for a future full-game purchase.

## How Do I Save My Game?

Go-Go Town! supports **64 manual save slots** plus **6 autosave slots**, per a developer response on the Steam Community forums. In the save menu, the first slot is labeled "Empty Slot" — selecting it creates a brand-new manual save instead of overwriting an old one, and autosaves appear in the same menu. Autosaves happen roughly every 5–10 minutes of play. Save files live under `AppData\LocalLow\Prideful Sloth\Go-Go Town!` on Windows.

One community-reported caveat [Unconfirmed]: starting a "New Game" can overwrite your active save after some playtime, so experienced players recommend always saving into a new or empty slot and enabling Steam Cloud. Make a manual backup before big building projects.

## Why Are My Workers Not Working?

This is the single most-asked Go-Go Town! question. Work through this checklist in order (from our [Tips and Tricks guide](/guides/tips-and-tricks)):

1. **Tools inside the zone boundary** — a machine with its tools placed outside its production zone confuses worker AI
2. **Storage inside the zone** — workers need a valid storage point close by
3. **Seeds available** — farm and fishery workers stop if inventories run dry; collect seeds from flower patches or import seed packs (exact spawns vary [Unconfirmed])
4. **Storage not full** — full worker backpacks and full bins stop production lines cold

If a specific worker is stuck, unhire and rehire them, or nudge them with a vehicle. And don't fight the couriers: if you place materials before the courier does, the AI can drop items on the ground — let workers do their jobs.

## How Many Players Can Play Co-op?

Up to **4 players on Steam**, in both online co-op and local split-screen, plus Steam Remote Play Together. Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 support 1–2 players locally and online. Cross-platform co-op between PC and consoles is not currently featured — check each platform's store page for its co-op details. See our [Co-op Multiplayer Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide) for setup and division-of-labor strategies.

## What Is EGO and What Do I Spend It On?

EGO is the blue-coin currency generated by tourist satisfaction — decor, cleanliness, and fulfilled desires all feed it. You spend EGO in the **Ego Tech app** on your phone to unlock buildings, tools (jackhammer, chainsaw), and automation features, and later to buy land parcels. There are no tricks to earning it: keep tourists happy and it accumulates steadily. Full breakdown in the [Money Making Guide](/guides/money-making-guide).

## How Do Vending Machines Work?

Vending machines are the perfect sink for fast-accumulating items. Rice, protein, and honeycomb pile up quickly once production zones run — fill vending machines with surplus and they sell in the background without staff. Anything your shops can't move fast enough is a vending machine candidate.

## How Do I Unlock More Land?

After the "Build and Bustle" update, the map is much larger, with new areas unlocked through the **Real Estate Agent, Stanley S. Trollson** — you buy land parcels with EGO and unlock new construction areas across the map. If you've run out of space for houses, check what land is still locked before giving up on population growth (see the [Townie Guide](/guides/townie-guide)).

## Can I Move Buildings After Placing Them?

Yes, freely and at no cost. Open the build menu, pick the last button (move mode), hover the building, and press F. Iterating on layout is cheap — many players finish the main quests first and then rearrange once all tech is unlocked.

## Can I Play 1.0 with My Early Access Save?

Early Access saves are **not compatible** with 1.0, but your progress isn't lost. A dedicated **"ggt_legacy"** game version lets you keep playing old saves: right-click Go-Go Town! in Steam → Properties → Game Versions & Betas → select `ggt_legacy`. Fresh 1.0 games start under the new systems, and you can switch back to the default public version at any time. Full details in the [1.0 Update Guide](/guides/one-point-zero-update-guide).

## Is There a Creative Mode?

Yes — Creative Mode is a relaxed sandbox with the full building toolset, no money, and no restrictions, on a dedicated new map. It was included free in the 1.0 update and is also the best place to test layouts before committing in Story Mode. See the [Creative Mode Guide](/guides/creative-mode-guide).

## Does Progress Carry Across Platforms?
