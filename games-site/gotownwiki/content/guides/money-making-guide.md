# How to Make Money Fast in Go-Go Town!

Go-Go Town! has three currencies: **Coins** (bought from tourist purchases), **EGO** (earned from tourist satisfaction, spent on tech unlocks), and **Import Tickets** (earned from export jobs, spent on equipment and vehicles). Each needs a different strategy.

## Early Game: Coins from Your First Shops

Coins come almost exclusively from tourists buying goods. In the first hour, that means one thing: **stock Baldo's shop**. Gather rocks and lumber, craft items at the machines, and keep the shelves full. Use your truck (48 slots) instead of your backpack (8 slots) to haul materials in bulk.

A faster early trick used by many players: **load the truck with raw farm produce and sell it directly at stalls or food trucks**. Stalls don't need employees — stock them manually and tourists buy on their own. Food trucks get auto-restocked by couriers once logistics is unlocked.

## The Train Station Is Your Economy

Build the **train station** as early as possible. Daily tourist arrivals are your economic lifeline — each visitor is a potential purchase. Place dessert shops and souvenir stalls near the station; tourists currently cluster there and don't disperse much, so proximity to the spawn point drives sales. Maxing the tourist slider at the station increases arrivals when you're ready to scale.

## Mid Game: Shops, Stalls, and Vending Machines

Your production zones (farm, fishery, forest, mine) overproduce quickly. The solution is **more sales points**:

- **Shops** — need staff, a menu, and supplied raw materials. Match each shop's menu to your production chain.
- **Stalls** — no staff required; stock manually.
- **Vending machines** — the perfect sink for fast-accumulating items like **rice, protein, and honeycomb**. Fill them and forget them.

Most items sell in the 10–15 coin range, so **volume matters more than margin**. The more shops and machines you keep stocked, the faster coins pile up.

## EGO: Earn It from Happy Tourists

EGO is generated by tourist satisfaction. Decor, cleanliness, and fulfilled Tourist Desires all feed it. You spend EGO in the **Ego Tech app** to unlock buildings, tools, and automation — so a decorated, clean town is also a faster-progressing one. There are no tricks here: keep tourists happy and EGO accumulates steadily.

## Import Tickets: Export App Jobs

Import Tickets come from **Export App jobs** on your phone, Ticket Pods, and loot boxes. They unlock equipment, vehicles, and storage upgrades in the Import App. Export jobs usually ask for specific items — check the requirements and deliver via courier or truck.

## Endgame: The 50,000-Coin Grind

Late-game content (a deed) costs 50,000 coins, which players report earning at roughly 500–1,800 coins per in-game day — around 4,200 coins per real-world hour at normal play. Strategies from players who made it:

- **Convert large buildings to theatres** — entertainment draws crowds and sells consistently.
- **Turn the farm into a popcorn factory** — popcorn sells for 15 coins each at market stands and has a fast production chain.
- **Max the tourist slider** at the train station to maximize daily arrivals.
- **Sell duplicate treasure finds** to Arizona Smythe III in the Town Hall — a reliable coin source that beats basic crafted goods.

## Money-Saving Habits

Since there's no rent or wages, the only real leak is **wasted materials**. Don't overproduce rice or fish — full worker backpacks stop production lines. Lock storage bins (press I on a storage box, select a product, press L) so workers deposit items automatically and nothing rots in the wrong box.
