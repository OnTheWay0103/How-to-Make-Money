# Go-Go Town! Creative Mode Guide — Build Without Limits

**Creative Mode** is one of the two flagship features of the Go-Go Town! 1.0 update (July 16, 2026), alongside Treasure Hunting. It gives you the **full building toolset with no money and no restrictions** on a dedicated new map — everything unlocked, everything free, nothing to manage. It's the mode for players who want to build, decorate, and experiment rather than run an economy.

## What Creative Mode Is

| Feature | Creative Mode | Story Mode |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| **Money** | None — everything is free | Coins + EGO economy |
| **Unlocks** | Everything available from the start | EGO Tech bundles + town rank |
| **Materials** | None required — no schematics to buy | Full production chains |
| **Map** | Dedicated sandbox map | The standard town map |
| **Economy** | Removed entirely | Core of the game |
| **Purpose** | Build and decorate | Progress and automate |

The pitch is simple: Creative Mode removes the *management* so you can flex your decorative muscles — plot out a whole town without the stress of collecting materials or paying for schematics.

## How to Get Started

- **Select Creative Mode from the main menu.** The game now offers two modes: Story Mode (intended progression) and Creative Mode.
- **Everything is unlocked.** Shops, houses, infrastructure, landmarks, decorations, and attractions are all available immediately — no EGO Tech grind, no town rank gate. See the [Building Unlock Guide](/guides/building-unlock-guide) for the full catalog of what exists.
- **Build freely on the dedicated map.** Roads, zones, buildings, and decor — the same tools as Story Mode, without the costs.

## Best Uses for Creative Mode

### 1. Test Layouts Before Committing
The best practical use: **plot out layouts and decorations before committing to them in a Story Mode playthrough**. The [Best Layout Guide](/guides/best-layout-guide) covers road design, zone organization, and courier pathing — build your ideal town in Creative first, screenshot it, then replicate the proven layout in your real save.

### 2. Experiment with Placement
Try building spacings, road patterns, and zone boundaries without consequence. What looks good on paper can clog courier paths in practice — Creative Mode is the zero-risk place to find out.

### 3. Pure Decoration
With no economy, decorating is the whole game. Use decor unlocked in Story Mode here without paying EGO (in Story Mode the 1.0 **Rebuy system** lets you repurchase unlocked decor with EGO instead of re-earning it — Creative skips even that).

### 4. Design Town Halls and Showcases
Plan your Town Hall collection displays (Treasure Hunting items, fish tanks, stands, plinths) and landmark placements in the sandbox before building them for real.

## Creative Mode Tips

- **Use it to reverse-engineer the tech tree.** Since everything is unlocked, you can preview every building and decoration the game offers — a preview of what to prioritize in Story Mode.
- **Practice zone-based logistics.** Even in a sandbox, laying out efficient production zones (forestry, mining, farming, fishing) is practice for the real economy — see the [Production & Crafting Guide](/guides/crafting-production-guide).
- **Share the fun in co-op.** Creative Mode works with the same co-op systems — up to 4 players on Steam — making it the easiest way to demo the game to a friend.
