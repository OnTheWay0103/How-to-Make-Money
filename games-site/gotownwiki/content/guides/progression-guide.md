# Go-Go Town! Progression Guide — How to Unlock Content Faster

New mayors often feel like Go-Go Town! doles out content in a confusing order. The good news: everything is driven by just **three interlocking progression tracks**, and once you see how they feed each other, the "right" route becomes obvious. This guide maps the route from your first hour to the endgame — for the full building catalog, see the [Building Unlock Guide](/guides/building-unlock-guide).

## The Three Progression Tracks

1. **Story / Boss quests** — Boss calls with objectives (place decor, build a building, sell a certain amount). Completing them unlocks specific buildings and tools directly.
2. **Town Ranking** — tourists leave reviews after visits; good reviews raise your town ranking. Each tier unlocks the next set of EGO Tech bundles and **raises your coin and EGO caps**.
3. **The EGO Tech tree** — spend EGO (earned from tourist satisfaction) in the phone's Ego Tech app on themed bundles of buildings, tools, and automation.

The loop that connects them: **Boss quests push your rank → rank tiers unlock EGO bundles → EGO bundles give you buildings and tools → better towns attract happier tourists → more EGO and reviews → the next rank.** Nothing is locked behind cash, and there are no missable unlocks — everything stays available once its tier is reached.

## Stage 1: Your First Hours

The opening is the only part where you do everything yourself: stock **Baldo's shop**, build the **train station** as early as you can afford it, scatter decor to raise EGO, open a second shop, and invite the first "+" tourists to move in. Walk through it step by step in the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) — the goal is not speed but the loop: tourists buy, their happiness generates EGO, reviews start climbing the ranking.

## Stage 2: The First Rank Tiers

Town ranking is your main early progression gate. Each tier unlocks new EGO Tech bundles (housing, shops, tools, automation) and raises your caps, so ranking up is the fastest way to expand your options. To rank up efficiently:

- **Check the Tourist Tracker app daily** and fix complaints — the Tourist Trap update added individual **Tourist Desires** (entertainment, rest, hunger, health, exploration, shopping) that hit your rating directly
- **Place trash bins in high-traffic areas** — cleanliness is the cheapest satisfaction investment in the game
- **Match shop menus to what tourists can afford** — star-rated recipes tier menus, and items that are "too fancy or too cheap" draw complaints

## Stage 3: Workforce and Automation

Once you have residents, hire them at **zone clocks** (farm, fishery, forest, mine) to automate production, then build the **Logistics building** for couriers and the **Sanitation Bureau** for cleaning. Automation options unlock gradually through the **economy tier** of the tech tree. This is the stage where the town starts paying for itself — full setup in the [Automation Guide](/guides/automation-guide).

## Stage 4: Climbing the EGO Tech Tree

The tech tree is a long ladder of tiers (nine tiers are achievement-tracked; the 1.0 update rebalanced and reorganized it). Experienced players' priority order:

1. **New Town** — housing plus your first small shops
2. **Basic Municipal** — trash bins keep tourists happy and ranking climbing
3. **Automation unlocks** — timeclocks, couriers, sanitation workers
4. **Landmarks** — once the economy is stable

The tree revamp in 1.0 changed exact costs, so older guides' prices are out of date — check the app in-game rather than trusting pre-1.0 numbers (see the [Building Unlock Guide](/guides/building-unlock-guide) for the full structure).

## Stage 5: Population and Land

Workforce growth is capped by housing: build houses and apartments to keep spare rooms for "+" tourists, and complete each Townie's unique recruitment quest before inviting them. The game's roster totals **83 townies** (per the community wiki), and milestones like getting **30 people to join the town** are achievement-tracked. When you run out of room, buy new land parcels from the **Real Estate Agent (Stanley S. Trollson)** with EGO. Details in the [Townie Guide](/guides/townie-guide).

## Stage 6: The Endgame

The 1.0 update reworked the endgame story (the mystery door plotline was finally resolved) and overhauled progression payouts, so pre-1.0 endgame numbers — like the old community-reported 50,000-coin deeds — no longer match [待确认]. The confirmed endgame milestones:

- **Reach the top of the Town Ranking** — achievement-tracked ("It's so lonely up here", with the halfway mark at "Living on a Prayer")
- **Accumulate 10,000 EGO** — the "Ego Boost" achievement
- **Build Major Landmarks** — the Lighthouse, Conservatory, Stadium, and Hot Springs draw crowds in bulk once supply chains can stock them
- **Finish Treasure Hunting collections** — fossils, artifacts, gems, relics, and bugs from the [Treasure Hunting Guide](/guides/treasure-hunting-guide), with duplicates sellable for coins
- **Complete the 56 achievements** — see the [Achievements Guide](/guides/achievement-guide) for the full documented list

## How to Progress Faster — Checklist

1. Build the train station early and max the tourist slider when you're ready to scale sales
2. Keep decor, trash bins, and sanitation coverage ahead of tourist volume
3. Fulfill Tourist Desires and fix complaints in the Tourist Tracker app daily
4. Complete Boss quests as soon as they pop — they are the direct unlock path
5. Lock storage bins so workers deposit automatically and nothing rots in the wrong box
6. Don't overproduce rice or fish — full backpacks stop production lines
7. Convert every "+" tourist you can afford to house — workforce is the multiplier
8. Buy EGO bundles in the priority order above, and buy land before space becomes the bottleneck

## Common Progression Blockers

**"My rank isn't climbing."** Reviews drive the ranking — if tourists are unhappy, fix desires and cleanliness first; the Tourist Tracker shows the complaints.

**"I have no EGO and nothing to build."** EGO comes from satisfied tourists. Decor, cleanliness, and fulfilled desires all feed it — a decorated town is a faster-progressing one.

**"I can't recruit anyone."** Recruitment volume tracks tourist volume, and "+" tourists need a house or apartment with a free room plus a completed request. See the Townie Guide's troubleshooting section.

**"I'm out of space."** Buy land parcels from the Real Estate Agent with EGO — and remember buildings can be moved freely afterward, so lock in the layout last.
