# Go-Go Town! Beginner Guide — Your First Hour

Go-Go Town! gives you a rundown town and almost no tutorial hand-holding. The good news: there is no timer, no rent, and no wage drain, so nothing you do in the first hour can ruin your save. The bad news: the open-ended tutorial leaves most new players wondering what to do first. This guide walks you through the core loop in order.

## The Core Loop

Everything in Go-Go Town! revolves around one cycle: **run the town → attract tourists → invite tourists to become residents → hire residents to work → use the income to build more → attract more tourists**. Every decision you make feeds into this loop. The beginning is the only part where you do the work yourself — by mid-game, hired Townies handle nearly everything.

## Step 1: Stock Baldo's Shop

Your first money-maker is the shop where the Townie **Baldo** works. Gather materials (rocks, lumber) from around town and stock the shop so tourists can buy things. You can assign Baldo to work the shop, or craft items yourself by facing the machine and pressing F.

Your starting backpack is small, so use the **truck** (parked on the road between the forest and the mine) to haul materials in bulk — it carries far more than your backpack and makes early runs dramatically faster. Remember that the truck can only drive on roads, not on brick or tile surfaces.

## Step 2: Build the Train Station Early

The **train station** is the single most important building in the early game. Tourists arrive by train every day, and tourist purchases are your economic lifeline. Community guides universally recommend building it as early as you can afford it, then placing dessert shops and souvenir stalls nearby — tourists cluster around the station and will buy whatever you stock there.

## Step 3: Raise Your EGO with Decor

Decorations make tourists happy, and happy tourists generate **EGO** — the currency you spend in the Ego Tech app on the phone to unlock new buildings, tools (jackhammer, chainsaw), and automation features. Once you have a little money, drop benches, statues, streetlights, and trees around the town square. Decor is also one of the fastest ways to push your town to the next rank.

## Step 4: Open a Second Shop

With your first coin income flowing, open a second shop — the **bookstore** is a popular early choice because paper reams come from the table saw in the forest, a chain you can often start feeding immediately. Each shop needs three things: assigned staff, a menu that matches your supply chain, and supplied raw materials.

## Step 5: Invite Tourists to Become Townies

Tourists with a **"+"** above their head want to move in. Talk to them, fulfill their request, and invite them to stay — but only after building a **house or apartment**, otherwise they have nowhere to live. Once a Townie moves in, you can assign them to work in the mines, forest, farm, shops, and more, which is how the town starts running itself.

## Step 6: Complete Boss Challenges

Boss will call with challenges — objectives that unlock new content and let you advance to the next town rank. Early challenges are usually simple (place decor, build a building, sell a certain amount). Complete them whenever they pop up; town rank is your main progression system and each tier unlocks new EGO Tech bundles.

## Quick Controls Reference

- **B** — build menu (last button = move mode; hover a building and press F to move it)
- **F** — use / interact
- **Q** — drop item
- **I** — backpack / storage
- **R** — switch recipe on machines, or pause the train station
- **TAB** — ring your bike bell
- **ENTER** — drift (yes, really)

## Common First-Hour Mistakes

1. **Overproducing rice** — plant only what your shops need. One rice paddy is usually plenty; rice overflows quickly and full worker backpacks stop production.
2. **Forgetting trash bins** — tourists litter, and trash lowers satisfaction. Place trash bins in high-traffic areas and hire sanitation workers once they unlock.
3. **Fighting the couriers for work** — if you place materials before your courier does, the AI can get confused and drop items on the ground. Let workers do their job.
4. **Blocking roads** — keep roads clear for delivery vehicles. If couriers can't drive, they run on foot and the whole supply chain slows down.

## What's Next?

Once you have the train station, a stocked shop, a house, and one or two Townies working, you've beaten the tutorial in practice.
