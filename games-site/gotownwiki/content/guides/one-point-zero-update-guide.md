# Go-Go Town! 1.0 Update Guide — Everything That Changed

Go-Go Town! left Early Access with the **1.0 update on July 16, 2026**, landing simultaneously on Steam, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2. It's the game's biggest update ever — two flagship features, a stack of quality-of-life changes, and a complete rework of systems veterans will need to re-learn. This guide catalogs everything 1.0 added and changed.

## Flagship Features

### Creative Mode
A brand-new mode with the **full building toolset, no money, and no restrictions** on a dedicated new map. Removes the economy entirely for pure building and decorating — and the best testing ground for layouts before you commit in Story Mode. Full breakdown in the [Creative Mode Guide](/guides/creative-mode-guide).

### Treasure Hunting
The collection layer: **bugs, artifacts, fish, gems, relics, and fossils** hidden across the map, tracked in a Collections app, displayable around town (fish tanks, stands, plinths), with duplicates sellable for coins. Full breakdown in the [Treasure Hunting Guide](/guides/treasure-hunting-guide).

## New Content & Systems

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Garbirds** | New nuisance creatures that root through trash and scare tourists — they won't leave until dealt with |
| **Twitch Integration** | Connect Twitch: chat members appear as named tourists and can trigger commands like !chat, !cash, !gift, !vomit, !stinky, !trash, !:) and !:( |
| **Rebuy System** | Repurchase unlocked decor with EGO instead of re-earning each item |
| **Imports** | Order vehicles or individual items (down to a single tomato) for a price |
| **Offroad Driving** | All player vehicles can now drive off-road |
| **Lost and Found** | The Maintenance Crew collects dropped items; unclaimed items are thrown out after 12 in-game hours |
| **Vehicle Garage** | Store your fleet; someone inside disposes of surplus vehicles |
| **Town Settings App** | New in-game settings app for town configuration |

## 30 New Achievements

1.0 grew the achievement list by **30 new achievements, for a total of 56**. The original 26 (tech tree tiers, town rank, and early-game milestones) carry over — the new 30 cover 1.0 systems like Treasure Hunting and Creative Mode. See the [Achievements Guide](/guides/achievement-guide) for the full documented list.

## Reworked Systems

- **Revamped tech tree** — the EGO Tech app was rebalanced and reorganized (see the [Building Unlock Guide](/guides/building-unlock-guide))
- **Tiered Town Ranking rewards** — progression payouts were overhauled
- **Reworked economy** — the ticket-based import/export currency was removed; exports now pay coins (see the [Money-Making Guide](/guides/money-making-guide))
- **Revised endgame story** — the story mode was reworked for full release
- **Character customization before gameplay** — create your mayor before the tutorial
- **New shops, recipes, decorations, vehicles, and sprinklers** — across every category
- **Rebuilt tutorial** — new for beginners, skippable for veterans, accessible from the main menu

## Early Access Saves — Important

**Early Access saves are NOT compatible with 1.0.** If you have an EA save, don't panic — EA progress isn't lost:

- A dedicated **"ggt_legacy" game version** lets you keep playing old saves
- Fresh 1.0 games start under the new systems

## Co-op in 1.0

- Up to **4 players on Steam** (local and online), **2 on Switch** — unchanged from the [Co-op Multiplayer Guide](/guides/coop-multiplayer-guide)
- More cooperative content is on the roadmap
