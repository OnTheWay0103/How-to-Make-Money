## What is Go-Go Town!?

Go-Go Town! is a co-op city builder and life simulation game developed and published by Prideful Sloth, the Australian studio behind Yonder: The Cloud Catcher Chronicles and Grow: Song of the Evertree. It launched on Steam in Early Access on June 18, 2024, and hit its 1.0 full release on July 16, 2026 — the same day the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 Editions arrived.

You play as the mayor of a rundown former tourist destination. The core loop is simple: **run the town → attract tourists → invite them to become residents → hire residents to work → use the income to build more and attract more tourists**. Production chains, resource allocation, and layout planning become increasingly important as your town grows — and most of the work can eventually be automated by hired Townies.

The game is deliberately chill: there is no timer, no rent, and no wage drain. The early game is the only part where you have to do manual work yourself; by mid-game, your town can largely run itself.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Coins** — earned from tourist purchases at shops and stalls; spent on buildings, decorations, and Town Challenges
- **EGO (blue coins)** — earned from tourist satisfaction; spent in the Ego Tech app on unlocks and upgrades
- **Import Tickets** — earned from Export App jobs, Ticket Pods, and loot boxes; spent in the Import App
- **Four Production Zones** — farming, fishing, forestry, and mining, each with machines and hired workers
- **Automation** — zone clocks, Logistics couriers, and sanitation workers make the town self-sufficient
- **Tourist Desires** — unique preferences (entertainment, rest, hunger, shopping) that drive your town rating
- **Treasure Hunting (1.0)** — fossils, artifacts, gems, and relics to collect, display, and sell
- **Chaotic Co-Op** — up to 4 players online or local split-screen on Steam, 2 players on Switch
