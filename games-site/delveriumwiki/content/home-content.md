## Featured Guides

### What is Delverium?

Delverium is a **1-8 player co-op survival sandbox RPG** from independent studio Sagestone Games (Brisbane, Australia). In a procedurally generated world, players gather resources, build shelters, plant and harvest crops, breed animals, fish, cook, recruit NPCs, and descend into dangerous dungeons to uncover the mystery of "the Faults" threatening the land. It is frequently compared to Terraria, Stardew Valley, Core Keeper and Minecraft.

### Key Facts

- **Developer/Publisher**: Sagestone Games (Scott Lowther, with music by BAFTA-winning composer Jeff van Dyck)
- **Release**: Steam Early Access, September 22, 2026 (PC)
- **Players**: 1-8 online co-op, 4-player split-screen, solo
- **Demo**: Free on Steam since February 7, 2025 (major update February 20, 2026)
- **Wishlists**: 80,000+ on Steam
- **Platforms**: PC (Steam), Steam Deck confirmed
- **Funding**: Screen Queensland Games Grants, Screen Australia Games Production Fund
