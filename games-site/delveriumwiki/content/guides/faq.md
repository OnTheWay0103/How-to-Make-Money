# Delverium FAQ — Quick Answers Before Early Access Launch

Delverium enters Steam Early Access on **September 22, 2026** — and before launch, the questions around it are louder than the answers. This FAQ covers what players actually ask, with everything grounded in official announcements and anything unconfirmed clearly marked `[待确认]`. For the deep dive on release details, see the [Release Date & Early Access Guide](/guides/release-date-ea-guide).

## The Basics

**What is Delverium?**

Delverium is an open-world survival sandbox fantasy adventure for **1–8 players** from Sagestone Games, an indie studio in Brisbane, Australia. It blends exploration, farming, crafting, settlement building, and dungeon crawling in a procedurally generated pixel-art world — frequently compared to Terraria, Stardew Valley, Core Keeper, and Valheim. The confirmed core loop: **gather resources → build shelters → farm → recruit and trade with NPCs → craft items → descend into dangerous dungeons.**

**When does it release?**

**Tuesday, September 22, 2026**, into Steam Early Access. Originally planned for 2025, it was moved to the current date.

**How much will it cost?**

**Not announced** `[待确认]`. The developers have confirmed the Early Access price will be **lower than the final 1.0 price**, but the actual number hasn't been published. The game already sits at **80,000+ Steam wishlists**, which suggests a modest indie price — that's speculation, not fact.

**Where can I play it?**

Steam on PC, plus confirmed **Steam Deck** support. No console or mobile versions have been announced as of August 2026.

**Is there a demo?**

Yes — a free demo has been on Steam since February 7, 2025. It's a slice of the game (roughly 1–2 hours): the initial biome, one dungeon, and every major system — gathering, building, farming, NPC recruitment and trading, crafting, and dungeon combat. A big update on February 20, 2026 added 8-player online co-op, a world boss, Journey Shrines (fast travel), and lore/quest systems to the demo.

## Multiplayer & Solo

**How many players can play together?**

Up to **8 players online** (via Steam) and up to **4 players in local split-screen** co-op — the demo supports both since the February 20, 2026 update. Drop-in/drop-out is supported.

**Can I play solo?**

Yes — the game is fully playable solo, and 1–8 players is the confirmed design. Solo play is slower (you cover every pillar yourself), but it's a designed option, not an afterthought.

**Is there PvP?**

Not announced. Everything confirmed about the game is co-operative — the demo has no PvP systems. Treat any claim about Delverium PvP as `[待确认]`.

**Can I play with random people?**

Online co-op works via Steam, so joining strangers' worlds is technically possible — but nothing has been announced about matchmaking, servers, or a lobby system `[待确认]`. Community play is expected to happen through friends and Discord groups.

## Gameplay Questions

**Is Delverium like Stardew Valley or Terraria?**

Both, by the developers' own framing. The farming, NPCs, and cozy loop draw on Stardew Valley; the procedural world, building, and co-op structure draw on Terraria (lead developer Scott Lowther started the project around 2016 after playing split-screen Terraria with his family). The confirmed loop places farming and dungeons side by side, so the comparison is fair.

**What is the story?**

A mystery called **"the Faults"** is invading and destabilizing the land. Secrets are hidden in the dungeons, and the confirmed lore/quest systems involve hunting down the Faults and uncovering "lost fragments of lore" to reveal the hidden truth.

**Can I fish?**

Yes — **fishing is confirmed** as part of the farming loop, and it's listed among the confirmed Early Access launch features.

**Can I farm and raise animals?**

Yes. Farming is a confirmed core pillar: tilling soil, planting seeds, watering crops, breeding animals, fishing, and cooking. **Chickens and sheep** are confirmed; other animals are `[待确认]`.

**Are there NPCs?**

Yes — NPCs wander the world and move into your settlement when your buildings have room. They trade with you and buy your excess resources for **copper coins** (confirmed in demo coverage). The starter tools — axe, pickaxe, hammer — are given by an NPC.

**Are there dungeons and bosses?**

Yes. The world includes **hidden caves and dangerous dungeons** where you mine ores, collect rare materials, and "loot unique items." The demo has one dungeon and a **world boss** (added February 20, 2026; name not announced). More bosses are planned during Early Access — see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

**What is the combat like?**

Melee and ranged weapon paths are both confirmed, with no classes — builds come from gear, role, and playstyle. See the [Melee Build Guide](/guides/melee-combat-build-guide) and [Ranged Build Guide](/guides/ranged-magic-build-guide).

## Early Access & Technical

**How long will Early Access last?**

Approximately **two years**. Sagestone plans to add new biomes, bosses, NPCs, and features during that window, steered by player feedback.

**Does demo progress carry over to the full game?**

**Not announced — `[待确认]`.** Sagestone has not confirmed whether demo saves transfer to the Early Access version. The demo is short (1–2 hours), so even a fresh start at launch is a small cost — but don't assume carryover.

**What are the system requirements?**

**Not published yet** — the Steam page doesn't list specs as of August 2026. The pixel-art style and confirmed Steam Deck support suggest modest requirements, but official numbers are pending.

**Is there controller support?**

Yes — full controller support is confirmed, and the demo already has full control rebinding.

**Who made the music?**

The audio is handled by BAFTA-winning composer/audio director **Jeff van Dyck** (Unpacking, Forts, Paint the Town Red) — one of the few production details the developer has shared.

**What languages will it support?**

Multiple languages are confirmed for the Early Access launch, though the full list is `[待确认]`.

**Are there microtransactions?**

No paid currency or microtransaction system has been announced. **Cosmetics** are confirmed as an Early Access launch feature — whether they're earnable in-game, paid, or both is unannounced `[待确认]`.

**Should I wishlist it?**

If it sounds like your game — yes. Wishlists are how the developers measure demand (the game already has 80,000+), and it costs nothing. You can also play the free demo right now.

## Bottom Line

Delverium's confirmed record is clear about its systems — 1–8 player co-op, farming, NPCs, crafting, dungeons, the Faults — and honest about its gaps: price, system requirements, save carryover, and boss specifics are all `[待确认]` until launch. Play the demo, wishlist the game, and check back after September 22 for the full picture.
