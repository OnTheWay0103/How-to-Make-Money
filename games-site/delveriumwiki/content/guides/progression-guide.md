# Delverium Progression Guide — How Unlocks & Progression Work

Delverium has no skill trees and no quest log with checkpoints — progression is a loop, and every stage of it unlocks the next one. Understanding that order is the whole game: each step's output (tools, food, gear, wares, fast travel) is the input the next step needs. This guide maps the confirmed unlock order, what the demo proves about it, and what to prioritize when Early Access launches on September 22, 2026.

> **Pre-release guide — will be updated for EA launch (Sept 22, 2026).** Everything below is built on confirmed systems and demo coverage; specifics marked `[待确认]` are unverified until launch.

## The Confirmed Progression Loop

The developers confirm the core loop in this exact order:

```
Gather resources → build shelters → farm → recruit & trade with NPCs
  → craft items → descend into dangerous dungeons
```

Every stage feeds the next. Here is how each step actually unlocks what comes after it.

### Step 1: Starter tools — an NPC, not a workbench

The first unlock is a gift: your opening **axe, pickaxe, and hammer are given by an NPC** (confirmed in demo preview coverage). This sets the game's pattern — progression leans on NPCs, not just crafting. You can't gather efficiently without tools, so the starter-tools NPC is the true "press Start" of the game.

### Step 2: Gathering → building

With tools, you gather wood, stone, and ores. Those feed **shelter building**, which is where progression becomes spatial: NPCs only move into your settlement **when your structures have sufficient space** (confirmed). Building room is not decoration — it is the unlock condition for the entire NPC layer.

### Step 3: Farming → sustainability

Farming (tilling, planting, watering, breeding animals, fishing, cooking) is a confirmed core pillar, expanded in the demo's February 20, 2026 update. Its role in progression is economic: food and cooked meals sustain you, and surplus produce is the currency of the next step.

### Step 4: NPCs → trading → copper coins

Recruit enough tenants and **trading opens up**: NPCs trade with you and **buy your excess resources for copper coins** (confirmed in demo coverage). Different NPCs offer different wares — community-documented demo path: it took **three or four tenants** before an NPC appeared offering **chickens and sheep**. More NPCs is the way to unlock variety. Copper coins are the confirmed trading currency; prices and wares lists are `[待确认]`.

### Step 5: Crafting → gear

The material pipeline turns everything above into equipment: tools, weapons, armor, and building materials. Crafting is confirmed as the step *before* dungeons — you craft weapons and armor, then descend. The [Crafting Guide](/guides/crafting-guide) covers the full system; the progression point is that gear is the gatekeeper to the danger layer.

### Step 6: Journey Shrines → fast travel

The demo's February 20, 2026 update added **fast travel via hidden "Journey Shrines"** (confirmed). Finding a shrine is a progression unlock in the exploration sense: it makes resupply cheap and deep dives retryable. Map as you go — shrines are hidden, and in a procedural world, your own notes are the map.

### Step 7: Dungeons → unique loot and lore

Dungeons are the confirmed end of the loop: "high risk dungeon crawling," where you mine ores, collect rare materials, and **loot unique items**. Dungeons also hide the story — secrets about **"the Faults"** are buried in them, and the confirmed lore/quest systems involve recovering "lost fragments of lore."

### Step 8: The world boss

The demo's world boss (added February 20, 2026; **name not announced**) is the current final step. It is a party boss by design — 8-player online co-op shipped in the same update. Expect the first EA weeks to revolve around gearing up and organizing world-boss kills. See the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

## Unlock Priorities: What to Do First at EA Launch

Based on the confirmed order, the efficient unlock path:

1. **Get your tools from the starter NPC** — the first five minutes, no exceptions
2. **Build shelter space early** — NPCs need room; building room is the unlock gate for trade
3. **Plant a farm in week one** — food sustains runs; surplus produce becomes copper coins
4. **Recruit to 3–4 tenants** — the community-documented threshold for livestock and richer wares
5. **Craft gear before you descend** — the loop places weapons and armor before dungeons
6. **Find a Journey Shrine before deep dives** — fast travel makes failed runs cheap
7. **Group up for the world boss** — it's a party boss; organize before you engage

## Solo vs Co-op: Progression Pacing

| | Solo | Co-op (2–4) | Co-op (5–8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | Slowest — you cover every pillar | Balanced | Fastest with role splits |
| Unlock gating | Building + farming + combat all on you | Shared | Specialization clears stages in parallel |
| Copper coins | All yours | Split by design | Group economy |
| World boss | Unconfirmed viability | Challenging | Intended scale |

Solo players should prioritize farming first — food and coin sustain everything else. Co-op parties should split the loop: one builder/farmer chain while the other gathers and scouts for shrines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the first thing to unlock in Delverium?**
A: Your tools — the starter NPC gives you the axe, pickaxe, and hammer. Everything else depends on gathering.

**Q: How do I unlock NPCs?**
A: Build shelter space. NPCs move in when your settlement has room, and they bring wares and copper-coin trading with them.

**Q: How do I get copper coins?**
A: Sell excess resources and surplus farm produce to NPCs — the confirmed trading loop.

**Q: How do I unlock fast travel?**
A: Find hidden Journey Shrines — added to the demo in the February 20, 2026 update. They're hidden; explore thoroughly.

**Q: When do I unlock the world boss?**
A: The world boss is in the demo now. In progression terms it's the end of the loop — gear up through the dungeon layer first.
