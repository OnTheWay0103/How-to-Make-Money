# Delverium Melee Combat Build Guide — Frontline Fighter

Delverium is a **1–8 player co-op survival sandbox RPG** where you gather, farm, craft and descend into dangerous dungeons. There is **no confirmed class system** — so "melee build" here means a **playstyle role**: the frontline fighter who stands between the party and whatever the dungeons throw at them. This guide covers the role, gear priorities, co-op responsibilities and solo viability — using only confirmed facts.

## The Frontline Role

| Role | What you do | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontline fighter** | Take point in dungeon fights and protect the group | Weapons + armor (both confirmed crafting categories) |
| **Melee bruiser** | Deal damage at close range while holding enemy attention | Weapon durability and food supply (cooking confirmed) |
| **Party anchor** | Anchor the group in 1–8 player co-op runs | Communication + gear (co-op confirmed) |
