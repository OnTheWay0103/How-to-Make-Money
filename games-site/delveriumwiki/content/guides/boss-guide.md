## The Preparation Checklist (Built on Confirmed Systems)

The game's own loop dictates the prep order: **you craft weapons and armor before you descend.** Walk into a boss fight with this list:

1. **Crafted weapons** — the loop places weapon crafting before dungeon descent; a boss fight with starter tools is a mistake
2. **Armor equipped** — armor is a confirmed category; protection is part of the design
3. **Food and meals** — cooking is confirmed, and farming was expanded specifically to sustain runs
4. **Known Journey Shrine** — fast travel (confirmed) makes a failed boss attempt cheap to retry; find a shrine before diving deep
5. **Party ready** — 1–8 player co-op is confirmed; the world boss shipped in the same update as 8-player online co-op, which is a strong signal about intended scale
6. **Spare materials banked** — failed runs still produce materials; a stocked base is how you outlast a hard boss

**The cardinal rule:** if you wouldn't fight the world boss with your current gear, don't descend yet. The loop is designed to be followed in order.

## The Boss Fight Framework

Boss mechanics are unannounced, so the framework below is universal: it works on any boss, in any arena, with any party.

### Phase 0 — Recon (the most important phase)

First attempts are reconnaissance, not kills. Spend your first fight **observing**: how the boss moves, what its tells are, when it commits, whether it summons. One person watches and calls out patterns; the rest fight defensively.

### Phase 1 — Engage with the right roles

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Tank / frontline** | Hold the boss's attention and absorb pressure (melee — see the [Melee Build Guide](/guides/melee-combat-build-guide)) |
| **DPS** | Damage from range or flanks ([Ranged Build Guide](/guides/ranged-magic-build-guide)) |
| **Support** | Food, healing, and mid-fight resupply |
| **Scout** | Watch the arena, warn of adds and hazards |

### Phase 2 — Handle the adds

Swarm phases are the universal boss pattern in survival games — plan for them: control the crowd first, keep moving, and let the arena funnel summons through one entry point if it can.

### Phase 3 — Retreat is a strategy

Boss mechanics are unverified, so a wipe teaches more than a desperate finish. Retreat rules: leave when the party is low, when the arena is chaos, or when the recon data stops flowing. Failed runs produce materials; runs you learn from produce knowledge.

### Phase 4 — Document everything

Nobody knows the mechanics yet — the party that documents them first sets the meta. Take notes, share them, and this guide will be updated to match.
