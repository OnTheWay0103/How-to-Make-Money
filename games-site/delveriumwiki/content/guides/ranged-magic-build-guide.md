# Delverium Ranged Build Guide — Ranged Support

Straight talk first: **Sagestone Games has not confirmed any specific ranged weapon types, ammo systems, or a magic system for Delverium as of August 2026.** Bows have not been explicitly confirmed, and whether "magic" exists as a system is completely unannounced. So this guide covers the **ranged support role** — how to play it, what's confirmed that supports it, and what you should check the moment Early Access launches on **September 22, 2026**.

## What Is Confirmed

These are the verified facts that a ranged/support playstyle can build on:

| Confirmed fact | Why it matters for ranged players |
|---|---|
| **Weapons crafting is a confirmed category** | The core loop confirms crafting "food, tools, weapons, armor, building materials" — ranged weapons, if they exist, would live in this category |

| **NPC trading is confirmed** | Recruit and trade with NPCs (core loop) — potential ammo/gear supply lines if ranged systems exist |
| **Crafting → dungeon loop** | The confirmed order (craft weapons, then descend) applies to every combat style |
