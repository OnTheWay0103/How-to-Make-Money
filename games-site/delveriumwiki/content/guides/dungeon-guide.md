# Delverium Dungeon Guide — Boss Strategies & Dive Tips

Dungeons are the heart of Delverium's danger and mystery. The confirmed loop ends with "**descend into dangerous dungeons**," the world hides "**secrets in the dungeons**," and a **world boss** was added to the demo in the February 2026 update. This guide is the strategy companion to the [Dungeons & Bosses Chart](dungeons-bosses-chart) — it covers how to *approach* dungeon content: preparation, exploration, loot priorities, boss-fight principles, and solo vs co-op dives.

> **Pre-release guide — will be updated for EA launch (Sept 22, 2026).** No boss names or mechanics have been announced as of August 2026; boss-fight advice below is a framework built on the confirmed loop, marked honestly where it's TBD / 待验证.

## The Pre-Dive Checklist

The confirmed loop makes the prep obvious: **you craft weapons and armor *before* you descend.** A checklist based on confirmed systems:

| Item | Why (confirmed basis) |
|---|---|
| **Weapons crafted** | The loop places weapon crafting before dungeon descent |
| **Armor equipped** | Armor is a confirmed category — protection is part of the design |
| **Food packed** | Cooking is confirmed; farming was expanded specifically to sustain runs |
| **Tools and supplies** | Gathering and building materials for on-site repair (expected, TBD) |
| **Party ready** | 1–8 player co-op confirmed — groups reduce the risk of dangerous dungeons |
| **Journey Shrine known** | Fast travel (confirmed) makes resupply cheap — find a shrine before diving deep |

**The cardinal rule:** if you wouldn't fight the world boss with your current gear, don't descend yet. The loop is designed to be followed in order.

## Dungeon Exploration Tips

1. **Loot everything unique** — "loot unique items" is confirmed; dungeon drops are a designed reward, not a bonus
2. **Look for story signs** — the Faults mystery hides secrets in the dungeons; explore off the obvious path
3. **Map as you go** — procedurally generated dungeons mean no walkthroughs; your own notes are the map
4. **Resupply via Journey Shrines** — fast travel (confirmed Feb 2026) means a failed deep dive is cheap to retry
5. **Don't greed** — danger is the confirmed design; a full retreat beats an empty run
6. **Learn the world boss's ground rules together** — boss mechanics are unannounced, so the first group that documents them (and shares) wins week one

## World Boss Strategy Framework

The demo's new world boss (name TBD) is a **party boss** — 8-player co-op was added in the same update for a reason. Until mechanics are documented, use this framework:

### 1. Roles before the fight

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Tank / frontline** | Hold the boss's attention, absorb pressure (melee-focused — see the [Melee Build guide](melee-combat-build-guide)) |
| **DPS** | Damage output from range or flanks ([Ranged/Magic Build guide](ranged-magic-build-guide)) |
| **Support** | Food, healing, and resupply mid-fight |
| **Scout** | Watch the arena, warn of adds and hazards |

### 2. Test with one, kill with eight

First attempts are reconnaissance: learn the boss's behavior with a small party, then bring the full 8-player group for the kill. Nobody knows the mechanics yet — that's fine, week one is for learning.

### 3. Farm to fund the fight

The loop applies here too: farm and craft *between* attempts. Failed runs still produce materials; a stocked base is how you outlast a hard boss.

### 4. Document what you learn

Boss mechanics are TBD — the community that documents them first sets the meta. Take notes, share them, and this guide will be updated to match.

## Solo vs Co-op Dives

| | **Solo** | **Co-op (2–4)** | **Co-op (5–8)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | Highest — everything is on you | Shared and balanced | Lowest with coordination |
| **Pacing** | Slow, careful | Steady | Fast — but needs pre-run chat |
| **Loot pressure** | All yours | Split by design | Competition for unique items possible |
| **World boss** | Unconfirmed viability | Challenging | The intended scale |

Difficulty scaling for solo vs co-op is **TBD / 待验证** — but the game is confirmed as 1–8 players, so solo dives are a designed option, just a slower one.
