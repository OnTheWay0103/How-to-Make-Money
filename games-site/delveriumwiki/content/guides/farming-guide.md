# Delverium Farming Guide — Crops, Animals, Fishing & Cooking

Farming is one of Delverium's confirmed pillars — the core loop places it between building and crafting: **gather resources → build shelters → farm → recruit and trade with NPCs → craft items → descend into dangerous dungeons**. The demo (updated February 20, 2026) expanded farming with tilling, planting, animal breeding, fishing and cooking, and reviewers describe the loop as "Stardew Valley meets Minecraft." This guide is the quick reference for everything confirmed about farming so far.

## The Confirmed Farming Loop

| Step | What's Confirmed |
|---|---|
| **Till soil** | Tilling soil is the confirmed starting action for crop planting |
| **Plant seeds** | Seeds are planted into tilled soil |
| **Water crops** | Crops are watered to grow |
| **Harvest** | Grown crops are used for food, cooking and trading |
| **Breed animals** | Animals can be bred and raised on your farm |
| **Fish** | Fishing is a confirmed food-gathering activity |
| **Cook** | Crops, fish and wild ingredients combine into cooked meals |

## Crops — What We Know

- Tilling, planting and watering are confirmed as the three-step crop loop.
- Grown crops feed into **cooking recipes** and **trading** (sell excess for copper coins — see the [NPC & Trading Guide](/guides/npc-trading-guide)).
- **Specific crop types are TBD.** No official crop list has been published as of August 2026. The demo preview cited mushrooms and wheat as food ingredients, but a complete seed/crop roster is unconfirmed.

## Animals — Breeding & Livestock

- **Breeding and raising animals** is confirmed as part of the Feb 2026 farming expansion.
- **Chickens and sheep** are the documented example livestock — both purchased from **recruited NPC traders** (the chicken/sheep seller is reported to appear after 3–4 NPCs move into your settlement).
- Animal products (eggs, wool) feed cooking and crafting — exact products and recipes are TBD.

## Fishing & Wild Gathering

- **Fishing** is a confirmed activity — catch fish in the world's waters.
- **Wild ingredients** (mushrooms, wheat) can be gathered in addition to farmed crops.
- Fish + crops + wild ingredients combine in cooking.

## Cooking & Food

- Cooking combines **crops, fish and wild ingredients** into a **wide range of recipes** (developer-confirmed breadth; exact recipe list TBD).
- Food is used to **eat** (sustain during exploration and long dungeon dives) and to **trade**.
- The demo preview specifically documented crafting food from **wild mushrooms and wheat**.

## Why Farming Matters (Beyond Survival)

| Use | Why |
|---|---|
| **Dungeon prep** | The confirmed loop puts farming before dungeon descents — food sustains long dives (see the [Dungeons & Bosses Chart](/guides/dungeons-bosses-chart)) |
| **Trade economy** | Excess crops and food buy copper coins from NPC traders |
| **Recruitment** | A working farm is part of a settlement that attracts NPCs |
| **Long sessions** | The Feb 2026 update made farming "expanded" — it is a designed, ongoing loop, not a tutorial |
