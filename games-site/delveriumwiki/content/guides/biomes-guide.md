# Delverium Biomes Guide — Every Area & What to Expect

Delverium's world is **procedurally generated** — no two worlds are the same — and the developers describe it as full of **"exotic biomes, hidden caves, and dangerous dungeons."** This guide maps what we know about the game's areas, what each area type is for, and how to explore them safely. Because no individual biome names have been announced as of August 2026, this guide covers the confirmed *area types* and what to expect in each.

> **Pre-release guide — will be updated for EA launch (Sept 22, 2026).** Biome names, layouts, and per-biome resources are unverified until the Early Access build is playable.

## The World Structure (Confirmed)

| Area type | Confirmed details | Your job there |
|---|---|---|
| **Exotic biomes** | Part of the confirmed world description ("exotic biomes, hidden caves and dangerous dungeons") | Gather, farm, build, and quest |
| **Hidden caves** | Confirmed; caves hold ores and rare materials | Mine ores and collect rare materials |
| **Dangerous dungeons** | Confirmed; the risk/reward heart of the game | Fight, loot unique items, find story secrets |
| **The Faults** | A mystery "threatening the land" — secrets hidden in the dungeons | Story progression |

The **core loop** ties them together: you gather and farm in the biomes, mine in the caves, and descend into the dungeons. Each area type feeds the next.

## Exotic Biomes — What to Expect

No biome names are announced, but the confirmed gameplay tells you what each biome is *for*:

| Expect | Confirmed basis |
|---|---|
| **Variety** — "exotic" is the developer's own word; different regions with different looks and resources | World description |
| **Surface gathering** — trees, plants, wild ingredients for cooking | Farming system includes "gather wild ingredients" |
| **Build sites** — open ground for shelters and farms | Building and farming pillars |
| **NPC encounters** — recruitable and tradable characters | Core loop: "recruit and trade with NPCs" |
| **Journey Shrines** — hidden fast-travel points scattered across the world | Confirmed Feb 2026 demo update |

**Exploration rule:** until you know a biome's dangers, treat it as unknown — clear edges first, mark safe routes, and never travel far from base without tools and food.

## Hidden Caves — The Mining Layer

Caves are where the material economy starts:

- **Mining ores** — the confirmed source of metal-grade materials for crafting
- **Rare materials** — collecting them is confirmed as part of the world description; expect the best finds deeper and more dangerous
- **Lost treasures** — the world description includes unearthing lost treasures and defeating foes

**Cave rule:** bring light, tools, and food; caves are dark, deep, and the confirmed home of threats. In co-op, the gatherer role lives here — keep the crafters fed with ore.

## Dangerous Dungeons — The Risk Layer

Dungeons are the confirmed climax of the loop — see the [Dungeon Guide](dungeon-guide) and [Dungeons & Bosses Chart](dungeons-bosses-chart) for the full treatment. In short:

- **"Loot unique items"** — dungeon drops are a designed reward
- **"The Faults"** — the story mystery hides secrets in the dungeons
- **World boss** — a new world boss was added to the demo in the February 2026 update (name unverified)
- **Co-op friendly** — up to 8 players can run dungeons together online

## How the World Grows Through Early Access

The world at EA launch (Sept 22, 2026) is not the final world:

| Milestone | World changes |
|---|---|
| **Demo (now)** | Initial biome + one dungeon, plus more biomes and dungeon content added Feb 2026 |
| **EA launch** | The full procedural world with the confirmed area types |
| **During EA (~2 years)** | **New biomes** confirmed as part of the roadmap, plus new bosses, NPCs, and features based on player feedback |
| **1.0** | The complete vision — exact final biome count unverified |

Every world is different, but the *types* of area stay the same: biomes to live in, caves to mine, dungeons to conquer.

## Exploration Tips (Confirmed-System Based)

1. **Follow the loop, not the horizon** — gather → build → farm → craft → descend; exploring exists to feed this
2. **Find Journey Shrines early** — hidden fast travel (confirmed) turns a long march into a shortcut; mark them as you find them
3. **Mine every cave mouth** — ores and rare materials live there; the gatherer who skips caves starves the crafting pipeline
4. **Pack for the biome** — food, tools, and weapons match where you're going; don't carry a farming hoe into a dungeon
5. **Quest as you explore** — lore and quest systems (demo-confirmed) give destinations purpose
6. **Explore in co-op** — split the map: gatherers and delvers cover more ground than a solo player ever will
