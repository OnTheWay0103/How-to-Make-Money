# Delverium Release Date & Early Access Guide

**Delverium launches into Steam Early Access on September 22, 2026** — and as of August 2026, PC via Steam is the only confirmed platform. If you've been watching this 1-8 player co-op survival sandbox RPG since its demo dropped in early 2025, this guide rounds up everything officially confirmed: the release date, Early Access plans, platforms, price, system requirements, and the roadmap.

> **Pre-release guide — will be updated for EA launch (Sept 22, 2026).** Details not yet announced are marked unverified.

## When does Delverium release?

**Delverium releases on Tuesday, September 22, 2026** into Steam Early Access. The game was originally planned for a 2025 release and was later moved to the current September 2026 date. A successful 3-day open playtest was held roughly a month before the release-date announcement.

The game is developed **and** published by **Sagestone Games**, an independent studio based in Brisbane, Australia. Lead developer Scott Lowther started the project around 2016 after playing split-screen Terraria with his family — which helps explain why local co-op is baked into the game's DNA.

## What is Early Access, and how long will it last?

Delverium's Early Access period is expected to last **approximately 2 years**. During that window, Sagestone Games plans to add new biomes, bosses, NPCs, and features — with the roadmap steered by player feedback, as is standard for Early Access development.

If you buy in during Early Access, expect:

- The **core gameplay loop** in place from day one: gather resources, build shelters, farm, recruit and trade with NPCs, craft items, and descend into dangerous dungeons.
- A mystery at the heart of the story — something called **"the Faults"** is threatening the land, and secrets are hidden in the dungeons.
- **Procedurally generated worlds** featuring exotic biomes, hidden caves, and dangerous dungeons, where you mine ores, collect rare materials, and loot unique items.
- Ongoing updates through the EA period, adding to the world based on what players ask for.

### Will Early Access be cheaper than the full release?

Yes. The developers have confirmed that the **Early Access price will be lower than the final 1.0 price** — though the actual price has **not been announced** as of August 2026.

## Delverium roadmap: what comes after launch?

The official roadmap detail is light — Sagestone has committed to a ~2-year Early Access plan that adds **new biomes, bosses, NPCs, and features based on player feedback**. Anything more specific (dates, named content, update cadence) has not been announced.

Known confirmed content to expect through EA:

- **New biomes** beyond the initial ones in the demo

- **New NPCs** to recruit and trade with
- **Gameplay features** driven by community feedback
- The story of **"the Faults"** unfolding, with secrets hidden in the dungeons

### What's already in the game (demo, as of Feb 2026)

For a sense of what the EA base will feel like, the demo already includes: the initial biome and one dungeon, farming (till soil, plant seeds, water crops, breed animals, fish, cook), building, crafting, combat, 8-player online co-op, 4-player split-screen, fast travel via hidden "Journey Shrines", lore and quest systems, world/character/difficulty customization, full control rebinding, and a graphics + ambient audio overhaul.
