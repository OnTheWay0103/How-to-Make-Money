## How to download the Delverium demo

1. Open the **Steam client** (or store.steampowered.com in a browser).
2. Search for **"Delverium Demo"** — make sure you pick the entry with "Demo" in the name, not the full game.
3. Click **Play Game / Add to Library** (it's free — no purchase needed).
4. Install it like any Steam game and launch.

## Delverium demo: first steps & beginner tips

The demo's loop is: **gather → build → farm → craft → dungeon**. Follow that order and your first 1-2 hours will go smoothly. These tips come from the confirmed gameplay loop only.

### 1. Prioritize tools first

Your opening minutes should go into **gathering resources and crafting basic tools** — you can't farm, mine, or fight efficiently without them. Mine ores and collect rare materials as you explore; everything feeds back into crafting (food, tools, weapons, armor, building materials).

### 2. Build shelter before nightfall

A **shelter** isn't optional decoration — it's the base your run grows from: storage, crafting, a safe place to rest. Build early, expand as materials allow.

### 3. Farm for food, don't just forage

The farming system is a core pillar: **till soil, plant seeds, water crops** — and later **breed animals, fish, and cook**. A steady food supply means you spend less time foraging and more time exploring. The February 2026 update expanded this system for a reason: it matters.

### 4. Prepare weapons before the dungeon

The demo has **one dungeon**, and it's the climax of the run. Craft **weapons and armor**, pack food, bring friends — the dungeon is where the "dangerous dungeon" part of the pitch lives, and where the story secrets (the **Faults**) start surfacing.

### 5. Explore caves and hunt ores

The world is **procedurally generated** with exotic biomes, hidden caves and dangerous dungeons. The demo's biome and caves hold the **ores and rare materials** you need for better gear — explore every cave mouth.

### 6. Find the Journey Shrines

There's **fast travel via hidden "Journey Shrines"** (added Feb 2026) — keep an eye out as you explore.

### 7. Try 8-player co-op for the real experience

Delverium is built around **1-8 player co-op**. Grabbing friends for online co-op or filling the couch with 4-player split-screen transforms the experience — see the [Co-op guide](coop-multiplayer-guide) for role ideas.

### 8. Customize your run

**World, character, and difficulty customization** (Feb 2026 update) let you tune the run to your taste — and rebind controls freely.

### 9. Check the quests and lore

The demo includes **lore and quest systems** since Feb 2026. They're the doorway to the game's mystery — "the Faults" threatening the land — and they structure your 1-2 hours so you see most of the content.
