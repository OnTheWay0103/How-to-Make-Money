# Late-Game & Endgame Content Guide (1.0)

Once your park is solidly profitable and you've pushed past 3 stars, Waterpark Simulator 1.0 opens its real endgame: the **Museum questlines**, the **Yogi's final quests**, **Diamond Stars**, and a stack of new systems (night mode, dynamic weather, breakdowns) that change how you run a big park. This guide covers all of it.

Last updated August 8, 2026 (1.0).

## Endgame Roadmap (In Order)

```
3 stars → Museum (2.5 stars) questlines
       → theme slides (one per Museum NPC)
       → all 3 theme slides → Yogi's quests
       → Diamond Stars rating cap
       → beach map park (fresh run, optional)
```

## The Museum & Theme Slides

The Museum unlocks at **2.5 stars** — across the street, left of your park. Three NPCs run three questlines:

| NPC | Theme | Endpoint Reward |
|-----|-------|-----------------|
| **Poseidon't** | Ocean & beach | Ocean mega-slide + ultimate pool |
| **Clara Loft** | Aztec & Mayan | Aztec mega-slide + ultimate pool |
| **Goldie** | Pirate | Pirate mega-slide + ultimate pool |

Quests are maintenance-flavored tasks, one at a time, paying theme currency for the Museum research bench. Full details in the [Museum Questline Walkthrough](/guides/museum-questline-guide). Strategy at this stage:

- **Commit to one theme first** — complete it to the slide, then start the next.
- The theme slide is a **huge popularity and rating boost** — build it as soon as the tree completes.
- Visit at off-hours (before opening / at closing) so the park doesn't collapse while you're inside.

## The Yogi's Quests

After finishing **all three** NPC questlines, **the Yogi** appears in front of the Museum. His quests are the true final content: mostly **buying and building intricate, expensive equipment** for your park. They exist to give you a reason to spend your endgame wealth — expect them to chain through the most expensive items in the game. Complete them at your own pace; they're the "100%" checklist.

## Diamond Stars

The 1.0 update raised the rating cap past 5 stars to **Diamond Stars**. Practical effect: there is no hard ceiling on your park's prestige, which means:

- Ticket prices can keep climbing as long as you keep the park clean, safe, and impressive
- The community's rating-relevant behaviors (hygiene, attractions, tasks) stay relevant forever
- It gives sandbox players a long-term target: a Diamond park with all three theme slides

## Night Mode (1.0)

Running the park after the classic 6 PM close:

- **Employees are paid more** — nights only pay if revenue beats the wage bill
- **Cheaters are easy to spot** — glow-in-the-dark bracelets
- Park lights, decorations, and atmosphere change

Endgame players typically run nights during events or when attendance is high (e.g. after a new theme slide opens), then close early on quiet days.

## Dynamic Weather & Special Events (1.0)

| Weather/Event | Effect | Endgame Response |
|---------------|--------|------------------|
| Rain | Attendance shifts, guests moody | Keep cleaning — wet park + rain = slip chaos |
| Heatwave | Attendance spikes | Raise umbrella/seat prices; staff up; water everywhere |
| Tornado | Map chaos | Repairs + cleanup afterwards |
| Halloween weather | Theme change, bonuses/nerfs | Roll with it — it's seasonal |
| Special events | Random big arrival waves and surprises | Pre-stock stalls and keep flyers off |
| Alien kidnappings | Guests get taken | Comedy event; rescue them if you like |

Breakdowns (1.0) mean attractions can **fully break down** until repaired — in a big park, repair routes matter more than ever. Assign the handyman and hammer at closing; in co-op, split zones.

## Sickness Management (1.0)

Visitors can get sick from seeing other sick visitors — a chain reaction that spirals during crowded events. Options:

- Eject sick guests early to break the chain
- Toggle the **"No Guest Sickness" mode** in settings if you want the mechanic gone (sandbox-friendly)
- Mascots (1.0 staff) put a happiness floor under busy parks, which reduces the knock-on mood crashes

## The Beach Map — The New-Game+ Option

The 1.0 **beach map** (Miami-neon beachfront, city backdrop) requires a **new park** — layouts don't transfer. It's the natural fresh run:

- Open space suits mega-slide parks
- Long coastline demands loop paths (see [Park Design & Layout](/guides/park-design-layout-guide))
- Pair with Ocean theme decor from the Museum for the full aesthetic

## Endgame Staff Loadout

| Slot | Role | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Cashier | Tickets | Non-negotiable, forever |
| 2-3 Janitors | Cleaning | Big parks outpace one |
| 2+ Vendors | Stalls | Food revenue scales with guests |
| Handyman | Repairs | Breakdowns + nightly hammer work |
| Lifeguard(s) | Pools | Ambulance bills are endgame profit-killers |
| Security | Troublemakers | Automates ejection at scale |
| Mascot(s) | Happiness floor | New in 1.0; great on crowded days |

Remember the one-extra-staff tax if you go over your slots — at endgame scale, one taxed extra hire is fine, two is a leak.

## When Is a Run "Done"?

There's no victory screen. Community-accepted "done" checkpoints:

1. All three Museum questlines complete → Yogi quests done
2. Diamond Stars reached
3. All three theme slides built
4. A beach-map park that repeats 1-3 (for the completionist)
5. (Optional) Duck water battle victories over friends
