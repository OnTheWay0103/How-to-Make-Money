# Waterpark Simulator Staff Management Guide

Your hands only go so far. Once your park holds more than ~15 guests, solo management stops working — the tickets pile up, the trash piles up, and the reviews turn sour. That is the moment to hire staff. This guide covers **every staff type, how hiring works, and the optimal hiring order** in Waterpark Simulator 1.0.

Last updated August 8, 2026.

## How Hiring Works

Staff are unlocked through the **Research Table** in your office, and hired through the **tablet (Tab) → Hire** menu:

1. **Research the Hiring Level** upgrades (Maintenance tree) at the Research Table.
2. Open the tablet and select **Hire** to see available candidates.
3. Each candidate has a **job role, daily wage, and efficiency stat** — they are randomized per day. The candidate list changes daily, so if today's pool is weak, wait for tomorrow's.
4. Hire them. Staff work automatically during open hours.

Key rules:

- You start with a **limited number of staff slots** (around 4 at launch). Each **Hiring Level** upgrade adds slots (roughly +1 per level; exact numbers can vary by update).
- You can hire **one extra staff member beyond your slot allocation** — but it costs extra in taxes.
- Staff have **stamina**; when depleted, they stop working until the next day.
- You can **fire staff** via the tablet's Manage option.

## All Staff Types

| Staff | Unlock | Job | Notes |
|-------|--------|-----|-------|
| **Cashier** | Research Table, Hiring Level 1 | Runs the ticket booth; sells the correct pass automatically | **First hire, always.** Frees you from the entrance entirely. |
| **Janitor** | Research Table, Hiring Level 2 | Cleans trash and debris around the park | Hire before vendor if cleanliness is your pain point. Janitors clean only during open hours. |
| **Vendor** | Research Table, Hiring Level 2 | Works food stalls (hot dog, lemonade, popcorn) | Add when stall queues get long. |
| **Handyman** | Research upgrade at higher park level | Repairs broken attractions | Common community advice: janitor + handyman early, food vendor and lifeguard next. |
| **Lifeguard** | Research upgrade (mid-game) | Watches over visitors in pools | Reduces rescue workload and ambulance incidents. |
| **Security** | 1.0 update | Throws out bad visitors (cheaters, vandals, drunks) | Late-game pick for unruly parks; automates the troublemaker job. |
| **Mascot** | 1.0 update | A moving happiness machine — boosts guest mood | Late-game happiness management. |

## Wages & Efficiency — Pick Stats, Not Names

Candidates are randomized with **unique daily cost and efficiency stats**, and the difference is huge. The exact wage numbers vary from day to day and patch to patch, so compare the stats shown in the hire menu rather than relying on remembered figures.

Rules of thumb:

- **Prioritize high efficiency over low price.** A cheap but slow employee costs you more in lost revenue than the wage difference.
- **Do not fire staff over a single bad day** — re-roll candidates tomorrow if the pool is bad today.
- **Stagger breaks.** If all staff break at the same time, your park falls apart. Hire with offset schedules in mind; in co-op, players can cover gaps.

## Optimal Hiring Order

| Phase | Hire | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| **Day 1-10 (early)** | **Cashier** as soon as Hiring Level 1 is researched | Automates ticket sales — the single biggest time-saver in the game. |
| **Day 10-20 (mid)** | Hiring Level 2 → **Janitor** first, then **Vendor** | Janitor stops the puddle/trash death spiral; vendor fixes food queues. |
| **Day 20+ (late)** | More Hiring Levels → **Handyman**, extra vendors, then **Lifeguard** | Repairs stop eating your closing time; lifeguard reduces ambulance bills. |
| **Endgame (1.0)** | **Security**, then **Mascot** | Automates troublemaker ejection and gives a happiness floor to busy parks. |

Early Access-era advice still holds: **two good staff beats four mediocre ones**, and quality of park over quantity of builds keeps wages affordable.

## Staff Management Mistakes

1. **Hiring vendors before a janitor** — a dirty park loses more money than hungry guests ever will.
2. **Ignoring the one-extra-staff tax** — it is occasionally worth it, but it is a silent profit leak.
3. **Hiring everyone at the same wage tier** — efficiency matters more than role fill.
4. **Never upgrading Hiring Level** — stuck at 4 slots while your park grows past 15 guests is a classic mid-game collapse.
5. **No night coverage in night mode** — 1.0 night mode pays employees more; make sure the extra hours actually cover the wage bill.
