# Museum Questline Walkthrough — All NPCs, Currencies & Rewards

The Museum is the flagship content of the Waterpark Simulator 1.0 update — a new building across the street from your park, three quest-giving NPCs, three themed research trees, and a massive waterslide at the end of each. This walkthrough covers everything, in order.

Last updated August 8, 2026 (1.0).

## How to Unlock the Museum

1. Reach **2.5 stars** on your park's star rating.
2. You will get a pop-up notification about the new attraction. If it does not appear immediately, keep playing — some players only saw the message after progressing toward 3 stars for a while.
3. **Exit your waterpark and head to the left side** — the Museum building is right across the street.
4. Enter it anytime to start quests or browse decor.

## The Three NPCs and Their Questlines

| NPC | Theme | Unlockable Decor | Special Currency |
|-----|-------|------------------|------------------|
| **Poseidon't** | Ocean & beach | Coral, sea life, ocean pools | Ocean currency (per-quest rewards) |
| **Clara Loft** | Aztec & Mayan | Carved stone temples, Aztec decor | Aztec currency |
| **Goldie** | Pirate | Pirate ships, pirate decor | Pirate currency |

Each NPC offers a **dedicated questline** with its own currency. You spend that currency at the **Research Bench inside the Museum** to unlock themed decorations and games — and each theme's research tree **ends in a massive waterslide** (and, per 1.0 reports, the ultimate pool for the theme).

## Quest Mechanics

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **One quest at a time** | Once you accept a quest from an NPC, you must complete it before taking another — from that NPC or anyone else in the Museum |
| **Quest types are random** | Mostly park maintenance and visitor management tasks, similar to your main questline |
| **Difficulty** | Not particularly hard — you do them alongside normal park operations |
| **Turn-in** | Return to the NPC to complete the quest and earn the theme currency |

## Tips for Completing Museum Quests

1. **Start early.** Pick up Museum quests as soon as they unlock (2.5 stars) so currency accumulates across your run — the theme trees are long.
2. **Visit at off-hours.** Go before opening the park or during closing. In co-op, have a friend watch the park while you handle the Museum.
3. **Prepare before leaving.** Solo players: put workers at stalls and the ticket counter before heading across the street, and try to complete + turn in the quest before the day ends.
4. **Stick to one theme.** They are all equal in content — commit to one so its research tree completes faster.
5. **Don't ignore the main park.** Museum tasks are maintenance-flavored; a messy park makes them take twice as long.

## The Research Bench & Theme Trees

Inside the Museum, the Research Bench works like your office Research Table but uses **theme currency** instead of research points:

```
Ocean theme tree → coral pools, sea-life decor → Ocean Mega-Slide
Aztec theme tree → temple decor, carved slides → Aztec Mega-Slide
Pirate theme tree → ship decor, pirate games → Pirate Mega-Slide
```

Each tree's endpoint slide is the largest single attraction in the game and gives a **major park-rating boost plus a customer draw** — the reason to finish a theme.

## The Yogi — Endgame Quests

After completing **all three** Museum NPC questlines, a special NPC called **the Yogi** appears (standing in front of the Museum). His quests are mostly about **purchasing and building more intricate, expensive equipment** for your park — the true endgame content list, one quest at a time.

## Is It Worth It?

**Yes** — with one honest caveat:

- Completing all Museum quests and unlocking most of one theme's decor gets you close to that theme's **ultimate waterslide and pool**. Building it boosts customers and significantly improves your park rating.
- The caveat: some players find the quest system boring — one Steam Community review called it a "to-do list" roadblock to new content. It is busywork-flavored, but the rewards (giant themed slides) are unique and can't be bought any other way.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Step | What to Do |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Reach 2.5 stars |
| 2 | Find the Museum across the street (left of the park) |
| 3 | Pick one NPC (Poseidon't / Clara Loft / Goldie) and start their questline |
| 4 | Complete quests one at a time; spend the currency at the Museum Research Bench |
| 5 | Push that theme's tree to the end → Mega-Slide + ultimate pool |
| 6 | Repeat for the other two themes |
| 7 | Finish all three → talk to **the Yogi** for the final expensive-equipment quests |
