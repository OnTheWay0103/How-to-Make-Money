# How to Make Money Fast in Waterpark Simulator

Money in Waterpark Simulator is not about doing more — it is about **revenue per guest** and **cost per hour**. This guide lays out the profit formula, the highest-ROI attractions and stalls, and the pricing strategy that turns a busy park into a rich one.

Last updated August 8, 2026 (1.0).

## The Profit Formula

```
Daily profit = Ticket revenue + Food revenue + Ejection bonuses
             − Staff wages − Restock costs − Repairs − Ambulance fees − Research spending
```

Every number on the left side is a lever you control. The classic mistakes — overbuilding, understaffing, ignoring puddles — all show up on the cost side. The single most profitable habit in the game is boring: **run a clean, safe park**, because it feeds the star rating.

## The Star Rating Growth Loop (The Real Money Machine)

```
Higher star rating → charge higher entry fees → more profit per guest
→ invest in better attractions → more stars → ...
```

Star rating is the master lever. Everything that raises your rating — clean water, completed tasks, popular attractions, safety — raises your ticket price ceiling. Two strategies that pair directly with this:

1. **Price to your rating.** Raise entry fees after every star upgrade; lower them slightly during slow days to keep the park busy. Watch for guest complaints as the signal.
2. **Build one S-tier centerpiece at a time.** A mega-slide or wave pool delivers a big popularity and rating jump. Two half-finished projects deliver nothing.

## Best ROI Attractions

| Attraction | ROI Profile | When to Build |
|------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Hot dog stand** | One of the best early money-makers. Restock a pack for ~$25; customers order plain/ketchup/mustard/both. | Day 1-2, next to the ticket booth |
| **Lemonade stand** | Sells at ~$12 per glass (research unlock) — near-pure margin | As soon as researched |
| **Wave pool** | Biggest crowd-per-square-meter; drives ticket + food revenue | Once staffed and clean |
| **Custom mega-slides** | Flagship earners; justify Slide Pass ticket add-ons | Mid-game, one at a time |
| **Vending machines** | Lower margin than stalls, but zero staff and zero breaks | Late-mid-game filler |
| **Popcorn stand** | New in 1.0 — same stall economics | Mid-game |

**Food revenue is often half your profit.** Place every stall where guests queue or pass — entrance plaza, pool edges, slide exits. A guest who just finished a slide buys a drink.

## The Ejection Economy

Pushing out intruders pays — roughly **~$30 per ejection**:

- Ticket cheaters (no bracelet)
- Drunks (green bubbles)
- Vandals (spray cans)
- Perverts (cameras)

That is money AND happiness AND safety in one action. The boxing glove at the front desk, then the water pressure gun at 4 stars, make ejections faster. Note: this is a *bonus* economy, not a farm — don't build a park around it, but never skip an ejection.

## Pricing Strategy

| Situation | Move |
|-----------|------|
| Star rating went up | Raise entry fees |
| Slow day / empty park | Lower admission or hand out flyers selectively |
| Sunny weather | Raise umbrella/seating prices (demand spikes) |
| Night mode (1.0) | Watch the wage bill — only run nights if revenue beats it |
| Slide Pass add-on | Only charge it when you have a slide worth the premium |

**Flyers cost $5 each** and draw waves of guests. Target older people, students, and guests who look like they have money — not drunks. Never spam flyers before your park can serve the crowd.

## Cost Control (The Other Half of Profit)

- **Restock before opening** — running out mid-day costs sales.
- **Staff efficiency over price** — a 100% efficiency cashier at $100/day beats a 70% one at $90.
- **Hire the one extra staffer only when needed** — the tax on over-allocation is a silent leak.
- **Repair at closing** (or use night hours in 1.0) — never during peak revenue hours.
- **Ambulance fees are avoidable** — full pools + life preservers = no injuries = no fees.

## Early-Game Milestones (First Week)

1. **Day 1-2:** pool + slide + hot dog stand; sell tickets; clean everything.
2. **Research Hiring Level 1** → cashier automates tickets.
3. **Research food stalls** → lemonade stand joins the hot dog stand.
4. **Hire janitor** once trash outpaces you.
5. **First star upgrade** → raise prices.
6. **Build the lazy river or splash zone** — then the wave pool.
