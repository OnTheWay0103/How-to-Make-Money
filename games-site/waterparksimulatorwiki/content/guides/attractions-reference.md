# All Attractions Quick Reference — Costs, Capacity & Upkeep

The fast-scroll reference for every attraction type in Waterpark Simulator (1.0). Because slides are built piece-by-piece rather than bought from a catalogue, exact prices and capacities vary with your design — this table gives the **type-level** picture: when each type unlocks, what it costs to stand up, how many guests it handles, and how much upkeep it demands.

Last updated August 8, 2026 (1.0). Exact numbers shift with patches; treat costs as estimates and verify in-game before big purchases.

## Reading the Table

- **Build Cost** — "Low" types are affordable in your first days; "Very high" types need a sustained profitable streak. Museum slides additionally cost the questline currencies earned from the three Museum NPCs.
- **Capacity** — a rough guest-per-hour figure. Wave pools and lazy rivers move big crowds; that is great for ticket revenue and terrible for a park without staff.
- **Upkeep / Risk** — the hidden tax. Every attraction needs repairs (hammer) and cleaning (mop/water gun). Safety risks (diving boards, empty pools) convert directly into ambulance fees and bad reviews.
- **Rating Impact** — the contribution to your star rating, which is the master lever for ticket prices.

## What to Build, In Order

| Phase | Build Priority |
|-------|----------------|
| **Day 1-2** | 1 pool, 1 small slide, 1 hot dog stand, trash cans at every junction |
| **Week 1** | Diving board, lemonade stand (research), second small slide |
| **Week 2-3** | Lazy river or splash zone, vending machines, upgrade hammer |
| **Once profitable** | Wave pool or custom mega-slide — one centerpiece at a time |
| **2.5 stars+ (1.0)** | Museum questlines → theme slides; night mode; popcorn stand |
| **Endgame** | All three Museum theme slides, zipline network, max-tier park |

## Notes & Known Gaps

- Exact in-game price and upkeep numbers for each attraction type have not been published by CayPlay and are not yet fully documented by the community. The table above is type-level guidance from 1.0 gameplay reports — we will fill in exact numbers as they get verified.
- The **duck slide** was a free exclusive for Early Access players and is not obtainable through normal progression.
- **Slide Pass** ticket add-ons pair best with parks that have at least one S-tier slide — guests pay extra for slide access only when the slides are worth it.
