# Waterpark Simulator Achievements Guide: All 71 Unlocks

Waterpark Simulator has 71 Steam achievements. Most unlock through ordinary park progression, but the rarest are deliberate grinds, and the most common reason nothing pops is that the save is on Easy or Sandbox. Below is every unlock by category with Steam's live global rates, followed by the fixes worth trying.

## How many achievements does Waterpark Simulator have?

Steam's official global achievement feed for app 3293260 returned **71 achievements** on 29 October 2026. Older guides quote 52, 55 or 60; those counts come from Early Access builds or console trophy sets and go stale after patches ([Community](https://waterparksimulator.wiki/guides/achievements/)). The live Steam list is the only number worth trusting. Every percentage below is Valve's global unlock rate, so it covers all players rather than your difficulty ([Official](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamUserStats/GetGlobalAchievementPercentagesForApp/v2/?gameid=3293260)).

## Why achievements are not unlocking

Five things account for almost every "nothing is popping" report.

1. **The save is on Easy or Sandbox.** Community sources say only Normal and Hard credit achievements, and that both Easy and Sandbox disable them ([Community](https://waterparksimulator.wiki/guides/difficulty-modes/)). CayPlay has not put that rule in a patch note, so it stays unconfirmed official policy, but it is the first thing to check. Difficulty is chosen when the park is created and cannot be changed later without a new save ([Community](https://waterparksimulatorgame.wiki/guides/difficulty/)).
2. **The counter is a personal action, not a park outcome.** Cleaning, repairs, rescues and bracelet catches only count when *you* do them. Hiring cleaners, handymen and security automates the work and quietly stops your progress ([Community](https://www.whisperofthehouse.com/waterpark-simulator/achievements-guide)).
3. **You are in someone else's multiplayer park.** Patch 1.0.91-era notes fixed achievements "not progressing as intended during multiplayer", which confirms co-op was broken ([Official](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3293260/view/1841579228669287)). Community reports still describe only the host receiving reliable credit, so repeat a stuck counter in a park you own ([Community](https://waterparksimulatorgame.wiki/guides/achievements/)).
4. **The build is behind.** CayPlay has shipped achievement fixes repeatedly: beach-park and zipline triggers (1.0.21), the city-map 3-star and 5-star unlocks (13 August), the "Nothing left to learn" trigger (28 August), and the painting achievement plus the frozen "Slip Happens" counter (1.0.91, 11 September) ([Official](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3293260/view/1843481262696049)). The current build is 1.0.120 (28 October 2026). Update before you assume a bug.
5. **The install is damaged.** The 4 August note says a corrupted download can leave the park looking empty after an update, and the fix is Steam's file verification ([Official](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3293260/view/1839676055898488)). Run it if a save loads wrong, then retry the achievement.

The Steam tracker itself can also lag: community guides report progress appearing minutes later, or after a reload ([Community](https://waterparksimulatorgame.wiki/guides/achievements/)). Give it a session before you start a new park.

## The fastest route to 100%

1. **Pick Normal or Hard and the Beach map at creation.** Difficulty is locked per save, and Beach lets one developed park cover the generic, Beach and Hard prestige lines ([Community](https://www.whisperofthehouse.com/waterpark-simulator/achievements-guide)).
2. **Stay manual until the personal counters are done.** Clean, mop, repair, rescue and catch bracelets yourself before hiring the staff that would do it for you.
3. **Keep one long save for money, sales, research and visitor counts.** Restarting resets cumulative progress.
4. **Use City only for its map-specific unlocks.** City three-star and five-star are separate saves in most community routes ([Community](https://www.whisperofthehouse.com/waterpark-simulator/achievements-guide)).
5. **Finish the counters before starting a second park.** Splitting progress across saves is the most common way players stall at 60–70%.
6. **Update, then verify files.** If an unlock still refuses to fire on 1.0.120, verify the install before treating it as a bug.

## What is not confirmed

- **Easy and Sandbox disabling achievements.** Multiple community sources agree, and the in-game mode warning reportedly says so, but CayPlay has not published it in a patch note. Treat it as **[Unconfirmed]** official policy.
- **Non-host credit in multiplayer.** A patch fixed "not progressing as intended", but no official statement says whether guests now earn every counter.
- **The exact Yogi scam trigger.** Community guides recommend saving before paying Yogi; the precise condition is not documented.
- **The PC "Full House" threshold.** Steam's API tracks a 100-visitor condition; console builds reportedly changed it to 70.
- **Console trophy parity.** PS5 and Xbox sets differ in count and patch timing; this guide covers the Steam list.
- **Whether the sixth prestige tier is named "Diamond Star" in every language.** Steam's API records the tier; the label is community-reported.

## Quick answers

- **Do achievements work in Sandbox?** No, according to community sources. Use Normal or Hard.
- **Can I switch an Easy park to Normal?** No. Difficulty is locked when the park is created.
- **Why is my counter stuck at 98%?** Several counters had patch-level bugs; update and replay the action.
- **Do mods disable achievements?** There is no official statement. Treat modded runs as [Unconfirmed].
