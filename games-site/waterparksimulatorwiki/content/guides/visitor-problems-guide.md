# Why Are My Visitors Unhappy? Waterpark Simulator Problems Solved

The park looks fine to you. The visitors disagree. Waterpark Simulator hides most of its problems behind a happiness meter, a star rating, and a queue of visibly miserable guests — and diagnosing the cause is half the battle. This guide works like a support ticket: every common visitor problem as a question, with the cause and the fix. For the full reference on guest needs, see the [Visitor Management Guide](/guides/visitor-happiness-guide) — this one is the diagnostic.

## The Big One: Why Are My Guests Angry at the Entrance?

**Cause:** The most common single problem in the game — the wrong ticket type is being sold, or the price doesn't match your star rating. Guests arrive, find the wrong pass on sale, and turn around angry before entering.

**Fix:** Hire a **cashier** (Hiring Level 1) who sells the correct passes automatically. Price entry to your star level — overpricing for your rating is a happiness killer even when the cashier is correct.

## Why Is My Star Rating Dropping?

**Cause:** Rating is driven by happiness + cleanliness + safety + completed tasks. A dropping rating means one of those four inputs is leaking — almost always cleanliness or safety.

**Fix:** Check the quick-diagnosis order: (1) hygiene HUD — water quality first, then slides, trash, puddles; (2) pools full and filtered?; (3) broken attractions?; (4) tasks overdue? Fix in that order and the rating follows within a day or two.

## Why Do My Guests Leave Early?

**Cause:** Guests leave when their needs can't be met — broken or empty attractions, long food queues, no benches to rest on, or a park that's simply too dirty.

**Fix:** Run the night hammer pass on every attraction. Check stalls are staffed and stocked. Add upgraded benches (Park research) so tired guests recover instead of walking out.

## Why Are There Injuries Everywhere?

**Cause:** Almost always empty pools. Guests cannonball into the shallow bottom, and panicking guests block diving boards. Ambulances cost money, and injuries wreck reviews.

**Fix:** Pools do **not** auto-refill — repair AND refill them manually. Throw life preservers into every pool at day start. Supervise diving boards (lifeguard or push panicking guests off quickly).

## Why Do My Guests Keep Slipping?

**Cause:** Puddles. Guests ragdoll on wet ground, lose happiness, and a slip chain in a crowded park spirals fast. Rain and events make it worse.

**Fix:** Mop aggressively and fill trash bags completely before tossing them. On rain days, cleaning is the entire job — the hygiene priority order applies: water quality, slides, then trash and puddles.

## Why Is Everyone Getting Sick?

**Cause:** 1.0 sickness spreads — visitors can get sick from seeing other sick visitors, and the chain reaction spirals during crowded events.

**Fix:** Eject sick guests early to break the chain. If the mechanic annoys you, toggle **"No Guest Sickness" mode** in settings — it's a sandbox-friendly official option, not a cheat.

## Why Are the Food Queues Endless?

**Cause:** Understaffed stalls. One vendor can't serve a crowd spike, and queues drain happiness (and your food revenue).

**Fix:** Vendors (Hiring Level 2) plus vending machines as backups. Pre-stock every stall before opening — restocking mid-rush is too slow.

## Why Are My Guests Getting Cheated or Harassed?

**Cause:** Troublemakers — ticket cheaters, drunks, vandals, and perverts — drain happiness and can model bad behavior to other guests.

**Fix:** The **devil emoji** above a guest's head is the universal warning. Eject them early: right-click push them out (cheaters even pay a cash reward), stop drunks at the street before they enter, and clean graffiti for bonus research points. At scale, **security staff** (1.0) automate ejection.

## Why Did My Reviews Suddenly Turn Bad?

**Cause:** A happiness crash — usually a puddle chain, trash overflow, or an event day that ran understaffed. Reviews lag reality by a day, so the cause is whatever broke 1–2 days ago.

**Fix:** Find the leak with the diagnosis order above, fix it, and hold for a day — reviews recover on a lag.

## Why Is My Rating Stuck Even Though Guests Are Happy?

**Cause:** You've hit the ceiling for your current content. Rating needs completed tasks and new attractions, not just happy guests.

**Fix:** Check the task list, build the next attraction tier, and push research. If you're past 5 stars, 1.0's **Diamond Stars** cap is the next rung — see the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide).

## Why Do Things Go Wrong During Events?

**Cause:** Events (tourist buses, heatwaves, tornadoes) stack crowds or chaos on a park that wasn't staffed or stocked for it. Breakdowns (1.0) also make attractions fully fail mid-day.

**Fix:** Pre-stock stalls and staff up before event days. Assign the handyman and hammer at closing. Full event playbook in the [Seasonal Events Guide](/guides/seasonal-events-guide).

## Why Are Night Guests Different?

**Cause:** Night mode is optional (1.0): a lever next to the open button adds a night shift at extra daily cost — staff get overtime. Cheaters get glow-in-the-dark wristbands, and the park changes mood.

**Fix:** Run nights only when attendance justifies the wage bill. Use the glowing bracelets for troublemaker sweeps. Close early on quiet days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I make guests happy fast?**
A: Clean water first, then trash, then queues. Happy guests come from a clean, safe, functioning park — not from decorations.

**Q: Should I eject or fix?**
A: Eject people, fix things. Troublemakers and sick guests get ejected; attractions, pools, and stalls get fixed.

**Q: Why do guests complain about price?**
A: Entry price should match your star rating. Overpriced parks get angry walk-ins — see the [Money Making Guide](/guides/money-making-guide) for the pricing formula.

**Q: Is the devil emoji serious?**
A: Yes — it marks a guest who will cause problems. Eject them early and your afternoon is calmer.

**Q: My park was fine and then collapsed — what happened?**
A: Event day, breakdown, or a slip chain. Check the diagnosis table, fix the leak, and remember reviews lag by a day — you'll recover.
