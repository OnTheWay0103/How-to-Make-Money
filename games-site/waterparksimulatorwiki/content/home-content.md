# Waterpark Simulator Wiki — Unofficial Guide Hub

**Welcome to the unofficial Waterpark Simulator Wiki — your guide hub for the first-person waterpark management simulator by CayPlay.**

Waterpark Simulator launched its **1.0 version on July 31, 2026** on Steam (PC), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, after a successful Early Access run that began **August 22, 2025**. ---

## What is Waterpark Simulator?

Waterpark Simulator puts you in charge of a rundown waterpark with one goal: turn it into a **five-star business empire**. You build and design attractions — from tiny kiddie slides to massive custom waterslides with loops and funnels — then sell tickets, keep guests fed and happy, hire staff, and research upgrades.

It's a comedy-tinged management sim with physics-driven ragdoll chaos: guests slip on puddles, cannonball into empty pools, sneak in without paying, and occasionally get kidnapped by aliens. Managing all of that is the job. The 1.0 update added full 4-player co-op, a brand-new beach map, the Museum questline, night mode, dynamic weather, and much more.
