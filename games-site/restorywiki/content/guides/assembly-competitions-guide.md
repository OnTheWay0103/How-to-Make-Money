## Overview

Assembly competitions are ReStory's contest layer: timed device assembly challenges separate from normal walk-in orders, where speed and correctness earn you wins — and one prize that money cannot buy. They're also the gate for three achievements and for the only license that is **won rather than purchased**. This guide collects everything documented about them so far.

## What Assembly Competitions Are

- **Standalone contests** you enter and must win — they appear later in the campaign
- Contest format: assemble a device against the clock/against competitors, judged on correctness and speed
- Winning is the point: participation alone doesn't unlock the rewards below

**⚠️ Note:** detailed competition mechanics (round structure, scoring, difficulty scaling) are still being mapped post-launch — the community is documenting them as runs complete. What's confirmed is the reward structure, which is solid.

## The Competition License

**One license cannot be bought — it must be won in the device assembly competition** that appears later in the campaign. This is the missing piece of the license collection:

- It's part of the full license roster (see the [License Guide](/guides/license-guide))
- Owning **every license** is the requirement for the **Platinum Partner** achievement
- So a 100% run (and the best license perks) is impossible without winning the competition at least once

## Competition Achievements

| Achievement | Condition |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Promise of Akiba** | Win a device assembly competition for the first time |
| **Star of Akiba** | Win three device assembly competitions |
| **Legend of Akiba** | Win at least one assembly competition for **every device type** |

The third is the completionist entry: it's not about total wins — you need a win in every device category the competitions cover, which means competing across the whole catalog.

## How to Prepare

### Master the Assembly Order
Competitions test what the [Repair Process Guide](/guides/repair-process-guide) covers: disassemble → clean → repair → reassemble, in the right order. Speed comes from knowing the sequence before the clock starts.

### Practice the Tricky Devices
Devices that give walk-in customers trouble will give the competition the same trouble — but you have no second chances. The [Repair Process Guide](/guides/repair-process-guide)'s hardest-repairs section (Nony Goman, small-board phones, soldering jobs) is your study sheet.

### Build a Parts Library
Competition builds need parts on hand — waiting to source components burns time. The [Money & Profit Guide](/guides/money-making-guide)'s spare-parts strategy (mystery boxes, stripping used devices) is what fills your bins before you enter.

### Prioritize Correctness Over Speed at First
One clean, slow win beats a fast failure. Win once (Promise of Akiba), then optimize for speed once the format is familiar.

## Strategy for "Legend of Akiba" (Every Device Type)

- **Enter every competition you see.** Wins in different categories require entering as categories rotate
- **Track which device types you've won.** The achievement needs every type — keep a checklist across the full [Device Database](/guides/device-database)
- **License order matters.** The [License Guide](/guides/license-guide) recommends phones early (they appear constantly); competition categories likely follow the same unlock structure, so early licenses mean more competition types available sooner

**Next steps:** Understand how the competition license fits the full progression in the [License Guide](/guides/license-guide), and study the devices you'll be racing on in the [Device Database](/guides/device-database).
