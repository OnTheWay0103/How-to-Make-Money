## Overview

In ReStory, money buys **licenses**, and licenses are the real progression system. So the question "how do I make more yen?" is really "how do I progress faster?" This guide covers the confirmed profit strategies from launch-week testing.

---

## The #1 Rule: Clean Before You Replace

The single biggest beginner mistake is assuming every broken device needs new parts. **Dirt, corrosion, sticky buttons and dust cause symptoms that look far worse than they are.**

- Cleaning uses **reusable tools** — clean-only restorations are nearly pure profit.
- Buy a replacement only after the notepad confirms a part is genuinely broken.
- This alone can double your early-game profit margins.

## 1. Build a Parts Library (Treat Parts as Working Capital)

- **Keep broken devices** — a cheap junk device can contain multiple usable parts worth far more than its purchase price.
- Salvage **LCD assemblies, button membranes, speakers, battery connectors, flex cables and screws**.
- **Stock the common failures** before you need them: batteries, screens, buttons, charging ports (speakers sometimes; decorative shells rarely).
- Once the **shredder** is available, shred truly unusable parts for yen recovery.

## 2. Batch & Plan Orders

- **Never use express shipping** — it destroys profits.
- Plan repairs days ahead and **batch orders** to save shipping fees.
- Buy guaranteed parts from licensed suppliers only when an urgent order demands it.

## 3. Use the Tip Jar

- The tip jar generates income from work you're already doing — walk-in and online customers leave tips automatically.
- One of the best early purchases (see the [Tools & Equipment Catalog](/guides/tool-equipment-guide)).

## 4. Flip Marketplace Devices (Carefully)

- Buy devices priced far enough below their finished sale value to survive a few bad parts.
- Restore and resell — 5 sales earn **Making Money!**, and flips build your parts library simultaneously.
- **Warning:** seller descriptions don't guarantee condition.

## 5. Know What's NOT Profitable Early

- **Painting** is cosmetic — no resale value.
- **Reprogramming** (rhythm mini-game) doesn't noticeably raise an item's worth.
- **Buying new parts for everything** is the fastest way to lose money.

## The Million-Yen Plan

1. Stabilize daily income with licenses (device categories = income ceiling).
2. Automate cleaning with the Sonic Bath; speed disassembly with screwdrivers.
3. Flip marketplace devices weekly; salvage everything.
4. Keep the bench focused: few open orders, mostly jobs that use stock parts.
5. Let reviews and tips compound — reputation brings better customers.
