## Overview

Cleaning is the heart of ReStory. Most "broken" symptoms — dead buttons, distorted screens, failed boot, crackling audio — are actually **dirt, corrosion, sticky residue or dust**. The fastest way to burn through your yen is to replace parts that only needed a scrub.

Cleaning is also your most profitable activity: the tools are reusable, and a clean-only restoration is nearly pure profit. This guide covers the mechanics, the tools, the hidden-dirt problem, and the fastest cleaning routines confirmed since launch.

---

## How Cleaning Works

Cleaning is part-by-part, not whole-device. You disassemble the device, identify which individual components are dirty (the notepad tells you), and clean each one:

1. Drag a dirty part to the cleaning supplies on your workbench.
2. Move the brush (or tool) **directly across the visible grime**.
3. The cleaning percentage only increases while the tool touches an area that is actually dirty — waving it over clean surfaces does nothing.

**If cleaning is stuck at 0%**, the pointer isn't touching the component. Move it directly over the dirt and watch the percentage tick up. If a part stops responding entirely, see the [stuck-cleaning bug](#the-stuck-cleaning-bug) below.

## The Three Cleaning Tools

| Tool | Use For | Cost | Notes |
|------|---------|------|-------|
| **Brush** | Routine cleaning of individual parts | Free (reusable) | Your default tool; upgrade it early — it appears in almost every job |
| **Canned air** | Large shells, panels, awkward pieces | Consumable | Saves time on big surfaces; reserve it — don't waste it on tiny buttons |
| **Sonic Bath (ultrasonic)** | Automated cleaning of up to 3 parts at once, ~30 seconds | One-time purchase | Run it while you hand-clean another part; cheapest model unlocks **Hands Free** |

**Where to buy the Sonic Bath:** open the screwdriver icon on the in-game browser (the "Gozilla Fairfox" Y2K browser), scroll to the bottom, and buy the cheapest Ultrasonic Bath model. That purchase alone earns the **Hands Free** achievement.

## Technique: Finding Hidden Dirt

Dirt hides. The percentage can stall at 90% while a speck sits on an edge you've never rotated into view. Community-confirmed technique:

- **Rotate every part** and check edges, backs, connector areas and recessed sections.
- Small hidden patches often sit on **connector pins, battery contacts, button membranes and the rim of screens**.
- Zoom in on suspicious areas — several players report that zooming reveals grime that is invisible at the default view.
- Keep the **notepad open**: it tracks every part's condition (clean / dirty / broken), so you always know exactly which parts still need work and which are done.

## Workbench Organization

Players who clean fast keep three separate zones on the bench:

| Zone | What Goes There |
|------|-----------------|
| Dirty parts | Everything that still needs cleaning |
| Clean parts | Finished components, ready for repair/reassembly |
| Broken parts | Components flagged broken by the notepad, waiting for soldering or replacement |

Mixing zones causes two classic mistakes: re-cleaning parts you already finished, and losing track of a broken part during reassembly. With many similar parts spread across the desk (phones are the worst), the zones are what keep you sane.

---

## The Golden Rule: Clean Before You Replace

Before ordering any replacement part, check the notepad — the part you think is "missing" may be dirty, sitting elsewhere on the table, or still inside the Sonic Bath. A "broken" button is often just sticky. Community testing shows **clean-only restorations are the single biggest profit lever in the game** — this habit alone can roughly double your early-game margins.

## The Stuck-Cleaning Bug

**Reported issue:** some parts get stuck at 74–95% clean — most commonly reported on the **Nony PlayMachine disc drive** — even with the brush, canned air and Sonic Bath. Community workarounds, in order of preference:

1. **Zoom in** and hunt for hidden grime you may have missed (this resolves most cases).
2. **Buy a whole device** of the same type and scrap the stuck part.
3. **Store the dirty part in the box on the left** of the workbench and retrieve it — this reportedly resets the part's cleaning state for some players.

If none of these work, the part may be genuinely bugged in your save. This is tracked in the [FAQ guide's Known Issues section](/guides/faq-content#known-issues-bugs).

## Night Grinding: Clean-Only Jobs After Midnight

Cleaning-only orders need **no parts**, which makes them the most reliable profit source in the game. A confirmed trick from the 100% completion community:

- Between **midnight and 8 AM**, the shop's workbench is free of walk-in interruptions — grind cleaning-only jobs during these hours.
- Day progress only advances when you **go home and sleep** (take the bike), so returning home before 8 AM ensures the day registers and you keep the income.

This routine is also the backbone of the **Frugal** achievement (10 days with no paid part purchases) — see the [Money & Profit Guide](/guides/money-making-guide).

## Cleaning Achievements

| Achievement | Condition |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Clean Job** | Clear all the dirty items on your desk when starting a new save |
| **Big Cleaning** | Clean 100 loose parts across multiple orders |
| **Galactic Cleaning** | Clean 1,000 loose parts across multiple orders |

The two counters accumulate across every order — hand-cleaning small parts alongside the Sonic Bath is the fastest path to **Galactic Cleaning** (1,000 parts).

## Speed Tips Summary

- Upgrade the **brush** early — it is used in nearly every job and upgrades are inexpensive.
- Run the **Sonic Bath continuously** while you hand-clean — up to 3 parts clean in ~30 seconds while you work on a fourth.
- **Clean in notepad order** — parts listed first in the assembly order are the parts you'll need first.
- Save **canned air** for large shells and panels; let the brush handle small parts.
- When a part looks clean but the percentage is low, **rotate it** before assuming a bug.
- Keep dirty, clean and broken parts in **separate bench zones**.
