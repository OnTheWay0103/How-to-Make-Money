## Game Basics

**What is ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs?**
A chill, narrative-driven electronics repair shop management simulator by **Mandragora** (I Am Future, Skyhill), published by **tinyBuild**. Set in mid-2000s Tokyo, you repair nostalgic Y2K devices, manage your shop, and shape a branching story through choices. Launched August 6, 2026 on Steam.

**Is it multiplayer?**
No. Strictly single-player — no co-op or multiplayer.

**Is it scary or stressful?**
No. It's a cozy game: no time limits, no combat, no fail state. The only deadlines are 3-day online order windows.

**How long is it?**
15+ hours for the main story per the developers (playstyle-dependent). One review (Gamekult) hit credits in ~10 hours. See the [Game Length & 100% Guide](/guides/game-length-guide) for the full breakdown.

## Devices & Licenses

**How many devices can I repair?**
~16 named device types across 7 categories at launch (see the [Device Database](/guides/device-database)). A marketing "2,000+ devices" figure is unconfirmed (unverified).

**Are Atari devices real?**
The Atari 2600, Lynx and CX40 joystick are officially licensed. Everything else is a parody (Pokia 3310, Autorolla Razor, Eggotchi, Nony Goman, Palaloid, etc.).

**How do licenses work?**
Buy licenses to unlock new device types and story progression. Without a license you can accept a device but not finish it. One license must be won in the assembly competition. Full details in the [License Guide](/guides/license-guide).

**Do I need licenses for everything?**
No — the starting device types are license-free. Licenses expand your catalog and raise your income ceiling.

## Gameplay Questions

**How do I get paid for online orders?**
Finished repairs are auto-packed — place the package **back on the counter** so the courier picks it up. No counter placement, no payment.

**How many online orders per day?**
Up to 11, each with a 3-day deadline.

**Can I sell restored devices?**
Yes — buy broken devices on the marketplace, restore them, sell for profit. 5 sales earn "Making Money!".

**Is soldering in the demo?**
No — the soldering iron is locked in the demo; soldering jobs appear in the full game once the station unlocks.

**What are the bills?**
Rent (landlord) + bank loan. Paying both earns "All Bills Paid"; refusing triggers "Bankrupt" and a warning.

**Does the bike matter?**
The bicycle ends your day, saves progress, and starts a new one. Not using it for 168 straight hours earns "Akiba Never Sleeps".

## Known Issues & Bugs

ReStory launched August 6, 2026 in strong shape — Overwhelmingly Positive on Steam — but the launch build has a handful of known issues. None are crash-level, but **one blocks a legitimate 100% run**, and a couple of others can waste real playtime if you don't know the workarounds. As of August 2026, no official hotfix has been announced.

### 1. The Bugged Achievement (Blocks 100%)

**The problem:** one achievement in the current build (v1.0.0) is reported as **not unlocking even when its condition is met**. Multiple guides confirm the report, but the specific achievement is not publicly named.

**Impact:** a legitimate 100% run may be impossible until a patch.

**Workaround:** none in-game. The only current route to the missing achievement is third-party trainer tools — use at your own risk, and note the achievement may still fail to trigger cleanly. We do not endorse trainers.

**What to do:** play normally and let the achievement unlock naturally; if the bugged one is still locked at the end of your run, wait for the hotfix and re-verify your triggers.

### 2. Parts Stuck at 74–95% Clean

**The problem:** certain parts stop responding to cleaning midway — most commonly reported on the **Nony PlayMachine disc drive** — with the brush, canned air and Sonic Bath all failing to move the percentage.

**Workarounds, in order:**
1. **Zoom in** and hunt for hidden grime (resolves most cases).
2. **Buy a whole device** of the same type and scrap the stuck part.
3. **Store the dirty part in the box on the left** of the workbench, then retrieve it — this reportedly resets the part's state for some players.

See the [Cleaning Guide](/guides/cleaning-guide) for the full technique context.

### 3. Day Counter Nuances

**The problem:** players report days not registering or work hours "wasted."

**How it actually works:** day progress only advances when you **go home and sleep** (take the bike). Work done between midnight and 8 AM counts toward the current day; returning home **before 8 AM** ensures the day registers properly.

**Related quirk:** **Business Shark** (100,000 Yen in a single day) is easier by *not* ending the day — income accumulates across a long open stretch, so don't take the bike until the total is where you want it.

### 4. Fixed Repair Pricing — "Repairs Cost More Than the Customer Pays"

**The problem:** a Steam forum thread complains that repair costs are fixed and that some jobs cost more in parts than the customer pays — and that marketplace units often arrive as junk regardless of seller rating.

**Context:** this is partly by design. The game's economy expects you to **clean first and salvage from your junk pile** — replacing everything with new parts *is* a money-loser for most devices (see the [Device Database](/guides/device-database) for the value profiles). Seller ratings genuinely don't guarantee condition; the gamble is intentional.

**Workaround:** treat listing descriptions as fiction. Only buy devices priced far below finished sale value, and always keep cash reserves.

### 5. Steam Deck Controller Layout

**The problem:** the default controller layout is unfinished — confirmed in community testing of the deck experience.

**Workarounds:**
- Use a **custom Steam Input layout** (community layouts are posted and recommended).
- Community-tested settings are listed in [Price & Platforms](/guides/price-platforms).

## Is It Worth Buying?

**Buy it if you like:**
- Cozy management sims (House Flipper, PowerWash Simulator, I Am Future)
- Y2K nostalgia and retro hardware
- Narrative games with branching choices
- Meditative, low-pressure gameplay

**Wait if you:**
- Need action/combat pacing
- Dislike cleaning and disassembly loops
- Want strict challenge or fail states

At $19.99 (or $17.99 at launch discount) with 15+ hours of story and Overwhelmingly Positive reviews, it's an easy recommendation for cozy-sim fans.
