## Game Basics

**What is ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs?**
A chill, narrative-driven electronics repair shop management simulator by **Mandragora** (I Am Future, Skyhill), published by **tinyBuild**. Set in mid-2000s Tokyo, you repair nostalgic Y2K devices, manage your shop, and shape a branching story through choices. Launched August 6, 2026 on Steam.

**Is it multiplayer?**
No. Strictly single-player — no co-op or multiplayer.

**Is it scary or stressful?**
No. It's a cozy game: no time limits, no combat, no fail state. The only deadlines are 3-day online order windows.

**How long is it?**
15+ hours for the main story per the developers (playstyle-dependent). One review (Gamekult) hit credits in ~10 hours.

## Devices & Licenses

**How many devices can I repair?**
~16 named device types across 7 categories at launch (see the [Device Database](/guides/device-database)). A marketing "2,000+ devices" figure is unconfirmed (待验证).

**Are Atari devices real?**
The Atari 2600, Lynx and CX40 joystick are officially licensed. Everything else is a parody (Pokia 3310, Autorolla Razor, Eggotchi, Nony Goman, Palaloid, etc.).

**How do licenses work?**
Buy licenses to unlock new device types and story progression. Without a license you can accept a device but not finish it. One license must be won in the assembly competition. Full details in the [License Guide](/guides/license-guide).

**Do I need licenses for everything?**
No — the starting device types are license-free. Licenses expand your catalog and raise your income ceiling.

## Gameplay Questions

**How do I get paid for online orders?**
Finished repairs are auto-packed — place the package **back on the counter** so the courier picks it up. No counter placement, no payment.

**How many online orders per day?**
Up to 11, each with a 3-day deadline.

**Can I sell restored devices?**
Yes — buy broken devices on the marketplace, restore them, sell for profit. 5 sales earn "Making Money!".

**Is soldering in the demo?**
No — the soldering iron is locked in the demo; soldering jobs appear in the full game once the station unlocks.

**What are the bills?**
Rent (landlord) + bank loan. Paying both earns "All Bills Paid"; refusing triggers "Bankrupt" and a warning.

**Does the bike matter?**
The bicycle ends your day, saves progress, and starts a new one. Not using it for 168 straight hours earns "Akiba Never Sleeps".

## Hardware Questions

**What are the system requirements?**
Windows min: Win10 x64, 2-core/4-thread CPU, 4 GB RAM, GTX 750 Ti, DX11, 1 GB storage. macOS min: macOS 14+, Apple M1, 8 GB RAM, integrated GPU, 1 GB storage. No recommended specs published. See [Price & Platforms](/guides/price-platforms).

**Does it run on Steam Deck?**
Yes. Community settings: 1280×800, Medium textures/shadows, Low shadow distance, film grain off, V-Sync on, 60 Hz/60 FPS cap, 11-12W TDP. The default controller layout is unfinished — use a custom Steam Input layout.

## Is It Worth Buying?

**Buy it if you like:**
- Cozy management sims (House Flipper, PowerWash Simulator, I Am Future)
- Y2K nostalgia and retro hardware
- Narrative games with branching choices
- Meditative, low-pressure gameplay
