## Overview

ReStory is a love letter to mid-2000s Akihabara, and half the fun is spotting the references. Nearly every brand in the game is a legally distinct parody of real Y2K hardware — with one notable exception: **Atari hardware is officially licensed** and appears under its real name.

This guide collects the confirmed references and secrets from the launch build. Anything unconfirmed is marked 待验证.

| Real-World Item | In-Game Name | Notes |
|-----------------|--------------|-------|
| Nokia 3310 | **Pokia 3310** | The iconic brick phone; your first repair is Hashimoto's Pokia |
| Nokia N-Gage | **Pokia Njoy** | Parody of Nokia's game-phone hybrid |
| Motorola Razr | **Autorolla Razor** | Flip phone; also the one profitable new-part build |

| Xbox / Nintendo consoles | **Xi-Box / Patendo** | Reported in reviews (待验证 for in-game appearances) |

The parody rule: real-world brand name → same consonant skeleton, legally distinct spelling. The developers reportedly kept these deliberately close to the originals for the nostalgia payoff.

## Officially Licensed: Atari

**The Atari 2600, Atari Lynx and Atari CX40 joystick appear under their real names** through an official collaboration — the only licensed hardware in the game. You'll meet the Atari CX40 early: the upstairs neighbor asks you to fix one for a **Pac-Man tournament**.

## Hidden Secrets in Gameplay

**1. Hidden dirt.** Some parts carry grime that is invisible at default zoom — inspect edges, backs and connector areas, and zoom in. (See the [Cleaning Guide](/guides/cleaning-guide).)

**2. Customers hide information.** Dialogue isn't flavor text — customers casually mention things like spilling coffee on a device months ago, or hint that something important is hidden inside the hardware. Listening reveals issues not listed on the repair ticket.

**3. The post-it number.** Inside the landlord's big phone repair you can find a phone number written on a post-it note — your choice of what to do with it is one of the game's branching moments. (See the [Story Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough-guide).)

**4. The slow Y2K browser.** The in-game internet is deliberately slow and period-accurate — the loading times are the joke. The Unlock-ToolKit CD (needed for the **11001** reprogramming achievement) is bought from Gozilla Fairfox.

**5. Mid-2000s music and atmosphere.** The lo-fi radio and soft art style are themselves period tributes — one of the game's most-praised features in reviews.
