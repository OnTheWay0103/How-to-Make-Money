## Overview

ReStory is a game of two very different lengths. The **main story** is a focused 10–20 hour campaign. **100% completion** is a marathon measured in in-game time — the achievements demand a full year of shop days, a week straight without sleep, and thousands of individual part repairs.

This guide breaks down both numbers and gives you a realistic roadmap for the full completion.

---

## Main Story Length

| Figure | Source |
|--------|--------|
| **15+ hours** | Developer statement — the official estimate, "depending on playstyle" |
| **~10 hours** to credits | Gamekult review |
| **20 hours** logged | TechRaptor reviewer |
| **Plenty of replayability** after the story | Developer statement; branching choices and randomized orders |

**Takeaway:** plan for ~15 hours for a normal playthrough. Speed-focused players can reach the credits around 10 hours; slow, zen-paced players will stretch past 20.

## What the 100% Actually Requires

The completionist grind is defined by the achievement list — see the [Achievement Guide](/guides/achievement-guide) for the full table. The long poles:

| Requirement | Effort | Notes |
|-------------|:---:|-------|
| **Play through 365 in-game days** | ★★★★★ | The **Akiba Feels Like Home** achievement — a full year of shop days |
| **Work 168 hours straight without the bike** | ★★★★ | **Akiba Never Sleeps** — 7 full days without sleeping |
| **Clean 1,000 loose parts** | ★★★★ | **Galactic Cleaning** — one part per scrub, no shortcuts |
| **Complete 100 online orders** | ★★★ | **Internet Business** — steady volume over time |
| **Buy 50 spare-part boxes** | ★★ | **Gambler** — mostly an expense, keep spare yen |
| **Apply 100 stickers** | ★★ | **Sticker Bombing** — cheap and fast once unlocked |
| **Earn 1,000,000 Yen total** | ★★★ | **Millionaire** — comes naturally from flipping |
| **Acquire every license** | ★★★ | **Platinum Partner** — includes the competition-only license |
| **Win one competition per device type** | ★★★★ | **Legend of Akiba** — requires the full catalog |
| **The bugged achievement** | ★ | One achievement is reported as not unlocking — see the [FAQ guide's Known Issues section](/guides/faq-content#known-issues-bugs) |

**Estimated total:** an honest estimate is **40–80+ hours** for 100%, dominated by the 365-day and 1,000-part grinds. (This is a community estimate — unverified — no official 100% time has been published.)

## How to Shorten the Grind

**The 365-day milestone:**
- Days advance only when you go home and sleep. Minimize sleep-free hours wasted — plan work so each day's sleep happens before 8 AM (returning home before 8 AM also ensures the day registers).
- Cleaning-only jobs between midnight and 8 AM need no parts and keep income flowing.

**The 1,000-part milestone:**
- Run the **Sonic Bath** while hand-cleaning — multiple parts clean in parallel.
- Every order is part-count progress; never skip cleaning a dirty part that a replacement would cost yen to fix.

**The 168-hour milestone:**
- **Akiba Never Sleeps** requires 7 full days straight without using the bike. Keep the shop stocked with parts first, because you cannot restock from sleep — and note this is separate from day-count progress (the two goals work on different clocks).

**The million Yen:**
- Flip marketplace devices and grind clean-only restorations — see the [Money & Profit Guide](/guides/money-making-guide).

**Earning 100,000 Yen in a single day (Business Shark):**
- Don't end the workday on the bike; accumulate income across a long stretch before the day flips. The income counter rolls up while the shop stays open.

---

## The Frugal Roadblock

**Frugal** (10 days with no paid part purchases) is best done **before** you finish the story, while part needs are low: build a parts library first, salvage aggressively, and clean everything. Mystery spare-part boxes do **not** break the streak, which makes them a safe purchase during the challenge.

## Completion by Playstyle: What to Expect

| Playstyle | Main Story | 100% Estimate |
|-----------|:---:|:---:|
| Speed-runner (story only) | ~10 hours | — |
| Average player | 15 hours | — |
| Zen / completionist | 20+ hours | 40–80+ hours |
| Achievement hunter (no bug) | 15 hours | 40–80+ hours |

The variance is almost entirely driven by the two long poles — the 365-day milestone and the 1,000-part clean counter — plus how aggressively you flip marketplace devices for the **Millionaire** and license achievements. Players who grind night-shift cleaning jobs cut the day-count grind dramatically; players who only repair what orders demand take the longest.

---

## The Demo vs. the Full Game

The free Steam demo (available since June 15, 2026) covers roughly the first few shop days, with the soldering station and reprogramming visible but locked. Demo saves do **not** carry over to the full game — treat the demo as a taste test, not a head start. If you're deciding whether to buy, the demo's overwhelmingly positive reception and the full game's massive wishlist count both point to the same conclusion: the loop holds up over dozens of hours.

---

## Is the Endgame Worth It?

After the credits the shop continues indefinitely: online orders keep arriving, the competition can be re-entered, and the long-tail achievements (365 days, 1,000 parts, 100 orders) are all post-credits work. Players who enjoyed the loop find the endgame relaxing rather than tedious; players who only wanted the story should stop at the credits.
