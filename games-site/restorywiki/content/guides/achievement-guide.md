## Overview

ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs has **50 Steam achievements**. Most unlock naturally through normal play — cleaning, repairing, online orders and money milestones. A handful require deliberate purchases or specific story choices.

**⚠️ Known issue:** one achievement in the current build (v1.0.0) is reported as **not unlocking even when its condition is met** — a 100% run may be impossible until a patch.

---

## Story & Early-Game Achievements

| Achievement | How to Unlock |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Clean Job** | Clear all the dirty items on your desk when starting a new save |
| **First Fix!** | Repair your first device — Hashimoto's Nokia (Pokia in-game) |
| **How Did I Do This?** | Repair any part using the Soldering Iron |
| **Master of the Internet** | Set up your internet connection and accept an online order |
| **All Bills Paid** | Pay off two bills (rent to the landlord + the bank loan) |
| **Hacking 101** | Reprogram a device for the first time |
| **Origami** | Complete a kid's request and refuse payment — the delivery agent returns with an origami gift |
| **Custom Orders Available** | Paint a client's device before returning it |
| **Jack of All Trades** | Finish one order that asks you to paint, clean, repair and hack the same device |
| **Bankrupt** | Refuse to pay a bill and receive the overdue warning |
