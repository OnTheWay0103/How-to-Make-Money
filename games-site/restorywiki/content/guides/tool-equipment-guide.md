## Overview

ReStory gives you a period-accurate repair toolset, plus a catalog of upgrades bought through the in-game Y2K web browser (the "Screwdriver" menu). Tools fall into two groups: **core tools** you use on nearly every repair, and **workshop upgrades** that speed up or automate parts of the loop.

**Prices are in Yen.** Only a few prices are publicly confirmed; the rest remain unverified.

---

## Core Tool Catalog

| Tool | What It Does | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **Screwdrivers** | Open cases, remove/replace screws | Multiple sizes; the correct driver for every screw — wrong sizes strip screws. **The single most-used tool.** |
| **Tweezers** | Handle delicate cables and tiny components | Mandatory for phones and keypads |
| **Brush** | Manual cleaning of loose parts | Move the brush across visible grime; progress only counts when touching actual dirt |
| **Canned air (compressed air)** | Blow dust off large shells/panels | **Consumable** — save it for big surfaces, not tiny buttons |
| **Soldering iron / station** | Repair broken circuit boards | Not in the demo — jobs requiring soldering appear once the station is unlocked in the full game |
| **Sonic Bath (ultrasonic)** | Machine-clean up to 3 parts in ~30 seconds | Convenience: slower than brushing small parts by hand early, but useful upgraded or run while working another device |
| **Shredder** | Shred unusable broken parts for yen recovery | Turns junk into cash; buy the cheapest model early-ish |
| **Tip jar** | Customers leave tips automatically | Free passive income on work you already do |

## Purchase Priority (Launch Build Consensus)

Confirmed order recommended by multiple launch-week guides:

| Priority | Purchase | Why |
|:---:|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Screwdriver upgrades** | Nearly every repair starts and ends with screws |
| 2 | **Brush upgrades** | Cleaning appears constantly; upgrades are inexpensive |
| 3 | **Tip jar** | Passive income on existing work |
| 4 | **Sonic Bath** | Convenience; better once upgraded |
| 5 | **Shredder** | Once you accumulate junk parts |
| 6 | **Storage trays & shelves** | More open projects, organized inventory |
| 7 | **Canned air** | Consumable, situational |
| 8 | **Painting / airbrush, reprogramming gear** | Cosmetic/creative — no resale value early |

**Golden rule:** licenses beat every tool except the screwdriver. See the [License System Guide](/guides/license-guide).

## Tool Tips

- **Cleaning is the highest-return activity** — it mostly uses reusable tools, so clean-only restorations are nearly pure profit.
- **If cleaning is stuck at 0%**, the brush pointer isn't touching the grime (the game may play the sound anyway). Move the pointer directly over the dirt and watch the percentage.
- **A part in the Sonic Bath may appear "missing"** until the cycle finishes — check the bath before ordering a replacement part.
- **Upgraded magnifying equipment** makes tiny board work (phones, consoles) much faster — worthwhile once phone repairs are routine.
- **Organize spare parts by device type** — checking inventory fast beats digging through one big pile.
