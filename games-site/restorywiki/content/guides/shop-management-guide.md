## Overview

The repair loop is the game's soul, but the **shop** is its engine. How you manage the workbench, the queue, the storage and the cash flow decides whether your shop grows or stalls — regardless of how well you solder.

This guide covers the operational side of ReStory: the workflow systems that keep a busy shop running, confirmed from launch-week community play.

---

## The Workbench: Your Limiting Factor

Every open device occupies workbench space, and every repair has phases. The confirmed workflow habits:

1. **Limit open projects.** Accept only what you can finish — crowding the bench with too many devices mixes up parts and slows every job.
2. **Use bench zones.** Dirty parts on one side, clean parts on the other, broken parts in their own area. This prevents re-cleaning and lost components (see the [Cleaning Guide](/guides/cleaning-guide)).
3. **Disassemble in layers.** Lay components out in the order you removed them so reassembly follows the same path in reverse — the notepad's assembly order is the ground truth.
4. **Run the Sonic Bath continuously.** It cleans up to 3 parts in ~30 seconds while your hands work on a fourth.

---

## Storage: Buy Shelves Before You Need Them

Your spare-parts library is working capital — but only if you can find what you own.

- **Buy shelves and storage trays as your inventory grows.** Community guides are explicit: storage space matters, and organization is what makes finding parts easy.
- A **disorganized stockpile defeats the purpose**: an hour of hunting for a battery you know you bought is time you could have spent on paid repairs.
- Keep **customer order parts separate from resale stock** — mixing them causes missed deadlines and wrong devices.

See the [Money & Profit Guide](/guides/money-making-guide) for the parts-library strategy.

## Business Hours: The Shutter Is Your Lever

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Shutter **open** | Walk-in customers arrive; courier deliveries happen |
| Shutter **closed** | No walk-ins, no courier deliveries |

**Confirmed play:** keep the shutter open while you want work, and close it only when you need an interruption-free bench session (e.g., a long board repair). The shutter is also your lever for the **night-shift routine** — see below.

---

## The Night Shift (Midnight–8 AM)

The 100%-completion community's most reliable routine:

- **Between midnight and 8 AM**, grind **cleaning-only jobs** — they need no parts and are nearly pure profit.
- **Day progress only advances when you sleep** (take the bike). Return home **before 8 AM** so the day registers.
- This is also the backbone of the **Frugal** achievement (10 days, no paid part purchases).

---

## Queue Management

- **Up to 11 online orders per day** appear on the shop computer, each with a **3-day deadline**.
- Accept only what you can finish — an unfulfilled device sits on the bench and blocks throughput.
- **Mix job types:** alternate quick cleaning jobs with repairs that use parts you already own. All-repair queues stall on parts; all-cleaning queues underuse your tools.
- Missed deadlines don't cost money directly — orders sit until completed — but they crowd the bench. Treat the 3-day deadline as a workflow budget, not a fail state.

## Bills & Cash Flow

| Obligation | Amount | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| **Rent** | 2,000 yen/week | Collected by the landlord; refusing triggers an overdue warning and the **Bankrupt** achievement |
| **Bank loan** | Due early game | Paid off as part of the **All Bills Paid** achievement |
| **Internet** | Recurring | Required for online orders — don't let it lapse |

**Cash-flow rules:**
- **Keep a reserve.** Spending nearly all your yen on one marketplace device turns a "profitable" flip into a loss the moment repairs surprise you.
- **Batch parts orders** to save shipping; **never use express shipping** — it eats profit.
- **Tip jar early.** It generates income from work you were already doing, with zero extra effort.

## Zen Points: The Hidden Business Stat

Shop decoration isn't just cosmetic — **Zen points from decorations attract customers** (community-confirmed). Each decoration grants Zen points; keep the shop tidy, hit 100+ Zen, and you unlock the **A Place of Zen** achievement while your foot traffic improves. Full point values are in the [Achievement Guide](/guides/achievement-guide).

## Daily Routine That Scales

1. **Morning:** check the mailbox, accept online orders you can finish within 3 days.
2. **Day:** shortest surest jobs first; Sonic Bath running throughout.
3. **Night (after midnight):** grind cleaning-only jobs; keep income flowing.
4. **Before 8 AM:** organize parts, batch an order if needed, take the bike.
5. **Weekly:** pay rent on time, reinvest surplus into licenses, then tools, then storage.
