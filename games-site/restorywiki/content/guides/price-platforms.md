| **Supporter Edition (bundle)** | Bundle pricing (discounted) | Game + digital artbook |

### Is There a Demo?
Yes. A **free demo** has been on Steam since June 15, 2026 (Steam Next Fest). It includes extra gadgets like the Patento BS handheld and an airbrush drawing tool. Demo progress does **not** carry over to the full game.

## System Requirements (PC)

The following minimums are as published on the official Steam store listing as of August 2026 (recommended specs not yet released — unverified).

**Minimum (Windows):**
- OS: Windows 10 x64
- CPU: 2-core / 4-thread
- RAM: 4 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA GTX 750 Ti
- DirectX: Version 11
- Storage: 1 GB

**Minimum (macOS):**
- OS: macOS 14 or newer
- CPU: Apple M1
- RAM: 8 GB
- GPU: Apple integrated (M1)
- Storage: 1 GB
- Note: requires an Apple processor

## Is It Worth Buying?

**Yes, if you like:**
- Cozy management sims (House Flipper, PowerWash Simulator)
- Y2K/retro nostalgia
- Narrative games with meaningful choices
- Low-pressure, meditative gameplay

**Wait, if you:**
- Need combat or high-stakes gameplay
- Dislike cleaning/disassembly loops
- Want a short game (15+ hours is a commitment)

At $17.99 during the launch window, with an Overwhelmingly Positive Steam rating and strong critical reception, this is one of the strongest cozy-sim launches of 2026.
