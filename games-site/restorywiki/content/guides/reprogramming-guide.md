## Overview

Not every broken device is broken hardware. Some arrive with **software faults** — corrupted firmware, failed updates, locked systems — and those need reprogramming: a rhythm-style hacking mini-game that rewrites the device's software without touching the physical internals.

Reprogramming is one of the two skill-based repair steps in ReStory, alongside [soldering](/guides/soldering-guide). It's simple, satisfying, and — importantly for your wallet — **not something you should do unprompted**.

---

## How the Mini-Game Works

Community-confirmed mechanics from launch-week play:

1. When a device has a software fault, the game switches from physical repair to the reprogramming screen.
2. **Press keys in rhythm to fill a bar** — a simple, rhythm-game-style mechanic that reviewers confirm "works well."
3. Fill the bar to completion and the device's software is restored.

The mini-game is deliberately low-stakes — like everything in ReStory, there is no twitch-pressure and no fail state reported in the launch build (待验证 on whether later, larger devices demand longer or faster sequences).

## Unlocking Reprogramming

Reprogramming is not available from the first day — it's an **upgrade** you unlock in the full game (it is not part of the demo):

- The **Unlock-ToolKit CD** is bought from the in-game browser (Gozilla Fairfox) and is required for the **11001** achievement.
- Once unlocked, devices with software faults begin appearing in your order queue — the game does not hand you a software job before you own the ability, mirroring how [soldering](/guides/soldering-guide) unlocks.

## When a Device Needs Reprogramming

| Symptom | Likely Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Device "has a software issue" flag on the order | Reprogramming |
| Corrupted firmware / failed update reports | Reprogramming |
| **Broken** parts in the notepad | Soldering or replacement, *not* reprogramming |
| Dirty parts in the notepad | Cleaning, *not* reprogramming |

**Rule of thumb:** hardware problems are marked **broken** in the notepad; software problems arrive as a software-fault flag. If you are unsure which tool to reach for, check the notepad first — a part that only needs cleaning will not respond to reprogramming.

---

## The Economy Question: Is It Worth Doing?

**Short answer: only when the order requires it.**

- Reprogramming **does not noticeably raise a device's resale value**.
- It is described by community guides as **"not immediately useful"** as a money-maker — its value is unlocking achievements and fixing required orders, not boosting profit.
- Unlike cleaning (nearly pure profit) or soldering (required for board repairs), reprogramming unprompted is **free labor for no return**.

**When to do it:** when an order flags a software fault, when you're chasing **Hacking 101** or **11001**, or when a **Jack of All Trades**-type order asks you to paint, clean, repair *and* hack the same device.

## Achievements

| Achievement | Condition |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Hacking 101** | Reprogram a device for the first time |
| **11001** | Reprogram any 25 devices |

**11001 tips:**
- The counter accumulates across all reprogrammed devices — every software-fault order counts.
- Software-fault jobs become more common as you unlock more device categories, so the 25-device total comes naturally on a long playthrough.
- If you're short, buy the Unlock-ToolKit CD (required for the achievement) and keep accepting online orders until software jobs appear.

## Reprogramming vs. Soldering at a Glance

| | Reprogramming | Soldering |
|---|---------------|-----------|
| **Fixes** | Software faults, corrupted firmware | Broken boards, snapped wires |
| **Tool** | Reprogramming kit / Unlock-ToolKit CD | Soldering iron |
| **Mini-game** | Rhythm — press keys to fill a bar | Slow, deliberate iron movement |
| **Achievement** | Hacking 101, 11001 | How Did I Do This? |
| **Profit** | Not profitable unprompted | Normal income — required for board repairs |
| **Unlock** | Upgrade (full game) | Upgrade (locked in demo) |

## How to Spot a Software-Fault Job Early

The order queue and customer dialogue give you the first signal before you even touch the device:

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| "Software issue" / "won't boot" / "stuck on logo" in the order description | Likely reprogramming |
| Customer mentions a failed update, a weird download, or "it started after I installed something" | Software fault hidden in dialogue |
| Notepad flags **broken** parts | Hardware — soldering or replacement, not reprogramming |

Community reports note that software faults are more common on newer, feature-rich devices (phones and handheld consoles) and rarer on simple hardware like flashlights — but the exact per-device rates are still being mapped (待验证). When in doubt, open the notepad: the game always labels the fix type.

## Tips Summary

- Check the notepad before touching the keyboard — software flags mean reprogram, broken parts mean solder/replace.
- Don't reprogram for resale value — it doesn't add any.
- Chase **11001** passively: accept software-fault orders as they appear rather than forcing them.
- The Unlock-ToolKit CD is a required purchase for the achievement — budget it when you plan your tool spending (see the [Tools & Equipment Catalog](/guides/tool-equipment-guide)).
