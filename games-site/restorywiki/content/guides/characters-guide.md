## Overview

ReStory's story lives in its customers. There is no endless parade of random NPCs — the game runs on a cast of **recurring regulars**, each with a personal storyline that advances through repairs, dialogue and your choices. Buying licenses and completing their orders pushes their stories forward; refusing, helping or revealing things changes how they end.

Reviewers praise the cast — one calls the upstairs neighbor their favorite character in the game — though a few critics note some figures feel like "serviceable archetypes" rather than standouts. This guide covers every character confirmed in the launch build, plus community-reported names that are still being verified.

## The Two Headline Characters

### The Landlord

The landlord is one of the first characters you meet, and he sets the tone: rent pressure is your first financial constraint (2,000 yen/week for the small space), and his big rubber-buttoned phone becomes one of your earliest repairs. Inside that phone you find a **phone number on a post-it note** — and whether to give it to him is one of the game's most-cited branching choices.

### The Upstairs Neighbor

The undisputed fan favorite. They never speak directly — communication is by post-it note, lowered in a smiley-faced bucket from the floor above. They ask you to fix an Atari joystick for a Pac-Man tournament and recommend review-reading software. Reviewers consistently single them out ("my favorite," per Kyusai Media), and their notes are some of the best flavor text in the game.

## How Choices Shape Their Stories

The game's branching narrative means the same customer can end in very different places depending on you:

- **The phone number** — hand the landlord the post-it number found inside his phone, or keep it.
- **The former gangster's phone** — decide whether to report disturbing content found inside a repair.
- **The lovestruck student** — help him confess, or let his crush stay oblivious.
- **The kid's request** — refuse payment to earn the **Origami** achievement and the character's gratitude.

There is no "perfect" answer per community reports — different choices lead to different endings, and the game rewards replays. Full details in the [Story Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough-guide).

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of the Cast

- **Read every line of dialogue.** Hidden issues and story hints live in customer chatter — including details not on the repair ticket.
- **Complete orders for the same customers repeatedly** — their stories advance with each visit.
- **Don't rush decisions.** Choice moments are the game's main branching mechanism; save-scumming is not required, but thinking before you click matters.
- **Keep the shop open.** Walk-in customers — and their stories — only arrive while the shutter is up.
