## Overview

ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs drops you into a small repair shop in mid-2000s Akihabara. Your job: take in broken gadgets, fix them, get paid, pay your bills, and buy **licenses** that unlock new device types and push the story forward. There is no combat, no timer and no fail state — the game is deliberately slow and meditative.

This guide covers your first few in-game days and the habits that set up a profitable shop.

---

## The Core Loop, Step by Step

1. **Open the shop.** The shutter controls business hours — closing it stops walk-in customers (and courier deliveries), so keep it open while you want work.
2. **Accept orders.** Walk-ins arrive at the counter; online orders (up to 11 per day) come through the shop computer with a **3-day deadline**. Accept only what you can finish.
3. **Receive the device.** The courier delivers online orders to your counter. Remember: a finished repair is **auto-packed, but you must place the package back on the counter** so the courier picks it up — otherwise you never get paid.
4. **Disassemble.** Unscrew the case with the correct screwdriver, remove components carefully, and use tweezers on delicate cables. Rushing strips screws.
5. **Inspect with the notepad.** The To-Do List tracks every part's condition (clean / dirty / broken), highlights parts you can't find, and lists the **assembly order**.
6. **Clean first.** Dirt, dust, corrosion and sticky buttons cause most "broken" symptoms. Clean everything before you buy a single replacement part.
7. **Repair or replace.** Broken boards get the soldering iron; hopeless parts get replaced from stock or ordered online.
8. **Reassemble in notepad order.** If a component won't install, a part listed before it is probably still missing.
9. **Return it.** Packed device on the counter → courier picks it up → you get paid + a review.
10. **End your day with the bicycle.** The bike saves progress and starts the next day.

## Your First-Day Checklist

| Task | Why |
|------|-----|
| Accept 2-3 simple walk-in jobs | Learn disassembly without pressure |
| Clean, don't replace | Saves the most money early; most jobs are dirt, not damage |
| Check the mailbox | Online orders are your steady income stream |
| Don't buy brand-new parts | Order only what you truly need; harvest from junk devices instead |
| Pay attention to customers | Their dialogue hints at hidden issues and shapes the story |

## 10 Mistakes Every New Player Should Avoid

1. **Buying new parts for everything.** The fastest way to destroy your profit margin. Clean and test every part first — a "broken" button is often just sticky.
2. **Rushing disassembly.** Stripped screws turn a 2-minute job into a 20-minute headache. Use the right screwdriver and remove screws in a consistent order.
3. **Using express shipping.** Express delivery fees eat your profit. Plan ahead, batch orders, and stock common parts (batteries, screens, buttons, charging ports).
4. **Forgetting the package on the counter.** Auto-packed devices must be placed back on the counter for courier pickup, or you don't get paid.
5. **Ignoring the notepad.** It tells you assembly order and prevents buying parts you already have — a part may be dirty, on the table, or still in the cleaning machine.
6. **Accepting every order.** Too many open jobs clog your workbench and similar devices get mixed up. Mix quick cleaning jobs with repairs that use parts you already own.
7. **Skipping the tip jar.** It generates free income from work you were already doing. Buy it early.
8. **Spending on cosmetics first.** Painting adds no resale value and reprogramming barely raises resale worth early on. Licenses and tools come first.
9. **Ignoring bills.** Rent and the bank loan are due — refusing to pay triggers warnings (and an achievement). Keep cash flow positive.
10. **Wasting canned air.** It's consumable — save it for large shells and awkward panels, not tiny buttons.

## Daily Routine That Works

- **Morning:** Check the mailbox and accept online orders you can actually finish within 3 days.
- **Day:** Do the shortest, surest jobs first; run the Sonic Bath while you hand-clean another part.
- **Evening:** Reorganize spare parts, batch a parts order if needed, then take the bike home.
- **Money rhythm:** Every repair funds the next license. Licenses > tools > cosmetics.
