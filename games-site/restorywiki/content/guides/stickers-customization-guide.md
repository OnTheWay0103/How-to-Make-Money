## Overview

ReStory lets you make every repair yours: **stickers** and **paint** turn finished devices into customized pieces of Y2K nostalgia — and chasing the sticker milestones is a fast, cheap path to three achievements. This guide covers where stickers come from, how painting works, and the fastest route to 100 applications.

## Stickers — What They Are

- **Cosmetic decorations** you buy and apply to devices — purely decorative, no gameplay effect on repair outcomes
- Bought from the **depot store's "accessory" tabs**
- Applied to devices before they go back to the customer (the same hand-off as painting)

Sticker application also counts toward **sticker-count achievements** (below), which makes the "useless" cosmetic system one of the easiest achievement farms in the game.

## Painting Devices

- **Custom Orders Available** — you can paint a client's device before returning it (tied to the "Custom Orders Available" achievement)
- Painting is part of the customization step in the repair flow — after repair, before return
- A device can combine paint, cleaning, repair, and reprogramming in one order (the **Jack of All Trades** achievement needs exactly that mix)

## Sticker Achievements

| Achievement | Condition | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| **Instant Cool** | Apply a sticker to a device | The first of the three; unlocks early (33.6% of players per Steam stats) |
| **Instant Cool-er** | Apply **25 stickers** to devices | Requires deliberate sticker use |
| **Sticker Bombing** | Apply **100 stickers** to devices | The grindy one — but cheap to farm |

All three are straightforward: buy stickers, apply them, repeat.

## Fastest Way to 100 Sticker Applications

1. **Buy stickers in bulk** from the depot accessory tabs whenever you visit the depot — don't wait for a specific occasion
2. **Sticker every device you repair** — even small walk-in jobs count one application each
3. **Combine with painting:** a painted-and-stickered device covers customization in a single order
4. **Chain with the repair grind:** the [Achievement Guide](/guides/achievement-guide) recommends folding sticker application into normal repair routines — no detour needed, roughly one sticker per device repaired
5. **Marketplace flips count too:** restored and resold devices (see the [Money & Profit Guide](/guides/money-making-guide)) can be stickered before resale — two uses per device

At roughly one device per order and one sticker per device, **100 stickers falls out of normal play in the mid-game** — you only need to remember to apply them.

## Sticker Economy Tips

- **Stickers are cheap** — they're the low-cost entry into the accessory tab, and they never affect repair success or customer satisfaction (awaiting confirmation on any hidden customer reactions, but no reports so far)
- **They're the cheapest achievements in the game** — no license, no rare parts, no competition wins required; just depot trips and device hand-offs
- **Customization before return** — remember the paint-and-sticker step happens at the return hand-off, the same step where kids' origami requests and delivery-agent interactions trigger (see the [Story Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough-guide))
