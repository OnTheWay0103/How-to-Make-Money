MineGeon: Renegades is a **co-op sci-fi bullet-hell roguelite focused on resource gathering**, developed by **Kickstone Studio** and released on **Steam in July 2026**. After a failed heist, your crew of Renegades crash-lands on the dying planet PAIMO and descends into its dangerous depths to mine minerals, fight for survival, and uncover secrets that should have stayed buried.

Choose from 3 playable Renegades — **Marshall** (resilient Tank), **Trevor** (deadly DPS Gunslinger), and **Ari** (balanced Engineer) — and descend into procedurally generated "minegeons" across **six hand-crafted biomes**. Every biome ends in a boss battle. Minerals get deeper and more valuable, but **if you die, what you carry is lost for good** — so extract, trade your haul for permanent ship and character upgrades, and go again.

Play solo or in **online co-op with up to two friends**, combine over **150 gadgets** (grenades, energy shields, combat drones), weapons, and ultimate abilities into synergistic builds, and customize the Galactic Express ship with 200+ decorations.

Whether you're going in solo or teaming up with friends, this unofficial wiki has everything you need: class guides, boss strategies, gadget advice, biome guides, and mining and extraction tips to help you survive and thrive in the depths of PAIMO.
