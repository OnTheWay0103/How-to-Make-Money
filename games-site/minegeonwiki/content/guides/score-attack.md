## Is There a Score Attack Mode in MineGeon: Renegades?

**No.** The Steam page confirms the game's modes are solo and online co-op expeditions — there is no score attack mode, no leaderboards, and no weekly challenge mode. If you searched for "MineGeon score attack," you found content about a mode that doesn't exist (the "scoring system" tables on some sites are invented).

What the game *does* have is a run structure worth optimizing: **every expedition is a risk/reward decision** — how deep, how greedy, when to extract. That's the real optimization game, and this guide covers it.

## What "Optimizing a Run" Actually Means

A good MineGeon run isn't the one with the deepest descent — it's the one that **banks the most minerals per unit of risk**. The scoreboard is your permanent upgrade track. Everything else is theater.

### The four run types
| Run Type | Goal | Discipline |
|----------|------|-----------|
| Farming | Bank minerals | Extract early and often |
| Boss | Clear the biome gate | Bank first, then fight with a fresh run |
| Exploration | New biome / lore | Low expectations, high information |
| Co-op | Team progress | Shared protection, shared extraction |

One goal per run. Mixing goals is the fastest way to lose both.

## Planning the Expedition

### Before you descend
1. **Bank first.** Unspent minerals earn nothing — spend before you leave
2. **Set the leave trigger.** "I extract when X happens" (wounded, rare find, boss taken, time elapsed) — decided *now*, honored *later*
3. **Match loadout to goal.** Farming wants mining safety (combat drones); boss runs want burst and shields
4. **Know the route goal.** The ancient monoliths let you vote on the descent path — have a preference before the vote comes

### During the run
1. **Clear the room, then mine.** A safe room mines faster than a contested one
2. **Mine with an exit plan.** Never commit to a node without knowing your escape direction
3. **Read the run.** Roguelite randomness means adapting — take the good weapon that drops, don't wait for a perfect one
4. **Gadgets over greed.** Spend gadgets to survive, not hoard them

### The extraction decision
The single most valuable skill in the game. Ask three questions:

1. **What's on me?** The bigger the haul, the sooner to leave
2. **What's my state?** Health, consumables, teammates (co-op)
3. **What's the risk ahead?** An unknown biome, a boss, or the Scavengers massing?

If the answer to any of them is "bad," extract. The minerals you have are worth more than the minerals you imagine.

## Optimizing by Class

| Renegade | Optimization Focus |
|----------|--------------------|
| Marshall (Tank) | Be the safe miner — resilience lets you farm contested nodes others must skip |
| Trevor (Gunslinger) | Fast clear times — optimize the path between veins; skip weak nodes |
| Ari (Engineer) | Adapt — let the run's gadget luck decide aggressive vs. safe |

## Optimizing Co-op Runs

- **Divide labor, not loot:** one guards, one mines, one scouts
- **The carry rule:** if one player has a big haul, the team's job is protecting them to extraction
- **Call the leave together.** The team trigger beats three individual opinions
- **Bank before the boss, always.** Boss fights are the #1 co-op haul killer

## Common Optimization Mistakes

1. **Chasing depth records.** Depth is a means, banking is the end
2. **Trusting "best routes."** Minegeons are procedural — fixed routes don't exist
3. **Fighting everything.** Kiting and skipping are strategies, not cowardice
4. **Holding gadgets "for later."** Later usually doesn't come
5. **Believing scoring-system guides.** There's no score, no multipliers, no leaderboards — just minerals and extraction

## The Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Minerals banked and spent before descending
- [ ] One goal per run
- [ ] Leave trigger set in advance
- [ ] Loadout matches the goal
- [ ] Rooms cleared before mining
- [ ] Escape direction known before committing
- [ ] Extract at the trigger, no exceptions

Follow the checklist and every run — good or bad — banks progress. That's the actual high score.
