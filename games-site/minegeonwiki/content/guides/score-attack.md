## Score Attack Mode in MineGeon: Renegades — Complete Guide

Score Attack is MineGeon's competitive mode where players compete for the highest score across procedurally generated runs. Unlike standard extraction mode, Score Attack adds a scoring system with multipliers, bonus objectives, and leaderboard rankings. This guide covers everything you need to climb the ranks.

---

## How Scoring Works

Your final score is calculated from four components:

| Component | Weight | Max Points | Description |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Loot Value | 40% | 40,000 | Total value of extracted ores and items |
| Enemy Kills | 25% | 25,000 | Points per enemy defeated |
| Speed Bonus | 20% | 20,000 | Time remaining when extracting |
| Bonus Objectives | 15% | 15,000 | Optional challenges completed during run |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100,000** | Perfect run cap (before multipliers) |

### Score Multipliers

Multipliers stack multiplicatively, not additively:

| Multiplier | Condition | Bonus |
|------------|-----------|-------|
| No Damage | Extract without taking any health damage | x1.5 |
| Speed Demon | Extract within 8 minutes | x1.3 |
| Full Clear | Kill all enemies on the map | x1.25 |
| Hoarder | Extract with full inventory of rare items | x1.2 |
| No Traps | Complete run without placing any traps | x1.15 |
| Combo Chain | Kill 10+ enemies within 30 seconds | x1.1 |

Theoretical maximum multiplier: 1.5 x 1.3 x 1.25 x 1.2 x 1.15 x 1.1 = **x3.70**

## Optimal Score Attack Route

### Phase 1: Sprint to Center (0:00 - 2:00)
- Ignore all surface-level ores (too low value)
- Use Speed Boost and Adrenaline Shot immediately
- Head directly to the central chamber where elite enemies and gems spawn
- Kill any enemies in your path without stopping to loot

### Phase 2: High-Value Harvest (2:00 - 5:00)
- Farm the central chamber for Gems and Artifacts
- Kill all elites for bonus score
- Activate the Combo Chain by grouping enemies with kiting
- Check your Radar for hidden ore deposits

### Phase 3: Boss Kill (5:00 - 7:00)
- Locate and engage the biome boss
- Use your remaining consumables
- Boss kills give 10,000 base + potential 5,000 solo bonus
- After boss kill, grab any remaining Gems near the arena

### Phase 4: Extraction Sprint (7:00 - 8:00)
- Head to extraction immediately
- Do not stop for additional loot
- The Speed Demon multiplier (x1.3) is worth more than a few extra Gems
- Extraction screen shows your final multiplier breakdown

## Co-op Score Attack Strategy

In co-op Score Attack, the scoring changes slightly:

| Change | Solo | Co-op (2-4 players) |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| Loot scoring | Individual | Shared pool, divided by players |
| Kill scoring | Individual | Each player gets full kill score |
| Speed Demon | 8 minutes | 10 minutes (2p), 12 minutes (3-4p) |
| Full Clear | All enemies | Your share of enemies |
| Multipliers | All available | Team must all qualify |

**Best co-op composition:** 2x Gunslinger + 1 Engineer + 1 Tank. Gunslingers focus on speed and kills. Engineer places traps to ensure Full Clear. Tank protects the team during boss fights and carries the highest-value loot.

## Weekly Leaderboard Tips

1. **Learn the seed** — Weekly Score Attack runs use a fixed seed. Run it multiple times to memorize enemy and loot locations.
2. **Sacrifice safety for speed** — Leaderboard runs rarely extract with full health. The No Damage bonus is nice, but Speed Demon + Combo Chain often outscore it.
3. **Watch the top replays** — The in-game leaderboard lets you watch replay runs from top players. Study their routes.
4. **Perfect your first 30 seconds** — Most Score Attack runs are decided in the first minute. A bad start rarely recovers.
5. **Know when to reset** — If you don't find at least 2 Gem deposits in the first 90 seconds, reset and try again.
