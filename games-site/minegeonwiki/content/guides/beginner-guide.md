## Getting Started in MineGeon: Renegades

MineGeon: Renegades is a co-op sci-fi bullet-hell roguelite focused on resource gathering, developed by Kickstone Studio. After a failed heist, your crew of Renegades crash-lands on the dying planet PAIMO. You descend into its depths to mine minerals, fight hostile creatures and the Scavengers faction, and trade your haul for permanent upgrades back on the ship.

The core loop is simple: **descend → mine → fight → extract → upgrade → go deeper**. This guide walks you through your first expeditions and the decisions that matter most early on.

## Choosing Your Renegade

At the start of the game you pick one of three playable classes, each with its own stats and ultimate ability:

| Renegade | Role | Best For |
|----------|------|----------|
| **Marshall** | Resilient Tank | Beginners, solo play, holding enemy attention in co-op |
| **Trevor** | Deadly DPS Gunslinger | Experienced shooters, boss damage, kiting |
| **Ari** | Balanced Engineer | Players who like gadgets and flexible support |

**Recommendation for new players:** start with Marshall. His resilient stats make early mistakes survivable while you learn enemy bullet patterns and the extraction rhythm. Trevor is a great second pick once you are comfortable dodging — this is a bullet-hell game, and Trevor pays for every hit you take.

> Note: the developer has confirmed each class has its own ultimate ability and stats, but detailed ability names and numbers have not been published. We mark those specifics as unconfirmed until the community documents them.

## Your First Run: Step by Step

### Step 1: Descend

Launch an expedition (solo, or with up to two friends in online co-op) and descend into the procedurally generated "minegeon." The layout, mineral placement, and hazards change every run, so treat each descent as a new puzzle.

### Step 2: Mine

Mineral deposits are your goal. PAIMO is filled with valuable minerals, and the deeper you go, the more valuable they become — along with the danger. Mine what you find, and keep an eye out for richer veins in harder-to-reach areas.

### Step 3: Fight

Enemies are everywhere: swarms of hostile creatures, and the Scavengers — a faction determined to stop your descent. This is a bullet-hell game, so dodging matters more than tanking. Keep moving, learn to read projectile patterns, and don't stand still while mining.

### Step 4: Extract (the most important step)

Everything you mine is at risk. **If you die, the minerals you are carrying are lost for good.** You can retreat and send your minerals back to the ship whenever you choose — the smart players extract early and often. Trade your minerals for permanent upgrades, then go back deeper.

### Step 5: Upgrade

At the ship you can trade minerals for permanent upgrades to the ship itself and to each Renegade's attributes. Leveling up also unlocks gadgets (150+ in the full game, including grenades, energy shields, and combat drones), weapons, and ultimate abilities. These carry over between runs — this is a roguelite, not a roguelike.

## Early Game Priorities

1. **Extract successfully a few times before pushing deep.** The mineral economy runs on successful extractions. Learn the retreat timing before you greed for rich veins.
2. **Spend minerals on permanent upgrades first.** Early upgrades compound — better stats and tools make every future run faster and safer.
3. **Try all three classes.** The demo and full game let you play Marshall, Trevor, and Ari freely. Knowing each class's role makes co-op teams much stronger.
4. **Learn the monolith mechanic.** Ancient monoliths found deep in PAIMO let your team vote on which path to descend next. Route choice is a big part of run planning (solo players just pick their own path).
5. **Customize your ship.** The Galactic Express PC lets you buy 200+ decorations and themed items. Purely cosmetic, but it is the game's "home base" identity — and you can visit other players' ships online.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Greed.** The #1 cause of lost minerals is pushing "one more vein" past the point of safety. Every death resets your haul to zero. Extract with what you have.
2. **Standing still while mining.** You are a target the moment you swing your tool. Check your surroundings before committing to a node.
3. **Ignoring co-op.** This game is designed for 1-3 players, and even a single friend changes the risk equation: someone can watch your back while you mine.
4. **Wasting permanent-upgrade minerals on cosmetics.** Decorate only after your ship upgrades and character attributes are in good shape.
5. **Not using gadgets.** Gadgets (grenades, energy shields, combat drones) are a major part of builds — ignoring them is playing with one hand tied.

## First Boss Tips

Every one of PAIMO's six biomes ends in a boss battle against a formidable ancient entity. Specific boss names and patterns had not been published as of early August 2026, but the general rules hold:

- Go in with upgraded gear and gadgets — bosses are a gear check as much as a skill check.
- In co-op, decide roles before the fight: who draws attention, who deals damage, who handles adds.
- Keep your escape in mind. If the fight is going badly, retreating with your minerals beats dying with nothing.
- Ultimate abilities are often best saved for boss arenas — they can turn a losing fight around.

## Next Steps

After 3-5 successful extractions you should:

- Have several permanent upgrades on your ship and chosen Renegade
- Understand your class's role and have a favorite gadget loadout
- Be ready to push past the first biome toward its boss
- Start planning runs around monolith route choices
