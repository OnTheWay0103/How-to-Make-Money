## Farming Efficiency Overview

Resource farming is the backbone of progression in MineGeon: Renegades — every ship upgrade, every Renegade attribute point, every permanent unlock is funded by extracted minerals. Efficient farming is less about "routes" (minegeons are procedurally generated, so fixed routes don't exist) and more about **run discipline**: extract more often, lose less, go deeper only when it's safe.

> **Accuracy note:** older guides promised exact per-run yields ("80-120 Iron per run"), named ore tables, and fixed routes through named biomes. Both are fabricated: minegeons are procedurally generated each run, ore and gem names are unpublished, and no official yield figures exist. This guide gives the confirmed strategy layer instead.

## The Farming Mindset

### Extraction is the scoreboard
A farming run's success is measured by one number: **minerals banked**. Not minerals found, not depth reached — banked. Two runs of equal length, one ending in death and one in extraction, differ by 100% of their value.

### Depth is a dial, not a destination
Deeper = richer minerals, always. But the risk curve climbs faster than most players expect. The efficient farmer asks "is this depth safe for me right now?" before every push.

## Is There a Score Attack Mode?

**No.** The Steam page confirms the game's modes are solo and online co-op expeditions — there is no score attack mode, no leaderboards, and no weekly challenge mode. If you searched for "MineGeon score attack," you found content about a mode that doesn't exist (the "scoring system" tables on some sites are invented).

What the game *does* have is a run structure worth optimizing: **every expedition is a risk/reward decision** — how deep, how greedy, when to extract. That's the real optimization game, and it's what this guide covers.

### The four run types
| Run Type | Goal | Discipline |
|----------|------|-----------|
| Farming | Bank minerals | Extract early and often |
| Boss | Clear the biome gate | Bank first, then fight with a fresh run |
| Exploration | New biome / lore | Low expectations, high information |
| Co-op | Team progress | Shared protection, shared extraction |

One goal per run. Mixing goals is the fastest way to lose both.

## Pre-Expedition Checklist

- [ ] **Bank spent first** — invest your minerals before the next run; unspent minerals earn nothing
- [ ] **Know your goal** — farming run (pure minerals), boss run, or exploration run (lore/new biome). Mixing goals wastes both
- [ ] **Set the leave trigger before you descend** — "I extract when X happens" (wounded, rare find, boss taken, time elapsed) — decided *now*, honored *later*
- [ ] **Gadget loadout matches the goal** — combat drones for solo mining safety, grenades for escape pressure, shields for deep runs
- [ ] **Know the route goal** — the ancient monoliths let you vote on the descent path; have a preference before the vote comes
- [ ] **Co-op roles assigned** — who mines, who guards, who carries (see below)

## Efficient Farming Patterns

### 1. The Two-Pass Run
- **Pass one:** descend carefully, mine what's safe, extract early with a modest haul
- **Pass two (same session, upgraded):** now with banked upgrades, push deeper and take more risk
- This is the compounding loop made explicit — each successful pass funds the next.

### 2. The Depth Ladder
- Farm the depth you can clear consistently
- When one level is trivial, step one depth down — not two
- The ladder approach avoids the classic "died at the new biome, lost everything" pattern

### 3. The Boss Bypass
- During farming runs, skip the biome boss unless the run's goal is the boss
- A dead farming run earns nothing; a boss attempt is its own run type

### 4. The Break Point Rule
- Set a mental "extract now" trigger before you descend: a threshold of damage taken, a rare find, or a time limit
- When the trigger hits, extract — no bargaining with yourself

## Gem Farming: Chasing High-Value Minerals

> **Accuracy note:** specific ore and gem names had not been published by early August 2026 — guides that name gems or list "gem tables" are inventing them. What is confirmed: PAIMO is filled with rare, valuable minerals, **deeper descents yield more valuable minerals**, and minerals bank only when extracted.

"Gem farming" in MineGeon: Renegades means one thing: **hunting the high-value minerals that spawn at depth** — and getting them out. The gem-equivalent items exist; the names do not (yet). This system works no matter what the final catalog is called.

### The depth math of gems

| Depth | Mineral Value | Risk | Gem Opportunity |
|-------|---------------|------|-----------------|
| Starter depths | Low | Very Low | None — common minerals only |
| Mid depths | Medium | Medium | First "gem-tier" veins appear |
| Deep biomes | High | High | The best gem spawns — and the worst death penalties |
| Biome depths + boss | Highest | Very High | Rare veins, legendary-tier minerals |

**The confirmed rule:** the deeper you go, the greater the rewards — and the risks. A gem find is a *depth reward*, which means every gem run is a negotiation with the risk curve.

### A gem find is an extract trigger

The single most important rule in gem farming: **the moment a rare mineral is in your bag, the run's goal changes.** Set your trigger *before* you descend:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Rare mineral found | Extract — the run is won |
| Depth budget reached | Extract — deeper is a new bet |
| Low health | Extract — gems die with you |
| Elite/boss spawn nearby | Extract — no fight is worth the bag |

A gem in the bag is not a reason to push further; it is a reason to leave. Greed streaks — "one more vein, one more room" — are how gem runs end as zero-mineral runs.

### Gear and gadgets for gem runs

| Loadout Slot | Best Choice | Why |
|--------------|-------------|-----|
| Offensive gadget | Grenades | Break swarms at the vein before they surround you |
| Defensive gadget | Energy shield | The extraction channel under fire — where gem runs die |
| Autonomous gadget | Combat drone | Scout deep rooms before you commit the bag |
| Weapons | Your most comfortable clear set | Depth is the risk; gear should remove the *other* risks |

**The rule:** on a gem run, the loadout serves the bag, not the build. Offensive flexibility is nice; extraction insurance is mandatory.

## Run Optimization: The Extraction Decision

A good run isn't the one with the deepest descent — it's the one that **banks the most minerals per unit of risk**. The scoreboard is your permanent upgrade track. When it's time to decide, ask three questions:

1. **What's on me?** The bigger the haul, the sooner to leave
2. **What's my state?** Health, consumables, teammates (co-op)
3. **What's the risk ahead?** An unknown biome, a boss, or the Scavengers massing?

If the answer to any of them is "bad," extract. The minerals you have are worth more than the minerals you imagine.

### Optimizing by class

| Renegade | Optimization Focus |
|----------|--------------------|
| Marshall (Tank) | Be the safe miner — resilience lets you farm contested nodes others must skip |
| Trevor (Gunslinger) | Fast clear times — optimize the path between veins; skip weak nodes |
| Ari (Engineer) | Adapt — let the run's gadget luck decide aggressive vs. safe |

## Co-op Farming & Optimization

Co-op changes farming math in your favor:

- **Split roles, not players.** One mines while another guards — then swap. Two miners with no guard is how co-op farming runs die.
- **Split the map with a voice call.** Both cover different wings, call out rich veins, and meet for extraction.
- **The escape buddy rule.** The team that extracts together banks together — never leave a teammate behind on a farming run unless they insist and the haul justifies it.
- **The carry rule.** If one player has a big haul, the team's job is protecting them to extraction.
- **Call the leave together.** The team trigger beats three individual opinions.
- **Bank before the boss, always.** Boss fights are the biggest co-op haul killer.
- **Teams can hit deeper safely.** With roles covered, a duo or trio can farm depths that would be reckless solo. Use that, but remember the death penalty applies per player.

## What to Farm When

| Game Stage | Farming Focus | Why |
|------------|---------------|-----|
| Early | Any extraction, any depth | The first upgrades compound hardest |
| Mid | The deepest biome you can clear safely | Richer minerals per minute of risk |
| Late | Boss runs + deep biomes | Legendary gear and endgame minerals |
| Always | Extraction discipline | The rule that never stops applying |

## Common Farming Mistakes

1. **Route-following.** Fixed "best routes" don't exist in procedurally generated minegeons — adapt to the map you get.
2. **Greed streaks.** One more vein, one more room, one more depth — each one is a bet, and the house always wins eventually.
3. **Fighting bosses on farming runs.** Bosses are run-ending risks, not farming yields.
4. **Farming without a bank.** If your minerals from last run are still unspent, you're farming in the red.
5. **Trusting yield tables.** No official yields exist. Anyone quoting exact ore numbers per run is guessing or inventing.
6. **Holding gadgets "for later."** Later usually doesn't come — spend gadgets to survive, not hoard them.
7. **Chasing depth records.** Depth is a means, banking is the end.
8. **Believing scoring-system guides.** There's no score, no multipliers, no leaderboards — just minerals and extraction.

## The Efficiency Formula

The whole farming strategy fits in one line:

**Bank more per run + die less often = exponential progression.**

Everything in this guide — depth ladders, break points, co-op roles, two-pass runs, gem triggers — is a way to push that formula. Master it and the depths of PAIMO open up.
