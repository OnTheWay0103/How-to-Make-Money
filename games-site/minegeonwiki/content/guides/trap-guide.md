## There Is No Trap System — Here's What Replaces It

If you searched for a MineGeon: Renegades trap guide, here's the truth: **the game has no trap system.** No spike traps, no turret skills, no trap skill trees — those detailed "trap type" tables on other sites are invented. Kickstone Studio's confirmed systems are **gadgets** (grenades, energy shields, combat drones, and 150+ more), weapons, and ultimate abilities.

The *function* traps would serve — controlling space, denying approaches, covering your back — is the job of **gadgets**. This guide covers area control the way the real game does it.

## The Area-Control Gadget Families (Confirmed)

| Family | What It Does | When It Wins Runs |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Grenades** | Explosive area denial — damage, pressure, crowd breaking | Swarm rooms, escape windows, breaking enemy charges |
| **Energy shields** | Survive incoming burst — defensive bubble | Boss phases, extraction under fire, deep-biome corridors |
| **Combat drones** | Autonomous damage while you do something else | Mining in contested rooms, solo protection, distraction |

Specific gadget names, stats, and cooldowns are unpublished — expect the real catalog to include many more families (the full game advertises 150+ unique gadgets).

## Area Control Fundamentals

### 1. Control the room before you commit
The classic mistake is mining first and fighting later. The area-control player:
- Enters a room, identifies the threats
- Uses a grenade to break the swarm's grouping
- Mines with a drone covering the approach
- Keeps the shield for the "oh no" moment

### 2. Control the chokepoint
Minegeon geometry is your ally:
- Fight in corridors where enemies arrive one at a time
- Grenade the crowd as it rounds the corner
- A drone in the corridor turns it into a kill lane while you mine the room beyond

### 3. Control the escape
Area control is defensive too:
- **Always have a grenade for the escape.** The most valuable grenade is the one that breaks the pursuing swarm at the extraction door
- **Shield for the channel.** If extraction has a channel/activate moment, the shield is its best friend
- **Drones hold the rear.** Solo, a drone covering your exit is the closest thing to a teammate

| Trevor (Gunslinger) | Control *range* — grenades keep pressure off you while you kite and shoot |

## Co-op Area Control

- **Designate the controller.** One player's gadget job is space — the team follows their calls
- **Grenade the boss adds.** Swarms summoned in boss fights are the classic area-control moment
- **Cover the reviver.** The player reviving a teammate is the most exposed position on the map — shield or grenade cover makes revives possible
- **Split gadget roles:** one offensive (grenades), one defensive (shields), one autonomous (drones)

## Area-Control Mistakes

1. **Holding grenades "for later."** Later is usually the moment you die
2. **Wasting shields early.** Shields are panic buttons — using them in calm rooms leaves you naked in boss fights
3. **Drones without a plan.** A drone mines with you only if you're mining — place it with intent
4. **Forgetting gadgets in extraction.** The extraction moment is the most gadget-critical moment in the game
5. **Trusting trap tables.** No traps exist. Anyone selling you "Spike Trap: 45 damage, 8 Iron" is selling fiction

## The Area-Control Mindset

The best MineGeon players think in terms of **space**, not kills: every room is a position to hold, every corridor is a funnel, every extraction is a door to protect. Gadgets are how you shape that space. Master the three families — grenades, shields, drones — and the depths of PAIMO get much more forgiving.
