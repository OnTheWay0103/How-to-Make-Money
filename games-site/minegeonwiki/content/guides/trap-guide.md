## Traps in MineGeon: Renegades — Complete Guide

Traps are one of the most powerful tools in MineGeon: Renegades, especially for Engineer-class players and anyone looking to control enemy movement during extraction runs. This guide covers every trap type, how to place them effectively, and the best strategies for each biome.

---

## Trap Categories

Traps fall into four main categories based on their function:

| Category | Purpose | Best Used By |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Damage Traps** | Deal direct damage to enemies | All classes, core for Engineer |
| **Crowd Control** | Slow, stun, or push enemies | Engineer, Support builds |
| **Utility Traps** | Reveal enemies, heal, or buff | Any class with gadget slots |
| **Environmental** | Exploit terrain features | All classes (free) |

## How Traps Work

### Placement Rules
- Traps can be placed on any solid surface (floor, wall, ceiling)
- You carry a limited number of each trap type per run
- Traps persist for the entire expedition unless triggered
- You can reclaim traps after use with the Engineer's Salvage ability

### Trigger Mechanics
- Most traps activate when an enemy walks within range
- Some traps require manual activation (Motion Sensor linked to Flame Emitter)
- Enemies can destroy traps if they attack them directly
- Boss enemies take reduced damage from traps (around 50%)

### Synergy System
Certain trap combinations trigger bonus effects:

| Trap Combo | Synergy Effect |
|------------|---------------|
| Electric Web + Tar Pit | Shock Tar — deals 50% more damage and slows 75% |
| Motion Sensor + Flame Emitter | Auto-turret mode — aims at nearest detected enemy |
| Concussion Mine + Spike Trap | Shrapnel — knockback enemies into spikes for 2x damage |
| Healing Pad + any CC trap | Fortified — 20% damage reduction while healing |

## Best Trap Loadouts by Playstyle

### Engineer Control Build
- **Core:** Electric Web, Concussion Mine, Motion Sensor
- **Strategy:** Place Motion Sensors at chokepoints to trigger Webs and Mines remotely. Focus on creating kill corridors rather than scattered traps.

### Solo Survival Build
- **Core:** Spike Trap, Healing Pad, Flame Emitter
- **Strategy:** Place Healing Pads near your mining area. Use Spike Traps at entry points. Flame Emitter for emergency crowd control during extraction.

### Co-op Support Build
- **Core:** Motion Sensor, Healing Pad, Electric Web
- **Strategy:** Place Motion Sensors to alert teammates of approaching enemies. Healing Pads keep the group healthy during extended mining. Webs cover retreats.

## Trap Placement Tips by Biome

### Biome 1: The Shallows
- Narrow tunnels make Spike Traps highly effective
- Place traps at natural choke points where the walls constrict
- One Flame Emitter can cover an entire dead-end room

### Biome 2: Crystal Depths
- Electric Webs work well on reflective crystal surfaces (unknown to most players, the web stun lasts 1 second longer on crystal)
- Use Motion Sensors to track Stalker enemies that phase through walls
- Avoid Tar Pits here — the native Spitters are immune to slow

### Biome 3: Magma Core
- Flame Emitters are 50% less effective due to ambient heat
- Concussion Mines shine here — the knockback can send enemies into lava pits for instant kills
- Healing Pads are essential due to constant environmental damage

### Biome 4: Void Rifts
- All trap durations are reduced by 30% in Void zones
- Focus on high-damage single-use traps (Concussion Mines, Cave-in Triggers)
- Motion Sensors are critical for detecting invisible Void Wraiths

## Common Trap Mistakes

1. **Clumping traps together** — Enemies with AOE attacks can destroy multiple traps at once. Space them out.
2. **Ignoring line of sight** — Traps behind walls are useless. Make sure the trigger path is clear.
3. **Using Flame Emitters in Biome 3** — As noted above, they are severely nerfed in Magma Core. Switch to electric or kinetic traps.
4. **Forgetting to reclaim** — Always salvage triggered traps before extracting. The resources add up over multiple runs.
5. **Over-relying on traps for bosses** — Bosses resist trap damage. Save your traps for crowd control and focus on weapon damage for boss fights.
