## MineGeon: Renegades Walkthrough — A Full Run From Descend to Extraction

"minegeon renegades walkthrough" is a top search — and the honest answer is that MineGeon: Renegades has no fixed walkthrough because **every run is procedurally generated**. What *can* be walked through is the run structure: the stages every expedition passes through, what happens at each stage, and what the decisions are. That's this guide.

> **Accuracy note:** specific room layouts, named biomes, and enemy placements change every run and are not published. The stage structure below is built on confirmed systems: procedural minegeons, six hand-crafted biomes, monolith path votes, one boss per biome, and the extraction rule.

## Stage 0 — Ship Prep

Every run starts on the **Galactic Express**, your ship and base between expeditions:

- **Bank spent first.** Unspent minerals from last run earn nothing — invest them in permanent upgrades before descending.
- **Pick your Renegade.** Marshall (Tank), Trevor (Gunslinger), or Ari (Engineer) — each with unique stats and an ultimate (see the [Classes Guide](/guides/classes-guide)).
- **Loadout matters.** Gadgets (grenades, energy shields, combat drones, and 150+ more) should match the run's goal — see the [Gadgets Guide](/guides/gadgets-guide).

## Stage 1 — Descent

Launch an expedition — solo or with up to two friends in online co-op — and enter a **procedurally generated "minegeon."** The layout, mineral placement, and hazards change every run. Treat each descent as a new puzzle: there are no memorized routes in this game.

**Confirmed structure:** six hand-crafted biomes, each explored through generated minegeons, each ending in a boss (see the [Biomes Guide](/guides/biomes-guide)). A full descent is a substantial time investment, and the only thing that matters at the end of it is what you manage to extract — there is no mid-run save for the minerals you are carrying.

## Stage 2 — Mining

PAIMO is filled with valuable minerals, and **the deeper you go, the more valuable they become** — along with the danger.

- **Mine what you find**, and watch for richer veins in harder-to-reach areas.
- **Don't stand still while mining.** You're a target the moment you swing your tool — clear the room first, then mine from chokepoints.
- The full mineral system is in the [Mining Guide](/guides/mining-guide).

## Stage 3 — Combat

Enemies come in two families: **swarms of hostile creatures** and the **Scavengers**, a faction determined to stop your descent. This is a **bullet-hell game** — dodging matters more than tanking:

- Keep moving and read projectile patterns.
- A role for every fight: someone holds attention (Marshall), someone deals damage (Trevor), someone supports (Ari).
- Enemy details are in the [Enemy Guide](/guides/enemy-guide).

## Stage 4 — Monolith Path Votes

Deep in PAIMO you'll find **ancient monoliths**: the team votes on which path to descend next, and the vote decides which hazards you meet next. Solo players simply pick their own path.

- Route choice is a big part of run planning — it's the game's substitute for fixed level layouts.
- Factor the monolith choice into your loadout: if you vote toward a hazard-heavy path, bring shields; toward a mineral-rich path, bring mining safety.

## Stage 5 — The Biome Boss

**Every biome ends in a boss battle** against a formidable ancient entity — the gatekeeper between you and the next depth (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)).

**The critical decision: go in or bank first.**

- A boss attempt with a full haul is how you lose everything. **Extract once, then return for the boss on a fresh run** — the boss is repeatable, your minerals aren't.
- Bosses are a gear check as much as a skill check — spend your permanent-upgrade minerals before the attempt.
- Save your ultimate for the arena; in co-op, assign roles before the pull.

## Stage 6 — Extraction (The Most Important Stage)

The game's core rule, announced plainly by the developer: **if you die, the minerals you are carrying are lost for good.** Everything you mined is at risk until it reaches the ship.

- **You can always retreat** — the choice of when to leave is yours, every run.
- There is **no extraction timer and no collapse mechanic** — the only clock is your judgment.
- Set your leave trigger *before* you descend: a wound trigger, a prize trigger, a time trigger. Mid-run negotiation with yourself is how hauls die.
- Full extraction strategy lives in the [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide).

## Stage 7 — Ship Return

Back on the Galactic Express:

1. **Bank your minerals** into permanent upgrades — ship systems and per-Renegade attributes.
2. **Leveling unlocks** gadgets, weapons, and ultimates across runs (this is a roguelite — progress persists).
3. **Customize the ship** (200+ decorations) and visit friends' ships online.

The compounding loop: each successful extraction funds upgrades that make the next run safer — which is the whole game (see the [Progression Guide](/guides/progression-guide)).
