## What "Gems" Mean in MineGeon

> **Accuracy note:** specific ore and gem names had not been published by early August 2026 — guides that name "Lifesteal Gems" or list "gem tables" are inventing them. What is confirmed: PAIMO is filled with rare, valuable minerals, **deeper descents yield more valuable minerals**, and minerals bank only when extracted.

So "gem farming" in MineGeon: Renegades means one thing: **hunting the high-value minerals that spawn at depth** — and getting them out. The gem-equivalent items exist; the names do not (yet). This guide gives you the farming system that works no matter what the final catalog is called.

## The Depth Math of Gems

| Depth | Mineral Value | Risk | Gem Opportunity |
|-------|---------------|------|-----------------|
| Starter depths | Low | Very Low | None — common minerals only |
| Mid depths | Medium | Medium | First "gem-tier" veins appear |
| Deep biomes | High | High | The best gem spawns — and the worst death penalties |
| Biome depths + boss | Highest | Very High | Rare veins, legendary-tier minerals |

**The confirmed rule:** the deeper you go, the greater the rewards — and the risks. A gem find is a *depth reward*, which means every gem run is a negotiation with the risk curve.

## The Gem Farming Mindset

### 1. A Gem Find Is an Extract Trigger

The single most important rule in gem farming: **the moment a rare mineral is in your bag, the run's goal changes.** The [Resource Farming Guide](/guides/resource-farming-guide) calls it the Break Point Rule — set your trigger *before* you descend:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Rare mineral found | Extract — the run is won |
| Depth budget reached | Extract — deeper is a new bet |
| Health below 30% | Extract — gems die with you |
| Elite/boss spawn nearby | Extract — no fight is worth the bag |

A gem in the bag is not a reason to push further; it is a reason to leave. Greed streaks — "one more vein, one more room" — are how gem runs end as zero-mineral runs.

### 2. The Depth Ladder for Gems

| Ladder Step | Move |
|-------------|------|
| 1 | Farm the depth you clear at 100% success |
| 2 | When it is trivial, step one depth down — not two |
| 3 | Farm the new depth until it is trivial again |
| 4 | Repeat — the ladder, not the leap |

The ladder exists because gem tiers are depth-gated: rushing two depths deep into unfamiliar hazards is the classic "died at the new biome, lost everything" pattern. One depth at a time keeps your gem access growing while your death rate stays flat.

### 3. The Two-Pass Run (Compounding Gems)

1. **Pass one:** descend to gem depth, mine what is safe, extract early with the modest haul.
2. **Pass two:** bank the haul, buy permanent upgrades, return deeper with better gear.

Each successful pass funds the next — the compounding loop is how gem farming becomes gem *income* instead of gem gambling.

## Gear and Gadgets for Gem Runs

| Loadout Slot | Best Choice | Why |
|--------------|-------------|-----|
| Offensive gadget | Grenades | Break swarms at the vein before they surround you |
| Defensive gadget | Energy shield | The extraction channel under fire — where gem runs die |
| Autonomous gadget | Combat drone | Scout deep rooms before you commit the bag |
| Weapons | Your most comfortable clear set | Depth is the risk; gear should remove the *other* risks |

**The rule:** on a gem run, the loadout serves the bag, not the build. Offensive flexibility is nice; extraction insurance is mandatory.

## Co-op Gem Runs

Teams change the gem math in your favor:

- **Split roles, not players** — one mines the vein while one guards the approach, then swap.
- **The escape buddy rule** — the team that extracts together banks together; never leave a gem carrier behind.
- **Multi-bag extraction** — a trio can fill three bags at gem depth and extract as a convoy, splitting the risk per player.
- **Voice-call the map** — call out rich veins and hazard rooms; shared navigation is shared safety.

## Gem Farm Checklist

- [ ] Depth budget and extract triggers set *before* the run
- [ ] Break Point Rule agreed — rare find = extract
- [ ] Defensive gadget equipped (shield or drone)
- [ ] Depth ladder planned — one step, not a leap
- [ ] Gems banked before any upgrade spending — unspent minerals earn nothing
