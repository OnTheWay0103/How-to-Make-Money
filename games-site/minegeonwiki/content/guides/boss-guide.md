## Boss Overview

Every one of PAIMO's six biomes ends in a **boss battle**. The developer describes these fights as tests against formidable, biome-ending entities — "towering" enemies that demand preparation, not just reflexes.

> **Accuracy note:** boss-per-biome is confirmed, but individual boss names, attack patterns, health numbers, and drops had not been published by early August 2026. This guide deliberately avoids invented boss names ("Crystal Crawler Queen," "Sporemother," etc. appear in no official material). The general strategy below is built on confirmed systems: weapons, gadgets, ultimate abilities, co-op, and the extraction rule.

## What Boss Fights Share (Confirmed)

- **One boss per biome.** The biome ends in the boss — it is the gatekeeper between you and the next depth.
- **Bosses are "ancient entities."** They are tied to PAIMO's buried secrets, which is also where lore fragments lead.
- **Preparation is expected.** The game's risk/reward structure means walking into a boss with a big haul is a decision, not an accident.
- **Your full kit applies.** Weapons, gadgets, ultimates, co-op coordination — everything the run gave you comes into play.

## Before the Boss: Preparation Checklist

- [ ] **Bank your minerals first.** A boss attempt with a full haul is how you lose everything. Extract once, then go back for the boss with a fresh run. The boss is repeatable — your minerals are not.
- [ ] **Upgrade your loadout.** Spend your permanent-upgrade minerals before the attempt. Boss fights are gear checks as much as skill checks.
- [ ] **Equip the best weapon and gadgets the run offers.** Legendary weapons and Ancient Ones weapons shine here.
- [ ] **Keep your ultimate ready.** Ultimates are the biggest single-use power in the game — spend them in the arena, not in the hall outside.
- [ ] **In co-op: assign roles.** Who holds attention (usually Marshall), who deals damage (usually Trevor), who supports (usually Ari).

## General Boss Strategy

### Phase 1: Learn, don't commit
The first time you reach a boss, treat it as reconnaissance. Learn its attack rhythm before you commit to dealing damage. In a bullet-hell game, surviving a pattern is the precondition for damaging through it.

### Phase 2: Use your toolkit in layers
1. **Weapon damage** in safe windows
2. **Gadgets** to create windows — energy shields to survive bursts, grenades for pressured moments, combat drones for passive damage while you focus on dodging
3. **Ultimate** for the phase change — most boss fights escalate as the boss weakens, and that is the moment ultimates swing the fight

### Phase 3: Decide the outcome
- **Winning:** finish it, then extract — the post-boss biome is usually richer and scarier.
- **Losing:** retreat. Dying in the arena costs you your carried minerals. A failed boss attempt with an intact haul is just a run you can repeat.

## Co-op Boss Tips

- **Marshall leads.** The Tank draws the boss's attention and gives Trevor and Ari safe damage windows.
- **Trevor focuses.** Damage on the boss whenever the window is safe; don't chase adds unless they threaten the team.
- **Ari supports.** Cover teammates, use gadgets to control the arena, and be ready to help whoever is pressured.
- **Call the phase change.** When the boss speeds up or changes pattern, the whole team should switch from damage to survival for a beat.
- **One escape voice.** Nominate who calls the retreat — one clear "we're leaving" beats three panicked opinions.

## After the Boss

- **Extract.** The run's biggest prize is now on your back — don't gamble it on the next room.
- **Expect the next biome to escalate.** Boss clears open the path deeper, where hazards and rewards both grow.
- **Watch for drops and lore.** Bosses are the natural place for legendary-tier drops and story payoffs — community documentation of specific drops was still in progress as of early August 2026.

## Common Boss Mistakes

1. **Fighting the boss with a full haul.** Lose the fight, lose everything. Bank first.
2. **Wasting the ultimate early.** The boss's final phase is when you need it most.
3. **Ignoring the arena.** Most boss arenas have geometry that helps — walls to break line of sight, chokepoints, etc.
4. **Solo-charging in co-op.** Uncoordinated teams feed the boss one easy kill at a time. Wait for the group.
5. **Believing fabricated boss lists.** If a guide names specific bosses with detailed patterns and it isn't from a post-launch community document, it's invented — check the source.
