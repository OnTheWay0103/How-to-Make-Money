## MineGeon: Renegades Demo Overview

The free demo of MineGeon: Renegades gives you a taste of the full mining roguelite experience before committing to a purchase. This guide covers everything about the demo version.

## How to Download the Demo

1. Open **Steam** and search for "MineGeon: Renegades"
2. Click on the game page
3. Look for the **"Download Demo"** button on the right sidebar
4. The demo is approximately 2-3 GB

## What's Included in the Demo

### Available Content
- **2 Biomes**: Shallow Mines and Fungal Caverns (out of 6 in the full game)
- **All 3 Classes**: Tank, DPS Gunslinger, and Engineer — all playable
- **2 Bosses**: Crystal Crawler Queen and Sporemother
- **Basic Base Building**: Workbench, Furnace, and Storage Vault
- **Crafting**: Up to Iron-tier recipes
- **Co-op**: Full 1-3 player co-op supported

### What's NOT in the Demo
- Biomes 3-6 (Magma Chambers, Crystal Mines, Abandoned Mineshaft, Deep Core)
- Advanced crafting tiers (Silver, Gold, Crystal, Mythril)
- Trophy Room and Garden base stations
- All decorations beyond basic furniture
- Late-game bosses (Magma Construct, Gem Guardian, Drill Dozer, Core Entity)

## Demo vs Full Game

| Feature | Demo | Full Game |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Biomes | 2 | 6 |
| Bosses | 2 | 12+ |
| Playtime | ~3-5 hours | 30-60+ hours |
| Crafting Tiers | Iron only | Iron → Mythril |
| Base Stations | 3 | 7 |
| Decorations | ~20 | 200+ |
| Co-op | ✅ | ✅ |

## Does Demo Progress Carry Over?

Yes! All progress from the demo carries over to the full game:
- Resources you've extracted
- Base upgrades you've built
- Crafted gear in your storage
- Class levels and unlocks

This means you can start grinding resources in the demo and hit the ground running when you buy the full game.

## Demo Tips

1. **Try all 3 classes** — The demo is the perfect opportunity to find your main
2. **Stockpile Iron and Copper** — You'll need hundreds for early full-game crafting
3. **Master the extraction loop** — Learn when to push deeper vs when to extract
4. **Play co-op** — The demo supports multiplayer, so invite friends
5. **Experiment with base layouts** — Figure out your preferred station arrangement
