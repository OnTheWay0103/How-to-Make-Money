## MineGeon: Renegades Demo Overview

The free demo of MineGeon: Renegades (released as **Demo 2.0**) gives you a real slice of the full co-op mining roguelite before you buy. It was available on Steam before and after launch, and the demo was well received on Steam — rated **"Very Positive"** at the time of writing, notably better than the full game's launch reception, which is worth knowing before you judge the game by reviews alone (check the store page for current ratings).

## How to Download the Demo

1. Open **Steam** and search for "MineGeon: Renegades"
2. Open the game's store page
3. Look for the demo button on the page (the demo is a separate Steam app — "MineGeon: Renegades Demo")
4. Click **Download Demo** — the demo is small; exact size was not published, ignore specific GB figures from other sites

## What's Included in the Demo (Confirmed)

| Content | Demo |
|---------|------|
| **Biomes** | The first of the six biomes |
| **Classes** | All 3 — Marshall (Tank), Trevor (Gunslinger), Ari (Engineer) |
| **Story** | The first chapter of the story |
| **Progression** | Base and character upgrades |
| **Enhancements** | Biome-transforming enhancements that change the biome on future runs |
| **Co-op** | Solo or with up to two friends |
| **Ship** | The Galactic Express and its customization |

### What's NOT in the Demo
- The other five biomes
- The biome bosses beyond the first biome's end-boss
- The deepest minerals and endgame gear
- Most of the 150+ gadget catalog (the demo unlocks a subset)

## Does Demo Progress Carry Over?

**Not officially confirmed.** The studio has not published a statement on demo-to-full-game save transfer. Common sense: the demo's purpose is to preview the game and mechanics, so **do not assume progress carries over** — treat the demo as training, and the full game as the real run. We'll update this guide if the studio confirms transfer.

## Demo Tips

1. **Try all 3 Renegades.** The demo is the perfect place to find your main before the full game.
2. **Learn the extraction rule.** Die with minerals in the demo enough times and the lesson sticks: extract early, extract often.
3. **Practice bullet-hell fundamentals.** The demo's first biome is the gentlest the game gets — learn to dodge here.
4. **Test co-op.** Invite a friend and learn the team rhythm — the full game's boss fights demand it.
5. **Judge the full game fairly.** The demo's Steam rating was stronger than the full game's launch rating. Read both before deciding — and remember the full game adds five biomes and most of the content.

## After the Demo

If the demo hooked you, the full game continues the story past the first chapter, opens the remaining five biomes, and turns the gadget catalog from a subset to 150+. Start with our [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) to carry your demo lessons into the full game.
