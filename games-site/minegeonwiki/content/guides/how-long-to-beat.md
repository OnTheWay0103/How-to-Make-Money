# How Long to Beat MineGeon: Renegades — Content & Playtime Guide

"How long to beat MineGeon: Renegades?" is one of the most-searched questions for the game — and the honest answer is: **no official figures exist, and any site quoting exact hour counts is guessing.** What we can do is give you a real content inventory, what the demo shows about pacing, and how to estimate your own playtime based on how you play.

## The Honest Answer Up Front

- **Official playtime figures:** none published by the developer
- **Common claims online ("30-60+ hours," "20-45 minute runs"):** fabricated — no official basis
- **The game's structure:** run-based roguelite — "beating" it means clearing all six biome bosses, and completion goes well beyond that

What follows is a grounded estimate built from the confirmed content list, not invented numbers.

That's a complete roguelite package: six biomes of content with procedural variety, a 150-item build catalog to discover, and a 49-achievement completionist layer.

## Run Structure and Pacing

### How a run works
Each expedition is a self-contained descent: descend, mine, fight, extract (or die). Runs end when you extract or die, and the length of a run is a player decision — greedy players run longer and die more, disciplined players run shorter and bank more.

### What the demo tells us
The demo (Demo 2.0) covers the **first of six biomes** and the first story chapter. Players report the demo is enough to learn the full loop — class selection, mining, combat, extraction, and base/ship upgrades. If you want a concrete feel for pacing, the demo is the most honest source available: play it and scale up by six for the biome count, then add the gadget catalog and achievement grind on top.

### What affects your playtime
- **Skill and genre familiarity.** Bullet-hell veterans will clear biomes faster than players learning the genre
- **Extraction discipline.** Disciplined players bank more per hour — ironically, the "slower" playstyle is the faster progression path
- **Co-op or solo.** Teams clear deeper content faster, though coordination adds its own time
- **Goal type.** Farming runs, boss runs, lore runs, and achievement runs are different games

## How Long to "Beat" It

### The story route
The game has a story (the Renegades' descent into PAIMO, the Scavengers, the buried secrets). "Beating" it means descending through all six biomes and clearing the biome bosses. This is the main content path — expect it to take a meaningful number of sessions, with the pace set by how fast you master the extraction loop.

### The completionist route
Beyond the story:
- **All 49 achievements** — includes co-op achievements, likely collection milestones, and maxed progression tracks
- **Full gadget catalog** — 150+ gadgets to unlock through leveling across runs
- **Maxed permanent upgrades** — ship and Renegade attribute tracks
- **Ship completion** — 200+ decorations

This is the long game. 100% completion in a 49-achievement roguelite with this much catalog is a genuine time investment — treat any specific total-hour claim as a guess.
