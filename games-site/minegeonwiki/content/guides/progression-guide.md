## Progression Overview

MineGeon: Renegades progression follows a clear arc: survive your early runs, build permanent upgrades, push through the biomes one boss at a time, and eventually conquer the deepest depths of PAIMO. Because the game is a roguelite, your power accumulates **between** runs — every extraction makes the next run easier.

> **Accuracy note:** hour-based milestones in other guides are invented — the developer has published no completion-time figures. This roadmap is organized by *achievements* (things you can verify) rather than clock time.

## Stage 1: First Runs

Your goal is simple: **extract successfully**. Not deeply, not greedily — successfully.

### Priority Checklist
- [ ] Complete your first extraction (any minerals count)
- [ ] Learn the death rule by heart: carried minerals are lost on death
- [ ] Try all three Renegades at least once (Marshall, Trevor, Ari)
- [ ] Bank minerals into ship upgrades
- [ ] Level enough to unlock your first gadgets

### Class Recommendation
Start with **Marshall** (the Tank). Resilient stats make the learning loop forgiving; Trevor and Ari reward game knowledge you won't have yet.

## Stage 2: Permanent Progression

Now that you can extract reliably, shift to the compounding layer.

### Priority Checklist
- [ ] Ship upgrades — the foundation of all future runs
- [ ] Attribute upgrades for your main Renegade
- [ ] Learn one class's role deeply (it will be your co-op main later)
- [ ] Build a gadget habit — grenades, shields, and drones should be part of every loadout

### Key Milestone
**Defeat the first biome boss.** This is the game's first real gate: it proves your build, your dodging, and your preparation are all at the level the depths demand. Bank your minerals *before* the attempt — the boss is repeatable, your haul isn't.

## Stage 3: Deep Runs

The depths between bosses are where the game opens up.

### Priority Checklist
- [ ] Push past the first biome into the second, third, and beyond
- [ ] Diversify: invest in a second Renegade for co-op flexibility
- [ ] Hunt legendary weapons and weapons of the Ancient Ones
- [ ] Use monolith route votes to steer runs toward your goals
- [ ] Start co-op runs — teams change the risk math completely

### Tips for This Stage
- **The deeper the biome, the richer the minerals** — and the harsher the hazards
- **Extraction discipline scales with depth:** the deeper you are, the more painful a death
- **Let the run shape the build.** Roguelite randomness means the best builds adapt to what appears, not to a pre-written plan

## Stage 4: Late Game

You have seen most of PAIMO. Now it's about optimization.

### Priority Checklist
- [ ] Clear all six biome bosses
- [ ] Max the permanent upgrade tracks (ship + your main Renegade's attributes)
- [ ] Fill out your gadget and weapon catalogs
- [ ] Hunt the lore: PAIMO's buried secrets are the story payoff

### The Final Biomes
The deepest biomes combine every system: the richest minerals, the Scavengers at their strongest, the hardest environmental hazards, and the lore fragments that explain what happened on PAIMO. Bring your best loadout, a clear head, and a plan for extraction.

## Stage 5: Endgame

After the final biome, the game opens up for completionists.

### Post-Game Goals
- **49 Steam achievements** — the official total on the store page (see our Achievements Guide)
- **Max all three Renegades** — experience each class fully
- **Complete your ship** — 200+ decorations, themed sets, a ship worth visiting
- **Speedrunning** — optimized routes for fast deep runs
- **Co-op mastery** — help new players, run challenge compositions

### Efficiency Tips for Endgame
- **Bank before bosses, always.** The rule never stops applying.
- **Specialize loadouts per goal.** A farming run, a boss run, and a lore run want different gear.
- **Watch the official Discord and patch notes.** The meta and the game will both evolve post-launch.
