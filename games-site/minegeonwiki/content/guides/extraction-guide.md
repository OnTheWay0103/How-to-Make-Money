# Extraction Guide — How to Extract & Keep Your Minerals Safe

If there is one skill that separates thriving MineGeon: Renegades players from everyone else, it is **knowing when to leave**. The game's core rule — announced plainly by the developer — is the most important sentence in the game:

> **If you die, the minerals you are carrying are lost for good.**

Everything about extraction strategy flows from that rule. This guide covers how extraction works, how to think about the risk/reward decision, and how to extract safely solo and in co-op.

## How Extraction Works (Confirmed)

The developer describes the loop directly:

- **PAIMO is filled with valuable minerals.** The deeper you go, the more valuable they become — and the more dangerous it gets
- **Minerals must reach the ship to count.** Only what you extract becomes permanent progression
- **Death is total loss.** Die with a haul and the haul is gone. Not reduced — gone
- **You can always retreat.** The choice of when to leave is yours, every run

There is no extraction timer, no collapse mechanic, and no "stability meter" — those appear in fabricated guides. The only clock is your own judgment.

## The Risk/Reward Decision

Every run is a series of decisions between two competing goals:

| Push deeper | Or extract early |
|-------------|------------------|
| Richer minerals | Bank what you have |
| Faster progression | Zero risk of losing it |
| New biomes, new gear | Steady compounding |

### When to push
- You're playing cleanly — full health, consumables intact, patterns read
- The next depth is a known quantity (you've seen it before)
- Your team is coordinated and calm

### When to extract
- You have a meaningful haul — the run is already a success
- You're wounded, low on consumables, or your gadgets are spent
- You've hit a rare find — the prize is on you, protect it
- You've been greedy once already. The second greed is how hauls die

The golden rule: **a successful extraction with half the potential loot beats a death with all of it.** Every run that banks progress is a good run, no matter how shallow.

## The Extraction Triggers

Set your leave trigger **before you descend**, not mid-run. Good triggers:

1. **The wound trigger.** "I extract when I'm below half health." Non-negotiable.
2. **The prize trigger.** "I extract when I find the rare vein / clear the boss." The run's purpose is complete — leave.
3. **The time trigger.** "I extract after X rooms/minutes." Discipline beats enthusiasm on long runs.
4. **The instinct trigger.** When the room feels wrong — too many enemies, unknown pattern — trust it. The instinct trigger is the cheapest insurance in the game.

Write your trigger down if you have to. Mid-run negotiation with yourself is how minerals die.

## Extraction Fundamentals

### 1. Mine with an exit plan
Before you commit to a node:
- **Check the room.** Enemies love ambushing busy miners
- **Know the way up.** Which direction is the exit? In a panic, hesitation is fatal
- **Mine from chokepoints.** Positions where enemies can only approach from one side

### 2. Clear the room before extracting
The extraction moment is the most vulnerable moment of the run. Clear the immediate area first — an extraction while fighting is a gamble, not a plan.

### 3. Gadgets are extraction insurance
- **Grenades:** break the pursuing swarm at the door
- **Energy shields:** survive the extraction burst
- **Combat drones:** hold the rear while you run

The players who extract with full hauls are the ones with a gadget left for the exit.

### 4. Extract early, not late
The math is brutal and simple: a run that extracts at 60% of its potential loot is worth 60% more than a run that dies at 100%. The difference between good players and great players is rarely the loot they find — it's the loot they keep.

## Co-op Extraction

Co-op changes extraction from a solo decision into a team operation.

### The carry rule
If one player has a big haul, the team's job is protecting them. The best player to carry is whoever is healthiest — sometimes that's a swap of the mineral load before extraction.

### The team trigger
Agree on the leave trigger as a team before descending: "we extract when anyone is wounded" or "we extract after the boss." Three individual triggers are three disagreements waiting to happen. One team trigger wins.

### The last-stand rule
If the run goes wrong, the rule is simple: **the team that extracts together, banks together.** Don't leave a teammate to die for a full inventory — unless they insist, and the haul justifies it.

### Protect the reviver
If someone goes down, the reviver is the most exposed player on the map. Shields and grenades make revives possible. And never all-revive at once — one person covers, one revives.

## Extraction by Class

| Renegade | Extraction Style |
|----------|------------------|
| Marshall (Tank) | The natural carrier — most likely to survive the run-out |
| Trevor (Gunslinger) | The quick extraction — kites the pursuit while teammates bank |
| Ari (Engineer) | The flexible extractor — adapts to whatever the exit looks like |

In co-op, the healthiest character carries the biggest haul. That's often Marshall — and it's a good reason for teams to route the valuable minerals to their anchor before extraction.

## Common Extraction Mistakes

1. **The last vein.** "One more node" is the most expensive sentence in the game
2. **Extracting while fighting.** Clear the exit first, always
3. **No leave trigger.** Decide before descending, not mid-run
4. **Carrying everything in one player.** In co-op, spreading the haul across two players halves the risk of losing it
5. **Panic routes.** Players who scout the way down extract faster — memorize the way back as you go
6. **Greed streaks.** The third deep push is usually the death. Bank, then go again

## The Extraction Ritual

Great extraction players run the same checklist every time:

- [ ] Leave trigger set before descent
- [ ] Exit direction known at all times
- [ ] Rooms cleared before mining
- [ ] Gadgets reserved for the exit
- [ ] Haul spread across the team (co-op)
- [ ] Trigger honored — no negotiation

The ritual is boring. That's the point. The game's drama is the depths; the game's profit is the routine.

## Why Extraction Is the Real Meta

Every system in MineGeon: Renegades — the mineral economy, permanent upgrades, the death penalty — is built to make extraction the true skill ceiling. Depth is easy to find. The game gives you richer and richer veins the deeper you go. What it never gives you is a safe haul. That's yours to earn, one disciplined run at a time.

Master extraction and everything else gets easier: more minerals, more upgrades, deeper runs, faster progression. It is the single highest-leverage skill in the game.
