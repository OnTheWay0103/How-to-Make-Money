# Anomaly President Campaign Bus Guide — Your Mobile Base

Every candidate needs a headquarters. In Anomaly President yours is a **campaign bus** — and it is not cosmetic. The
Steam store page describes it as a movable base you design "from scratch, add unique sections, and create a personalized
mobile base" [Official]. Developer posts go further and call the bus **"the backbone of your journey"**: unlocked rooms
affect your abilities and playstyle each run [Road to EA #2, Community].

If the campaign layer is the game's meta-progression engine, the bus is the chassis that engine is bolted to. This guide
collects what is known about bus rooms, what the developer is adding, and how to think about build-order.

## What the Bus Is For

- **A hub between battles.** Virtual Grip describes improving your headquarters as the connecting activity between
  night runs [Community].
- **A playstyle switch.** Because bus rooms change how a run plays, the same candidate can feel completely different
  depending on which sections are installed.
- **A Fluffy workplace.** Bus modules are where assigned Fluffies work — campaign, equipment, or combat departments
  (see the [Fluffies guide](/guides/fluffy-guide)).
- **Your permanent base.** Improvements to the bus survive death. A night run resets its own build, but campaign
  upgrades never reset [Community] — so every room you add is a permanent step forward, not a run-specific gamble.
  This is why the bus, not any single run, is where your long-term power actually lives.

### How the Bus Fits the Game's Pillars

The developer describes Anomaly President around three pillars — action, base building, and exploration [Official]. The
bus is the physical home of the **base-building** pillar: you fight at night (action) to earn resources, travel the
overworld (exploration) to unlock new places, and return to the bus to convert what you earned into permanent rooms,
gear, and Fluffy workplaces.

## Rooms Confirmed in Early Access

Reviewer hands-on coverage of the EA build names four room types [GameBrief, Community]:

| Room | What it does (as reported) |
|------|----------------------------|
| **Bedroom** | Personal space / rest area on the bus. |
| **TV studio** | Media appearances — your image-management tool. |
| **Upgrade room** | "Blacksmith-style" upgrade station for your gear. |
| **Canvassing room** | Runs vote-focused campaign work. |

These four map cleanly onto the campaign loops described in the [campaign management guide](/guides/campaign-management):
media (TV studio), votes (canvassing), and gear (upgrade room). Read together, they cover most of the day-side economy:
the TV studio feeds your image and public appearances, the canvassing room produces votes, and the upgrade room is where
you convert materials into the permanent gear that carries into night runs. The bedroom is the anchor — the space you
start from and return to each day.

## Rooms in Development (Road to EA #2)

The developer's July 1 "Road to Early Access #2" post announced three new rooms that were work-in-progress before launch
[Community]:

- **Dance Room** — unlocks a **Dancing minigame** to win over voters.
- **Gift Room** — unlocks a **gift-giving minigame**.
- **Marketing Room** — unlocks new **campaign map areas** that generate more revenue.

**Whether all three have shipped into the Early Access build by September 2026 is [Unconfirmed].** Treat them as roadmap
content until verified in-game; the [patch notes & roadmap guide](/guides/patch-notes-roadmap-guide) tracks their status.

## Room Choice Is Run Strategy

Because the bus is a "personalized mobile base" [Official] whose rooms shape each run, think about room selection the
way you would think about a loadout:

- **Stuck on a boss?** Prioritize the upgrade room so your gear keeps pace with enemy health. Boss structure is covered
  in the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide).
- **Starved for resources?** Prioritize revenue and vote rooms (canvassing, and later Marketing) so you can buy more.
- **Trying a new build?** Some rooms favour particular playstyles — switching sections is cheaper than re-rolling your
  weapon collection. Weapon builds are in the [weapons guide](/guides/weapons-guide).

## Build-Order Advice

Early Access is still being balanced, so treat this as a starting heuristic, not a law:

1. **One income room first** (canvassing for votes, or the TV studio for image) — you need renewable daytime resources
   before you can afford anything else.
2. **Then the upgrade room** — gear upgrades are the most direct way to stop dying at night.
3. **Expand into the specialty rooms** as your Fluffy workforce grows — each new section wants staff, and understaffed
   rooms are wasted investment.

### A Typical Morning on the Bus

Once you have two or three rooms installed, a sustainable morning routine looks like this (editorial advice built on
the confirmed systems above):

1. **Check your Fluffies** first — welfare is tracked, and neglect is the fastest way to lose a team member (see the
   [Fluffies guide](/guides/fluffy-guide)).
2. **Do the income activity** for whichever room you are feeding — a TV-studio appearance or a canvassing session —
   before spending, so you know what you have to work with.
3. **Spend on one thing at a time.** Add or upgrade a single room per cycle rather than spreading thin across three
   half-finished sections.
4. **Assign staff to match your next target.** Heading into a boss district? Shift Fluffies toward the upgrade room;
   running low on votes? Put them on canvassing.
5. **Depart for a night run** knowing your bus is still generating value while you are away.

### Common Bus Mistakes

- **Adding rooms faster than you can staff them.** An unstaffed room is a sunk cost that produces nothing. Grow rooms
  in step with your Fluffy roster.
- **Hoarding resources.** Because bus improvements never reset, spending on rooms and gear is almost always better than
  banking currency for a run that death may end.
- **Ignoring the upgrade room before a boss.** When a district's boss is the wall, the most direct fix is usually gear
  — and gear upgrades live on the bus.

Exact costs, unlock order, and room tiers are [Unconfirmed]; the developer is still "refining and expanding everything"
during Early Access [Official].

## What Not to Assume

- **The bus is not a full city-builder.** It is a single mobile base you customize — "base building" is one of the
  game's three stated pillars [Official], but on the scale of a bus, not a settlement.
- **The room list will grow.** The developer has explicitly said more of the map and more content are coming [Official],
  and new Fluffies and bus rooms are in active development [Road to EA #2, Community].
