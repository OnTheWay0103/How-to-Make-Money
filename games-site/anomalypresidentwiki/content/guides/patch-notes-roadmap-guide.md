# Anomaly President Patch Notes & Roadmap Guide — What's Live in Early Access

Anomaly President entered **Steam Early Access on August 3, 2026**, and the developer has patched quickly since. This
guide is the running record of what is confirmed live, what the roadmap promises, and what is still unverified — useful
before you buy, and before you trust an older review.

## Early Access Status

The store page describes the EA build as **"currently fully playable from start to finish"**, but two things are
explicitly unfinished: the **story's ending** and **parts of the map** [Official]. The developer plans **at least one
year of Early Access** (possibly more), with the price rising as content ships [Official]. In short: you can play a full
loop now, but you are buying a game that will keep growing.

## What the Patches Tell You

Reading across the August patches, a few developer priorities are clear:

- **Boss polish is ongoing.** The "Dean" boss (see the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide)) was tuned twice in a week
  (v0.5.0.11, v0.5.0.16).
- **Finishers are being made more reliable.** v0.5.0.17's "boss ranged attacks cancel during finishers" is a direct buff
  to the stamina-break → finisher playstyle.
- **Base Building is a live target.** Multiple patches touch Base Build mode, room menus and bus UI — the campaign-bus
  system (see the [campaign bus guide](/guides/campaign-bus-guide)) is still being shaped.
- **Elemental balance is moving.** v0.5.0.18 buffed Fire (Ground Flame), which matters for the "Ice is overpowered"
  launch discussion in the [builds guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide).

## The Roadmap (Revealed August 6, 2026)

The developer published an **Early Access Roadmap** on Steam around **August 6** [Community], organised into three
windows. Confirmed buckets [Official roadmap, Community]:

**First week (mostly shipped already):**
- Damage reduction on ranged "snake" enemies and shielded enemies.
- Balance adjustments and minor bug fixes.
- Base Building QoL: visual improvements, and the ability to **move the room you are standing in**.

**First month:**
- Support Fluffies deal more damage (battle support buff).
- New bus room: **the Exchange** — trade upgrade currencies with each other for faster progression.
- New Fluffy character: **the Merchant** — lets you buy extra upgrades during a run.
- More Base Building visual improvements.

**September:**
- **New enemies** that show "the true scale of the Evil President's tyranny."
- **New weapons** "to make the revolution bloodier."

## What Is Still [Unconfirmed]

Early Access reporting is full of announced-but-unverified content. As of September 5, 2026, these remain **unconfirmed**
and should not be treated as live facts:

- **The Food Truck is confirmed** (v0.5.0.18, August 20) — but whether it works exactly as described and whether later
  patches changed it is something to verify in-game.
- **Dance, Gift and Marketing bus rooms** — announced pre-launch as work-in-progress; no patch note we found confirms all
  three have shipped. See the [campaign bus guide](/guides/campaign-bus-guide).
- **The Bigman boss** — announced as one of the president's strongest henchmen, animations were work-in-progress before
  launch; no patch note confirms he is live (see the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide)).
- **"The Viking" and "The Gentleman" enemies** — shown as working titles pre-launch; ship status unconfirmed (see the
  [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-guide)).
- **Any v0.5.0.19+ patch** released after August 20.

## Price and the EA Discount

The launch price was **$14.99 / €15.49 / £13.49** with regional pricing, discounted **20% to about $11.99** for the
first two weeks; the launch discount ended around **mid-August (about August 17)**, returning the game to its base price
[Community]. The developer has stated the price will **rise gradually** as content and features ship [Official]. For the
full price/platform breakdown, see the [price & platforms guide](/guides/price-platforms-guide).
