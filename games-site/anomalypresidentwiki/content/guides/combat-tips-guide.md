# Anomaly President Combat Tips — Win the Fights That Win the Campaign

Anomaly President's night loop is a **movement-first side-scrolling beat-'em-up**: reviewers describe the combat as
"weaving between attacks, repositioning, chaining abilities together" [Virtual Grip, Community]. But the single most
discussed combat topic in Early Access is the **parry** — a move critics say is both essential and too strict.

This guide collects the tips that survive the current build's balance, with the parry-timing controversy labelled
honestly.

## The #1 Debate: Parry Timing

Every serious combat review agrees on two things [GameBrief, CultureCombine, Respawning, Community]:

1. The parry is **central and satisfying** once it clicks — Respawning describes successful parries as "incredibly
   satisfying," with parries able to activate small companions that deal elemental damage or finish combos.
2. The **timing window is too tight** as of launch, and multiple reviewers say it "needs tuning."

What that means in practice:

- **If the parry feels unfair, it is not just you.** The window is widely criticised. Do not conclude the game's combat
  is broken; conclude the parry is a high-skill tool that needs practice.
- **Watch the colours, not the animation.** Blockable attacks flash **yellow**; unblockable attacks flash **red**
  [Respawning, Community]. Parry only yellow. For red, dodge.
- **Press parry late, not early.** The common failure is parrying at the wind-up instead of at the moment the attack
  lands. Err on the side of pressing as the strike is about to connect.

For the buttons and settings side of this, see the [controls & settings guide](/guides/controls-settings-guide).

## Positioning Beats Reflexes

Before you master the parry, you can win most fights with **movement**. Reviewers describe the character movement as
"slightly weighty, but tight and responsive" [CultureCombine, Community] — which rewards deliberate spacing:

- **Weave between attacks, then punish.** Do not stand in the crowd. Move past the enemy's swing, attack once or twice
  from the safe side, and reposition.
- **Use dodge for red, not panic.** Dodge is your answer to unblockable attacks; spamming it between every swing leaves
  you out of position. Move first, dodge only when a red telegraph demands it.
- **Do not fight in the middle of a clump.** Enemies stack up and readability collapses (see the
  [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-guide)). Pull enemies into a line, or use a reach/crowd weapon.

## Break Stamina, Then Finish

The most reliable damage in the game is the **finisher**. Enemies have health *and* stamina; break the stamina bar and
they become open to an instant finisher [Respawning, Community]. Combat-tip implications:

- **Parry is the fastest stamina-breaker.** A successful parry opens the enemy for a finisher window — this is why the
  swordmaster build leans on it (see the [builds guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide)).
- **Finishers interrupt bosses.** Patch v0.5.0.17 confirmed that **boss ranged attacks are cancelled during finisher
  animations** [Official, Community] — so landing a boss finisher is also a defensive move.
- **Picking the finisher target matters.** Against a crowd, finish the highest-threat enemy (a ranged or shielded one)
  rather than the nearest mob.

## Use the Elemental Advantage

Elemental damage is a real combat lever, not a stat-sheet decoration:

- **Ice is the reported strong element at launch** — reviewers describe it as "vastly overpowered" relative to Fire and
  Electric [CultureCombine, Community]. If you find freeze-style control, it solves crowds and buys parry windows.
- **Fire was just buffed.** Patch v0.5.0.18 increased the starting damage of the Ground Flame Anomaly Power [Official,
  Community]. Fire builds are getting stronger.
- **Elemental procs come from parries and powers too.** Because parries can trigger elemental companions and Anomaly
  Powers deal elemental damage, your "element build" is assembled from the parry + power layers, not just the weapon.
  See the [builds & strategy guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide).

## Fighting Specific Problems

- **Enemies that feel immune mid-air:** reviewers report a bug where enemies in the air "no-sell" hits [Respawning,
  Community]. If your attack connects visually but does nothing, it may be this bug — ground your offence and use
  finishers rather than aerial gambles.
- **Cluttered boss fights:** boss arenas get visually busy and hard to read [Respawning, Community]. When that happens,
  stop watching the whole screen and focus on the boss's telegraph colour plus audio cues.
- **Ranged enemies in the back:** the roadmap confirmed ranged "snake" enemies exist and were strong enough to be nerfed
  [Official, Community]. Close the distance on ranged threats first; do not let them shoot into a melee scrum.

## A Short Practice Drill

1. Run an early, easy district with **one weapon** (the sword/katana is the best teacher).
2. For the first few rooms, **do not attack** — only move, weave and dodge red telegraphs. Learn the spacing.
3. Then add parries on **yellow-only** attacks. Accept that you will eat hits while learning the window.
4. Finally, chain it together: weave → parry yellow → break stamina → finisher → reposition.

This "movement first, parry second" order mirrors what reviewers say about the game's skill curve [Virtual Grip,
Community] and is covered from the run-structure side in the [night combat guide](/guides/night-combat).
