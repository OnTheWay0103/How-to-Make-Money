# Anomaly President Price & Platforms Guide — What You Pay, Where You Play

Anomaly President is a **PC-first Early Access title**, and most of the "is it on PS5 / Switch / mobile?" questions have
the same answer: **not yet, and nothing has been announced.** This guide collects the confirmed price, platform, language
and requirement facts in one place, and labels the unconfirmed parts honestly.

## Platforms: PC (Windows) Only — For Now

As of September 5, 2026, Anomaly President is available **only on PC (Windows) via Steam** [Official]. The store page
lists Windows 10 as the minimum OS and **Single-player** as the only mode [Official].

- **PS5 / PS4 / Xbox:** no version has been announced. Searches and store coverage show no console release in progress
  **[Unconfirmed]** — treat any "coming to console" claim as speculation.
- **Nintendo Switch:** no version has been announced **[Unconfirmed]**.
- **Mobile (iOS/Android):** no version has been announced **[Unconfirmed]**.
- **Other PC stores (Epic, GOG):** no Epic or GOG listing has been announced **[Unconfirmed]**; the demo and EA game are
  Steam-only.

The frequent Turkish-market search for a mobile version reflects demand, not supply — there is no official mobile build
to download.

## Price: $14.99 Early Access, With a History

The confirmed price picture [Community store-tracking + reviews]:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| **Base price (EA)** | About **$14.99 USD / €15.49 / £13.49** |
| **Launch discount** | **20% off ≈ $11.99** for roughly the first two weeks (to about August 17) |
| **Regional pricing** | Yes — e.g. ¥48 (China), ₹719 (India), R$ 53.49 (Brazil), S$16.49 (Singapore) |
| **Price direction** | The developer plans **gradual increases** as content and features ship [Official] |

Practical takeaway: the cheapest the game has been is the ~$11.99 launch window, and the price is more likely to rise
than fall as Early Access continues. There is also a **free demo** (separate Steam listing, App 4254970) if you want to
test before paying — see the [FAQ guide](/guides/faq-guide).

## Steam Deck: Verified Reports, With a Caveat

Steam Deck is the most common "second platform" question, and the answer is genuinely mixed:

- **Mostly positive.** Multiple reviewers describe the game as **Steam Deck Verified** and running like a native console
  experience — N-Gamz's French Deck test reports excellent controller support and only occasional slowdowns in dense
  crowd phases, and Behind Gaming calls Deck performance "extremely pleasant" [Community].
- **One strong counterpoint.** Steam Deck HQ's August 18, 2026 review states the game "surprisingly isn't stable on
  Steam Deck" [Community]. This directly contradicts the more positive outlets.

The honest read: **expect the game to run and control well on Deck, but test your own unit against the current patch** —
Deck stability has been patch-dependent in Early Access, and an August 18 report may already be outdated. The
[controls & settings guide](/guides/controls-settings-guide) has a Steam Deck setup section.

## Languages: 12 With Full Audio and Subtitles

The store page lists **12 languages with full audio and subtitles** [Official]: English, French, German, Spanish (Spain),
Spanish (Latin America), Portuguese-Brazil, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, Japanese, and
Korean. Notably, the developer is Turkish, and the Turkish-language support (and Turkish player community) is a strong
part of the game's early reception.

## PC System Requirements

The **minimum** requirements from the Steam store page [Official]:

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| **OS** | Windows 10 |
| **Processor** | Intel Core i5-3470 / AMD FX-6300 |
| **Memory** | 4 GB RAM |
| **Graphics** | GeForce GTX 660 / Radeon HD 7870 (2 GB VRAM, Shader Model 5.0) |
| **Storage** | 1 GB available space |
| **Input** | Keyboard and mouse |

**Recommended requirements have not been published** [Official] — the store page lists minimums only. The modest minimum
explains why the game runs on Steam Deck and low-end laptops; the pixel-art presentation is not demanding. Note there is
**no difficulty setting** in the current build (see the [controls & settings guide](/guides/controls-settings-guide)).

## Content Rating and Mature Content

Anomaly President is **cartoon-violent political satire**, and it carries a mature rating. Steam's content descriptors
include **Frequent Violence or Gore** and **General Mature Content** [Official]; European rating is **PEGI 18**. There is
no sexual content listed. This matters if you plan to buy for a younger player, and it is relevant to the site's ad
compliance review.

## Should the Platform Status Affect Your Purchase?

If you own only a console or a Mac, **the current answer is no version for you yet** — wait and watch official channels
rather than third-party "console port" rumours. If you have a Windows PC or a Steam Deck, the game is available now, and
the launch-window discount period has already ended (the base price applies). Weigh the Early Access trade-offs
(incomplete story, rising price later) against the current ~91% "Very Positive" Steam rating — a fuller decision
framework is in the [FAQ guide](/guides/faq-guide).
