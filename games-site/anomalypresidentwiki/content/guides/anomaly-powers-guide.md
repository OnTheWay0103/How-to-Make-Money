# Anomaly President Anomaly Powers Guide — Steal the Campaign's Magic

Anomaly Powers are the heart of Anomaly President's build fantasy. The store page frames them as the reason you fight
the president's inner circle: **enemies and "vice president bosses" each have unique Anomaly Powers**, and **defeating a
boss lets you acquire its power** [Official]. The store even names one example — the president's PR chief "has the power
of HEART" [Official].

If weapons are your melee identity, Anomaly Powers are your supernatural special moves. This guide explains how the
system works and how to think about power choice.

## Where Powers Come From

Two acquisition paths are confirmed by official and reviewer sources:

1. **Bosses carry them.** Every vice-president boss has a named power. GameBrief describes the loop as chasing
   "anomaly-power drops from bosses" [Community].
2. **Defeating the boss grants it.** The store page states that beating a boss lets you acquire their Anomaly Power
   [Official]. GameBrief adds the crucial roguelike detail: boss kills grant **permanent** Anomaly Powers "usable
   immediately" [Community] — meaning they survive death and become part of your meta-progression.

The demo already demonstrated the shape of this: its first boss, the **Minister of Public Relations**, is a
vice-president-style fight (covered in the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide)).

### What "Permanent and Usable Immediately" Means

That short phrase from GameBrief carries two separate promises, and it is worth separating them:

- **Usable immediately** means the power is a reward for the run still in progress. When you finish a boss, the power
  you just took is available right away — you do not wait until the next run to try it. You can spend it in the same
  run that earned it, which is often exactly the power spike you need to clear the district's remaining fights and
  reach the next boss.
- **Permanent** means the power joins your campaign's growing toolbox and survives death. A boss you have beaten is a
  power your build can always call on later, which is why the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide) frames repeatable
  boss clears as a way to farm easier powers before you push into harder districts.

One honest gap: what a **repeat** clear of an already-conquered boss grants — a duplicate, an upgrade, a smaller
reward, or nothing at all — is **[Unconfirmed]** in the current Early Access build. The confirmed reward is the first
acquisition. If you are re-running a boss you have already beaten, check what you actually receive in-game before you
assume the trip is worth it.

## The Confirmed Example: The Power of HEART

The store page's named case is the **PR chief's power of HEART**, which "makes people fall in love with her" [GameBrief,
Community]. It is a reminder that powers in Anomaly President are not only combat abilities — they are thematically tied
to the political satire. A power of HEART is as much about charm and manipulation as it is about damage.

Reviewers describe HEART mostly by its flavour — making people fall in love with its holder. **What HEART does
mechanically — whether it charms enemies in combat, boosts the day-side campaign (speeches, image, votes), or both — is
[Unconfirmed]**; no source we checked specifies its in-game effect. Treat it as the confirmed *example* of the system,
not as a power whose exact rules you can build around yet.

The **full named list of powers in the current Early Access build is [Unconfirmed]**. Guides will expand this page as the
community documents each boss's power after updates.

## How Powers Fit the Loop

- **Permanent, so choose with your build in mind.** Because acquired powers persist, they are a long-term investment.
  Pair a power with a compatible weapon (see the [weapons guide](/guides/weapons-guide)) and element (see the
  [builds & strategy guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide)).
- **The cyber-mage fantasy is power-driven.** The store page describes a **cyber mage dealing mass damage with anomaly
  powers** [Official]. For that archetype your Anomaly Power selection matters more than your weapon: where a
  swordmaster's identity lives in the parry and the blade, a cyber mage's identity lives in which powers and elements
  it stacks over a run. See the [builds & strategy guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide) for the archetype breakdown.
- **Bosses are the gate.** Since powers come from bosses, the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide) is effectively the
  acquisition map: each boss you can beat is a power you can own. If you are chasing a specific fantasy — mass elemental
  damage, crowd control, survivability — the boss list doubles as a shopping list for the power that enables it.

## Anomaly Power Tips

1. **Do not hoard boss progress.** Because powers are permanent, any boss you can beat is a permanent upgrade. Farm
   bosses you can reliably clear before attempting harder districts.
2. **Match the power to the build.** A parry swordmaster and a mass-damage cyber mage want different powers. Decide the
   archetype first (see [builds & strategy](/guides/builds-strategy-guide)), then chase the power that fits.
3. **Expect the list to grow.** The developer's Early Access plan includes new content and new map areas [Official];
   new vice-president bosses (and therefore new powers) are a stated part of that plan.

## Powers FAQ

- **Do powers really survive death?** Yes. GameBrief describes boss-kill powers as permanent, and they survive death
  like the rest of your meta-progression [Community].
- **Can I use a power the moment I steal it?** Yes — "usable immediately" is the documented wording, meaning the power
  is available before the run that earned it ends [Community].
- **Is every power a combat ability?** No. HEART is charm-themed, which is a strong hint that some powers feed the
  political/campaign side of the game rather than pure damage [Community]. The day-side effects of most powers are
  [Unconfirmed].
- **Is there a published list of all powers?** Not one we have found. The only power named in official store copy is
  HEART (the PR chief's). The full roster in the current build is [Unconfirmed].
