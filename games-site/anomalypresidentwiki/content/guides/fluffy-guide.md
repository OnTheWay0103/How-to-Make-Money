# Anomaly President Fluffies Guide — Your Campaign Team

The store page is blunt about it: *"You Can't Become President By Yourself; You'll Need a Team of… FLUFFIES!"* [Official].
Fluffies are the oppressed creatures you recruit, and they are the closest thing Anomaly President has to a party system.
They work the campaign, upgrade your gear, and fight beside you — but they are also a responsibility. Neglect them and
they "might abandon you or worse, lose their lives" [Official].

This guide covers what Fluffies are, the three confirmed departments, and the care rules that keep your team alive.

## What Fluffies Are

Fluffies are creatures living under the current president's regime — the store page calls them **oppressed** [Official].
You recruit them as you travel the country and liberate people. Developer posts describe new Fluffies as part of the
planned Early Access content cadence [Road to EA #2, Community], so the roster is expected to grow.

Reviewers describe them as the game's "team" system, staffed onto modules of the **campaign bus** (see the [campaign bus
guide](/guides/campaign-bus-guide)). Think of them as a workforce that also needs feeding — not a passive bonus. Every
Fluffy you add is simultaneously a new worker and a new creature to look after, which is why your Fluffy count grows
your power *and* your daily obligations at the same time.

## The Three Departments

The store page confirms Fluffies can be assigned to three kinds of work [Official]:

| Department | What the Fluffy does |
|------------|----------------------|
| **Campaigning** | Works for votes — the political machine. |
| **Upgrading equipment** | Maintains and improves your gear (the bus's upgrade room). |
| **Assisting in battle** | Fights alongside you on night runs. |

Because the same Fluffy can only be in one place, every assignment is a trade-off between votes, gear, and combat
support. Rotate assignments based on what your campaign needs that cycle: short on damage → move Fluffies to battle
support; short on resources → put them on the campaign trail.

### Reading the Trade-Off by Situation

- **Early game, low income:** keep most Fluffies campaigning. Votes and revenue unlock the rooms that make everything
  else possible, so starving the campaign department early slows your whole run.
- **Before a boss district:** shift staff toward the equipment department. Gear upgrades are permanent, and arriving at
  a boss with current gear is the single most reliable advantage you can stack (see the [bosses guide](/guides/bosses-guide)).
- **Mid-run, dying in the first rooms:** pull a Fluffy or two into battle support. Even modest combat help matters in
  the opening waves, but it is rarely worth emptying your campaign or upgrade departments permanently — treat combat
  support as a temporary assignment for a specific push.

## The Care System (the Part Players Keep Warning About)

Multiple reviewers flag that Fluffy welfare is **tracked** and punishing if ignored [GameBrief, Community]:

- Fluffies have needs you must attend to. Neglect them and they can **abandon your campaign**.
- Worse, they can **lose their lives** [Official] — and nothing in the current Early Access material suggests that is
  reversible.
- One GameBrief reviewer summarised the layer as "you basically have to play Tamagotchi on the side" [Community] — a
  fair warning for action-first players that this management loop is real, not decorative.

**Practical takeaway:** build a daily check-in habit. A few seconds of care each in-game day is cheaper than losing a
Fluffy permanently.

### A Daily Care Checklist

If you want the habit spelled out (editorial, based on the confirmed care loop):

1. **Visit the bus each morning** and check every assigned Fluffy before you do anything else — welfare is tracked, so
   the state you ignore is the state that decays.
2. **Act on the lowest-status Fluffy first.** If one is closer to leaving than the rest, it gets priority over adding a
   new room.
3. **Do not over-expand your roster faster than you can care for it.** Recruiting every Fluffy you see sounds good until
   you are neglecting half of them. Grow the team at a pace your daily routine can sustain.
4. **Before a long night session, confirm nobody is in a bad state.** A Fluffy that abandons you mid-push is a loss you
   cannot undo that run.

## Recruiting Fluffies

The confirmed flow is: recruit from the local Fluffy population as you progress, then staff them on the bus [CultureCombine, Community]. The
specific species, their unlock locations, and any stat differences between individual Fluffies are **[Unconfirmed]** in
the current Early Access build. What is confirmed is the mechanical loop: rescue → assign → care → benefit. If a guide
promises a precise "where to find Fluffy X," treat it as speculation until a patch or official post names it.

## Fluffy Tips

1. **Never leave a department understaffed AND neglected.** An understaffed room is wasted investment; a neglected
   Fluffy is a lost one.
2. **Assign by bottleneck.** Dying at night? Battle-support Fluffies. Out of money for rooms? Campaign Fluffies. About
   to attempt a boss? Consider shifting staff to the upgrade room to max your gear first.
3. **Check on them every day.** Because day activities are where care happens, skipping the day loop is how teams die
   (see the [campaign management guide](/guides/campaign-management)).
4. **Expect the roster to grow.** New Fluffies are in active development [Road to EA #2, Community]; a guide written now
   will expand as they land.

## Fluffy FAQ

- **Can a lost Fluffy come back?** Nothing in the current Early Access material suggests abandonment or death is
  reversible [Official]. Until a patch or post says otherwise, treat a loss as permanent.
- **Can one Fluffy cover two departments?** The store page assigns Fluffies to a single kind of work at a time
  [Official]. Moving a Fluffy between departments is a rotation, not a way to double its output.
- **Do Fluffies help in night combat automatically?** Fluffies assigned to **assisting in battle** help on night runs
  [Official]; campaign and equipment Fluffies are doing their jobs back on the bus while you fight.
