# Anomaly President Weapons Guide — Choose Your Weapon

The store page gives the player one of its clearest instructions: **"Choose Your Weapon and Anomaly Power!"** [Official].
Anomaly President is a melee-first game — you are a warrior at night — and your weapon decides how your build plays.

This guide covers the arsenal the developer has stated, how two-weapon loadouts work, and how weapon choice maps onto the
two confirmed build fantasies: the **swordmaster** and the **cyber mage**.

## The Developer-Stated Arsenal

In the July "Road to Early Access #2" post, the developer listed the intended launch arsenal and said players can
**carry two weapons simultaneously** [Community]:

| Weapon | Notes from developer coverage |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **Sword** | The poster weapon — the katana/sword is what the parry-focused swordmaster uses in reviewer coverage. |
| **Nunchucks** | Part of the launch lineup. |
| **Claws** | Fast option in the launch lineup. |
| **Kusarigama** | "Newly added" in a separate announcement — strong for crowd control and extended reach, slower in one-on-one combat. |
| **Staff** | Part of the launch lineup. |
| **Hammer** | Was work-in-progress (WIP) as of the July 1 post. |
| **Octo-Arms** | Was work-in-progress (WIP) as of the July 1 post. |
| **Kicks & punches** | Unarmed options alongside the weapons. |

**Important caveat:** the Hammer and Octo-Arms were still being animated in that July post, and the demo shipped with only
**four distinct weapons** [Road to EA posts, Community]. The exact roster in the September 2026 Early Access build is
**[Unconfirmed]** — reviewers consistently confirm the katana/sword, but full per-weapon details should be verified in-game.

## Two-Weapon Loadouts

You carry **two weapons at once** [Road to EA #2, Community]. That creates the core weapon decision: what do you pair?

- A **reach weapon + a fast weapon** pairing lets you control space and then punish up close (the Kusarigama's extended
  reach is explicitly described as crowd-control strength [Community]).
- A **weapon + your element** pairing matters more than two similar weapons — because Anomaly Powers and elements do
  much of your damage (see the [Anomaly Powers guide](/guides/anomaly-powers-guide)).

### How Loadout Choice Falls Out by Archetype

Which pairing is *right* depends on which of the two confirmed fantasies you are building toward (editorial reading of
the confirmed systems):

- **Swordmaster.** The sword/katana is the documented centrepiece of the parry build [Official, Community]. Your second
  slot is best used to cover the sword's weakness — a reach option for crowds or a fast option for openings — rather
  than doubling down on the same role. If you only carry one weapon you can still play, but the second slot is where
  you solve your blind spots.
- **Cyber mage.** Weapon choice matters less than your Anomaly Powers and elements, so carry something comfortable for
  defence and repositioning and let the power layer do the damage (see the [builds & strategy guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide)).

## Weapon Choice Maps to Build

The store page's two named fantasies are a parry-capable **swordmaster** and an area-damage **cyber mage** [Official]:

- **Swordmaster** — a sword/katana build centered on parrying enemies "at the right time" [Official]. This leans on the
  parry system, so if you pick the sword path, spend time in the [combat tips & parry guide](/guides/combat-tips-guide).
- **Cyber mage** — a build dealing mass damage through anomaly powers and elements (Ice, Fire, Electric) rather than raw
  melee. Weapon choice matters less than your element and power picks here — see [builds & strategy](/guides/builds-strategy-guide).

In other words: **the weapon is your melee identity, and your Anomaly Power is your identity's special move.** They are
chosen together, not separately.

## Which Weapon Should You Start With?

Starting advice (editorial, based on confirmed systems):

1. **Start with the sword/katana.** It is the most-documented weapon in reviewer coverage, it teaches the parry, and it
   keeps the "swordmaster" door open while you learn the movement.
2. **Try Kusarigama when enemy crowds frustrate you.** Its stated crowd-control strength and reach [Community] make it
   the natural pick when waves of mobs are the problem.
3. **Hold off on judging "best."** With per-weapon damage numbers unconfirmed and balance actively changing in Early
   Access, a "best weapon" tier list written now would be guesswork. The [builds & strategy guide](/guides/builds-strategy-guide)
   explains why.

## Weapons FAQ

- **Is the sword actually the best weapon?** The sword/katana is the most-documented weapon and the swordmaster's
  anchor, but whether it is objectively *best* is **[Unconfirmed]** — per-weapon damage numbers are not published, and
  Early Access balance is still moving. Pick the weapon whose feel you like and build around it.
- **Do I need a weapon at all as a cyber mage?** You still carry two weapons into every run, but a cyber mage leans on
  powers and elements rather than raw melee [Official]. Treat your weapon as defence and utility, not your main damage
  source.
- **Are Hammer and Octo-Arms in the game yet?** **[Unconfirmed]**. They were still work-in-progress in the July 1
  developer post [Community], and no patch note we checked confirms they shipped. Verify in-game before planning a
  build around them.
- **Can I fight unarmed?** Yes — kicks and punches are part of the stated launch lineup [Community], making them a
  fallback when you want to drop a weapon slot or are between weapons.
- **How do weapons get stronger?** Permanent gear upgrades are handled through the campaign bus's upgrade room, which
  carries into runs — see the [campaign bus guide](/guides/campaign-bus-guide) for how that room fits the loop.
