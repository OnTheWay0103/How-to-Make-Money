# Anomaly President FAQ Guide — Straight Answers Before You Buy

This FAQ focuses on the **purchase and mechanics questions** players keep asking — whether Anomaly President is worth
buying in Early Access, how long it is, and how progress actually works. For quick general answers, the site's
[FAQ page](/faq) is a good companion; this guide goes deeper on the decisions. Everything reflects the September 2026
Early Access build and is labelled where it is not yet verifiable.

## Is Anomaly President worth buying?

**Mostly yes, with two caveats.** The Steam rating is strongly positive — around **91% "Very Positive" from ~1,300
reviews** as of early September 2026 [Steam, Community], and it briefly sat even higher at launch [GameRant, Community].
Independent scores cluster in the 7.5–8.5/10 range [GameBrief, Community].

The two caveats:

1. **It is Early Access.** The story ending is not written and part of the map is closed [Official]. You are buying an
   incomplete game that will grow (see the [patch notes & roadmap guide](/guides/patch-notes-roadmap-guide)).
2. **The management layer is mandatory, not optional.** The campaign bus and Fluffy care loop are core to progression.
   If you want pure action with no base management, this is not that game [Community].

If you like roguelites, action-platformers, and absurd political satire, the current reception suggests you will get your
money's worth. If you are price-sensitive, note the launch discount has ended and the price is planned to **rise**, not
fall [Official].

## How long is Anomaly President?

Playtime estimates vary because the game is a roguelite, so separate the two answers:

- **Unique current content:** reviewers estimate roughly **3–4 hours** of distinct Early Access content [Community].
- **Completing what exists:** around **7–10 hours** — the Turkish IGN reviewer finished the current main-story content
  in about 7 hours, and HowLongToBeat lists roughly **10 hours** for the main story [Community].
- **Total playtime with replay:** much higher. Steam's average playtime sits around 4 hours (many players are early), but
  players who chase builds and unlocks report 14+ hours [Community], and individual runs are short (roughly 10–15
  minutes per full run cycle).

In short: **a weekend of content now, with a roguelike's replay tail** — not a 40-hour campaign yet.

## Do I need to play Anomaly Agent first?

**No.** Anomaly Agent is the same developer's previous game and shares the "Anomaly Universe," but Anomaly President is a
standalone story with a different genre. Playing Anomaly Agent gives you context for the studio's style, not a required
backstory. See the [similar games guide](/guides/similar-games-guide) for the full comparison.

## Is Anomaly President on PS5, Switch or mobile?

**No version has been announced** as of September 2026 **[Unconfirmed]** — the game is PC (Windows) via Steam only, and it
is single-player. The full platform breakdown is in the [price & platforms guide](/guides/price-platforms-guide).

## Is there a free download?

There is **no free version of the full game** — Anomaly President is a paid Early Access title on Steam. There **is a
free demo** (separate Steam listing, App 4254970) that lets you play the opening region, fight the first boss (the
Minister of Public Relations), try four weapons, and sample the meta-progression tree. The demo is the honest way to test
the game before paying. Beware any "free full download" site — the legitimate free options are the demo and nothing else.

## Is Anomaly President on Epic Games?

**No Epic version has been announced** as of September 2026 **[Unconfirmed]**. The demo and the Early Access game are
Steam-only. If a future Epic/GOG release happens, it will be announced by the developer; nothing in the current record
supports it.

## Does my progress reset when I die?

Two different things reset, and it is worth keeping them straight:

- **On death**, the current run resets — the abilities and power-ups you picked up that run are gone, and you return to
  the campaign bus [Community].
- **What you keep** is everything permanent: **materials, money, campaign upgrades, boss-earned Anomaly Powers, Fluffies
  and bus rooms** [Community].

As for **full save wipes** during Early Access: no developer announcement of a planned wipe exists as of September 2026
**[Unconfirmed]**. The patches so far (v0.5.0.x) have adjusted upgrades and costs but we found no note of a forced reset.

## Is Anomaly President multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** The store page lists **Single-player** as its only mode [Official]. The "team" is made of AI **Fluffy**
companions you recruit and assign, not other players.

## Does it have difficulty options?

**Not as of the Early Access launch** — reviewers consistently report the absence of difficulty settings [Community].
That is a frequently-requested feature and could change in an update; see the [controls & settings guide](/guides/controls-settings-guide) for what the settings menu does contain.

## How do the day and night loops actually connect?

The game alternates two halves that feed each other: **by day** you campaign — speeches, canvassing, image management,
Fluffy care, bus building — and **by night** you run side-scrolling combat missions. Combat wins bank materials and
Anomaly Powers that strengthen the campaign; campaign improvements unlock better tools for future runs. The
[beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide) walks the full cycle step by step.

## What is the current build like?

The latest reported patch is **v0.5.0.18 (August 20, 2026)**, which added the **Food Truck** to the World Map and buffed
Fire [Community]. Known Early Access rough edges as of September: a **parry window** reviewers call too tight, **thin
enemy variety**, **Ice** reported as overpowered, and no difficulty setting. None of these are deal-breakers for the
game's fans, but they are real. The [patch notes & roadmap guide](/guides/patch-notes-roadmap-guide) tracks what is
fixed and what is coming (new weapons and enemies are promised for September).
