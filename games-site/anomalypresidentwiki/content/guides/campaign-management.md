# Anomaly President Campaign Management — Winning the Day

The store page sets the fantasy in one line: *"gather votes and run for presidency by day."* Campaign management is the
half of Anomaly President that happens before the fighting — speeches, canvassing, image, and recruiting. It is easy to
underrate it as "the boring part," but reviewers make a consistent point: the daytime side is **load-bearing**. Your
campaign is the meta-progression engine that makes night missions winnable.

This guide covers the confirmed shape of the campaign layer and how to run it well. For the bus itself — where you spend
a lot of this time — see the [campaign bus guide](/guides/campaign-bus-guide).

## What "Campaign by Day" Means

The store description and developer material describe the daytime goal as **winning the hearts and minds of the people**
[Official]. Reviewer coverage breaks that into concrete activities [GameBrief, Virtual Grip, Community]:

- **Canvassing for votes** — going out and asking citizens for support.
- **Giving speeches** — public appearances, often resolved through timing/rhythm minigames.
- **Managing your image** — the store's "media" angle; reviewer-confirmed bus rooms include a **TV studio for media
  appearances**.
- **Recruiting a team** — adding **Fluffies** to your campaign departments.
- **Strengthening the campaign** and **improving headquarters** between runs.
- **Preparing for the next election cycle** — Virtual Grip frames the whole game as running on an "election-cycle
  cadence" with a "just one more run" hook [Community].

These activities feed **votes** and **campaign revenue**, the daytime resources that buy upgrades, rooms, and allies.

## Dialogue and Minigames

Two interaction systems appear consistently in critic coverage:

1. **Card-based dialogue** — conversations (with voters, allies, and Fluffies) are resolved through cards [GameBrief,
   Community].
2. **Timing minigames** — public appearances use rhythm/timing inputs. The most-reported is a **dancing minigame**
   where you chain "perfect" inputs; GameBrief's hands-on coverage references a nine-perfect-input combo streak, and
   reviewers criticize the perfect-timing window as too short in the current build [Community].

Developer posts confirm more minigame systems are coming via new bus rooms — a **Dance Room** (dancing minigame), a
**Gift Room** (gift-giving minigame), and a **Marketing Room** (unlocks campaign-map areas that generate more revenue)
[Road to EA #2, Community]. Whether all three have shipped into Early Access by September 2026 is [Unconfirmed].

## Your Team: Fluffies on the Campaign Trail

You do not campaign alone. Fluffies assigned to the **campaign department** work for votes. But the same creatures can
be assigned to **equipment upgrades** or **battle support** [Official], so you are always deciding where to put limited
labor. Because Fluffies can leave or die if neglected [Official], the campaign layer is also a **care system**: daily
check-ins matter. See the [Fluffies guide](/guides/fluffy-guide) for the full department breakdown.

## Votes and Revenue Feed Everything

Campaign success is the resource engine:

- **Votes** are the obvious win condition — you are running for president.
- **Revenue** funds the bus and its rooms. The Marketing Room is explicitly described as generating more revenue from
  campaign-map areas [Road to EA #2, Community].

In practice this means a run of night missions that earns materials and money should be **reinvested into the campaign**
— new rooms, new Fluffies, upgraded equipment — rather than hoarded. Death resets the run but never the campaign.

## Campaign Management Tips

1. **Treat Fluffy care as a daily task, not a chore.** The "Tamagotchi on the side" criticism [GameBrief, Community] is
   really a warning: this system punishes neglect, and losing a Fluffy is permanent.
2. **Prioritize rooms that produce resources early.** A canvassing room (votes) and a TV studio (image/media) give you
   renewable income. The developer's "backbone of your journey" framing for the bus [Road to EA #2, Community] means
   room choice is strategy, not decoration.
3. **Do the day activities even when you want to fight.** Skipping canvassing starves the campaign, which makes later
   districts harder than they need to be.
4. **Watch the election-cycle clock.** Virtual Grip describes the cadence as election cycles [Community]; treat each
   cycle as a deadline to strengthen the campaign before the pressure rises.
5. **Expect the ending to be incomplete.** The developer states the story's ending is not yet written [Official]. Win
   the cycle, but do not expect a finished narrative arc in the current Early Access build.

## How It Connects to Night

Every campaign improvement is a tool for the night half: better equipment from the upgrade room, combat-ready Fluffies,
and Anomaly Powers unlocked through story progress. CultureCombine summarises the synergy: combat victories feed the
campaign's meta-progression, and the campaign unlocks better tools for future expeditions [Community]. If you are losing
night missions, the fix is often on the day side of the loop.
