# Anomaly President Enemies Guide — Know the President's Army

At night you fight the **criminal gangs controlled by the current president** [Official]. The official framing is blunt:
the president keeps power through gangs and **brainwashed fanatics** who enforce his rule [Publisher, Community]. This
guide collects what is actually confirmed about the enemy roster, how the enemy systems work, and what reviewers say is
still thin.

The honest starting point: **no official enemy compendium exists yet**, and reviewers describe the Early Access enemy
roster as limited. Anything that looks like a complete list is a guess. Here is what has real sourcing.

## The Two Confirmed Factions

Official and publisher copy consistently describe the president's forces in two overlapping groups:

1. **Criminal gangs / hidden crime syndicates** — the president's enforcement arm across the regions you raid at night.
2. **Brainwashed fanatics / enemy supporters** — regular citizens turned into loyalists who fight on the president's
   behalf [Publisher, Community].

Thematically this matters: the "enemy" is partly the city's own people, which is what makes the political satire land.
Mechanically, though, both groups fill the same role — waves of melee mobs in side-scrolling runs.

## Confirmed Enemy Behaviour Types

Patch notes and the official roadmap name a few concrete enemy behaviours that are in the build:

- **Ranged "snake" enemies** — the Early Access roadmap's first-week update explicitly reduced damage from "ranged snake
  enemies," confirming a ranged enemy type exists [Official roadmap, Community].
- **Shielded enemies** — the same roadmap patch reduced damage on "enemies with shields," confirming a shielded/blocking
  type [Official roadmap, Community].
- **Red-eyed enemies with clubs** — GameBrief's hands-on coverage describes "red-eyed enemies with clubs" as the basic
  ground mob [Community].
- **Visually identical mobs** — reviewers note waves of **reused, visually similar enemies**, which is the most common
  enemy complaint in Early Access [GameBrief, Community].

## How Enemy Fights Work: Health, Stamina, Finishers

The enemy system that *does* feel fully realised is the **health + stamina** pairing [Respawning, Community]:

- Every enemy has a **health bar** and a **stamina bar**.
- Land enough hits (or a successful parry) to **break the stamina bar**, and the enemy becomes open to an **instant
  finisher**.
- Finisher animations vary by enemy and weapon, which adds variety even when the enemy models themselves repeat.

This is the single most important enemy mechanic to learn: **your goal is often not to drain health but to break
stamina.** The [combat tips guide](/guides/combat-tips-guide) explains the rhythm.

## Combat-Readability Issues to Expect

Reviewers flag two combat-readability problems with the current enemies [Respawning, Community]:

- **Mid-air no-sell:** enemies in the air sometimes "no-sell" hits — your attack connects visually but does nothing.
  If a crowd feels unfair, it may be this bug rather than your timing.
- **Cluttered waves:** when many similar mobs stack, it is hard to read which one is about to attack. Rely on the
  yellow/red telegraphs and audio cues rather than trying to watch everything.

## Announced Enemies: "The Viking" and "The Gentleman"

In the developer's "Road to Early Access" material, two enemies were shown as work-in-progress under **working titles**
[CultureCombine, Community]:

- **"The Viking"** — described as part of the president's "superhero team," able to **deflect attacks**, deal heavy
  damage, and move constantly with a long-range dodge.
- **"The Gentleman"** — shown with work-in-progress animations; fewer mechanical details were published.

**Whether either has shipped into the Early Access build by September 2026 is [Unconfirmed].** Until a patch names them
as live, treat them as roadmap content. The [patch notes & roadmap guide](/guides/patch-notes-roadmap-guide) tracks their
status.

## The "Thin Roster" Criticism (Honest Labelling)

Multiple reviewers call enemy variety the game's weakest Early Access area [GameBrief, Steam Deck HQ, Community]:

- Waves of **visually similar mobs** and **reused models**.
- A small set of behaviour types (basic melee, ranged, shielded) rather than a deep bestiary.
- Difficulty that "swings sharply" because a few enemy types do most of the work.

This is an Early Access limitation, not a final design statement. The official roadmap explicitly promises **new enemies
in September** that show "the true scale of the Evil President's tyranny" [Official roadmap, Community], so the roster is
expected to grow quickly. Any tier-list-style enemy ranking written in September 2026 would be obsolete within weeks.

## Practical Enemy Tips

1. **Break stamina, not just health.** Against basic mobs, a parry plus a finisher kills faster than mashing attack.
2. **Handle ranged enemies first.** Ranged snake enemies were nerfed because they hurt; they still force you to move.
   Close the gap before they stack damage.
3. **Bring a crowd tool for shield/mob waves.** A reach or crowd-control weapon (the Kusarigama is the documented
   example) solves clumped waves better than a single-target sword. See the [weapons guide](/guides/weapons-guide).
4. **Do not over-commit in the air.** With the mid-air no-sell bug reported, ground combos into finishers are more
   reliable than aerial gambles.
5. **Expect the list to grow.** New enemies are confirmed for September — re-check this guide and the
   [patch notes & roadmap guide](/guides/patch-notes-roadmap-guide) after each update.
