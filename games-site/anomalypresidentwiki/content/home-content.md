# Anomaly President Wiki — Complete Guide Hub

**Everything you need to win votes by day and win fights by night in the presidential-campaign roguelike action game by Phew Phew Games.**

Anomaly President entered **Early Access on August 3, 2026** on Steam for PC (Windows). It is a single-player
presidential-campaign roguelike: by day you run for president — canvassing, speechmaking, managing your image and your
team — and by night you fight the corrupt president's criminal gangs as a side-scrolling warrior. This wiki covers the

## Featured Guides

### Beginner Guide — the Dual Loop
You cannot win an election by fighting alone, and you cannot win a war by giving speeches. Anomaly President alternates
a **daytime campaign loop** with a **nighttime combat loop**, and each one feeds the other. [Start the beginner guide →](/guides/beginner-guide)

### Fluffies Guide
Recruit oppressed Fluffies and assign them to **campaign, gear-upgrade, or combat** departments. They must be cared
for — neglect them and they may abandon you or lose their lives. [Read the Fluffies guide →](/guides/fluffy-guide)

### Campaign Bus Guide
The campaign bus is your mobile base. Add rooms like a TV studio, an upgrade room, or a canvassing room, and each
section changes your abilities and playstyle each run. [Tour the bus →](/guides/campaign-bus-guide)

### Anomaly Powers Guide
Every vice-president boss carries a unique Anomaly Power — the PR chief has the power of HEART. Defeat a boss to
acquire its power, permanently. [Learn how powers work →](/guides/anomaly-powers-guide)

## What is Anomaly President?

Anomaly President is a **presidential-campaign roguelike action game** developed by **Phew Phew Games** (the studio
behind Anomaly Agent, set in the same "Anomaly Universe"). It was published with Gamedev.ist and Gamersky Games.

By day, you run a campaign in a dystopian city: canvass for votes, give speeches, manage your image, recruit
**Fluffies**, and expand your **campaign bus** — a mobile base you design from scratch. By night, you become a warrior
and fight the criminal gangs controlled by the current president. The store page summarises the fantasy in one line:
*gather votes and run for presidency by day, then transform into a warrior to take down your enemies by night.*

Combat is a **side-scrolling beat-'em-up roguelike run**: deploy from the overworld, grab abilities and power-ups,
and fight through waves to a boss. Melee weapons and **Anomaly Powers** stolen from bosses define your build — a
parry-focused **swordmaster**, an area-damage **cyber mage**, or anything in between. Death resets the run but you keep
materials, money, and campaign meta-progression.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Day / Night Dual Loop** — campaign management by day, combat runs by night; each feeds the other.
- **Campaign Management** — speeches, canvassing, image, card-based dialogue and minigames, votes and revenue.
- **Campaign Bus** — mobile base with rooms (bedroom, TV studio, upgrade room, canvassing room confirmed in EA; Dance, Gift and Marketing rooms in development).
- **Fluffies** — recruitable companions for campaign, equipment, or combat; must be cared for.
- **Night Combat** — side-scrolling roguelike runs with melee weapons and elemental damage (Ice, Fire, Electric).
- **Weapons** — developer-stated launch arsenal: Sword, Nunchucks, Claws, Kusarigama, Staff, Hammer, Octo-Arms, plus kicks and punches; carry two [Unconfirmed roster details].
- **Anomaly Powers** — abilities stolen from vice-president bosses (e.g. the PR chief's HEART).
- **Vice-President Bosses** — the president's inner circle; the demo's first boss is the Minister of Public Relations.
- **Meta-Progression** — boss kills grant permanent powers; campaign improvements persist between runs.
- **Early Access** — playable start to finish; story ending and part of the map still to come; roadmap through September 2026 and beyond.
