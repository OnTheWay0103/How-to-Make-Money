## What is Sovereign Tower?

Sovereign Tower is a story-rich, round-table management RPG developed by **WILD WITS** (a Rennes-based independent French studio) and published by **Curve Games**. You play the new Sovereign of a magical tower — an Arthurian-inspired ruler of Britannia — and must manage a kingdom full of problems, eccentric knights, and demanding factions.

The daily loop combines visual-novel dialogue with management depth: hold audiences with your subjects, assign knights to quests at the Round Table, and review the results. Five attributes (Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, Wit) decide which knight fits which quest — send the wrong knight and they may fail, storm off, or die. A Demon in the crypts can rewind time to key moments, letting you keep your knowledge and rewrite fate.
