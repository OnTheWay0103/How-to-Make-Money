# Romance Options & Relationships Guide in Sovereign Tower

Yes — **Sovereign Tower has romance.** The official feature list promises "a little forbidden medieval romance on the side" between quests, and knights who stay satisfied can develop into "deep friendships or even romances" that matter for long-term loyalty. Romance is not a side minigame: it is woven into the affinity system, the story branches, and even the diplomacy. Here is how it works, who is romanceable, and why it can literally stop a war.

## The Foundation: Knight Affinity

Romance in Sovereign Tower grows out of **knight affinity**, the loyalty meter that runs roughly −10 to +10 (per community guides):

- **Affinity governs dialogue unlocks, personal scenes, and retention.** Low affinity — around −7 per community data — makes a knight resign for good, taking their levels.
- **Favourite meals are the cheapest boost** (~+1.5 affinity each). The six meal types are Galette-Saucisse, Croque-Monsieur, Préfou, Crêpe, Brizhian Butter Shortbread, and Lion's Taco — a French-indie nod from the WILD WITS kitchen. Chester and Dullahan accept all six; everyone else has favourites to learn.
- **Sending knights on quests they enjoy raises affinity; tasks they hate sour it.** Matching preferences to quests is relationship management disguised as strategy.
- **Two knights never resign: Alwena and Ari** — the community-verified loyalty anchors.

## From Friendship to Romance

Per the official press material and reviews:

- Knights who stay satisfied unlock **personal scenes and deeper dialogue** over time — this is where relationships evolve into "deep friendships or even romances."
- **Romance options are character-specific.** Reviewers describe losing "a possible romantic option" as a genuine motivation to use the time rewind — the game expects you to care about these knights, and it punishes neglect.
- **"Forbidden" is part of the design** — the official copy leans into courtly-intrigue flavour: romancing a knight who outranks you socially, or one your factions object to, has consequences the game does not label in advance [Unconfirmed — the exact consequences of specific pairings are not documented by the community at the time of writing].
- **Knight personality feeds the writing.** Reviews singled out the knights' "genuine agency" — characters who suggest alternate solutions (kidnapping, assassination) and hold grudges across timelines. Relationships with them are less like dating sims and more like managing complicated friends.

## Romance as a Diplomatic Weapon

The most surprising mechanic: **marriage can stop a war.** One reviewer describes a noble uprising that was only avoided by **marrying a recruited neighbouring knight** — the wedding assuaged the nobles and prevented the conflict. Practically, this means:

- Keep a **romanceable knight from the faction you are struggling with** as a political card.
- **Faction standing and romance interact**: the [Factions guide](/guides/factions-guide) covers how marriage fits the diplomacy picture.
- The game's own framing — "Your Reign, Your Rules" — encourages using every tool, relationships included.

## How to Build Relationships (Step by Step)

1. **Feed favourites early.** Learn each knight's favourite meal and rotate the +1.5 affinity boosts. Costs little, pays all run — and the meals are thematically fun to collect.
2. **Match quests to preferences.** Read quest text for hints (bold/highlighted words); Angelica's animal-love and mediation preference is the documented example — she stays satisfied on diplomatic missions and creature-pacifying quests, and *dissatisfied* on kill-the-beast quests.
3. **Spend free time talking to knights.** Personal scenes unlock hidden traits and relationship progress (per the IGN preview). Free time exists for exactly this — use it.
4. **Never let affinity drop low.** A knight who leaves takes their romance options with them — and their levels.
5. **Rewind to save a relationship.** If a knight dies or a relationship sours irrecoverably, the Demon is the escape hatch — but rewind erases good branches too (see the [Time Rewind guide](/guides/rewind-guide)).

## Relationship-Specific Notes

- **Rufus (Loyal)** and **The Wolf** have an arc tied to the Groveshire curse removal — completing it deepens the bond (see the [Wolf & Rufus guide](/guides/wolf-rufus-guide)).
- **Victoria's trial chain** (Act 3) is the deepest single-knight storyline, and reviewers single out her arc's payoff — her romance potential comes with the highest stat ceiling in the game (see the [Tier List guide](/guides/tier-list)).
- **Chester never gets upset** — the easiest knight to keep happy, whatever your romance intentions. His random stats make him the lowest-maintenance relationship in the game.
- **Angelica** is your first knight and the first relationship opportunity — her animal-lover trait is documented in the IGN preview, making her preference-matching the game's tutorial in relationships.
