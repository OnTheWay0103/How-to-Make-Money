# How to Unlock Every Patch 1.2.0 Faction Audience in Sovereign Tower

Patch **1.2.0** is the biggest free content drop Sovereign Tower has received since launch. Alongside a new epilogue, extra unexpected outcomes, and tighter affinity timing, it adds four **high-satisfaction faction audiences** that hand out some of the strongest rewards in the game: a large Treasury payment, a relic, a mount that can save a knight's life, and a new route to recruiting Daguez.

The catch is that most players will never see them. These audiences only appear once a faction's **satisfaction reaches at least 50**, and the game does not announce that you have crossed the line. This guide explains exactly what unlocks, how the threshold works, and how to push each meter without collapsing the rest of the realm.

## What Patch 1.2.0 Added

The October 15, 2026 update is labelled a **Major Narrative Update**, and its headline feature is a new epilogue after the tower-destruction ending. Characters who can appear include Angelica, Goberto, Gwendan, Ursula, Gideon, Ligia, Brunhilda, Tarcus, Childeric, Gothild, Oliver, Rowan, Alwena, the Lady of the Tower, Arlin, and Epicrates.

The patch also adds new unexpected outcomes, changes several audience follow-ups, and restricts some affinity conversations by act. For example, Gideon's fourth affinity step and full romance are now Act 3 only, and Gwendan's full romance is also Act 3 only.

The four faction audiences are the part that matters for progression. The patch notes state that they unlock at **satisfaction of at least 50 with each group**. That is faction satisfaction, not individual knight affinity — keep the two meters separate when you plan a run.

## How the Satisfaction Threshold Works

Sovereign Tower tracks five factions: **Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, and the People**, plus the Treasury as a sixth pressure point. Every audience choice and quest resolution moves those meters, usually raising one group while lowering another.

Satisfaction is not a single "reputation" score. Each faction has its own meter, and the 1.2.0 rewards are tied to the specific group that reaches 50. Raising the Merchants does nothing for the Scholars' audience.

The practical consequence is that you cannot drift into these rewards. You have to choose a target faction early, favour it consistently, and accept the losses elsewhere. If you spread your choices evenly, you may finish a reign with every faction in the middle and none of the four audiences unlocked.

## The Four Faction Rewards

| Faction | Reward | What It Gives You |
|---------|--------|-------------------|
| **Merchants** | **1,477 funds** | A direct Treasury injection, useful for repairs, equipment, and expensive audience choices |
| **Nobles** | **Absolution** | A powerful relic that references *Crown Gambit* |
| **Scholars** | **The Unicorn** | A strong mount with the **"unicorn tears"** passive, which prevents its rider from dying on a quest |
| **The People** | **Knight one of their own** | Unlocks the recruitment of **Daguez** |

[Source: Official — Patch 1.2.0 notes]

The Unicorn is the standout reward for most runs. A knight dying on a quest is one of the few genuinely irreversible losses in Sovereign Tower: you lose the character, their levels, their affinity, and any story threads attached to them. A mount that cancels that outcome changes how aggressively you can assign your best knights.

Absolution is the more build-defining pick if you are chasing a powerful relic, while the 1,477 funds are the safest choice for a reign that is already struggling financially. Daguez is the roster pick — useful if you want a high-Strength knight and the associated achievement, but not worth tanking the People purely for power.

## How to Raise Each Faction to 50

There is no confirmed numeric breakdown for each audience option, so treat this as a planning framework rather than a spreadsheet [Editorial].

### Pick a target before Act 2

By Act 2 you should know which reward you want. The earlier you commit, the more audience choices you can tilt in that direction. Switching targets halfway through usually leaves two factions in the 30s instead of one at 50.

### Use audience choices as the main lever

The morning audience is the most reliable way to move a faction meter. When a petition names a group — a merchants' trade dispute, a scholars' funding request, a nobles' privilege — the choice that explicitly favours that group is the one that moves its satisfaction. The cost is almost always a drop somewhere else, so check the Treasury and the other meters before you commit.

### Back the meter with quests

Faction-tagged quests and successful outcomes reinforce what your audience choices started. Send knights whose stats and traits match the quest requirements; a failed assignment can undo several cycles of goodwill. The [Knight Recruitment Guide](/guides/knight-recruitment-guide) and [Knight Stats guide](/guides/knight-stats) cover the matching rules.

### Keep the Treasury solvent

You cannot buy satisfaction if you are broke. Repairs, equipment, and some audience options all draw on the Treasury. The [Economy Guide](/guides/economy-guide) explains how to keep enough gold in reserve to survive a bad cycle.

### Do not ignore the other four factions

A faction at 50 is worthless if another group hits an emergency floor and triggers an uprising or a fail state. Keep every meter above crisis level, even while you push one upward. The [Factions Guide](/guides/factions-guide) covers the crisis thresholds and the rewind escape hatch.

### Rewind bad audience calls

The Demon's rewind is the cleanest way to undo a choice that tanked your target faction. Use it for reputation spirals, not for small losses — the [Time Rewind Guide](/guides/rewind-guide) explains the trade-offs.

## Recruiting Daguez Through the People's Audience

The People's reward is not an item. It is an offer to **knight one of their own**, which the patch explicitly ties to recruiting **Daguez**.

Community reporting on the Daguez audience adds the practical details [Community]:

- You need an **open knight seat**. The practical windows are Act 2's eight-seat cap and Act 3's ten-seat cap.
- The audience is a four-line unrest event. The recruitment line is the one that offers to knight a representative at your Round Table.
- Daguez joins with **Strength 15, Luck 9, and armour 6**, plus a locked giant sword. The rest of his stat line is zero.
- His listed favourite meals are Crêpe, Galette-Saucisse, and Préfou.

Those numbers come from the community wiki, not the 1.2.0 patch notes, so treat them as a snapshot rather than a guarantee [Community]. The important part is the seat: if the Round Table is full, the audience may fire without giving you a recruit.

## Which Reward Should You Chase First?

[Editorial] Our order for most players:

1. **The Unicorn (Scholars)** — the safety net is worth more than raw power, especially in a long reign where one bad quest can delete a knight you have invested in for hours.
2. **Absolution (Nobles)** — take this if your combat or quest-resolution build already has enough safety and you want a stronger relic.
3. **1,477 funds (Merchants)** — the recovery option. Take it when the Treasury is the thing limiting your choices.
4. **Daguez (The People)** — take him when you want the roster depth or the achievement, not as a first pick.

If you are chasing multiple rewards in one reign, start with the faction whose meter is already highest. Forcing a second faction to 50 from a low base usually costs more than the reward is worth.

## What Is Still Unconfirmed

The patch notes are precise about the rewards but quiet about the surrounding rules. The following are **not confirmed** as of October 23, 2026 [Unconfirmed]:

- The numeric scale behind "satisfaction 50" — whether it is 0–100, a hidden points total, or a threshold on a smaller band.
- Whether each faction audience can fire more than once per reign.
- Whether a missed audience can be re-triggered later.
- Whether all four rewards can be earned in a single playthrough.
- Whether Patch 1.2.0 requires a new save or applies cleanly to an existing one.

Do not restart a run solely to chase a reward until the community has confirmed the repeatability rules.

## FAQ

**Does the 1.2.0 audience replace the normal faction audience?**
No. It is an additional audience that becomes available at the satisfaction threshold. Normal petitions and crisis events still fire.

**Is faction satisfaction the same as knight affinity?**
No. Knight affinity is the personal loyalty meter for individual knights. Faction satisfaction is the realm-wide opinion of Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, and the People.

**Can I get the Unicorn without the Scholars at 50?**
No. The patch ties each reward to its own faction's threshold.

**Is Daguez a new knight in 1.2.0?**
The patch ties his recruitment to the new People audience, but the character and his stat line predate the update in community documentation.

**What happens if I miss the audience?**
Unknown [Unconfirmed]. The safest approach is to keep a save from before the cycle in which you expect the audience to fire.
