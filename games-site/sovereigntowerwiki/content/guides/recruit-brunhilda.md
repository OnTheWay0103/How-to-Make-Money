# How to Recruit Brunhilda in Sovereign Tower (Gavault Chain)

**Brunhilda** is the "Fire Lady" of the Gavault questline — a Magic/Strength knight (STR 7, AGI 2, CHA 6, MAG 7, WIT 3, LCK 0 per community stat tables) whose recruitment chain starts on the **second day of the game**. Miss the opening decision and you can lock yourself out of her route for the whole run. She was also one of the headline additions to the Steam Next Fest extended demo, so veteran demo players may already know her.

## The Route (Act 1)

Per Prodigygamers and Tpose Gaming:

1. **On the second day, side with Gavault** when Damian and Arnold appear at court. This is the branch point that opens the Gavault questline. The alternative (siding against Gavault) sends the chain down the Tarcus path or closes it entirely.
2. **Investigate the people and taverns of Fort Gavault.** Complete the investigation chain — the taverns and locals hold the clues you need to understand what is really happening in the town.
3. **Refuse to help arrest her mother.** When the choice comes, take the daughter's side. This is the step that actually earns Brunhilda's trust — and it is the step that diverges from Tarcus.
4. **Alternative route:** use the **Demon's rewind** to undo an earlier choice and re-take the chain (per Prodigygamers) — but only if you already know which early pick went wrong.

## The Catch: Brunhilda vs Tarcus

Brunhilda is **mutually exclusive with Tarcus** (per Tpose Gaming and Bonus Action):

- **Daughter's side** (refuse the arrest) → **Brunhilda** — the Magic/Charisma hybrid.
- **Mother's side** (confirm you will stop Brunhilda) → **Tarcus** (STR 6, AGI 3, CHA 1, MAG 0, WIT 10, LCK 5) — the Wits specialist for investigation quests.

Both knights share the same questline gate: the Gavault chain gates **Brunhilda and Tarcus**, and no playthrough sees both. Choose by roster gap:

| Need | Pick |
|------|------|
| Magic + Charisma hybrid for lich/diplomacy quests | Brunhilda (MAG 7, CHA 6) |
| Wits specialist for investigations | Tarcus (WIT 10) |
| A False Bank send | Either — Brunhilda's Wits 3 qualifies ("3 or lower") |

## What Brunhilda Is Good For

- **Magic quests**: The lich will not return (Magic 10+), A Thesis Thief (Magic 10+), The Possessed Reverend (Magic 7+), Hydra Hunt (Magic 7+).
- **Diplomacy**: her Charisma 6 keeps her a solid pick for mediation quests and speeches.
- **The False Bank** is a trap for most knights — with Wits 3 she meets the "3 or lower" bar, making her one of the few viable sends.

## Warnings

- **The second-day choice is mandatory.** Side against Gavault and the chain may never open [Unconfirmed — alternate triggers not confirmed at the time of writing; community guides all route through the Gavault siding].
- **The tavern investigation must be completed in full.** Skipping steps can strand the chain mid-way — take the tavern and people routes, not just one.
- **Refusing the arrest costs faction standing with the Nobles** — see the [Factions guide](/guides/factions-guide) for how to repair it afterwards.
- If you already passed the second day and sided wrong, the [Time Rewind guide](/guides/rewind-guide) explains the rewind escape hatch — but rewinding erases good branches too, so weigh your run before using it.

## Demo Players: What Changed

Brunhilda was one of the headline additions to the **Steam Next Fest extended demo** (June 15, 2026), where her Gavault chain was first playable (per Turn Based Lovers). If you played the demo, you already know the shape of her route — but the full release reportedly expands the chain beyond the demo's scope, and the second-day choice now interacts with the full faction system in ways the demo did not show. Do not assume a demo-era walkthrough still matches the release; re-verify choices against the current game, and check the [Factions guide](/guides/factions-guide) for the standing costs the demo did not display.

## What If You Sided Wrong?

If the second-day choice went against Gavault (or you arrested her mother), the route is closed — but not permanently:

- **Rewind via the Demon** to the second day and re-pick (see the [Time Rewind guide](/guides/rewind-guide)). This is the documented alternative route per Prodigygamers.
- **Or commit to Tarcus.** The mother's side is a complete route, not a dead end — Tarcus's 10 Wits covers the investigation quests Brunhilda's Magic would not have. A Gavault chain either way is better than abandoning it, because the chain also gates faction content in the region.
- **New Game Plus** is the clean fix if you want both outcomes across runs (see the [Knight Recruitment guide](/guides/knight-recruitment-guide)).

## Brunhilda in Your Roster

She is an Act 1 answer to the game's Magic quest wall: most early knights (Angelica, Goberto, Gwendan) have 0 Magic, and the lich/vampire/possessed-reverend quests all demand Magic 7+. Brunhilda arrives with Magic 7 already, and her Charisma 6 covers mediation. She is the single best Act 1 answer to both arcane and diplomatic questlines — the trade-off is losing Tarcus's 10 Wits for the investigation quests.
