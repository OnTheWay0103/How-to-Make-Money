# Knight Attributes & Stats Explained — Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, Wit

Every quest in Sovereign Tower is a stat check in disguise. The official press release lists **five attributes — Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, and Wit** — and community stat tables add a sixth, **Luck**. Understanding what each stat does, and how to read a knight's stat sheet, is the core skill of the game.

## The Five (or Six) Attributes

| Attribute | What It Reads Like | Used For |
|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| **Strength** | Brawn, combat power | Fighting, wrestling, animal handling, jousting |
| **Agility** | Speed, reflexes | Scout work, chases, tournaments, escape |
| **Charisma** | Presence, persuasion | Diplomacy, speeches, court intrigue |
| **Magic** | Arcane ability | Lich hunting, exorcisms, mystical research |
| **Wit** | Intellect, cunning | Investigations, schemes, riddles |
| **Luck** (community) | Fortune | Treasure hunts, gambling, "wild card" quests |

Attributes are reported on a **0–15 scale** in community stat tables (per Tpose Gaming and AllThings). Zero is a genuine liability, not a "low but usable" stat — see the False Bank quest below.

## How Stat Checks Work

Each quest demands a threshold in a specific stat. Known community-verified requirements (per Tpose Gaming):

| Quest | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Repel the dragon | Magic 14+ |
| Suspects interrogations | Wits 12+ |
| In search of the gem | Luck 11+ |
| The lich will not return | Magic 10+ |
| A Thesis Thief | Magic 10+ |
| The Vampire of Clovermont | Charisma 10+ |
| Animal bodyguards | Strength 10+ |
| Diplomatic Meeting | Wits 8+ |
| Hydra Hunt | Magic 7+ |
| The Possessed Reverend | Magic 7+ |
| **The False Bank** | **Wits 3 or lower** |

The False Bank is the game's favourite trick: it *rewards* stupidity. Knights with 0 Wits — **Goberto, Dullahan, Daguez** — are the correct send, and Gwendan (Wits 3) works too.

## Reading a Knight's Sheet

Community stat tables list every knight with their six stats plus armour, level, and total. A few archetypes from the roster (per Tpose Gaming):

| Knight | STR | AGI | CHA | MAG | WIT | LCK | Notes |
|--------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|-------|
| Angelica | 4 | 8 | 3 | 0 | 2 | 7 | Starter knight; animal lover |
| Brunhilda | 7 | 2 | 6 | 7 | 3 | 0 | Gavault questline |
| Daguez | 15 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 9 | One-stat wonder: pure Strength |
| Dullahan | 8 | 8 | 0 | 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 Wits — ideal False Bank send |
| Goberto | 8 | 1 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | Cheesemaker; late-game armour god |
| Victoria | 13 | 13 | 9 | 13 | 10 | 9 | Highest totals in the game |
| The Wolf | 3 | 7 | 3 | 3 | 7 | 1 | Wit/Agility specialist; fast mount |

**Watch the total, but don't only watch the total.** A knight with 24 total who is *perfectly shaped* for one niche (Daguez's 15 Strength) can outperform a balanced 40-total knight on the right quest — because quests demand single-stat thresholds, not averages.

## Hidden Traits Matter More Than the Sheet

Every knight has **hidden traits and preferences** you must discover:

- **Bold or highlighted words in quest text are hints** about which traits will matter (per the IGN preview).
- **Angelica loves animals.** On creature quests she can be the worst pick (if you want a kill) or the only pick (if you want the tamed outcome — she is the sole knight who triggers The Wolf's recruitment).
- **Chester's stats are random** — his "Jester" attribute rerolls them every time the game reads them. He accepts all six meals and never gets upset, making him the safest wildcard.
- **Preferences also work against you.** Sending a knight to a quest they hate costs affinity and can end with them leaving the court.

Uncover hidden traits by **spending free time talking to knights**, or by having your **Intendant spy on them** (per the IGN preview).

## Level Ups and Equipment

- Knights **level up** through quests, and levels travel with the knight — if a knight resigns, their levels go with them.
- **Equipment exists but is scarce.** The blacksmith (Carina's Forge) repairs one suit of armour per cycle; the alchemy room (Belladonna's) brews stat potions. See the [Tower Upgrades guide](/guides/tower-upgrades).
- A few knights carry signature gear — **Victoria's locked sword** adds +5 STR, +4 AGI, +2 CHA, +1 MAG at a cost of 3 Luck (per Tpose Gaming).

## Practical Rules of Thumb

1. **Match the quest threshold first**, then consider traits, then affinity.
2. **Keep at least one specialist per stat** — a Magic monster for lich quests, a Wits brain for investigations, a Strength wall for combat.
3. **Never send a 0-stat knight to a quest that needs that stat.** Dullahan's 0 Wits is a feature on the False Bank and a death sentence on "Suspects interrogations."
4. **Check the knight's favourite meal** and feed them when affinity creeps down (see the [Romance guide](/guides/romance-guide)).
