# How to Recruit All Knights in Sovereign Tower — Full Roster Guide

The heart of Sovereign Tower is its roster: **two dozen eccentric knights** (community guides list 23–24) with backstories, secrets, grudges, and romantic entanglements. The catch — and it is a big one — is that **you cannot recruit them all in one run**. The Round Table holds only **6 seats in Act 1, 8 in Act 2, and 10 in Act 3**, some routes are mutually exclusive, and you cannot dismiss a knight once invited (per Tpose Gaming and Prodigygamers). New Game Plus is the intended way to collect the full roster.

## The Rules of Recruitment

1. **Seats are the cap.** 6 → 8 → 10 across the acts. Every recruit you accept takes a seat for the rest of the run.
2. **Some knights are mutually exclusive.** Brunhilda and Tarcus share the Gavault chain (you can only finish one side). The Wolf and Silgur are opposite outcomes of the same beast questline.
3. **You cannot dismiss knights.** Choose your invites carefully.
4. **Some routes have second chances (Map Room for ~25 gold), others do not.** Alwena is a one-time offer — miss her and she never returns.

## Recruitment by Act

### Act 1 (6 seats)

| Knight | How to Recruit |
|--------|----------------|
| **Angelica** | Joins during the opening story — your first knight |
| **Goberto** | Candidacy audience in Cycle 2 |
| **Gwendan** | Candidacy audience in Cycle 3 |
| **Ursula** | Candidacy audience in Cycle 5, no prerequisite |
| **Dullahan** | Let Goberto die; arrives 2 cycles later wearing his armour |
| **Chester** | Cleankeeper goose contract in Milkford (see [dedicated guide](/guides/recruit-chester)) |
| **The Wolf** | Send Angelica on Fight the Werewolf in Almor — only she triggers the tamed outcome |
| **Silgur** | Same beast questline — pick the kill option, send anyone except Angelica |
| **Brunhilda** | Gavault chain, daughter's side (see [dedicated guide](/guides/recruit-brunhilda)) |
| **Tarcus** | Gavault chain, mother's side — mutually exclusive with Brunhilda |
| **Arron** | Beat the Dragon Knight — he presents Arron and asks you to knight him (see [Dragon Knight guide](/guides/dragon-knight-guide)) |

### Act 2 (8 seats)

| Knight | How to Recruit |
|--------|----------------|
| **Gideon** | Candidacy audience around Cycle 13; Map Room fallback costs 25 gold |
| **Alwena** | One-time offer: every knight unavailable with a free seat open, then visit the Round Table. No second chance. Never dies or resigns; max one-cycle quests |
| **Ligia** | Southbay storyline — her father must survive, and you must win the lamp quest within its 3-cycle deadline |
| **Childeric** | Win the Almor jousting tournament — do **not** send Goberto |
| **Zolta** | Kutnar storyline — cheat through the derby with Tarcus or The Wolf, then pay a 250 gold fee |
| **Oliver** | Spare your attacker in the opening instead of executing him; candidacy arrives around Cycle 19 |
| **Daguez** | Let the People fall to your lowest faction to trigger the civil-war event, then knight one of the revolt's leaders |

### Act 3 (10 seats)

| Knight | How to Recruit |
|--------|----------------|
| **Epicrate** | Win his trial or break him out — if breaking out, don't send Tarcus |
| **Edith** | Ask Countess Medea about a knight who knows dragons and spells, or summon from the Map Room as "the mystical knight" (25 gold) |
| **Victoria** | Ask if a knight of her caliber would join, then clear her trials |
| **Gothild** | Send a high-Wits knight on Suspect Interrogations until Victoria is implicated, or follow up on a successful Victoria ambush |
| **Ari** | During the Basalt Isles succession audience, speak with the merchant guilds (25 gold), or summon from the Map Room as "the younger brother of the Basalt brothers" |
| **Rufus** | Complete the Mystical Research quest in Groveshire with enough Scholars' satisfaction, or recruit The Wolf first and reach his second free-time conversation (see [Wolf & Rufus guide](/guides/wolf-rufus-guide)) |

## The Mutually Exclusive Pairs

| Pair | Decision |
|------|----------|
| **Brunhilda vs Tarcus** | Same Gavault chain — daughter's side (refuse to arrest her mother) gets Brunhilda; mother's side (stop Brunhilda) gets Tarcus |
| **The Wolf vs Silgur** | Same werewolf quest — tamed outcome (Angelica) gets The Wolf; kill outcome (anyone else) gets Silgur |
| **Goberto vs Dullahan** | Dullahan only arrives if Goberto dies. Goberto himself transforms in Almor into a top-tier tank — so this choice genuinely costs you |

## The Recruitment Order We Recommend

1. **Angelica** (free, first cycle).
2. **Goberto** (Cycle 2) — he becomes a monster after the Almor transformation; keep him alive for the False Bank quest.
3. **Gwendan** (Cycle 3) — cheap, cheerful, enables Chester's chain.
4. **The Wolf** (via Angelica on the werewolf quest) — the fast mount is a tempo machine.
5. **Brunhilda or Tarcus** (Gavault) — pick the one matching your roster gap (Magic or Wits).
6. **Chester** (Milkford) — random stats mean he fits wherever you are short.
7. **Arron** (beat the Dragon Knight) — closes Act 1 with a free 6th seat filler.
8. Acts 2–3: **Alwena first** (one-time offer), then Ligia/Childeric/Zolta, then the Act 3 monsters (Victoria, Edith, Ari).

## Solo Routes and Limits

Some knights are locked behind specific behaviour earlier in the run:

- **Oliver** requires sparing your attacker in the *opening* — a decision most players make before knowing it gates a knight.
- **Daguez** requires deliberately tanking the People — plan for it or you will never see the civil war.
- **Rufus** needs Scholars' satisfaction high enough for the Mystical Research quest — faction management is recruitment.
