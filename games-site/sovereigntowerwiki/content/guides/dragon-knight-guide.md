# How to Beat the Dragon Knight in Sovereign Tower (Act 1 Boss)

The **Dragon Knight** is the first major boss of Sovereign Tower — a "towering" figure who walks into your court in Act 1, and whose defeat closes the act. He is an **ancestor of Sir Arron** (of the Drakovic line), and the fight is wrapped in a quest called **"Dragon Knight's Ultimatum"**, which appears in **Cycle 7** (per intoindiegames.com's walkthrough and Bonus Action's Arron guide). Beating him hands you the act's real prize: **Arron joins your Round Table** — accept him when the Dragon Knight presents the boy and asks you to knight him.

## What We Know for Sure

- **The quest is "Dragon Knight's Ultimatum," appearing in Cycle 7.** The walkthrough advises completing **all quest requirements with a day to spare** — the ultimatum has a deadline, and leaving requirements until the last day is how players fail it.
- **The Dragon Knight is an ancestor of Arron of the Drakovic line** (per Bonus Action), and the fight carries story weight: defeating him is the moment the Drakovic legacy passes to your court.
- **Defeating him closes Act 1** and triggers the Arron recruitment scene.
- **Quest success depends on sending the right knight with the right stats, equipment, and skill matching** — the general quest rule, but with far higher stakes (per multiple reviews). Wrong sends end in failure, offence, or death.

## What Is [Unconfirmed]

Honestly: **specific boss tactics — exact stat thresholds, phase behaviour, and the ultimatum's failure conditions — are not yet documented by the community** at the time of writing. The game is 19 days old and the boss is mid-Act 1, which means most guides cover the lead-up rather than the fight itself. What follows is the preparation checklist that follows from the mechanics we do know — not a step-by-step boss strategy. When reliable tactics surface, this guide will be updated.

## Preparation Checklist

1. **Max out your best combat knights' armour.** The blacksmith repairs one suit per cycle — start repairs **two cycles before the ultimatum**, not the same day (see the [Economy guide](/guides/economy-guide)).
2. **Match stats to the quest prompt.** When "Dragon Knight's Ultimatum" appears, read the quest text for its stat requirements and bolded trait hints — a Strength wall (Daguez's 15, Victoria, Gothild) is your likely anchor, with Magic knights for anything arcane (see the [Knight Stats guide](/guides/knight-stats)).
3. **Brew potions for the gap.** If your best knight is one point short of the quest's threshold, Belladonna's Alchemy Room potions bridge it (see the [Tower Upgrades guide](/guides/tower-upgrades)).
4. **Keep affinity high on your sends.** A knight who goes in unhappy performs worse and may refuse the outcome — feed favourites before the fight (see the [Romance guide](/guides/romance-guide)).
5. **Do not waste Goberto here if you want Dullahan** — and do not throw Goberto away if you want his Almor transformation (see the [Dullahan guide](/guides/recruit-dullahan)). The boss fight is a natural place for a planned sacrifice, so decide before Cycle 7, not during it.
6. **Keep the Round Table full going in.** Fewer knights available on ultimatum day means fewer assignment options — plan your previous cycle's sends around having your best two knights rested.

## The Reward: Arron

Defeat the Dragon Knight and he presents his descendant **Arron** (per Bonus Action):

- **Accept immediately** — Arron joins as a knight on the spot.
- **Decline or miss it** — Arron later becomes available as a standard **10-fund Map Room invitation** (a cheaper fallback than the usual 25 gold).
- Arron's stats (per community tables): STR 2, AGI 2, CHA 3, MAG 6, WIT 7, LCK 8 — a "Timid" magic/utility knight whose Luck 8 makes him a decent wildcard send despite the low total.

## What Your Roster Looks Like at Cycle 7

Context matters: by Cycle 7 you are deep into Act 1 with a **6-seat Round Table**. A typical Act 1 roster by then is Angelica, Goberto, Gwendan, The Wolf or Silgur, and one Gavault knight (Brunhilda or Tarcus) — plus whoever you recruited in Cycles 2–5 (Ursula at Cycle 5 if you took her candidacy). That means your ultimatum sends are usually: a Strength anchor (Goberto after his Almor transformation is a favourite), a Magic knight (Brunhilda if you chose her — most other Act 1 knights have 0 Magic), and a flex send. If your roster lacks a Magic knight by Cycle 7, the [recruitment guide](/guides/knight-recruitment-guide) lists who can still join before the fight closes the act.

## If You Lose the Fight

- **Rewind.** Knight deaths and failed boss quests are exactly what the Demon is for — you keep your knowledge and can re-prepare (see the [Time Rewind guide](/guides/rewind-guide)).
- **A death here is permanent if you don't rewind** — armour zero means a knight is gone for good.
- The act continues either way, but the Arron route changes — and some knights' recruitments (like Dullahan's) can even *depend* on losses elsewhere, so a lost fight is not automatically a ruined run. It may even be a Dullahan run.
