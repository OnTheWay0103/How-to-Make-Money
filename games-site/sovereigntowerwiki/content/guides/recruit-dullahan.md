# How to Recruit Dullahan in Sovereign Tower (The Cost of Goberto)

**Dullahan** is the "Intangible" knight — a silent, headless-armour figure who arrives with the game's cruelest recruitment condition: **Goberto must die for him to be recruitable.** Per community guides, after Goberto dies, Dullahan's recruitment event can trigger in any act, and he arrives **two cycles later wearing Goberto's armour** (per Prodigygamers and Tpose Gaming). He is the game's clearest example of loss-as-content — and one of its best strategic puzzles.

## The Route

1. **Let Goberto die** — on a quest, through neglect, or in the Dragon Knight fight. There is no gentler route [Unconfirmed — no alternative trigger has been confirmed by the community at the time of writing].
2. **Wait two cycles.** Dullahan arrives at the Round Table wearing Goberto's armour.
3. **Invite him.** His event plays out like a normal candidacy — no quest chain, no faction requirement.

## The Cost: What You Give Up

| | Goberto | Dullahan |
|---|---------|----------|
| Stats (STR/AGI/CHA/MAG/WIT/LCK) | 8/1/3/0/0/0 (12 total) | 8/8/0/9/0/0 (25 total) |
| Signature ability | Almor armour transformation: 7 → 17 max armour, Perfect Armour (pulls damage off the party) | 0 Wits (perfect for the False Bank) |
| Armour | 7 | 7 |
| Meals | Favourite meals only | Accepts all six meals |
| Early game | Recruited Cycle 2, core tank | Arrives after a loss |
| Role | Party-wide damage shield | Combat/arcana hybrid |

**Goberto's transformation is the sleeper.** On paper he is the worst knight in the game (12 total), but after the Almor armour upgrade he becomes a top-tier tank whose **Perfect Armour protects the whole party** — community guides rate him among the best knights for the late game. Killing him for Dullahan is a real strategic trade, not a free upgrade.

## Why You Might Still Want Dullahan

- **The False Bank quest** needs a knight with **Wits 3 or lower** — Dullahan's 0 Wits makes him one of the three perfect sends (with Goberto and Daguez), and the False Bank is one of the most-repeated quests in the game.
- **He is a balanced hybrid** — 8 Strength, 8 Agility, 9 Magic covers combat and arcane quests, including the Magic 10+ lich/vampire thresholds.
- **He accepts all six meals**, making affinity trivial to maintain (see the [Romance guide](/guides/romance-guide)) — the cheapest knight in the game to keep loyal.
- **His total (25) is double Goberto's on paper** — if you need an all-rounder mid-game and already have tank coverage elsewhere, he fills the seat better.

## When the Trade Is (and Isn't) Worth It

**Worth it:** You already have a tank (Childeric, Gothild) and you keep failing Magic quests. Dullahan's 9 Magic and 8 Strength cover both arcs.

**Not worth it:** You are still in Act 1 with a 6-seat table. Goberto's Perfect Armour protects your fragile starters, and the Almor transformation comes online before most players reach the Magic quest wall.

**Test it safely:** Finish one run keeping Goberto alive (experience the transformation), then collect Dullahan in a **New Game Plus** run — NG+ is designed for roster collection (per Tpose Gaming). Or rewind after his death if you change your mind — but the [Time Rewind guide](/guides/rewind-guide) warns rewinding erases good branches too.

## How to Lose Goberto Deliberately (If You Want Dullahan)

If you have decided on Dullahan, engineer the death rather than leaving it to chance — a Goberto who dies *in the wrong place* may cost you more than a seat:

- **Send Goberto on a quest he is catastrophically mismatched for** — any Strength-or-Wits-heavy quest he fails badly. His 0 Wits makes investigation quests the natural choice, but be aware a failed quest also costs faction standing (see the [Factions guide](/guides/factions-guide)).
- **Let him face the Dragon Knight** in Cycle 7 — a boss fight is a plausible death that also advances the story (see the [Dragon Knight guide](/guides/dragon-knight-guide)).
- **Do not kill him by starving repairs** — a slow death from armour decay wastes cycles of his usefulness and may trigger the resignation threshold instead of a death event [Unconfirmed — resignation-vs-death edge cases are not documented by the community].
- **Decide before Cycle 7, not during it.** If you want both outcomes across runs, take Goberto alive through run one (his Almor transformation is genuinely strong), then sacrifice him in a New Game Plus run.

## Dullahan in Your Roster

He is the definition of a mid-game pickup: no chain, no cost, no faction damage — just a dead cheesemaker and a two-cycle wait. Plan his arrival around the False Bank quest window and he will carry that quest for the rest of the run.
