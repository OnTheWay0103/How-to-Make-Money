# Knight Tier List — The Honest State of Sovereign Tower Rankings

A quick honesty note before the list: **Sovereign Tower is 19 days old at the time of writing.** Real tier lists need play data, and the community is still learning the game. What follows is a *community-sourced* ranking — stat values come from Tpose Gaming's collected roster table, and the tier assignments are our editorial reading of those numbers. Treat everything below as a starting point, not gospel.

## The Data

## Why Raw Totals Lie

Quests demand **single-stat thresholds**, not averages:

- "Repel the dragon" needs **Magic 14+** — only a handful of knights qualify, and Victoria (MAG 13) *still* needs a potion or gear.
- "The False Bank" needs **Wits 3 or lower** — the best "knight" for it is Goberto (WIT 0), the *worst* knight on paper (12 total).
- Daguez (15 Strength, 0 everywhere else) is a monster on combat quests and dead weight elsewhere.

So the tier list below is organised by *role*, not by total.

## Our Tiers (Editorial)

### S Tier — Role-Defining

- **Victoria** (Act 3, trials). Highest totals in the game and her locked sword adds +5 STR / +4 AGI / +2 CHA / +1 MAG (at −3 Luck). She is the boss-killer; the only question is getting her through her trial chain.
- **Alwena** (Act 2, one-time offer). 46 total, never dies, never resigns, and can't be sent on quests longer than one cycle — the perfect intendant. **Miss her once and she is gone.**

### A Tier — Specialists You Build Around

- **Ari** (Act 3): 13 Agility — speed quests and tournaments are solved.
- **Edith** (Act 3): 12 Magic + 10 Agility — arcane and mobile.
- **Epicrate** (Act 3): 12 Wits + 9 Magic — investigations and rituals.
- **Daguez** (Act 2, civil-war route): 15 Strength — the single strongest combat stat in the game.
- **Goberto** (Act 1, starter): 12 total looks terrible, but his armour transformation in Almor takes him from 7 to 17 max armour with Perfect Armour that pulls damage off his party — and he is the best False Bank knight in the game.

### B Tier — Reliable Core

- **Gothild, Childeric, Oliver, Zolta, Ligia, Brunhilda, Chester** (wildcard: random stats), **The Wolf / Rufus** (fast mount, −2 cycle duration), **Angelica** (starter; uniquely unlocks The Wolf), **Dullahan** (0 Wits is a feature; arrives only after Goberto dies).

### C Tier — Situational

- **Gwendan, Silgur, Tarcus, Arron, Ursula, Gideon** — fine, but the game's mutually exclusive routes and seat limits mean they usually lose roster spots to A/B-tier picks.

### The "Never Resign" Club

**Alwena and Ari never resign** (per community data). If your run is falling apart and you need loyalty, they are the anchors.

## Caveats

- **Chester's stats are random and reroll on every read** — he is unrankable, and intentionally so.
- **Ursula is Immortal** — she can be re-recruited even after dying on a quest. In a run where knights are dying, she is worth more than her stats.
- **The Wolf's mount** (built-in −2 cycle duration) changes the *tempo* of quests, which no stat table captures.
- This list will change. Check back after the community has more play data.
