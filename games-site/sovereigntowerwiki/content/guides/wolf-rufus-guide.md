# The Wolf & Rufus Guide — Recruiting and Transforming the Wolf Knight

Sovereign Tower hides a two-stage recruitment: **The Wolf** (a literal wolf with "Wolf Habits" and a built-in fast mount) can be recruited in Act 1 — and then transformed into **Rufus**, a fully human knight, later in the run. Both stages have strict requirements, and both are gated behind quests in **Almor** and **Groveshire**. This is the game's most story-flavoured recruitment, and reviewers singled out exactly this kind of knight arc as a writing highlight.

## Stage 1: Recruit The Wolf (Act 1)

Per Prodigygamers, Tpose Gaming, and Whisper of the House:

1. **Wait for the "Fight the Werewolf" quest in Almor.**
2. **Send Angelica — and only Angelica.** She is the sole knight who triggers the **tamed outcome** instead of a kill. Her animal-loving trait is the key (she also loves animals per the IGN preview — the game's bolded-hint system flags this if you read the quest text).
3. Complete the quest with the tamed result → **The Wolf joins your Round Table.**

The Wolf's stats (per community tables): STR 3, AGI 7, CHA 3, MAG 3, WIT 7, LCK 1 — a **Wit/Agility specialist** with a **built-in −2 cycle-duration mount** (quests involving him resolve faster, which compounds across a run).

### The Alternative: Silgur

The same quest has a second outcome — choose the **kill** option with anyone except Angelica and you recruit **Silgur** (STR 3, AGI 8, CHA 3, MAG 0, WIT 8, LCK 1) instead. The Wolf and Silgur are **mutually exclusive** (per Tpose Gaming). If you want the Wolf/Rufus line, Angelica must be your send — and the choice matters all run, because the Groveshire questline gates Silgur, The Wolf, and therefore Rufus.

## Stage 2: Transform The Wolf into Rufus (Act 3)

Per community guides, two known paths:

1. **The Mystical Research quest in Groveshire.** Complete it with **enough Scholars' satisfaction** (the faction gates the quest — see the [Factions guide](/guides/factions-guide)). If you tanked the Scholars early, this path may never appear.
2. **Free-time conversation route.** With The Wolf on your roster, reach his **second free-time conversation** — the talk triggers the transformation. This is the fallback when the Scholars are unhappy.

Either way, the transformation is tied to **Belladonna the Witch lifting his curse** — the same Belladonna who runs the Alchemy Room (per Whisper of the House and Prodigygamers).

**Rufus** (per community tables): STR 6, AGI 6, CHA 1, MAG 3, WIT 7, LCK 1 — identical to The Wolf's stat line, but human. His trait is **Loyal**, which fits: he is the only knight whose recruitment is a transformation arc rather than an invitation.

## Why Bother?

- **The Wolf's mount** speeds up every quest he touches — tempo is real value in a game where the blacksmith repairs one armour per cycle and deadlines loom (Dragon Knight's Ultimatum, Ligia's lamp quest).
- **Rufus is a Wits 7 knight** — useful for investigations ("Suspects interrogations" needs Wits 12+, so pair him with a Wits specialist) and the Diplomatic Meeting (Wits 8+).
- **The Wolf/Rufus line is one of the few knight arcs with a genuine story payoff** — reviewers singled out knights with "genuine agency" and transformation arcs as a highlight of the writing.
- **Rufus's Loyal trait pairs with the romance system** — see the [Romance guide](/guides/romance-guide) for why loyalty matters in relationships.

## Warnings

- **The Groveshire questline gates Silgur, The Wolf, and therefore Rufus** (per community guides). Botch the beast quest in Almor and the whole line closes for that run.
- **Scholars' satisfaction is a requirement for the Mystical Research path** — if you tanked the Scholars early, the transformation quest may never appear; the free-time route is your backup.
- **Do not confuse the transformation with a new recruit** — Rufus is The Wolf's upgraded form, not a separate knight, and the seat count stays the same.
- **Angelica must survive Almor.** If she dies or resigns before the werewolf quest, the tamed outcome is unreachable [Unconfirmed — no alternate trigger documented at the time of writing].

## The Wolf vs Rufus: When to Transform

The Wolf's mount is the stronger *tempo* tool; Rufus's human form is the stronger *stat* tool (double Strength, same Wits). There is no community consensus on which is better — transform when you need combat power over speed, and consider keeping The Wolf if your run is deadline-heavy.

The mount's −2 cycle duration is most valuable against the game's clock-based quests: the Dragon Knight's Ultimatum (Cycle 7), Ligia's 3-cycle lamp deadline, and the Almor jousting tournament all favour faster quest resolution. If those deadlines are behind you when the transformation opportunity arrives, the stat upgrade wins; if Act 3 still has clock quests ahead, the mount may be worth more than the stats.
