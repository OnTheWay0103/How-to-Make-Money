# Sovereign Tower Price, Platforms & System Requirements (2026)

Before you hit buy — here is the full picture on cost, platforms, the demo, and whether your machine can run it. All figures are as of **August 25, 2026**, and this guide will be updated as prices and platforms change.

| Platform | Support |

The "is sovereign tower on switch / xbox" searches are common — the answer is currently **no**. Windows and Linux only, and the game's own minimum specs list SteamOS alongside Windows, confirming the Linux support is official rather than community-driven.

## The Demo

Two demos exist, and both are worth knowing about:

1. **itch.io demo** — released **January 16, 2026**; free, Windows, DRM-free, built on the Godot engine. This is the original "sneak peek" build that built early word of mouth.
2. **Steam Next Fest extended demo** — from **June 15, 2026**; includes **Act 0 (the prologue) and Act 1**, roughly **2–3 hours** of play. The extended demo introduced the branching story, new recruitable knights (including **Brunhilda**), the Demon time-rewind mechanic, and the **Bard voiced by Hildegard Von Blingin'** with her in-game song "There Stands the Tower." It also added localization for the first time in six languages.

The Steam demo remains the best way to try the game before buying — it covers the exact content the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) walks through, and progress knowledge carries into the full game's opening act.

## Languages

Six languages at launch: **English, French, Simplified Chinese, German, Korean, Japanese** (per the Next Fest demo announcement and store listings). The studio is French-speaking (Rennes/Brittany), which reviewers noted explains the occasional late-game grammar quirks in English text.

## Buying Advice

- **Try the demo first** if you are unsure — 2–3 hours of Act 0 + Act 1 is a real slice of the game, and it includes the signature rewind mechanic.
- **Steam Deck players**: it is Verified; the text-heavy UI reportedly suits the Deck well (per the verification announcement and PC Gamer's review metadata).
- **Wait for a sale if $19.99 stings** — Curve Games titles discount regularly, and the first-week 15% discount already established a $16.99 baseline.
- **Wishlist now if you are on the fence**: first-week sales were reported at **50,000+ copies** (per Curve's official launch announcement), and the game's third-party-tracked review count grew ~6.8x in its first 19 days. This one is moving fast, so sale discounts should keep coming.

## Getting the Best Price

- **Steam is the only official PC storefront** at the time of writing — no Epic, GOG, or console store listings have been announced. Buying anywhere else means a reseller key (see the platform table above for why).
- **Wishlist-and-wait works.** The game's momentum — 50K+ first-week sales, ~6.8x review growth in 19 days, active third-party discounting already seen — makes a 15–20% Steam sale highly likely within the first few months (per the launch discount pattern Curve Games set).
- **Regional pricing applies** on Steam, and the 6-language launch (including Simplified Chinese) means the Chinese storefront price may differ meaningfully from the US one — worth checking if your region qualifies.
