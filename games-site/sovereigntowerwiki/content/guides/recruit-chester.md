# How to Recruit Chester in Sovereign Tower (Milkford Goose Contract)

**Chester** is the game's wildcard knight — his "Jester" attribute means his stats are **random and reroll every time the game reads them** (per Tpose Gaming's roster data). He accepts all six meals, never gets upset, and can never be tier-ranked because his sheet changes between reads. He is also one of the easiest recruits to *break*: a single wrong dialogue option ends his route. Here is the exact chain, per community guides.

## The Route (Act 1)

1. **Accept the Cleankeeper goose contract in Milkford.** This multi-step quest chain is Chester's entry point — it appears in the Milkford area during Act 1, and the community guides treat it as mandatory for his route.
2. **Part 1 — send Angelica.** Chester's first quest prompt should be completed by **Angelica specifically**. She is the documented send for this step (per Prodigygamers and Tpose Gaming).
3. **Part 2 — read the egg description.** When Chester describes the eggs, the wording tells you who to send:
   - **"Shiny"** → send **Gwendan**
   - **"Delicious Aroma"** → send **any knight with a mount**
   - **"Bad" smell** → send **any knight carrying cheese**
   The description is the hint — ignore it and you risk failing the step.
4. **Part 3 — the dialogue trap.** When Chester arrives at the Tower to thank you, **do NOT click the "You're Welcome" option** — let the scene play out, or pick the line about the bird "looking for a little company" (per Prodigygamers). Choosing "You're Welcome" breaks the recruitment.

## The Dialogue Trap Explained

The "You're Welcome" failure is the single most documented miss in Act 1 — multiple guides flag it as the reason players lose Chester. The mechanic behind it is the game's dialogue system working as intended: Chester's route cares about *how* you respond to gratitude, and the "safe" polite answer is the wrong one. If you hit the trap, the [Time Rewind guide](/guides/rewind-guide) is your escape — rewind to the arrival scene and pick differently.

## What Chester Is Good For

- **Roster patch**: because his stats are random, he fills whatever gap you currently have. Need a Magic knight this cycle? Chester can be one. Need Strength next cycle? He can be that too [Unconfirmed — the reroll-on-read behaviour per community data; in practice players report using him as a flex send].
- **The six meals**: Chester accepts *all* meal types — feed him anything for the +1.5 affinity (see the [Romance guide](/guides/romance-guide)). No other knight accepts the full set except Dullahan.
- **Never gets upset**: he is the safest knight to send anywhere on a whim — a genuine luxury in a game where wrong sends cost knights their lives or their loyalty.
- **Zero maintenance**: no favourite-meal research, no preference tracking, no resignation risk reported by the community.

## What to Watch Out For

- **The egg-description step is mandatory reading.** Ignoring the hint and sending the wrong knight fails the contract step [Unconfirmed — the exact failure behaviour is not independently confirmed; per the community guides it fails the step].
- **His random stats mean he is unrankable** — see the [Tier List guide](/guides/tier-list) for why he is intentionally excluded from rankings.
- **He arrives mid-Act 1 when seats are scarce** (6 in Act 1). Only recruit him if you have the seat — you cannot dismiss knights, so a wasted seat is permanent (see the [Knight Recruitment guide](/guides/knight-recruitment-guide)).

## Preparing for the Egg Step

The egg-description step needs the right knight ready *when the contract asks* — you cannot reroute a knight mid-contract. Prepare before starting Part 2:

- **"Shiny" → Gwendan**: recruit Gwendan at her Cycle 3 candidacy and keep her available. She is a low-cost recruit with no prerequisites, so this is the easiest branch to satisfy.
- **"Delicious Aroma" → any knight with a mount**: mounts are equipment — the blacksmith can equip horses (see the [Economy guide](/guides/economy-guide)), and The Wolf has a built-in mount if you recruited him first (see the [Wolf & Rufus guide](/guides/wolf-rufus-guide)).
- **"Bad" smell → any knight carrying cheese**: cheese is a carried item — buy or acquire it before the contract step, and send a knight who has it in inventory [Unconfirmed — the exact item acquisition method is not documented by the community; per the guides the requirement is that the knight carries cheese].

Because Chester's chain runs parallel to the Gavault and Almor questlines, plan your roster around it: if you want the Shiny branch, take Gwendan at Cycle 3; if you want the Aroma branch, recruit The Wolf before starting Milkford.

## Chester in Your Roster

Chester pairs best with specialists: send your stat-locked knights on their guaranteed quests and let Chester cover whatever the cycle throws at you. He is not a carry — he is insurance, and the game's affinity system makes him the cheapest knight to keep happy all run.
