# Kunai & Kirin Boss Guide — How to Beat the Twin Fight

> Kunai and Kirin are the two-boss fight of Beast of Reincarnation — a melee-and-ranged pairing that tests your ability to split defense: **parry Kunai, dodge Kirin, never confuse the two.** You face them twice: first in Chapter 8, with the real fight — where you put them down for good — in Chapter 12 (v1.0.6, August 2026).

---

## Fight Overview

| | First Fight | Rematch |
|---|-------------|---------|
| **Chapter** | 8 — Worm Food | 12 — The Firmament |
| **Health bars** | Shared pool, two bosses | The "real fight" — tougher versions |
| **Shared weakness** | **Poison** | **Poison** |
| **Kunai** | Melee half — attacks can be **parried** | Same, faster |
| **Kirin** | Ranged half — **nothing can be parried** | Same, faster |

Their movesets stay nearly identical across both encounters, so the first fight is a rehearsal for the rematch.

> Note: early chapter tables placed this fight at ~Chapter 11; current walkthroughs verify the first fight in Chapter 8 and the rematch in Chapter 12. The rematch is the encounter players search for.

---

## Preparation

- **Poison everything**: equip a **Poison weapon on Emma** (Hunter's Sword is the classic) and **Poison skills on Koo** (Dokuzeri: Fang + Status Effect Amp) so both bosses take steady chip damage while you're defending or repositioning.
- **Don't split your build** — both bosses share one weakness, which makes this the friendliest twin fight in the game to prepare for.
- **Bring a good bow** — Kirin stays at range, and you want to chip her between attacks without committing to close range.

---

## The Core Rule: Parry Kunai, Dodge Kirin

The entire fight is one rule applied repeatedly:

- **Kunai's attacks are parryable** — her combos are slow and well-telegraphed, making them your FP economy. A clean parry rhythm here funds every Bloom Art you use in the fight.
- **Kirin's attacks are never parryable** — and several of them can **instantly kill you**. Every Kirin attack is a dodge-or-die check.

If you find yourself parrying a Kirin missile, you're already dead — learn to identify the attacker before you commit to an input.

## Moveset & Counters

### Kunai (Melee — Parry)

| Attack | Counter |
|--------|---------|
| **Giant weapon slashes** | Her weapon grows huge, then she swings three horizontal slashes — parry each; slow and easy to time |
| **Branch pounce** | Jump attack using branches (same move as Shidou) — parry or dodge |
| **Kick + slash** | Two-hit combo — parry both hits |

### Kirin (Ranged — Dodge Everything)

| Attack | Counter |
|--------|---------|
| **Missiles** | Dodge every one — a hit can instantly kill you |
| **Pounce attack** | Dodge on reaction |
| **Skid marks** | Hazards left on the ground — don't step on them or you take a thunder hit (placed hazard, not a direct strike) |
| **Laser** | Dodge to the side |

## Fight Strategy

1. **Apply Poison to whichever boss is aggressive, then swap attention to the other** — the ailment keeps ticking while you handle the second threat.
2. **Keep both on screen.** The danger of this fight isn't either boss alone; it's losing track of the inactive one. React to whoever commits first.
3. **Punish Kunai's slow combos** with clean parries, then spend the FP on Koo's Bloom Arts during her recovery.
4. **Use Kirin's ranged lulls** to reposition, heal, and reapply poison — her attack cycles are the fight's natural breathing room.
5. **Never chase Kirin through skid marks** — always route around them.
6. **Rematch**: everything above, but faster and with more health. Don't change your plan; change your patience.
