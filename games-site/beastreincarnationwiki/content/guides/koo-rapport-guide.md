# Koo Skills & Rapport Guide — Every Bloom Art Ranked

> Koo is the second half of Beast of Reincarnation's "one person, one dog" combat system. This guide covers everything verified about **Rapport**, Koo's **Bloom Arts**, and his **Root passives** (v1.0.6, August 2026).

---

## What is Rapport?

Rapport measures your bond with Koo. Higher Rapport:

- Unlocks **new skills to research**
- Grants **extra skill slots**
- Is checked by talking to Koo inside the **Cleanse Walker** (where you also research skills and items)

**Falling in battle lowers Rapport** — but it never drops below its current level, so each level is permanent once earned.

---

## How to Raise Rapport

| Method | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Pet Koo** | Do it daily at base — free rapport |
| **Feed treats** | Your treat pack auto-replenishes each day when leaving the Cleanse Walker; use the quick menu (down on d-pad) |
| **Feed food** | Replenishes Koo's hunger bar |
| **Shower with Koo** | At base — requires water, which the base collects automatically while exploring |

**Daily routine**: pet → feed treats → feed food → shower → leave the Walker. Two minutes of routine prevents ever being stuck without skill slots.

## Koo's Bloom Arts (Active Skills — Ranked)

Bloom Arts use **FP (Florescence Points)**, which fill when Emma parries. Issued from the slow-motion quick menu; can't use the same Art twice in a row.

| Rank | Bloom Art | Unlock | Why |
|------|-----------|--------|-----|
| **S** | **Sakura: Javelin** | Nushi Skill (Rangifer, Ch. 1) | Damage + Shock application + grapple mobility. Carries the entire mid-game |
| **S** | **Healing Bouquet** | Early research | AoE healing is scarce early — your main sustain |
| **A** | **Higanbana Roots** | Base skill | Groups enemies and applies Bind; interrupts bosses |
| **A** | **Dokuzeri: Fang** | After 2nd boss (Ch. 2) | Poison DoT — melts Nue Malefact and sustained fights |
| **A** | **Acid Burst** | Nushi Skill (Corvus, Ch. 7) | Koo's only Acid Art; core of the Acid build |
| **B** | **Bolt Shard** | Research | 100 Shock buildup — pairs with the Shock build |
| **B** | **Lay Landmine / Lay Flame Trap** | Pounce research | Zone control for multi-enemy fights |
| **B** | **Emergency Medicine** | Research | Emergency heal for Emma |
| **C** | Other researched arts | Research | Situational — check the Cleanse Walker research menu |

## Koo's Roots (Passive Skills)

Roots are passives in **three categories** — you must feed the matching attribute to unlock them:

| Root Category | Attribute | Verified Passives |
|---------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Incantation** | Florescence | **Status Effect Amp** (statuses build 2x faster) — S-tier; **Full Bloom Crit** (all hits crit with full FP bar) |
| **Guard** | Vitality | Armor buffs, HP recovery, Entanglement Gauge recovery, status cleansing; Mod: Battleborn (use weapon special effects); Mod: Healing Energy (cleanses statuses) |
| **Pounce** | Strength | **Taunt** (aggro grab — S-tier), **Detect Hidden Item** (Pounce 2+, reveals seeds/strongboxes — S-tier), insta-kill Pounce command, trap skills |

**Koo's strongest skills require completing two different root paths** — e.g., Pounce (Taunt + Detect) + Incantation (Status Effect Amp) covers utility and damage.

## Pounce Command (Insta-Kill)

- Command "Run Ahead" (**L2 + R3**) makes Koo instantly kill weak enemies
- The success chance is shown above enemies' heads
- **Doesn't work** on strong enemies or Nushi — don't waste it
- Also lets Koo **sniff out enemies, chests, and valuables** — level him up and he collects items too

---

## Koo Combat Rules

1. **Don't use Koo while enemies are stunned** — his attacks are less effective; wait for recovery, then land statuses.
2. **Multi-hit Arts with +1 Bloom on hit are free attacks** — cycle them for near-infinite pressure.
3. **Parries feed Koo** — the more you parry, the more Koo fights.
4. **Match elements** — Shock Javelin + Lightning Arrows for the Shock build, Dokuzeri for poison, Acid Burst for corrosion.
5. **Keep Koo fed and leveled** — his stats are separate from Emma's; a weak Koo is a liability in every boss fight.
