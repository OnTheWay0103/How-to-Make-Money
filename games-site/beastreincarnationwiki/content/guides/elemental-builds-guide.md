# Elemental Builds — Shock, Poison & Acid Full Loadouts

> The strongest builds in Beast of Reincarnation are elemental. Match affinities across your **sword, arrows, Spirit Stones, Koo's Bloom Arts, and Koo's Charm** to inflict your chosen status more often — statuses out-damage raw parry chains on every boss. Verified from launch guides (v1.0.6, August 2026).

---

## Build Fundamentals

Every elemental build follows the same skeleton:

```
Element Sword + Element Arrows/Bolts + Status Amp (Koo Root)
  + Element Bloom Art (Koo) + Element Charm (Koo) → Status stacks fast → punish windows
```

**Status Effect Amp** (Koo Incantation Root) is the backbone — it makes all statuses build twice as fast. Take it in every elemental build.

---

## 1. Shock Build — Best Against Bosses (S-Tier)

Shock paralyzes foes briefly, opening them for strong attacks and combos — the best status for boss fights.

| Slot | Piece | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Sword | **Thunderwave Sword** (AoE lightning) or **Fall Thunder** | Fall Thunder = better single-target Shock damage |
| Arrows/Bolts | **Lightning Arrows & Bolts** | Midair shots apply Shock safely |
| Spirit Stones | Homing Shots + any crit/attack stones | See Spirit Stones guide for the full set |
| Koo Bloom Art | **Sakura: Javelin** | Shock application + mobility |
| Koo Charm | Any **Shock** ability charm | Match the element |
| Koo Item | **Bolt Shard** (100 Shock buildup) | Guaranteed Shock proc |

**How to play**: Parry to fill FP → Javelin/Bolt Shard to Shock → punish the paralysis window with combos → repeat. Works on every boss including Corvus (weak to Shock).

---

## 2. Poison Build — Best Sustained Damage (A-Tier)

Poison causes passive HP loss over time; consistent reapplication is the key.

| Slot | Piece | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Sword | **Hunter's Sword** | The only Poison-inflicting sword found |
| Arrows/Bolts | **Poison Arrows & Bolts** | Ranged DoT layering |
| Spirit Stones | Status-oriented stones + Entanglement series | Keep the gauge full |
| Koo Bloom Art | **Dokuzeri: Fang** | The only Poison Art for Koo — unlock after Ch. 2's boss |
| Koo Charm | Any **Poison** ability charm | — |

**How to play**: Keep Dokuzeri: Fang on cooldown, layer Poison Arrows between combos, and let DoT melt tanky fights. **Nue Malefact is weak to poison** — this build trivializes Chapters 1–3's repeated Nue fights.

---

## 3. Acid / Corrosion Build — Best vs Armor (A-Tier, late-game)

Corrosion slows enemies and tears apart armored foes' defenses. Requires Chapter 7 for Koo's Acid Art.

| Slot | Piece | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Sword | **Hazebringer** | The only Acid sword found |
| Arrows/Bolts | **Acid Arrows & Bolts** | — |
| Spirit Stones | **Homing Shots** + Sharpshooter | Build Corrosion from range |
| Koo Bloom Art | **Acid Burst** | Drops from Corvus (Ch. 7, Lv. 38) — the only Acid Art for Koo |
| Koo Charm | Any **Corrode** ability charm | — |

**How to play**: Pelt from range with Homing Shots + Acid Arrows to stack Corrosion, then close in with Hazebringer combos once defenses crumble. **Taurus is weak to Acid** — if you have Acid access by Ch. 4, it ends the fight quickly; otherwise use guaranteed statuses (Shock/Flame/Poison) since Taurus rarely stands still.
