# Rangifer Boss Guide — How to Beat the Giant Stag

> Rangifer is the first Nushi of Beast of Reincarnation — a giant, Blighted stag that anchors Chapter 1 and returns with a vengeance in Chapter 5. Everything below is verified against launch guides (v1.0.6, August 2026).

---

## Fight Overview

| | First Fight | Second Fight | Rematch |
|---|-------------|--------------|---------|
| **Chapter** | 1 — Seedling | 1 — Seedling | 5 — Branching Paths |
| **Location** | Ogouchi Buffer Zone | Sealed Lair | Matsukawa Zone |
| **Level** | Lv. 9 | Lv. 12 | Lv. 32 |
| **Health bars** | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| **Weakness** | **Flame** | **Flame** | **Flame** |
| **Key reward** | Rangiferine Memories → **Nushi Skill: Sakura Storm** | **Sakura: Javelin** Bloom Art + Sakura Blade Art | Progression |

The moveset stays largely the same across all three fights — only speed, aggression, and health change. Learn the Lv. 9 pattern and the later fights are mostly rhythm, not new information.

## Preparation (Do This First)

- **Level check**: around Lv. 6–7 for the first fight, Lv. 9–10 for the second. If you're under-leveled, clear the Grizzly and Corvid Malefact elites off-path in the Ogouchi Buffer Zone for Amber and materials.
- **Upgrade your weapon with Amber** at the Cleanse Walker Workbench between fights — weapon damage is the difference between a three-minute fight and a six-minute one.
- **Unlock the Cliff-Facing Tower campsite** before both fights — it is the closest respawn point.
- **Grab the Chain Mail** from the strongbox in the tower near the Cliff-Facing Tower camp — it has the best defense you can wear in Chapter 1.
- **Find the Fire Arrow Manual** in the hideout in the bottom-left of the map (guarded by Golems) so you can craft Fire Arrows — Rangifer melts to Flame buildup.
- **Equip Charms with burn buffs** if you have them, and make sure **Recovery Effect Boost** and **Roll** are unlocked in Emma's tree.

**A free opening**: before the first fight, use Emma's hair to create a bridge above the arena from the camp, then drop down with a **Death from Above** assassination — it removes a large chunk of Rangifer's health before the fight properly starts.

## Moveset & Counters (Both Fights)

| Attack | Type | Counter |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Antler swipe** (1–3 hits) | Parryable | Your bread-and-butter: parry each swipe to build FP. Don't attack during the combo itself |
| **Antler charge** | Parryable (single hit) | Stamps a hoof, then charges with its head raised — parry or sidestep |
| **Rock throw** | Blockable | Digs antlers into the ground and tosses rocks — block or sprint to the side |
| **Stomp / ground slam** | Unblockable | Red tell — roll or jump, then punish |
| **Lunge** | Unblockable | Raises front legs and lunges; red spots mark the landing zone — dodge sideways |
| **Tree growth / branch trail** | Unblockable | Ground glows red before trees sprout — run or dodge to the side |
| **Projectile spike shots** | Parryable (difficult) | Guard or dodge; parrying them is unreliable — don't risk it |
| **Red-glow empowered combo** (phase 2) | Mixed | Four-hit antler flurry ending in an unblockable slam — play fully defensive until the glow ends |
| **Heal** (phase 2) | Interruptible | At roughly half health it spawns trees and starts healing — **grapple to it immediately** and attack to cancel |

**Blight imbue**: Rangifer can temporarily imbue itself with Blight mid-fight, speeding up every animation. Don't panic — the patterns are identical, just faster. The imbue runs out on its own.

## Fight Strategy

1. **Parry the antlers, always.** Each clean parry fills Koo's FP — and the whole fight is about funding Koo.
2. **Spend FP on Flame**: **Higanbana: Seeds** and **Higanbana: Roots** (which briefly roots the boss) are the fastest Flame buildup you have in Chapter 1. Fire Arrows from range stack burn on top.
3. **Punish the head-low pause.** After certain attacks Rangifer recovers with its head hanging low — that's your damage window. Use **Entanglement Overdrive** here to slow time and land extra hits.
4. **Watch the stagger bar** (yellow, under its health). When it's near collapse, stop parrying and commit — when it falls, unload your heaviest attacks and a full-FP Bloom Art for the multiplier.
5. **Stay close, but not greedy.** Close range keeps Rangifer limited to its swipes; backpedaling draws out the charge and rock throw. Get one or two hits between defensive actions.
6. **Use the bow when it backs off.** Zangetsu Bow fire at range is free damage — and Flame Arrows push the burn meter.

## Rewards

- **First victory (Lv. 9)**: **Rangiferine Memories** → unlocks **Nushi Skill: Sakura Storm** (attack 4 times, then press the skill button).
- **Second victory (Lv. 12)**: **Sakura: Javelin** — Koo's Shock Bloom Art that skewers targets and creates a grapple point — plus the **Sakura Blade Art** finisher for Emma. (Some guides call Koo's reward the Lightning Javelin; same skill.)

Sakura: Javelin is widely ranked as the best Bloom Art in the early game — Shock buildup, mobility, and damage in one — so this second fight is worth doing properly rather than rushing past.

## Chapter 5 Rematch (Lv. 32)

Rangifer returns in Chapter 5 as a beefier version with **two full health bars** and the same moveset accelerated. By this point you should have Sakura: Javelin II upgrades, an upgraded sword, and elemental options beyond Flame. The fight is a rhythm check: if you can parry the Ch. 1 moveset in your sleep, this is just a longer version of it. Keep Flame arrows equipped for the burn tick between phases.
