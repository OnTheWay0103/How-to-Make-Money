# Corvus Boss Guide — How to Beat the Crow Nushi

> Corvus is the crow-like Nushi of Chapter 7 ("Root Rot") in the Giant Tree Zone — and the fight is actually **two bosses back-to-back**: a Lv. 38 Corvus on Line 0, then a tougher Lv. 41 Corvus on Line 1 with two phases (v1.0.6, August 2026).

---

## Fight Overview

| | First Corvus | Second Corvus |
|---|--------------|---------------|
| **Location** | Giant Tree Zone — Line 0 | Giant Tree Zone — Line 1 |
| **Level** | Lv. 38 | Lv. 41 |
| **Health bars** | 1 | 2 |
| **Weakness** | **Shock / Lightning** | **Shock / Lightning** |
| **Deals** | **Acid** | **Acid** |
| **Key reward** | **Nushi Skill: Acid Burst (Nobudou)** + Nobudou: Burst Bloom Art + Blade Art | **Corvine Memories** + **Nobudou: Burst II** |

Both function essentially the same way; the second is tougher — higher level, a new Dive Bomb attack, summoned corvid adds, and a second health bar. (Early launch coverage reported Lv. 35 → 38; current walkthroughs verify 38 → 41.)

---

## Preparation

- **Stack Shock**: upgrade the **Fall Thunder sword**, equip **Lightning Arrows** for Emma and **Bolt Shard** for Koo, and use **Sakura: Javelin** — it deals Lightning, builds Shock, and after it lands you can grapple onto Corvus for aerial melee combos.
- **Must-have mods**: **Status Effect Amp** (statuses build 2x faster), **Enhance Bow / Enhance Crossbow** (ranged damage — Corvus spends most of the fight airborne), **Battleborn** (activates a sword's special effects), and **Shocking Vigour** (+3 Vigour against Shocked enemies).
- **Acid defense**: Corvus deals Acid damage, so equip the **Amber Breastplate** or another Acid-resistance armor, plus status-cleanse items.
- **Sustain**: Higanbana Seeds with the **Perennial Bloom** mod regenerate HP over time — the cheapest heal for a long flight fight.
- **Higanbana Roots**: when Corvus hovers close to the ground, Roots can pull it down and stun it briefly — your melee window.

## Moveset & Counters

| Attack | Type | Counter |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Acid bombing run** | Unblockable | Flies around with glowing red feet, releasing acid bombs that leave corrosive puddles — dodge the barrage, then use grapple points to escape the acid-covered ground |
| **Peck dive / lunge** | Dodge or parry | Pulls its head back and dives — dodge-roll sideways or parry |
| **Claw stab / talon combo** | Parryable | Three consecutive claw stabs — **the best parry opportunity in the fight**, refilling Koo's FP. Watch out: terrain can make parry timing tricky |
| **Acid globes** | Unblockable | Glows red, spreads its wings, launches three acid globes — dodge, jump, or grapple away |
| **Acid mines** | Unblockable | Glows red, plants talons on the ground, and acid bombs appear around it, exploding after ~3 seconds — sprint or dodge out of the blast radius |
| **Dive Bomb** (second fight only) | Unblockable | Soars high, glows red, then dives — parry or dodge-roll sideways |
| **Flying summons** (second fight only) | Adds | Summons smaller corvid Malefacts — take them out quickly with the bow before refocusing on the boss |

## Fight Strategy

1. **Ranged pressure, constantly.** The crossbow or Zangetsu Bow with Lightning arrows staggers Corvus, especially the first one — ranged fire is how you ground an airborne boss.
2. **Parry the talon combo.** Three clean parries = three FP bars worth of Koo ammunition. Do not mash through it.
3. **Use grapple points** in both arenas to reposition, dodge attacks, and escape acid puddles — they are your escape valves.
4. **Ground it, then chain.** Apply Shock, use Higanbana Roots at ground level, and break its stagger bar. Once grounded: chain Blade Arts, slow time, and pour Bloom Arts in to keep it down as long as possible.
5. **Second arena discipline.** The Line 1 arena is suspended in the air with gaps you can fall through, and its acid bombing runs are deadlier — fight on the long stretch of land rather than the smaller platforms.
6. **Budget for phase 2.** The Lv. 41 Corvus restores its health bar for phase two — conserve healing for the second half of the second fight.

## Rewards

- **First Corvus (Lv. 38)**: **Nushi Skill: Acid Burst (Nobudou)** — Koo's only Acid Art and the core of the Acid build — plus the **Nobudou: Burst** Bloom Art and a Blade Art.
- **Second Corvus (Lv. 41)**: **Corvine Memories** and the upgraded **Nobudou: Burst II** Bloom Art.

The Acid Burst Nushi Skill is the last piece of the Acid build: paired with the Hazebringer sword, it turns Chapter 8 onward into corrosion playground.
