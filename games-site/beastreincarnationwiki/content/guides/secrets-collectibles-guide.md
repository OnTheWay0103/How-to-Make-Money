# Hidden Secrets & Collectibles — Everything You Can Miss

> The verified secrets of Beast of Reincarnation (launch version, August 2026). The game rewards patient exploration: Dragon Shrines, hidden strongboxes, environmental hints, and a few genuinely nasty surprises. Late-game secrets are still being discovered by the community — this page will grow.

---

## Verified Secrets (Chapter 1–7)

### 1. Dragon Shrines — 10 Skill Points Each

- Dragon Shrines are collectible shrines hidden across zones
- Each shrine grants **10 skill points** — that's a meaningful chunk of a build
- Found off the main path; Koo's **Detect Hidden Item** (Pounce 2+) marks them on approach
- **Priority**: shrine-hunt early — 2–3 shrines fund a full early skill branch

### 2. Hidden Strongboxes (Unidentified Spirit Stones)

- Strongboxes hide **unidentified Spirit Stones** — their effects randomize when you rest at a campsite
- Some are only reachable with **steerable hair**: push the direction key while ascending to turn corners and find hidden platform routes
- Koo alerts you to nearby valuables — watch for his sniff notification and Emma's dialogue

### 3. Static Spirit Stone Spawns

- A few per zone: on the ground, near **Rusted Golems**, inside strongboxes
- Example: a **Resonance Increase** stone spawns near the **Daibokurin-mae fast-travel point**
- **Floral Wraps** (6-slot armor) is in a chest near the Map 3 bonfire

### 4. The Crescent Moon

The most infamous secret in the early game: **looking at the crescent moon is fatal**. The tutorial warns you — trust it. The moon sighting mechanic is tied to Blight lore (red spider lilies, Buddhist symbolism). Full mechanic details are still being datamined.

## Environmental Clues

- **Directions are etched on walls, signs, and floors** when the path is unclear — the game signs routes this way instead of a minimap arrow
- **Midair slow-motion** makes bow shots easier: gain height, aim, and land multiple shots while time is slowed
- **Hair traversal** isn't just for combat — every wall is a potential hidden route

## Koo's Discovery System (The Key to Everything)

Koo is a walking secret detector:

| Koo Ability | What It Finds |
|-------------|---------------|
| Sniffing | Enemies, chests, valuables — watch for notifications |
| Detect Hidden Item (Pounce 2+) | Seeds, strongboxes, unusual objects |
| Item collection | Leveled Koo collects items for you |

**Rule of thumb**: if Koo perks up, look around. He's never bored by nothing.

## Early-Game Secrets Checklist (Ch. 1–4)

- [ ] Pet Koo daily at the Cleanse Walker (free Rapport)
- [ ] Find both Dragon Shrines in Chapter 1's Ogouchi Buffer Zone
- [ ] Clear the Door Golem → **Zangetsu Bow** (don't skip it)
- [ ] Find the Chapter 2 Reservoir secret arena entrance (Lacerta's lair)
- [ ] Unlock **Detect Hidden Item** before leaving Chapter 2 — every zone after is full of finds
- [ ] Check off-path Elites (Grizzly Malefact Lv. 10, Corvid Malefact Lv. 10, Headless Golem Lv. 16) for stones

- **10 skill points per shrine** = early build acceleration
- **Strongbox stones** = free build slots without merchant grinding
- **Elite drops** = the best mid-game gear
- **Exploration skill points** let you unlock high-attribute skills earlier than pure grinding would
