# Chapter 2 Walkthrough — Creeping Tendrils (Ancient Civilization Zone)

> Chapter 2 ("Creeping Tendrils") moves the story into the **Ancient Civilization Zone** — ruins of the "ancient Kanto region" where Emma and Koo hunt their second Nushi, **Lacerta**. This chapter also hands you the **Thunderwave Sword**, the game's best early weapon. Verified from launch walkthroughs (launch version, August 2026).

## Objectives in Order

### 1. Leave the Cleanse Walker

**Upgrade your gear at the Workbench first** — fast travel is available, so this is a free power check before the chapter's fights. Follow the marker to the **first Dragon Vein Shrine** (atop stacked containers), then cross a locked gate using Emma's branch ability.

### 2. Wolf Miniboss — The Vicious Malefact

A corrupted wolf with slow, telegraphed swings — the ideal parry practice before Lacerta. Parry and punish; it drops the **Binding Fang Skill Amber**.

### 3. The Generator Puzzle

- Pull a lever in the **Control Room** to open a gate below.
- Take elevators and grapple points down to an **Ancient Golem**, who explains that the reservoir gate needs power.
- Fight the **giant red Golem miniboss** — slow but hard-hitting; as soon as it locks a **blue laser** onto Emma, start dodging (Koo's Sakura: Javelin works well at range). It drops the **Biting Tempest Nushi Skill** and unlocks the **Thick Cuirass** armor in a nearby strongbox.
- Activate **both power generator levers** to raise the sealed gate. (You can sprint straight to the second generator and skip most of its fight — your choice, but the fight drops the skill.)

### 4. The Reservoir — First Lacerta Fight (Lv. 13)

- Rest at the **campsite on the reservoir's edge** and grab the **Life Fruit** from the Dragon Vein Shrine to the left before stepping into the water — entering the water triggers the fight.
- Lacerta is fast but short-ranged: chip with the bow, parry the bites and tail swipes. It **retreats rather than dying**, dropping the **Lacertine Memories** and **Nushi Skill: Dokuzeri Shower**.
- Full moveset and counters: [Lacerta Boss Guide](/guides/lacerta-boss-guide).

### 5. Mikoto & the Lightning Stone

- Pull the reservoir Control Room lever.
- **Save Mikoto** from a monster — the elite Golem drops the **Lightning Stone** and the **Thunderwave Sword** (Tempest ability: lightning strikes on nearby enemies). This is the sword every guide tells you to get — it carries the Shock build for the rest of the game.
- Use the **Water Tank Control Panel** with the Lightning Stone to open the sealed doorway to the lair.

### 6. Ruins Slope & the Lair

Rest at the **Ruins Slope campsite** — the final staging point — then head to the **Nushi's Lair** (a short distance from the campsite). Consider fast-traveling back to the Walker to upgrade the Thunderwave and top off supplies first.

### 7. Lacerta Rematch (Lv. 16)

Two phases: the boss "fully heals and immediately enters a much tougher second phase." Budget consumables across both phases. Victory ends the chapter with a **Violet backstory cutscene**.

## Boss Quick Cards

### The Vicious Malefact (wolf)
Slow, telegraphed swings — parry, counter with short sword strings. Free parry practice.

### Giant Red Golem
Blue laser lock-on is the kill move — dodge the moment it locks. Use Sakura: Javelin at range. Drops Biting Tempest + Thick Cuirass.

### Lacerta (Lv. 13 → Lv. 16)
Weak to **Shock**. Parry Mouth Snap and Tail Swipe, run out of the Mud Pool, wait for it to fully surface before attacking. The rematch fully restores for phase two. Full breakdown in the [Lacerta Boss Guide](/guides/lacerta-boss-guide).

## What Chapter 2 Unlocks

- **Thunderwave Sword** — the Shock build's foundation
- **Nushi Skill: Dokuzeri Shower** (Lacerta) and **Dokuzeri: Fang** (rematch) — the Poison build's foundation
- **Biting Tempest Nushi Skill**
- Mikoto joins the story — she becomes the respec NPC later
- Spirit Stone basics (duplication arrives with later Cleanse Walker research)
