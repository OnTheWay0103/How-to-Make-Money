# Skill Tree Overview — Emma & Koo Full Branch List

> The skill system in Beast of Reincarnation has two separate trees (Emma and Koo) sharing **one pool of skill points** and one set of **attribute points**. This page maps every verified branch and skill as of the launch version (August 2026).

---

## How the Skill System Works

- **Experience**: Killing enemies fills the XP bar; each level gives **one attribute point each** for Emma and Koo across three attributes: **Strength**, **Vitality**, and **Florescence**.
- **Skill points** come from kills, bosses, and collectibles (Dragon Shrines give 10 each). Emma and Koo **share the same pool** — choose carefully.
- **Attribute gates**: You cannot unlock a node without meeting its attribute prerequisite. Don't spread attributes evenly — commit to the attributes your build needs.
- **Respecs** are possible at campsites after obtaining purification items.

## Koo's Skill Tree — Two Parts

### 1. Branches — Active Bloom Arts (use Florescence Points / FP)

FP refills when Emma **successfully parries**. Open the slow-motion quick menu (Triangle on PS5) to issue a command — you can't use the same Bloom Art twice in a row.

| Bloom Art | How to Get | Effect |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **Higanbana Roots** | Base skill | Groups enemies, applies Bind (mini-stagger) |
| **Healing Bouquet** | Early research | AoE healing |
| **Sakura: Javelin** | Nushi Skill | Solid damage, scales well, applies Shock, mid-fight mobility |
| **Dokuzeri: Fang** | After 2nd boss | Venom-laced fangs — **Poison** damage over time |
| **Acid Burst** | Nushi Skill (Corvus, Ch. 7) | The only **Acid** Art for Koo |
| **Lay Landmine / Lay Flame Trap** | Pounce research | Battlefield control traps |
| **Emergency Medicine** | Research | Emergency healing for Emma |
| **Bolt Shard** | Research | High Shock buildup projectile |

### 2. Roots — Passive Skills (three categories)

| Root Category | Attribute | Examples |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| **Incantation** | Florescence | Curse enemies, modify Bloom Arts, **Status Effect Amp** (statuses build 2x faster), **Full Bloom Crit** (all hits crit with full FP) |
| **Guard** | Vitality | Armor buffs, HP recovery, Entanglement Gauge recovery, status cleansing; Mod: Battleborn (use weapon special effects), Mod: Healing Energy (cleanses statuses) |
| **Pounce** | Strength | Insta-kills vs weak enemies, new skills, traps, **Taunt**, **Detect Hidden Item** (requires Pounce 2+) |

Koo's strongest skills require completing **two different root paths** — plan your Roots early.

## Skill Point Economy Tips

1. **Parry constantly** — FP from parries is free Koo damage; never waste it.
2. **Prioritize Recovery Effect Boost + Roll** on Emma in the first chapter (see [Tier List](/guides/tier-list)).
3. **Get Koo's Detect Hidden Item early** (Pounce 2+) — hidden strongboxes pay for the 10 skill points (each Dragon Shrine gives 10).
4. **Attribute points are the real gate** — a build's skill tree direction is decided by whether you feed Strength, Vitality, or Florescence.
5. **Respec when your build fails** — purification items unlock respecs at campsites, so experimentation isn't punished permanently.
