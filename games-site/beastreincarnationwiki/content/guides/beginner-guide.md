# Beast of Reincarnation — Beginner's Guide (First 5 Hours)

> Launch build v1.0.6 · Updated August 8, 2026

Welcome to post-apocalyptic Japan, year 4026. You are **Emma the Sealer** — an 18-year-old woman fused with the parasitic plant force called the **Blight** — and you have one companion: **Koo**, a white dog who is secretly a Malefact, one of the very creatures you hunt. Together you travel from the eastern countryside toward the Capital to slay the Beast of Reincarnation, absorbing the power of every giant **Nushi** you defeat along the way.

This guide covers everything you need to survive your first five hours — and avoid the mistakes that make new players quit.

## The First Hour: What to Do

1. **Turn on English audio** (main menu → settings) if desired.
2. **Read the tutorial prompts** — Beast of Reincarnation teaches through tutorial cards; skim-read and move on, but don't skip the parry one.
3. **Practice parrying on weak enemies** for 15 minutes. Not kidding. The entire combat economy runs on parries.
4. **Find the first campsite** (bonfire-style checkpoint) before anything else — you respawn there on death.
5. **Meet the first Nushi** — **Rangifer**, the giant wooden stag. Your first fight is Lv. 9; a second, tougher fight (Lv. 12) closes Chapter 1.

---

## The Combat Loop (Learn This or Suffer)

```
Enemy attack → PARRY (clean timing) → FP generated → Koo Bloom Arts via slow-mo menu → damage
```

- **Parrying** prevents damage *and* fills the **FP gauge** — the resource for Koo's abilities. If you never parry, Koo is useless and fights take forever.
- **Koo commands** are issued from a slow-motion quick menu (Triangle on PS5 / Y on Xbox). Time slows while the menu is open — it's free strategy time.
- **Down Gauge**: enemies have a stagger bar. Fill it → **takedown finisher** for huge damage. Track break progress, not just HP.
- **Unblockable attacks** glow red — you can't parry them. Unlock **Roll** (dodge x2) early and use it.

## Skill Priorities for the First 5 Hours

Skill points are **shared between Emma and Koo** — spend deliberately.

| Priority | Skill | Owner | Why |
|----------|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | **Recovery Effect Boost** | Emma | Up to +42.5% healing — healing is scarce early |
| 2 | **Roll** | Emma | Your answer to unblockable attacks |
| 3 | **Detect Hidden Item** | Koo (Pounce 2+) | Finds seeds, strongboxes, and valuables — pays for itself |
| 4 | **Taunt** | Koo | Aggro off Emma = free healing and combos |
| 5 | **Helm Splitter** | Emma | Aerial AoE that dodges ground attacks |

**Attributes gate skills**: Strength, Vitality, and Florescence points unlock tree sections. Don't spread evenly — commit to the attributes your build wants.

## Koo's Daily Routine (2 Minutes, Every Day)

At the Cleanse Walker (your base):

1. **Pet Koo**
2. **Feed treats** (pack auto-replenishes daily; quick menu, down on d-pad)
3. **Feed food**
4. **Shower together** (water is collected automatically while exploring)

Rapport unlocks new skills and skill slots. Falling in battle lowers Rapport, but it never drops below its current level.

## 10 Mistakes New Players Make

1. **Never parrying** — starves Koo of FP and doubles fight length.
2. **Skipping the Roll skill** — unblockable red-light attacks one-shot new players.
3. **Mashing attacks into red-glow combos** — Rangifer's 4-hit slam exists to punish this.
4. **Ignoring the Down Gauge** — finishers are your highest-damage window.
5. **Fighting bosses without scouting the campsite** — you respawn at the last one.
6. **Spending skill points evenly** — commit to a direction; attributes gate everything.
7. **Ignoring Koo's menu** — Bloom Arts with +1 Bloom on hit are nearly free attacks; cycle them.
8. **Using Koo on stunned enemies** — his attacks are weaker then; wait for recovery.
9. **Not using slow-motion** — midair bow shots and hair traversal are free safe time.
10. **Looking at the crescent moon** — literally fatal. Trust the tutorial warnings.

## Exploring Like You Mean It

- **Emma's hair is a movement tool** — grapple, climb, and steer it around corners for hidden platforms.
- **Midair healing works** — gain height with hair, heal, drop back in.
- **Holding the crossbow attack turns it into rapid fire** — great for ambushes.
- **Clear Keeper Golem objectives** before chapter bosses — they unlock stronger gear.
- **Campsites** are save points, healing stations, and (later) respec points with purification items.

---

## Before You Fight Rangifer

- Unlock **Roll** and **Recovery Effect Boost** first.
- **Rangifer is weak to Flame** — use Koo's Higanbana: Seeds and Fire Bolts if available.
- Parry the antler sweeps; dodge the charge; the red-glow 4-hit combo must be rolled.
- First win drops **Sakura Storm** (Nushi Skill); the second win (Lv. 12) drops **Sakura: Javelin**.
