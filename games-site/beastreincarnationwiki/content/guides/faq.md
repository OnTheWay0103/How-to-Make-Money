# Beast of Reincarnation FAQ

> Structured Q&A for the most common Beast of Reincarnation questions. Last verified August 8, 2026 (launch version).

---

## Game Basics

### What is Beast of Reincarnation?
A single-player action RPG by **Game Freak** (creators of Pokémon), published by **Fictions**. Set in post-apocalyptic Japan (4026), you play as Emma the Sealer and her dog Koo, hunting giant **Nushi** creatures and absorbing their power to fight the Beast of Reincarnation. Launched **August 4, 2026**.

### Is it multiplayer or co-op?
**No.** Beast of Reincarnation is exclusively single-player — no co-op, multiplayer, or PvP. The whole game is built around the one-person, one-dog bond between Emma and Koo.

### Is it a Soulslike?
It has Soulslike elements (parry combat, campsite checkpoints, boss arenas), but it's more accessible: three difficulty settings, a wide-parry-window Story Mode, no penalty for difficulty changes, and no death-run corpse runs. Reviewers compare it to Sekiro and NieR: Automata.

### What platforms is it on?
PC (Steam + Microsoft Store), **PS5**, and **Xbox Series X|S**. Physical editions exist for PS5 and Xbox; PC is digital-only.

### Is it on Xbox Game Pass?
**Yes** — day-one on Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass, with Xbox Play Anywhere and Cloud Gaming support.

## Price & Editions

### How much does it cost?
- **Standard Edition — $59.99**: base game + brown Shiba skin for Koo + 30,000 Amber
- **Deluxe Edition — $69.99**: adds the Oni's Hat, Big Dipper sword, black and brown Shiba skins, 100,000 Amber, and vegetable seedlings

### Are there microtransactions?
No in-game microtransactions were reported at launch. Only the two editions exist.

### Is there a demo?
No separate demo was announced. The easiest way to try it is Xbox Game Pass (day-one).

## Language & Audio

### Is there English voice acting?
**Yes, but it's off by default.** Enable it in the main menu settings — the game starts with Japanese audio (Yui Ishikawa as Emma). This is one of the most common beginner complaints.

### Is the text localized?
Yes — full text localization including Simplified and Traditional Chinese on Steam China.

## Combat & Progression

### How does combat work?
Hybrid: Emma fights in real time with a katana (attacks, parries, perfect dodges, executions), while Koo's **Bloom Arts** are commanded through a slow-motion menu. Parries generate **FP**, which powers Koo's abilities.

### What is a Nushi?
A **Nushi** is a giant boss creature grown massive from Blight corruption. You defeat them, absorb their power, and gain Nushi Skills — the core progression loop.

### What is Koo Rapport?
Your bond level with Koo, raised by petting, feeding treats (daily), feeding food, and showering at base. Higher Rapport unlocks new skills and skill slots. Falling in battle lowers it, but never below the current level.

### Can I respec skills?
Yes — at campsites using purification items.

### How do attributes work?
Each level grants one attribute point each in **Strength, Vitality, and Florescence** for Emma and Koo. Skill nodes are gated by attribute requirements, so commit to a direction.

## Length & Content

### How long is Beast of Reincarnation?
~20 hours fast, **25–28 hours average**, 30–35 hours completionist.

### How many chapters?
**13 chapters plus an epilogue**: Seedling → Creeping Tendrils → The Vessel → Stems → Branching Paths → Necrosis → Root Rot → Worm Food → Branching Out → Malefact → Budding Realization → The Firmament → To Bloom, Then Fall → Epilogue: Bearing Fruit.

### Is there New Game+?
Yes — after the ending, New Game+ on **Reincarnation+ difficulty** is available (needed for the Platinum/100%).

### How many achievements/trophies?
46 trophies on PS5 and 45 achievements on Xbox/Steam at launch. None missable, none difficulty-locked. Platinum needs two playthroughs (~30h).

## Technical

### What are the PC system requirements?
**Minimum**: Windows 10/11 64-bit, Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 3600, 16 GB RAM, GTX 1070 / RX 580 (8 GB), DirectX 12, SSD required, 45–60 GB storage. These requirements are from the official Steam store listing and may change with updates.

### Does it work on Steam Deck?
Yes — Steam Deck/SteamOS and controller support are listed on the Steam page.

### Are there performance issues?
Launch-day reports flag PC and Xbox as the least stable (FPS drops, camera issues); **PS5 is the most stable** version. Game Freak has said it will rework and patch the game after the mixed launch. Steam user reviews were "Mixed" at launch, largely due to these issues.

### How do reviews look overall?
Launch reviews were mixed. Praised: the Emma–Koo bond, atmosphere, parry combat. Criticized: story pacing, repetitive enemies, launch optimization.
