# New Game+ Guide — What Carries Over & Reincarnation+ Tips

> New Game+ is where Beast of Reincarnation's second half lives: the **Platinum requires it**, Reincarnation+ difficulty waits there, and it holds game-exclusive rewards like **Blossoms of Reincarnation** and a final blade. This guide covers everything verified about the second playthrough (v1.0.6, August 2026).

---

## How to Unlock New Game+

1. Finish the main story — all 13 chapters plus the epilogue.
2. Let the credits play.
3. Accept the **New Game+ prompt** using your completed save file.

Your completed save **gains a star marker** after the final boss, and you're free to explore and clean up before starting the second run. There's no rush — NG+ is available from the main menu whenever you're ready.

---

## What Carries Over (Verified)

The build-defining list is confirmed by post-launch coverage:

| Carries Over | Notes |
|--------------|-------|
| **Skills** | Both Emma's and Koo's skill trees |
| **Attributes** | Strength, Vitality, Florescence, etc. |
| **Weapons** | All equipped and owned weapons |
| **Enhancements** | Weapon upgrades and modifications |

**Not fully spelled out** (check the in-game transition screen): currency, consumables, ammo, crafting materials, and quest/key items — reports are inconsistent, so plan your consumables before ending the run.

## Reincarnation+ Difficulty

- NG+ forces **Reincarnation+** — a challenge tier **above Hard**: enemies hit harder, last longer, and aggression rises.
- One outlet reports it feels roughly equivalent to the first playthrough's **Normal** — so the jump is most noticeable if you played the base game on Easy or Story.
- **No trophies are difficulty-locked**, so you can play Reincarnation+ on the easiest available settings for achievement purposes.
- The game reportedly gets harder with each additional cycle — "the more you beat it, the harder it gets" — suggesting **multiple NG+ cycles** are possible (exact scaling **awaiting confirmation**).

## New Game+ Exclusives

- **Blossoms of Reincarnation** — Koo's huge-radius AoE Bloom Art, **New Game+ only**.
- **A final blade** — one additional endgame sword unlocks in NG+.
- **Remaining skills** — several Emma/Koo skills are NG+-exclusive research.
- **Keeper Golem recount** — previously visited Keeper Golems count again toward the 30-conversation requirement.

## How to Prepare (Before the Credits)

1. **Build the Thunderwave endgame loadout** — skills, weapons, and enhancements all carry over, so a complete build means a fast NG+. ([Endgame Build Guide](/guides/endgame-build-guide))
2. **Duplicate your best Spirit Stones** at the Workbench — the [Spirit Stones Guide](/guides/spirit-stones-guide) has the duplication setup.
3. **Max Koo Rapport** — Rapport levels and research carry over; the second playthrough is where they pay off. ([Koo Rapport Guide](/guides/koo-rapport-guide))
4. **Stock consumables conservatively** — since consumable carry-over is unverified, enter NG+ with a healthy supply banked.
5. **Farm Amber** — upgrades carry, but new NG+ weapons will need Amber to keep pace with Reincarnation+ scaling.

---

## Second-Playthrough Strategy

- **Run 1 (base)**: play on Normal (or Story to learn the parry system), finish the story, farm the Thunderwave build, duplicate key stones.
- **Run 2 (NG+)**: Reincarnation+ with the carry-over build — the difficulty is mostly a formality if you bring a complete Thunderwave setup; use it to finish any remaining trophies.
- **Platinum time**: ~30 hours total across both runs (46 trophies on PS5 / 45 achievements on Steam and Xbox).

---

## Trophy Notes for the Second Run

- The **"Reincarnated" trophy** requires completing NG+ — the only true two-playthrough requirement.
- **No missables** exist — everything is cleanable after the story, in either run.
- Koo and Kagura Rapport milestones ("Bestie", "Knowing Me, Knowing You") are natural NG+ pickups.
