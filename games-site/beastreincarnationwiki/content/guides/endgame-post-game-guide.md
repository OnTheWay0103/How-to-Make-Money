# Endgame & Post-Game Content — New Game+ and Beyond

> What happens after you defeat the Beast of Reincarnation. Verified from launch coverage (v1.0.6, August 2026); the post-Ch. 13 specifics will grow as post-launch guides publish.

---

## The Ending & What Unlocks

- **13 chapters + epilogue** ("Epilogue: Bearing Fruit") finish the main story
- Completing the story unlocks:
  - **New Game+**
  - **Reincarnation+ difficulty** — the game's hardest setting, required for the Platinum trophy
  - Post-game clean-up opportunities (missables do **not** exist — every trophy is collectible after the story)

---

## New Game+ & Reincarnation+ Difficulty

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **When** | After the credits — New Game+ is available from the main menu |
| **Difficulty** | Reincarnation+ raises enemy levels, health, and aggression |
| **What carries over** | Skills, Spirit Stones, gear, Koo Rapport and research (verify specifics after launch patches) |
| **Why play it** | Required for the Platinum/100% — the achievement list needs a second playthrough |

**Strategy**: run your first playthrough on Normal (or Story to learn the parry system), build out the [Thunderwave endgame build](/guides/endgame-build-guide), then use the second run to finish any remaining trophies on Reincarnation+.

| Platform | Count | Breakdown |

**Key facts**:

## Endgame Farming Loop

Once the story is done, the farming loop is:

1. **Amber**: equip a **Koo Charm with Amber Gain Up** (+~20% farming)
2. **Spirit Stones**: farm Elites (Grizzly/Lupine Malefacts, Headless Golems) and strongboxes; buy from the **Map 3 wandering merchant** (near the Refuse Wall Front bonfire); duplicate best stones at the Workbench
3. **Materials**: clear Keeper Golem objectives and static spawns each cycle
4. **Koo Rapport**: keep the daily pet/feed/shower routine for skill slots
