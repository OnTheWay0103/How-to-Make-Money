# Nue Malefact Boss Guide — How to Beat Every Nue Fight

> Nue Malefact is the most-fought boss in Beast of Reincarnation — a recurring, raccoon-cat-tiger chimera Malefact you face more often than any other enemy: a Chapter 1 flashback, three fights in Chapter 3, a paired showdown with the Male Purifier, and later rematches alongside Shidou (launch version, August 2026).

**Weakness**: **Poison** — every encounter. **Deals**: Flame and Shock damage, so gear Flame/Shock resistance where possible.

## Preparation (Every Fight)

- **Hunter's Sword** on Emma (obtained from the Storyteller Golem in the Great Forest) applies poison buildup on hit — your best weapon against Nue.
- **Skill Amber: Poison Fang** for Koo, and use the **Dokuzeri** Bloom/Blade Arts learned from Lacerta — poison is the whole game plan.
- **Poison Arrows** from the Crossbow or Zangetsu Bow keep the poison ticking at range.
- **Spirit Stones**: Momentum, Onslaught, Homing Shots, and Fluorescent Recovery are the community-recommended picks for this fight.
- **The hand-swipe combo can be parried** — it's your fastest FP refill, so treat Nue's melee strings as an economy, not a threat.

## Moveset & Counters

| Attack | Type | Counter |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Claw swipes** | Parryable | A long six-hit combo with odd pauses — the most dangerous attack in the set. Watch for the arm pulling back, then parry **deliberately** (don't mash); it ends in a slam that can also come alone as an unblockable |
| **Tail swipes** | Parryable | Spins twice for two hits — parry both (parry a second time after a short hop) or back away |
| **Red-glow leap** | Unblockable | Glows red, jumps high, then lands with an unblockable stomp — dodge as it comes down |
| **Tail growth / tail root** | Unblockable | Extends its tail toward you — dodge as it approaches |
| **Thorn shots / projectile seeds** | Parryable | Tracking projectiles — parry, block, or simply run left or right |
| **Lightning strike** | Unblockable | Tracks your movement — stay out of the electric patches and dodge when red light appears at your feet. Exploit: Nue stands still while calling lightning — stand next to its body and land perfect-dodge counterattacks |
| **Lunge** | Mixed | If its mouth glows red, it's about to lunge — parry or dodge into it |

## Chapter 1 Flashback

A forced fight in the Ogouchi Colony that **flees once reduced to ~half HP**. It's a scripted taste of the moveset — take the time to learn the claw-combo rhythm here, because the Chapter 3 fights reward it. First flee reward: **Ursine Memories** → unlocks Nushi Skill: **Rampaging Asagao** and Blade Art: Asagao (input: Square/X or E twice after Helm Splitter), plus **Asagao: Boa** for Koo.

---

## Chapter 3: The Triple Fight

In Chapter 3 you fight Nue **three times at Lv. 16**, and the damage you deal **carries over between encounters** — you only need to deplete roughly 1/3 of its HP each time. Don't burn consumables aggressively; keep the poison ticking and disengage cleanly. Second flee reward: **Erymanthian Memories** → Nushi Skill: **Creeping Kiku** and Blade Art: Kiku (R1 → R1 → Square on PS5), plus **Kiku: Bloom Strike** for Koo.

## Chapter 3: Nue Malefact + Male Purifier (Lv. 20)

The paired fight at the Great Wall Terraces. The two have **separate health bars** but conveniently **attack one at a time, rotating turns** — treat it as a controlled one-on-one duel and switch your target lock to whichever boss is actively attacking.

**Game plan:**

1. **Poison the Male Purifier first** with bow and Koo's abilities — he's the more dangerous of the two once Nue is down.
2. **Dodge his drop attack** from above rather than parrying it.
3. **Parry his grounded sword strings** — he has an obvious unsheathing cue before them.
4. **Bind the Purifier** with Koo's Higanbana: Roots for a safe punish window.
5. When the Purifier backs off and Nue rotates in, switch focus and get ready for the beast's combo rhythm — parry its multi-hit combo deliberately (roughly five fast hits).
6. **Interrupt the heal**: when the Male Purifier starts stealing/healing HP from Nue, **shoot him immediately with an arrow** to cancel it and stun him — conserve your ranged ammo for this exact moment.
7. Use Koo's abilities often for extra damage and the brief slow-time pause.
8. Keep reapplying poison pressure to both bosses.
9. Dodge the lightning cue — there's a low whoosh just before it lands.
10. **Finish the Male Purifier first**, then close out Nue as a single-target fight.

**Final victory reward**: **Nue Memories**, the Bloom Art **Erica: Strangle** (large-radius bind, high damage — a late-game staple), Blade Art Finisher **Blade Art: Nue**, and the **Black Blossom** sword.

## Later Rematches (Shidou, Fuji Frontier, Mount Hiei)

- **Chapter 6 — with Shidou**: avoid wearing Shidou down too far — he siphons health from Nue when low. **Don't fire projectiles at Shidou** — he can deflect them.
- **Lv. 35 (Fuji Frontier)** and **Lv. 48 (Mount Hiei)**: same moveset, higher stats, more adds. By now your Poison build (Hunter's Sword + Dokuzeri: Fang + Status Effect Amp) should melt the health bar — the fight is mostly a rhythm check.
