## Progression Systems to Track

### Cleanse Walker (Your Hub)

The Cleanse Walker is your mobile base:

- **Research** new skills and items (Koo's Emergency Medicine, item use)
- **Upgrade** the Walker so weapon upgrade caps don't stall
- **Talk to Koo** — check Rapport level and research options
- **Workbench** — duplicate owned Spirit Stones (needs Calculation rank up)
- **Bathe** — shower with Koo (requires water, collected automatically while exploring)

### Campsites

Campsites are bonfire-style checkpoints: save, heal, restock, and **respec** (with purification items). Scout the path to the next campsite before every boss fight — you'll respawn at the last one.

### Keeper Golems & Strongboxes

- **Keeper Golem objectives** before chapter bosses give stronger gear — always clear them first
- **Strongboxes** drop unidentified Spirit Stones — they randomize their effects when you rest at a campsite

### Nushi Powers

Defeating a Nushi lets Emma **absorb its power** and unlocks its Nushi Skill — bosses cannot be skipped. This is the game's core progression loop.

## Game Length by Chapter

- Chapters 1–4: ~8–10 hours (learning curve + bosses)
- Chapters 5–9: ~8–10 hours (mid-game, repeated Nushi rematches)
- Chapters 10–13 + epilogue: ~7–9 hours (final stretch)
- Total: **~20h fast / 25–28h average / 30–35h completionist** (incl. New Game+ for the Platinum)
