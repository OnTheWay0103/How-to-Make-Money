## Universal Boss Rules

1. **Every Nushi is fought at least twice.** Rematches add a second phase with a fully restored health bar.
2. **Elemental weaknesses win fights.** Rangifer falls faster to Flame, Nue Malefact to Poison, Taurus to Acid, Corvus to Shock — statuses (Shock/Flame/Poison/Acid) immobilize or melt bosses faster than raw parries.
3. **Parries build your offense.** A clean parry prevents damage *and* fills FP for Koo's Bloom Arts — never fight a Nushi without parrying.
4. **Koo commands slow time.** Open the quick menu (Triangle on PS5) during a boss's telegraph to issue a Bloom Art without taking damage.
5. **Bosses drop Nushi Skills.** Absorbing a Nushi's power unlocks signature skills (Sakura Storm, Charging Botan, Acid Burst) — the core progression loop.
