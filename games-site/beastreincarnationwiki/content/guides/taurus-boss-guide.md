# Taurus Boss Guide — How to Beat the Bull Nushi

> Taurus is the bull-like Nushi of Chapter 4 ("Stems" / Pilgrim's Rest), fought in the Pilgrim's Zone. Like Rangifer and Lacerta before it, you face it twice — and the lair rematch is the first fight in the game that really tests your ability to manage multiple threats (launch version, August 2026).

---

## Fight Overview

| | First Encounter | Lair Rematch |
|---|-----------------|--------------|
| **Location** | Pilgrim's Zone | Taurus's Lair (end of Chapter 4) |
| **Level** | Lv. 26 | Lv. 29 |
| **Health bars** | 1 (flees at ~50% HP) | 2 (two full health bars) |

## Getting to the Lair (Belfry Puzzle)

Before the final fight you must complete a belfry puzzle: **light the pyres in the order Sun > Earth > Moon** (the in-game note reads "Light the celestial bodies in the order they were born"), then ring the bell to open the path to Taurus's lair. The chapter itself opens with a swarm of Fanatic Golems before the first Taurus appearance.

---

## Preparation

- **Taurus is weak to Acid.** Equip the **Corrosive Fang Skill Amber** (found during Chapter 4), give Koo **Bottles of Acid**, and lean on acid-inflicting weapons, ammo, and consumables — the status staggers it faster and shortens both fights.
- **Range tools**: Taurus gallops constantly, so the **Zangetsu Bow and crossbow with elemental arrows** are your chip damage while it creates distance.
- **Sunrise Sword**: picked up near the Scorched Grasslands campsite, it fires flaming projectiles after parries — a strong option for sustained pressure.
- **Parrying is critical** — parries fill FP, and most of your damage should come through Koo's abilities. A dodge-only approach starves Koo and drags the fight out.
- **Death from Above**: in the first encounter, grapple up to the ruined bridge above Taurus and drop in with a stealth attack for a large chunk of free opening damage.

## Moveset & Counters (First Encounter)

| Attack | Type | Counter |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Spiked rocks / root slam** | Unblockable | Stomps the ground, sending jagged rocks erupting from underground — dodge sideways as red indicators shine |
| **Horn swipes** | Parryable | One to three (usually two) swings when its head tilts — parry each swing to build FP/Entanglement |
| **Ground thorns** | Unblockable | Rears up on hind legs, then stomps, popping thorns at marked spots — watch the red lights on the ground, then dodge |
| **Tail thorns / projectile bombs** | Parryable | Fires a barrage of spikes or seed bombs from its tail, often while retreating — parry, dodge, or block each (slight delay between them) |
| **Lunge** | Unblockable | Raises its body and slams down, summoning horns from the ground — dodge or run; parry timing is tight |
| **Horn Charge** (second fight only) | Unblockable | Grows a horn and charges straight at you — dodge sideways, then punish the **long recovery** — the best Entanglement Overdrive window in the fight |

## Lair Rematch Differences (Lv. 29)

The rematch is a different beast:

- **Three Golems at the start.** Use a **Bind Bloom Art** on the boss and mobs to stun everything, then focus melee/Blade Arts on the Golems to clear them quickly before they can heal Taurus or flank you. Leaving them alive turns the fight into a war of attrition you lose.
- **Blight phase (phase 2)**: Taurus imbues itself with Blight and **glows pink** — every attack is faster. The horn swipe combo extends to **four or five swipes** from alternating sides, and it can glow red before a five-hit combo (four rapid swipes, then a backing-away horn thrust). Parry each hit to bank 5 FP and punish the long recovery.
- **Removed tells**: the spikes/roots attack no longer has the pawing warning animation; roots may grow flowers at their base (dodge backwards). The horn strike can hit once, then Taurus dashes across the arena to follow up with a shockwave slam or tail projectile volley.
- **Bind is your friend**: interrupting Taurus's charging/repositioning with a Bind Art opens windows for Koo and resets the tempo.

## Fight Strategy

1. **First encounter**: open with Death from Above, then play the parry rhythm — horn swipes in, bows at range. When it flees at ~50% HP, the encounter is over; finish the chapter's objectives to reach the lair.
2. **Rematch, phase 1**: clear the Golems first (Bind + Blade Arts), then treat Taurus like the first encounter but faster.
3. **Rematch, phase 2**: the fight becomes a patience test. Don't overcommit to long combos — one or two hits between defensive actions. Parry the extended swipe chains for FP, then spend it on Koo during the recovery pauses.
4. **Use statuses**: Shock, Flame, Poison, Burn, Bind, and Weaken all help — Poison adds damage over time, Shock creates punish windows, Bind interrupts charges.
5. **Watch the camera**: standing under Taurus's head can obscure horn attacks and make parries impossible to read — back off a step.

**If you die before the second fight**: the Merchant is on the plateau above the Campsite, reachable via an elevator — he restocks acid consumables and the Corrosive Fang amber if you missed it.

## Rewards

- **First encounter**: **Taurean Memories** → unlocks **Nushi Skill: Charging Botan** (usable after Petal Barrage — a forward charge that closes distance and deals heavy damage).
- **Rematch**: **Botan: Chaser** Bloom Art and the **Botan Blade Art/Finisher** for Emma, plus Chapter 4 completion.
