# Lacerta Boss Guide — How to Beat the Giant Newt

> Lacerta is the second Nushi of Beast of Reincarnation — a giant, newt-like Malefact fought in the Ancient Civilization Zone in Chapter 2 ("Creeping Tendrils"). You fight it **twice**: a warm-up at the reservoir, then the real two-phase fight at its lair (launch version, August 2026).

---

## Fight Overview

| | First Fight | Rematch |
|---|-------------|---------|
| **Location** | The Reservoir | Nushi's Lair (Ancient Civilization Zone) |
| **Level** | Lv. 13 | Lv. 16 |
| **Health bars** | 1 (it flees instead of dying) | 2 (fully restores for phase 2) |
| **Weakness** | **Shock / Lightning** | **Shock / Lightning** |
| **Deals** | **Poison** | **Poison** |
| **Key reward** | Lacertine Memories → **Nushi Skill: Dokuzeri Shower** | **Dokuzeri: Fang** Bloom Art + Dokuzeri Blade Art |

The first fight's moveset is identical to the rematch's — treat the reservoir encounter as paid practice for the two-phase showdown at the lair.

---

## Preparation

- **Exploit the weakness**: Lacerta is weak to **Shock**, so bring the **Thunderwave Sword** (its Tempest ability calls down lightning strikes on nearby enemies) and **Lightning Arrows** for ranged play.
- **Koo loadout**: equip **Bolt Shard** (Koo auto-throws Shock damage) and the **Frailty Fang** skill amber to weaken the boss. Save the **Antidote** and **Recovery Tonic** for when Poison stacks up.
- **Poison defense**: the **Thick Cuirass** (high Poison resistance, earned earlier in Chapter 2) is the single best armor for this fight.
- **Bind helps**: a Bind Bloom Art (Higanbana: Roots) locks Lacerta in place for a free punish window — useful when the salamander summons its adds.
- **Stock lightning projectiles** — this fight has a strong ranged component and runs through ammo fast.

**Before stepping into the water**: rest at the Campsite on the reservoir's edge and grab the **Life Fruit** from the Dragon Vein Shrine to the left. Entering the water triggers the boss fight, and the campsite is your respawn point.

## Moveset & Counters

| Attack | Type | Counter |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Mouth Snap** | Parryable | Three-part bite combo, often finishing with a tail swipe — parry each bite to refill FP |
| **Tail Swipe** | Parryable | Turns and spins its tail, sometimes twice — parry or block |
| **Mud Pool** | Unblockable | Surrounds itself with a slowing mud pool, glows red, then sprouts damaging spikes — **run out of the pool** (or perfect dodge), then punish with the bow. Note: it has **no red flash** — you must know it's dodge-only |
| **Underground lunge / swimming jump** | Unblockable | Dives underwater, then leaps out mouth-first — dodge or parry as it surfaces. **You cannot damage it while submerged** — wait until it fully surfaces |
| **Swamp summons** | Adds | Trails you underwater and spawns small Salamandrine copies — run from the water trail, let Koo clear the minions while you focus the boss |
| **Purple Glob** | Unblockable | Backs off, glows red, and fires poison projectiles — sprint clear, fire your bow between shots. Also no red flash on the projectiles themselves |

**Red-glow rule**: both Mud Pool and Purple Glob are dodge-only. There's no parry check to react to — you have to recognize the setup animations.

---

## Fight Strategy

1. **Chip at range first.** While Lacerta is swimming or keeping distance, Lightning Arrows from the Zangetsu Bow or crossbow are your safest damage.
2. **Parry the bites and tails.** Each Mouth Snap and Tail Swipe parry refills Koo's FP — you need that FP for the burst windows.
3. **Punish the recovery pause.** After a bite or tail combo Lacerta rests for a beat — that's when you commit to your heaviest attacks.
4. **Manage the adds.** When Salamandrine copies spawn, put Koo on them (or use a Bind Art) and keep your damage on the boss.
5. **Phase 2 is a marathon.** The rematch empties its first health bar, then **fully heals and immediately enters a much tougher phase two**. Don't blow all consumables on phase one — budget them.
6. **Fast-travel between phases if needed**: the Cleanse Walker's Workbench lets you upgrade gear and top off supplies before the lair fight.

## Rewards

- **First fight (Lv. 13)**: **Lacertine Memories** → unlocks **Nushi Skill: Dokuzeri Shower**.
- **Rematch (Lv. 16)**: **Dokuzeri: Fang** — Koo's Poison Bloom Art, the backbone of every Poison build — plus the **Dokuzeri Blade Art** for Emma and the "Lacerta's End" trophy.

Dokuzeri: Fang is the reason the very next boss (Nue Malefact, weak to Poison) becomes significantly easier — make sure Koo has it equipped before Chapter 3.
