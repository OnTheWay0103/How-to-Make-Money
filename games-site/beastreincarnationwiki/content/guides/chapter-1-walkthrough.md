# Chapter 1 Walkthrough — Seedling (Ogouchi Buffer Zone)

> Chapter 1 ("Seedling") is the tutorial zone of Beast of Reincarnation: a blighted forest where Emma and Koo hunt their first Nushi, **Rangifer**. Everything below is verified from launch walkthroughs (launch version, August 2026). Expect 4–6 hours here on a first playthrough — it's the learning chapter, so the length is the point.

## Objectives in Order

### 1. Combat Tutorial & the Rival

The prologue is an unwinnable gauntlet: practice parrying and Koo's Bloom Arts, then face the rival **Shidou** — you lose on purpose. Take the tutorial seriously; the parry muscle memory carries the whole game.

### 2. Investigate the Village

Talk to a dying Golem who points you toward a subcity foreman. A **bear ambush** serves as the parry tutorial ("you need to parry twice to win"). Follow Koo's scent-colored trail, cross a ravine with Emma's hair, and reach **The Village by the Bridge** campsite.

### 3. Death from Above Tutorial

From a tower, drop onto deer, a bear, and an **Elite bear** — the Elite survives the sneak attack, so be ready to fight it properly. This is your introduction to stealth kills and the game's habit of teaching one mechanic per landmark.

### 4. The Door Golem (Miniboss) → Zangetsu Bow

Clear six Golems, then the **Door Golem** opens the green door. It's fast, sword-wielding, chains up to five hits, and favors long-range lunges — bind it with **Higanbana: Roots** and parry to stagger. Victory unlocks the **Zangetsu Bow** after a vase-shooting tutorial (hold to charge, release to fire).

### 5. First Rangifer Fight (Lv. 9)

- Grab the **Chain Mail** from the tower strongbox, rest at the **Cliff-Facing Tower** campsite.
- See the [Rangifer Boss Guide](/guides/rangifer-boss-guide) for the full moveset — parry the antler swipes, exploit the Flame weakness, and open with a Death from Above from the hair bridge.
- Victory grants **Rangiferine Memories** → **Nushi Skill: Sakura Storm**.

### 6. Cleanse Walker Downtime

Return to the base to upgrade gear and **raise Koo Rapport** (petting unlocks here after the Rangifer win + flashback — see the [Koo Rapport Guide](/guides/koo-rapport-guide)).

### 7. Flashback: Hunt the Pale Malefacts

A bear teaches the **Perfect Dodge** — parry-adjacent timing for unblockable attacks. This mechanic is mandatory for every later boss.

### 8. Subcity 03

- A Rusted Golem gives the **Manual: Recovery Tonic (S)**.
- A dying Golem gives the **Red Key**.
- Find the **Cirrus Sword** ("Clear Skies" — +attack power to Emma and Koo for 15 seconds). It's a Chapter 1 weapon that stays useful all game — don't skip it.

### 9. Blue & Yellow Keys

- **Blue Key**: on a metal tower near a small lake north of the entrance.
- **Yellow Key**: in a northeast cave past the rice fields, guarded by an **Elite bear** — fire is its weakness, so bring Flame arrows/consumables. The cave's back room holds the **Fried Egg** recipe.

### 10. Flashback: Ogouchi Colony

Door puzzle, chase sequence, then the **Nue Malefact fight (Lv. 16)** — scripted, it flees at ~half HP. See the [Nue Malefact Boss Guide](/guides/nue-malefact-boss-guide) for the moveset; the reward is the **Rampaging Asagao** skill line.

### 11. Final Rangifer Fight (Lv. 12)

The Sealed Lair — beyond the door sits a **Merchant** (Manual: Torch, Acid Arrows, Fire Bolts, Fortification 2 and Onslaught 2 Spirit Stones). Victory unlocks **Sakura: Javelin** for Koo and the **Sakura Blade Art** for Emma. Chapter 2 begins.

## Boss Quick Cards

### Door Golem
Fast sword-wielder, up to five-hit chains, loves long-range lunges. **Bind with Higanbana: Roots, parry to stagger, punish.** Drops the Zangetsu Bow.

### Rangifer (Lv. 9 → Lv. 12)
Weak to **Flame**. Parry the antler swipes for FP, dodge the red-glow 4-hit combo, grapple-interrupt the phase-2 heal. Full breakdown in the [Rangifer Boss Guide](/guides/rangifer-boss-guide).

### Nue Malefact (Lv. 16, flashback)
Weak to **Poison**. Six-hit claw combos with odd pauses — parry deliberately, not on mash. Flees at half HP. Full breakdown in the [Nue Malefact Boss Guide](/guides/nue-malefact-boss-guide).
