# Skills & Raids Gear Upgrade Guide — Enhance, Refine, and Optimize

**Skills & Raids Open Beta / Updated: July 27, 2026**

Gear upgrades are the backbone of character progression in Skills & Raids. Whether you are enhancing weapon damage, refining armor defense, or socketing gems for bonus effects, understanding the upgrade system is essential for surviving harder extraction maps. This guide covers every upgrade path available.

---

## 1. Enhancement System Overview

Enhancement increases the base stats of your equipped gear. Each successful enhancement adds a flat stat bonus plus a small percentage multiplier.

### 1.1 Enhancement Tiers

| Level | Weapon Stat Bonus | Armor Stat Bonus | Success Rate (Base) |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| +1 to +3 | +5 per level | +3 per level | 100% |
| +4 to +6 | +8 per level | +5 per level | 85% |
| +7 to +9 | +12 per level | +8 per level | 60% |
| +10 to +12 | +18 per level | +12 per level | 40% |
| +13 to +15 | +25 per level | +18 per level | 20% |

### 1.2 Materials Required

| Enhancement Level | Material | Quantity |
|------------------|----------|----------|
| +1 to +3 | Enhancement Shard | 3 per attempt |
| +4 to +6 | Enhancement Crystal | 2 per attempt |
| +7 to +9 | Pure Enhancement Crystal | 3 per attempt |
| +10 to +12 | Radiant Shard | 1 per attempt |
| +13 to +15 | Mythic Essence | 1 per attempt |

## 2. Gear Refinement System

Refinement differs from enhancement. While enhancement increases raw stats, refinement unlocks bonus modifiers and set effects.

### 2.1 Refinement Slots

| Slot | Unlock Condition | Bonus Examples |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Prefix | Gear Level +5 | "Vampiric" (lifesteal), "Piercing" (armor ignore) |
| Suffix | Gear Level +10 | "of the Fox" (dodge chance), "of the Bear" (bonus health) |
| Set Bonus | Gear Level +12, 2+ refined pieces | "Shadow Walker Set" (movespeed on kill) |

### 2.2 Best Prefixes by Build

| Build Type | Priority Prefix | Priority Suffix |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| DPS | Piercing | of Accuracy |
| Tank | Vampiric | of the Bear |
| Support | Blessed (bonus healing) | of the Guardian |

### 3.1 Gem Types

## 4. Upgrade Priority Path

Not all upgrades are equal. Follow this priority order to maximize your power per resource spent.

| Priority | Upgrade | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Weapon to +9 | Damage is king in extraction. Faster kills mean safer runs. |
| 2 | Chest Armor to +7 | Surviving one extra hit is the difference between extract and wipe. |
| 3 | Weapon Prefix (Vampiric) | Lifesteal keeps you topped off between fights. |
| 4 | Boots to +5 | Movement speed helps disengage and rotate. |
| 5 | Gems (Weapon slot) | Flawless Ruby adds significant burst. |
| 6 | Accessories to +5 | Lower priority but still valuable for stat smoothing. |

## 5. Risk Management

Upgrading gear carries risk of failure and, at high levels, destruction.

### 5.1 Failure Consequences

| Enhancement Level | Failure Result | Recovery |
|------------------|---------------|----------|
| +1 to +6 | Nothing lost | Try again |
| +7 to +9 | Material lost, gear intact | Can retry immediately |
| +10 to +12 | Gear level drops by 1 | Use Protection Scroll |
| +13 to +15 | Gear may be destroyed | Use Guardian Stone (100%) or risk it |

### 5.2 Recommended Safety Usage

| Item | Effect | When to Use |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Protection Scroll | Prevents level loss on failure | +10 and above |
| Guardian Stone | Prevents destruction on failure | +13 and above |
| Fortune Charm | +15% success rate | Always at +10+ |

## 7. Common Upgrade Mistakes

- **Enhancing everything evenly.** Focus one weapon to +9 before starting armor.
- **Skipping Protection Scrolls at +10.** One bad roll costs more than the scroll.
- **Ignoring set bonuses.** Two refined pieces with the same set bonus outperform unrefined gear with higher base stats.
- **Wasting Radiant Shards on low-tier gear.** Only use rare materials on gear you plan to keep for 10+ hours.
