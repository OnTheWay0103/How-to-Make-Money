# Skills & Raids Gear Guide — Rarity Tiers and Progression

**Skills & Raids 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Gear is one of the two crafted outputs in Skills & Raids (alongside skills). The confirmed facts are simple — gear exists, uses five rarity tiers, and is crafted from extracted resources — and this guide builds honest progression advice on top of them without inventing enhancement systems, gem sockets, or refinement mechanics that aren't in the game.

---

## 1. What Is Confirmed About Gear

- **Gear exists** with rarity tiers **Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary** (confirmed on the Steam page).
- **Gear is crafted** from resources extracted from enemies (confirmed).
- **Quest unlocks** can provide new equipment (confirmed — completing quests unlocks "new equipment and skills").
- **Heroes and roles are fixed:** Shelldon tanks, Clawrk damages, Owlivia heals — gear should follow those roles.

**What is NOT confirmed (and therefore not covered with fake numbers):**
- Weapon types (swords, bows, staves...): no verified list exists.
- Armor slots (helmet, chest, boots...): unconfirmed.
- Stat systems (ATK/DEF/HP numbers): unconfirmed.
- Any enhancement/upgrade system: **not confirmed** — the game's confirmed gear progression is crafting new gear, not upgrading existing pieces.
- Gem sockets, set bonuses, refinement: **not confirmed** — guides that describe these are describing a different game.

---

## 2. The Gear Progression Path (Confirmed-Safe)

Since gear progression is crafting + quest unlocks, the path looks like this:

| Stage | What You Have | How to Progress |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| Early | Common gear, whatever drops | Complete early quests (11 quests in the demo show the early curve) |
| Early-Mid | Common + Uncommon | Craft Uncommon upgrades for core slots (heal, mitigation, damage) |
| Mid | Uncommon + Rare | Deeper biome runs (5 biomes in the full game) + quest unlocks |
| Late | Rare + Epic | Late quests and the hardest combat areas |
| Endgame | Epic + Legendary | The final quests — including the content around "The Edge of the Abyss" |

This is deliberately conservative: the exact pace is unconfirmed, but the shape (craft → quest → deeper content) follows the confirmed systems.

---

## 3. Upgrade Priority by Hero

Rarity color matters less than role fit. Upgrade in this order:

| Priority | Slot | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | Owlivia's gear (healing) | The healer is the run's foundation |
| 2 | Shelldon's gear (defense) | The tank converts gear into absorbed damage |
| 3 | Clawrk's gear (damage) | Fights must end before resources run out |
| 4 | Synergy enablers | Debuff/status gear if it feeds a synergy |

**The rule:** spend crafting resources on the hero whose failure ends runs — that's Owlivia first, always.

---

## 4. Rarity Strategy

- **Higher rarity = better base stats** is the safe assumption (unconfirmed exact numbers).
- **Effect fit beats color.** A Common item that serves the build beats a Rare that doesn't.
- **Don't discard lower-rarity items that have useful effects** — swap them in for quests that need specific abilities or statuses.
- **Legendary is the top tier** — if one drops or is craftable, center the build around it.

---

## 5. Gear and Quests

Quests unlock "new equipment and skills" (confirmed). Two implications:

1. **Quest progress is gear progress.** If you're stuck, check whether an active quest unlocks a gear reward.
2. **Resource-collection quests compete with crafting.** Decide per run: craft now, or save for the quest? Generally, complete the quest — the reward is usually worth more than the resources.

---

## 6. Common Gear Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Upgrading rarity for looks | A flashy Rare that doesn't fit the role underperforms | Fit first, rarity second |
| Ignoring the healer's gear | Owlivia dies, run ends | Gear Owlivia first |
| Hoarding resources | They're useless until crafted | Convert between runs |
| Following fake enhancement guides | The game has no confirmed upgrade system | Craft new gear instead |
| No gear plan per quest | Quest requirements change needs | Re-equip between runs |
