# Why Your Skills & Raids Cooldowns Got Longer — The August Skill Rework Explained

If you played Skills & Raids at launch, or you are following a guide written in late July, the game you return to does not play the same. The **Big Skill Rework Update on August 2, 2026** changed cooldowns, cast times, damage, healing, mana costs and enemy behaviour in one pass. The developer's stated goal was to **slow combat down** so players think about skill synergies instead of spamming buttons. [Official]

That single sentence explains most of the changes. The rework is not a random balance pass; it is a deliberate attempt to turn a two-slot loadout into a timing decision. This guide breaks down what actually changed and what it means for the six abilities you bring into a run.

## The design goal, in the developer's words

The announcement opens with the intent: "The goal of these changes is to slow down combat so it allows players to think about skill synergies and strategy a little more and reduce the skill spamming issues." [Official]

Skills & Raids gives each hero **two active abilities**, so your entire party runs six buttons. When cooldowns are short, the optimal play collapses into pressing whatever is available. When cooldowns are long, the question becomes which button you save for the next enemy intent. The rework is trying to move the game from the first state to the second. [Official]

An independent review of the game describes the UI as keeping cooldown timers, buffs, debuffs and enemy intent readable at a glance. That readability matters more after the rework, because you now have longer dead windows to plan through. [Community]

## The new cooldown bands

The rework did not give every skill the same cooldown. It created three practical bands.

### 10-second cooldown

Twenty-two skills were pushed to **10 seconds**:

Fury Strike, Gods Blessings, Rupture, Shield Burst, Spear Thrust, Venom Execute, Toxic Shield, Spreading Plague, Septic Assault, Harvest Decay, Freeze Frenzy, Ice Bash, Purifying Chill, Thunder Strike, Conductive Surge, Shock Guard, Blighted Scar, Decay Wound, Profaned Oath, Eraser Bite, Eye of the Abyss and Ferocious Feast. [Official]

This is the "big button" band. A 10-second cooldown means the skill is available roughly once per meaningful enemy cycle, not once per exchange. If you spend it on a trivial target, you will not have it for the next real threat. [Editorial]

### 6-second cooldown

Five sustain skills were set to **6 seconds**:

Holy Surge, Putrid Mend, Frost Fang Strike, Life Drain and Touch of Winter. [Official]

The shorter band is deliberate. These are the buttons that keep a run alive, and a 10-second heal would make attrition unwinnable. The rework slows offense and rotation without removing the healer's ability to respond. [Editorial]

### Skills outside the two lists

The announcement does not assign every one of the game's 30 skills to a band. Some skills kept their existing cooldowns or were changed only through damage, healing or cast time. Treat any complete cooldown table for all 30 skills as unconfirmed unless it matches your current build. [Editorial]

## Cast times changed too

Cooldowns are only half the pacing change. The rework also set cast times, which determines how long you are committed before the skill resolves.

| Cast time | Skills |
|---|---|
| **0.5 seconds** | Fury Strike, Spear Thrust, Holy Surge, Putrid Mend, Spreading Plague, Venom Execute, Freeze Frenzy, Frost Fang Strike, Ice Bash, Touch of Winter, Conductive Surge, Fury of the Storm, Thunder Strike, Blighted Scar, Decay Wound, Life Drain |
| **1.5 seconds** | Shield Burst, Purifying Chill, Haste Voltage, Shock Guard, Static Renewal, Corrupted Shield, Profaned Oath, Rot Bloom |
| **1 second** | Rupture (explicitly set in the announcement) |

The pattern is role-based. Fast, direct skills resolve in half a second; defensive and high-impact skills take a second and a half. That makes the 1.5-second band the punishable one — if you start a Shield Burst or Purifying Chill at the wrong moment, the enemy has a real window to act. [Official]

## Damage and healing changes

The rework was not purely defensive. Several skills received compensatory buffs so that longer cooldowns did not simply make them worse.

- **Fury Strike** now deals **four consecutive hits**, changing it from a single strike into a short channel of damage.
- **Rupture** received increased base damage and a **1-second cast time**.
- **Gods Blessings** and **Holy Surge** heal for more.
- **Putrid Mend** and **Healing Decay** healing increased.
- **Toxic Shield** shield amount increased.
- **Frost Fang Strike**, **Ice Bash** and **Freeze Frenzy** damage increased.
- **Purifying Chill** healing increased.
- **Touch of Winter** healing **decreased** — the one sustain skill explicitly nerfed in the pass.
- **Decay Wound** bleeding damage increased. [Official]

The shape is clear: the developers raised the payoff of the long-cooldown buttons and raised the healing of most sustain tools, while trimming Touch of Winter, which was presumably overperforming relative to its new 6-second cooldown. [Editorial]

## The supporting changes nobody talks about

A pacing rework only works if the surrounding economy supports it, and the same patch adjusted several systems:

- **Mana:** the mana cost of all skills was tweaked, the mana cost of Eternal Dunes skills was reduced, and mana gained from gear was reduced. The last change is the important one — you can no longer solve rotation problems by stacking mana gear. [Official]
- **Enemies:** Cold Peaks base damage and Eternal Dunes base stats were increased, The Corrupter attacks faster and hits harder, and enemy AI was adjusted so enemies do not use all their skills at the same time. The AI change is a pacing fix, not just a difficulty one. [Official]
- **Drops:** Core Threads drop chance increased, and skill drop chance increased in Cold Peaks, Sinking Graves and Edge of the Abyss. Longer cooldowns make each skill more valuable, so the patch also gives you more chances to find them. [Official]
- **Dodge and evasion:** dodge chance on items decreased, and evasion from Spreading Plague decreased. [Official]
- **Bug fix:** enemy skills were incorrectly triggering the player's cooldown-end function; that is fixed. [Official]

That last bug fix is worth pausing on. If enemy skills could end your cooldowns early, pre-rework cooldown behaviour was not just short — it was inconsistent. The rework's 10-second bands are the first version of the system that behaves the same way in every fight. [Editorial]

## What this means for your two-slot loadouts

With two abilities per hero, the rework changes the pairing rule.

**Do not pair two 10-second panic buttons.** If both slots are long-cooldown defensive tools, you have a dead window in every extended fight. Pair one long cooldown with a 6-second sustain skill or a fast 0.5-second cast skill. [Editorial]

**Respect the 1.5-second cast band.** Shield Burst, Purifying Chill, Shock Guard, Static Renewal, Corrupted Shield, Profaned Oath and Rot Bloom all commit you for a second and a half. Start them before the enemy commits, not after. [Official]

**Use the pause mechanic to read cooldowns.** The game lets you pause to inspect skill and status descriptions. After the rework, checking whether your 10-second button is available before you commit is a real decision, not a formality. [Community]

**Rebuild the healer around the 6-second band.** Holy Surge, Putrid Mend, Life Drain and Touch of Winter are the skills the rework deliberately kept responsive. A healer loadout that ignores them is fighting the patch. [Editorial]

## What the hotfixes changed after the rework

The rework landed on August 2, and the following week was fixes rather than further balance:

| Date | Patch | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-03 | v1.3.1 | Fixed a retreat bug during combat |
| 2026-08-03 | v1.3.2 | Fixed character selection after a run in Emberwatch |
| 2026-08-06 | Minor fix | Updated Fury Strike and Haste Voltage text; hid cooldown VFX on the cursor when a selected skill is unavailable |
| 2026-08-10 | v1.3.5 | Fixed a quest not appearing if the game was closed during the intro lore; enlarged the Delete Progress button |

None of those hotfixes announced additional cooldown or cast-time changes, so the August 2 numbers are still the best public reference. They are not guaranteed to be the live values forever; the developer can tune them without a headline patch. [Official]

## What is still unconfirmed

The full 30-skill roster is not publicly documented, so there is no complete, current cooldown table for every skill. The rework announcement names 22 skills at 10 seconds and five at 6 seconds; the remaining skills are either unchanged or documented only in-game. Any guide that claims to know all 30 values without a build reference is guessing. [Editorial]

It is also not confirmed whether later hotfixes changed individual values silently. The patch notes do not list any, but patch notes are not a complete diff. [Editorial]

## FAQ

**Did every skill get a 10-second cooldown?**
No. Twenty-two skills were explicitly set to 10 seconds, and five sustain skills were set to 6 seconds. The rest were not listed in those bands. [Official]

**Why did the developer slow combat down?**
To encourage skill synergies and strategy and to reduce skill spamming. That is the stated goal of the August 2 rework. [Official]

**Which skills are safest to build around now?**
The 6-second sustain band — Holy Surge, Putrid Mend, Frost Fang Strike, Life Drain and Touch of Winter — is the most responsive group. Pair them with one 10-second impact skill rather than stacking two long cooldowns. [Editorial]

**Did the rework change mana?**
Yes. Mana costs for all skills were tweaked, Eternal Dunes skill costs were reduced, and mana gained from gear was reduced. [Official]
