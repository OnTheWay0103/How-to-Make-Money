# Skills & Raids Characters Guide — Shelldon, Clawrk & Owlivia

**Updated: August 11, 2026**

Skills & Raids has no character creator and no class selection — your party is fixed, and that's by design. You lead three animal heroes on every run: **Shelldon** the turtle, **Clawrk** the tiger, and **Owlivia** the owl. Together they form the classic tank / damage / healer triangle, which means your entire build game is about the abilities they carry and how those abilities combine. This guide covers everything publicly known about each hero — their confirmed roles, what press coverage has revealed, and how to build them.

---

## 1. The Fixed Party: Why It Matters

The three heroes are the game's only playable characters (confirmed in the Latin American Games Showcase coverage and Steam Next Fest 2026 demo reviews). Because you never swap the roster, three consequences follow:

1. **Your "team composition" is your loadout.** With the party fixed, the only composition decision is which two abilities each hero carries — six slots total.
2. **Roles are stable.** Every build is a variation on "turtle tanks, tiger damages, owl heals." There's no build that turns Clawrk into a healer.
3. **Hero knowledge is permanent value.** Learning each hero's job transfers to every future run.

The party structure is also the game's difficulty dial: the developer's design assumes this triangle, and the hardest content (per demo-era coverage) punishes parties that lose any leg of it — especially the healer.

---

## 2. Shelldon — The Turtle Tank

**Confirmed:** Shelldon is a turtle who serves as the team's tank (Steam Next Fest 2026 demo review, thinksofathoughter.com).

### The Role

Shelldon is the party's damage buffer. Every hit he absorbs is a hit Owlivia doesn't have to heal off Clawrk — which is a turn Clawrk gets to spend killing instead of surviving. In a game where status effects and cooldowns decide fights, the tank is the one who converts "reaction time" into "recoverable mistakes."

### How to Build Shelldon

Two ability slots, two jobs to cover. The confirmed-safe allocation:

| Slot | Function | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Mitigation** (shield, defense buff, damage reduction) | The core tank job — turn incoming damage down |
| 2 | **Threat / aggro control, self-sustain, or debuff** | Threat keeps enemies off the party; self-sustain makes the healer's job easier; debuffs turn the front line into a synergy engine |

**Build variants:**

- **Classic Turtle** (mitigation + threat): maximum protection for the party. The default.
- **Self-Reliant** (mitigation + self-heal): lets Owlivia focus on Clawrk. Great for deep runs.
- **Debuff Tank** (mitigation + debuff): Shelldon applies debuffs that Clawrk's damage then exploits — the party's best answer to tanky enemy teams.

### Playstyle Notes

- Keep mitigation rolling — reapply on cooldown when pressure is up.
- Position yourself between enemies and the rest of the party (positioning is a confirmed mechanic).
- Remember: the tank's HP is the party's shared budget. Spend it deliberately.

## 3. Clawrk — The Tiger DPS

**Confirmed:** Clawrk is a tiger who fills the DPS role (Steam Next Fest 2026 demo review).

### The Role

Clawrk is the fight-ender. In a game where resource pools run dry and long fights let status effects stack, damage output is a survival mechanic: the faster fights end, the less the party has to survive. Clawrk is also the counter to the game's hardest enemy teams — demo reviewers hit enemy compositions of two strong healers plus a tank that were nearly unbeatable, and the only answers were burst damage or damage-over-time that kills through healing.

### How to Build Clawrk

| Slot | Function | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Burst / primary damage** | The core DPS job |
| 2 | **Debuff setup, damage-over-time, or finisher** | Debuffs feed synergy; DoT kills through healing; finishers close fights |

**Build variants:**

- **Pure Burst** (burst + finisher or second damage): maximum kill speed. Best when fights must end now.
- **Debuff Engine** (debuff + payoff damage): the synergy-first build — debuff the target, then burst it. This is where "combining skills creates synergies" becomes real.
- **DoT Grinder** (DoT + secondary damage): the anti-healer build. Stack damage over time and out-sustain enemy healing.

### Playstyle Notes

- **Kill order:** enemy healers first, always. A fight against a healing team that drags on is a fight you lose.
- Open with the setup ability (debuff), follow with the payoff (burst). Rotation discipline is the whole game with two slots.
- Let Shelldon take the hits; if Clawrk is the one eating damage, the rotation is broken.

## 4. Owlivia — The Owl Healer

**Confirmed:** Owlivia is an owl who is the team's healer (Steam Next Fest 2026 demo review). A note on spelling: early press coverage at the Latin American Games Showcase spelled the name "Wolivia"; the demo review coverage that identifies her as the owl healer uses "Owlivia." Both names refer to the same character.

### The Role

Owlivia is the most important hero in the game. The evidence is direct: demo-era coverage documented fights stalling into **infinite loops** when the only surviving character was a healer facing an enemy healer, and described enemy healer teams that ended runs. A dead Owlivia isn't a setback — it's usually the run.

### How to Build Owlivia

| Slot | Function | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Direct heal** | The core healer job — the emergency button |
| 2 | **Heal-over-time, cleanse, or protective buff** | HoT sustains long fights; cleanse counters the status effects that "heavily influence every encounter"; buffs pre-empt damage |

**Build variants:**

- **Classic Medic** (heal + HoT): simple, reliable sustain. The starter build.
- **Status Doctor** (heal + cleanse): for status-heavy areas. Watch party status icons and remove them immediately.
- **Protector** (heal + shield/buff): pre-buff before hard fights, heal through the aftermath.

### Playstyle Notes

- **Heal at half HP, not at 10%.** The heal used at half HP prevents the panic heal at 10%.
- **Protect yourself.** Keep Owlivia positioned safely — if she falls, the run falls.
- **Read statuses.** Pause and read what the enemy is applying; cleanse what matters.

## 5. The Trio as a Team

The three heroes form the game's core loop in miniature:

```
Shelldon absorbs → Owlivia converts damage into HP → Clawrk ends fights
```

**Team principles:**

1. **Protect the healer.** Every other principle is downstream of this one.
2. **Kill through healing.** Somewhere in the loadout there must be power that beats enemy healers (burst or DoT) — demo reviewers learned this the hard way.
3. **Build pairs, not islands.** A debuff on Shelldon that feeds Clawrk's burst is worth more than two unrelated strong abilities.
4. **Revisit the loadout constantly.** With free re-equipping between runs and 30 skills in the full game (15 in the demo), the trio's best configuration changes as your collection grows.

## 6. What Is Not Known About the Heroes

Honesty section — none of this is confirmed:

- Their **individual abilities**: the full 30-skill roster isn't publicly documented, and no verified source maps specific skills to specific heroes (whether skills are hero-locked or freely equippable is unknown).
- Their **backstories**: the lore premise (a world "fallen into the grasp of a creature that erases everything, even thoughts") suggests the heroes are survivors of the erasure, but their personal stories are unconfirmed.
- Their **stat profiles** (HP, damage, healing values): no verified numbers exist.
- The **"Wolivia" vs. "Owlivia"** spelling: official in-game spelling unconfirmed; coverage uses both.

Any guide that lists specific hero abilities, stats, or skill trees is unverified — the roster isn't public yet.
