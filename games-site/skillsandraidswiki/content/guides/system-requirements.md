# Skills & Raids System Requirements — PC Specs & Performance Guide

**Updated: August 11, 2026**

Skills & Raids is a party-based extraction RPG with real-time ability combat — and its published requirements are modest. This guide explains where to find the official specs and what to expect on your machine.

## Where to Find the Official Requirements

**Minimum and recommended specifications are published on the official Steam store page** — check there for the current requirements before installing. The store page is the authoritative source, and it is updated by the developer if requirements change.

Some general notes about the published requirements:

- The game targets **Windows (64-bit)** on PC via Steam.
- The published requirements are modest — the game's **handcrafted 2D-art presentation** keeps graphics demands low compared to heavy 3D renderers.
- The **recommended tier** and any optional settings guidance are best read from the store page itself.

## Performance Notes (Honest)

- The game's presentation is **handcrafted fantasy art with real-time combat** — not a heavy 3D renderer. Expect light GPU load.
- The **pause mechanic** is the real performance friend: mid-fight pauses let you read skill and status descriptions, and it also lets a slower machine catch up between action beats.
- **The demo is your benchmark.** The free demo runs the same engine as the full game — if the demo runs well on your PC, the full game very likely will (see the [Release Info Guide](/guides/release-date-guide) for demo access).

## Is My PC Good Enough?

The honest answer is: **check the published specs on the official store page, then test with the free demo.** The demo is the definitive test for your specific hardware — if it runs well, you're set. As a rule of thumb, the published requirements are low enough that any PC comfortably above them will be fine.
