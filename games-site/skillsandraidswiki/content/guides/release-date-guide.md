# Skills & Raids Release Info Guide — Launch Status & Game Length

**Updated: August 11, 2026**

Skills & Raids is a party-based extraction RPG on PC via Steam from solo developer **Eddie H. Hinestroza**. If you are deciding whether to jump in, this page collects what is known about the release: launch status, the demo, game length, and what the full game contains.

## Launch Status

**Skills & Raids is available on Steam for PC.** For the official release date, current availability, and pricing, check the game's **official Steam store page** — it is the authoritative source for release information. The developer also posts announcements through the Steam page and community hub; those announcements are the best place to follow release news and updates.

A **free demo** is available through the same store page. It is a preview of the core loop — a great way to try the game before committing.

## What Kind of Game It Is

Skills & Raids blends **party management** with the **high-stakes loot runs of extraction games**, layered over mechanics inspired by Darkest Dungeon and auto-battlers:

- You control a **fixed party of three animal heroes** — Shelldon (tank), Clawrk (DPS), and Owlivia (healer). This is a **single-player game**: you command the whole party; there are no other players in your runs.
- **Core loop:** enter hostile raid zones with your party → fight in real time → loot and extract resources → craft stronger gear and skills. Die and you lose your loot.
- **Two abilities per hero** (six total per run), real-time rotations, cooldowns, and resource management — see the [Skills Guide](/guides/skills-guide).
- **30 skills** in the full game, **15 in the demo**; gear and skills share **five rarity tiers** (Common → Legendary).
- **A pause mechanic** lets you read skill and status descriptions mid-fight.
- Setting: a dark handcrafted fantasy world overtaken by a creature that erases everything — even thoughts and memories.

## How Long Is Skills & Raids?

The reported full-game length is **~2–5 hours** per complete playthrough (per the Steam page). Things to know:

- Runs are **roguelite-style expeditions**: short runs, deaths lose loot, and progression comes from extraction, crafting, and skill collection — total hours depend heavily on how many runs you spend re-gearing.
- The extraction loop (see the [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide)) is designed for repeat play, so 2–5 hours is a baseline, not a cap.
