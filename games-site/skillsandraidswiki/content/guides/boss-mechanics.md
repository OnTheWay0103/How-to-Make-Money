## Boss Mechanics Guide — Skills & Raids

Boss encounters in Skills & Raids are the most dangerous moments in any extraction run. Each boss has unique attack patterns, weak points, enrage timers, and phase transitions that require specific strategies to overcome. This guide covers every major boss and the mechanics you need to master to defeat them consistently.

### Boss Overview

Skills & Raids currently features 8 major bosses across 4 raid zones. Each boss becomes more complex as you progress.

| Boss | Zone | Level Range | Phases | Enrage Timer | Weak Point |

### Bandit Leader (Foothills)

The Bandit Leader is the introductory boss, designed to teach basic boss mechanics:

- **Phase 1 (100%-0% HP):** Single phase with three attack patterns.
  - **Slash:** A wide melee swipe. Dodge backward to avoid.
  - **Charge:** The Boss rushes at the furthest player. Sidestep at the last moment — the Boss becomes briefly stunned after missing.
  - **Call Reinforcements:** At 50% HP, the Boss whistles for two Bandit adds. Kill them quickly before focusing the Boss again.
  - **Weak point:** When the Boss charges and misses, its head is exposed for 3 seconds. Attacks during this window deal 50% extra damage.
  - **Strategy:** Have your Tank position the Boss away from the party. DPS should save burst skills for the stun window after a missed charge.

### Timber Beast (Timberland)

A massive corrupted bear:

- **Phase 1 (100%-60% HP):** Standard bear fight. The Beast uses claw swipes, a ground slam (AoE around itself), and a roar that reduces party damage by 20% for 5 seconds.
  - **Tip:** The roar can be interrupted with a stun skill. Time your interrupt when you see the Beast inhale.
- **Phase 2 (60%-0% HP):** The Beast goes into a frenzy. Attack speed increases by 50%. It gains a new attack: "Timber Toss," where it uproots a tree and throws it at a random player.
  - **Weak point:** The hind legs take 30% increased damage. Assign one DPS to focus on the hind legs.
  - **Enrage:** 10-minute timer. After enrage, the Beast gains 100% attack speed and a damage aura. Do not let the fight last this long.

### Stone Guardian (Stone Quarry)

An ancient construct awakened by the players' presence:

- **Phase 1 (100%-50% HP):** Slow, powerful attacks. The Guardian slams the ground creating shockwaves, spins its arms in a 360-degree arc, and occasionally stomps.
  - **Mechanic:** Crystals on the Guardian's back glow before a shockwave attack. Turn away to avoid a brief stun.
- **Phase 2 (50%-0% HP):** The Guardian's outer shell cracks, revealing a glowing crystal core on its back. It becomes more aggressive and gains a laser beam attack.
  - **Weak point:** The exposed core takes 100% bonus damage but is only accessible from behind.
  - **Strategy:** One player must consistently circle behind the Guardian to hit the core. The Tank keeps the Guardian facing away from the party.

### Shadow Witch (Shadow Fen)

A spellcasting boss with illusion mechanics:

- **Phase 1 (100%-70% HP):** The Witch uses shadow bolts, creates pools of darkness on the ground, and teleports around the arena.
  - **Tip:** Spread out to minimize darkness pool overlap.
- **Phase 2 (70%-35% HP):** The Witch summons two Shadow Clones. The clones copy the Witch's attacks but have 30% HP. Kill them quickly.
  - **Weak point:** The Summoning Orb the Witch holds glows before summoning. Destroying the orb (requires 3 attacks) prevents the summon.
- **Phase 3 (35%-0% HP):** The arena is covered in darkness. Light orbs spawn at random locations — stay near them to avoid damage.
  - **Enrage:** 8-minute timer. After enrage, darkness covers the entire arena. Kill the Witch before the timer expires.

### Iron Golem (Iron Depths)

A massive metal construct requiring coordinated DPS burns:

- **Phase 1 (100%-65% HP):** Standard golem fight. Punches, ground pounds, and a charge attack. The Golem is immune to most crowd control.
  - **Mechanic:** Occasionally, the Golem exposes its core on its chest for 5 seconds. This is your only damage window worth using cooldowns.
- **Phase 2 (65%-30% HP):** The Golem heats up. Attacks now apply a stacking burn effect. Its charge attack leaves a trail of fire on the ground.
  - **Weak point:** The core is now permanently exposed but protected by a rotating shield. Time attacks between shield rotations.
- **Phase 3 (30%-0% HP):** The Golem overheats. It pulses area damage every 3 seconds. All players must stay spread out.
  - **Strategy:** This is a DPS check. Use all cooldowns and consumables. Do not stop attacking.

### Frost Wyrm (Frost Caverns)

An ice dragon requiring position management:

- **Phase 1 (100%-65% HP):** The Wyrm uses frost breath (frontal cone), tail swipe (behind), and wing gust (knockback).
  - **Positioning:** Stay at the Wyrm's side. Both head and tail are dangerous.
- **Phase 2 (65%-30% HP):** Ice spikes erupt from the ground targeting random players. The Wyrm also summons ice shards that home in on the party.
  - **Weak point:** After a frost breath attack, the Wyrm's belly is exposed for 4 seconds. Melee DPS should rush in during this window.
- **Phase 3 (30%-0% HP):** Blizzard. All players take constant damage. Ice walls block parts of the arena, shrinking the playable space.
  - **Enrage:** 6-minute timer.
  - **Strategy:** Assign one player to call out ice wall spawns. The party must rotate around the arena to avoid being trapped.

### Abyss Lord (Abyss Gate)

The penultimate boss with complex multi-phase mechanics:

- **Phase 1 (100%-75% HP):** The Lord uses dark energy bolts, void slashes, and teleport strikes.
  - **Mechanic:** Four Soul Gems float around the arena. Destroying one reduces the Lord's damage by 15%.
- **Phase 2 (75%-50% HP):** The Lord creates Void Rifts that pull players toward them. Destroy rifts quickly.
- **Phase 3 (50%-25% HP):** The Lord becomes untargetable and spawns Abyss Adds. Kill adds to make the Lord vulnerable again.
- **Phase 4 (25%-0% HP):** The Lord enters a frenzy. All previous mechanics happen simultaneously.
  - **Strategy:** Save at least one Soul Gem for this phase. Breaking a gem during phase 4 gives your party breathing room.

### The Collector (Collector's Domain)

The current final boss of Skills & Raids:

- **Phase 1 (100%-75% HP):** The Collector uses collected weapons from fallen adventurers. It cycles through three weapon stances: Sword (fast slashes), Hammer (slow powerful hits), Spear (range and charge).
  - **Strategy:** Each stance has a 15-second duration. Adjust positioning based on the current weapon.
- **Phase 2 (75%-50% HP):** The Collector traps players in crystal prisons. Allies must break the crystals to free trapped players.
- **Phase 3 (50%-25% HP):** The Collector copies a random player's build. It uses their skills against the party.
- **Phase 4 (25%-0% HP):** The Collector unleashes its full collection. All phases overlap with reduced intensity.
  - **Enrage:** 4-minute timer. Extremely tight.
  - **Weak point:** The Collector's collection core on its chest. Only visible during phase 2 when it is capturing players.

### General Boss Tips

1. **Learn the tells.** Every boss attack has a visual or audio cue. Learn to recognize them and you will dodge 90% of incoming damage.
2. **Positioning is everything.** Most wipes are caused by poor positioning, not lack of gear. Always be aware of your location relative to the boss, your party, and hazards.
3. **Save consumables for boss fights.** Using a Healing Salve on trash mobs is wasteful. Save them for boss encounters.
4. **Know when to stop attacking.** Some phases require you to stop DPS and focus on mechanics. A dead DPS deals no damage.
5. **Communicate phase transitions.** Call out phase changes so your party can prepare for new mechanics.
