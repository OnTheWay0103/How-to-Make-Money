# Skills & Raids Quests & Achievements Guide — What Replaces "Events"

**Skills & Raids 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

**The honest truth first:** Skills & Raids has **no live events** — no daily challenges, no weekly boss rushes, no seasonal content, and no PvP tournaments. The game is **single-player** with a fixed content scope (2–5 hours, 21+ quests, 5 biomes, 30 skills), so the "limited-time event" systems described in some guides are fabricated — they describe a live-service game that doesn't exist. What the game *does* have is the progression structure that makes it fun: **quests** and **achievements**. This guide covers both.

---

## 1. Quests: The Real Progression System

Quests are the game's confirmed progression backbone (21+ in the full game, 11 in the demo). Completing quests **unlocks new equipment and skills**.

### Confirmed Quest Types

| Quest Type | What You Do | Why It Matters |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Target objectives** | Defeat specific enemies or achieve combat goals | Teaches kill priorities |
| **Resource collection** | Gather and extract specific resources | Drives the extraction loop |
| **Using specific abilities** | Equip and use certain skills | Forces build experimentation |

### Quest Management Tips

1. **Always have quests active.** Progression comes from quest completion — a run that doesn't advance a quest is a farming run at best.
2. **Rotate abilities for ability-quests.** The game deliberately asks you to use skills you'd normally skip. The unlock reward is worth the awkward run.
3. **Plan runs around quests.** Choose your biome and route based on what the active quests demand.
4. **Keep a resource reserve.** Resource-collection quests compete with crafting for the same pool — save some materials.

The game ships with **12 Steam achievements** (confirmed). Four achievement names are visible in official material:

| Achievement (name confirmed) | What It Suggests |
|------------------------------|------------------|
| **Questing** | Progressing through quests (likely a first-quests achievement) |
| **Crafting** | The crafting system (likely first-craft achievement) |
| **Extract** | The extraction loop (likely first-extraction achievement) |
| **Discover The Edge of the Abyss** | Exploring the endgame area |
| **Discover The Eternal Dunes** | Exploring another biome |
| *(Full list: 12 total; remaining names unconfirmed)* | — |

**Achievement-based goals** are a great way to structure a single-player game with no live events:

- Early: get **Questing**, **Crafting**, and **Extract** — they'll come naturally in the first hour.
- Mid: aim for the biome-discovery achievements — they push you to explore all 5 biomes.
- Endgame: **"The Edge of the Abyss"** discovery — the endgame content.
- Completionist: all 12 achievements.

## 3. What "Events" Guides Get Wrong

Guides describing daily challenges, weekend boss rushes, seasonal currencies, PvP tournaments, or "double loot weekends" are fabricated. Why you can be confident:

- The game is **single-player** (confirmed) — PvP tournaments are impossible.
- Content scope is **2–5 hours** (confirmed) — a live-event calendar makes no sense.
- The developer is **one person** (confirmed) — Eddie H. Hinestroza, a solo developer who shipped a complete single-player game.
- No event system appears in any verified source.

**The closest real analog to "events":** the quest list and achievement list are your content calendar. Use them the way you'd use event schedules in other games.

## 4. Post-Launch Reality (Solo-Dev Game)

What to actually expect after launch:

- **Patches and balance changes** from the developer (solo-dev games update directly from community feedback — the demo's Steam Next Fest coverage shows this pattern).
- **Bug fixes** — the demo era surfaced issues like the healer-vs-healer infinite loop; the release build and patches address these.
- **Possible future content** — a solo developer may add content over time, but nothing is announced.

Check the game's Steam page and community hub for announcements rather than third-party "event calendars."
