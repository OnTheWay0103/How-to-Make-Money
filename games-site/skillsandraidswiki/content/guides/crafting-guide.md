# Skills & Raids Crafting Guide — Turning Resources Into Gear and Skills

**Updated: August 11, 2026**

Crafting is one of the two confirmed sinks for extracted resources in Skills & Raids (the other is quests). The Steam page is explicit: extracted resources are used to **"craft equipment and skills."** Everything beyond that — recipes, materials, costs — is not publicly documented, and this guide honestly separates what is confirmed from what is not.

---

## 1. The Confirmed Crafting Loop

```
Fight → enemies drop valuable resources
  → extract safely (or lose them on a failed run)
  → craft equipment and skills between runs
  → run the next quest stronger
```

Confirmed facts:

- **Resources are extracted from enemies** in combat areas.
- **Extracted resources craft equipment and skills** — both gear and abilities are craftable.
- **Quests also consume resources** (resource collection quests), so crafting and quests compete for the same pool.
- The **demo had limited craftable equipment** (per the demo description), suggesting the full game's crafting catalog is bigger.

---

## 2. What You Can Craft (What's Confirmed vs. Not)

| Category | Confirmed? | Details |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Equipment / gear | Yes (confirmed) | Five rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary |
| Skills | Yes (confirmed) | Skill crafting is explicitly stated on the Steam page |
| Consumables | No | Potions/healing items are not explicitly confirmed as craftable (though field healing exists in runs) |
| Upgrades/enhancement | No | No upgrade system is confirmed — only crafting new items |

**Recipe names, material types, and costs are unconfirmed.** No verified source documents them, and the game is small enough that circulating "recipe lists" are unverifiable — treat them as unreliable unless matched in-game.

---

## 3. How to Farm Crafting Resources

The confirmed resource source is enemies ("enemies drop valuable resources that... are used to craft"). Beyond that:

- **Demo scope:** the demo contained 2 biomes and 11 quests — its resource economy gives you a sense of early-game farming (small, safe runs).
- **Full-game scope:** 5 biomes and 21+ quests — deeper biomes presumably yield better resources, but this is **inference, not confirmed**.
- **Skill drops double as crafting value:** enemies "may also drop their own skills" — those are craft-equivalent value (permanent additions) without spending resources.

**Farming best practice (confirmed-safe):**
1. Run the safest area you can complete reliably.
2. Extract everything valuable — bank it in the safe pocket.
3. Craft between runs.
4. Keep a resource reserve for resource-collection quests.

---

## 4. Spending Priorities

Because exact costs are unconfirmed, spend conservatively in a priority order that is safe for any economy:

| Priority | Spend On | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| 1 | Owlivia's heal + Shelldon's mitigation | Survival is the run's foundation |
| 2 | Clawrk's damage abilities | Fights must end |
| 3 | Skills that complete active quests | Quest rewards are progression |
| 4 | High-rarity gear for core roles | Direct power |
| 5 | Experimentation | With free re-equipping between runs, new skills are cheap to test |

**Don't:** craft a full set of Rare gear before your survival core is solid. **Do:** craft one slot at a time and measure the difference.

---

## 5. Rarity and Crafting

The five rarity tiers apply to crafted gear (confirmed: gear uses Common through Legendary). What's unconfirmed is how rarity outcomes are determined — material quality? recipes? randomness?

**Safe assumptions:**
- Higher-rarity craft inputs (if materials have rarities) should yield higher-rarity outputs — but this is an assumption.
- Crafted gear rarity is probably a range, not a guarantee.
- **Test cheaply:** craft the cheapest thing first, inspect the result, and learn the system with minimal spend.

---

## 6. Common Crafting Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Spending everything on gear | Resource-collection quests stall | Keep a reserve for quests |
| Crafting before extracting | Resources need to be extracted first | Only craft with banked resources |
| Ignoring skill crafting | Skills are half the build game | Craft/build skills as often as gear |
| Copying fake recipe lists | Costs and materials online are unverified | Learn the system in-game, cheaply |
| Hoarding everything | Resources are useless until processed | Convert to gear and skills between runs |
