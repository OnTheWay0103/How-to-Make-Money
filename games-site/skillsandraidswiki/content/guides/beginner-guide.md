# Skills & Raids Beginner Guide — Getting Started

**Skills & Raids 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Welcome to Skills & Raids, a party-based extraction RPG by solo developer **Eddie H. Hinestroza**, released on Steam on **July 27, 2026**. You lead a fixed party of three animal heroes through run-based expeditions: fight, loot resources, extract what you can, and craft gear and skills between runs. This guide covers everything you need for your first hours — who your heroes are, how combat works, how quests and extraction work, and how to avoid the mistakes that end runs early.

---

## 1. What Kind of Game Is Skills & Raids?

Skills & Raids is described by its developer as a **party-based Extraction RPG** with **real-time, ability-driven combat**. If you know Darkest Dungeon, the run structure and party-based pressure will feel familiar; the combat itself is closer to an auto-battler — you assign **two abilities to each character** and manage skill rotations, cooldowns, and resources while reacting to enemy mechanics in real time.

Three things define the loop:

- **Run-based expeditions.** You navigate a map of combat areas, making risk-versus-reward decisions about how deep to push.
- **Extraction.** Enemies drop valuable resources. If you successfully extract, those resources are used to **craft equipment and skills** or to **complete quests**. On a failed run, anything not kept in your safe pocket is lost.
- **Skill building.** Enemies may drop their own skills. Combining skills into builds is the heart of progression — the official pitch emphasizes that combining multiple skills creates synergies that change your approach to combat.

The full game contains **21+ quests, 5 biomes, and 30 skills** (the free demo has 11 quests, 2 biomes, and 15 skills) and roughly **2–5 hours of content** per the Steam page. It is a **single-player** game — the party is yours to command.

## 2. Your Party: Shelldon, Clawrk, and Owlivia

You always command the same three animal heroes:

| Hero | Animal | Role |
|------|--------|------|
| **Shelldon** | Turtle | The team's tank — the durable front line |
| **Clawrk** | Tiger | The DPS — the team's damage dealer |
| **Owlivia** | Owl | The healer — keeps the party alive |

(Note: some early coverage spells the healer's name "Wolivia"; the demo review coverage from Steam Next Fest 2026 consistently identifies the owl healer as Owlivia.)

This trio means you do not choose *which* roles you bring — a tank, a damage dealer, and a healer are always in the party. Your real build choices are:

1. **Which two abilities** each hero equips (from the abilities you have unlocked).
2. **Which skills** you combine into synergies across the party.
3. **Which quests and areas** you attempt with the loadout you have.

The exact list of the 30 skills is not fully documented publicly yet; the demo's 15 skills give a good preview of how ability selection works. When the game says an enemy can drop "its own skills," it means those skills become equippable build pieces for your heroes.

## 3. Understanding Combat

Combat in Skills & Raids is **real-time** with a **pause mechanic**:

- Manage **skill rotations and cooldowns** — don't spam abilities; plan which two abilities each hero uses and when.
- **Status effects heavily influence every encounter** — buffs, debuffs, and effects like poison or heals-over-time can decide a fight. Use pause to read skill and status descriptions mid-fight.
- **Positioning matters.** The developer lists character positioning management as a core feature, so keep an eye on where your heroes are relative to enemies.
- **Auto-battler elements.** Combat plays out with automated action following your ability assignments, so what you equip and in what order matters as much as clicking.

**Practical tips for new players:**
- Use the **pause mechanic liberally** while you learn — it exists so you can read what every skill and status does.
- Keep your healer (Owlivia) alive at all costs. If she goes down and the enemy has healing too, fights can stall indefinitely — a known rough edge reported in demo reviews.
- Watch for **enemy teams with healers and tanks**. One Steam Next Fest review noted a late-run enemy team of two strong healers plus a tank that was effectively unbeatable — plan your route so you don't get trapped against compositions you can't break through.

## 4. Quests Are Your Progression

Unlike games with experience levels and skill trees, Skills & Raids progresses through **quests**. The Steam page lists multiple quest types:

- **Target objectives** — defeat specific enemies or complete specific combat goals.
- **Resource collection** — gather and extract specific resources.
- **Using specific abilities** — the game pushes you to experiment with different skills.

Completing quests **unlocks new equipment and skills**, which is how your power grows between runs. With 21+ quests in the full game (11 in the demo), the quest list is effectively your progression path — plan runs around the quests you have active.

---

## 5. The Extraction Loop

The loop of every run:

1. **Prepare** — equip two abilities per hero, review active quests.
2. **Deploy** — pick an area and navigate the map.
3. **Fight and collect** — enemies drop resources; some drop their own skills.
4. **Extract** — return safely with your haul.
5. **Craft and complete quests** — turn resources into gear and skills between runs.

**The golden rule of extraction games applies here:** what you don't extract, you don't keep. On a failed run, anything not left in your **safe pocket** is lost forever (per the Steam Next Fest review coverage). Two useful mechanics to look for during runs: ways to **heal heroes in the field**, and ways to **send items back when your bags get full** — use them early rather than carrying risk.

Extract early and often. A modest haul you keep beats a perfect haul you die with.

## 6. Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Ignoring quests | You miss out on skill and gear unlocks | Check active quests before every run |
| Neglecting Owlivia | A dead healer ends most runs | Protect her, keep her healed, don't overextend without her |
| Never using pause | You misread cooldowns and statuses | Pause and read skill descriptions mid-fight |
| Overstaying a run | Failed run = everything not in the safe pocket is lost | Extract once you have a decent haul |
| Ignoring enemy-skill drops | Free build pieces left behind | Grab skills enemies drop — they fuel your builds |
| Sticking to one loadout | Quests require using specific abilities | Rotate abilities to complete quests |

---

## 7. Key Tips

- **Play the free demo first** if you're unsure — it includes 11 quests, 2 biomes, and 15 skills, and progress will teach you the loop before you commit.
- **Craft between runs.** Raw resources are only useful once extracted and processed into gear or skills.
- **Read the in-game lore.** The world "has fallen into the grasp of a creature that erases everything, even thoughts" — the unnamed antagonist is central to the story and the final quests.
- **Follow the developer's Steam announcements** — solo-dev game, so patch notes and feedback responses come directly from Eddie H. Hinestroza.
