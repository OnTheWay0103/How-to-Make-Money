## Boss Farming Guide — Skills & Raids

Boss farming is the fastest way to earn rare loot, skill scrolls, and upgrade materials in Skills & Raids. Unlike regular mob farming, bosses drop guaranteed high-tier items on every kill — but they also spawn on fixed timers, are contested by other squads, and require you to survive the extraction back. This guide covers every farming route, respawn schedule, and loot table you need to farm efficiently.

### Boss Respawn Timers

Every boss in Skills & Raids respawns on a fixed timer after being killed. The timer starts the moment the boss dies, regardless of who killed it.

| Boss | Zone | Respawn Timer | Contest Risk | Farm Value |
|------|------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| Bandit Leader | Foothills | 15 minutes | Low | Low |
| Timber Beast | Timberland | 20 minutes | Low | Medium |
| Stone Guardian | Stone Quarry | 30 minutes | Medium | Medium |
| Shadow Witch | Shadow Fen | 45 minutes | High | High |
| Iron Golem | Iron Depths | 60 minutes | High | High |
| Frost Wyrm | Frost Caverns | 90 minutes | Very High | Very High |
| Abyss Lord | Abyss Gate | 2 hours | Extreme | Extreme |

### Efficient Farming Routes

1. **Low-level loop (Levels 1-10):** Clear Bandit Leader → Timber Beast → extract via Foothills exit. Full loop takes 8-10 minutes with a 15-minute respawn, letting you run it back-to-back.
2. **Mid-tier rotation (Levels 11-25):** Stone Guardian → Shadow Witch → Iron Golem in one run. Budget 45-60 minutes per loop and bring at least 3 Healing Salves.
3. **Endgame rotation (Levels 26+):** Frost Wyrm → Abyss Lord, farming both in a single deep run. Requires a full squad and at least Tier 3 gear.
4. **Route stacking:** If another squad camps your route, skip ahead to the next boss and circle back — the first boss will be freshly spawned on return.

### Loot Tables by Boss

- **Bandit Leader:** Scrap metal, Basic Skill Scroll, 40-80 Gold.
- **Timber Beast:** Timber Planks, Sticky Resin, chance of the Timber Axe (1-star weapon).
- **Stone Guardian:** Stone Shards, Guardian Core (crafting material), chance of Stone Gauntlets.
- **Shadow Witch:** Shadow Essence, Witch's Tome (skill scroll), guaranteed 2-star weapon.
- **Iron Golem:** Iron Ingots, Golem Core, guaranteed 2-3 star weapon.
- **Frost Wyrm:** Frost Scales, Wyrm Fang (high-value trade item), guaranteed 3-star weapon.
- **Abyss Lord:** Abyss Soul Gems, Lord's Seal (raid key), guaranteed 3-4 star weapon.

### Solo vs Squad Farming

| Playstyle | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| Solo | Full loot share, fast extraction | Slow kills, high risk | Low-tier bosses only |
| Duo | Balanced speed and safety | Split loot, communication needed | Mid-tier rotation |
| Full squad | Fastest kills, boss spawn control | Heavily split loot | Frost Wyrm and Abyss Lord |

### Farming Preparation Checklist

1. Bring 3-4 Healing Salves and at least 1 Antidote per run.
2. Repair all gear before leaving the lobby — a broken weapon halves your DPS.
3. Memorize the extraction points along your route before engaging the boss.
4. Use a Scout skill or recon ability to check the boss arena before entering.
5. Always carry one high-value item slot free — bosses drop loot that exceeds your bag cap.
