# Skills & Raids Progression Guide — Quests, Unlocks & Content Path

**Skills & Raids 1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

**The most important correction in this guide:** Skills & Raids has **no character levels, no gear score, and no account levels**. Progression runs on **quests** — completing quests unlocks new equipment and skills (confirmed on the Steam page). Guides that describe level requirements, gear score thresholds, or tier-unlock raid progression are describing a game that doesn't exist. Here's how progression actually works.

---

## 1. The Confirmed Progression Model

| System | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Quests (21+ in full game, 11 in demo) | Confirmed — the progression backbone |
| Quest unlocks (equipment + skills) | Confirmed — "unlock new equipment and skills" |
| Crafting from extracted resources | Confirmed — the second progression axis |
| Character levels / XP | **Not confirmed — don't plan around levels** |
| Gear score / ilvl | **Not confirmed** |
| Skill trees / talent points | **Not confirmed** |
| Zone unlock gating | Not confirmed (biome access rules unknown) |

**The two progression axes that are real:**

1. **Quest completion** → new equipment and skills → new build options.
2. **Crafting** → extracted resources become gear and skills → higher rarity gear (five confirmed rarity tiers).

---

## 2. Quest Types (Confirmed)

The Steam page lists the quest types:

| Quest Type | What You Do | What It Teaches |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Target objectives** | Defeat specific enemies or achieve combat goals | Kill priorities and build pressure |
| **Resource collection** | Gather and extract specific resources | The extraction loop |
| **Using specific abilities** | Equip and use certain skills | The skill system and synergies |

The "using specific abilities" quests are the game's way of forcing experimentation — they're design, not busywork. **Equip the requested skills, complete the quest, keep the unlock.**

---

## 3. The Content Path

Confirmed content totals: **21+ quests, 5 biomes, 30 skills** in the full game; 11 quests, 2 biomes, 15 skills in the demo. The shape of the path (inference from confirmed facts, marked as such):

| Stage | Content | What Progression Looks Like |
|-------|---------|-----------------------------|
| Early | Demo-equivalent early quests | Complete quests, collect first skills, learn the loop |
| Early-Mid | First full-game biomes | 2-biome demo content mastered → new biomes (5 total) |
| Mid | Mid-game quests | Rarer skills and gear; synergy-based builds |
| Late | Late quests and hard combat areas | Epic gear, full skill collection progress |
| Endgame | Final quests / "The Edge of the Abyss" | Legendary tier, the world-erasing creature's content |

Everything marked *inference* (biome order, difficulty curve) is our best reading of confirmed facts — not verified.

## 4. A Preparation Checklist for Each Stage

### Early (First Quests)
- [ ] Learn the two-abilities-per-character system
- [ ] Build the balanced loadout (tank/damage/heal)
- [ ] Use the pause mechanic every fight
- [ ] Complete every quest you can — quests are progression

### Mid (New Biomes)
- [ ] Farm skill drops to expand the collection
- [ ] Build a synergy pair (setup → payoff)
- [ ] Craft Uncommon/Rare gear for core slots
- [ ] Add a cleanse to Owlivia for status-heavy areas

### Late (Hard Combat Areas)
- [ ] Kill-through-healing power (burst or DoT)
- [ ] Debuff power to break tanky teams
- [ ] Bank everything before hard fights
- [ ] Study runs before carrying loot into new areas

### Endgame (Final Quests)
- [ ] Epic/Legendary gear on all three heroes
- [ ] A tested counter-build for every enemy team type
- [ ] Complete collection of skills you need for synergies

---

## 5. What "Raid" Means in This Game

The game's name says "Raids," and the extraction framing uses raid-style language — but a "raid" here is a **run into a combat area**, not a matchmade multiplayer dungeon. The game is single-player; your party is always Shelldon, Clawrk, and Owlivia. When guides describe "raid tiers," "party finder," or "gear score checks," they're fabricating multiplayer systems that don't exist.

## 6. Common Progression Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Grinding "levels" | There are no levels — XP grinding is wasted effort | Complete quests |
| Ignoring ability-quests | You skip the skill unlocks that grow builds | Rotate abilities for quests |
| Hoarding resources | They only matter when crafted or quested | Convert between runs |
| Pushing new areas with full bags | First runs are learning runs | Bank, study, then farm |
| Following level/gear-score guides | They describe a fake game | Progression = quests + crafting |

---

## 7. How to Track Your Progress

Since there are no levels, measure progress by:

1. **Quest list completion** (21+ to go).
2. **Skill collection size** (30 skills available).
3. **Biomes cleared** (5 total).
4. **Highest gear rarity** on each hero (Common → Legendary).
