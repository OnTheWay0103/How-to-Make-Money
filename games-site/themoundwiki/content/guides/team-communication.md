# The Mound Team Communication Guide — Callouts, Roles & Staying Linked

> Launch Version / Updated: August 6, 2026

Communication in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu** is a survival mechanic, not a nicety. The game's **proximity voice chat** means you can only hear teammates who are close — and the horror design punishes the separation: sanity hallucinations, impostor visuals, and silent ambushes all live in the gap between players who cannot talk. This guide covers the communication systems, the callout vocabulary that wins expeditions, and the roles that keep a 4-player squad talking.

---

## The Two Communication Systems

| System | How It Works | When It Matters |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Proximity voice chat | Only teammates within ~5 meters are audible | Every moment — separated players become unreachable |
| Gesture wheel | Pre-set gestures/emotes for non-verbal callouts | When you cannot speak, or across distances |
| Lobby codes | Crossplay lobby join via code | Getting the squad together |

**The golden rule:** stay in visual range of your teammates. The game explicitly punishes separation — a squad member out of voice range is a squad member who cannot warn you about a hallucination or a flanking Y'm-bhi.

---

## Designate a Squad Leader

Every successful squad has exactly **one voice calling the shots**:

- The leader decides **when to fight** and **when to extract** — no debates mid-ambush.
- The leader assigns **watch zones** during loot phases and boss pulls.
- The leader makes the final call on **sanity reports** — if two players disagree on what they see, the leader's call wins and everyone verifies.

Tie-breaker rule: the **Vanguard** (or the most experienced player) leads. In a squad of four voices, silence during danger is a decision — and usually the wrong one.

---

## Callout Vocabulary That Works

Keep callouts short, specific, and repeated — the team needs to act on them in seconds.

| Situation | Say This | Response |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Enemy spotted | "Y'm-bhi scout, north, two" | Confirm count, choose stealth or pull |
| Sanity event | "Hallucinating, don't trust my left call" | Team verifies visuals before acting |
| Impostor / false ally | "He's not a teammate, don't engage" | Wait for second confirmation before shooting |
| Boss / elite pull | "Elite on me, need Vanguard" | Vanguard moves, Striker flanks |
| Extraction decision | "Extracting now, moving to flare" | Everyone moves; no solo heroics |
| Firearm noise | "Shot fired at 3 o'clock" | Assume the area is alerted |

**The verification rule:** in The Mound, one player may see a monster where another sees a teammate — or a clear path where another sees spikes. **Never act on a lone visual.** Confirm what you see with at least one teammate before swinging, shooting, or stepping.

## Role-Based Communication

Communication should map onto the [Team Roles](/guides/team-roles) system:

| Role | Communication Duty |
|------|--------------------|
| Vanguard | Calls enemy focus, announces guard breaks ("stamina out, peeling") |
| Warden | Reports squad Sanity status, calls cleansing windows |
| Striker | Calls kill priority, confirms backstab windows |
| Harrier | Kites and announces repositioning ("pulling them right") |
| Specialist | Reports traps/disarm progress, calls objectives |

When the Warden says Sanity is below 30%, the squad's default answer is **move toward the Ox Cart** — not "keep looting."

---

## The No-Mic Playbook

No microphone? You can still be a reliable teammate:

1. **Use the gesture wheel** — signal "enemy," "extract," and "follow me" deliberately.
2. **Stay glued to the team** — without voice, distance is the only thing you control. Never wander.
3. **Move last, move slow** — silent players should trail the group and watch the rear.
4. **Pre-agree simple signals** before the expedition starts (e.g., two quick pings = extract).

---

## Expedition Communication Checklist

- [ ] Squad leader designated before the boat departs
- [ ] Callout vocabulary agreed (enemy, extract, hallucination, flare)
- [ ] Sanity reporting interval agreed (every area transition)
- [ ] No-split rule confirmed — stick to visual/voice range
- [ ] Extraction trigger decided ("30 minutes or first hallucination")
