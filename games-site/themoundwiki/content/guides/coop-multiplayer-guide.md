## Co-op Overview

The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu is a **1–4 player online co-op** extraction game, and co-op is the intended way to play — the sanity system is built to break trust between teammates. There is **no couch or local co-op**; every player needs their own copy and device.

| Mode | Players | Verdict |
|------|:--:|--------|
| True Solo | 1 | Hard — enemy swarms and no one to verify hallucinations |
| Solo + AI companion | 1 + AI | Viable but weaker than human teammates |
| Duo | 2 | The **sweet spot** — tense, manageable, and communication stays clear |
| Trio / Full Squad | 3–4 | Maximum chaos and treasure capacity |

PC (Steam), PS5, and Xbox Series X/S play together — crossplay is enabled by default. Online services (PlayStation Plus / Xbox Game Pass Core) are required on console.

## How to Add Friends

There are two layers of friendship: your **platform friend list** and the **in-game lobby system**.

1. **Platform friends:** add each other through your normal Steam friends, PlayStation Network, or Xbox network friends list — The Mound reads these lists for invites.
2. **In-game:** the host creates a lobby and shares its **6-character lobby code**. Anyone on any platform can join with the code — no cross-platform account linking required.

### Hosting a Game

1. From the main menu select **Expedition**
2. Choose **Host Game**
3. Set privacy to **Friends Only**
4. Share the 6-character lobby code with your friends
5. Wait for everyone to ready up, then launch the expedition

### Joining a Friend

1. Select **Expedition** → **Join Game**
2. Enter the host's lobby code
3. Pick your character and loadout, then ready up

You can also join directly from your platform friend list if the host's privacy is set to Friends Only.

## Crossplay Parties

Crossplay needs no configuration:

| Party | Supported |
|------|:--:|
| PC (Steam) + PS5 | ✅ |
| PC (Steam) + Xbox Series X/S | ✅ |
| PS5 + Xbox Series X/S | ✅ |
| All three together | ✅ |

Voice chat and lobby codes work across platforms. The only platform split is progression accounts — your Rank and tokens live on the platform you play on.

## Plan the Shared Weapon Pool

The most overlooked co-op mechanic: **gear on the Galleon is shared across the whole party.** If the contract offers a single Flintlock Pistol, only one player can carry it. Before departure:

- Decide who takes the one Crossbow (usually your quietest player)
- Assign the Medallion and Oil Lamp roles — these are party tools, not personal loot
- Make sure everyone carries a Machete as their fallback

A squad that argues over the weapons rack at the Galleon is a squad that dies to the first ambush.

## Proximity Voice Chat and the Gesture Wheel

Communication is a core mechanic — a microphone is effectively **required**:

- **Proximity voice chat:** you can only hear teammates who are close. Separated squad members become unreachable — and the game punishes that with hallucinations.
- **Gesture wheel:** a limited set of non-verbal signals for when you cannot speak.
- **No health bars:** The Mound shows no teammate status. The only way to know someone is hurt or losing their mind is to ask them.

Stay in visual range of your teammates, and agree on callouts before you leave the boat.

## Trust: Hallucinations and Impostors

The Mound's horror is *social*:

- **Hallucinations differ per player.** One teammate may see a monster where you see a friend — or a clear path where you see spikes. Never shoot on a lone sighting; verify with your teammate first.
- **Monsters impersonate teammates.** A creature can wear your friend's face, distinguishable only by occasional beast-like noises. If a "teammate" sounds wrong, it is not your teammate.
- **Corrupted allies:** a downed teammate who is not revived in time becomes an **AI-controlled corrupted enemy** that hunts you. Revive fast — Ammonia Salts are the item to carry for emergencies.
- **Noise attracts enemies for everyone** — one player sprinting or shooting can pull an ambush onto the whole squad.

## Team Survival Rules

1. **Assign roles before departure** — point man, support, rearguard (see our [Co-op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide) for full role breakdowns)
2. **Designate a squad leader** — one voice calls the shots in combat
3. **Use the Ox Wagon as your meeting point** — its paint streak anchors navigation, and standing near it reduces everyone's Insanity
4. **Extract when sanity breaks down** — if two players are hallucinating, the haul is not worth the wipe. Dying means losing all gathered loot
5. **Never split up in the dark** — separated players cannot communicate, and that is exactly when the impostors hunt

## Troubleshooting Multiplayer

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Friend cannot join | Re-check the 6-character lobby code and host privacy (Friends Only) |
| Crossplay not working | Crossplay is on by default — ensure everyone is on the latest patch |
| Cannot invite from friend list | Verify the host's privacy setting, then use the lobby code as fallback |
| Voice chat inaudible | Proximity voice only — get within 5 meters, or use the gesture wheel; headphones strongly recommended |
