# The Mound Ox Cart Guide — Why It Won't Follow You and How to Control It

You turn around, and the cart is gone. You find it parked against a tree, walk back to the loot you dropped — and it has moved again. You are not doing anything wrong: across the **1,185 English Steam reviews** we sampled for this guide, **74 mention the cart and 45 of those are negative**, against a 36.5% negative rate across the same sample.

## What the Ox Cart Actually Is

One cart per expedition, driven by an NPC and pulled by the ox **Carlito** — the animal you can pet on the Galleon since Patch 1.03. The developers' own pre-launch post frames the loop around it: *"As you venture into it, you'll need to escort your ox cart, scavenge valuable loot such as forbidden relics, and survive against the growing threats."* [Official]

Treat it as a walking stash and regroup point, not a companion. It has its own pathing, its own pace and **no obligation to be where you are**.

## Why the Cart Won't Follow You — Five Causes

**1. It paths on its own, not on you.** This is the root cause of most cart complaints. It is NPC-driven AI following its own navigation, and when that navigation fails it does not teleport back — it improvises. *"The cart following you is slow, but the real problem is that it randomly gets completely lost."* [Community, [Steam review, Aug 13 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197966871219/recommended/2569760/)] Another review puts it more bluntly: *"the AI pathfinding of the cart is super buggy."* [Community, [Steam review, Aug 16 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198353569589/recommended/2569760/)]

**2. Terrain you can stand on, it cannot path over.** The most actionable finding in the community reports: the cart loses you when you are on a ledge or near a drop. *"Cart cannot be given ability to stay in place at least momentarily and can run away from you frequently depending on where you are standing (usually higher up/near dropdowns)."* [Community, [Steam review, Jul 21 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198203985453/recommended/2569760/)] Looting from height, expect to walk back down to it.

**3. It can decide it is following someone else.** One player traced a failure to late joiners: *"Why do new players spawn next the the ox cart? Only for the ox cart to follow them instead of us."* [Community, [Steam review, Aug 13 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197996931998/recommended/2569760/)] [Unconfirmed] — the cause is a single report, but the symptom is common: the cart is chasing whoever it last locked onto.

**4. In at least one mission, it is deliberately unavailable.** In the **Portal Mission** the wagon does not come with you: *"The Ox-Wagon is unavailable."* / *"When the expedition begins, the Ox-Wagon may remain suspended beside the Tempestad."* / *"This is part of the Portal Mission rather than a broken Contract."* [Editorial] No workaround exists — *"you cannot depend on the wagon's storage, mobile shelter, or normal return function."* Carry only what you need for that route.

**5. Until Patch 1.04 there was no way to tell it to wait.** Players asked for exactly this months before it shipped: *"Cart cannot be given ability to stay in place at least momentarily."* [Community] The developers answered it by name in 1.04.

## How to Control It — Command Wheel 2.0 (Patch 1.04, August 31, 2026)

The official 1.04 patch notes describe the change as a direct response to requests:

> *"Command Wheel 2.0: By popular demand we have added a new function to allow you to stop the cart and give it instructions. Proximity determines whether or not you use the horn."* — [Official, Patch 1.04 patch notes, Aug 31 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2569760/view/1842212951310330)

Two things follow. **You can now stop the cart and issue instructions** — the fix for cause 5 above. And **proximity governs the horn**: shouting from across the map does nothing. If the horn is not working, you are too far away.

**The horn is loud, and loud is a mechanic.** *"The wagon can be summoned using its horn, but doing so creates a great deal of noise."* / *"Because nearby creatures are attracted to loud sounds"* / *"calling the wagon should be treated as an emergency option rather than a convenient shortcut."* [Editorial] A co-op guide lists it as standard support kit — *"Secondary item: Horn (to call the wagon)"* — and adds *"The wagon never dies and can be called with the horn."* [Editorial] [Unconfirmed] — a single guide's claim; the horn-as-kit advice is consistent across guides.

## The Five Cart Functions Most Crews Forget

The cart is not just a chest. A 1,229-helpful-vote review and a dedicated walkthrough agree on the rest: [Community + Editorial]

| Function | What it does | Source |
|----------|--------------|--------|
| **Storage** | Holds loot and objectives so your slots stay free — *"your mobile storage and return point"* | Editorial (NoobFeed) |
| **Revive** | *"The cart can be used to revive allies if you pick them up and place them in it."* The onboard priest *"resurrect[s] teammates whose bodies have been recovered."* | Community + Editorial |
| **Mobile light** | *"providing a mobile light source"* while it moves with you | Editorial (ChapterCheats) |
| **Sanity** | *"reducing sanity loss for nearby players"* per two guides; one player reports the opposite — *"Staying near the cart seems to do nothing for sanity."* | Editorial vs Community **[Unconfirmed]** |
| **A trail home** | *"leaves behind a salt trail"*; guides call the same thing *"visible wheel tracks"* and *"the white path back to the boat."* | Community + Editorial ×2 |

The trail matters more than it sounds: three independent sources describe a visible line the cart leaves behind, and one co-op guide builds its retreat plan on it — *"Call the wagon with the horn, group up around it, and follow the white path back to the boat."* / *"The wagon guy will lead you out."* [Editorial]

## Should You Send the Cart Back Before You Extract?

The community has a consistent report here; the developers have not commented.

- The in-game guidance points one way: *"Ingame guide tells you to send the cart back once ready to leave but it's actually the worst you could be doing because it will wake the forest and things will go down from there fast."* [Community, [Jul 16 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197969741894/recommended/2569760/)]
- A second review independently reports the same cost: *"You can tell the cart driver to return with the ox, but doing this appears to alert the forest and spawn additional enemies."* [Community, [Aug 1 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198038864751/recommended/2569760/)]
- One player reports it is not mandatory at all: *"Learning after the fact that in the current game state, you can extract without sending said cart back to start, was salt in the wound."* [Community, [Jul 23 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198026087779/recommended/2569760/)] **[Unconfirmed]**

**Officially confirmed:** 1.04 lets you stop the cart and instruct it, and proximity gates the horn. **Not confirmed:** what sending it back costs, or whether extraction requires it. Both the "it wakes the forest" and "you can skip it" claims are player reports, flagged **[Unconfirmed]** — the second comes from a single reviewer, and behaviour may have shifted across 1.01–1.05. Our [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide) covers the extraction sequence itself. In the moment, the safe read is that sending the cart away is a noise event, and noise is never free in this game.

## Practical Cart Rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Park the cart before you climb — it cannot path to ledges | Expect it to reach you on high ground or near drops |
| Use the Command Wheel stop order before you scatter to loot | Let it keep moving while the squad spreads out |
| Bank heavy treasure and recovered bodies in the cart | Load it with low-value items you could carry |
| Keep the horn for emergencies — it is a loud noise event | Spam the horn to shortcut a walk back |
| Learn its trail; it is your return line in bad visibility | Assume the trail will still be there after a hallucination |
| On the Portal Mission, travel light — the wagon stays behind | Blame the AI for a mission that is designed around it |
