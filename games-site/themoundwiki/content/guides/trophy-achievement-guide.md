# The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu — Trophy & Achievement Guide

> **Rewritten September 17, 2026.** The previous version of this page printed a full list of **28 trophies** — 1 Platinum, 5 Gold, 10 Silver, 12 Bronze — with named trophies ("Lurker Slayer", "Mound Breaker", "Faction Favorite", "Speed Runner"), conditions tied to the fabricated Reputation system and to bosses that do not exist, plus lore-page and blueprint checklists. **The developer has never published an achievement list for this game.** Every trophy name on that page was invented, and there is no way for anyone outside the studio to have known the tier counts. This page now contains only what can be sourced, and tells you how to get the real list.

This is the shortest guide on the wiki, and that is the point. An honest page about an unpublished achievement list is a short page. What it can do is tell you **what is confirmed to exist**, **what is confirmed to be broken or fixed**, and **how to find your own list** — which is the only authoritative source available to players.

Worth quoting because it is the only player testimony on the subject, and because it cuts against the idea of a rich trophy list:

Treat that as one player's experience rather than a definitive statement — but it is a strong signal that this is not a game with a deep, curated trophy list to chase.

## Every official achievement reference that exists

This is the complete set. There are three, and two of them come from the same patch note.

| # | Official text | Patch | What it tells you |
|---|---------------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | *"Fixed spear and headshot achievements that were not unlocking under certain scenarios."* | 1.03 | There is **at least one spear achievement** and **at least one headshot achievement**. Both were bugged and are now fixed. |
| 2 | *"Progress recovery tool for all platforms based on achievement / trophies progress."* | 1.02 | Achievements/trophies are used as the **basis for a save-recovery tool** — so if you lose progress, your achievement state is what the game restores from. |
| 3 | *"What new achievements have you unlocked?"* | Pre-launch blog post | Confirms achievements were part of the design from the start. |

That is genuinely all of it.

### Practical consequences

1. **Spear and headshot achievements exist and now unlock.** If you were playing before patch 1.03 and wondered why they never fired, that was a bug, not you.
2. **Play with achievements in mind and they double as a backup save.** Because patch 1.02's recovery tool restores from achievement state, keeping your achievement progress healthy is a genuine safety net against save loss. See the [Progress & Save Recovery Guide](/guides/progress-save-recovery-guide) for the recovery procedure.
3. **Headshot achievements are harder than they look.** The official Y'm-bhi post states plainly: *"Headshots aren't always fatal - even devastating ones."* Do not assume a headshot streak is guaranteed by aim alone.

## Verified progress facts that matter more than trophies

While chasing achievements, these are the progression facts that are actually documented — and they are more useful than a trophy roadmap:

| System | Documented detail | Source |
|--------|-------------------|--------|
| **Save recovery** | *"Progress recovery tool for all platforms based on achievement / trophies progress."* | Patch 1.02 |
| **Weapon upgrades persist** | The **Weapon Upgrade Anvil** is *"a new station on the main ship lets you spend tokens to improve or upgrade weapons given by contracts."* | Demo update patch notes |
| **Upgrades cost Tokens** | *"Slight increase for tokens given for contract tiers."* | Patch 1.02 |
| **Saints were reworked** | *"Modified the Saints shop so these are no longer bought at the woodshop. Saints are now equipped for a smaller cost at the ox wagon before an expedition."* and *"Reduced Saints blessing costs."* | Patches 1.01, 1.02 |
| **Lost progress on PS5 was a real bug** | *"Fix for losing progress on PS5."* | Patch 1.02 |
| **Carlito the Ox can be petted** | *"You can now pet 'Carlito' the Ox on the ship."* | Patch 1.03 |

That last one is not a trophy. It is, however, the only officially confirmed cosmetic interaction in the game, and it is real — unlike everything in the removal table.

## Efficient approach, given what is known

You cannot chase a list nobody has published. What you *can* do:

1. **Open your platform's achievement panel before you start playing**, and read the list for yourself. That is your roadmap.
2. **Note the spear and headshot achievements specifically.** They are the only two categories confirmed by name, and they were bugged until patch 1.03 — make sure you are patched before farming them.
3. **Do not build a run around a boss or a Reputation threshold.** Neither exists. Any guide that sends you to fight a named boss for a trophy is sending you after a fabrication.
4. **Keep achievements healthy as save insurance.** Patch 1.02's recovery tool restores from achievement progress.
5. **Expect a thin endgame.** Reviewers consistently describe the game as front-loaded — good early progression, limited endgame content.
