# The Mound Money Guide — How to Earn Gyldenblood and Upgrade Tokens Fast

> **Rewritten September 17, 2026.** This page used to describe three currencies — *Gyldenblood*, *Upgrade Tokens* and *Reputation* — and a farming table built on Nightmare contracts, Legendary Hunts and Endless Expeditions. **Gyldenblood** and **Reputation** do not exist: each returns **0 hits** in all 39 official announcements and **0** in the 576-review corpus. Nightmare contracts, Legendary Hunts and Endless Expeditions do not exist either. See [Why this page was rewritten](#why-this-page-was-rewritten).

Every expedition in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu** is an investment: you sail out with gear and return with loot — or you do not return at all. The players who climb fastest are not the ones who loot the most; they are the ones who understand what each currency buys and what is worth carrying back.

The developer's own launch summary of the reward screen is the clearest statement of the economy:

> *"See what awaits you upon a successful return: Experience to sharpen your skills, and Tokens to spend at the occult shop. These are no mere coins. They are the currency of the damned, used to acquire artifacts that blur the line between salvation and madness."*
> — Official launch announcement

So: **coins** you spend inside a run or on gear, **Experience** on your level, and **Tokens** on the permanent shop.

## The Three Things You Actually Earn

| What | Earned By | Spent On | Carries Over? |
|------|-----------|----------|---------------|
| **Coins** | Selling loot inside the run; looting currency from enemies and chests | In-run gear, weapon upgrades before an expedition, Saints | **No** — coins are a run resource |
| **Experience** | Completing missions; exceeding the contract's treasure target pays extra | Your level, which is where the handful of permanent unlocks come from | Yes |
| **Tokens** | Extracting with loot — the haul converts at the results screen. Patch 1.02: *"Slight increase for tokens given for contract tiers"* | The occult shop and the Weapon Upgrade Anvil | Yes |

**The critical distinction:** Tokens are only paid when you *extract with* the loot. Die on the mainland and that run's haul converts to nothing. That difference is the entire economy of the game — and the reason wipes feel so expensive.

> **Two fabricated currencies were removed from this page.** *Gyldenblood* and *Reputation* were both presented here as fact: **0 hits** in all 39 official Steam announcements, **0** on the store page, **0** across 576 reviews. There is no reputation gate on maps, and loot is not valued in a currency called Gyldenblood.

## How to Earn Tokens Faster

Tokens are the permanent currency: they buy shop stock and Weapon Upgrade Anvil work that persists. What is actually documented about earning them:

| Source | Detail | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Extracting with loot | The haul converts to tokens at the results screen. Everything else about the run is temporary | Player-reported, consistent across reviews |
| Contract tier | Patch 1.02: *"Slight increase for tokens given for contract tiers"* | Patch 1.02 notes |
| Exceeding the target | *"Getting more than the mission demands nets you extra money and XP"* — one player reports it caps around 2x the goal | Player report |
| Richer contracts | *"The further you venture, the greater the dangers you will face"* — deeper contracts pay more | Official |

**What does *not* pay tokens:** there is no daily board, no boss kill bonus, no boss at all, and no post-story 1.5x tier. If a guide tells you to farm those, it is describing a game that was never shipped.

### Token Spending Priority

1. **The Weapon Upgrade Anvil** — *"a new station on the main ship lets you spend tokens to improve or upgrade weapons given by contracts."* This is the closest thing the game has to a progression system, and it works on the weapons you were issued.
2. **Occult-shop support items** — the Medallion (*"uncover hidden treasures"*), the Mask of Nyarlathotep (disguise — *"move among the Y'm-bhi unnoticed"*), the Crucifix (reads enemy stress through their eyes), and the Rosary (added in patch 1.04, unlocked by finding the hidden jewel boxes).
3. **Saints** — patch notes: *"Saints are now equipped for a smaller cost at the ox wagon before an expedition"*, and patch 1.02 *"Reduced Saints blessing costs."*
4. **Nothing else.** There is no perk to buy, no blueprint tree, and no permanent weapon arsenal — the arsenal is shared per contract.

> **Golden rule:** tokens buy permanence; coins buy this run. If a purchase does not persist across expeditions, spend coins on it, not tokens.

## Coins — the Run Currency

Coins are what you sell loot for and what you find in the field. They are spent inside the game's economy on gear, weapon upgrades before an expedition, and Saints. They do not carry over in a meaningful way, and players describe the loop bluntly:

> *"You earn 'coins,' but … you can only spend them on upgrades or new items. However, since you don't keep anything and the weapons are random, these coins are completely useless."*
> — Steam review

The honest read: **coins are not a savings account.** Treat every coin as spent-or-lost and you will make better decisions than the player hoarding for an upgrade tier that does not exist.

### What Sells

There is no published price list. No official source gives loot values, and `Gyldenblood` — the currency this page used to quote prices in — does not exist. What players report is a **single loot-to-token conversion at extraction**, plus a shop where the Medallion is used to *"show shrines with idols to sell"*, i.e. locating sellable idols is itself a real activity.

Player-reported sellables worth watching for: relics and forbidden relics (the developer explicitly names *"scavenge valuable loot such as forbidden relics"*), idols found via the Medallion, and anything the shop prices highly.

> **The old price table on this page was invented.** "Gold Silk — 2,000+ Gyldenblood", "Chitin Plate", "Ectoplasm", "Centipede Carapace", "Vine Fiber" — none of these item names appears in the official announcements or the review corpus. They have been removed rather than re-priced with different invented numbers.

## What Actually Constrains Your Haul

Not weight, and not a materials system — **slots**.

- There is **no weight system**: `weight` returns 0 hits across all 39 official announcements and 0 on the store page. What limits you is inventory slots.
- *"Additional inventory slots are gradually unlocked as you level"*, and permanent unlocks are otherwise scarce — one player describes *"no permanent unlocks aside from skins and a couple of inventory slots."*
- The **Ox Wagon** is your overflow: most missions include it and it takes items you would otherwise carry. Players also warn that *"there is really no point in sending the cart back"* — it spikes enemy spawns.

## The Honest Money Routine

1. **Take the richest contract you can actually finish.** Token payout scales with contract tier (patch 1.02).
2. **Fill the Ox Wagon, not your pack.** Heavy treasure goes in the cart, freeing your personal slots for tools and healing.
3. **Extract on a schedule, not a whim.** *"The mandatory hard cap at the end of missions has been removed: players can now remain on the mainland as long as they can survive"* — which means staying too long is your decision, and the forest is the clock. *"Enemies take longer to spawn when the forest wakes up."*
4. **Bank the tokens, not the gear.** The weapon dissolves into tokens at extraction; that is the design, not a bug.
5. **Spend tokens at the Anvil or the shop.** Nowhere else persists.

### The Loot-Runner Approach

For pure farming runs: **Crossbow + Machete**, a couple of Bandages, one Flare, and every remaining slot empty. The goal is to arrive at the shore with the haul in hand, not to win fights.

## Common Money Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | The Fix |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| Hoarding found weapons | Contract gear is issued, not owned — *"the flintlock that kept you alive on that same run just evaporates"* | Plan around the contract in front of you |
| Chasing loot after the forest wakes | Spawns climb and your window closes | Extract on a schedule, not on the loot table |
| Leaving slots full of consumables | You cannot carry the haul out | Keep at least two slots empty on departure |
| Expecting a big payout tier at the end | There is no Nightmare contract and no post-story 1.5x bonus | The tier ladder is the contract board in front of you |
| Buying "perks" or "blueprints" | Neither exists | Tokens go to the Anvil, the occult shop and Saints |
