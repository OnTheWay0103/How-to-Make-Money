# The Mound Money Guide — How to Earn Gyldenblood and Upgrade Tokens Fast

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Every expedition in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu** is an investment: you sail out with tools and return with loot — or you do not return at all. The players who climb fastest are not the ones who loot the most; they are the ones who understand what each currency is for and what is worth carrying back. This guide breaks down all three currencies, the best ways to earn each, and the fastest farm routes in the game.

---

## The Three Currencies

| Currency | Earned By | Spent On | Carries Over? |
|----------|-----------|----------|---------------|
| **Reputation** | Completing contracts, boss kills, story objectives | Unlocks new maps, contracts, and story phases | Yes — the story gate |
| **Upgrade Tokens** | Contract rewards, vendor trade-ins, daily challenges | Galleon vendor: torches, Medallion, maps, weapon upgrades, blueprints | Yes — permanent purchases |
| **Gyldenblood** | Selling extracted loot, gold-tier sellables, Nightmare bonuses | Crafting materials, vendor refresh, consumables | Yes — as long as you extract with the loot |

**The critical distinction:** Reputation and Upgrade Tokens are awarded for *completing* objectives. Gyldenblood is only earned by *extracting with* loot. That difference is the entire economy of the game — and the reason wipes feel so expensive.

---

## How to Earn Upgrade Tokens Fast

Tokens are the meta-progression currency: they buy permanent upgrades that make every future run easier. Prioritize earning them over everything else early.

| Source | Tokens | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Contract completion | Base reward per contract | Higher difficulty = more tokens |
| Boss kills | Large one-time rewards | The Lurker Below: 800 Reputation; High Priest: 3,000 |
| Daily challenges | Tiered rewards | Fastest repeatable token source after the story |
| Vendor trade-ins | Turn in found weapons and gear | Found loot is mission-only — trade it in instead of hoarding it |
| Legendary Hunts | Fixed per-cycle reward | One hunt reward per cycle per account |

### Token Spending Priority

1. **Medallion upgrades first** — Medallion: Range II alone cuts extraction time by ~30% (portals placed from safe distance).
2. **Crossbow and Machete upgrades** — your permanent stealth core; they work in every weather.
3. **Blueprints for craftable gear** — the Crossbow (tutorial reward), The Purifier (fire Crossbow, final boss), Shadow Armor set (5 Nocturnal Hunt expeditions).
4. **Maps and torches** — cheap quality-of-life buys that pay off every run.

> **Golden rule:** tokens buy permanence. If a purchase does not persist across expeditions, it is usually not worth tokens — that is what Gyldenblood is for.

## How to Earn Gyldenblood Fast

Gyldenblood is made by selling extracted loot. The value ladder is simple: know what sells, carry the best, and never lose it to a greedy extra minute.

### Top-Selling Loot

| Item | Value | Where It Comes From |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| Gold Silk | 2,000+ Gyldenblood | The Gilded Widow (Mansion of Fools hunt) |
| Relics (Blue Relic tier) | High | Chests, boss rooms, secret caches |
| Hunt materials (Veil Shard, Drowned Idol, Chitin Plate) | High | Legendary hunt targets |
| Centipede Carapace | Medium | Giant Centipedes — Catacombs is the best farm |
| Ectoplasm | Medium | Wraiths — but needed for potions, see below |
| Currency drops (5–50) | Low | Regular enemies — take them only if slots are free |

### The Fastest Farms

| Method | Setup | Rate |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Catacombs carapace loop** | Crossbow + Machete, clear the 2 centipede zones | Reliable medium-value material every run |
| **Legendary Hunt weekly** | Full squad, oil + flares, one target | Best-in-slot gear + top-tier sellables; 20-minute despawn timer |
| **Nightmare contracts** | Unlocked post-story; 1.5x contract payout bonus | The highest token + gold density per expedition |
| **Daily challenges** | Any squad size | Tiered rewards, resets daily — always clear them |
| **Endless expeditions** | Blighted Mound (Cursed Relic weapons), Nocturnal Hunt (Shadow Armor), The Gauntlet (title) | Exclusive loot that sells or equips; repeatable |

## Sell vs Keep — The Loot Rulebook

Not everything that glitters is for the vendor. Crafting materials are worth more inside a recipe than on the counter.

| Item | Verdict | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| Gold Silk, Drowned Idol, Veil Shard | **Sell** | No major recipe demand; 2,000+ on the best sellers |
| Chitin Plate | **Sell unless crafting armor** | Armor material, but the market price is high |
| Centipede Carapace | **Keep first, sell overflow** | Needed for armor crafting |
| Ectoplasm | **Keep** | Used in high-tier potions — selling it cripples your crafting |
| Medicinal Herbs | **Keep** | Base of every Healing Salve recipe |
| Bat Wing | **Keep** | Sanity Tea recipe ingredient |
| Vine Fiber | **Keep** | Rope crafting |
| Rare Crystal, Artifact Core, Essence of Time | **Never sell** | Medallion upgrades require them (Range II, Recall, Group Recall) |

**The one-line rule:** materials you cannot extract with are worth zero. If your pack is full and the choice is "sell the herbs" or "lose the relic," drop the herbs.

## Money Sinks — Where Gyldenblood Goes

| Sink | Cost | Worth It? |
|------|------|-----------|
| Medallion upgrades | Scrap Metal, Copper Wire, Iron Ingot, Rare Crystal, Essence of Time | Yes — highest ROI in the game |
| Consumables (Salves, Bandages, Tonics, Whisper Charms, Flares) | Low per unit | Yes — they are the survival floor |
| Vendor refresh purchases | Moderate | Only if a blueprint or upgrade is on the table |
| Losing loot to a failed extraction | Everything in the pack | Never worth it — extract 5 minutes early |

## The Efficient Money Routine (Post-Story)

1. **Check the contract board for a Legendary Hunt** — if one is up and your party is ready, it out-values everything else on the board (one reward per cycle per account).
2. **Clear the daily challenges** — tiered tokens and gold for objectives you would do anyway.
3. **Run one Nightmare contract with the 1.5x payout** — this is the repeatable gold farm.
4. **Fill the Ox Cart, not your pack** — heavy treasure goes in the cart, freeing your limited personal slots for tools and healing.
5. **Extract on the schedule, not the whim** — set a mental timer at ~25 minutes. The hidden timer (the forest awakens) turns a full cart into a total loss when it hits.

### The Loot-Runner Loadout

For pure farming runs: **Crossbow + Machete**, 3 Bandages, 1 Flare, nothing else. Weight stays under ~35%, so you keep full sprint speed and maximum payload space. Grab the carapace, relics, and sellables, and be out before the first wind shift.

## Common Money Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | The Fix |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| Selling crafting materials | Recipe locks later cost more than the sale | Keep the keep-list above; sell only the overflow |
| Hoarding found weapons | Found gear is mission-only; it vanishes if you do not extract or trade it | Trade in weapons at the vendor before they rot in the pool |
| Chasing loot after the forest wakes | 3x spawns, overrun cart, extraction shifts | Extract on the timer, not the loot table |
| Solo-farming Nightmare unprepared | 1.5x payout means nothing if you wipe | Bring a Whisper Charm + Salve stock; or squad up |
| Ignoring the daily board | Free tokens left on the table every day | Clear challenges before any exploratory run |
