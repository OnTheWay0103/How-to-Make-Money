# Legendary Hunts Guide — Named Monsters, Prep, and Rewards

> Launch Version / Updated: July 31, 2026

Legendary Hunts are The Mound's signature endgame content: named monsters with fixed spawns, unique mechanics, and drops that exist nowhere else. Unlike normal contracts, hunts are announced on the contract board with a target name and a skull icon — and unlike Nightmare contracts, a hunt can fail even if you extract, because the kill is the objective. This guide covers every known hunt target and how to walk out alive.

---

## How Legendary Hunts Work

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Availability | One legendary contract per expedition cycle |
| Objective | Kill the named target — extraction alone is not enough |
| Failure | Hunt target escapes (despawns) after 20 minutes or if it reaches its lair |
| Party | 1–4 players; scales with party size |
| Lockout | One hunt reward per cycle per account |

The hunt target is a boss-tier enemy with a fixed lair location. It patrols a wide area; you choose when and where to fight it. That choice is the whole game.

## Hunt Targets

### The Drowned Baron (Shallow Graves)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Undead / Water |
| Lair | Sunken Chapel, northeast corner |
| Signature mechanic | Drains sanity on proximity; summons drowned adds in 3 waves |
| Weakness | Fire weapons, torch attacks |
| Loot | Baron's Seal (Legendary ring), Drowned Idol (materials) |

**Strategy**: pull him onto dry ground. In water he regenerates and his adds hit harder. Burn the adds each wave before re-engaging — a single wave of 6 drowned can end a run through sanity drain alone.

### The Pale King (The Hollow Woods)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Aberration / Hallucination |
| Lair | The Clearing, map center |
| Signature mechanic | Spawns 3 perfect hallucinations per phase; killing fakes refunds his HP |
| Weakness | Melee strikes to the back after a successful sanity check |
| Loot | Pale Crown (Legendary helmet), Veil Shard (unique material) |

**Strategy**: this hunt tests sanity discipline more than aim. Assign one player to call "real / fake" — never attack a hallucination you have not verified. Phase 2 adds the confusion aura: call checks louder, and do not swing on teammates' targets.

### The Crawling King (The Deep Warrens)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Insectoid / Burrower |
| Lair | The Nest, deep warrens |
| Signature mechanic | Burrows between attacks; armor break requires flare or oil |
| Weakness | Fire; oil-soaked targets take 200% burn damage |
| Loot | King's Mandible (Legendary dagger), Chitin Plate (armor material) |

**Strategy**: bring oil. When he burrows, he is immune to damage — drop oil on his burrow trail and flare it. Three oil-burn cycles break his carapace, after which he is stationary for a full burst window.

### The Gilded Widow (Mansion of Fools)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Arachnid / Poison |
| Lair | The Grand Hall, mansion interior |
| Signature mechanic | Web-locks a player every 20 seconds; poison DoT on extraction |
| Weakness | Fire + stamina management |
| Loot | Widow's Thread (Legendary utility), Gold Silk (sells for 2,000+ Gyldenblood) |

**Strategy**: spread out. Web-lock targets the closest player — if the whole team clusters, the Widow locks three at once. Carry a stamina tonic; breaking webs costs 40 stamina.

## Hunt Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Read the target card — each hunt has a weakness; build for it
- [ ] Fire weapons + oil for every hunt (universal counter to 3 of 4 targets)
- [ ] 3 Healing Salve, 3 Bandages, 1 stamina tonic minimum
- [ ] One player carries 2 flares — for fakes and for burrow reveals
- [ ] Assign a sanity caller before entering the lair
- [ ] Scout the lair once without engaging — know the escape route first
- [ ] Fight on your terms: pull the target away from its lair before burning it

## Reward Table

| Reward | Source | Value |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Legendary weapon/armor piece | Target kill (guaranteed first kill per cycle) | Best-in-slot items |
| Hunt materials | Kill + lair chest | Galleon upgrade tier |
| Exclusive cosmetic | Weekly first hunt | Repeats weekly |
| Gold Silk / rare sellables | Certain targets | 2,000+ Gyldenblood |

Legendary hunt drops are the only source of most best-in-slot items — the weapons guide ranks them, but you cannot buy them. If a hunt is up and your party is ready, do it before anything else on the board.
