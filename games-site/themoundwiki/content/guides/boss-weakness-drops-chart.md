## One-Sentence Strategy per Boss

| Boss | Winning Move |
|------|--------------|
| **The Lurker Below** | Stay at mid-range, roll *toward* the tentacle sweep, and clear each Leech Swarm with a single Oil Lamp throw from the raised platforms. |
| **High Priest of Cthulhu** | Disperse before Phase 2, stay above 60% Sanity at all times, kill summoned Wraiths instantly, and burn everything you have in Phase 3. |
| **The Drowned Baron** | Pull him onto dry ground — in water he regenerates — and burn each wave of adds before re-engaging. |
| **The Pale King** | Assign one player to call "real / fake" — never attack a hallucination you have not verified, or you heal him. |
| **The Crawling King** | Drop oil on his burrow trail and flare it; three oil-burn cycles break his carapace for a full burst window. |
| **The Gilded Widow** | Spread out so she can only web-lock one player, and carry a stamina tonic — breaking webs costs 40 stamina. |

## Universal Rules (applies to every boss)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Fire is the universal counter** | Oil Lamp and explosives are the weakness of 5 of 6 bosses; every hunt prep should include oil |
| **Rain breaks guns** | Matchlock and Flintlock misfire in rain — bring a Crossbow or Machete as a weatherproof backup |
| **Never enter a boss arena low on Sanity** | Sanity-driven mechanics (scream, hallucinations, proximity drain) punish unprepared parties |
| **Scout the arena once without engaging** | Every boss lair has an escape route — know it before you pull the boss |
| **Legendary Hunts can fail even on extraction** | The kill is the objective — if the target escapes (20 min despawn or reaches its lair), the hunt fails |
