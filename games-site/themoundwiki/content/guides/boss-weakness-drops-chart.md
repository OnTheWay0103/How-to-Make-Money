# Is There Boss Content in The Mound? What the Record Shows

> **Rewritten September 17, 2026.** This page used to be a "Boss Weakness & Drops Chart — All 6 Bosses
> Quick Reference". All six bosses, their weaknesses, their phases and their drop tables were invented.
> See [Why this page was rewritten](#why-this-page-was-rewritten).

**No. The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu has no boss tier.** There is no boss arena, no boss health bar, no
boss loot table and no named boss enemy anywhere in the game's official record. The word `boss` does
not appear once in all **40** official Steam announcements for app ID `2569760` — not in the launch
post, not in any of the five patch notes, not in the creature-profile posts, not in the store-page
copy carried in the news feed.

If you came here looking for a weakness table to study before a boss pull, the honest answer is that
there is nothing to pull. This page now covers what the game *does* escalate, so you know what is
actually coming for you.

corpus, and no independent web source describes them either (audit web cross-check). They were not
misnamed, and they were not cut content — they were written into existence by a previous version of
this wiki.

The drops table went with them: *Baron's Seal, Drowned Idol, Pale Crown, Veil Shard, King's Mandible,

Mask of Dagon* — **0 hits each**, in both corpora. Even the currency was invented: there is no
"Gyldenblood". The game's reward currency is called **tokens**, and it is documented.

## What the game escalates with instead

The absence of a boss tier is not the absence of escalation. The Mound escalates along three real
axes, and all three are officially documented.

### 1. The Forest Awakens — the escalating clock

This is the game's genuine endgame pressure, and the developer patches it directly:

> *"Enemies take longer to spawn when the forest wakes up."* — Patch 1.01 notes

The forest waking is a timed escalation inside every expedition: noise and destruction raise it, and
once it is awake the island hunts you. Patch 1.02 also shipped *"Fixed time warnings from the forest
state not appearing"*, which confirms there is a displayed warning state you are supposed to watch —
the closest thing the game has to an enrage timer. Because patch 1.01 *lengthened* the spawn delay,
the developers have been tuning this dial in your favour since launch.

### 2. Contract tiers — the difficulty axis

Contracts are how the game gets harder. There is no difficulty menu; there are better-paying, more
dangerous tiers. Patch 1.02 lists *"Slight increase for tokens given for contract tiers"* as a
balance change, and the launch notes mention *"Contracts now display more mission information and a
greater variety of treasure requirements"*. Players describe the tiers directly — one writes about
running *"Legendary treasure-missions"* and then taking a *"Basic portal-mission"*, and the difficulty
inverting on them. That tier vocabulary is **player-reported**, not developer-published, but the
underlying tier system is official.

### 3. The deep-forest horrors

New threats arrive by patch, not by boss gate:

- Patch 1.04: *"3 new enemies that appear in the depths of the forest. These horrors appear when you least expect them."*
- Patch 1.01: *"Crawling canopy enemy (spider) does 50% less damage"* and *"Fixed Mi-Go enemy behavior which did not cause them to search for players"*
- Patch 1.03: *"Improved backstabbing mechanic: Players can now backstab enemies that have aggro to deal significant damage."*

Note the direction of travel: the studio is adding **more enemies and more counterplay**, not adding
bosses. Since launch they have added a block (patch 1.02, `Q` / `RB` / `R1`), a stun on blocking
(patch 1.03), backstab-on-aggro (patch 1.03) and a reworked melee feel (patch 1.04). Every one of
those is an answer to a *crowd* problem, which is the fight this game actually gives you.

## The threats that are real

If you want a quick-reference table, this is the honest version: only creatures that appear in the
official announcements or in the review corpus. Behaviour notes are quoted from the developer where
the source is a creature profile.

| Threat | Source | What it does |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| **Y'm-bhi** | Official creature post *"The Y'm-bhi: Killing isn't enough"* | Sentries, not chargers. They probe, parry melee and can disarm you, headshots are not always fatal, some pin a player with tentacles, some carry oil lamps and set themselves alight. Killing one does not always end it — the forest can reanimate the body |
| **Tue Tue** | Official creature post *"The Tue Tue: don't wake it"* | Territorial, not a hunter. Get close and it screams, then drops a green-black cloud that takes visibility to near zero. Run from it or shoot it — it screams again when it dies |
| **Mi-Go / Migo** | Patch 1.01 (behaviour fix) + 11 review mentions | Grabs and captures players; patch 1.02 *"Migo enemy captures players for less time in Single-Player"* |
| **Crawling Canopy** | Patch 1.01 damage nerf | A spider. Its damage was cut by 50% because it was over-tuned |
| **Centipedes** | Patch 1.01 known-issues list ("the centipedes around the Logbook Camp") | Swarming, tied to a specific camp location |
| **Doppelgänger** | Patch 1.0 / launch-adjacent notes: *"Improvements to doppelganger behaviour"* | Impersonation — the reason you verify your teammates |
| **The Prowler**, **The Haunter** | **Player-reported only** — 2 and 1 review mentions | Players describe both as run-enders on even Basic missions. Not named in any announcement |

> **Careful with this table.** The bottom row is player testimony, not documentation, and the game
> has a **third-party creature roster** of roughly 25 entries (Deep One, Shoggoth Worm, Dark Young of
> Shub-Niggurath, Hound of Tindalos, Formless Spawn and others) that appears **0 times** in the
> official announcements and **0 times** in the reviews. We have not merged those into this page. Our
> [Enemy Bestiary](/guides/enemy-bestiary) keeps them in a clearly-labelled third-party section.

## What players say about the missing boss tier

This is worth reading before you buy, because the gap is one of the most consistent criticisms in the
corpus — and a player asking for a feature is evidence the feature is absent, not evidence it exists.

- *"no bosses, every mission is pretty much the same, no real progression"* — review `234197520`, 31 Aug 2026
- *"was dissapointed in ending, was hoping a boss fight or something more"* — review `234630362`, 6 Sep 2026
- *"We need some mini bosses or more of a threat from certain baddies"* — review `234478102`, 4 Sep 2026
- *"more weapons, more enemy types, more eldritch horrors, more 'bosses', more tools and equipment"* — review `232115269`, 5 Aug 2026, 422 upvotes

One caution when reading other coverage: players use "boss" loosely for anything big. A highly upvoted
review complains that *"Boss combat is a joke"* — but in context that is a complaint about fighting the
game's large creatures, not a reference to a boss tier. When you see the word in a review, check
whether the reviewer means a *mechanic* or just *a big enemy*.

| Phase-2 / Phase-3 boss mechanics ("50% ballistic reduction", "AoE Sanity scream breaks anyone below 40% Sanity", "enrage at 25%") | Invented. The only announcement uses of "phase" are *"during this testing phase"* and *"the current phase of The Mound"* — neither is a boss phase |

| "Never enter a boss arena low on Sanity" / "Scout the arena once" | Arena advice for arenas that do not exist |

The old page also carried a *"Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026"* banner, which gave an invented
table the appearance of a dated, maintained reference. A date is not a source.

### Method

- **Official corpus:** Steam `ISteamNews` for app ID `2569760`, all **40** items, full text.
  Reproduced for this page with a case-insensitive, word-boundary regex over the 40 news blocks:
  `\bboss` = **0**; `reputation` = **0**; `Isle of Voices` = **0**; `Sunken Grotto` = **0**;
  `Shallow Graves` = **0**; `Hollow Woods` = **0**; `Deep Warrens` = **0**; `Mansion of Fools` = **0**;
  `Lurker Below` = **0**; `Drowned Baron` = **0**; `Pale King` = **0**; `Crawling King` = **0**;
  `Gilded Widow` = **0**; `High Priest` = **0**.
- **App-ID check:** `2569760` plus four separately-listed add-on apps (`4630450`, `4630440`,
  `4630460`, `4925780`), each returning **0 news items** — there is no second announcement stream to
  miss, and no separate demo/playtest app feed.
- **Community corpus:** Steam reviews, **576** unique items, IDs retained for every quote.
  `\bboss` = **8 hits**, and reading the context of all eight shows six are requests for bosses, one is
  a loosely-worded complaint about large enemies, and one is an "ending" complaint.
  `reputation` = **0**; `legendary hunt` = **0**; `Gyldenblood` = **0**.
- **Names, not substrings:** every count above was read in context. `Crawling King` is a different
  string from the real creature `Crawling Canopy`, and `boss` was counted with a word boundary so
  "combat" substring noise could not inflate the number.
- **Web cross-check:** third-party sources were checked by the audit lead; we cite those conclusions
  but did not re-run the searches for this page.
