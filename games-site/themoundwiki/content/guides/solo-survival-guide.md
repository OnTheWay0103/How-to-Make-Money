# The Mound Solo Survival Guide — Staying Alive Alone

> **Launch Version / Updated: July 27, 2026**

Survival in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu** is a constant battle against the environment, the enemies, and your own Sanity. Playing solo removes your safety net — no teammate to watch your back, confirm hallucinations, or revive you when the Y'm-bhi corner you. This guide focuses on the survival fundamentals you need to extract alive, every time.

---

## The Four Pillars of Solo Survival

| Pillar | Priority | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Stealth** | Highest | Avoid fights you cannot win — detection is death |
| **Resource Management** | High | Solo loot economy is unforgiving; waste nothing |
| **Sanity Maintenance** | High | Low Sanity kills more solo players than enemies |
| **Extraction Planning** | Critical | Know your exit before you enter |

---

## 1. Stealth — Your Primary Defense

Without teammates to draw aggro or cover your flanks, stealth is your most important survival tool.

### Stealth Fundamentals

| Mechanic | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Crouch ** | Reduces detection range by 60%. Use this 90% of the time. |
| **Light sources** | Torches and lanterns increase detection range by 100%. Turn them off when near enemies. |
| **Movement speed** | Walking is silent. Running is audible from 20m. Sprinting is audible from 40m. |
| **Noise sources** | Gunfire (200m), melee (30m), opening chests (15m), river wading (25m) |
| **Enemy vision** | 120-degree cone during day. 60-degree cone at night. Y'm-bhi have poor night vision. |

### Solo Stealth Priority

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| You hear enemy patrol sounds | Crouch, stop moving, locate the source |
| Enemy is 30m+ away | Circle around (wide berth) |
| Enemy is 15-30m away | Hide behind terrain, wait for patrol to pass |
| Enemy is < 15m away | Do not move. Wait. They may pass without detecting you. |
| Enemy has detected you | Run to the nearest obstacle that breaks line-of-sight, then hide and wait 30s |

### The Solo Stealth Route

1. **Spawn → first cover**: Move immediately to dense foliage or rock cover within 10s of spawning
2. **Initial scan**: Wait 15s in cover. Listen for patrol sounds. Identify nearest enemy positions.
3. **Route planning**: Plot a path that uses terrain (trees, rocks, ruins) to break line-of-sight
4. **Objectives**: Complete contract objectives only if they are on your extraction route
5. **Extraction**: Move to extraction point using the least-traveled path — even if it takes twice as long

## 2. Resource Management

Solo players must be ruthless about resource efficiency. You have no one to share inventory with and no one to carry extra supplies.

### Essential Solo Loadout

| Item | Quantity | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| **Machete** | 1 (equipped) | Silent kills on isolated Y'm-bhi. Saves ammo. |
| **Crossbow** | 1 (equipped) | Silent ranged kills. 2-shots most standard enemies. |
| **Healing Salve** | 3 | Your only reliable heal. Do not use until below 50% HP. |
| **Sanity Tea** | 2 | Essential — without it, hallucinations will waste time |
| **Oil Lamp** | 1 (full fuel) | Light without the detection penalty of torches |
| **Empty Bottles** | 2 | Fill at any water source for emergency healing |

### Resources to Prioritize

| Resource | Priority | Use |
|----------|----------|-----|
| Herbs (all types) | High | Craft healing salves and sanity tea |
| Ammo (crossbow bolts) | High | Ammo is scarce in solo — every shot counts |
| Purified Water | Medium | Sanity restoration, healing base |
| Ancient Relics | Medium | High sell value, but weigh 2 units each |
| Lore Pages | Low (solo) | Useful for story but no survival benefit |

### Resource Conservation Rules

- **Do not engage groups larger than 2 enemies** — the resource cost is never worth it
- **Harvest only what you need** — 10 herbs is a full expedition supply
- **Repair gear between expeditions**, not during — carrying repair kits wastes inventory space
- **Drop low-value loot** to make room for high-value items (see our [Extraction Guide](/themound/content/guides/extraction-guide) for loot value tiers)

## 3. Sanity Maintenance

Sanity management is arguably harder solo because no one can confirm hallucinations for you.

### Solo Sanity Tips

| Challenge | Solo Solution |
|-----------|---------------|
| No one to confirm hallucinations | Close your eyes for 3 seconds. Hallucinations despawn. Real enemies stay. |
| Faster Sanity drain | Sanity drains 25% faster solo. Bring extra Sanity Tea. |
| False extraction markers | Hallucination-caused. Check your compass — if the needle is spinning, it is fake. |
| Auditory hallucinations | You hear whispers, footsteps, or gunfire that is not real. Ignore all audio cues during Stage 2+ Sanity. |

### Sanity Management Priority

| Sanity Level | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| **100-60% (Stage 1)** | Normal gameplay. Minor visual distortion — ignore it. |
| **60-25% (Stage 2)** | Drink Sanity Tea. Hallucinations begin. Do not trust what you see. |
| **Below 25% (Stage 3)** | Extract immediately. You are at severe disadvantage. Enemies deal bonus Sanity damage. |

### Sanity Restoration Methods

| Method | Sanity Restored | Resource Cost | Solo Viability |
|--------|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| Sanity Tea | 25% | 3 Herbs + 1 Purified Water | Best option — craft before expedition |
| Campfire (rest) | 10% per minute | 3 Wood | Good — find or build a campfire |
| Shrine offering | 40% | 1 Ancient Relic | Viable if you have spare relics |
| Completing contract objective | 15% | None (objective-based) | Always worth doing |
| Extracting | Full restore | None | End-of-run refresh |

## 4. Extraction Planning

Extraction in solo is harder than in co-op. The hidden timer ("The Forest Awakens") makes late-game escapes much more dangerous without a teammate covering your back.

### Solo Extraction Strategy

| Phase | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Pre-drop** | Memorize 2-3 extraction point locations on your map before entering |
| **Early (0-10 min)** | Loot edge areas. Do not go deep. Keep extraction route open. |
| **Mid (10-20 min)** | If you have good loot, extract now. 80% of solo deaths happen after 15 minutes. |
| **Late (20+ min)** | The Forest Awakens. Enemies respawn, become more aggressive, and new patrols appear. Extract at the nearest point immediately. |

### When to Abandon

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Below 50% HP with no Healing Salve | Abandon objective. Extract now. |
| Sanity below 25% | Abandon everything. Extract now. |
| More than 3 enemies between you and objective | Not worth fighting through. Extract with what you have. |
| You hear gunfire near your extraction point | Wait 2 minutes or find a different extraction point |
| Inventory full of high-value loot | Extract immediately — a full bag of green loot is worth more than a death bag |

### Solo Extraction Priority

1. **Full inventory of useful loot?** → Extract
2. **Contract completed?** → Extract
3. **Sanity low?** → Extract
4. **Heard The Forest Awakens?** → Extract at nearest point
5. **Empty inventory, early in run?** → Keep looting for 5 more minutes, then extract

## Solo Survival Quick Reference

### Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Crouch 90% of the time | Sprint through open areas |
| Carry 2 Sanity Tea minimum | Engage groups of 3+ enemies |
| Memorize extraction points | Go deep without a clear exit plan |
| Abandon fights that take too long | Waste resources on optional enemies |
| Use the environment for cover | Stay in one position for more than 5 minutes |
| Extract with good loot early | Push deeper just because you are still alive |

### Survival Stats for Solo Players

| If you... | Your estimated survival rate... |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Always crouch near enemies | 75% extraction rate |
| Use gunfire on patrol enemies | 40% extraction rate (attracts nearby enemies) |
| Manage Sanity with Tea | 70% extraction rate |
| Ignore Sanity completely | 30% extraction rate |
| Plan extraction route pre-drop | 80% extraction rate |
| Wander randomly | 45% extraction rate |
