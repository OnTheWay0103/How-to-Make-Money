# The Mound Stealth Guide — Noise Control, Silent Kills, and Avoiding Detection

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

In **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu**, combat is a failure state. Every gunshot you fire, every sprint through undergrowth, and every chest you wrench open tells the forest where you are — and the forest remembers. The players who extract consistently are not the best fighters; they are the ones who never had to fight. This guide covers the full stealth toolkit: how noise works, how detection works, how to kill silently, and how to move through enemy territory without ever being seen.

---

## How Noise Works — The Core Stealth Stat

The Mound's monsters are attracted to **noise**, not sight alone. Every action you take broadcasts at a distance, and the game is honest about it:

| Action | Noise Radius | Risky? |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| Gunfire (Matchlock / Flintlock) | ~200m | Very — this is a map-wide dinner bell |
| River wading | ~25m | Moderate — cross water only when needed |
| Melee attacks | ~30m | Low — but it adds up over a fight |
| Opening chests | ~15m | Low — wait for patrols to pass first |
| Running | Loud footsteps | High — walking is half the distance |
| Walking | Quiet footsteps | Safe |
| Crouching | Almost silent | Safest — near-invisible to hearing |

### The Noise Rules

1. **Never fire a gun unless the objective demands it.** A 200m noise radius means Y'm-bhi from three patrol zones converge on you.
2. **Run in bursts.** Sprint across open ground only when you have verified no patrol is within hearing range — then resume walking.
3. **Crouch through enemy territory.** Crouching nearly eliminates footstep noise and shrinks your detection radius.
4. **Open chests on a timer.** A chest takes a moment to open; if a patrol is within 15m, the sound gives away your position. Clear the area first.
5. **Cross rivers at the shallowest point.** Wading noise is a compromise, not a free pass. Do it fast and move on.

## How Detection Works

Enemies detect you through three channels: **hearing** (noise above), **sight** (line of sight and light), and **territory** (entering their patrol zone).

### Sight and Light

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Standing in open light | Visible from maximum distance |
| Standing in shadows | Detection distance cut roughly in half |
| Carrying an unshaded lamp | Enemies see the light before they see you — shade it or keep distance |
| Direct eye contact with a Berserker | Triggers aggression even at range |

### Enemy Detection Radius Differences

| Enemy | Detection Notes |
|-------|-----------------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry | Standard radius; predictable patrol pauses |
| Y'm-bhi Scout | Detects from farther away; calls reinforcements when alerted |
| Y'm-bhi Berserker | Largest radius; 50% chance of detecting a backstab approach |
| Wraith | Senses through walls in its territory — stealth is nearly impossible |
| Vine Creature | Vibration-based; triggered by footsteps, not sight |

### The Golden Rules of Moving Undetected

1. **Patrols are clocks, not guards.** Every Y'm-bhi has a fixed route with predictable pauses. Watch one full loop before crossing its path.
2. **Angle away, then commit.** Cross behind a patroller as it turns away from you — never in front of its turn point.
3. **Let the scout lead.** In a squad, one player watches the enemy while the rest move; if a patrol changes timing, the watcher calls it.
4. **If you are seen, break line of sight first.** Duck behind cover and stop moving. Most Y'm-bhi lose track of you if you disappear from sight before they fully aggro.

## Silent Kills — Backstab and Finishers

The backstab is the most efficient kill in the game: silent, instant, and it does not alert the rest of the map.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Position | Directly behind the enemy (180-degree rear arc) |
| Weapon | Any melee weapon, or the unarmed prompt |
| Enemy type | Humanoids only (all Y'm-bhi variants) |
| Noise | Silent — nearby enemies are not alerted |
| Animation | ~2 seconds — you are vulnerable while it plays |

### Backstab Setup Checklist

- [ ] Confirm no other enemy can see you (the prompt will not appear if they can)
- [ ] Crouch to the enemy's rear arc
- [ ] Wait for the patrol to angle away from you
- [ ] Commit only when the animation will not end inside another enemy's patrol path
- [ ] Do not backstab mid-group — one Berserker's detection roll can turn a silent kill into a brawl

### Backstab Priority Targets

| Enemy | Backstab Result | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry | Instant kill | Easiest target in the game |
| Y'm-bhi Scout | Instant kill | Worth it — prevents reinforcement calls |
| Y'm-bhi Berserker | One-shot, harder approach | 50% detection chance during approach; use a Crossbow headshot instead |
| Y'm-bhi Priest | Not possible | Never faces away; kill with Crossbow headshot + melee |

## Silent Tools and Weapons

| Tool | Why It's Silent | Best Use |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Crossbow | No gunshot; bolts are near-silent | Picking off Y'm-bhi before they alert others; the stealth primary |
| Francesca Axe | Silent throw, retrievable | Ranged silent kill without line-of-sight commitment |
| Machete | No noise on hit; backstab finisher | The close-range stealth finisher every loadout carries |
| Oil Lamp | Not a weapon — a light | Stuns Y'm-bhi for 2–3s; instantly kills Ghost Bats; repels Shadow Wraiths |
| Mask of Nyarlathotep | Makes enemies ignore you entirely | Crossing open ground a patrol controls |
| Whisper Charm | Prevents one failed Sanity check | Insurance for stealth routes that push low-Sanity territory |
| Flare | Distracts / fakes detection | Reveals burrowed enemies and denies areas without a gunshot |

### Mask of Nyarlathotep — The Emergency Stealth Tool

The Mask lets you walk among Y'm-bhi completely undetected. It is the most powerful stealth tool in the game — and the most dangerous.

| Mask Mechanic | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| Aggro immunity | Nearby enemies ignore you while worn |
| Cost | Sanity drains continuously; rate increases over time |
| Friendly fire | Teammates can still damage you — they may mistake you for an enemy |
| Safe window | ~30 seconds before Sanity becomes critical |

**Mask safety rules:** announce "Mask on" every time you activate it; move predictably so teammates do not shoot you; keep the window short; have a healing or Sanity item ready for when it comes off. Never wear it when your Sanity is already low, and never wear it mid-fight.

## Light Management — Stealth's Second Currency

Light is a double-edged sword: it is the best crowd-control tool against light-sensitive enemies, but it makes you visible.

| Light Source | Enemy Effect | Visibility Cost |
|--------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Oil Lamp | Stuns Y'm-bhi 2–3s; kills Ghost Bats; repels Shadow Wraiths | Visible beacon at range |
| Torch | Same effects, shorter range | Slightly smaller beacon |
| No light | — | Dark zones drain Sanity; caves are pitch black |

### Light Rules for Stealth

1. **Stun before you strike.** A lamp flash stuns a Y'm-bhi long enough for a follow-up kill without noise.
2. **Extinguish to cross.** In forest maps, kill the lamp before crossing an open field, then relight in cover.
3. **Night expeditions are lamp-mandatory.** The sanity drain of darkness outweighs the visibility risk — shade the lamp when you can, never drop it.
4. **Ghost Bats are free kills.** One flash, one instant kill, zero noise, zero ammo. Do not waste bolts on them.

**Shared-pool note:** the weapons rack has a shared party pool — if there is one Crossbow, only one player can carry it. Decide the silent role before departure.

## The Stealth Route — A Complete Plan

1. **Deploy, then stand still for 10 seconds.** Let the drop-in noise settle and read the first patrol loop.
2. **Mark your extraction point early.** Every stealth route needs a known exit.
3. **Clear your return path first.** Stealth-kill the patrols along your escape route before pushing to the objective — the way out is worth more than the way in.
4. **Chest on the way back, not the way in.** Fully-looted players move slower and panic louder.
5. **If the forest awakens, stop being clever.** When enemy spawns multiply and patrols start hunting, stealth windows shrink — switch to the extraction checklist and leave.
