# The Mound Stealth Guide — Noise Control, Silent Kills, and Avoiding Detection

> **Rewritten September 17, 2026.** This page previously assigned noise radii in metres, detection radii per fabricated enemy tier ("Y'm-bhi Sentry", "Y'm-bhi Scout", "Y'm-bhi Berserker", "Wraith", "Vine Creature"), a "~30 second Mask safe window", and two items that do not appear in any source ("Whisper Charm", "Flare"). Those figures and names are gone. Everything below is either the developer's own wording or a documented patch change.

In **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu**, the loudest thing you can do is fire a gun, and the game tells you so in its own promotional copy. Combat is not a failure state because the developers are being poetic — it is a failure state because noise is the mechanism the whole threat system runs on.

You are not trying to win fights. You are trying to finish a contract without having one.

---

## How noise works

The developer's environment post states the rule plainly: *"Dense vegetation and branches slow your movement. You can cut through with a machete, but too much noise risks drawing out hidden creatures."* And *"sometimes the danger you see isn't as bad as the danger you might awaken if you choose to use your firearms."*

What the sources support is an **ordering**, not a set of distances. Patch notes and official posts never publish noise radii, so any number claiming to be one is invented. What you can rely on:

| Action | Relative noise | Source for the claim |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Firearms (Matchlock, Flintlock) | **Loudest thing you can do.** Rain disables them; noise does not. | Official: a soaked fuse means *"your gunpowder weapons won't fire"* — the loudness is the documented trade-off for carrying them. |
| Striking the Medallion | **Deliberately loud.** It is designed to draw attention. | Official: *"The impact sends a pulse through the forest, an audible sound that attracts unwanted attention while also agitating the environment itself."* |
| Cutting vegetation with the Machete | Loud enough to *"risk drawing out hidden creatures"*. | Official environment post, quoted above. |
| Sprinting / running | Louder than walking. | Reviewers and the general stealth consensus, not an official number. |
| Walking | Quiet. | — |
| Crouching | Quietest. | — |
| A Tue Tue's scream | **Area-wide alarm.** Not yours to control once triggered. | Official Tue Tue post: *"The kind of noise that tells every creature in the area exactly where you are."* |

### The noise rules

1. **Never fire a gun for a target you could have avoided.** The gun's cost is not the ammo, it is the alarm.
2. **Treat the Medallion as a flare, not a tool.** It is struck on purpose to reveal buried treasure and it *"agitates the environment itself"* — use it when you are ready for what it brings, not as a routine sweep.
3. **Cut only when you must.** Dense growth slows you, but the machete is a noise source.
4. **Run in bursts across open ground, then resume walking.**
5. **Crouch as your default in occupied territory.**
6. **Never throw anything near a Tue Tue.** That is the documented trigger for the loudest single noise event in the game.

## How detection works

Detection has three real channels, and the third one is the one players forget.

| Channel | How it works | Counter |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| **Hearing** | Noise above. | Crouch, walk, avoid firearms. |
| **Sight** | Line of sight, and light you are carrying. | Break sightlines; shade the lamp. |
| **Stress** | Y'm-bhi *"watching, testing distance, and building stress"* rather than attacking outright. | **Leave during the probe window.** The creature is still deciding. |

That third channel is the important one and it is officially documented. A Y'm-bhi that has spotted you has not necessarily decided to fight you: *"When a Y'm-bhi spots players, it often probes instead of attacking."*

### Reading a Y'm-bhi patrol

The developer gave you a genuine tool for this, and it is not a fabricated stat table — it is a shop item:

> *"Using a crucifix reveals their stress level through the glow of their eyes, giving squads a chance to anticipate what comes next."*

So the stealth loop against Y'm-bhi is: **hold the Crucifix, read the eye-glow, and act on the reading** — leave while stress is low, reposition when it climbs, and expect the peak to be either a charge *or* a retreat into an ambush (*"Sometimes, they retreat into the forest, repositioning for an ambush when players move on"*).

### What light does

Light is documented, specific, and narrower than most guides claim:

- *"Light can briefly blind Y'm-bhi in darkness - **unless they carry light of their own**."*

That exception is the whole mechanic. Blinding works, right up until the Y'm-bhi is the one holding the lamp — at which point it may *"set themselves ablaze and charge players in flames."* Do not build a plan that assumes light always wins.

## Silent kills — what the backstab actually does

The fabricated version of this page promised an instant-kill rear-arc execution on every Y'm-bhi tier. The real mechanic is narrower and better, because it was patched into something deliberate.

**Official patch 1.03:** *"Improved backstabbing mechanic: Players can now backstab enemies that have aggro to deal significant damage. (Enemies with no aggro are still executed)"*

That single patch note is the entire documented backstab system, and it gives you two distinct moves:

| Situation | Result | How to use it |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Enemy has **no aggro** on you | **Execution** — the kill | The classic stealth approach. Get behind an unaware enemy. |
| Enemy **has aggro** | **Significant damage**, not a kill | This is a combat tool. Backstabbing a Y'm-bhi that is already fighting you is now worth doing. |

What the sources do **not** support, and what has been removed from this page: a "180-degree rear arc", a "2-second animation", a per-tier backstab success table, and claims about which fabricated variants can or cannot be backstabbed. The patch note does not describe arcs or timings, and no other source does either.

### Backstab discipline

- [ ] Approach crouched and slow — you are trading speed for silence.
- [ ] Confirm nothing else is watching. A backstab that pulls a second patrol is worse than no backstab.
- [ ] Use the **execute** on unaware targets and the **damage** version mid-fight when you get a free angle.
- [ ] Remember the Y'm-bhi can **parry and disarm** you. A failed approach against an aware Y'm-bhi costs you your weapon, not just your position.

## Silent tools

| Tool | Why it keeps you quiet | Documented basis |
|------|------------------------|------------------|
| **Crossbow** | No gunshot; bolts pierce armour at close range. | Official: *"Unlike a traditional bow, bolts can pierce armor at very close range."* |
| **Francesca Axe** | Thrown, and **retrievable** — a ranged kill with no gunpowder. | Official: *"It is a weapon of precision... A well-placed strike can shift an encounter, while a misjudged throw leaves you exposed."* |
| **Machete** | No ammo, no weather dependency, no gunshot. Also your vegetation-cutter. | Official (movement): *"You can cut through with a machete."* |
| **Crucifix** | Not a weapon — an **information** tool. | Official: reveals Y'm-bhi stress through eye-glow. |
| **Mask of Nyarlathotep** | Documented infiltration tool. See below. | Official: *"allows its wearer to move among the Y'm-bhi unnoticed."* |
| **Oil Lamp / Torch / Lantern** | Light you control. The Lamp is rain-proof; the torch flickers. | Official: *"Unlike the torch, it won't flicker or die under the rain."* |

> **Removed:** "Whisper Charm" and "Flare". Both return **0 hits** across all 39 announcements and all 576 reviews. If you have seen them on other pages of this wiki, they are not real items.

### The shared arsenal — plan it before you leave

This is official, it is quoted from *"The Captain's bargain"*, and it changes how you allocate silent gear:

> *"Each contract comes with a selection of equipment for your party — a handful of weapons, a few precious supplies, a single lamp against the oppressive dark. These are not individual loadouts; they are a shared arsenal."*

You cannot decide at the dock that "everyone brings a Crossbow". If the contract gave the party one Crossbow, then one player has it. The stealth-relevant consequence: **assign the silent role out loud before departure**, because the loud role is not a choice you get to make for everyone.

## The Mask of Nyarlathotep — documented cost, not invented numbers

The Mask is real, official, and its costs are documented. Read the developer's own description before you plan around it:

| Mask property | Developer's words |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Effect | *"allows its wearer to move among the Y'm-bhi unnoticed"* |
| Condition | *"provided it's donned before attention is drawn"* |
| Duration | *"For a time, the undead will accept you as one of your own."* |
| Mental cost | *"Wearing the mask gnaws at the mind, inflicting a creeping dread that leaves the wearer vulnerable if violence erupts."* |
| Friendly-fire risk | *"the illusion isn't selective: to your allies, you may look no different from the creatures you're trying to fool."* |

**Removed from this page:** the "~30 second safe window" and the "Sanity drains continuously, rate increases over time" model. Neither appears in any source. The developer says *"for a time"* and does not quantify it — so neither does this page.

**Mask safety rules that survive the cut:**

1. **Don it before you are seen.** This is the documented condition. Putting it on mid-approach does nothing.
2. **Announce it on voice every time.** The illusion is not selective — your squad sees a monster.
3. **Move predictably.** Your teammates cannot tell you apart from the undead, so give them nothing to misread.
4. **Do not wear it into a fight.** *"Vulnerable if violence erupts"* is the documented outcome.
5. **Watch your Sanity.** *"Creeping dread"* is the documented direction of travel, even without a number attached.

## Light management

Light is a real trade: it keeps the dark and its Sanity drain at arm's length, and it makes you a beacon.

| Source | Documented behaviour | Stealth cost |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Oil Lamp** | *"it won't flicker or die under the rain, and its steady glow offers players a brief sense of control"* | Steady, and therefore a steady beacon. |
| **Torch** | Flickers and dies in rain. | Shorter, less reliable. |
| **Lantern** | A light source the game treats as gear. | Same beacon problem. |
| **No light** | — | The dark is the reason Sanity drains. |

### Light rules for stealth

1. **Blind with light, then strike.** Officially supported — *"Light can briefly blind Y'm-bhi in darkness."*
2. **Expect the exception.** The Y'm-bhi carrying its own lamp does not get blinded; it gets lit on fire and charges you.
3. **Shade or extinguish to cross open ground, relight in cover.**
4. **Never drop the lamp to be stealthy in a dark zone.** Trading Sanity for concealment in a cave is how hallucinations start.

## Stealth loadouts

Because the contract supplies a shared rack, these are **role assignments**, not shopping lists.

| Role | What you are asking for from the rack | Why it works |
|------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| **Solo / quiet** | Crossbow + Machete | No gunshot, no weather dependency, works in rain when firearms do not. |
| **Solo / dark** | Crossbow + Oil Lamp + Machete | Lamp for the dark and for blinding; Crossbow stays silent. |
| **Squad scout** | Crossbow (+ Crucifix if the party has one) | You read the patrol with the Crucifix and break it with the Crossbow. |
| **Squad light-bearer** | Oil Lamp + Machete | You own the darkness and the blind windows. |
| **Infiltrator** | Mask of Nyarlathotep when the shop offers it | The only documented way to walk through Y'm-bhi. Requires voice discipline. |

**Departure checklist:** who has the Crossbow, who has the Lamp, who holds the Crucifix, and who is carrying the Medallion. Four items, four names, agreed before you land.

## The stealth route

1. **Land, then stop moving.** Let the drop-in noise settle and read the first patrol loop.
2. **Decide your exit before your entry.** Stealth runs fail on the way out, not the way in.
3. **Use the Crucifix to read stress, not to fight.** Low glow means you can still pass.
4. **Leave during the probe.** A Y'm-bhi watching you is a Y'm-bhi you can still walk away from.
5. **Never run from a Y'm-bhi that has committed.** They are *"slightly faster than you through the jungle"*. Break line of sight and hold still instead.
6. **If the forest wakes up, stop being clever.** Enemy spawns lengthen rather than stop (*"Enemies take longer to spawn when the forest wakes up"*), and once you are loud enough the windows close. Extract.
