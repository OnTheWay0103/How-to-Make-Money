## Crossplay Status — Updated for Patch 1.02

Crossplay in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu** is real, but it is not the seamless "everyone plays together" system the launch marketing implied. Here is the honest picture as of **Patch 1.02 (late July 2026)**, based on player reports and reviews:

| Platform Combination | Status | Notes |
|------|:--:|------|
| PC (Steam) ↔ Xbox Series X/S | ✅ **Working** | Confirmed working by multiple player reports and reviews — invite via lobby code or Steam/Xbox friend systems |
| PC (Steam) ↔ PS5 | ⚠️ **Inconsistent** | Crossplay exists, but PS5 lobbies are unreliable — console players frequently cannot find PC-hosted rooms |
| PS5 ↔ Xbox Series X/S | ⚠️ **Inconsistent** | Same lobby-finding issues as above; mixed results |
| All three together | ⚠️ **Works when it connects** | Possible, but expect lobby-finding failures and voice chat problems |

**The short version:** PC and Xbox players can reliably play together. PS5 crossplay is technically supported but buggy in practice — players on PS5 report long waits or failure to find rooms hosted on other platforms. If you are planning a mixed squad, assume the PC ↔ Xbox pairing is the dependable one and the PS5 pairing may need retries or third-party voice.

## How Many Players?

The Mound supports **up to 4 players** in online co-op. You can also play solo with AI companions or true solo (no AI teammates).

| Mode | Players | Difficulty |
|------|:--:|------|
| Solo (with AI) | 1 + 3 AI | Medium |
| True Solo | 1, no AI | Hard |
| Duo Co-op | 2 | Medium-Hard |
| Trio Co-op | 3 | Medium |
| Full Squad | 4 | Balanced (intended experience) |

## Is There Couch Co-op?

**No.** The Mound does not support local/couch co-op. All multiplayer is online only. Each player needs their own copy of the game and their own device.

## Known Lobby & Matchmaking Problems (1.02)

These are the most-reported multiplayer issues — worth knowing before you plan a session:

| Problem | What Happens | Workaround |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| **Console players can't find PC rooms** | PS5 (and sometimes Xbox) players searching for games rarely match with PC-hosted lobbies | Have the PC player join the console player's room instead, or use the lobby code/invite flow directly |
| **No match ID system** | There is no visible match ID to enter for precise joining; random search is the only "public" path | Always use the **6-character lobby code** or a platform invite (see below) |
| **Mixed-language lobbies** | Random matchmaking can drop you into lobbies with players who speak a different language — no region or language filter exists | Play with friends; avoid random matchmaking for serious runs |
| **Cross-platform voice broken** | In-game voice chat is unreliable across platforms — it cuts out or never connects | Use a third-party voice app (Discord/Party Chat) for cross-platform squads |
| **No in-game friends list across platforms** | The game has no cross-platform friend system; you cannot see another platform's friends in-game | Exchange lobby codes on Discord/other chat before launching |

### Why the Lobby Code Is Your Best Tool

Because matchmaking is unreliable and there is no match ID, the **6-character lobby code** is the reliable path to crossplay:

1. Host starts a game from the **Expedition** menu.
2. Set privacy to **Friends Only** (or Public for code-based joins).
3. Share the **6-character code** with friends on any platform — via Discord, phone, whatever.
4. Friends select **Join Game** and enter the code.

If the code join fails, the fallback is a **platform invite**: on Xbox use the Xbox party invite flow; on PC invite through Steam if your friend is on Steam. Mixed platform pairs (PC host → PS5 friend) are the most likely to need several attempts.

## How to Play With Friends — Step by Step

### Hosting
1. From main menu, select **"Expedition"**
2. Choose **"Host Game"**
3. Set privacy to **Friends Only**
4. Share the **6-character lobby code** with friends
5. Wait for everyone to ready up, then start

### Joining
1. Select **"Expedition"** → **"Join Game"**
2. Enter the lobby code from the host
3. Select your character and loadout
4. Ready up

### Crossplay Session Tips
- **PC ↔ Xbox is the dependable pairing** — plan around it for serious progression runs
- **PS5 in the squad?** Budget extra setup time and have a third-party voice chat ready
- **Difficulty scales with the host** — if you are bringing a new player, let them host so the contract difficulty matches their level (see the [Contracts Guide](/guides/contracts-guide))

## Crossplay Troubleshooting Checklist

Work through these in order when crossplay fails:

- [ ] Both games updated to **Patch 1.02** (version mismatch blocks crossplay)
- [ ] Host recreated the lobby **after** friends tried to join (stale lobbies are a common failure)
- [ ] Lobby code entered exactly — 6 characters, case-sensitive if the game distinguishes
- [ ] Try the reverse direction: **console player hosts, PC player joins**
- [ ] Restart the game fully (not just to menu) on all devices
- [ ] Check **NAT type** — open NAT is required for reliable hosting; UPnP enabled on the router
- [ ] Platform accounts online: PSN online / Xbox Live / Steam online all required
- [ ] Firewall/antivirus not blocking the game executable
- [ ] If voice fails specifically: use Discord/Party Chat for the session
- [ ] Still stuck? Check the [Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes) for known issues, and report on the official Discord

## Proximity Voice Chat

The Mound features **proximity-based voice chat** — a key gameplay mechanic. Players can only hear each other when close together. This ties into the sanity system: when separated, you can't communicate, making hallucinations more convincing.

**Tips for voice chat:**
- Stay close to maintain communication
- Agree on a meeting point if separated
- Use the ping system for silent communication
- The proximity system also affects enemy awareness — loud voices attract monsters
- **Cross-platform squads:** in-game voice may cut out across platforms — fall back to a third-party app, and treat the proximity gimmick as a PC↔Xbox bonus rather than a guarantee

## Online Requirements

- Internet connection required for co-op
- Solo play may be available offline (check platform-specific requirements)
- No subscription needed on PC; PS5/Xbox require their respective online services
