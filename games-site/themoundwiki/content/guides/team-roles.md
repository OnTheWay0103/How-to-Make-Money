## Overview

The Mound is designed for **4-player co-op**, but every slot matters. A balanced team can push 3 floors deeper than a group of solo-minded players. This guide covers the five primary roles, recommended builds, and how they fit together.

---

## The Five Roles

| Role | Primary Job | Difficulty | Recommended For |
|------|------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Vanguard | Absorb damage, control enemy positioning | Medium | Players who enjoy tanking |
| Warden | Sanity management, cleansing, healing | Hard | Support-oriented players |
| Striker | Single-target damage, elite elimination | Easy | New players |
| Harrier | Area denial, kiting, speed | Medium | Experienced players |
| Specialist | Utility, traps, forbidden lore | Hard | Puzzle-solvers, lore hunters |

---

## Vanguard

The frontline. Your Vanguard holds enemy attention while the rest of the team deals damage and handles objectives.

**Core Stats:** Vitality > Fortitude > Strength

**Recommended Loadout:**

| Slot | Item | Alternative |
|------|------|-------------|
| Weapon | Bulwark Shield + Broadsword | Greatmace (slower, higher stagger) |
| Armor | Iron Sentinel Set | Crypt-Warden Plate (better curse resist) |
| Accessory | Taunt Amulet | Aggro Shard |
| Consumable | Health Salve x3 | Iron Bark Tonic |

**Key Skills:**
- **Shield Wall** — reduces incoming damage by 60% for 8 seconds
- **Taunt** — forces up to 3 enemies to target you for 10 seconds
- **Rally** — restores 10 sanity to all nearby allies

**Tips:**
- Position yourself between the team and chokepoints
- Save Taunt for when a Harrier pulls too many enemies
- Your Rally skill is the team's emergency sanity button — use it when the Warden is out of charges

---

## Warden

The sanity and health anchor. Without a Warden, deep-floor expeditions become suicide.

**Core Stats:** Spirit > Intellect > Vitality

**Recommended Loadout:**

| Slot | Item | Alternative |
|------|------|-------------|
| Weapon | Ceremonial Staff | Sanity Censer |
| Armor | Pilgrim's Robes | Lightweave Set |
| Accessory | Amulet of Calm | Pendant of Dawn |
| Consumable | Calming Herbs x5 | Sanctuary Candles x2 |

**Key Skills:**
- **Calm Aura** — AoE sanity regen (5/sec) for 12 seconds
- **Cleanse** — removes one negative status effect from a target
- **Sanctuary** — places a zone that restores 3 HP/sec and 2 sanity/sec for 20 seconds

**Tips:**
- Keep Calm Aura active during combat; pre-cast before entering known fear-check rooms
- Save Sanctuary for boss arenas — the 20-second duration covers most of a damage phase
- Communicate when your Spirit potions are on cooldown so the team pulls back

---

## Striker

Highest single-target DPS. The Striker melts bosses and elites while the Vanguard holds aggro.

**Core Stats:** Agility > Critical > Strength

**Recommended Loadout:**

| Slot | Item | Alternative |
|------|------|-------------|
| Weapon | Twin Blades of Echoing | Shadow Bow (ranged) |
| Armor | Assassin's Leathers | Flayer Hide Set |
| Accessory | Ring of Sharpness | Brooch of Precision |
| Consumable | Sharpening Stone x2 | Phantom Oil |

**Key Skills:**
- **Backstab** — +300% damage from behind; guaranteed critical
- **Adrenaline Rush** — +50% attack speed for 10 seconds, then fatigue
- **Finishing Blow** — deals bonus damage based on target's missing HP

**Tips:**
- Wait for the Vanguard's Taunt before unleashing Adrenaline Rush
- Backstab is your highest damage-per-second — learn boss attack patterns to position behind them
- Manage your stamina; Overextending with Adrenaline Rush leaves you vulnerable

---

## Harrier

The mobile disruptor. Harriers excel at controlling crowds, pulling specific enemies, and creating space.

**Core Stats:** Speed > Agility > Perception

**Recommended Loadout:**

| Slot | Item | Alternative |
|------|------|-------------|
| Weapon | Barbed Whip + Throwing Knives | Ensnaring Bow |
| Armor | Windrunner Set | Light Chainmail |
| Accessory | Boots of Swiftness | Grappling Hook |
| Consumable | Caltrops x3 | Flash Powder x2 |

**Key Skills:**
- **Whip Pull** — drags an enemy toward you (or you toward it)
- **Smoke Screen** — blinds enemies in an area for 6 seconds
- **Disengage** — instantly dash backward 8 meters

**Tips:**
- Use Whip Pull to separate dangerous enemies from packs
- Smoke Screen can cancel boss abilities — time it during wind-up animations
- Harriers are the primary scout — you should be 1 room ahead of the team, marking threats

---

## Specialist

The wildcard. Specialists bring unique tools that no other role provides, from trap-setting to forbidden lore translation.

**Core Stats:** Intellect > Spirit > Luck

**Recommended Loadout:**

| Slot | Item | Alternative |
|------|------|-------------|
| Weapon | Tome of Binding | Trap Launcher |
| Armor | Scholar's Garb | Infiltrator Coat |
| Accessory | Relic Pouch | Glyph Kit |
| Consumable | Binding Scroll x3 | Nullification Charm |

**Key Skills:**
- **Bind** — roots an enemy in place for 15 seconds (bosses: 5 seconds)
- **Trap Array** — places 3 proximity mines (high damage, medium radius)
- **Lore Interpretation** — reads cursed texts without triggering sanity damage (team-wide)

**Tips:**
- Pre-place Trap Array at chokepoints before boss fights
- Use Lore Interpretation to safely read lore tablets for permanent team buffs
- Bind is invaluable for delaying enemy waves during puzzle sequences

## Team Composition Matrix

| Comp Type | Roles | Best For |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Balanced | Vanguard + Warden + Striker + Harrier | General progression |
| Safe | Vanguard + Warden + Striker + Specialist | Hardcore mode, first clears |
| Speed | Striker + Harrier + Harrier + Vanguard | Speedruns, low-floor farming |
| Full Support | Warden + Specialist + Harrier + Vanguard | Achievement hunting, exploration |
