# The Mound Extraction Guide — How to Get Out Alive

> Launch Version / Updated: August 10, 2026

Extraction is the most critical moment of any expedition in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu**. You can nail the entire run — perfect stealth, full loot, zero damage — and lose everything in the last five minutes if you mishandle the extraction phase. Everything you gather during an expedition — loot, artifacts, resources — is lost if you fail to extract.

This guide covers the hidden timer, extraction points and how to find them, the signals that it's time to leave, route planning, and the most common ways players fail to extract.

---

## The Extraction Process

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Complete or abandon your Contract objective |
| 2 | Navigate to the marked extraction point on your map |
| 3 | Activate the extraction flare / signal |
| 4 | Survive the waiting period (typically 60-90 seconds) |
| 5 | Board the rowboat or climb the rope to the Galleon |
| 6 | Confirm extraction on the results screen |

**Group extraction timing:** when extracting as a group, the timer counts down for the first player to enter the zone. All other players must reach the zone before the timer expires. If one player fails to make it, they are left behind — the extraction does not wait.

## Extraction Zones

Each island has multiple extraction points scattered across the map. Not all points are active at any given time — zones activate and deactivate based on expedition progress, weather, and how long you've been deployed.

| Zone Type | Icon Color | Activation Condition | Extraction Time | Risk Level |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|-----------------|------------|
| Primary Beacon | Green | Always active at expedition start | ~15 seconds | Low |
| Secondary Flare | Blue | Activates after the first major objective | ~20 seconds | Medium |
| Emergency Raft | Yellow | Permanent but one-use per expedition | ~25 seconds | Medium |
| Hidden Cache Exit | Purple | Requires finding a map fragment | ~30 seconds | Very High |

### How to Determine Active Zones

Your Compass Tool is the primary method for locating extraction zones. It has three modes:

- **Standard Mode** — Shows the nearest active extraction point. The compass needle glows green when pointed toward a safe extraction zone.
- **Survey Mode** — Ping the compass to reveal all active extraction points on your map for a few seconds, then it goes on a long cooldown.
- **Crisis Mode** — When the Forest Awakens phase is approaching, the compass switches to crisis mode, showing the fastest path to the nearest extraction point with a pulsing red indicator.

Additionally, you can determine active zones by:

1. **Observing flare colors** — Active extraction points send up colored flares visible from across the map. Green = Primary Beacon, Blue = Secondary Flare.
2. **Listening for audio cues** — Active zones emit a low hum that grows louder as you approach. The hum changes pitch based on remaining extraction stability.
3. **Checking your map** — Open the full map. Active zones are marked with pulsing icons; inactive zones appear grayed out.

### Extraction Zone Priority

When multiple extraction zones are active, pick based on where you are in the expedition:

- **Early expedition (0-15 minutes):** Primary Beacons are your best option. They are safest and fastest.
- **Mid expedition (15-25 minutes):** Secondary Flares become more attractive as Primary Beacons may be far from your current position.
- **Late expedition (25+ minutes):** Head to the nearest active zone immediately. Do not chase a specific zone type.

## The Hidden Timer: "The Forest Awakens"

The Mound features a hidden timer mechanic that dramatically changes the difficulty of extraction. The game does not show you a countdown, but the world gives you clear warnings.

### Timer Trigger Points

| Event | Typical Time Elapsed | Warning Signal |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| Expedition start | 0:00 | Drop-in sequence |
| First warning | ~15-20 minutes | Wind picks up, ambient audio shifts |
| Second warning | ~25-30 minutes | Distant howls, screen vignette darkens |
| "Forest Awakens" | ~30-35 minutes | Enemies spawn at 3x rate, extraction point may shift |
| Critical phase | ~40+ minutes | Almost guaranteed enemy swarm at extraction |

Completing major objectives (boss kills, artifact retrievals) early can extend the safe window — smart groups chain objectives to delay the danger. But the clock always wins: there is no way to reset it.

### How the Forest Awakening Changes the Game

| Before Awakening | After Awakening |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Standard enemy patrol density | 3x enemy spawns |
| Enemies have predictable patrol routes | Enemies actively hunt player positions |
| Extraction point is static | Extraction point may relocate |
| Y'm-bhi behave normally | Berserkers become more common |
| Ghost Bats in fixed locations | Bats swarm more aggressively |
| Ox Cart path is safe | Ox Cart may be overrun |

## Signals That It Is Time to Extract

Knowing when to leave is a skill that separates experienced players from dead ones.

### Green Light (Keep Exploring)
- Plenty of time since deployment (<15 min)
- Squad Sanity is above 50% for all members
- No unusual environmental audio
- Contract objective not yet complete
- You have open inventory slots

### Yellow Light (Start Heading Toward Extraction)
- 20+ minutes elapsed
- Wind has picked up noticeably
- Any squad member has Sanity below 30%
- Contract objective complete
- Inventory is full or near full
- You heard a distant howl

### Red Light (Extract Immediately)
- 30+ minutes elapsed
- "Forest Awakens" audio cue has played
- Squad member is down and cannot be revived quickly
- Multiple enemies aggroed and pursuing
- Any squad member has critical Sanity (<15%)
- Extraction point marker appears unstable or you see a fake extraction point

**Sanity matters at extraction:** low Sanity slows the extraction interaction and risks a hallucinated abort (you "extract" somewhere that isn't there). Restore Sanity before the final stand — and have a high-Sanity player confirm the extraction point with the map before anyone commits to it.

## Strategies for Reaching Extraction Safely

### Pre-Extraction Preparation

Before you even think about extracting, set yourself up for success:

- **Mark your extraction point early.** As soon as you know which zones are active, place a custom waypoint on your map.
- **Clear a retreat path.** As you push toward objectives, clear enemies along a return route rather than carving a new path back.
- **Save a Medallion ping for the final approach.** The Medallion marks your route — don't spend every charge early.

### During the Extraction Run

1. **Communicate with your team.** Call out enemy positions, obstacles, and your intended route. The spatial voice chat carries distance, so stay close enough to hear each other.
2. **Use terrain to your advantage.** High ground gives you sightlines to approaching threats. Cliff edges funnel enemies into kill zones.
3. **Save your sprint.** Running triggers the Sanity drain mechanic. Walk when possible and sprint only when enemies are in pursuit.
4. **Drop non-essential loot.** If you are carrying too much weight, your movement speed decreases. Dump low-value items to maintain mobility.

### Dealing with Threats During Extraction

| Threat | How to Handle |
|--------|---------------|
| Standard enemies | Kite them away from the extraction point, then loop back |
| Elite enemies | Use crowd control tools (Oil Lamp fire, traps) to buy time |
| Boss aggro | One player kites the boss while others extract, then breaks line of sight and loops back |
| Forest Awakens | Head to the nearest active zone immediately — every safe zone may be compromised the longer you wait |

## Common Extraction Failures

### 1. Waiting Too Long
**The problem:** Greed. One more chest. One more room. One more fight.
**The result:** The forest awakens, enemies swarm, extraction becomes impossible.
**The fix:** Set a mental timer. When you hear the first wind shift, start moving toward extraction. Do not deviate.

### 2. Splitting the Squad on the Way Out
**The problem:** Two players take a "shortcut" while the others take the main path.
**The result:** One group gets pinned. The other cannot reach them in time to help. Someone dies.
**The fix:** Stick together. A five-minute detour is better than a permanent death.

### 3. Not Accounting for the Ox Cart
**The problem:** The Ox Cart is still deep in the map with valuable loot when the team decides to extract.
**The result:** Either someone dies retrieving the cart, or the loot is abandoned.
**The fix:** Plan your route so the cart is always between you and extraction during the second half of the expedition.

### 4. False Extraction Points
**The problem:** A player with low Sanity sees a fake extraction marker and leads the team there.
**The result:** Wasted time, potential ambush, actual extraction point becomes unreachable.
**The fix:** Have a high-Sanity player confirm the extraction point. Cross-reference with the map. If no one else sees the marker, it is a hallucination.

### 5. Panic During the Waiting Period
**The problem:** The extraction flare takes 60-90 seconds. Players panic, break formation, and get picked off.
**The result:** Downed players cannot be revived during the final countdown.
**The fix:** Form a defensive circle. Assign watch directions. Stay calm. You have survived the whole expedition — you can survive 90 more seconds.

### 6. Ignoring Sanity on the Way Out
**The problem:** Low Sanity slows extraction and invites a hallucinated abort.
**The fix:** Use Sanity-restoring items before the final stand — Prayer Beads or Holy Water — and confirm the zone with the team.
