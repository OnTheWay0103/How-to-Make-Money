## Weapon Tier List Overview

The Mound features six weapons, and each contract offers a fixed selection from the Galleon's weapons rack. Here is how they rank for most expeditions, based on damage, reliability, and weather resilience:

| Tier | Weapon | Why |
|:--:|--------|-----|
| **S** | Crossbow | Highest sustained damage — outperforms firearms in most fights. Silent, rain-proof, and bolts can be reused. The universal best pick. |
| **S** | Machete | Silent backstabs one-shot most enemies. No ammo, works in every weather. The only weapon you should never leave behind. |
| **A** | Matchlock Arquebus | Devastating single-shot burst and armor-piercing at close range. Completely useless in rain, so its value depends on the forecast. |
| **B** | Flintlock Pistol | Fast-draw sidearm for emergencies. Weak at range, rain-vulnerable, low ammo — a backup, not a primary. |
| **B** | Oil Lamp | Not a damage weapon: light, fire crowd-control, and the only tool that keeps night expeditions safe. Take it whenever darkness is a factor. |
| **C** | Francesca Axe | High skill ceiling and retrievable, but a miss is lost forever and it cannot save you in a panic. For experienced throwers only. |

## Best Weapon for Every Situation

| Situation | Best Pick | Why |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Stealth approach | Crossbow + Machete | Silent, silent, silent |
| Rain / storm contract | Crossbow | The only strong ranged option when firearms fail |
| Night expedition | Crossbow + Oil Lamp | Lamp is mandatory; Crossbow keeps you quiet |
| Boss fight (clear weather) | Matchlock Arquebus + Oil Lamp | Highest burst + fire damage for the High Priest |
| Leech Swarm / group | Oil Lamp throw | One fire burst clears a whole swarm |
| Solo play | Crossbow + Machete | The loadout that works in every condition |

## How to Get Weapons

- **Contract gear:** each contract on the Galleon has a fixed weapon selection — some offer choices, others do not. Weapons are drawn from a **shared party pool**: if there is one Flintlock Pistol, only one player can carry it. Plan who takes what before departure.
- **Vendor purchases:** spend Upgrade Tokens at the Galleon vendor to buy new gear — torches, the Medallion, maps — and to **upgrade or trade in** weapons between expeditions.
- **Blueprints and crafting:** story and challenge rewards unlock craftable gear — the Crossbow (tutorial reward), The Purifier fire-crossbow (defeat the High Priest), and the Shadow Armor set (five Nocturnal Hunt expeditions).

## Are Weapon Upgrades Permanent?

**Yes for your collection, no for the mission.**

- Upgrades bought with Tokens and weapons crafted from blueprints **persist permanently** and stay in your pool across all future expeditions.
- The **mission loadout itself resets every contract** — you pick gear fresh from the rack each time.
- Found loot, by contrast, is **mission-only**: it is worth tokens only if you extract with it, and it does not carry between expeditions.

The practical rule: spend Tokens on upgrades and blueprints early (they are permanent investments), and treat the per-contract rack as your flexible loadout.

## Gear Optimization

- **Inventory slots expand as you Rank up** — early on, every slot counts, so keep your personal carry light.
- **Idols and heavy treasure go in the Ox Wagon**, not your pack — the wagon frees your limited slots for tools and healing.
- **The Medallion is the best utility item in the game** — it detects chests and danger and marks targets, but ringing it attracts monsters, so use it deliberately.
- **Check the weather before every loadout.** A Matchlock is S-tier in clear weather and dead weight in rain.

## Recommended Loadouts

| Setup | Loadout | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Solo (any weather) | Crossbow + Machete | Quiet, self-sufficient, extracts safely |
| Duo (sweet spot) | Crossbow + Machete / Matchlock + Machete | One silent, one loud — the loud one handles armored foes |
| Full squad (clear) | Matchlock ×2, Crossbow, Oil Lamp | Max DPS with a dedicated light-bearer |
| Full squad (rain) | Crossbow ×2, Machete ×2, Oil Lamp | Everyone stays effective when firearms fail |
