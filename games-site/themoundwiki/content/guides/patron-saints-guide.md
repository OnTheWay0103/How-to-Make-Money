# The Mound Patron Saints — How to Unlock Every Blessing and Which One to Equip

Most *The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu* crews never use the Patron Saints system. Not because it is hidden, but because unlocking it takes two separate discoveries and the game explains neither. The payoff is real: a Patron Saint is the only way to leave the Galleon with a passive buff already running, and for some builds it changes how an entire expedition plays out.

The developers' patch notes call these **Saints**. Community guides call them **Patron Saints**. They are the same system.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is a Patron Saint? | A blessing you equip at the ox-wagon before deploying |
| How do you unlock the system? | Rescue the Woodworker, then recover Sacred Figures from the jungle |
| How many can you carry at once? | One blessing at a time (community-reported) |
| What does it cost? | Currency at the ox-wagon. The patch notes say only "a cost" — community write-ups say **Tokens** [Unconfirmed] |
| Does it carry over between runs? | No. A blessing applies to that expedition |

## What the Developers Have Actually Confirmed

The Saints system is not a community rumour. ACE Team has patched it three times, and the patch notes are specific:

- **Patch 1.01 (July 21, 2026)** moved the system: "Modified the Saints shop so these are no longer bought at the woodshop. **Saints are now equipped for a smaller cost at the ox wagon before an expedition**."
- The same 1.01 notes listed a known issue: "**Saints are disappearing after one expedition** unless you restart a lobby."
- **Patch 1.02 (July 29, 2026)** cut the price — "**Reduced Saints blessing costs**" — and fixed the regression: "Fixed Saints not loading after missions (which was introduced in patch 1)."

Two things follow from that wording. Saints are **bought with currency at the ox-wagon** before an expedition, not crafted and not looted mid-run. And the system's early reputation for being buggy comes from a real 1.01 defect that was fixed in 1.02, so if Saints appear to do nothing, confirm you are not on a launch-window build.

What the patch notes do **not** say is how many Saints exist, what they do, or how the system is unlocked. That part comes from community documentation, and is labelled as such below.

## Step 1 — Unlock the System

Unlocking takes two separate actions, and both are easy to miss because neither appears on the contract board:

1. **Rescue the Woodworker.** He is a survivor found in the jungle. Recover him and extract him to the Galleon (community guides describe knocking him out and loading him onto the wagon to carry him home).
2. **Recover Sacred Figures.** Small devotional figures are hidden at shrines and altars across the expedition regions. Carrying one back to the ship is what converts it into something usable.

The Woodworker is the conversion step: once he is aboard, he **carves the recovered Sacred Figures into the larger Patron Saint statues** that mount on your wagon. This is why the slot stays empty even after you find a figure — a figure in your inventory is not yet a usable blessing.

Community sources agree there are **nine** Sacred Figures in total. Because the Woodworker is a rescued survivor and not a shop, crews that never extract him can progress a long way without ever enabling the system.

## Step 2 — Equip a Blessing at the Ox-Wagon

Before you deploy, equip your chosen Saint **at the ox-wagon**, not at a Galleon vendor — this is exactly what Patch 1.01 changed, and it is why older write-ups still send players to the woodshop.

- **One at a time.** Community documentation is consistent that a single blessing can be carried per expedition, which makes the choice a build decision rather than a shopping list.
- **It costs currency.** Patch 1.01 made it "a smaller cost"; Patch 1.02 reduced it again. Community write-ups name Tokens as the currency, but the patch notes never name one — treat the currency as unconfirmed and check the wagon prompt in your build. If it is Tokens, note that this is one of the few token spends that buys a single-run effect rather than a permanent unlock (see the [Progression Guide](/guides/progression-system-guide)).
- **It lasts one run.** This is the behaviour the 1.01 "disappearing after one expedition" bug was about.

## Step 3 — Which Saint Should You Pick?

The blessing list below is **community-documented, not officially published**. The saint names line up across independent community guides, and each corresponds to a real devotional figure whose traditional patronage matches the in-game effect — but ACE Team has not printed an official table, so treat the exact numbers as unconfirmed.

| Patron Saint | Blessing (community-reported) | Pick it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Archangel Saint Michael** | Improves swords and bladed weapons | You are running a melee build on the Machete or a blade |
| **Archangel Saint Raphael** | Steady health regeneration | Long solo runs where every Salve counts |
| **Saint Barbara** | Increases firearm effectiveness | You are building around the Matchlock Arquebus or Flintlock Pistol |
| **Saint George** | Improves spears and polearms | You are on a halberd / polearm build |
| **Saint Sebastian** | Improves bows | You want quiet ranged damage instead of loud firearms |
| **Saint Benedict** | Survive one fatal hit each day | Learning a hard contract, or the squad's designated carrier |
| **Saint Dymphna** | Dispels altered mental states | Your crew keeps losing runs to hallucinations |
| **Saint Eulalia** | Prevents rain | Your plan depends on firearms, which rain disables |
| **Saint Paschal Baylón** | Food restores more health | Long expeditions on a thin consumable budget |

Two of these are worth calling out.

**Saint Barbara and Saint Eulalia are weather insurance.** Rain shuts down firearms in *The Mound*, which is why a firearm build that draws a storm contract is a build with no weapon. Barbara raises the ceiling on guns; Eulalia removes the condition that counters them outright. If your squad's plan is built on the Matchlock ([Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide)), one of these two is the difference between a plan and a gamble.

**Saint Dymphna is the only hard counter to the Sanity system.** Community documentation describes using a **prayer prompt at the ox-wagon statue** to dispel active altered states — clearing hallucinations you are currently experiencing. Read the limit carefully: it clears the current effect, it does **not** stop Sanity from continuing to fall, and it will not prevent the next hallucination. Treat it as a reset button, not a cure. The full madness rules live in the [Sanity Guide](/guides/sanity-guide).

Note that blessing effects target weapon *categories*. If you are unsure which category your loadout falls into, check the [Loadout Guide](/guides/loadout-guide) before spending.

## Step 4 — The Nine Guardians Achievement

If you are chasing 100%, the Sacred Figures are also a collectible set. The **Nine Guardians** trophy/achievement (Gold on PlayStation) requires the **Woodworker to craft all nine Sacred Figures** — not simply to find them.

Two consequences that catch people out:

- **Finding all nine is not enough.** You must return to the ship and complete the Woodworker's crafting step.
- **It may not pop on pickup.** Community reports describe the unlock resolving through the ship-side flow, so confirm with the captain and the Woodworker before assuming the trophy is bugged.

Because the figures are spread across different expedition regions, community location guides suggest collecting one per destination rather than sweeping the jungle in a single run. We are not reproducing exact coordinates here: regional naming differs between community sources and the map regions described on this wiki, so the safest route is to check the [Maps & Locations Guide](/guides/maps-locations-guide) for the region you are already running and search its shrines and altars while you are there.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it goes wrong |
|---|---|
| Looking for Saints at the woodshop | Patch 1.01 moved equipping to the ox-wagon |
| Expecting a figure to work the moment you find it | The Woodworker must carve it first |
| Assuming Saints are a permanent unlock | A blessing is spent per expedition |
| Buying a blessing for a weapon you are not carrying | Blessings target weapon categories — match them to your loadout |
| Treating Saint Dymphna as a Sanity fix | It clears the current hallucination, not the underlying Sanity drain |
| Reporting "Saints are broken" on a launch build | The 1.01 disappearing bug was fixed in 1.02 — update first |
