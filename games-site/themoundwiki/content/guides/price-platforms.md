## Price & Editions

The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu launched on **July 15, 2026** with two editions:

| Edition | Price | Includes |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Standard Edition** | $29.99 | Base game, all 4 characters |
| **Deluxe Edition** | $39.99 | Base game + Fortune Hunters Character Pack (2 bonus characters) + Digital Artbook + Soundtrack |

### Is There a Demo?
Yes! A **free demo** is available on Steam. The demo includes a limited number of expeditions and lets you try all 4 base characters. A typical demo session lasts 20-30 minutes. Progress does NOT carry over to the full game.

### Xbox Game Pass
As of July 2026, The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu is **not on Game Pass**. The developers have not announced any Game Pass plans.

## System Requirements (PC)

**Minimum:**
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- CPU: Intel i7-8700K / AMD Ryzen 5 1600X
- RAM: 16 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA GTX 1660 SUPER 6GB / AMD RX 5600 XT 6GB
- Storage: 25 GB

**Recommended:**
- OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit
- CPU: Intel i7-11700K / AMD Ryzen 5 5500
- RAM: 16 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX 3070 8GB / AMD RX 6750 XT 12GB
- Storage: 25 GB SSD

## Is It Worth Buying?

**Yes, if you like:**
- Co-op horror games (Left 4 Dead, Phasmophobia)
- Extraction shooters with PvE focus
- Lovecraftian atmosphere and lore
- Challenging team-based gameplay

**Maybe wait, if you:**
- Only play solo (solo is much harder)
- Don't like horror or jump scares
- Want a casual experience
