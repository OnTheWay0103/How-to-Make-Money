## Overview

Speedrunning **The Mound** requires a fundamentally different approach from casual play. Where a normal run values safety and exploration, a speedrun trades both for blistering pace and precise execution. This guide covers the current meta for Any% and 100% categories.

## Pre-Run Preparation

Speedruns are won or lost before you enter the Mound.

### Character Selection

| Character | Tier (Any%) | Reason |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| The Vagrant | S | +15% movement speed passive, no armor penalty |
| The Seeker | A | Built-in compass reveals optimal path on Floors 1–5 |
| The Brute | B | High starting damage but slow; only useful for 100% category |
| The Scholar | C | Int-focused; slow clear speed offsets utility gains |

### Starting Loadout

- **Weapon:** Dagger (fastest attack speed, enables wall-jump skips)
- **Accessory:** Compass Shard (prevents getting lost in dark corridors)
- **No armor —** movement speed penalty is not worth the protection in a speedrun

## Route Strategy by Floor

### Floors 1–3 (The Shallows)

| Floor | Time Target | Key Action |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| 1 | 1:30 | Rush straight to exit — no loot |
| 2 | 2:00 | Grab Calming Herbs if directly on path |
| 3 | 3:30 | Kill the Grove Warden (skipable in Any%) |

**Skip:** On Floor 2, a wall-jump at the collapsed staircase (coordinates X:14, Y:8) lets you skip the entire middle section, saving ~1 minute.

### Floors 4–6 (The Descent)

These floors introduce fear checks and Null Shards. The priority shifts to maintaining sanity above 50.

- Floor 4 — bring a light source; the darkness drain is doubled
- Floor 5 — use the **Elevator Skip**: drop a lit candle at the elevator shaft entrance, then roll into the gap. Saves 45 seconds
- Floor 6 — break the vase behind the merchant to find an Elixir of Speed (+30% move speed for 60 seconds)

### Floors 7–9 (The Core)

| Floor | Time Target | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| 7 | 4:00 | Boss: The Warden. Use Dagger's fast attack to stagger-lock |
| 8 | 2:30 | Puzzle floor — memorize the sequence: Circle, Triangle, Square, Diamond |
| 9 | 3:00 | Gauntlet. Use Smoke Screen to skip two of the three combat arenas |

### Floor 10 (The Heart)

Final boss. Time target: **5:00**.

**Strategy:**
1. Ignore adds — focus the Heart's core
2. When the Heart pulses red, hide behind a pillar to avoid the AoE sanity wipe
3. Use Memory Lotus if sanity drops below 30
4. Final phase: the Heart exposes its core for 10 seconds — burn all cooldowns

## Advanced Techniques

| Technique | Input | Time Saved | Difficulty |
|-----------|-------|------------|-----------|
| Wall Jump Skip | Jump → hit wall → jump again within 4 frames | 10–60 sec per use | Hard |
| Roll Cancel | Roll → immediately attack → roll again | 1–2 sec per roll | Medium |
| Zip Glitch | Unequip all items → open map → close map while moving | Varies | Hard (bannable in NMG) |
| Loot Cancel | Pick up item → roll during pickup animation | 0.5 sec per pickup | Easy |
| Fear Check Bypass | Use Sanctuary Candle during check animation to auto-pass | — | Medium |

## Gear Priority in a Speedrun

1. **Boots of Swiftness** — +25% move speed. Game-changing for Any%.
2. **Elixir of Speed** — consumable burst speed. Save for Floors 7+.
3. **Memory Lotus** — sanity insurance for the final boss.
4. **Compass Shard** — prevents wrong turns. Essential for routing.
5. **Sanctuary Candle** — emergency fear-check bypass.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Looting unnecessary chests | +10–30 seconds per chest | Only loot if the item is on the priority list |
| Killing all enemies | +2–5 minutes per floor | Skip every fight that isn't mandatory |
| Forgetting light source on Floor 4 | Massive sanity drain, potential death | Always carry at least one candle |
| Overusing Roll Cancel | Stamina depletion, slower movement | Use in bursts of 3–5 rolls maximum |
| Missing the Elevator Skip on Floor 5 | +45 seconds | Practice the candle-drop timing |
