## Characters Overview

The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu features four playable conquistador characters, each with unique stats, starting equipment, and special abilities. While no character is locked to a specific playstyle, their innate traits make them naturally suited to different roles.

## All Four Characters

### Alonso de Medina — The Veteran Soldier
- **Role**: Tank / Frontline
- **HP**: Highest
- **Stamina**: High
- **Special Trait**: *Iron Will* — Takes 15% less sanity damage from eldritch horrors
- **Starting Weapon**: Sword & Shield
- **Best For**: Beginners, tanking in co-op, solo survival

### Father Miguel — The Jesuit Priest
- **Role**: Support / Healer
- **HP**: Medium
- **Stamina**: Medium
- **Special Trait**: *Divine Protection* — Healing items are 25% more effective
- **Starting Weapon**: Cross & Holy Water
- **Best For**: Co-op support, keeping teammates alive, exorcising spirits

### Catalina de Vega — The Scout
- **Role**: DPS / Scout
- **HP**: Low
- **Stamina**: Highest
- **Special Trait**: *Keen Eyes* — Can spot traps, treasures, and enemies from further away
- **Starting Weapon**: Dual Daggers
- **Best For**: Exploration, finding hidden loot, hit-and-run combat

### Rodrigo de Silva — The Heavy Hitter
- **Role**: Burst DPS
- **HP**: High
- **Stamina**: Low
- **Special Trait**: *Berserker Rage* — Deal 30% more damage when below 50% HP
- **Starting Weapon**: Two-Handed Axe
- **Best For**: Boss killing, high-risk/high-reward play, experienced players

## Best Character for Solo Play

**Alonso de Medina** is the best solo character. His high HP and sanity resistance let you survive mistakes that would kill other characters. The sword & shield combo provides both offense and defense.

**Catalina** is the best for experienced solo players who want to find every secret and maximize loot per expedition.

## Best Character for Co-op

A balanced team of four covers all roles:
- **Alonso** (Tank) — Frontline, draws enemy attention
- **Catalina** (Scout) — Finds threats and treasures early
- **Rodrigo** (DPS) — Melts bosses and elites
- **Father Miguel** (Support) — Keeps everyone alive and sane

## Character Progression

Characters don't have traditional level-ups. Instead, progression comes from:
- **Equipment** — Better weapons and armor found or crafted
- **Perks** — Unlocked through achievements and milestones
- **Expedition Rank** — Increases with successful extractions, unlocking new loadout slots
- **Logbook** — Records discoveries; completing entries grants permanent bonuses
