## Combat System Overview

Combat in The Mound is deliberate and punishing. Unlike hack-and-slash games, every swing, block, and dodge must be intentional. Stamina management is the key to survival.

## How to Block

Blocking reduces incoming damage and prevents stagger from most attacks.

### Blocking Basics
- **Hold the block button** (default: Right Mouse Button / LT on controller)
- Your character raises their weapon or shield
- Incoming melee attacks deal **reduced damage** while blocking
- Blocking consumes **stamina** — when stamina runs out, your guard breaks

### What Can Be Blocked
| Attack Type | Blockable? | Notes |
|-------------|:--:|------|
| Melee slashes | ✅ | Full damage reduction |
| Melee heavy attacks | ⚠️ | Partial reduction, heavy stamina cost |
| Projectiles | ❌ | Must dodge |
| Eldritch/magic attacks | ❌ | Unblockable |
| Boss grab attacks | ❌ | Must dodge |

### Perfect Block / Parry

Time your block **just before an attack lands** to execute a perfect parry:
- Enemy is staggered for 2-3 seconds
- Next attack deals double damage
- No stamina cost for a perfect parry
- Works on most melee attacks, including some boss attacks

**Parry timing**: Press block **0.3 seconds** before the attack connects. The window is tight — practice on basic enemies first.

## Dodging

Dodging provides invincibility frames (i-frames) and is the only way to avoid unblockable attacks.

- **Dodge roll** (default: Space / B button) — Fast, covers distance, has i-frames
- **Side step** (dodge + direction) — Faster recovery, shorter i-frames
- Dodging costs stamina — don't spam it

### When to Dodge vs Block
- **Block**: Predictable melee attacks, conserving stamina, setting up parries
- **Dodge**: Unblockable attacks, AoE attacks, repositioning, escaping grabs

## Weapon Combos

Each weapon type has unique combos:

| Weapon | Light Combo | Heavy Attack | Special |
|--------|------------|-------------|---------|
| Sword & Shield | 3-hit slash | Shield bash (stuns) | Block counter |
| Dual Daggers | 5-hit flurry | Backstab (massive crit) | Bleed DoT |
| Two-Handed Axe | 2-hit cleave | Overhead slam (AoE) | Armor break |
| Crossbow | Single shot | Charged bolt (pierces) | Headshot bonus |
| Musket | Single shot | Bayonet charge | Knockback |

## Stamina Management

Stamina is your most important resource:
- **Maximum stamina**: ~100 (increases with gear)
- **Regeneration**: Slower when moving, faster when standing still
- **Block cost**: ~15-25 per blocked attack
- **Dodge cost**: ~20 per dodge
- **Attack cost**: ~10-30 depending on weapon

### Stamina Tips
1. Never let stamina drop below 20% — you need reserves to dodge
2. Stand still briefly between exchanges to regen faster
3. Green herbs temporarily boost stamina regen
4. The *Iron Will* perk (Alonso) reduces stamina costs by 15%

## Enemy-Specific Combat Tips

| Enemy | Strategy |
|-------|----------|
| Cultists | Parry their slow telegraphed attacks |
| Jungle Beasts | Dodge the pounce, punish the recovery |
| Eldritch Horrors | Unblockable attacks — dodge and use ranged |
| Bosses | Learn patterns, parry when possible, conserve stamina |
