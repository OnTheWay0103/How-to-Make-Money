# Perks & Progression Guide — What Actually Exists

> **Rewritten September 17, 2026.** This page used to be a 25-perk tree with named perks, tier
> numbers, unlock conditions and build templates. None of it was real. See
> [Why this page was rewritten](#why-this-page-was-rewritten).

**The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu has no perk system.** There are no passive skills, no talent tree, no
skill points, and no equippable perk slots. If you came here looking for a list of perks, there is
nothing to list — and that is the accurate answer, not a placeholder.

> **One honest caveat.** Third-party guides describe an XP **Rank Rewards** ladder (extra knife,
> extra inventory slots, a weapon grip bonus, a Ring of Isolation Resistance), and the developer's own
> Steam discussion posts use the word "perks" loosely when describing what XP unlocks. Those reward
> names return **0 hits** in the official announcement corpus and **0** in the 576-review corpus, so
> this wiki does not assert them. What it also does not do is claim the game has *no* progression — it
> plainly does. The claim this page makes is narrower and fully sourced: **there is no perk tree, and
> none of the 25 named perks the old page listed exists anywhere.**

What the game *does* have is a progression loop built on **gear, tokens and map knowledge**, all of
it routed through the Galleon between expeditions. That loop is real, it is documented, and it is
what this page now covers.

## The actual progression systems

### 1. Contract gear — the shared arsenal

Each expedition starts with a contract. The developer's description of what a contract gives you is
the single best summary of the gear system:

> *"The Captain provides, but he is not generous. Each contract comes with a selection of equipment
> for your party — a handful of weapons, a few precious supplies, a single lamp against the
> oppressive dark. These are not individual loadouts; they are a shared arsenal."*
> — *The Captain's bargain: Choosing your contract*

Two things follow from that, and both change how you play:

- **You do not own a permanent arsenal.** Gear is issued per contract, which is why players
  complain that *"the flintlock that kept you alive on that same run just evaporates."*
- **Allocation is a team decision.** The developer's own example: *"Will you entrust the only
  arquebus to the marksman with the steadiest hand? Who carries the lantern when the shadows begin
  to crawl?"*

There is also weapon looting — added in the Steam Next Fest demo update and carried into launch:
*"you can now pick up weapons directly from enemy bodies."*

### 2. The Weapon Upgrade Anvil

The launch update added a station on the main ship:

> *"**Weapon Upgrade Anvil** — a new station on the main ship lets you spend tokens to improve or
> upgrade weapons given by contracts."*
> — *What's new since the demo?*

Note the limitation carefully: you upgrade **the weapons a contract gave you**. This is the closest
thing The Mound has to a build system, and it is a token-spend station — not a talent tree, and not
a crafting bench.

### 3. The Logbook — map unlocks, not stat bonuses

> *"Discover abandoned forts during your expedition and recover their logbooks to unlock them as new
> starting point for future expeditions."*
> — Official Steam store page

That is the documented reward: **new places to start from on the map.** A player with 425 upvotes
describes the same mechanic: *"Remember to collect Logbooks whenever you enter a new area — they
unlock additional locations and are essential for progressing further."*

A Logbook onboarding contract exists (it appears in the closed-beta known-issues list), and the
launch update added *"hand-drawn art pieces"* that *"now accompany logbook narrations on the
rowboat map."*

**What the Logbook does not do:** there is no evidence anywhere — official or community — that
completing logbook entries grants permanent stat bonuses. An earlier version of the Characters &
Classes page claimed exactly that; it has been corrected.

### 4. Tokens and the occult shop

Extraction pays out tokens. Where do they go?

| Sink | What it does | Source |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Weapon Upgrade Anvil | Upgrade the weapons a contract issued | Launch update |
| Occult shop | Buy support items and relics | *The Captain's bargain*; patches 1.04 |
| Contract blessings | Patch 1.02 *"Reduced Saints blessing costs"*; patch 1.02 *"Slight increase for tokens given for contract tiers"* | Patch notes |

Officially named occult-shop stock:

- **The Medallion** — *"powerful support items like The Medallion to uncover hidden treasures."*
  (A support item, **not** a weapon.)
- **The Mask of Nyarlathotep** — *"allows its wearer to move among the Y'm-bhi unnoticed —
  provided it's donned before... "* (the developer's sentence trails off there).
- **The Crucifix** — *"Using a crucifix reveals their stress level through the glow of their eyes."*
- **The Rosary** — new occult item in patch 1.04, unlocked by finding all the hidden jewel boxes.

## Why this page was rewritten

The previous version of this page described a perk system that does not exist. Every element of it
was invented, and the evidence against it is unusually clean:

| Claim in the old page | Evidence |
|-----------------------|----------|
| A perk system at all | `perk` = **0 hits** in all 39 official Steam announcements for app ID `2569760`, **0 hits** on the Steam store page |
| "Total perks available at launch: 25" | No official source states any number of perks |
| 25 named perks (Tough Hide, Iron Skin… Iron Stomach, Second Wind, Restful Sleep, Numbed Mind, Unyielding, Blade Master, Marksman, Critical Eye, Adrenaline Rush, Executioner, Bulwark, Cat's Paws, Shadow Dweller, Slippery, Ghost, Pack Mule, Scavenger, Field Medic, Artisan, Quick Exit, Strange Mind, Whisperer, Unsettling Aura, Eldritch Sight) | **Not one** of these names appears in any announcement, the store page, or the review corpus |
| Perk categories with counts (Survival 6 / Combat 6 / Stealth 4 / Utility 5 / Eldritch 4) | Invented |
| Unlock conditions ("Survive 10 expeditions", "Block or parry 50 attacks") | Invented — and note the second one required a **parry**, which the game does not have |
| "Max perks equippable at once: 6" | Invented |
| Perk build templates ("The Silent Collector", "The Frontline", "The Cursed", "The Speedrunner") | Invented |
| "Perk unlocks are permanent and account-wide" | Invented |

**The only uses of the word "perk" in the public corpus are players asking for the feature:**

- *"I believe small perks to the characters would be a good addition, like the lady who stabbed a man have benefits to the knife, priest more effective with the crucifix…"* (1,230 upvotes)
- *"A more meaningful progression system, such as permanent perks, weapon upgrades or different builds, could add a lot of replayability."*
- *"es wäre toll, wenn man unterschiedliche Skills freischalten kann, oder besondere Artefakte finden, die starke Perks geben"* — "it would be great if you could unlock different skills, or find special artefacts that give strong perks."

A player asking for a feature is **evidence the feature is missing**. Under this wiki's sourcing
rules that is a gap signal, not support — and treating it as support is exactly how the old page
came to exist.

### Method

- **Official corpus:** Steam `ISteamNews` for app ID `2569760`, all **39** items (36 developer
  announcements, 2 press feeds, 1 SteamDB feed), full text.
- **App-ID check:** The Mound's store presence is app `2569760` plus four separately-listed
  add-ons (`4630450`, `4630440`, `4630460`, `4925780`). Each add-on was queried for its own news
  feed and returned **0 items**, so there is no second announcement stream to miss. There is no
  separate demo or playtest app with its own feed — the demo-era posts ("Playable demo is LIVE for
  Steam Next Fest!", "Demo Update - Patch Notes", "What's new since the demo?") are all published
  under `2569760`.
- **Store page:** `appdetails` for `2569760`, English.
- **Community corpus:** Steam reviews API (`appreviews`, with `json=1`), independent-review IDs
  retained for every quote above.
