## Extraction Zones Guide — The Mound

Extraction is the single most important mechanic in The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu. Everything you gather during an expedition — loot, artifacts, resources, and medallion data — is lost if you fail to extract. This guide covers every extraction zone type, how to determine which zone is active, hidden timer mechanics, and proven strategies to reach extraction safely.

### Extraction Zones Overview

Each island in The Mound has multiple extraction points scattered across the map. However, not all points are active at any given time. The game uses a dynamic extraction system where zones activate and deactivate based on weather conditions, expedition duration, and player proximity to objectives.

| Zone Type | Icon Color | Activation Condition | Extraction Time | Risk Level |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|-----------------|------------|
| Primary Beacon | Green | Always active at expedition start | 15 seconds | Low |
| Secondary Flare | Blue | Activates after first major objective | 20 seconds | Medium |
| Storm Extraction | Red | Only during storm weather | 10 seconds | High |
| Emergency Raft | Yellow | Permanent but one-use per expedition | 25 seconds | Medium |
| Hidden Cache Exit | Purple | Requires finding map fragment | 30 seconds | Very High |

### How to Determine Active Zones

Your Compass Tool is the primary method for locating extraction zones. It has three modes:

- **Standard Mode** — Shows the nearest active extraction point. The compass needle glows green when pointed toward a safe extraction zone.
- **Survey Mode** — Ping the compass to reveal all active extraction points on your map for 10 seconds. This has a 90-second cooldown.
- **Crisis Mode** — When the storm is approaching, the compass enters crisis mode, showing the fastest path to the nearest extraction point with a pulsing red indicator.

Additionally, you can determine active zones by:

1. **Observing flare colors** — Active extraction points send up colored flares visible from across the map. Green = Primary Beacon, Blue = Secondary Flare, Red = Storm Extraction.
2. **Listening for audio cues** — Active zones emit a low hum that grows louder as you approach. The hum changes pitch based on remaining extraction stability.
3. **Checking your map** — Press M to open the full map. Active zones are marked with pulsing icons. Inactive zones appear grayed out.

### Hidden Timer Mechanics

The Mound hides several timer mechanics that most players miss:

**The 30-Minute Hard Cap.** Every expedition has a hard 30-minute time limit from the moment you deploy. After 28 minutes, the Storm Warning begins. At 30 minutes, the Storm arrives and all standard extraction zones deactivate. Only Storm Extraction points remain active, and they are far more dangerous to reach.

**Objective-Based Timer Extensions.** Completing major objectives (boss kills, artifact retrievals, ritual disruptions) adds 2-3 minutes to the expedition timer. Smart groups chain objectives to extend their safe window.

**The Sanity Timer.** Your Sanity level affects extraction speed. At full Sanity, extraction takes the standard time. At 50% Sanity, extraction takes 50% longer. At 25% Sanity or below, extraction takes 100% longer and has a 30% chance of failing entirely (your character hallucinates and aborts the extraction).

**Group Extraction Timing.** When extracting as a group, the timer counts down for the first player to enter the zone. All other players must reach the zone before the timer expires. If one player fails to make it, they are left behind. The extraction does not wait.

### Strategies for Reaching Extraction Safely

#### Pre-Extraction Preparation

Before you even think about extracting, set yourself up for success:

- **Mark your extraction point early.** As soon as you know which zones are active, place a custom waypoint on your map.
- **Clear a retreat path.** As you push toward objectives, clear enemies along a return route rather than carving a new path back.
- **Conserve medallion charges.** The Medallion tool creates portals that can shortcut you to extraction. Save at least one charge for emergencies.

#### During the Extraction Run

When it is time to leave, follow these principles:

1. **Communicate with your team.** Call out enemy positions, obstacles, and your intended route. The spatial voice chat carries distance, so stay close enough to hear each other.
2. **Use terrain to your advantage.** High ground gives you sightlines to approaching threats. Cliff edges funnel enemies into kill zones.
3. **Save your sprint.** Running triggers the Sanity drain mechanic. Walk when possible and sprint only when enemies are in pursuit.
4. **Drop non-essential loot.** If you are carrying too much weight, your movement speed decreases. Dump low-value items to maintain mobility.

#### Dealing with Threats During Extraction

| Threat | How to Handle |
|--------|---------------|
| Standard enemies | Kite them away from the extraction point, then loop back |
| Elite enemies | Use crowd control tools (tar bombs, stun grenades) to buy time |
| Boss aggro | One player kites the boss while others extract, then the kiter uses a Medallion portal |
| Other squads (PvP) | Flank wide and use the storm timer as pressure — they cannot stay forever |
| Storm arrival | Only use Storm Extraction zones; wear storm-resistant gear if available |

### Extraction Zone Priority System

When multiple extraction zones are active, the game prioritizes them based on your current situation:

- **Early expedition (0-15 minutes):** Primary Beacons are your best option. They are safest and fastest.
- **Mid expedition (15-25 minutes):** Secondary Flares become more attractive as Primary Beacons may be far from your current position.
- **Late expedition (25-30 minutes):** Head to the nearest active zone immediately. Do not chase a specific zone type.
- **Storm phase (30+ minutes):** Storm Extraction zones are your only option. They are marked by red flares and require special tactics to reach safely.

### Common Extraction Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Is Dangerous | The Fix |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| Waiting too long to extract | Storm arrival eliminates safe zones | Set a personal timer for 25 minutes |
| Splitting the group | Solo players are easy targets | Stay within voice chat range |
| Ignoring Sanity | Low Sanity slows extraction | Use Sanity-restoring items before extraction |
| Over-encumbrance | Reduced movement speed = death | Drop loot strategically |
| Wrong compass mode | Wastes precious time | Always use Survey Mode before moving |
