# The Mound Maps & Locations Guide — Every Island, POI, and Loot Route

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Every expedition in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu** begins the same way: you sail from the Galleon — your floating hub — toward an island on the horizon. Where you land decides everything: the enemies you face, the loot you can carry, and the bosses you might trigger. This guide maps every confirmed location in the New World, with points of interest, enemy profiles, and the fastest loot routes on each map.

---

## Map Unlock Order

The New World opens up as your Reputation grows. You are never locked out of early maps — contracts can revisit any cleared location — but the story introduces them in a fixed order.

| Map | Unlock Condition | Difficulty | Key Threat |
|-----|-----------------|------------|------------|
| Isle of Voices | Start (tutorial) | Starter | Y'm-bhi Sentries, Ghost Bats |
| Forest Regions | Start (contracts) | Starter | Vine Creatures, Berserkers (night) |
| Catacombs | Early contracts (Phase 1–2) | Medium | Giant Centipedes, Priests |
| Sunken Grotto | 2,000 Reputation | Medium | Wraiths, Leech Swarms, The Lurker Below |
| Shallow Graves | Legendary Hunt contract | Hard | The Drowned Baron |
| The Hollow Woods | Legendary Hunt contract | Hard | The Pale King |
| The Deep Warrens | Legendary Hunt contract | Hard | The Crawling King |
| Mansion of Fools | Legendary Hunt contract | Hard | The Gilded Widow |
| The Mound Interior | 6,000 Reputation | Very Hard | Elite Y'm-bhi, High Priest of Cthulhu |

> **Rule of thumb:** a location you have not visited comes with enemies a step above the last map you cleared — budget your loadout accordingly.

## Isle of Voices

The starting island where every expedition begins: beaches, dense forest, and one central cave system. Perfect for learning patrol routes, stealth kills, and extraction timing — the tutorial "The Lost Patrol" takes place here.

### Key Points of Interest

| POI | What's There | Why It Matters |
|-----|--------------|----------------|
| Eastern beach | 3 Y'm-bhi Sentries on patrol | First backstab practice; low noise risk |
| Central cave | Tutorial logbook, Ghost Bats, scripted hallucination | Story objective; bring a lamp |
| Southern beach | Tutorial extraction point | No hidden timer in the tutorial |
| Ruined shrine areas | Lore pages, small loot caches | Fast Reputation on Recon contracts |

### Enemy Profile

| Enemy | Typical Count | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry | 3–5 | Patrol the beaches and forest paths |
| Ghost Bat | 2 | Cave ceilings — one lamp flash kills them |
| Y'm-bhi Scout | 1–2 (night) | Fast, long detection range; avoid |

### Loot Route

Land on the eastern beach → clear the shoreline patrols with backstabs → sweep the central cave for the logbook and any caches (lamp in hand for Ghost Bats) → hit the shrines on the way to the southern beach extraction. The whole loop takes under 15 minutes and is the safest farm for early Herbs and currency.

## Forest Regions

Forest areas appear on several islands and contracts, and they change completely between day and night — the day version is a stealth playground, the night version is a Wraith-infested maze.

| Condition | Common | Uncommon | Rare |
|-----------|--------|----------|------|
| Day | Sentry (4–6), Vine Creature (2–3) | Berserker (1) | Centipede (1, underground) |
| Night | Sentry (2–3), Wraith (2) | Berserker (2) | — |

### Key Points of Interest

- **Forest Crossroads:** a ruined chapel marks the central junction. Patrol loops of 50m make it the best backstab spot in the game.
- **Vine-heavy undergrowth:** visually distinct darker patches — walk, do not run, or you trigger Vine Creatures.
- **Rivers and streams:** wading creates noise at 25m. Cross where the water is narrowest and shallowest.
- **Ox Cart staging areas:** on contract maps, the Ox Cart parks near the crossroads before heading deeper.

### Route Tip

Day forest contracts are the best early source of Herbs and Vine Fiber: clear the chapel loop first (predictable patrols), then sweep the undergrowth edge with a lamp ready. At night, give the forest a wide berth unless a contract requires it — Wraiths alert everything nearby when they wail.

## Catacombs

An underground network beneath the ruins, reachable through cave entrances on contract maps. Tighter corridors, darker rooms, and the game's first real "dungeon" feel.

### Key Points of Interest

| POI | What's There | Why It Matters |
|-----|--------------|----------------|
| Entrance gate | 2 Sentries facing outward | Crouch past or double backstab |
| Central chambers | Centipede burrow zones, Ghost Bats | Listen for the burrow rumble |
| Shrine room | 1 Y'm-bhi Priest | Kill it before it summons 4 Sentries (3s cast) |

### Enemy Profile

| Enemy | Typical Count | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry | 4 | Gate guards plus corridor patrols |
| Giant Centipede | 2 | Burrow ambushes in wide chambers |
| Ghost Bat | 4–6 | Ceilings throughout |
| Y'm-bhi Priest | 1 | High-value loot: Sanity items, lore pages |

### Route Tip

The Catacombs are the best early source of **Centipede Carapace** (armor crafting material) — fight centipedes in wide chambers, never corridors. The Priest's shrine room holds Sanity Restore items, but clear it first, before he summons reinforcements.

## Sunken Grotto

The flooded cave system unlocked at 2,000 Reputation. Water dominates: corridors flood, Leech Swarms drift through pools, and staying dry is a survival mechanic on its own. Story boss **The Lurker Below** awaits in the lowest chamber, and the **Drowned Captain** mini-boss roams the middle level.

### Key Points of Interest

| POI | What's There | Why It Matters |
|-----|--------------|----------------|
| Spore vent chambers | 3 side chambers with vent traps | Disable all three before the boss (contract "Whispers in the Deep") |
| Flooded lower level | Leech Swarms, rising water | Raised platforms are the only dry ground |
| Boss chamber | The Lurker Below | Weak to Oil Lamp fire; 50% ballistic resistance |
| Drowned Captain spawn | Mid-level pools | Mini-boss; highest non-boss loot on this map |

### Enemy Profile

| Enemy | Typical Count | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Wraith | 3–4 | Phase through walls; fire hurts them |
| Giant Centipede | 1 | Underground sections |
| Berserker | 1–2 | Patrol the dry ridges |
| Leech Swarm | Groups of 4+ | Water only; one Oil Lamp throw clears a swarm |

### Route Tip

Bring an Oil Lamp to the Grotto — it answers the Leech Swarms, the boss, and the darkness. Route: clear the three spore chambers top to bottom, loot the dry ridge caches, then drop to the boss floor with a clear escape path. Water drains Sanity, so keep pool crossings short.

## Legendary Hunt Locations

Four maps exist primarily for the Legendary Hunt contracts. Each is a lair for a named monster with fixed spawn and unique drops. Full fight breakdowns live in the [Legendary Hunts Guide](/guides/legendary-hunts); here is how the maps themselves play.

| Map | Lair | Hunt Target | Map-Specific Rule |
|-----|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Shallow Graves | Sunken Chapel (northeast) | The Drowned Baron | Water heals him — fight on dry ground |
| The Hollow Woods | The Clearing (center) | The Pale King | Verify targets before swinging — fakes heal him |
| The Deep Warrens | The Nest | The Crawling King | Immune while burrowed; oil his trail |
| Mansion of Fools | The Grand Hall | The Gilded Widow | Web-lock every 20s; spread out |

### Route Tips

- **Scout first:** every lair has an escape route — walk the perimeter once before pulling the target. You want to know the way out before the fight.
- **Pull away from the lair:** the targets patrol a wide area; fight them on ground you have already cleared.
- **Hunt timers:** the target despawns after 20 minutes or when it reaches its lair. Do not loop the map three times before committing.
- **Bring oil and flares to all four.** Oil is the universal weakness of three of the four targets, and flares reveal the Crawling King's burrow path.

## The Mound Interior

The final area, unlocked at 6,000 Reputation. A three-floor descent into the mountain with **permanent darkness**, 25% faster Sanity drain, and the highest enemy density in the game. Final boss: **High Priest of Cthulhu** in the Inner Sanctum.

| Floor | Name | Key Objective | Threats |
|-------|------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | The Antechamber | Activate 3 glyph pedestals | Elite Stalkers, Wraith Archers |
| 2 | The Hall of Whispers | Reach the Obsidian Seal | Whispering walls drain Sanity fast |
| 3 | The Inner Sanctum | Boss arena access | Constant Sanity drain before the fight |

### Enemy Profile

| Enemy | Typical Count | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Y'm-bhi Priest | 2 | Interrupt the summoning cast |
| Berserker | 3 | Charge attacks; feints |
| Wraith | 2 | Phase through walls |
| Giant Centipede | 2 | Burrow zones in the Antechamber |

### Route Tip

The Interior is a resource war, not a firefight. Enter with full Sanity-restore supplies (Whisper Charms, Moonwater Flasks, Salves), move floor by floor, and do not backtrack. Permanent darkness makes the Oil Lamp mandatory — assign a dedicated light-bearer in co-op and pull groups with the Crossbow from range.

## Navigation Essentials

| Tool / Mechanic | How It Helps | Where It Comes From |
|-----------------|--------------|---------------------|
| Compass (Standard / Survey) | Points to the nearest active zone; Survey reveals all active points for 10s (90s cooldown) | Starting tool |
| Map (M key) | Pulsing icons mark active zones; gray = inactive | Always available |
| Medallion portal | Shortcuts across any map — save one charge for extraction | Vendor purchase / crafting |
| Map fragments | Unlock Hidden Cache exits (high risk, high value) | Found loot, secret rooms |
| Flare colors | Green = Primary, Blue = Secondary, Red = Storm extraction | Read them from any high ground |

### Route Planning Rules

1. **Mark your extraction point early** — waypoint it as soon as you know which zones are active.
2. **Clear a return path** — push in on one side, keep the other side clean for the way out.
3. **Keep the Ox Cart between you and extraction** in the second half — never let valuable loot sit deeper than you are.
4. **Learn one route per map, then optimize it** — consistent extractors are the ones who know exactly what each zone holds.
