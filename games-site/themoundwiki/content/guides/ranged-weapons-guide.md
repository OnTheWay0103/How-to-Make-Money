# The Mound Ranged Weapons Guide — Crossbow, Matchlock, Flintlock, and Axe

> Launch Version / Updated: August 4, 2026

Four of **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu's** six weapons are ranged: the Crossbow, the Matchlock Arquebus, the Flintlock Pistol, and the Francesca Axe. Each fills a different role — stealth marksman, burst damage dealer, panic sidearm, and precision thrower — and each has a hard rule you must respect to survive. This guide is the deep dive: how every ranged weapon actually handles, how ammo works, how weather breaks them, and which one to bring for every enemy on the island.

> For the full six-weapon overview and loadout planning, see the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide).

## Ranged Weapon Comparison

| Weapon | Damage | Silent? | Rain-Proof? | Ammo | Role |
|--------|--------|---------|-------------|------|------|
| Crossbow | High (armor-piercing up close) | **Yes** | **Yes** | Bolts (reusable) | Stealth marksman — the best all-rounder |
| Matchlock Arquebus | Very high single shot | No | **No** | Shotgun-style shot (limited) | Burst damage on armored targets |
| Flintlock Pistol | Medium | No | **No** | Low capacity | Fast-draw backup |
| Francesca Axe | Variable (arc-dependent) | Yes | Yes | 1 (retrievable) | Precision throws, zero ammo economy |

## Crossbow — The Stealth Marksman (S-Tier)

The Crossbow is the best weapon in the game for most expeditions: silent, rain-proof, armor-piercing at close range, and its bolts can be **recovered and reused**.

### Strengths
- **Silent** — no gunshot noise; picks off Y'm-bhi without alerting their patrol
- **Rain-proof** — the only strong ranged option when firearms misfire
- **Armor-piercing at close range** — three headshots kill a Berserker
- **Reusable bolts** — a well-aimed fight costs you nothing

### Weaknesses
- Slow, deliberate shots — you cannot panic-fire it
- Limited bolt capacity — run out mid-fight and you are on your Machete

### Crossbow Tactics

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry patrol | Headshot from cover; the body drops silently |
| Y'm-bhi Berserker | 3 headshots; keep the range — his charge has a long recovery you can punish |
| Y'm-bhi Priest | Headshot + melee follow-up before his 3-second summon completes |
| Giant Centipede | Headshot in the 1-second pause after it surfaces |
| Rain / storm contract | Your primary; carry extra bolts and expect to use them |
| Ghost Bats | Do not bother — one lamp flash kills them for free |

### The Purifier (Endgame Crossbow)

Defeating the High Priest of Cthulhu rewards the **Purifier** blueprint: a fire-enhanced Crossbow that keeps the silent profile and adds burn damage — the ranged answer to every boss with a fire weakness.

## Matchlock Arquebus — The Burst Cannon

The Matchlock is the highest single-shot damage in the game. It deletes armored enemies in clear weather — and it is completely useless in rain.

### Strengths
- Devastating single shot with armor-piercing power at close range
- The best boss damage in clear weather
- One clean shot removes the worst threat on the field

### Weaknesses
- Long reload — you will not get a second chance quickly
- Can misfire
- **Fuse dies in rain** — a sudden storm turns your arquebus into a club

### Matchlock Tactics

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Clear-weather boss fight | Save the shot for the boss or the Berserker; do not waste it on Sentries |
| Armored Y'm-bhi | Close range punch-through — fire from shelter, then fall back behind the team |
| Rain starts mid-fight | Stop firing immediately; swap to the Crossbow or Machete |
| Reload window | Reload only in cover — the animation leaves you defenseless |

**Golden rule:** check the sky before you leave the Galleon. Clear weather: the Matchlock is S-tier. Storm forecast: leave it on the rack.

## Flintlock Pistol — The Panic Sidearm

The Flintlock is not a primary weapon. It is the "oh sh—" button that lives in your second slot until you actually need it.

### Strengths
- Fastest draw in the game
- Decent close-range punch
- Saves you when stealth fails and the Machete cannot reach

### Weaknesses
- Limited range
- Rain-vulnerable
- Low ammo capacity

### Flintlock Tactics

1. **Holster it by default.** If you are firing the Flintlock, something has already gone wrong.
2. **Fire once, switch to melee.** The pistol's job is to stagger or finish a single pursuer — then the Machete takes over.
3. **Save it for extraction.** A 10-second window of "one pursuer too many" is exactly what the pistol solves during the waiting period.
4. **Never rely on it in rain.** Same fuse problem as the Matchlock.

---

## Francesca Axe — The Precision Throwable

The Axe is the highest skill-ceiling weapon in the game: silent, retrievable, and capable of one-shot headshot kills — but a miss is a lost weapon.

### Strengths
- Silent throw — ranged kill without a gunshot
- Retrievable — walk over and pick it up
- Damage scales with throw accuracy — headshots are brutal
- Rain-proof

### Weaknesses
- High skill ceiling — you must learn the arc
- **Lost forever if it lands in an inaccessible spot**
- Cannot save you in a panic

### Axe Tactics

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Open field Sentry | Headshot from max comfortable range; retrieve after the kill |
| Backstab alternative | Can't close distance? The Axe is your ranged backstab |
| Tight corridors | Do not throw — a ricochet or a miss in a tunnel is a lost weapon |
| Practicing the arc | Throw at tree trunks during low-risk Recon runs before relying on it in fights |

## Ammo Economy — The Shared Pool

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Shared party pool** | The weapons rack has limited copies — if there is one Flintlock, only one player carries it. Plan who takes what before departure. |
| **Bolts are reusable** | A Crossbow player who recovers bolts pays nothing per fight |
| **Enemy drops** | Regular enemies drop ammo (5–50 currency value); loot Sentries for Herb and ammo supplies |
| **Found ammo is mission-only** | Like all found loot, it counts only if you extract with it |

**Practical rule for squads:** one silent ranged role (Crossbow) + one loud burst role (Matchlock) covers both worlds — the Crossbow handles patrols, the Matchlock deletes the priority target.

## Weather Rules — The Ranged Weapon Decider

| Weather | Firearms? | Crossbow? | Axe? | Best Ranged Pick |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------|------------------|
| Clear / Night | ✅ Yes | ✅ | ✅ | Matchlock (burst) + Crossbow (silent) |
| Light Rain | ❌ No | ✅ | ✅ | Crossbow only |
| Heavy Storm | ❌ No | ✅ | ✅ | Crossbow + Axe |

**If a storm is forecast, the ranged slot is Crossbow — full stop.** The Axe is the backup, and the Machete is the floor.

## Ranged Matchups Quick Reference

| Enemy | Best Ranged Answer | Notes |
|-------|--------------------|-------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry | Crossbow headshot | Silent, one or two bolts |
| Y'm-bhi Berserker | Crossbow (3 headshots) or Matchlock shot | Keep range; punish the charge recovery |
| Y'm-bhi Priest | Crossbow headshot + melee | Interrupt the 3s summon cast |
| Ghost Bat | Oil Lamp flash | Any ranged weapon is a waste of ammo |
| Wraith | Fire (Oil Lamp), Crossbow as backup | Takes 50% less physical damage |
| Giant Centipede | Crossbow headshot after surface pause | Matchlock works but the reload window is risky |
| The Lurker Below | Oil Lamp fire (bonus damage) | 50% ballistic resistance — Crossbow is slow DPS, lamp throws win |
| High Priest of Cthulhu | Oil Lamp / explosives (fire) | High ballistic resistance; The Purifier is the ideal endgame pick |

## Loadout Combos

| Setup | Ranged Loadout | Why |
|-------|----------------|-----|
| Solo (any weather) | Crossbow + Machete | Silent, self-sufficient, extracts safely |
| Duo (clear) | Matchlock + Crossbow | One loud burst, one silent scout |
| Duo (rain) | Crossbow ×2 | Everyone stays effective when firearms fail |
| Full squad (clear) | Matchlock ×2 + Crossbow + Oil Lamp | Max DPS with a dedicated light-bearer |
| Full squad (rain) | Crossbow ×2 + Machete ×2 + Oil Lamp | Firearms stay on the Galleon |
