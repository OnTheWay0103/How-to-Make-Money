## Squad Loadout & Supplies Chart — The Mound

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

The Mound has **no classes — your loadout defines your role.** Every expedition starts from the squad's **shared arsenal**: one musket, one sword, one lamp, one bundle of bandages, and limited ammo. Divide supplies and assign roles **before** leaving the galleon — wandering off or talking roles past the dock is the leading cause of failed expeditions.

---

## Role Chart (4-Player Squad)

| Role | Weapon Priority | Supplies | Job On Expedition |
|------|-----------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Marksman** | Matchlock Arquebus / Crossbow | Most ammo, keeps the dry powder | Quiet kills from range; drops cursed birds and Bandurria before they trigger |
| **Melee** | Machete | None (no ammo) — takes surplus bandages | Front line, backstabs Y'm-bhi, buys time when the jungle wakes |
| **Healer** | Flintlock Pistol (backup) | Bandages + crafted salves | Keeps the squad alive; the Ox Wagon revives downed players — drag them back to it |
| **Light Carrier** | Oil Lamp | Lamp oil, spare fuel | Lights dark paths, stuns light-sensitive creatures, throws fire at tree creatures |

---

## Squads by Size

| Squad Size | Minimum Roles | Notes |
|------------|---------------|-------|
| **Solo (1)** | Marksman + Melee in one loadout | Crossbow + Machete; stay silent, extract early — solo is not recommended by reviewers |
| **Duo (2)** | Marksman + Melee | Healer role folds into the melee player (carry bandages) |
| **Trio (3)** | Marksman, Melee, Healer/Light | Lamp rotates to whoever is in front |
| **Full (4)** | All four roles | The intended experience — scales difficulty and rewards |

## Supplies Quick Reference

| Supply | What It Does | Who Carries It |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Musket (Matchlock)** | Highest single-shot damage; jams in rain | Marksman |
| **Sword / Machete** | Silent, weatherproof, backstab kills | Melee |
| **Oil Lamp** | Light, stuns light-sensitive foes, throwable fire | Light Carrier |
| **Bandages** | Heal wounds and sanity-restoring rests | Healer |
| **Ammo** | Shared pool — always limited, always track it | Marksman (announced, not hoarded) |
| **Medallion (Tulu-metal)** | Versatile tool — **ringing it wakes the jungle**, use sparingly | Squad leader |
| **Black Ichor lure** | Lures creatures to a single spot (one jar can draw 5) | Healer or Melee |

## Before You Dock — Checklist

1. Assign roles and call them out on voice chat
2. Divide the shared arsenal (nobody holds two weapons they can't use)
3. Agree on retreat signal — extraction discipline is the whole game
4. Confirm who carries the lamp (someone always must)
5. Set the rules: sanity is individual — **verify hallucinations with the party before shooting anything**
