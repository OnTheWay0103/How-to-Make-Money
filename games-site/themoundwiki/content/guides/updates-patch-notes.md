# Update Log and Patch Notes — Version History and Changes

> Last verified: September 11, 2026

This page tracks confirmed updates for **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu**. We only include information that can be verified against official developer announcements or consistent community reports.

**Note:** The game launched on July 15, 2026 and is actively being updated. Some mechanics and values below may change. Check the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2569760) and [Steam Community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2569760) for the latest official information.

## Confirmed Updates

### Version 1.05 — Voice Chat Rework & Other Hotfixes

**Released:** September 9, 2026

*"Patch 1.05 — Voice Chat Rework & Other Hotfixes"* is, in the developers' own words, "a relatively small patch" released while a larger one is in development. The focus is the in-game voice chat:

- **Voice chat rework.** The developers state they had "reports of voice chat sounding robotic or not working at all," that it had been working as intended for some players, and that they overhauled the system and tested it extensively. The patch note line reads: **fixed voice chat corruption during crossplay and on certain systems**.
- **Why use the in-game chat.** Because the game uses spatial audio and some sanity effects use voice, the developers recommend playing with the in-game voice chat rather than an external app.
- **Upscaler profile fix** — addressed profile setting issues with **TSR** and **XeSS**.
- **Mound level orb** — fixed the orb not handling damage properly.

Players still seeing voice chat problems are asked to report them on the official Discord or in the Steam Discussions.

### Version 1.04 — Content Update

**Released:** August 31, 2026

*"Patch 1.04 — Content Update"* is the game's first major post-launch content patch. The developers describe it as "part of our plans for the continued expansion and development of the game."

**New content**

- **3 new enemies** that appear in the depths of the forest.
- **New hidden jewel boxes** scattered across the mainland. Find them all using the map to unlock a new Occult Item from the shop.
- **New Occult Item: The Rosary** — obtained via the jewel boxes above.

**Features / improvements**

- **Command Wheel 2.0** — you can now **stop the cart and give it instructions**; proximity determines whether or not you use the horn.
- **New Lobby Codes** — the game now uses **automatically generated join codes** instead of manual passwords to search for specific lobbies. See the [Multiplayer Connection Fix Guide](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide).
- **Optional Passwords** — you can add an optional, encrypted password for private sessions.

**Bug fixes** — more crash, bug and freezing fixes, **including getting stuck in the Galleon's shops**, plus miscellaneous fixes and **adjustments to the melee combat**.

### Version 1.03 — Quality of Life Hotfixes

**Released:** August 13, 2026

*"Patch 1.03 — Quality of Life Hotfixes"* focused on matchmaking, optimisation and comfort, and the developers flagged a bigger patch was already in the works:

- **Matchmaking filters and Quickmatch** — filters to show region, language and more, plus a Quickmatch option that puts you into a game without visiting the server list.
- **Teleport to safety** — a new option for the rare cases where players get stuck, preventing the loss of an entire mission.
- **Performance** — geometry streaming technology incorporated for better memory management, plus other optimisations.
- **Backstabbing reworked** — players can now **backstab enemies that have aggro** for significant damage (enemies with no aggro are still executed).
- **Blocking** — a minor stun was added to enemies when they are blocked.
- **Voice chat** — audio corruption fixed and audio spatialization improved.
- **Comfort / QoL** — you can now **pet Carlito the Ox** on the ship; hotbar inventory can be reorganised with a gamepad; spear and headshot achievements that were not unlocking under certain scenarios were fixed.
- **New sanity illusion** added.
- **Known issue:** the developers confirmed in this patch that crossplay friend invites were still being worked on (official workaround: find friends via the online server list) — see the [Multiplayer Connection Fix Guide](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide).

### Version 1.02 — Block Ability & Hotfixes

**Released:** July 29, 2026

*"Patch 1.02 — Block Ability & Hotfixes"* was a short patch with one large change — the developers noted it "could have a big effect on the balance and difficulty of the game":

- **New block ability** — on **Q** on keyboard or **RB/R1** on controller, to avoid being overrun by approaching creatures.
- **Progress recovery tool** — an achievement/trophy-based tool on all platforms that rebuilds lost permanent progression (see the [Progress Loss & Save Recovery Guide](/guides/progress-save-recovery-guide)).
- **PS5 progress-loss fix** — addressed the console-specific profile-reset scenario.
- **Performance fix** — players no longer see low performance on Ultra settings when relaunching the game.
- **Single-player & ally fixes** — fixed knives not appearing in single-player, and Saints not loading after missions (a regression introduced in the previous patch).
- **Other fixes** — duplicate same-class enemy spawns in some missions, weapon appearance on ultrawide configurations, the Mi-Go grabbing players for less time in single-player, torches losing their flame, and unused captain dialogue lines. Local voice improvements were noted with "some issues remain that we're looking into."
- **Balance** — reduced Saints blessing costs, a slight increase to tokens given for contract tiers, and rescuing a character on the mainland now counts as a successful contract regardless of whether the treasure was returned.

### Version 1.01 — Post Launch Hotfixes, Balancing Pass + Crossplay Friend Finder

**Released:** July 21, 2026

The first post-launch patch covered performance, camera comfort, crossplay lobby-finding and a balance pass:

- **Performance profiles improved for PC**, plus far LODs added where pop-in was visible and console optimisation improvements.
- **Camera choice** — a pre-configuration screen on first launch lets players choose a **cinematic camera or a standard camera** (the standard camera may help with feelings of delayed interaction or motion sickness) — see the [Motion Sickness & Camera Fix Guide](/guides/motion-sickness-camera-fix-guide).
- **Server password setting** — the ability to find sessions using a password was added to server settings. This is the ancestor of the lobby-code system introduced in 1.04.
- **Key bindings are now saved between sessions.**
- **Balance** — a machete was added to the starting gear of all missions so players are never stuck with only knives; the Crawling Canopy spider does 50% less damage; the Censer mission's purple corruption areas were modified and are slower to kill players; general enemy damage difficulty adjusted; stamina and run speed slightly buffed when tired; knives do slightly more damage; pole weapons have a bit more range; enemies take longer to spawn when the forest wakes up.
- **Bug fixes** — replication fixes for death animations that caused T-posing, the bleeding state not showing on clients, players getting stuck on walls after climbing, the "Game Saving" icon not displaying, the motion blur slider, and **Mi-Go enemy behaviour that stopped them searching for players**. Also a crash related to isolation fog.
- **Known issues at the time:** Saints disappearing after one expedition unless the lobby was restarted, and **microphones not being correctly picked up in-game** — the earliest appearance of the voice chat problem that Patch 1.05 later reworked.

### Version 1.0.0 — Launch

**Released:** July 15, 2026

The launch version introduced the core game systems:

- **Extraction Loop:** Deploy, complete contracts, gather loot, extract
- **Sanity System:** Psychological horror mechanics affecting perception and gameplay
- **Contracts System:** Multiple contract types with varying objectives
- **Co-op:** 1-4 player online cooperative play with full crossplay
- **Perk System:** Unlockable passive abilities
- **Crafting System:** Camp-based item creation and repair

**Launch Content:**

| Content Type | Notes |
|-------------|-------|
| Maps | Multiple expedition zones |
| Contract types | Stealth, combat, collection, boss hunt |
| Enemy types | Multiple variants including Y'm-bhi |
| Boss encounters | Several major encounters |
| Weapons | Melee, ranged, and tools |
| Perks | Multiple unlockable abilities |
