# The Mound Temple of Yig DLC — How to Claim It and Play It With Friends

> **Last verified: October 23, 2026.** The DLC is live. The base game is on patch 1.06.

The **Temple of Yig Exclusive Mission** is the easiest piece of content to miss in The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu. It is free, it launched alongside the game on July 15, 2026, and on Steam it is **not added to your account automatically**. If you bought the game and went straight into co-op, there is a good chance you do not own it.

This guide covers the claim, the co-op rule that makes it unusually friendly, how to reach the mission, and what to expect when you get there. It also separates what Nacon has actually said from what the community wiki has inferred.

---

## 1. What the Temple of Yig Is

The official store description is one sentence: *"Expand your adventure with the Temple of Yig Exclusive Mission Pack! Take on a new contract and unearth ancient mysteries in a unique area of the cursed jungle."*

The launch post adds that it is *"a free DLC"* and that it sends players into *"a unique area of the cursed jungle."*

So the honest summary is: **one new contract, in one new area, free.** It is not a new character, a new weapon pack, or a new game mode. It is a mission — an evening of content rather than a second campaign.

---

## 2. Is It Actually Free?

Yes, at launch. Nacon's own launch post calls it a free DLC, and the separate Steam store page exists for the mission pack itself (app 4925780) rather than only as part of the Deluxe bundle.

Two caveats:

- **Free does not mean automatic.** The launch post is explicit that the DLC must be **manually claimed** from the store page and *"won't be added to your account"* by itself.
- **The Deluxe Edition also includes it.** If you bought Deluxe, the mission is bundled. You still need the base game installed, because the DLC page states: *"This content requires the base game The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu on Steam in order to play."*

Do not confuse it with the **Lost Explorers' Swords Pack**. That is a separate cosmetic entitlement, free only during the launch window (through July 29, 2026) and potentially a paid item afterward. Claiming the sword pack does not claim Temple of Yig, and vice versa.

## 3. How to Claim It on Steam

The PC claim is short but easy to do in the wrong order.

1. Open Steam and go to your **Library**.
2. Find **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu**, then open its store or hub page.
3. Scroll to the **Downloadable Content** section.
4. Find **Temple of Yig Exclusive Mission** and select **Add to Account** / **Install**.
5. Confirm the DLC now appears under the game's owned content **before** you host a co-op session.

If the DLC does not appear in the list, open the standalone store page directly (app 4925780) and claim it there. Regional storefronts sometimes surface a free add-on only on its own page rather than inside the base-game DLC carousel. **[Unconfirmed]** for any specific region.

### Epic Games Store

Check the game's **DLC tab** in the Epic launcher or store page. The community wiki reports the same manual-claim pattern as Steam, but Nacon's launch post only documents Steam explicitly. **[Unconfirmed]**

### PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S

Check the console store's DLC/add-ons section for the game. The official launch post does not give console-specific claim steps, and the community wiki states that only the PC/Steam procedure was confirmed. If the add-on is not listed, search the store by the mission name rather than browsing the game page. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 4. The Co-op Rule: Only the Host Needs It

This is the best part of the DLC's design, and it is official: *"Only the host needs the DLC for the whole party to play."*

In practice:

- The **host** must own and install Temple of Yig.
- **Other squad members do not** need to buy or claim anything.
- The party can still be up to **four players**, the base game's co-op limit.
- **Crossplay still applies**, but all players should be on matching game versions. If a direct invite fails, use the server browser; that is the same workaround as the base game's connection issues.

One consequence worth planning for: if your usual host has not claimed the DLC, nobody in the group can start the mission. Before your next session, have the host launch the game alone once and confirm the mission appears in their contract or mission list.

## 5. How to Access the Mission

Nacon has not published a step-by-step "where to click" guide, and this is where most confusion lives.

What is confirmed:

- It is a **contract** — the mission is framed the same way as the base game's expeditions.
- It takes place in a **unique area** of the cursed jungle, not the standard procedural mainland.
- The host must own it.

What is not confirmed is whether the mission appears as a separate entry on the Captain's contract board, as a distinct destination when selecting an expedition, or as a new marker after a story milestone. The community wiki describes it as an "exclusive mission contract" but does not document the exact menu path. **[Unconfirmed]**

The practical order to try:

1. Host the lobby, with the DLC confirmed installed.
2. Check the **Captain's contract selection** for a Yig-labelled contract.
3. Check the **destination/area select** for a unique area separated from the normal jungle.
4. If nothing appears, restart the game after claiming; store entitlements sometimes need a fresh launch to register.

If all four fail, verify the DLC is ticked in the platform's installed add-ons, not merely owned.

## 6. What Is Inside

Confirmed by Nacon:

- A **new contract**
- A **unique area** of the cursed jungle
- "Ancient mysteries" as the narrative hook

Reported by the community wiki, and **not confirmed by the developer**:

- A **serpent-cult** theme tied to Yig
- "Enclosed temple-adjacent zones" and tighter sightlines
- Unspecified "unique threats and environmental hazards"
- Token rewards that feed the **Weapon Upgrade Anvil** between runs

The wiki explicitly says the area's proper name, specific enemy types, mission length, and exact rewards are **not specified** in public sources. Treat any guide that gives you a named boss or a drop table for this mission as unverified. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 7. How to Prepare

These recommendations are editorial, based on the mission's reported enclosed spaces and the base game's systems.

**Bring a dedicated lamp carrier.** The base game's darkness is a resource, and enclosed temple areas compress sightlines. Assign one player to hold and manage the lamp rather than letting it bounce between inventories.

**Bring salts and a revive plan.** In co-op, reviving is a shared responsibility. The reported enclosed layout makes it harder to retreat and reset a fight, so the player with salts should stay central.

**Do not over-pack.** The contract supplies a shared arsenal, and inventory space is limited. Free slots matter more than a fourth backup weapon.

**Plan a partial extraction.** The community wiki's advice is to leave with partial loot rather than wipe while chasing the last chest. That matches the base game's economy: a successful partial run pays Tokens, a wipe pays nothing.

**Expect the forest timer to still apply.** Nothing in the official material says the DLC area removes the base game's escalation clock. Do not assume this is a safe sightseeing map.

## 8. Troubleshooting

**"The DLC is not in my library."** Check owned add-ons, not the base game's store carousel. Free add-ons are sometimes claimed on a separate page.

**"My friend owns it but I cannot join."** The host must own it, not the other way around. Confirm who is hosting.

**"The mission does not appear."** Restart after claiming, then check the contract board and destination select. Store entitlements can take a relaunch to register.

**"Crossplay invite fails."** Match game versions and use the server browser. This is the same fix as the base game's connection errors.

**"I claimed the sword pack instead."** The Lost Explorers' Swords Pack is a separate entitlement. Claim Temple of Yig explicitly.

## 9. Is It Worth Playing?

Yes, with the right expectation. It costs nothing, it works with one owned copy for a full squad, and it adds a distinct area to a game whose map criticism has been about repetition. For solo play, the base game's one-bot limitation applies, and the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) is the better starting point.

## 10. FAQ

**Is Temple of Yig free?** Yes, Nacon released it as a free DLC at launch.

**Does every player need it?** No. Only the host needs the DLC for the whole party to play.

**Is it on consoles?** The base game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Nacon has not published console-specific claim steps, so check the store's add-ons section. **[Unconfirmed]**

**Is it a new map or a new mode?** A new contract in a unique area, not a new mode.

**Can I play it solo?** The DLC is a contract, and contracts can be run solo, but the mission is built for the co-op loop. Solo viability is **[Unconfirmed]**.

**Does it affect the main story?** No official statement connects it to the ending. Treat it as side content.
