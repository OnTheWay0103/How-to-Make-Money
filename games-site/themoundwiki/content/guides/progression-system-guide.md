# The Mound Progression Guide — What Carries Over & Tokens Explained

> Launch Version / Updated: August 1, 2026

"I just extracted with a legendary matchlock — where did it go?" This is the most common question new players ask in **The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu**, and the answer is the game's most misunderstood system: **weapons and armor never leave the expedition**. When you extract, in-run loot "dissolves" into **tokens**, and tokens buy **permanent store unlocks**. Your personal level persists too — but the gear in your hands does not.

That design makes many players feel like successful runs are pointless. They aren't. This guide explains exactly what carries over, how tokens work, what to buy first, and how to reframe the loop so every run feels like the deposit it actually is.

---

## What Carries Over Between Runs

| What Persists | How It Works |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Tokens** | The meta currency. Earned by extracting with loot — the loot converts to tokens at the results screen. Tokens are banked permanently |
| **Permanent store unlocks** | Weapons, cosmetics, and perks bought with tokens stay unlocked forever |
| **Personal level** | Your level and its progression persist across every run, win or lose |
| **Cosmetics & perks** | Once purchased from the store, always available in your loadout |
| **Achievements / trophies** | Platform-side records — they even act as a backup for recovery (see below) |

## What Does NOT Carry Over

| Lost at the End of a Run | Notes |
|--------------------------|-------|
| **Weapons** | Run-only. They dissolve into tokens on extract, regardless of rarity |
| **Armor** | Run-only, same as weapons |
| **In-run loot** | Materials, consumables, and treasures found during the expedition convert to tokens — they do not persist as items |
| **Ox Cart contents** | Converted at extract like the rest; if the cart or the run is lost, that loot is gone |
| **In-run perks & buffs** | Temporary, gone when the expedition ends |

The key sentence: **items are tools for the run, tokens are the profit.** If you extract, everything you carried becomes wealth. If you die, the run's loot is lost — but your already-banked tokens, unlocks, and level are untouched.

---

## Why This Design Exists (The Extraction Loop)

This is not an oversight — it is the genre's core loop applied deliberately:

1. **Deploy** with a loadout you own (from store unlocks).
2. **Loot** during the expedition.
3. **Extract** — loot converts to **tokens**.
4. **Spend** tokens on **permanent** store unlocks.
5. **Repeat** with a stronger permanent arsenal.

Because gear never leaves the run, every expedition is a *resource run* that funds permanent growth. It also keeps the game fair between friends of different playtimes: a level-1 friend and a level-40 friend always bring comparable run gear into the jungle. What separates them is what they have unlocked — not an unkillable borrowed weapon.

## Tokens Explained

**What they are:** the meta currency that represents your expedition profits.

**How you earn them:**

| Source | Token Value |
|--------|-------------|
| Extracting with loot | Loot rarity converts to token value at the results screen (common items = small amounts, rare items = large amounts) |
| Contract rewards | Bonus tokens for completing the contract objective |
| Contract difficulty | Harder contracts pay higher multipliers |

**How you lose them:** you don't. Banked tokens never decay, and only unextracted loot fails to convert. Die in the jungle and you lose that run's potential — but never what is already in the bank.

**What you spend them on:** the permanent store — weapons (permanent unlock, so you can bring them on future runs), cosmetics, perks, and quality-of-life upgrades.

---

## Store Unlock Priority (Recommended Order)

Because tokens are earned slowly at first, spend them in this order:

| Priority | Unlock Type | Why |
|:--:|------------|-----|
| 1 | **Inventory / backpack upgrades** | More slots = more loot per run = more tokens per run. The compounding investment |
| 2 | **Perk unlocks** | Permanent power that affects every run |
| 3 | **Weapon unlocks** | Build variety and a stronger starting arsenal for your playstyle |
| 4 | **Quality-of-life unlocks** | Anything that reduces friction (repair, ammo economy) |
| 5 | **Cosmetics** | Last — they change nothing gameplay-wise |

**The compounding logic:** prioritize anything that increases how much loot you extract per run *first*. Every slot and every perk you buy makes the next run pay out more, which buys the next unlock faster.

## "Every Run Feels Wasted" — Why It Isn't

The frustration is real: you find a beautiful rare weapon, extract, and it is gone. Here is the reframe that makes the loop click:

- **The weapon was never the reward — the tokens are.** The rare weapon you extracted was converted into the largest token payout of the run. It literally *became* your permanent progression.
- **Banked progress is permanent.** Tokens, unlocks, and level never reset (and if a bug ever wipes them, Patch 1.02's achievement/trophy-based **Progress Recovery Tool** can rebuild them — see the [Save Recovery Guide](/guides/progress-save-recovery-guide)).
- **Failed runs still pay.** Even a run where you die banks nothing from that expedition — but it also costs you nothing you owned: no weapon, no armor, no tokens you already had. You only ever risk the run.
- **Every run is a deposit.** Think of each expedition as a trip to the bank with the jungle as your mine. The gear is the cart; the tokens are the savings.

Players who internalize this stop judging runs by "what did I keep" and start judging them by "how much did I bank" — and the game's loop opens up.

## The Meta-Progression Roadmap

| Loop | Timeframe | What Progresses |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Short loop** | One expedition | Loot → extract → tokens |
| **Mid loop** | Several expeditions | Tokens → store unlocks → better loadouts |
| **Long loop** | Weeks | Personal level → perk slots → harder contracts → higher token multipliers |
| **Backup loop** | Always | Achievements/trophies recorded server-side → recovery tool in 1.02 |

Patch 1.02 (late July 2026) strengthened the long game in one important way: the **achievement/trophy-based progress recovery tool** means your permanent progression has a safety net on all platforms, and the PS5 progress loss issue was fixed. The bank is safer now — which makes depositing in it a much better bet. See the [Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes) for the full changelog.

## FAQ

**Q: Why can't I keep my weapons?**
Design choice. Gear is run-bound; what you keep is the wealth it represents. The game converts loot to tokens instead of letting you hoard items, which keeps every expedition self-contained and every player's loadout fair.

**Q: Do tokens carry over if I quit mid-run?**
No — tokens only materialize when you extract. Abandoning or dying converts nothing.

**Q: Is there a stash or bank where I can store items?**
No item stash exists. Your permanent inventory is the store: unlocks are permanent, run items are not.

**Q: Do cosmetics carry over?**
Yes — anything purchased in the store is permanent across all runs.

**Q: Does my level reset if I die?**
No. Your personal level and all banked unlocks persist regardless of how the run ends. The only reported level resets were the launch-window progress bug, which the 1.02 recovery tool addresses.

**Q: What is the fastest way to earn tokens?**
Extract reliably over greedily: a steady stream of successful medium-difficulty extractions compounds faster than a spectacular run that ends in death. Spend early tokens on inventory space to increase per-run capacity.
