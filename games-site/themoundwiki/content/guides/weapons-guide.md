# The Mound Weapons Guide — All 6 Weapons & Best Loadouts

> **Launch Version / Updated: August 10, 2026**

The Mound features six distinct weapons: the Crossbow, the Matchlock Arquebus, the Flintlock Pistol, the Francesca Axe, the Machete, and the Oil Lamp. Your loadout defines your role — there are no classes. Understanding each weapon's traits is essential for surviving expeditions.

---

## Weapon Overview

| Weapon | Type | Silent? | Rain-Proof? | Best For |
|--------|------|---------|-------------|----------|
| Matchlock Arquebus | Firearm | No | **No** | Burst damage, armored enemies |
| Flintlock Pistol | Firearm | No | **No** | Sidearm, close-range backup |
| Crossbow | Ranged | **Yes** | Yes | Stealth kills, rainy conditions |
| Francesca Axe | Throwable | Yes | Yes | Precision throws, risk/reward |
| Machete | Melee | Yes | Yes | Reliable close combat |
| Oil Lamp | Utility | — | Flickers | Light source, throwable fire |

## Deep Dives — Every Weapon

### Crossbow — The Stealth Marksman

The Crossbow is the best weapon in the game for most expeditions: silent, rain-proof, armor-piercing at close range, and its bolts can be **recovered and reused**.

**Strengths**
- **Silent** — no gunshot noise; picks off Y'm-bhi without alerting their patrol
- **Rain-proof** — the only strong ranged option when firearms misfire
- **Armor-piercing at close range** — three headshots kill a Berserker
- **Reusable bolts** — a well-aimed fight costs you nothing

**Weaknesses**
- Slow, deliberate shots — you cannot panic-fire it
- Limited bolt capacity — run out mid-fight and you are on your Machete

**Crossbow Tactics**

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Y'm-bhi Sentry patrol | Headshot from cover; the body drops silently |
| Y'm-bhi Berserker | 3 headshots; keep the range — his charge has a long recovery you can punish |
| Y'm-bhi Priest | Headshot + melee follow-up before his 3-second summon completes |
| Giant Centipede | Headshot in the 1-second pause after it surfaces |
| Rain / storm contract | Your primary; carry extra bolts and expect to use them |
| Ghost Bats | Do not bother — one lamp flash kills them for free |

**The Purifier (Endgame Crossbow):** defeating the High Priest of Cthulhu rewards the **Purifier** blueprint — a fire-enhanced Crossbow that keeps the silent profile and adds burn damage. It is the ranged answer to every boss with a fire weakness.

### Matchlock Arquebus — The Burst Cannon

The Matchlock is the highest single-shot damage in the game. It deletes armored enemies in clear weather — and it is completely useless in rain.

**Strengths**
- Devastating single shot with armor-piercing power at close range
- The best boss damage in clear weather
- One clean shot removes the worst threat on the field

**Weaknesses**
- Long reload — you will not get a second chance quickly
- Can misfire
- **Fuse dies in rain** — a sudden storm turns your arquebus into a club

**Matchlock Tactics**

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Clear-weather boss fight | Save the shot for the boss or the Berserker; do not waste it on Sentries |
| Armored Y'm-bhi | Close-range punch-through — fire from shelter, then fall back behind the team |
| Rain starts mid-fight | Stop firing immediately; swap to the Crossbow or Machete |
| Reload window | Reload only in cover — the animation leaves you defenseless |

**Golden rule:** check the sky before you leave the Galleon. Clear weather: the Matchlock is S-tier. Storm forecast: leave it on the rack.

### Flintlock Pistol — The Panic Sidearm

The Flintlock is not a primary weapon. It is the "oh sh—" button that lives in your second slot until you actually need it.

**Strengths**
- Fastest draw in the game
- Decent close-range punch
- Saves you when stealth fails and the Machete cannot reach

**Weaknesses**
- Limited range
- Rain-vulnerable
- Low ammo capacity

**Flintlock Tactics**
1. **Holster it by default.** If you are firing the Flintlock, something has already gone wrong.
2. **Fire once, switch to melee.** The pistol's job is to stagger or finish a single pursuer — then the Machete takes over.
3. **Save it for extraction.** A window of "one pursuer too many" is exactly what the pistol solves during the waiting period.
4. **Never rely on it in rain.** Same fuse problem as the Matchlock.

### Francesca Axe — The Precision Throwable

The Axe is the highest skill-ceiling weapon in the game: silent, retrievable, and capable of one-shot headshot kills — but a miss is a lost weapon.

**Strengths**
- Silent throw — ranged kill without a gunshot
- Retrievable — walk over and pick it up
- Damage scales with throw accuracy — headshots are brutal
- Rain-proof

**Weaknesses**
- High skill ceiling — you must learn the arc
- **Lost forever if it lands in an inaccessible spot**
- Cannot save you in a panic

**Axe Tactics**

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Open field Sentry | Headshot from max comfortable range; retrieve after the kill |
| Backstab alternative | Can't close distance? The Axe is your ranged backstab |
| Tight corridors | Do not throw — a ricochet or a miss in a tunnel is a lost weapon |
| Practicing the arc | Throw at tree trunks during low-risk Recon runs before relying on it in fights |

### Machete — The All-Purpose Blade

The Machete is the one weapon you should never leave behind. Silent, weatherproof, and ammo-free, it is your fallback when everything else fails — and its backstab one-shots most Y'm-bhi.

**Strengths**
- Always works, no ammo, no weather effects
- Silent — melee is the only weapon class that never attracts noise
- Backstab one-shots most standard enemies

**Weaknesses**
- Short range
- Lower damage than specialized weapons

### Oil Lamp — The Utility Light

The Oil Lamp is not a damage weapon — it is light, fire crowd-control, and the only tool that keeps night expeditions safe.

**Strengths**
- Steady light (rain-proof), weakens light-sensitive creatures
- Throwable for a fire burst — one throw within 2m kills an entire Leech Swarm
- Fire barriers stop enemy pursuit for 4–5 seconds

**Weaknesses**
- Flickers in moisture, limited fuel
- Not a primary weapon

**Tactics:** throw at Y'm-bhi carrying lamps — they self-immolate. Use it to illuminate dark areas. Essential for teams without a dedicated light-bearer.

## Weapon Tier List

The Mound features six weapons, and each contract offers a fixed selection from the Galleon's weapons rack. Here is how they rank for most expeditions, based on damage, reliability, and weather resilience:

| Tier | Weapon | Why |
|:--:|--------|-----|
| **S** | Crossbow | Highest sustained damage — outperforms firearms in most fights. Silent, rain-proof, and bolts can be reused. The universal best pick. |
| **S** | Machete | Silent backstabs one-shot most enemies. No ammo, works in every weather. The only weapon you should never leave behind. |
| **A** | Matchlock Arquebus | Devastating single-shot burst and armor-piercing at close range. Completely useless in rain, so its value depends on the forecast. |
| **B** | Flintlock Pistol | Fast-draw sidearm for emergencies. Weak at range, rain-vulnerable, low ammo — a backup, not a primary. |
| **B** | Oil Lamp | Not a damage weapon: light, fire crowd-control, and the only tool that keeps night expeditions safe. Take it whenever darkness is a factor. |
| **C** | Francesca Axe | High skill ceiling and retrievable, but a miss is lost forever and it cannot save you in a panic. For experienced throwers only. |

## Ammo Economy — The Shared Pool

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Shared party pool** | The weapons rack has limited copies — if there is one Flintlock, only one player carries it. Plan who takes what before departure. |
| **Bolts are reusable** | A Crossbow player who recovers bolts pays nothing per fight |
| **Enemy drops** | Regular enemies drop ammo (5–50 currency value); loot Sentries for Herb and ammo supplies |
| **Found ammo is mission-only** | Like all found loot, it counts only if you extract with it |

**Practical rule for squads:** one silent ranged role (Crossbow) + one loud burst role (Matchlock) covers both worlds — the Crossbow handles patrols, the Matchlock deletes the priority target.

|---------|---------------|-----------|--------------|
| Clear / Night | Yes | Steady | Matchlock + Flintlock |
| Light Rain | No | Flickers | Crossbow + Machete |
| Heavy Storm | No | Dim | Crossbow + Axe |

> **Golden Rule**: always check the sky before choosing firearms. A sudden rainstorm mid-expedition turns your arquebus into a club. **If a storm is forecast, the ranged slot is Crossbow — full stop.** The Axe is the backup, and the Machete is the floor.

## How to Get Weapons

- **Contract gear:** each contract on the Galleon has a fixed weapon selection — some offer choices, others do not. Weapons are drawn from a **shared party pool**: if there is one Flintlock Pistol, only one player can carry it. Plan who takes what before departure.
- **Vendor purchases:** spend Upgrade Tokens at the Galleon vendor to buy new gear — torches, the Medallion, maps — and to **upgrade or trade in** weapons between expeditions.
- **Blueprints and crafting:** story and challenge rewards unlock craftable gear — the Crossbow (tutorial reward), The Purifier fire-crossbow (defeat the High Priest), and the Shadow Armor set (five Nocturnal Hunt expeditions).

### Are Weapon Upgrades Permanent?

**Yes for your collection, no for the mission.**

- Upgrades bought with Tokens and weapons crafted from blueprints **persist permanently** and stay in your pool across all future expeditions.
- The **mission loadout itself resets every contract** — you pick gear fresh from the rack each time.
- Found loot, by contrast, is **mission-only**: it is worth tokens only if you extract with it, and it does not carry between expeditions.

The practical rule: spend Tokens on upgrades and blueprints early (they are permanent investments), and treat the per-contract rack as your flexible loadout.

## Gear Optimization

- **Inventory slots expand as you Rank up** — early on, every slot counts, so keep your personal carry light.
- **Idols and heavy treasure go in the Ox Wagon**, not your pack — the wagon frees your limited slots for tools and healing.
- **The Medallion is the best utility item in the game** — it detects chests and danger and marks targets, but ringing it attracts monsters, so use it deliberately.
- **Check the weather before every loadout.** A Matchlock is S-tier in clear weather and dead weight in rain.

## Loadout Recommendations

| Setup | Loadout | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Solo (any weather) | Crossbow + Machete | Quiet, self-sufficient, extracts safely |
| Duo (clear) | Matchlock + Crossbow | One loud burst, one silent scout |
| Duo (rain) | Crossbow ×2 | Everyone stays effective when firearms fail |
| Trio (any weather) | Crossbow + Machete / Matchlock + Machete | One silent, one loud — the loud one handles armored foes |
| Full squad (clear) | Matchlock ×2, Crossbow, Oil Lamp | Max DPS with a dedicated light-bearer |
| Full squad (rain) | Crossbow ×2, Machete ×2, Oil Lamp | Firearms stay on the Galleon |

**Squad allocation:** before departure, decide who takes the shared Crossbow (usually your quietest player) and assign the Medallion and Oil Lamp roles — these are party tools, not personal loot. Everyone carries a Machete as fallback. See the [Co-op Strategy Guide](/guides/coop-strategy-guide) for the full role breakdown.
