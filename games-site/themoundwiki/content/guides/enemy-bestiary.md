## Class 1: Y'm-bhi (Undead Conquistadors)

The Y'm-bhi are the most common enemy type. These undead Spanish conquistadors patrol the forests, ruins, and caves of the New World. They are intelligent, organized, and deadly in groups.

### Behavior Patterns (All Variants)

| Behavior | Description | Counter |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Patrol** | Walk set routes with predictable timing. | Use patrol windows to sneak past or set up backstabs. |
| **Feint** | Y'm-bhi fakes an attack to bait your dodge or parry. | Do not panic-dodge. Wait for the actual strike. |
| **Parry** | They can deflect your melee attacks. | Do not spam attack. Mix in feints or use ranged weapons. |
| **Disarm** | A special attack that knocks your weapon out of your hands. | Keep distance; this has short range. Use Machete as backup. |
| **Tentacle Grab** | A ranged tentacle attack that holds you in place. | Teammate must shoot/melee the tentacle to free you. |
| **Self-Immolation** | When low on health, a Y'm-bhi may light itself on fire and charge. | Run perpendicular. Do not try to block. The fire spread is short. |

### Y'm-bhi Difficulty Variants

| Variant | Appearance | Special Traits |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Sentry | Standard armor, patrols | Has all standard behaviors |
| Berserker | Larger, red-tinted eyes | More health, cannot be staggered easily, faster charge |
| Scout | Light armor, moves quickly | Detects players from farther away, calls reinforcements |
| Shaman | Robed, carries a staff | Can heal nearby Y'm-bhi, drains Sanity from range |

### Combat Strategy Against Y'm-bhi

1. **Prioritize stealth.** Avoid engaging more than one at a time.
2. **Backstab first.** If you can approach from behind, the kill is instant.
3. **Use light.** Oil Lamp or torch light stuns Y'm-bhi for 2-3 seconds — long enough for a follow-up strike.
4. **Watch for the feint.** Experienced players learn to read the shoulder twitch that precedes a real attack.
5. **The tentacle grab is a team emergency.** If grabbed, call it out immediately. Your teammates have about 4 seconds to free you.
6. **Berserker self-immolation:** Do not group up. Spread out so the fire does not chain.

### Y'm-bhi Sentry

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 2/5 |
| **HP** | ~120 |
| **Spawn Locations** | Isle of Voices, Catacombs, Forest paths (all maps) |
| **Light Sensitivity** | High — torch or lantern stuns for 3s |
| **Backstab** | Yes — easiest backstab target |
| **Loot** | Low (Herbs, Ammo, 5-15 Currency) |
| **Behavior** | Patrols set routes, pauses at checkpoints, investigates noises |

**Lore**: Y'm-bhi Sentries are the least corrupted of the undead. They retain fragments of their human discipline, following patrol patterns drilled into them centuries ago. Their armor has rusted but remains functional.

**Strategy**: Sentries are your primary stealth kill target. Approach from behind while crouched. Backstab with Machete for an instant kill. If detected, a torch flash stuns them long enough to land 2-3 Crossbow bolts.

**Notable Spawn Locations:**
- Isle of Voices: 3 Sentries on the eastern beach patrol
- Catacombs Entrance: 2 Sentries at the gate, facing outward
- Forest Crossroads: 1 Sentry patrols a 50m loop past the ruined chapel

### Y'm-bhi Berserker

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 3/5 |
| **HP** | ~200 |
| **Spawn Locations** | Deep forest, Sunken Grotto, The Mound interior |
| **Light Sensitivity** | Partial — light slows but does not stun |
| **Backstab** | Yes (harder — higher chance of detection during approach) |
| **Loot** | Medium (Herbs, Rare Ammo, 15-30 Currency, sometimes Blue Relic) |
| **Behavior** | Aggressive patrol, charges on detection, can feint attacks |

**Lore**: Y'm-bhi Berserkers were once elite soldiers who gave themselves fully to the corruption in exchange for power. Their eyes glow faintly in the dark, and they are driven by an insatiable rage.

**Strategy**: Do not engage Berserkers in melee unless you have no choice. They have a feint attack that punishes early dodges. Use Crossbow headshots from range (3 headshots to kill). Their charge attack has a long recovery window — side-step and counter-attack. Berserkers also have a larger detection radius than Sentries — approach from further away and time your sprint.

### Y'm-bhi Priest

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 4/5 |
| **HP** | ~150 |
| **Spawn Locations** | The Mound interior, Shrine locations |
| **Light Sensitivity** | None |
| **Backstab** | No |
| **Loot** | High (Rare Herbs, Sanity Restore item, 50-100 Currency, Lore Pages) |
| **Behavior** | Stationary (praying) or slow patrol. Summons reinforcements when alerted. |

**Lore**: The Y'm-bhi Priests were the religious leaders of the original expedition. They are the most deeply corrupted, having willingly opened their minds to the entity beneath The Mound. They are the closest living connection to Cthulhu.

**Strategy**: Priests are the priority target in any encounter. If you see a Priest, kill it first before it summons reinforcements. Crossbow headshot + follow-up melee is the fastest kill. If it completes its summoning animation (3 seconds), 4 additional Sentries spawn.

## Class 2: Ethereal Entities

### Ghost Bat

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 1/5 |
| **HP** | ~40 |
| **Spawn Locations** | Caves, Catacombs, The Mound interior ceilings |
| **Light Sensitivity** | Extreme — direct light kills instantly |
| **Backstab** | No |
| **Loot** | Low (Bat Wing — sanity-restoring recipe component) |
| **Behavior** | Patrols ceiling, dives at players who pass below. |

**Lore**: Ghost Bats are not truly bats but rather fragments of corrupted spirit energy that took the form of flying creatures. They are drawn to heat signatures and the life force of intruders.

**Strategy**: Shine a lantern or torch at a Ghost Bat to kill it instantly with no noise. Crossbow is also effective but wastes ammo. Ghost Bats are more nuisance than threat — they deal only ~10 damage per dive, but a swarm attacking a single player is dangerous if you are alone.

### Wraith

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 3/5 |
| **HP** | ~80 |
| **Spawn Locations** | Sunken Grotto, night expeditions in forest areas |
| **Light Sensitivity** | Medium — light pushes them back but does not damage |
| **Backstab** | No (ethereal — cannot be backstabbed) |
| **Loot** | Medium (Ectoplasm — used in high-tier potions) |
| **Behavior** | Phases through walls, patrols in set areas, wails to alert nearby enemies |

**Lore**: Wraiths are the souls of those who died in the depths of the New World without proper burial. They are bound to specific locations and cannot leave, but within their territory they are relentless.

**Strategy**: Wraiths cannot be stealth-killed and they alert nearby enemies. If possible, avoid their patrol routes entirely. If forced to fight, use fire-based weapons (lantern oil + torch) for bonus damage. Wraiths take 50% less damage from physical weapons.

## Class 3: Flora and Fauna

### Vine Creature

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 2/5 |
| **HP** | ~100 |
| **Spawn Locations** | Forest areas with dense ground vegetation |
| **Light Sensitivity** | None |
| **Backstab** | No (burrowed — cannot approach unseen) |
| **Loot** | Low (Vine Fiber — used in rope crafting) |
| **Behavior** | Burrowed in ground, springs up when stepped on, grabs and holds |

**Lore**: The corruption of The Mound has spread to the plant life. Vine Creatures are the mutated result — ambush predators that blend perfectly with the undergrowth.

**Strategy**: Watch the ground. Vine Creature burrows appear as slightly darker patches of vegetation — and vine-heavy areas have a distinct visual texture, so slow down. Walk around them; never sprint through tall grass (footsteps trigger them). If grabbed, mash the interact key (E) to break free, then back away and shoot with Crossbow (3 bolts to kill). A lit Oil Lamp or torch burns vine patches, clearing the area permanently.

### Giant Centipede

| Stat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Threat Level** | 3/5 |
| **HP** | ~150 |
| **Spawn Locations** | Catacombs, underground caves, The Mound lower levels |
| **Light Sensitivity** | High — light confuses them, making them attack randomly |
| **Backstab** | No (burrows and surfaces unpredictably) |
| **Loot** | Medium (Centipede Carapace — armor crafting material) |
| **Behavior** | Burrows underground, surfaces to attack, burrows again |

**Lore**: These enormous arthropods are a direct result of the entity's influence on the local ecosystem. They can grow up to 4 meters in length and have been observed burrowing through solid stone.

**Strategy**: Listen for the burrowing rumble — the ground vibrates audibly before it emerges, and it tells you where it will surface. Keep moving (standing still makes you an easy target for the emergence attack). When it surfaces, it pauses for ~1 second before attacking — use that window to land a Crossbow headshot. Centipedes are vulnerable to fire (oil + torch does heavy bonus damage), and they turn slowly — circle strafe to its flank and punish. **Note:** some players report that pure melee works, but that advice is unverified — Machetes struggle against the armored segments, and it is best to keep distance so it cannot coil-grab. Watch the tail: some centipedes whip it in a wide arc.

## Class 4: Other Confirmed Creatures

### Cursed Bird

Red-eyed birds that perch in trees. Shoot them down the moment you spot them — they die to one aimed shot. **Do not let them dive:** a dive blinds you and lays false shadow trails. They are a priority kill before they trigger.

### Bandurria

An airborne creature that flies across open ground. It cannot be melee'd — shoot it mid-flight. Long-range shots (50m+) count toward the "Long Shot" achievement.

### Ghostly Figure (Lost Explorer Apparition)

An apparition that appears near where travelers died. Sustained light breaks it apart — shine an Oil Lamp or torch at it, then finish with melee. Drops lore pages for the Creature Codex.

### Corrupted / Puppet Copy

A creature that wears your teammate's face, distinguishable only by occasional beast-like noises. If a "teammate" sounds wrong, it is not your teammate. Do **not** engage until confirmed with your party — the real danger is friendly fire.

### Lurker

Body parts lunging from bushes. It telegraphs its lunge — listen for it and melee on the lunge; counter-attack after it whiffs.

### Giant Tree Creature

A massive walking tree that creaks loudly before waking. Avoid it entirely, or burn it with thrown Oil Lamp fire. Noise discipline keeps it asleep.

### Huemul (Deer)

Huntable wildlife. Silent kills (Crossbow or Francesca Axe) keep the pack from scattering. Bring the carcass to the ship's cook for better rations.

## Backstab Mechanic — Complete Guide

Backstabbing is the most efficient kill method in The Mound.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Position | Directly behind the enemy (180-degree rear arc) |
| Weapon | Any melee weapon or unarmed prompt |
| Enemy Type | Works on humanoid enemies (Y'm-bhi variants) |
| Noise | Silent — does not alert other enemies |
| Animation | 2-second execution animation (you are vulnerable during this) |

### Backstab Tips
- **Crouch** to reduce footstep noise while approaching.
- **Patience.** Wait for the enemy patrol path to angle away from you.
- **Berserkers have a larger detection radius.** Approach from further away and time your sprint.
- **Do not backstab in a group.** If another enemy can see you, the backstab prompt may not appear.
- **The animation locks you in place.** Make sure no other enemy is nearby before committing.

## Light-Sensitive Enemies

Several enemies react negatively to light. Understanding this mechanic saves resources.

| Enemy | Light Effect | Optimal Tool |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Y'm-bhi (all variants) | Stunned for 2-3 seconds | Oil Lamp flash |
| Ghost Bat | Instant kill | Any light source |
| Shadow Wraiths (rare) | Repelled completely | Torch or Lamp |

## Universal Rules (Applies to Every Creature)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Noise pulls everything** | Gunfire, sprinting, clanking armor, and ringing the Tulu-metal medallion raise jungle aggression and draw creatures to the source |
| **Rain breaks guns** | Matchlock and Flintlock misfire in rain — carry a Crossbow or Machete as a weatherproof backup |
| **Backstab first** | Most humanoid creatures can be one-shot with a Machete backstab from behind while crouched |
| **Light beats darkness** | Oil Lamp light weakens light-sensitive creatures and reveals ghostly figures |
| **Verify hallucinations** | At high madness, teammates render as monsters and the environment lies — confirm with voice before attacking anything |
| **Ox Wagon is your anchor** | Staying near the wagon reduces insanity; its white paint trail guides you back to extraction |
