# The Mound Blood Moon Guide — Why the Moon Turns Red, Enemies Swarm, and Going Quiet Won't Save You

You spend twenty minutes doing fine: silent backstabs, one kicked chest, a single musket shot at a Berserker you did not want to melee. Then the heartbeat starts, the moon turns red, and the forest vomits out a wave of enemies that keeps coming no matter how still you stand.

Community guides call this event a **Blood Moon** — the visible payoff of a hidden meter that fills as you make noise. The game never explains it, and the first thing most players try, going quiet and hiding, is exactly what does *not* work once it has begun.

Honesty note: **ACE Team has not published an official Blood Moon breakdown**, and the UI never prints the name. "Blood Moon" is the term the guide that covers it most directly uses; other sources describe the same red-moon event differently. Single-guide details are marked **[Unconfirmed]** below.

---

## What a Blood Moon Actually Is

The shortest description that fits every source: **noise is a resource, and when you spend too much of it, the forest escalates.** A ChapterCheats expedition hint calls it a "hidden danger meter" that "triggers a hostile wave of enemies once enough noise has accumulated." NoobFeed is more specific: *"Most routine Blood Moons in The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu build from the noise created during an expedition."*

So a Blood Moon is not random — it is the consequence of everything loud you have done since landing. Aggressive squads (firearms, sprinting, kicked chests) trigger it; stealthy ones rarely do.

Keep two things separate: **the noise meter** (your behavior, which you control) and **the Blood Moon event** (the consequence, which you cannot stop by being quiet once it fires).

Sources: [NoobFeed — How to Avoid Blood Moons](https://noobfeed.com/articles/the-mound-omen-of-cthulhu-blood-moons) (Editorial); [ChapterCheats — Managing Noise During Expeditions](https://www.chaptercheats.com/cheat/playstation5/702343/the-mound-omen-of-cthulhu/hint/219169) (Editorial).

## What Feeds the Meter

| Action | Impact | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Firearms (Matchlock, Flintlock, musket) | Very high | The single worst source; nearly every shot pushes toward red |
| Sprinting in chest armor | High | Armor rattles; unarmored sprint is quieter |
| Snapping stiff branches | Medium | Cut them with a melee weapon instead |
| Alarming red-eyed birds | Medium | Crouch past; use a bow if you must kill them |
| Making the ox bellow | High | Protect the wagon — a hit ox makes noise |
| Wagon horn / repeated Medallion | Medium | For deliberate attention, not routine use |
| Kicking locked chests | Medium | Pick them or accept the noise |

Gameplay.tips lists the same family of triggers as "forest aggression": frequent firearm use, excessive noise, breaking branches, sprinting in heavy armor, repeated Medallion use, and blowing the horn. The through-line: **anything loud, repeated enough, moves you toward a red moon.**

Sources: [NoobFeed](https://noobfeed.com/articles/the-mound-omen-of-cthulhu-blood-moons) and [Gameplay.tips — Essential Survival Tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/the-mound-omen-of-cthulhu-essential-survival-tips.html) (Editorial).

## Warning Signs — Before It Fires

The game warns you *before* the wave:

1. **Gray noise indicators** at the screen edges — lower-level noise building.
2. **Red indicators** — NoobFeed: *"Gray indicators show lower-level noise, while red indicators signal a major increase toward a Blood Moon."*
3. **Heartbeat + reddening moon** — *"A heartbeat warns that the Blood Moon threshold is close, and the moon begins turning red."*

Gameplay.tips matches the sky-tell: *"Red or purple moons generally indicate extremely dangerous conditions."*

When the heartbeat starts, **stop making noise and back off** — wait in a safe area until the warning fades. That can prevent the event entirely.

---

## The Rule Nobody Tells You: Quiet Won't Cancel It

Once a Blood Moon fully begins, reducing noise does not stop it — and this is why the guide exists.

- NoobFeed: you must *"defeat the spawned enemies or survive until the event ends."* Hiding and holding still will not despawn the wave.
- Higher-level contracts escalate the event: *"stronger waves, teleport the group to different locations, or introduce contract-specific creatures."* **[Unconfirmed — single guide for the teleport detail]**
- ChapterCheats frames the same consequence mechanically: accumulated noise triggers a hostile wave.

Flip your mental model at the moment the moon goes red: **before** it fires, noise discipline prevents it; **after** it fires, noise discipline is irrelevant — the fight is now mandatory.

Sources: [NoobFeed](https://noobfeed.com/articles/the-mound-omen-of-cthulhu-blood-moons) and [ChapterCheats](https://www.chaptercheats.com/cheat/playstation5/702343/the-mound-omen-of-cthulhu/hint/219169) (Editorial).

## Scripted Blood Moons

Some Blood Moons are not your fault. Certain objectives force the event regardless of prior noise. NoobFeed's example: picking up a **Tulu metal cylinder** starts a Blood Moon even if you have been silent — but securing the cylinder on the wagon ends that forced event.

If the moon turns red the instant you grab a scripted item, you did not slip up — finish the objective and the forced event resolves.

---

## How to Survive One

The goal changes from "avoid the forest" to "survive the forest":

1. **Fight as a group near the Ox Wagon** — your mobile safe zone and a choke point. Splitting up mid-wave invites Isolation ambushes on top of the spawns (see the [Isolation Guide](/guides/isolation-guide)).
2. **Kill the loud threats first** — firearm users and shrieking birds draw more enemies while they live.
3. **Use Black Ichor to buy space.** NoobFeed: a *"Jar of Black Ichor can distract supernatural creatures and open a quieter route."* **[Unconfirmed — single guide]**
4. **For scripted Blood Moons, complete the objective** rather than fighting everything.
5. **Don't over-commit.** Defeat the minimum and reposition; the event ends on its own. Wasting ammo on every spawn turns a survivable wave into a wipe.

After it ends, the noise meter has reset, but the expedition timer from the [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide) is still running.

## Blood Moon vs. the "Forest Awakens" Timer

Readers of this wiki will notice overlap with the timed escalation our [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide) and [Combat & Survival Guide](/guides/combat-survival-guide) call the **Forest Awakens** — the ~30–35 minute hidden timer that multiplies spawns and can relocate the extraction point.

The honest relationship: **editorial sources do not cleanly separate the two.** Gameplay.tips treats "the forest begins to awaken" and "the timer expires" as two triggers for the same continuous-spawn outcome. NoobFeed treats the Blood Moon as noise-driven and never mentions a timer. The game may have two escalation triggers (a noise meter and a mission timer) that overlap — or different guides may describe one system with two names. **We do not have an official answer.**

What you can rely on regardless of naming: noise discipline prevents the red-moon wave; extracting before ~30 minutes prevents the timed awakening. If the moon is red *and* you are past the half-hour mark, you are in the worst case — fight to the wagon and extract.

## Practical Rules

- **Watch the screen edges, not your crosshair.** Gray is a warning; red means "stop now."
- **A heartbeat and a reddening moon are the last exit.** Back off and wait it out — you can still prevent the wave here.
- **Once the wave spawns, quiet does nothing.** Fight or reposition; do not hide.
- **Group at the Ox Wagon before the wave lands.** A squad spread across the map invites Isolation on top of the spawns.

## Quick FAQ

**Q: Is the Blood Moon random?**
A: No — most build from accumulated noise; scripted objectives can force one regardless.

**Q: Can I cancel a Blood Moon by going quiet?**
A: No. Quiet prevents the *next* one. Once started, you must defeat the enemies or survive until it ends.

**Q: Why did the moon turn red when I picked up that cylinder?**
A: Some objectives are scripted to force a Blood Moon. Securing the item on the wagon ends it.

**Q: Is a Blood Moon the same as the "Forest Awakens" timer?**
A: Guides disagree. Treat both as "the forest escalating" and respect both triggers.
