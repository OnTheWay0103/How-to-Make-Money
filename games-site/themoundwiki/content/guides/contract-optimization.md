# The Mound Contract Optimization — Every Expedition Counts

**The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu / Updated: August 4, 2026**

The Contract Board on the Galleon (near the navigation table) is where every expedition starts — and most players pick contracts by vibes. This guide fixes that: which contract types pay what, the host-level scaling rule that silently ruins mixed-level squads, and a pick-order system that optimizes your time for money, rank, or materials.

---

## 1. Contract Basics

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Location | Contract Board on the Galleon, near the navigation table |
| Availability | Multiple contracts available per expedition cycle |
| Shared progress | Story progress is shared across the entire team |
| Personal rank | Each player keeps their own rank and unlocks |
| Risk vs reward | Higher difficulty = better loot and more progression |

Two rules shape everything else: **contract difficulty scales with the HOST's personal level** (not the team average), and **personal rank** — not team progress — unlocks your long-term rewards.

---

## 2. Contract Types and What They Actually Pay

| Contract Type | Difficulty | Time | Best Reward | Best For |
|---------------|-----------|------|-------------|----------|
| Reconnaissance | Low | 10-15 min | Rank XP, map knowledge | Learning routes, low-risk ranking |
| Salvage | Low-Medium | 15-20 min | Materials, coin | Farming crafting resources |
| Elimination | Medium | 20-30 min | Enemy drops, weapon materials | Combat practice, loot density |
| Escort | Medium-High | 25-35 min | High coin, unique gear | Money runs with a competent squad |
| Legendary Hunt | High | 40+ min | Legendary loot, exclusive materials | Endgame players — see the [Legendary Hunts Guide](/guides/legendary-hunts) |

**The optimization rule:** match contract type to your goal. Money → Escort. Materials → Salvage. Rank → Reconnaissance (fastest rank-per-minute). Trying to do all three in one contract type is how players waste evenings.

---

## 3. The Host-Scaling Trap (Mixed-Level Squads)

Difficulty is set by whoever hosts the lobby. This is the single most misread rule in The Mound:

| Situation | What Happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| High-level player hosts | ALL contracts scale up — even the "Easy" options |
| Low-level player hosts | Contracts scale to their level — genuinely easier maps |
| Mixed-level squad | Difficulty matches the host; brutal for the low-level player or trivial for the high-level one |
| Solo play | Difficulty always matches your own level — no surprises |

**Workarounds:**

| Workaround | How It Works | Best For |
|------------|--------------|----------|
| Let the newcomer host | Contracts scale to their level; whole squad gets an easier map | Bringing new players — the standard fix |
| Pick Reconnaissance or Easy Salvage | The most forgiving types at any difficulty bracket | Learning maps and mechanics |
| Newcomer picks the contract | If you must host, let them choose something comfortable | Quick sessions |
| Expect harder maps, bring more Salves | High-level host = harder contracts than labeled | Mixed squads where hosting is fixed |

## 4. The Optimization Loop (Per Session)

1. **Check the board for your goal.** Money night? Escort. Materials? Salvage. Rank push? Reconnaissance chain.
2. **Check the host.** If you're in a mixed-level squad, confirm who's hosting before anything else (Section 3).
3. **Plan the extraction from the contract.** Salvage contracts can be abandoned at the objective — but an early extraction forfeits the material bonus, so weigh it against the Forest Awakens timer (see the [Extraction Guide](/guides/extraction-guide)).
4. **Bank the rank.** Personal rank unlocks better board options over time — run the fastest contract you can clear cleanly, not the hardest you can survive.
5. **Stack with the daily rotation.** Daily challenges (see the [Daily Challenges Guide](/guides/daily-challenges)) often share objectives with board contracts — completing both in one expedition doubles your rewards per hour.

---

## 5. Common Contract Mistakes

1. **Always picking the hardest available contract.** Rank scales with *completions*, not difficulty — a clean Reconnaissance run outranks a failed Elimination.
2. **Ignoring host scaling with newcomers.** A high-level host quietly triples the difficulty for the new player — and then wonders why they died to a patrol.
3. **Abandoning Salvage before the objective.** The material bonus is the entire point of the contract type; the coin is secondary.
4. **Doing Escort without a plan.** Escort pays the best money but requires the most coordination — read the contract briefing before accepting, and confirm your squad has heals.
5. **Not stacking daily + contract objectives.** Free rewards per hour, wasted by most players.

---

## 6. Contract Pick Order — Quick Reference

| Your Goal | First Pick | Backup | Avoid |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-------|
| Money | Escort | Elimination | Reconnaissance (pays nothing) |
| Materials | Salvage | Elimination | Escort (loot-poor) |
| Rank | Reconnaissance | Salvage | Escort (slow per rank) |
| New player learning | Reconnaissance (low host) | Easy Salvage | Legendary Hunt |
| Veteran with fresh friends | Easy Salvage (newcomer hosts) | Reconnaissance | Anything with a high-level host |

**Final note:** the board also rotates availability each expedition cycle — if the ideal contract for your goal isn't up, the second pick beats waiting around on the Galleon. And when your rank is high enough to unlock them, Legendary Hunts become the best hour-per-reward content in the game — provided your squad treats them like raids, not pick-up runs.
