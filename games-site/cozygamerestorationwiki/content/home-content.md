# Cozy Game Restoration Wiki — Unofficial Guide Hub

**Welcome to the unofficial Cozy Game Restoration Wiki — your guide hub for the retro cartridge cleaning and restoration sim from Parasol Corp.**

Cozy Game Restoration launches on **November 4, 2026 on Windows and macOS via Steam** for **$14.99**. It has no timers and no fail states. You buy mystery parcels, unwrap them, clean and cosmetically restore the cartridges inside, and decide whether to keep them for your collection room or sell them to fund the next parcel.

---

## What is Cozy Game Restoration?

Cozy Game Restoration is a single-player restoration and collection game from Parasol Corp, the solo Melbourne studio of developer Adam Stormhardt. The premise is simple: forgotten retro cartridges arrive in sealed parcels, and you bring them back to a presentable condition with tweezers, cotton swabs, brushes, solvent, polish and a UV torch.

The game is deliberately pressure-free. There are no timers, no fail states, and no penalty for leaving a cartridge half-cleaned and returning later. Cleanliness affects resale value, but reaching 100% is optional.

The content scale is much larger than the demo suggests. The full version is described as having **108 fictional titles** inspired by games from the 1980s through the early 2000s, across **six fictional platforms**, each in **PAL and NTSC regional variants** — 216 cartridge variants in total. Rarity levels and shell types add further variation, and **around 300 collection-based quests** at launch reward decorations and cosmetics for a customizable collection room.

## Our Approach to Accuracy

Cozy Game Restoration is not out yet. That means a lot of the information circulating online is inference from the demo, previews and press releases. We label the difference.

- **Official** claims come from the Steam store page, the demo page and Parasol Corp press material.
- **Community** claims come from hands-on coverage of the demo and playtest.
- **Editorial** claims are our recommendations.

Where something is unknown — the names of the six fictional platforms, exact quest requirements, final system specifications — we say so instead of inventing a plausible-sounding answer. If you see a guide naming cartridge titles, quests or achievements that have no official source, be skeptical.
