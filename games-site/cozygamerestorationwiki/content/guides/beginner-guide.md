# Cozy Game Restoration Beginner Guide — Your First Restoration Day

Cozy Game Restoration is a quiet restoration simulator about rescuing forgotten retro game cartridges. You buy a sealed mystery parcel, open it by hand, clean and cosmetically restore the cartridge inside, then decide whether to keep it for your collection or sell it for the money to buy another parcel. There are no timers and no fail states. The pressure you feel is the pressure you bring with you.

That makes the first session deceptively open-ended. This guide gives you a simple plan: what to do first, what to ignore, and how to build habits that still work once the full 108-title collection opens up.

## What the Core Loop Actually Is

The loop described by the developer and shown in the demo has five steps:

1. **Earn or hold money.** Selling restored cartridges funds the next parcel.
2. **Buy a mystery parcel.** Parcels come sealed with tape. Their contents vary in title, platform, region, rarity and condition.
3. **Unwrap it.** You peel the tape with tweezers rather than pressing a single open button. It is a small physical ritual, and it sets the tone.
4. **Restore the cartridge.** Move it to the workbench and remove dust, grime, adhesive, stickers, ink, and surface marks with the available tools.
5. **Keep or sell.** A cleaner cartridge sells for more. A cartridge you keep goes into your collection and can be displayed in the collection room.

Everything else — quests, decorations, regional variants, crossover cartridges — hangs off that loop. If you understand the loop, you understand the game.

## Your First Session: A Calm Plan

### 1. Open one parcel and inspect before cleaning

Do not start scrubbing the moment the workbench loads. Rotate the cartridge and look at it. Where is the tape residue? Are there stickers, ink marks, scuffs, cracks, or discoloration? The demo's UV torch is specifically useful for finding dirt you have missed, and it is easier to plan a cleaning order when you know what is on the shell.

A simple inspection order:

- Tape and adhesive first
- Stickers and label glue second
- Loose dust and edge grime third
- Ink and stubborn marks fourth
- Scratches and polish last

Our full [cleaning guide](/guides/cleaning-guide) explains why this order works.

### 2. Clean your tools as you work

In the demo, tools get dirty. A dirty cotton swab or brush can smudge grime back onto the cartridge instead of removing it. Clean or swap tools between dirty areas, and especially before moving from adhesive or ink to a clean surface. This is the single most common cause of a cartridge looking worse after five minutes of work.

### 3. Do not chase 100% on your first cartridge

Cleaning does not have to reach 100%. The demo lets you leave a cartridge partially cleaned and return to it later. A dirtier cartridge sells for less, so there is a reason to do a good job, but there is no punishment for stopping. On your first day, aim for "clearly better than it arrived," not "perfect."

### 4. Decide keep or sell with a rule

A simple beginner rule:

- **Keep** the first copy of a title, any regional variant you do not own, any high-rarity shell, and any crossover cartridge.
- **Sell** duplicates and commons once you have a cleaner or more useful copy.

This keeps money moving without gutting the collection you will need for collection-based quests. Selling everything immediately is the fastest way to afford parcels and the slowest way to finish a collection.

### 5. Spend your first profits on parcels, not cosmetics

Decorations are a real part of the game and quests reward them, but they do not improve your ability to restore cartridges. Early money is better spent on more parcels: more parcels mean more titles, more variants, and more chances to find the specific cartridge a quest is asking for. Decorate once you have a shelf worth decorating.

## Understanding Condition and Value

The game's economy is built on a simple relationship: **cleanliness affects resale value**. A cartridge that is only partly clean is not a failure state; it is a lower offer. That has two consequences.

First, cleaning has an economic ceiling that is worth reaching on cartridges you intend to sell. If you are going to sell, finish the job — use the UV torch to check for missed specks, clean your tools, and do a final pass.

Second, cleaning has no economic floor. You can sell a rough cartridge immediately and take the lower price if you need the money for a parcel you want. Nothing breaks. Nothing expires.

The market adds a second layer: previews report that selling prices fluctuate daily, and that the genres found in parcels can vary. That means holding a finished cartridge for a better day is a legitimate strategy, especially for high-rarity or crossover items. It also means you should not assume today's price is the cartridge's permanent value.

## Habits That Scale to the Full Game

The demo is a slice. The full game is described as 108 fictional titles across six fictional platforms, each in PAL and NTSC regional versions — 216 cartridge variants — plus rarity and shell variation and around 300 collection-based quests at launch. The habits that will serve you in that larger collection are not complicated:

- **Keep a broad first copy of every title you can.** Breadth is what collection quests reward.
- **Treat regional pairs as a set.** If you find a PAL copy, an NTSC copy is a natural next target.
- **Do not sell your last copy of anything.** Duplicates are sellable; the only copy of a title is collection progress.
- **Check the UV torch before selling.** It is the difference between a good price and a mediocre one.
- **Leave hard cartridges and come back.** No timer means no reason to force a result.

## What Not to Worry About

New players often look for systems that are not there. Based on official material and the demo, you do not need to worry about:

- **A timer.** There is none.
- **A fail state.** There is none.
- **A wrong cleaning order that ruins the cartridge.** The game is not built around irreversible punishment; you can return to a cartridge later.
- **Missing a limited-time event.** No such system has been announced.
- **Combat, character builds, or power tiers.** This is a restoration and collection game.

If a guide or video tells you a cartridge can be permanently ruined by cleaning it in the wrong order, treat that claim skeptically until it is documented in the full release.

## A Good First-Day Goal

If you do nothing else on your first day, do this: open three parcels, take one cartridge from "rough" to "clearly improved," sell one duplicate, and keep one title you like. That gives you a feel for the tools, the value of cleanliness, and the keep/sell decision without turning a cozy game into a grind.

The collection room can wait. The parcels will still be there tomorrow, and so will the cartridges you did not finish.
