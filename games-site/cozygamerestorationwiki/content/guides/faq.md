# Cozy Game Restoration FAQ — Platforms, Requirements, Timers & Steam Deck

This page collects the questions players ask most often about Cozy Game Restoration, with a clear separation between confirmed official information and community-reported details. If a question has no confirmed answer yet, we say so rather than guessing.

## Release and Purchase

### When does Cozy Game Restoration release?

November 4, 2026, on Steam. The game was originally announced for September 24, 2026 and was later delayed to November 4. Any page still listing September 24 is out of date.

### How much does it cost?

$14.99 USD. Regional pricing varies by Steam store region.

### Who develops and publishes it?

Parasol Corp, the solo Melbourne-based studio of developer Adam Stormhardt. Parasol Corp is both developer and publisher.

### What platforms is it on?

Windows and macOS via Steam. The store page lists Steam Deck optimization, and the developer has described the game as built for desktop and Steam Deck. No PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo Switch versions have been announced.

### How many languages does it support?

The Steam store page lists 19 supported languages. Interface and subtitle support can differ by language, so check the language table on the store page for the specific language you need.

### Is there multiplayer or online play?

No. Cozy Game Restoration is a single-player game.

## Gameplay and Design

### Is there a timer?

No. The developer states there are no timers.

### Is there a fail state?

No. There are no fail states. You can leave a cartridge partially cleaned, return to it later, and work at your own pace.

### Do I have to clean a cartridge to 100%?

No. Cleaning does not have to reach 100%. A dirtier cartridge sells for less, so there is an economic incentive to clean well, but nothing forces you to finish. The UV torch helps you find missed dirt if you want a proper final pass.

### What happens if I stop in the middle of a restoration?

You can return to the cartridge later. The game is designed around short, interruptible sessions rather than a session-long commitment.

### Is there combat, character progression or a power tier list?

No. Cozy Game Restoration is a cleaning and collection game. There are no character builds, combat stats or power tiers. Any "tier list" for this game is an editorial workflow suggestion, not official data.

### How many games are in the full version?

108 fictional titles inspired by games from the 1980s through the early 2000s. They span six fictional platforms, and each title has PAL and NTSC regional versions — 216 cartridge variants in total. Rarity and shell type add further variation.

### How many quests are there?

Around 300 quests at launch. They are collection-based objectives that reward decorations and cosmetics for the collection room, rather than story missions with fail states.

### Are there customer orders or commissions?

No such system has been confirmed. Official material describes collection-based quests, and hands-on coverage of the demo reports no customer-order or commission system. Treat claims about named customers and delivery deadlines as unverified.

### Are there crossovers?

Yes. Crossover cartridges based on real indie games have been confirmed, including Abiotic Factor and CONSCRIPT, with more to be announced. They appear as collectible cartridges within the game, not as separate modes.

### Is the game content-complete at launch?

The developer describes around 300 quests "at launch," which suggests the launch build is intended to be substantial. More content and crossovers may be added later, but no post-launch roadmap has been detailed in the material we reviewed.

## Demo and Playtest

### Is there a demo?

Yes. A public demo is available on Steam. The demo store page describes it as limited to four in-game days of parcels.

### How big is the demo?

It is a slice of the loop, not a shortened campaign. It includes parcel opening, cleaning, the confirmed tool set, selling and a limited set of cartridges. It does not include the full 108-title collection, the complete quest structure or full collection room progression.

### What was the playtest?

An earlier, limited invited build used before the public demo. Parasol Corp reported 149,745 playtest sign-ups in May 2026. It is not the version new players should look for now.

### Does demo progress carry over to the full game?

No carry-over has been announced. Do not assume your demo save will transfer. Treat the demo as a trial.

### What are the macOS requirements?

Community hardware databases list macOS support for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, with a relatively small storage footprint. macOS specifications are less consistently documented than Windows specifications. Check the Steam store page for the current requirement block.

### Does it run on Steam Deck?

Yes. The game is described as built for desktop and Steam Deck, and the store page lists Steam Deck optimization. Controller support is available.

### Is a mouse required?

A mouse with two buttons and a scroll wheel is commonly listed as required for camera control. Previews have found a mouse more precise than an analog stick for cleaning, and a graphics tablet has been suggested as an even better option by at least one hands-on writer. A controller works, but fine cleaning is easier with a pointing device.

### How long is the game?

No official playtime estimate has been published. The full game's scale — 108 titles, 216 regional variants, around 300 collection quests and a collection room — suggests a long-tail collection game rather than a short narrative. We will update this with verified playtime after launch.

## Troubleshooting Expectations

### Can a cartridge be permanently ruined?

No permanent-ruin mechanic has been confirmed. The game has no fail states, and you can return to a restoration later. Claims that a specific cleaning order irreversibly damages a cartridge should be treated as unverified until documented in the full release.

### Does the game simulate save batteries or electrical repair?

No. The demo and official material describe cosmetic cleaning and restoration. Real-world battery replacement, soldering, contact repair and read-failure diagnostics are not confirmed game systems. Our [cartridge battery guide](/guides/cartridge-battery-guide) separates the real-world knowledge from the game mechanics.

### Why does my cartridge still show specks after cleaning?

Small specks can remain even at high cleanliness, and the UV torch is the tool for finding them. Clean your tools before the final pass; a dirty swab or brush can smudge dirt back onto the surface. If a mark persists, it may be a scratch or discoloration rather than dirt.
