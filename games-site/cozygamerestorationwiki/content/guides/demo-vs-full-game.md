# Cozy Game Restoration Demo vs Full Game — Differences, Release Date & Price

Cozy Game Restoration has had three public-facing versions: an earlier **playtest**, a **Steam Next Fest demo**, and the upcoming **full release**. They are not the same build, and the differences matter if you are deciding whether to play now or wait.

Here is the confirmed comparison as of October 24, 2026.

## The Short Version

| Version | Status | What it is |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Playtest | Closed, earlier 2026 | A limited invited build used to test the cleaning loop |
| Demo | Available on Steam | A public slice limited to four in-game days of parcels |
| Full game | Launches November 4, 2026 | The complete 108-title collection, full quest set and collection room progression |

The full game is the only version that includes the complete collection. The demo is a taste of the loop, not a shortened campaign.

## Release Date and Price

| Detail | Confirmed information |
|--------|-----------------------|
| Release date | November 4, 2026 |
| Previous date | September 24, 2026 (delayed) |
| Price | $14.99 USD |
| Platforms | Windows and macOS via Steam |
| Steam Deck | Optimized; described as built for desktop and Steam Deck |
| Languages | 19 supported languages |
| Developer / publisher | Parasol Corp (solo developer Adam Stormhardt, Melbourne) |
| Multiplayer | None; single-player |

### About the delay

The game was originally announced for September 24, 2026. It was later moved to November 4, 2026. If you see a page still listing September 24, it is out of date. The November date is the current release window.

### About the price

$14.99 is the listed price. Regional pricing will vary by store region, as with any Steam release. The price is low enough that the demo is a reasonable way to decide whether the loop clicks for you before launch.

## What the Demo Includes

The Steam demo is a public slice of the full loop. The demo store page describes it as **limited to four in-game days of parcels**. Within that limit, the demo includes:

- Buying and opening mystery parcels
- Peeling tape and stickers with tweezers
- Cleaning cartridges at the workbench
- Using the confirmed tool set, including the UV torch
- Selling restored cartridges
- A limited set of titles and variants
- The basic keep/sell decision

The demo does **not** represent:

- The full 108-title collection
- The full six-platform spread
- The complete 300-quest structure
- The full collection room progression
- Late-game rarity or shell variety

Hands-on coverage of the demo reports eight tools (seven clearly identified), tool-cleaning upkeep, daily market fluctuation, and the ability to leave a cartridge and return later. Those mechanics are a good indication of the full game's feel, but the content scale is not.

### Demo scope caveat

Different builds have been described with different content counts in previews. The reliable official statement is the four-in-game-days limit on the Steam demo page. We are not going to assert a specific number of demo titles, because the public coverage is inconsistent on that point.

## What the Playtest Was

The playtest was an earlier, limited build used before the public demo. It was not the full game, and it is no longer the relevant version for new players. If you missed it, you did not miss a permanent opportunity; the demo and the full release are the versions that matter now.

Parasol Corp reported **149,745 playtest sign-ups** during the May 2026 playtest period. That figure is a measure of interest, not of content.

## What the Full Game Adds

The full release is described as including:

- **108 fictional titles** inspired by games from the 1980s through the early 2000s
- **Six fictional platforms**
- **PAL and NTSC regional variants** — 216 cartridge variants in total
- **Multiple rarity levels**, including rare and special shells
- **Around 300 quests at launch**, collection-based, rewarding decorations and cosmetics
- **The full collection room**, with display shelves and decorations
- **Crossover cartridges**, including Abiotic Factor and CONSCRIPT, with more to be revealed
- **19 languages**
- **No timers and no fail states**

That is the content scale the demo is sampling. If the demo feels small, that is by design; if the loop feels good, the full game is the same loop with a much larger collection.

## Interest and Milestones

The game's pre-launch numbers give a sense of the audience:

- **400,000+ Steam wishlists** as of October 2026
- **149,745 playtest sign-ups** in May 2026
- **171,679 demo downloads** during the June 2026 Steam Next Fest
- **PAX Aus 2026** showcase

These are popularity signals, not indicators of review quality. They do explain why the game has attracted guide coverage before release.

## Should You Play the Demo First?

**Play the demo if:**

- You are unsure whether the cleaning loop is satisfying.
- You want to test mouse versus controller precision.
- You want to see whether the no-timer design feels relaxing or aimless.
- You are sensitive to repetition and want to check the tool variety.

**Wait for the full game if:**

- You already know you enjoy cleaning and restoration games.
- You want the complete collection and quest progression from the start.
- You do not want to replay the opening loop twice.

### Save transfer

No save-transfer or carry-over from the demo to the full game has been announced. Do not assume demo progress will persist. Treat the demo as a trial, not as the first chapter of a save file.

## The Bottom Line

The demo is a four-in-game-day slice of a much larger collection game. The full version arrives November 4, 2026 for $14.99 with 108 titles, six platforms, PAL/NTSC variants, around 300 quests and a collection room. If you like the loop in the demo, the full game is the same loop with a substantially bigger shelf. If you are unsure, the demo is exactly the right size to find out.
