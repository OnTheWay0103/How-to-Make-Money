## What is BOMBANANA!?

BOMBANANA! is a **chaotic, explosively funny co-op multiplayer game for three**, where each player has only part of the information. A blind monkey (🙈) who cannot see colors or read screens, a deaf monkey (🙉) who cannot hear, and a mute monkey (🙊) who cannot speak must work together to defuse a bomb — or everyone goes BOOM!

Communication is the whole game: pointing, waving, gesturing, flailing wildly, the emote wheel, and "slapping — obviously an essential feature." Some players can talk in voice chat, some cannot, and every player sees, hears, and acts differently.

Developed by **Lefto Studio** and published by **TARK**, the game features 30 handcrafted campaign levels, an Endless procedurally generated mode, a Custom / Free Mode bomb editor, puzzle modules with different rules, a level system, built-in voice chat, and 15 languages.

## Key Systems at a Glance

- **Three Roles** — Blind (🙈 touches the bomb, can't see colors/screens), Deaf (🙉 sees and speaks, can't hear), Mute (🙊 reads the manual, can't speak).
- **Information Triangle** — Mute → Deaf → Blind: one-way information chain, with the Deaf Monkey as the bottleneck.
- **Puzzle Modules** — Wires, D-pad, Numpad/Math, Switch Panels, and Braille panels confirmed from the demo; the full release may add more [Unconfirmed].
- **Campaign Mode** — 30 handcrafted levels with increasing difficulty.
- **Endless Mode** — Procedurally generated, wave-based levels that keep getting harder.
- **Custom / Free Mode** — Adjust bomb difficulty, timer, module types, environmental hazards, and mistakes allowed.
- **Environmental Hazards** — Power outages, obstructions, and loud interruptions.
- **Level System** — Defuse the bomb to unlock new levels; the better you get, the harder they are.
- **Color Blind Mode** — Adapted for two types of color blindness.
