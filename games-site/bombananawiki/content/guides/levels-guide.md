# BOMBANANA! Levels & Progression

BOMBANANA! uses its levels as a **difficulty ladder and a progression currency in one**. The official rule is simple: **"Defuse the bomb to unlock new levels. The better you get, the harder they are."** [Official]

If your team has searched "how to play BOMBANANA level 2" — this guide is for you.

## The Core Progression Loop

1. **Play a level.** A bomb with a set of modules and a timer starts.
2. **Defuse it** to **unlock the next level**.
3. **Fail, and you retry** — the level does not get easier on its own; your team gets better.
4. **As you climb, the bombs get harder** — more modules, trickier combinations, tighter timers, and environmental hazards [Official].

The game does not hand you a difficulty menu — it gates difficulty behind **successful defusals**. That is the whole "the better you get, the harder they are" loop [Official].

## The Three Modes and Their Progression

| Mode | Progression style |
|------|--------------------|
| **Campaign** | 30 handcrafted levels with **increasing difficulty** [Official]. |
| **Endless** | Procedurally generated, wave-based levels that **keep getting harder** — no fixed end [Official]. |
| **Custom / Free Mode** | No built-in ladder — you set the difficulty dials yourself [Official]. |

### Campaign (30 levels)
The Campaign is the designed experience: **30 handcrafted levels** that introduce modules and hazards in a curated order [Official]. Expect each level to teach or combine mechanics before the timer gets nasty. After launch we will map each level's modules, hazards, and timers here [Editorial].

### Endless
Endless mode strips away the handcrafted pacing and generates **wave-based levels procedurally** that keep escalating [Official]. This is where the "how long can your team survive" question lives. Endless is the mode for teams that outgrow the Campaign.

### Custom / Free Mode
Free Mode has no level ladder — you build the bomb yourself, choosing difficulty, timer, module types, hazards, and mistake limits [Official]. It is the training ground for the levels you find in Campaign and Endless. See the [Free Mode guide](/guides/free-mode-guide).

## What Makes Levels Harder?

Based on the confirmed systems, difficulty in BOMBANANA! comes from stacking these factors [Official]:

- **More modules per bomb** — more information to relay, more manual pages to open.
- **Harder module types** — numpad/math and switch panels are harder than wires [Community].
- **Tighter timers** — less time to run the information triangle.
- **Environmental hazards** — power outages, obstructions, and loud interruptions disrupt communication mid-bomb [Official].
- **Fewer mistakes allowed** — some levels or settings punish wrong inputs more harshly.

## Tips for Climbing the Levels

1. **Master wires first.** Wires are the fastest module and the base of most early bombs. Solve them quickly to save timer for the hard stuff [Community].
2. **Standardize your callouts.** The same callout order every time (module name → position → details → manual answer → repeat-back → confirm → act) keeps the team fast as modules stack [Community].
3. **Learn the Mute Monkey role early.** The manual-reading role is the bottleneck on harder levels; if no one can play it well, every level past the early ones is a slog [Community].
4. **Use Free Mode between Campaign levels.** Rehearse a specific module or hazard in Free Mode before it appears in a handcrafted level [Editorial].
5. **Retry deliberately.** Failures are information: if a level keeps ending on switch panels, practice switch panels until the callout is muscle memory.

## What to Expect as Levels Ramp

With the confirmed systems in mind, a level's difficulty is built from the same few dials the Free Mode editor exposes: module count and type, timer, hazards, and mistake limits [Official]. Expect early levels to feel like a guided tutorial and late levels to combine everything [Editorial]:

- **Early Campaign levels** will likely lean on wires and D-pad — the two simplest modules — with forgiving timers [Community].
- **Mid Campaign levels** introduce numpad/math and switch panels, where the manual branching and the go-back gesture start to matter [Community].
- **Late Campaign and Endless** layer in environmental hazards (power outages, obstructions, loud interruptions) on top of hard module combos and short timers [Official].

The exact per-level mix is not published, so this is a framework, not a table — we will map each of the 30 levels after launch [Unconfirmed].

## Session Structure: What One Play Session Looks Like

A typical BOMBANANA! session, based on the confirmed modes and demo pacing [Editorial]:

1. **Warm up** with a Free Mode bomb you know your team can solve.
2. **Push the Campaign** a few levels — defusing unlocks the next, failing means retrying [Official].
3. **Cap it with an Endless run** to see how far the procedural difficulty ramp takes you before the team taps out [Official].

This structure mixes the handcrafted Campaign progression with the endless survival test and the Free Mode sandbox — the three confirmed modes [Official].
