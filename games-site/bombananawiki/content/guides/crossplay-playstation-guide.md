# BOMBANANA! Crossplay Explained

BOMBANANA! became a much bigger conversation on October 22, 2026, when the free demo launched on **PlayStation**. That immediately raised the question every three-player co-op game faces: **can PlayStation players join Steam players?**

The short answer is no. The official announcement says **crossplay is not currently available**, though it adds that crossplay **may be considered** in the future. That single sentence matters more than any store-page tag, because BOMBANANA! is a game built on exactly three players and shared voice communication — a split player base is a real practical problem.

This guide separates what is confirmed from what is still unknown, so you can plan a trio without buying the wrong version.

## The Short Answer

- **Crossplay is not currently available** [Official].
- **Steam PC and Mac players share lobbies** because they are on the same Steam ecosystem [Community].
- **PlayStation has a demo**, but crossplay between PlayStation and Steam is not confirmed or available [Official].
- **Xbox is planned**, with no release date announced [Community/Media].
- **Mobile is not an official platform**, despite some third-party pages claiming otherwise [Community]. Treat mobile claims as [Unconfirmed].

If your friends are split between PlayStation and PC, you cannot currently play together. The only confirmed way to play as a trio is for all three players to be on the same platform.

The Steam store page lists **Online Co-op**, **Steam Achievements**, and **Family Sharing**, and the game requires **exactly three players** — no solo mode and no two-player mode [Official]. Those constraints apply on every platform, which is why crossplay matters so much: a platform with a small player pool can struggle to fill a three-person lobby.

## What the PlayStation Demo Changed

The October 22 announcement confirmed four things [Official]:

1. **The demo is live on PlayStation.** It is the same free demo concept as the Steam version.
2. **The demo includes Color Blind Mode.** The store page lists color-blind support, and the PlayStation post explicitly mentions it.
3. **The team is asking for feedback from PlayStation players.** This is a demo feedback push, not a full launch.
4. **Crossplay is not currently available**, but may be considered.

The post does **not** say whether the PlayStation demo supports online multiplayer, whether it requires PlayStation Plus, or whether it includes the full three-role set. Those details are [Unconfirmed]. If you are planning a PlayStation trio, test the demo with your group before assuming online play works exactly like Steam.

The demo is also not a progress carry-over: demo saves do not transfer to the full release [Community]. Treat it as a trial, not a head start.

## Why Crossplay Is Not Available

The official wording is careful: **"not currently available"** and **"may be considered."** That is not a promise, and it is not a technical explanation.

[Editorial] The practical reason is that BOMBANANA! launched as a Steam-first game with Steam lobbies, Steam invites, and Steam voice chat. Adding PlayStation means building a separate lobby and account layer, then bridging the two. The October 22 post frames the PlayStation demo as a feedback step, which suggests the team is still deciding how much platform infrastructure to build.

The game's design makes crossplay more valuable than it is in a typical co-op title. Because the Blind, Mute, and Deaf monkeys each hold different information, a trio usually wants to play together repeatedly. If one friend is on console and two are on PC, the group cannot form at all. That creates pressure for crossplay, but it does not guarantee it will happen.

## How to Play With Friends Today

The confirmed route is to keep all three players on the **same platform** [Official][Community].

### On Steam (PC and Mac)

1. One player hosts and creates a room.
2. The host invites two Steam friends, or enables public matchmaking to fill the third slot.
3. All three pick a role — Blind, Mute, or Deaf — and ready up.
4. Use the in-game voice chat or an external call. The Mute Monkey cannot speak in-game, so many teams run Discord alongside the gesture system.

Patch **v1.0.2** added **matchmaking** and improved level balancing, controller UI, and lobby-code visibility [Official]. If you are playing with strangers rather than friends, the [Co-op Guide](/guides/co-op-guide) covers the invite flow, lobby codes, and troubleshooting.

### On PlayStation

The demo is live, but the exact multiplayer flow is not documented in the announcement [Unconfirmed]. Start by launching the demo and looking for an online co-op option. If the demo is local-only or limited, that will be clear before you buy anything.

### Mixed groups

There is no confirmed workaround for mixed Steam/PlayStation groups. The only reliable options are [Editorial]:

- **Everyone plays on Steam.** This is the only fully documented trio setup.
- **Everyone plays on PlayStation.** Possible if the demo supports online play, but unconfirmed for the full game.
- **Wait for crossplay.** The official statement leaves the door open but gives no timeline.

Do not buy a second copy on another platform expecting crossplay to arrive on a specific date. There is no announced date.

## What About Xbox and Mobile?

### Xbox

A Russian-language report from October 17, 2026 says an Xbox release is planned, with a new trailer and no announced date [Community/Media]. That is consistent with the pattern: BOMBANANA! is expanding from Steam to consoles gradually. Crossplay for Xbox has not been discussed [Unconfirmed].

### Mobile

Some third-party pages claim BOMBANANA! is available on mobile. The official Steam page does not list an Android or iOS version, and community platform tracking says there is **no official mobile build** [Community]. Be careful with APK and browser-clone sites: they are not the official game and may be unsafe.

The safest statement as of October 23, 2026 is: **PC/Mac via Steam is the only confirmed full-release platform; PlayStation has a demo; Xbox is planned; mobile is unconfirmed.**

## What Not to Do

[Editorial] A few avoidable mistakes:

- **Do not assume "Online Co-op" means crossplay.** It does not. Online co-op means players on the same platform ecosystem can play together.
- **Do not buy a PlayStation copy for a Steam group.** The versions cannot currently share a lobby.
- **Do not plan a two-player run and expect a random fill.** The game requires exactly three players; matchmaking can fill the slot on Steam, but the third player is a stranger with their own communication style.
- **Do not trust "BOMBANANA APK" downloads.** There is no official mobile version.
