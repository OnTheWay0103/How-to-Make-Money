# BOMBANANA! FAQ

The questions every team asks before launch — solo play, mobile, Mac, game size, and game length. Answers are honestly labeled: things not yet officially confirmed carry **[Unconfirmed]**.

## Can I play BOMBANANA! solo?

**No.** BOMBANANA! is strictly **3-player co-op**. The official store description says: *"3-Player Co-op — No more, no less. Exactly three monkeys required."* There is no solo mode and no two-player mode [Official]. Every player holds only part of the information, so a solo experience is not possible by design.

## Is there a mobile version (Android / iOS / APK)?

**No.** BOMBANANA! is **PC-only** via Steam. There is no Android, iOS, or APK version [Official]. If you see "BOMBANANA APK download" results, treat them as unofficial and likely unsafe — the game is not on mobile.

## Does BOMBANANA! work on Mac?

**Yes.** The Steam store page lists macOS 11.0 (Big Sur) or later, with minimum requirements of an Apple M1 or Intel Core i3 (6th gen or later) and 4 GB RAM [Official]. It runs on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs meeting the minimums.

## What are the system requirements?

Requirements are modest. Windows minimum: Windows 10/11 64-bit, Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD Ryzen 3 1200, 8 GB RAM, Intel HD Graphics 520 / AMD Radeon Vega 3, ~300 MB storage. macOS minimum: macOS 11.0+, Apple M1 or Intel Core i3 (6th gen+), 4 GB RAM, ~600 MB storage. Full tables are in the [price & platforms guide](/guides/price-platforms-faq) [Official].

## How big is the game?

Around **300 MB** on Windows and **600 MB** on macOS according to the store page [Official]. Very lightweight.

## How long is BOMBANANA!?

There is no published playtime figure [Unconfirmed]. What is known:

- **Campaign:** 30 handcrafted levels with increasing difficulty [Official].
- **Endless:** Procedurally generated, wave-based levels that keep getting harder — effectively unlimited [Official].
- **Custom / Free Mode:** You design the bombs; playtime is whatever you make of it [Official].

Because the game is about communication skill rather than story content, total playtime depends on how long your team can survive the escalating bombs [Editorial]. A single Campaign level can take anywhere from a few minutes (quick wires bomb) to much longer on the harder modules [Community].

## How much does BOMBANANA! cost?

**Not announced as of September 1, 2026** [Unconfirmed]. The game is not available for pre-order, and the store page lists no price. A launch price in the **$10–20 range** is a reasonable editorial expectation based on comparable indie co-op puzzle titles, but the exact price is unconfirmed. Check the Steam page on September 2 [Editorial].

## When does BOMBANANA! release?

**September 2, 2026** on Steam [Official].

## Is there a demo?

**Yes.** A free demo is available on Steam (App 4747510), played by more than 6 million players. It teaches the three roles and the core modules (wires, D-pad, numpad, switch panels). See the [demo guide](/guides/demo-guide).

## Can I play with two players?

**No.** Exactly three players are required [Official]. Some community reports describe an AI filling the third role, but this is not confirmed and is a poor substitute [Unconfirmed].

## Does BOMBANANA! have voice chat?

**Yes**, built-in voice chat — but it is **role-limited**: "some of you can talk, some of you can't" [Official]. The Mute Monkey cannot speak, so teams combine voice with gestures and the emote wheel. Many teams also run external voice chat alongside the in-game tools [Community].

## What is the emote wheel?

The emote wheel is one of the non-verbal communication tools the Mute Monkey uses. Alongside pointing, waving, gesturing, and "flailing wildly," the store page lists an emote wheel as part of the communication kit [Official]. Exact emote options are not yet documented [Unconfirmed].

## Does BOMBANANA! support color-blind players?

**Yes.** The store page confirms a Color Blind Mode: colors across objects and the UI are adapted for **two types of color blindness** [Official]. This matters — the Blind Monkey cannot see colors, but the color-blind setting helps the players who can.

## What languages does BOMBANANA! support?

**15 languages** with interface, full audio, and subtitles: English, Turkish, French, German, Spanish (Spain), Simplified Chinese, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Russian, Polish, Italian, Spanish (Latin America), Traditional Chinese, and Ukrainian [Official].

## Is BOMBANANA! suitable for kids?

The developer states the game is **non-violent, stylized bomb-defusal gameplay focused on cooperative puzzle-solving**, with no adult content [Official]. It is family-friendly and comedic.
