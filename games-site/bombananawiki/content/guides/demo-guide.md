# BOMBANANA! Demo Guide

The **BOMBANANA! demo** is the free way to try the game before the September 2, 2026 release. It was one of the breakout hits of the **June 2026 Steam Next Fest** — the festival's most popular demo — and has been played by **more than 6 million players**, making it one of the most-played demos on Steam [Official].

If you are on the fence about the full game, the demo answers most of your questions.

## How to Get the Demo

- Search **BOMBANANA! Demo** on Steam (App 4747510) and click **Play Game** / **Add to Library**.
- It is **free** — no purchase needed.
- It supports **online co-op with exactly three players**, just like the full game. You can play it with two friends who also have the demo installed.
- Demo requirements are lighter than the full game; any machine that can run the full game can run the demo [Community].

## What the Demo Teaches

The demo is structured as an **onboarding ramp** for the three-role system [Community]:

| Area | What the demo covers |
|------|----------------------|
| **The three roles** | Blind, Deaf, and Mute monkeys — what each can and cannot do, and the information triangle. |
| **Wires module** | The starter module — wire counts, colors, and the cut decision. |
| **D-pad module** | Directional pad with a centre value. |
| **Numpad / Math module** | The hardest common module — math expressions, jumbled keypad, manual branching. |
| **Switch Panels** | The multi-state switch module with the enter-button payoff. |
| **Callout discipline** | The universal callout order (module name → position → details → manual answer → repeat-back → confirm → act). |

By the end of the demo, you should understand **why the Mute Monkey gestures, why the Deaf Monkey relays, and why the Blind Monkey repeats everything back** — the entire communication system the full game is built around.

## What the Demo Does NOT Tell You

The demo guide from whisperofthehouse is explicit about the demo's limits [Community]:

- **It is not the full manual.** Do not treat demo tables as the complete final-release manual. The demo warns against overclaiming exact table answers.
- **It is not the full module list.** The demo teaches several module types, but the full release may include more modules.
- **It is not full level progression.** Demo levels are a slice; the full game's Campaign has 30 handcrafted levels plus an Endless mode [Official].

In other words: the demo is a perfect **role-and-communication teacher** but an incomplete **reference**.

## Practical Demo Tips

1. **Do not skip the gesture agreement.** The demo teaches the roles, but you still need to agree on yes/no/wait gestures before your first bomb. See the [roles guide](/guides/roles-guide).
2. **Play all three roles.** Rotate so each of you tries the Mute Monkey at least once — it is the hardest role and the demo is the safe place to learn it.
3. **Solve wires first, always.** Wires are the fastest module and build confidence for the harder numpad and switch panels [Community].
4. **Repeat-back every instruction.** Even in the demo, form the habit: the Blind repeats, the Deaf confirms, then the Blind acts.
5. **Use the demo to check performance.** If the demo runs smoothly on your machine, the full game will too — the full-game requirements are modest (8 GB RAM minimum on Windows) [Official].

## Why 6 Million Players Tried It

The demo's success is a useful signal for what the full game is going to feel like. BOMBANANA! won the **June 2026 Steam Next Fest** demo race and went on to become one of the most-played demos on Steam [Official]. That kind of demo uptake usually tracks three things that carry into the full release [Editorial]:

1. **A watchable premise.** Three monkeys, three missing senses, and a bomb is instantly understandable — and instantly funny when it goes wrong.
2. **A strong party-co-op loop.** The "one part of the information each" design creates natural moments of triumph and catastrophe that teams want to replay with different friends.
3. **Accessibility.** A free demo with modest requirements (8 GB RAM minimum on Windows) means nearly any Steam player's machine can run it [Official].

For your team, the lesson is practical: the demo is a proven on-ramp, so use it to recruit your two teammates before launch. If they have not played BOMBANANA! at all, the demo is a better introduction than a video.

## A Suggested Demo Practice Plan

If you want to be genuinely good at launch, use the demo deliberately rather than casually [Editorial]:

| Session | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| **Session 1** | Learn the roles. Each player plays all three roles once. Focus on the information triangle, not speed. |
| **Session 2** | Master wires and the universal callout order. Every call starts with module name and position. |
| **Session 3** | Add numpad/math and switch panels. Practice the go-back gesture for switch corrections. |
| **Session 4** | Speed run. Tighten callouts and repeat-backs until the team sounds "boring and robotic" [Community]. |

The demo's structure — a slice of levels teaching wires, D-pad, numpad, and switch panels — maps directly onto this plan [Community].
