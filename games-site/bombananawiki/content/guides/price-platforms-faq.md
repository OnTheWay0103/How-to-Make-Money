# BOMBANANA! Price, Platforms & Release FAQ

Everything about **when, where, and how to play BOMBANANA!** at launch — honestly labeled. The price has not been officially announced yet, so the pricing section is marked [Unconfirmed].

## Release Date

BOMBANANA! releases on **September 2, 2026** on Steam [Official]. The game is currently not yet available on the store page and has no pre-order. A free demo has been available on Steam for some time and remains the best way to try it before launch.

## Price

**Not officially announced as of September 1, 2026.** [Unconfirmed]

- The game is **not available for pre-order**, and the store page does not list a price.
- Only a **Standard Edition** has been mentioned in coverage; no Deluxe or Ultimate editions have been announced [Community].
- Based on comparable indie co-op puzzle titles, a launch price in the **$10–20 USD / €10–20** range is a reasonable expectation — but this is editorial and not confirmed [Editorial].

**Check the Steam store page on September 2 for the confirmed price.** We will update this guide as soon as the price is public.

Crossplay is **not currently available** — see the [BOMBANANA Crossplay Guide](/guides/crossplay-playstation-guide) for the platform-by-platform breakdown.

## Can You Play Solo?

**No.** The game is strictly **3-player co-op** — the official store description says "3-Player Co-op — No more, no less. Exactly three monkeys required." There is no solo mode and no two-player mode [Official].

## Game Size

Around **300 MB** on Windows and **600 MB** on macOS according to the store page [Official]. Very lightweight — easy to fit on any drive.

## Demo

Yes — a **free demo** is available on Steam (App 4747510). The demo was one of the most-played demos during the June 2026 Steam Next Fest and has been played by **more than 6 million players**. It teaches the three roles and several module types (wires, D-pad, numpad, switch panels). See the [demo guide](/guides/demo-guide).

## Languages

15 languages with interface, full audio, and subtitles [Official]: English, Turkish, French, German, Spanish (Spain), Simplified Chinese, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Russian, Polish, Italian, Spanish (Latin America), Traditional Chinese, and Ukrainian.

## Steam Features

Online Co-op, Steam Achievements, and Steam Family Sharing are listed on the store page [Official]. Built-in voice chat is role-limited — "some of you can talk, some of you can't" [Official].

## Quick Answers

- **Where can I play BOMBANANA!?** Steam on PC (Windows and macOS); a free demo is live on PlayStation.
- **Is there a mobile version?** No official version confirmed. No APK, no official Android or iOS release.
- **Is there a console version?** A PS5 demo is live; the full PS5 version has no announced date. Xbox is planned with no date [Community].
- **Can I play with just one friend?** No — exactly three players are required.
- **Does it work on Mac?** Yes — macOS 11.0 or later [Official].
- **How big is the download?** About 300 MB on Windows, 600 MB on macOS [Official].
