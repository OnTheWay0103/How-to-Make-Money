# Tears of Metal Achievements Guide — Full List & How to Unlock Them

> **Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

> **Honesty note:** achievement names, counts, and unlock conditions below are community-tracked, not officially published by Paper Cult. The 89-achievement total and individual conditions may be inaccurate — we label everything we could not verify and update as the community database confirms details.

Tears of Metal ships with **89 Steam achievements** on its launch roster. This guide lists the **67 achievements we've confirmed with their exact unlock conditions** — boss kills, block and parry mastery, campaign self-challenges, currency goals, and battalion achievements — organized so you can hunt them efficiently. The remaining ~22 are unverified at the time of writing; we'll fill them in as the community database confirms them.

> **Reading the tables:** "Campaign" means a single run (one act-to-act campaign, 1–2 hours). "Throughout your Campaigns" means progress carries across multiple runs — these are the grind-friendly ones.

There are no "collect 50 trinkets" filler rows; nearly every achievement teaches a mechanic or a strategy.

## Block & Parry Mastery

The biggest cluster of achievements is defensive. Tears of Metal's block system rewards both raw blocking and perfect timing — and the achievements reflect both styles.

### Block Volume (Grind-Friendly)

| Achievement | Condition | Type |
|------------|-----------|:----:|
| **Blocker Extraordinaire** | Block 20 attacks in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Turtle Master** | Block 50 attacks in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **The Last Wall** | Block 100 attacks in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Wall of One** | Avoid 1500 damage by Blocking (all campaigns) | Total |
| **Great Wall Of Scotland** | Avoid 1000 damage by Blocking in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Bastion of Defense** | Avoid 1500 damage by Blocking in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Moving Target** | Block 100 ranged damage in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Arrow Catcher** | Block 200 ranged damage in a single Campaign | Campaign |

**How to grind them:** hold block through Act 1's early packs instead of dodging. Grunt and pikeman groups are slow enough to feed you block counts safely. The ranged variants are easiest against spotter units in Act 1 — let their shots hit your block intentionally.

### Perfect Timing

| Achievement | Condition | Type |
|------------|-----------|:----:|
| **Razor's Edge** | Perfect Block 30 times in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Not Today!** | Block an attack that would otherwise kill you | Any |
| **Vengeful Spirit** | Parry an enemy that just damaged you 5 times (all campaigns) | Total |
| **Viceral Instinct** | Parry 125 times (all campaigns) | Total |

**Tip:** perfect blocks (timed parries) require tapping block just before impact. Grunt-type enemies with slow, telegraphed swings are the safest parry practice (see [Combat Tips](/guides/combat-tips)). For "Viceral Instinct," parrying is also the correct play against most humanoid packs, so it unlocks organically across runs.

### Block Damage (The "Retaliation" Builds)

| Achievement | Condition | Type |
|------------|-----------|:----:|
| **Vicious Retaliator** | Deal 5000 extra Block damage with Block damage multipliers (all campaigns) | Total |
| **Sweet Sweet Revenge** | Deal 10000 extra Block damage with Block damage multipliers (all campaigns) | Total |
| **Indomitable Fortress** | Deal 50000 extra Block damage with Block damage multipliers (all campaigns) | Total |

**Strategy:** these reward block-damage builds — stack artifacts and emblems that convert blocked hits into retaliation damage. The 50,000 milestone is one of the long grinds; if you're chasing it, commit a hero build to block synergy rather than hoping it happens incidentally.

### Getting Block Broken (Yes, Really)

| Achievement | Condition | Type |
|------------|-----------|:----:|
| **Shieldwright's Best Friend** | Get Block broken 5 times in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Shieldwright's Hero** | Get Block broken 10 times in a single Campaign | Campaign |
| **Forearm Splinters** | Get Block broken 15 times in a single Campaign | Campaign |

**Tip:** blocking a heavy attack (or over-blocking) breaks your guard. These are deliberately silly anti-mastery achievements — let shield bearers and heavy enemies break your block. Do it early in a campaign when you can afford the chip damage, and never intentionally break your block in Act 3.

- **20-second combo / 200 kills streaking:** crowd-dense fights with a fast hero (Brienne is ideal); just don't stop attacking. Streaking mechanics count sustained kill chains

## The Campaign Self-Challenge Set

These are the "hard mode" achievements — finishing acts with restrictions. They're the closest thing Tears of Metal has to optional difficulty settings, and several are genuinely difficult:

| Achievement | Condition | Difficulty |
|------------|-----------|:----------:|
| **Saving It For A Rainy Day** | Finish Act 1 without spending any coin | Easy |
| **Superstitious** | Finish Act 1 without opening any Treasure Chest | Easy |
| **Minimalistic Creed** | Finish Act 1 without equipping any active Emblem | Medium |
| **Barely Made It** | Finish a level with less than 10% HP left | Easy |
| **Not One Left Behind** | Finish Act 2 without any ally dying | **Hard** |
| **Voluntary Discomfort** | Finish Act 2 without opening any Treasure Chest | Medium |

**How to hunt them:**
- **The Act 1 trio** — plan a dedicated Act 1 run each; skip shops, skip chests, skip emblem equips. Act 1 is forgiving enough for all three separately
- **"Barely Made It"** — happens organically in Act 3 if you play aggressively; otherwise, let an Act 1 boss take you to single digits before the killing blow
- **"Not One Left Behind"** — the hard one. Your battalion takes permanent losses, so this demands defensive play: keep soldiers retreating at low health, use commands to pull aggro, and consider Wallace (forgiving defense) or co-op where allies can cover. See the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) and [Battalion & Companion Guide](/guides/battalion-companion-guide) for soldier-protection tactics

## Hunting Plan (Recommended Order)

1. **Play one full campaign** — unlocks all three boss kills, most currency achievements, and organic combat totals
2. **Act 1 challenge run 1:** "Saving It For A Rainy Day" (no spending)
3. **Act 1 challenge run 2:** "Superstitious" (no chests)
4. **Act 1 challenge run 3:** "Minimalistic Creed" (no emblems) — combine with 2-3 if you're brave
5. **Block grind run:** Act 1 with a block build — stack "Blocker Extraordinaire," "Turtle Master," "Moving Target," "Arrow Catcher," and shield-break feeding
6. **Combo run:** Brienne into dense crowds for "No Intermission," "Unstoppable Force," "Whirlwind of Death"
7. **Act 2 flawless attempt:** "Not One Left Behind" + "Voluntary Discomfort" in one focused run
8. **Long-term totals:** "There will be blood...", "Viceral Instinct," "Sweet Sweet Revenge," "Indomitable Fortress," Triskeles milestones — these land across many campaigns, no special hunting needed

**Co-op caveats:**

## Common Misunderstandings

A few conditions trip up hunters; here's the clarification:

| Confusion | Truth |
|-----------|-------|
| **"Single Campaign" vs "Throughout your Campaigns"** | Campaign = one run's worth of counting; Throughout = progress banked across every run you ever play. Grind the second type passively, never actively |
| **"Streaking"** | Sustained kill chains without your combo dropping — the same counter as "No Intermission," just tracked differently |
| **"Perfect Block" vs "Parry"** | The game's defensive system counts a perfectly-timed block as a Perfect Block; parries are the same timing on a different input context. Both count into their own achievements |
| **"Ally"** | Means your soldiers, not co-op teammates |
| **"Rescue"** | Picking up a downed soldier — always do it; it's both correct play and achievement progress |
| **"Captains"** | The elite-tier enemies (not your promoted soldiers) — the "kill 100 Captains" and "kill 5 Captains with Ally Commands" achievements refer to enemy captains |
| **"Dragonstones"** | Listed in community trackers as smashable fragments, but not verified in current builds; treat the item as unconfirmed |

## FAQ

**Q: How many achievements does Tears of Metal have?**
89 on Steam at Early Access launch. 67 are confirmed with unlock conditions in this guide; the rest are unverified.

**Q: Do achievements carry over between Early Access and full release?**
Unverified — EA achievement lists often shift. We'll update this guide whenever the roster changes (see [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes)).

**Q: Can I get all achievements solo?**
Mostly yes — the confirmed list is single-player friendly. Co-op only eases the ally-focused ones ("Army of One," "By My Command...").

**Q: Are achievements in co-op shared?**
Unverified. If achievements are per-player (as is common), everyone in the party needs to satisfy the condition individually.

**Q: Which achievements should I hunt first?**
The passive ones — block counts, parry totals, Triskeles, healing totals, "There will be blood..." — because they bank across all campaigns for free. Then the Act 1 challenge trio (fast, easy, three separate short runs). Save "Not One Left Behind" and the block-damage grinds for dedicated sessions.

**Q: Do achievements unlock retroactively?**
Unverified. If you've already hit a condition before a patch adds or fixes an achievement, Steam may not retro-grant it. If a milestone doesn't pop when you clearly passed it, restart the game once before assuming it's bugged.

**Q: How long does 100% take?**
Estimate, not fact: the rarest achievements sit under 0.5% unlock rate, which strongly suggests multiple focused grinds (the 50,000 block-damage one alone is a long campaign loop). Plan for dozens of hours across many runs — the game's 1–2 hour campaign structure means each "run" is a significant time block (see [Save System Guide](/guides/save-system-guide)).

**Q: What does "Strike damage" mean for "There will be blood..."?**
The game's damage categories count Strike damage from standard attacks (as opposed to status, block, or command damage). Regular play with any hero accumulates it — it's the free long-term achievement.
