# Tears of Metal Brienne Build Guide — Best Weapons, Emblems & Strategy

> **Early Access v1.0 / Updated: August 1, 2026**

Brienne is Tears of Metal's unarmed speedster — the fastest attack rate in the game and a machine-gun of hits that stacks per-hit effects absurdly well. She trades burst for sustained DPS, and she trades range for mobility. She also has the highest skill floor of the three heroes: new players die more with Brienne before they learn her rhythm.

This guide covers how to build her around what makes her special: **per-hit effects**.

---

| Attribute | Detail |

| Weapon | Unarmed (fast attacks) |
| Role | Melee pressure DPS |
| Solo | 7/10 — steady, not spiky |

**Strengths:** fastest attack rate, best per-hit artifact/emblem synergy, excellent mobility, great co-op pressure.
**Weaknesses:** short range (must close distance; swarms hurt more), high skill floor, lower burst — DPS is steady, not spiky.

## Best Emblems for Brienne

Brienne's identity is **per-hit scaling**: every emblem that triggers on hit fires far more often for her than for anyone else. Two emblems, Primary + Secondary:

| Emblem | Slot | Why |
|--------|------|-----|
| **Fire** (attacks apply burn, 5% HP/sec for 4 sec) | Primary | Burn stacks; her attack rate keeps 5-6 stacks up on bosses |
| **Attack Speed** (+20% attack speed) | Secondary | Multiplies both raw DPS and burn-stacking speed |
| **Lightning** (attacks chain to nearby enemies) | Alternative | Chained hits at her attack rate shred crowds |
| **Freeze** (attacks slow 30%, freeze at 5 stacks) | Alternative | Locks enemies in place while her short range is a liability |

**Recommended start:** Fire + Attack Speed — the fastest burn-stacking build in the game. For group-heavy regions, Lightning + Freeze turns her into a crowd-control blender.

## Weapon & Scaling Notes

Brienne fights **unarmed** — her damage comes from her attack rate, and her build is defined by emblems and artifacts rather than weapon tiers. Two honest caveats:

- Whether unarmed attacks benefit from weapon-specific artifact bonuses is **still being documented** — treat weapon-type artifact descriptions as unverified until patch notes confirm.
- Regardless, the correct direction is certain: stack attack speed and per-hit sources. The [Weapon Upgrade Guide](/guides/weapon-upgrade-guide) still matters for the merge math if weapon-like equipment for her kit is added later.

## Skill Priorities (Ability Points)

1. **Attack Speed branches** — every point multiplies her per-hit value
2. **Dodge/evasion capstones** — short range means repositioning is survival
3. **Combo finishers** — end-of-combo hits carry her biggest burst and stack effects

> AP is per-run and does not carry over — always calculate a full branch cost before committing points.

---

## Playstyle Tips

- **Stack burn first, then keep it topped up** — land 3-4 quick hits, then stay at the edge of reach while burn ticks
- **Do not facetank** — Brienne's short range means swarms hurt more than for any other hero. Hit, dodge, reposition
- **Dodge is your defense** — she has the fastest mobility in the game; treat every captain combo as dodge-or-die
- **Focus one target at a time** — burn stacks are single-target; spreading hits around wastes the build
- **Flank in co-op** — while Wallace holds the line or Ruadh staggers a boss, apply pressure from the side

## FAQ

**Q: Is Brienne good for beginners?**
No — she has the highest skill floor of the three heroes. Pick Wallace first, then graduate to Brienne.

**Q: Why does Brienne feel weaker than Ruadh?**
Her DPS is steady, not spiky. Against stationary or long fights (bosses, elites) she out-damages everyone; she just does not delete things in one swing.

**Q: What is the fastest Brienne clear build?**
Fire + Attack Speed with Dragon Stone Fragment — the fastest burn-stacking combination in the game.
