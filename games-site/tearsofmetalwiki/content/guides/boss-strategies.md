## Story Bosses — Detailed Strategies

### Scrap King

A hulking mech with slow, telegraphed attacks. The easiest boss in the game.

**Phases:**
- **Phase 1 (100–50% HP):** Two attacks — Arm Slam (tells by raising left arm, 1.5s delay) and Scrap Spread (AoE projectile burst).
- **Phase 2 (50–0% HP):** Gains a charge attack. Stuns itself on walls — free damage window.

**Strategy:**
- Stay at mid-range to bait Arm Slam, then punish.
- Dodge through Scrap Spread (not away from it — the spread widens at range).
- In Phase 2, position with your back to a wall so the charge stuns the King.

**Recommended Loadout:** Lightning Hammer + Any Shield

### Necro Commander

Summons skeletons. Fight is about add management.

**Strategy:**
- Kill skeletons immediately — they empower the Commander's attacks (+5% damage per skeleton alive).
- Use Sonic Twin Blades to interrupt summoning animations.
- When the Commander raises both arms, dodge backwards twice (AoE necrotic burst).

**Recommended Loadout:** Sonic Twin Blades + Fire Axe (for adds)

### Iron Colossus

A massive golem with three distinct phases. Long fight (8–12 minutes).

**Phases:**
- **Phase 1:** Arm sweeps and stomps. Attack arms (each arm has its own HP bar). Destroy one arm to enter Phase 2.
- **Phase 2:** Core exposed. Beam attack (constant damage, strafe left). Colossus spawns smaller golems.
- **Phase 3:** Enrage mode. Attacks 50% faster. Timer: 2 minutes until self-destruct.

**Strategy:**
- Focus one arm in Phase 1. Do not split damage.
- In Phase 2, keep moving sideways. Use Corrosive weapons on the core.
- In Phase 3, save your Weapon Art for burst. If you can't kill in 2 minutes, the self-destruct is instant-death regardless of HP.

**Recommended Loadout:** Corrosive Scythe + Ice Spear (Phase 2 adds)

### The Frozen Duchess

Fast caster boss with heavy ice damage.

**Strategy:**
- Roll towards her, not away — most of her attacks have a larger hitbox at range.
- When she casts Blizzard, break line of sight behind pillars.
- After every 3 casts, she is exhausted for 4 seconds — your biggest damage window.

**Recommended Loadout:** Fire Greatsword + Pyro Grenade consumables

### Void Speaker

Teleporting boss with high mobility.

**Strategy:**
- Watch for the purple flash — that's where the Void Speaker will teleport. Pre-position for an attack.
- The purple orbs the boss spawns heal it. Destroy them immediately.
- In Phase 2, the Void Speaker becomes invisible. Look for footprints in the dust.

**Recommended Loadout:** Sonic Twin Blades + Whip (to track invisible phase)

### General Ruin

The final story boss. A two-phase fight with a humanoid commander and a mech form.

**Strategy:**
- Phase 1 (humanoid): Parry his sword swings. His stamina bar depletes after 3 parries → 6s stun.
- Phase 2 (mech): Destroy the legs first (slows him), then the chest cannon. Dodge the orbital strike (shadow circle on ground, 2s delay).

**Recommended Loadout:** Lightning Hammer + Corrosive Scythe

## Optional & Post-Game Bosses

### Titan of Rust

Huge health pool, very slow attacks. A DPS check.

| Attack | Cue | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Rust Breath | Hisses for 1s before | Move behind the Titan |
| Fist Slam | Raises fist for 2s | Dodge sideways at the 1.5s mark |
| Pulse Wave | Arms spread wide | Jump over the wave |

**Drop:** **Rust Heart** (crafting component for Titan-ranked weapons)

### Overlord Xyclos (Post-Game)

The hardest required boss for endgame access. Has 5 phases.

| Phase | HP Threshold | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100–80% | Tentacle slams, void orbs |
| 2 | 80–60% | Summons void portals (kill 4 portals to cancel) |
| 3 | 60–40% | Gravity well — dodge out of the pull radius |
| 4 | 40–20% | Void clone — mirrored attacks, double damage |
| 5 | 20–0% | Enrage — attack speed tripled, no new mechanics |

**Strategy for Phase 4:** Look at the clone's weapon glint. If the glint is red, the clone attacks first. Parry to cancel.

**Drop:** **Xyclos's Eye** (accessory — +30% all elemental damage, -20% physical damage)

## Universal Boss Tips

1. **Learn the tell, not the attack** — Every boss has a visual tell 0.5–2.5 seconds before an attack. Focus on memorising tells and you can react to any attack.
2. **Don't get greedy** — Two hits then dodge. Bosses have anti-trading mechanics (bonus damage if they hit you during your attack animation).
3. **Use the arena** — Walls, pillars, and elevation changes block certain boss attacks. Learn which geometry works for each fight.
4. **Save Weapon Arts for phase transitions** — A full Weapon Art rotation during a 4-second exhaustion window can do 15–20% of a boss's HP.
