# Tears of Metal Character Unlock Guide — How to Unlock Every Hero

> **Applies to Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Tears of Metal launches with three playable heroes, and only one of them starts unlocked. William Wallace is yours from the first run, but Ruadh Stonecrusher and Brienne require real campaign progress — and Paper Cult has already teased two more heroes that are visible in the game but not yet obtainable. This guide covers every current unlock requirement, what each hero's archetype means for how they play, and the fastest route to a full roster.

---

## Every Character in Tears of Metal

| Hero | Weapon | How to Unlock |
|------|--------|---------------|
| **William Wallace** | Longsword | Default — unlocked from the start |
| **Ruadh Stonecrusher** | Giant hammer | Defeat the Banshee (Iseult, Act 2 boss) |
| **Brienne** | Unarmed (fast attacks) | Complete her dedicated quest chain |
| Two mystery heroes | Unknown | Teased, not yet unlockable (see [Patch Notes & Roadmap](/guides/patch-notes)) |

Only Wallace is available on a fresh save. The other two unlocks both resolve during a full campaign, so if you want the complete roster, plan your first complete run around the conditions below.

## How to Unlock William Wallace (Default Hero)

Wallace needs no unlock — he is the game's default hero, ready on your first run. He is a **longsword specialist**: his combos change with the longsword type you carry, so weapon choice is a bigger decision for him than for any other hero. His kit leans on crowd control and defense — the "Committed Charge" skill grants notable damage resistance (community-reported), and his block/parry timing is the most forgiving in the game, which makes him the best hero for learning combat fundamentals.

Notable Wallace weapons include special longswords sold by the village merchant and dropped by elites and bosses — exact costs and stat values are community-documented rather than official, so treat any specific number you see elsewhere as approximate. See our [Wallace Build Guide](/guides/wallace-build-guide) for the full weapon and emblem breakdown.

## How to Unlock Ruadh Stonecrusher (Hammer Bruiser)

**Unlock requirement: defeat the Banshee.** Ruadh is the game's hammer bruiser — the highest single-hit damage and best stagger in the roster, and the strongest pick against armored elites and bosses. Beating Iseult, The Banshee (the Act 2 campaign boss) unlocks him.

As of a recent update, the requirement is this single condition: defeating the Banshee is the whole unlock. If you already owned Ruadh from an earlier build, you keep him; the gate applies to fresh saves.

**How to beat the Banshee:** this fight famously filters new players — there is even an achievement for it ("Witch Hunter"). Dodge sideways rather than blocking, punish her recovery windows, and save your battle-energy super for the phase transition. A launch softlock on this fight was fixed in a recent update, alongside the save recovery system. Full strategy in our [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

---

## How to Unlock Brienne (Fast Attacker)

**Unlock requirement: complete her dedicated quest chain.** Brienne — the game's fastest attacker, fighting unarmed with a machine-gun of hits — is not a boss-kill unlock like Ruadh. She comes from her own quest chain: start it early in your campaign, complete the objectives as they open up across acts, and she joins after the chain's final step.

If her chain ever stalls, update the game first: a gate-stuck bug that could block progress on the chain was fixed in a recent update. Post-update, the chain plays through normally.

## Hero Archetypes Explained

Early articles about Tears of Metal described classes called "Vanguard," "Berserker," and "Marksman" — **those class names do not exist in the game.** The real roster is Wallace, Brienne, and Ruadh Stonecrusher. What the older guides described were *archetypes* — playstyle families — and the real heroes map onto them cleanly:

| Archetype | Real Hero | Playstyle |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Vanguard (crowd control + defense) | **Wallace** | Holds the line, controls chokepoints, best for solo beginners |
| Berserker (high aggression, positioning-critical) | **Ruadh Stonecrusher** | Enormous stagger, slow wind-ups — whiffing a swing is the most expensive mistake in the game |
| Marksman (ranged specialist) | None yet | No ranged hero in the launch roster; the two teased heroes are the best bet for this role later |

**Vanguard-style play is the best starting point:** Wallace's crowd control and damage resistance give solo beginners room to learn enemy patterns without dying to a single mistake. **Berserker-style play** (Ruadh) is high aggression with the worst whiff-punishment in the game — excellent once you can read fights, brutal before you can. A **Marksman-style ranged hero** does not exist at launch; if that is your preferred fantasy, watch the roadmap.

## Tips for Unlocking Heroes Faster

- **Unlock Ruadh on your first real campaign.** The Banshee is the Act 2 boss, and the run that kills her also banks permanent currencies — one focused campaign unlocks the bruiser and progresses your village at the same time.
- **Start Brienne's quest chain immediately.** Her objectives complete passively across acts; only the gate-stuck bug (fixed in a recent update) can block them.
- **Bring a full squad for better drops.** Co-op supports up to 4 players, and full squads get **higher drop rates** — triskelle and weapon duplicates come far faster in a four-stack (see the [Co-op Guide](/guides/co-op-guide)).
- **Bank triskelle every run.** Win or lose, the meta currency banks — village upgrades and merchant stock get far more affordable if you never skip the village between acts.
- **Do not waste Dragon Stone Shards early.** Shards are the scarcest material; save them for Armory Level 3 and your confirmed endgame weapon (see the [Weapon Merge Guide](/guides/weapon-merge-guide)).

## Which Hero Should You Unlock First?

| Playstyle | Unlock order |
|-----------|--------------|
| New to action games / solo | **Wallace** (default) — then Ruadh once you can beat the Banshee |
| Like heavy hits and boss fights | **Wallace → Ruadh** (Banshee kill) — his stagger ends boss patterns |
| Like speed, combos, per-hit builds | **Wallace → Brienne** (quest chain) — highest skill floor, worth it later |
| Co-op with friends | **Unlock everything** — Wallace anchors, Ruadh staggers, Brienne stacks effects |

Wallace is the honest answer for most players: he starts unlocked, and his archetype — crowd control plus defense — is exactly what solo beginners need. After him, pick the unlock that matches your taste: Ruadh if you want the biggest hits, Brienne if you want the fastest combos.
