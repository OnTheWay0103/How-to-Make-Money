## Tears of Metal Character Builds Guide

Tears of Metal offers four distinct build archetypes, each suited to different playstyles and content types. While the game does not enforce strict classes, your skill tree choices and equipment selections define your role. Understanding how to build effectively for your chosen playstyle is crucial for surviving the wasteland and climbing the Survival Mode leaderboards. This guide covers every major archetype in detail.

## Build Archetypes Overview

| Archetype | Primary Role | Best Weapon Types | Difficulty |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|------------|
| **Scrapper** | Melee DPS, close-range brawler | Blades, Clubs, Gauntlets | Easy |
| **Sniper** | Ranged DPS, stealth, precision | Sniper Rifles, Bows | Medium |
| **Medic** | Healing, support, crowd control | Pistols, SMGs | Medium |
| **Heavy** | Tank, suppression, area denial | Machine Guns, Rocket Launchers | Hard |

## Scrapper Build

The Scrapper excels in close-quarters combat, using aggressive melee attacks and high mobility to overwhelm enemies. This is the most straightforward archetype and ideal for new players.

### Stat Allocation

Each level grants 5 stat points. Stat priorities for Scrapper:

- **Strength**: 2 points per level. Increases melee damage by 2% per point and stagger chance.
- **Agility**: 2 points per level. Increases attack speed by 1.5% per point and movement speed.
- **Vitality**: 1 point per level. Increases HP by 25 per point and reduces damage taken by 0.5%.

At level 30, a Scrapper should have approximately 60 Strength, 60 Agility, and 30 Vitality.

### Skill Tree Recommendations

| Skill Tree | Points | Key Skills |
|------------|--------|------------|
| **Brawler** | 25 | Relentless Strikes, Blood Rush, Ground Pound |
| **Survivor** | 15 | Adrenaline Rush, Second Wind, Iron Hide |
| **Technician** | 10 | Scrap Recycling, Quick Repair, Field Crafting |

### Weapon Preferences

Scrappers perform best with fast melee weapons that have high attack speed and moderate damage.

- **Best Early Weapon**: Salvaged Machete — fast attack speed, decent damage, easy material requirements
- **Best Mid Weapon**: Reinforced Claws — highest attack speed in the game, excellent for applying bleed
- **Best Endgame Weapon**: Masterwork Twin Blades — balanced speed and damage, 4 special effect slots

### Playstyle Tips

- Stay mobile. Scrappers rely on dodging rather than blocking.
- Use Blood Rush (heals based on damage dealt) as your primary sustain.
- Apply Bleeding Edge effect to stack DoT damage on tough enemies.
- In group play, your role is to engage priority targets and keep pressure on ranged enemies.

## Sniper Build

The Sniper excels at eliminating high-value targets from distance before they become a threat. Stealth, positioning, and precision are the Sniper's core tools.

### Stat Allocation

- **Dexterity**: 3 points per level. Increases ranged damage by 2.5% per point and critical hit damage.
- **Perception**: 1 point per level. Increases critical hit chance by 0.5% per point and detection range.
- **Agility**: 1 point per level. Increases movement speed and reload speed.

At level 30, aim for 90 Dexterity, 30 Perception, and 30 Agility.

### Skill Tree Recommendations

| Skill Tree | Points | Key Skills |
|------------|--------|------------|
| **Marksman** | 30 | Steady Aim, Critical Focus, Executioner |
| **Scout** | 15 | Light Foot, Eagle Eye, Shadow Step |
| **Survivor** | 5 | Field Medic, Emergency Bandage |

### Weapon Preferences

- **Best Early Weapon**: Hunting Rifle — reliable damage, available early
- **Best Mid Weapon**: Modified Sniper Rifle with scope attachment — critical for long-range engagements
- **Best Endgame Weapon**: Masterwork Widowmaker — highest single-shot damage in the game

### Playstyle Tips

- Always engage from stealth. Your first shot from stealth deals double damage.
- Use Eagle Eye to mark enemies through walls. Prioritize marked targets.
- Position at elevation when possible. Snipers gain a 15% damage bonus when above their targets.
- In group play, your role is eliminating elite enemies and bosses. Leave standard enemies for the Scrapper and Heavy.

## Medic Build

The Medic is the backbone of any group, providing healing, buffs, and crowd control. While Medics deal less damage than other archetypes, their utility is irreplaceable in high-difficulty content.

### Stat Allocation

- **Spirit**: 2 points per level. Increases healing output by 3% per point.
- **Vitality**: 2 points per level. Increases survivability.
- **Intelligence**: 1 point per level. Reduces cooldown of support abilities.

At level 30, aim for 60 Spirit, 60 Vitality, and 30 Intelligence.

### Skill Tree Recommendations

| Skill Tree | Points | Key Skills |
|------------|--------|------------|
| **Field Medic** | 25 | Healing Burst, Revive, Purification |
| **Support** | 20 | Damage Amp, Fortifying Aura, Adrenaline Shot |
| **Tactician** | 5 | Battle Scanner, Mark Target |

### Weapon Preferences

- **Best Early Weapon**: Standard Pistol — light, fast, leaves room for support tools
- **Best Mid Weapon**: Salvaged SMG — decent damage output while maintaining healing uptime
- **Best Endgame Weapon**: Advanced Pistol with Vampiric Strike — self-sustain while healing others

### Playstyle Tips

- Stay behind the frontline. Your survival is more important than your damage output.
- Use Healing Burst for group healing and Revive for picking up downed allies.
- Keep Fortifying Aura active during combat — it reduces damage taken by all nearby allies.
- In solo play, the Medic relies on Vampiric Strike and self-healing abilities. Kiting is essential.

## Heavy Build

The Heavy is the wasteland's walking fortress, wielding massive weapons and absorbing incredible amounts of damage. Slow but devastating.

### Stat Allocation

- **Strength**: 2 points per level. Increases weapon damage and reduces weapon weight penalty.
- **Vitality**: 3 points per level. Maximum survivability.

At level 30, aim for 60 Strength and 90 Vitality.

### Skill Tree Recommendations

| Skill Tree | Points | Key Skills |
|------------|--------|------------|
| **Juggernaut** | 25 | Bullet Sponge, Immovable Object, Fortress |
| **Demolitions** | 20 | Explosive Rounds, Suppressing Fire, Shockwave |
| **Brawler** | 5 | Heavy Strike, Iron Grip |

### Weapon Preferences

- **Best Early Weapon**: Scrap Shotgun — solid damage, forgiving accuracy
- **Best Mid Weapon**: Reinforced Machine Gun — high ammo capacity, suppressive capability
- **Best Endgame Weapon**: Masterwork Thunderstrike (Hammer) — slow but devastating per-hit damage

### Playstyle Tips

- Use your body to block doorways and chokepoints. Your large hitbox becomes an asset.
- Fortress ability makes you immune to stagger and knockback — use it when surrounded.
- Suppressing Fire slows enemies in a cone, controlling crowds effectively.
- In group play, you are the anchor. Position between enemies and your Medic.

## Group Composition Synergies

The most effective group compositions combine complementary archetypes:

| Composition | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Scrapper + Medic + Sniper | Balanced, excels in standard content | Struggles against heavy enemy groups |
| Heavy + Medic + Sniper | Excellent defense, boss killing | Low mobility, weak against swarms |
| Scrapper + Scrapper + Medic | Aggressive, fast clear speed | Requires coordinated play |
| Heavy + Medic + Medic | Nearly unkillable | Low damage output |
