# Tears of Metal Elite Enemy Guide — Captains, Boss Bodyguards & Corrupted Elites

> **Applies to Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Elites are where Tears of Metal separates players from victims. They are the game's second hardest threat after bosses — and, thanks to the **Threat Level** system, the game spawns more of them the better you play. This guide covers the elite types that actually exist in the game, why they appear, what they drop, and how to fight them without feeding the Threat Level meter.

> **Note on older guides:** some pages described "Elite Scavengers" and "Power Cores" tied to a wasteland setting — those enemies, locations, and drops do not exist in Tears of Metal. The elites below are the real ones.

---

## What Elites Are

Elites are upgraded enemy units — more HP, more damage, and usually a special ability on top of a normal unit's kit. They appear in three confirmed forms:

| Elite Type | Where | Why It Spawns | Best Loot |
|------------|-------|---------------|-----------|
| **Extra Captains** | Any act, later fights | Threat Level rises from clearing objectives | Artifacts, weapons |
| **Boss Bodyguards** | Boss arenas | Escort elite groups | Adds with boss loot |
| **Corrupted Elites** | Act 3 — The Scar (Corruption zones) | Zone spawns | Corruption-resist gear, high-tier materials |

---

## Threat Level: Why Clearing Makes More Elites

The Threat Level system is the most misunderstood mechanic in the game — **the more you clear and collect, the more elites and reinforcements spawn**:

| What Raises Threat | What It Spawns |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Clearing map objectives / wiping groups | **Extra captains** in subsequent fights |
| Picking up rewards / loot caches | **Archer towers** in later battles |
| Killing elites and bosses fast | **Buffed final boss** of the region |

**The practical consequence:** a full-map clear walks into the region boss with a maxed-out escalation. Plan routes that clear *just enough* — every optional camp you wipe is an extra captain at the boss arena. See the [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide) for the full mechanic.

## Fighting Elites: The Tactics

### Extra Captains (Any Act)

- **Profile:** captain-grade HP with squad support; they buff nearby units and carry bigger tells than regular enemies.
- **Counter:** single-target burst from your best hero; kill the captain, and the squad it buffs collapses. Parry the big wind-up, dodge the follow-up string.
- **Route tip:** if Threat Level is high, do not clear optional groups near the boss arena — the captain spawns are not worth the adds.

### Boss Bodyguards

- **Profile:** elite bodyguards that turn boss fights into 1v5s.
- **Counter:** **kill them before the boss** — if they enter the fight as adds, they become the fight. The [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) notes this is the difference between a clean boss kill and a death spiral.

### Corrupted Elites (Act 3 — The Scar)

- **Profile:** apply **Corruption damage over time** on hit; their attacks stack the DoT.
- **Counter:** **kill them fast** — long fights bleed you dry. Corruption Resist (emblem or artifact) is mandatory for this region, and ending engagements quickly is the best defense (see the [Corruption Guide](/guides/corruption-guide)).

## Elite Loot

| Item | Drop Source | Use |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Artifacts** | Captains, boss bodyguards | Armory loadouts |
| **Weapons** | Captains | Merge material — feed duplicates into your primary weapon |
| **Dragon Stone Shards** | Act bosses | Premium village-upgrade currency |
| **Corruption-resist gear** | Act 3 corrupted elites | Surviving The Scar |

Elite kills also raise your **Triskelle** income — each captain and bodyguard drops currency. Exact drop rates are not officially published; treat any specific percentage you see elsewhere as community-documented at best.
