# Tears of Metal Elite Enemy Guide — Captains, Elite Scavengers & How to Handle Them

> **Early Access v1.0 / Updated: August 6, 2026**

Elites are where Tears of Metal separates players from victims. They are the game's second hardest threat after bosses — and, thanks to the **Threat Level** system, the game spawns more of them the better you play. This guide covers every elite type, why they appear, what they drop, and how to fight them without feeding the Threat Level meter.

---

## What Elites Are

Elites are upgraded enemy units — more HP, more damage, and usually a special ability on top of a normal unit's kit. They appear in four confirmed forms:

| Elite Type | Where | Why It Spawns | Best Loot |
|------------|-------|---------------|-----------|
| **Elite Scavengers** | Wasteland, Scavenger Camp | Random spawns (4-5 at the camp) | **Power Cores — 15% drop chance** |
| **Extra Captains** | Any act, later fights | Threat Level rises from clearing objectives | Artifacts, weapons |
| **Boss Bodyguards** | Boss arenas | Escort elite groups | Adds with boss loot |
| **Corrupted Elites** | Act 3 Corruption zones | Zone spawns | Corruption-resist gear, blueprints |

---

## Threat Level: Why Clearing Makes More Elites

The Threat Level system is the most misunderstood mechanic in the game — **the more you clear and collect, the more elites and reinforcements spawn**:

| What Raises Threat | What It Spawns |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Clearing map objectives / wiping groups | **Extra captains** in subsequent fights |
| Picking up rewards / loot caches | **Archer towers** in later battles |
| Killing elites and bosses fast | **Buffed final boss** of the region |

**The practical consequence:** a full-map clear walks into the region boss with a maxed-out escalation. Plan routes that clear *just enough* — every optional camp you wipe is an extra captain at the boss arena.

## Fighting Elites: The Tactics

### Elite Scavengers (Act 1-2)

- **Profile:** fast, aggressive, armored. They punish slow weapons.
- **Counter:** AoE weapons (Hammer + Lightning is the community standard) — the Scavenger Camp has 4-5 elite spawns, and packs group up.
- **Priority:** kill elites before their adds — an elite with a pack is a wipe.

### Extra Captains (Any Act)

- **Profile:** captain-grade HP with squad support; they buff nearby units.
- **Counter:** single-target burst from your best hero; kill the captain, and the squad it buffs collapses.
- **Route tip:** if Threat Level is high, do not clear optional groups near the boss arena — the captain spawns are not worth the adds.

### Boss Bodyguards

- **Profile:** elite bodyguards that turn boss fights into 1v5s.
- **Counter:** **kill them before the boss** — if they enter the fight as adds, they become the fight. The [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) notes this is the difference between a clean boss kill and a death spiral.

### Corrupted Elites (Act 3)

- **Profile:** apply **Corruption DoT** on hit; their attacks stack the DoT.
- **Counter:** **kill them fast** — long fights bleed you dry. Corruption Resist (emblem or artifact) is mandatory, and ranged options let you end engagements before the DoT stacks (see the [Corruption Guide](/guides/corruption-guide)).

## Elite Loot: The Power Core Economy

| Item | Drop Source | Use |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Power Core** | Elite Scavengers (15% drop) | Masterwork ascension, high-tier upgrades |
| Circuits | Military bunkers (Old World) | Crafting, upgrades |
| Blueprints | High-level zones, elites | Masterwork weapons (The Reaper, Thunderstrike, etc.) |
| Artifacts | Captains, boss bodyguards | Armory loadouts |

**The Power Core farm:** the Scavenger Camp loop is the confirmed best source — 4-5 elite spawns per reset, respawning every ~3 minutes on Hard difficulty (12-18 gold per kill on top).
