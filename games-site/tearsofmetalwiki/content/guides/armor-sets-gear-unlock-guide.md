# Tears of Metal Armor Sets — How Gear Unlocks Work Per Hero

New armor in Tears of Metal does not drop from bosses. Players report that each run contains a single gear-unlock challenge quest, and that the act you complete it in decides whether you get Commanding, Warmonger or Duelist pieces — three tiers per hero, unlocked permanently once earned. [Community]

That is the short answer. The rest of this page covers what is confirmed, what is only player-tested, and how to stop wasting runs on a quest you were never going to see.

## What actually unlocks gear

Tears of Metal tracks gear **per hero**, not per account — Paper Cult's launch announcement describes the game as having "Three playable Heroes, with unique gear, synergies and playstyles." [Official] In practice, players describe four routes to a new item, and only one of them is armor:

| Route | What you get | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Gear-challenge quest inside a run | One armor piece; the tier depends on the act | Player-reported only [Community] |
| Upgraded hero shops | More stock available to buy | Official, but not armor-specific [Official] |
| Weapon quests and Dragonstones | Weapons, Charm chests and statue discounts — no verified armor use | Player-reported [Community] |
| Trials / Mother Stone ladder | Charms and Threat reduction, not armor | Official reward list [Official] |

The gear quest is the one people mean when they ask how to unlock armor, and it is the one the game explains least.

## The gear-challenge quest, as players describe it

Challenges are an official system: Paper Cult defines them as "optional multi-wave objectives you encounter during fights." [Official] The October 21 experimental notes add that Challenges "can now reward healing, currency and passive Emblems," and add a Village item described as "New Cartographer Item in Village: You can unlock new Challenge Rewards." [Official]

What the game never states is that one specific challenge hands out armor. Players figured that out themselves. In the Steam thread "Armor quests are way too rare," the working model is: [Community]

- Each run offers **roughly one** gear-unlock quest.
- Completing it **during a fight** opens a reward chest containing an armor piece.
- The piece is **not random** — it is tied to the act the quest was completed in.

That last point is the whole system. It also explains the thread's title.

## The three act tiers

The mapping below comes from a **single community thread**, not from an official post: treat the act-to-set pairing as `[Unconfirmed]` until it is independently corroborated. Read it as the shape of the system (later act = later tier), not as a confirmed table. [Community]

| Act you complete the quest in | Set tier you receive |
|---|---|
| Act 1 | Commanding gear |
| Act 2 | Warmonger gear |
| Act 3 | Duelist gear |

Read the table again, because it produces a trap. **Gear tiers run in the same order as the campaign.** Once you own a hero's full Commanding set, an Act 1 gear quest can no longer give you anything new — your remaining pieces can only come from Acts 2 and 3. A player who keeps drawing the quest in Act 1 late in their progression is effectively getting nothing for it. [Community]

The same thread suggests the sets are not purely cosmetic: a Wallace strategy discussion references alternate armor sets granting extra army command damage and special generation. [Community] No official post itemizes armor stats, so treat stat claims as unverified. [Unconfirmed]

## Solo versus co-op rules

The two modes do not behave the same way, and this is the most common source of confusion: [Community]

| Mode | Who receives the piece |
|---|---|
| Solo | Guaranteed new piece for the hero you are playing |
| Co-op | A piece for one of the heroes someone in the party is playing |
| Co-op completion bonus | The piece unlocks for **everyone** in the lobby, including heroes nobody played |

So co-op is the faster route to filling out a roster — a four-stack can feed four different heroes from one quest — **provided** your group is actually completing the challenge rather than skipping the node. Solo is more predictable per hero, but you only ever progress the character in your hands.

## Why the quest sometimes never appears

Players in the same thread do not agree that the quest is guaranteed, and their reports explain why: [Community]

1. **Act placement looks random.** The quest can land in any act, so whether it is useful depends on what you already own.
2. **Routing can skip it.** If the node is off your path and you take the short route to the boss, you never see it.
3. **It may not surface on the map early.** Players specifically ask for the quest to be shown on the map at all times rather than only when you are close.
4. **Co-op assignment can feel unfair.** One piece per quest means three players can finish a run with nothing new, which is why the thread asks for each lobby member to unlock any piece they are still missing.

Practical response: before you commit to a route, look for the challenge marker; if your goal this run is gear rather than currency, take the path that passes through it even when it costs you a stronger build. [Editorial]

## Shops, the Village and the pace of unlocks

Paper Cult has already conceded the pacing problem in its own words: "We've heard the feedback from the game's launch that unlocking stuff was kind of a grind and too slow," followed by "so we've made sure players get unlocks much more frequently than before." [Official] The September 24 patch also states that "Some shops can now be upgraded to unlock more items," which is the confirmed route to expanding what each hero's shop offers. [Official]

On the experimental branch, the Cartographer item adds a second lever — you can buy access to new Challenge Rewards, which is the system the gear quest belongs to. [Official] If you are specifically farming armor, the Cartographer is worth checking before you spend on cosmetics-adjacent upgrades. [Editorial]

## Can you customize how your hero looks?

No. A separate Steam thread asking whether players can create their own character settles it: heroes are fixed, and outfits and skins are premade unlocks tied to that hero rather than sliders or a creator. [Community] This matters for expectations — "armor set" here means a named fixed outfit tier, not a mix-and-match cosmetic system.

## A realistic time budget

One poster's estimate in the gear-quest thread is "10 runs with every char"; another reports reaching Trial 8 on Ruadh with all weapons but still missing armor pieces, outfits and boots. [Community] Both are player estimates, not published figures, but they point the same direction: filling three sets on three heroes is a long tail, and the bottleneck is quest appearances rather than difficulty. [Editorial]

If you want to shorten it:

- Play the hero you are gearing, in solo, when a quest appears early in the run.
- Use co-op to spread pieces across heroes, not to concentrate them.
- Do not burn a run chasing a quest in an act whose tier you have already completed. [Editorial]

## What is not confirmed

- **How many pieces each set contains.** No official or community source itemizes a full set list. [Unconfirmed]
- **Whether the quest is truly guaranteed once per run.** Players in the same thread disagree. [Unconfirmed]
- **Exact post-October-21 reward tables.** The healing, currency and passive Emblem rewards are branch-only. [Official] [Unconfirmed]
- **Whether armor affects stats or only appearance.** Player reports imply stat effects; nothing official confirms it. [Unconfirmed]

## FAQ

**Why did I get the same armor tier twice?**
Because the tier is set by the act, not by what you are missing. If you already own that act's tier, the quest cannot advance you. Push your route into the next act. [Community]

**Does co-op unlock armor for heroes I am not playing?**
Yes, on completion. The piece is awarded to one of the heroes in the lobby, but the unlock is applied to every player. [Community]

**Do Dragonstones give armor?**
No source shows them doing it. Dragonstones are a shared currency spent on weapon routes, Charm chests and statue discounts; no official post or community thread describes buying armor with them, so the armor path stays the challenge quest. [Community] One related loose end: an achievement description mentions an item called "dragonstones" that could not be verified in current builds — see the [achievements guide](/guides/achievements-guide) for that caveat. [Unconfirmed]

**Is the armor system different on the experimental branch?**
The branch changes what Challenges can reward — healing, currency and passive Emblems — and adds a Cartographer item for unlocking new Challenge Rewards. [Official] No branch note mentions armor itself, so do not expect the gear quest to behave differently there. [Editorial]
