## Tears of Metal Weapon Upgrades Guide

Weapon upgrades in Tears of Metal are the primary path to power progression. The wasteland is littered with Scrap, rare alloys, and salvaged components that can transform a rusty pipe into a devastating instrument of destruction. Understanding the upgrade system, material economy, and special effects is essential for surviving the higher difficulty levels. This guide covers everything from basic upgrades to endgame masterwork weapons.

## Weapon Tier System

Weapons progress through six tiers. Each tier unlocks additional stat bonuses and special effects.

| Tier | Name | Max Level | Scrap Cost | Special Effect Slots |
|------|------|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Junk** | 3 | — | 0 |
| 2 | **Salvaged** | 5 | 50 | 0 |
| 3 | **Modified** | 7 | 150 | 1 |
| 4 | **Reinforced** | 10 | 400 | 2 |
| 5 | **Advanced** | 12 | 1000 | 3 |
| 6 | **Masterwork** | 15 | 2500 | 4 |

Junk-tier weapons are what you find on defeated enemies and in containers. They are functional but lack any special properties. Each upgrade tier requires the weapon to be at maximum level for its current tier before ascending.

## Upgrade Materials

Three material types are used in the upgrade system. Each has multiple rarity tiers.

### Scrap Metal

The universal upgrade currency. Scrap comes in three grades:

- **Common Scrap**: Drops from most enemies, found in containers. Used for levels 1-5 upgrades.
- **Refined Scrap**: Crafted from Common Scrap at a Workbench (5:1 ratio). Used for levels 5-10 upgrades.
- **Reinforced Scrap**: Found in high-level zones and boss drops. Used for levels 10-15 upgrades.

### Salvage Components

Special components that enable tier ascension and special effects:

- **Gears**: Dropped by mechanical enemies. Required for tier 2 and 3 upgrades.
- **Circuits**: Found in old world ruins. Required for tier 4 and 5 upgrades.
- **Power Cores**: Dropped by elite enemies and bosses. Required for Masterwork ascension.
- **Alloy Ingots**: Crafted at the Forge. Used as a substitute for missing components but reduces upgrade success chance by 10%.

### Rarest Materials

- **Wasteland Relic**: Found in hidden caches. Used for unique special effects.
- **Boss Essence**: Dropped by Survival Mode wave bosses. Required for Masterwork weapon crafting.
- **Old World Data**: Found in military bunkers. Used to unlock advanced special effect combinations.

## Special Upgrade Effects

Starting at Modified tier, weapons can gain special effects. These are applied using Salvage Components and each occupies a slot.

### Weapon Effects

- **Fire Coating**: Attacks apply burning damage over time. Crafted with 2 Circuits + 1 Fuel Canister.
- **Shock Module**: Chance to stun enemies on hit. Crafted with 3 Circuits + 1 Power Cell.
- **Bleeding Edge**: Attacks cause bleeding damage that stacks up to 5 times. Crafted with 2 Gears + 1 Razor Blade.
- **Vampiric Strike**: Heals for 5% of damage dealt. Crafted with 1 Power Core + 2 Medical Supplies.
- **Armor Shredder**: Reduces enemy armor by 10% per hit, stacking up to 5 times. Crafted with 3 Gears + 1 Alloy Ingot.

### Defensive Effects

- **Reinforced Grip**: Reduces stamina cost of attacks by 20%. Crafted with 2 Alloy Ingots.
- **Weighted Strike**: Increases stagger chance by 15%. Crafted with 2 Gears + 1 Weighted Core.
- **Radiant Blade**: Reveals hidden enemies within 10 meters. Crafted with 1 Circuit + 1 Light Module.

## Upgrade Strategy

### Progression Priority

- **Early Game (Tiers 1-2)**: Salvage every weapon you find for Scrap. Do not hoard materials — upgrade your primary weapon to Salvaged immediately. The damage difference is substantial.
- **Mid Game (Tiers 3-4)**: Focus on reaching Modified tier and adding one special effect. Fire Coating is the best all-rounder for this stage.
- **Late Game (Tiers 5-6)**: Save Power Cores and Boss Essence for Masterwork ascension. Plan your special effects carefully — they cannot be swapped once applied.

### Material Farming Tips

- **Scrap Farm**: The Junkyard map in Survival Mode yields an average of 200 Common Scrap per run.
- **Circuit Farm**: Military bunkers in the Old World zone contain 3-5 Circuits per visit. Bunkers reset every 30 minutes.
- **Power Core Farm**: Elite Scavengers in the Wasteland have a 15% drop chance for Power Cores. The Scavenger Camp has 4-5 elite spawns.

## Masterwork Weapons

Masterwork weapons are the pinnacle of Tears of Metal crafting. Each requires a specific blueprint found in high-level zones or Survival Mode wave 20+ rewards.

### Known Masterwork Blueprints

- **The Reaper**: Scythe-type weapon. Bleeding + Vampiric + Armor Shredder. Found in the Ruined Cathedral.
- **Thunderstrike**: Hammer-type weapon. Shock + Weighted + Reinforced Grip. Found in the Power Plant.
- **Inferno**: Assault Rifle. Fire + Shock + Radiant. Survival Mode wave 25 reward.
- **Widowmaker**: Sniper Rifle. Armor Shredder + Bleeding + Vampiric. Found in the Military Bunker Alpha.

Masterwork weapons cannot be further upgraded but can be repaired using Alloy Ingots. Their durability is 200, making them viable for extended Survival Mode runs.
