## Endgame Missions Guide — Elite Missions, Builds & Loot

Endgame Tears of Metal lives on the mission board: elite mission tiers scale enemy composition, hazard rules, and loot tables, and they gate the emblems and artifacts that define late builds. This guide covers every elite tier, what your build needs before queueing, and which hero to bring for each mission type.

### Elite Mission Tiers

| Tier | Unlock | Enemy Scaling | Hazard Rule | Best Loot |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| Elite I | Level 30 | +40% HP | Reinforced armor | Tier 3 emblems |
| Elite II | Level 40 | +70% HP, +30% damage | No health drops | Tier 4 emblems |
| Elite III | Level 50 | +100% HP | Timer missions | Tier 5 artifacts |
| Elite IV | Level 60 | +150% HP, enrage timers | Single hero lock | Exclusive weapons |

### Mission Types and Best Heroes

| Mission Type | Mechanic | Best Hero | Why |
|--------------|----------|-----------|-----|
| Siege Break | Destroy 3 gates under fire | Berserker | Highest burst on structures |
| Extraction Hold | Survive 4 waves | Vanguard | Taunts keep mobs off the point |
| Assassin Hunt | Kill target before it escapes | Marksman | Range prevents escapes |
| Gauntlet Run | No heals, 8 fights | Vanguard | Self-sustain passive |

### Build Requirements by Tier

1. **Elite I:** Two tier 2 emblems, weapon at +8. Any coherent build works.
2. **Elite II:** Two tier 3 emblems with synergy (e.g., Onslaught + Executioner), weapon +12.
3. **Elite III:** Tier 4 emblem set, artifact slots filled, and one defensive emblem (Steadfast or Vampiric).
4. **Elite IV:** Full tier 5 emblem build, artifact synergy, and a weapon with a legendary affix.

### Loot Tables by Tier

- **Elite I:** Emblem shards, tier 2-3 emblems, village upgrade materials.
- **Elite II:** Tier 3-4 emblems, artifact cores, guaranteed weapon blueprint.
- **Elite III:** Tier 4-5 emblems, exclusive artifact set pieces.
- **Elite IV:** Legendary emblems, exclusive weapons, title cosmetics.

### Endgame Loop

1. Farm Elite I-II missions for emblem shards to fill your build.
2. Run survival mode to floor 15 weekly — survival tokens buy artifact rerolls.
3. Push Elite III once your emblem set is complete.
4. Spend rerolls on artifact slots before Elite IV attempts.
5. Upgrade the village when between missions — village bonuses apply to all missions.

### Elite Mission Checklist

1. Confirm your emblem synergy matches the mission type — Siege Break rewards burst emblems, Extraction Hold rewards sustain.
2. Bring one artifact reroll scroll per attempt; a bad artifact roll can gate an Elite IV clear.
3. Communicate hero picks in co-op — two Berserkers on Extraction Hold wipe faster than a balanced pair.
4. Save village upgrade materials until the week's mission board rotates; some missions require village tier bonuses.
