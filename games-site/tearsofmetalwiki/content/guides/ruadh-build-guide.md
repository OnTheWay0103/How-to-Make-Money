# Tears of Metal Ruadh Stonecrusher Build Guide — Best Weapons, Emblems & Strategy

> **Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Ruadh Stonecrusher is Tears of Metal's giant-hammer powerhouse — the game's highest single-hit damage and best stagger. Captains that would combo other heroes get knocked around instead. He is the definitive boss killer: stagger chains shut down boss patterns outright.

The trade-off: slow wind-ups and positioning-critical play. Whiffing a hammer swing is the most expensive mistake in the game.

> **Values caveat:** artifact names and stat values below are community-reported and unverified — exact numbers may differ in current builds. Treat them as directional guidance, not official data.

## Best Weapons for Ruadh

The hammer is Ruadh's identity — merge duplicates at the Smithy for higher tiers (Base + Base = Reinforced+1, doubling per tier up to Reinforced+3; see the [Weapon Merge Guide](/guides/weapon-merge-guide)).

| Weapon | Why It's Best | Target Tier |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Great hammer (best you can find)** | Highest single-hit damage and the game's best stagger scaling | Reinforced+2 minimum |
| **Hammer with stagger/weight bonuses** | Secondary stats that feed his stagger identity | Reinforced+1+ |

**Merging rule of thumb:** specialize by Act 2 — Reinforced+3 needs 8 base copies, so feed every hammer duplicate into your primary.

> **Honest note:** specific named hammers are not yet publicly documented. Until patch notes confirm unique weapons, treat "best hammer" as the highest merged tier you can sustain.

## Best Emblems for Ruadh

Slow, heavy hits make every per-hit proc land with full value. Two emblems — Primary + Secondary:

| Emblem | Slot | Why |
|--------|------|-----|
| **Lightning** (attacks chain to nearby enemies) | Primary | One hammer swing chains as hard as a full fast-attacker combo |
| **Freeze** (attacks slow 30%, freeze at 5 stacks) | Secondary | Locks enemies in place while you line up the next swing |
| **Physical Damage** (+25% physical damage) | Alt Primary | Pure burst — crit hammer swings are the biggest numbers in the game |
| **Critical Strike** (+15% crit chance) | Alt Secondary | Pairs with Physical Damage for the one-shot build |

**Recommended start:** Lightning + Freeze for crowd control and stagger chains. **For boss rushing:** Physical Damage + Critical Strike — a crit hammer swing can end a captain in one hit.

## Skill Priorities (Ability Points)

1. **Heavy Attack / stagger branches** — more stagger and more damage per swing
2. **Stamina Recovery** — whiffing a hammer swing is expensive; recover faster
3. **Positioning utilities** — anything that keeps you at the right range instead of overextending

> AP is per-run and does not carry over — always calculate the full branch cost before committing points.

## Playstyle Tips

- **Bait, then punish** — let enemies and bosses commit to an attack, then wind up your hammer into their recovery
- **Stagger chains win boss fights** — against Gilles the Hog, Iseult the Banshee, and beyond, interrupt attacks outright instead of dodging them
- **Never swing at nothing** — a missed hammer swing is the most punishable mistake in the game
- **Mind the elite-heavy late regions** — The Scar's captain swarms punish slow wind-ups; pick your moments, not your fights
- **Let teammates hold attention** — in co-op, Wallace or Brienne occupy the swarm while you delete priority targets

## FAQ

**Q: Is Ruadh the best boss killer?**
Yes — his stagger interrupts boss patterns outright, and his single-hit damage is unmatched. Wallace is the safer pick while learning.

**Q: Is Ruadh good for new players?**
Medium difficulty. His slow wind-ups punish positioning mistakes, but he is more forgiving than Brienne's skill floor.

**Q: Why do I keep missing with the hammer?**
Whiffing is Ruadh's core weakness. Bait an enemy attack, then commit — never swing at nothing.
