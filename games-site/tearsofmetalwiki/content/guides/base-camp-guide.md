# Tears of Metal Base Camp Guide — Pre-Run Preparation at the Village Hub

> **Early Access v1.0 / Updated: August 6, 2026**

Every Tears of Metal campaign starts in the **Village Hub** — your base camp between runs and the only place where your progress is permanent. The players who win campaigns are not the ones who fight best; they are the ones who prepare best before the first gate. This guide covers what the Hub is, what each building does, and the pre-run checklist that turns a doomed campaign into a farmed one.

---

## What the Village Hub Is

The Village Hub is your **permanent progression system**. Everything you spend here — gold and Dragon Stone Shards — carries forward to every future run, while everything earned inside a campaign is lost when the run ends (see the [Save System Guide](/guides/save-system-guide) for the exact rules).

| Building | Purpose | Priority |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Armory** | Stores artifacts/emblems between runs — carry them into expeditions | 1st |
| **Recruitment Tent** | Recruits soldiers; Level 3 unlocks elite prospects | 2nd |
| **Smithy** | Weapon merging and special effects | 3rd |
| **Apothecary** | Potions and consumables | 4th |
| **Mystic Altar** | Runes and situational power | 5th |

**Upgrade order:** Armory → Recruitment Tent → Smithy → Apothecary → Mystic Altar maximizes power-per-gold. Full costs are in the [Village Upgrade Guide](/guides/village-upgrade-guide).

## The Pre-Run Checklist

Run this list at the Hub before every campaign — it takes 5 minutes and saves 30:

### 1. Hero Selection

| Hero | Best For | Avoid When |
|------|----------|------------|
| Vanguard | First campaigns — coastal chokepoints favor wide swings | You need ranged clear |
| Berserker | Aggressive play, high clear speed | Cliff-edge maps (positioning) |
| Marksman | Safe ranged damage | Tight corridors (Act 2) |
| Brienne / Ruadh / Wallace | Once you know their builds | Your first run — learn with Vanguard |

Match the hero to the act: Act 1's open coast favors the Vanguard; Act 3's Corruption favors heroes with survivability and ranged options.

### 2. Emblems

- **Act 1 default:** Physical Damage + Critical Strike — simple and effective.
- **Swap before Act 3:** add **Corruption Resist** — the DoT drains you through healing potions without it.
- See the [Emblem & Artifact Guide](/guides/emblem-artifact-guide) for the full list.

### 3. Armory Artifacts

The Armory is the single most impactful preparation step:

- **Artifact slots:** at least 1 slot (Armory Level 2) before Act 1; 2-3 before Act 3.
- **What to carry:** artifacts that match your act — survivability artifacts for Act 3, damage artifacts for farming runs.
- **Never start at zero:** a run without artifacts is a run without a build.

### 4. Battalion Roster

- Check your soldiers at the Recruitment Tent — veterans (especially Blood-Steel Knights) are investments; losing one sets your roster back an act.
- **Elite prospects** unlock at Tent Level 3 — recruit before long campaigns so they survive toward promotion.
- See the [Battalion & Companion Guide](/guides/battalion-companion-guide) for roster management.

### 5. Weapons and Repairs

- Upgrade your primary weapon to at least **Salvaged** tier before Act 1's fort; the damage difference is substantial.
- **Repair before you depart** — a broken weapon mid-campaign is a dead weight (weapons are your main gold sink, but durability matters more).
- Plan special effects ahead: they **cannot be swapped** once applied at the Smithy.

## What Carries Over vs. What Is Lost

| Carries Over (Permanent) | Lost on Run End |
|--------------------------|-----------------|
| Village upgrades | In-run artifacts and emblems (unless Armory-stored) |
| Unlocked heroes and recruits | In-run battalion progress |
| Gold and Dragon Stone Shards | Weapons found mid-run |
| Meta progression | The campaign itself |

**The golden rule of the Hub:** if it is not Armory-stored or a permanent upgrade, it is a campaign loan — spend it or lose it.

## Campaign Planning from Camp

| Run Type | Hub Prep |
|----------|----------|
| Farming run | Damage artifacts, cheap hero, no potion investment — quit early if needed |
| Boss run | Survivability artifacts, full potions, best hero, full roster |
| Act 3 Corruption run | Corruption Resist mandatory, ranged options, veteran battalion |

**Budget the time too:** campaigns run 1-2 hours with no mid-run save. Do not start a boss run you cannot finish — the [Save System Guide](/guides/save-system-guide) explains why.
