# Tears of Metal Progression Walkthrough — Act-by-Act Guide

> **Applies to Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

This walkthrough covers every act, the enemy archetypes you will meet, the objectives that matter, and the bosses that end each act. Follow this guide for sensible routing, resource management, and survival strategies. Each section includes enemy behavior notes and the tactics that make each act manageable.

> **Region names:** Tears of Metal is a medieval hack-and-slash — there is no "Mining Complex" or industrial wasteland. Act 1 is the **Scottish Coast**, Act 2 is an unnamed **mid-campaign region**, and Act 3 is **The Scar** (also called the Corruption Zone). Older pages that described excavation sites, crystals, and industrial machinery described the wrong game.

---

## Act 1: Scottish Coast

The Scottish Coast is your introduction to Tears of Metal. Rolling green hills, ruined villages, and the remnants of an ancient fortification set the scene. The Threat Level here starts low and rises gradually, giving you room to learn mechanics.

### Recommended Preparation

| Hero | Recommended | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Wallace** (longsword) | Best first pick | The coastal chokepoints favor wide swings and his block/parry timing is the most forgiving |
| **Ruadh Stonecrusher** (hammer) | Viable | Watch your positioning — hammer wind-ups are slow |
| **Brienne** (unarmed) | Difficult first pick | Dodge-heavy play works, but one mistimed approach is punishing while you learn |

**Emblems**: Physical Damage + Critical Strike (simple, effective)
**Armory**: At least 1 artifact slot (Level 2 recommended but not mandatory)

### Enemy Archetypes

| Enemy | Behavior | Counter | Threat Level |
|-------|----------|---------|--------------|
| Grunt | Charges directly, slow attack | Parry or block, then punish | Low |
| Pikeman | Keeps distance, thrust attack | Side-dodge, attack during recovery | Low |
| Shield Bearer | Blocks front, bash attack | Wait for bash, dodge sideways, hit | Medium |
| Trebuchet Spotter | Ranged, marks target for bonus damage | **Priority target** — close distance fast | High (as support) |
| Banner Bearer | Roaming, buffs nearby enemies | **Priority target** — kill before engaging his squad | Medium |

### Objective Priority

1. **Kill Banner Bearers first** — They are marked by a glowing banner. Any enemy near a Banner Bearer deals more damage. Clear them before engaging groups.
2. **Clear Trebuchet Spotters second** — Their mark ability amplifies damage against the marked unit. Do not let them mark your Shield Bearer.
3. **Loot every building** — The ruined village has buildings with breakable crates. Each crate has a chance to drop artifacts or weapons — and every duplicate weapon is merge material (see the [Weapon Merge Guide](/guides/weapon-merge-guide)).
4. **Find the breakable wall** — Near the village well, look for a cracked wall section. Breaking it reveals **Fergus the Unyielding's** shield (see the [Battalion & Companion Guide](/guides/battalion-companion-guide)).

### Optimal Route

1. Spawn at coast entrance → follow path to village ruins
2. Clear village (all buildings, breakable wall, well chest)
3. Fort approach (Banner Bearer + Pikeman squad)
4. Fort courtyard (Trebuchet Spotter on the rampart — climb or flank)
5. Fort battlements → **Gilles the Hog** boss arena

### Boss: Gilles the Hog

The Act 1 end boss and the free demo's finale. **No healing in the arena** — arrive at full health, clear adds first, and master block/parry/dodge before the door (full breakdown in the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide)).

### Transition to Act 2

After defeating Gilles, the path to Act 2 opens through the eastern fort gate. **Before moving on**:

- Spend all triskelle at the Village (prioritize the Armory)
- Check your artifact slots — equip your best artifacts for Act 2
- Ensure you have at least one healing potion

## Act 2: Mid-Campaign Region

Act 2 is larger and more dangerous than the Scottish Coast. Threat Level rises faster here — you should be comfortable with your build by the time you reach the region's end boss.

### Recommended Preparation

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Reinforced+1 weapon minimum | Act 2 enemies are noticeably tankier than Act 1's |
| Survival artifact equipped | Ironheart Pauldrons or similar |
| One emblem unlocked | At minimum, you should have your primary emblem |
| 3+ battalion soldiers | Act 2 has wider corridors and more flanking routes |

### Enemy Archetypes

| Enemy | Behavior | Counter | Threat Level |
|-------|----------|---------|--------------|
| Overseer | Ranged, calls reinforcements, debuffs player damage | **Highest priority** — rush with battalion charge | Very High |
| Hardened Grunt | Faster than coastal Grunt, armored | Parry-able; stun-lock with heavy attacks | Low-Medium |
| Veteran Shield Bearer | Same as Act 1 but tougher block | Two parries to break guard, or flank | Medium |
| Captain bodyguards | Escort groups around the region's objectives | Kill before the boss pull — see the [Elite Enemy Guide](/guides/elite-enemy-guide) | High |

### Mechanics

#### Overseer Reinforcements

When an Overseer spots you, they call reinforcements until killed. Reinforcements arrive from the direction the Overseer is facing. If you do not kill the Overseer quickly, you will be overwhelmed — treat every Overseer as an emergency objective.

### Objective Priority

1. **Overseer elimination** — Whenever you enter a new zone, scan for Overseers. They are the tall, cloaked figures on elevated platforms. Kill them before engaging ground enemies.
2. **Chest routing** — Act 2 hides weapon chests in side passages. Weapon duplicates are your merge fuel — do not skip them.
3. **Threat Level check before the boss** — if you full-cleared Act 2, the end boss arrives with extra captains and archer towers (see the [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide)).

### Optimal Route

1. Entrance → first fortified camp (clear, loot)
2. Overseer platform (kill Overseer first, then clear ground)
3. Central stronghold (loot all crates, weapon chests)
4. The region's final approach → **Iseult the Banshee** boss arena

### Boss: Iseult the Banshee

The Act 2 end boss (community-reported placement). Beating her unlocks **Ruadh Stonecrusher**. A launch softlock on this fight was fixed in a recent update, alongside the save recovery system — the fight now plays normally. Dodge sideways through her patterns rather than blocking (see the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) and [Character Unlock Guide](/guides/character-unlock-guide)).

### Transition to Act 3

After the Banshee, you enter **The Scar**. **Before proceeding**:

- **Corruption Resist is non-negotiable.** If you do not have a Corruption Resist emblem or artifact, seriously consider running Act 1-2 again to find one
- Reinforced+2 weapon is strongly recommended
- Stock up on corruption-cure consumables from the Apothecary (Level 2 recommended)
- Save triskelle for emergency supplies

## Act 3: The Scar (Corruption Zone)

Act 3 is the final act of Tears of Metal's Early Access campaign — **The Scar**, described by reviewers as "absolutely unforgiving." Purple Corruption energy seeps from the ground and corrupts everything it touches. This act is significantly harder than the previous two.

### Recommended Preparation

| Requirement | Non-Negotiable? | Why |
|-------------|-----------------|-----|
| Corruption Resist | **Yes** | Without it, Corruption DoT kills you in seconds |
| Reinforced+2 weapon | **Yes** | Act 3 enemies are much tankier |
| 5+ battalion soldiers | Strongly recommended | The final fights need bodies for pressure and aggro |
| Full healing potions | Strongly recommended | No health stations in Act 3 |
| Regen emblem | Recommended | Passive healing counteracts Corruption DoT |

### Corruption Mechanics

Corruption manifests in three ways in Act 3:

1. **Corruption Pools** — Purple pools on the ground. Standing in them drains health as DoT. Avoid at all costs.
2. **Corruption Mist** — Periodic mist that fills the zone. During mist, all players take DoT regardless of position. Corruption Resist reduces this significantly.
3. **Corrupted Enemies** — Every enemy in Act 3 is corrupted. Killing them can leave a temporary Corruption Pool at their death location.

### Enemy Archetypes

| Enemy | Behavior | Counter | Threat Level |
|-------|----------|---------|--------------|
| Corruption Spawn | Small, fast, applies Corruption on death | Kill from range; do not let them reach you | High (in groups) |
| Blood-Steel Knight | Elite, heavily armored, applies Corruption with every hit | Parry required; cannot block through armor | Very High |
| Corrupted Pikeman | Faster Pikeman, attacks apply Corruption | Same as Act 1 but more aggressive | Medium |
| Corrupted Grunt | Charges, explodes on death (leaves a Corruption Pool) | Dodge, do not block (explosion bypasses block) | Medium |

### Objective Priority

1. **Blood-Steel Knight elimination** — Their Corruption on hit is the deadliest attack in the act. Kill them the moment they appear.
2. **Corruption Pool management** — When you kill a corrupted enemy, move away from the corpse. The pool persists for a while.
3. **Direct path to the final region** — Act 3 punishes over-exploration. Corruption Mist drains your resources faster than you regain them.

### Optimal Route

1. The Scar's entrance (immediate corrupted encounter)
2. Blood-Steel Knight patrol zone (3 knights, skip if low on resources)
3. Corrupted camp (kill elites first, then loot)
4. Corrupted village (shortcut path — avoid the long way through the mists)
5. The final approach (waves before the boss)
6. The final arena → **unnamed final boss**

### Final Boss Strategy

The Act 3 end boss is **unnamed in public sources** — older pages claiming "Corrupted Warlord" and "Dragon Core" phase breakdowns are not verified. What is known:

- The final boss **arrives with scaled-up stats when your Threat Level is high** — route planning matters more than gear
- **Corruption Resist is mandatory** — boss attacks carry Corruption DoT
- **Healing is scarce** — no heal points between fights and none in the arena. Enter at full health or don't enter
- **Bring your built battalion** — the final fights need bodies

Full strategy in the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide).

## Post-Game and Replayability

After completing Act 3, Tears of Metal offers several reasons to keep playing:

### Higher-Difficulty Replays

Replay the campaign with more aggressive clearing — the **Threat Level system** turns every extra captain, tower, and buffed boss into optional challenge (see the [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide)). There is no separate "New Game Plus" mode confirmed; the scaling lives in the Threat Level meter.

### Achievement Hunting

Hidden objectives worth pursuing (see the [Achievements Guide](/guides/achievements-guide) for the full list):

- **No Soldier Lost** — Complete all three acts without a single soldier death (community-reported unlock: Morag of the Mist)
- **Emblem Master** — Unlock all 18 emblems
- **Pure Solo** — Complete all three acts without recruiting any soldiers

### Build Experimentation

Each hero has multiple viable builds. After your first clear, try (see the [Best Builds Guide](/guides/best-builds)):

- **Wallace heavy-attack longsword** — crowd-cleaving swings and the forgiving parry rhythm
- **Brienne per-hit emblem build** — machine-gun hits trigger per-hit effects
- **Ruadh stagger build** — Impact stacking that interrupts elites and bosses outright
