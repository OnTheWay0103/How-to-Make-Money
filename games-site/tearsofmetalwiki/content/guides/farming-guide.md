# Farming Guide — Materials, Gold & XP Routes

Farming in Tears of Metal is about efficiency — minimising downtime between kills while maximising drops. This guide covers the **best farming spots** for every resource type, with optimal routes, respawn timers, and recommended loadouts.

---

## Resource Overview

| Resource | Used For | Primary Source | Best Farm Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | Upgrades, items, repairs | Enemy drops, treasure chests | Scavenger Camp (Hard difficulty) |
| **Scrap Metal** | Weapon upgrades | Mechanical enemies | Junkyard Depths |
| **Elemental Essence** | Elemental infusion | Elemental elite enemies | Elemental Rifts |
| **XP** | Leveling | Enemy kills, quests | Horde Mode (Wave 15+) |
| **Blueprint Fragments** | New weapon recipes | Boss drops, rare chests | All zones (boss rotation) |
| **Refined Alloy** | Endgame upgrades | Weekly challenges, raid bosses | The Crucible (weekly) |

---

## Gold Farming

### Best Route: Scavenger Camp Loop (Hard)

The **Scavenger Camp** in the Rustbelt region has tightly packed enemies that respawn every **3 minutes**. On Hard difficulty, each kill drops 12–18 gold.

**Route:**
1. Spawn at the **Rustbelt Outpost** fast-travel point.
2. Run east into Scavenger Camp (12 enemies).
3. Clear the camp using AoE weapons (Hammer + Lightning recommended).
4. Continue east to the **Supply Depot** (8 enemies, 2 chests).
5. Circle back west through the **Ravine** (6 enemies, 1 chest).
6. Reset — camp has respawned by now.

**Per run:** ~350 gold, ~1.5 minutes with a good AoE build.

**Recommended loadout:**
- Weapon: Lightning Hammer (mob clearing)
- Secondary: Fire Greatsword (single-target for elites)
- Armor: Any with +Gold Find stat

## Scrap Metal Farming

### Best Route: Junkyard Depths Full Clear

The Junkyard Depths is a mid-level zone (level 25–30) filled with mechanical enemies. Every enemy has a **60–80% chance** to drop Scrap Metal.

| Enemy Type | Scrap Metal Drop | Count per Run |
|---|---|---|
| Rust Drone | 2–4 | ~30 |
| Junker Walker | 5–8 | ~12 |
| Salvage Golem (elite) | 15–20 | ~3 |
| Oxidation Beast (rare spawn) | 25–30 | ~1 (random) |

**Route:**
1. Enter Junkyard Depths from the **Sewer Entrance** (south-east).
2. Clear the Main Corridor (10 Rust Drones, 3 Junker Walkers).
3. Drop into the **Pit** — 2 Salvage Golems, 8 Rust Drones.
4. Climb the **Conveyor Belt** — 5 Junker Walkers, 4 Rust Drones.
5. Check the **Hidden Alcove** for the Oxidation Beast (30% spawn chance).
6. Exit through the **North Gate** and rest at the bonfire to respawn.

**Per run:** 150–250 Scrap Metal, ~5 minutes.

## XP Farming

### Best Route: Horde Mode Wave 15 Farm

Horde Mode rewards scale exponentially per wave. Waves 10–20 are the sweet spot for XP per minute.

| Wave Range | XP per Kill | XP per Run | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | 50–80 | ~8,000 | 8 min |
| 11–15 | 100–150 | ~12,000 | 5 min |
| 16–20 | 150–250 | ~20,000 | 7 min |
| 21–25 | 250–400 | ~35,000 | 10 min |

**Optimal farm:** Clear waves 1–15 (recommended for level 40+ characters). This takes ~13 minutes and gives ~20,000 XP. Restart at wave 15 rather than pushing further, because enemy HP scaling makes waves 16+ less XP-efficient.

**Loadout for Horde Mode:**
- **Sonic Twin Blades** (Sonic damage is effective against most Horde enemies)
- **Band of Healing** (ring — passive HP regen between waves)
- **Corrosive Grenade** (consumable — AoE crowd clear for waves 10+)

## Elemental Essence Farming

### Elemental Rifts

Elemental Rifts are instanced zones that spawn after killing 3 elite enemies of the same element. Each rift contains 5–8 elite enemies and a boss.

| Rift Type | Essence Drops | Best Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire Rift** | Fire Essence (3–5 per run) | Scorched Fields |
| **Ice Rift** | Ice Essence (3–5 per run) | Frostbite Tundra |
| **Lightning Rift** | Lightning Essence (3–5 per run) | Storm Peaks |
| **Corrosive Rift** | Corrosive Essence (3–5 per run) | Toxic Marshes |
| **Sonic Rift** | Sonic Essence (3–5 per run) | Echo Caverns |

**Efficient farm route:** Cycle between Scorched Fields and Frostbite Tundra. Kill 3 fire elites → enter Fire Rift → clear → fast travel to Frostbite Tundra → kill 3 ice elites → enter Ice Rift → clear → repeat. This gives you ~10 elemental essence of each type per 15 minutes.

## Blueprint Fragments

Blueprint Fragments are used to craft new weapon recipes at the Forge.

| Source | Drop Rate | Fastest Farm |
|---|---|---|
| World bosses | 100% (1 per kill) | Rotation: kill all 4 world bosses (2h respawn) |
| Dungeon final bosses | 40% chance | Clear Scavenger Dungeon (fastest, 8 min) |
| Rare chests | 15% chance | Rustbelt zone has 5 rare chest spawns |
| Weekly challenge reward | 100% (3 per week) | Complete any challenge tier |

Best method for focused farming: clear **Scavenger Dungeon** on repeat. At 40% drop rate and 8 minutes per run, you average one fragment every 20 minutes.

| Progression Stage | Farm Focus | Time Investment |
|---|---|---|
| Early (level 1–20) | Gold + Scrap Metal | 50% farming, 50% questing |
| Mid (level 21–35) | XP + Blueprint Fragments | 60% farming, 40% questing |
| Late (level 36–45) | Elemental Essence + Refined Alloy | 80% farming |

## Efficiency Tips

- **Use +Gold Find gear** while farming gold; swap to combat gear for bossing.
- **Stack +Item Find** consumables before opening rare chests — they affect drop rates.
- **Farm during server events** — Double Drop weekends happen every 2 weeks (check the in-game calendar).
- **Party up** — Gold and XP are shared in a party. A 4-man party clears Scavenger Camp in 30 seconds.
