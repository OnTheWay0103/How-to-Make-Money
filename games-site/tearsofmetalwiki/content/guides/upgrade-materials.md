# Tears of Metal Upgrade Materials — The Complete Farming Guide

**Tears of Metal Early Access v1.0 / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Every power system in Tears of Metal runs on materials: weapon tiers need scrap, tier ascension needs salvage components, and village upgrades need Dragon Stone Shards. This guide maps every material — what it is, where it drops, and the routes that farm it efficiently — so you stop buying the wrong upgrades with the wrong currency.

---

## 1. The Three Upgrade Materials

### Scrap Metal (The Universal Currency)

| Grade | Source | Used For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Common Scrap | Most enemies, containers | Levels 1-5 upgrades |
| Refined Scrap | Crafted at a Workbench (5 Common : 1 Refined) | Levels 5-10 upgrades |
| Reinforced Scrap | High-level zones, boss drops | Levels 10-15 upgrades |

### Salvage Components

Enables tier ascension and special effects — dropped by elite enemies, bosses, and high-value containers. Rarer than scrap; don't convert it.

### Dragon Stone Shards

The village-upgrade currency (Armory Level 3+ needs 3, Level 4 needs 8, and the Mystic Altar consumes them). Drops from act bosses and endgame expeditions — plan shard spending carefully, since village upgrades are permanent while weapon tiers are per-run.

## 2. The Weapon Tier Ladder (What You're Funding)

| Tier | Name | Max Level | Scrap Cost | Special Effect Slots |
|------|------|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Junk | 3 | — | 0 |
| 2 | Salvaged | 5 | 50 | 0 |
| 3 | Modified | 7 | 150 | 1 |
| 4 | Reinforced | 10 | 400 | 2 |
| 5 | Advanced | 12 | 1000 | 3 |
| 6 | Masterwork | 15 | 2500 | 4 |

Each tier requires the weapon at max level for its current tier before ascending — and **ascension is per-run**: a Masterwork weapon you built last run is gone this run. The permanent investment is the **Weapon Merge** system (see below) and the **Village upgrades**.

---

## 3. Weapon Merge Costs (The Permanent Investment)

The merge system combines two identical weapons into a stronger version — and this *is* permanent progression:

| Target Tier | Combine | Base Weapons Needed |
|-------------|---------|--------------------|
| Reinforced+1 | Base + Base | 2 |
| Reinforced+2 | Reinforced+1 + Reinforced+1 | 4 |
| Reinforced+3 | Reinforced+2 + Reinforced+2 | 8 |
| Reinforced+4 (if added later) | — | 16 |

**The math lesson:** merges double in cost every tier, so **specialize early**. Pick your endgame weapon by Act 2 and feed every duplicate into it. Merging requires Smithy Level 1 — see the [Village Upgrade Guide](/guides/village-upgrade-guide) for upgrade order.

## 4. Farming Routes by Material

| Material | Best Route | Expected Yield |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| Common Scrap | Act 1 enemy packs + containers | 20-40 per expedition |
| Refined Scrap | Workbench conversion (5:1) — or Act 2 elite packs | 4-8 per elite run |
| Reinforced Scrap | Act 3 zones, boss drops | 2-5 per expedition |
| Salvage Components | Act 2-3 elites and boss chests | 1-3 per boss kill |
| Dragon Stone Shards | Act bosses, endgame expeditions | 1-3 per act clear |
| Emblems (upgrade fuel) | Expedition chests and vendors | Varies — see the [Emblem & Artifact Guide](/guides/emblem-artifact-guide) |

**The workbench rule:** converting Common to Refined at 5:1 is always worth it *before* a boss expedition — 50 Common Scrap is worthless against a boss, 10 Refined is a real upgrade. The [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide) maps the exact container locations per act.

## 5. The Upgrade Order That Wastes Nothing

1. **Merge first, tier second.** Merges are permanent; tier upgrades are per-run. Spend permanent currency before temporary currency.
2. **Tier your main weapon to Advanced (Level 12) before side weapons.** The exponential merge math punishes split investment.
3. **Buy the Workbench early.** 5:1 conversion is the cheapest route to Refined Scrap — it pays for itself within two expeditions.
4. **Bank Dragon Stone Shards for Armory Level 3.** The Armory's inter-run artifact carry is the biggest permanent power spike in the village (see the [Village Upgrade Guide](/guides/village-upgrade-guide)).
5. **Don't tier-craft Junk weapons.** A tier 2 weapon's scrap cost is better spent on the weapon you're merging toward.

---

## 6. Common Material Mistakes

1. **Selling scrap.** Scrap is never vendor trash — a full expedition of Common Scrap is a free tier ascent.
2. **Converting Reinforced Scrap.** Reinforced only drops in high-level zones; there is no cheaper source. Never down-convert it.
3. **Splitting merges.** Merging two different weapons to Reinforced+1 each instead of one to +2 doubles your cost forever.
4. **Upgrading armor with weapon scrap.** Armor uses the same scrap pool; the [Equipment Guide](/guides/equipment-guide) covers what armor actually benefits from.
5. **Ignoring special effect slots.** A Reinforced weapon with 2 effect slots is two free emblems — the slots alone justify the tier cost.

## 7. The Endgame Material Loop

By the endgame, the loop is: expedition → Common/Refined scrap + Reinforced Scrap from Act 3 → merge duplicates toward your one endgame weapon → bank shards for Armory Level 4 → repeat.
