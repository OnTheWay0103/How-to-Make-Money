## Tears of Metal Survival Mode Guide

Survival Mode in Tears of Metal is an endless horde mode that challenges you to survive increasingly difficult waves of wasteland enemies while managing limited resources and maintaining your base defenses. With leaderboards, unique rewards, and escalating difficulty, Survival Mode is both a proving ground and the most reliable source of high-tier materials. This guide covers everything you need to survive longer and score higher.

## Survival Mode Basics

Survival Mode takes place on one of three maps: Junkyard, Wasteland Outpost, or Ruined City. Each map has unique terrain and environmental hazards.

| Map | Difficulty | Best For | Environmental Hazard |
|-----|------------|----------|---------------------|
| **Junkyard** | Easy | Material farming, early practice | Toxic puddles |
| **Wasteland Outpost** | Medium | Balanced runs, mid-tier loot | Sandstorms |
| **Ruined City** | Hard | High scores, Masterwork blueprints | Collapsing buildings |

Each run consists of waves of enemies with short intermission periods between waves. During intermissions, you can repair defenses, craft ammunition, and heal.

## Wave System

Waves are organized into sets of 5, with every 5th wave being a boss wave. The difficulty escalates continuously.

### Wave Composition

| Wave Type | Composition | Enemy Count | Special Rules |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Standard** | Mixed enemy types | 10-15 | None |
| **Swarm** | Fast, weak enemies | 20-30 | Enemies move 20% faster |
| **Elite** | Strong enemies with shielded variants | 5-8 | Elites have 50% more HP |
| **Boss** | One boss + support enemies | 1 boss + 5 adds | Boss has unique mechanics |
| **Nightmare** | All enemy types, amplified stats | 15-20 | Enemies deal 2x damage |

### Wave Scaling

Enemy stats scale with wave number:

- **Waves 1-10**: Standard enemies. Base difficulty. No modifiers.
- **Waves 11-20**: Enemies gain +50% HP and +25% damage. New enemy types appear.
- **Waves 21-30**: Enemies gain +100% HP and +50% damage. Environmental hazards intensify.
- **Waves 31-40**: Double enemy spawns. All enemies have at least one modifier (Shielded, Toxic, or Regenerating).
- **Waves 41-50**: Every wave is Nightmare type. Bosses appear every 3 waves instead of every 5.
- **Waves 51+**: Exponential scaling. Enemy HP increases by 25% per wave. Only the most optimized builds survive past wave 60.

## Resource Management

Resources are the lifeblood of Survival Mode. Between waves, you can use Scrap earned from kills to improve your position.

### Scrap Economy

- **Kill Reward**: 1-5 Scrap per enemy, scaling with wave number
- **Wave Completion Bonus**: 25 Scrap per wave + 50 bonus for boss waves
- **Objective Bonus**: 10 Scrap for optional objectives (defend civilians, destroy spawn points)

### Resource Allocation Priority

1. **Ammunition**: Always keep your primary weapon topped up. Ammo crates cost 10 Scrap per refill.
2. **Repairs**: Wall and barricade repairs cost 15-30 Scrap per segment. Prioritize walls that are actively under attack.
3. **Weapon Upgrades**: Available every 5 waves. Costs increase with each upgrade.
4. **Traps**: Landmines (15 Scrap), Bear Traps (10 Scrap), and Turrets (50 Scrap). Place them at chokepoints.
5. **Consumables**: Medkits (20 Scrap), Stamina Boosters (15 Scrap), temporary damage buffs (25 Scrap).

## Base Defense

Your base consists of walls, barricades, and defensive structures. Keeping them intact is critical.

### Defense Types

- **Scrap Walls**: Basic barrier. 100 HP. Costs 20 Scrap per segment to build. Repairable for 15 Scrap.
- **Reinforced Walls**: Upgraded barrier. 300 HP. Requires Reinforced Scrap to build. Repairable for 25 Scrap.
- **Barbed Wire**: Slows enemies passing through. 50 HP. Costs 10 Scrap per segment.
- **Auto Turret**: Fires at enemies in range. 150 HP. Costs 50 Scrap + 2 Power Cores. Deals moderate damage.
- **Flame Trap**: Burns enemies in a cone. 80 HP. Costs 30 Scrap + 1 Fuel Canister. Excellent for chokepoints.

### Defense Placement Strategies

| Map | Optimal Defense Layout | Notes |
|-----|----------------------|-------|
| Junkyard | U-shaped funnel at main entrance | Natural walls on three sides reduce building costs |
| Wasteland Outpost | Cross-shaped killbox at center | Enemies approach from all directions |
| Ruined City | Vertical defense on rooftops | Enemies climb walls; place traps on ladders |

### Repair Priority

When multiple walls are damaged, prioritize by threat level:

1. **Entry walls** — The main path enemies take to reach you
2. **Flanking walls** — Secondary paths that, if breached, give enemies rear access
3. **Turret positions** — Defenses protecting your turrets
4. **Outer perimeter** — Walls that have not yet been tested by enemies

## Scoring System

Your final score is calculated from multiple factors. Leaderboard ranking is determined by total score.

| Scoring Factor | Points | Notes |
|----------------|--------|-------|
| Wave reached | 1000 points per wave | Wave 50 = 50,000 base points |
| Enemies killed | 50 points per kill | Boss kills worth 500 points |
| Headshot kills | 100 points per headshot | Multiplier: 1.5x for consecutive headshots |
| No-damage waves | 2000 points per perfect wave | No player damage taken during the wave |
| Objectives completed | 5000 points per objective | Optional survival objectives |
| Scrap remaining | 10 points per Scrap | Encourages resource efficiency |
| Time bonus | Up to 10000 points | Faster completion = higher bonus |

## Recommended Loadouts

### Beginner Loadout (Waves 1-20)

- **Weapon**: Scrap Shotgun (Salvaged tier) — excellent wave clear
- **Special Effect**: Fire Coating — damage over time helps with mobs
- **Defense Priority**: Build two Reinforced Walls at main entry, place 4 Bear Traps in front
- **Strategy**: Stay near your defenses. Let walls and traps thin the herd before engaging.

### Advanced Loadout (Waves 21-40)

- **Weapon**: Modified Assault Rifle — range and DPS for elite enemies
- **Special Effects**: Armor Shredder + Shock Module — controls elites and bosses
- **Defense Priority**: Auto Turrets covering two approach vectors, Flame Traps at chokepoints
- **Strategy**: Kite enemies through trap zones. Use Shock Module to stlock dangerous targets.

### Endgame Loadout (Waves 41+)

- **Weapon**: Masterwork weapon matching your build
- **Special Effects**: Three damage effects + one defensive effect
- **Defense Priority**: Full perimeter of Reinforced Walls, Turrets on elevated positions
- **Strategy**: Constant movement. Do not stay in one position for more than 10 seconds.
