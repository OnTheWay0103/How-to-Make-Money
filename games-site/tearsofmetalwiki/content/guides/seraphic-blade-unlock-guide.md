# Tears of Metal Seraphic Blade — Wallace's 4th Weapon Guide

The Seraphic Blade is Wallace's hidden fourth longsword. You unlock it by finishing a campaign as Wallace first, then collecting three pieces — pommel, crossguard, blade — inside a **single** new run. Miss one and the chain breaks. Since September 8, 2026 you no longer have to finish the Act 3 mission to keep it.

> **Last verified: October 26, 2026 — Early Access, post-September 30 build (v0.17.60380).**

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who can use it | Wallace only |
| Prerequisite | Finish a campaign with Wallace once |
| Pieces needed | 3 (one per Act), all in the same run |
| Coin budget | About 575+ coins for the two shop pieces |
| Still need to finish Act 3? | No — changed September 8, 2026 |
| Worth using? | Collection value yes; the second sword is stronger |

## 1. Prerequisite: Finish a Campaign as Wallace

The pieces do not spawn until you have beaten the campaign's final boss **as Wallace**. A clear on Brienne or Ruadh does not count, and this is the part most players miss first.

The September 8 patch closed a loophole here: multiplayer no longer bypasses the "finish a campaign with Wallace first" requirement ([official patch, September 8, 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1913120)). Before that fix, co-op groups could pull a player into a run and hand them pieces they had not earned. If you played co-op in August and the weapon never appeared, that history is why.

## 2. The Three Pieces

All three must be collected **in order, in one run**. This is the single most important rule on the page.

| # | Act | Piece | Where | Cost | Breaks if… |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Act 1 | Intricate Pommel | Traveling merchant | ~225 coins | You skip it — the Act 2 chest will not appear |
| 2 | Act 2 | Gilded Crossguard | Special chest with a golden eye | ~315–350 coins | You open the wrong chest or run out of coins |
| 3 | Act 3 | Sanctified Blade | Challenge standard marked with an eye | Free — win the fight | You leave the mission before the reward is registered |

Costs are community-reported and discounts apply, so treat the exact prices as **[Unconfirmed]**. The Act 2 figure in particular is reported as both 315 and 350 coins depending on the run ([Niche Game Guides](https://nichegameguides.com/games/tears-of-metal/seraphic-blade/)).

### Piece 1 — Intricate Pommel (Act 1)

Buy it from the traveling merchant. Players report that the piece may not appear in the shop until the merchant's upgrade row has been purchased ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1913120/discussions/0/586182294117555037/)) — if you see an empty slot where the pommel should be, upgrade the merchant before assuming the run is bugged.

Do not leave Act 1 without it. Every later step depends on this purchase.

### Piece 2 — Gilded Crossguard (Act 2)

A chest that stands out from the normal ones: whiter, with a golden eye motif. Paying opens it. If you have not bought the pommel, this chest does not appear at all — which is exactly why players who "did everything right" in Act 2 end up empty-handed.

### Piece 3 — Sanctified Blade (Act 3)

A challenge standard marked with a white flag and an eye, roughly four waves of captains. Clear it and loot the reward chest.

Historically this was the step that lost people the weapon: you had to finish the Act 3 mission after collecting the blade, and dying or quitting voided it. The September 8 patch changed that — **the Seraphic Blade now unlocks immediately upon collecting every piece**, without requiring the level to be completed ([official patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1913120)). If you are on an older build, finish the mission anyway.

## 3. The Coin Budget

Two purchases inside one run means the run has to be a farming run, not a speed run.

| Spending target | Amount | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pommel | ~225 coins | Mandatory, Act 1 |
| Crossguard | ~315–350 coins | Mandatory, Act 2 |
| Reserve | 100+ coins | Healing and a wasted chest |
| **Total to bank** | **~575–700 coins** | Community-reported figures |

This competes directly with gear and healing, which is why the [Healing Guide](/guides/healing-guide) matters here: a run that dies in Act 2 with 500 coins spent on the pommel has burned both the coins and the attempt. If your coin discipline is shaky, do the unlock on a run where you are already comfortable with the Act 2 difficulty curve.

## 4. What Actually Breaks the Chain

| Mistake | Result | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Collecting pieces across separate runs | Pieces do not combine, no weapon | Start over; all three in one run |
| Skipping the Act 1 pommel | Act 2 chest and Act 3 challenge never appear | Restart the run |
| Merchant not upgraded | Pommel slot appears empty | Upgrade the merchant row, then re-check |
| Dying or quitting right after the Act 3 piece (pre-Sept 8 builds) | Weapon lost | Update; current builds unlock on pickup |
| Expecting co-op to skip the prerequisite | Pieces will not spawn | Finish a solo or co-op campaign as Wallace first |

The "pieces do not combine across runs" rule is the one that catches completionists. It is not a bug, and it has not been changed by any patch documented so far.

## 5. Is the Seraphic Blade Worth Using?

Mostly no, and the community is consistent about it. The blade is stylish, has no standout passive effect, and its combos are long and awkward relative to what Wallace already has ([Niche Game Guides](https://nichegameguides.com/games/tears-of-metal/seraphic-blade/)). Wallace's second sword is the common recommendation for actual play.

That said, the move data is not trivial. The kit is documented at four combo sequences, 19 move steps and 5 finishers, with a base damage band of 15–100 ([Tears of Metal Wiki](https://tearsofmetal.wikitactics.com/weapons/wallace-weapon-4/)). Community-compiled means **[Unconfirmed]** — treat the numbers as directional:

| Sequence | Input | Hits | Reported total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dodge › Heavy › Heavy | 2 | 60 |
| 2 | Light › Light › Heavy › Heavy | 4 | 215 |
| 3 | Block › Light › Heavy › Heavy | 3 | 170 |
| 4 | Light ×5 | 5 | 180 |

The pattern is the same one that governs Wallace generally: the heavy-finisher lines pay, the light chain does not. If you are chasing damage, the second sequence is the only one that competes with his standard kit.

**Verdict:** unlock it for completion, the collection screen entry, and the novelty. Do not rebuild your Wallace loadout around it. The [Wallace Build Guide](/guides/wallace-build-guide) covers what to run instead, and the [Weapon Merge Guide](/guides/weapon-merge-guide) covers how the sword you actually use should be merged.

> **Note on the build guide:** it currently states that specific named longswords are not publicly documented. That is no longer accurate for the Seraphic Blade — this page documents it. Treat the build guide's weapon table as general guidance and this page as the named-weapon exception.

## 6. Should You Do the Unlock Run Now?

Do it when all three of these are true:

1. **You have cleared the campaign as Wallace.** Otherwise the pieces will not spawn and the run is wasted.
2. **You can comfortably reach Act 3.** A run that dies in Act 2 costs you the pommel money and nothing else, but it also costs a full session.
3. **You are not mid-way through a build you care about.** The unlock run should be a coin run, not your best attempt at a personal best.

If you are still working through the campaign, the [Progression Walkthrough](/guides/progression-walkthrough) is the better read first — the unlock only makes sense once the Acts are familiar.

## FAQ

**Do I need to finish the Act 3 mission after getting the blade?**
No, not on current builds. Since September 8, 2026 the weapon unlocks immediately when the third piece is collected. On older builds you had to finish the mission.

**Can I collect the pieces over several runs?**
No. All three must be collected in one run, in order. Splitting them across runs does not combine.

**Does a Brienne or Ruadh clear count as the prerequisite?**
No. The campaign must be finished as Wallace.

**Can a friend in co-op give me the pieces?**
No. The September 8 patch removed the multiplayer bypass, and the prerequisite is now enforced in co-op too.

**The Act 1 merchant has no pommel — is my game bugged?**
Check the merchant's upgrade row first. Players report the piece can be gated behind shop upgrades.

**Is the Seraphic Blade the best Wallace weapon?**
No. It is a collection weapon. Wallace's second sword is the usual recommendation.
