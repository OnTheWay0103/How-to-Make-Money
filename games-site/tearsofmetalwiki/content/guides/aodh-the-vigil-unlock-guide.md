# Aodh the Vigil in Tears of Metal — How Players Actually Unlock the Third Companion

> **Applies to Early Access / Updated: October 25, 2026**

Aodh, the Vigil was added to Tears of Metal on **August 13, 2026**, and the patch notes say only that he is "now unlockable". They do not say where, when, or as whom. Within a day, players who had finished the campaign were opening threads asking how to get "the new companion", and a day after that, others were replying that they still could not find him.

The short version: Paper Cult has never published the route. What exists is a consistent community route reported by multiple independent players, plus a list of reasons it silently fails. This guide keeps those two things separate, because a player who treats a Reddit answer as a patch note ends up re-running Act 2 for an hour.

---

## What Paper Cult actually confirmed

| Question | Official answer |
|---|---|
| Does Aodh exist? | Yes — teaser on **August 7, 2026**, in-game from **August 13, 2026** |
| Is he unlockable? | Yes — changelog: "New companion: Aodh, the Vigil, is now unlockable" |
| Where is he found? | **Not stated** |
| Which hero must you play? | **Not stated** |
| Is the event random? | **Not stated** |

His official description is a behavior hint, not a location: an old scholar with a book, who "keeps watch when a fight breaks out", and whose effect is that "the first blows never quite land the way the enemy intends". That reads as an opening damage-mitigation effect. No values are published *[Unconfirmed]*.

Everything below this line comes from players, not from Paper Cult.

---

## The community route

Three conditions appear together in every successful report collected for this page:

1. **Play as Brienne Ironleg.** Not Wallace, not Ruadh.
2. **Reach Act 2.**
3. **Visit Blackrock Vestiges and look for the recruitment event.**

A fourth condition — having room for another companion — is reported by several players but is not confirmed as a requirement. The September 8 patch is relevant here: the **Companion x3 Barracks upgrade now only requires Companion x2**, rather than also needing the upgrade from the row above. If you were blocked by that chain before September 8, the block is gone.

### Why Brienne specifically

The single strongest player observation is a controlled comparison. One Reddit reply states that many runs as Wallace never produced the event, and that the first visit to Blackrock Vestiges as Brienne triggered the quest immediately. That is not proof of a hard requirement — it is one player's A/B result — but it matches every other success report collected, and the community wiki states the same condition independently: "Recruit Aodh through his one-time campaign event while playing Brienne Ironleg."

If you have been farming the area as Wallace, that is the first thing to change.

### What the event looks like

Reports describe a **quest marker over a group of figures** on the map — one player describes "symbols with all the people huddled in a group and a bubble that said 'quest'". This matters because players who were looking for a lone NPC or a boss walked past it. Watch the map for a clustered group icon with a quest bubble, not for a single character standing apart.

### Is the spawn random?

Probably, but not confirmed. The follow-up thread exists precisely because a player had met the other conditions and still saw nothing *[Unconfirmed]*. Community answers treat it as RNG within Act 2 rather than a fixed scripted tile: "Just RNG. He spawns in act 2 on Blackrock Vestiges." If the event does not appear, the community answer is to finish the act and try again, not to keep re-entering the same tile.

How many attempts this normally takes is not documented anywhere *[Unconfirmed]*.

## Step by step

1. **Start a run as Brienne Ironleg.** If you have not unlocked her yet, her quest chain is covered in the [Character Unlock Guide](/guides/character-unlock-guide).
2. **Play into Act 2.** Do not detour to the area in Act 1; the reported event is Act 2 only.
3. **Check your companion slots.** Barracks companion upgrades are the reported gate. After September 8 the third slot needs only the Companion x2 upgrade.
4. **Clear toward Blackrock Vestiges and watch the map**, not the ground. Look for a group of figures under a quest bubble.
5. **Complete the event.** Reports do not describe a fight requirement — the marker itself is the recruitment.
6. **Check the Codex.** A September 8 fix addressed companions not appearing correctly there. If he is missing from the Codex after the event, that bug is fixed; restart before assuming a failed run.

## Why it keeps failing

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Cleared Act 2, no event | Ran it as Wallace or Ruadh |
| Visited the area in Act 1 | Reported event is Act 2 |
| Walked the area, saw nothing | Looked for an NPC, not the group quest bubble |
| Event appeared but no companion | Companion slot not upgraded |
| Unlocked him, Codex empty | Pre-September-8 bug; fixed in the September 8 patch |
| Tried once, gave up | The spawn is reported as RNG within the act *[Unconfirmed]* |

One distinction worth repeating: "unlockable" in the August 13 notes was a statement that the content exists, not a promise that it appears on every Act 2 run. The volume of "still couldn't get him" threads is consistent with a random event that players were searching for with the wrong hero.

## Co-op and the rest of the roster

Whether a co-op client also receives Aodh from the host's event is **not documented** *[Unconfirmed]*. The safe assumption is that the player who triggers the event gets him; if you are playing co-op specifically for this unlock, run the attempt solo first.

Paper Cult has also not published how many companions exist in total, or the full roster. Community sources disagree — one names Marriott of Clan McGregor, another names none at all. Your Codex is the only reliable count. The [Battalion & Companion Guide](/guides/battalion-companion-guide) explains why this page does not print a roster.

## FAQ

**Can Wallace unlock Aodh?**
No successful report with Wallace was found, and one player specifically reports many Wallace runs with no event. Every success report names Brienne. Paper Cult has not stated a hero requirement, so this remains a community conclusion *[Unconfirmed]*.

**Which act?**
Act 2, in **Blackrock Vestiges**, per multiple players. Paper Cult has not said this in any post checked for this page.

**Is Blackrock Vestiges a required stop or just a high-spawn area?**
Not confirmed. It is the only area named in success reports *[Unconfirmed]*.

**Do I need the Companion x3 upgrade?**
You need room for him. The September 8 patch reduced the x3 upgrade's prerequisites, so check your Barracks row before blaming the spawn.

**Is the event missable forever?**
"One-time campaign event" describes the event firing once per campaign, not once per save *[Unconfirmed]*. If you complete the act without it, start another run.

**Does he die permanently like a soldier?**
He is a named companion, not a procedural soldier. Permanent death is documented for the rank-and-file, not for named companions *[Unconfirmed]*.
