# Tears of Metal Co-op Guide — Team Comps, Roles, and Boss Coordination

> **Applies to Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Tears of Metal supports up to 4-player co-op, and the game changes significantly when you bring a team. Enemies scale with player count, but so do rewards — full squads also get higher drop rates, which makes the weapon merge system dramatically faster in multiplayer. This guide covers team composition, role distribution, battalion tactics, and the communication habits that separate wipe parties from victory screens.

---

## Best Team Compositions

### The Standard Comp (Most Consistent)

| Role | Hero | Primary Job |
|------|------|-------------|
| Anchor / Shot-caller | **Wallace** (longsword) | Holds the line, controls chokepoints, calls engagements |
| Pressure DPS | **Brienne** (unarmed) | Machine-gun per-hit damage, flanks, chases spawns |
| Stagger DPS | **Ruadh Stonecrusher** (hammer) | Interrupts elites and bosses with stagger, elite elimination |
| Flex | Any hero | Covers the team's weak spot or doubles the strongest role |

**Why this works:** Wallace anchors aggro and peel while Brienne applies relentless pressure and Ruadh shuts down the scariest enemy on the field with stagger. The flex slot doubles whatever the map demands — a second Wallace for safety, a second Ruadh for boss burst.

### The Double Wall (Safe but Slow)

| Role | Hero |
|------|------|
| Main Anchor | Wallace |
| Off-Anchor | Wallace (defensive emblems) |
| Pressure DPS | Brienne |
| Stagger DPS | Ruadh Stonecrusher |

**Best for:** First-time Act 3 attempts where survival matters more than speed. Two Wallaces make chokepoints nearly impassable.

### The Speedrun Comp (High Risk, High Reward)

| Role | Hero |
|------|------|
| Aggro Holder | Wallace (damage emblems) |
| Glass Cannon | Brienne (attack speed) |
| Glass Cannon | Ruadh Stonecrusher (impact) |
| Flex | Brienne or Ruadh (damage) |

**Best for:** Experienced teams farming materials. Everyone runs damage — higher drop rates from full squads make this the fastest way to feed the merge system. Do not attempt for first clears.

### The Beginner-Friendly Comp

| Role | Hero |
|------|------|
| Anchor / Guide | Wallace (experienced player) |
| Learn DPS | Brienne (new player) |
| Learn DPS | Ruadh Stonecrusher (new player) |
| Learn DPS | Wallace (new player) |

**Best for:** Teaching new players. The experienced Wallace player leads and calls shots while the rest learn their hero loops in relative safety.

## Role Distribution and Responsibilities

### Wallace (Anchor / Team Leader)
- **Call the shots** — The anchor should be the team's primary shot-caller. You control engagement timing.
- **Hold chokepoints** — Position in doorways and narrow corridors where your longsword hits everything.
- **Mark targets** — Use the ping system to highlight priority enemies (captains, spotters, banner bearers).
- **Watch the team's back** — In co-op, enemies often spawn behind the group. Wallace is tanky enough to peel.

### Brienne (Pressure DPS)
- **Focus the marked target** — The anchor marks, Brienne melts it with per-hit effects. Do not chase random enemies.
- **Flank during boss fights** — Position behind the boss to avoid cleave attacks aimed at the anchor.
- **Handle spawns** — When the anchor calls out a spawn, be the first to react — you are the fastest hero.
- **Do not overextend** — Brienne has no reach. Do not chase enemies into unknown rooms.

### Ruadh Stonecrusher (Stagger DPS)
- **Interrupt the scariest enemy** — Your hammer's stagger cancels wind-ups outright; call your interrupt so the team can punish.
- **Elite and boss duty** — Stagger chains shut down boss patterns. Save multi-bar commands for the stun windows you create.
- **Respect the wind-up** — Whiffing a hammer swing is the most expensive mistake in the game. Commit only on confirmed openings.

### Flex
- **Cover the gap** — If the team lacks a role (no stagger, no peel), the flex picks it up with their build.

## How Progression Works in Co-op

| Mechanic | Shared? | Details |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Triskelle earned | Yes — each player gets full drops | Everyone earns independently |
| Artifact drops | No — each player sees their own drops | No fighting over loot |
| Dragon Stone Shards | Yes — each player gets their own shards | Boss kills grant shards to all |
| Village upgrades | No — each player upgrades their own village | Progression is individual — your upgrades do not apply to teammates |
| Battalion soldiers | No — each player has their own battalion | Soldiers are per-player |
| Weapon merges | No — per-run for each player | Bring your own merge plan; squads just multiply the drops feeding it |

### The Host Advantage

The player who hosts the session gets slightly more control:

- **Threat Level** visibility is shared, but the host controls when to advance
- **Village access** between runs is per-player, but the host can pause or end the session

## How Battalions Work in Multiplayer

Each player commands their own battalion of soldiers. Battalions do not share units — your Pikeman is yours, and your teammate's Shield Bearer is theirs.

### Multiplayer Battalion Tactics

- **Combine chokepoints** — Two battalions in the same corridor create an impassable wall. Use this for boss fights.
- **Stagger commands** — Do not use multi-bar commands simultaneously. One player using a multi-bar while others cover creates more value than everyone shouting at once.
- **One player manages spawns** — If the anchor holds the frontline, a DPS player's battalion handles incoming spawns from behind.
- **Veteran protection** — In co-op, veterans are even more valuable because the team covers each other. If a veteran is low, call for cover (see the [Battalion & Companion Guide](/guides/battalion-companion-guide)).

## Boss Fight Coordination

### General Boss Rules for Co-op

1. **Anchor gets first hit** — Let Wallace engage first to establish aggro
2. **Position in a half-circle** — Anchor at center, DPS flanking, flex covering spawns
3. **Rez priority** — Anchor first, then stagger DPS (aggro control before damage)
4. **Stagger coordination** — Count down to 3 for synchronized heavy attacks to stagger bosses
5. **Save multi-bar commands for stun windows** — Stunned boss + 4 multi-bar commands = massive burst

### Recommended Team for Each Act

| Act | Boss | Team Comp | Key Strategy |
|-----|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Act 1 | **Gilles the Hog** | Any balanced comp | No healing in the arena — arrive full and don't trade hits |
| Act 2 | **Iseult the Banshee** | 1 Wallace + 1 Brienne + 1 Ruadh + 1 flex | Ruadh interrupts her phase combos; everyone dodges sideways through the recovery windows |
| Act 3 | Unnamed (The Scar) | 1 Wallace + 1 Brienne + 1 Ruadh + 1 flex | Everyone needs Corruption Resist; dedicated spawn killer, full-aggro discipline |

## Communication Tips

### Essential Ping Uses
- **Ping enemy** — Highlight a priority target
- **Ping location** (double-tap ping) — "Go here" or "danger here"
- **Ping item** (look at item + ping) — "Pick this up" or "I need this"

### Callouts to Use

| Situation | Callout | Response |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Captain spotted | "Captain [direction]" | Everyone stops, focus fire |
| Reinforcements incoming | "Reinforcements [direction]" | Fastest player peels off, handles spawn |
| Low on stamina | "Stamina low, need a beat" | Anchor holds aggro, team backs off |
| Boss telegraph | "Big swing incoming" | Everyone dodge or block |
| Stagger ready | "Stagger in 3-2-1" | Team syncs heavy attacks into the stun window |

### Pre-Run Checklist

Before entering the expedition, confirm with your team:

1. Everyone has **Corruption Resist** for Act 3? (If no, reset planning)
2. Emblem preferences — avoid doubling roles (two damage Briennes are fine; two anchors are not)
3. Who leads (shot-caller) — usually the Wallace player
4. Merge targets — whose weapon is feeding this run? Squads should feed one player's merge at a time for the fastest Reinforced tiers (see the [Weapon Merge Guide](/guides/weapon-merge-guide))

## Co-op Etiquette Tips

| Do | Do Not |
|----|--------|
| Ask what emblem/artifact the team is running | Join without checking party composition |
| Share your extra potions if a teammate is struggling | Hoard resources your teammate needs |
| Wait for the team before engaging bosses | Rush ahead and trigger encounters solo |
| Ping items and enemies | Mark everything (ping fatigue is real) |
| Stay in voice chat or text chat | Play silently — coordination matters |

## Emblem Stacking in Co-op

When multiple players run the same emblem, effects compound — stack a second attacker's per-hit emblem and the team's proc rate roughly doubles, and two players stacking the same aura extend the value across the team's positioning. Exact multipliers are community-documented and have shifted between updates, so the practical rule is: **one strong shared emblem across two players beats two different half-leveled emblems.** See the [Emblem & Artifact Guide](/guides/emblem-artifact-guide) for the full emblem list.
