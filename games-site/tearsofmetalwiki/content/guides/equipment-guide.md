# Tears of Metal Equipment Guide — Armor, Gear Slots & Stat Priority

> **Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Your loadout in Tears of Metal is built from four layers: the **weapon** (merge-scaling damage), **two emblems** (attack elements and synergies), **artifacts** (passive bonuses from the Armory), and **defensive gear** (armor and block/parry tools). This guide explains each layer, how many slots you get, and how to prioritize stats for each hero.

> **Values caveat:** artifact names and stat values below are community-reported and unverified — exact numbers may differ in current builds. Treat them as directional guidance, not official data.

---

## The Four Loadout Layers

| Layer | What It Is | Where It Lives |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Weapon** | Your attack's base damage and skill tree | Carried in-run; merged at the Smithy |
| **Emblems** | 2 equipped — Primary (element) + Secondary (synergy) | Unlocked via Mystic Altar |
| **Artifacts** | Passive bonus items (a large pool) | Equipped from the Armory |
| **Defensive gear** | Armor, shields, and block/parry equipment | Found in-run and between runs |

---

## Gear Slots: How Many Do You Get?

The confirmed part is the **Armory**: its upgrade level determines how many artifacts you can carry between runs.

| Armory Level | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| Level 1 | Baseline storage |
| Level 2 | +1 artifact slot — unlocks carrying artifacts between runs |
| Level 3+ | More slots for build consistency |

**The rule:** every run starts from scratch unless you upgrade the Armory. New players should make it the first village investment (see the [Village Upgrade Guide](/guides/village-upgrade-guide) for the full priority order).

> **Honest note:** the exact maximum slot counts for artifacts and defensive gear are not publicly itemized. What is confirmed is the Armory's role — treat specific slot numbers as unverified until documented.

---

## Armor and Block: The Defensive Layer

Armor in Tears of Metal is not just a stat — it is a system tied to blocking:

| Mechanic | How It Works |
|----------|--------------|
| **Block** | Hold block to reduce incoming damage by 60%; costs stamina |
| **Parry** | Tap block just before an attack lands — negates all damage and staggers the enemy |
| **Armor effects** | Equipment with armor/block bonuses (e.g., Stag Helm: +10% block efficiency) |
| **Unblockable attacks** | Red-glowing strikes cannot be blocked — dodge instead |

**Block efficiency** (more stamina per block) and **parry window** (easier timing) are the two defensive stats that matter most — they are why survivability-oriented equipment outperforms raw damage on new players.

## Stat Priority by Hero

| Hero | Priority Order |
|------|----------------|
| **Wallace** (longsword) | Survivability > damage > utility — damage reduction, block efficiency, health |
| **Brienne** (unarmed) | Attack speed > per-hit effects > mobility — she must not facetank |
| **Ruadh Stonecrusher** (hammer) | Stagger > single-hit damage > stamina recovery — positioning is survival |

**Universal rule:** synergy beats rarity. A Common artifact that fits your build outperforms a Legendary that does nothing for it.

## Rarity Tiers

| Rarity | Relative Power | Special Effect |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| Common | Baseline | None |
| Uncommon | Slightly stronger than Common | Minor bonus effect |
| Rare | Notably stronger than Common | Major bonus effect |
| Legendary | Much stronger than Common | Unique, build-defining effect |

Legendaries define builds (e.g., Guardian's Pendant — survive lethal damage at 1 HP once per run) but only when their effect matches your playstyle.

---

## Stat Priority Rules of Thumb

1. **Damage reduction and health first** — the healing economy is brutal; not dying is the best DPS
2. **Block/parry stats for defensive play** — block efficiency and parry window compound into less chip damage
3. **Attack speed and per-hit stats for speedsters** — Brienne scales with triggers, not single-hit numbers
4. **Stagger and weight for heavy hitters** — Ruadh's kit is built around interrupting, not trading
5. **Corruption Resist before Act 3** — mandatory for the final region (see the [Corruption Guide](/guides/corruption-guide))

## FAQ

**Q: How do I get more gear slots?**
Upgrade the Armory in the Village Hub — it determines how many artifacts you carry between runs.

**Q: Does rarity matter more than synergy?**
No. A synergistic Common beats a mismatched Legendary every time.

**Q: What is the best defensive stat?**
Block efficiency and parry window — they make the game's core defense (block/parry) cheaper and easier. Damage reduction is second.

**Q: Is there armor beyond blocking?**
Armor appears as equipment effects (damage reduction, block efficiency) rather than a single armor number. Specific armor piece types beyond these are not yet documented.
