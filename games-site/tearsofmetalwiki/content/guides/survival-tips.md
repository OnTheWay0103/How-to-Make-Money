# Tears of Metal Survival Tips — The Discipline That Keeps You Alive

**Tears of Metal Early Access v1.0 / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Tears of Metal kills players in three ways: running out of stamina at the wrong moment, missing a parry window, and refusing to retreat. All three are discipline problems, not gear problems. This guide covers the survival fundamentals — the stamina economy, the parry timing table, elemental weaknesses, and the retreat rules — that keep your battalion and your hero intact through all three acts.

---

## 1. The Stamina Law (Everything Governed by One Bar)

| Action | Stamina Cost | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| Light Attack | 8 | Fast, low damage, builds combo meter |
| Heavy Attack | 20 | Slow, high damage, breaks guard |
| Dodge Roll | 15 | I-frames for 0.4s |
| Sprint | 10/s | Cannot block while sprinting |
| Block | 5/s | Reduces damage by 60%; chip damage still applies |
| Parry | 10 | 0.2s window; consumes enemy stamina if successful |
| Weapon Art | 30 | Powerful, costly — use deliberately |

**The rule of thumb:** never let stamina drop below 20. Below that you cannot dodge or parry, and a single mistake means eating a full combo. Practically:

1. **End your combos before the bar empties.** Two light attacks + a roll leaves you safe; a full combo + empty bar leaves you dead.
2. **Block costs more than you think.** 5/s sounds cheap, but holding block through a four-hit combo is 20 stamina — a parry costs half that and pays back.
3. **Sprint is a decision, not a habit.** Sprinting into a pack means no block for the first two seconds of the fight.
4. **The "20 stamina floor" is law.** Under 20, disengage — the recovery is faster than the respawn.

## 2. Parry Windows by Enemy Type

| Enemy Type | Parry Window | Visual Cue | Audio Cue |
|------------|-------------|------------|-----------|
| Humanoid | 0.25s before impact | Weapon flashes white | High-pitched ring |
| Beast | 0.15s before impact | Eyes glow red | Deep growl |
| Mechanical | 0.3s before impact | Joints spark | Metallic screech |
| Boss | 0.2s before impact | Screen vignette pulse | Boss-specific roar |

**Practice rule:** visit the **Training Grounds** in the starting hub — the dummy cycles through all four enemy type patterns, and 15 minutes there beats 50 deaths in the field. The parry timing is the difference between a free counter and a full combo on your health bar.

## 3. The Elemental Weakness System

Every enemy has a weakness to one of five elements and a resistance to another:

| Element | Weak Against | Resisted By |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Fire | Corrupted and armored enemies | Fire-resistant beasts |
| Frost | Fast, low-armor enemies | Frost-resistant hulks |
| Lightning | Swarms | — |
| Corruption | Act 3 specialists | — |
| Arcane | Heavily armored elites | — |

**Why this matters for survival:** fighting with the wrong element doubles your time-to-kill — and time-to-kill is survival. A fight that takes 40 seconds instead of 20 is a fight that gives the enemy 20 more seconds to break your stamina. Match elements (via your equipped emblems — see the [Emblem & Artifact Guide](/guides/emblem-artifact-guide)) before you commit to a zone.

## 4. Threat-Level Awareness

| Threat Level | What It Means | Survival Response |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Low | New spawn zones | Farm freely, practice parries |
| Medium | Standard expedition | Normal discipline: stamina floor, element match |
| High | Late act, corrupted zones | Retreat rules in effect (below) |
| Extreme | Act 3 Corruption Zone | Full loadout check: Salves, element match, veteran battalion |

The [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide) explains how threat scales with act progression — the short version: high-threat zones demand the same fights you'd win at medium, but with zero margin for sloppy stamina.

## 5. The Retreat Rules (Survival's Most Underrated Skill)

1. **Retreat before the first battalion death, not after.** When a soldier drops below 40% HP in a high-threat zone, that's the retreat signal — one death often chains into a wipe.
2. **Retreat is a direction, not a fight.** Disengage to a cleared area, heal, re-pull with fresh stamina. The enemy line does not chase far.
3. **Corrupted zones compound.** The [Corruption Guide](/guides/corruption-guide) details how corruption drains your battalion — corrupted zones are the #1 place players refuse to retreat and pay for it.
4. **Extraction over completion.** A failed objective with a living battalion beats a completed objective with a dead one. Salvage the run, keep the veterans.
5. **The 40% rule for heroes.** If your hero drops below 40% HP with the bar under 20 stamina — retreat. Those are the deaths that end runs.

## 6. Survival Gear and Consumable Checklist

| Item | Use | Priority |
|------|-----|----------|
| Salves / healing consumables | Post-hit recovery | Always carry 3+ |
| Stamina consumables | Emergency recovery | Carry 1-2 for boss fights |
| Anti-corruption consumables | Corrupted zones | Mandatory in Act 3 |
| Element-match emblems | Damage efficiency | Equip before the zone |

**Budget order:** consumables > weapon upgrades > armor, for survival purposes — a weapon you can swing is worth more than armor you're too dead to wear. See the [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-guide) for where consumables and upgrade materials drop reliably.

---

## 7. The Survival Loop, Summarized

Enter every fight with stamina over 20 and an element-matched loadout → parry on the cues (0.25s humanoid / 0.15s beast / 0.3s mechanical / 0.2s boss) → retreat on the 40% rules → restock consumables at the village → repeat. Master that loop and the [Survival Mode Guide](/guides/survival-mode) becomes the natural next challenge — survival mode is the same discipline with the margin removed entirely.
