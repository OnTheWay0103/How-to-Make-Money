# Tears of Metal Corruption Guide — How the Corruption Mechanic Works

> **Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Corruption is Tears of Metal's signature late-game threat. It arrives in Act 3 and changes everything: zones drain your health while you stand in them, enemy attacks carry Corruption damage over time, and the final region punishes players who arrive unprepared. This guide explains what is confirmed about the mechanic, how to counter it, and how to plan your Act 3 runs around it.

---

## What Corruption Is

Corruption is a **health-draining damage-over-time (DoT) mechanic** tied to the game's third act. What is confirmed:

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Corruption zones** | Areas in Act 3 that drain your health rapidly while you are inside them |
| **Corruption attacks** | Enemy and boss attacks in the corrupted region apply Corruption DoT on hit |
| **Counter** | Corruption Resist (emblem or artifact) reduces both zone drain and DoT damage |
| **Why it matters** | DoT kills you through healing potions — raw healing is not enough without resist |

Thematically, Corruption is the "infected highlands" of the Scottish campaign — the corruption you have been fighting against all game becomes the environment itself.

---

## How Corruption Damage Works

Based on confirmed sources and the emblem system documentation:

- **Zone drain** — standing in a Corruption zone applies continuous damage. The deeper into Act 3, the stronger the drain.
- **Corruption DoT** — hits from corrupted enemies and boss attacks apply a damage-over-time effect that ticks independently of normal damage.
- **The potion problem** — DoT outpaces single heals. Players without resist die through healing potions, which is why every late-game guide calls resist mandatory.

---

## The Counter: Corruption Resist

The **Corruption Resist emblem** is the primary counter:

| Source | Known Effect |
|--------|--------------|
| Corruption Resist emblem | Substantial corruption resistance; reduces both zone drain and DoT damage (exact percentages are community-documented) |
| Corruption Resist artifacts | Resistance sources also exist as artifact effects — exact bonuses vary by drop |

**Second layer:** the **Regen emblem** (passive healing over time) offsets Corruption tick damage, reducing consumable usage in the final region. **Guardian** (shield at low HP) is a third-layer safety net for first-time Act 3 completions.

---

## Corruption Management Strategy

### Before Act 3 (Act 1-2 prep)

1. **Slot at least one Corruption Resist source before the final region** — do not discover you need it after the first zone drain
2. **Keep Regen available** — it stacks with resist for the best survival package
3. **Plan your route** — the more you clear, the higher your Threat Level, and Threat Level compounds the final region's difficulty (see [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide))

### During Act 3

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Do not stand in zones** | Zone drain is avoidable — path around corrupted ground |
| **Kill corrupted elites fast** | DoT from their attacks stacks; long fights bleed you dry |
| **Heal before the boss arena** | As everywhere, boss arenas have no healing — enter at full health |
| **Boss DoT > zone DoT** | Boss attacks carry the strongest Corruption application; dodge or block over trading hits |

### The Anti-Corruption Checklist

- [ ] Corruption Resist emblem or artifact equipped
- [ ] Regen emblem as backup
- [ ] Full healing stock before the final region
- [ ] Route planned to minimize zone exposure

- Whether zone drain scales with difficulty level
- Whether specific corrupted enemies apply different DoT strengths
- Exact Corruption Resist percentages on artifacts (only the emblem's documented values are reliable)

## FAQ

**Q: Is Corruption only in Act 3?**
Yes — Corruption zones and the health drain arrive with Act 3 (the Corruption Zone / The Scar). Earlier acts have corrupted enemies but no zone mechanic.

**Q: Do I really need Corruption Resist?**
Yes. Without at least one resist source, Act 3's DoT kills you through healing potions. It is the most frequently repeated late-game advice in the community.

**Q: Does Regen replace Corruption Resist?**
No — Regen mitigates, Resist prevents. Run them together if possible; Regen alone is not enough.

**Q: Why does Act 3 feel like the difficulty spikes?**
Corruption DoT plus Threat Level escalation plus no healing relief. See the [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide) and the [Progression Walkthrough](/guides/progression-walkthrough) for the full picture.
