## Patch History

### Launch Build (July 22, 2026)

The Early Access launch build introduced the full campaign:

- **Three heroes:** Wallace (longsword), Brienne (unarmed fast attacks), Ruadh Stonecrusher (giant hammer)
- **Campaign structure:** run-based acts across the invaded Scottish island — start in your village, move from battlefield to battlefield, collect run-based upgrades, and bank permanent currencies that carry over whether you win or lose
- **Core systems:** battalion commands (spearmen, infantry, archers), 100+ artifacts, emblems and charms, settlement expansion between runs, permanent soldier progression
- **Co-op:** 1–4 players online, crossplay between Steam and PC Game Pass
- **Reception:** Very Positive reviews (87–92% depending on the reporting window), No. 7 on Steam's Popular New Releases chart, peaking around 4,000–4,500 concurrent players

**Known launch issues:** multiplayer desync (hosts that are far away or on unstable connections), no mid-campaign saves, and scattered reports of lost progress after crashes. All three are addressed below.

### Patch V.0.8.57278 (July 24, 2026)

The first post-launch patch, shipped two days after release:

- **Character unlock change:** "Ruadh is now unlocked after defeating the Banshee." If you previously had Ruadh available from the start, that changed with this patch — the hammer hero now requires a story boss kill (Iseult, The Banshee)
- **Crossplay disabled in this version:** Paper Cult turned crossplay off in the live build while they stabilize it. Players who need crossplay can opt into the **"Crossplay" Beta branch** on Steam to stay on the previous patch (Steam → Properties → Betas)
- **Minor fixes:** bundled with the above, per the patch announcement

> **Why this matters for guides on this site:** our [Heroes Tier List](/guides/heroes-tier-list), [Brienne Build Guide](/guides/brienne-build-guide), and [Ruadh Build Guide](/guides/ruadh-build-guide) reflect the post-patch unlock reality. If an older video or article tells you Ruadh is free at start, it predates July 24.

### Stability Patch (late July 2026)

The first major stability update focused on the worst launch complaints. Confirmed contents:

| Fix | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| **Save recovery system** | Detects corrupted/missing save data and restores the last good state instead of forcing a total reset — the fix for launch-window progress-loss reports |
| **Iseult the Banshee fixes** | Resolved multiple issues with the boss, including a reported softlock |
| **Health bar & Charm bug fixes** | Several health-bar display issues and Charm-related bugs fixed |
| **Castle Records tracking** | Improved the campaign record-keeper's tracking |
| **Enemy spawn logic** | Improved spawn behavior (part of the stability pass) |
| **Texture size reductions** | Smaller textures — real help for Steam Deck and lower-end PCs (see our [Steam Deck Guide](/guides/steam-deck-guide)) |
| **Level improvements** | Several levels updated with gameplay and environmental improvements |

**Not fixed yet:** multiplayer desync and mid-campaign saves remain on the confirmed to-do list (details below).

### 100K Sales Milestone (August 2026)

Paper Cult announced Tears of Metal passed **100,000 copies sold on Steam** in the first weeks of Early Access. The announcement doubled as a roadmap update: the studio confirmed **mid-campaign saves are their top community request and top priority**, and reiterated that netcode/desync fixes come next before new content.

## Known Issues & Developer-Confirmed Priorities

Paper Cult has been unusually transparent about what's broken. The publicly confirmed list, in priority order:

### 1. Mid-Campaign Saves — IN DEVELOPMENT (Top Priority)

- **Status:** confirmed "in development," no release window
- **Why it's hard:** the team says mid-run saving requires reworking the meta-progression system so save-scumming can't be abused (a saved run's artifacts and battalion state would otherwise become free rerolls)
- **Current state:** campaigns run 1–2 hours with no mid-campaign save; quitting or failing ends the run (permanent meta progression is still banked)
- See the [Save System Guide](/guides/save-system-guide) for the full picture and planning tips

### 2. Multiplayer Desync — CONFIRMED, BEING FIXED

- **Status:** acknowledged as the #2 priority, in progress
- **Symptoms:** desync when connecting to hosts who are far away or running less stable connections; rubber-banding and invisible join failures
- **Current mitigation:** host choice matters most — see the [Multiplayer & Connection Fix Guide](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide)
- **Crossplay note:** crossplay itself was temporarily disabled in V.0.8.57278 (Beta branch available); expect it to return once netcode stabilizes

### 3. Meta-Progression & Difficulty Balance — UNDER REVIEW

- The team says they are revisiting **meta-progression systems** and **game difficulty balance** before adding content. What that means concretely (nerfs, buffs, progression tuning) has not been detailed yet

## What the Ruadh Unlock Change Means for Players

The V.0.8.57278 change ("Ruadh is now unlocked after defeating the Banshee") had real gameplay consequences that still confuse players:

| Before the patch | After the patch |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Ruadh Stonecrusher available at the start | Ruadh requires defeating Iseult, The Banshee (Act 2's boss) |
| Fresh saves could pick any hero | Fresh saves start with Wallace; Brienne's unlock state is unchanged (Brienne is available as a starter hero) |
| Hammer mains could skip the longsword entirely | Every account plays through at least one longsword campaign before the hammer unlocks |

**Practical effects:**
- **New players:** your first campaign is effectively Wallace-first. The [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) and [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) both assume this reality
- **Veterans on old saves:** if you already had Ruadh, nothing changes — the gate applies to unlock progression, not existing rosters
- **Co-op parties:** a host without Ruadh can't play him; a host who has him can — unlock state follows the host's progression in party sessions (see the [Multiplayer & Connection Fix Guide](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide))

If a future patch moves unlock gates again, we'll flag it here the day it lands.

## The Difficulty & Meta-Progression Review — What Might Change

Paper Cult confirmed they're revisiting **meta-progression systems** and **game difficulty balance** before adding content. Nothing has been announced — the following is the community's working list of candidates, clearly labeled as speculation:

**Meta-progression candidates (community discussion, not confirmed):**
- **Save-scumming guardrails** — mid-campaign saves need anti-abuse design; any meta-progression change likely pairs with that system (see [Save System Guide](/guides/save-system-guide))
- **Village cost curve** — early players report the Armory/Recruitment Tent costs feel steep; rebalancing is plausible given the review
- **Soldier progression pacing** — veterans are so valuable that losing them feels catastrophic; the team may smooth the replacement curve

**Difficulty candidates (community discussion, not confirmed):**
- **Threat Level tuning** — the "clear the map, die to the boss" complaint is the most common negative review theme (see [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide))
- **The Scar / Act 3 tuning** — corruption DoT plus boss buffs is the consensus spike point
- **Boss fight adjustments** — Iseult the Banshee already received fixes; further tuning is plausible

**Bottom line:** expect balance changes in the coming patches. Treat tier lists and build guides as living documents — including ours, which we re-check after every update.

## FAQ

**Q: What version is Tears of Metal on right now?**
The latest publicly confirmed patch is V.0.8.57278 (July 24, 2026), plus the stability patch that followed. We will update this page within 48 hours of each new announcement.

**Q: How long will Early Access last?**
4–12 months per the Steam page. No firm full-release date yet.

**Q: Was there a demo or beta before launch?**
Yes — a Steam open beta ran in April 2025 (distilled Act 1), and a separate demo app was listed on Steam pre-launch. Beta feedback went through dedicated Discord channels.

**Q: Does the game get more expensive later?**

**Q: Will my progress carry to full release?**
Unverified, but standard EA practice for roguelites is that meta progression carries. The developer's mid-campaign save work is explicitly designed around preserving the meta progression economy — a good sign. We'll confirm when Paper Cult states it.

**Q: Why do some guides on this site reference old hero names?**
Older pages here (and elsewhere) used placeholder names like "Vanguard/Berserker/Marksman." The real roster is Wallace, Brienne, and Ruadh Stonecrusher — corrected pages are flagged with a note. This page uses only verified names.
