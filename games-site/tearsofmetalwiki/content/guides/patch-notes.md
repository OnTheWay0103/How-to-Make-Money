## What Has Shipped So Far

### Launch (July 22, 2026)

The Early Access launch build introduced the full campaign:

- **Three heroes:** Wallace (longsword), Brienne (unarmed fast attacks), Ruadh Stonecrusher (giant hammer)
- **Campaign structure:** run-based acts across the invaded Scottish island — start in your village, move from battlefield to battlefield, collect run-based upgrades, and bank permanent currencies that carry over whether you win or lose
- **Core systems:** battalion commands and soldier recruitment with permadeath and permanent skill progression, emblems (18 types) and artifacts, and village expansion between runs (Armory, Recruitment Tent, Smithy, Apothecary, Mystic Altar)
- **Platforms:** Steam, the Microsoft Store, and PC Game Pass — all three from day one
- **Co-op:** 1–4 players online, with crossplay between the Steam and Microsoft Store / PC Game Pass builds
- **Reception:** "Very Positive" on Steam (checked August 25, 2026), with an all-time peak of roughly 6,200 concurrent players (SteamCharts)

**Known launch issues:** multiplayer desync with distant or unstable hosts, no mid-campaign saves, and scattered reports of lost progress after crashes. All three are addressed below.

### Patch History — Every Confirmed Build (July 22 – August 14, 2026)

Paper Cult shipped seven public builds in the first three weeks of Early Access. This is the version history as it stands on **September 11, 2026** — **v0.14.58630 (August 14, 2026) is the newest confirmed build**, and no public patch has landed since.

| Version | Date | Headline |
|---------|------|----------|
| **v0.8.57278** | July 24, 2026 | First full post-launch patch: hero-unlock changes (Ruadh becomes unlockable by defeating Iseult the Banshee), boss block-breaking attacks made parryable, fixes for an invincible Act 3 Giant and an invisible Banshee, a new Common Charm, and the max-framerate setting finally applying (default cap cut from 120 to 60) |
| **v0.9.57408** | July 28, 2026 | Castle Records overhaul and the **save-recovery system** — detects corrupted or missing save data and restores the last good state instead of forcing a total reset — plus boss fixes (Iseult the Banshee, Harold), enemy-spawn fixes and level rebuilds |
| **v0.10.57598** | July 31, 2026 | Optimisation hotfix for Acts 1 and 2, plus army, commander and Castle Records fixes |
| **v0.11.57863** | August 1, 2026 | Large balance patch: meta-progression and economy rework, Charm tier reclassification, Act 2 difficulty-curve smoothing, and Beacons |
| **v0.11.57874.1** | August 9, 2026 | Hotfix for a sudden mouse-stutter issue traced to the Epic Online Services overlay |
| **v0.12.58540** | August 13, 2026 | Networking overhaul; **co-op revive changed from a coin cost to a health cost**; first named companion; Charm and Emblem balancing (see the [Co-op Revive Guide](/guides/co-op-revive-guide)) |
| **v0.14.58630** | August 14, 2026 | Multiplayer bug fixes — desync, boss spawn and campaign map fixes. **This is the current build.** |

**Two things to know about this table:**

- **There is no v0.13 in any public channel.** The build numbers jump from 0.12 to 0.14. The numbers are internal build identifiers, not a release counter — don't read a missing number as a withdrawn patch.
- **Version mismatch is real, and it explains most "can't join" errors.** Steam approves builds faster than the Microsoft Store / PC Game Pass pipeline, so after each patch crossplay is temporarily unavailable on the default Steam branch until the Microsoft Store build catches up. A separate Steam beta branch named **"crossplay"** is kept matched to the Game Pass build so the two can still play together in the meantime. See the [Multiplayer & Connection Fix Guide](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide).

**Still not fixed:** multiplayer desync (improved, not eliminated) and mid-campaign saves remain on the confirmed to-do list — details below.

## The Official Early Access Roadmap (Announced Late August 2026)

> **This is a plan, not shipped content.** In the same news cycle as the 100K sales announcement (late August 2026), Paper Cult outlined a **three-phase plan** for the rest of Early Access. **None of Phase 2 or Phase 3 is in the live build as of September 11, 2026**, and the studio attached no dates, no version numbers and no ETAs to any of it. Treat everything in this section as intent, not as a feature list you can play today.

| Phase | Focus | What Paper Cult actually said |
|-------|-------|-------------------------------|
| **Phase 1 — in progress** | Stability | Networking, crash and performance work continues. The **next patch is described as a "big push for stability"** — i.e. the patch after v0.14.58630 is not a content patch. |
| **Phase 2 — planned** | System reworks driven by feedback | More **build-crafting agency** and clearer distinction between build directions (Emblems / Charms / Allies); **Emblems made more meaningful** with reworked Emblem families and better synergies between Emblems, other elements and powers; **meta-progression rebalanced to reduce grind**. Stated goal: runs should feel less repetitive. |
| **Phase 3 — later** | New content | Major new content. Paper Cult said some content will be "peppered throughout" Phase 2, but the big additions come after the Phase 2 reworks. The studio **named the Plague Doctor as a hero "coming later."** |

**Also on the table:** Paper Cult said it is **considering a new Steam Beta Branch** to test in-progress patches — such as the Phase 2 system reworks — before they reach the main branch, keeping the default branch more stable. This would be a second beta branch alongside the existing "crossplay" branch described above.

**On the Plague Doctor specifically:** the only confirmed facts are that Paper Cult named it and that it arrives "later" (Phase 3). **No unlock conditions, no mechanics, no abilities and no date are public.** Do not treat any guide, video or list that "explains" the Plague Doctor's kit as sourced — nothing has been published. Note also that this is a *separate* item from the two unnamed heroes already visible in-game but not unlockable (see the [Character Unlock Guide](/guides/character-unlock-guide)); Paper Cult has not said the Plague Doctor is one of those silhouettes.

## Developer-Confirmed Priorities

Paper Cult has been unusually transparent about what's next. The publicly confirmed list, in priority order:

### 1. Mid-Campaign Saves — IN DEVELOPMENT (Top Priority)

- **Status:** confirmed "in development," no release window
- **Why it's hard:** the team says mid-run saving requires reworking the meta-progression system so save-scumming can't be abused (a saved run's artifacts and battalion state would otherwise become free rerolls)
- **Current state:** campaigns run without a mid-campaign save; quitting or failing ends the run (permanent meta progression is still banked)
- See the [Save System Guide](/guides/save-system-guide) for the full picture and planning tips

### 2. Multiplayer Desync — CONFIRMED, BEING FIXED

- **Status:** acknowledged as the #2 priority, in progress
- **Symptoms:** desync when connecting to hosts who are far away or running less stable connections; rubber-banding and invisible join failures
- **Current mitigation:** host choice matters most — see the [Multiplayer & Connection Fix Guide](/guides/multiplayer-connection-fix-guide)

### 3. The "Big Push for Stability" — CONFIRMED AS THE NEXT PATCH

- **Status:** announced. Paper Cult described the patch following v0.14.58630 as a "big push for stability" — networking, crash and performance work rather than new systems (Phase 1 of the roadmap above)
- **What that means for you:** don't wait for the next patch to add content. If you are holding off on buying until the game gets "more stuff," the roadmap says the systems work (Phase 2) comes *after* the stability pass, and the big content (Phase 3) comes after that
- **No date is public.** We update this page within 48 hours of the announcement

## FAQ

**Q: What is the current version of Tears of Metal?**
**v0.14.58630 — August 14, 2026** (multiplayer desync, boss spawn and campaign map fixes). That is the newest build in any public channel: no patch has shipped since, and Paper Cult's next one is the announced "big push for stability." Build numbers here come from the developer's own Steam announcements and are cross-checked against the community update-history wiki (see the patch table above).

**Q: What was the last patch before that?**
**v0.12.58540 — August 13, 2026**, the one that replaced coin-cost co-op revives with health-cost revives. There is no public v0.13; the numbers jump from 0.12 to 0.14, and they are internal build identifiers rather than a release counter.

**Q: What's coming in the next update?**
Paper Cult described the patch after v0.14.58630 as a **"big push for stability"** — networking, crash and performance work, not content. No date has been given.

**Q: Is Tears of Metal on console or Game Pass?**
**It has been on PC Game Pass since day one.** The Early Access launch on **July 22, 2026** covered Steam, the **Microsoft Store** and **PC Game Pass** simultaneously, and crossplay works between the Steam and Microsoft Store builds. What is *not* out yet is the **Xbox Series X|S console version**, announced for later in 2026 (it will join Xbox Game Pass then, with Xbox Play Anywhere). There is no announced PlayStation version.

**Q: When are mid-campaign saves coming?**
No date. Confirmed in development and the top community request. Note the dependency Paper Cult has described: mid-run saving needs the **meta-progression rework** (Phase 2) first, because a saved run's artifacts and battalion state would otherwise become free rerolls. Track this page for the announcement.

**Q: Are there really two new heroes coming?**
Paper Cult confirmed two heroes are visible in-game but not yet obtainable. Names, unlock conditions, and release timing are not public. Separately, the studio has **named one further hero — the Plague Doctor — as coming "later" in Phase 3**. Whether the Plague Doctor is one of the two in-game silhouettes has not been said, and no abilities or unlock conditions have been published for it.

**Q: How long will Early Access last?**
4–12 months per the Steam store page. No firm full-release date yet.

**Q: Was there a demo before launch?**
Yes — a free demo was listed on Steam before Early Access. Gilles the Hog, the Act 1 boss, was the demo's finale.

**Q: Does the game get more expensive later?**
The store page notes a planned price increase after Early Access. $24.99 is the Early Access price.

**Q: Will my progress carry to full release?**
Not officially stated. Standard Early Access practice for roguelites is that meta progression carries, and the developer's mid-campaign save work is explicitly designed around preserving the meta-progression economy — a good sign. We'll confirm when Paper Cult states it.

**Q: Why do some guides on this site reference old hero names?**
Older pages here (and elsewhere) used placeholder names like "Vanguard/Berserker/Marksman." The real roster is Wallace, Brienne, and Ruadh Stonecrusher — corrected pages are flagged with a note. This page uses only verified names.
