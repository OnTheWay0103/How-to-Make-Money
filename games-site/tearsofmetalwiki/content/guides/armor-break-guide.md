# Tears of Metal Armor & Shield Break Guide — How to Beat Armored Enemies

Your biggest hits are landing and the armored brute's health bar is barely moving. You are not under-leveled and your weapon is not broken — you are hitting **shield health** instead of health. Armored enemies in Tears of Metal carry a **grey bar above their red health bar**, and until that grey bar is gone, normal attacks are heavily blunted.

**The short answer:** stop trying to DPS through the grey bar. Every hero and weapon has at least one **armor-breaking combo** that strips the shield layer; use that until the grey bar is gone, then switch back to your normal damage rotation on the now-exposed red health.

> **Honesty note:** Paper Cult has not published a bestiary or a controls reference. The grey-bar mechanic below is corroborated across multiple independent community guides (listed in Sources), and anything they do not agree on — exact keybindings in particular — is labeled **[Unconfirmed]**.

> **Scope note:** this guide is about **armored and shielded enemies** — the ones that visibly shrug off your hits. For elite spawning rules (captains, boss bodyguards, corrupted elites) see the [Elite Enemy Guide](/guides/elite-enemy-guide); for block/parry/dodge fundamentals see the [Combat Tips Guide](/guides/combat-tips).

## What the Grey Bar Is (and Isn't)

Multiple independent guides describe the same system:

| Element | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| **Red health bar** | The enemy's real HP — damage here kills it |
| **Grey bar(s) above it** | Shield / armor health — a damage gate in front of the red bar |
| **Standard light & heavy attacks** | Deal very little to the grey layer; raw attack spam barely moves it |
| **Armor-breaking combos** | Specific moves that drain the grey bar efficiently — the intended counter |
| **Elites** | Can carry **two or three stacked grey bars**, so they take multiple armor-break passes before their real health is exposed |

Sources: [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-defeat-armored-enemies-in-tears-of-metal/), [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/tears-of-metal-how-to-break-armored-enemy-shields/), [moyens.net (ES)](https://es.moyens.net/gaming/guias-gaming/como-derrotar-enemigos-acorazados-lagrimas-metal/), [dungen.ru (RU)](https://dungen.ru/article/kak-slomat-bronu-tears-metal).

The grey bar is the game telling you this enemy is an **armor problem, not a damage problem**. Treat it as a targeting puzzle: strip the shield first, then kill.

## The Core Rule: Armor-Breaking Combos, Not Raw Damage

The single most important habit: **when you see a grey bar, switch to your armor-breaking move.** Community guides are consistent that:

- Every hero — and every weapon, including starter weapons — has **at least one armor-breaking combo**, so you are never locked out of the fight.
- Not all armor-break combos are equal — they deal **different amounts of shield damage**. If you have more than one, use the one that strips the grey bar fastest, especially against elites with multiple bars.
- Once the grey bar(s) are gone and only red health remains, **switch back to your normal light/heavy rotation** — armor-break moves are usually not your best pure DPS.

Source: [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/tears-of-metal-how-to-break-armored-enemy-shields/).

## Wallace's Documented Example: "Block Thrusts"

The most widely cited starter example belongs to Wallace (the longsword all-rounder). Community guides name the move **Block Thrusts** and report the input as:

> **Space, then left mouse button twice** — a thrust that drains shield health.

The exact binding is **[Unconfirmed]**: Paper Cult has not published an official controls reference, and default keybinds can be remapped. If the reported input does not match your bindings, open the controls menu and look for the move name (or test attacks while blocking) rather than assuming the game is broken.

Source for the reported input: [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/tears-of-metal-how-to-break-armored-enemy-shields/).

## Fighting Armored Elites (Two to Three Grey Bars)

Elite armored enemies are where the mechanic kills runs. The community-reported rules of engagement:

1. **Identify how many grey bars are stacked.** More bars = more shield passes before you can deal real damage.
2. **Open with your strongest armor-break combo and repeat it** until the grey layer is gone.
3. **Do not spend your specials / big burst on the grey bar.** Your ultimate may look impressive on the shield but is wasted while the red bar is still gated — save the burst for the exposed health.
4. **Armored captains interrupt careless aggression.** Fighting them the wrong way is how a run ends; treat a shielded elite like a mini-boss and respect its tells (see the [Elite Enemy Guide](/guides/elite-enemy-guide) and [Combat Tips Guide](/guides/combat-tips)).
5. **Let the battalion create angles.** Your soldiers can pressure an armored target while you circle for a safe shield-break opening — you do not have to tank the counter-attack to land your combo.

## Where You'll Meet Armored Enemies

- **Act 1:** armored enemies begin appearing — early, the game uses them to teach the grey-bar lesson.
- **Act 2:** they become **much more common**, and Act 2 is where the difficulty spike bites (see the [Threat Level & Difficulty Guide](/guides/threat-level-difficulty-guide)). Community-tracked patch notes for **v.0.11.57863** show Paper Cult rebalancing several Act 2 captain archetypes — a rework that includes sword-and-shield-style captains **[Unconfirmed]** — which points at this exact enemy family.
- **Higher Threat Level runs:** expect more elites and more stacked grey bars as the Threat meter rises.

Because Threat Level scales elite spawns, an armor-heavy route late in a run can pile up grey bars faster than you can strip them. Route with that in mind — see the [Progression Walkthrough](/guides/progression-walkthrough) for act-by-act routing.

## FAQ

**Q: Why does my weapon barely damage this enemy?**
Because you are hitting its grey shield bar, not its health. Use an armor-breaking combo until the grey layer is gone, then normal attacks work normally.

**Q: Is every armored enemy weak to the same combo?**
No — different armor-break moves deal different shield damage. Use the strongest one you have, and favor heavy, high-stagger heroes for groups of shielded enemies.

**Q: Do elites take longer because of more health?**
Not exactly — elites can carry two or three **grey bars**, so they need multiple armor-break passes before you can reach the red bar.

**Q: I tried the reported Block Thrusts input and nothing happened.**
The exact keybind is [Unconfirmed] and default bindings can be remapped. Check your controls menu for the move rather than assuming the mechanic is broken.
