# Tears of Metal Wallace Build Guide — Best Weapons, Emblems & Strategy

> **Early Access / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Wallace is Tears of Metal's longsword all-rounder and the game's default "learning hero" — the community and media pick, and the safest recommendation for a first run. He has no unique gimmick; instead, everything he does is solid: balanced damage, decent reach, and the most forgiving block/parry timing in the game.

This guide covers how to build him for solo play and co-op alike.

> **Values caveat:** artifact names and stat values below are community-reported and unverified — exact numbers may differ in current builds. Treat them as directional guidance, not official data.

## Best Weapons for Wallace

The longsword is Wallace's signature, and the **Weapon Merge system** is how you push it into endgame territory — two identical weapons combine at the Smithy for a stronger tier (Base + Base = Reinforced+1, doubling each tier up to Reinforced+3).

| Weapon | Why It's Best | Target Tier |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Longsword (best you can find)** | Balanced damage, reach, and parry timing — his kit is built around it | Reinforced+2 minimum |
| **Any longsword with block/parry bonuses** | Secondary stats that feed his defensive identity beat raw damage stats | Reinforced+1+ |

**Merging rule of thumb:** pick your primary longsword by Act 2 and merge every duplicate into it — Reinforced+3 requires 8 base copies, so specialization pays (see the [Weapon Merge Guide](/guides/weapon-merge-guide) for the full merge math).

> **Honest note:** specific named longswords are not yet publicly documented. Until patch notes confirm unique weapons, treat "best longsword" as the highest merged tier you can sustain.

## Best Emblems for Wallace

You equip **two emblems** — a Primary (attack element) and a Secondary (synergy). For Wallace:

| Emblem | Slot | Why |
|--------|------|-----|
| **Physical Damage** (+25% physical damage) | Primary | Direct multiplier on every longsword swing |
| **Critical Strike** (+15% crit chance) | Secondary | Burst against high-HP captains and bosses |
| **Regen** (heal 1% HP every 3 sec) | Swap-in | Sustain through fights without spending potions |

**Recommended start:** Physical Damage + Critical Strike. If you die to attrition, swap Critical Strike for Regen — Wallace's identity is outlasting fights, not one-shotting them.

## Best Artifacts for Wallace

**Survivability > damage > utility.** This is the single most important build rule for Wallace — stacking raw damage without survival is how new players die in Act 3.

| Artifact | Rarity | Effect | Priority |

| Ironheart Pauldrons | Common+ | +15% damage reduction | Core |
| Stag Helm | Common | +10% block efficiency | Core |
| Veteran's Targe | Uncommon | +15% parry window | High |
| Highland Stone | Rare | +12% physical damage, +8% move speed | High |
| Warlord's Medallion | Rare | +25% health, +10% damage below 50% HP | Medium |

**Artifact rule:** synergy beats rarity. A Common artifact that fits your defensive build outperforms a Legendary that does nothing for it (full breakdown in the [Emblem & Artifact Guide](/guides/emblem-artifact-guide)).

## Skill Priorities (Ability Points)

AP is per-run and does not carry over, so plan branches before spending:

1. **Block Efficiency** — more stamina per block; Wallace's defense is his identity
2. **Heavy Attack Mastery** — capstone cleave clears crowds in one swing
3. **Stamina Recovery** — chain blocks, parries, and attacks without pausing

> Branch names may vary by weapon roll — the principle is: defense first, crowd-cleave second, stamina third.

## Playstyle Tips

- **Parry to open fights** — Wallace's parry window is the most forgiving in the game. Tap block just before an attack lands, then punish the stagger with a riposte
- **Block, don't dodge, vs regular enemies** — blocking costs less stamina against telegraphed swings; save the dodge roll for AoEs and unblockable (red-glowing) attacks
- **Heavy attacks in crowds** — the longsword's heavy cleave hits multiple enemies; use it in doorways and choke points
- **Hold the line in co-op** — Wallace is the anchor. Let Brienne and Ruadh flank while you keep the swarm's attention

## FAQ

**Q: Is Wallace the best hero in the game?**
He is the best *all-rounder* and the safest pick. Ruadh has higher damage/stagger and Brienne has higher speed — Wallace wins on consistency.

**Q: Should a new player pick Wallace?**
Yes. He is the game's learning hero: easiest defensive timing, no bad matchups, and the strongest solo carry.

**Q: What if I keep seeing "Vanguard" builds online?**
Those guides predate/copy misreported information. The real hero with the defensive, sword-and-board-style kit is **Wallace**.
