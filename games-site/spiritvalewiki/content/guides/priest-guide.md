## Priest Overview

The Priest is the Acolyte's advanced class (unlocked at Job Level 50 through "Divine Calling" quest chain) and SpiritVale's dedicated healer and support class. No raid group succeeds without a skilled Priest. While low on damage, the Priest's healing output and utility buffs are unmatched.

## Priest Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Best healer in the game | Lowest damage output |
| Essential for all raid content | Very fragile — dies quickly |
| Powerful party buffs | Slowest solo leveling |
| Always in demand for groups | Mana-hungry |

## Best Priest Builds

### Pure Healer (Raid)
- **Skills**: Holy Light (max), Regen Aura (max), Purify, Resurrection, Holy Sanctuary
- **Gear**: INT (heal power), VIT, mana regen
- **Playstyle**: Position safely behind the group, heal priority targets, save Holy Sanctuary for phase emergencies

### Solo Priest (Leveling)
- **Skills**: Smite, Holy Light, Regen Aura, Blessing
- **Gear**: INT + VIT hybrid
- **Playstyle**: Smite enemies down, top yourself off with Holy Light. Slow but steady. Always party when possible.

## Priest Leveling Guide

Priests level the slowest. **Always find a party** — solo leveling takes longer than any other class.

| Level Range | Strategy |
|-------------|----------|
| 1-15 (Acolyte) | Party with DPS for faster clears |
| 15-30 (Acolyte) | Focus on healing dungeons for fast XP |
| 30-50 (Acolyte) | Join raid training groups, practice healing rotations |
| 50 (Priest) | Run "Divine Calling" and respec into your final build |
