## Priest Overview

The Priest is SpiritVale's dedicated healer and support class. No raid group succeeds without a skilled Priest. While low on damage, the Priest's healing output and utility buffs are unmatched.

## Priest Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Best healer in the game | Lowest damage output |
| Essential for all raid content | Very fragile — dies quickly |
| Powerful party buffs | Slowest solo leveling |
| Always in demand for groups | Mana-hungry |

## Best Priest Builds

### Pure Healer (Raid)
- **Skills**: Healing Wave (max), Guardian Spirit (max), Prayer of Mending, Mass Resurrection
- **Gear**: Full Wisdom, healing power, mana regen
- **Playstyle**: Position safely behind the group, heal priority targets, save Guardian Spirit for tank emergencies

### Shadow Priest (Solo Leveling)
- **Skills**: Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast, Vampiric Touch, Healing Wave (1 point)
- **Gear**: Intelligence + Wisdom hybrid
- **Playstyle**: DoT enemies, kite, self-heal. Slow but steady. Always party when possible.

## Priest Leveling Guide

Priests level the slowest. **Always find a party** — solo leveling takes 2-3x longer than other classes.

| Level Range | Strategy |
|-------------|----------|
| 1-15 | Party with DPS for questing |
| 15-30 | Focus on healing dungeons for fast XP |
| 30-50 | Join raid training groups, practice healing rotations |
