## Overview

World Bosses are SpiritVale's premier open-world endgame content. These massive encounters spawn on a fixed schedule across all five continents, dropping the highest-tier gear, crafting materials, and exclusive cosmetic items. This guide covers every active World Boss as of the **Sovereign's Wrath** patch.

---

## World Boss Schedule

All times are server time (ST). Bosses respawn **72 hours** after death, with a ±2 hour variance window.

| Boss | Continent | Spawn Day | Window | Recommended Players |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| The Verdant Titan | Dawnwood | Monday | 14:00–16:00 ST | 15–25 |
| Emberfang | Ashfall Steppes | Tuesday | 18:00–20:00 ST | 20–30 |
| Tidecaller Leviathan | Coral Abyss | Wednesday | 20:00–22:00 ST | 25–40 |
| Shadow Weaver | Gloomfen | Thursday | 16:00–18:00 ST | 15–20 |
| Storm Herald | Skyreach Summit | Friday | 19:00–21:00 ST | 20–25 |
| **Sovereign of Wrath** | The Rift | Saturday | 20:00–23:00 ST | 40–60 |

> **Note:** The Sovereign of Wrath is a 3-phase, multi-hour raid event requiring server-wide coordination.

## Boss Mechanics Breakdown

### The Verdant Titan (Dawnwood)

| Phase | HP Threshold | Mechanic | Counter |
|-------|-------------|----------|---------|
| 1 | 100%–70% | **Root Grab** — traps a random player in vines for 8 seconds | Assign 2 DPS to destroy roots instantly |
| 2 | 70%–30% | **Blight Spores** — AoE poison clouds that expand over time | Ranged DPS kite spores away from the raid |
| 3 | 30%–0% | **Enrage** — +100% damage, −50% cast time | Pop all defensive cooldowns; burn phase |

**Loot Table Highlights:**
- Verdant Bulwark (Tank Shield) — 8% drop chance
- Titan's Bloom (Healing Staff) — 5% drop chance
- Sprouting Heart (Accessory, +25% HP) — 3% drop chance

### Emberfang (Ashfall Steppes)

A pure DPS check. Emberfang has no adds, but its damage ramps continuously.

| Mechanic | Effect | Countermeasure |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| Inferno Breath | Cone AoE, 15k damage per tick | Tank faces boss away from raid |
| Lava Pool | Drops pools that linger for 45 seconds | Move the boss every 30 seconds |
| Ember Storm | Pulses 5k damage to all players; intensifies below 50% | Healer AoE rotations on cooldown |

**Loot Table Highlights:**
- Emberfang Greatsword (Two-Handed) — 10% drop chance
- Cindercloak (Back Armor) — 6% drop chance
- Schematic: Ashforged Set — 2% drop chance

### Tidecaller Leviathan (Coral Abyss)

The most mechanically complex World Boss. The Leviathan requires boats, diving, and coordinated platforming.

| Phase | Duration | Objective |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Surface | 2 min | DPS the exposed shell; dodge Water Spout |
| Dive | 3 min | Swim to the seabed, destroy 3 Coral Cores |
| Surface 2 | 2 min | Exposed weak point — burn all DPS |
| Enrage | Until dead | Rampaging, all damage doubled |

**Loot Table Highlights:**
- Leviathan's Trident (Spear) — 7% drop chance
- Depths Walker (Boots, +Swim Speed) — 4% drop chance
- Coral Crown (Helmet Cosmetic) — 1% drop chance

## Optimal Raid Composition

### Normal Difficulty

| Role | Count | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Tank | 2–3 | 1 main + 1–2 off-tanks for adds |
| Healer | 4–6 | Mix of single-target and AoE healers |
| Melee DPS | 6–8 | Keep behind the boss |
| Ranged DPS | 6–8 | Handle adds and mechanics |
| Support | 2–3 | Buffs, debuffs, cleansing |

### Nightmare Difficulty

Nightmare doubles boss HP and damage, adds new mechanics, and reduces the respawn timer to 48 hours.

| Role | Count | Key Difference |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Tank | 4 | Shared debuff — tanks must swap every 2 stacks |
| Healer | 8 | Required: 4 dedicated tank healers + 4 raid healers |
| DPS | 12–16 | Must execute mechanics perfectly; one mistake can wipe |
| Support | 4 | Two bards + two shamans for max uptime |

## Loot Distribution

Most guilds use a **DKP** (Dragon Kill Points) or **LC** (Loot Council) system. For pug raids, **Need Before Greed** is standard.

| Rarity | DKP Cost | Drop Rate |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Common (Blue) | 10–20 | 40% |
| Rare (Purple) | 40–60 | 30% |
| Epic (Gold) | 80–120 | 20% |
| Legendary (Red) | 200+ | 5% (Sovereign only) |

## Preparation Checklist

Before every World Boss attempt:

- [ ] Full gear repair
- [ ] 50+ potions of your role's primary stat
- [ ] 10+ Mana/Energy potions
- [ ] Food buff (check which stat caps you need)
- [ ] World boss resistance flask (unique to each boss)
- [ ] Scroll of Resurrection (3x)
- [ ] Discord voice channel ready
