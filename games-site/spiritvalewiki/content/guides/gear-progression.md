# SpiritVale Gear Progression Guide — From Level 1 to Endgame

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: July 31, 2026**

Gear in SpiritVale is a staircase, not a ladder: you climb it tier by tier, and skipping steps wastes gold, materials, and time. This guide maps the full gear progression from your first drop at Level 10 to the raid tier at Level 50+, and shows where enchanting, refinement, and the market fit into the climb.

---

## 1. Gear Tiers and Gear Score

Every item in SpiritVale has a tier and a Gear Score. Gear Score is the gate: content checks it before it checks your skill.

| Tier | Color | Typical Gear Score | Source |
|------|-------|--------------------|--------|
| Common | White | 50–150 | Quests, random drops |
| Uncommon | Green | 150–300 | Dungeons, vendors |
| Rare | Blue | 300–450 | Heroic dungeons, world bosses |
| Epic | Purple | 450–650 | World bosses, raid wings |
| Legendary | Orange | 650+ | Raid end-bosses, rare world drops |

**Milestones:**

| Content | Minimum Gear Score |
|---------|-------------------|
| Normal dungeons | 200 |
| Heroic dungeons | 450 |
| World bosses | 550 |
| Raid wings | 650+ |

## 2. The Progression Path

### Phase 1 — Leveling (Levels 1–50)

- Do not invest in gear below Rare — it is replaced within hours
- Complete the class advancement quest at Job Level 50 before farming seriously
- Buy cheap Uncommon gear from the market to fill empty slots; never craft at this stage

### Phase 2 — Normal to Heroic (Level 50, Gear Score 450)

- Farm normal dungeons until every slot is Rare (Gear Score ~500)
- Enchant each piece to +5 or +6 — enchanting is cheap at this tier and the transfer system carries it forward
- Heroic dungeons drop Epic gear; run them with a party — solo queue clears are slow

### Phase 3 — World Bosses (Gear Score 550+)

| Boss | Recommended GS | Notable Drop |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| World boss rotations (daily) | 550+ | Epic gear chance, raid entry materials |
| Weekly world boss | 600+ | Guaranteed Epic, small Legendary chance |

- World boss kills earn **Raid Tokens** — these buy tier gear, so never skip the weekly
- Refinement starts mattering here: refine Epic gear toward +10 before raiding

### Phase 4 — Raids (Gear Score 650+)

| Raid | Wings | Minimum GS | Group Size |
|------|-------|------------|------------|
| First raid | 3 wings | 650 | 8 players |
| Second raid | 4 wings | 750 | 8 players |

- Every boss kill grants Raid Tokens, redeemable for Legendary tier gear
- Legendary gear from raids is the endgame baseline; refine it to +15 for best-in-slot

## 3. Where Enchanting and Refinement Fit

Two upgrade systems stack on top of base gear:

### Enchanting (+1 to +15)

| Enchant Level | Risk | Protection |
|---------------|------|------------|
| +1 – +5 | None | — |
| +6 – +10 | Downgrade on fail | Protection scrolls |
| +11 – +15 | Destruction risk | High-tier scrolls + gold |

**Rule**: enchant to +6 on every slot first — that is the efficiency sweet spot. Push to +10 only on Epic+ gear. Never enchant past +10 without Protection Scrolls.

### Refinement (base stat boost + bonus effects)

- Refinement Stones are guaranteed (100%) — always use them
- Refinement carries to higher-tier gear at a 20% reduction via the Transfer System
- **Transfer before enchanting**: refine your old gear, transfer to the new piece, then enchant — you lose less value that way

## 4. Slot-by-Slot Priority

Upgrade order matters because upgrade materials are the bottleneck:

| Order | Slot | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| 1 | Weapon | Largest stat share and damage contribution |
| 2 | Chest | Best armor value, main defensive slot |
| 3 | Gloves | Attack speed / cast speed affixes |
| 4 | Boots | Move speed + dodge |
| 5 | Helm / Shoulders | Small gains, cheap filler |
| 6 | Accessories | Last — affix value varies by class |

---

## 5. Market and Crafting Shortcuts

- **Buy your upgrades**: Epic gear with wrong stats sells cheap on the market — check before crafting
- **Crafting is for accessories** — crafted accessories are the cheapest reliable Epic source
- **Sell, don't dismantle, Common/Uncommon**: materials from dismantling are rarely worth the lost gold
- Check the trading market guide for price trends before big purchases — raid tier prices drop after each server's first clears

## Stat Priorities per Class

When affix choice appears, pick by class role — the right stat on the right slot out-values a whole tier of gear:

| Class | Priority 1 | Priority 2 | Avoid |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Knight / Paladin | VIT, defense | HP, block | Cast speed |
| Berserker / Warrior | STR, attack power | Crit damage | Magic stats |
| Mage / Wizard | INT, magic power | Cast speed | Melee attack |
| Priest / Acolyte | INT, healing power | Max SP | Crit rate |
| Shinobi / Rogue | AGI, crit rate | Crit damage | VIT stacking |
| Sniper / Gunslinger | DEX, ranged attack | Crit rate | AoE stats |

**Rule**: a Rare piece with your top two priority stats beats an Epic piece with neither. Check affixes before equipping anything purple — most endgame gear poverty comes from wearing wrong-stat Epics.

## Common Progression Mistakes

- **Enchanting Common/Uncommon gear** — the +1 to +5 cost is trivial, but you replace the piece in hours; the gold compounds
- **Rushing raids at 620 Gear Score** — raid groups check minimums for a reason; the 650 gate is the damage check, not the entry fee
- **Selling Epic gear with wrong stats** — it is still worth 60%+ of a crafted Epic's value on the market; list it, do not vendor it
- **Ignoring the weekly world boss** — the Raid Tokens alone justify the trip every single week
- **Refining before enchanting** — refine first, enchant second; refinement transfers, enchanting loses value on replacement

## Quick Progression Checklist

- [ ] No enchanting below Rare gear
- [ ] +6 enchanting on every slot before Heroic dungeons
- [ ] Weekly world boss + daily rotations every week
- [ ] Refinement on Epic+ only, transfer before replacing
- [ ] Raid at 650 GS minimum, never under-geared
