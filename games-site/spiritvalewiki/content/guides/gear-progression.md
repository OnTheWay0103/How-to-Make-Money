# SpiritVale Gear Progression Guide — From Level 1 to Endgame

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Gear in SpiritVale is a staircase, not a ladder: you climb it tier by tier, and skipping steps wastes gold, materials, and time. This guide maps the full gear progression from your first drop at Level 10 to the raid tier at Level 50+, and shows where refinement and the market fit into the climb.

---

## 1. Gear Tiers and Gear Score

Every item in SpiritVale has a tier and a Gear Score. Gear Score is the gate: content checks it before it checks your skill.

| Tier | Color | Source |
|------|-------|--------|
| Common | White | Random drops |
| Uncommon | Green | Dungeons, vendors |
| Rare | Blue | Dungeons, world bosses |
| Epic | Purple | World bosses, raid tiers |
| Legendary | Orange | Raid end-bosses, rare world drops |

**Milestones:**

| Content | Minimum Gear Score |
|---------|-------------------|
| Normal dungeons | Low |
| Heroic dungeons | Mid |
| World bosses | High |
| Raid tiers 3–4 | 650 / 750 |

## 2. The Progression Path

### Phase 1 — Leveling (Levels 1–50)

- Do not invest in gear below Rare — it is replaced within hours
- Complete the class advancement quest at Job Level 50 before farming seriously
- Buy cheap Uncommon gear from the market to fill empty slots; never craft at this stage

### Phase 2 — Normal to Heroic (Level 50)

- Farm normal dungeons until every slot is Rare
- Refine each piece a few levels — refinement is cheap at this tier and the transfer system carries it forward
- Heroic dungeons drop Epic gear; run them with a party — solo queue clears are slow

### Phase 3 — World Bosses

| Boss | Recommended GS | Notable Drop |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| World boss rotations | High | Epic gear chance, raid entry materials |
| Weekly world boss | Higher | Strong Epic chance, small Legendary chance |

- World boss kills earn **Raid Tokens** — these buy tier gear, so never skip the weekly
- Refinement starts mattering here: refine Epic gear toward +10 before raiding

### Phase 4 — Raids

| Raid Tier | Group Size | Level / Minimum GS |
|-----------|------------|--------------------|
| Tier 1 (Stone Colossus, Corrupted Wyrm) | 4 players | Level 25–30 |
| Tier 2 (Frost Oracle, Shadow Lich) | 6 players | Level 40–45 |
| Tier 3 (Void Kraken, Phoenix Sovereign) | 8 players | Level 50, GS 650 |
| Tier 4 (Primordial Titan, Celestial Serpent) | 10 players | Level 50+, GS 750 |

- Raids reset weekly (Monday 05:00) and every boss kill grants **Raid Tokens**, redeemable for Legendary tier gear
- Legendary gear from raids is the endgame baseline; refine it toward +10 for best-in-slot

## 3. Where Refinement Fits

There is one upgrade system on top of base gear: **refinement**.

### Refinement (+1 to +10)

| Refine Level | Risk | Protection |
|---------------|------|------------|
| +1 – +5 | None | — |
| +6 – +10 | Downgrade on fail (never destroys the item) | Protection Scrolls |

- Refinement uses **Refine Stones**, which drop from monsters and world bosses
- **Rule:** refine to +6 on every slot first — that is the efficiency sweet spot. Push toward +10 only on Epic+ gear
- A failed refine can drop the item a level or two — it never destroys the item
- **Protection Scrolls** prevent the downgrade on the higher levels; they are worth using on pieces you plan to keep

### Transfer System

- Refinement carries to higher-tier gear at **a small reduction** via the Transfer System
- **Transfer before refining your new piece**: refine your old gear, transfer to the new item, then keep refining — you lose less value that way

## 4. Slot-by-Slot Priority

Upgrade order matters because upgrade materials are the bottleneck:

| Order | Slot | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| 1 | Weapon | Largest stat share and damage contribution |
| 2 | Chest | Best armor value, main defensive slot |
| 3 | Gloves | Attack speed / cast speed affixes |
| 4 | Boots | Move speed + dodge |
| 5 | Helm / Shoulders | Small gains, cheap filler |
| 6 | Accessories | Last — affix value varies by class |

---

## 5. Market and Crafting Shortcuts

- **Buy your upgrades**: Epic gear with wrong stats sells cheap on the market — check before crafting
- **Crafting is for accessories** — crafted accessories are the cheapest reliable Epic source
- **Sell, don't dismantle, Common/Uncommon**: materials from dismantling are rarely worth the lost gold
- Check the trading market guide for price trends before big purchases — raid tier prices drop after each server's first clears

## Stat Priorities per Class

When affix choice appears, pick by class role — the right stat on the right slot out-values a whole tier of gear:

| Class | Priority 1 | Priority 2 | Avoid |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Knight / Paladin | VIT, defense | HP, block | Cast speed |
| Berserker / Warrior | STR, attack power | Crit damage | Magic stats |
| Mage / Wizard | INT, magic power | Cast speed | Melee attack |
| Priest / Acolyte | INT, healing power | Max SP | Crit rate |
| Shinobi / Rogue | AGI, crit rate | Crit damage | VIT stacking |
| Gunslinger / Scout | DEX, ranged attack | Crit rate | AoE stats |

**Rule**: a Rare piece with your top two priority stats beats an Epic piece with neither. Check affixes before equipping anything purple — most endgame gear poverty comes from wearing wrong-stat Epics.

## Common Progression Mistakes

- **Refining Common/Uncommon gear** — the early refine levels are trivial to reach, but you replace the piece in hours; the stones and gold compound
- **Rushing raids at 620 Gear Score** — raid groups check minimums for a reason; the 650 gate is the damage check, not the entry fee
- **Selling Epic gear with wrong stats** — it still sells for a good share of a crafted Epic's value on the market; list it, do not vendor it
- **Ignoring the weekly world boss** — the Raid Tokens alone justify the trip every single week
- **Refining before transferring** — refine, transfer, then keep refining; you lose less value that way

## Quick Progression Checklist

- [ ] No refinement below Rare gear
- [ ] Moderate refinement on every slot before Heroic dungeons
- [ ] Weekly world boss + daily rotations every week
- [ ] Refinement on Epic+ only, transfer before replacing
- [ ] Raid at 650 GS minimum, never under-geared
