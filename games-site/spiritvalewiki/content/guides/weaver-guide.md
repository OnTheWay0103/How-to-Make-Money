## Weaver Overview

The **Weaver** is the most unusual advanced class in SpiritVale — a hybrid damage-and-support specialization that **any base class can unlock** at Job Level 50. Unlike every other advanced class, Weaver is not tied to a starting class. Instead of promoting to your normal specialization (Berserker, Wizard, Paladin, and so on), you can choose the Weaver's path instead.

Because Weaver is a hybrid, its playstyle is defined by two things: the **base-class skill tree you carry in**, and how heavily you lean into the game's **autocast** mechanics. Two Weavers can play completely differently — one as a mobile ranged fighter, another as a spellcasting support.

## How to Become Weaver

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Base class | Any of the 7 base classes (Knight, Warrior, Mage, Summoner, Rogue, Scout, Acolyte) |
| Level | **Job Level 50** |
| Location | Speak to the **Weaver Master in Starfall Tundra** |
| Cost | Standard class advancement fee |

Steps:

1. Level any base class to **Job Level 50** (see the [Class Leveling Guide](/guides/class-leveling-guide) for the fastest routes).
2. Travel to **Starfall Tundra** in the far north and find the **Weaver Master**.
3. Complete the promotion. Choosing Weaver **replaces your normal advanced class** — it is not added on top of it.

**Important:** Starfall Tundra is a high-level region. If your character is not geared for it, bring a party or ask a higher-level player to escort you to the Weaver Master safely. The promotion itself is straightforward once you arrive.

**Can you swap back?** Once you have unlocked an advanced class, the Waybinder NPC in any major town lets you switch between advanced classes you have unlocked. If you later complete your standard class's advancement quest, you can alternate between it and Weaver on the same character.

## How Weaver Plays

Weaver's core identity is **flexibility through autocast**. Many weapons, cards, and artifact sets trigger extra abilities automatically — autocast effects on gear fire on their own during combat. Weaver builds stack these effects, so your gear is doing damage and casting skills even while you move, attack, or support your party.

Your starting class still matters:

| Base Class You Bring In | Weaver Style It Enables |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Scout / Rogue | Mobile ranged or hybrid burst Weaver |
| Mage / Summoner | Caster Weaver with spell synergy |
| Knight / Warrior | Durable Weaver with melee utility |
| Acolyte | Heal-focused utility Weaver |

## Best Weaver Builds

### 1. Ranged Weaver (Strafing Volley + Traps)
- **Focus:** Mobile physical damage with **Strafing Volley** and trap skills.
- **Stats:** DEX primary, AGI secondary.
- **Playstyle:** Keep distance, strafe while volleying, drop traps on chokepoints and retreat.
- **Best for:** Solo farming, open-world content, and players who want ranged mobility without a caster's fragility.

### 2. Caster Weaver (Free Cast)
- **Focus:** Magic damage built around **Free Cast** — casting while moving and without interruption.
- **Stats:** INT primary, DEX for cast speed.
- **Playstyle:** Weave spells between movements; your base magic tree supplies the damage, Weaver supplies the flexibility.
- **Best for:** Players who want a caster that cannot be locked down.

### 3. Utility Weaver (Heal, Cure, Haste)
- **Focus:** Hybrid support with **Heal**, **Cure**, **Haste**, and crowd control.
- **Stats:** INT or WIS-leaning hybrid, then VIT for survivability.
- **Playstyle:** Support your party while contributing steady damage and CC — a flexible off-healer that a Priest cannot fully replace.
- **Best for:** Group play and duos.

### 4. Autocast Weaver
- **Focus:** Stack equipment, cards, and artifact sets with autocast triggers.
- **Stats:** Follow your weapon scaling; let gear do the work.
- **Playstyle:** Trigger extra abilities constantly in combat; your rotations are simple because your gear casts for you.
- **Best for:** Endgame players who love build-crafting around the [Cards Guide](/guides/cards-guide) and artifact system.

## Weaver Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Any class can unlock it | Replaces your normal advancement — no second chance without re-unlocking |
| Extremely flexible builds | Not the top DPS, tank, or healer at anything |
| Autocast synergy with gear | Effectiveness depends heavily on your equipment |
| Strong solo and group utility | Unlock zone is dangerous to reach early |

## Is Weaver Right for You?

| If you want... | Pick |
|----------------|------|
| Best-in-role performance | Your class's normal advanced class |
| A unique, flexible hybrid | Weaver |
| Pet gameplay | Summoner → Necromancer |
| Pure healing | Acolyte → Priest |
| Ranged mobility with spells | Caster Weaver |

Weaver is at its best when you enjoy **build-crafting**: your gear, cards, and autocast setup matter more than any single skill. If you want to play the same character in many different styles, Weaver is the most flexible advanced class in the game.
