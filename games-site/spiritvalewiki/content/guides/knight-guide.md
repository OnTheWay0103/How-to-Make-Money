## Knight Overview

The Knight is SpiritVale's premier tank class, excelling at absorbing damage and protecting allies. With high HP, strong defense, and crowd control abilities, the Knight is essential for challenging group content. At Job Level 50, Knights advance to the **Paladin**, gaining holy damage and party support on top of the defensive toolkit (see the [Paladin Guide](/guides/paladin-guide)).

## Knight Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Highest defense in the game | Lowest damage output |
| Excellent crowd control | Slow movement speed |
| Self-sustain through blocks | Limited ranged options |
| Essential for raid bosses | Solo leveling is slow |

## Best Knight Builds

### Tank Knight (PvE/Raid)
- **Focus**: Maximum defense, aggro management
- **Skills**: Guard Stance (max), Shield Bash (max), Endure, Iron Will
- **Gear**: Heavy armor, tower shield, HP accessories
- **Playstyle**: Stand in front, hold aggro, cycle defensive cooldowns

### Solo Knight (Leveling)
- **Focus**: Balanced offense and defense
- **Skills**: Holy Strike (max), Shield Bash, Endure
- **Gear**: Medium-heavy armor, one-handed sword + shield
- **Playstyle**: Pull small groups, use Holy Strike for extra damage

## Knight Leveling Guide (1-50)

| Level Range | Area | Focus |
|-------------|------|-------|
| 1-15 | Vale's Rest / Sunny Meadows | Learn blocking and Shield Bash timing |
| 15-30 | Whisperwind Plains | Group with DPS for faster clears |
| 30-45 | Frostpine Ridge | Start building a tank gear set |
| 45-50 | Soulfire Citadel Exterior | Join raid groups as main tank |

**Leveling tip**: Knights level slowest of all classes. Always party with DPS classes. Your role is to group enemies so DPS can AoE them down.

## Knight Gear

### Gear Path (Endgame)
| Slot | Recommendation | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Weapon | Tank sword — highest defense roll you can find | Drops, raid tokens |
| Shield | Tower shield with block stats | Drops, crafted |
| Armor | Heavy plate with VIT and defense | Dungeon drops, raid tokens |
| Accessory | HP and defense accessories | Drops, market |

There is no single best-in-slot list for Knights in EA — balance patches move the top picks. The rule is simple: defense stats beat everything, and raid token gear is the strongest tier available.

## Knight in PvP

Knights are strong in group PvP but struggle in 1v1:
- **In group PvP**: Frontline initiator, absorb enemy burst, CC priority targets
- **In 1v1**: Out-sustain opponents; your damage is low but you're hard to kill
