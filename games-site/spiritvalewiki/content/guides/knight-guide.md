## Knight Overview

The Knight is SpiritVale's premier tank class, excelling at absorbing damage and protecting allies. With high HP, strong defense, and crowd control abilities, the Knight is essential for challenging group content.

## Knight Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Highest defense in the game | Lowest damage output |
| Excellent crowd control | Slow movement speed |
| Self-sustain through blocks | Limited ranged options |
| Essential for raid bosses | Solo leveling is slow |

## Best Knight Builds

### Tank Knight (PvE/Raid)
- **Focus**: Maximum defense, aggro management
- **Skills**: Shield Wall (max), Taunt (max), Guardian's Oath, Holy Strike
- **Gear**: Heavy armor, Tower Shield, HP accessories
- **Playstyle**: Stand in front, hold aggro, cycle defensive cooldowns

### Solo Knight (Leveling)
- **Focus**: Balanced offense and defense
- **Skills**: Holy Strike (max), Shield Bash, Iron Skin
- **Gear**: Medium-heavy armor, one-handed sword + shield
- **Playstyle**: Pull small groups, use Holy Strike for sustain

## Knight Leveling Guide (1-50)

| Level Range | Area | Focus |
|-------------|------|-------|
| 1-15 | Starting Zone | Learn blocking, taunt timing |
| 15-30 | Forest of Whispers | Group with DPS for faster clears |
| 30-45 | Crimson Peaks | Start building tank gear set |
| 45-50 | Endgame Zones | Join raid groups as main tank |

**Leveling tip**: Knights level slowest of all classes. Always party with DPS classes for questing. Your role is to group enemies so DPS can AoE them down.

## Knight in PvP

Knights are strong in group PvP but struggle in 1v1:
- **In group PvP**: Frontline initiator, absorb enemy burst, CC priority targets
- **In 1v1**: Out-sustain opponents; your damage is low but you're hard to kill
