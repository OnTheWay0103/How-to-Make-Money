# SpiritVale Weekly Reset Guide — Plan Your Week in 10 Minutes

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 4, 2026**

SpiritVale runs two reset cycles: dailies at **05:00 server time** and weeklies on **Monday at 05:00 server time**. The weekly cycle contains most of the game's best rewards — world boss drops, raid lockouts, and guild war chests — and missing a window costs you a full week. This guide lays out the complete weekly schedule, the efficient clearing order, and what to prioritize by playtime.

---

## 1. What Resets Weekly (Monday 05:00 ST)

| Content | Reset | Time Investment | Primary Rewards |
|---------|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Weekly quests | Monday 05:00 ST | 30 min - 2 hours | Gold, XP tokens, token exchange items |
| Raid boss lockouts (Tiers 1-4) | Monday 05:00 ST | 2-6 hours total | Tier gear, crafting essences (Stoneheart Essence, etc.) |
| Guild war registration | Weekly (register by Sunday) | 1-2 hours | Guild war chests, guild currency |
| Arena weekly ranking | Monday 05:00 ST | Variable | PvP tokens, seasonal titles |
| Dungeon weekly | Monday 05:00 ST | 30-45 min | Dungeon tokens, rare materials |

**World bosses do NOT reset on Monday** — they respawn **72 hours after death** with a ±2 hour variance, so their window drifts each week. Check the [World Boss Guide](/guides/world-boss-guide) for the current kill status; the schedule below shows the traditional spawn days.

## 2. The World Boss Week (Traditional Spawn Days)

| Day | Boss | Continent | Window | Recommended Players |
|-----|------|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Monday | The Verdant Titan | Dawnwood | 14:00-16:00 ST | 15-25 |
| Tuesday | Emberfang | Ashfall Steppes | 18:00-20:00 ST | 20-30 |
| Wednesday | Tidecaller Leviathan | Coral Abyss | 20:00-22:00 ST | 25-40 |
| Thursday | Shadow Weaver | Gloomfen | 16:00-18:00 ST | 15-20 |
| Friday | Storm Herald | Skyreach Summit | 19:00-21:00 ST | 20-25 |
| Saturday | **Sovereign of Wrath** | The Rift | 20:00-23:00 ST | 40-60 (server-wide) |

The Sovereign of Wrath is a 3-phase, multi-hour event requiring server-wide coordination — it is the single biggest weekly reward in the game and worth scheduling your Saturday around.

---

## 3. The Efficient Clearing Order (5-10 Hours/Week)

1. **Monday morning:** finish any weekly quests still open from last week (they expire at 05:00), then pick up all new weekly quests.
2. **Monday-Thursday:** clear raid lockouts in tier order — Tier 1 and 2 first (they are fast), then Tier 3 with your regular party. Leave Tier 4 for the weekend when your full 10-player roster is online.
3. **Wednesday-Saturday:** attend the world bosses that fit your schedule. Priority: Sovereign of Wrath > Tidecaller Leviathan > Emberfang > the rest.
4. **Before Sunday:** register your guild for the weekly war (see the [Guild Guide](/guides/guild-guide)) and run the dungeon weekly — it only takes 30 minutes and it resets on Monday.
5. **Sunday night:** spend tokens before the reset; token exchange inventories rotate with the [Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes) cadence.

---

## 4. Priority by Playtime

| Playtime | Must-Do | Skip |
|----------|---------|------|
| 2-4 hrs/week | Weekly quests + dungeon weekly | Raid tiers 3-4 (unless you're in a scheduled group) |
| 5-10 hrs/week | Raid tiers 1-3 + 2 world bosses | Guild wars (unless your guild needs you) |
| 10+ hrs/week | Everything: all raid tiers, all six world bosses, guild war | Nothing — you're the backbone of your server |

---

## 5. Common Mistakes

1. **Letting weekly quests expire.** They take 30 minutes and pay XP tokens; abandoning them is leaving gold on the table.
2. **Missing the Sovereign of Wrath.** It drops the best world-boss loot in the game and only runs 3 hours on Saturday. Set an alarm.
3. **Raid lockout overlap.** Tier lockouts share a Monday reset — don't save Tier 1 for Sunday, or you'll rush all four tiers in one night.
4. **Not registering for guild wars.** Registration closes before the weekend; a missed registration is a missed chest for the whole guild.
5. **Spending tokens on Sunday night.** Token traders rotate stock at the weekly reset — check tomorrow's stock before spending today's tokens.

**Final note:** if your server is quiet at your play hours, remember server transfer is free and account-wide — a busier server means world boss trains actually fill.
