# SpiritVale Echoing Spire & Endgame Tower Guide — Party Comp, Resets & Rewards

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 28, 2026**

So you hit the level cap in SpiritVale and the map is no longer handing you upgrades. The next stop, according to the community, is the **tower at the far end of the game** — an endless, party-based climb that exists to farm top-tier gear. This guide explains what we actually know about the Spire, who you should bring, what the August 17 patch changed about how it resets, and why the gear you get there matters.

---

## 1. What Is the Spire? [Unconfirmed]

Two things are worth separating right away: what the community agrees on, and what nobody has confirmed.

**Confirmed references to "Spire":** the official patch log for **0.30.14 (August 17, 2026)** lists fixes to "Spire resets" and a new ability to **kick members from Spire parties**. That tells us the Spire is a real, named game mode with **parties** and a **reset** — not a rumor. It is also, at minimum, party content: the kick feature exists because runs can go wrong with a bad member.

**The "tower" description:** a deep Russian player review describes the endgame as "at the far end stands a tower — an endless group climb, for items of the highest tier" (`free-play-online.ru`, August 2026). The same reviewer admitted they had spent little time there and could not judge it honestly.

**Why this is [Unconfirmed]:** official patch notes never say the Spire *is* a tower, and no official source gives the tower an English name. The equation **"Spire = the endgame tower"** is an inference we are making by joining the patch-log references to the tower described in community reviews. Treat it as the most likely reading, not a confirmed fact.

**The name "Echoing Spire":** only one third-party guide we found names an endgame boss "Echoing Spire" (`PowerUpGaming.co.uk`, July 21, 2026). No official source uses this name. **The English name is third-party corroboration only — [Unconfirmed].**

> **How to verify in-game:** if you are at endgame and near the climb, check whether your party UI lets you kick members (the 0.30.14 feature) and watch for the reset timer the patch fixed. If you can confirm the tower's in-game name, tell the community — the official Discord is where players share these details.

## 2. Why This Matters Now: What 0.30.14 Changed

The August 17 patch (0.30.14) is the reason this guide exists — players were hitting real problems in the Spire and the devs shipped fixes for them:

| Change | What it means for you |
|--------|-----------------------|
| **Spire reset fixes** | Before 0.30.14, community reports of Spire runs resetting (losing progress or loot) were common. The patch addressed these resets directly. |
| **Kick members from Spire parties** | You can now remove a member mid-run instead of disbanding the party and losing the climb. |
| **Bosses and dummies no longer trigger idle detection** | The anti-AFK system no longer flags you while fighting bosses or attacking training dummies — a quality-of-life fix that indirectly helps long Spire fights. |
| **1000-stack key (Ctrl+Shift)** | A new keybind for moving large item stacks — relevant for banking the materials the tower can drop. |

*(Source: vgspoilers patch log for 0.30.14.)* The same patch carried a larger weapon rebalance (Lockdown, Blacksteel, Razors Edge, Solaris Blade all changed) — see the [Update & Patch Notes Guide](/guides/updates-patch-notes) for the full list.

## 3. The Proven Party Composition for the Spire

The one third-party guide that covers the Spire recommends a **"tanky bossing"** comp for endgame bosses like Echoing Spire ([Unconfirmed] name — see section 1):

| Slot | Class | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| Main Tank | **Paladin** (Knight → Paladin) | Holds aggro; Sacrifice/Devotion share or mitigate damage for the whole group |
| Healer | **Priest** (Acolyte → Priest) | Keeps the group alive; buffs stack well against undead/dark enemies |
| Damage + Wall | **Necromancer** (Summoner → Necromancer) | Minions soak projectiles and absorb incoming damage while DoTs tick |
| Damage | **Shinobi** (Rogue → Shinobi) | Melee burst during movement-light windows |
| Damage | **Gunslinger** (Scout → Gunslinger) | Safe ranged DPS that keeps contributing during movement-heavy phases |

**Why this works:** the Paladin/Priest core turns lethal mechanics into survivable ones, and the Necromancer's minions are the real trick — they "can soak all the projectiles," so the ranged classes only need to keep moving and shooting. The guide's summary advice is to let the supports mitigate and keep the DPS on the boss.

*(Source: PowerUpGaming.co.uk, "SpiritVale Best Party Compositions & Synergies," July 21, 2026.)* For the general class roles and synergy pairings, see the [Party Composition Guide](/guides/party-composition).

### The Berserker Question [Community Dispute]

Should a **Berserker** be in your Spire party? Players disagree, and it is worth knowing before you build a group:

- One camp: **"Berserkers aren't taken into the tower because they don't deal damage."**
- The other: **"Berserkers do fine damage, and a completely different class suffers."**

That is a direct quote from a player review (`free-play-online.ru`), which also notes the developer adjusts numbers on the fly — meaning today's "excluded class" can be tomorrow's meta. **Our take:** if your Berserker is geared and proven, run them; if you are joining a pick-up group, expect some leaders to hesitate. During Early Access balance swings, comp flexibility matters more than any single ranking.

## 4. Reset Mechanics — What We Know and What We Don't

The patch log confirms the Spire **has resets** and that they were buggy before 0.30.14. What we do **not** have is an official explanation of the reset schedule — whether the climb resets weekly, daily, or per-floor is community-reported territory and unverified.

Practical reset advice (based on the patch fixes + community reports):

1. **Treat pre-0.30.14 reports with suspicion.** Old forum threads describing lost runs may describe the bug the August 17 patch fixed.
2. **Screen your party before the pull.** The kick feature exists because bad members waste the climb. Set expectations (roles, boss order) in chat first.
3. **Bank valuable drops immediately.** The economy tie-in below is why this matters.

## 5. Rewards: Why the Climb Matters (and the Market Tie-In)

The tower's purpose, per the player review, is **items of the highest tier** — the "endgame begins after the grind" gear that normal maps stop giving you. Two things make those drops valuable:

**Random rolls.** Gear drops with random options on top of base stats. That means an otherwise-unwanted roll can be exactly what another build needs — which is where trading comes in.

**The Auction Hall is back.** After a long shutdown, the trade system returned **August 4, 2026** with a **5% seller tax** and a **30-listing cap** (PTR-tested August 2). The same Russian review that described the tower called a disabled market "an amputation, not a broken button" — because the market is what turns someone else's unwanted roll into your build. With trading back online, a lucky Spire drop can now be sold instead of hoarded. See the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) for the current rules.

**The gear path:** tower drops feed directly into endgame progression — refine, slot cards, and push into harder content. See the [Gear Progression Guide](/guides/gear-progression) for what to do with top-tier pieces once you have them.

## 6. The Honest Bottom Line

- The Spire is **real, party-based, and has resets** — that much is official via patch 0.30.14. **[Unconfirmed]** whether it is literally "the endgame tower" and whether its English name is "Echoing Spire."
- The strongest reported comp is **Paladin / Priest / Necromancer / Shinobi / Gunslinger**, built around minions soaking damage.
- The **Berserker exclusion debate** is a live community dispute — expect it in pick-up groups, and expect it to change with the next balance pass.
- **0.30.14 fixed the reset bugs and added party kicks**, so old horror stories about lost runs may already be outdated.
- The climb exists to farm **top-tier gear with random rolls**, and with the Auction Hall back (Aug 4), those rolls are tradable again.

**Before you take any of this as gospel:** verify the tower's in-game name and reset timer yourself, and check the [Update & Patch Notes Guide](/guides/updates-patch-notes) after every patch — Early Access balance changes weekly.
