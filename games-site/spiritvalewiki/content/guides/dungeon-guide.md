# SpiritVale Dungeon Guide — All Dungeons, Entrances, Bosses & Loot

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Dungeons are SpiritVale's instanced group content — the bridge between open-world grinding and raid progression. Each dungeon has a boss at the end with a unique loot table, and every dungeon has a **Heroic difficulty** version that is the first stop on the endgame gear ladder. This guide lists every dungeon, where to find it, what level you should be, and what it drops. Boss-by-boss mechanics live in the [Boss Guide](/guides/boss-guide) and [Raid Boss Guide](/guides/raid-boss-guide); this page is the map.

---

## Dungeon Basics

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Entry | Dungeon entrances are open-world portals in the listed zones |
| Party size | 3-5 players recommended (soloable at low tiers with strong gear) |
| Respawn | Instance-based — bosses respawn per run |
| Difficulty | Normal + Heroic (Heroic unlocks at Level 50, Gear Score 500+) |
| Daily limit | 3 runs per dungeon (Heroic) |

**Gear Score gates:** normal dungeons check around **200 GS**, Heroic around **450-500 GS**. Content checks Gear Score before it checks your skill — don't queue under-geared. Full ladder in the [Gear Progression Guide](/guides/gear-progression).

## All Dungeons (Normal Difficulty)

| Dungeon | Location | Level | Boss | Notable Loot |
|---------|----------|-------|------|--------------|
| Sunken Hollow Depths | Sunken Hollow | 30+ | Corrupted Golem — splits into 3 smaller golems, kill evenly | Golem Core (crafting), Stone Shell Card, Rare weapons |
| Frostpine Cavern | Frostpine Ridge | 38+ | Frost Queen — ice blocks fall from the ceiling, destroy them for safe zones | Frost Bear Hide Armor, Frost Bear Card, Serpent items |
| Soulfire Citadel | Soulfire Citadel | 48+ | Infernal Lord — floor tile pattern, stand on the matching color | Void Staff (BiS for Wizards), Void Mage Card (Epic) |
| Vale's Heart | Endgame zone | 50+ | Corrupted Spirit — combines Spirit of the Vale mechanics | Epic gear, raid entry materials |

**Field boss bonus:** most dungeon zones also spawn field bosses (30-60 min respawn) with unique named drops — Alpha Fang, Guardian Blade, and friends. Rotating between dungeon runs and field boss spawns is the most efficient loot loop in the mid-game.

## Heroic Dungeons (Level 50, Endgame Start)

Heroic difficulty reuses the same maps with stronger enemies, new boss mechanics, and exclusive loot tables — this is where the endgame gear ladder begins.

### Unlock Requirements

| Requirement | Threshold |
|-------------|-----------|
| Minimum Level | 50 |
| Gear Score | 500+ |
| Party Size | 3-5 players (solo queue clears are slow — bring a party) |
| Daily Limit | 3 runs per dungeon |

### Heroic Rewards

| Item | Drop Rate | Use |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Epic Gear | ~25% | Equipment upgrade base |
| Refinement Stones | 100% (guaranteed) | Gear refinement material |
| Heroic Seals | 10-15 per run | **Raid entry currency — save them, you need 50 for the first raid wing** |
| Rare Cards | ~5% | Card collection bonuses |

**Why run them:** Heroic dungeons are the fastest reliable Epic gear source in the game, and the daily Heroic Seals bank is the gate to raids. Run all three of your daily runs every day until every slot is Rare+ with your class's stats.

## Raid Dungeons (8-Player, Weekly Lockout)

Raids are the top of the instanced-content pyramid: 8-player encounters with weekly lockouts and the only Legendary-tier loot sources. The first raid gates at **Gear Score 650** (3 wings), the second at **750** (4 wings) — details and boss-by-boss mechanics are in the [Raid Boss Guide](/guides/raid-boss-guide), including the current roster (Stone Colossus, Corrupted Wyrm, Frost Oracle, Shadow Lich, and the Tier 3-4 encounters).

Key raid rules:

- **Weekly lockout per wing** — resets Mondays; never skip a week
- **Raid Tokens per boss kill** — redeemable for tier gear
- **Bring consumables** — raid potions, scrolls, and food buffs matter at this tier
- **Know your role** — tanks study phase transitions, DPS handle interrupts and adds

## Dungeon Tips & Common Mistakes

- **Soloing Heroic is a trap** — solo queue clears are slow and the daily limit punishes wasted runs. Party up.
- **Don't vendor blue+ drops** — list them on the Player Market first; even mediocre Epics sell for real gold.
- **Bring a balanced comp** — tank, healer, and damage dealers; a Priest is mandatory in Heroic and raid content.
- **Watch the daily limit** — 3 Heroic runs per dungeon per day; pick your best dungeon and run it consistently.
- **Bank your Seals** — spending Heroic Seals on anything but raid entry is almost always a mistake.
- **Under-geared = liability** — groups check minimums for a reason; a 620 GS player in a 650 gate wastes nine other people's lockout.

## Quick Dungeon Checklist

- [ ] Gear Score meets the gate (200 normal / 450-500 Heroic / 650+ raids)
- [ ] Party assembled (3-5 for Heroic, 8 for raids)
- [ ] Daily Heroic runs banked every day
- [ ] Heroic Seals saved for raid wings
- [ ] Blue+ drops listed on the market, not vendored
- [ ] Weekly raid lockout cleared before Monday reset
