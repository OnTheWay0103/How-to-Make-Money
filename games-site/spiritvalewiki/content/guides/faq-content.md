# SpiritVale FAQ — Most Common Questions

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 11, 2026**

Quick answers to the most frequently asked questions about SpiritVale Early Access.

---

## 1. Is SpiritVale free to play?

**Yes — SpiritVale is free to play** on Steam Early Access. The in-game shop is cosmetics-only — no pay-to-win items.

---

## 2. What is the level cap?

- **Base Level cap:** 99
- **Job Level cap:** 50

Early Access currently supports content up to Base Level 50 and Job Level 50. Higher levels and associated content (maps, dungeons, bosses) will be added in future updates.

---

## 3. Can I change my class after picking one?

Yes. The **Waybinder NPC** in any major town allows you to:
- Respec all attribute points for free
- Respec all skill points for free
- Switch between advanced classes you've unlocked (must be Job Level 50+)

There is no cost, cooldown, or limit to respecs. You can switch between advanced classes freely once you have unlocked them.

---

## 4. Can I change my base class?

No. Your base class (Knight, Warrior, Acolyte, Mage, Scout, Rogue, or Summoner) is chosen at character creation and cannot be changed. However, all classes reach an advanced specialization at Job Level 50, so this only affects your starting experience.

---

## 5. How many classes are there?

7 base classes and 8 advanced classes (15 total): **Paladin** (Knight), **Priest** (Acolyte), **Wizard** (Mage), **Sniper** (Scout), **Shinobi** (Rogue), **Berserker** (Warrior), **Necromancer** (Summoner), and **Gunslinger** (a special advanced class that any Job Level 50 class can unlock). See the [Classes Guide](/guides/classes-guide) for the full tree.

---

## 6. Is there PvP?

Yes. SpiritVale has:
- **Colosseum arena PvP** (1v1, 3v3, and 6v6 modes) — queued from any major town
- **Guild Wars** — scheduled 15v15 Warfront battles (Saturdays 20:00)

Arena matchmaking uses gear score brackets, so new players are not matched against fully-geared veterans. See the [PvP Arena Guide](/guides/pvp-arena) for the full breakdown.

---

## 7. How does the card system work?

Cards are equippable items that slot into your gear (weapon, armor, accessories) to provide stat bonuses. Cards drop from monsters, with rarer monsters having higher drop rates. There are 227 cards in the game. See the [Cards Guide](/guides/cards-guide) for full details.

---

## 8. How does trading work?

SpiritVale has an **Auction Hall** — a global player marketplace. Two important current facts:

- The Auction Hall is currently **down for a rebuild** — no reopen date has been announced.
- **Vending stalls and direct trade are the live alternatives right now** — you can buy and sell through player stalls in town. There is a transaction tax on stall sales.
- **Bots are a known problem** — report them via the official Discord's Bot Hunter Program.

Current status is tracked on the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide).

---

## 9. Will my progress be wiped when Early Access ends?

**No.** The developers have publicly committed to preserving player data into full release — no further wipes or rollbacks are planned.

---

## 10. Can I play solo, or do I need a party?

**Partially — and honestly: solo gets hard.** You can solo most of the early and mid game (1–50), but maps shrink as you level, world bosses require teams, and late content effectively needs a party. Best solo class: **Paladin**. Grouping is easy to arrange via Discord and channel chat. See the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) for the full breakdown.

---

## 11. What are the best classes for beginners?

- **Knight → Paladin** — Safest, tankiest, easiest to gear, and the community's #1 solo class
- **Warrior → Berserker** — Easiest raw damage, forgiving
- **Summoner → Necromancer** — Strong solo sustain via pets ("extremely OP" in the current meta)

Note: the Gunslinger is a special advanced class that any Job Level 50 class can unlock — it is not tied to Scout.

---

## 12. How do I get better gear?

There is **no main quest line** in SpiritVale — gear comes from grinding and the systems below:

1. **Monster drops** — farm zones for their drop tables (see the [Farming Guide](/guides/farming-resource-guide))
2. **Crafting** — reliable endgame gear path
3. **Boss drops** — each boss has unique equipment drops (boss summons drop at roughly 0.3%; bank them for max level)
4. **Player stalls / direct trade** — buy from other players (the Auction Hall is down for a rebuild)
5. **Refinement** — upgrade existing gear at the Blacksmith's Anvil; skip refining low-level gear

---

## 13. What is the difference between Base Level and Job Level?

| Base Level | Job Level |
|------------|-----------|
| 1-99 | 1-50 |
| Gained from all XP sources | Gained from killing monsters with class skills |
| Grants attribute points | Grants skill points |
| Core character progression | Class proficiency progression |
| Increased by any activity | Increased only by combat |

---

## 14. Is there an auction house?

**Yes — the Auction Hall exists**, but it is currently **down for a rebuild**. Vending stalls and direct trade are the temporary market while it's reworked. See the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) for live status.

---

## 15. How often does the game update?

During Early Access, the developers release:
- **Weekly patches:** Bug fixes, balance adjustments
- **Monthly content updates:** New maps, bosses, cards
- **Major updates:** New classes, new systems, level cap increases

Patch notes are posted on the Steam Community page and the official Discord. See the [Update & Patch Notes Guide](/guides/updates-patch-notes).

---

## 16. Known Issues & Workarounds (EA)

| Issue | Status | Workaround |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Server lag / disconnects / jitter | Improving, not gone | Pick a stable server (free transfer); wired connection; see the [Connection & Server Guide](/guides/connection-server-guide) |
| Progress rollback | Acknowledged; developers committed to preserving player data | Bank valuables; report to Discord if suspected |
| Movement stops responding (WASD/click) | Known bug | Dodge roll temporarily restores input |
| Desync / soft-lock | Known | Relog from character select |
| Crowded/empty maps (no mobs) | Known EA issue | Channel-hop or change play hours |
