# SpiritVale Artifact Sets Guide — Which Set to Wear and Where Each One Drops

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: September 12, 2026**

You hit Level 50, your Gear Score clears the heroic gate, and your damage still feels flat. Most of the time the missing piece is not a better weapon — it is an **artifact set**.

Artifacts are one of SpiritVale's two collection systems (cards are the other), and the one the game explains least. Here is what they are, why a partial set does almost nothing, and which set to wear for your damage type.

> **Honesty note:** system facts come from the official Steam store description and patch notes. Set names, bonuses and drop tables come from the community-maintained SpiritVale JP Wiki artifact database — the only structured public artifact database we found — so treat exact percentages as that database's figures and confirm them on the live tooltip. **[Unconfirmed]** marks anything resting on a single source.

---

## 1. What an Artifact Actually Is

The official store description puts it plainly:

> "Cards and Artifacts are 2 important features in SpiritVale, both allow you to build your character the way you want or need for specific content! With more than 220 cards and **33 Artifact Sets**, you can freely mix and match to adapt to any build or biome."

Three things define the system:

**They come in four piece types.** Every artifact series is made of a **Rune, a Jewel, a Scroll and a Relic**, and the set effect activates only when all four are equipped.

**The set bonus is the point.** Each piece carries the same base stat — for the damage sets, a flat **+3%** to one damage type — climbing by roughly **+0.125% per enhancement**. Completing the four-piece set adds **All Stats +1** on top of that. One piece is a small stat stick; four pieces is where the set bonus lives.

**Artifacts are their own equipment category.** The community wiki files runes, jewels, scrolls and relics under a separate heading from weapons, armor and accessories, and never states a slot. Some third-party guides call it an accessory slot. Treat the exact slot as **[Unconfirmed]** and check your own panel.

### Artifacts vs cards

| | Cards | Artifacts |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | Single cards slotted into gear card slots | Four-piece sets (Rune / Jewel / Scroll / Relic) |
| Bonus trigger | Per-card effect, plus combos for matching groups | Only when all four pieces of one series are worn |
| Count | 220+ (official) | 33 sets (official) |
| Deep dive | [Cards Guide](/guides/cards-guide) | this page |

If your build advice mentions "Weapon 2 / Armor 2 / Accessory 1 / Accessory 2", that is the card system — see the [Cards Guide](/guides/cards-guide). Artifacts are a different screen.

## 2. The Four Sets to Chase First

Drop rates are the community database's recorded **area drop** figures: the piece can drop from any qualifying monster in that area at that rate. All four sets share the same shape — +3% to one damage type per piece, about +0.125% per enhancement, and **All Stats +1** on the full set.

| Set | For | Per-piece stat | Full-set bonus | Where it drops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pioneer** | Everyone, Levels 1–20 | HP +20, MP +10 (rune) | All Stats +1, HP Regen +5, MP Regen +2, ATK +3, MATK +3 | Sunny Meadows 1 (Pollen, 10%), Sunny Meadows 2 (Bee, Rooster, 5%), Forest Labyrinth 2 (Rooster, 5%) |
| **Steelheart** | Melee | Melee Damage +3% | All Stats +1, Melee Damage +3% | Treant Trail (Maple Sapling, Maple Ancient, 5%), Nevaris Sewers (Sewer Rat, 5%) |
| **Arcanum** | Magic | Magic Damage +3% | All Stats +1, Magic Damage +3% | Forest Field 1 (Frost Wisp, 5%), Forest Field 2 (Sparkit, 5%), Forest Labyrinth 2 (Frost Wisp, 5%) |
| **Stormquiver** | Ranged | Ranged Damage +3% | All Stats +1, Ranged Damage +3% | Windy Desert (Voltpaw, 4%), Windy Desert North (Hellhound, 4%) |

**Start with Pioneer.** It drops in the first field you ever grind — the [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner-guide) already flags Pioneer pieces alongside the Golden Hoop accessory — and its bonus is broad rather than specialised, so it holds up while you level.

**Then pick the set that matches your damage type**, not your class name.

Two caveats. Some third-party guides give different monster names for Steelheart than the database's per-item page; where they disagree, trust the per-item page. And some sources write "Trent Trail" — the database's area is **Treant Trail**. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 3. Which Set for Which Class

| Damage type | Base classes | Advanced classes | Set |
|---|---|---|---|
| Melee | Warrior, Knight, Rogue | Berserker, Paladin, Shinobi | **Steelheart** |
| Magic | Mage, Acolyte, Summoner | Wizard, Priest, Necromancer | **Arcanum** |
| Ranged | Scout | Gunslinger | **Stormquiver** |
| Any | — | Weaver | Match the weapon you build around |

Weaver is the flexible one — any Job 50 character can unlock it (see the [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list)) — so match the set to the damage type you actually scale, not the class label.

One exception worth knowing early: **summoner builds do not scale like other magic builds.** Summons inherit your equipment and artifact stats rather than your attribute points (see the [Summoner Guide](/guides/summoner-guide) and [Necromancer Build Guide](/guides/necromancer-build-guide)), which is why those pages recommend **Corporeal** — the element-damage set documented as poison / undead / dark / holy +3% per piece, dropping at The Forge (Razor Robot, Spiderling Robot, Snout Robot — 1%). Whether Corporeal is "for summons" specifically or simply the element set pet builds want, the sources do not settle. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 4. Late-Game and Specialist Sets

The store page advertises **33 artifact sets**; the database lists **34 series** (it carries both "Arcanum" and "Arcanum Verge", which the store count may fold together). **[Unconfirmed]** The ones players actually talk about:

| Set | What it does | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Oathbound** | +1 Bond Targets | The only artifact set the developer has named in official patch notes (0.31.0) |
| **Corporeal** | Poison / undead / dark / holy +3% per piece | The set the Summoner and Necromancer pages build around |
| **Furybrand** | CRIT Damage +5% on the full set; rune CRIT +5, +0.25% per enhancement | Crit-scaling physical builds |
| **Titanplate** | DEF +10 on the rune | The defense set |
| **Starfire** | MATK +5, MP +5%, **HP −10%** | Glass-cannon magic set |
| **Warglyph** | ATK +5, HP +5%, **MP −15%** | Physical mirror of Starfire |

Starfire and Warglyph both *cost* you something — those are sets you swap in for one fight, not sets you live in.

## 5. The Seven Class-Locked Sets (and the Grimoire Thread)

Seven series are locked to a single class:

Arcane Origin (Mage), Far Sight (Scout), Holy Vow (Acolyte), Honored Oath (Knight), Iron Wake (Warrior), Silent Death (Rogue) and Spirit Pact (Summoner).

These behave differently. The database describes them as material for **grimoires**, and the playtest patch notes for the Echoing Spire update spell out the trade: you hand in a full artifact set for a Grimoire, with **base-class Grimoires coming from Box of Origin sets** and advanced-class ones from Box of Mastery sets. Boxes of Origins themselves drop from monsters (the database records Ruby Gazer at 1%). Their numbers are larger too — around **+2% per enhancement** and a set bonus of **All Stats +3** instead of +1.

Grimoires are their own class-locked equip slot — three at a time, each granting a unique passive. The official 0.31.0 Dark Fortress notes add that **Grimoires are now character bound** (the old "convert Grimoires into cosmetics" route is closed) and preview "**Advanced Artifacts and Grimoires**" as work in progress. So do not vendor a Box of Origins — but none of these sets' unlock conditions is officially documented. **[Unconfirmed]**

## 6. How to Farm Artifacts Without Wasting Weeks

1. **Farm the area, not the monster.** These are area drops — camp a dense spawn and clear it.
2. **Expect a grind.** Recorded rates run 1–10% per monster, and you need four different pieces of one series.
3. **Do not over-refine the starter set.** +0.125% per enhancement is tiny; spend materials on level-appropriate gear instead (see the [Crafting Guide](/guides/crafting-guide)).
4. **A partial set of the right damage type beats a full set of the wrong one.** The per-piece +3% is most of the value.
5. **Mind the map cap.** Maps hold roughly 30 players and spawns do not scale, so a full map means no mobs — channel-hop or farm off-peak (see the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide) and [Farming & Resource Guide](/guides/farming-resource-guide)).

---

## 7. Common Mistakes

- **Wearing one piece and wondering where the set bonus is.** All four types, or nothing.
- **Chasing Steelheart on a mage** because a build guide used it. Match the damage type.
- **Confusing artifacts with cards** and farming the wrong route entirely.
