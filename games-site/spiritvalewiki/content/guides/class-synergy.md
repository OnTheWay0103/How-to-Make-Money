## Class Synergy Guide — SpiritVale

Party composition is the difference between a smooth dungeon clear and a frustrating wipe session in SpiritVale. While any group of classes can clear content, certain combinations unlock powerful synergy bonuses that dramatically increase your party's effectiveness. This guide covers class pairings, buff and debuff stacking rules, and optimal compositions for every content type.

### Understanding Synergy Bonuses

SpiritVale's synergy system rewards smart class combinations. When specific classes are in the same party, they unlock passive bonuses that stack on top of normal class abilities.

| Class Pairing | Synergy Bonus | Requirements |
|---------------|---------------|--------------|
| Paladin + Priest | Holy Conviction: +15% healing received, +10% holy damage | Both must be advanced class |
| Wizard + Sniper | Ranged Supremacy: +12% ranged damage, +8% cast speed | Both level 40+ |
| Berserker + Shinobi | Blood and Shadow: +20% critical damage on bleeding targets | Berserker level 35+, Shinobi level 35+ |
| Necromancer + Summoner | Undead Army: Summoned units deal +18% damage | Both must have summon skills equipped |
| Knight + Acolyte | Steadfast Guardian: +10% damage reduction for support classes | Knight in standard form |
| Berserker + Priest | Blood Rites: Healing skills have a 15% chance to grant a damage buff | Priest level 30+ |
| Wizard + Necromancer | Arcane Decay: Magic damage reduces enemy magic defense by 5% (stacks 3 times) | Both level 45+ |
| Paladin + Gunslinger | Light and Lead: +8% damage when attacking the same target | Paladin level 40+, Gunslinger level 40+ |

### Buff and Debuff Stacking Rules

Understanding how buffs and debuffs stack is critical for optimizing party performance:

#### Buff Stacking

- **Same buff type does not stack.** Two Priests casting the same attack buff on the same player will only get the highest value. Always coordinate buffs between support classes.
- **Different buff types stack multiplicatively.** An attack buff (+20% damage) combined with a critical rate buff (+15% crit) applies both effects independently. This is why having diverse support options matters.
- **Class-specific buffs stack with generic buffs.** A Paladin's "Holy Strength" (+15% holy damage) stacks with a Priest's "Blessing" (+10% all damage).

#### Debuff Stacking

- **Elemental debuffs stack to a cap.** Fire, Ice, and Lightning exposure debuffs each stack up to 5 times on a target. Different elements stack independently.
- **Armor reduction debuffs have diminishing returns.** The first source applies at full value, the second at 50%, and the third at 25%.
- **Crowd control debuffs do not stack.** A frozen enemy cannot be stunned — one CC effect overwrites another. Coordinate crowd control to avoid wasting cooldowns.

### Tank + Healer + DPS Synergy Combos

#### The Classic Trio (Knight + Acolyte + Warrior)

This is the most reliable composition for dungeons. The Knight provides consistent aggro and damage reduction. The Acolyte keeps the party healed with efficient single-target heals. The Warrior dishes out steady melee damage and can off-tank if needed.

- **Strengths:** Easy to play, forgiving, works with any two DPS additions
- **Weaknesses:** Lower DPS output than advanced compositions
- **Best for:** Leveling dungeons, learning new content

#### The Aggressive Push (Berserker + Priest + Wizard)

High-risk, high-reward. The Berserker draws aggro with raw damage output while the Priest provides reactive healing. The Wizard adds AoE burst to clear trash packs quickly.

- **Strengths:** Fast clear times, excellent for farming
- **Weaknesses:** Requires precise positioning, punishing on mistakes
- **Best for:** Farming runs, overgeared content

#### The Fortress (Paladin + Priest + Sniper)

Maximum survivability. The Paladin's defensive toolkit combined with the Priest's healing creates an almost unkillable core. The Sniper provides safe, consistent ranged damage.

- **Strengths:** Extremely safe, excellent for progression
- **Weaknesses:** Slow clear speed, lacks burst damage
- **Best for:** First-time boss kills, hard mode dungeons

### Advanced Synergy Strategies

#### Elemental Stacking

Building a party around a single element amplifies damage significantly. A fire-focused party (Wizard, Berserker with fire weapon, Acolyte with fire buff) can stack fire exposure to the 5-cap quickly, dealing 25% increased fire damage across the entire party.

#### Cooldown Rotation

Coordinate defensive and offensive cooldowns to stack synergy bonuses during burn phases. For example, a Paladin's "Guardian Shield" (50% damage reduction for 8 seconds) combined with a Priest's "Divine Barrier" (30% damage reduction for 6 seconds) overlapping during a boss's enrage phase creates an 80-second window of nearly invulnerable tanking.

#### Role Flexibility

Advanced compositions benefit from flexible roles. A Shinobi can off-tank in emergencies with evasion skills. A Necromancer's summons can body-block boss mechanics. A Gunslinger provides consistent damage regardless of positioning. Building flexibility into your composition reduces wipe risk.

### Common Composition Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Double Tank on easy content | Halves your DPS output | One Tank is sufficient for Tier 1 |
| No AoE DPS | Trash packs take too long | Include at least one AoE class (Wizard, Berserker) |
| Overlapping support buffs | Wasted potential | Coordinate which support uses which buff |
| Ignoring elemental exposure | Missing 25% free damage | Build around one or two elements |
| Same-class stacking | No synergy bonus | Diversify your class selection |
