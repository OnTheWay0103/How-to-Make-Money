# SpiritVale Daily Quests Guide — Daily & Weekly Routines

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 9, 2026**

SpiritVale offers a rich system of daily and weekly repeatable quests that provide consistent experience, gold, reputation, and rare materials. This guide breaks down every repeatable quest, the optimal order to complete them, and how to maximize your rewards per minute.

---

## 1. Understanding the Reset Cycle

SpiritVale operates on two reset cycles:

- **Daily Quests:** Reset at 05:00 server time every day. Each daily takes 5-15 minutes to complete.
- **Weekly Quests:** Reset on Monday at 05:00 server time. Weekly quests take 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on the objective.

Server time is displayed in the top-right corner of the game UI. Plan your sessions around these resets to avoid wasting potential rewards.

**What resets weekly (Monday 05:00):** weekly quests, raid lockouts (Tiers 1-4), arena weekly ranking, and guild war registration windows. World bosses do **not** reset on Monday — they respawn on their own timers (see the [World Boss Chart](/guides/world-boss-chart)), so their windows drift through the week.

## 2. Daily Quest Guide

| Quest Name | NPC Location | Level Requirement | Time to Complete | Primary Rewards |
|------------|-------------|-------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Bounty Board | Vale's Rest Bulletin Board | 15+ | 5 min | Gold, XP tokens |
| Material Delivery | Any Town Material Trader | 20+ | 3 min | Crafting materials |
| Dungeon Daily | Dungeon Entrance NPC | 25+ | 10-15 min | Equipment, dungeon tokens |
| Arena Daily | Colosseum Registrar | 30+ | 8-12 min | PvP tokens, gold |
| Gathering Run | Harvest Guild NPC | 10+ | 5 min | Herbs, ores, cooking ingredients |

### Optimal Daily Quest Order

For maximum efficiency, follow this route:

1. **Log in at Vale's Rest.** Pick up the Bounty Board quest (usually kill 10-15 mobs in a specific zone).
2. **Accept Gathering Run** from the Harvest Guild (located two buildings north of the Bulletin Board).
3. **Complete both in the same zone.** Choose a zone that has both the bounty targets and gather-able resources. Most zones overlap well.
4. **Return to town. Hand in both.** Turn around and accept Material Delivery — turn in crafting materials you likely already have in your inventory.
5. **Queue for Dungeon Daily** and **Arena Daily** while in town. These require group matching; queue time is a good moment to organize your inventory or manage guild activities.

**Total time:** 25-35 minutes for all 5 dailies.

### Daily Reward Details

| Quest | Gold | XP | Tokens | Notes |
|-------|------|-----|--------|-------|
| Bounty Board | Modest | Large | — | Scales with level |
| Material Delivery | Small | Small | Material Tokens | Use tokens for rare crafting items |
| Dungeon Daily | Moderate | Large | Dungeon Tokens | Exchange for gear at Token Trader |
| Arena Daily | Moderate | — | PvP Tokens | Win not required, participation counts |
| Gathering Run | — | Small | — | Keeps your crafting stock full |

## 3. Weekly Quest Guide

Weekly quests offer significantly larger rewards and should be prioritized each reset.

### Main Weekly Quests

| Quest | Requirements | Time | Rewards |
|-------|-------------|------|---------|
| World Boss Weekly | Defeat 3 world bosses (any) | 30-60 min | Rare weapon box, large gold payout, XP elixir |
| Card Collector | Collect 50 cards (any rarity) | Variable (playtime) | Random Rare card, Card Dust |
| Dungeon Marathon | Clear 5 different dungeons | 60-90 min | Epic equipment chest, Dungeon Tokens |
| PvP Champion | Win 10 arena matches | 45-90 min | PvP weapon box, PvP Tokens |
| Crafting Expert | Craft 100 items (any level) | Variable | Master Crafter title, Material Tokens |

### Weekly Reputation Quests

Each major faction offers weekly reputation quests. Raising reputation unlocks exclusive gear recipes, cosmetics, and discounts:

| Faction | Quest | Max Rep Unlock |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Vale's Rest Guard | Kill 50 enemies in Whisperwind Plains | Guard armor set (Honored) |
| Adventurer's League | Complete 10 dungeons | Teleport scroll (Revered) |
| Colosseum Committee | Win 15 PvP matches | Gladiator weapon skin (Exalted) |
| Merchant's Union | Sell a large volume of items | Reduced market fees (Honored) |

## 4. The Efficient Weekly Clearing Order

1. **Monday morning:** finish any weekly quests still open from last week (they expire at 05:00), then pick up all new weekly quests.
2. **Monday-Thursday:** clear raid lockouts in tier order — Tier 1 and 2 first (they are fast), then Tier 3 with your regular party. Leave Tier 4 for the weekend when your full 10-player roster is online.
3. **Wednesday-Saturday:** attend world boss spawns that fit your schedule — watch the official Discord for callouts; the flagship Spirit of the Vale is the one to prioritize.
4. **Before Sunday:** register your guild for the weekly war (see the [Guild Guide](/guides/guild-guide)) and run the dungeon weekly — it only takes 30 minutes and it resets on Monday.
5. **Sunday night:** spend tokens before the reset; token exchange inventories rotate with the update cadence.

## 5. Time-Efficient Routine Templates

### Casual Routine (30-45 minutes daily)

1. Bounty Board — 5 min (kill zone quest)
2. Gathering Run — combined with bounty zone — 5 min
3. Material Delivery — quick turn-in — 3 min
4. Queue Arena Daily while doing other activities — 10 min active

**Weekly target:** Complete World Boss Weekly + Card Collector + one reputation quest.

### Hardcore Routine (1-2 hours daily)

1. All 5 dailies in optimal route order — 30 min
2. One dungeon run (ticking Dungeon Weekly counter) — 15 min
3. 3-5 arena matches (ticking PvP Weekly counter) — 20 min
4. Crafting overflow (passive — craft while waiting for queues) — 10 min
5. Guild quests — 15 min

**Weekly target:** All weekly quests completed by Wednesday, reputation quests done by Friday, world boss circuit on Saturday.

### Efficiency Tips

- **Stack quest zones.** Never accept a quest that sends you to a different zone than your other active quests.
- **Use Warp Scrolls.** Keep a stack of Warp Scrolls for rapid travel between towns. They're inexpensive from the Magic Shop.
- **Pre-craft.** If you know the Crafting Expert weekly is coming, stockpile simple crafts ready to turn in immediately after reset.
- **Party for world bosses.** World boss spawns are advertised in World Chat and the official Discord. Join a party for credit — soloing is inefficient.
- **Skip low-value dailies.** If a Bounty Board quest sends you across the map, abandon it and take a different one. You can reroll once per day for free.

## 8. Common Mistakes

1. **Letting weekly quests expire.** They take 30 minutes and pay tokens; abandoning them is leaving value on the table.
2. **Missing world boss windows.** The flagship world boss drops the best open-world loot in the game — set an alarm and join Discord callouts.
3. **Raid lockout overlap.** Tier lockouts share a Monday reset — don't save Tier 1 for Sunday, or you'll rush all four tiers in one night.
4. **Not registering for guild wars.** Registration closes before the weekend; a missed registration is a missed chest for the whole guild.
5. **Spending tokens on Sunday night.** Token traders rotate stock at the weekly reset — check tomorrow's stock before spending today's tokens.

**Final note:** if your server is quiet at your play hours, remember server transfer is free and account-wide — a busier server means world boss trains actually fill.
