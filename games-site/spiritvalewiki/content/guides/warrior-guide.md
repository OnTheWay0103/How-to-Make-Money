## Warrior Overview

The Warrior is SpiritVale's aggressive melee class, built around heavy weapons and wide, sweeping attacks. Warriors fight with **axes, swords, and scythes**, deal strong physical damage and critical hits, and have the **highest natural health growth of any base class** — a beginner-friendly package that scales cleanly from your first axe to endgame.

## Warrior Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Highest natural HP growth of base classes | Fewer defensive tools than Knight |
| Powerful AoE (Whirlwind, Vortex Slash) | Positional — must close distance |
| Beginner-friendly rotation | Burns mana quickly in extended fights |
| Strong combo finishers | Falls off without weapon upgrades |

## Stat Allocation

| Stat | Priority | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| **STR** | Max to 99 | Primary damage stat — melee attack, skill damage, resists Bleed/Stagger |
| **VIT** | To 50 | HP and defense — resists Stun/Decay |
| **LUK** | Optional | Crit chance and crit damage |
| **AGI** | Situational | Attack speed once damage is settled |

**Efficient order:** base STR to 50 → VIT to 50 → finish STR to 99 — durability without delaying your damage.

## Warrior Skill Tree

| Skill | Points | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Axe Mastery** | 10/10 | Core passive — raises axe/sword/scythe attack and crit |
| **Whirlwind** | 10/10 | Spinning AoE that hits all nearby enemies over time |
| **Axe Quicken** | 5/5 | Self-buff for attack speed and crit chance |
| **Stomp** | 5/5 | Ground slam that damages and stuns enemies in a radius |
| **Honed Blade** | 5/5 | Passive — increases critical hit damage |
| **Natural Resistance** | 5/5 | Passive resistance to all elements except Neutral |
| **Vortex Slash** | Partial | Spinning slash that pulls enemies toward you |
| **Twin Cleave** | Combo finisher | High-damage finisher — use inside a Combo Ready window |

**Combo system:** **Bash** creates a brief **Combo Ready** window; finishers like Twin Cleave inside it deal roughly **50% more damage**. Learn the rhythm: stun → group → finisher → repeat.

**Leveling rotation:** Stomp (stun) → Vortex Slash (pull the pack together) → Twin Cleave (finisher) → Bash → repeat.

## Best Warrior Builds

### Leveling Build (AoE Grind)
- **Focus:** Whirlwind clears and sustain
- **Skills:** Axe Mastery (max), Whirlwind (max), Axe Quicken, Stomp, Natural Resistance
- **Gear:** Any good axe early — upgrade your weapon every 10 levels
- **Playstyle:** Pull 3-5 monsters, Stomp to stun, Whirlwind them down

### Combo Burst Build (Single Target)
- **Focus:** Combo Ready finisher damage
- **Skills:** Bash, Twin Cleave, Honed Blade, Vortex Slash
- **Gear:** Crit-focused weapons and cards
- **Playstyle:** Bash → finisher inside the window for the bonus damage

### Endgame Hybrid (Melee Pressure)
- **Focus:** Ranged pressure with melee burst
- **Skills:** Whirlwind, Stomp, Twin Cleave, Bash
- **Gear:** Current-tier weapon with crit affixes; defensive artifact set
- **Playstyle:** Trigger Combo Ready with Bash, then unload finishers at range advantage

## Warrior Leveling Guide (1-50)

| Level Range | Area | Focus |
|-------------|------|-------|
| 1-15 | Starting zone | Learn Stomp → Vortex Slash → Twin Cleave combo |
| 15-30 | Whisperwind Plains / Ruins | AoE grind with Whirlwind; upgrade axe at 20 |
| 30-40 | Ironforge Mines / Blackwater Marsh | Farm gear and crit cards |
| 40-50 | Frostpeak Tundra | Prep for Berserker advancement at J50 |

**Leveling tips:** Whirlwind is your best friend — always fight packs. Keep your axe within 10 levels of your character; kill speed drops off hard with a stale weapon.

## Warrior Gear

| Slot | Recommendation | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Headgear | Any +attack/defense helm | Drops |
| Armor | Heavy armor with STR and defense | Zone drops |
| Weapon | Current-tier axe — highest base damage | Drops, market |
| Accessory | STR and crit accessories | Drops |
| Card | Crit rate and crit damage cards | Card system |

There is no single best-in-slot list for Warriors in EA — balance patches move the top picks. The rule is simple: STR and crit affixes beat everything else.

## Berserker Preview (Job Level 50)

At **Job Level 50**, complete the **"Blood and Steel"** quest chain from your class trainer — a trial that ends with a duel against the Ironforge Champion — to advance from Warrior to **Berserker**. You keep your Warrior skills and gain the Berserker tree on top.

Key Berserker skills:

| Skill | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Rampage** | Signature buff — greatly increased attack speed for a short time at the cost of taking more damage |
| Wild Charge | Mobility gap closer |
| Axe Throw | Ranged physical option |

**Build advice:** pick **one** direction — critical hit, attack speed, or skill damage — rather than spreading across every offensive mechanic. Rampage is your burst window: time it with your Combo Ready finishers and back off when it ends.

## Warrior in PvP

Berserkers are high-pressure arena fighters: gap close, burst inside Combo Ready and Rampage windows, and out-sustain skirmishes. The weakness is predictability — opponents will crowd control you the moment you charge. Time engagements around your buffs and Combo Ready windows.
