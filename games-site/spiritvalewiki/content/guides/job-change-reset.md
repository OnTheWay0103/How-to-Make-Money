## Overview

SpiritVale lets you change your class, reset skills, and reallocate stats — and the good news is it's **completely free**. This guide covers every respec option, class advancement, and when it's worth doing.

## How to Respec (Free, at the Waybinder)

**Respeccing is free.** Visit the **Waybinder NPC** in Vale's Rest (or any major town) and select "Reset" to refund all of your spent stat points and skill points. There is no gold cost and no cooldown — you can respec as often as you like.

This means:

- Experimenting with a new stat allocation costs nothing
- Switching from a solo leveling build to a group/raid build is a 30-second stop at the Waybinder
- You never need to hoard respec items or wait for balance patches to respec

**The only catch:** the Waybinder resets points within your current class. Changing your *class* is a different system (below).

## How to Change Job/Class

### Class Advancement (Job Level 50)

Advanced classes are not a class change you pay for — they are an evolution you unlock:

1. Reach **Job Level 50** with your base class (Acolyte, Knight, Mage, Rogue, Scout, Summoner, or Warrior).
2. Talk to your **class trainer** and accept the advancement quest chain (each advanced class has its own chain — see the [Advanced Classes Guide](/guides/advanced-classes-guide)).
3. Defeat the class-specific trial boss.
4. Return to unlock your advanced class.

### Switching Between Advanced Classes

Once an advanced class is unlocked, you can switch between your unlocked advanced classes **for free** at the Waybinder NPC — no fee, no cooldown. You must complete each class's quest chain once to unlock it.

**What does NOT change:**

- Base level and job level stay as they are
- Your gear and inventory are preserved
- You can never return to a base class after advancing — you are permanently an advanced class

## How to Reset Skills and Stats

Skill and stat points are both reset at the **Waybinder NPC** (not a paid trainer):

1. Visit the **Waybinder** in any major town.
2. Select "Reset Skill Points" and/or "Reset Stat Points".
3. All points are refunded for redistribution. No fee.

### Recommended Stat Allocation

| Class | Primary Stat | Secondary |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Knight | VIT (Vitality) | STR |
| Paladin | STR | VIT |
| Priest | INT (Intellect) | VIT |
| Shinobi | AGI (Agility) | LUCK |
| Wizard | INT | VIT |
| Warrior | STR | VIT |
| Gunslinger | DEX | AGI |

## How to Change Stance

Stances are combat styles within a class. To change:

1. Open your **Skills menu** (K)
2. Click the **Stance** tab
3. Select a new stance (must be unlocked first)
4. Stances can be swapped freely outside of combat

Stances are unlocked through class quests and leveling, not purchased.

## When Should You Respec?

### Good Times to Respec
- Hitting Level 50 and optimizing for endgame
- Switching from solo leveling build to group/raid build
- After major game balance patches
- When joining a guild that needs a specific role

### Don't Respec For
- Minor skill point adjustments (wait until you have more points)
- Testing builds (the Waybinder makes this free — go ahead, but change one thing at a time)
- Chasing the meta on patch day (wait a week for the meta to settle)
