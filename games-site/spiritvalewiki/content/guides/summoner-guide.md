## Summoner & Necromancer Overview

The Summoner fights alongside companions that absorb enemy pressure while you support from range — one of **SpiritVale's strongest solo classes**, since pets tank while you debuff and the combo handles content well above your level. At **Job Level 50** the Summoner advances into the **Necromancer**, adding undead minions, Decay damage-over-time, corpse-based abilities, and persistent area damage.

## How Summons Scale (The Critical Mechanic)

This is the single most important thing to understand about the class:

**Summons do NOT inherit your attribute points.** Instead, they inherit your **equipment stats, artifact stats, cards, gems, enchantments, and autocast effects**. Autocast effects on gear can even be triggered independently by your summons.

The practical result: **gear matters more than your stat allocation** — two Summoners with the same INT but different equipment can have wildly different summons.

## Summoner & Necromancer Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Pet tanks — you take almost no damage | Heavily gear-dependent (summons scale off gear, not stats) |
| Excellent solo farming and bossing | Pet pathing can be awkward in tight spaces |
| Strong DoT and area damage (Necromancer) | Requires minion management (resummon, reposition) |
| Flexible — magic, melee, or minion archetypes | Weaker burst than Wizard or Berserker |

## Stat Allocation

- **INT first** — almost all Summoner damage is magic-based, and INT also feeds your mana pool.
- **VIT second** — survivability, since your pets take the hits but a stray AoE can still kill you.
- **DEX** only if you need cast speed breakpoints.
- **Planning a STR-scythe Necromancer later?** Most Necromancer builds scale with STR (scythe builds) or convert STR into Magic Attack on mace builds, since many magic skills double-scale. Keep INT early and respect your stat plan when you promote.

## Summoner Skill Priority (Leveling 1-50)

| Order | Skill | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | **Soul Strike** (first 5 points) | Reliable single-target attack for early game |
| 2 | **Summon Angel** (3 points) | Healing companion that keeps you alive |
| 3 | **Dissonance Well** (max) | Your main leveling skill — AoE damage plus slow |
| 4 | **Invoker** | Aura boosting magic damage and casting speed |
| 5 | **Suppression Field** | Pulls enemies in; can seal casting (100% silence vs. mobs at level 5) |
| 6 | **Guardian Bond** | Vitality + regeneration if your Angel keeps dying |
| 7 | **Summon Mastery** | Hold off until your gear can feed it — summons scale off equipment |

**Leveling rotation:** Dissonance Well on the pack → Suppression Field to pull and silence → Soul Strike stragglers while your pet tanks.

## Best Summoner Builds

### Pet-Focused Build (Solo)
- **Focus:** Let summons do the heavy lifting; you stay at range
- **Skills:** Summon Mastery, Dissonance Well, Invoker, Suppression Field
- **Gear:** **Jack-o'-Lantern** headgear (chance to autocast Death Coil, healing summons), **Soulbinder Set** (defense, HP, Summon HP), **Corporeal artifact set** (strengthens summons directly)
- **Playstyle:** Send pet in, debuff, resummon below 30% HP

### Auto-Attack Healing Farm Build (High-Level Grinding)
- **Focus:** Let your summon hold aggro while you auto attack and procs heal — handles monsters 8-10 levels above you
- **Gear:** Elixir Gourd (chance to cast Mass Heal on attack), Soul Binder Armor (+5 summon stats), Grouping Cat headgear (triggers Thunderbolt)
- **Playstyle:** Stand behind the summon, auto attack, let heal procs carry you

### Direct-Nuke Build (Necromancer, Endgame)
- **Focus:** Bone Spear, Death Nova, and Corpse Explosion burst
- **Gear:** **Binding Spirits Staff** (faster cast, boosts Death Nova and Death Coil), Sapphire Crown, Scholar Glasses, Mind set
- **Playstyle:** Apply Decay, explode corpses, nuke with Death Nova

## Summoner & Necromancer Leveling Guide (1-50)

| Level Range | Area | Focus |
|-------------|------|-------|
| 1-15 | Starting zone | Soul Strike; keep Summon Angel alive |
| 15-30 | Whisperwind Plains / Ruins | Dissonance Well packs; farm early gear (Combat Knife, Jack-o'-Lantern) |
| 30-40 | Ironforge Mines / Blackwater Marsh | Invoker + Suppression Field; build Soulbinder set |
| 40-50 | Shadowfen Depths | Prep for Necromancer advancement at J50 |

At **Job Level 50**, travel to **Festering Woods** and find the **Necromancer Master** (top-right corner of the second map) to advance to **Necromancer** (advanced job levels 1-70, new skill tree on top of your Summoner tree).

## Necromancer Skills & Minions

| Skill / Minion | Effect |
|----------------|--------|
| **Skeleton Mastery** | Must-have passive — raises the power of all undead summons |
| **Bone Spear** | High-damage projectile that applies Decay |
| **Bone Spikes** | AoE ground spike damage |
| **Death Nova** | Massive AoE nuke |
| **Corpse Explosion** | Burst damage from defeated enemies |
| **Death Coil** | Damages enemies while healing your summons |
| **Summon Command / Summon Recall** | Minion positioning control for boss fights |
| **Abomination / Skeleton / Skeleton Mage / Wraith** | Core minions — **summon the Wraith last** (longest cooldown) |

**Never equip a shield.** It cancels your two-handed stance, costing a 25% bonus to physical and magic attack — the damage loss is almost never worth the defense.

**Necromancer archetypes:** the class supports three primary paths — **Magic** (staff/scythe/mace), **Melee** (STR-scaling scythe), and **Minion** (semi-AFK, summons deliver your build power). Everything else is a hybrid of these.

**Endgame tip:** once you can reliably defeat the Sting boss, make it your primary reanimation target — it is the strongest early-endgame minion you can field.
