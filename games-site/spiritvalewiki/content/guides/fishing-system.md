## Fishing System Guide — SpiritVale

Fishing in SpiritVale is far more than a side activity. It is a fully fleshed-out system with dedicated zones, multiple bait types, rare fish with unique properties, quest lines, and crafting applications. Whether you are chasing the biggest catches or farming materials for potions, this guide covers everything you need to know.

### Getting Started with Fishing

To begin fishing, you need a Fishing Rod and access to a fishing spot. The basic Fishing Rod is obtained from the **Fisherman's Guild** quest in Vale's Rest (level 10 requirement). The quest takes approximately 10 minutes and teaches the core mechanics.

**Fishing mechanics overview:**

- Cast your line by pressing the interact key at a fishing spot
- A bobber appears in the water. Watch for it to dip below the surface
- When the bobber dips, click to set the hook. The timing window varies by fish rarity
- A mini-game begins: keep the tension bar in the green zone while the fish struggles
- Higher-tier rods give a wider green zone and reduce fish struggle time

### Bait Types and Crafting

Bait determines which fish you attract and your catch rate. Higher-quality bait increases rare fish chances but costs more to craft.

| Bait Type | Source | Effect on Catch Rate | Unlock Level |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|--------------|
| Earthworm | Digging (any soil) | Common fish only | 10 |
| Crickets | Caught in fields (net) | Small boost to rare chances for zone fish | 15 |
| Shrimp Bait | Purchased (Fishing Guild) | Modest boost to rare chances in coastal zones | 20 |
| Iron Lure | Crafted (Iron Ore, Feather) | Good boost to rare chances, attracts heavy fish | 25 |
| Ice Worm | Glacier Stream fishing (rare drop) | Strong boost to rare chances in cold zones | 35 |
| Luminescent Bait | Crafted (Glowing Moss, Abyss Essence) | Very strong boost to rare chances in dark zones | 45 |
| Starlight Lure | Celestial quest reward | The strongest rare boost, exclusive to Celestial Pond | 50+ |
| Master Bait | Fishing Guild reputation reward | Good boost to rare chances in all zones | Fishing Level 50 |

### Fishing Quests and Progression

The Fisherman's Guild offers a quest chain that unlocks new zones, better rods, and exclusive rewards:

**Tier 1 Quests (Level 10-20):**
- "The First Catch" — Catch 5 fish in Vale's Rest Pond. Reward: Basic Fishing Rod
- "Expanding Horizons" — Fish in Silvertide Harbor. Reward: Tackle Box (+5 inventory slots for fish)

**Tier 2 Quests (Level 20-35):**
- "River Master" — Catch 10 different fish species. Reward: Fiberglass Rod (wider tension zone)
- "The Big One" — Catch a rare fish. Reward: Rare Fish Bait recipe

**Tier 3 Quests (Level 35-50):**
- "Deep Waters" — Fish in Glacier Stream. Reward: Carbon Fiber Rod (significantly wider tension zone)
- "Fishing Tournament" — Compete in the weekly fishing tournament. Reward: Trophy (furniture item)

**Tier 4 Quests (Level 50+):**
- "Abyssal Angler" — Catch the Void Serpent. Reward: Master Rod (best rod in the game)
- "Heavenly Catch" — Reach Fishing Level 50 and catch the Astral Serpent. Reward: "Angler of Legend" title

### Fishing Tips and Strategies

1. **Level fishing early.** Fishing levels unlock bait crafting recipes and better rods. Even if you are not interested in rare fish, leveling fishing to 25 for the Iron Lure is worthwhile.
2. **Use the right bait for the zone.** Using Earthworm in Glacier Stream will catch only common fish. Always match bait to zone for best results.
3. **Fish during your downtime.** Fishing spots are located near town waypoints. Fish while waiting for party members or during cooldown periods between dungeons.
4. **Stockpile rare fish for crafting.** The consumables crafted from rare fish are valuable for raid progression. Moonlight Potions alone are worth the grind.
5. **Join the weekly tournament.** Even if you do not win, participation rewards include bait packs and fishing XP scrolls.

### Fishing and Crafting Integration

Fishing directly supports SpiritVale's crafting system:

- **Cooking:** Fish are primary ingredients in stat-boosting meals
- **Alchemy:** Rare fish create potent potions and elixirs
- **Gear Crafting:** Certain equipment pieces require fish-derived materials
- **Furnishing:** Decorative fish trophies for player housing
