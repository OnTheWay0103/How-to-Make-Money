## Overview

Enchanting is the primary gear upgrade system in SpiritVale. Each piece of equipment can be enchanted up to **+15**, with each level providing increasing stat bonuses. However, failure carries consequences — from downgrading to outright destruction. This guide covers everything from basic enchanting to endgame +15 strategies.

---

## Enchantment Tiers

| Level | Bonus per Slot | Failure Result | Success Rate (Base) |
|-------|---------------|----------------|---------------------|
| +1 to +3 | +2% primary stat | Nothing | 100% |
| +4 to +6 | +3% primary stat | Downgrade 1 level | 80% → 60% |
| +7 to +9 | +5% primary stat | Downgrade to +6 | 50% → 30% |
| +10 to +12 | +7% primary stat | Downgrade to +9 | 25% → 15% |
| +13 to +14 | +10% primary stat | Downgrade to +10 | 10% → 5% |
| +15 | +15% primary stat | **Item destroyed** | 3% |

> **Warning:** At +15, failure destroys the item completely. Only attempt this with a spare copy of the gear or a Destruction Protection Scroll.

## The Safe Upgrade Path

For players who want to minimize risk, follow this progression:

```text
Phase 1: Full +6 on all gear
  - Cost: ~50,000 gold
  - Time: 1–2 days
  - Risk: Near zero (downgrade only from +4)

Phase 2: Full +9 on weapon + chest
  - Cost: ~300,000 gold
  - Time: 1 week
  - Risk: Medium (downgrade to +6)

Phase 3: Full +12 on all major pieces
  - Cost: ~2,000,000 gold
  - Time: 2–3 weeks
  - Risk: High (use protection scrolls)

Phase 4: +15 weapon (attempt at your own risk)
  - Cost: 5,000,000+ gold
  - Time: 1 month+
  - Risk: Extreme (item destruction)
```

## Protection Scrolls

| Scroll | Effect | Price (AH) | Source |
|--------|--------|------------|--------|
| Preservation Scroll | Prevents downgrade on failure (+4 to +9) | 15,000g | Daily login reward |
| Greater Protection | Prevents downgrade (+10 to +12) | 80,000g | Crafting (10 Preservation) |
| Destruction Shield | Prevents item destruction on +15 failure | 500,000g | Sovereign drop, cash shop |
| Blessing of Fortune | +15% success rate for next attempt | 40,000g | Nightmare dungeon weekly |

> **Pro tip:** Always use a Blessing of Fortune when attempting +12 or higher. The expected value improvement is significant.

## Enchanting Events

SpiritVale runs **Enchanting Boost Events** approximately once per month.

| Event | Effect | Best Strategy |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| Double Success Rate | Base rates doubled | Attempt all +10 to +12 pushes |
| No Downgrade Week | Failure never downgrades | Push everything to +9 |
| Free Destruction Shield | One free shield per character | Attempt +15 on your main weapon |
| Essence Discount | 50% less essence consumed per attempt | Stock up and enchant alt gear |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Enchanting low-tier gear | Wasted materials on gear you will replace | Wait until you have BiS or near-BiS |
| Skipping protection scrolls on +10 | Expensive downgrades | Always use Preservation at +10+ |
| Spreading essence across alts | No character reaches power breakpoints | Focus one DPS + one Tank main |
| Enchanting without event bonus | 50%+ more expensive | Save Sovereign Essence for events |
| +15 on wrong item | Catastrophic gold loss | Triple-check the item before clicking |
