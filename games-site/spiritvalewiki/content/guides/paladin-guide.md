## Paladin Overview

The Paladin is a hybrid class that combines melee combat with holy magic. Capable of both dealing damage and healing, the Paladin is one of the most versatile classes in SpiritVale.

## Paladin Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Can heal and DPS | Jack of all trades, master of none |
| Strong solo class | Lower pure DPS than Shinobi |
| Party buffs | Lower pure healing than Priest |
| Good survivability | Mana management required |

## Best Paladin Builds

### Healadin (Group Support)
- **Skills**: Divine Light (max), Lay on Hands (max), Blessing of Light, Cleanse
- **Gear**: Wisdom-focused armor, healing power accessories
- **Playstyle**: Stay behind tanks, heal party, cleanse debuffs on cooldown

### Retribution Paladin (Solo/DPS)
- **Skills**: Crusader Strike (max), Holy Wrath (max), Judgment, Zeal
- **Gear**: Strength + Wisdom hybrid, two-handed weapon
- **Playstyle**: Self-buff with Zeal, rotate damage skills, self-heal as needed

### Hybrid Paladin (Flex)
- **Skills**: Crusader Strike, Divine Light, Blessing of Light, Cleanse
- **Gear**: Balanced stats, one-handed + shield
- **Playstyle**: Fill whatever role the party needs moment-to-moment

## Paladin Leveling Guide

| Level Range | Area | Tips |
|-------------|------|------|
| 1-20 | Starting Zone + Forest | Go Retribution for faster clears |
| 20-35 | Crimson Peaks | Start building a healing gear set |
| 35-50 | Endgame Zones | Respec to Healadin for raid groups |

Paladins level faster than Knights thanks to self-healing. The Retribution path is best for solo leveling.
