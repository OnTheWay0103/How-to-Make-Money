## Paladin Overview

The Paladin is the Knight's advanced class (unlocked at Job Level 50 through "The Light's Judgment" quest chain) — a hybrid that combines melee combat with holy magic. Capable of tanking, dealing damage, and supporting the party, the Paladin is one of the most versatile classes in SpiritVale and the community's #1 solo pick.

## Paladin Strengths & Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Can tank, heal, and DPS | Jack of all trades, master of none |
| Strong solo class | Lower pure DPS than Shinobi |
| Party buffs | Lower pure healing than Priest |
| Good survivability | Mana management required |

## Best Paladin Builds

### Tank Paladin (Group Content)
- **Skills**: Holy Shield (max), Consecrate, Smite, Guardian Aura
- **Gear**: STR + VIT, heavy armor, one-handed sword + shield
- **Playstyle**: Hold aggro, keep Consecrate under the boss, block telegraphed hits

### Retribution Paladin (Solo/DPS)
- **Skills**: Smite (max), Holy Strike (max), Consecrate, Sacred Charge
- **Gear**: STR + VIT hybrid, one-handed + shield or two-handed
- **Playstyle**: Charge in, drop Consecrate, rotate damage skills, heal yourself as needed

### Support Paladin (Flex)
- **Skills**: Holy Light, Blessing of Light, Purify, Holy Shield
- **Gear**: Balanced stats, one-handed + shield
- **Playstyle**: Fill whatever role the party needs moment-to-moment

## Paladin Leveling Guide

| Level Range | Area | Tips |
|-------------|------|------|
| 1-20 (Knight) | Vale's Rest / Sunny Meadows | Focus on Shield Bash and Holy Strike |
| 20-35 (Knight) | Whisperwind Plains | Group grinding for faster clears |
| 35-50 (Knight) | Frostpine Ridge | Build the STR/VIT base for Paladin |
| 50 (Paladin) | Soulfire Citadel Exterior | Run "The Light's Judgment" and respec into your final build |

Paladins level faster than Knights thanks to self-healing. The Retribution path is best for solo leveling; the tank path is what raid groups want at 50.

## Paladin Gear

| Slot | Recommendation | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Weapon | Tank or holy-damage sword — raid token gear is the top tier | Drops, raid tokens |
| Shield | Shield with block stats | Drops, crafted |
| Armor | Heavy plate with STR and VIT | Dungeon drops, raid tokens |
| Accessory | HP and defense accessories | Drops, market |

There is no single best-in-slot list for Paladins in EA — balance patches move the top picks. Prioritize STR and VIT, then defensive affixes.
