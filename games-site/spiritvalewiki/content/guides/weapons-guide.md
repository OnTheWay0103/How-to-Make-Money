# SpiritVale Weapons Guide — All Weapon Types, Best Weapons & Where to Farm

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Your weapon is the single largest stat share in SpiritVale — it contributes more to your damage than any other gear slot, and upgrading it is the highest-return investment at every level. This guide covers every weapon type, how weapons scale with your attributes, the two-handed stance rule, and the best-in-slot weapons per class for the current EA build.

## How Weapon Stats Work

### Attribute Scaling

Every weapon has a primary scaling stat — melee weapons scale with STR, bows and pistols with DEX, and staffs/tomes with INT. Attribute points in the matching stat multiply the weapon's base damage, so **keep your weapon's scaling stat in mind when allocating** — a Warrior with a level-appropriate sword but STR neglected will underperform.

### The Two-Handed Stance Rule (25% Bonus)

All two-handed weapons — two-handed swords, axes, scythes, staffs, tomes, bows, pistols, and daggers — benefit from a **two-handed stance bonus: +25% physical and magic attack**. Equipping a shield in the off-hand **cancels this bonus entirely**. For every class except Knight/Paladin, the 25% damage loss is almost never worth the block rating.

### Attack Speed Cap

Attack speed (ASPD) hard-caps at **193** in the current build. Do not waste stats, gems, or card slots past the cap — the cap is a real mechanic, not a soft target.

## Where to Farm Weapons

| Source | What It Drops | Effort |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| Leveling zones | Common/Uncommon weapons with class matching | Free, but replaced hourly |
| Field bosses (30-60 min respawn) | Unique named weapons (Alpha Fang, Guardian Blade) | Rotate spawns with a party |
| Dungeon bosses | Rare/Epic weapons — a good chance per unique piece | Group content |
| World bosses | Epic weapon chance, raid entry materials | 8-12 hour respawns |
| Raids | Epic/Legendary weapons (Corrupted Fang, World Breaker) | Weekly lockout |
| Player Market | Everything above — Epic weapons with wrong stats sell cheap | Buy low, check price trends |

**Market tip:** Epic weapons with unusable affixes are the best budget buys in the game — players vendor-quality gear sells for a fraction of crafted cost. See the [Trading Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) for price patterns.

## Weapon Upgrade Priority

Your weapon is **slot #1** in the upgrade order from the gear progression path:

1. **Keep it current while leveling** — a weapon 10+ levels stale cuts kill speed dramatically. Upgrade every 10 levels; skip armor upgrades first.
2. **Refine to +6 first** — the early refine levels are risk-free, and +6 is the efficiency sweet spot. Push toward +10 only on Epic+ weapons with Protection Scrolls.
3. **Never refine Common/Uncommon weapons** — you replace them in hours; the stones and gold compound.
4. **Refine before you transfer, transfer before you replace** — refinement carries to your next weapon at a small reduction.

## Common Weapon Mistakes

- **Equipping a shield on a class that doesn't need block** — you pay 25% damage for it
- **Stacking ASPD past 193** — wasted stats, wasted card slots
- **Ignoring your weapon's scaling stat** — DEX bows with zero DEX investment hit like toys
- **Holding a 5-level-old weapon to "save gold"** — it costs more in kill speed than it saves
- **Refining your leveling weapon** — the transfer system exists because refinement value is lost if you replace without transferring

## Quick Weapon Checklist

- [ ] Weapon within 10 levels of your character (leveling)
- [ ] No shield equipped on two-handed classes
- [ ] Scaling stat matches your weapon type
- [ ] ASPD 193 cap respected
- [ ] +6 refine before Heroic content; Epic+ before raids
- [ ] Epic weapons checked on the market before you craft one
