## Overview

SpiritVale is a **real-time action MMORPG** — there is no auto-battle or idle combat. You move your character, aim your attacks, and manage skills manually. If you are coming from a tab-target or auto-play MMO, the controls take a few hours to click. This guide covers the control scheme, how auto attack works, how to change stance, dual wielding, and the combat basics every new player needs.

## Basic Controls

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Move | WASD |
| Auto Attack | **C key** (press or hold) |
| Skills | Assigned to the skill bar at the bottom of the screen |
| Skills / Stance Menu | **K** |
| Interact / Loot | Click the highlighted target or use the interact prompt |

The enemy you are currently looking at is "highlighted" on screen — most attacks and skills target whatever is highlighted, so keep your cursor and camera pointed at the right monster.

## How Auto Attack Works

A dedicated **auto attack hotkey** defaults to the **C key**.

- Press or hold **C** to auto attack the highlighted enemy.
- Auto attack **automatically starts after you use a physical targeted attack skill** — so your skill rotation naturally flows into basic attacks.
- Holding C keeps you swinging until the target dies or you move away.

Auto attack matters most for **melee classes** (Knight, Warrior, Rogue) and for **farming weak monsters** without spending mana. Casters should treat C as a filler between skill cooldowns — your real damage comes from skills like Meteor Shower or Holy Light, not your staff swings.

**Tip:** When farming low-level mobs, hold C while your character runs between packs. You will chip down stragglers for zero mana cost.

## How to Change Stance

Stances are combat styles within a class that change how your skills behave. To change stance:

1. Open your **Skills menu** (K)
2. Click the **Stance** tab
3. Select a stance you have unlocked

Key rules:

- Stances are **unlocked through class quests and leveling** — they are not bought or equipped.
- You can swap stances **freely outside of combat**.
- Entering combat locks your current stance, so pick your stance before you pull.

Many melee classes have a defensive stance (better block and damage reduction) and an offensive stance (better attack speed or damage). Part of mastering your class is knowing which stance to enter before each fight.

## Dual Wielding in SpiritVale

Dual wielding is **class- and weapon-restricted** — you cannot put a second weapon in your off-hand on every class.

| Class | Weapon Setup |
|-------|--------------|
| Rogue | Dual daggers (fast, crit-focused) |
| Gunslinger | Dual pistols (advanced class, J50+) |
| Most melee classes | Two-handed weapon **or** one-handed + shield |
| Caster classes | Staff / tome, optional off-hand |

What most players are actually choosing between is a **two-handed stance vs. weapon + shield**:

- **Two-handed weapons** (great axes, scythes, staves) grant a stance bonus to attack — for example, a two-handed stance that boosts physical and magic attack.
- **Equipping a shield cancels the two-handed stance bonus.** A Summoner or Necromancer who slots a shield loses roughly 25% physical and magic attack — it is almost never worth it unless you are hard-tanking.
- **Shields** are the right choice for Knights and Paladins, who scale their defense and block off the shield slot.

Always read the weapon tooltip: it lists the required class and the stance it grants before you commit.

## Stat & Skill Reset (Respec)

If you experiment and dislike your build, respecs are **free** — visit the **Waybinder NPC** in any major town (Vale's Rest, Ironforge Outpost, Silvertide Harbor) to reset all stat points and skill points with no gold cost and no cooldown. See the [Job Change & Skill Reset Guide](/guides/job-change-reset) for the full breakdown, including how to change your advanced class.

## Combat Tips for New Players

1. **Keep your target highlighted.** Skills fire at the highlighted enemy — swinging at nothing is the most common first-hour mistake.
2. **Use auto attack between skills.** Holding C while abilities cool down keeps your DPS up and saves mana.
3. **Movement is your defense.** Dodging a telegraphed attack beats healing through it. Casters should stay at max range and kite.
4. **Learn combo windows.** Melee finishers deal roughly 50% more damage inside a "Combo Ready" window (created by skills like Bash) — time your big hits.
5. **Hotbar your potions.** Potions are manual, so bind HP/MP pots to easy keys before you need them.
6. **Respec freely.** The Waybinder is free — test different skill layouts instead of guessing.
