# SpiritVale Card Optimization — Getting More Out of Every Slot

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 4, 2026**

Cards are the cheapest power source in SpiritVale: six slots, no crafting materials beyond duplicate cards, and every monster, elite, and boss has a chance to drop its own card. Most players waste this system by slotting whatever drops first. This guide covers the stat priorities per advanced class, the upgrade math, and the farm routes that get you to a full legendary set without wasting dupes.

---

## 1. Slot Anatomy and What Goes Where

Every piece of equipment has card slots: **Weapon (2), Armor (2), Accessory 1 (1), Accessory 2 (1)** — six slots total, all unlockable from Level 8. Rarity tiers run Common (gray, +1-3) → Uncommon (green, +3-6) → Rare (blue, +6-12) → Epic (purple, +12-20) → Legendary (orange, +20-35).

The rule for slotting: **weapon slots are for damage stats, armor slots are for survival, accessory slots are for flexibility.** A legendary defensive card in a weapon slot is a wasted slot.

| Slot | Best Stat | Best Card Type |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Weapon 1 | Main damage stat (STR/INT/AGI) | Your class's primary stat |
| Weapon 2 | Secondary damage or attack speed | ASPD (remember the 193 ASPD cap) |
| Armor 1 | VIT / HP | Pure survival |
| Armor 2 | Defense or resistance | Elemental resistance for current content |
| Accessory 1 | Utility | Move speed, drop rate, or class-specific |
| Accessory 2 | Flex | Counter a boss's resistance (swap per fight) |

## 2. Stat Priority by Advanced Class

| Advanced Class | Priority 1 | Priority 2 | Priority 3 |
|----------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Paladin | VIT | STR | HP |
| Berserker | STR | ASPD | VIT |
| Priest | INT (heal power) | VIT | Cast speed |
| Wizard | INT | ASPD | Crit |
| Gunslinger | AGI | ASPD | Crit |
| Shinobi | AGI | ASPD | Move speed |
| Necromancer | INT | VIT | Pet damage |
| Summoner | INT | VIT | Pet damage |

**Two hard caps to plan around:** the ASPD cap (193) means over-stacking attack speed cards is wasted budget on fast classes like Shinobi — reallocate to crit. And elemental cards matter more in raids than in open-world farming; the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide) explains which bosses resist which elements.

## 3. Forge vs. Exchange — The Upgrade Math

There are two ways to convert duplicates into higher rarities, and they have different costs:

| Method | Cost | Result | Best Use |
|--------|------|--------|----------|
| Card Forge (Level 20+) | 5 cards of same rarity | 1 random card of next rarity (100% success) | Converting *useless* cards — anything you won't slot |
| Card Exchange NPC (Ironforge Outpost) | 10 duplicates of the **same card** | 1 random card of the next rarity | Converting *useful* cards — you keep the card identity |

**The strategy:** never exchange cards you want to keep slotting; always forge trash into value. A full Epic set is realistic for a casual player in 2-3 weeks if you forge every Common and Uncommon drop. Legendary requires boss farming — see the route below.

## 4. Farm Routes by Rarity Target

| Target | Where to Farm | Expected Rate | Time |
|--------|--------------|---------------|------|
| Common/Uncommon | Normal mobs (any zone, 1-5% / 10-15% on elites) | Dozens per hour | Passive — happens while leveling |
| Rare/Epic | Elite camps and dungeon bosses (25-50% boss drop, guaranteed min Uncommon) | 1-3 per dungeon run | 1-2 hours per day |
| Legendary | Raid bosses and world bosses | Very rare (best odds on Tier 3-4 raids) | Weekly, tied to raid resets |

**Pro tip:** the Card Exchange NPC in Ironforge Outpost is also the fastest way to fix a bad duplicate stash — bring 10 dupes of a card you already have two of and convert the extras. See the [Daily Quests Guide](/guides/daily-quests-guide) for the dungeon daily route that maximizes boss kills per hour.

## 5. Common Mistakes

1. **Slotting before planning.** A +35 legendary of the wrong stat is worth less than a +12 of the right one. Decide your stat priority (Section 2) before spending.
2. **Exchanging 10 dupes of a slotted card.** You lose a card you use. Forge identical spares only when you have more than 2 copies.
3. **Ignoring event cards.** Limited-time events and daily login rewards occasionally grant exclusive cards that beat boss drops of the same rarity — hoard them, they rarely return.
4. **ASPD overshoot.** Attack speed cards are great until you hit the 193 cap; past it, every ASPD card is dead weight. Re-slot to crit or utility.
5. **No flex accessory.** Raid bosses have resistances; keeping one spare accessory loaded with a counter-element card costs you nothing and saves wipes.

**Final note:** cards are the one system you can optimize *before* gear — the [Gear Progression Guide](/guides/gear-progression) is the natural next step once your slots are full, and the [Builds Guide](/guides/builds-guide) shows how card stat priorities change per endgame build.
