# SpiritVale Connection & Server Guide — Lag, Disconnects, Rollbacks & Best Server

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 1, 2026**

If SpiritVale kicks you out, rubber-bands you around, or — worst of all — rolls your progress back, you are not doing anything wrong. **The server instability at launch is the single biggest reason the game's rating dropped from a 93% demo to Mixed.** This guide covers the full timeline, what the developers are actually doing about it, and every workaround that currently works.

---

## The Launch Timeline (What Happened, in Order)

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 7/15/2026 | Early Access launch. 15,000–20,000 concurrent players immediately overwhelm the new backend architecture; rating crashes to 15–38% within 12 hours |
| 7/15–7/21 | Disconnects, jitter, and **progress rollbacks** — thousands of players lost progress, some 20+ levels |
| 7/21 | Developer fights an economy-wrecking **bot/RMT wave**; the free demo is closed because "most demo users were breaking the rules" |
| 7/22 | **Official apology + promises:** the dev team confirms **no more wipes/rollbacks**, announces hiring a full-time backend developer (Kickstarter-funded), and says the Auction Hall rework is a top priority |
| 7/15+ | The team acknowledges "the game was ready but the new backend couldn't handle unexpected player volume" — and confirms **"NA 1 server CPU never maxed out"** — the problem is architecture, not capacity |

**The takeaway for you:** the devs know, they apologized publicly, they've committed to no more rollbacks, and they're hiring the backend help the game needs. Player sentiment has moved from "rage" to "waiting to see."

---

## What Works Right Now (Player-Verified)

1. **Pick the NA server if you're in Asia/SEA.** Community testing consistently shows NA servers are the most stable — SEA/TW players report smoother play on NA than on their local node. Switching servers is free and character progress is account-wide.
2. **Wired connection for jitter** — Wi-Fi jitter is your own worst enemy on top of server instability. Ethernet if you can.
3. **Close heavy background usage** — streams, downloads, and other devices on the network amplify packet loss.
4. **Switch channels during peak hours** — full maps are both a spawn problem and a lag problem; channel-hopping to a quieter map improves both.
5. **Don't panic-buy "game boosters."** VPN/booster claims are unverified; most launch issues were server-side and are now much improved. Test plain NA-server play first.

## Progress Rollbacks — Current Status

- **The July 15–21 rollback incidents are acknowledged and apologized for** (7/22 official statement).
- **The developers have committed to no further wipes or rollbacks** — this is a firm, repeated promise, including for the Auction Hall rework ("we will preserve player data").
- What you can still do if you suspect a rollback: report to the official Discord with your character/server/time; the team has been responsive on individual reports.

**Peace of mind rules:** keep your most valuable items in the bank (bank data survived the incidents better than inventory), and screenshot absurd events if you want evidence for support.

## The Auction Hall Situation (Related But Important)

The **Auction Hall (AH)** — the game's global player marketplace — **has been down for long stretches since launch** and is **being rebuilt**, not just restarted:

- Why: the AH was **one of the biggest lag sources**, plus bot/RMT abuse ("成灾的机器人") was wrecking the economy.
- The developer called trading "one of the most important features of our game" — the shutdown was an admission, not a decision they wanted.
- **Vending stalls returned in patch 0.30.0** — player-to-player selling works again through stalls and direct trade.
- The **Bot Hunter Program** is live: report botters on the official Discord.
- Full status + trading alternatives: [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) and [Update & Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes).

## Known Client-Side Workarounds (When It's NOT the Server)

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| WASD / click-move stops responding | Known movement bug | Do a dodge roll — temporarily restores input; repeat as needed (devs aware) |
| Desync (mobs desync from position) | Client-server desync | Relog or switch channel |
| Soft-lock (can't act, no UI) | Session desync | Log out and back in from the character select |
| FPS drops | Client-side | Lower effects/shadow settings; the game is not GPU-heavy but effects-heavy |

## FAQ

**Q: Will there be another wipe?**
The developers have publicly promised **no further wipes or rollbacks** (July 22 statement). The Auction Hall rework will preserve player data.

**Q: Which server should I pick?**
NA is the community's stability pick right now, including for SEA/TW players. Transfers are free, so move if yours lags.

**Q: Is the game playable now?**
Yes — dramatically better than launch week. Peak-hour disconnects still happen, but the "completely unplayable" launch state is over. Rating has climbed back toward ~43–51% from the 15–38% trough as stability improved.

**Q: Where do I report server bugs?**
Official Discord, with character/server/time details. The devs read it daily.

**Q: Will server fixes make the game faster for me?**
Depends. If your problem is jitter/disconnect, yes. If your problem is FPS or latency to a far region, use the client fixes above and pick a closer server.
