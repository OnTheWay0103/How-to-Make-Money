# SpiritVale Connection & Server Guide — Lag, Disconnects, Rollbacks & Best Server

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 28, 2026**

If SpiritVale kicks you out, rubber-bands you around, or — worst of all — rolls your progress back, you are not doing anything wrong. **Server instability around launch was the single biggest complaint against the game.** This guide covers what happened, what the developers are actually doing about it, and every workaround that currently works.

---

## The Launch Timeline (What Happened, in Order)

| Phase | Event |
|-------|-------|
| Early Access launch | The player count immediately overwhelmed the new backend architecture; disconnects and jitter started within hours |
| Launch week | Disconnects, jitter, and **progress rollbacks** — some players lost significant progress |
| Bot wave | Developers fought an economy-wrecking **bot/RMT wave** during the same period |
| Official apology + promises | The dev team publicly confirmed **no more wipes or rollbacks**, announced hiring a full-time backend developer, and made the Auction Hall rework a top priority |

**The takeaway for you:** the devs know, they apologized publicly, they've committed to no more rollbacks, and they've hired the backend help the game needs. Player sentiment has moved from "rage" to "waiting to see."

---

## What Works Right Now (Player-Verified)

1. **Pick a stable server if you're in Asia/SEA.** Community reports consistently suggest NA servers have been the most stable — SEA/TW players often report smoother play on NA than on their local node. Switching servers is free and character progress is account-wide.
2. **Wired connection for jitter** — Wi-Fi jitter is your own worst enemy on top of server instability. Ethernet if you can.
3. **Close heavy background usage** — streams, downloads, and other devices on the network amplify packet loss.
4. **Switch channels during peak hours** — full maps are both a spawn problem and a lag problem; channel-hopping to a quieter map improves both.
5. **Don't panic-buy "game boosters."** VPN/booster claims are unverified; most launch issues were server-side and have improved. Test plain NA-server play first.

## Progress Rollbacks — Current Status

- **The launch-week rollback incidents are acknowledged and apologized for** (official statement).
- **The developers have committed to no further wipes or rollbacks** — a firm, repeated promise, including for the Auction Hall rework ("we will preserve player data").
- What you can still do if you suspect a rollback: report to the official Discord with your character/server/time; the team has been responsive on individual reports.

**Peace of mind rules:** keep your most valuable items in the bank (bank data survived the incidents better than inventory), and screenshot anything unusual if you want evidence for support.

## The Auction Hall Situation (Related But Important)

The **Auction Hall (AH)** — the game's global player marketplace — was **down for long stretches after launch** and was **being rebuilt**, not just restarted. That wait is over:

- The AH **returned online on August 4, 2026** (PTR-tested August 2) with a **5% seller tax** and a **30-listing cap**, alongside party, item-exploit, login-timeout, and chat fixes.
- Why it went down originally: the AH was **one of the biggest lag sources**, plus bot/RMT abuse was wrecking the economy.
- The developer called trading "one of the most important features of our game" — the shutdown was an admission, not a decision they wanted.
- **Vending stalls are live** — player-to-player selling works through stalls and direct trade too.
- The **Bot Hunter Program** is live: report botters on the official Discord.
- Full status + trading alternatives: [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) and [Update & Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes).

## Known Client-Side Workarounds (When It's NOT the Server)

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| WASD / click-move stops responding | Known movement bug | Do a dodge roll — temporarily restores input; repeat as needed (devs aware) |
| Desync (mobs desync from position) | Client-server desync | Relog or switch channel |
| Soft-lock (can't act, no UI) | Session desync | Log out and back in from the character select |
| FPS drops | Client-side | Lower effects/shadow settings; the game is not GPU-heavy but effects-heavy |

## FAQ

**Q: Will there be another wipe?**
The developers have publicly promised **no further wipes or rollbacks**. The Auction Hall rework will preserve player data.

**Q: Which server should I pick?**
NA has been the community's stability pick, including for SEA/TW players. Transfers are free, so move if yours lags.

**Q: Is the game playable now?**
Yes — dramatically better than launch week. Peak-hour disconnects still happen, but the "completely unplayable" launch state is over.

**Q: Where do I report server bugs?**
Official Discord, with character/server/time details. The devs read it daily.

**Q: Will server fixes make the game faster for me?**
Depends. If your problem is jitter/disconnect, yes. If your problem is FPS or latency to a far region, use the client fixes above and pick a closer server.
