# SpiritVale Pets and Mounts Guide — Cosmetic Companions, Pickup Pets, and Mount Speed

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: September 16, 2026**

Pets and mounts in SpiritVale are **cosmetic collectibles first**. The official Early Access launch notes file the entire "Mounts & Pets" list inside the **Cosmetics Store** block, right alongside costume sets, weapon skins and emotes — and the March 2026 cosmetics update states the binding rule plainly: premium cosmetics are account bound, while world-droppable monster pets and mounts are tradable.

They are not *only* decorative, though. Official notes document exactly **two** effects, and this guide sticks to them:

1. **Pets periodically pick up items for you.**
2. **Mounts grant a movement-speed bonus out of combat.**

Everything below is sourced to the official announcement archive and the Steam review corpus, with the dates attached. There is a short section at the end listing the pet and mount systems we looked for and could **not** find in any official or community source — that list matters just as much as the tables that used to be here.

---

## 1. What pets and mounts actually are

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Where do they live in the game? | The **wardrobe** — a cosmetic menu, added March 2026, that holds an appearance permanently across all your characters | 0.14.0 cosmetics update |
| Are they gear? | No. They are cosmetics; the store page markets "an almost limitless number of cosmetic options" | Store page; 0.30.0 |
| Do they have stats? | No official source describes pet or mount stats, rarity tiers, or levelling | See §6 |
| Can you trade them? | **World-droppable** monster pets and mounts can be traded; **premium** cosmetics are account bound | 0.14.0 |
| Do they do anything in the world? | Pets pick up items; mounts add out-of-combat movement speed | 0.14.0; 0.31.0 |

A player review summarises the same split from the other direction: the shop's pets and mounts are the only things a reviewer could even argue about as "pay to win", and only because **mounts carry movement speed** and **pets handle auto loot** — not because either grants combat power.

---

## 2. How you get pet and mount cosmetics

All five routes below are named in official notes. None of them requires a progression system.

| Route | What you get | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **World drops** | Monster pets and mounts drop as cosmetics you can wear or trade | 0.14.0 |
| **Monster-exclusive drops** | Some monsters can drop **their own pet version**, plus a few mounts | 0.14.0 |
| **Boss drops** | Boss-exclusive cosmetics | 0.14.0 |
| **Mystery boxes** | `Mystery Pet Box` and `Mystery Mount Box` are world drops that give a random monster pet or mount | 0.14.0 |
| **Cosmetic shop** | Bought with the game's premium currency; the March 2026 notes also stress "a lot of new cosmetic things you can hunt without premium currency" | 0.14.0; 0.30.0 |

The Early Access launch notes add that the cosmetics store **"is now purchased from inside the game (click on balance)"** and is no longer tied to Ko-Fi donations.

**Player trading and stalls.** Because world-drop pets and mounts are tradable, the Auction House and player vending stalls are a legitimate route to a specific companion — reviews describe buying pets and mounts from the shop and off other players. Trading was disabled for a period after launch while the developers rebuilt the item infrastructure, so check the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) for the current state.

## 3. Pets: what they do

**Pets periodically pick up items for you.** That is the whole of the officially documented pet effect, and it is a real, live feature rather than a launch promise: the July 17, 2026 hotfix includes `Fix: pet pickup / pickup with V issues`.

Player reviews treat this as the reason pets matter in practice — one calls pets "essential for auto loot and helpful in the early game", and notes they are more common than mounts. If you are levelling and leaving loot on the ground, a pet is the quality-of-life purchase; the [Farming Resource Guide](/guides/farming-resource-guide) covers what is worth picking up.

Note that "pickup" is also a manual action: a separate patch added `hold V to pickup`, which is why the hotfix line pairs "pet pickup" with "pickup with V".

---

## 4. Mounts: what they do

**Mounts give you movement speed out of combat.** The March 2026 cosmetics update states the figure directly:

> `Mount: give you 40% movespeed out of combat`

That is a Playtest-era value (March 15, 2026). It has since been adjusted: the **August 25, 2026** patch notes say

> `Movespeed Bonus now capped at +100% (increased on non-combat Mounts)`

So by late August 2026 the out-of-combat bonus on mounts had been **increased**, and the overall movespeed bonus was capped at +100%. Treat 40% as the documented March 2026 number rather than a current stat, and note that official notes never state a per-mount speed ladder — see §6.

## 5. Wardrobe and trading rules

These are the binding rules from the March 2026 cosmetics update, and they decide what you can pass to another character or another player:

| Rule | Verbatim |
|---|---|
| Cosmetics are account-wide | "adding it permanently to your wardrobe across all characters" |
| World drops are movable | "World drop cosmetics like pets and mounts can be added/removed from wardrobe and traded" |
| Premium cosmetics are locked | "All premium cosmetics are account bound" |
| World-drop monster pets and mounts are not | "world droppable monster pets and mounts are tradable" |

In short: a pet or mount you **farm** can be traded; a pet or mount you **buy** cannot. The separate cosmetic layer — converting your own gear into an appearance, and what the shop sells — is covered in the [Cosmetics & Wardrobe Guide](/guides/cosmetics-wardrobe-guide).

## 7. What we could not confirm

This page previously carried tables for a pet rarity ladder, per-rarity stat bonuses, role-recommended pets, a pet XP levelling table, an evolution system with materials, mount speed tiers, mount acquisition difficulty, mount training tiers, cosmetic mount armour/saddlebag/dye options, and a collector's milestone reward list. **None of those tables survived verification, and they have been removed.**

Each was checked against the full official announcement archive (both app 3767850 and the Playtest app 3918510 — 84 announcements in total), the Steam store page body, and the complete Steam review corpus (**5,211 reviews, 100% of the total listed for the app**). Every named entity and every precise number in those tables scored **zero hits**:

- **Pet and mount names:** Iron Shell Turtle, Ember Fox, Luminous Sprite, Wind Runner Cub, Celestial Griffin, Void Stalker, Tamed Horse, Riding Ram, Swift Stag, Armored Wolf, Shadow Panther, Crystal Drake, Primal Unicorn — 0 hits each.
- **Materials and rewards:** Evolution Stones, Mythic Core, Rare Crystal, Training Manuals, Rare Herbs, Legendary Pet Cache, Pet XP Boost Scrolls, "Master Collector" title — 0 hits each.
- **Systems and numbers:** pet stat bonuses, pet active abilities (the page had assigned "Shield Bash" to a pet — Shield Bash is a Paladin skill in the patch notes), pet evolution level thresholds 20/30/40/50, the Common→Legendary rarity ladder for pets, mount sprint-speed tiers, mount training times and costs, mount armour and saddlebags, collector milestones.

Two disambiguations worth recording, because raw keyword counts can look like support when they are not:

- **"Level 20/30/40/50"** appears in the review corpus, but every instance refers to **character or monster** levels — never a pet evolution threshold.
- **"Rarity"** appears in official notes only for **essence** rarity (Flow → Growth → Destruction → Rebirth → Chaos) and for boss drop rarity — never for pets.

We did **not** find evidence that any of this was officially denied; it simply does not appear anywhere. Absence of evidence is the reason for removal, not a contradiction.

**One lead we could not resolve.** Search summaries of the March 2026 and July 2026 MassivelyOP coverage mention a Kickstarter stretch goal for "enhanced pets and mounts" that was **not** reached. That would be a plausible explanation for why no pet progression system exists — but MassivelyOP returned HTTP 403 and Kickstarter returned HTTP 403 when fetched directly from this machine, so we are recording it as **unverified** rather than asserting a stretch goal was missed.

## 8. Practical tips

Grounded in the sources above, not in a progression system:

- **Pick up a pet early if you are farming.** Auto-pickup is the only pet effect official notes document, and reviewers call it the reason pets matter while levelling.
- **Farm your pets and mounts, don't buy them, if trading matters to you.** World drops are tradable; premium cosmetics are account bound.
- **Hunt monster-exclusive variants.** Some monsters drop their own pet version, and a few drop mounts — that is the free route to a companion.
- **Watch for Mystery Pet Box / Mystery Mount Box world drops** if you want a random monster pet or mount.
- **Don't buy a mount expecting a combat edge.** The documented mount effect is out-of-combat movement speed, not combat power — which is also why reviewers struggle to call the shop pay-to-win.
- **Check the patch notes for current mount speed.** The out-of-combat bonus was increased in the August 25, 2026 patch, with an overall movespeed cap of +100%; older figures you find in guides may be stale.
