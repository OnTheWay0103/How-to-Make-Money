# SpiritVale Update & Patch Notes — Server Fixes, Auction Hall Status & Balance Changes

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 1, 2026**

SpiritVale's developers ship on a weekly-to-daily cadence, and during the rocky launch month that cadence mattered. This page tracks what actually changed — server work, the Auction Hall rework, and balance patches — so you can tell "outdated guide" from "current build." Compilations sourced from official announcements, SteamDB/aggregators and the official Discord.

---

## Current Build: 0.30.0

| Area | What shipped |
|------|--------------|
| **Server architecture** | Major upgrade — regional server clusters added; a direct response to the launch-week collapse (see the [Connection & Server Guide](/guides/connection-server-guide)) |
| **Trading** | **Vending stalls returned** — player-to-player selling is live again (the Auction Hall remains down for rebuild) |
| **Inventory** | Backpack paging added — the "no sorting" complaint got its first real fix |
| **Persistence** | Summons and status effects now persist across channel changes |
| **Gunslinger** | Skill reworks for sniper rifle, shotgun, gatling and rocket launcher stances — the class's identity was rebuilt for launch data |
| **Economy** | Bot Hunter Program active — bot/RMT reporting via official Discord; the free demo remains closed ("most demo users were breaking the rules") |

## Auction Hall Rework Timeline (Tracked)

| Date | Status |
|------|--------|
| 7/15 launch | Auction Hall live at launch, immediately overwhelmed (one of the biggest lag sources) + bot/RMT abuse |
| ~7/16+ | Auction Hall **shut down**; stalls also affected |
| 7/22 | Developer apology: trading is "one of the most important features of our game"; rework announced, player data will be preserved |
| 0.30.0 | **Vending stalls return**; Auction Hall rebuild still in progress — no reopen date yet |
| Now | Use stalls + direct trade (see the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide)); this page updates within 48h of any announcement |

## Balance & Meta Changes Worth Knowing

- **Summoner** became the community's "extremely OP" class after launch balancing — expect attention from the devs in upcoming patches.
- **Acolyte path** remains support-focused; solo viability is the community's ongoing complaint (see the [Solo & Party Guide](/guides/solo-party-guide)).
- **Gunslinger** (Scout's advanced class) rework changed its whole feel — old beta builds for it are obsolete.

> General rule: any SpiritVale build or tier list older than the current weekly patch may be stale. The [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) page is version-stamped for this reason.

## FAQ

**Q: Where are the official patch notes?**
Steam Community announcements and the official Discord. We compile major changes here within 48 hours.

**Q: How often does the game update?**
Weekly patches at minimum; the team has shipped daily fixes during rough weeks.

**Q: Will the Auction Hall come back?**
Yes — the devs have called trading one of the game's most important features and confirmed the AH is being rebuilt, not scrapped. No date yet.

**Q: Do balance patches wipe builds?**
No wipes — but they do invalidate tier lists. Re-check the [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) after every patch.
