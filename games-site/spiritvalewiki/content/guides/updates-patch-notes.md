# SpiritVale Update & Patch Notes Guide — Official Channels & What's New

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: September 6, 2026**

SpiritVale's developers ship on a regular cadence, and during Early Access that cadence matters — balance changes can arrive weekly. This guide tells you where to find official patch notes, what the current hot topics are (the Auction Hall's return, bot crackdowns, server stability), and how to tell "outdated guide" from "current build."

---

## Official Patch Note Channels

Always check these two sources first — anything else is a rumor:

| Source | What It Has | How Often |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Steam Community announcements** | Official patch notes, development updates, hotfixes | Weekly at minimum |
| **Official Discord** | Patch notes, developer comments, Bot Hunter Program, known-issue threads | Daily |

**General rule:** any SpiritVale build, tier list, or guide older than the latest weekly patch may be stale. The [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) page is version-stamped for this reason.

---

## Update Cadence During Early Access

- **Weekly patches:** Bug fixes and balance adjustments.
- **Monthly content updates:** New maps, bosses, cards, and systems.
- **Hotfixes:** Shipped during rough weeks, sometimes daily.
- **Major updates:** New classes, new systems, and level cap increases arrive with bigger releases.

## What's New in 0.31.0 "Dark Fortress" (August 25, 2026)

The biggest content update since launch shipped **August 25, 2026** as **0.31.0 "Dark Fortress."** Everything below is drawn from the official Steam announcement of the same name. Because no independent third-party guide or wiki had covered 0.31.0 as of September 6, 2026, treat the per-refine specifics as **developer-published but not yet community-corroborated**, and verify against in-game tooltips after the patch installs.

**To install:** restart Steam. If you see "client configuration is incompatible," right-click SpiritVale → Browse local files → delete the contents → do a fresh install (official instruction).

### New weapons: the Dusk line

A new family of **"Dusk" weapons** was added, each with a signature effect described in the notes:

| Weapon | Type | Signature effect from the official notes |
|--------|------|------------------------------------------|
| Duskbreaker | Unique axe | +Str/+Atk/+Crit per refine; grants **Iron Will** (1% final damage reduction per 2% lost HP, up to 30%) |
| Duskmire | Staff | +Matk/+HP per refine; grants **Blood Magic** (50% of MP cost paid as HP, +1% magic damage per 1500 HP) |
| Duskharvest | Scythe | Melee skill area +3; +2% Atk/+2 Crit per refine; 5% CDR per refine |
| Duskveil | (1-slot) | 15% of damage received is prevented and dealt over the next 3 seconds |
| Dusktome | Book | +3% Matk and +1% Holy damage per refine; Holy Wrath and Exorcism autocast Damnation |
| Duskedge | Unique shuriken | +3 Crit/+2% Atk per refine; +1 attack-speed limit; chance to autocast Shuriken Fan |
| Duskscourge | Launcher | Status damage +100% (+10% per refine); auto-attack splash +3; grants Frost Rounds; Frost Grenade autocasts Frozen Ground (Frost Bite DoT) |
| Duskbringer | Gatling gun | Multistrike 150; auto-attack chain +1; +1 Crit per refine; chance to Stagger on hit; grants Enchant Holy and Enchant Shadow |

*(Source: official announcement, "[0.31.0] Dark Fortress.")* See the [Weapons Guide](/guides/weapons-guide) for how weapon refining and slots work generally.

### New armor and cards

- **Echo Set** arrived in an **Atk** version and a **Matk** version. Per the notes the chest piece gives +1 Atk (or Matk) per refine with a damage-reduction bonus, legs add a resist, feet add pierce, and the full set grants **+10% Atk** or **+10% Matk**.
- **Face gear** came in 1-slot "half bonus" versions and **character-bound 2-slot** versions (Speed, Life, Power, Recovery, Magic, Force, Precision, Piercing) with per-refine scaling.
- **Base-class cards (accessory)** for Knight, Scout, Mage, Acolyte, Rogue, Summoner, and Warrior — e.g. Summoner grants **+5 to all summon stats per refine**.
- A **Weaver combo group** of cards (Weaver/Paladin/Wizard/Berserker/Shinobi/Gunslinger/Priest/Necromancer) with set-combo bonuses; the Necromancer card's combo grants **+1 Bond Targets**.
- **Master cards** (head, unique) for Weaver, Gunslinger, Wizard, Priest, Shinobi, Necromancer, and Berserker with per-refine scaling.

See the [Cards Guide](/guides/cards-guide) for the card system basics and the [Echoing Spire Guide](/guides/echoing-spire-endgame-guide) for endgame context.

### Systems and balance changes (official)

- **Grimoires are now character bound.** The notes also list as a bug fix that "Grimoires could be converted into cosmetics" — that conversion is no longer possible.
- A new stat, **Bond Targets**, was introduced, and **skill splash no longer affects Bonds**. Bond Target sources listed in the notes: **all books +1**, **Oathbound Artifact Set +1**, **Spirit Familiar / Chains of Binding +1**, **Soulbinders Armor Set +2**, plus the Necromancer Weaver-combo card.
- **Summons now take 50% less splash damage** when hit indirectly, and can auto-attack and cast at the same time.
- **Wraith** summon stats shifted (Str 30→45, Luk 45→30); rebalances to Grim Scythe, Life Drinker, Blight Reaver, and the **Necronomicon** (gains +1 to all summon stats per refine).
- **Sanctum maps were rebuilt to be larger**; **ET** (the endgame tower) lag was reduced through load-balancing across several instances, and a **vote-kick** option was added to ET.
- The **PvP arena queue system was rewritten to function across servers**.
- **Movespeed bonus is now capped at +100%**; **boss gear drop rates were rebalanced**; and skill-chain range was reverted (12 → 15).
- Other noted changes: Tenacity Gem (+1% → +2% per refine), Ragebound Fury rework, Earthspire Staff and Bloomroot Staff changes, plus a batch of bug fixes (Ice Mage Ice Shard / Demon Lord Fireball damage, Lockdown stacking, Corpse Explosion on multiple targets, Projectile Chain with pierce, Enchant/Coating buff refresh, Weapon Swap buff persistence, mount dodge animations, chat-bubble setting persistence, AGI tooltip, and more).

> **How to read this section:** every bullet is taken from the official 0.31.0 announcement and is labeled **Official** in the page sources. The per-refine numbers and gimmick names (Iron Will, Blood Magic, Bond Targets, Frost Rounds) are the developers' own published wording — cross-check the live tooltips, since the meta can shift again in the next weekly patch.

## Current Hot Topics

### The Auction Hall Rework — Resolved (Returned August 4, 2026)

- The **Auction Hall** — the game's global player marketplace — was **down for a rebuild** for much of the launch period.
- Why it went down: it was one of the biggest lag sources, and **bot/RMT abuse** was wrecking the economy.
- On **August 2** the developers opened a **PTR** to test item trading, guild storage, and the auction house.
- On **August 4, 2026**, the trade system **returned online**: the Auction Hall is back with a **5% seller tax** and a **30-listing cap**, alongside party, item-exploit, login-timeout, and chat fixes.
- **Vending stalls and direct trade remain live** (see the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide)).

### Bot Crackdowns

- The **Bot Hunter Program** is live: report botters via the official Discord.
- Bot-driven inflation is an acknowledged problem; the team has been actively banning.

### Server Stability

- Launch-week instability (disconnects, jitter, rollbacks) has improved significantly; the team hired a dedicated backend developer.
- The developers have publicly committed to **no further wipes or rollbacks** — player data will be preserved into full release.
- See the [Connection & Server Guide](/guides/connection-server-guide) for the full timeline and workarounds.

### Balance Watch

- **Summoner** became the community's "extremely OP" class after launch balancing — expect attention from the devs in upcoming patches.
- **Acolyte path** remains support-focused; solo viability is the community's ongoing complaint (see the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide)).

## FAQ

**Q: Where are the official patch notes?**
Steam Community announcements and the official Discord. We compile major changes here shortly after each release (0.31.0 "Dark Fortress" was added here on September 6, 2026).

**Q: What changed in 0.31.0 "Dark Fortress"?**
The August 25, 2026 update added the **Dusk weapon line**, **Echo armor sets**, **face gear**, new base-class/Weaver-combo/Master cards, made **Grimoires character bound**, introduced the **Bond Targets** stat, reduced splash damage to summons by 50%, rebuilt **Sanctum** maps larger, added **vote kick to ET**, and rewrote the **PvP arena queue to work across servers**. See the "What's New in 0.31.0" section above; details are compiled from the official Steam announcement only.

**Q: How often does the game update?**
Weekly patches at minimum; the team has shipped daily hotfixes during rough weeks.

**Q: Will the Auction Hall come back?**
It already did — the trade system returned **August 4, 2026** (after PTR testing on August 2) with a 5% seller tax and a 30-listing cap. Vending stalls and direct trade remain live.

**Q: Do balance patches wipe builds?**
No wipes — but they do invalidate tier lists. Re-check the [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) after every patch.
