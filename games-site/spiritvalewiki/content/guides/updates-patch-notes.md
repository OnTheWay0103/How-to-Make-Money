# SpiritVale Update & Patch Notes Guide — Official Channels & What's New

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 11, 2026**

SpiritVale's developers ship on a regular cadence, and during Early Access that cadence matters — balance changes can arrive weekly. This guide tells you where to find official patch notes, what the current hot topics are (the Auction Hall rework, bot crackdowns, server stability), and how to tell "outdated guide" from "current build."

---

## Official Patch Note Channels

Always check these two sources first — anything else is a rumor:

| Source | What It Has | How Often |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Steam Community announcements** | Official patch notes, development updates, hotfixes | Weekly at minimum |
| **Official Discord** | Patch notes, developer comments, Bot Hunter Program, known-issue threads | Daily |

**General rule:** any SpiritVale build, tier list, or guide older than the latest weekly patch may be stale. The [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) page is version-stamped for this reason.

## Update Cadence During Early Access

- **Weekly patches:** Bug fixes and balance adjustments.
- **Monthly content updates:** New maps, bosses, cards, and systems.
- **Hotfixes:** Shipped during rough weeks, sometimes daily.
- **Major updates:** New classes, new systems, and level cap increases arrive with bigger releases.

## Current Hot Topics

### The Auction Hall Rework

- The **Auction Hall** — the game's global player marketplace — has been **down for a rebuild** since the launch period.
- Why: it was one of the biggest lag sources, and **bot/RMT abuse** was wrecking the economy.
- The developers have called trading "one of the most important features of our game" and confirmed the AH is **being rebuilt, not scrapped**. No reopen date yet.
- **Vending stalls are live** — player-to-player selling works right now through stalls and direct trade (see the [Trading & Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide)).

### Bot Crackdowns

- The **Bot Hunter Program** is live: report botters via the official Discord.
- Bot-driven inflation is an acknowledged problem; the team has been actively banning.

### Server Stability

- Launch-week instability (disconnects, jitter, rollbacks) has improved significantly; the team hired a dedicated backend developer.
- The developers have publicly committed to **no further wipes or rollbacks** — player data will be preserved into full release.
- See the [Connection & Server Guide](/guides/connection-server-guide) for the full timeline and workarounds.

### Balance Watch

- **Summoner** became the community's "extremely OP" class after launch balancing — expect attention from the devs in upcoming patches.
- **Acolyte path** remains support-focused; solo viability is the community's ongoing complaint (see the [Solo Guide](/guides/solo-guide)).

## FAQ

**Q: Where are the official patch notes?**
Steam Community announcements and the official Discord. We compile major changes here within 48 hours.

**Q: How often does the game update?**
Weekly patches at minimum; the team has shipped daily hotfixes during rough weeks.

**Q: Will the Auction Hall come back?**
Yes — the devs have called trading one of the game's most important features and confirmed the AH is being rebuilt, not scrapped. No date yet.

**Q: Do balance patches wipe builds?**
No wipes — but they do invalidate tier lists. Re-check the [Class Tier List](/guides/class-tier-list) after every patch.
