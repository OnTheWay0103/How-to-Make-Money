## Raid Boss Guide — SpiritVale

SpiritVale features a variety of raid bosses that test your group composition, coordination, and mechanical execution. Each boss has unique attack patterns, multiple phase transitions, and specific loot tables. This guide covers every major raid boss, their mechanics, and the strategies needed to defeat them.

### Raid Boss Overview

Raid bosses in SpiritVale are organized by difficulty tier. Each tier requires a higher party level and better gear. The game currently has 8 major raid bosses spread across 4 tiers.

### Tier 1 Raid Bosses

#### Stone Colossus

The Stone Colossus is the introductory raid boss, designed to teach basic raid mechanics. It has 2 phases:

- **Phase 1 (Full HP):** The Colossus uses slow, telegraphed attacks. It stomps the ground creating shockwaves, swipes its massive arms in a 180-degree arc, and occasionally summons rock pillars from the ground.
  - **Key mechanic:** When it raises both arms, all players must move behind a rock pillar to avoid a room-wide blast.
  - **Strategy:** Spread out to avoid multiple players getting hit by the same shockwave. The Tank holds aggro at the boss's front while DPS attacks from the sides.

- **Phase 2 (Low HP):** The Colossus crumbles, revealing a crystalline core. It becomes enraged, attacking faster and adding a laser beam attack from its core.
  - **Key mechanic:** The laser beam tracks a random player. The targeted player must kite the beam away from the group.
  - **Loot:** Stoneheart Essence (used for Tier 1 equipment crafting), Colossus Gauntlets (Tank gloves).

#### Corrupted Wyrm

A serpentine dragon corrupted by void energy. 3 phases with increasing complexity:

- **Phase 1:** Standard dragon fight — tail swipe, fire breath, wing gust. Stay behind the Wyrm to avoid the breath cone.
- **Phase 2:** The Wyrm burrows underground and surfaces at random locations. Players must spread out to minimize damage from eruptions. The Wyrm leaves void pools on the ground that deal damage over time.
- **Phase 3:** The Wyrm gains a corruption aura. Players standing in the aura take increasing damage. The boss also summons corrupted adds that must be killed quickly.
- **Loot:** Wyrm Scale Armor (DPS chest), Corrupted Fang (dagger weapon).

### Tier 2 Raid Bosses

#### Frost Oracle

A spellcasting ice giant with powerful area control abilities:

- **Recommended composition:** 1 Tank, 2 Healers, 3 DPS
- **Key phases:** Phase 1 focuses on dodging ice spikes and breaking frozen players free. Phase 2 introduces an ice storm that limits visibility and slowly damages all players. Phase 3 has the Oracle channel a massive blizzard that can wipe the party if not interrupted.
- **Strategy:** The Healers must rotate their cleansing abilities to remove ice stacks. DPS should focus the ice crystals that spawn during Phase 2 before they explode.

#### Shadow Lich

An undead sorcerer with 4 phases and heavy debuff mechanics:

- **Recommended composition:** 1 Tank, 2 Healers, 3 DPS
- **Key phases:** Phase 1 is a standard caster fight. Phase 2 summons shadow clones of party members that must be identified and killed. Phase 3 covers the arena in darkness — players must stay near light orbs. Phase 4 is a burn phase where the Lich drains life from the entire party.
- **Strategy:** Save defensive cooldowns for Phase 4. The Healers should use their ultimate abilities during the drain phase to keep the party alive.

### Tier 3 Raid Bosses

#### Void Kraken

A massive deep-sea horror fought in an underwater arena:

- **Recommended composition:** 2 Tanks, 2 Healers, 4 DPS
- **Key phases:** Phase 1 involves destroying tentacles while dodging ink blasts. Phase 2 has the Kraken dragging players into the void — rescued players must free trapped allies. Phase 3 introduces a suffocation mechanic where players must surface for air. Phase 4 is a multi-target phase with the Kraken's main head and remaining tentacles.
- **Strategy:** Assign one Tank to the main head and one to the tentacles. Healers need AoE healing for the suffocation mechanic.

#### Phoenix Sovereign

A fire bird fought on a platform above the clouds:

- **Recommended composition:** 2 Tanks, 2 Healers, 4 DPS
- **Key phases:** Phase 1 covers fire feathers and dive bombs. Phase 2 destroys sections of the platform — players must reposition constantly. Phase 3 spawns fire orbs that chase players. Phase 4 is the revive phase where the Phoenix must be killed while it attempts to resurrect. Phase 5 is an enrage burn phase.
- **Strategy:** The platform destruction in Phase 2 is the run-ending mechanic. Assign safe zones and communicate when platforms break.

### Tier 4 Raid Bosses

#### Primordial Titan

An ancient earth giant requiring 10-player coordination:

- **Recommended composition:** 2 Tanks, 3 Healers, 4 DPS, 1 Summoner
- **Key phases:** Phase 1 has the Titan using earthquake and boulder toss. Phase 2 splits the party into two groups to handle separate adds. Phase 3 introduces gravity wells that pull players toward the Titan. Phase 4 has the Titan channeling a world-ending attack that requires all players to stack in a shield bubble. Phase 5 is a burn phase with lethal enrage timer.
- **Loot:** Titan's Core (legendary crafting material), World Breaker (greatsword), Primordial Crown (Tank helmet).

#### Celestial Serpent

The current final raid boss of SpiritVale:

- **Recommended composition:** 2 Tanks, 3 Healers, 4 DPS, 1 Summoner
- **Key phases:** 6 phases with unique mechanics including celestial alignment puzzles, starfall dodging, dimensional rifts, and a final phase where the Serpent splits into three celestial aspects.
- **Strategy:** Phase 4's dimensional rift mechanic requires the most coordination. Assign pairs to enter rifts and close them simultaneously.

### General Raid Tips

1. **Know your role.** Tanks should study phase transitions to time their defensive cooldowns. Healers need to anticipate damage spikes. DPS must prioritize interrupt and add-kill mechanics over boss damage.
2. **Bring consumables.** Raid potions, scrolls, and food buffs make a significant difference, especially in Tier 3 and above.
3. **Communicate.** Call out phase transitions, targeted mechanics, and when you need healing. SpiritVale does not have a built-in raid warning system — voice chat is strongly recommended.
4. **Learn from wipes.** Each wipe teaches you something. Review what killed your party and adjust your strategy.
5. **Gear check before queueing.** Check minimum gear score requirements for each tier. Undergeared players are a liability to the entire party.
