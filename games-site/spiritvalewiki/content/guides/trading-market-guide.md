# SpiritVale Trading Market Guide — Buy, Sell, and Profit

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 28, 2026**

SpiritVale features a fully player-driven economy where nearly every item can be traded. Understanding the market and its trends is essential for building wealth. This guide breaks down how trading works, which items generate the highest profit, and how to read market signals.

> **⚠️ Market status:** the **Auction House is back online** (returned **August 4, 2026** after its long shutdown) with a **5% seller tax** and a **30-listing cap** — the rework was PTR-tested on August 2. Live player-to-player trading also continues through the **vending stalls** in town squares. The fee structure below describes the AH system, and flipping works the same way at stalls (minus the listing fees). See the [Update & Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes) for current trading status.

## 1. Auction House Basics

The Auction House (AH) is the central trading hub accessible from any major city. Players list items for set prices or via timed auctions.

### 1.1 Listing Types

| Type | Duration | Fee | Best Use |
|------|----------|-----|----------|
| Direct Sale | 48 hours | 5% listing fee | Common materials, gear |
| Timed Auction | 24 hours | 2% listing fee | Rare items, legendary gear |
| Bulk Listing | 7 days | 8% listing fee | Stackable crafting materials |

### 1.2 Market Fees

- **Listing Fee:** Non-refundable percentage of the starting price.
- **Success Fee:** Additional 5% deducted from the final sale price.
- **Premium Pass:** Reduces all fees by 50%. Available from the cash shop or earned via achievement milestones.

**The 25% rule:** if your expected net margin is under 25% after fees, it is not a flip — it is a gamble. Move to the next item.

## 2. High-Profit Item Categories

Not all items are equally profitable. The table below shows the best categories based on current market conditions.

| Category | Buy Low From | Sell High To | Profit Margin | Risk Level |
|----------|-------------|-------------|---------------|------------|
| Refinement Stones | Dungeon farmers | Endgame raiders | High | Low |
| Epic Gear (low roll) | Heroic dungeon runs | Dismantlers | Medium | Medium |
| Crafting Reagents | Gatherers | Crafters | High | Low |
| Rare Card Drops | World boss trains | Collection buyers | Very high | High |
| Cosmetic Skins | Loot box sellers | Fashion collectors | Extreme | Very High |

**Key Insight:** The highest consistent profit comes from Refinement Stones. Farming them in heroic dungeons and listing them in bulk generates steady income with minimal price volatility.

**The two-tier strategy:** run a high-volume, low-margin book (reagents, Refinement Stones) for steady income, and keep a small high-margin book (cards, cosmetics) for the spikes.

## 3. Timing: When the Market Bleeds and Feasts

| Day/Period | Market State | Action |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| Evenings & weekends | Peak farming — supply floods in | **Buy** — sellers undercut to move stock |
| Monday raid lockout reset | Demand spikes for consumables/gear | **Sell** — the feast day |
| Patch days | Old meta crashes, new meta spikes | Buy old-meta gear cheap; sell new-meta early |
| Off-peak (late night) | Thin supply, desperate buyers | Sell at a premium if listed already |

**The cycle in one line:** buy on evenings and weekends when supply peaks, sell on Monday resets when demand peaks.

## 4. Profitable Trade Routes

Certain items can be bought in one location and sold for more in another. These routes exploit regional supply and demand differences.

### 4.1 Cross-Zone Trading

| Route | Item | The Edge |
|-------|------|----------|
| Ironforge Mines → Vale's Rest | Iron Ore and refine materials | Mines produce surplus, towns consume it |
| Frostpeak Tundra → Ironforge Outpost | Frost Crystals | Frost material has no use in the mine — it sells at a premium in town |
| Shadowfen Depths → Soulfire Citadel | Shadow Essence | Endgame crafters pay well for late-zone materials |
| Whisperwind Plains → Any city | Rare herbs | Gatherers dump stock; crafters buy in bulk |

### 4.2 Flipping Fundamentals

- **Buy low during peak farming hours** (evenings and weekends) when supply is highest.
- **Sell high during raid lockout resets** (Mondays) when demand spikes.
- **Track price history** using the in-game market tracker. Items that swing a lot week over week are the flip candidates; steady items are storage.

### 4.3 Undercutting: Win Without the War

Undercutting wars are where flippers lose margins to each other. The counter-discipline:

1. **Never list at the current lowest price.** List at the second-lowest, or use bulk lots that undercut per-unit without starting a visible war.
2. **Use odd lot sizes.** A stack of 13 at a slightly lower per-unit price outsells a stack of 20 at the same price — buyers see the smaller number first.
3. **Sell in small stacks to budget buyers.** List crafting materials in 10-20 unit lots to capture players who cannot afford bulk.
4. **Let the war burn out.** If someone is racing you down, pull your listing and wait a day — the price recovers faster than your patience costs.

### 4.4 Flip Candidates: What to Watch

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Item swings a lot within a week | Flip candidate | Buy the dip, sell the spike |
| New raid tier announced | Old gear will crash | Sell old-meta now, buy after the crash |
| World boss train schedule | Card drop supply spike | Buy during the train, sell after it ends |
| Seasonal event live | Event materials flood in | Buy cheap, hold, sell post-event |
| Premium Pass holders flooding | Fee-reduced sellers dump stock | Buy — their volume is your discount |

## 5. Building a Merchant Strategy by Capital

### Stage 1: 0–10,000 Gold — The Reagent Trader
- Farm and sell Refinement Stones directly; flip crafting reagents exclusively (high margins, near-zero risk)
- List in 10-20 unit stacks; never touch cards or cosmetics
- Goal: 10,000 gold in a week of disciplined flipping

### Stage 2: 10,000–100,000 Gold — The Gear Flipper
- Start flipping epic gear. Focus on weapons; they sell fastest. Add Refinement Stones to your book
- Buy in bulk from dungeon farmers at off-peak prices, resell in smaller lots
- Invest in the Premium Pass to cut every fee — it pays for itself in roughly 20 flips

### Stage 3: 100,000+ Gold — The Market Player
- Corner the market on a single material during off-peak hours and resell at peak
- Use alt characters to increase daily listing slots
- Trade in rare card drops. The margins are high, but so is the risk — your capital now absorbs it

## 6. Market Warnings

| Mistake | Why It Costs You |
|---------|-----------------|
| Listing at current lowest price | You start an undercutting war. List at the second-lowest price or use bulk lots. |
| Ignoring patch notes | New content shifts demand. A new raid tier crashes old gear prices. |
| Over-investing in one item | If the market shifts, your entire capital is stuck. Diversify. |
| Forgetting listing fees | A 5% fee on a slow-moving item can erase profit. Calculate fees before listing. |
| Hoarding through a meta change | Gear that was worth a fortune is worth a fraction after a rebalance |
