# SpiritVale Trading Market Guide — Buy, Sell, and Profit

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: July 27, 2026**

SpiritVale features a fully player-driven economy where nearly every item can be traded. Understanding the Auction House and market trends is essential for building wealth. This guide breaks down how the trading system works, which items generate the highest profit, and how to read market signals.

---

> **⚠️ Status note (Aug 2, 2026):** As of build 0.30.0 the **Auction House is temporarily down for a rebuild** — the live player-to-player trading method is the **vending stalls** (reintroduced in 0.30.0, with the revamped Auction House announced for later). Details below describe the announced AH system and may change when it reopens. See the [Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes) for current trading status.

## 1. Auction House Basics

The Auction House (AH) is the central trading hub accessible from any major city. Players list items for set prices or via timed auctions.

### 1.1 Listing Types

| Type | Duration | Fee | Best Use |
|------|----------|-----|----------|
| Direct Sale | 48 hours | 5% listing fee | Common materials, gear |
| Timed Auction | 24 hours | 2% listing fee | Rare items, legendary gear |
| Bulk Listing | 7 days | 8% listing fee | Stackable crafting materials |

### 1.2 Market Fees

- **Listing Fee:** Non-refundable percentage of the starting price.
- **Success Fee:** Additional 5% deducted from the final sale price.
- **Premium Pass:** Reduces all fees by 50%. Available from the cash shop or earned via achievement milestones.

## 2. High-Profit Item Categories

Not all items are equally profitable. The table below shows the best categories based on current market data.

| Category | Buy Low From | Sell High To | Profit Margin | Risk Level |
|----------|-------------|-------------|---------------|------------|
| Refinement Stones | Dungeon farmers | Endgame raiders | 40–60% | Low |
| Epic Gear (low roll) | Heroic dungeon runs | Disenchanters | 20–35% | Medium |
| Crafting Reagents | Gatherers | Crafters | 50–80% | Low |
| Rare Card Drops | World boss trains | Collection buyers | 100–200% | High |
| Cosmetic Skins | Loot boxes | Fashion collectors | 200–500% | Very High |

**Key Insight:** The highest consistent profit comes from Refinement Stones. Farming them in heroic dungeons and listing in bulk on the AH generates steady income with minimal price volatility.

## 3. Profitable Trade Routes

Certain items can be bought in one location and sold for more in another. These trade routes exploit regional supply and demand differences.

### 3.1 Cross-Zone Trading

| Route | Item | Buy Price | Sell Price | Profit per Unit |
|-------|------|-----------|------------|----------------|
| Ash Wastes → Glacial Ridge | Fire Essence | 5 gold | 12 gold | 7 gold |
| Glacial Ridge → Scorched Basin | Ice Shards | 8 gold | 18 gold | 10 gold |
| The Rift → Any City | Void Crystals | 3 gold | 15 gold | 12 gold |

### 3.2 Flipping Fundamentals

- **Buy low during peak farming hours** (evenings and weekends) when supply is highest.
- **Sell high during raid lockout resets** (Mondays) when demand spikes.
- **Track price history** using the in-game market tracker. Items that swing more than 30% are good flip candidates.

## 4. Building a Merchant Strategy

### 4.1 Beginner Merchant (0–10,000 Gold)

- Farm and sell Refinement Stones directly.
- List crafting materials in small stacks (10–20 units) to attract budget buyers.
- Avoid rare cosmetics until you understand pricing patterns.

### 4.2 Intermediate Merchant (10,000–100,000 Gold)

- Start flipping epic gear. Focus on weapons; they sell fastest.
- Invest in a Premium Pass to cut fees.
- Buy in bulk from dungeon farmers and resell in smaller lots.

### 4.3 Advanced Merchant (100,000+ Gold)

- Corner the market on a single material during off-peak hours.
- Use alt characters to increase daily Auction House listing slots.
- Trade in rare card drops. The margins are high, but so is the risk.

---

## 5. Market Warnings

| Mistake | Why It Costs You |
|---------|-----------------|
| Listing at current lowest price | You start a undercutting war. List at the second-lowest price or use bulk lots. |
| Ignoring patch notes | New content shifts demand. A new raid tier crashes old gear prices. |
| Over-investing in one item | If the market shifts, your entire capital is stuck. Diversify. |
| Forgetting listing fees | A 5% fee on a slow-moving item can erase profit. Calculate fees before listing. |
