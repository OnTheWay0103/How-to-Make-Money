## Necromancer Build Overview

The Summoner path is **S-tier "extremely OP"** in the current EA meta — the community consensus is that pets tanking while you debuff lets the class clear content well above its level. This guide goes beyond the class overview: three complete endgame Necromancer builds with stat plans, skill priorities, slot-by-slot gear, cards, artifacts, and rotations. The [Summoner & Necromancer Class Guide](/guides/summoner-guide) covers class mechanics and leveling 1-50; this guide is for the build decisions at Job Level 50 and beyond.

**The one rule that shapes everything:** summons do **not** inherit your attribute points — they inherit your **equipment stats, artifact stats, cards, gems, refinement, and autocast effects**. Two Necromancers with the same INT but different gear can have wildly different pets. Gear first, stats second.

## The Three Necromancer Archetypes

| Archetype | Weapon | Role | Verdict |
|-----------|--------|------|---------|
| **Minion Master** | Staff / mace | Semi-AFK farming, safe bossing | The meta pick — strongest all-rounder in EA |
| **Death Nuke** | Staff | Burst AoE caster | Highest damage ceiling, squishier |
| **Scythe Reaper** | Scythe | STR-scaling melee hybrid | Niche — strong when it works, awkward to build |

Every Necromancer build is a variant of these three. Pick one direction and commit — spreading points across pet, nuke, and melee lines makes you mediocre at all three.

## Build 1: Minion Master (Recommended)

The community's favorite. Your summons do the damage and take the hits; you stand at range, debuff, and resummon.

### Stats
- **INT to 99** — magic damage and mana pool
- **VIT to 50** — a stray AoE can still kill you; this is your insurance
- **DEX** only for cast speed breakpoints — never past the point of diminishing returns
- **STR: 0** — this build never touches scythes

### Skill Priority

| Order | Skill | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | **Skeleton Mastery (max)** | The must-have passive — raises the power of all undead summons |
| 2 | **Death Coil (max)** | Damages enemies and heals your summons — your sustain engine |
| 3 | **Summon Mastery (max)** | Now that your gear feeds summons, max it |
| 4 | **Bone Spear** | Decay-applying filler for when you need to contribute damage |
| 5 | **Corpse Explosion** | Burst damage from defeated enemies — huge in farm pulls |
| 6 | **Suppression Field** | Seals casting on most non-boss enemies — stops casts, locks down packs |

### Gear (slot-by-slot)

| Slot | Best Item | Source |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Headgear | **Jack-o'-Lantern** (chance to autocast Death Coil, healing summons) | Drops |
| Weapon | **Binding Spirits Staff** (faster cast, boosts Death Nova and Death Coil) | Drops |
| Face | Scholar Glasses — INT + Magic Attack | Drops |
| Armor | **Soulbinder Set** — defense, HP, Summon HP | Zone drops |
| Artifact | **Corporeal artifact set** — strengthens summons directly | Artifact system |
| Card | Grim Reaper card | Card system |
| Utility | Fruit Bowl / Red Shell — weight limit for long farm sessions | Drops |

**Never equip a shield.** It cancels your two-handed stance and costs the 25% bonus to physical and magic attack — the damage loss is almost never worth the defense.

### Rotation
1. Summon your core minions — **summon the Wraith last** (longest cooldown)
2. Send the pet in, cast Suppression Field to lock the pack
3. Death Coil to heal the pet as it tanks
4. Corpse Explosion the first kills to chain-clear the pull
5. Resummon any minion below 30% HP — a dead pet is zero DPS

## Build 2: Death Nuke (Burst Caster)

The direct-damage Necromancer: Bone Spear, Death Nova, and Corpse Explosion burst. Highest damage ceiling, more positioning required.

### Stats
- **INT to 99**, then **DEX to 40** — cast speed matters more here than in the pet build
- **VIT to 30** — you trade pet-tank safety for nuke range

### Skill Priority

| Order | Skill | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | **Bone Spear (max)** | Your main projectile — applies Decay for the nuke chain |
| 2 | **Death Nova (max)** | Massive AoE nuke — the build's identity |
| 3 | **Corpse Explosion (max)** | Free burst damage from every kill |
| 4 | **Bone Spikes** | Ground AoE for pack control |
| 5 | **Death Coil** | One point for sustain, then back to damage |

### Gear
- **Binding Spirits Staff** (boosts Death Nova and Death Coil)
- **Sapphire Crown** headgear
- **Scholar Glasses** (INT + Magic Attack)
- **Mind set** armor — magic attack over summon stats
- **Corporeal artifact set** still helps — Decay damage counts as magic

### Rotation
Apply Decay with Bone Spear → drop Bone Spikes → Death Nova the pack → Corpse Explosion the kills → repeat. The build farms almost as fast as a Wizard but keeps the pet safety net for sticky moments.

## Build 3: Scythe Reaper (Melee Hybrid)

STR-scaling scythe Necromancer — the off-meta pick. Many magic skills double-scale with STR, so the scythe build converts STR into Magic Attack while swinging melee.

### Stats
- **STR to 99**, **VIT to 50**, **INT to 40** — the hybrid split
- **AGI** for attack speed once damage is settled

### Skills & Gear
- Skills: scythe attack skills first, Bone Spear second, Death Coil third
- Gear: STR-scaling scythe, Corporeal artifact set, crit cards
- Playstyle: melee range with minions flanking — your summons take the hits while you swing

**Honesty note:** this archetype is strong in theory and patch-dependent in practice. It rewards double-scaling when the balance is favorable and underperforms when it isn't. Build it because you enjoy it, not because it's the meta.

## Necromancer by Content Type

| Content | Best Build | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Farming / gold | Minion Master | Pets tank 8-10 levels of monsters above you; semi-AFK routes |
| Dungeons / bossing | Minion Master | **Reanimate a powerful field boss** once you can defeat it — the strongest early-endgame minion |
| Speed clears | Death Nuke | Death Nova + Corpse Explosion chains |
| PvP | Minion Master | Pets are B-tier vs players — they die fast, so your pet pressure must be constant |
| Group support | Any | Decay debuffs and Corpse Explosion add real group DPS |

## Common Necromancer Mistakes

- **Wearing a shield** — that 25% two-handed stance bonus is a quarter of your damage
- **Ignoring the Corporeal artifact set** — it directly strengthens summons; nothing else in the artifact system matches it for this class
- **Spreading points across all three archetypes** — commit to one
- **Resummoning on cooldown instead of at 30% HP** — Wraith has the longest cooldown; wasting it costs uptime
- **Building the Scythe Reaper without checking the current patch notes** — double-scaling changes every balance pass
