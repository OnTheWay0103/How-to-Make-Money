# SpiritVale Market Flipping Guide — Margins, Timing & Profit Playbook

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 6, 2026**

Farming earns you gold by the hour. **Flipping** earns you gold by the trade — buying when sellers are desperate and selling when buyers are desperate. In SpiritVale's player-driven economy, a flipper with 10,000 gold can out-earn a farmer with twice the playtime. This guide covers the margin math, the best flip categories, and the timing rules that make flipping profitable.

> **⚠️ Market status (Aug 2026):** as of build 0.30.0 the **Auction House is temporarily down for a rebuild** — live player-to-player trading runs through the reintroduced **vending stalls**. The fee structure below describes the AH system and flips work the same way at stalls (minus the listing fees); see the [Trading Market Guide](/guides/trading-market-guide) for current status.

## Flipping Fundamentals

Flipping is one rule with three parts: **buy underpriced, hold briefly, sell above your all-in cost.** The formula every flip must survive:

```
Net Profit = Sale Price − Buy Price − Listing Fee (5%) − Success Fee (5%)
```

| Fee Type | Amount | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Listing fee (Direct Sale) | 5% of start price | Non-refundable |
| Listing fee (Timed Auction) | 2% of start price | Cheaper — use for rare items |
| Success fee | 5% of final sale | Deducted at sale |
| Bulk Listing (7 days) | 8% | Only for stackables |
| Premium Pass discount | −50% all fees | Cash shop or achievements |

**The 25% rule:** if your expected net margin is under 25% after fees, it is not a flip — it is a gamble. Move to the next item.

## The Best Flip Categories

| Category | Buy From | Sell To | Net Margin | Risk | Volume |
|----------|----------|---------|------------|------|--------|
| Crafting Reagents | Gatherers (evening supply flood) | Crafters | 50-80% | Low | Very High |
| Refinement Stones | Dungeon farmers | Endgame raiders | 40-60% | Low | High |
| Epic Gear (low rolls) | Heroic dungeon runners | Disenchanters | 20-35% | Medium | Medium |
| Rare Card Drops | World boss trains | Collection buyers | 100-200% | High | Low |
| Cosmetic Skins | Loot box sellers | Fashion buyers | 200-500% | Very High | Very Low |

**The two-tier strategy:** run a high-volume, low-margin book (reagents, Refinement Stones) for steady income, and keep a small high-margin book (cards, cosmetics) for the spikes.

## Timing: When the Market Bleeds and Feasts

| Day/Period | Market State | Flipper Action |
|------------|--------------|----------------|
| Evenings & weekends | Peak farming — supply floods in | **Buy** — sellers undercut to move stock |
| Monday raid lockout reset | Demand spikes for consumables/gear | **Sell** — the feast day |
| Patch days | Old meta crashes, new meta spikes | Buy old-meta gear cheap; sell new-meta early |
| Off-peak (late night) | Thin supply, desperate buyers | Sell at a premium if listed already |

**The cycle in one line:** buy on evenings and weekends when supply peaks, sell on Monday resets when demand peaks.

## Undercutting: Win Without the War

Undercutting wars are where flippers lose margins to each other. The counter-discipline:

1. **Never list at the current lowest price.** List at the second-lowest, or use bulk lots that undercut per-unit without starting a visible war.
2. **Use odd lot sizes.** A stack of 13 at a slightly lower per-unit price outsells a stack of 20 at the same price — buyers see the smaller number first.
3. **Sell in small stacks to budget buyers.** List crafting materials in 10-20 unit lots to capture the players who cannot afford bulk.
4. **Let the war burn out.** If someone is racing you down, pull your listing and wait a day — the price recovers faster than your patience costs.

## Flip Candidates: What to Watch

Build a watchlist from these signals:

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Item swings >30% in a week | Flip candidate | Buy the dip, sell the spike |
| New raid tier announced | Old gear will crash | Sell old-meta now, buy after the crash |
| World boss train schedule | Card drop supply spike | Buy during the train, sell after it ends |
| Seasonal event live | Event materials flood in | Buy cheap, hold 2 weeks, sell post-event |
| Premium Pass holders flooding | Fee-reduced sellers dump stock | Buy — their volume is your discount |

**Track price history** with the in-game market tracker — items that swing more than 30% are the flip candidates; steady items are storage.

## The Flipping Playbook by Capital Stage

### Stage 1: 0-10,000 Gold — The Reagent Trader
- Flip crafting reagents exclusively (50-80% margins, near-zero risk).
- List in 10-20 unit stacks; never touch cards or cosmetics.
- Goal: 10,000 gold in a week of disciplined flipping.

### Stage 2: 10,000-100,000 Gold — The Gear Flipper
- Add epic weapons (they sell fastest) and Refinement Stones.
- Buy bulk from dungeon farmers at off-peak prices, resell in smaller lots.
- Invest in the Premium Pass to halve every fee — it pays for itself in ~20 flips.

### Stage 3: 100,000+ Gold — The Market Player
- Corner one slow-moving material during off-peak hours and resell at peak.
- Use alt characters to multiply daily listing slots.
- Trade rare card drops — high margin, and your capital now absorbs the risk.

## Flip Mistakes That Cost Gold

| Mistake | Why It Hurts |
|----------|--------------|
| Listing at the lowest price | Starts an undercutting war that burns your own margin |
| Ignoring patch notes | New content shifts demand; old-stock flips become losses |
| Over-investing in one item | One market shift strands your entire capital |
| Forgetting fees | A 5% fee on a slow item can erase a 20% margin |
| Hoarding through a meta change | Gear that was worth 100k is worth 20k after a rebalance |
