# SpiritVale Cards Guide — How to Farm, Slot, and Combine Cards

**SpiritVale EA / Updated: August 9, 2026**

Cards are the primary horizontal progression system in SpiritVale. There are 227 cards in the game, and a well-optimized card setup can double your effective combat power. This guide covers everything about the card system — from basics to slot plans, upgrade math, and rare card farming routes.

---

## Card System Basics

Cards are equippable items that slot into your gear. Each piece of equipment has a set number of card slots.

| Gear Slot | Max Card Slots | Unlock Level |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| Weapon | 2 | 8 |
| Armor | 2 | 8 |
| Accessory 1 | 1 | 8 |
| Accessory 2 | 1 | 8 |

### Card Rarity

| Rarity | Color | Stat Range | Drop Rate |
|--------|-------|------------|-----------|
| Common | Gray | Small | Very common |
| Uncommon | Green | Small to moderate | Common |
| Rare | Blue | Moderate | Uncommon |
| Epic | Purple | Large | Rare |
| Legendary | Orange | Very large | Very rare |

## Slot Anatomy: What Goes Where

Every piece of equipment has card slots: **Weapon (2), Armor (2), Accessory 1 (1), Accessory 2 (1)** — six slots total, all unlockable from Level 8.

The rule for slotting: **weapon slots are for damage stats, armor slots are for survival, accessory slots are for flexibility.** A legendary defensive card in a weapon slot is a wasted slot.

| Slot | Best Stat | Best Card Type |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Weapon 1 | Main damage stat (STR/INT/AGI) | Your class's primary stat |
| Weapon 2 | Secondary damage or attack speed | ASPD (remember the 193 ASPD cap) |
| Armor 1 | VIT / HP | Pure survival |
| Armor 2 | Defense or resistance | Elemental resistance for current content |
| Accessory 1 | Utility | Move speed, drop rate, or class-specific |
| Accessory 2 | Flex | Counter a boss's resistance (swap per fight) |

### Stat Priority by Advanced Class

| Advanced Class | Priority 1 | Priority 2 | Priority 3 |
|----------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Paladin | VIT | STR | HP |
| Berserker | STR | ASPD | VIT |
| Priest | INT (heal power) | VIT | Cast speed |
| Wizard | INT | ASPD | Crit |
| Gunslinger | AGI | ASPD | Crit |
| Shinobi | AGI | ASPD | Move speed |
| Necromancer | INT | VIT | Pet damage |
| Weaver | [Unconfirmed] | [Unconfirmed] | [Unconfirmed] |

**Two caps to plan around:** the ASPD cap (193) means over-stacking attack speed cards is wasted budget on fast classes like Shinobi — reallocate to crit. And elemental cards matter more in raids than in open-world farming; the [Endgame Guide](/guides/endgame-guide) explains which bosses resist which elements.

## How to Obtain Cards

### 1. Monster Drops
Every monster in SpiritVale has a chance to drop its associated card. The drop rate varies by monster rarity:
- **Normal mobs:** low chance
- **Elite mobs:** modest chance
- **Bosses:** good chance (guaranteed minimum Uncommon)

### 2. Card Exchange NPC
Located in Ironforge Outpost. Trade 10 duplicate cards of the **same card** for 1 random card of the next rarity tier.

### 3. Card Forge
The Card Forge (unlocked via quest at Level 20) allows:
- Combine 5 cards of the **same rarity** → 1 random card of next rarity
- 100% success rate, no materials needed beyond the cards

### 4. Event Drops
Limited-time events and daily login rewards sometimes grant exclusive cards.

## Forge vs. Exchange — The Upgrade Math

There are two ways to convert duplicates into higher rarities, and they have different costs:

| Method | Cost | Result | Best Use |
|--------|------|--------|----------|
| Card Forge (Level 20+) | 5 cards of same rarity | 1 random card of next rarity (always succeeds) | Converting *useless* cards — anything you won't slot |
| Card Exchange NPC (Ironforge Outpost) | 10 duplicates of the **same card** | 1 random card of the next rarity | Converting *useful* cards — you keep the card identity |

**The strategy:** never exchange cards you want to keep slotting; always forge trash into value. A full Epic set is realistic for a casual player in a few weeks if you forge every Common and Uncommon drop. Legendary requires boss farming — see the routes below.

## How to Equip Cards

1. Open your **Inventory** (I) and select the **Cards** tab.
2. Right-click a card, then click **Slot into Equipment**.
3. Select the gear piece to install it.
4. Cards can be removed with a **Card Remover** tool (sold at General Goods NPC for 500 gold).

## Rare Card Farming Routes

### Whisperwind Plains (Level 15-25)
- **Iron Boar Card** — Common, starter ATK card
- **Wind Runner Card** — Common, movement speed
- **Queen Bee Card (Rare)** — Bee Hive area, low drop rate

### Sunken Hollow (Level 25-35)
- **Minotaur Card (Rare)** — Minotaur spawn in central cave
- **Lava Elemental Card (Epic)** — Lava pit area, rare
- **Cave Bat Card (Uncommon)** — Cave entrance, high density

### Soulfire Citadel Exterior (Level 42-50)
- **Void Mage Card (Epic)** — Citadel courtyard
- **Arcane Wraith Card (Rare)** — Citadel perimeter
- **Soulkeeper Card (Legendary)** — Citadel boss area, extremely rare

### Endgame Farming
- **Rare/Epic:** Elite camps and dungeon bosses — farm the dungeon daily (see the [Daily Quests Guide](/guides/daily-quests-guide)) for the most boss kills per hour
- **Legendary:** Raid bosses and world bosses — the best odds are on Tier 3-4 raids, tied to weekly raid resets

## Common Mistakes

1. **Slotting before planning.** A legendary of the wrong stat is worth less than a rare of the right one. Decide your stat priority before spending.
2. **Exchanging 10 dupes of a slotted card.** You lose a card you use. Forge identical spares only when you have more than 2 copies.
3. **Ignoring event cards.** Limited-time events and daily login rewards occasionally grant exclusive cards that beat boss drops of the same rarity — hoard them, they rarely return.
4. **ASPD overshoot.** Attack speed cards are great until you hit the 193 cap; past it, every ASPD card is dead weight. Re-slot to crit or utility.
5. **No flex accessory.** Raid bosses have resistances; keeping one spare accessory loaded with a counter-element card costs you nothing and saves wipes.
